## ExyliaSurvivalCore

Both plugins run side by side. When ExyliaSpawners is installed, ExyliaSurvivalCore switches its own spawner
features off by itself:

- its spawner stacking in the optimisation module does not start, and the console says
  "Spawner stacking is off: ExyliaSpawners handles it";
- its item spawners module yields to ExyliaSpawners and stays off.

Its mob and item stacking keep working. Item spawners are built here instead, as `ITEM` types. See
[Types and tiers](/docs/exyliaspawners/types-and-tiers#item-types).

## ExyliaMarket

- **Prices**: storage sells at the prices ExyliaLib's price registry quotes, which ExyliaMarket's worth list and
  shop provide. See [ExyliaMarket prices](/docs/exyliamarket/prices).
- **Sell wands**: an ExyliaMarket sell wand used on a spawner sells its storage at the wand's multiplier. See
  [Sell wands](/docs/exyliaspawners/storage-and-selling#sell-wands).

Any other plugin that quotes prices through ExyliaLib, or uses its sell targets for a wand, works the same way.

## Economy

Sales pay, and upgrades and cleansing charge, through ExyliaLib's economy: [ExyliaEconomy](/docs/exyliaeconomy/introduction)
currencies by id, or the server's default currency (ExyliaEconomy or Vault). A blank `currency` is always the default
one.

## ExyliaMobs

`MOB` types spawn ExyliaMobs templates in CLASSIC mode. See
[Mob types](/docs/exyliaspawners/types-and-tiers#mob-types).

## Claims and regions

ExyliaProtections, WorldGuard, ExyliaLib regions, Lands, GriefPrevention, Towny and SuperiorSkyblock2 are asked
before a spawner is placed, mined, stacked or opened, through ExyliaLib build zones. See
[Trust and protection](/docs/exyliaspawners/trust-and-protection#claims-and-regions).

## Mob stackers

A CLASSIC spawner spawns ordinary mobs with the `SPAWNER` reason, each marked with the persistent data key
`exyliaspawners:spawner`, so a mob stacker stacks them like any spawner mob. The nearby limit counts mobs of the
spawner's type within eight blocks, stacked or not. VIRTUAL spawners spawn nothing, so a mob stacker never sees them.

## Floodgate

Bedrock players, told apart through Floodgate, get a **Collect everything** button in the storage screen in place of
shift-clicks. Without Floodgate, nobody is treated as a Bedrock player.

## PlaceholderAPI

ExyliaSpawners registers no `%exyliaspawners_...%` placeholders of its own. PlaceholderAPI placeholders work inside
the hologram's lines. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaspawners/placeholders).
