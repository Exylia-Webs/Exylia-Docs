## Is ExyliaSpawners free?

No. It is a premium plugin bought on [BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/exylia-spawners.136376/) and installed as a loader jar that checks the licence on every start. The licence is embedded in that jar, so there is no key to enter. See [Installation](/docs/exyliaspawners/installation).

## My spawner stopped producing

Open the spawner and read its header:

- **Waiting for a player**: nobody is within 16 blocks (`production.activation-range`). Spectators do not count.
- **Corrupted** with the halt effect: it waits to be cleansed. Hold crouch next to it. See [Corruption](/docs/exyliaspawners/corruption).
- **Storage full**: what a cycle makes and does not fit is lost. Take it, sell it or buy the storage upgrade. See [Take and sell](/docs/exyliaspawners/storage-and-selling).
- The type was disabled or removed from `types.yml`: its spawners stay but produce nothing.

## Why did my spawner corrupt?

Corruption is on by default. A spawner corrupts after a random time of running, counted only while a player is near it: 6 to 12 hours at tier 1, 4 to 8 at tier 2, 3 to 6 at tier 3 (higher tiers use the 6 to 12 default). A cleanse protects it for 30 minutes. To switch it off, set `corruption.enabled: false`, or `effect: NONE` on the tiers you want spared. See [Corruption](/docs/exyliaspawners/corruption).

## I cannot mine the spawner

- You need a pickaxe with Silk Touch (`mining.tools`, `mining.require-silk-touch`; `exyliaspawners.nosilk` skips the Silk Touch check).
- Only the owner, trusted players and staff can mine it, and the claim must allow breaking. See [Trust and protection](/docs/exyliaspawners/trust-and-protection).
- Mining the last spawner of a stack that still holds items or experience asks you to confirm: it collects the storage and picks the stack up in one step (`mining.require-empty-storage`). With that option off, the storage is lost with the spawner.
- With `mining.require-type-permission` on, you also need `exyliaspawners.mine.<type>`, which only operators hold by default.

## The spawner I mined disappeared

With `mining.chance` below 100, a failed roll destroys it ("The spawner shattered"). With the default 100, look in your inventory: mined spawners go there, not to the ground (`mining.to-inventory`). See [Spawners](/docs/exyliaspawners/spawners).

## Somebody sold my spawner's storage

Only you, your trusted players and staff can open it, and every sale pays you, whoever sells. Check the trust list on the spawner and run `/exylialib logs <player> ExyliaSpawners` to see who did what. See [Trust and protection](/docs/exyliaspawners/trust-and-protection).

## Does a trusted player get the money when they sell?

No. Sales always pay the spawner's owner, also when a trusted player, a sell wand or autosell sells. The receipt goes to whoever sold. See [Take and sell](/docs/exyliaspawners/storage-and-selling).

## `/spawners sell` says I have no permission

It needs `exyliaspawners.remote`, which nobody has by default, as does selling with a click in `/spawners`. Selling from the spawner's own screen needs no extra permission.

## Autosell does not sell

It sells every `selling.autosell-interval` (60 seconds by default), only while the owner is online on that server and holds `exyliaspawners.autosell` (nobody by default), and only items that have a price.

## Items do not sell

Storage sells at the prices ExyliaLib's price registry quotes, from ExyliaMarket's worth list or shop, or from another plugin that provides prices. An item without a price stays in the storage. See [Take and sell](/docs/exyliaspawners/storage-and-selling).

## A player placed a copied spawner

They cannot. Every spawner item carries a unique id that is spent once. A copy is refused, the player is told, and the attempt is logged as `spawner-item-rejected` with the item's id.

## Will my upgrades survive mining the spawner?

Only while spawners stay on the block. Upgrade levels belong to the stack, so mining or picking up the last spawner loses them; the confirmation says so. See [Upgrades and boosters](/docs/exyliaspawners/upgrades-and-boosters).

## I removed SmartSpawner, WildStacker or another plugin. Can I keep the spawners?

Import them before removing the old plugin's files: `/spawnersadmin import <source> -dry`, then again without `-dry`. See [Importing](/docs/exyliaspawners/importing).

## Does it run with ExyliaSurvivalCore?

Yes. ExyliaSurvivalCore turns off its own spawner stacking and item spawners when ExyliaSpawners is installed. See [Integrations](/docs/exyliaspawners/integrations#exyliasurvivalcore).

## What happens when a player is reset?

When ExyliaLib's player reset deletes a player's spawners from the database, those spawner blocks are also taken out of the world, so they cannot be written back or picked up later. A reset that keeps them leaves them in place.
