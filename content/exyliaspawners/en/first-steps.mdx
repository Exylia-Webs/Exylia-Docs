---
title: "First steps"
description: "From the first spawner to selling its storage: give, place, stack, open, sell, upgrade and mine it, in five minutes."
---

This walk-through uses the files exactly as they are generated. Join as an operator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a spawner">
    `/spawnersadmin give <you> zombie 3` hands you one item holding three zombie spawners of tier 1. The
    item's name says how many it holds: `ZOMBIE SPAWNER [3x]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Place the item like any block. The three spawners become one stack in one block, owned by you. A
    hologram over it shows the type, the stack, the tier and how full the storage is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stack more">
    Get another item with `/spawnersadmin give <you> zombie 5` and right-click the placed spawner with it:
    one spawner goes in. Sneak and right-click and the rest of the item goes in, up to the tier's stack limit
    (64 for tier 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it produce">
    Stand within 16 blocks. Every 20 seconds the stack makes two zombies per spawner. No zombie appears: their
    rotten flesh, iron, carrots and potatoes go into the storage, and their experience with them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Right-click the spawner with an empty hand. The hub shows the stack, the owner, the mode, the time until
    the next cycle and how full the storage is, all moving while it is open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take and sell">
    Open **Storage**. Click an item to take one, right-click for a stack, shift-click for all of it. **Claim
    experience** repairs your Mending items first. **Sell all** sells everything that has a price; with
    ExyliaMarket installed its worth list sets the prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade it">
    Open **Upgrades** from the hub. Each of the four tracks (speed, amount, storage, experience) shows its
    current and next value and the price of the next level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mine it">
    Mine the spawner with a pickaxe that has Silk Touch: one spawner leaves the stack and goes into your
    inventory. Sneak while mining to take the whole stack. The last spawner cannot be mined while the storage
    holds anything, so empty it first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Next

- Change what spawners drop, their speed and their limits in [Types and tiers](/docs/exyliaspawners/types-and-tiers).
- Corruption is on by default: a spawner left running for hours stops until it is cleansed. Read
  [Corruption](/docs/exyliaspawners/corruption) before players meet it.
- Give players `exyliaspawners.autosell` and `exyliaspawners.remote` if you want autosell and selling
  from `/spawners`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaspawners/permissions).
