By the end you will have a zombie spawner that produces loot into its storage, which you take and sell. This uses the files exactly as ExyliaSpawners generates them. Join as an operator.

## Before you start

ExyliaSpawners and ExyliaLib installed, and the server started once. See [Installation](/docs/exyliaspawners/installation).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a spawner">
    Run `/spawnersadmin give <your name> zombie 3`. You get one item holding three tier 1 zombie spawners, named `ZOMBIE SPAWNER [3x]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Place the item like any block. The three spawners become one stack in one block, owned by you. A hologram over it shows the type, the stack, the tier and how full the storage is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stack more">
    Run `/spawnersadmin give <your name> zombie 5`, then right-click the placed spawner with the item: one spawner goes in. Sneak and right-click to put in the rest, up to the tier's stack limit (64 for tier 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it produce">
    Stand within 16 blocks. Every 20 seconds the stack makes two zombies' worth of loot per spawner. No zombie appears: their rotten flesh, iron, carrots and potatoes go into the storage, and their experience with them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Right-click the spawner with an empty hand. The hub shows the stack, the owner, the mode, the time until the next cycle and how full the storage is, all moving while it is open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take and sell">
    Open **Storage**. Click an item to take one, right-click for a stack, shift-click for all of it. **Claim experience** repairs your Mending items first. **Sell all** sells everything that has a price: with ExyliaMarket installed its worth list sets the prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade it">
    Open **Upgrades** from the hub. Each of the four tracks (speed, amount, storage, experience) shows its current and next value and the price of the next level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mine it">
    Mine the spawner with a pickaxe that has Silk Touch: one spawner leaves the stack and goes into your inventory. Sneak while mining to take the whole stack. The last spawner cannot be mined while the storage holds anything, so empty it first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Wait one cycle (20 seconds) next to the spawner and open **Storage**: it lists items and shows experience. If it stays empty, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliaspawners/troubleshooting).

<Callout type="warning" title="Corruption is on by default">
A spawner left running for hours stops until a player cleanses it. Read [Corruption](/docs/exyliaspawners/corruption) before players meet it.
</Callout>
