## Upgrades

The **Upgrades** screen of a spawner has one card per track: speed, amount, storage and experience. Each shows
the current level and its highest, the value now, the value of the next level and its price, all live. A click
buys the next level.

- The player who clicks pays, whoever owns the spawner. Anyone who may open the spawner may upgrade it.
- One purchase per spawner and track at a time: a second click while the first is still paying is told an
  upgrade is already under way. A level is charged once; if the spawner changed meanwhile, or a plugin cancels
  [`SpawnerUpgradeEvent`](/docs/exyliaspawners/api), the price goes back.
- Levels belong to the stack: every spawner in it, including the ones stacked later, works at those levels.
  Mining the last spawner loses them.

The value shown already counts the stack: the amount card says the mobs the whole stack makes per cycle, the
storage card the items the whole stack holds.

### Costs

Each tier writes, per track, what its levels cost under `upgrades` in `tiers.yml`:

```yaml
tiers:
  '2':
    # ...
    upgrades:
      speed:
        cost: 5000 * (%level% - 1) ^ 2
        currency: ''
        permission: ''
        requirements:
          stack: 16
        levels:
          '5':
            cost: '64'
            currency: diamonds
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `cost` | A formula for the price of the level being bought. `%level%` is that level: 2 for the first upgrade. The default `2500 * (%level% - 1) ^ 2` charges 2,500, 10,000, 22,500 and 40,000. |
| `currency` | An ExyliaLib economy currency id, or an item currency; blank is the server's default currency. |
| `permission` | Needed to buy any level of this track; blank for none. Without it the card says "Locked for your rank". |
| `requirements` | Minimums to buy: `stack` (spawners in the stack) and `tier`. |
| `levels` | Single levels written on their own, each with its own `cost`, `currency`, `permission` and `requirements`. |

The highest level of a track is the number of values the tier lists for it. A formula the economy cannot read is
reported in the console when the plugin loads. A track with no `upgrades` entry costs the built-in
`1000 * (%level% - 1) ^ 2`.

## Boosters

A booster is an item a player inserts into a spawner from its **Boosters** screen. It multiplies one thing for a
time:

| Kind | Multiplies |
|---|---|
| `SPEED` | Cycles: a 2× speed booster halves the delay. |
| `LOOT` | Items each produced mob drops. |
| `XP` | Experience each produced mob gives. |
| `MONEY` | What selling the storage pays. |

One booster of each kind per spawner, so up to four at once. The screen has one card per kind with its live
countdown. A click on an empty card inserts a booster of that kind from the main hand, else from anywhere in the
inventory; a click on a running one takes it out and hands back an item with the time left. **A booster's time
only runs while it is inside**, so it can be moved to another spawner without losing anything. When the whole
stack leaves (mined, picked up), running boosters come back as items to whoever broke it.

### `boosters.yml`

The boosters staff can hand out, by id:

```yaml
boosters:
  speed_2x_30m:
    kind: SPEED
    multiplier: 2.0
    duration: 30m
  loot_2x_30m:
    kind: LOOT
    multiplier: 2.0
    duration: 30m
  xp_2x_30m:
    kind: XP
    multiplier: 2.0
    duration: 30m
  money_1_5x_1h:
    kind: MONEY
    multiplier: 1.5
    duration: 1h
```

`/spawnersadmin booster give <player> <id> [amount]` hands up to 64 at once. Shops and crates give them by
running that command. The items look like `lang/<language>/booster-item.yml` says, one entry per kind, with the
placeholders `%kind%`, `%kind_upper%`, `%multiplier%` and `%time%` (the time left on the item). Boosters are
recognised by values stored on the item, not by their name.

### Global and personal boosts

Spawners are a source in ExyliaLib's modifiers, listed as `spawners` with the money, drops and experience kinds.
A booster plugin, a rank perk or a server event that boosts that source multiplies, for the spawner's **owner**:
the drops of every cycle, its experience, and what its sales pay. They stack with the spawner's own boosters.
