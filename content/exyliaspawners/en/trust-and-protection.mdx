---
title: "Trust and protection"
description: "Owners, trusted players, staff access, ownerless spawners, and the claim and region plugins asked through ExyliaLib build zones."
---

Two checks guard every spawner, and both must allow an action: the spawner's own **owner and trusted players**,
and the **claim and region plugins** of the server.

## Owners

Whoever places a spawner owns it. The owner:

- opens, mines and stacks it, and cleanses it when it corrupts;
- is paid for every sale of its storage, whoever sells (see
  [Who gets paid](/docs/exyliaspawners/storage-and-selling#who-gets-paid));
- gets the corruption notices, and is the player whose global boosts apply to it;
- is the only one, besides staff, who decides who else is trusted.

## Trusted players

The **Trusted players** card of the hub opens the trust list: every trusted player with the date they were added.
**Trust a player** asks for a name; right-clicking a player on the list stops trusting them, after a confirmation.
The same from chat, looking at the spawner (within six blocks):

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/spawners trust <player>` | Trusts that player on the spawner you look at. |
| `/spawners untrust <player>` | Stops trusting them. |

The name must belong to a player who is online or has played on the server. Only the owner and staff can change
the list; trusted players see it read-only.

A trusted player can open the spawner, take from and sell its storage (the money still goes to the owner), buy
upgrades with their own money, insert boosters, stack, mine and cleanse it. The list is kept per spawner in the
database and goes away with the last spawner of the stack.

## Staff

Players with `exyliaspawners.admin` pass the trust check on every spawner: they can open, mine and stack any of
them. They still need the claim plugins to allow it.

## Ownerless spawners

A vanilla spawner converted on first touch, and a spawner brought in by an import that kept no owner, has
**no owner**. Until it gets one, anyone the claim plugins allow can open, mine and sell it, and its sales pay the
player who sells. The first player to stack a spawner onto it or change it with a spawn egg becomes its owner.

<Callout type="warning" title="Imported spawners">
SmartSpawner, WildStacker and RoseStacker keep no owner, so every spawner imported from them starts ownerless. In
a claim, the claim plugin still decides who may touch it; in the wild, claim the important ones by stacking onto
them. See [Importing](/docs/exyliaspawners/importing).
</Callout>

## Claims and regions

Before a spawner is placed, mined, stacked or opened, ExyliaSpawners asks the region and claim plugins through
ExyliaLib's build zones:

| Action | Asked as |
|---|---|
| Placing a spawner | building |
| Stacking onto one | building |
| Mining it | breaking |
| Opening it (and selling with a wand, cleansing) | opening a container |

```yaml
protection:
  enabled: true
  sources:
  - WORLDGUARD
  - EXYLIA_REGIONS
  - EXYLIA_PROTECTIONS
  - LANDS
  - GRIEF_PREVENTION
  - TOWNY
  - SUPERIOR_SKYBLOCK
```

| Source | Denies when |
|---|---|
| `WORLDGUARD` | WorldGuard's own build test with the action's flag says no. |
| `EXYLIA_REGIONS` | An ExyliaLib region declares `build`, `break` or `interact: false`. |
| `EXYLIA_PROTECTIONS` | An [ExyliaProtections](/docs/exyliaprotections/introduction) protection lacks the player's permission. |
| `LANDS` | The player's role in a Lands area lacks the action's flag. |
| `GRIEF_PREVENTION` | A claim lacks the player's build, container or access trust. |
| `TOWNY` | Towny's build, destroy or switch permission says no. |
| `SUPERIOR_SKYBLOCK` | A SuperiorSkyblock2 island lacks the player's privilege. |

A source that is not installed always allows; an unknown name in the list is ignored. `protection.enabled: false`
stops asking any of them, leaving only the owner and trust check.
