ExyliaSpawners turns spawners into a survival economy that cannot be duplicated. A spawner is mined with
Silk Touch and goes straight into the player's inventory, stacks into one block, and produces while its
owner is near: loot and experience land in a storage with a cap instead of mobs on the floor. From one
screen the owner takes the loot, claims the experience, sells everything, buys upgrades, inserts boosters
and trusts friends. A spawner left running for hours slowly **corrupts** and has to be cleansed on site.

It is a **premium** plugin, installed through a licence loader. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliaspawners/installation).

## How a spawner works

- **Obtaining.** Mining with a pickaxe that carries Silk Touch gives the spawner as an item, straight into
  the inventory. Explosions take one spawner off a stack. Dungeon spawners become ExyliaSpawners the first
  time someone clicks or mines them. Staff give spawners with a command.
- **Stacking.** Right-clicking a placed spawner with a matching item adds one; sneaking adds the whole item.
  The stack produces as one block, so a farm of a hundred spawners is one block and one storage.
- **Producing.** While a player is within 16 blocks, every cycle the stack makes mobs. In the default
  **VIRTUAL** mode no mob appears: their loot and experience go into the storage. **CLASSIC** mode spawns
  real mobs next to the block instead.
- **Selling.** Storage sells at the price the server's shops and worth lists quote, through ExyliaLib.
  Money always goes to the spawner's owner, whoever sells.

## Built not to be duplicated

- Every spawner item carries a unique id recorded in the database. An item placed twice, copied, or edited
  is refused and reported to staff, also across servers.
- Taking, selling and claiming experience are transactions on the storage: a double click, two players on
  the same screen or a repeated packet never pay twice.
- Pistons, explosions and chunk unloads cannot move or delete a spawner behind the plugin's back, and
  production is written to the database on a timer, on chunk unload and on shutdown.
