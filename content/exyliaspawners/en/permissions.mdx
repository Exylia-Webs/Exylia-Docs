Players can place, stack and open spawners without any setup. Autosell and selling from anywhere are off until you grant them, and only operators get `/spawnersadmin`. Give a node with your permissions plugin, for example with LuckPerms: `/lp group vip permission set exyliaspawners.autosell true`.

## Gate spawner types by rank

`exyliaspawners.mine.*` and `exyliaspawners.place.*` are granted to operators only, but the per-type check is off by default: turn on `mining.require-type-permission` or `placement.require-type-permission` in `config.yml` and players then need `exyliaspawners.mine.<type>` or `exyliaspawners.place.<type>` (for example `exyliaspawners.place.blaze`) or the wildcard. Grant the wildcard to the groups that should keep mining and placing everything, and the single types to the rest.

## Numbered permissions

For `stack`, `limit` and `chance` the highest number a player holds wins. A `stack.<n>` never lowers the limit of the type or tier, and a `chance.<n>` never lowers `mining.chance`. A `limit.<n>` replaces `placement.player-limit`, so it can also be lower. A player limit counts blocks, not spawners: a stack of 64 is one block. In network mode it counts the player's blocks on every server.
