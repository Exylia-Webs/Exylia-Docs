Place a spawner, stack more into it, open it and mine it back. This page also covers the limits you can set and what happens on explosions, spawn eggs and vanilla spawners.

## Before you start

You need ExyliaSpawners running and a spawner item. Operators get one with `/spawnersadmin give <player> <type> [amount] [tier]`; the same item is what players buy or receive from you. See [Your first spawner](/docs/exyliaspawners/first-steps).

## The spawner item

A spawner item holds a type, a tier and a number of spawners. One item can hold many: `/spawnersadmin give <player> zombie 500` hands over one item of 500. Items do not merge with each other in the inventory (each stacks to 1).

Its look comes from `lang/<language>/spawner-item.yml` (an ExyliaLib item, see [Menus](/docs/exylialib/edit-menus)). Restyle it freely: spawners are recognised by the values stored on the item, never by its name. The material is always `SPAWNER`.

<Callout type="note" title="Copies are refused">
Each item is recorded when it is made and spent when it is placed or stacked. An item that was already used, was never handed out, or no longer matches its record is refused with "This spawner is not valid", and the attempt goes to the audit log as `spawner-item-rejected`. On a [network](/docs/exyliaspawners/network), if two servers race to place the same copy, one spawner is kept and the second is taken back.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Place the item like any block. The block becomes a spawner owned by the player, holding every spawner of the item. An item holding more than the stack limit places a full stack and hands the rest back as a new item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stack more">
    Right-click the placed spawner with an item of the same type and tier: one spawner goes in. Sneak and right-click to put in the whole item, up to the stack limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Right-click the spawner with anything else. The hub opens: **Storage**, **Upgrades**, **Boosters**, **Trusted players**, **Corruption**, **Sell all** and **Pick up**. Sneak with a block in hand to place the block instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mine it back">
    Mine it with a pickaxe that has Silk Touch. One spawner leaves the stack and goes into your inventory; sneak while mining to take the whole stack. Or click **Pick up** in the hub (it asks to confirm) to take the whole stack from up to six blocks away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only the owner, their [trusted players](/docs/exyliaspawners/trust-and-protection) and staff can open, stack or mine a spawner. Every spawner in a stack shares the stack's tier, upgrade levels and storage; the storage cap grows with the stack.

## Check that it works

Place a spawner with `/spawnersadmin give <your name> zombie 3`: a hologram appears over it with the type and stack. Right-click it and the hub opens. Mine it with a Silk Touch pickaxe and the item comes back into your inventory.

## Going further

### Placing limits

Placing runs these checks in order:

1. The type exists and is enabled in `types.yml`.
2. The player has `exyliaspawners.use`, and `exyliaspawners.place.<type>` when `placement.require-type-permission` is on (off by default; operators hold `place.*`).
3. Every claim and region plugin allows building. See [claims and regions](/docs/exyliaspawners/trust-and-protection#claims-and-regions).
4. The chunk holds fewer spawner blocks than `placement.chunk-limit` (default 0, no limit).
5. The player owns fewer spawner blocks than `placement.player-limit` (default 0, no limit), or the highest `exyliaspawners.limit.<n>` they hold. In [network mode](/docs/exyliaspawners/network) this counts the blocks on every server.

### Stack size

The stack limit is the type's `stack-limit`, else the tier's, raised by the highest `exyliaspawners.stack.<n>` the player holds. See [Types and tiers](/docs/exyliaspawners/types-and-tiers).

### Mining rules

| Rule | Default |
|---|---|
| Tool must match `mining.tools` | `*_PICKAXE` |
| Tool must carry Silk Touch (`exyliaspawners.nosilk` skips it) | `mining.require-silk-touch: true` |
| Chance the spawner drops (a failed roll destroys it: "The spawner shattered") | `mining.chance: 100`, raised by `exyliaspawners.chance.<n>` |
| The last spawner of a stack cannot be mined while its storage holds items or experience | `mining.require-empty-storage: true` |
| Mined spawners go straight into the miner's inventory; what does not fit drops at their feet | `mining.to-inventory: true` |

Mining also needs `exyliaspawners.use`, `exyliaspawners.mine.<type>` when `mining.require-type-permission` is on (off by default), ownership or trust, and a claim that allows breaking. Players in creative mode skip the tool and Silk Touch checks.

<Callout type="warning" title="Upgrade levels belong to the block">
A spawner item carries a type, a tier and a count, not upgrade levels. Mining spawners off a stack leaves the levels on the block; mining or picking up the last one loses them. Boosters running in it come back as items.
</Callout>

### Explosions

With `explosions.enabled` on (the default), an explosion that reaches a spawner takes one spawner off its stack, and `explosions.drop-chance` (50 by default) decides whether it drops as an item. A spawner whose storage holds anything is never blown up. Claim plugins that protect a block from a blast are respected. With `explosions.enabled` off, explosions leave spawners alone.

### Spawn eggs

Off by default. With `egg-change.enabled: true`, right-clicking a spawner with a spawn egg changes its type to that mob's, using one egg per spawner in the stack (none in creative). It needs `exyliaspawners.egg` (everyone has it), the right to stack the spawner, and `exyliaspawners.place.<type>` when `placement.require-type-permission` is on. The vanilla egg change never happens on a plugin spawner.

### Vanilla spawners

With `conversion.enabled` on (the default), a vanilla spawner (a dungeon's, or one another plugin set) becomes an ExyliaSpawners spawner of tier `conversion.tier` (1 by default) when a type for its mob exists. `conversion.when` chooses the moment: `mine` (the default) converts it once it is mined, and until then it spawns real mobs like vanilla; `touch` converts it the first time a player clicks or mines it. Trial spawners are never touched. A converted spawner has no owner: the first player to stack onto it or change it with an egg becomes its owner. See [ownerless spawners](/docs/exyliaspawners/trust-and-protection#ownerless-spawners).

### What protects the block

- Vanilla spawning never runs on a plugin spawner: production goes through the plugin only.
- The block is registered with ExyliaLib's block protection, so pistons cannot push or pull it.
- A spawner whose block was replaced behind the plugin's back (WorldEdit, another plugin) is removed from the records the next time production looks at it, and the removal is written to the audit log.
