## The spawner item

A spawner item holds a type, a tier and a number of spawners, plus a unique id. One item can hold many
spawners: `/spawnersadmin give` with an amount of 500 hands over one item of 500. Items never merge with
each other in the inventory (each stacks to 1), because each one is a single record.

Its look comes from `lang/<language>/spawner-item.yml`, an ExyliaLib item with the placeholders `%type%`,
`%type_upper%`, `%tier%`, `%stack%` and `%mode%`. Restyle it freely: spawners are recognised by the values
stored on the item, never by their name. The material is always `SPAWNER`.

<Callout type="note" title="Copies are refused">
Every id is recorded when the item is made and spent when it is placed or stacked. An item whose id was
already used, was never handed out, or whose type, tier or count no longer match the record is refused with
"This spawner is not valid", and the attempt is written to the audit log as `spawner-item-rejected`. On a
network, two servers racing to place the same copy end with one spawner: the second one is taken back off.
</Callout>

## Placing

Placing the item makes a spawner block holding that many spawners, owned by the player. The checks, in
order:

1. The type still exists and is enabled in `types.yml`.
2. The player has `exyliaspawners.use`, and `exyliaspawners.place.<type>` when
   `placement.require-type-permission` is on.
3. Every claim and region plugin allows building there. See
   [Trust and protection](/docs/exyliaspawners/trust-and-protection#claims-and-regions).
4. The chunk has fewer spawner blocks than `placement.chunk-limit`.
5. The player owns fewer spawner blocks than their limit: `placement.player-limit`, or the highest
   `exyliaspawners.limit.<n>` they hold. In [network mode](/docs/exyliaspawners/network) this counts the
   player's spawner blocks on every server.

An item holding more spawners than the stack limit places a full stack and hands the rest back as a new item.

## Stacking

Right-clicking a placed spawner with an item of the **same type and tier** adds one spawner; sneaking adds the
whole item, up to the stack limit. The stack limit is the type's `stack-limit`, else the tier's, raised by the
highest `exyliaspawners.stack.<n>` the player holds. Stacking needs the same rights as opening the spawner
(owner or trusted, and the claim allowing building).

Every spawner in a stack shares the stack's tier, its upgrade levels and its storage, and the storage cap grows
with the stack.

## Opening

Right-clicking a spawner with anything else opens its hub: storage, upgrades, boosters, trusted players,
corruption and **Sell all**. Sneaking with a block in hand places the block instead. Only the owner, their
trusted players and staff can open a spawner. See [Storage and selling](/docs/exyliaspawners/storage-and-selling).

## Mining

| Rule | Default |
|---|---|
| The tool must match `mining.tools` | `*_PICKAXE` |
| The tool must carry Silk Touch | `mining.require-silk-touch: true` |
| Chance the spawner drops | `mining.chance: 100`, raised by `exyliaspawners.chance.<n>` |
| The last spawner of a stack cannot be mined while its storage holds items or experience | `mining.require-empty-storage: true` |
| Mined spawners go into the miner's inventory | `mining.to-inventory: true` |

A mine takes **one** spawner off the stack; sneaking takes all of them. The block only disappears with the last
one. With `mining.to-inventory` on, the item goes straight into the miner's inventory, and whatever does not
fit drops at their feet, so nobody standing near the block can pick it up. A failed chance roll destroys the
spawners mined with the message "The spawner shattered".

Players in creative mode skip the tool and Silk Touch checks. Mining needs `exyliaspawners.use`,
`exyliaspawners.mine.<type>` when `mining.require-type-permission` is on, ownership or trust, and the claim
allowing breaking.

The hub's **Pick up spawner** button (asks to confirm) takes the whole stack under the same rules, from up to
six blocks away, with the item in the main hand counting as the tool. It always hands the item over to the
inventory.

<Callout type="warning" title="Upgrade levels belong to the block">
A spawner item carries a type, a tier and a count, not upgrade levels. Mining spawners off a stack leaves the
levels on the block; mining or picking up the last one loses them. Boosters running in it come back as items.
</Callout>

## Explosions

With `explosions.enabled` on, an explosion that reaches a spawner takes **one** spawner off its stack, and
`explosions.drop-chance` (50 by default) decides whether it drops as an item where the block was. A spawner whose
storage holds anything is never blown up. Claim plugins that protect a block from a blast are respected. With
`explosions.enabled` off, explosions leave spawners alone.

## Spawn eggs

With `egg-change.enabled` on, right-clicking a spawner with a spawn egg changes its type to the type of that
mob, for one egg per spawner in the stack (none in creative). It needs `exyliaspawners.egg`, the right to stack
the spawner, and `exyliaspawners.place.<type>` when `placement.require-type-permission` is on. The vanilla egg
change never happens on a plugin spawner, so stacks and storage cannot be bypassed.

## Vanilla spawners

With `conversion.enabled` on (the default), a vanilla spawner (a dungeon's, or one another plugin set) becomes an
ExyliaSpawners spawner of tier `conversion.tier` the first time a player clicks or mines it, when a type for its
mob exists. Trial spawners are never touched. A converted spawner has no owner: the first player to stack onto it
or change it with an egg becomes its owner. Until then anyone the claims allow can use it. See
[Trust and protection](/docs/exyliaspawners/trust-and-protection#ownerless-spawners).

## What protects the block

- Vanilla spawning never runs on a plugin spawner: production goes through the plugin only.
- The block is registered with ExyliaLib's block protection, so pistons cannot push or pull it.
- A spawner whose block was replaced behind the plugin's back (WorldEdit, another plugin) is removed from the
  records the next time production looks at it, and the removal is written to the audit log.
