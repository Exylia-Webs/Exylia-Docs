## The storage

A VIRTUAL spawner keeps what it makes as counters: one per kind of item, plus one for experience. Two caps apply,
both for the whole stack: items in all (the tier's `storage` value × spawners in the stack) and experience (the
`xp` value × spawners). Upgrading the storage or experience track, or stacking more spawners, raises them.

Production is kept in memory and written to the database every `storage.flush-interval` (30 seconds), when its
chunk unloads and on shutdown. Taking, selling and claiming are written at once, as transactions: the same click
twice, two players on the same screen or a repeated packet never take or pay twice. Items are handed over only
once the transaction succeeded, and whatever does not fit in the inventory goes back into the storage.

## The storage screen

Open it from the spawner's hub (**Storage**). One row per stored item, live while the screen is open.

| Click on an item | Does |
|---|---|
| Left-click | Takes one. |
| Right-click | Takes a stack. |
| Shift-left-click | Takes all that fits. |
| Shift-right-click | Sells that item. |

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **Sell all** | Sells every item that has a price. |
| **Take all** | Moves as much as fits into the inventory. |
| **Claim experience** | Gives every stored point of experience. |
| **Autosell** | Turns autosell on or off for this spawner. |
| **Collect everything** | Bedrock players only (with Floodgate): every item that fits and all the experience, in one tap, since Bedrock has no shift-click. |

### Experience and Mending

With `collecting.mending-repair` on (the default), claimed experience repairs the player's Mending items first,
like an experience orb would, and the rest goes to the player.

### Full-to-Collect

With `collecting.full-to-collect` on, items can only be taken out once the storage is full; until then the
screen answers with how full it is. Selling is not affected.

## Prices

Storage sells through ExyliaLib's prices: whatever the server's shops and worth lists quote for each item,
in that quote's currency. With [ExyliaMarket](/docs/exyliamarket/prices) installed, its worth list and shop
set them. An item nobody quotes stays in the storage, and so does the remainder of an item quoted per lot
(quoted per 64, a stored 100 sells 64 and keeps 36).

What a sale pays is multiplied by the spawner's **money** booster, by the factor of a sell wand, and by any
global or personal money boost other plugins grant under the `spawners` source of ExyliaLib's modifiers (a rank
perk, a server-wide event). See [Upgrades and boosters](/docs/exyliaspawners/upgrades-and-boosters#boosters).

## Who gets paid

<Callout type="note" title="Sales always pay the owner">
Money from a spawner's storage goes to the spawner's **owner**, also when a trusted player sells from its
screen, when a sell wand sells it, and when autosell sells it. The receipt goes to whoever sold. Only a spawner
with no owner (a converted dungeon spawner nobody has claimed) pays the player who sold.
</Callout>

An offline owner is paid through the economy all the same. Every sale is written to the audit log and fires
[`SpawnerSellEvent`](/docs/exyliaspawners/api).

## Ways to sell

| Where | Needs | Sells |
|---|---|---|
| **Sell all** on the hub or the storage screen | Opening the spawner | Everything with a price. |
| Shift-right-click an item in the storage | Opening the spawner | That item. |
| A sell wand on the spawner | Opening the spawner | Everything with a price, at the wand's multiplier. |
| Autosell | `exyliaspawners.autosell` | Everything with a price, every `selling.autosell-interval`. |
| `/spawners sell` | `exyliaspawners.remote` | Every spawner the player owns on this server, with one receipt. |
| A click in `/spawners` | `exyliaspawners.remote` | That spawner, here or on another server of the network. |

### Autosell

Turned on per spawner from the storage screen; turning it on needs `exyliaspawners.autosell` (not granted by
default), turning it off does not. While on, the storage sells itself every `selling.autosell-interval`
(60 seconds), quietly, **only while the owner is online on this server and still holds the permission**. The
flag is kept in the database and survives restarts.

### Sell wands

Sell wands of any plugin that asks ExyliaLib's sell targets work on spawners: right-clicking a spawner with an
[ExyliaMarket sell wand](/docs/exyliamarket/sell-wands) sells its whole storage at the wand's multiplier. The
player using it must be allowed to open the spawner; anyone else
is told "You cannot sell this spawner". There is no separate spawner wand.

### My spawners and `/spawners sell`

`/spawners` lists every spawner the player owns, with its world, location, tier, mode, state, storage and
experience. With `exyliaspawners.remote`, clicking one sells its storage from wherever the player stands, and
`/spawners sell` sells all of this server's at once. With [network mode](/docs/exyliaspawners/network) the list
holds the player's spawners on every server, and a click sells on the server that owns it.
