Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

The console names what is missing. ExyliaSpawners needs ExyliaLib (Vault, PlaceholderAPI and the others are optional). See [Installation](/docs/exyliaspawners/installation).

## A spawner stopped producing

Look at the hub's header:

- **Waiting for a player**: nobody is within 16 blocks (`production.activation-range`). Spectators do not count.
- **Corrupted**, with the halt effect: it waits to be cleansed. Hold crouch next to it for 3 seconds. See [Corruption](/docs/exyliaspawners/corruption).
- **The storage is full**: whatever a cycle makes that does not fit is lost. Take items, sell them, or buy the storage upgrade.
- Its type was disabled or removed from `types.yml`: the spawner stays but produces nothing.

## A spawner corrupted

Corruption is on by default: a spawner corrupts after a random 3 to 12 hours of running time depending on its tier, counted only while a player is near. Cleansing protects it for 30 minutes. To switch it off, set `corruption.enabled: false` in `config.yml`, or `effect: NONE` for the tiers you want spared.

## I cannot mine a spawner

- It needs a pickaxe with Silk Touch (`mining.tools`, `mining.require-silk-touch`), or the `exyliaspawners.nosilk` permission.
- The last spawner of a stack cannot be mined while its storage holds items or experience: empty it first. The hub's **Pick up** button has the same rule.
- Only the owner, trusted players and staff can mine it, and the claim must allow breaking. See [Trust and protection](/docs/exyliaspawners/trust-and-protection).

## The spawner I mined is gone

With `mining.chance` below 100, a failed roll destroys it. With the default 100, look in your inventory: mined spawners go there, not on the ground.

## `/spawners sell` says I have no permission

It needs `exyliaspawners.remote`, which nobody has by default, and so does selling with a click in **My spawners**. Selling from the spawner's own screen needs no extra permission. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaspawners/permissions).

## Autosell does not sell

It sells every `selling.autosell-interval` (one minute by default), only while the owner is online on that server, holds `exyliaspawners.autosell`, and the items have a price. Items nobody quotes stay in the storage.

## Items do not sell

Storage sells at the prices ExyliaLib's price registry quotes, from ExyliaMarket's worth list or shop, or another plugin that provides prices. An item without a price stays in the storage. See [Storage and selling](/docs/exyliaspawners/storage-and-selling).

## A player cannot place or stack a spawner

They need `exyliaspawners.use`, a claim that allows building, and to be under the placement limit (`placement.player-limit`, raised by `exyliaspawners.limit.<n>`). If you turned on `placement.require-type-permission`, they also need `exyliaspawners.place.<type>`.
