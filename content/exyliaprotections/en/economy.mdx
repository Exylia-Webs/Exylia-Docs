Four features put money into protections. Each one is off by default and turned on with its own switch
under `submodules` in `config.yml`:

| Switch | Feature |
|---|---|
| `bank` | A shared bank per protection |
| `upkeep` | Protections pay every period; unpaid or inactive land is abandoned, then deleted |
| `market` | Selling protections to other players, and giving them away |
| `rent` | Renting plots, or whole protections, for a number of periods |

Level upgrades also cost money; they are on [Land tools](/docs/exyliaprotections/land-tools#level-upgrades).
Raids sell lives back for `raids.restore-price`; see [Raids](/docs/exyliaprotections/raids#lives).

## Currencies

Every amount goes through ExyliaLib's economy, which serves the currencies of
[ExyliaEconomy](/docs/exyliaeconomy/currencies) and of any Vault economy. A currency key left blank is
ExyliaLib's default currency, `default-currency` in `plugins/ExyliaLib/economy.yml`; any other value is a
currency id from there.

| Key | Used for |
|---|---|
| `bank.currency` | What banks hold, and what upkeep is paid in |
| `market.currency` | Sale prices |
| `rent.currency` | Rent |
| `upgrades.currency` | Level upgrades |
| `raids.restore-currency` | Buying a life back |
| `settings.move-currency`, `selection.currency`, `tiers.<id>.currency` | Moving a core, selections and the shop |

Money always leaves a player before it is used, and goes back to them when what they paid for does not
happen. A currency with fewer decimals than the amount asked rounds it down, and only what actually left
the player is credited or paid back. Without any economy installed, every payment is refused as not
enough money.

<Callout type="note" title="The bank and another currency">
The bank only takes rent, and only pays upgrades, when `rent.currency` or `upgrades.currency` is the same
as `bank.currency`. Otherwise that money goes to, or comes from, the owner. Changing `bank.currency` does not
convert what banks already hold: the numbers are read in the new currency.
</Callout>

## The bank

Every protection has one shared bank, created with the protection. Members put money in, and it pays the
upkeep first, and rent and upgrades when you set it to.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `bank.currency` | blank | The currency banks hold and upkeep is paid in |
| `bank.max-balance` | `0` | The most one bank may hold. `0` is no limit |

| Command | Who |
|---|---|
| `/protections bank` | Shows the balance. Needs `bank-deposit` or `bank-withdraw` |
| `/protections bank deposit <amount>` | Role permission `bank-deposit` |
| `/protections bank withdraw <amount>` | Role permission `bank-withdraw` |

All three act on the protection the player stands in. In the protection's **Economy** section, left-click
**Bank** to deposit and right-click it to withdraw; both ask for an amount. The default `member` and
`trusted` roles have `bank-deposit`. No default role has `bank-withdraw`, so only the owner withdraws until
a role is given it. Each movement is written to the protection's log.

- A deposit leaves the player first. If the bank would go over `max-balance`, or is busy, the money goes
  back.
- A withdrawal is cut to the decimals the currency keeps. If the bank does not hold it nothing moves; if
  the player cannot receive it, it goes back into the bank.
- A bank is **busy** when another server is changing it at the same moment and it could not wait its turn.
  Nothing moves and the player is told to try again.

A bank is changed by one server at a time, under a lock held in the database, so two servers depositing at
once queue instead of overwriting each other.

When a protection is deleted, for any reason, tenants still renting in it are paid back first (see
[Rent](#rent)), and what is left in the bank is paid to the owner, online or not. Merging two protections
moves the second bank into the first before the land is joined.

## Upkeep

With upkeep on, protections pay every period. One that stops paying, or whose players stop playing, is
**abandoned**: open to everyone for a few days, then deleted.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `upkeep.period-hours` | `24` | Hours between two payments |
| `upkeep.tier-costs` | `small: 15`, `medium: 40`, `large: 100` | What one period costs per tier |
| `upkeep.default-cost` | `15` | Cost of a tier left out of `tier-costs` |
| `upkeep.cost-per-block` | `0` | Added to every period per block of protected area |
| `upkeep.charge-owner` | `true` | Whether an owner online on the protection's server pays what its bank cannot |
| `upkeep.grace-periods` | `3` | Unpaid periods that abandon a protection |
| `upkeep.inactive-days` | `30` | Days without the owner or a member playing that abandon it. `0` turns it off |
| `upkeep.abandoned-days` | `3` | Days an abandoned protection stays open to everyone before it is deleted |
| `upkeep.warn-hours` | `12` | Hours before a payment the bank cannot cover when members are warned |
| `upkeep.check-minutes` | `10` | Minutes between two checks of this server's protections. Read when the server starts |
| `upkeep.announce` | `false` | Whether an abandonment is announced to everyone with raid announcements on |

### The arithmetic

One period costs the tier's cost plus `cost-per-block` × the protected area (width × depth). A protection by
selection pays as the tier `selection.tier` names. Upkeep is always paid in `bank.currency`.

The first period is free. A protection made while upkeep is on owes its first payment one period after it
was made. One that already existed owes it one period after upkeep first checks it.

Every `check-minutes`, the server a protection belongs to works out how many periods are due. Only that
server charges it: a protection on a server that is down is not charged until it is back.

1. **The bank pays** every due period it can cover in full. With 50 in the bank, a cost of 20 and two
   periods due, it pays both and keeps 10. With 30, it pays one and one stays owed.
2. **The owner pays the rest** when `charge-owner` is on and the owner is online on that server. All the
   periods still owed are charged at once; if their balance cannot cover all of them, nothing is charged and
   they stay owed. The owner is told what was paid.
3. **Abandonment.** The protection is abandoned once it owes `grace-periods` periods, or once
   `inactive-days` have passed since the owner or any player member last joined or left a server. It stops
   being abandoned the moment neither is true.

A period counts as owed from the moment it is due. With 24-hour periods and 3 grace periods, a protection
whose bank is empty is abandoned 48 hours after its payment was due, as the third period falls due.

Only the owner and players added as members count towards activity, not clan members or plot members.
"Last played" is kept in the database for every server of the network, and is now while one of them is
online on the protection's server. When protections load, an owner or member without a record counts as
seen at that moment, so nobody is inactive before the plugin has watched them.

### Warnings and paying

- Within `warn-hours` of a payment the bank cannot cover, the owner and members online on the protection's
  server are told once for that payment, at the first check inside the window.
- An owner or member joining is told, two seconds in, which of their protections owe upkeep or are
  abandoned.
- Any member pays what is owed with `/protections upkeep pay`, or by clicking **Upkeep** in the Economy
  section. The full debt leaves their own balance, in `bank.currency`, not the bank.
- `/protections upkeep` shows the state, the cost per period, the period length, the time to the next
  payment and what is owed. Any member may run it.

While upkeep is **due**, the core cannot be picked up: placing it again would start a new account and wipe
the debt.

### Abandoned

While a protection is abandoned:

- Every player who is not the owner or a member may do what `raids.raidable-permissions` allows there,
  whether raids are on or not. See [Raids](/docs/exyliaprotections/raids#the-raid).
- Anybody may enter, whatever the lock or the bans say.
- It cannot be picked up, moved, deleted, listed for sale, given away, offered for rent, upgraded or merged.
  A listed sale is cancelled and the seller told.
- Member perks stop, its warp is refused or hidden, and nobody may rent in it.
- Members online on its server are told, and with `announce` on so is every player online with raid
  announcements on.
- The Upkeep button and `%exyliaprotections_protections_upkeep_due%` show how long is left before it is
  deleted. It is drawn in the `abandoned` colour on BlueMap and Dynmap.

<Callout type="note" title="With the WORLDGUARD backend">
An abandoned protection's mirrored region lets everybody build and drops its entry denial, as during a raid,
and with `raids.explosions-while-raidable` on it lets explosions break blocks too.
</Callout>

It is restored when its debt is paid, or, when inactivity was the reason, when the owner or a member comes
back: joining re-checks it at once, as long as that player is on the protection's server. A member can also
clear an inactivity abandonment with nothing owed by running `/protections upkeep pay`, which charges
nothing.

Once `abandoned-days` have passed, the next check reads the account again from the database and, if it is
still abandoned, deletes the protection. Its members online are told, tenants are paid back out of the bank,
and the rest of the bank goes to the owner.

<Callout type="warning" title="abandoned-days: 0 deletes at the next check">
The deletion is due as soon as the protection is abandoned. The next check, within `check-minutes`, removes
it.
</Callout>

Turning `submodules.upkeep` off stops charging and ends the effect of every abandonment. What was owed and
when a protection was abandoned stay stored, and apply again when upkeep is turned back on.

| Upkeep state | When |
|---|---|
| `Off` | Upkeep is off |
| `Paid` | Nothing owed |
| `Due` | At least one period owed |
| `Abandoned` | Abandoned |

The labels are `state-upkeep-paid`, `state-upkeep-due` and `state-upkeep-abandoned` in the messages file;
`state-upkeep-overdue` is what the due time reads once a payment is late. Admins forgive an abandonment
from the admin screen, and the tier editor writes a tier's `upkeep.tier-costs` entry. See
[Administration](/docs/exyliaprotections/administration).

## Selling a protection

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `market.currency` | blank | Currency protections are sold in |
| `market.tax-percent` | `5` | Percent of the price kept as tax; the seller gets the rest. Kept between 0 and 100 |
| `market.max-price` | `500000` | The highest price. `0` is no limit |
| `market.keep-members` | `false` | Whether members, clans and overrides stay when the protection changes owner |

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/protections market` | Opens every protection for sale, on any server, newest first |
| `/protections sell <price>` | Lists the protection you stand in. Owner only. Run again, it replaces the price |
| `/protections unsell` | Takes it off sale |

In the Economy section, left-click **Sell or give away** to list the protection, typing the price, or to
cancel its sale when it is listed. **For sale** in `/protections` opens the market.

**Listing.** The price must be above 0 and at most `max-price`. It is refused while the protection is
raidable, its core is damaged, it is abandoned, or somebody rents in it. The listing keeps the currency
`market.currency` names at that moment. It is cancelled on its own, and the seller told, when the protection
becomes raidable or abandoned, or changes owner. While a protection is listed, it cannot be offered for rent
or rented, and it cannot be merged.

**Buying.** In the market, left-click buys and right-click teleports to the protection's home to look
first. The buyer cannot be the owner, and must pass the same checks as placing that tier: its permission,
their protection limit, the tier's limit and the world's limit. After a confirmation the price leaves the
buyer. If somebody else bought it first, it stopped being for sale, or the change of owner cannot be
written, the money goes back.

The seller receives the price minus `tax-percent`: a protection sold for 10,000 at 5% pays 9,500. The seller
is paid even when offline, and both players are told. With the survival core installed, the sale counts
`protections.sold` for the seller and `protections.bought` for the buyer.

## Giving a protection away

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/protections transfer <player>` | Gives the protection you stand in to a player. Owner only |

Or right-click **Sell or give away** in the Economy section and type the name. It also needs
`submodules.market`. The new owner must be online on the same server, since their limits are read from their
permissions. The tier's permission and every limit are checked against them. After a confirmation marked as
dangerous the protection is theirs, for free. It is refused while the protection is raidable, damaged,
abandoned or rented.

Admins hand a protection to anybody, whatever their limits, from the admin screen.

## What changes hands

A sale, a gift or an admin transfer keeps with the land its bank, its upkeep, its lives and raid, its level,
its flags, bans, greeting and farewell, its plots, its perks, time and weather. The new owner is never left
banned from, or a member of, their own land.

| `market.keep-members` | What happens to the people |
|---|---|
| `false` | Players, clans and their overrides are removed, plot members too, and the roles go back to the `roles` of `config.yml`. Whoever sold it keeps no way in |
| `true` | Members, clans, overrides, roles and plot members all stay |

Every rent running ends with the old owner: each tenant is paid back for the time left out of the bank, as
far as it holds it, and every rent offer is withdrawn.

## Rent

An owner rents out a plot, or with `rent.whole-protection` the whole protection, for a price per period. The
tenant becomes a member of what they rent until it ends.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `rent.whole-protection` | `false` | Whether whole protections may be rented, not only plots |
| `rent.currency` | blank | Currency rent is paid in |
| `rent.max-price` | `50000` | The highest rent per period. `0` is no limit |
| `rent.min-period-minutes` | `60` | The shortest period an offer may have |
| `rent.max-periods` | `4` | The most periods one rent may be paid for, the first included |
| `rent.tenant-role` | `tenant` | The role tenants hold. A protection without it gets it |
| `rent.tenant-permissions` | `break`, `place`, `containers`, `doors`, `redstone`, `buckets`, `animals`, `ride`, `decorations`, `trade`, `leash` | What that role starts with when it is created |
| `rent.pay-to-bank` | `true` | Whether rent goes into the protection's bank |
| `rent.owner-can-evict` | `false` | Whether a tenant can be evicted before the rent ends |
| `rent.relist` | `true` | Whether a target is offered again, on the same terms, when its rent ends |

### Offering

`/protections rent offer <price> <period>` offers the plot you stand in, or the whole protection when you
stand outside every plot or plots are off. The period is a duration such as `12h`, `1d` or `7d`. Run again,
it replaces the offer. `/protections rent cancel` withdraws it. Both need the role permission `manage-rent`.

In menus, **Rent out** on a plot's screen, and in the Economy section when `whole-protection` is on, asks
for the price and the period in one form with a summary at the end, the period starting at
`min-period-minutes`. Clicking it again while an offer exists cancels it.

An offer is refused when:

- it is for the whole protection and `whole-protection` is off,
- somebody rents it right now,
- the price is not above 0 or is over `max-price`,
- the period is shorter than `min-period-minutes`,
- the protection is raidable, its core is damaged, or it is abandoned,
- the protection is listed for sale.

The offer keeps `rent.currency` and `rent.max-periods` as they stand when it is made.

### Renting

`/protections rent`, or **For rent** in `/protections`, opens the player's own rentals first and then every
offer on any server. Left-click an offer to rent it; right-click teleports to its protection's home to look.

Renting is refused to the owner, to anybody already a member of that plot or protection, to players banned
from it, while the protection is raidable, abandoned or listed for sale, and when the target is already
rented. After a confirmation the tenant pays one period. If somebody else took it first, or the protection
changed meanwhile, the money goes back.

The tenant then holds `tenant-role` in the plot, or in the whole protection. The role is created with
`tenant-permissions` in a protection that lacks it. A tenant may enter the protection while it is locked,
and teleport to its home. With the survival core installed, renting counts `protections.rented`.

**Where the money goes.** Into the protection's bank when `pay-to-bank` is on, banks are on and
`rent.currency` is the same as `bank.currency`. If the bank is full or busy, and in every other case, it goes
to the owner.

### Renewing and ending

The tenant renews by left-clicking their rental in the rent menu; right-click teleports to the protection's
home. Each renewal pays one more period, at the price agreed when the rent started, and adds one period to
the end. A rent may be paid for `max-periods` periods in total, the first included: with the default 4, a
tenant rents once and renews three times. A rent that has ended cannot be renewed.

When the time runs out the tenant loses their permissions at once. At the next check, every
`upkeep.check-minutes` whether upkeep is on or not, the tenant is removed and told, and with `relist` on the
target is offered again on the same terms.

While anybody rents in a protection, it cannot be picked up, moved, deleted, listed for sale, given away or
merged. A rented plot cannot be deleted, and a tenant's role in it cannot be changed.

### Eviction

Removing a tenant from the members of what they rent is refused while `owner-can-evict` is `false`. With it
on, the remover is asked to confirm, pays the tenant back for the time left, and the rent ends. Evicting
needs `manage-members` for a whole protection and `manage-subregions` for a plot.

The refund is the price × the time left ÷ one period, rounded down to cents. It counts every period paid: a
rent of 100 a day, renewed once and evicted 12 hours in, pays back 150.00. It is paid in the rent's
currency, from the remover's own balance, and the eviction does not happen if they cannot afford it.

When a rent ends early because the protection is deleted, or changes owner, the tenant is paid back for the
time left out of the protection's bank instead, as far as the bank holds it and only when the rent's
currency is the bank's. A tenant the bank cannot pay is told so.

## Placeholders

These read the protection the player stands in, and are blank outside one.

| Placeholder | What it returns |
|---|---|
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_bank%` | Its bank balance. Blank while banks are off |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_upkeep_cost%` | What one period costs. Blank while upkeep is off |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_upkeep_due%` | Time to the next payment, time left before deletion when abandoned, or `Overdue`. Blank while upkeep is off |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_sale_price%` | Its price when for sale |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_rent_tenant%` | Who rents the plot the player stands in, or the whole protection outside every plot |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_rent_expiry%` | How long that rent has left |

## All keys

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaProtections/config.yml"
submodules:
  upkeep: false
  bank: false
  market: false
  rent: false
bank:
  currency: ""
  max-balance: 0.0
upkeep:
  period-hours: 24
  tier-costs:
    small: 15.0
    medium: 40.0
    large: 100.0
  default-cost: 15.0
  cost-per-block: 0.0
  charge-owner: true
  grace-periods: 3
  inactive-days: 30
  abandoned-days: 3
  warn-hours: 12
  check-minutes: 10
  announce: false
market:
  currency: ""
  tax-percent: 5.0
  max-price: 500000.0
  keep-members: false
rent:
  whole-protection: false
  currency: ""
  max-price: 50000.0
  min-period-minutes: 60
  max-periods: 4
  tenant-role: tenant
  tenant-permissions: [break, place, containers, doors, redstone, buckets, animals, ride, decorations, trade, leash]
  pay-to-bank: true
  owner-can-evict: false
  relist: true
```

Banks, sales and rent offers are kept in the tables `protections_accounts`, `protections_listings` and
`protections_rent_offers`; running rents live in the protection's own row.
