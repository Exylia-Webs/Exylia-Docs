A protection is a box of blocks with an owner. It is made in one of two ways: by **placing a core**, a
block bought from the shop or handed out by staff, or by **selecting two corners** with the selector.
Both run the same checks and end in the same kind of protection; a selected one simply has no core block.

Every protection on the network is one database row. A player's limits count every server, but the
overlap and distance rules only compare protections of the server and world the new land is on.

## Tiers

A **tier** is a kind of core: the block it is, the item players hold, the land it protects and the flags
a new protection starts with. `tiers` in `config.yml` holds them, keyed by tier id. Three ship:

| Tier | Block | `radius` | Area | Shop price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `small` | `AMETHYST_BLOCK` | 10 | 21x21 | 2500 |
| `medium` | `PURPUR_BLOCK` | 20 | 41x41 | 10000 |
| `large` | `CRYING_OBSIDIAN` | 35 | 71x71 | 40000 |

The radius is counted on every side of the core, so a side is `radius × 2 + 1` blocks. All three ship
with `height: FULL`, no permission, no limit of their own and the default currency.

| Tier key | Default for a new tier | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `material` | `AMETHYST_BLOCK` | The core block and item. Anything that is not a placeable block falls back to `AMETHYST_BLOCK`. |
| `name` | `{primary}&lPROTECTION CORE` | The item's name. The shipped tiers read `PROTECTION CORE [SMALL]` and so on. |
| `lore` | empty | The item's lore. The shipped tiers describe the area and say the height is full. |
| `radius` | 10 | Blocks protected on every side of the core. |
| `height` | `FULL` | `FULL` protects the whole column, from the world's floor to its ceiling. `CUBOID` protects only `y-radius` blocks above and below the core. |
| `y-radius` | 10 | Used by `CUBOID` only: the protection is `y-radius × 2 + 1` blocks tall. |
| `price` | 0 | Price in the protection shop. `0` keeps the tier out of the shop. |
| `currency` | blank | Which currency the shop charges. Blank is the default currency. |
| `permission` | blank | Needed to place the core, and to buy it from the shop. Blank for none. |
| `limit` | 0 | How many protections of this tier one player may own. `0` for no limit of its own. |
| `flags` | none | The flags a new protection of this tier starts with **on**. Every flag left out starts off. |

The shipped tiers start with `mob-spawning`, `crop-growth`, `leaf-decay`, `liquid-flow` and
`visitor-entry` on, and `pvp`, `explosions` and `fire-spread` off. The flags themselves are on
[People and access](/docs/exyliaprotections/people-and-access#flags).

Tiers can be edited in game from the [tier editor](/docs/exyliaprotections/administration), which writes
these same keys. Lives per tier live in `raids.tier-lives` and upkeep per tier in `upkeep.tier-costs`, not
inside the tier.

<Callout type="note" title="Roles and flags are copied when a protection is made">
A new protection copies the `roles` section and its tier's `flags` at that moment. Editing either later
changes the protections made afterwards, never the ones that already exist.
</Callout>

## The protection shop

`/protections` → **Protection shop** lists every tier with a `price` above 0, with its area, its height
and its price. The button is hidden while no tier has a price. Clicking a tier:

1. Refuses it when the tier has a `permission` the player lacks.
2. Charges the price in the tier's currency, and refuses when the player cannot pay.
3. Hands over one core. A core that does not fit in the inventory is kept and delivered the next time
   they join.

Staff hand out cores with `/protections admin give <player> <tier> [amount]`, 1 to 64 at a time.

## Placing a core

Placing a core item protects the land around it. The placement is refused, and the core stays in the
hand, when any of these fails:

1. The player may place a block there. A core cannot be placed inside somebody else's protection without
   `place` there.
2. The item's tier still exists in `tiers`. A core of a deleted tier is refused with "There is no
   protection tier called …".
3. The tier's `permission`, if it has one.
4. The world is not in `settings.disabled-worlds` (`world_the_end` by default).
5. The [limits](#limits) below.
6. The land overlaps no other protection, and keeps its distance from other owners'. See
   [Overlap and distance](#overlap-and-distance).
7. With WorldGuard enabled, the land overlaps no WorldGuard region other than this plugin's own, the
   global region and the ones in `settings.allowed-world-guard-regions`. This is checked in both
   [backends](/docs/exyliaprotections/backends).

The checks run once before the block goes down, so it can be refused, and again once no other plugin
cancelled it. If something changed in between, the block is removed and the core handed back.

A new protection is named `%player%'s land #%number%` (the `default-name` line of `messages.yml`), where
the number is how many the player owns plus one. Its home starts where the player stands. With
`visualization.show-on-create` on, its border is drawn to them at once.

## Protecting by selection

The **Select land** button of `/protections` hands the player a selector. They left-click one corner,
right-click the other, then **shift + left-click** to confirm, or drop the selector to cancel. They are
then shown the area and the price and asked to confirm.

The button shows only while `selection.enabled` is on and the player holds `exyliaprotections.select`
(granted to everybody by default).

<Callout type="note" title="The selector exists only for the player's client">
The selector is sent with PacketEvents as an item only that player's client sees. It never reaches the
real inventory, so it cannot be dropped for someone else, stored in a chest or sold. Without PacketEvents
the selection is refused with "Protecting land by selection is not available."
</Callout>

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `selection.enabled` | `true` | Whether players may protect land by selection. |
| `selection.tier` | `medium` | The tier a selection counts as: for the tier's `limit` and its starting flags. |
| `selection.height` | `FULL` | `FULL` protects the whole column of both corners. `NORMAL` protects only the blocks between the two corners' heights. |
| `selection.price-per-block` | 5 | Price per block of area (width × length, whatever the height). |
| `selection.currency` | blank | Blank is the default currency. |
| `selection.min-side` | 9 | The shortest side a selection may have. |
| `selection.max-area` | 10000 | The largest area it may cover. |
| `selection.full-height-tool` | `GOLDEN_HOE` | The selector used for full-height selections. |
| `selection.normal-height-tool` | `GOLDEN_AXE` | The selector used for `NORMAL` selections. |

A 20x20 selection covers 400 blocks and costs 2000. Everything a placed core checks is checked when the
player confirms the corners, and again after they accept the price, right before they are charged. A
selected protection has **no core block**: it cannot be picked up or hidden, only moved or deleted. Its
home is where the player stood when they confirmed.

<Callout type="warning" title="The selection tier's permission is not asked">
A selection counts against `selection.tier`'s `limit`, but that tier's `permission` is not checked. If the
tier is meant to be restricted, restrict selecting itself with `exyliaprotections.select`.
</Callout>

Both selector tools are also used for [plots](/docs/exyliaprotections/land-tools).

## Limits

Three limits apply to a new protection, whether placed or selected. A moved protection is not counted
against them again.

| Limit | Where it is set | What it counts |
|---|---|---|
| Protections per player | The highest `exyliaprotections.limit.<n>` the player holds, or `settings.default-limit` (3) without one | Every protection they own, on every server. |
| Per tier | The tier's `limit` | Their protections of that tier, on every server. |
| Per world | `settings.world-limits`, a map of world name to number | Their protections in that world **on this server**. A world left out has no limit of its own. |

```yaml
settings:
  default-limit: 3
  world-limits:
    world: 2
    world_nether: 1
```

The limit node also accepts the legacy prefix `exyliasurvivalcore.protections.limit.<n>`, so limits granted
while protections lived in ExyliaSurvivalCore keep working. When a player holds nodes of both prefixes the
highest number wins.

<Callout type="warning" title="A limit node of 0 does not mean zero">
Only a number above 0 counts. A player holding only `exyliaprotections.limit.0` gets `default-limit`, not
zero protections. To stop a player protecting land, take away `exyliaprotections.use` or set
`default-limit` and grant the others a higher node.
</Callout>

The `/protections` hub shows how many a player owns out of their limit. Members of a protection do not
count it against their own limit; only its owner does.

## Overlap and distance

| Rule | Applies to |
|---|---|
| Protections never overlap. | Every protection, whoever owns it. |
| At least `settings.min-distance` (8) blocks of land between them, horizontally. | Protections of **other** owners. A player's own protections may touch. |

Both rules only compare protections whose heights overlap. A `CUBOID` or `NORMAL` protection can sit
directly above or below another one, even another owner's, as long as their height ranges do not share a
single block.

## Height

| How it was made | Height |
|---|---|
| Core of a `FULL` tier | The whole column, from the world's minimum height to its maximum. |
| Core of a `CUBOID` tier | `y-radius` blocks above and below the core. |
| Selection with `selection.height: FULL` | The whole column. |
| Selection with `selection.height: NORMAL` | From the lower corner to the higher one. |

The selection's height comes from `selection.height` alone, not from the tier it counts as. Level
upgrades widen a protection by a radius bonus; see [Economy](/docs/exyliaprotections/economy). A
protection grown by a [merge](/docs/exyliaprotections/land-tools) keeps the merged box.

## Removing or moving a protection

**Picking up.** The owner breaking their own core is asked to confirm. They get back an item that
rebuilds the same protection wherever it is placed: its name, roles, members, clans, overrides, flags,
bans, plots, level and the exact box it covered. The bank is paid out to the owner, a public warp is taken
down, and a sale ends. The level is clamped to the tier's highest, plots that would stick out of the new
spot are dropped, and nothing a tenant rented survives. A carried core placed again owes its next upkeep
period at once.

Anybody else breaking a core is refused — or, with raids on and the player an enemy, hits it. Explosions,
pistons, fire and liquids never touch a core.

**Hiding** removes the core block and keeps the land protected; showing it puts it back. It needs
`manage-core` and the player standing on the protection's server and world. While raids are on, a core can
only be hidden with `raids.allow-hidden-core`.

**Moving** puts the core on top of the block the player looks at, within five blocks, in the same world.
The whole protection moves with it, same shape and size. It needs `manage-core`, runs every placement
check except the limits, costs `settings.move-price` (1000) in `settings.move-currency`, and moves the home
to where the player stands. If the move cannot be written, the price is paid back.

**Deleting**, from the protection's screen, is the owner's alone and asks for confirmation. The core block
is removed and the bank goes to the owner, after paying back any tenant for the time they had left.

None of these work while the protection is under attack, raidable, abandoned or rented. Picking up is
also refused while upkeep is due.

<Callout type="warning" title="Admins count as the owner of every protection">
A player with `exyliaprotections.admin` (operators, by default) passes every "owner only" check. Breaking
another player's core asks them to pick it up, and confirming hands **them** the carried core.
</Callout>
