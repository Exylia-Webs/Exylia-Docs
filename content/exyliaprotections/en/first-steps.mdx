title: "First steps"
description: "A first protection from the shop or the selector, then a member, a flag and a name, all from /protections."
---

Everything below is done by a player with the default permissions. Use an account **without operator**:
operators hold `exyliaprotections.bypass` and no protection ever stops them, which makes testing
misleading.

## Get some land

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the hub">
    `/protections` (or `/ps`, `/claims`). The top shows how many protections you own and your limit —
    3 by default. **My protections** is empty for now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy a core">
    **Protection shop** lists every tier with a price: `small` (21x21, 2500), `medium` (41x41, 10000)
    and `large` (71x71, 40000). Click one. The price is taken and the core item lands in your inventory.
    This needs an economy plugin; without one, give yourself a core from the console with
    `/protections admin give <player> small`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Place the core where the middle of your land should be. The land around it is protected — the
    whole column, from the bottom of the world to the top — and the chat says
    "Protection *&lt;name&gt;* created". Its border is drawn for 10 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The placement is refused, and the core stays in your hand, in `world_the_end`, over another protection,
closer than 8 blocks to another player's land, or over a WorldGuard region that is not the plugin's own.
See [Creating protections](/docs/exyliaprotections/creating-protections).

<Callout type="tip" title="Or select the land">
**Select land** on the hub gives you a selector, a golden hoe only your client sees. Left-click the first
corner, right-click the second, then shift + left-click to confirm. You are asked whether to protect that
many blocks for 5 per block of area; confirming charges you and protects the whole column between the
corners. A selection needs sides of at least 9 blocks and at most 10000 blocks of area, and has no core
block. Drop the selector to cancel.
</Callout>

## Manage it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your protection">
    `/protections` → **My protections**. Left-click your protection to manage it; right-click teleports
    you to its home. The overview shows its name, owner, size and location, and one button per section:
    **Land**, **People**, **Access**, and **Defense** for the audit log. **Economy**, **Growth** and
    **Extras** appear once one of their features is switched on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a member">
    **People** → **Members** → **Add player**, and type the player's name. They must have played on the
    network before. Pick a role: `member` builds, opens containers and uses doors and redstone;
    `trusted` can also add members and set the home. The chat confirms "Added *&lt;player&gt;* as
    *&lt;role&gt;*".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a flag">
    Back to the overview, **Access** → **Flags**. Each flag is one click: turn **PvP** on, or
    **Visitor entry** off to keep everyone who is not a member out. A new protection starts with mob
    spawning, crop growth, leaf decay, liquid flow and visitor entry on, and PvP, explosions and fire spread
    off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    **Land** → **Rename**, and type a name of up to 32 characters. It replaces the default
    "*&lt;player&gt;*'s land #1" in the list, the titles players see walking in, and the web map.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Stand inside it and run `/protections info`: it tells you the name, the owner, your role, the size and how
many members it has. That is a working protection.
