At the end of this page you own one protected area, a friend can build in it, and a stranger cannot. You only use the core item and `/protections`.

A protection is a box of land with an owner. Players make one by placing a **core**, a block that protects the land around it. Three sizes ship by default: `small` (21 x 21 blocks), `medium` (41 x 41) and `large` (71 x 71). The whole height of the world is protected, from the bottom to the sky.

## Before you start

Install the plugin, see [Install ExyliaProtections](/docs/exyliaprotections/installation). By default every player may own 3 protections, and `world_the_end` cannot be protected. Both are changed in [Configuration](/docs/exyliaprotections/configuration).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a core">
    Run `/protections` (or `/ps`, `/claims`) and click **Shop**. Click a size: its price is taken from your balance and the core lands in your inventory. The shop needs an economy plugin and a price on at least one size. Without one, staff can hand out a core from the console or in game: `/protectionsadmin give <player> small`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the core">
    Place it where the middle of your land should be. The chat says "Protection *name* created" and the border is drawn for 10 seconds. The default name is "*player*'s land #1".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at it">
    Stand inside and run `/protections info`. It tells you the name, the owner, your role, the size and how many members there are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Run `/protections`, click **My protections**, then left-click your land. In **Land**, click **Rename** and type a name of up to 32 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a friend">
    Stand inside and run `/protections add <player>`. Or open **People**, then **Members**, then **Add player**. Without a role they join as `member` and can build; `/protections add <player> trusted` lets them also add members and set the home.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Ask the friend to break a block inside your land: it works. Ask anybody else, or a second account: the block does not break. If the friend is refused, run `/protections info` while they stand next to you and check their name is in the members list. To remove them, run `/protections remove <player>`.

If the core is refused, the chat says why and the core stays in your hand. See [Common problems](/docs/exyliaprotections/troubleshooting).

<Callout type="tip" title="Protect by selecting two corners">
Selecting is off by default. Turn on `selection.enabled` in `config.yml`, install PacketEvents, and **Select land** appears in `/protections`. You get a selector that only your client sees: left-click one corner, right-click the opposite one, then Shift + left-click to confirm and pay. Each side needs at least 9 blocks, and the area is limited to 10000 blocks by default.
</Callout>
