## Plugin nodes

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliaprotections.use` | everyone | `/protections` and every subcommand outside `admin`, and with them the menus they open. |
| `exyliaprotections.select` | everyone | Protecting land by selecting two corners, when `selection.enabled` is on (off by default). Without it the hub hides the option. |
| `exyliaprotections.admin` | operators | `/protectionsadmin`, the old `admin` subcommands, the admin menus and every action in them. On every protection it also counts as the owner and as holding every role permission. |
| `exyliaprotections.bypass` | nobody | Building, using and entering anything inside any protection. |
| `exyliaprotections.bypass.warmup` | operators | Teleporting home, to warps and to plots at once, with no countdown and no cooldown. The node is `teleport.bypass-permission`; blank turns the bypass off. |

`exyliaprotections.use` gates commands only. Placing a core a player already holds needs nothing but the
tier's own permission, when the tier names one.

## Feature nodes

Every player feature has a node of its own, held by everybody by default. To make one a rank perk, deny it to
the default group and grant it to the rank. A player without the node is told "Your rank does not include
that feature." (`feature-locked` in the messages file). The feature must still be on under `submodules`, and
the role permission on that protection still applies.

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliaprotections.home` | everyone | `/protections home` and **Teleport home** |
| `exyliaprotections.sethome` | everyone | Moving a protection's home to where you stand |
| `exyliaprotections.rename` | everyone | Renaming a protection |
| `exyliaprotections.greeting` | everyone | Setting its greeting and farewell |
| `exyliaprotections.hide` | everyone | Hiding and showing its core |
| `exyliaprotections.move` | everyone | Moving its core |
| `exyliaprotections.show` | everyone | `/protections show` and **Show border** |
| `exyliaprotections.members.add` | everyone | `/protections add` and adding players from the members screen |
| `exyliaprotections.members.clan` | everyone | Adding a whole clan |
| `exyliaprotections.roles` | everyone | Creating, editing and deleting roles |
| `exyliaprotections.flags` | everyone | Changing its flags |
| `exyliaprotections.lock` | everyone | `/protections lock` |
| `exyliaprotections.ban` | everyone | `/protections ban` and banning from the bans screen |
| `exyliaprotections.logs` | everyone | `/protections logs` and the logs screen |
| `exyliaprotections.bank.deposit` | everyone | `/protections bank deposit` and depositing from the menu |
| `exyliaprotections.market.buy` | everyone | `/protections market`, browsing, visiting and buying on the market |
| `exyliaprotections.market.sell` | everyone | `/protections sell` and listing from the menu |
| `exyliaprotections.transfer` | everyone | `/protections transfer` and giving it away from the menu |
| `exyliaprotections.rent.take` | everyone | Renting somebody else's land |
| `exyliaprotections.rent.offer` | everyone | `/protections rent offer` and offering from the menu |
| `exyliaprotections.subregions` | everyone | `/protections subregion create` and `delete`, and managing plots in the menu |
| `exyliaprotections.upgrade` | everyone | `/protections upgrade` and the upgrade button |
| `exyliaprotections.warp.visit` | everyone | `/protections warps`, `/protections warp`, visiting and rating warps |
| `exyliaprotections.warp.manage` | everyone | Publishing, moving and editing a protection's warp |
| `exyliaprotections.perks` | everyone | Turning its unlocked perks on and off |
| `exyliaprotections.environment` | everyone | Changing its time and weather |

Taking land back never needs one of these: deleting a protection, paying upkeep, renewing a rent, withdrawing
from the bank, taking a protection off sale, withdrawing a rent offer, removing members and unbanning keep
working after a rank runs out.

<Callout type="note" title="The lock button">
`exyliaprotections.lock` is checked by `/protections lock` only. The **Lock** button in the Access section asks
for the `toggle-lock` role permission and not for this node.
</Callout>

## What admin lets through

`exyliaprotections.admin` is a management node, not a building one:

- Every subcommand and menu button that asks for **Owner** or a role permission passes: selling,
  transferring, upgrading, banking, editing members, roles and flags of anybody's protection.
- Entering is always allowed, whatever the protection's lock or bans say.
- A player holding it cannot be banned from a protection, and may edit their own entry in a member list.
- It does **not** let the player break, place or open anything in someone else's land. That is
  `exyliaprotections.bypass`.

## What bypass lets through

A player who bypasses passes every check inside a protection: blocks, containers, doors, entities, entry,
locks and bans. They also act in worlds whose protections have not finished loading after a restart, where
everybody else is told to wait.

The node only counts when it is *set* on the player, and since 1.0.2 it defaults to nobody: operators are
bound by protections like anybody else, so they can test their own land. Grant it to whoever must bypass:

```text
/lp user Notch permission set exyliaprotections.bypass true
```

<Callout type="note" title="Before 1.0.2">
`exyliaprotections.bypass` defaulted to `op`, and a default counts as set, so every operator bypassed every
protection out of the box.
</Callout>

## Per-player limits

| Permission | What it sets | Without one |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliaprotections.limit.<n>` | The player may own `n` protections in total. | `settings.default-limit` (3) |
| `exyliaprotections.limit.<tier>.<n>` | The player may own `n` protections of that tier. `0` is no limit of its own. | That tier's `limit` |
| `exyliaprotections.select.area.<n>` | The largest area the player may select. | `selection.max-area` (10000) |

For each prefix the highest number a player holds wins, and replaces the `config.yml` value, lower or higher.
`exyliaprotections.limit.0` is a limit like any other: that player may own none. A node whose end is not a
number is ignored. These nodes are not declared in `plugin.yml`; grant them by name.

`settings.world-limits` still caps how many a player may own per world, with no node of its own. See
[Creating protections](/docs/exyliaprotections/creating-protections#limits).

## Permissions named in config.yml

| Where | What it gates | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `tiers.<id>.permission` | Placing a core of that tier, protecting land by selection when it is `selection.tier` (since 1.0.2), buying it from the shop and buying a protection of that tier on the market or receiving it by transfer. | Empty: anybody. |
| `upgrades.tiers.<tier>.levels.<n>.permission` | Upgrading to that level. | Empty: anybody. |

They can be any node, such as `rank.vip`.

## ExyliaSurvivalCore names

Since 1.0.2 the old `exyliasurvivalcore.protections*` nodes, and the `exyliasurvivalcore.protections.limit.<n>`
limits, are no longer declared or read. Grant the `exyliaprotections.*` names instead.

## Role permissions are not these

What a member may do inside one protection — `break`, `containers`, `manage-members`, `bank-withdraw` and
the rest — is a role permission, set per protection from its menu. They are not server permissions and no
permissions plugin grants them. See [People and access](/docs/exyliaprotections/people-and-access).

## Examples

Letting the VIP rank own six protections:

```text
/lp group vip permission set exyliaprotections.limit.6 true
```

Letting the VIP rank own up to three `large` protections and select up to 40,000 blocks:

```text
/lp group vip permission set exyliaprotections.limit.large.3 true
/lp group vip permission set exyliaprotections.select.area.40000 true
```

Making moving the home a VIP perk:

```text
/lp group default permission set exyliaprotections.sethome false
/lp group vip permission set exyliaprotections.sethome true
```

Keeping new players off selections on a server that turned `selection.enabled` on:

```text
/lp group default permission set exyliaprotections.select false
```

Handing the admin menu to moderators who are not operators, without letting them build anywhere:

```text
/lp group moderator permission set exyliaprotections.admin true
```
