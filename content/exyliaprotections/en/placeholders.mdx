Every placeholder below is written in full, and clicking one copies it.

## How the name is written

The plugin registers its placeholders through ExyliaLib as one group, `protections`. PlaceholderAPI reaches
them with the plugin's name in front, so a scoreboard, a hologram or a tab plugin asks for:

```text
%exyliaprotections_protections_name%
```

That is the form this page documents. Inside an Exylia plugin's own files — menus, messages, another Exylia
plugin's scoreboard — the registered name also stands on its own: `%protections_name%`.

No expansion needs downloading. Without PlaceholderAPI installed only the Exylia form works.

<Callout type="note" title="Coming from ExyliaSurvivalCore">
`%exyliasurvivalcore_protections_…%` belonged to the old protections module. Replace
`exyliasurvivalcore_` with `exyliaprotections_`; the rest of each name is the same.
</Callout>

Every placeholder answers for a player. Read without one — from the console, or a global line of another
plugin — it is empty, and the console reports it once.

## About the player

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_count%` | Protections the player owns. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_max%` | How many they may own: their highest `exyliaprotections.limit.<n>`, or `settings.default-limit`. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_effects_active%` | The member perks active on them right now, by name, comma-separated; empty when none. |

## About the protection they stand in

Everything here is about the protection at the player's position, and empty outside one.

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_name%` | Its name. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_owner%` | Its owner's name. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_role%` | The player's role in it: `owner`, the id of the role they hold, or `visitor`. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_level%` | Its level, `0` before any upgrade. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_lives%` | Lives it has left. Empty while raids are off. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_max_lives%` | Lives it can hold. Empty while raids are off. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_raid_state%` | Its raid state, in the words and colours `messages.yml` gives it: Safe, Shielded, Under siege, Raidable or Cooling down by default. Empty while raids are off. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_bank%` | What its bank holds, formatted in the bank's currency. Empty while banks are off. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_upkeep_cost%` | What one upkeep period costs. Empty while upkeep is off. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_upkeep_due%` | Time until the next payment, short; the overdue text when it is late; the time until it is deleted when it is abandoned; `-` when upkeep has never run for it. Empty while upkeep is off. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_sale_price%` | Its price while it is listed for sale, in the listing's currency. Empty otherwise, and while the market is off. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_subregion%` | The plot the player stands in. Empty outside every plot, and while subregions are off. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_rent_tenant%` | Who rents the plot the player stands in — or the whole protection, outside every plot. Empty when nobody does. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_rent_expiry%` | Time left on that rent, short. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_warp_rating%` | The average rating of its public warp, one decimal. Empty without a warp, and while warps are off. |
| `%exyliaprotections_protections_warp_visits%` | Visits its warp has had. Same conditions. |

`role` reads the player's own role first and then their clan's. A staff member managing somebody else's
land through `exyliaprotections.admin` is still a `visitor` there.

The names of perks and of raid states come from `messages.yml`, so they follow the server's language. See
[Configuration](/docs/exyliaprotections/configuration#messagesyml).

## Examples

A scoreboard block that only fills in inside a protection:

```text
Land: %exyliaprotections_protections_name%
Owner: %exyliaprotections_protections_owner%
Role: %exyliaprotections_protections_role%
```

Raid status for a server with raids on:

```text
Lives: %exyliaprotections_protections_lives%/%exyliaprotections_protections_max_lives% (%exyliaprotections_protections_raid_state%)
```

The player's allowance:

```text
Protections: %exyliaprotections_protections_count%/%exyliaprotections_protections_max%
```
