Everything players do with their land happens in menus opened from `/protections`. Every button carries
the protection's id in its action, so a screen holds no state of its own: a click is checked again
against the protection as it is when the click arrives, and refused when the player lost the right to do
it in the meantime.

## The files

Every screen is a file under `lang/<language>/menus/`, in the language folder ExyliaLib is set to (`en`,
`es`, `pt` or `fr`).

| Folder | Owner | On update |
|---|---|---|
| `menus/user/` | Yours. Restyle, move and reword freely. | Kept. Only missing files are written. A release that has to change a screen's layout raises its `menu-version`; that file is then replaced and your previous one kept next to it. |
| `menus/admin/` | The plugin's. | Rewritten from the jar on every start, so staff screens always match the release. |

Each file's header comment lists the placeholders it receives. Buttons show only while their `condition`
holds; the `%…_enabled%` placeholders are `true` or `false` from `submodules`. An action prefixed with
`left:`, `right:`, `shift_left:` or `shift_right:` runs on that click only; without a prefix it runs on any
click.

Menu files are read when the plugin enables and again on `/exylialib reload`. A file that does not parse
is named in the console and left out, instead of opening as an empty screen.

## The hub

`/protections` (or `/ps`, `/claims`) opens `menus/user/main.yml`, a 27-slot screen.

| Slot | Button | Shown while | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Your land**: how many they own out of their limit, and how many they own or belong to | always | none |
| 12 | **My protections** | always | `list` |
| 13 | **Protection shop**, with how many tiers are for sale | some tier has a price | `shop` |
| 14 | **Select land** | `selection.enabled` and the player holds `exyliaprotections.select` | `select` |
| 21 | **For sale**, with how many are listed | `submodules.market` | `market` |
| 22 | **For rent** | `submodules.rent` | `rent` |
| 23 | **Public warps** | `submodules.warps` | `warps` |

## The list

`menus/user/list.yml`: every protection the player owns or belongs to, on any server, oldest first, 36 to
a page. Each shows its owner, the player's role, size, location, server and member count.

| Click | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left-click | Opens the protection's [overview](#the-overview). |
| Right-click | Teleports home, with the warmup. |
| **Back** (slot 49) | The hub. |

## The overview

`menus/user/manage.yml` is one protection's own screen: 45 slots, a summary at the top and one button per
section. It needs membership; admins open anybody's.

| Slot | Button | Shown while | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | The protection: owner, the player's role, tier, size, members, location, server | always | none |
| 19 | **Land** | always | `section <id> land` |
| 20 | **People** | always | `section <id> people` |
| 21 | **Access** | always | `section <id> access` |
| 22 | **Economy** | `bank`, `upkeep` or `market` is on, or `rent` with `rent.whole-protection` | `section <id> economy` |
| 23 | **Growth** | `upgrades`, `subregions` or `merge` is on | `section <id> growth` |
| 24 | **Defense** | `raids` or `logs` is on | `section <id> defense` |
| 25 | **Extras** | `warps`, `effects` or `environment` is on | `section <id> extras` |
| 36 | **Teleport home** | always | `home <id>` |
| 40 | **Back** | always | `manage_back <id>` |
| 44 | **Delete** | always | `delete <id>` |

**Back** returns to the list, except for an admin looking at somebody else's protection, who goes back to
the admin screen they came from. Every button is shown to every member; one their role does not allow
answers "You cannot do that in this protection."

### Sections, and the section that sticks

Each section is a 36-slot screen, `menus/user/manage_<section>.yml`, whose **Back** (slot 31) returns to
the overview with `section <id> hub`.

Opening a section remembers it for that player and that protection. Every later reopening of the
protection's screen — a **Back** from members, roles, bans or logs, or the screen coming back after a
change — lands on that section again, not on the overview. It is forgotten when the player goes back to
the overview, opens another protection, or leaves the server. Opening a protection from the list always
starts on the overview.

## The other screens

Every list below is a 54-slot paged screen: up to 36 entries a page (fewer on the grid-shaped ones),
previous and next page buttons, and **Back** in slot 49.

| File | Opened by | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `user/members.yml` | `members` | Members and clans; see [Members and clans](/docs/exyliaprotections/people-and-access#members-and-clans). Clans use `clan_template`. **Add player** (45), **Add clan** (46). |
| `user/roles.yml` | `roles` | Roles, `visitor` last with `visitor_template`. **Create role** (45). |
| `user/permissions.yml` | `flags`, `role_edit`, `overrides`, `subregion_flags`, `subregion_overrides` | One list of switches. Its title names what it edits. Clicking a switch runs `toggle`; **Back** runs `back`. |
| `user/shop.yml` | `shop` | Every tier with a price. A click buys one core. |
| `user/bans.yml` | `bans` | Running bans. Right-click lifts one. **Ban player** (45). |
| `user/logs.yml` | `logs` | The audit log with its action and player filters (45, 46). |
| `user/market.yml` | `market` | Protections for sale. Left buys, right visits. |
| `user/rent.yml` | `rent` | The player's own rentals first (`rented_template`: left renews, right teleports home), then every offer (left rents, right visits). |
| `user/subregions.yml` | `subregions` | A protection's plots. **Create plot** (45). |
| `user/subregion.yml` | `subregion` | One plot's members (left role, shift + left overrides, right removes) and its buttons: add member (45), plot flags (46), private (47), rent out (51, while `rent` is on), delete (53). |
| `user/warps.yml` | `warps` | Public warps. Left visits, right rates. **Sort** (45) cycles visits, rating, newest. |
| `user/warp.yml` | `warp` | One protection's warp: publish, land here, rename, icon, description. |
| `user/perks.yml` | `perks` | Perk switches for the owner, then time (38) and weather (42) while `environment` is on. |
| `user/merge.yml` | `merge` | The owner's protections that touch this one. A click merges it in. |
| `admin/admin.yml` | `/protections admin`, `admin` | Every protection on the network, with filters. See [Administration](/docs/exyliaprotections/administration). |
| `admin/admin_protection.yml` | `admin_protection` | One protection's admin buttons. |
| `admin/tiers.yml`, `admin/tier.yml` | `admin_tiers`, `admin_tier` | The tier editor. |
| `admin/cleanup.yml` | `admin_cleanup` | The inactive cleanup preview. |

A switch in `permissions.yml` shows **ON** / **OFF** for flags and roles, **ALLOW** / **DENY** / **ROLE**
for a member's overrides, and **ALLOW** / **DENY** / **PROTECTION** (follow the protection) for a plot's
flags.

## Actions

Every button runs an action written `exyliaprotections:<name> <arguments>`, so a restyled menu can call any
of them. Placeholders such as `%protection_id%` fill the arguments. Up to four arguments are read.

Before running, every action is refused while its feature is off ("That feature is turned off."), when
the protection no longer exists ("That protection no longer exists."), or when the requirement below does
not let the player through. `<id>` is a protection id.

### Land and the protection itself

| Action | Arguments | Needs | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `open` | | | The hub |
| `list` | | | The list |
| `shop` | | | The shop |
| `select` | | `exyliaprotections.select`, `selection.enabled` | Hands out the selector |
| `buy` | `<tier>` | the tier's permission | Buys one core of a tier with a price |
| `manage` | `<id>` | member | The overview, or the section that stuck |
| `section` | `<id> <land\|people\|access\|economy\|growth\|defense\|extras\|hub>` | member | Opens a section and remembers it; `hub` or anything else opens the overview and forgets it |
| `manage_back` | `<id>` | | The list, or the admin screen for an admin outside the protection |
| `home` | `<id>` | member or tenant | Teleports home with the warmup |
| `sethome` | `<id>` | `set-home` | Moves the home to where the player stands, inside |
| `rename` | `<id>` | `rename` | Asks for a name, up to 32 characters of plain text |
| `hide` | `<id>` | `manage-core` | Hides or shows the core |
| `move` | `<id>` | `manage-core` | Moves the protection onto the block looked at, for `settings.move-price` |
| `delete` | `<id>` | owner | Deletes after a confirmation |
| `raid_restore` | `<id>` | member, `raids` | Buys a life back after a confirmation |
| `show` | `<id>` | member, `visualization` | Draws the border |

| Action | Arguments | Needs | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `members` | `<id>` | `manage-members` | The members screen |

| `clan_add` | `<id>` | `manage-members` | Asks a clan tag, then a role |
| `member_role` | `<id> <uuid\|clan:<clan>>` | `manage-members` | Asks a new role |
| `member_remove` | `<id> <uuid\|clan:<clan>>` | `manage-members` | Removes a member or clan |
| `overrides` | `<id> <uuid>` | `manage-members` | A member's override switches |
| `roles` | `<id>` | `manage-roles` | The roles screen |
| `role_create` | `<id>` | `manage-roles` | Asks a name and opens the new role |

| `role_rename` | `<id> <role>` | `manage-roles` | Asks a new name |

| `toggle` | `<id> <target> <key>` | depends on the target | Flips one switch; see below |
| `back` | `<id> <target>` | | Leaves a switch screen: a role to roles, a member to members, a plot target to the plot, anything else to the protection |
| `lock` | `<id>` | `toggle-lock`, `lock` | Locks or unlocks |
| `bans` | `<id>` | `manage-bans`, `bans` | The bans screen |

| `greeting` | `<id> <greeting\|farewell\|clear>` | `rename`, `enter-leave` | Asks the greeting or farewell, or clears both. Anything but `farewell` and `clear` edits the greeting |
| `logs` | `<id> <action\|all> <player\|_>` | `view-logs`, `logs` | The log, filtered; `all` and `_` mean no filter |
| `logs_action` | `<id> <action\|all> <player\|_>` | `view-logs`, `logs` | The log with the next action filter |
| `logs_player` | `<id> <action\|all>` | `view-logs`, `logs` | Asks a player name to filter by |

`toggle` targets, as `PermissionTarget` spells them:

| Target | Switches | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| `flags` | The protection's flags | `edit-flags` |
| `role:<name>` | A role's permissions | `manage-roles` and holding the permission; not the role the player holds; never a management permission for `visitor` |
| `player:<uuid>` | A member's overrides, cycling role, allow, deny | `manage-members` and holding the permission; not themselves |
| `sub:<plot>:flags` | A plot's flags, cycling follow, on, off; `visitor-entry` excluded | `manage-subregions` |
| `sub:<plot>:player:<uuid>` | A plot member's overrides | `manage-subregions` and holding the permission; not themselves |
