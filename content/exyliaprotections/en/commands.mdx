Two roots: `/protections` for players, also answering to `/ps` and `/claims`, and `/protectionsadmin` for
staff, also answering to `/psadmin` and `/padmin`. Almost everything a player does happens in the menus
`/protections` opens; the subcommands are shortcuts for what is done standing in your own land.

## How the land is picked

Every subcommand that acts on a protection acts on **the one you are standing in**. Outside every
protection it answers "there is no protection here" and does nothing.

Three checks run before anything happens, in this order:

1. **The command permission.** `exyliaprotections.use` for every player subcommand,
   `exyliaprotections.admin` for `/protectionsadmin` and the `admin` ones.
2. **The feature.** A subcommand belonging to a feature turned off under `submodules` in `config.yml` says
   the feature is disabled. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaprotections/configuration).
3. **Who you are there.** The column **Who** below says what you need on that protection: a role
   permission such as `manage-bans` (held through your role or your clan's), **Owner**, or **Any member**.
   The owner always passes.

`exyliaprotections.admin` counts as the owner and as holding every role permission on every protection, so
staff can run all of these anywhere. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaprotections/permissions).

## Player commands

Every command here needs `exyliaprotections.use`, which everybody has by default, and runs for players
only, except `help`.

| Command | Feature | Who | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/protections` | | | Opens the protections hub. |
| `/protections help` | | | Lists the main commands. Also works from the console. |
| `/protections home [name]` | | | Teleports to a protection's home. Without a name: straight there if you have exactly one, otherwise opens your list. Suggests the protections you own or belong to. |
| `/protections info` | | | Name, owner, your role, size and member count of the protection you stand in. |
| `/protections ban <player> [duration] [reason]` | `bans` | `manage-bans` | Bans a player. |
| `/protections unban <player>` | `bans` | `manage-bans` | Lifts a ban. Suggests the names banned there. |
| `/protections lock` | `lock` | `toggle-lock` | Locks the protection to members, or unlocks it. |
| `/protections logs` | `logs` | `view-logs` | Opens its log. |
| `/protections market` | `market` | | Opens every protection for sale. Works anywhere. |
| `/protections sell <price>` | `market` | Owner | Lists it for sale. |
| `/protections unsell` | `market` | Owner | Takes it off sale. |
| `/protections transfer <player>` | `market` | Owner | Gives it to another player, who must be online on this server. |
| `/protections bank` | `bank` | `bank-deposit` or `bank-withdraw` | Says what its bank holds. |
| `/protections bank deposit <amount>` | `bank` | `bank-deposit` | Pays into its bank. |
| `/protections bank withdraw <amount>` | `bank` | `bank-withdraw` | Takes money out of its bank. |
| `/protections upkeep` | `upkeep` | Any member | Its upkeep state, cost per period, period, time until the next payment, and debt. |
| `/protections upkeep pay` | `upkeep` | Any member | Pays what it owes. |
| `/protections subregion create` | `subregions` | `manage-subregions` | Hands you the selector to mark a new plot. |
| `/protections subregion delete <name>` | `subregions` | `manage-subregions` | Deletes a plot after a confirmation. Suggests its plots. |
| `/protections subregion info` | `subregions` | | The plot you stand in: name, size, members, private or shared, rent state. Anybody may ask. |
| `/protections rent` | `rent` | | Opens your rents and everything offered for rent. Works anywhere. |
| `/protections rent offer <price> <period>` | `rent` | `manage-rent` | Offers the plot you stand in for rent, or the whole protection when you stand outside every plot. |
| `/protections rent cancel` | `rent` | `manage-rent` | Withdraws that offer. |
| `/protections upgrade` | `upgrades` | Owner | Buys the next level, after a confirmation. |
| `/protections warps` | `warps` | | Opens the public warps, most visited first. Works anywhere. |
| `/protections warp <name>` | `warps` | | Visits a public warp. Suggests the warps you may visit. |
| `/protections show` | `visualization` | Any member | Draws its border. |

### Arguments

- **`<price>` and `<amount>`** are read the way players type money: `500`, `2.5k`, `1m`.
- **`<period>`** is a duration: `30m`, `12h`, `1d`, `1w`. A bare number is seconds.
- **`[duration]`** of a ban is a duration as above, or `permanent` (also `perm`, `forever`). Leaving it out
  bans for good. Anything after it is the reason, spaces included, cut to `bans.reason-max-length`
  characters.
- **`<player>`** for `ban` and `transfer` suggests the players online.

When `subregions` is off, `rent offer` and `rent cancel` always act on the whole protection. Whether a whole
protection may be rented at all is `rent.whole-protection`. See
[Economy](/docs/exyliaprotections/economy) for sales, transfers, banks, upkeep and rent, and
[Land tools](/docs/exyliaprotections/land-tools) for plots and levels.

There is no `/protections merge` any more: since 1.0.2 merging only happens when a core is placed touching
your own land, with `submodules.merge` on. See [Land tools](/docs/exyliaprotections/land-tools).

## Staff commands

Everything below needs `exyliaprotections.admin` (operators by default). Subcommands that act on land act
on **the protection you stand in**, and answer "there is no protection here" outside one.

| Command | Console | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/protectionsadmin` | No | Opens the admin hub. See [Administration](/docs/exyliaprotections/administration#the-admin-hub). |
| `/protectionsadmin give <player> <tier> [amount]` | Yes | Gives an online player protection cores of a tier, 1 to 64 (1 when left out). Suggests every tier. What does not fit in their inventory is kept and handed over later. |
| `/protectionsadmin list [owner]` | No | Opens the admin search of every protection on the network, or only the protections of owners whose name contains `owner`. Says so when that owner has none. |
| `/protectionsadmin here` | No | Opens the admin screen of the protection you stand in. |
| `/protectionsadmin delete` | No | Deletes the protection you stand in, after a dangerous confirmation. |
| `/protectionsadmin transfer <player>` | No | Hands the protection you stand in to a player, online or not, whatever their limits say. |
| `/protectionsadmin level <level>` | No | Sets its upgrade level, kept between 0 and the tier's highest. |
| `/protectionsadmin lives <lives>` | No | Sets its raid lives, kept between 1 and its maximum. Refused while `raids` is off. |
| `/protectionsadmin flag <flag> <allow\|deny\|default>` | Yes | Forces a flag on every protection and plot, or gives it back to each owner with `default`. Suggests every flag. |
| `/protectionsadmin feature <feature> <on\|off>` | Yes | Turns a whole feature of `submodules` on or off, by its key: `raids`, `enter-leave`, `merge`... |
| `/protectionsadmin settings [general\|teleport\|features\|flags]` | No | Opens one group of in-game settings, or the hub without one. |
| `/protectionsadmin tiers` | No | Opens the tier editor. |
| `/protectionsadmin migrate <INTERNAL\|WORLDGUARD>` | Yes | Switches the backend and moves every protection over. Refused when WorldGuard is not installed. |
| `/protectionsadmin reload` | Yes | Reads `config.yml`, the messages and the menus again, re-applies the backend and restarts the timers. |
| `/protectionsadmin import protectionstones [--dry-run]` | Yes | Turns ProtectionStones regions into protections. `--dry-run` only reports what it would do. Needs WorldGuard. |
| `/protectionsadmin cleanup <days> [preview\|confirm] [--include-unknown]` | Yes | Lists the protections whose owner and members have not played for that many days; with `confirm`, removes them. |

- **`flag`** and **`feature`** write `config.yml` on the spot, under `forced-flags` and `submodules`, and apply
  at once; with the WORLDGUARD backend the mirrored regions are updated too. A value the file does not take
  is refused with the reason. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaprotections/configuration#forced-flags).
- **`migrate`** reports the backend it ended on and how many protections it moved. See
  [Backends](/docs/exyliaprotections/backends).
- **`import protectionstones`** reports how many regions were imported, already imported, overlapping and
  skipped, then lists the first ten problems. A dry run also warns when `import.remove-source-regions` is on.
  See [Migration](/docs/exyliaprotections/migration).
- **`cleanup`** without `confirm` is a preview: how many would go, how many were kept for having no record
  of their players, a tenant or money in the bank, and the first ten. `--include-unknown` also removes those
  without a record. Days below 1 count as 1. See [Administration](/docs/exyliaprotections/administration).

### The old /protections admin

`/protections admin` still answers the subcommands it had before `/protectionsadmin` existed, and runs the
same code: `/protections admin` (the hub), `give`, `migrate`, `reload`, `import protectionstones` and
`cleanup`. Every other staff subcommand is only under `/protectionsadmin`.

<Callout type="note" title="/ps and ProtectionStones">
ProtectionStones also registers `/ps`, and only one of the two can answer to it while both are installed.
`/protections` and `/claims` always reach this one. Remove ProtectionStones once its regions are imported.
</Callout>
