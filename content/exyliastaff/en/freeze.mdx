---
title: "Freeze"
description: "The client-side pin, what a frozen player sees and cannot do, the freeze chat, the sidebars, the replay, and what happens when they log out."
---

Freezing a player pins them in place on the client: every position packet is dropped and answered
with a teleport back, so there is no rubber-banding fight with a movement plugin. They are blinded,
their armour is taken off and replaced with a block of ice on the head, they glow, and the only chat
they read is staff talking to them.

Module id `freeze`, switch `modules.freeze`, files under `modules/freeze/`. Needs PacketEvents.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/freeze <player>` | `exyliastaff.freeze` | Freezes. `/ss` is an alias. |
| `/unfreeze <player>` | `exyliastaff.freeze` | Releases. `/unss` is an alias. |
| `/freezetoggle <player>` | `exyliastaff.freeze` | One or the other. `/sstoggle` is an alias. |

The console can freeze too. The packed ice in the hotbar toggles the freeze of the player you look
at, within five blocks. A player cannot freeze themself, and a target holding
`exyliastaff.freeze.bypass` cannot be frozen.

## What the frozen player gets

- The `frozen` lines, with the staff member's name, and then a reminder every `reminder-seconds`:
  a title, an action bar and a sound, set by `reminder` in the messages file.
- The `frozen` sidebar: who froze them and for how long.
- Glow, the potion effects in `look.effects`, and the helmet, after the armour is saved to a snapshot.
  The armour comes back on release, and after a crash on the next join, on the server it came off:
  a player who reconnects to a lobby gets it back when they walk into that server again, not where a
  hub plugin might empty their inventory. The helmet is recognised by a tag, so changing its name or
  the language mid-freeze still takes it off.

## What they cannot do

| Always | Configurable |
|---|---|
| Move | Take or deal damage (`block-damage`) |
| Break, place, drop, pick up items | Open any inventory (`block-inventories`) |
| Run commands outside `allowed-commands` | Be teleported, except by a plugin (`block-teleports`) |

Plugin teleports pass on purpose: a staff `/tphere` on a frozen player works. The "you cannot do that"
line is sent at most once every two seconds.

## The chat

A screenshare is one conversation. What the frozen player and the staff member holding them type in
public chat is taken out of it, before any other plugin or a Discord bridge reads it, and goes to the
freeze chat on every server instead: the frozen player's lines in `chat-format`, the staff member's in
`chat-staff-format`. Every staff member with `exyliastaff.freeze` reads every session, so a colleague
can follow and step in; the frozen player reads only the staff member holding them.

For as long as the freeze lasts, the plugin's chat rule also keeps the rest of public chat away: the
frozen player hears staff and nobody else, and the staff member holding them hears staff and frozen
players. Global chat bypasses this rule like every other.

## Logging out

A freeze never survives a disconnect. On quit the freeze ends: the row is deleted, every server drops
the session, and the player rejoins free. Logging out is how a player refuses a screenshare, so that is
what the server reacts to instead. If `alert-quit` is on, every staff member with `exyliastaff.freeze`
on every server is told they logged out, and `quit-commands` run with `%player%`:

```yaml
quit-commands:
  - 'console-proxy:ban %player% Logged out while frozen'
```

Other plugins hear it as `FrozenPlayerQuitEvent`; see the [API](/docs/exyliastaff/api#freezes-and-reports).

A `/stop` ends every freeze the same way, but runs no quit commands, fires no event and raises no
alert: a server going down is not a player running away. A row left behind by a crash is dropped on
the next join, and the armour handed back.

## The replay

The moment a freeze starts, the last minute before it is saved as a [replay](/docs/exyliastaff/replays),
with `capture.freeze` in the replay module: what the staff member is about to ask about. Another plugin
can refuse a freeze before anything happens, through `PlayerFreezeEvent`.

## Across servers

Every freeze and release is published on the `freeze` channel. Staff on every server get the
`alert-frozen` and `alert-unfrozen` lines with the server name, and a server that has the player
online applies or releases the freeze when another one says so.

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/freeze/config.yml"
reminder-seconds: 5
look:
  strip-armor: true
  helmet: PACKED_ICE
  glow: true
  effects:
    - BLINDNESS
    - DARKNESS
block-damage: true
block-inventories: true
allowed-commands:
  - msg
  - r
  - reply
  - tell
  - w
block-teleports: true
alert-quit: true
quit-commands: []
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `reminder-seconds` | `5` | Seconds between reminders. |
| `look.strip-armor` | `true` | Take the armour off, give it back on release. |
| `look.helmet` | `PACKED_ICE` | Worn on the head. Empty for none. Its name is `helmet-name` in the messages file. |
| `look.effects` | blindness, darkness | Potion effects held for the whole freeze. Unknown names are skipped. |
| `allowed-commands` | `msg`, `r`, `reply`, `tell`, `w` | Commands a frozen player may still run, matched on the bare label. |
| `quit-commands` | `[]` | Run on logout. `console-proxy:` needs the library's proxy bridge. |

## Messages

`lang/<language>/modules/freeze/messages.yml`: `frozen` and `unfrozen` are lists of lines with
`%staff%`; `froze`, `unfroze`, `already-frozen`, `not-frozen` and `bypass` with `%target%`; `self`,
`no-target`; the three alerts `alert-frozen`, `alert-unfrozen` and `alert-quit` with `%staff%`,
`%target%` and `%server%`; `chat-format` and `chat-staff-format` with `%server%`, `%player%` and
`%message%`; `blocked-command` and `blocked-action`; `reminder`, the library effect played on every
reminder; and `helmet-name`.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/lang/en/modules/freeze/messages.yml"
reminder:
  title:
    text: '{error}&lFROZEN'
    subtitle: '{letters}Do not disconnect'
    stay: 3
    fade-out: 1
  action-bar:
    text: '{warning}➥ Wait for staff instructions'
    duration: 4
  sound:
    name: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BASS
    pitch: 0.6
helmet-name: '{error}&lFROZEN'
```

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_freeze_by%` | Who froze the viewer. Empty otherwise. |
| `%staff_freeze_time%` | How long, as a clock: the viewer's own freeze, or the one they are holding. |
| `%staff_freeze_target%` | Who the viewer has frozen on this server. |
| `%staff_frozen%` | `true` while frozen, from the core. |
