Freezing locks a suspect in place for a screenshare. The frozen player cannot move, act or run commands, sees only a reminder and staff chat, and stays frozen if they log out. You freeze with one command or one click.

## Before you start

- Give the staff rank `exyliastaff.freeze`. To make a rank immune, give it `exyliastaff.freeze.bypass`.
- The module switch is `modules.freeze` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`.
- With [PacketEvents](/docs/exyliastaff/installation), the client is pinned by dropping every position packet and answering with a teleport back, so there is no rubber-banding. Without it freeze still works but pins the player with server-side movement checks instead.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Freeze the player">
    Run `/freeze <player> [reason]` (alias `/ss`), or look at the player within five blocks and click the **Packed ice** (**FREEZE**, slot 8) in the [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) hotbar. The reason is shown to the player and kept with the session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk to them">
    Type in public chat. Your lines and theirs go to a private freeze chat that every staff member with `exyliastaff.freeze` can read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release">
    Run `/unfreeze <player>` (alias `/unss`), or click the ice again. `/freezetoggle <player>` (alias `/sstoggle`) does one or the other.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The console can freeze too. You cannot freeze yourself, and a target holding `exyliastaff.freeze.bypass` cannot be frozen.

## Check that it works

Freeze a test player. They should be unable to move, see a blinding effect with a **FROZEN** title and a block of ice on their head, glow for staff, and receive a reminder every five seconds. Release them: armour comes back and the effects clear.

## What the frozen player gets

- The `frozen` message lines with your name and the reason (a line holding `%reason%` is left out when there is none), then a reminder every `reminder-seconds`: a title, an action bar and a sound.
- The `frozen` sidebar: who froze them and for how long.
- Glow, the potion effects in `look.effects`, and a helmet, after their armour is saved. The armour comes back on release, and after a crash on the next join, on the server it came off. The helmet is recognised by a tag, so changing its name or the language mid-freeze still takes it off.

They cannot move, break, place, drop or pick up items, or run commands outside `allowed-commands` (by default `msg`, `r`, `reply`, `tell`, `w`). Configurable: damage (`block-damage`), opening inventories (`block-inventories`) and being teleported except by a plugin (`block-teleports`). Plugin teleports pass on purpose, so a staff `/tphere` on a frozen player works. The "you cannot do that" line is sent at most once every two seconds.

## Going further

### The chat

What the frozen player and the staff member holding them type in public chat is taken out of it before any other plugin or a Discord bridge reads it, and goes to the freeze chat on every server: the frozen player's lines use `chat-format`, the staff member's `chat-staff-format`. Every staff member with `exyliastaff.freeze` reads every session so a colleague can step in; the frozen player reads only the staff member holding them. The frozen player hears staff and nobody else, and the staff member holding them hears staff and frozen players. Global chat bypasses this like every other chat rule.

### Logging out

Logging out does not end a freeze. With `persist-on-quit` on (the default), the player comes back frozen on whichever server of the network they join, and staff everywhere get `alert-rejoin`. With it off, the freeze ends on quit and the player rejoins free.

Either way, logging out is how a player refuses a screenshare, so the server reacts. With `alert-quit` on, every staff member with `exyliastaff.freeze` on every server is told, and the `quit-commands` run with `%player%`:

```yaml
quit-commands:
  - 'console-proxy:ban %player% Logged out while frozen'
```

Other plugins hear it as `FrozenPlayerQuitEvent`; see the [API](/docs/exyliastaff/api#freezes-and-reports). A `/stop` ends every freeze whatever `persist-on-quit` says, but runs no quit commands and raises no alert.

### Replay and other servers

The moment a freeze starts, the last minute before it is saved as a [replay](/docs/exyliastaff/replays) (`capture.freeze` in the replay module). Every freeze and release is published on the `freeze` channel: staff on every server get the `alert-frozen` and `alert-unfrozen` lines with the server name, and a server that has the player online applies or releases the freeze.

<Details title="Configuration (modules/freeze/config.yml)">

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/freeze/config.yml"
reminder-seconds: 5
look:
  strip-armor: true
  helmet: PACKED_ICE
  glow: true
  effects:
    - BLINDNESS
    - DARKNESS
block-damage: true
block-inventories: true
allowed-commands:
  - msg
  - r
  - reply
  - tell
  - w
block-teleports: true
alert-quit: true
persist-on-quit: true
quit-commands: []
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `reminder-seconds` | `5` | Seconds between reminders. |
| `look.strip-armor` | `true` | Take the armour off, give it back on release. |
| `look.helmet` | `PACKED_ICE` | Worn on the head. Empty for none. Its name is `helmet-name` in the messages file. |
| `look.glow` | `true` | The frozen player glows. |
| `look.effects` | blindness, darkness | Potion effects held for the whole freeze. Unknown names are skipped. |
| `block-damage`, `block-inventories`, `block-teleports` | `true` | The configurable restrictions above. |
| `allowed-commands` | `msg`, `r`, `reply`, `tell`, `w` | Commands a frozen player may still run, matched on the bare label. |
| `alert-quit` | `true` | Tell staff on every server when a frozen player logs out. |
| `persist-on-quit` | `true` | Keep a player frozen when they log out, until they come back on any server. |
| `quit-commands` | `[]` | Run on logout. `console-proxy:` needs the library's proxy bridge. |

Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/freeze/messages.yml`: `frozen` (list of lines, `%staff%` and `%reason%`), `unfrozen`, `froze`, `unfroze`, `already-frozen`, `not-frozen` and `bypass` (`%target%`), `self`, `no-target`, the alerts `alert-frozen`, `alert-unfrozen`, `alert-quit` and `alert-rejoin` (`%staff%`, `%target%`, `%server%`), `chat-format` and `chat-staff-format` (`%server%`, `%player%`, `%message%`), `blocked-command`, `blocked-action`, `reminder` (title, action bar and sound played on every reminder) and `helmet-name`. See [ExyliaLib messages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages) and [colours](/docs/exylialib/colours).

Placeholders: `%staff_freeze_by%` (who froze the viewer), `%staff_freeze_time%` (how long, as a clock), `%staff_freeze_target%` (who the viewer has frozen here) and `%staff_frozen%`. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliastaff/placeholders).
