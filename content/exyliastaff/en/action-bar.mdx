A staff member on duty has half a dozen toggles running at once. The action bar puts them on one line above the hotbar, with a tick or a cross each, next to the server's TPS, MSPT and CPU. It redraws itself every second, and there is no command to learn.

## Before you start

- The module switch is `modules.action-bar` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`, on by default. You can also switch it from `/staffadmin settings` (**Modules**, then the communication card).
- The line is drawn for players in [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode), and for players who are [vanished](/docs/exyliastaff/vanish) while off duty. Nobody else sees it.
- TPS, MSPT and CPU in the shipped line are `%server_tps%`, `%server_mspt%` and `%server_cpu_process:percent%`, provided by ExyliaLib's server placeholders.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="See the default line">
    Run `/staff`. Above your hotbar you read `STAFF ▎ ✔ Vanish · ✔ Alerts · ✖ Chat ▎ TPS 20.0 · MSPT 12.3 · CPU 8%`. The first three are the toggles for [vanish](/docs/exyliastaff/vanish), [mining alerts](/docs/exyliastaff/mining) and [staff chat](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-chat).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the text file">
    Open `plugins/ExyliaStaff/lang/<language>/modules/actionbar/messages.yml`. `staff-mode` is the line shown on duty, `vanish` is the line shown when you are hidden off duty. Both are plain text, so [colours and gradients](/docs/exylialib/colours) work in them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or remove a toggle">
    To show a toggle, write `%staff_actionbar_check_<placeholder>%` followed by its name. The placeholder goes without its percent signs: `%staff_actionbar_check_staff_vanished%` asks `%staff_vanished%` and prints a green tick when it is on, a red cross when it is off. To remove one, delete its piece of the line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/staffadmin reload`. The bar redraws with the new text at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/staff` and read the bar, then `/staff` again to leave: it goes away. Run `/vanish` without staff mode and a yellow `VANISH ▎ Hidden and off duty` line appears; `/vanish` again removes it. If you are on duty and vanished you read the staff line, since that one already shows the vanish tick.

## Going further

### Change how often it redraws

`refresh-ticks` in `modules/actionbar/config.yml` is the pause between redraws, `20` by default (once a second). The client fades an action bar after about three seconds, so keep it well under `60`.

### Change the ticks and crosses

`icon-on` (default `{success}✔`) and `icon-off` (default `{error}✖`) in the same `messages.yml` are what a toggle prints. `true`, `yes`, `on`, `1` and `enabled` get the tick; everything else, including a placeholder that does not resolve, gets the cross. A module that is switched off shows a cross, which is more honest than an error across the screen.

### Show any value, not only toggles

Anything PlaceholderAPI resolves can go in the line: `%server_tps%`, a balance, the number of open reports. Ticks and crosses are only for true or false placeholders.

### Turn one line off

Leave `staff-mode` or `vanish` empty and that situation shows no bar. To turn the whole thing off, set `modules.action-bar: false` and restart, or stop it from the module list in `/staffadmin modules`.

<Details title="Technical details">

| Key | File | Default | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| `refresh-ticks` | `modules/actionbar/config.yml` | `20` | Ticks between redraws. |
| `staff-mode` | `lang/<language>/modules/actionbar/messages.yml` | the line above | Shown while in staff mode. It wins over `vanish`. |
| `vanish` | same | `VANISH ▎ Hidden and off duty ▎ TPS … · MSPT …` | Shown while vanished with staff mode off. Without it nothing on screen says you are hidden, since `/vanish` alone has no hotbar or sidebar. |
| `icon-on`, `icon-off` | same | `{success}✔`, `{error}✖` | What a toggle prints. |

The module id is `actionbar`. The client has one action bar: if another plugin writes to it, the newest writer wins and ExyliaStaff puts its line back on the next check.
