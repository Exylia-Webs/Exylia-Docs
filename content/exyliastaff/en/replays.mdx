---
title: "Replays"
description: "The last minute around any player, saved on a death, a report, a freeze, a capture rule or on request, and watched from any server, alone or together, with ping, server lag, hit cards and anticheat flags."
---

The server keeps the last minute of everything around every player in memory. Nothing is written
anywhere until something worth keeping happens: a death, a report, a freeze, a
[capture rule](#capture-rules), a staff member or a player asking for it. Then that window is cut, stored in the database, and any staff member on any
server can step into it as a flying camera, with the controls on their hotbar and a board that says
how the server and both players' connections were doing at every moment.

That board is the answer to "I died to lag": a ping of 900 or a server at 12 TPS is either on it or
it is not.

Module id `replay`, switch `modules.replays`, files under `modules/replay/`. Watching needs
PacketEvents.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/staffreplay <player>` | `exyliastaff.replay` | Opens that player's saved replays. `/sreplay` is an alias. |
| `/staffreplay capture <player>` | `exyliastaff.replay.capture` | Saves the last minute around a player on this server, now. |
| `/staffreplay debug <id>` | `exyliastaff.replay` | Lists what a saved replay holds, in chat. |
| `/staffreplay leave` | `exyliastaff.replay` | Stops watching. |
| `/staffreplay watch <id>` | `exyliastaff.replay` | Opens a replay by its number. The capture rule alert runs it. |
| `/staffreplay invite <player>` | `exyliastaff.replay` | Asks a staff member on this server to [watch with you](#watching-together). |
| `/staffreplay accept`, `/staffreplay decline` | `exyliastaff.replay` | Answers an invite. |
| `/staffreplay rules log` | `exyliastaff.replay.rules` | What the capture rules decided lately, and why. |
| `/savereplay` | `exyliastaff.savereplay` | A player saves their own last minute. `/saveclip` and `/clip` are aliases. |

The bare `/staffreplay` answers with its usage lines. The player argument is anybody the network
knows, online or not; `capture` needs them on this server, because the minute is only in this
server's memory.

The command is not `/replay` on purpose: a practice server already has one, for its duels.

<Callout type="tip" title="Grant /savereplay to everyone">
`exyliastaff.savereplay` is meant for the default group, like `/report` and `/helpop`. A player who
wants to report somebody and cannot record their own screen saves the minute instead, and the staff
member reading the report watches it. It costs one database row and a cooldown.
</Callout>

## When a replay is saved

| Cause | Saved when | Switch |
|---|---|---|
| Death | Somebody dies. The replay carries on `after-death` seconds past the death, so the fall is in it. | `capture.death` |
| Report | Somebody is reported, if they are on the reporter's server. | `capture.report` |
| Freeze | A freeze starts. | `capture.freeze` |
| Staff save | `/staffreplay capture`, or **Save now** in the list. | Always on |
| Player save | `/savereplay`, every `player-saves.cooldown-seconds`. | `player-saves.enabled` |
| Rule | A [capture rule](#capture-rules) matched: anticheat flags, a combat log, a big hit and more. | `rules.list.<name>.enabled` |

The anticheat module saves one too when a player sets off a burst of alerts; see
[Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web#anticheat-alerts).

Everybody is recorded, staff in vanish included: only staff watch these, and a staff member reviewing
their own fight, or a report against somebody who was spectating in vanish, needs them in it. A save
of somebody who was not recorded on this server in that window is "Nothing of them was recorded".

## Watching

`/staffreplay <player>` lists their replays, newest first, each drawn with the icon of its cause and
its length, how many players and things are in it, the date, the server and the coordinates. A replay
with anticheat flags in it says how many after its name. Left click watches; shift and right click
deletes, with `exyliastaff.replay.delete`. **Save now** keeps the last minute around that player, with
`exyliastaff.replay.capture`. A death at another player's hands is listed under the killer too, as a
**Kill**, without being saved twice.

The replay is staged on a temporary world before anybody moves, so one that cannot be shown never
takes the viewer anywhere. They are put into it as a flying camera; leaving puts them back where they
were. Opening another replay while watching goes straight to the next one.

| Slot | Item | Left click | Right click | Sneak and click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Spectral arrow | 5 seconds back | 5 seconds on | Back to the start |
| 1 | Clock | Play or pause; at the end it starts again | | |
| 2 | Arrow | One frame on | Ten frames on | One frame back (left), ten back (right) |
| 3 | Compass | Previous hit, death, teleport or disconnection | Next one | |
| 4 | Sugar | Slower | Faster | |
| 5 | Writable book | The [event log](#the-event-log) | | |
| 6 | Ender eye | First person, through each player in turn | The list of who is on screen | Next camera |
| 7 | Spyglass | Server or attacker view, while following | | |
| 8 | Barrier | Leave | | |

Each frame step pauses on the frame it lands on, which is how a single hit is read. The controls are
drawn on the client, never put in the inventory: the viewer's own items stay underneath and come back
when they leave. The layout is `lang/<language>/modules/replay/hotbar.yml`.

The **players** list shows everybody on screen at that moment with their ping: left click flies to
them, right click watches through their eyes. With `reveal-invisible` on, invisible players are drawn
glowing, so nobody hides in a replay.

### Cameras and views

Sneaking and clicking the ender eye cycles the camera: **free** flight, **first person** through a
player, **behind** them, and an **orbit** around them. The button's name shows the camera in use.

The spyglass decides where everybody else is drawn while you watch through somebody's eyes:
**server**, where the server had them, or **attacker**, where the followed player's screen had them.
The gap between the two is the lag the attacker played with. The view only applies in first person.

### What the replay tells you

As the replay passes them, chat lines, deaths, teleports, disconnections and totems are written into
the viewer's chat. With `narration.hits` on, so is every attack: who hit whom, for how much, which hit
of theirs on that target it was, and its reach, look angle and flick; an attack the client sent and the
server never counted is marked as such. With `narration.flags` on, so is every anticheat flag (Grim,
Vulcan, Spartan or Matrix) with its check and count. A look more than `narration.wide-angle` degrees
off the target is drawn as an error.

The action bar shows the time, the frame, the speed, whose eyes the camera is in, the view, and what
happened on the frame on screen.

### The event log

The writable book opens every moment of the replay worth jumping to: hits, flags, deaths, totems, chat,
teleports, disconnections and bursts of block changes. It opens on the page of the moment on screen;
a click jumps there, paused. Three filters at the bottom show or hide **combat**, **flags** and
**other** moments, and are kept while you watch that replay.

Each hit carries a **hit card**: whether the server counted it, the damage of that hit and of the pair
in all, the reach by the server and by the attacker's screen, how far off the look was, the flick just
before, the lag the attacker saw, their ping, and the attacker's flags within a second of it. The
screen is `lang/<language>/menus/replay/events.yml`.

## Watching together

`/staffreplay invite <player>` asks another staff member on this server, holding `exyliastaff.replay`,
to watch the replay you are in. They get a centred invite with **ACCEPT** and **DECLINE** buttons
(`/staffreplay accept` or `decline`), valid for a minute, and join at the moment you are on.

The time is shared: anybody in the room can pause, seek, step or change the speed, and the others are
told in one short line who did what, folded so holding rewind is one line, not twenty. Joining and
leaving are told too.

## Capture rules

Rules save a replay by themselves when something suspicious happens. Every rule ships turned off, and
a trigger with no rule on registers nothing, so a server that never turns one on pays nothing for them.

| Trigger | Sets it off |
|---|---|
| `ANTICHEAT_FLAG` | `count` flags within `within-seconds`, of a check whose name contains one of `checks` (empty is any). |
| `COMBAT_LOG` | Somebody leaves while tagged in combat. |
| `BIG_HIT` | One hit takes at least `hearts` after armour. |
| `TOTEM_POP` | Somebody pops a totem. |
| `LOW_HEALTH` | Somebody falls to `hearts` or fewer and lives. |
| `KILL_STREAK` | `count` kills within `within-seconds`. |
| `RAPID_DEATHS` | `count` deaths within `within-seconds`: spawn camping. |
| `VALUABLE_BLOCK` | `count` of `materials` broken within `within-seconds`: x-ray. |

`subject` picks whose replay is saved: `ACTOR`, who set it off (the flagged, the one who quit, the
attacker, the killer, the miner); `VICTIM`, who was hurt; or `KILLER`, who hurt them. `seconds` is how
far back it reaches (`0` uses `seconds` above), and `cooldown-seconds` how long before the same rule
saves the same player again.

A match still goes through a few guards, and the first that stops it is what the log records: the
player holds `exyliastaff.replay.rules.exempt`; the rule's cooldown; a capture of them, by a rule, a
death or a report, within `rules.aggregate-seconds`, which this one folds into; the server-wide
`rules.max-per-minute`; or `rules.max-concurrent` captures being written. What passes is saved under the
cause **Rule**.

Staff holding `exyliastaff.replay.rules` get a line for every capture with a **WATCH** button, which
runs `/staffreplay watch <id>`, and the `replays` [Discord webhook](/docs/exyliastaff/configuration#discord-webhooks)
posts it. `/staffreplay rules log` shows the totals and the last twenty decisions: captured, cooldown,
folded, over budget, busy, exempt, or nothing in the box.

## From reports and restores

A replay is one click from where staff need it:

- The report alert ends with **▶ Replay**, which runs `/reports replay <id>`.
- In `/reports`, shift and left click a report; in one player's reports, press Q on a report, or open
  **Replays** for their whole list.
- In the inventory restore screen, a death backup has **Watch what happened**: the death replay
  nearest to that backup.

All of them need `exyliastaff.replay`. When the replay saved with a report is missing, because the
player was not recorded or it is still being written, the click opens that player's list instead.

## Across servers

Replays are rows in the shared database, so a death on one server is watched from any other. With
`terrain` on, the ground the replay happened on is stored with it, from `vertical-margin` blocks
above and below where people went, up to `max-chunks` chunks, so it can be watched anywhere and after
the place has changed. Without it, a replay can only be watched on the server it was recorded on,
over whatever is there now.

The file writes every key of every rule; the ones a trigger does not read are left out above.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `seconds` | `60` | How far back a replay reaches. The server keeps this much around every player in memory at all times. |
| `radius` | `32` | Blocks around a player that are recorded. Whoever was this close to anywhere they went is in their replay. |
| `after-death` | `3` | Seconds a death replay carries on after the death. |
| `death-seconds` | `30` | How far back a death replay reaches. Deaths are saved by the thousand on a busy server and the fight is rarely longer. `0` uses `seconds`. |
| `terrain` | `true` | Keep the ground, so a replay can be watched on any server. |
| `vertical-margin` | `6` | Blocks above and below where people went that the ground is kept. |
| `max-chunks` | `400` | Chunks one replay keeps. A long chase keeps its end. |
| `max-entities` | `1500` | Things that are not players followed at once, server-wide. |
| `player-saves.cooldown-seconds` | `120` | Between two `/savereplay` by the same player. |
| `keep-days` | `14` | Days a replay is kept. The ground goes with the last replay that needed it. |
| `death-keep-days` | `3` | Days a death replay is kept. They decide a restore, which happens within a day or two, and are most of what is stored. `0` uses `keep-days`. |
| `keep-per-player` | `30` | Replays kept per player; the oldest go first. |
| `reveal-invisible` | `true` | Draw invisible players glowing while watching. |
| `board` | | The sidebar shown while watching. |
| `icons` | | The icon of each cause in the list. |
| `narration.hits` | `true` | Write every attack into the viewer's chat. |
| `narration.flags` | `true` | Write every anticheat flag into the viewer's chat. |
| `narration.wide-angle` | `10.0` | Degrees the look may be off the target's body before the angle is drawn as an error. |
| `rules.aggregate-seconds` | `20` | A capture of somebody within this many seconds of another capture of them folds into it. |
| `rules.max-per-minute` | `6` | Captures rules may save in any minute, server-wide. `0` is no limit. |
| `rules.max-concurrent` | `2` | Captures rules may have being written at once. `0` is no limit. |
| `rules.list` | | The rules, by a name of your choosing. See [Capture rules](#capture-rules). |

A replay [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) marks as evidence is passed by `keep-days`,
`death-keep-days` and `keep-per-player` until the web hands it back.

Expired replays are purged when the module starts and every five minutes after that, by one server of
the network at a time and in small pages, so a backlog drains in steps instead of all at once.

## Messages

`lang/<language>/modules/replay/messages.yml`: `saved` with `%target%`, `%seconds%` and `%id%`;
`player-saved` with `%seconds%` and `%id%`; `player-cooldown` with `%time%`; `player-disabled`;
`nothing-recorded`, `no-replays`, `no-replay-for-backup`; `loading`, `load-failed`, `unsupported`,
`deleted`, `not-watching`; `following` with `%player%`, `following-none`, `nobody-on-screen`,
`no-more-events`; the chat lines `line-chat`, `line-death`, `line-teleport`, `line-quit` and
`line-totem`; `line-hit` with `%player%`, `%target%`, `%damage%`, `%total%`, `%number%` and
`%analysis%`, `line-hit-missed`, `hit-analysis`, `hit-angle` and `hit-angle-wide`; `line-flag` with
`%player%`, `%check%`, `%count%` and `%detail%`, and `flag-detail`; `hud` with `%time%`, `%length%`,
`%speed%`, `%state%`, `%bar%`, `%following%`, `%frame%`, `%view%` and `%happened%`, and its pieces
`state-playing`, `state-paused`, `bar-done`, `bar-left`, `hud-following`; `unknown`, `no-opponent`;
`usage`; `causes`, the name of each cause, with `kill` for a death the player caused and `rule` for a
capture rule; `log`, the event log's filter states, hit card values and `list-flags` with `%count%`;
`view`, the view button's lines and states; `happened`, the moment shown at the end of the hud;
`camera`, the camera names and lines; `room`, the invite and what the others did; and `rules`, with
`captured` (`%player%`, `%rule%`, `%detail%`, `%id%`), the `/staffreplay rules log` lines and the
`reasons`.

## Placeholders

Read by the viewer, while watching; empty otherwise.

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_replay_time%` | Time on screen. |
| `%staff_replay_length%` | Length of the replay. |
| `%staff_replay_speed%` | Playback speed. |
| `%staff_replay_state%` | `state-playing` or `state-paused`. |
| `%staff_replay_following%` | Whose eyes the camera is in, empty for none. |
| `%staff_replay_player%` | The player the replay is about. |
| `%staff_replay_player_ping%` | Their ping on the tick on screen. |
| `%staff_replay_opponent%` | Who they were last fighting on that tick, or `no-opponent`. |
| `%staff_replay_opponent_ping%` | That player's ping. |
| `%staff_replay_tps%` | The server's TPS on that tick. |
| `%staff_replay_mspt%` | The server's MSPT on that tick. |
| `%staff_replay_cause%` | Why it was saved. |
| `%staff_replay_server%` | The server it was recorded on. |
| `%staff_replay_view%` | The view button's state: `view.state-none`, `state-server` or `state-attacker`. |
| `%staff_replay_camera%` | The camera in use: `camera.free`, `first-person`, `behind` or `orbit`. |

A figure that was not recorded reads as `unknown`.

## Storage

`staff_replays` for what was saved, `staff_replay_data` for the recordings and `staff_replay_chunks`
for the ground.
