ExyliaStaff keeps the last minute around every player in memory and saves it the moment something worth keeping happens. You open it as a flying camera, with the controls on your hotbar and a board that shows the ping of both players and the server's TPS at every moment. That board answers "I died to lag" on the spot.

## Before you start

- Staff need `exyliastaff.replay` to watch. Saving, deleting and the automatic-capture alerts have their own permissions, listed at the end.
- Watching needs [PacketEvents](/docs/exyliastaff/installation) on the server. Without it you are told "Replays need PacketEvents on this server."
- The module switch is `modules.replays` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`, on by default. Its settings are in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/replay/config.yml`.
- Nothing to set up for the common cases: deaths, reports and freezes are saved by default.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the player's replays">
    Run `/staffreplay <player>` (alias `/sreplay`). Any name the network knows is accepted, online or not. The **REPLAYS** list shows the newest first. Each one has the icon of its cause (Death, Report, Freeze, Staff save, Player save, Automatic), its length, how many players and things it holds, the date, the server and the coordinates. A replay with anticheat flags in it shows how many after its name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch one">
    Left click a replay. You are put in as a flying camera, and leaving puts you back where you were. The hotbar is the remote control; see below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what happened">
    Pause on the moment, step frame by frame with the arrow, or open the **EVENT LOG** (the book) to jump straight to a hit, flag or death.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave">
    Click the barrier (**LEAVE**, ninth hotbar slot), or run `/staffreplay leave`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

On a test server, run `/staffreplay capture <a player online here>`. You are told "Saved the last 60 s around the player" with the replay number. Run `/staffreplay <player>`: a replay with the **Staff save** cause is on top. Open it and click the clock to play and pause.

## Going further

### Save a replay by hand

- `/staffreplay capture <player>` (needs `exyliastaff.replay.capture`) saves their last minute now. The player must be on this server, because the minute lives in this server's memory. The same action is **Save now** in the replay list, which only appears for a player who is online.
- A death at another player's hands is also listed under the killer, as a **Kill**, without being saved twice.

### Let players save their own

`/savereplay` (aliases `/saveclip`, `/clip`) lets a player keep their own last minute, so a report arrives with the evidence already attached. `exyliastaff.savereplay` is granted to everyone by default, there is a cooldown of 120 seconds (`player-saves.cooldown-seconds`), and `player-saves.enabled: false` turns it off.

### When a replay is saved

| Cause | Saved when | Switch |
|---|---|---|
| Death | Somebody dies. The replay carries on 3 seconds past the death (`after-death`). | `capture.death` |
| Report | Somebody is reported, if they are on the reporter's server. | `capture.report` |
| Freeze | A [freeze](/docs/exyliastaff/freeze) starts. | `capture.freeze` |
| Staff save | `/staffreplay capture`, or **Save now**. | Always on |
| Player save | `/savereplay`. | `player-saves.enabled` |
| Automatic | A [capture rule](#capture-rules) matched. | `rules.list.<name>.enabled` |

The anticheat module also saves one when a player sets off a burst of alerts; see [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web#anticheat-alerts).

Everybody is recorded, staff in vanish included: only staff watch replays, and a report against somebody who was spectating in vanish needs them in it. A replay of somebody who was not recorded on this server in that window answers "Nothing of the player was recorded on this server just now".

### The hotbar controls

The controls are drawn on your screen and never touch your inventory: your own items come back when you leave. Sneaking changes what a click does.

| Hotbar position | Item and name | Left click | Right click | Sneak and click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spectral arrow, **TIME** | 5 seconds back | 5 seconds on | Back to the start |
| 2 | Clock, **PLAY / PAUSE** | Play or pause (at the end it starts again) | Same | Same |
| 3 | Arrow, **FRAME BY FRAME** | One frame on | Ten frames on | One frame back (left), ten back (right) |
| 4 | Compass, **EVENTS** | Previous hit, death, teleport or disconnection | Next one | |
| 5 | Sugar, **SPEED** | Slower | Faster | |
| 6 | Writable book, **EVENT LOG** | Open the [event log](#the-event-log) | Same | Switch the replay chat |
| 7 | Ender eye, **PLAYERS** | First person, through each player in turn | List of who is on screen | Next camera |
| 8 | Spyglass, **VIEW** | Switch server or attacker view | Same | Same |
| 9 | Barrier, **LEAVE** | Leave | Same | Same |

Each frame step pauses on the frame it lands on, which is how a single hit is read. The speed at a quarter shows whether a hit landed or not.

### Cameras and views

Sneak and click the ender eye to cycle the camera: **Free** flight, **First person** through a player, **Behind** them, **Orbit** around them. The button name shows the one in use. Right click opens the list of everybody on screen at that moment with their ping: left click flies to them, right click watches through their eyes. With `reveal-invisible` on (the default) invisible players are drawn glowing, so nobody hides in a replay.

The spyglass decides where everybody else is drawn while you watch through somebody's eyes. **Server** shows them where the server had them; **Attacker** shows them where the followed player's screen had them. The difference is the lag that player was playing with. It needs a followed player first, or you are told to follow one.

### Replay chat

What the replay writes into your chat as it passes is set by sneak and clicking the event log. Three modes cycle:

| Mode | Your chat shows |
|---|---|
| **Conversation** (default) | Only the lines written by the people the replay is about: the reported player and whoever reported them, or the one who died and their killer. A replay a player saved with `/savereplay` shows every line, since there is no other side to name. |
| **All chat** | Every chat line in the replay, whoever wrote it. |
| **Everything** | Chat plus deaths, teleports, disconnections, totems, and, with `narration.hits` and `narration.flags` on, every attack and every anticheat flag. |

Hits and flags are always in the action bar and in the event log; chat is for the conversation unless you ask for more. In Everything, an attack reads who hit whom, for how much, which hit of theirs on that target it was, and its reach, look angle and flick. An attack the client sent and the server never counted says so. A look more than `narration.wide-angle` degrees (10) off the target is drawn as an error.

The action bar shows the time, the frame, the speed, whose eyes you are in, the view, and what happened on the frame on screen.

### The event log

The book lists every moment worth jumping to: hits, flags, deaths, totems, chat, teleports, disconnections and bursts of block changes. It opens on the page of the moment on screen, and a click jumps there, paused. The **Filter** buttons at the bottom show or hide **Combat**, **Flags** and **Other** moments, and are kept while you watch that replay.

Each hit has a **hit card**: whether the server counted it, the damage of that hit and of the pair in all, the reach by the server and by the attacker's screen, how far off the look was, the flick just before, the lag the attacker saw, their ping and the attacker's flags within a second of it.

### Watch together

Run `/staffreplay invite <player>` while you are watching. The other staff member must be on this server and hold `exyliastaff.replay`. They get a centred invite with **ACCEPT** and **DECLINE** buttons (`/staffreplay accept` or `decline`), valid for a minute, and join at the moment you are on. Time is shared: anybody in the room can pause, seek, step or change speed, and the others are told in one short line who did what. Joining and leaving are told too.

### Capture rules

Rules save a replay by themselves when something suspicious happens. Every rule ships turned off, and a trigger with no rule on costs nothing. Turn one on by setting `enabled: true` under `rules.list.<name>` in `modules/replay/config.yml` (or from `/staffadmin` settings, **Replays**).

| Trigger | Sets it off | Starter rule (off) |
|---|---|---|
| `ANTICHEAT_FLAG` | `count` flags within `within-seconds`, of a check whose name contains one of `checks` (empty is any) | `anticheat-flags` (10 in 30 s), `reach-flags` (3 in 20 s, reach, killaura, aim) |
| `COMBAT_LOG` | Somebody leaves while tagged in combat | `combat-log` |
| `BIG_HIT` | One hit takes at least `hearts` after armour | `big-hit` (8 hearts) |
| `TOTEM_POP` | Somebody pops a totem | `totem-pop` |
| `LOW_HEALTH` | Somebody falls to `hearts` or fewer and lives | `low-health` (2 hearts) |
| `KILL_STREAK` | `count` kills within `within-seconds` | `kill-streak` (5 in 120 s) |
| `RAPID_DEATHS` | `count` deaths within `within-seconds`: spawn camping | `spawn-camping` (3 in 60 s) |
| `VALUABLE_BLOCK` | `count` of `materials` broken within `within-seconds`: x-ray | `x-ray` (8 in 120 s) |

`subject` picks whose replay is saved: `ACTOR` (who set it off), `VICTIM` (who was hurt) or `KILLER` (who hurt them). `seconds` is how far back it reaches (`0` uses the main `seconds`), and `cooldown-seconds` how long before the same rule saves the same player again.

A match goes through guards, and the first that stops it is what the log records: the player holds `exyliastaff.replay.rules.exempt`; the rule's cooldown; a capture of them within `rules.aggregate-seconds` (20), which this one folds into; `rules.max-per-minute` (6), server-wide; or `rules.max-concurrent` (2) captures already being written. What passes is saved as **Automatic**.

Staff holding `exyliastaff.replay.rules` get a line for every capture with a **WATCH** button that runs `/staffreplay watch <id>`, and the `replays` [Discord webhook](/docs/exyliastaff/configuration#discord-webhooks) posts it. `/staffreplay rules log` shows the totals and the last 20 decisions: captured, on cooldown, folded, over budget, busy, exempt, or nothing in the box.

### From reports and restores

A replay is one click from where staff need it:

- The report alert ends with **▶ Replay**, which runs `/reports replay <id>`.
- In `/reports`, shift and left click a report. In one player's reports, press Q (drop) on a report, or click **Replays** for their whole list.
- In the [inventory restore](/docs/exyliastaff/inventory-restore) screen, a death backup has **Watch what happened**: the death replay nearest to that backup.

If the replay saved with a report is missing, because the player was not recorded or it is still being written, the click opens that player's replay list instead.

### Delete a replay

Shift and right click a replay in the list, then confirm. It needs `exyliastaff.replay.delete`.

### How long replays are kept

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `keep-days` | `14` | Days a replay is kept. The ground goes with the last replay that needed it. |
| `death-keep-days` | `3` | Days a death replay is kept, since a restore is decided within a day or two. `0` uses `keep-days`. |
| `keep-per-player` | `30` | Replays kept per player; the oldest go first. |

A replay that [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) marks as evidence is passed by all three until the web hands it back. Expired replays are purged when the module starts and every five minutes after, by one server of the network at a time.

### Watch from any server

Replays live in the shared database, so a death on one server is watched from any other. With `terrain: true` (the default) the ground is stored with the replay, from `vertical-margin` (6) blocks above and below where people went, up to `max-chunks` (400), so it plays anywhere and after the place has changed. Without it, a replay can only be watched on the server it was recorded on, over whatever is there now.

<Details title="Configuration reference">
```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/replay/config.yml"
seconds: 60
radius: 32.0
after-death: 3
death-seconds: 30
terrain: true
vertical-margin: 6
max-chunks: 400
max-entities: 1500
capture: { death: true, report: true, freeze: true }
player-saves: { enabled: true, cooldown-seconds: 120 }
keep-days: 14
death-keep-days: 3
keep-per-player: 30
reveal-invisible: true
narration: { chat: CONVERSATION, hits: true, flags: true, wide-angle: 10.0 }
rules:
  aggregate-seconds: 20
  max-per-minute: 6
  max-concurrent: 2
  list:
    big-hit:
      enabled: false
      trigger: BIG_HIT
      seconds: 20
      subject: ACTOR
      cooldown-seconds: 120
      hearts: 8
```

The file also holds `board` (the sidebar while watching, with the replay placeholders below), `icons` and the rest of the rules. Only `big-hit` is shown; the nine starter rules are listed above.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `seconds` | `60` | How far back a replay reaches. The server keeps this much around every player in memory. |
| `radius` | `32` | Blocks around a player that are recorded. |
| `after-death` | `3` | Seconds a death replay carries on after the death. |
| `death-seconds` | `30` | How far back a death replay reaches. `0` uses `seconds`. |
| `max-entities` | `1500` | Things that are not players followed at once, server-wide. |
| `narration.chat` | `CONVERSATION` | Chat mode when somebody starts watching: `CONVERSATION`, `ALL_CHAT` or `EVERYTHING`. |
| `icons` | | Icon per cause in the list: death `SKELETON_SKULL`, report `WRITABLE_BOOK`, freeze `PACKED_ICE`, manual `SPYGLASS`, player `PLAYER_HEAD`, rule `OBSERVER`, raid `TNT`. |
| `board` | | The sidebar shown while watching, updating every 5 ticks. |

The **Raid** cause is only saved by other plugins through the ExyliaStaff [API](/docs/exyliastaff/api); the plugin itself never saves one.

Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/replay/messages.yml`; the hotbar in `lang/<language>/modules/replay/hotbar.yml`; the list, players and event log screens in `lang/<language>/menus/replay/`. See [messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages) and [edit menus](/docs/exylialib/edit-menus).

`/staffreplay debug <id>` prints in chat what a saved replay holds (ticks, actors, scenes), for tracking down a broken one.
</Details>
