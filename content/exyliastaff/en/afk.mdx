ExyliaStaff knows which players are actually at the keyboard. Random teleport stops dropping staff onto empty bodies, the staff sidebar can say how much of the player count is real, and [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) flags idle players.

## Before you start

- There is no command and no permission: the answer is read from what the player does, not a state they set.
- The module switch is `modules.afk` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`, on by default. Its setting is in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/afk/config.yml`.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the idle time">
    In `modules/afk/config.yml` set `after-seconds`. The default is `300`: five minutes without moving a block, interacting, chatting or typing a command before a player counts as AFK.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show it in the sidebar">
    The staff sidebar already has a line `AFK » %staff_afk_count%` (see [Placeholders](/docs/exyliastaff/placeholders)). Add the placeholder to any other board that reads PlaceholderAPI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Skip idle players when teleporting">
    In `modules/teleport/config.yml`, `random-skip-afk` is `true` by default; set it to `false` to stop skipping. [Random teleport](/docs/exyliastaff/teleport) then skips idle players. That key needs this module: with the AFK module off, nobody is skipped for being idle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Stand still on a test account for longer than `after-seconds`, then read `%exyliastaff_staff_afk_count%` with `/papi parse me %exyliastaff_staff_afk_count%`: it goes up by one. Move and it goes back down at once.

## Going further

### How idle is measured

ExyliaStaff keeps one timestamp per online player. Moving a block, interacting, chatting or typing a command marks them as at the keyboard, and a player who just joined counts as present whatever they did last session. The answer is the time between now and that timestamp, so nothing runs on a timer and the cost does not grow with the player count. The timestamp is written at most once a second per player.

The [staff log](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-log) counts staff-mode time as AFK with its own threshold, `afk-after-seconds`; changing one does not change the other.
