An invisibility potion hides a player from everyone, staff included. Reveal gives a staff member back what the potion took: they see who is standing there, and nobody else's screen changes.

## Before you start

- Staff need `exyliastaff.reveal`.
- Reveal needs [PacketEvents](/docs/exyliastaff/installation). Without it the module does not turn on, rather than pretending to work.
- The module switch is `modules.see-invisible` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`. Its setting is in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/reveal/config.yml`.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Run `/seeinvis`. It toggles, and tells you the new state; `/seeinvis on` and `/seeinvis off` set it. The aliases are `/seeinvisible` and `/reveal`. In the [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) hotbar, click **INVISIBLES** (slot 31) to do the same.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for them">
    Players hidden by an invisibility potion now appear to you. By default they stay invisible and are outlined through walls; see below to draw them whole.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Give a test account an invisibility potion, turn on `/seeinvis` and look at it: you should see the outline, while the test account's other viewers see nothing. Run `/seeinvis` again and the player goes back to being hidden for you.

## Going further

### Choose how they are drawn

`style` in `modules/reveal/config.yml` picks how a hidden player is drawn.

| `style` | What you see |
|---|---|
| `OUTLINE` (default) | They stay invisible and are outlined through walls. It reads faster in a cave. |
| `SOLID` | They are drawn whole, as if the potion were not there. |

Either way it is a packet your client receives. The potion is still running, the player still has it, and every other player still sees nothing.

### It remembers you

Your choice is saved per staff member. When you join with it on, you are told you still see invisible players from your last session. If you lose `exyliastaff.reveal`, it turns off and you are told so.

### Reveal and vanish

[Vanish](/docs/exyliastaff/vanish) hides a staff member from players; reveal shows a staff member a player who is hiding. They are opposite directions of the same question and neither touches the other: revealing does not break a vanish, and a vanished staff member can reveal.
