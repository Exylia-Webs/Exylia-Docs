title: "Inspect"
description: "Silent mirrors of a player's inventory, ender chest and any container, online or offline, editable or read-only by permission, and /seen."
---

An inspection opens a live mirror of somebody's inventory: no chest lid, no sound, the mirror follows
the source while it is open, and with the edit permission what you move is written back. A player who
is offline is read from the file this server keeps for them.

Module id `inspect`, switch `modules.inspect`, files under `modules/inspect/`. Needs PacketEvents.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/inspect <player>` | `exyliastaff.inspect` | The player's inventory. `/invsee` and `/examine` are aliases. |
| `/echest <player>` | `exyliastaff.inspect.enderchest` | Their ender chest. `/enderchest` is an alias. |
| `/seen <player>` | `exyliastaff.inspect` | When they were last here, and how long they have played. `/lastseen` is an alias. |

Each one takes anybody, online or not. Somebody here is mirrored live. Somebody online on another
server is pointed there — "is online on Survival. Inspect them there." — because that server holds the
real copy. Somebody offline is read from this server's player data.

The book in the hotbar does the same on the player you look at, within five blocks: right click for
the inventory, left click for the ender chest. Right clicking a chest, barrel or any other container
with an empty hand opens it silently, with `exyliastaff.inspect.containers`.

## Offline players

Paper keeps one player-data file per player on each server, and that is what an offline inspection
opens: their inventory, armour and off hand, or their ender chest, as they were when they last left
**this** server. Several staff looking at the same player share one copy.

With `exyliastaff.inspect.edit` the copy is editable, and it is written back to the file when the last
staff member closes it. If the player logs in while it is open, every window on it closes and the copy
is saved before the server reads the file, so they load what staff left. A file written by an older
server version is shown but never rewritten. The title says `(offline, editable)` or
`(offline, read-only)`.

## /seen

For somebody here or online elsewhere, the server they are on and their play time. For somebody
offline, from this server's records: how long ago they were last here and the date, their first join,
their play time, and the world and coordinates they left from. A player who never joined this server
is "has never played on this server". [`/tpoffline`](/docs/exyliastaff/teleport) takes you to that
spot.

## Read-only or editable

With `exyliastaff.inspect.edit` the inventory and ender chest mirrors are editable and the title says
so; without it they are read-only. Container mirrors are always read-only.

A mirror closes on its own when the viewer leaves staff mode, loses `exyliastaff.inspect`, or the
module is disabled.

## Configuration

The window titles are text, so they live with the language:

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/lang/en/modules/inspect/config.yml"
inventory-title: '{primary}&lINVENTORY {letters_black}» {letters}%target%'
enderchest-title: '{primary}&lENDER CHEST {letters_black}» {letters}%target%'
container-title: '{primary}&lCONTAINER {letters_black}» {letters}%target%'
```

`%target%` is the player's name, or the container's block name for containers.

## Messages

`lang/<language>/modules/inspect/messages.yml`: `opened` and `opened-enderchest` with `%target%` and
`%mode%`; `no-target`; `open-failed`; `online-elsewhere` with `%target%` and `%server%`; `seen`, the
lines for an offline player, with `%target%`, `%ago%`, `%date%`, `%first%`, `%playtime%` and
`%location%`; `seen-online` with `%target%`, `%server%` and `%playtime%`; `seen-unknown`; and the four
words `%mode%` reads: `mode-editable`, `mode-read-only`, `mode-offline-editable` and
`mode-offline-read-only`.
