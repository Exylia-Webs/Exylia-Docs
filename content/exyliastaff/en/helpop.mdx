A player runs `/helpop <message>` to ask for help. Every staff member on every server gets an alert with a **Claim** button. The one who claims it answers, and from then on whatever the player types into `/helpop` goes to that staff member, wherever they are. The request closes when staff close it, or on its own if nobody answers in time.

## Before you start

- Players need `exyliastaff.helpop`. Staff who answer need `exyliastaff.helpop.manage`.
- The module switch is `modules.helpop` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="A player asks">
    The player runs `/helpop My chest was emptied, please look`. They are told their request number. A player has one live request at a time: asking again while it is unclaimed answers "You already have request #12 waiting".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim it">
    Staff see the alert with **[ ✔ Claim ]**, **[ ✦ Open list ]** and **[ ➥ Teleport ]**. Click **Claim**, or run `/hs claim <id>`. The player is told who is on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply">
    Run `/hs reply <id> <message>`. The player answers by typing `/helpop <message>` again, and you see it as part of the same conversation. Replying to an unclaimed request claims it; one claimed by somebody else is refused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close it">
    Run `/hs close <id> [reason]`. Without a reason the default close reason is sent to the player. Any staff member can close any request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

As a player run `/helpop test`, then as staff on another server confirm the alert arrived, claim it and reply. The player should see your lines as `#<id> <your name> » <message>`.

## Going further

### The list

`/hs` (long form `/helpop-staff`) opens the **HELPOP** menu, and so does the turtle helmet (slot 14) in the [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) hotbar. Each request is a head:

- Left click claims an unclaimed request.
- Right click teleports you to the player, following them to their server.
- Shift left click teleports you on duty (into [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) and [vanish](/docs/exyliastaff/vanish) first).
- Shift right click closes it.

`/hs list` prints the open and claimed requests, oldest first, in chat. The console can use it.

### Limit how often players ask

`cooldown-seconds` (default 30) is the wait between two requests. Give a rank its own with `exyliastaff.helpop.cooldown.<seconds>`, for example `exyliastaff.helpop.cooldown.10`; the lowest one a player holds wins. Once a request is claimed, replies are not subject to that wait, only to a two second pause between lines so a flood is dropped silently. If the handler is not online right now, the player is told and their message is delivered on the handler's server.

### Timeouts

Every thirty seconds the module closes requests that waited too long. An unclaimed request closes after `timeout-minutes` (default 15) with the `timeout-reason`, and the player is told nobody was available. A claimed request with no message for `claimed-timeout-minutes` (default 30) closes with the `inactive-reason`. Set either to `0` to turn it off.

<Details title="Configuration and messages">

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/helpop/config.yml"
cooldown-seconds: 30
timeout-minutes: 15
claimed-timeout-minutes: 30
alert:
  sound:
    name: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL
    pitch: 1.2
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `cooldown-seconds` | `30` | Wait between two requests by one player. A wait under five minutes is not kept across a restart. |
| `timeout-minutes` | `15` | Minutes before an unclaimed request closes. `0` never. |
| `claimed-timeout-minutes` | `30` | Minutes without a message before a claimed request closes. `0` never. |
| `alert` | bell sound | Effect played for staff on every server; add a title or an action bar to it. |

Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/helpop/messages.yml`: `sent` and `already-open` (`%id%`); `cooldown` (`%time%`); `alert-header`, `alert-line` and `alert-actions` (`%id%`, `%player%`, `%server%`, `%message%`; the last is a clickable row you may empty); `list-header`, `list-line`, `list-empty`; `not-found`, `already-claimed`, `not-yours`; `claimed`, `claimed-notice`; `transcript-staff` and `transcript-player`; `handler-offline`; `closed`, `closed-notice`, `timed-out`; `default-close-reason`, `timeout-reason`, `inactive-reason`; `player-offline`, `player-not-here`, `teleported`, `following`. See [ExyliaLib messages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages) and [colours](/docs/exylialib/colours).
