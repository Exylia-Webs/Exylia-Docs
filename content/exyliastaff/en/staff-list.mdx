`/stafflist` prints every staff member online on every server of your network, one block per server in alphabetical order. Staff also get a short line when a colleague connects, leaves or changes server.

## Before you start

- Give the rank `exyliastaff.stafflist` to see the list. A player is listed when they hold `exyliastaff.staff`; that is the only thing that makes someone "staff" here.
- The module switch is `modules.staff-list` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`. The servers share the list through Redis, like the rest of the network features.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    Run `/stafflist` (alias `/slist`), or click your own head, **STAFF ONLINE** (slot 34), in the [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) hotbar. The console can run it too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read it">
    The header shows how many staff you can see, then each server with its members. Staff viewers see `[V]` after the name of a [vanished](/docs/exyliastaff/vanish) member and `[S]` after one in [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Have a second staff member join another server and run `/stafflist`: their server appears with their name under it. Staff online right now also see a line such as "Name » connected (server)" when they join.

## Going further

### Who sees vanished staff

A vanished member is shown only to a viewer who holds `exyliastaff.staff` **and** whose vanish level is at least the member's. The console sees everyone. Other viewers see plain names, no markers and no vanished members, and the header counts only what the viewer can see. This is on purpose: a list that names who is hidden would undo vanish.

### Join, leave and server-change lines

Staff holding `exyliastaff.staff` get a line when a colleague connects, disconnects or moves to another server of the network. A server change is two reports (one server loses the member, another gains them), so a disconnect is held back for `switch-grace-seconds` (default 5); if the member shows up elsewhere in that time it is announced as a change instead of a leave. A vanished member is announced only to staff who could see them in the list. You never get a line about yourself. Empty the `announce-join`, `announce-quit` or `announce-switch` message to switch that line off.

### How the servers stay in sync

Each server publishes its staff on the `presence` channel when a staff member joins or quits and every `heartbeat-seconds`. A server not heard from for `expire-seconds` is dropped from the list. Disabling the module sends an empty list so the others drop the server at once. AFK is not tracked here.

## Commands and permissions

<Commands group="staff list" />

<Permissions group="staff list" />

<Details title="Configuration and messages">

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/stafflist/config.yml"
heartbeat-seconds: 30
expire-seconds: 90
switch-grace-seconds: 5
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `heartbeat-seconds` | `30` | Seconds between presence reports to the other servers. |
| `expire-seconds` | `90` | Seconds without a report before a server leaves the list. Keep it above `heartbeat-seconds`. |
| `switch-grace-seconds` | `5` | Time a member who left one server has to appear on another before it counts as a disconnect. |

Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/stafflist/messages.yml`: `header` (`%total%`), `server` (`%server%`), `entry` (`%player%`, `%markers%`), `vanished-marker`, `staff-mode-marker`, `empty`, `footer`, and the three announcements `announce-join`, `announce-quit` (`%player%`, `%server%`) and `announce-switch` (`%player%`, `%from%`, `%to%`). See [ExyliaLib messages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages) and [colours](/docs/exylialib/colours).
