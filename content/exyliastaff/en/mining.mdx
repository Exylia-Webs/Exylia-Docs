ExyliaStaff watches how players find ore, not how much they mine. Somebody who walks straight at buried diamonds in the dark, seconds apart, gets a suspicion score from 0 to 100, and staff on every server are alerted when it crosses a level.

## Before you start

- Staff who receive alerts need `exyliastaff.mining.alerts`. Staff who read records need `exyliastaff.mining.view`, and `exyliastaff.mining.reset` to wipe one.
- The module switch is `modules.mining` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`. Its settings live in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/mining/config.yml`.
- Optional: the [Discord](/docs/exyliastaff/configuration#discord-webhooks) module posts each mining alert to the alerts webhook.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn your alerts on">
    Run `/mining`. It toggles your alerts and tells you the new state; `/mining on` and `/mining off` set it. In the [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) hotbar, right click the diamond pickaxe (slot 27, **MINING**) to do the same. `/notifications` and `/xraynotify` are aliases of `/mining`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See who stands out">
    Run `/mining top`, or left click the pickaxe. The menu **SUSPICIOUS MINERS** lists the 45 highest scores, decayed to now, one head per player with their level and score. From the console the command prints the top ten as text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a player's record">
    Click a head, or run `/mining view <player>` (works for offline players). The menu **MINING** shows one item per ore they mined. **Teleport** takes you to them (shift click goes on duty first), **Reset** wipes the record after a confirm screen, and **Suspicious miners** goes back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset a record">
    Run `/mining reset <player>`. It only previews what would be erased; add `confirm` to do it: `/mining reset <player> confirm`. Staff with alerts on are told who reset it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

In survival, dig a tunnel in the dark to a buried diamond vein, then another, a few seconds apart. Run `/mining view <your name>`: you should see your ores, and `/mining top` should list you with a level. When the score reaches a higher level than the last one announced, staff with alerts on get `<player> level <level> » <score>/100 (<server>)` with a sound that gets more urgent at each level.

## Going further

### What counts as evidence

Only ores of a family marked `valuable` feed the score; the rest count like stone. Of those, only the first block of a vein counts (a vein is up to `vein.max-size` connected blocks, 32 by default, remembered 30 minutes), and only a buried one: at most `ore-exposure.partial-faces` faces open to air, 1 by default. An ore on a cave wall is what a torch finds.

A block a player placed is ignored when broken for `placed-blocks.remember-minutes` (30). Creative breaks and breaks another plugin cancelled are ignored too.

### Levels and when alerts go out

The score never drops when a new reading is lower; it halves every `decay-half-life-minutes` (15), so a player who stops fades back to normal.

| Level | Score from |
|---|---|
| Warning | 16 |
| Suspicious | 26 |
| Very suspicious | 51 |
| Critical | 76 |

An alert goes out when a player climbs to a higher level than the last one announced, and again every `alerts.interval-seconds` (120) while they stay at suspicious or above. A warning is never repeated on the timer. Staff leaving staff mode have their alerts turned off.

### Change the watched ores

Each ore in `ores` has `materials` and `valuable`. By default diamond, emerald, ancient debris and gold are valuable; lapis, redstone, iron, copper and coal are tracked but not valuable. Add a block with the new ore's materials, then reload. A material the server does not know is skipped with one console warning.

### Make it stricter or looser

Each factor has `enabled` and its own thresholds and points, and `levels` holds the four score limits. `factors.min-veins` (3) is how many buried veins the ratio factors need before saying anything, so one lucky diamond is not a pattern. Reloading re-reads ores and thresholds; the vein and placed-block memories keep their minutes until a restart.

<Details title="The nine factors">
All of them read the last `window-minutes` (10) of breaks. The points of every factor that triggers are added, and the sum is capped at 100.

| Factor | Triggers when | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 4, 8 or 13 buried veins in the window | 15, 30, 50 |
| Valuable ratio | More than 80 %, or 90 %, of broken blocks were buried valuable ore | 20, 35 |
| Straight line | The last 5 buried veins lie within 2 blocks of one line | 25 |
| Ambient light | 80 % of buried veins broken in darkness (light 0), or in dim light (up to 7) | 25, 12 |
| Deep mining | Below Y 16 and more than 60 % of the last 20 breaks were buried valuable ore | 15 |
| Discovery timing | 70 % of the gaps between veins were under 5 seconds | 25, 10 for moderate |
| Ore exposure | 70 % of fresh veins fully hidden, or partially | 25, 12 |
| Approach cost | 70 % of the gaps between veins were 8 blocks or fewer | 22, 10 for moderate |
| Beeline | 60 % of veins were walked at directly over the last 20 seconds, from 8 blocks or more | 25 |

Default `modules/mining/config.yml` keys, abridged:

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/mining/config.yml"
window-minutes: 10
decay-half-life-minutes: 15.0
factors:
  min-veins: 3
  frequency: { enabled: true, suspicious: 4, very-suspicious: 8, critical: 13 }
  straight-line: { enabled: true, block-count: 5, tolerance: 2.0, points: 25 }
  deep-mining: { enabled: true, y-level: 16, ratio-percent: 60, points: 15 }
levels: { warning: 16, suspicious: 26, very-suspicious: 51, critical: 76 }
alerts:
  interval-seconds: 120
  critical:
    sound: { name: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL, volume: 0.7, pitch: 0.8 }
vein: { max-size: 32, remember-minutes: 30 }
placed-blocks: { remember-minutes: 30 }
```

Until 1.8.0 the critical alert played the ender dragon growl. A critical sound still exactly as shipped (`ENTITY_ENDER_DRAGON_GROWL`, pitch `1.8`) moves to the note block bell on the first start; one you picked yourself stays.

Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/mining/messages.yml`: `alerts-on`, `alerts-off`, `alert`, `reset`, `reset-preview`, `confirm-reset`, `top-header`, `top-line`, `ore-line`, and the five level names under `levels`. Colours and placeholders in them follow [colours](/docs/exylialib/colours) and [messages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

The `staff_mining` table keeps one row per player: ore counts, the raw score with its timestamp, the level and the last alert. Dirty rows are written every thirty seconds, on quit and before the top list. The window, the trail and the last announced level live in memory only.
</Details>
