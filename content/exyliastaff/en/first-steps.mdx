At the end of this page one staff member can enter staff mode, vanish, freeze a player and open the punishment screen on them. You need two accounts to test the last part: a staff account and a normal one.

## Before you start

Install the plugin first: [Install ExyliaStaff](/docs/exyliastaff/installation). The commands below use LuckPerms; any permission plugin works.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a group staff">
    Give your helper group these permissions. `exyliastaff.staff` is what makes a player staff for every module, and staff mode needs it together with `exyliastaff.staffmode`.
    ```text
    exyliastaff.staff
    exyliastaff.staffmode
    exyliastaff.vanish
    exyliastaff.vanish.see
    exyliastaff.freeze
    exyliastaff.inspect
    exyliastaff.teleport
    exyliastaff.punish
    exyliastaff.reports.manage
    exyliastaff.helpop.manage
    ```
    With LuckPerms: `/lp group helper permission set exyliastaff.staff true`, once per node. Operators already hold all of them. Every node is listed on [Permissions](/docs/exyliastaff/permissions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter staff mode">
    Run `/staff`. Your inventory is saved, the hotbar of tools appears, and you vanish. The staff sidebar shows your session time and the open reports and help requests. See [Staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try the hotbar">
    The clock teleports you to the block you look at. The dye toggles vanish, the music disc teleports you to a random player, the book inspects the player you look at, the netherite axe opens the punishment screen on them and the packed ice freezes them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Freeze a test player">
    With the second account online, look at it and click the packed ice, or run `/freeze <player> test`. The player cannot move and sees the reason. Release them with `/unfreeze <player>`. See [Freeze](/docs/exyliastaff/freeze).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the punishment screen">
    Run `/punish <player>`. Pick a category, then a reason. If the server asks for it, a confirm screen shows **Confirm** and **Cancel**: click **Cancel** for now. See [Punishments](/docs/exyliastaff/punishments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave staff mode">
    Run `/staff` again, or open your inventory and click the barrier. Your inventory, game mode, health and position come back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

In staff mode the second account does not see you, and you see it. After leaving, your own inventory is exactly as it was. If `/staff` says you have no permission, you are missing `exyliastaff.staff` or `exyliastaff.staffmode`: see [Common problems](/docs/exyliastaff/troubleshooting).

<Callout type="note" title="Why /tp says Enable staff mode">
By default `config.yml` only lets staff run `tp`, `gamemode`, `fly`, `give`, `vanish`, `freeze`, `xray` and a few more commands while in staff mode, so every use lands in a logged session. Players with `exyliastaff.gate.bypass` are never gated. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliastaff/configuration#command-gate).
</Callout>
