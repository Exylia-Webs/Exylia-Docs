ExyliaStaff keeps copies of what players carry, so a staff member reading a report three days later can look at what the player really had and give it back, whole or in parts. Copies are database rows, not files, so every server of the network reads the same history.

## Before you start

- Staff need `exyliastaff.restore`. Two buttons need more: `exyliastaff.restore.log` (restore history) and `exyliastaff.restore.purge` (delete every backup of a player).
- The module switch is `modules.inventory-restore` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`, on by default. Settings are in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/restore/config.yml`.
- Backups are taken automatically and stored in the plugin's [database](/docs/exylialib/database); there is nothing to set up.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the player's inventories">
    Run `/invrestore <player>` (aliases `/inventoryrestore` and `/restoreinv`). Any name connected to the proxy is suggested, online or not. If the player's backups come from more than one server, the **SERVERS** screen opens first, current server first; pick one, or **Servers ▸ Every server**. With a single server it goes straight to **INVENTORIES**. Console cannot use the command.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the backup">
    **INVENTORIES** lists the backups newest first, each with the icon of its cause and a line saying what the event was. The tabs filter them: **Everything**, **Deaths**, **Joins and quits**, **World changes** and **Staff saves**. Click a backup to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what they had">
    **BACKUP** shows the inventory with its armour and off-hand zones. Empty slots are shown as air, so what you see is what they had. A death backup also shows the cause and **Killed by**. To give back a single item, click it in the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to hand back">
    Click **Backup ▸ Restore**. On **RESTORE**, click each part to tick it. The **Items** button switches between replacing what they carry and adding to it. Click **Restore ▸ Confirm** to hand it back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/invrestore <a player online>` and click **Backups ▸ Save now**. A backup with the **Staff saves** cause appears in **INVENTORIES** right away. Open it, tick **Inventory** and confirm: the player is told that you restored it (`notify-target`).

## Going further

### What you can hand back

Eleven parts, ticked one by one: Inventory, Armor, Off hand, Ender chest, Experience, Health, Hunger, Potion effects, Game mode, Flight and Location.

`default-parts` is what is already ticked when the screen opens: inventory, armour, off hand, experience, health and hunger. A list that leaves nothing valid falls back to those six.

The four parts made of items (inventory, armour, off hand, ender chest) can be written over what the player carries or added to it. `default-replace` (`true`) decides which one the screen opens on. Either way their current items are saved first as a **Before a restore** backup, so a wrong choice is one restore away from being undone. Location is never applied across servers.

### Save a copy now

In **INVENTORIES**, **Backups ▸ Save now** copies what the player carries at this moment. The player must be on this server; otherwise you are told there is nothing to copy.

### Restore a player who is offline

The hand-back is queued with its own copy of the items and the parts you ticked, and lands the moment they join (their arrival backup is written first). If they are online on another server, that server is notified so the delivery happens at once. **Backups ▸ Waiting [n]** drops what is still owed.

### Delete backups and read the history

**Backup ▸ Delete** on a backup removes it, after a confirm screen. **Backups ▸ Delete everything** (shift left click, needs `exyliastaff.restore.purge`) removes every backup of that player. **Backups ▸ Restore history** (needs `exyliastaff.restore.log`) shows who handed what to whom, readable from either end: what a staff member gave out and what a player received.

### Watch what happened

A death backup has **Backup ▸ Watch what happened** when the [replays](/docs/exyliastaff/replays) module is on: the minute before that death, to decide whether it was fair. If [item revocation](/docs/exyliastaff/item-revocation) is on, the restore screen of a death that dropped marked items also has the **Revoke** switch.

### How long backups are kept

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `keep-per-player` | `20` | Backups kept per player (never more than 100). The oldest go as new ones arrive. |
| `keep-days` | `14` | Days a backup is kept. `0` keeps them until the count drops them. |
| `history-keep-days` | `90` | Days the restore history is kept. `0` keeps it forever. |

Deaths and manual saves are not pushed out by the count; they stay until `keep-days` takes them. The cleanup runs at start and every five minutes, from one server of the network at a time.

<Details title="When a copy is taken, and the config keys">
| Cause | Icon | Switch |
|---|---|---|
| Death | `SKELETON_SKULL` | `causes.death` |
| Quit | `IRON_DOOR` | `causes.quit` |
| Join | `OAK_DOOR` | `causes.join` |
| World change | `ENDER_PEARL` | `causes.world-change` |
| Manual save | `WRITABLE_BOOK` | always on |
| Before a restore | `CLOCK` | always on |

All four switches are `true` by default. The last two have no switch on purpose: a manual save is a staff member asking, and the copy before a restore is what makes a restore undoable.

A copy is written only when there is something to copy, ender chest included. `min-seconds-between` (60) is the gap between two ordinary backups of the same player; deaths, quits and manual saves ignore it. The quit and join copies are skipped while the server is stopping, because Paper disconnects every player before it disables plugins.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/restore/config.yml"
keep-per-player: 20
keep-days: 14
history-keep-days: 90
min-seconds-between: 60
default-parts: [INVENTORY, ARMOR, OFF_HAND, EXPERIENCE, HEALTH, HUNGER]
default-replace: true
notify-target: true
causes: { death: true, quit: true, join: true, world-change: true }
```

The argument of `/invrestore` is a network name. A name this server has never seen is asked of the proxy through the staff member who typed it, which is why console cannot use it.
</Details>
