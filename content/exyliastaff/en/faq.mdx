Each answer is short and links to the guide that has the whole story. For a problem that needs steps, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliastaff/troubleshooting).

## Does ExyliaStaff need PacketEvents?

No, it is optional. Without it the plugin loads, but [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode), [x-ray vision](/docs/exyliastaff/xray-vision) and [reveal](/docs/exyliastaff/reveal) stay off. Vanish, freeze and inspect fall back to plain Bukkit paths, and [replays](/docs/exyliastaff/replays) are saved but cannot be watched. See [Installation](/docs/exyliastaff/installation).

## The plugin does not start

ExyliaLib is listed as soft only so the loader can install it, but the plugin needs it to run. Check it is in `plugins/`, then read the console. See [Installation](/docs/exyliastaff/installation).

## A module shows as failed

A module throws on enable when, for example, its table cannot be created. The console has the reason, the module keeps its commands off, and the next start retries. `/staffadmin modules` shows the state of each. A module that only needs PacketEvents stays off instead of failing: see the first question.

## Staff cannot see each other while vanished

A viewer needs `exyliastaff.vanish.see` and a level at least the vanished player's. With no `exyliastaff.vanish.level.<n>` node everybody is level 0 and sees everybody. Give an admin `exyliastaff.vanish.level.3` and only staff at level 3 or above see them. See [Vanish](/docs/exyliastaff/vanish).

## A player was frozen and then logged out

With `persist-on-quit` on, the default, they come back frozen on whichever server they join, and staff are told they are back. With it off, they rejoin free. Staff on every server are told they logged out, and `quit-commands` in `modules/freeze/config.yml` may already have punished them. See [Freeze](/docs/exyliastaff/freeze).

## The ladder skipped a step, or did not escalate

The ladder counts punishments this plugin recorded for the same reason id within `ladder-reset-days` (30 by default). A ban typed straight into the ban plugin, or a different reason, does not count. See [Punishments](/docs/exyliastaff/punishments).

## The punishment command did nothing

With `engine: commands` the template of the step's type runs as the console with `%player%`, `%duration%`, `%reason%` and `%staff%`, and the duration is passed as written in the ladder. Check that the command exists for your ban plugin and reads `7d` the way you wrote it. "No command for TEMPBAN" means the type has no template in `commands`. See [Punishments](/docs/exyliastaff/punishments#engines).

## Alerts do not reach staff on another server

Cross-server delivery needs Redis in ExyliaLib's `database.yml`, with a distinct `server-id` per server. `/staffadmin redis` says whether this server is connected. Without Redis every channel still delivers locally. See [Installation](/docs/exyliastaff/installation).

## Mining alerts are quiet even though a player is suspicious

A staff member receives them only with `exyliastaff.mining.alerts` and alerts on, which leaving staff mode turns off. `/mining on` turns them back on. A warning is announced once and never repeated on the timer. See [Mining](/docs/exyliastaff/mining).

## A staff member lost staff mode with "You lost permission"

`exyliastaff.staff` or `exyliastaff.staffmode` was removed while they were online. The plugin notices within five seconds. See [Staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode).

## Can staff approve their login from Discord?

Yes, with ExyliaLink and the staff member's Minecraft and Discord accounts linked. See [Two-factor](/docs/exyliastaff/two-factor#discord-approval).

## How do I get alerts for alternate accounts of banned players?

Link the network to [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) and keep the `alts` module on. Staff with `exyliastaff.alts` are told when someone sharing an address with a banned account joins.

## My edit to a menu vanished

Since 1.4.0 it does not: menus and hotbars are yours, and a release only adds what they are missing. Check that you edited the folder of the language in use: `language` in `config.yml` picks `lang/<language>/`, and `default` follows ExyliaLib's language. See [Languages](/docs/exyliastaff/languages).

## Where did /exyliastaff and the admin panel go

The in-game panel moved to [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) in 1.4.0. The audit is still recorded in game, and `/notes` reads and writes a staff member's file. Since 1.5.0 the technical command is `/staffadmin`, which replaces `/exyliastaff` and `/es`. Since 1.8.0 a player typing `/staffadmin` gets a small hub for modules, settings and reload. See [Commands](/docs/exyliastaff/commands#admin-hub).

## Where did /staffadmin wipe go

It was removed in 1.8.0. ExyliaLib's `/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaStaff @account @history` removes what it removed, and more, with a dump first. See [Commands](/docs/exyliastaff/commands#wiping-a-players-data).

## Another plugin needs /tp or /top

List the names in `disabled-commands` in `config.yml` and restart. The rest of the module keeps working, and `/stafftp` and `/stafftop` stay. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliastaff/configuration#disabled-commands) and [Teleport](/docs/exyliastaff/teleport).

## Is there a staff chat prefix character, or a claim button for reports?

Neither exists. Staff chat is a toggle or `/sc <message>`; reports are resolved or dismissed, and several staff may look at the same one. See [Staff chat](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-chat) and [Reports](/docs/exyliastaff/reports).
