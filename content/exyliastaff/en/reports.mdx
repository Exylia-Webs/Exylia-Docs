title: "Reports"
description: "How a player reports, what stops a report, how priority is computed, and the screens staff review them in."
---

A player reports another with `/report`, picking a reason from a menu or writing one. Staff on every
server get a clickable alert, and `/reports` lists what is open by priority, highest first.

Module id `reports`, switch `modules.reports`, files under `modules/reports/`.

## Reporting

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/report <player>` | `exyliastaff.report` | Opens the reasons menu. |
| `/report <player> <reason>` | `exyliastaff.report` | Sends it. A reason id such as `cheating` becomes that reason; any other text is kept as written, without colours. |

Only online players can be reported. A report is refused, in this order, when the reporter names
themself, the target holds `exyliastaff.staff` and `allow-report-staff` is off, the reporter is still
in the `cooldown-seconds` from their last report, or they already have `max-open-per-reporter` open
reports. Nothing stops the same player from reporting the same target twice.

## Priority

Every reason has a `weight`. A report's score is that weight, plus one per other open report on the
same target, up to three. Free text weighs 1.

| Score | Level |
|---|---|
| 7 or more | `CRITICAL` |
| 5 or 6 | `HIGH` |
| 3 or 4 | `NORMAL` |
| below 3 | `LOW` |

The score is computed when it is read, never stored, so changing a weight reorders the open reports.
`HIGH` and `CRITICAL` alerts play `alert-urgent` instead of `alert`.

## Reviewing

`/reports`, with `exyliastaff.reports.manage`, opens the list; `/reports <player>` opens that player's
open reports, whether they are online, on another server or gone. The blaze powder in the hotbar
opens the list. Alerts reach every online staff member with the same permission on every server, and
end with three buttons: **Open** their reports, **Teleport** on duty with `/tpstaff`, and **▶ Replay**,
which runs `/reports replay <id>`.

| Screen | Rows | Clicks |
|---|---|---|
| List | One head per open report, in the chosen order | Left: the player's reports. Shift left: watch the replay. Right: resolve. Shift right: dismiss. |
| Player | Every open report against them | Left: teleport, following them to their server. Shift left: teleport on duty. Right: resolve. Shift right: dismiss. Middle: everything ever filed against them. Q: watch the replay. |

The list also has **Resolve all** and **Dismiss all**, a spyglass that asks for a name, and an
**Order** button: left click for the next order, right click for the previous one. The orders are
priority, newest, oldest and most reported, which groups the reports on one player together; each
staff member keeps their own, priority until they change it. The player screen has a **Replays**
button for that player's whole replay list.

## Replays

With the [replay](/docs/exyliastaff/replays) module on, the minute before a report is saved the moment
it is filed, when the reported player is on the reporter's server. Every replay button opens that one;
when it is missing, because they were not recorded or it is still being written, the button opens the
player's replay list instead. Watching needs `exyliastaff.replay` as well. Resolving
and dismissing both close the report, recording who did it; they differ only in the status kept for
history. There is no claim step, and the reporter is not told when their report is closed. Closed rows
stay in `staff_reports` forever.

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/reports/config.yml"
reasons:
  cheating:
    name: Cheating
    description: Hacks, macros or unfair clients.
    icon: DIAMOND_SWORD
    weight: 5
  exploiting:
    name: Exploiting
    description: Abusing bugs or glitches.
    icon: COMMAND_BLOCK
    weight: 4
  griefing:
    name: Griefing
    description: Destroying or stealing from others.
    icon: TNT
    weight: 3
  toxicity:
    name: Toxicity
    description: Insults, harassment or spam.
    icon: WRITABLE_BOOK
    weight: 2
  other:
    name: Other
    description: Something else worth a look.
    icon: PAPER
    weight: 1
cooldown-seconds: 60
allow-report-staff: false
max-open-per-reporter: 3
alert:
  sound:
    name: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING
    pitch: 1.4
alert-urgent:
  sound:
    name: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL
    pitch: 0.7
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `reasons.<id>.name` | | Shown in the menu and stored on the report. Plain text. |
| `reasons.<id>.weight` | | The base score. |
| `cooldown-seconds` | `60` | Between two reports by the same player. Under five minutes it is not kept across a restart. |
| `allow-report-staff` | `false` | Let players report somebody with `exyliastaff.staff`. |
| `max-open-per-reporter` | `3` | Open reports one player may have. `0` for no limit. |
| `alert`, `alert-urgent` | | Library effects for staff, on every server. |

## Messages

`lang/<language>/modules/reports/messages.yml`: `submitted`, `self`, `staff-protected`, `cooldown` with `%time%`,
`too-many-open` with `%count%`; `alert-header`, `alert-line` and `alert-actions` with `%id%`,
`%reporter%`, `%target%`, `%reason%`, `%server%`, `%priority%` and `%priority_score%`, the last one
a clickable row you may empty; `resolved` and `dismissed` with `%id%`; `closed-all`; `not-found`;
`target-offline`, `teleported`, `following`; `filter-prompt`, `history-prompt`; `no-reports`,
`no-history`; `no-permission-for-player`; `no-handler`, who closed a report when nobody did;
`no-replay`, sent when no replay can be opened; and `sorts`, the names of the four orders, `priority`,
`newest`, `oldest` and `most-reported`.

Other plugins can file reports and refuse them before they are stored; see the
[API](/docs/exyliastaff/api#reports).

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%staff_reports_open%` | Open reports on the network. |
