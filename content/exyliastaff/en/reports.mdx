A player runs `/report <player>` and picks a reason from a screen. Staff on every server get a clickable alert, and `/reports` lists what is open, highest priority first, so the worst case is never at the bottom.

## Before you start

- Players need `exyliastaff.report`. Staff who review need `exyliastaff.reports.manage`.
- The module switch is `modules.reports` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`.
- Optional: the [replay](/docs/exyliastaff/replays) module attaches the last minute of the reported player to each report.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="A player reports">
    The player runs `/report <player>`. A menu titled "Report" followed by the player's name opens with one card per reason (Cheating, Exploiting, Griefing, Toxicity, Other by default), each with an icon and a line of description. Clicking a card files the report. Players on clients with screen menus get the same reasons as a screen instead of a chest menu. To skip the menu, write the reason: `/report <player> cheating`. Any other text is kept as written, without colours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff are alerted">
    Every online staff member with `exyliastaff.reports.manage`, on any server, gets a message with three buttons: **[ ✦ Open ]** (that player's reports), **[ ➥ Teleport ]** (on duty, like `/tpstaff`) and **[ ▶ Replay ]**. Urgent reports play a different sound.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the queue">
    Run `/reports`, or click the blaze powder **REPORTS** (slot 12) in the [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) hotbar. `/reports <player>` opens that player's open reports, online or not.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close each report">
    Right click a report to **resolve** it, shift right click to **dismiss** it. Both remove it from the queue and record who did it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

As a player run `/report <someone online>` and pick **Toxicity**. As staff you should see the alert, and `/reports` should list one head named after the reported player with the reason, priority and age on it.

## Going further

### Who can be reported, and how often

Only online players can be reported. A report is refused, in this order, when the reporter names themself, the target holds `exyliastaff.staff` and `allow-report-staff` is off, the reporter is still inside `cooldown-seconds` from their last report, or they already have `max-open-per-reporter` open reports. Nothing stops two players reporting the same target. A rank gets its own wait with `exyliastaff.report.cooldown.<seconds>`; the lowest one held wins.

### Add or change a reason

Reasons are `reasons.<id>` in `modules/reports/config.yml`: a `name`, a `description`, an `icon` (a material) and a `weight` from 1 to 5. Add a block, reload, and it appears as a card in the report screen. The `punish-reasons` list names [punishment](/docs/exyliastaff/punishments) reason ids that [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) suggests when a report with this reason is acted on.

### How priority is worked out

A report's score is its reason's `weight` plus one per other open report on the same target, up to three extra. Free text weighs 1. The score is read when shown, never stored, so changing a weight reorders the queue at once.

| Score | Level |
|---|---|
| 7 or more | `CRITICAL` |
| 5 or 6 | `HIGH` |
| 3 or 4 | `NORMAL` |
| below 3 | `LOW` |

`HIGH` and `CRITICAL` reports play `alert-urgent` instead of `alert`.

### Clicks in the menus

| Screen | Clicks |
|---|---|
| Report list (**REPORTS**) | Left: that player's reports. Shift left: watch the replay. Right: resolve. Shift right: dismiss. |
| Player reports | Left: teleport, following them to their server. Shift left: teleport on duty. Right: resolve. Shift right: dismiss. Middle: everything ever filed against them. Drop (Q): watch the replay. |

The list also has **Filter** (type a name), **History** (type a name), **Resolve all**, **Dismiss all** and **Order**: left click for the next order, right click for the previous one. Orders are priority, newest, oldest and most reported (which groups reports on one player). Each staff member keeps their own, priority until they change it. The player screen has a **Replays** button for that player's whole replay list.

### Replays

With the [replay](/docs/exyliastaff/replays) module on, the minute before a report is saved when it is filed, if the reported player is on the reporter's server. Every replay button opens that one; if there is none (not recorded, or still being written) it opens the player's replay list instead. Watching needs `exyliastaff.replay` too.

### What happens to old reports

There is no claim step, and the reporter is not told when their report is closed. A report nobody handled closes itself after `expire-days` (default 3): it leaves the queue but stays in the player's history, so a repeat offender still shows it. `0` keeps reports open forever. Closed rows stay in the database.

<Details title="Configuration and messages">

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/reports/config.yml"
reasons:
  cheating:
    name: Cheating
    description: Hacks, macros or unfair clients.
    icon: DIAMOND_SWORD
    weight: 5
    punish-reasons:
      - hacks
      - xray
  exploiting:
    name: Exploiting
    description: Abusing bugs or glitches.
    icon: COMMAND_BLOCK
    weight: 4
    punish-reasons:
      - hacks
  griefing:
    name: Griefing
    description: Destroying or stealing from others.
    icon: TNT
    weight: 3
    punish-reasons:
      - grief
  toxicity:
    name: Toxicity
    description: Insults, harassment or spam.
    icon: WRITABLE_BOOK
    weight: 2
    punish-reasons:
      - toxicity
      - spam
      - advertising
  other:
    name: Other
    description: Something else worth a look.
    icon: PAPER
    weight: 1
    punish-reasons: []
cooldown-seconds: 60
allow-report-staff: false
max-open-per-reporter: 3
expire-days: 3
alert:
  sound:
    name: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING
    pitch: 1.4
alert-urgent:
  sound:
    name: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL
    pitch: 0.7
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `reasons.<id>.name` | | Shown in the menu and stored on the report. Plain text. |
| `reasons.<id>.weight` | | The base score, 1 to 5. |
| `reasons.<id>.punish-reasons` | | Punishment reason ids suggested on the web, best match first. Empty suggests none. |
| `cooldown-seconds` | `60` | Wait between two reports by one player. Under five minutes it is not kept across a restart. |
| `allow-report-staff` | `false` | Let players report somebody with `exyliastaff.staff`. |
| `max-open-per-reporter` | `3` | Open reports one player may have. `0` for no limit. |
| `expire-days` | `3` | Days before an unattended report closes itself. `0` never. |
| `alert`, `alert-urgent` | | Effects played for staff on every server. |

The report screen for clients with screen menus is `screens/report_reasons.html` in the plugin folder; its words are in `lang/<language>/screens/report_reasons.yml`, its look in `theme.css`, and your own rules go in `custom.css` (see [ExyliaLib screens](/docs/exylialib/screens)). It reads the reasons from this config, so it needs no edits of its own.

Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/reports/messages.yml`: `submitted`, `self`, `staff-protected`, `cooldown` (`%time%`), `too-many-open` (`%count%`); `alert-header`, `alert-line` and `alert-actions` (`%id%`, `%reporter%`, `%target%`, `%reason%`, `%server%`, `%priority%`, `%priority_score%`; the last is a clickable row you may empty); `resolved` and `dismissed` (`%id%`); `closed-all`; `not-found`; `target-offline`, `teleported`, `following`; `filter-prompt`, `history-prompt`; `no-reports`, `no-history`; `no-permission-for-player`; `no-handler`; `no-replay`; and `sorts`, the names of the four orders. See [ExyliaLib messages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages), [menus](/docs/exylialib/menus) and [colours](/docs/exylialib/colours). Other plugins can file or refuse reports; see the [API](/docs/exyliastaff/api#reports).
