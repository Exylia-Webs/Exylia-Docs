ExyliaStaff gives your moderators one set of tools on Paper and Folia servers and networks: a staff mode with a hotbar, vanish, freeze, silent inventory checks, reports, help requests, punishments with a ladder, replays, a staff log and two-factor codes. Everything a staff member does leaves a trail, and every tool is a module you can switch off.

## What it does

- **On duty:** [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) with a hotbar of tools, [vanish](/docs/exyliastaff/vanish) with rank levels, [teleports](/docs/exyliastaff/teleport), [x-ray vision](/docs/exyliastaff/xray-vision) and a [staff chat](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-chat) that reaches every server.
- **Catching cheaters:** [freeze](/docs/exyliastaff/freeze) for screenshares, [inspect](/docs/exyliastaff/inspect) for silent inventory mirrors, [mining](/docs/exyliastaff/mining) suspicion alerts and [replays](/docs/exyliastaff/replays) of the last minute around a player.
- **Handling players:** [reports](/docs/exyliastaff/reports), [help requests](/docs/exyliastaff/helpop), [punishments](/docs/exyliastaff/punishments) with a ladder that escalates by itself, and [inventory restore](/docs/exyliastaff/inventory-restore) with [item revocation](/docs/exyliastaff/item-revocation).
- **Running the team:** the [staff list](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-list), the [staff log](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-log) that counts time and actions per session, the [audit and notes](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-admin) and [two-factor](/docs/exyliastaff/two-factor) codes for staff accounts.
- **Outside the game:** [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) puts the whole network's punishments, reports and logs in one panel.

The source registers 27 modules, each with its own switch in `config.yml`. A module that is off registers no commands, no listeners and no tables. Menus and messages ship in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Russian and Turkish.

## What you need

| Need | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.1 or newer, or Folia. The plugin is built against Paper 1.21.4 and declares `folia-supported`. |
| Java | 21. |
| [ExyliaLib](/docs/exylialib/introduction) | Installed by the loader. ExyliaStaff always builds against the newest ExyliaLib release, so keep the library up to date. |
| A permission plugin | Any one, such as LuckPerms, to give `exyliastaff.staff` to your team. |

PacketEvents, Redis, a ban plugin and the rest are optional: see [Installation](/docs/exyliastaff/installation).

## What you'll have in ten minutes

You install the plugin, give one helper the staff permission, and that helper enters staff mode, vanishes, freezes a test player and opens the punishment screen. [Installation](/docs/exyliastaff/installation) gets the plugin running, and [Your first shift in staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/first-steps) walks the rest.

## How it is organised

**Getting started** installs the plugin and walks a first shift. **Guides** has one page per tool: what it does, how to set it up, its files and its rules. **Reference** lists every command, permission and placeholder, the files of the core and the API. **Help** has common problems and this FAQ.

Every default, message and rule in these pages was read from the plugin's source at version 1.8.0. When a page says a value cannot be configured, no key exists for it.
