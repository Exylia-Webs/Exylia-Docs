ExyliaStaff is a moderation toolkit for a server or a network. It is built as modules on ExyliaLib,
each one switchable from `config.yml`, each one with its own files under `modules/<id>/`: a staff mode
with a hotbar of tools, a packet-level vanish with rank levels, a freeze for screenshares, silent
inventory mirrors for online and offline players, staff teleports, x-ray vision, a staff chat across
servers, a global-chat bypass, reports, help requests, a punishment ladder, an x-ray suspicion
detector, inventory restore with item revocation, replays of the last minute around any player, a
staff list, a staff log and an audit of the team. Menus and messages ship in English, Spanish,
Portuguese and French.

Everything a staff member does on duty leaves a trail: the log counts time and actions per session,
and the audit records every command. Linked to [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web), the
whole network's punishments, reports and logs reach one panel outside the game. A module that is off
registers nothing: no commands, no listeners, no tables.

## How it is organised

**Getting started** installs the plugin and walks through a first shift. **Modules** has one page per
feature: what it does, its file, its commands and its rules. **Reference** collects every command,
permission and placeholder, the core files, the API and the questions that come up most.

<Callout type="note" title="Where the numbers come from">
Every default, message and rule on these pages was read from the plugin's source at version 1.4.0.
Where a page says a value is not configurable, that is because no key exists for it.
</Callout>
