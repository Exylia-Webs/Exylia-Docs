title: "Punishments"
description: "Categories, reasons and an escalation ladder, enforced by the plugin itself or through your ban plugin's commands, with approvals, evidence, appeals and a history per player."
---

The punishments module holds the reasons and, for each one, a ladder: first offence, second offence,
and so on. When a staff member picks a reason, the module counts how many times that player was
punished for it before and takes the matching step. Who enforces that step is up to you: the plugin
itself, with its own login screens, mutes and warnings (`engine: exylia`), or the command you
configured for the step's type, answered by LiteBans, AdvancedBan or anything else with a `/ban`
(`engine: commands`, the default).

Module id `punishments`, switch `modules.punishments`, files under `modules/punishments/`.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/punish <player>` | `exyliastaff.punish` | Opens the categories menu. The player may be offline if the server has seen them. |
| `/punish <player> <reason-id>` | `exyliastaff.punish` | Executes at once, with no confirm screen. |
| `/history <player>` | `exyliastaff.punish.history` | Their punishments, newest first. `/phistory` is an alias. |
| `/punish requests` | `exyliastaff.punish` | The ten newest pending [requests](#approvals), each with approve and deny buttons. |
| `/punish approve <id>` | `exyliastaff.punish` | Applies a request, with you as the staff member. |
| `/punish deny <id>` | `exyliastaff.punish` | Turns a request down. |
| `/staffadmin import <plugin>` | `exyliastaff.admin` | Imports a ban plugin's history once. See [Importing a ban plugin](#importing-a-ban-plugin). |
| `/staffadmin history clear <player> [reason-id] [confirm]` | `exyliastaff.admin.history.clear` | Deletes a player's history, or one reason's. An active ban is not lifted. |

The netherite axe in the hotbar opens the menu on the player you look at. A player cannot punish
themself.

## The menu

Categories, then reasons. Each reason shows its ladder with the steps already used, the current one
marked, and the step that will be applied now. Its last line says whether a click punishes or sends
the step for [approval](#approvals). Clicking it opens a confirm screen when `require-confirmation`
is on, otherwise executes. Cancel goes back to the reasons.

## The ladder

A ladder line is `TYPE duration`, or just `TYPE`, with the types `BAN`, `TEMPBAN`, `MUTE`, `TEMPMUTE`,
`KICK`, `WARN` and `IPBAN`. The step is the number of earlier punishments of that player with the
**same reason id** within `ladder-reset-days`; when the ladder runs out, the last step repeats.
Different reasons do not advance each other, and `ladder-reset-days: 0` counts everything ever.

<Callout type="warning" title="Only what this plugin issued counts">
The history is `staff_punishments`. With `observe` on, a ban typed straight into LiteBans is recorded
there too, but under the ban plugin's name as its reason (`litebans`), so it is not a step on any
ladder. Punish through the menu and the ladder is always right.
</Callout>

## Engines

`engine` decides who enforces a step.

| Engine | Who enforces | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| `commands` | Your ban plugin, through the template of the step's type in `commands`. | A ban plugin, or vanilla. |
| `exylia` | This plugin: the row in `staff_punishments` is the punishment. | Nothing else. Redis and [ExyliaProxyUtils](/docs/exyliaproxyutils/login-bans) refuse bans at the proxy. |

### The native engine

With `engine: exylia`, `commands` and `revoke-commands` are not used.

- **Bans**, `BAN`, `TEMPBAN` and `IPBAN`, are refused at login on every backend, from the database,
  with the `screen-ban` screen. Each active ban is also mirrored to Redis, where ExyliaProxyUtils
  refuses it at the proxy before a backend is reached; the mirror is written again on every start and
  reload, so a flushed Redis heals itself. When the database cannot be read at login, the player is let
  in rather than locking everybody out.
- **IP bans** hold the address the player was on when issued. One issued while they were offline
  learns the address at their next login attempt.
- **Kicks** show `screen-kick`. A player on another server is kicked through the proxy.
- **Mutes**, `MUTE` and `TEMPMUTE`, cancel every chat line and every command in
  `mute-blocked-commands`, answered with the `muted` lines.
- **Warnings** show the `warned` lines at once, or at the player's next join on any server, and only
  once.

A ban or mute lifted or edited from [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) reaches the
player's server and the mirror straight away.

### Execution through commands

With `engine: commands`, the template for the step's type in `commands` runs with `%player%`, `%duration%`, `%reason%` and
`%staff%`. The duration is passed **as written** in the ladder, `7d` or `1h30m`, so write it the way
your ban plugin reads it; a bare type passes `perm`. `console:` runs here, `console-proxy:` on the
proxy through the library's bridge. A step type with no template is refused with "no command for
TEMPBAN" and nothing is recorded.

Either way, the row is recorded, the issuer told, and the punishment published on the `punishments`
channel: every server announces it to staff with `exyliastaff.punish`, or to everyone when
`broadcast.to-staff-only` is off.

## Evidence and appeals

Every punishment keeps the chat that led to it: the window the web sets, or, when there was none, the
target's last `evidence-lines` lines. The screens show it as `%evidence%`, one `evidence-line` per
line, stripped of any formatting the player typed.

`appeal-url` is the appeal link on the screens, with `%id%` as the punishment's id. `auto` builds it
from the linked web (`<web>/p/<workspace>/appeal?id=%id%`) and shows nothing while the network is not
linked; empty never shows one.

## Approvals

`exyliastaff.punish` lets a staff member punish. The [punishment limit nodes](/docs/exyliastaff/permissions#punishment-limits)
narrow it per type, permanent steps, category and reason; each is allowed unless it is set to
`false`. A step one of them blocks is not refused: it becomes a request.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The request">
    The staff member is told the step needs a superior's approval and gets its number. Every online
    staff member with `exyliastaff.punish`, on every server, for whom the same step is not blocked,
    gets `request-alert` with clickable approve and deny buttons. The web is told too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval">
    `/punish approve <id>`, or the button. The step is worked out again at that moment, so a ladder
    that moved meanwhile is respected, and applied with the approver as the staff member and
    `Requested by <name>` as its note. Nobody may resolve their own request, and an approver the step
    is blocked for is refused with the nodes that block it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The answer">
    The requester is told who approved or denied it, on whichever server they are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The console is never blocked. A punishment from the web is checked against the staff member's
LuckPerms nodes.

## Observing other ban plugins

With `observe` on, punishments made through LiteBans, LibertyBans, AdvancedBan or BanManager are
recorded in the history, even a plain `/ban` that never touched this plugin, together with their
lifts. They are read on any backend where the ban plugin is installed and, with ExyliaProxyUtils, on
the proxy. On a network every entry goes through one Redis stream that each server reads in the same
group, so it is recorded once. With `engine: commands`, the ban plugin's own copy of a punishment this
plugin just issued is linked to it rather than recorded twice. IP punishments that name no player are
skipped.

## Importing a ban plugin

`/staffadmin import <litebans|libertybans|advancedban|banmanager>` reads that plugin's whole history
once: through its API when it is installed on this server, otherwise through the proxy's
`punish-import` (see [ExyliaProxyUtils](/docs/exyliaproxyutils/punishments)). Rows arrive the same
way as observed ones, and reach the web as they are read. It works with `observe` off. From LiteBans it reads
bans, mutes, warnings and kicks. "is installed neither here nor on the proxy" means neither could run it.

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/punishments/config.yml"
categories:
  cheating:
    name: Cheating
    icon: DIAMOND_SWORD
    reasons:
      hacks:
        name: Hacked client
        description:
          - Killaura, fly, speed or any unfair modification.
        icon: DIAMOND_SWORD
        ladder:
          - TEMPBAN 7d
          - BAN
      xray:
        name: X-Ray
        description:
          - Ore tracing or resource pack x-ray.
        icon: DIAMOND_ORE
        ladder:
          - TEMPBAN 3d
          - TEMPBAN 14d
          - BAN
  chat:
    name: Chat
    icon: WRITABLE_BOOK
    reasons:
      spam:
        name: Spam
        description:
          - Flooding or repeating messages.
        icon: PAPER
        ladder:
          - MUTE 1h
          - TEMPMUTE 1d
          - TEMPBAN 3d
      toxicity:
        name: Toxicity
        description:
          - Insults, harassment or hate speech.
        icon: TNT
        ladder:
          - TEMPMUTE 6h
          - TEMPMUTE 3d
          - TEMPBAN 7d
          - BAN
      advertising:
        name: Advertising
        description:
          - Promoting other servers or links.
        icon: OAK_SIGN
        ladder:
          - TEMPMUTE 1d
          - BAN
  griefing:
    name: Griefing
    icon: TNT
    reasons:
      grief:
        name: Griefing
        description:
          - Destroying or stealing from other players.
        icon: TNT
        ladder:
          - WARN
          - TEMPBAN 3d
          - BAN
  other:
    name: Other
    icon: BOOK
    reasons:
      staff-disrespect:
        name: Staff disrespect
        description:
          - Ignoring or insulting staff.
        icon: BARRIER
        ladder:
          - WARN
          - KICK
          - TEMPBAN 1d
commands:
  BAN: 'console:ban %player% %reason%'
  TEMPBAN: 'console:tempban %player% %duration% %reason%'
  MUTE: 'console:mute %player% %reason%'
  TEMPMUTE: 'console:tempmute %player% %duration% %reason%'
  KICK: 'console:kick %player% %reason%'
  WARN: 'console:warn %player% %reason%'
  IPBAN: 'console:ipban %player% %reason%'
revoke-commands:
  BAN: 'console:unban %player%'
  TEMPBAN: 'console:unban %player%'
  IPBAN: 'console:unbanip %player%'
  MUTE: 'console:unmute %player%'
  TEMPMUTE: 'console:unmute %player%'
ladder-reset-days: 30
require-confirmation: true
broadcast:
  enabled: true
  to-staff-only: true
engine: commands
appeal-url: auto
mute-blocked-commands:
  - msg
  - tell
  - w
  - whisper
  - r
  - reply
  - me
  - say
  - mail
evidence-lines: 1
observe: true
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `engine` | `commands` | `commands` runs the templates; `exylia` enforces natively. |
| `appeal-url` | `auto` | The appeal link, `%id%` being the punishment. `auto` from the linked web, empty for none. |
| `mute-blocked-commands` | `msg`, `tell`, `w`, `whisper`, `r`, `reply`, `me`, `say`, `mail` | Commands a muted player may not run, without the slash, matched on the bare label (`minecraft:msg` counts as `msg`). Only with `engine: exylia`. |
| `evidence-lines` | `1` | How many of the target's last chat lines are kept when the web attached none. |
| `observe` | `true` | Record what LiteBans, LibertyBans, AdvancedBan and BanManager do. |

Reason ids must be unique across categories; the menus list categories and reasons in file order.
`%reason%` in a template is the reason's display name, not its id. `revoke-commands` is how a
punishment revoked from [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) is undone, per type, with the
same placeholders; a type with nothing to undo, such as `KICK` or `WARN`, has no entry. `alert` is a library effect played
to staff on every announcement; it is empty by default.

## Messages

`lang/<language>/modules/punishments/messages.yml`: `executed` with `%type%`, `%target%` and `%reason%`; `alert` and
`broadcast` with `%staff%`, `%target%`, `%type%`, `%duration%`, `%server%` and `%reason%`;
`never-played`, `unknown-reason`, `command-failed` with `%detail%`, `history-empty`, `self`,
`no-target`; the three ladder lines `ladder-current`, `ladder-step` and `ladder-done` with `%index%`,
`%type%` and `%duration%`; `duration-permanent`.

Approvals: `request-sent`, `request-alert`, `requests-header`, `requests-line`, `requests-empty`,
`request-approved`, `request-denied`, the requester's `request-approved-notice` and
`request-denied-notice`, `request-missing`, `request-own`, and `request-blocked` with `%nodes%`; the
last line of a reason in the menu is `reason-click` or `reason-click-approval`.

The native engine: `screen-ban` and `screen-kick`, and the `muted` and `warned` lines, all lists
taking `%reason%`, `%staff%`, `%date%`, `%expires%`, `%remaining%`, `%id%`, `%evidence%`, `%appeal%`
and `%appeal_url%`; `evidence-line` with `%message%`, `evidence-none`, `appeal-line` with
`%appeal_url%`, and `expires-never`. The import: `import-usage`, `import-started`, `import-proxy` and
`import-unsupported` with `%origin%`.
