Punishments in ExyliaStaff are a menu of reasons, each with a **ladder**: first offence, second offence, and so on. A staff member picks a reason, and the plugin counts how often that player was punished for it before and applies the matching step. You choose who enforces it: the plugin itself, or your ban plugin.

## Before you start

- Give staff `exyliastaff.punish`. Add `exyliastaff.punish.history` for `/history`. To stop a rank from using harsh steps, see [Approvals](#approvals).
- The module switch is `modules.punishments` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`.
- Decide the engine in `modules/punishments/config.yml`: `engine: commands` (the default) runs your ban plugin's commands (LiteBans, AdvancedBan, vanilla); `engine: exylia` enforces everything natively and needs no other plugin. See [Engines](#engines).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Run `/punish <player>`, or click the netherite axe **PUNISH** (it targets the player you look at) in the [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) hotbar. The player may be offline if the server has seen them. A player cannot punish themself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a category and a reason">
    Categories first (Cheating, Chat, Griefing, Other by default), then reasons. Each reason shows its ladder with the steps already used, the current one marked, and the step that will be applied now. The last line says whether a click punishes at once or sends the step for [approval](#approvals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    With `require-confirmation` on (the default) a confirm screen opens: click **Confirm**, or **Cancel** to go back to the reasons. With it off, the click executes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To skip the menu, run `/punish <player> <reason-id>` (for example `/punish Steve hacks`): it executes at once, with no confirm screen.

## Check that it works

Punish a test account with a ladder's first step (for example **Spam**, first step `MUTE 1h`). The issuer is told, staff with `exyliastaff.punish` get an announcement on every server, and `/history <player>` (alias `/phistory`) lists it, newest first.

## Going further

### The ladder

A ladder line is `TYPE duration`, or just `TYPE`. The types are `BAN`, `TEMPBAN`, `MUTE`, `TEMPMUTE`, `KICK`, `WARN` and `IPBAN`; a duration is `1h`, `7d`, `1h30m` or `perm`. The step is the number of earlier punishments of that player with the **same reason id** within `ladder-reset-days` (default 30): the first offence uses the first line, and when the ladder runs out the last step repeats. Different reasons do not advance each other, and `ladder-reset-days: 0` counts everything ever.

<Callout type="warning" title="Only what this plugin issued counts">
The history is the plugin's own. With `observe` on, a ban typed straight into LiteBans is recorded too, but under the ban plugin's name as its reason (`litebans`), so it is not a step on any ladder. Punish through the menu and the ladder is always right.
</Callout>

### Engines

`engine` decides who enforces a step.

| Engine | Who enforces | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| `commands` | Your ban plugin, through the template of the step's type in `commands`. | A ban plugin, or vanilla. |
| `exylia` | This plugin: the stored row is the punishment. | Nothing else. Redis and [ExyliaProxyUtils](/docs/exyliaproxyutils/login-bans) refuse bans at the proxy. |

**Execution through commands.** With `engine: commands`, the template for the step's type runs with `%player%`, `%duration%`, `%reason%` and `%staff%`. The duration is passed **as written** in the ladder, `7d` or `1h30m`, so write it the way your ban plugin reads it; a bare type passes `perm`. `console:` runs here, `console-proxy:` on the proxy through the library's bridge. A step type with no template is refused with "no command for TEMPBAN" and nothing is recorded.

**The native engine.** With `engine: exylia`, `commands` and `revoke-commands` are not used.

- **Bans** (`BAN`, `TEMPBAN`, `IPBAN`) are refused at login on every backend, from the database, with the `screen-ban` screen. Each active ban is also mirrored to Redis so ExyliaProxyUtils refuses it at the proxy before a backend is reached; the mirror is rewritten on every start and reload. If the database cannot be read at login, the player is let in rather than locking everybody out.
- **Kicks** show `screen-kick`. A player on another server is kicked through the proxy.
- **Mutes** (`MUTE`, `TEMPMUTE`) cancel every chat line and every command in `mute-blocked-commands`, answered with the `muted` lines.
- **Warnings** show the `warned` lines at once, or at the player's next join on any server, and only once.

A ban or mute lifted or edited from [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) reaches the player's server and the mirror straight away. Either engine records the row, tells the issuer and publishes the punishment: every server announces it to staff with `exyliastaff.punish`, or to everyone when `broadcast.to-staff-only` is off.

### IP bans

An `IPBAN` step with `engine: exylia` bans the addresses the banned player was seen on, but not blindly, because a home address gets reassigned and a mobile address is shared by strangers.

- Each address is held for `ip-ban-hold-days` (default 14) after the banned player last used it, or for as long as they had used it if that is longer. A fixed home line stays held; a dynamic address is let go after it moves on. A value of 0 behaves like 1 day.
- Wherever the banned player tries to log in from is held from then on. An IP ban issued while they were offline learns the address at their next login attempt.
- On a held address, only accounts that look like theirs are refused: accounts created since the ban, and accounts that used that address before it. Established players the ISP handed the address to are let in, and so is every account on a crowded address (mobile, CGNAT, public wifi).
- Local addresses (the proxy, a LAN, loopback) are never held. The proxy mirror carries account bans only, so IP bans are enforced by the backend at login.

### Evidence and appeals

Every punishment keeps the chat that led to it: the window the web sets or, when there was none, the target's last `evidence-lines` lines (default 1). The screens show it as `%evidence%`, with formatting the player typed stripped. `appeal-url` is the appeal link on the screens, `%id%` being the punishment: `auto` builds it from the linked web and shows nothing while the network is not linked; empty never shows one.

### Approvals

`exyliastaff.punish` lets a staff member punish. The [punishment limit nodes](/docs/exyliastaff/permissions#punishment-limits) narrow it per type, permanent steps, category and reason; each is allowed unless it is set to `false`. A step one of them blocks is not refused: it becomes a request.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The request">
    The staff member is told the step needs a superior's approval and gets its number. Every online staff member with `exyliastaff.punish`, on every server, for whom the same step is not blocked, gets `request-alert` with clickable approve and deny buttons. The web is told too. `/punish requests` lists the ten newest pending ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval">
    `/punish approve <id>`, or the button. The step is worked out again at that moment, so a ladder that moved meanwhile is respected, and applied with the approver as the staff member and `Requested by <name>` as its note. Nobody may resolve their own request, and an approver the step is blocked for is refused with the nodes that block it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The answer">
    `/punish deny <id>` turns it down. The requester is told who approved or denied it, on whichever server they are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The console is never blocked. A punishment from the web is checked against the staff member's LuckPerms nodes.

### Observing other ban plugins

With `observe` on (default), punishments made through LiteBans, LibertyBans, AdvancedBan or BanManager are recorded in the history, even a plain `/ban` that never touched this plugin, with their lifts. They are read on any backend where the ban plugin is installed and, with ExyliaProxyUtils, on the proxy. On a network every entry goes through one Redis stream read in the same group, so it is recorded once. With `engine: commands`, the ban plugin's own copy of a punishment this plugin just issued is linked to it rather than recorded twice. IP punishments that name no player are skipped.

### Importing a ban plugin

`/staffadmin import <litebans|libertybans|advancedban|banmanager>` (needs `exyliastaff.admin`) reads that plugin's whole history once: through its API when it is installed on this server, otherwise through the proxy's `punish-import` (see [ExyliaProxyUtils](/docs/exyliaproxyutils/punishments)). Rows arrive like observed ones and reach the web as they are read; it works with `observe` off. From LiteBans it reads bans, mutes, warnings and kicks. "is installed neither here nor on the proxy" means neither could run it.

To delete a player's history, or one reason's, run `/staffadmin history clear <player> [reason-id] [confirm]` (needs `exyliastaff.admin.history.clear`). An active ban is not lifted. See [Commands](/docs/exyliastaff/commands).

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/punishments/config.yml"
categories:
  cheating:
    name: Cheating
    icon: DIAMOND_SWORD
    reasons:
      hacks:
        name: Hacked client
        description:
          - Killaura, fly, speed or any unfair modification.
        icon: DIAMOND_SWORD
        ladder:
          - TEMPBAN 7d
          - BAN
      xray:
        name: X-Ray
        description:
          - Ore tracing or resource pack x-ray.
        icon: DIAMOND_ORE
        ladder:
          - TEMPBAN 3d
          - TEMPBAN 14d
          - BAN
  chat:
    name: Chat
    icon: WRITABLE_BOOK
    reasons:
      spam:
        name: Spam
        description:
          - Flooding or repeating messages.
        icon: PAPER
        ladder:
          - MUTE 1h
          - TEMPMUTE 1d
          - TEMPBAN 3d
      toxicity:
        name: Toxicity
        description:
          - Insults, harassment or hate speech.
        icon: TNT
        ladder:
          - TEMPMUTE 6h
          - TEMPMUTE 3d
          - TEMPBAN 7d
          - BAN
      advertising:
        name: Advertising
        description:
          - Promoting other servers or links.
        icon: OAK_SIGN
        ladder:
          - TEMPMUTE 1d
          - BAN
  griefing:
    name: Griefing
    icon: TNT
    reasons:
      grief:
        name: Griefing
        description:
          - Destroying or stealing from other players.
        icon: TNT
        ladder:
          - WARN
          - TEMPBAN 3d
          - BAN
  other:
    name: Other
    icon: BOOK
    reasons:
      staff-disrespect:
        name: Staff disrespect
        description:
          - Ignoring or insulting staff.
        icon: BARRIER
        ladder:
          - WARN
          - KICK
          - TEMPBAN 1d
commands:
  BAN: 'console:ban %player% %reason%'
  TEMPBAN: 'console:tempban %player% %duration% %reason%'
  MUTE: 'console:mute %player% %reason%'
  TEMPMUTE: 'console:tempmute %player% %duration% %reason%'
  KICK: 'console:kick %player% %reason%'
  WARN: 'console:warn %player% %reason%'
  IPBAN: 'console:ipban %player% %reason%'
revoke-commands:
  BAN: 'console:unban %player%'
  TEMPBAN: 'console:unban %player%'
  IPBAN: 'console:unbanip %player%'
  MUTE: 'console:unmute %player%'
  TEMPMUTE: 'console:unmute %player%'
ladder-reset-days: 30
require-confirmation: true
broadcast:
  enabled: true
  to-staff-only: true
engine: commands
appeal-url: auto
mute-blocked-commands:
  - msg
  - tell
  - w
  - whisper
  - r
  - reply
  - me
  - say
  - mail
evidence-lines: 1
observe: true
ip-ban-hold-days: 14
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `categories.<id>.reasons.<id>` | see above | A reason: `name`, `description` lines, `icon` and its `ladder`. Reason ids must be unique across categories; menus list them in file order. |
| `commands` | see above | Template per step type for `engine: commands`. `%reason%` is the reason's display name, not its id. |
| `revoke-commands` | see above | How a punishment revoked from [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) is undone, per type. `KICK` and `WARN` have none. |
| `ladder-reset-days` | `30` | Older punishments no longer advance the ladder. `0` counts everything. |
| `require-confirmation` | `true` | Show the confirm screen. |
| `broadcast.enabled`, `broadcast.to-staff-only` | `true`, `true` | Announce punishments, and to whom (`false` announces to everyone). |
| `engine` | `commands` | `commands` runs the templates; `exylia` enforces natively. |
| `appeal-url` | `auto` | The appeal link, `%id%` being the punishment. `auto` from the linked web, empty for none. |
| `mute-blocked-commands` | `msg`, `tell`, `w`, `whisper`, `r`, `reply`, `me`, `say`, `mail` | Commands a muted player may not run, without the slash, matched on the bare label (`minecraft:msg` counts as `msg`). Only with `engine: exylia`. |
| `evidence-lines` | `1` | Last chat lines kept when the web attached none. 0 to 20. |
| `observe` | `true` | Record what LiteBans, LibertyBans, AdvancedBan and BanManager do. |
| `ip-ban-hold-days` | `14` | Days an IP ban keeps holding an address after the banned player last used it. Only with `engine: exylia`. |

`alert` is a library effect played to staff on every announcement; it is empty by default.

Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/punishments/messages.yml`: `executed` (`%type%`, `%target%`, `%reason%`); `alert` and `broadcast` (`%staff%`, `%target%`, `%type%`, `%duration%`, `%server%`, `%reason%`); `never-played`, `unknown-reason`, `command-failed` (`%detail%`), `history-empty`, `self`, `no-target`; the ladder lines `ladder-current`, `ladder-step` and `ladder-done` (`%index%`, `%type%`, `%duration%`); `duration-permanent`. Approvals: `request-sent`, `request-alert`, `requests-header`, `requests-line`, `requests-empty`, `request-approved`, `request-denied`, `request-approved-notice`, `request-denied-notice`, `request-missing`, `request-own`, `request-blocked` (`%nodes%`); the last line of a reason in the menu is `reason-click` or `reason-click-approval`. Native engine: `screen-ban` and `screen-kick`, `muted` and `warned`, all taking `%reason%`, `%staff%`, `%date%`, `%expires%`, `%remaining%`, `%id%`, `%evidence%`, `%appeal%` and `%appeal_url%`; `evidence-line` (`%message%`), `evidence-none`, `appeal-line`, `expires-never`. Import: `import-usage`, `import-started`, `import-proxy`, `import-unsupported` (`%origin%`). See [ExyliaLib messages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages), [menus](/docs/exylialib/menus) and [colours](/docs/exylialib/colours).
