title: "Teleport"
description: "Teleport to a player anywhere on the network, to coordinates, a world, the surface or where somebody logged off; bring one, visit a random player, jump and phase."
---

Module id `teleport`, switch `modules.teleport`, files under `modules/teleport/`.

## Commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/tp <player>` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | To that player, following them to their server if they are elsewhere. `/teleport`, `/stafftp` and `/tpto` are aliases. |
| `/tp <player> <destination>` | `exyliastaff.teleport.here` | Moves the first player to the second, who must be on this server. The first is brought from another server if needed. |
| `/tpstaff <player>` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | Enters staff mode and vanish first, then teleports to them. `/staffmodetp` and `/tpduty` are aliases. |
| `/tppos <x> <y> <z> [world]` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | To coordinates, in this world or a named one. `/tpcoords` is an alias. |
| `/tpworld <world>` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | To the spawn point of a world. `/staffworld` is an alias. |
| `/tpoffline <player>` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | To where the player logged off. `/tpoff` is an alias. |
| `/top` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | Up to the highest block above you. `/stafftop` is an alias. |
| `/stafftphere <player>` | `exyliastaff.teleport.here` | Brings the player, from another server if needed. `/tphere` is an alias. |
| `/staffrtp` | `exyliastaff.teleport.random` | To a random player you have not visited yet. `/staffrandomtp` is an alias. |
| `/staffrtp reset` | `exyliastaff.teleport.random` | Clears your visited list. |
| `/phase` | `exyliastaff.teleport.phase` | Through the wall you look at. `/staffphase` is an alias. |
| `/jumpto` | `exyliastaff.teleport.phase` | On top of the block you look at. `/staffjump` is an alias. |

<Callout type="warning" title="/tp, /teleport and /top are taken">
The module registers the bare `tp`, `teleport` and `top` labels, and `/tp` takes no coordinates. If
another plugin also registers them, the server's command priority decides which one answers. List the
names you want to leave to the other plugin in
[`disabled-commands`](/docs/exyliastaff/configuration#disabled-commands): `/stafftp` and `/stafftop`
keep working. `/rtp` and `/randomtp` are deliberately not registered, so a survival random teleport
keeps its command.
</Callout>

Every player argument takes anybody the network knows, online on any server or not, and suggests
every name on the proxy. Teleports have no cooldown and no warm-up; the confirmation is sent once the
teleport has landed.

## Coordinates, worlds and the surface

`/tppos` reads numbers or `~` relative to where you stand, like vanilla, so coordinates copied from a
report or a log paste straight in. Coordinates outside the world border are refused. `/tpworld`
completes the loaded worlds and lands on that world's spawn point, keeping your view. `/top` puts you
on the highest block above where you stand; with nothing above, "There is nothing above you to stand
on".

## Where somebody logged off

`/tpoffline` reads the spot a player left from out of this server's player data: the hole, the chest,
the base they logged off next to the moment staff arrived. Somebody online is simply teleported to,
here or on their server, because their live position is the true one. A player whose last session was
on another server left their position there, and this server answers "has no record of where they
left from" rather than guessing. [`/seen`](/docs/exyliastaff/inspect#seen) shows the same spot
without going.

## Random

The candidates are every online player except you, outside `exclude-worlds`, and, when
`random-skip-staff` is on, neither vanished nor holding `exyliastaff.staff`. With `random-skip-afk` on
and the [AFK](/docs/exyliastaff/afk) module running, idle players are skipped too. Each staff member has
their own visited list; when every candidate has been visited the list clears and the round starts
over. The message says how many are left. The list is also cleared on quit.

## Jump and phase

**Jump** traces up to `jump-max-distance` blocks along your view, ignoring passable blocks and fluids,
and lands you on the first two free blocks above what it hit, keeping your view. Nothing hit, and
nothing solid under that point, is "Nothing to land on".

**Phase** walks up to `phase-max-distance` blocks along your view from eye level and stops at the
first open spot, two free blocks tall, after at least one blocked one. No wall in range is
"No wall within 10 blocks".

## Configuration

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/teleport/config.yml"
exclude-worlds: []
random-skip-staff: true
random-skip-afk: true
phase-max-distance: 10
jump-max-distance: 300
```

## Messages

`lang/<language>/modules/teleport/messages.yml`: `teleported`, `brought`, `following` and
`summoning` with `%target%`; `moved` with `%target%` and `%destination%`; `random` with `%target%` and
`%remaining%`; `random-none`, `random-reset`; `phased`, `phase-none` and `jump-none` with
`%distance%`; `jumped`; `topped` with `%y%`, `top-none`; `positioned` with `%x%`, `%y%`, `%z%` and
`%world%`, `position-invalid`, `position-outside`; `offline-teleported` with `%target%`, `%world%`,
`%x%`, `%y%` and `%z%`, `offline-none`; `target-offline`.
