Teleport gives staff the movement tools they need on duty: go to a player (even on another server), bring one to you, jump to the block you look at, walk through a wall, or visit a random player you have not checked yet.

## Before you start

- Give the staff rank `exyliastaff.teleport`. Add `exyliastaff.teleport.here` to move other players, `exyliastaff.teleport.random` for random visits and `exyliastaff.teleport.phase` for jump and phase.
- The module switch is `modules.teleport` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`.

<Callout type="warning" title="/tp, /teleport and /top are taken">
The module registers the bare `tp`, `teleport` and `top` labels, and `/tp` takes no coordinates. If another plugin also registers them, the server's command priority decides which one answers. List the names you want to leave to the other plugin in [`disabled-commands`](/docs/exyliastaff/configuration#disabled-commands): `/stafftp` and `/stafftop` keep working. `/rtp` and `/randomtp` are deliberately not registered, so a survival random teleport keeps its command.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to a player">
    Run `/tp <player>` (aliases `/teleport`, `/stafftp`, `/tpto`). If they are on another server of the network you follow them there. `/tpstaff <player>` (aliases `/staffmodetp`, `/tpduty`) enters [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) and [vanish](/docs/exyliastaff/vanish) first, then teleports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring a player to you">
    Run `/stafftphere <player>` (alias `/tphere`), or `/tp <player> <destination>` to move the first player to the second, who must be on this server. Both need `exyliastaff.teleport.here`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Visit a random player">
    Run `/staffrtp` (alias `/staffrandomtp`), or click the **Music disc** (**RANDOM PLAYER**, slot 3) in the staff hotbar. `/staffrtp reset` clears your visited list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move around the world">
    `/jumpto` (alias `/staffjump`) puts you on the block you look at. `/phase` (alias `/staffphase`) takes you through the wall you look at. Both are the **Clock** (**TELEPORT**, slot 0) in the hotbar: left click jumps, right click phases.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every player argument takes anybody the network knows, online on any server or not. Teleports have no cooldown and no warm-up; the confirmation is sent once the teleport has landed.

## Check that it works

Run `/tp <player>` with a player online: you should land next to them and see a "teleported" confirmation. Look at a wall and run `/phase`: you should land on the other side.

## Going further

### Coordinates, worlds and the surface

`/tppos <x> <y> <z> [world]` (alias `/tpcoords`) reads numbers or `~` relative to where you stand, like vanilla, so coordinates copied from a report or a log paste straight in. Coordinates outside the world border are refused. `/tpworld <world>` (alias `/staffworld`) completes the loaded worlds and lands on that world's spawn point, keeping your view. `/top` (alias `/stafftop`) puts you on the highest block above you; with nothing above, "There is nothing above you to stand on".

### Where somebody logged off

`/tpoffline <player>` (alias `/tpoff`) reads the spot a player left from out of this server's player data: the hole, the chest, the base they logged off next to. Somebody online is simply teleported to, here or on their server. A player whose last session was on another server left their position there, and this server answers "has no record of where they left from" rather than guessing. [`/seen`](/docs/exyliastaff/inspect#seen) shows the same spot without going.

### Random visits

The candidates are every online player except you, outside `exclude-worlds`, and, when `random-skip-staff` is on, neither vanished nor holding `exyliastaff.staff`. With `random-skip-afk` on and the [AFK](/docs/exyliastaff/afk) module running, idle players are skipped too. Each staff member has their own visited list; when every candidate has been visited the list clears and the round starts over. The message says how many are left. The list is also cleared on quit.

### Jump and phase

**Jump** traces up to `jump-max-distance` blocks along your view, ignoring passable blocks and fluids, and lands you on the first two free blocks above what it hit, keeping your view. Nothing hit, and nothing solid under that point, is "Nothing to land on". **Phase** walks up to `phase-max-distance` blocks along your view from eye level and stops at the first open spot, two free blocks tall, after at least one blocked one. No wall in range is "No wall within 10 blocks".

## Commands and permissions

<Commands group="teleport" />

<Permissions group="teleport" />

<Details title="Configuration (modules/teleport/config.yml)">

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/teleport/config.yml"
exclude-worlds: []
random-skip-staff: true
random-skip-afk: true
phase-max-distance: 10
jump-max-distance: 300
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `exclude-worlds` | none | Worlds whose players random visits skip. |
| `random-skip-staff` | `true` | Skip vanished players and staff in random visits. |
| `random-skip-afk` | `true` | Skip idle players. Needs the AFK module. |
| `phase-max-distance` | `10` | How far `/phase` looks for a wall, in blocks. |
| `jump-max-distance` | `300` | How far `/jumpto` looks, in blocks. |

Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/teleport/messages.yml`: `teleported`, `brought`, `following` and `summoning` (`%target%`), `moved` (`%target%`, `%destination%`), `random` (`%target%`, `%remaining%`), `random-none`, `random-reset`, `phased`, `phase-none` and `jump-none` (`%distance%`), `jumped`, `topped` (`%y%`), `top-none`, `positioned` (`%x%`, `%y%`, `%z%`, `%world%`), `position-invalid`, `position-outside`, `offline-teleported` (`%target%`, `%world%`, `%x%`, `%y%`, `%z%`), `offline-none` and `target-offline`. See [ExyliaLib messages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

</Details>
