title: "Commands"
description: "Every command of every module, with its aliases, its arguments and its permission."
---

Commands belong to modules. A module that is off has none of its commands on the server: `/freeze`
with freeze disabled is an unknown command, not a refusal. Toggling a module with `/exyliastaff module`
re-registers the set and refreshes every client's command list. A single command or alias can be left
unregistered with [`disabled-commands`](/docs/exyliastaff/configuration#disabled-commands).

`<player>` is required, `[player]` optional. Most commands take anybody the network knows, online on
any server or not, and suggest every name connected to the proxy. The ones that act on somebody in
front of you — freezing them, reporting them, switching their vanish, staff mode or x-ray — need them
online on this server, and say so with "is not online".

## Core

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/exyliastaff` | `exyliastaff.admin` | Lists the modules and their state. `/es` is an alias. |
| `/exyliastaff reload` | `exyliastaff.admin.reload` | Reloads every file, the modules and the command gate. |
| `/exyliastaff modules` | `exyliastaff.admin.modules` | The same list. |
| `/exyliastaff module enable <id>` | `exyliastaff.admin.modules` | Switches a module on, and its commands with it. |
| `/exyliastaff module disable <id>` | `exyliastaff.admin.modules` | Off, together with every module that depends on it. |
| `/exyliastaff redis` | `exyliastaff.admin` | This server's id, whether Redis is connected, and its stats. |

The root itself needs `exyliastaff.admin`. All of it works from the console.

## Staff mode

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/staff` | `exyliastaff.staffmode` | Toggles your own staff mode. `/staffmode` and `/mod` are aliases. |
| `/staff toggle [player]`, `/staff on [player]`, `/staff off [player]` | `exyliastaff.staffmode` | Toggles or sets it, for you or somebody else. |
| `/staff spectator` | `exyliastaff.staffmode` | Spectator view. `/staff spec` is the same. |

Another player needs `exyliastaff.staffmode.others`.

| `/tpstaff <player>` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | Staff mode and vanish first, then to them. `/staffmodetp`, `/tpduty`. |

| `/tpworld <world>` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | To the spawn of a world. `/staffworld`. |
| `/tpoffline <player>` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | To where they logged off. `/tpoff`. |
| `/top` | `exyliastaff.teleport` | Up to the surface above you. `/stafftop`. |
| `/stafftphere <player>` | `exyliastaff.teleport.here` | Brings them. `/tphere`. |
| `/staffrtp [reset]` | `exyliastaff.teleport.random` | Random player, or clear the visited list. `/staffrandomtp`. |
| `/phase` | `exyliastaff.teleport.phase` | Through the wall. `/staffphase`. |
| `/jumpto` | `exyliastaff.teleport.phase` | On top of what you look at. `/staffjump`. |
