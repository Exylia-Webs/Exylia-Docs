X-ray vision outlines the ores around a staff member so they can see what a suspect is digging towards. Each ore is an invisible, glowing, client-only display entity shaped like the block: nothing is spawned on the server and nobody else sees it.

## Before you start

- Install [PacketEvents](/docs/exyliastaff/installation). X-ray vision is one of the few features that needs it; without PacketEvents the module stays off and everything else keeps working.
- Give the staff rank `exyliastaff.xrayvision`, and `exyliastaff.xrayvision.others` for toggling it on somebody else.
- The module switch is `modules.xray-vision` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Run `/xray` (alias `/xrayvision`) to toggle, or `/xray on` and `/xray off` to set it. In [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) the **Glowstone** in the hotbar (**X-RAY**, slot 29) does the same, and shows ON or OFF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look around">
    Diamond, emerald, ancient debris, gold and iron ore within range glow in their own colour through the stone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle it for somebody else">
    Run `/xray on <player>`, `/xray off <player>` or `/xray toggle <player>`. The player is told who did it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Stand in a cave with ore a few blocks away behind stone and run `/xray on`. You should see "X-ray is on" and the ore outlined through the wall. `/xray off` removes the outlines.

## Going further

### What is scanned

The square of chunks within `radius` of your chunk, so `3` is a 7 by 7 square, from `y-radius` blocks below your feet to as many above, clamped to the world's height. Only loaded chunks are read, and the search runs off the main thread. Moving into a new chunk drops the outlines of the chunks that left the range and scans the ones that entered; changing world rescans everything. With `refresh-seconds` at `0` nothing is rescanned until you change chunk, so an ore mined in the meantime keeps its outline until then. Set it to a number of seconds to rescan on that interval.

`max-blocks-per-player` is a budget over every outline you have: once it is spent, further chunks add nothing. Lower it if the outlines make clients lag.

### When it turns off

Leaving staff mode turns x-ray off and remembers it off. Losing the permission does the same, a few seconds later. Quitting or a module disable turns it off without forgetting it, so it comes back on at your next session with "X-ray is still on from your last session".

### Choosing the ores

`outlines` maps a block material to its colour, as `#RRGGBB` or a colour name such as `AQUA`. Add or remove entries freely; an entry that is not a block or not a colour is dropped silently.

<Details title="Configuration (modules/xrayvision/config.yml)">

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/xrayvision/config.yml"
radius: 3
y-radius: 24
refresh-seconds: 0
max-blocks-per-player: 2000
outlines:
  DIAMOND_ORE: '#5DE5E5'
  DEEPSLATE_DIAMOND_ORE: '#5DE5E5'
  EMERALD_ORE: '#3FE05B'
  DEEPSLATE_EMERALD_ORE: '#3FE05B'
  ANCIENT_DEBRIS: '#FF5555'
  GOLD_ORE: '#FFD24A'
  DEEPSLATE_GOLD_ORE: '#FFD24A'
  IRON_ORE: '#E8E8E8'
  DEEPSLATE_IRON_ORE: '#E8E8E8'
```

Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/xrayvision/messages.yml`: `enabled`, `disabled`, `enabled-other` and `disabled-other` (`%target%`), `enabled-by` and `disabled-by` (`%staff%`), `permission-lost`, `restored`, `status-on` and `status-off`. See [ExyliaLib messages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

</Details>

| `%staff_xrayvision_active%` | `true` while the viewer has x-ray vision on, without the colour `status` carries. |
