Every stretch in [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) is recorded as a session: when it started, how long it lasted, how much of it was idle, which server it was on and how many freezes, inspections, resolved reports and so on it held. `/stafflog` opens those sessions as a menu, with a ranking and a summary on top. Use it to see who actually works the shift.

## Before you start

- The module switch is `modules.staff-log` in `plugins/ExyliaStaff/config.yml`, on by default. It needs the [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) module: sessions are only recorded while a player is in staff mode.
- Staff read their own log with `exyliastaff.stafflog`. Reading somebody else's, the summary of another member and the ranking need `exyliastaff.stafflog.others`. Both are op by default.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your own log">
    Run `/stafflog`, or click the written book in the [staff mode](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-mode) hotbar. The menu lists your sessions of the last seven days, one entry each. A green entry is the session you are in now. Each shows its duration, AFK time, server and the actions counted in it. For another window, give the days: `/stafflog 30`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the totals">
    Run `/stafflog summary`. One card holds the last 7 days and another the last 30: sessions, total time, active time, AFK time and the actions counted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at somebody else">
    Run `/stafflog sessions <player> [days]` for their sessions, or `/stafflog summary <player>` for their totals. Both need `exyliastaff.stafflog.others`. Their name goes after `sessions` on purpose, so the command offers its four screens first and not every player on the network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rank the team">
    Run `/stafflog top [days]`. Every staff member with a session in that window is listed, most active first. Each head shows active time, total time, AFK time with its percentage, sessions, and actions with a rate per hour.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Enter staff mode with `/staff`, run a command such as `/freeze <player>` and leave. Then run `/stafflog`: you should see one session of a few minutes with `freeze: 1` in its actions. If it says you have no sessions, the module is off or you never entered staff mode in this window.

## Going further

### How idle time is counted

The ranking sorts by active time, which is time on duty minus idle time, because time online is a number anybody can raise by leaving the client open. A staff member counts as idle after `afk-after-seconds` (default 300) without moving to another block, clicking, chatting or running a command, and every counted action also resets the clock. The first five minutes of an idle stretch are free; only what comes after is AFK. The clock is checked every thirty seconds.

The [AFK](/docs/exyliastaff/afk) module has its own idle timer, used by random teleport; the log keeps its own `afk-after-seconds`.

### What gets counted

While a session is open on this server, the log counts what the other modules announce: `freeze`, `unfreeze`, `inspect`, `enderchest`, `container`, `teleport`, `report_resolve`, `report_dismiss`, `helpop_reply`, `helpop_close`, `punish`, `mining_reset` and `xray_toggle`. Actions done on another server count in that server's session.

### What a session is

A session opens when a player enters staff mode, or when the module starts while somebody already is. It closes when they leave staff mode, quit, or the module stops. A quit closes the row and the next join opens a new one, so rows are per connection and the summaries add them up.

### Keep less, or more

`keep-days` (default 90) deletes sessions older than that once a day. `0` keeps everything. With the network linked, [Exylia Staff Web](/docs/exyliastaff/staff-web) reads the same sessions for its team activity page.

### Show minutes on a scoreboard

`%staff_stafflog_today%` gives the minutes in staff mode today, live session included, and `%staff_stafflog_week%` the last seven days.

<Details title="Technical details">

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaStaff/modules/stafflog/config.yml"
afk-after-seconds: 300
keep-days: 90
```

Module id `stafflog`, files under `modules/stafflog/`. Tables: `staff_log_sessions` (one row per session) and `staff_log_daily` (one total per player and day). Messages are in `lang/<language>/modules/stafflog/messages.yml`: `no-sessions` with `%target%` and `%days%`, `never-played`, `action-line` with `%action%` and `%count%`, `no-actions` and `no-staff` with `%days%`. The three menus are in `lang/<language>/menus/stafflog/` (`sessions.yml`, `summary.yml`, `top.yml`).
