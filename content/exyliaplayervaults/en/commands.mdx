Two commands: `/pv` for players and `/pvadmin` for staff. Player names are suggested as you type, and the player may be online, offline or on another server, as long as this server has seen them before. Both commands are for players in game; only `/pvadmin` on its own and `/pvadmin reload` also work from the console.

<Commands />

## Aliases

`/pv` also answers to `/playervault`, `/playervaults`, `/vault` and `/vaults`. `/pvadmin` also answers to `/playervaultsadmin`.

## Things you do from the list, not by command

Renaming a vault, choosing its icon and buying a new one are buttons in the vault list that `/pv` opens. See [Name vaults and search them](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/names-and-search) and [Sell extra vaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/purchases).

## What reload does

`/pvadmin reload` reads `config.yml` and the messages again, restarts the save timer with the new `vaults.checkpoint`, and compiles the menus again. Vaults that are open are not touched: a new `default-rows`, `hidden-slots` or `blocked-materials` applies to the next vault a player opens.

If a menu file has a mistake, the console names the file and that screen does not open until you fix it and reload.

<Details title="Technical details">
`<number>` starts at 1 and is not checked against the owner's allowance, so staff can open a vault the owner cannot reach yet. `/pv <number>` for a vault the player does not have answers "You do not have vault `<number>`." `/pvadmin list`, `view`, `edit`, `history` and `clear` are for players only. `clear` asks for the same confirmation as the button in the vault list.
</Details>
