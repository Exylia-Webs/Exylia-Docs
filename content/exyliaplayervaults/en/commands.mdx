---
title: "Commands"
description: "The player command /pv and the staff command /pvadmin, with their aliases, permissions and what each argument accepts."
---

Two commands. `/pv` is everything a player does with their own vaults; `/pvadmin` is everything staff do
with somebody else's. A player argument accepts anybody this server has seen, online, offline or on
another server, and suggests their names as you type.

## /pv

`/pv` has the aliases `/playervault`, `/playervaults`, `/vault` and `/vaults`, and needs
`exyliaplayervaults.use`. It is for players only.

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/pv` | `exyliaplayervaults.use` | Opens the vault list. |
| `/pv <number>` | `exyliaplayervaults.use` | Opens that vault, after the [restrictions](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/vaults#where-and-when). |
| `/pv search <item>` | `+ exyliaplayervaults.search` | Which of your vaults hold an item, by material or name. |

`/pv <number>` past the vaults a player has answers *"You do not have vault `<number>`."*. Renaming,
changing icons and buying are done from the list, not by command.

## /pvadmin

`/pvadmin` has the alias `/playervaultsadmin`. On its own it prints the usage, with
`exyliaplayervaults.admin.view`.

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/pvadmin list <player>` | `exyliaplayervaults.admin.view` | Their vault list; each vault opens to edit or read only, as the sender may. |
| `/pvadmin view <player> <number>` | `exyliaplayervaults.admin.view` | A vault as stored, read only, without closing it for whoever has it open. |
| `/pvadmin edit <player> <number>` | `exyliaplayervaults.admin.edit` | A vault, to change it; a window open elsewhere is written and closed first. |
| `/pvadmin history <player> <number>` | `exyliaplayervaults.admin.history` | Its earlier copies, to look at, or put back with `admin.edit` too. |
| `/pvadmin clear <player> <number>` | `exyliaplayervaults.admin.clear` | Empties it after a confirmation; the contents go to its history first. |
| `/pvadmin reload` | `exyliaplayervaults.admin.reload` | Reloads `config.yml`, the messages and the menus. |

Every subcommand except `reload` is for players only. `<number>` starts at 1 and is not checked against
the owner's allowance. See [Staff tools](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/staff).

### What reload does

Two steps: `configs` reads `config.yml` and `messages.yml` again, applies the prefix and restarts the
checkpoint timer at the new `vaults.checkpoint`; `menus` compiles both screens again, rewriting
`menus/admin/` from the jar first. The `menus` step also runs when ExyliaLib itself is reloaded, so a
palette change reaches the screens.

Open vaults are not touched by a reload. A new `default-rows`, `hidden-slots` or `blocked-materials` applies
to the next window opened.
