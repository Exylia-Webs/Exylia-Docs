Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin stays off

ExyliaLib is missing or did not load. The console says where to get it. Install ExyliaLib in `plugins/` and restart. See [Install ExyliaPlayerVaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/installation).

## Every vault looks empty after installing

The plugin is reading a different database from the one the vaults are in, usually a fresh H2 file in its own folder while the vaults are in another database. Nothing is lost: set the right database in `plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults/database.yml` and restart. See [Installation](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/installation#the-database).

## "That vault is open somewhere else right now"

Somebody has the vault open: the player in another window, on another server, or staff. After a server crash its vaults stay shut for up to a minute, until the lock expires. If staff cannot close a vault open on another server, Redis is not enabled on every server in `database.yml`. See [Share vaults across servers](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/network).

## A player lost a rank and their items are gone

They are not. With `vaults.hidden-slots: keep`, the default, stacks past the vault's new size stay stored out of sight and come back when the vault has enough rows again. Staff see them with `/pvadmin edit <player> <number>`, which is always six rows. Vaults past the player's new count show as locked and keep everything inside. With `return`, the stacks were handed back to the player or wait for their next join. See [Give ranks more and bigger vaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/vaults).

## `/pv 2` says "You do not have vault 2"

The player's rank gives them fewer vaults. Give them `exyliaplayervaults.amount.<number>`, or let them buy more. The number is also capped by `vaults.max-amount`.

## A player cannot open a vault

One of the restrictions refuses it: a disabled world, a disabled game mode (spectator by default), combat, or the cooldown. The message says which. Operators skip the world, game-mode and combat checks with `exyliaplayervaults.bypass.restrictions`. See [Limit where and when vaults open](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/restrictions).

## A player cannot put an item in a vault

The item is in `vaults.blocked-materials`, or it is a shulker box or bundle holding one. They see "That item cannot go in a vault." Only `exyliaplayervaults.bypass.blocked` skips the list, and nobody has it by default.

## A player cannot buy another vault

Purchases are off (`purchase.enabled`), they bought `purchase.max-purchases`, or their rank's vaults plus the ones they bought already reach `vaults.max-amount`. Raise `max-amount` when you add ranks with more vaults. See [Sell extra vaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/purchases).

## A purchase took the money but gave no vault

This only happens when the database stopped answering mid-purchase. The console logs an error with the player, the price and the value `playervaults_buyers` should hold. Check that row and refund the player if it was not raised.

## The vault list shows the wrong number

A permission plugin may be giving a lower node than you think: the largest `amount` or `rows` number a player holds wins. Check with `/lp user <player> permission info`.

## A placeholder shows as text

PlaceholderAPI is not installed, the player is not online, or the name is misspelled. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/placeholders).

## My edits to a staff menu disappeared

Files under `lang/<code>/menus/admin/` are replaced from the jar on every start and reload. Edit only `menus/user/`.

## A crash lost a few items

Open vaults are written every `vaults.checkpoint` (30 seconds) and on close, so a crash costs at most that much. `/pvadmin history <player> <number>` lists earlier copies, and any of them can be put back.
