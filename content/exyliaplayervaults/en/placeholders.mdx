## How the name is written

The placeholders are registered through ExyliaLib as one group, `playervaults`. PlaceholderAPI reaches them
by putting the plugin in front, so a scoreboard, a hologram or a tab plugin asks for:

```text
%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_unlocked%
```

That is the form this page documents. Inside an Exylia plugin's own files — menus, messages, another Exylia
plugin's scoreboard — the registered name also stands on its own: `%playervaults_unlocked%`.

No expansion needs downloading. Without PlaceholderAPI installed only the Exylia form works.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Returns |
|---|---|
| `%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_unlocked%` | How many vaults the viewer may open: their rank's plus the ones they bought, under `max-amount`. |
| `%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_max%` | `vaults.max-amount`, the most anybody may have. |
| `%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_bought%` | How many vaults the viewer bought. |
| `%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_rows%` | How many rows each of the viewer's vaults has, 1 to 6. |
| `%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_next_price%` | What the next vault costs, formatted in the purchase currency: `$7,500.00`. Empty when purchases are off or the viewer cannot buy another. |

Every one but `max` is about the player reading it, so it needs a viewer. They all read memory and never
ask the database on the thread that renders them. The bought count is read when a player joins, so for a
moment after joining `bought` reads `0`.

## Inside the plugin's own screens

The vault list and the history screen have values of their own, such as `%vault_used%` or
`%snapshot_when%`. They are listed on
[Configuration](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/configuration#menus).
