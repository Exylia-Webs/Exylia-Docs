Use these to show vault numbers in other plugins that support PlaceholderAPI, such as scoreboards, tab lists, holograms and chat. Click a placeholder to copy it.

<Placeholders />

## Two ways to write the name

The list above is the PlaceholderAPI form, with the plugin name in front. Inside files of Exylia plugins (menus, messages, another Exylia plugin's scoreboard) the short form works too: `%playervaults_unlocked%` instead of `%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_unlocked%`.

Without PlaceholderAPI installed only the short form works, and only in Exylia files. Nothing needs to be downloaded with `/papi ecloud`.

## Good to know

- Every placeholder except `max` is about the player who reads it.
- `next_price` is formatted in the purchase currency and is empty when purchases are off or the player cannot buy another vault. See [Sell extra vaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/purchases).
- The bought count is loaded when a player joins, so for a moment after joining `bought` reads `0`.

## Examples

```text
Vaults: %exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_unlocked%/%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_max%
Next vault: %exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_next_price|Maxed out%
```

The `|` form shows the text after it when the value is empty.
