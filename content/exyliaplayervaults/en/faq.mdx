## Is it free?

Yes. ExyliaPlayerVaults is free and open source, under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Download it
from [GitHub releases](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults/releases); the source is in the
same repository. There is no licence key and nothing to buy.

## I used the vaults of ExyliaSurvivalCore. Do my players keep them?

Yes, as long as this plugin opens the same database. The vaults stay in the same table, `sc_vaults`: point
`plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults/database.yml` at the database ExyliaSurvivalCore used and every vault is there.
On the default H2 file nothing is copied for you. The settings and the permissions have new names, and
`/vaultadmin` is now `/pvadmin`. See
[Coming from ExyliaSurvivalCore](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/network#coming-from-exyliasurvivalcore).

## Every vault looks empty after installing

The plugin is reading a different database from the one the vaults are in — usually a fresh H2 file in its
own folder while the vaults are still in ExyliaSurvivalCore's. Nothing has been lost; set the right
database in `database.yml` and restart.

## "That vault is open somewhere else right now"

Somebody has that vault open: the player in another window, on another server, or a member of staff. A
vault closed on one server and opened on another straight away is handed over within a moment. After a
server crash, its vaults stay shut for up to a minute, until the lock's lease runs out. See
[Network](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/network#one-window-anywhere).

## A player lost a rank and their items are gone

They are not. With `hidden-slots: keep`, the default, the stacks past the vault's new size stay stored out
of sight and come back when the vault has enough rows again; staff see them with `/pvadmin edit`, which is
always six rows. Vaults past the player's new count show as locked and keep everything inside. With
`hidden-slots: return`, the stacks were handed back to the player, or are waiting for their next join.

## A player cannot buy another vault

Either purchases are off (`purchase.enabled`), they reached `max-purchases`, or their rank's vaults plus the
ones they bought already reach `vaults.max-amount`. Bought vaults count under that cap, so raise
`max-amount` when you add ranks with more vaults. See
[Purchases](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/purchases#the-limits).

## A purchase took the money but gave no vault

That only happens when the database stopped answering mid-purchase, and the console logs it as an error
with the player, the price and the count `playervaults_buyers` should hold. Check that row and refund the
player if it was not raised.

## Staff cannot edit a vault open on another server

Closing a vault on another server travels over Redis. Without Redis enabled in `database.yml` on every
server, staff are told the vault is in use until its viewer closes it.

## A player put something in, the server crashed, and it is gone

Open vaults are written every `vaults.checkpoint` (30 seconds) and on close, so a crash costs at most that
much of a vault. `/pvadmin history <player> <number>` shows the copies kept before each change, and any of
them can be put back.

## My edits to the history menu disappeared

`lang/<code>/menus/admin/history.yml` is replaced from the jar on every start and every reload. The vault
list under `menus/user/` is yours and keeps your edits.

## Where do I get help?

On the Exylia Discord: [discord.exylia.net](https://discord.exylia.net). Bug reports and feature requests
are also welcome as issues on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults).
