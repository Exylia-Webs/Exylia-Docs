A vault is a real chest window, filled from the database when it opens and saved while it is open and when it closes. This page shows how to decide how many a player has, how big they are, and how the list and the ender chest replacement look.

## Before you start

A permissions plugin such as LuckPerms. See the [numbered permissions](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/permissions) for the exact names.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the defaults for everybody">
    In `config.yml`, `vaults.default-amount` (1) and `vaults.default-rows` (6) are what a player has with no permission of their own. `vaults.max-amount` (9) is the most anybody may have, bought vaults included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a rank more vaults">
    `/lp group vip permission set exyliaplayervaults.amount.3 true`. The largest number a player holds wins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a rank's vaults bigger">
    `/lp group vip permission set exyliaplayervaults.rows.4 true`. Rows are from 1 to 6 and are the same for every vault a player has.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/pvadmin reload` if you changed `config.yml`. Permissions apply at the next vault a player opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Open `/pv` as a player of that rank. The header shows how many vaults are unlocked of the maximum, and the rows. The vaults past their count are greyed out as locked. `/pv <number>` past the count answers "You do not have vault `<number>`."

Losing a permission never deletes anything: locked vaults keep what they hold until the player can reach them again. The number of vaults a player can open is their rank's amount plus the vaults they [bought](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/purchases), never more than `max-amount`.

## When a vault gets smaller

A vault can hold more than its window shows: the player lost the rank that gave more rows, or `default-rows` was lowered. `vaults.hidden-slots` decides what happens to the stacks past the last slot.

| Value | What happens |
|---|---|
| `keep` (default) | The stacks stay stored out of sight and come back when the vault is big enough. The player is told how many stacks are kept out of sight. |
| `return` | The stacks are handed back to the owner: into their inventory, or queued for their next join if it is full. |

Staff opening a vault with `/pvadmin edit` always see six rows, so nothing is out of their sight.

## The vault list

`/pv` opens a list with 28 vaults per page, a header with the player's unlocked count, bought count and rows, and a **Search** button. Each entry depends on the vault:

| Entry | Clicks |
|---|---|
| A vault the player has, with its name, icon and how full it is | Left-click: open. Right-click: rename. Shift-right-click: change icon. |
| The next vault, when one can be bought, with its price | Left-click: buy. See [Sell extra vaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/purchases). |
| Any other vault, drawn locked | None. |

How full a vault is comes from its last save, so a vault open right now shows as of its last write.

## Add previous and next buttons under a vault

Turn on `vaults.toolbar` in `config.yml` to give every vault a bottom row of buttons: **Search**, the previous vault, **Back** to the list, the next vault and a name tag for renaming and changing the icon. The row takes a row from the vault: a vault of up to 5 rows gets one more row for the buttons, and a vault of 6 rows shows 5. Items already stored in a 6-row vault's last row move to free slots when it opens, and what does not fit is handed back to the owner. The look is `lang/<code>/menus/user/toolbar.yml`.

## Replace the ender chest

Set `replace-ender-chests: true` in `config.yml` to open the vault list instead of a player's own ender chest, whichever way they open it (the block, or another plugin's `/enderchest`). It applies to players with `exyliaplayervaults.use`. What the real ender chest already holds stays there, out of reach while the option is on. A region or combat plugin that refuses the ender chest still refuses it, and staff opening somebody else's ender chest are left alone.

<Details title="Technical details">
`/pv` draws one entry per number from 1 up to `max-amount`, or further when a player has more. On servers with ExyliaLib's full-screen menu style, the same list and the search results are drawn from `screens/vaults.html` with its look in `screens/theme.css` (your own rules go in `custom.css` beside it) and its words in `lang/<code>/screens/vaults.yml`. A screen that cannot be read falls back to the chest menu. Values and actions for the list file are in [Configuration](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/configuration#the-vault-list).
</Details>
