---
title: "Purchases"
description: "Selling extra vaults from the vault list: turning it on, which currency pays, how the price grows, the limits, and what happens when a payment goes wrong."
---

Players can buy vaults past what their rank gives them, from the vault list. Purchases are off until you
turn them on.

## Turning it on

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults/config.yml"
purchase:
  enabled: true
  currency: ''
  base-price: 5000.0
  price-multiplier: 1.5
  max-purchases: 9
```

`/pvadmin reload` applies it. From then on, the first locked vault in a player's list is drawn with
`buy_template` and its price, as long as they can still buy one. Buying needs
`exyliaplayervaults.buy`, granted to everyone by default.

## Which currency pays

`purchase.currency` is a currency id as ExyliaLib knows it. Empty, the default, uses the server's default
currency: `default-currency` in `plugins/ExyliaLib/economy.yml`. Any currency ExyliaLib reaches works — an
[ExyliaEconomy](/docs/exyliaeconomy/currencies) currency such as `shards`, `vault` for whatever plugin
serves Vault, PlayerPoints, and so on. Prices are written the way that currency writes them.

```yaml
purchase:
  currency: shards
```

## How the price grows

The first vault a player buys costs `base-price`. Each later one costs `price-multiplier` times the one
before:

```text
price = base-price × price-multiplier ^ (vaults already bought)
```

Rounded to two decimals. With the defaults:

| Vault bought | Price |
|---|---|
| 1st | 5,000.00 |
| 2nd | 7,500.00 |
| 3rd | 11,250.00 |
| 4th | 16,875.00 |
| 5th | 25,312.50 |

`1.0` keeps every vault at `base-price`.

## The limits

A player cannot buy another vault when either is true:

- they already bought `max-purchases` (9);
- their rank's vaults plus the ones they bought already reach `vaults.max-amount`.

Bought vaults count on top of the rank, under `max-amount`. A player whose rank later rises can therefore
end up with fewer usable bought vaults than they paid for, because the total is capped; raise `max-amount`
along with your ranks if that matters.

With nothing left to buy, the list shows no buy entry, `%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_next_price%` is empty and a stray
click answers *"You cannot buy any more vaults."*

## Buying

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the buy entry">
    The list closes and a confirmation asks *"Buy vault `<number>` for `<price>`?"*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The money is taken, then the purchase is counted. The player is told
    *"Vault `<number>` is yours. Paid `<price>`."* and the list opens again with the new vault in it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Declining reopens the list. A player who cannot pay is told *"You need `<price>` to buy that vault."*

## When something goes wrong

The money is always taken **before** the vault is counted, because a vault counted before the charge is a
free vault whenever the charge fails. The count is a compare-and-set in the database, so two purchases
racing on two servers, or a double click, can never count the same vault twice.

| What happened | What the player gets |
|---|---|
| Another purchase changed the count between the price shown and the click | *"The purchase did not go through and you were not charged."* Nothing is charged. |
| The count was taken by a purchase on another server | The money is refunded, with the same message. |
| The database did not answer, and reading it back three times did not tell | *"That vault could not be read."* The money stays taken, and the console logs an error naming the player, the price and the count `playervaults_buyers` should hold, for staff to check and refund. |

Every purchase is recorded in the audit log as `vault-buy`, with the vault and the price.

## Where the count lives

How many vaults each player bought is one row per player in `playervaults_buyers`, created the first time
they join. It is read when they join and kept in memory while they are online, which is what the list and
the placeholders show.
