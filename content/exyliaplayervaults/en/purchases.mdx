Players buy extra vaults from their vault list, on top of what their rank gives them, paying with any currency ExyliaLib reaches. Purchases are off until you turn them on.

## Before you start

- Players need [vaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/vaults) working. Buying needs `exyliaplayervaults.buy`, which everyone has by default.
- The currency must exist. The server's default currency is set in `plugins/ExyliaLib/economy.yml`; see [Economy](/docs/exylialib/economy) for adding others.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn purchases on">
    Run `/pvadmin`, open **Settings** and the **Purchase** page, and switch **enabled** on. Or edit `purchase.enabled` in `plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults/config.yml` and run `/pvadmin reload`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the currency">
    Set `purchase.currency` to the id of a currency. Left empty it uses the server's default currency. An [ExyliaEconomy](/docs/exyliaeconomy/currencies) currency such as `shards`, `vault` (whatever plugin serves Vault), PlayerPoints and so on all work. Prices are written the way that currency writes them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price and the limit">
    `base-price` is what the first vault costs, `price-multiplier` how much each later one grows, and `max-purchases` how many one player may buy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults/config.yml"
purchase:
  enabled: true
  currency: shards
  base-price: 5000.0
  price-multiplier: 1.5
  max-purchases: 9
```

## Check that it works

Open `/pv` as a player with fewer vaults than `max-amount`. The first locked vault is drawn as a buy entry with its price. Click it: the list closes and a confirmation asks *"Buy vault `<number>` for `<price>`?"*. After you confirm, the player is told *"Vault `<number>` is yours. Paid `<price>`."* and the list opens again with the new vault in it. Declining just reopens the list. A player who cannot pay is told *"You need `<price>` to buy that vault."*

## How the price grows

The first vault a player buys costs `base-price`. Each later one costs `price-multiplier` times the one before:

```text
price = base-price × price-multiplier ^ (vaults already bought)
```

Rounded to two decimals. With the defaults:

| Vault bought | Price |
|---|---|
| 1st | 5,000.00 |
| 2nd | 7,500.00 |
| 3rd | 11,250.00 |
| 4th | 16,875.00 |
| 5th | 25,312.50 |

`1.0` keeps every vault at `base-price`.

## The limits

A player cannot buy another vault when either is true:

- they already bought `max-purchases` (9 by default);
- their rank's vaults plus the ones they bought already reach `vaults.max-amount`.

Bought vaults count on top of the rank, under `max-amount`. A player whose rank later rises can therefore end up with fewer usable bought vaults than they paid for, because the total is capped. Raise `max-amount` along with your ranks if that matters.

With nothing left to buy, the list shows no buy entry, `%exyliaplayervaults_playervaults_next_price%` is empty and a stray click answers *"You cannot buy any more vaults."*

## When something goes wrong

The money is always taken **before** the vault is counted, because a vault counted before the charge is a free vault whenever the charge fails. The count is a compare-and-set in the database, so two purchases racing on two servers, or a double click, never count the same vault twice.

| What happened | What the player gets |
|---|---|
| Another purchase changed the count between the price shown and the click | *"The purchase did not go through and you were not charged. Try again."* Nothing is charged. |
| The count was taken by a purchase on another server | The money is refunded, with the same message. |
| The database did not answer, and reading it back three times did not tell | *"That vault could not be read. Nothing has been lost; try again."* The money stays taken, and the console logs an error naming the player, the price and the count `playervaults_buyers` should hold, for staff to check and refund. |

Every purchase is recorded in the audit log as `vault-buy`, with the vault and the price. Read it with `/exylialib logs`; see [Check and restore a player's vaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/staff#the-audit-log).

<Details title="Where the count lives">
How many vaults each player bought is one row per player in `playervaults_buyers`, created the first time they join. It is read when they join and kept in memory while they are online, which is what the list and the placeholders show.
</Details>
