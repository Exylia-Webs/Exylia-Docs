At the end of this page you have opened a vault in game, renamed it, given a rank more and bigger vaults, and looked at a player's vault as staff. You only use commands and the vault list.

## What a fresh server gives

With nothing configured, every player (`exyliaplayervaults.use` is on by default) gets one vault of six rows, 54 slots. Nobody can have more than nine vaults, buying is off, and each vault keeps its ten latest earlier copies. Vaults cannot be opened in spectator mode or while in combat, and there is half a second between two opens.

## Before you start

Install the plugin and ExyliaLib, see [Install ExyliaPlayerVaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/installation). You need a permissions plugin such as LuckPerms to follow the rank step.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    Run `/pv`. The list shows your vaults with how full each one is, and the ones a rank would unlock greyed out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a vault">
    Left-click a vault in the list, or run `/pv 1`. Put something in and close it: it is saved the moment it closes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and give it an icon">
    In the list, right-click the vault and type a name such as `Tools`. Shift-right-click picks an icon: the item in your hand, a material or a head.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find an item">
    Run `/pv search diamond`. It tells you which vaults hold diamonds, counting what is inside shulker boxes and bundles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a rank more and bigger vaults">
    With LuckPerms: `/lp group vip permission set exyliaplayervaults.amount.3 true` gives three vaults and `/lp group vip permission set exyliaplayervaults.rows.6 true` makes them six rows. Everyone else keeps one vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at it as staff">
    As an operator run `/pvadmin list <player>` to open their vault list, or `/pvadmin view <player> 1` to look inside their first vault without disturbing them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

A player in the VIP group runs `/pv` and sees three vaults with a six-row window each. A default player sees one. If the list shows a different number, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/troubleshooting).

<Callout type="tip" title="Nobody goes past the maximum">
`vaults.max-amount` (9 by default) caps everybody, whatever number their permission says. Raise it in `config.yml` if a rank gets more than nine.
</Callout>
