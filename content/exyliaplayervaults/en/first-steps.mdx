## What a fresh server gives

With nothing configured, every player who has `exyliaplayervaults.use` (everyone, by default) gets:

| | Default | Set by |
|---|---|---|
| Vaults | 1 | `vaults.default-amount`, or `exyliaplayervaults.amount.<number>` |
| Most vaults anybody may have | 9 | `vaults.max-amount` |
| Rows per vault | 6 (54 slots) | `vaults.default-rows`, or `exyliaplayervaults.rows.<1-6>` |
| Buying vaults | Off | `purchase.enabled` |
| Earlier copies kept per vault | 10 | `history.keep` |

Vaults cannot be opened in spectator mode or while in combat, and there is half a second between two
opens. See [Vaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/vaults#where-and-when).

## A first look

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    `/pv` opens the vault list: your vaults with how full each one is, and the ones a rank would unlock,
    greyed out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a vault">
    Left-click a vault in the list, or type `/pv 1`. Put something in and close it: it is written the
    moment it closes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    In the list, right-click the vault and type a name, such as `Tools`. Shift-right-click picks an icon:
    the item in your hand, a material, or a head.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find something">
    `/pv search diamond` says which of your vaults hold diamonds, counting what is inside shulker boxes and
    bundles too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## More and bigger vaults for a rank

The largest number a player holds wins, for both nodes:

```text
/lp group vip permission set exyliaplayervaults.amount.3 true
/lp group vip permission set exyliaplayervaults.rows.6 true
/lp group default permission set exyliaplayervaults.rows.3 true
```

A default player now has one vault of three rows; a VIP has three vaults of six rows. Nobody goes past
`max-amount`, however high their node. See
[Vaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/vaults#how-many-and-how-big).

## Selling vaults

Turn purchases on in `config.yml` and reload:

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults/config.yml"
purchase:
  enabled: true
  currency: ''
  base-price: 5000.0
  price-multiplier: 1.5
  max-purchases: 9
```

The next vault a player can buy appears in their list with its price. See
[Purchases](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/purchases).

## A first look as staff

With `exyliaplayervaults.admin` (operators, by default):

- `/pvadmin list <player>` opens their vault list; clicking a vault opens it for editing.
- `/pvadmin view <player> <number>` shows a vault as stored, read only, without disturbing whoever has it
  open.
- `/pvadmin history <player> <number>` lists earlier copies of it, to look at or put back.

See [Staff tools](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/staff).
