ExyliaPlayerVaults gives every player personal vaults: real chests they open from anywhere with `/pv`. They are kept in the database and each one is open in one place at a time, so an item can never be duplicated, not with two windows, not when switching servers and not after a crash.

In about ten minutes you will have the plugin running, a rank with more and bigger vaults, and a vault you can open in game. It is free and open source (GPL-3.0), published on [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/plugin/exyliaplayervaults) and [GitHub](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults).

## What your players get

- A list of vaults with `/pv`, showing how full each one is, the ones a higher rank unlocks and the next one for sale.
- Names and icons for each vault, and `/pv search diamond` to find which vault holds an item.
- Extra vaults to buy, if you turn purchases on.

## What you get

- Rank-based amount and size of vaults, set with permissions.
- Where, when and what: disabled worlds, combat, cooldown and blocked items.
- Staff tools to look inside, edit, empty and restore any player's vault, with every action in the audit log.
- Several servers sharing the same vaults.

## What you need

- Paper or Folia 1.21.4 or newer, and Java 21.
- [ExyliaLib](/docs/exylialib), installed first.
- Optional: PlaceholderAPI for [placeholders](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/placeholders), and an economy plugin (`ExyliaEconomy`, or any plugin behind `Vault`) only if you want to sell vaults.
