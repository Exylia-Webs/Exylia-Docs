Restrictions decide where, when and with what a vault opens. They live in `config.yml` and are checked every time a player opens a vault.

## Before you start

None of these apply to staff opening somebody else's vault with `/pvadmin`. For your own trusted players, see the bypass permissions in [Permissions](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/permissions).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick what to restrict">
    Edit the `restrictions` section of `config.yml`:

    ```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaPlayerVaults/config.yml"
    restrictions:
      open-cooldown: 500ms
      block-in-combat: true
      disabled-worlds:
        - world_pvp
      disabled-game-modes:
        - SPECTATOR
        - CREATIVE
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep items out (optional)">
    Under `vaults`, list materials in `blocked-materials`, for example `[ELYTRA, TNT]`. Names are compared ignoring case. Shulker boxes and bundles holding one are refused too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    `/pvadmin reload`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each one does

| Setting | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `open-cooldown` | `500ms` | Wait between opening two vaults. `0s` turns it off. |
| `block-in-combat` | `true` | Refuses to open a vault while combat-tagged, and closes an open one when the player hits or is hit by a player. |
| `disabled-worlds` | none | Worlds, by name, where vaults cannot be opened. |
| `disabled-game-modes` | `SPECTATOR` | `SURVIVAL`, `CREATIVE`, `ADVENTURE` or `SPECTATOR`. |
| `vaults.blocked-materials` | none | Materials that cannot be put in a vault. |

## Let someone skip them

| Permission | Skips |
|---|---|
| `exyliaplayervaults.bypass.cooldown` | The open cooldown. |
| `exyliaplayervaults.bypass.restrictions` | Worlds, game modes and combat. |
| `exyliaplayervaults.bypass.blocked` | The blocked items list. Not granted to anyone by default: it lets players store what you chose to keep out. |

## Check that it works

As a player without a bypass, stand in a disabled world and run `/pv`. You get the `disabled-world` message instead of the list. Try a blocked item: the click is refused with the `blocked-item` message. All messages are in `messages.yml`.

<Details title="Technical details">
Staff windows opened with `/pvadmin edit` are not held to `blocked-materials` and are not closed by combat. The same restrictions are in the in-game settings card, under the Access page.
</Details>
