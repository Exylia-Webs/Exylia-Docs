`/hiteffect` opens a menu where players wear, preview, favourite and buy hit effects. You choose between an inventory window and a 3D selector standing in the world.

## Before you start

The menu only opens when players are allowed to pick effects in it. With `behaviour.mode: weapon` it answers that it is disabled instead of opening: see [Modes and tokens](/docs/exyliahiteffect/modes-and-tokens). Players also need an effect to wear, see [Your first hit effect](/docs/exyliahiteffect/first-steps).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Run `/hiteffect`. You get the inventory window: categories down the left, the effects of the open category in the grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wear, preview or favourite an effect">
    Left-click an effect to wear it (left-click it again to take it off). Right-click to preview it. Shift-click to favourite it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try the 3D selector">
    Run `/hiteffect 3d`. It opens the 3D selector whatever the setting says. `/hiteffect classic` opens the inventory window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make one of them the default">
    In `plugins/ExyliaHitEffect/config.yml` set `menu.style` to `CLASSIC` (the default) or `SPATIAL`, then run `/hiteffectsadmin reload`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/hiteffect`, left-click an effect and hit a player or a zombie: the effect plays on the body you hit. With `menu.style: SPATIAL`, `/hiteffect` opens the 3D selector, and a player on a client older than 1.21.4 gets the inventory window instead.

## Going further

### The 3D selector

The same catalogue drawn in the world in front of the player. The categories run along the top as tabs, the effect being looked at is named over a pedestal where a mannequin takes the hits, and the effects stand in an arc round it.

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| The arc | Look at an effect to read about it, click it to try it on the mannequin. Trying costs nothing and changes nothing yet. |
| **EQUIP** | Puts the effect being tried on the player's hits. On a locked effect that is for sale it reads **BUY**, then **CONFIRM** with the price, and the second click pays and equips. |
| The icons | Favourite, unequip, revert, random, replay, the crate, help and leave. |
| The tabs | Filter by category, with favourites (`★`) and every effect at once. |

It follows the same rules as the inventory menu, so the two never disagree about what a player owns. Without `exyliahiteffect.preview` clicking an effect still picks it, but the mannequin stays still. Without `exyliahiteffect.favourites` the `★` tab refuses in both screens. The first open plays a short guided tour; the **?** icon shows it again, and `/hiteffectsadmin spatial tour <player>` replays it for someone.

To leave a selector in the world for players to click, stand where it should go and run `/hiteffectsadmin spatial place`. Move its pieces with `/hiteffectsadmin spatial edit`. See [Commands](/docs/exyliahiteffect/commands).

<Callout type="note" title="Older clients get the inventory menu">
The 3D selector needs a 1.21.4 client or newer. A player on an older client gets the inventory menu instead, with nothing to configure.
</Callout>

### Does the menu have a "screen" style?

Not in this plugin's own setting: `menu.style` only accepts `CLASSIC` and `SPATIAL`. ExyliaHitEffect does ship a full-screen version of the effect menu (`screens/effects.html`), which ExyliaLib's `SCREEN` menu style draws for players whose client can show screens. That style is chosen in ExyliaLib, not here: see [Screens](/docs/exylialib/screens) and [Menus](/docs/exylialib/menus). A screen file that cannot be read keeps the inventory menu.

### Favourites

The first tab is the player's own shortlist. Starring is free: a player does not need to own an effect to star it, so a lapsed rank does not wipe the list. Stars stay in the order they were added. Locked favourites are still drawn on this tab even on a server that hides locked effects elsewhere. To empty it, Shift-click the tab while it is open (it asks first) or run `/hiteffect favourites clear`. Players need `exyliahiteffect.favourites`.

### Show only what players own

The **Filter** button (top of the window) switches the grid between every effect and only the ones the player owns. The choice is remembered like the open tab. To drop locked effects from the grid for everyone, set `behaviour.hide-without-permission: true` (default `false`); the favourites tab still shows them.

### Random effect

The **Random effect** button makes every hit play a different effect, drawn from the ones the player owns. Players need `exyliahiteffect.random`.

### Let players buy locked effects

With `shop.enabled` on and an economy installed, a locked effect with a price shows its price and a left-click asks the player to confirm, charges them and unlocks it. Players without `exyliahiteffect.buy` see the locked effect with no price. Set prices and currencies in [Configuration](/docs/exyliahiteffect/configuration#shop); economies are covered in [ExyliaLib economy](/docs/exylialib/economy).

### Change how the menu looks

The window is `plugins/ExyliaHitEffect/lang/<code>/menus/effects.yml`: titles, icons and lore use [ExyliaLib colours](/docs/exylialib/colours), and the words are translated per language, see [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages). Edits are kept across updates. The general rules for menu files are in [Menus](/docs/exylialib/menus). The 3D layout is `spatial/effects.yml`, and its words are under `spatial` in `messages.yml`.

<Details title="Technical details">
Fixed buttons: **Your effect** at slot `3` (click to take the effect off), **Filter** at `4`, **Crate** at `5`, **Particle visibility** at `48`, **Close** at `49`, **Random effect** at `50`. Categories sit at slots `9,18,27,36` (paged at `0` and `45`), the grid at `11-16,20-25,29-34,38-43` (paged at `47` and `52`). An effect a player plays because their rank gives it carries `menu.default-marker` (see `behaviour.defaults` in [Configuration](/docs/exyliahiteffect/configuration)). Effects the player may use are drawn first, then locked ones. The menu redraws whatever windows a player has open when their row changes (their click, a command, an admin, or an expired rank).

Extra tokens not in the tables below: placeholders `%effect_default%`, `%current_default%`, `%filter_status%`, template `none_owned_template` (what a tab shows when the filter leaves nothing) and action `hiteffect:filter`.

### Templates

Each list picks a template per row. `item_template` is the plain state and must stay first.

| Section | Template | Used when |
|---|---|---|
| `categories` | `item_template` | Another tab is open. |
| | `open_template` | This is the tab being looked at. |
| | `favourites_template` | The shortlist tab, while another tab is open. |
| | `favourites_open_template` | The shortlist tab while it is open — the only one carrying the "empty the list" action. |
| `grid` | `item_template` | An effect the player owns. |
| | `selected_template` | The effect currently worn. |
| | `locked_template` | An effect they do not own. |
| | `purchasable_template` | An effect they do not own that the shop sells, while an economy can take its price. |
| | `empty_template` | Drawn on the favourites tab when nothing is starred. |

These are the menu's own tokens, filled in while the screen is drawn. They are not PlaceholderAPI

| `%category_name%` | `categories` | The tab's display name. |
| `%category_material%` | `categories` | The material its icon is drawn with. |
| `%category_total%` | `categories` | How many effects the tab holds. |
| `%category_owned%` | `categories` | How many of those the player owns. |
| `%category_status%` | `categories` | `menu.tab-open` or `menu.tab-closed`, depending on whether this is the tab being looked at. |
| `%effect_id%` | `grid` | The effect's id. |
| `%effect_name%` | `grid` | Its display name, with its colours. |
| `%effect_material%` | `grid` | The material it is drawn with. |

### Actions

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| `hiteffect:open` | Opens the screen. |
| `hiteffect:category <id>` | Switches tab. `favourites` is a valid id. |
| `hiteffect:choose <id>` | Wears an effect, or takes it off when it is already worn. |
| `hiteffect:clear` | Takes the effect off. |
| `hiteffect:favourite <id>` | Stars or unstars. |
| `hiteffect:favourites_clear` | Empties the shortlist. |
| `hiteffect:buy <id>` | Asks the player to confirm, then buys a locked effect the shop sells. |
| `hiteffect:preview <id>` | Previews an effect and reopens the menu afterwards. |
| `hiteffect:visibility` | Steps the viewer's visibility on by one. |
| `hiteffect:random` | Turns the random choice on. |
| `hiteffect:crate` | Opens the crate. |
| `hiteffect:crate_open <amount>` | Spends that many keys and opens that many reels. |

### Wording

The few strings one template draws for every row live in `messages.yml`, because no menu file can
spell them out row by row:

```yaml title="lang/<code>/messages.yml"
menu:
  empty: "{muted}Nothing"
  tab-open: "{success}✔ You are here"
  tab-closed: "{warning}➥ Click to open"
  weapons-any: "Any weapon"
  favourites-name: "Favourites"
  favourite-on: "{highlight}★ In your favourites"
  favourite-off: "{muted}☆ Not in your favourites"
  random-name: "{highlight}Random"
  random-on: "{success}✔ Every hit is a surprise"
  random-off: "{warning}➥ Click to surprise yourself"
```
