title: "The menu"
description: "The inventory screen and the 3D selector: the tabs, the grid, the favourites, and every template menus/effects.yml declares."
---

`/hiteffect` opens one of two selectors, picked by `menu.style` in `config.yml`: `CLASSIC` (the
default) is the inventory window below, `SPATIAL` the [3D selector](#the-3d-selector).
`/hiteffect classic` and `/hiteffect 3d` open either one whatever the setting says.

The inventory menu is one window of 54 slots. Nothing in it touches a weapon — an effect gets onto a
weapon through its token, which is the thing a server sells.

<Callout type="note" title="It refuses to open in weapon mode">
With `behaviour.mode: weapon` nothing on this screen would ever be read, so the menu answers
`menu-disabled` instead of opening a window that changes nothing.
</Callout>

## The 3D selector

The same catalogue, drawn in the world in front of the player on ExyliaLib's centre stage instead of
in a chest window. The categories run along the top as tabs, the effect being looked at is named over
a pedestal where a mannequin takes the hits, the effects stand in an arc round it, and under them sit
one primary button and a row of icons.

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| The arc | Look at an effect to read about it, click it to try it on the mannequin. Trying costs nothing and changes nothing yet. |
| **EQUIP** | Puts the effect being tried on their hits. On a locked effect for sale it reads **BUY**, then **CONFIRM** with the price, and the second click pays and equips. |
| The icons | Favourite, unequip, revert to what they wore, random, replay, the crate, help and leave. The one looked at says what it does. |
| The tabs | Filter the arc by category, with the favourites (`★`) and every effect at once. |

Owning, wearing and buying go through the same rules as the inventory menu, permissions included, so
the two screens never disagree about what a player has: without `exyliahiteffect.preview` clicking an
effect still picks it, but the mannequin stays still, and without `exyliahiteffect.favourites` the ★ tab
answers `feature-locked` in either screen. A locked effect is marked with a cross (`✘`); the padlock used before 1.6.1 has no glyph in
the vanilla font and drew as a box. Locked lines under `spatial.stage` still as shipped are changed on
update, edited ones are left alone. Closing on an effect that was tried but not
saved says so and keeps what they wore. The first open plays a short guided tour; the **?** icon, or
`/hiteffectsadmin spatial tour <player>`, shows it again.

<Callout type="note" title="Older clients get the inventory menu">
The 3D selector needs a 1.21.4 client or newer. A player on an older client who opens it, through the
command, `menu.style` or a placed spot, gets the inventory menu instead, with nothing to configure.
</Callout>

Where every piece stands is `spatial/effects.yml`; every word on it is under `spatial` in
`messages.yml`, so it follows the server's language. Move pieces in game with
`/hiteffectsadmin spatial edit`, and leave selectors in the world for players to click with
`/hiteffectsadmin spatial place` (see [Commands](/docs/exyliahiteffect/commands#spatial-place)).

## The two lists

| Section | Slots | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `categories` | `9,18,27,36` | The tabs down the left, paged with the buttons at slots `0` and `45`. |
| `grid` | `11-16,20-25,29-34,38-43` | The open category's effects, paged at slots `47` and `52`. |

The fixed buttons: **YOUR EFFECT** at slot `3` (click to take the effect off), **EFFECT CRATE** at
`5`, **PARTICLE VISIBILITY** at `48`, **CLOSE** at `49`, **RANDOM EFFECT** at `50`.

## The owned-only filter and the screen style

A filter button switches the grid between every effect and only the ones the player owns (`menu.filter-all` / `menu.filter-owned` in `messages.yml`; the choice is remembered like the open tab). An effect a player plays because their rank gives it, not because they picked it, carries `menu.default-marker` (see `behaviour.defaults` in [Configuration](/docs/exyliahiteffect/configuration)).

Where ExyliaLib's screen menu style applies, `/hiteffect` opens one native screen instead of the inventory: `screens/effects.html` with `screens/theme.css` (words in `lang/<code>/screens/effects.yml`). The effect under the cursor plays live in a window on the screen, and the hints show the player's own sprint and sneak keys. A screen file that cannot be read keeps the chest menu above. `/exylialib screens check` lists what is wrong.

## The favourites tab

The first tab is always the player's own shortlist — it is not a category in `effects.yml`, because it
holds different effects for every player. It is named by `menu.favourites-name` in `messages.yml` and
drawn as `menu.favourites-icon` in `config.yml`.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Starring is free | Owning an effect is not required to star it. A star that vanished the day a rank lapsed would lose them the list they built. |
| Their order, not yours | Starred effects stay in the order they were starred. Nothing is re-sorted. |
| Locked rows still show | On this tab a locked effect is drawn even on a server that hides locked effects everywhere else. |
| Emptying it | Shift click the tab while it is open (it asks to confirm first), or `/hiteffect favourites clear`. |

An empty shortlist draws `empty_template` so the tab reads as empty rather than as a screen that
failed to load.

## The random choice

The button at slot `50` is not an effect: it says "surprise me". A player carrying it plays a
different effect on every hit, drawn afresh out of the ones they own — nothing is remembered between
hits, so it is a surprise every time rather than a shuffled order. Its line is `menu.random-on` while
it is the choice being carried and `menu.random-off` while it is not.

## The crate

The button at slot `5` opens `menus/crate.yml`: one key in, one effect out, weighted by rarity. It
draws how many keys the player holds and how much of the catalogue they have unboxed. See
[The crate](/docs/exyliahiteffect/crate).

## What a click does

| Click | On a grid row |
|---|---|
| Left | Wears the effect — or takes it off, when it is already the one worn. On a locked row for sale, buys it. |
| Right | Previews it on the server's preview stage, set with `/hiteffectsadmin preview location`: the effect plays for them alone, and they are put back where they were. Locked rows can be previewed too. |
| Shift | Stars it, or unstars it. |

## Ordering and locked rows

Effects the player may use are drawn first, then the locked ones — so what they can use is never
behind a page of rows they cannot. With `behaviour.hide-without-permission: true` the locked ones are
dropped from the grid entirely, except on the favourites tab.

## Buying a locked effect

With `shop.enabled` on and an economy installed, a locked effect that has a price is drawn with
`purchasable_template` instead of `locked_template`: the same row with its price on it. A left click
asks the player to confirm, charges them and unlocks it, and the menu comes back with the row now
theirs. A player without `exyliahiteffect.buy` sees the locked row with no price at all. Prices and currencies are set in [Configuration](/docs/exyliahiteffect/configuration#shop).

## Templates

Each list picks a template per row. `item_template` is the plain state and must stay first.

| Section | Template | Used when |
|---|---|---|
| `categories` | `item_template` | Another tab is open. |
| | `open_template` | This is the tab being looked at. |
| | `favourites_template` | The shortlist tab, while another tab is open. |
| | `favourites_open_template` | The shortlist tab while it is open — the only one carrying the "empty the list" action. |
| `grid` | `item_template` | An effect the player owns. |
| | `selected_template` | The effect currently worn. |
| | `locked_template` | An effect they do not own. |
| | `purchasable_template` | An effect they do not own that the shop sells, while an economy can take its price. |
| | `empty_template` | Drawn on the favourites tab when nothing is starred. |

## Placeholders

These are the menu's own tokens, filled in while the screen is drawn. They are not PlaceholderAPI
placeholders and only work inside this file.

| Placeholder | Where | What it draws |
|---|---|---|
| `%category_id%` | `categories` | The tab's id — `favourites` on the shortlist tab. |
| `%category_name%` | `categories` | The tab's display name. |
| `%category_material%` | `categories` | The material its icon is drawn with. |
| `%category_total%` | `categories` | How many effects the tab holds. |
| `%category_owned%` | `categories` | How many of those the player owns. |
| `%category_status%` | `categories` | `menu.tab-open` or `menu.tab-closed`, depending on whether this is the tab being looked at. |
| `%effect_id%` | `grid` | The effect's id. |
| `%effect_name%` | `grid` | Its display name, with its colours. |
| `%effect_material%` | `grid` | The material it is drawn with. |
| `%effect_description%` | `grid` | Its description from `effects.yml`. |
| `%effect_weapons%` | `grid` | What it may be bound to, in words. |
| `%effect_favourite%` | `grid` | `menu.favourite-on` or `menu.favourite-off`, depending on whether it is starred. |
| `%effect_tier%` | `grid` | The rarity's name, in the rarity's colour. |
| `%effect_tier_id%` | `grid` | The rarity's id. |
| `%effect_tier_color%` | `grid` | That colour on its own. |
| `%effect_price%` | `grid` | What it sells for, with its currency. Empty when it is not for sale. |
| `%current_effect%` | Anywhere | The effect the player is wearing, `menu.random-name` on random, or `menu.empty`. |
| `%random_status%` | Anywhere | `menu.random-on` or `menu.random-off`. |
| `%favourites_count%` | Anywhere | How many effects they have starred. |
| `%keys%` | Anywhere | Crate keys they hold. |
| `%unlocked_count%` | Anywhere | How many effects they have won from the crate. |
| `%catalogue_count%` | Anywhere | How many effects the server declares. |
| `%particle_visibility%` | Anywhere | The player's current visibility mode, named as `particles.name-*` says. |
| `%visibility_all%` | Anywhere | The name of the "everyone" mode, for a button that offers it. |
| `%visibility_none%` | Anywhere | The name of the "nobody" mode. |
| `%visibility_self_only%` | Anywhere | The name of the "only me" mode. |
| `%visibility_others_only%` | Anywhere | The name of the "only others" mode. |

## Actions

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| `hiteffect:open` | Opens the screen. |
| `hiteffect:category <id>` | Switches tab. `favourites` is a valid id. |
| `hiteffect:choose <id>` | Wears an effect, or takes it off when it is already worn. |
| `hiteffect:clear` | Takes the effect off. |
| `hiteffect:favourite <id>` | Stars or unstars. |
| `hiteffect:favourites_clear` | Empties the shortlist. |
| `hiteffect:buy <id>` | Asks the player to confirm, then buys a locked effect the shop sells. |
| `hiteffect:preview <id>` | Previews an effect and reopens the menu afterwards. |
| `hiteffect:visibility` | Steps the viewer's visibility on by one. |
| `hiteffect:random` | Turns the random choice on. |
| `hiteffect:crate` | Opens the crate. |
| `hiteffect:crate_open <amount>` | Spends that many keys and opens that many reels. |

## Wording

The few strings one template draws for every row live in `messages.yml`, because no menu file can
spell them out row by row:

```yaml title="lang/<code>/messages.yml"
menu:
  empty: "{muted}Nothing"
  tab-open: "{success}✔ You are here"
  tab-closed: "{warning}➥ Click to open"
  weapons-any: "Any weapon"
  favourites-name: "Favourites"
  favourite-on: "{highlight}★ In your favourites"
  favourite-off: "{muted}☆ Not in your favourites"
  random-name: "{highlight}Random"
  random-on: "{success}✔ Every hit is a surprise"
  random-off: "{warning}➥ Click to surprise yourself"
```

<Callout type="tip" title="The window follows the choice">
Every way a player's row can change — their own click, a command, an admin, or a rank that ran out
under them — redraws whatever windows they have open. Nothing has to be closed and reopened.
</Callout>
