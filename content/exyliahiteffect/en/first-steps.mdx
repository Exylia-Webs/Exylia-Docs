At the end of this page you wear an effect, you have hit something and seen it play, and one rank can use a whole category.

## Before you start

Install the plugin and make sure ExyliaLib and `packetevents` are loaded: see [Install ExyliaHitEffect](/docs/exyliahiteffect/installation). Operators own every effect (`exyliahiteffect.effect.*` defaults to op), so you can test right away.

## Wear an effect and hit something

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Run `/hiteffect`. The categories are the tabs down the left, the effects of the open tab fill the grid, and the first tab is your favourites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wear an effect">
    Left-click an effect. Right-click it to preview it instead. The menu shows a lock on effects you do not own, and a price on the ones you can buy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hit something">
    Hit another player or a zombie. The effect plays on the body of whoever you hit. Hitting a cow plays nothing: by default only `PLAYER`, `MANNEQUIN` and `ZOMBIE` count. To change it, see [Configuration](/docs/exyliahiteffect/configuration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it off">
    Click the **Your effect** button in the menu, or run `/hiteffect clear`. Nothing plays until you pick another one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Let a rank use it

Players open `/hiteffect` by default, but they own no effect yet. Give a rank one category:

```text
/lp group vip permission set exyliahiteffect.category.impact true
```

A category holds fifteen effects. The categories are `impact`, `crimson`, `ember`, `sculk`, `prism` and `goofy`. For a single effect use `exyliahiteffect.effect.crit_spark`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliahiteffect/permissions).

## Put an effect on a weapon

By default players can both pick an effect in the menu and carry one on a weapon. See [Modes and tokens](/docs/exyliahiteffect/modes-and-tokens).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give a token">
    Run `/hiteffectsadmin item give <player> crit_spark 1`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop it onto a weapon">
    Pick the token up with the cursor and click it onto a weapon in your own inventory. The weapon keeps everything it had and a line with the effect appears under its lore.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it off again">
    Run `/hiteffectsadmin item remover <player>` and click the remover onto the weapon. It unbinds the effect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the preview stage

Previews need a place to play. Until you set one, right-clicking an effect or running `/hiteffect preview <effect>` tells the player previews are not set up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stand where previews should play">
    An empty room, a corner of the hub or a platform in the void. A preview always faces north.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the wizard">
    Run `/hiteffectsadmin preview location` and confirm in the wizard it opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

| Question | How to answer it |
|---|---|
| Does the effect play? | `/hiteffect preview <id>` plays it on the preview stage. |
| Is the effect on the weapon? | Its line is drawn under the weapon's own lore. |
| Did a permission take? | `/hiteffectsadmin player validate <player>` takes off what they may no longer use, now. |
| Did the catalogue load? | `/hiteffectsadmin list` prints every effect. |
