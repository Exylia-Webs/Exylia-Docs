## The menu path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Let players open the menu">
    ```text
    /lp group default permission set exyliahiteffect.command.hiteffect true
    ```
    Without this the catalogue cannot be opened at all — and a player who cannot see it cannot want
    anything in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell a category">
    ```text
    /lp group vip permission set exyliahiteffect.category.impact true
    ```
    Fifteen effects, one node.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    `/hiteffect`. The tabs are down the left, the effects beside them, and the first tab is the
    player's own favourites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one and hit something">
    Left click wears it. The effect plays on the body of whatever they hit — as long as its type is
    allowed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Only three victims count by default">
`effects.all-mobs` is `false` and `effects.allowed-mobs` lists `PLAYER`, `MANNEQUIN` and `ZOMBIE`.
Hitting a cow plays nothing, and that is the setting, not a bug.
</Callout>

## The weapon path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give a token">
    ```text
    /hiteffectsadmin give <player> crit_spark 1
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop it onto a weapon">
    Pick the token up with the cursor and click it onto a weapon **in your own inventory**. The
    weapon keeps everything it had; one value is written onto it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it off again">
    ```text
    /hiteffectsadmin remover <player>
    ```
    The remover unbinds the effect and hands the token back, so nothing is destroyed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking it works

| Question | How to answer it |
|---|---|
| Is the effect there? | `/hiteffect preview <id>` — it plays on the preview stage. |
| Does the weapon carry one? | Its line is drawn under the weapon's own lore. |
| Did a permission take? | `/hiteffectsadmin player validate <player>` takes off what they may no longer use, now. |
| Did the file load? | `/hiteffectsadmin list` prints every effect the catalogue declares. |
