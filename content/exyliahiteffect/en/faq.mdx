For problems that need a checklist start with [Common problems](/docs/exyliahiteffect/troubleshooting). These are the quick answers.

## Why does nothing play when a player hits somebody?

Check, in order: the victim is allowed (`effects.all-mobs` is `false` by default, so only `PLAYER`, `MANNEQUIN` and `ZOMBIE` count); the hit was not cancelled by another plugin; the player has an effect that the [mode](/docs/exyliahiteffect/modes-and-tokens) reads (in `weapon` the menu choice is ignored, in `player` a bound weapon is ignored); they still own it; and the world is not in `effects.disabled-worlds`. A player whose visibility is `NONE` sees nothing, including their own. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliahiteffect/configuration#effects).

## Why does the menu say hit effects are off?

`behaviour.mode` is `weapon`. On that server the menu would change nothing, so it refuses to open instead of pretending. Set `mode` to `player` or `both` to use the menu.

## Why did a player's effect disappear?

The permission or rank that granted it ran out, and a choice a player can no longer use is taken off instead of played. They get the rank default if you set one in `behaviour.defaults`, and when the node comes back they pick the effect again. Effects won from [the crate](/docs/exyliahiteffect/crate) or bought in the menu are unlocked on the account and stay.

## Does losing a permission break a bound weapon?

No. A bound weapon plays its effect for whoever holds it, and binding a token asks for no permission: holding the token is the proof. That is what makes a bound weapon worth trading.

## An arrow played the wrong effect

An arrow plays the effect resolved when it was fired, so switching weapons mid-flight changes nothing. An arrow this plugin never saw launched, for example after a reload, falls back to what the shooter holds when it lands.

## Effects stack on top of each other in a fight

An effect longer than about six tenths of a second is still playing when the next blow lands, which is why the shipped ones are short. If you wrote your own, shorten `life:` and use fewer lines. One player also starts at most five effects a second. See [Effects](/docs/exyliahiteffect/effects).

## Can I use a kill effect's sequence here?

The syntax is the same, so it loads, and it plays on every blow. Read the budget in [Effects](/docs/exyliahiteffect/effects) before you paste one in, and the shapes in [Sequences](/docs/exyliahiteffect/sequences).

## Can players hide each other's effects?

Yes. Each player picks `ALL`, `NONE`, `SELF_ONLY` or `OTHERS_ONLY` with `/hiteffect visibility` or in the menu. The server default is `particles.default-visibility`.

## Does the weapon lore get written to the item?

No. The lines are added to the copy that leaves the server, so any plugin reading the weapon finds the lore it always had, and renaming an effect updates every weapon at once. `weapon-lore.enabled` turns them off.

## Can I write my own effects?

Yes, in `effects.yml`; the in-game editor and the [web editor](/docs/exylialib/web-editor) check it before applying. The shapes and tokens are in [Sequences](/docs/exyliahiteffect/sequences). A line that cannot be read is reported on the console and skipped, and the rest of the effect still plays.

## Can I turn effects off in a region or a world?

Yes. List the world in `effects.disabled-worlds`, or deny the WorldGuard flag `hit-effects` (registered by ExyliaLib) in a region. Without WorldGuard the flag simply allows.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, the plugin declares Folia support.
