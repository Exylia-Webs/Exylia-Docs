Describe an effect in a paragraph, copy a ready-made prompt, send it to an AI and paste what comes back into your catalogue. The prompt already carries the step language, the budget and three shipped effects, so you only supply the idea.

## Before you start

Set the preview stage so you can watch the result, see [Your first hit effect](/docs/exyliahiteffect/first-steps). You do not need to know the step syntax, but it is documented in [Write your own effect steps](/docs/exyliahiteffect/sequences).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the idea">
    Write a paragraph: the real blocks and items it is made of (`GOLD_BLOCK`, `IRON_SWORD`), one gesture and which way it goes, the mood and the sound. It plays on every blow, so it must be two to five steps, under six tenths of a second and one sound.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the prompt">
    Type the idea in the box below, copy the prompt with your idea already inside it, paste it into the AI you use and send it.

    <AiPrompt id="hiteffect" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the answer">
    Open `plugins/ExyliaHitEffect/lang/<code>/effects.yml` and paste the block under `hit_effects:`, at the same indentation as the effects already there. Its `category` must be one that exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload and read the console">
    Run `/hiteffectsadmin reload`. A step that cannot be read is dropped and printed with the line that caused it: if nothing appears, every line was understood.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview it">
    Run `/hiteffect preview <id>` and watch it on the preview stage.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

`/hiteffectsadmin list` shows the new effect. The preview plays in a fraction of a second. Then test in a real fight, not on a dummy: twenty hits in five seconds is the test that matters. If the screen fills up, cut a step or halve a `life`.

## Going further

### Example of a good idea

"Glass. On the hit, six `GLASS_PANE` shards burst outwards from the chest, small, tumbling, shrinking to nothing in a third of a second. One flat `WHITE_STAINED_GLASS` ring flattens under them. A high, brittle break sound. Nothing falls to the ground, nothing lingers."

"Something cool" produces something generic; a paragraph produces the effect you pictured.

### Fix a wrong answer

The model can write a material that does not exist or a parameter a shape does not understand, and the console says so. A bad line is dropped and the rest of the effect still plays. Give the console error back to the AI and it usually fixes it in one turn.

### Who gets the effect

The key you paste becomes `exyliahiteffect.effect.<key>`, so nobody sees the new effect until they are granted it. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliahiteffect/permissions). A reload re-checks every player's choice against the catalogue, so an effect that stops existing is taken off whoever had it.
