Hand out keys, bind a block, and a player opens the crate and wins kill effects at the odds you set.

## Before you start

- `crate.enabled` is `true` in `config.yml` (the default).
- Players need `exyliakilleffect.crate` (granted by default). Without it they are told the feature is not part of their rank.
- To hand out weapon tokens (`crate.reward: ITEM` or `BOTH`), `behaviour.mode` must be `weapon` or `both`. See [Modes and tokens](/docs/exyliakilleffect/modes-and-tokens).

## Open your first crate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give yourself a key">
    Run `/killeffectsadmin keys give <player> 1`. The key is a number on the player's row, so nothing can be dropped or lost. For a physical key, run `/killeffectsadmin item key <player>` instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bind a block">
    Look at the block that should be the crate, run `/killeffectsadmin crate block add` and confirm in the wizard it opens. A bound block cannot be broken, blown up or pushed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Right click the block empty-handed, or run `/killeffect crate`. Pick how many keys to spend and the reels fall.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

| Question | How to answer it |
|---|---|
| Did the key arrive? | `/killeffectsadmin keys check <player>` prints how many they hold. |
| Is the block bound? | `/killeffectsadmin crate block list` prints every bound block. |
| Did a win land? | Chat shows the rarity and the effect, and the effect is now in the player's menu. |
| Did a duplicate refund? | Landing on an owned effect hands back `crate.duplicate-refund` keys (default 1). |

## Opening

`/killeffect crate`, the button in the corner of the effects menu, or a bound block clicked
empty-handed. Every way in needs `exyliakilleffect.crate`; a player without it is told the feature is
not part of their rank. The first screen asks only one thing: how many at once. Picking four spends four keys and drops four reels side by
side on the next screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="How many">
    `menus/crate.yml`. Choices are drawn up to `crate.max-at-once`, or the `<n>` of the highest
    `exyliakilleffect.crate.max.<n>` the player holds, capped at seven by the width of the screen, and spread the way the reels will be — where you click is where that reel falls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The reels fall">
    `menus/crate_open.yml`. One reel per crate, each running the full height of the chest, each with
    its own faces and its own finishing line `crate.stagger-seconds` after the one before it. Every
    face keeps its name and its lore the whole way down, so a reel can be read while it falls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What went past stays">
    Nothing is cleared when a reel stops. What it came within one slot of stays on screen, because
    half of what a crate is worth is the legendary that went past the line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paid out as each reel lands">
    Keys are spent and prizes decided before the first frame, but nothing is handed over until a
    reel actually stops on the line. Four crates opened at once pay out one at a time, `stagger`
    seconds apart, each with its own sound and its own line in chat. Closing the window mid-fall
    costs nothing — the reels stop being drawn and the prizes still land on their own clock. Leaving
    the server ends the timer, so a reel still in the air is settled on the way out: unlocked on the
    row now, and any token kept in the reward queue until their next join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The 3D opening

With `crate.opening: SPATIAL` the reels are replaced by a crate standing in the world in front of the
player: a reel slows onto the prize, the prize is revealed, and the effect plays as a miniature on a
mannequin that falls as the kill lands. Sneaking skips to the end and hands the prize over at once. The
prize is drawn exactly as in the inventory opening, keys are spent the same way, and its look is
`spatial/crate.yml`. Without packetevents, or on a client older than 1.21.4, the inventory opening is
used instead. A player without `exyliakilleffect.preview` sees the prize as its icon, with no mannequin.

## Going further

- **Change the odds.** Edit `chance` under `tiers` in `config.yml`. It is a weight, not a percentage. See [Rarities](#rarities).
- **Let ranks open more at once.** Raise `crate.max-at-once`, or grant `exyliakilleffect.crate.max.<n>` to a rank.
- **Give new players keys.** Set `crate.start-keys`.
- **Keep the effect on the weapon instead.** Set `crate.reward` to `ITEM` or `BOTH`.
- **Open in 3D.** Set `crate.opening: SPATIAL`, see [the 3D opening](#the-3d-opening).
- **Colours and texts.** [Colours](/docs/exylialib/colours) and [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages) cover the wording; [Menus](/docs/exylialib/menus) covers `menus/crate.yml` and `menus/crate_open.yml`.

### Rarities

Every effect declares a `tier` in `effects.yml`, and `config.yml` declares what the tiers are:

```yaml
tiers:
  common:
    name: "Common"
    material: GRAY_DYE
    color: "{muted}"
    chance: 60.0
    priority: 1
  rare:
    name: "Rare"
    material: LIGHT_BLUE_DYE
    color: "{info}"
    chance: 25.0
    priority: 2
  epic:
    name: "Epic"
    material: PINK_DYE
    color: "{accent}"
    chance: 12.0
    priority: 3
  legendary:
    name: "Legendary"
    material: ORANGE_DYE
    color: "{highlight}"
    chance: 3.0
    priority: 4
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `name` | What menus and placeholders call it. |
| `material` | The item the rarity is drawn with. |
| `color` | The colour its name and its effects are written in. |
| `chance` | How often a crate lands on it, **relative to the other rarities**. |
| `priority` | Where it sits in menus; lower comes first. |

Nothing here is hard-coded. Delete `legendary`, invent `mythic`, rename all four: the key is the id
`effects.yml` writes as `tier`, and an effect naming a rarity that no longer exists falls back to the
first one — it loses its colour, never its existence.

<Callout type="note" title="chance is a weight, not a percentage">
The shares shown in the menu are worked out from the weights, so four rarities weighted `60/25/12/3`
and four weighted `6/2.5/1.2/0.3` behave identically. Deleting a rarity re-spreads its odds across
the rest instead of leaving a three-percent hole.
</Callout>

### Keys

Two kinds, and the crate takes both.

#### On the row

A number on the player's own row. Nothing can be dropped, duped, or lost to a full inventory:

```text
/killeffectsadmin keys give <player> <amount>
/killeffectsadmin keys take <player> <amount>
/killeffectsadmin keys set <player> <amount>
/killeffectsadmin keys check <player>
```

Safe to run from console — a shop, a vote listener, a reward plugin or a crate on another plugin all
hand keys over the same way.

```text
[console] killeffectsadmin keys give %player% 1
```

#### In the hand

An item, so it can be sold at a shop stall, put in a kit, dropped, and given to a friend:

```text
/killeffectsadmin item key <player> [1-64]
```

Right click a bound crate block holding one and it opens on the spot: one key, one reel, no question
screen. The key leaves the hand the moment the reel starts falling. Left clicking never spends one —
that is how a player asks what a block is — and neither does right clicking anywhere else: a key
cannot be placed as a block, so it cannot be lost to a wall.

Its material is `key-item` in `config.yml`, its name and lore `key-item` in `messages.yml`. It is told apart by what it carries in its data rather
than by its name or material, so renaming one changes nothing and a lookalike opens nothing.

<Callout type="note" title="Which kind to sell">
An item is worth having when keys are traded between players or sold in a physical shop. A number on
the row is worth having when they are not: it cannot be scammed, lost on death, or duplicated by a
duping bug in another plugin. Servers that want both simply run both commands.
</Callout>

### Duplicates

Landing on something already owned hands keys back — as many as `crate.duplicate-refund` says. At the
default of `1` a duplicate is a free re-roll rather than a loss, which is what keeps a nearly complete
collection worth opening.

Under `crate.reward: ITEM` nothing is unlocked at all, so no opening is ever a duplicate and the
refund never comes into it: every key pays out a token.

### Settings

```yaml
crate:
  enabled: true
  start-keys: 0
  duplicate-refund: 1
  reward: UNLOCK
  blocks: []
  max-at-once: 4
  spin-frames: 34
  stagger-seconds: 2.0
  opening: INVENTORY
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `true` | Off, the button says so instead of opening. |
| `start-keys` | `0` | Keys a player is given the first time they are seen. |
| `duplicate-refund` | `1` | Keys handed back for something already owned. |
| `reward` | `UNLOCK` | What a crate hands over. `UNLOCK` keeps the effect on the account, `ITEM` hands over only its token, `BOTH` does both. `ITEM` and `BOTH` want `behaviour.mode` on `weapon` or `both`. |
| `blocks` | `[]` | The blocks that open the crate. Bind them in game, not by hand. |
| `max-at-once` | `4` | How many crates one player may open in one go. The screen fits seven. `exyliakilleffect.crate.max.<n>` raises it per rank. |
| `spin-frames` | `34` | How many faces the first reel runs through before it stops. Capped at 200, about eleven seconds. |
| `stagger-seconds` | `2.0` | How long after one reel stops the next one does. `0` stops them together. |
| `opening` | `INVENTORY` | `INVENTORY`: the reels in `menus/crate_open.yml`. `SPATIAL`: [the 3D opening](#the-3d-opening). |

`on-spin`, `on-win` and `on-duplicate` are full ExyliaLib effect blocks — title, action bar, boss bar,
sound, particle and firework — played on every falling tick, and then once per reel the moment it
lands: the win, or the duplicate instead when that reel stopped on something already owned.

### Crate blocks

A physical block, anywhere in the world, that opens the crate when it is clicked:

```text
/killeffectsadmin crate block add      # the block you are looking at
/killeffectsadmin crate block remove
/killeffectsadmin crate block list
/killeffectsadmin crate block clear
```

They are written to `config.yml`, not into the world, so a bound block survives a schematic paste, a
world edit and a rollback. While it is bound it cannot be broken, blown up or pushed away, and
clicking it no longer does what its material does — a crate drawn on a barrel must not open a barrel.

A block in a world that loads after the plugin — a hub a world manager brings up later — starts
answering the moment that world loads.

### What a crate hands over

Whatever comes out is written to the player's row rather than granted as a permission, so it outlives
a rank running out. `/killeffectsadmin unlocks grant|revoke` is the same door for a reward
plugin that wants to hand an effect over without a crate.

<Callout type="tip" title="Permissions and unlocks are two separate ways in">
A player may use an effect they own from a crate **or** one their rank grants. Selling a rarity as a
rank is one line — `exyliakilleffect.tier.legendary` — and it is independent of anything they unboxed.
</Callout>
