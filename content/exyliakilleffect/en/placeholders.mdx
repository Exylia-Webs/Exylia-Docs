ExyliaKillEffect registers its placeholders with PlaceholderAPI under `exyliakilleffect`, so install [PlaceholderAPI](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/placeholderapi.6245/) first. Use them in scoreboards, tab lists, holograms and menus. Clicking a placeholder below copies it.

<Placeholders />

## What the player wears

The `effect` family answers what plays when the player kills: what they picked in `/killeffect`, or the default their rank gives while they have not picked. A player who turned on random is not wearing one effect, so `%exyliakilleffect_effect%` answers `random`, `%exyliakilleffect_effect_name%` answers the text of `menu.random-name` in `messages.yml`, and `%exyliakilleffect_has_effect%` stays `true`.

The `weapon_` family answers the same fields for the weapon in the player's main hand.

## With nothing chosen

| Placeholder | Answer |
|---|---|
| `%exyliakilleffect_has_effect%` | `false` |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_description%` | Empty |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_material%` | `BARRIER` |
| `%exyliakilleffect_effect_tier_color%` | Empty |
| Everything else | The text of `placeholders.none` in `messages.yml`: `None` by default |

The same applies to the `weapon_` family when the weapon carries nothing, so they are safe in a scoreboard with no fallback:

```yaml
lines:
  - " {muted}❙ {letters}Effect: {highlight}%exyliakilleffect_effect_name%"
  - " {muted}❙ {letters}Weapon: {highlight}%exyliakilleffect_weapon_effect_name%"
```

<Callout type="note" title="Colours in placeholder answers">
`effect_tier_color` answers a colour code such as `{info}`, which ExyliaLib turns into a colour where the text is shown. See [Colours](/docs/exylialib/colours).
</Callout>
