## Nothing plays when a player kills

Check four things in order. The victim must be allowed: `effects.all-mobs` is `false` by default, so only `PLAYER`, `MANNEQUIN` and `ZOMBIE` count. An effect must exist for that kill: `mode: weapon` never reads the menu choice and `mode: player` never reads a bound weapon. The killer must still own the effect, and the observer must not be on `NONE`. The full checklist is in [Common problems](/docs/exyliakilleffect/troubleshooting#nothing-plays-when-i-kill-something).

## The menu says it is off

`behaviour.mode` is `weapon`. The menu would change nothing on that server, so it refuses to open. See [Modes and tokens](/docs/exyliakilleffect/modes-and-tokens).

## An effect I bound to a weapon vanished

The weapon stores the effect's id. If the key in `effects.yml` was renamed, that id names nothing any more and the effect does not play. Renaming a key is not free: it is also a different permission node.

## A player says their effect disappeared

Their permission for it went away, usually a rank expiring. A menu choice the player no longer owns is taken off instead of played, and picking it again works once the node is back.

## Does losing a permission break a weapon?

No. A bound weapon plays its effect for whoever holds it, which is what makes a bound weapon worth trading. See [Modes and tokens](/docs/exyliakilleffect/modes-and-tokens).

## Can players see each other's effects?

Each player decides for themselves: `ALL`, `NONE`, `SELF_ONLY` or `OTHERS_ONLY`, set with `/killeffect visibility` or from [the menu](/docs/exyliakilleffect/menu). A player on `NONE` sees nothing, not even their own.

## Are the bodies real entities?

No. `[RAGDOLL]` and `[NPC]` draw displays, so nothing can hit them, loot them or collide with them, and they disappear when their `life` runs out.

## Why is the body black, or made of blocks?

The body is waiting for its real skin. ExyliaLib gets each piece of a skin from [Exylia Skins](https://skins.exylia.net), free and with no key, and draws it in blocks of the nearest colour until it arrives; a piece any server already made arrives at once. If bodies never get their skin, check that the server can reach `https://skins-api.exylia.net` and that ExyliaLib is up to date. The setting is explained on [Ragdolls](/docs/exylialib/ragdolls#real-skins-ragdoll-skin-quality).

## Does the weapon lore get written to the item?

Never. It is added to the copy that leaves the server, so any plugin reading the weapon finds the lore it always had, and renaming an effect updates every weapon at once. Turn it off with `weapon-lore.enabled` in [Configuration](/docs/exyliakilleffect/configuration#weapon-lore).

## Can I write my own effects?

Yes, that is what `effects.yml` is for. [Sequences](/docs/exyliakilleffect/sequences) lists the shapes, `PIXELS` for pictures and words, and the display parameters; a line that cannot be read is reported on the console and skipped while the rest of the effect still plays. [Create with AI](/docs/exyliakilleffect/create-with-ai) shows a faster way to start.

## Effects lag during big fights

Displays are animated by the client, so they are cheap to move, but every line is still a packet per viewer. Look at how many lines an effect has and at `count:` on particle lines; `ticks:` spreads a shape over frames instead of sending it at once. One player also starts at most three effects a second.

## Can a region turn effects off?

Yes. ExyliaLib registers a WorldGuard flag, `kill-effects`: denying it in a region stops effects playing where somebody died inside it. Without WorldGuard the flag simply allows. To switch a whole world off use `disabled-worlds` in [Configuration](/docs/exyliakilleffect/configuration#disabled-worlds-and-random-worlds).

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, the plugin declares Folia support.
