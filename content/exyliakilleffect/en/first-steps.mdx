At the end of this page you wear a kill effect, you have killed something and seen it play where it fell, and one rank can use a whole category.

## Before you start

Install the plugin and make sure ExyliaLib and `packetevents` are loaded: see [Install ExyliaKillEffect](/docs/exyliakilleffect/installation). Operators own every effect (`exyliakilleffect.effect.*` defaults to op), so you can test right away. You need a second target to kill: a zombie, or another player.

## Wear an effect and kill something

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Run `/killeffect`. The categories are the tabs down the left, the effects of the open tab fill the grid, and the first tab is your favourites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wear an effect">
    Left-click an effect, for example **HIT THE GRIDDY** in the Meme tab. Right-click it to preview it instead. The menu shows a lock on effects you do not own and a price on the ones you can buy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kill something">
    Kill a zombie or another player. The effect plays where the victim fell, and the player who made the kill is part of the scene. Killing a cow plays nothing: by default only `PLAYER`, `MANNEQUIN` and `ZOMBIE` count. To change it, see [Configuration](/docs/exyliakilleffect/configuration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it off">
    Click the **Your effect** button in the menu, or run `/killeffect clear`. Nothing plays until you pick another one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Let a rank use it

Players open `/killeffect` by default, but they own no effect yet. Give a rank one category:

```text
/lp group vip permission set exyliakilleffect.category.meme true
```

The categories are `meme` (30 effects), `void`, `cataclysm`, `lovestruck` and `cosmic` (20 each). For a single effect use `exyliakilleffect.effect.griddy`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliakilleffect/permissions).

## Put an effect on a weapon

By default players can both pick an effect in the menu and carry one on a weapon. See [Modes and tokens](/docs/exyliakilleffect/modes-and-tokens).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give a token">
    Run `/killeffectsadmin item give <player> griddy 1`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop it onto a weapon">
    Pick the token up with the cursor and click it onto a weapon in your own inventory. The weapon keeps everything it had and a line with the effect appears under its lore.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it off again">
    Run `/killeffectsadmin item remover <player>` and click the remover onto the weapon. It unbinds the effect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the preview stage

Previews need a place to play. Until you set one, right-clicking an effect or running `/killeffect preview <effect>` tells the player previews are not set up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stand where previews should play">
    An empty room, a corner of the hub or a platform in the void.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the wizard">
    Run `/killeffectsadmin preview location` and confirm in the wizard it opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

| Question | How to answer it |
|---|---|
| Does the effect play? | `/killeffect preview griddy` plays it on the preview stage. |
| Is the effect on the weapon? | Its line is drawn under the weapon's own lore. |
| Did a permission take? | `/killeffectsadmin player validate <player>` takes off what they may no longer use, now. |
| Did the catalogue load? | `/killeffectsadmin list` prints every effect. |
