The 3D selector is the effect menu drawn in the world in front of the player instead of in a chest: categories along the top, effects on an arc at eye level, and the one being tried playing as a miniature on a mannequin in the middle. Players try, wear, favourite and buy effects without an inventory window.

## Before you start

- Players need a Minecraft 1.21.4 client or newer. Anyone on an older client who asks for the selector gets the inventory menu instead, so there is nothing to configure for them.
- `behaviour.mode` must not be `weapon`: on a weapon-only server the selector, like the [menu](/docs/exyliakilleffect/menu), answers that it is disabled.
- Previews of effects play on the selector's own mannequin; the preview stage from [Your first kill effect](/docs/exyliakilleffect/first-steps) is not needed.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Try it yourself">
    Run `/killeffect 3d` (alias `/killeffect showroom`). The selector appears in front of you. Look at an effect to read about it, click one to play it on the mannequin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it the default">
    In `config.yml` set `menu.style: SPATIAL`, then run `/killeffectsadmin reload`. From now on `/killeffect` opens the selector, and `/killeffect classic` still opens the [inventory menu](/docs/exyliakilleffect/menu).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place one in the world (optional)">
    Stand where players should use it and run `/killeffectsadmin spatial place`. It leaves a small clickable hologram. A player who clicks it is walked onto the spot and the selector opens facing the way the spot faces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a spot">
    Stand within four blocks of it and run `/killeffectsadmin spatial remove`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Open it with `/killeffect 3d`. You should see the category tabs, an arc of effects, a mannequin in the middle and a primary button. Click an effect: it plays as a draft and nothing is worn yet. Press **EQUIP**, then kill a mob and see the effect play. The first time a player opens it a short guided tour points out the arc, the preview, the button and the icons.

## Going further

### How players use it

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tabs | The categories as plain words. The mouse wheel steps through them. |
| The arc | Four effects either side of the preview. The wheel or the arrows turn a page; owned effects come first. |
| Looking at an effect | Its name, rarity and status show above the arc, its description once the crosshair rests on it. |
| Clicking an effect | Tries it on the mannequin. Nothing is worn yet. |
| Primary button | **EQUIP** wears the effect being tried. On a locked effect the shop sells it reads **BUY · price**, then **CONFIRM · price** on the second click that pays. Otherwise **LOCKED**. |
| Icons | Favourite, unequip, revert the draft, random choice, replay, crate, the guided tour again, and leave. The words of the one looked at show under it. |

Closing on an effect that was tried but not saved drops it, and the player is told. The `?` icon shows the tour again. Make it show on a player's next open with `/killeffectsadmin spatial tour <player>`.

### Permissions

The same [permissions](/docs/exyliakilleffect/permissions) apply as in the menu: opening needs `exyliakilleffect.command.killeffect`, the crate icon `exyliakilleffect.crate`, random `exyliakilleffect.random`, a new star `exyliakilleffect.favourites`, and a price is shown only with `exyliakilleffect.buy`. Without `exyliakilleffect.preview` clicking an effect still picks it, but the mannequin stays still.

### Choose which side an effect is seen from

`preview.effect-facing: AUTO` (in `spatial/effects.yml`) watches an effect's first play and turns it so its motion runs across the view, never showing the victim's back. One effect can set its own side with `preview-facing: AUTO`, `FRONT`, `SIDE` or degrees in [the catalogue](/docs/exyliakilleffect/effects).

### Move the pieces

Run `/killeffectsadmin spatial edit`: the selector opens for you to aim at a piece, click it and move it with the mouse wheel. The result is saved in `spatial/effects.yml`.

### Open the crate in 3D too

`crate.opening: SPATIAL` plays the crate in front of the player, see [Set up the crate](/docs/exyliakilleffect/crate#opening).

<Details title="Technical details: spatial/effects.yml, words, commands">

Where every piece stands is `plugins/ExyliaKillEffect/spatial/effects.yml`. Every word it draws is under `spatial` in `lang/<code>/messages.yml` (see [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages)). Coordinates are blocks on a board standing `anchor.distance` (4.5) in front of the player's eyes, and every line is drawn at least as large as `theme.min-glyph-degrees` reads from there.

| Block | What it sets |
|---|---|
| `anchor` | How far ahead the board stands and how high it is lifted, so its lowest piece stays above the floor. |
| `theme` | Text sizes, the page arrows, the locked marker `✘` and the equipped marker `✔`. |
| `panels` | The tabs, the title, the description, the primary button, the icon row and the hint line. |
| `preview` | The mannequin: its size, `effect-scale` (`0.44`), the skins it wears (`[others, viewer]` by default) and `effect-facing`. |
| `carousel` | The arc: how many effects (`slots: 8`), their spacing and curve. |
| `intro` | The guided tour's steps. |
| `backdrop` | The soft dark backdrop behind the board, so it reads on sky and ground alike. |

Spots are stored in `spatial/spots.yml`. Every way in asks for `exyliakilleffect.command.killeffect`.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/killeffect 3d` | Always the 3D selector. |
| `/killeffect classic` | Always the inventory menu. |
| `/killeffectsadmin spatial place` | Sets a selector down where you stand, facing the way you face. |
| `/killeffectsadmin spatial remove` | Removes the placed selector nearest you, up to four blocks away. |
| `/killeffectsadmin spatial edit` | Opens the selector to move its pieces. |
| `/killeffectsadmin spatial tour <player>` | Shows the guided tour to a player on their next open. |

`/killeffectsadmin` commands need `exyliakilleffect.admin`. A locked effect is marked with `✘`; the padlock used before 1.9.1 had no glyph in the vanilla font and drew as a box.

</Details>
