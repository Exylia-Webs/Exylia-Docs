---
title: "The 3D selector"
description: "The centre stage in the world: browse, try, buy and wear effects without an inventory window."
---

The 3D selector is the same catalogue as [the menu](/docs/exyliakilleffect/menu), drawn in the world in
front of the player instead of in a chest. The categories run along the top, the effects on an arc at eye
level, and the one being tried plays as a miniature on a mannequin in the middle. Owning, wearing and
buying go through the same code as the inventory menu, so the two screens never disagree about what a
player has.

## Opening it

| How | What opens |
|---|---|
| `/killeffect 3d` (alias `showroom`) | Always the 3D selector. |
| `/killeffect classic` | Always the inventory menu. |
| `/killeffect` | Whichever `menu.style` in `config.yml` names: `CLASSIC` (default) or `SPATIAL`. The API's `openMenu` and `/killeffectsadmin menu open` follow the same setting. |
| A placed spot | `/killeffectsadmin spatial place` leaves a small clickable hologram where you stand. Clicking it walks the player onto the spot and opens the selector there, facing the way the spot faces. Spots are kept in `spatial/spots.yml`. |

Every way in asks for `exyliakilleffect.command.killeffect`, and on a `weapon` server it answers
`menu-disabled` exactly as the inventory menu does.

<Callout type="note" title="Older clients get the classic menu">
Drawing it needs a 1.21.4 client or newer. A player on an older client who asks for the 3D selector is
shown the inventory menu instead, with nothing to configure.
</Callout>

## Using it

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tabs | The categories, as plain words. The mouse wheel steps through them. |
| The arc | Four effects either side of the preview. The wheel or the arrows turn it a page; owned effects come first. |
| Looking at an effect | Its name, rarity and status show above the arc, and its description once the crosshair rests on it. |
| Clicking an effect | Tries it: it plays on the mannequin as a draft, nothing is worn yet. |
| The primary button | **EQUIP** wears the effect being tried. On a locked effect the shop sells it reads **BUY · price**, then **CONFIRM · price** for the second click that pays. Otherwise **LOCKED**. |
| The icons | Favourite, unequip, revert the draft, random choice, replay, crate, the guided tour again, and leave. The words of the one looked at show under it. |

Closing on an effect that was tried but not saved drops it, and the player is told so. The first time a
player opens the selector a short guided tour points out the arc, the preview, the primary button and the
icons; the `?` icon shows it again, and `/killeffectsadmin spatial tour <player>` makes it show on their
next open.

The same [permissions](/docs/exyliakilleffect/permissions) apply as in the inventory menu: the crate icon
needs `exyliakilleffect.crate`, random needs `exyliakilleffect.random`, a new star needs
`exyliakilleffect.favourites`, and a price is only shown to a player holding `exyliakilleffect.buy`.

## The layout

Where every piece stands is `spatial/effects.yml`; every word it draws is under `spatial` in
`lang/<code>/messages.yml`. Coordinates are blocks on a board standing `anchor.distance` (4.5) in front of
the player's eyes, and every line is drawn at least as large as `theme.min-glyph-degrees` reads from there.

| Block | What it sets |
|---|---|
| `anchor` | How far ahead the board stands and how high it is lifted, so its lowest piece stays above the floor. |
| `theme` | Text sizes, the page arrows, the locked and equipped markers. |
| `panels` | The tabs, the title, the description, the primary button, the icon row and the hint line. |
| `preview` | The mannequin: its size, `effect-scale` (`0.44`), the skins it wears (`[others, defaults]`) and `effect-facing`. |
| `carousel` | The arc: how many effects, their spacing and curve. |
| `intro` | The guided tour's steps. |
| `backdrop` | The soft dark backdrop behind the board, so it reads on sky and ground alike. |

Pieces are moved in game rather than by hand: `/killeffectsadmin spatial edit` opens the selector for an
admin to aim at a piece, click it and move it with the mouse wheel.

### The side an effect is seen from

`preview.effect-facing: AUTO` watches an effect's first play and turns it so its motion runs across the
view, never showing the victim's back. One effect can override it in `effects.yml`:

```yaml title="effects.yml"
kill_effects:
  GRIDDY:
    preview-facing: SIDE   # AUTO, FRONT, SIDE or degrees
```

## Admin commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `spatial place` | Sets a 3D selector down where you stand, facing the way you face. |
| `spatial remove` | Removes the placed selector nearest you, up to four blocks away. |
| `spatial edit` | Opens the selector to move its pieces. |
| `spatial tour <player>` | Shows the guided tour to a player again on their next open. |

All under `/killeffectsadmin`, so all need `exyliakilleffect.admin`.

<Callout type="tip" title="The crate has a 3D opening too">
`crate.opening: SPATIAL` plays the crate in the world in front of the player. See
[The crate](/docs/exyliakilleffect/crate#opening).
</Callout>
