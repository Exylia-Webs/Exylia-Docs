In about ten minutes your Tebex store feeds the dashboard, so you can see what the server earns, who pays, which
packages sell, and what a new player is worth. Revenue does not come from the agent: your Tebex store sends each
payment to the dashboard as a signed webhook, and the dashboard matches it to a player by their Minecraft UUID. That
is what lets revenue, lifetime value and campaign ROI use real payments.

## Before you start

- A Tebex store and access to its creator panel.
- A workspace member with **Connect and disconnect integrations** (`manage_integrations`) to connect it.
- To read the page, **Revenue** (`view_revenue`); the Viewer role is seeded without it. See
  [Team and settings](/docs/exyliaanalytics/team).
- Your store's currency set under Settings: amounts are shown in it.

## Steps

On **Integrations**, open Tebex.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a webhook endpoint in Tebex">
    In the Tebex creator panel, open **Integrations → Webhooks → Endpoints**, add an endpoint and copy its **webhook
    secret**. Enable `payment.completed`, `payment.refunded`, the `payment.dispute.*` events and, for subscriptions,
    `recurring-payment.started`, `recurring-payment.renewed`, `recurring-payment.cancellation` and
    `recurring-payment.ended`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the secret into the dashboard">
    Paste it into **Webhook secret** and click **Connect Tebex**. It is stored encrypted and never shown again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the endpoint URL into Tebex">
    The dashboard shows the URL to use. Tebex sends a validation request first; the status turns to **Receiving**
    with the first payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import past payments (optional)">
    Under **Import past payments (optional)**, add a **Game server secret key** (Tebex: **Game Servers →** your
    server **→ secret key**) and click **Import past payments**. The dashboard imports every payment Tebex still
    lists. Importing again never duplicates a payment. Refunds imported this way are dated on the payment's day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Buyer names, e-mail addresses, IPs and postal codes are dropped before anything is stored. **Disconnect Tebex** stops
new payments; the ones already stored stay.

## Check that it works

Open **Revenue** (the **Store** tab). Once a payment has arrived, **Revenue after refunds** and **Paying players** fill
in, and **Every payment** lists it with its player. If **Integrations** shows **Waiting for Tebex**, send a test event
from the endpoint in Tebex, then run the check on the page again. Before you connect, the page shows **Connect your
Tebex store**.

## Going further

### The Revenue page

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| Over time | Revenue over time per period with refunds and chargebacks below the line, the running total, and buyers and payments. |
| Subscriptions and one-off purchases | Money from recurring payments against single purchases. |
| What a new player is worth | Lifetime value over a player's first 90 days, by cohort and by campaign. |
| Time to first purchase | Days from a player's first session to their first payment, with the typical wait. |
| Packages, Revenue by country, Creator codes | Revenue after refunds by each. Packages show sales, buyers, refunded and the share of first purchases. |
| Revenue by kind of package | Packages sorted by Exylia AI from their names: Ranks, Crates and keys, Cosmetics, Currency, Bundles, Other, Unclassified. |
| Revenue by source | Money by the address each buyer first joined through; **Not linked to a player** is money from buyers who never joined the network. |
| Top payers | The players who spent the most in the range, after refunds. |
| Subscriptions | Active, cancelling, started and ended, and churn (**Share cancelled**). Counted only once the store also sends the recurring-payment end and cancellation events. |
| Every payment | Each transaction, newest first; search by player, package, source or id, filter by status (Linked, Not linked, Refunded, Chargeback, Free) and sort by newest or largest amount. |
| Free orders | Orders fully paid with a coupon or gift card. They are not sales and never make someone a paying player. |

If you set a monthly goal in **Settings › Menu**, a line at the top says whether you are on pace.

### Creator codes

Open **Revenue › Creator codes**: the codes buyers typed at checkout. Revenue is after refunds: refunds and chargebacks
are taken off the code that brought the payment. The page shows **Revenue with a code** and its **Share of revenue**,
**Codes used**, the average **Per transaction**, revenue with and without a code over time, **Top codes**, and
**Every code** with its payments, buyers, average and last payment. It has its own permission, **Creator codes**
(`view_creators`).

<Details title="Definitions">
Amounts are in the workspace's currency (Settings). Refunds and chargebacks subtract on the day they happen.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Sales (gross revenue) | Completed payments. |
| Revenue after refunds (net) | Sales minus refunds and chargebacks. |
| What you keep | Net revenue minus the tax and gateway fees Tebex reports per payment. |
| Buyers (payers) | Unique players with a completed payment. |
| Players who paid (conversion) | Buyers ÷ active players. |
| Spend per paying player (ARPPU) | Net revenue ÷ buyers. |
| Yearly pace (run rate) | Net revenue of the last 30 days × 12. |
| What a new player is worth (LTV) | Net revenue a cohort brings within N days of its first day, per player. |
| Revenue per new player | Net revenue in the range from players whose first join is in the range, ÷ those players. |
| Cost per new player (CAC) and Return (ROI) | See [Campaigns](/docs/exyliaanalytics/audience#campaigns). |

A payment from a player never seen on the network is still revenue; it is simply **Not linked to a player**, so it
counts in every total but belongs to no campaign, source or join-date group. Totals count the store currency only;
payments in other currencies are listed apart.
</Details>
