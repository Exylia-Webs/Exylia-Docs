In a few minutes you can see how often your own plugins' events fire (crates opened, quests finished, duels won)
and follow players through the steps you expect them to take, such as joined, opened the shop, bought a rank.

## Before you start

- A linked server, see [Connect your server](/docs/exyliaanalytics/linking).
- For your own events, a plugin that calls the agent's API (one line, below) and the **Custom events** module on in
  the workspace's **What the plugin collects** settings. With the module off, the agent drops them.
- To create or edit journeys, the **Create campaigns, map hostnames and edit journeys** permission
  (`manage_campaigns`). See [Team and settings](/docs/exyliaanalytics/team).

## Steps

### See an event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send it from your plugin">
    One line is enough. The rules, the threading and a full example are on the [API page](/docs/exyliaanalytics/api).

    ```java
    ExyliaAnalytics.track(player.getUniqueId(), "crate_open", Map.of("crate", "legendary"));
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Events">
    Go to **Activity › Events** (titled **Events from other plugins**). Pick the event from the **Event** list; each
    entry shows how often it fired, how many players fired it and when it was last seen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Break it down">
    Read **The event over time**, then the breakdown **By** each property the event carries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Build a journey

A journey (the older name is funnel) is an ordered list of events a player is expected to walk through, and a
**window** they have to do it in. Each step shows how many players got that far and how many were lost on the way.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Journeys">
    Go to **Activity › Journeys**. Under **Your journeys**, start **New journey**. You can begin from a template
    (**New players coming back**, **Store visit to purchase**), **From scratch**, or copy an existing journey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and set the window">
    Give it a **Name** (up to 40 characters) and pick how long a player has to reach the last step, counted from their
    first one: 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours, 2 days, 1 week or 30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the steps">
    Add up to **8** steps from **Quick add** or **Add another event…**, then reorder them with **Move up** and
    **Move down**. A step is one of your events (**Your events**), one of the plugin's own events (**Plugin
    events**) or a ready-made step (**Built-in steps**: First session, First chat message, First command, First
    advancement, Came back another day, Still playing a week later, First purchase).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can enter">
    Under **Who can enter?**, pick **Any player** or **New players only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create journey**. It opens with its real numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

A custom event shows up on the Events page within a minute of being sent, with a count and a player count. A journey
shows **Entered**, **Completed** and **Finished** for the selected range, and each step has a bar with the players
who reached it. A journey counts nothing until it has two steps, and a step with no data yet reads 0 until
something sends its event.

## Going further

### Narrow a step to one value

When you add a step, **Count only one kind of event** limits it to events that carry a given detail, such as
`crate` = `legendary`: pick the property under **Only when** and the value under **Is** (or **Any value**).

### Find who stopped

On a journey's page, **Who stopped where** lists the players who reached a step and never made the next one, most
recent first. Pick the step under **Stopped after**.

### Built-in journeys

**Built-in journeys** are ready to read with no setup and are read-only: **First steps**, **First purchase** and
**Voters**, plus one for each ExyliaJourney journey you run. Use **Duplicate and edit** to change the steps of one.

<Details title="How the numbers are defined">
| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Entered | Players who sent the first step in the range. |
| Completed | Players who then sent every step, in order, within the window of their first step. |
| Finished (conversion) | Completed ÷ entered. |
| Of previous step | Players who reached a step ÷ players who reached the one before it. |
| Typical time | Median time from first step to last, over the players who finished. |

Events that are not steps never break the chain. Journeys and the Events page read the raw event log, which is
kept for **90 days**, so they never look back further. A journey is deleted with **Delete journey**; no event data
goes with it.
</Details>

<Details title="Limits">
- A journey needs at least two steps and holds at most 8.
- Names are unique per workspace and at most 40 characters.
- The steps you can pick come from the events seen in the selected range, plus the built-in ones.
</Details>
