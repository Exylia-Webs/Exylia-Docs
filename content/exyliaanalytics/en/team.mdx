In about ten minutes you can invite your staff with the right access, build a role for a corner of the dashboard
(moderators who see no revenue, for example) and set the timezone, currency and player-address privacy of your
workspace.

## Before you start

You need a workspace with at least one linked server, see [Connect your server](/docs/exyliaanalytics/linking). The
owner and the Admin role can do everything below. Anyone else needs the permission named in each step.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite a member">
    Open **Team & access**, then the **Members** tab, and click **Invite**. Pick a role, create the link and copy it:
    it is shown **once**. Send it to the person. They sign in with Discord, open the link and join with that role.
    You need **Invite, remove and change the role of members**, and you can only hand out a role whose permissions
    you hold yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the right role">
    The role list on the same tab shows what each role reaches. The four roles every workspace starts with are
    Owner, **Full access** (Admin), **Analyst** and **Viewer**. Viewer is the default one new invites start with:
    it sees every page except revenue, the AI analyst and player IP addresses, and changes nothing. That is the right
    one for moderators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a custom role (optional)">
    Click **Manage roles** (the **Web access** tab), then create a role: a name (2 to 24 characters), a colour, and
    either a described profile or the permissions one by one. Needs **Create, edit and delete roles**. Back on
    **Members**, change a member's role from the role selector on their row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set timezone and currency">
    Open **Settings**, then **General**. Pick the **Timezone** where most of your players live, and the
    **Currency** of your Tebex store. Click save. Charts use the new values at once. Needs **Change IP storage and the
    plugin settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how player addresses are stored">
    In **Settings**, open **Player addresses** and pick **Hash only** (the default) or **Encrypted address**. See
    [Player addresses](#player-addresses) below for what each one keeps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

The new member shows under **Members** with their role and a join date, and the open link disappears from
**Pending invites**. Ask them to open the dashboard: the sidebar should show only the pages their role allows, and a
link to a page they may not open is refused. In the charts, the first hour of a day should now match your timezone.

## Going further

### Members

Everyone who can open the workspace, with their role and when they joined.

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| Invite someone | Members with **Invite, remove and change the role of members**. |
| Change a member's role | The same, except the owner's, and never your own. |
| **Remove** a member | The same, except the owner. |
| **Leave** | Anyone but the owner. Coming back needs a new invite. |
| **Make owner** | The owner only. The old owner becomes an Admin and cannot undo it. |

### Invites

An invite is a link with a role, for **one person**. It expires after 7 days by default (the platform can set
another length, 1 to 90 days, for links created from then on), can be cancelled with **Cancel invite** while open,
and is shown only when created. If it expires or was used, create a new one. Deleting a role cancels its open
invites.

### Player addresses

How the IP of each **new** session is stored. Sessions already stored are not rewritten. Reading an address on a
player's page needs the **Player IP addresses and shared-IP accounts** permission.

| Option | Stored |
|---|---|
| **Hash only** (default) | A keyed hash per address, with a secret of this workspace. Enough to find accounts that share an IP; the address itself can never be read back. |
| **Encrypted address** | The hash plus the address, encrypted. Members with the IP permission can read it on a player's page. |

If the dashboard has no encryption key configured, it says so under the option, and encrypted addresses cannot be
stored or shown until one is set. More on what leaves your server in [Privacy](/docs/exyliaanalytics/privacy).

### Other settings

- **What the plugin collects**: the intervals and the eleven modules every server of the workspace uses, applied
  without a restart. See [Remote configuration](/docs/exyliaanalytics/remote-config).
- **Integrations**: Tebex (see [Revenue](/docs/exyliaanalytics/revenue)), Exylia Staff and Tickets, your own AI
  model for the [analyst](/docs/exyliaanalytics/analyst). Plugins the agent reads on your servers are covered in
  [Connect other plugins](/docs/exyliaanalytics/integrations).
- **Chart notes**: marks you add to the charts (an update, an event), with **Manage chart notes**.
- **Menu**: the default period and comparison every page opens with, a monthly revenue goal, and which pages show in
  the sidebar. Hidden pages stay reachable by link, and roles still decide who may open them.
- **Developer keys**: read-only tokens for your own tools, with a scope each (profile, sanctions, purchases,
  shared-IP accounts), an optional expiry and a limit on live tokens. A token is shown once; revoking it stops the
  tool within a minute. Needs **Create and revoke API tokens for third-party tools**. See
  [API](/docs/exyliaanalytics/api).
- **Erase a player**: for a player who asks to have their data deleted. Type a UUID or the exact current username
  and confirm. It finishes within 24 hours, usually in minutes, and cannot be undone. If the player keeps joining,
  new data is collected again, so exclude them on your server.
- **Delete workspace** (**Advanced**, owner only): removes members, servers, integrations, campaigns and settings at
  once, and your servers stop sending within a minute. Statistics are deleted within 24 hours. Delete or hand over a
  workspace before deleting your account.

<Details title="Roles in detail">

Roles are per workspace and fully custom: a name, a colour and a list of permissions. Every page and every action
names the permission it needs, and the dashboard checks it on the server; hiding a link is only cosmetic.

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, always, including deleting the workspace. Never assignable and never editable; it changes hands only through **Make owner**. |
| Admin (**Full access**) | Every permission except deleting the workspace. |
| Analyst | Every page, revenue and the AI analyst included, but not player IPs; plus managing campaigns and chart notes. |
| Viewer | Every page except revenue, the AI analyst and player IPs. The **default** role: the one invites start with. |

Nobody can grant a permission they do not hold themselves. A role that is the default, or still held by members,
cannot be deleted: make another role the default and move the members first.

</Details>

### Workspace settings

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| Name | 2 to 48 characters. |
| Timezone | Where every day, week and hour of the charts starts. Usually where most of your players live. |
| Currency | Your Tebex store's currency; revenue is shown in it. |
