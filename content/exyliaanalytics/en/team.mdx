## Members

Everyone who can open the workspace, with their role and when they joined. From here:

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| Invite someone | Members with **Invite, remove and change the role of members** (`manage_members`). |
| Change a member's role | The same, except the owner's. |
| Remove a member | The same, except the owner. |
| Leave | Anyone but the owner. Coming back needs a new invite. |
| Make owner | The owner only. The old owner becomes an Admin and cannot undo it. |

### Invites

An invite is a link with a role, for **one person**. It expires after 7 days (a dashboard can set
another length), can be revoked while open, and is shown **once** when created: copy it then. The person
opening it signs in with Discord and joins with that role.

## Roles

Roles are per workspace and fully custom: a name, a colour and a list of permissions. Every page and
every action names the permission it needs, and the dashboard checks it on the server; hiding a link is
only cosmetic.

Four roles are created with every workspace:

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, always. Never assignable and never editable; it changes hands only through **Make owner**. |
| Admin | Every permission. |
| Analyst | Every page except player IPs, plus managing campaigns, funnels and annotations. |
| Viewer | Every page except revenue, the AI analyst and player IPs. The **default** role: the one invites start with. |

A role is edited with **Create, edit and delete roles** (`manage_roles`), and nobody can grant a
permission they do not hold themselves. A role still held by members cannot be deleted; move them first.
Deleting a role revokes its open invites.

`manage_settings`.

## Settings

### Workspace

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| Name | 2 to 48 characters. |
| Timezone | Where every day, week and hour of the charts starts. Usually where most of your players live. |
| Currency | Your Tebex store's currency; revenue is shown in it. |

### Player addresses

How the IP of each **new** session is stored. Sessions already stored are not rewritten.

| Option | Stored |
|---|---|
| Hash only (default) | A keyed hash per address, with a secret of this workspace. Enough to find accounts that share an IP; the address itself can never be read back. |
| Encrypted address | The hash plus the address, encrypted. Members with `view_ips` can read it on a player's page. |

If the dashboard has no encryption key configured, it says so under the option, and encrypted addresses
cannot be stored or shown until one is set.

### Agent

The settings every server of the workspace collects with, applied without a restart: the send,
heartbeat, metrics and AFK intervals, and the nine modules. Each is described on
[Remote configuration](/docs/exyliaanalytics/remote-config).
