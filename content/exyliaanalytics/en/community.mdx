In a few minutes you can see who gets punished and by whom, which ranks players earn and how long they take to get
there, and any number your game shows (balance, level, kills) charted per player. None of these pages follows the
server filter: a punishment, a rank or a placeholder value belongs to the network.

## Before you start

- A linked server or proxy, see [Connect your server](/docs/exyliaanalytics/linking).
- The plugin each page reads, running on a linked server or proxy: LiteBans, LibertyBans or AdvancedBan for
  moderation; LuckPerms for ranks; PlaceholderAPI on a Paper or Spigot server for game stats. How each is detected
  is on [Integrations](/docs/exyliaanalytics/integrations).
- The matching module on in **What the plugin collects**: **Punishments**, **Ranks** or **Game stats**. A page stays
  empty (*No punishments in this range*, *No ranks in this range*, *No game stats yet*) until it is.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Moderation">
    In the sidebar, open **Moderation** (the **Punishments** tab). Read the KPIs, **Punishments over time**, and
    **Staff**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Ranks">
    Open **Players › Ranks** to see who gains a rank, who renews it and how each rank plays.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the values to chart">
    For game stats, list the placeholders in each server's `config.yml` (see [Placeholders](#placeholders)), restart
    it, then open **Activity › Game stats** and pick one under **Stat**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Moderation shows the punishments of the range split by kind. After a staff member bans someone, the ban appears
under **Staff** with their name. Ranks shows **Rank grants** after you give a player a LuckPerms group. Game stats
lists your placeholder with a player count once players have left a server after the restart.

## Going further

### Moderation

Punishments from LiteBans, LibertyBans and AdvancedBan: how many, what kind, and who hands them out.

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| KPIs | **Punishments** in the range (split by kind: Ban, Mute, Kick, Warn), **Players punished**, their **Share of active players**, and **Lifted** (unbans and unmutes). |
| Punishments over time | Issued per period, stacked by kind. |
| Staff | Punishments issued by each staff member, by kind; the console as **Console**. |
| Reasons | What punishments were given for, sorted by Exylia AI from the reason staff typed: Cheating, Toxicity, Spam and advertising, Griefing, Exploiting, Ban evasion, Other or Unclassified (the model was not sure enough, or has not answered yet). |

A punishment is counted once by its plugin and its id. LiteBans running on the proxy and on a backend both report the
same ban; the dashboard keeps one.

### Ranks

LuckPerms groups: who gains a rank, who renews it, and how each rank plays.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Rank grants | A group added to a player, including a renewal: a temporary group whose expiry changed. |
| First-time grants | The first time a player gets that group. |
| Renewals | Grants that are not first-time. |
| Removals | Groups taken away. A temporary group that simply expires is not reported. |
| Players granted a rank | Unique players with a grant in the range. |

| Chart | Shows |
|---|---|
| Rank grants over time | Grants per period, by rank. |
| Active players by rank | Each active player once per period, under the rank they had when their latest session started. |
| By rank | Players, share of active players, visits per player and average visit length per rank. |
| Time to first rank | Days from a player's first session to their first rank: the typical wait (median) and buckets from **Same day** to **90+ days**. |

- The `default` group is never counted: LuckPerms gives it back whenever a player loses every other one.
- The rank a player *plays as* is their LuckPerms **primary group** at the moment their session starts. Without
  LuckPerms, or before LuckPerms has loaded the player, it is **Unknown**.
- Grants are recorded from the moment the agent sees them. History from before is not imported.
- A change is reported by the one server or proxy where it was made, so a network counts it once.

### Placeholders

Any PlaceholderAPI value you choose to collect: balances, levels, kills, anything a placeholder shows. In the
dashboard the page is called **Game stats**.

#### Choosing what to collect

Placeholders are listed per server, in that server's [`config.yml`](/docs/exyliaanalytics/configuration), up to 16:

```yaml
placeholders:
  - '%vault_eco_balance%'
  - '%statistic_mob_kills%'
```

Each time a player **leaves a Paper or Spigot server**, the agent resolves every listed placeholder for them and
sends the results. A placeholder PlaceholderAPI cannot resolve (it answers with the placeholder itself) is left out,
colour codes are stripped, and a value is cut to 200 characters. A proxy has no PlaceholderAPI and sends none.

<Callout type="tip" title="Use the raw variant">
The dashboard charts a value only when it is a plain number: `1520.25`, `-3`, `1,520.25` or `1 000`. Formatted values
such as `1.5k`, `12 coins` or `1.520,25` are kept as text. Most expansions offer a raw variant; list that one.
</Callout>

#### The page

Pick a stat under **Stat**; the list shows how many players each one has values for. Every statistic uses **each
player's latest value** in the period, so a player who left ten times counts once.

| A numeric placeholder | Shows |
|---|---|
| Typical (median), Average, Top 10% (90th percentile) | Of the players' latest values, over time, in the chart titled with the stat's name and "per player". |
| Sum | Of the players' latest values, only for the players seen in the period, so it is not a server-wide total. |

A placeholder is treated as numeric when at least half of its values are numbers. A text placeholder shows players by
their latest value instead, in a chart titled with the stat's name and "values".
