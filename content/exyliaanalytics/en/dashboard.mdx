In a few minutes you will know where everything is on [analytics.exylia.net](https://analytics.exylia.net),
how to pick the period and the server a chart covers, and how to leave a note on a chart so a jump or a dip
has its reason next to it. Every number on the dashboard is computed from the events your servers send;
nothing is typed in by hand except campaigns, costs and chart notes.

## Before you start

You need a workspace with at least one linked server that has seen a player join. Until then most pages show
"No server connected yet" and the Overview shows the setup checklist. See
[Connect your server](/docs/exyliaanalytics/linking).

A **workspace** is one network. It owns its servers, members, campaigns, integrations and settings, and every
chart reads only its own data. One Discord account can belong to several: the switcher at the top moves
between them and creates new ones. Who can see and change what inside a workspace is decided by roles, see
[Team and settings](/docs/exyliaanalytics/team).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    Sign in and pick the workspace in the switcher. You land on **Overview**, the front page: your key
    numbers against the previous period, **Players online**, **What stands out**, your servers and your
    top sources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the period">
    In the filter bar, open **Period** and choose a preset (last 6 hours up to **All time**) or **Custom**.
    **Compare to previous** is on by default: every number is set against the previous period of the same
    length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the server">
    Open **Server** and choose **All servers** or one entry from the **Network** (proxies) or **Game servers**
    group. What each choice reads is explained below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an area">
    The sidebar lists the areas (Players, Retention, Player sources, Revenue, Activity, Moderation, Servers,
    Team & access, Settings). Related pages are tabs under the area title. A member only sees the pages their
    role allows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Press `Ctrl` + `K` (`⌘` + `K` on a Mac), or `/` anywhere outside a text field, and type a page name or a
    player.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark a moment on the charts">
    Open **Settings**, then **Chart notes**, and add a note with a date and time (see
    [Chart notes](#chart-notes)).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Open **Overview** with a period that contains a player session. You should see **Online now** and **New
players** filled in, a Players online chart and your servers listed as connected. If a tile shows a
change against the previous period, **Compare to previous** is working. If a page says
*"Analytics are unavailable right now"*, the data store is not answering; nothing is lost, agents keep their
events on disk and deliver them once it is back.

## The pages

The sidebar has two sections, **Analytics** and **Workspace**. A section with nothing left in it disappears.
Each area opens on its first tab.

### Analytics

| Area (tabs) | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Overview | Is the server growing or shrinking, and why. The period at a glance, **What stands out**, servers, top sources, a link to **Ask the analyst**. See [AI analyst](/docs/exyliaanalytics/analyst). |
| Weekly report | The same numbers per calendar week, against the week before. |
| Players (All players, Growth, Ranks, First steps, Tutorials & quests) | Everyone ever seen, how the player base moves, rank grants, what happens to new players. See [Players and sessions](/docs/exyliaanalytics/players) and [Growth, retention and sources](/docs/exyliaanalytics/audience). |
| Retention (Summary, Full analysis) | Do new players come back, and who returns after a break. |
| Player sources (Countries & addresses, Campaigns, Creator codes, Votes) | Where new players come from and what each campaign is worth. |
| Revenue (Store, In-game economy, Market, Audit) | What the store earns and whether the in-game economy is healthy. See [Revenue](/docs/exyliaanalytics/revenue) and [Economy](/docs/exyliaanalytics/economy). |
| Activity (Hours & playtime, Sessions, Journeys, Game stats, Events) | How much, when and where players play; your own events and journeys. See [Events and journeys](/docs/exyliaanalytics/events). |
| Moderation (Punishments, Anticheat) | Punishments by kind and staff member, and anticheat flags. See [Moderation, ranks and placeholders](/docs/exyliaanalytics/community). |

### Workspace

| Area (tabs) | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Servers (Status, Health, Connect a server) | Linked servers, pause and revoke, the proxy mapping, lag and uptime. See [Servers and health](/docs/exyliaanalytics/servers). |
| Team & access (Members, Web access) | Who is in the workspace, invites, roles. |
| Settings (General, What the plugin collects, Integrations, Chart notes, Menu, Developer keys) | Name, timezone, currency, player addresses, the agent's settings, the Tebex store, chart notes. |

<Details title="Which events feed each area">
| Area | Fed by |
|---|---|
| Overview | Sessions, heartbeats, metrics, Tebex. |
| Players, Retention, Player sources | Sessions, presence, Floodgate, hostnames, countries, languages, client versions, votes. |
| Revenue | Tebex, economy flows, supply and balances. |
| Activity | Sessions, presence, AFK, activity counters, PlaceholderAPI, `ExyliaAnalytics.track(...)` and agent events. |
| Moderation | LiteBans, LibertyBans, AdvancedBan and the anticheats. |
| Ranks (under Players) | LuckPerms. |
| Servers | Heartbeats, metrics, starts and stops. |

Settings › **Menu** lets a workspace hide pages from the sidebar, tabs and search. Hidden is not
forbidden: the URL still opens for a member whose role allows it.
</Details>

## Going further

### The filter bar

Every analytics page shares one row of filters. They live in the URL, so a link shows exactly what you were
looking at, and moving between pages keeps them. The last choice per workspace is remembered and restored
when a page is opened without filters. **Show by** and **Server** sit behind the **Filters** button on narrow
screens.

### Picking a server

| Picked | Reads |
|---|---|
| A proxy or a standalone server | The players who **connected through it**: its network sessions. |
| A game server (backend) | The players who were **on it**: an active player joined it that day, playtime is time spent on it, a session is one stay, and a player is new there the first hour they appear. Hostname, country, campaign, client and rank stay what the network saw when they connected. |

Money, purchases, punishments, ranks and votes belong to no single server and ignore the filter. Economy and
placeholders only come from game servers, so picking a proxy there reads the whole network.

### Chart notes

Chart notes are markers on every time chart. Some are automatic: every server start is a **Restart**, a start
whose previous run never sent a stop is a **Crash**, and a start with a new agent version is a **Plugin
update**. Creating a campaign marks it too.

To add your own, open Settings › **Chart notes**, fill **What happened** and **When** (in the workspace
timezone; up to 80 characters of text), then **Add note**. Fill **Until** as well and it becomes a period, an
event weekend for instance, shaded across the charts. Adding and deleting notes needs the **Add and delete
chart notes** permission.

### Live activity

On the Overview, **Live activity** lists the last 24 hours, newest first: joins and leaves, purchases (only
for members who can see revenue), punishments, and lag spells where a server dropped to a low TPS. It
refreshes every 20 seconds. Next to the online count it shows players online now against an hour ago.

### Search

The palette finds pages and the tabs inside them by name and by keyword, and players by name or UUID
(only for members who may open Players). A found player shows whether they are banned or muted.

### Languages and theme

The dashboard speaks English, Spanish, French, Simplified Chinese, Russian and Norwegian Bokmål. It follows
the browser's language until you pick one, and the choice is shared with the other Exylia sites. It has a
light and a dark theme, or follows the system.

<Details title="Filter values and chart behaviour">
| Filter | Values |
|---|---|
| Period | Last 6 hours, 12 hours, 24 hours, 2 days, 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 90 days, 6 months, last year, all time, or **Custom** whole days. |
| Show by | **Auto**, or every minute, every 5 minutes, every 15 minutes, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly. Auto follows the period: every minute up to 12 h, every 5 minutes up to 2 days, every 15 minutes up to 7 days, daily up to 90 days, weekly up to a year, monthly beyond. Buckets below an hour only exist for what is read from raw data (players online, performance, custom events) and only inside the last 90 days. |
| Compare to previous | On by default. Draws the previous period behind each chart and shows the change on every number. Shows "No data before this range to compare against" when there is nothing earlier. |
| Server | **All servers**, or one server grouped under **Network** or **Game servers**. |

Days, weeks and hours are cut in the **workspace timezone** (Settings › General; `UTC` by default). Weeks
start on Monday.

Every chart has a **Definition** (how the number is computed), a table view and a CSV download. Time charts
also draw the previous period as a neutral shape behind the data, with now, previous and the change in the
tooltip; shade the bucket still in progress; zoom to the buckets you drag across; and mark restarts,
crashes, plugin updates, campaign launches and your own notes.

Right after linking, a chart can say *"Collecting since ..."*: players already known from the server's own
records never count as new, and breaks, comebacks, resurrected and churned players need time between
sessions to fill in.
</Details>

<Details title="The Overview, section by section">
| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| KPI tiles | **Online now** (with the most at once in the period), **New players**, **Played again on Day 7**, and **Revenue** (only with the revenue permission). Each tile shows the change against the previous period; opening one shows its detail and how it is measured. |
| Players online | Average and peak online at the finest buckets the period allows. |
| What stands out | Problems and changes worth a look, each with a link to its cause (offline servers, lag, players at risk, outdated plugin, new players, sources). Marked **Attention**, **Good** or **Info**. |
| Servers | How many of your servers are connected and what each is doing now. The proxy counts every player once; the servers behind it show where those same players are. |
| Top sources | Where this period's new players first joined from, with the share who played again on Day 7 and the revenue. |
| What changed | The largest moves against the previous period, largest first, each marked **Good**, **Needs attention** or **Neutral**. Anything that moved less than 5% is left out. |
| What new players do | Everyone who played for the first time in the period, step by step: joined, stayed at least 5 minutes, came back on another day, still playing a week later, bought something. |
</Details>
