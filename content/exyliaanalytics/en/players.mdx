## Players

Everyone who ever joined the network, one row each.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Segment tabs | All, or one lifecycle segment: new, core, regular, casual, at risk, churned. See [the definitions](/docs/exyliaanalytics/audience#lifecycle-segments). |
| Search | Part of a name, or a full UUID. |
| Sort | Last seen, first seen, playtime, sessions, or amount spent (the last only with the revenue permission). |

The columns are the player, their segment, country, first and last seen, days active in the last 30,
sessions, playtime, and — with the revenue permission — what they spent.

A player appears after their first session. On a network that means after they connect through a
linked proxy; a backend alone does not create players.

## A player's profile

Clicking a player opens their page.

| Section | What it holds | From |
|---|---|---|
| Header | Name, UUID, online or offline, and a ban or mute that is still running, with its reason and end. | Sessions, punishments. |
| KPIs | First and last seen, playtime (with AFK and session count), active days in the last 30, amount spent net of refunds (revenue permission), votes, punishments issued, first hostname and its campaign, client brand and version. | Sessions, presence, votes, punishments, Tebex. |
| Playtime, last 90 days | Hours per day, with AFK stacked on top. | Presence. |
| Timeline | Stays on each server with their activity counters (chat, commands, deaths, kills), purchases and refunds, votes, punishments issued and lifted, newest first. | Presence, Tebex, votes, punishments. |
| Time per server | Active and AFK time on each backend, and when they were last there. | Presence. |
| Where they came from | First hostname and its campaign, country, client. | Their first session. |
| Address | Only for members with **Player IP addresses** (`view_ips`). See below. | Sessions. |
| Recent sessions | The last 50 network sessions: start, hostname, the server that reported it, language, how it ended and its length. | Sessions. |

How a session ended:

| Shown | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Online | Still open. |
| Quit | The player left. |
| Kicked | Kicked by a plugin or staff, or kicked by the proxy for logging in twice. |
| Server stopped | The server or proxy shut down cleanly while they were on it. |
| Timed out | The server that held the session went silent — it missed three heartbeats, usually a crash — and the session was closed at its last heartbeat. |

A stay that shows *"still there or left without a leave event"* belongs to a server that did not report
the leave, typically because it crashed.

### The address section

What it shows depends on the workspace's **Player addresses** setting (see
[Team and settings](/docs/exyliaanalytics/team#player-addresses)):

| Setting | Shown |
|---|---|
| Hash only | *"Stored as a hash only"* and the first characters of the hash. The address itself can never be read. |
| Encrypted address | The last IP, decrypted. |

Either way it lists **accounts sharing an IP**: other players who joined from any of this player's
addresses, with when they were last seen. The match is made on the hash, so it works in both modes.

## Tutorials & quests

With [ExyliaJourney](/docs/exyliajourney/analytics) on your servers, **Players › Tutorials & quests**
fills in on its own: every journey it reports, with nothing to configure. Without it, the tab says
what the plugin adds.

| Shown | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Started | Players who started the journey in the period, each counted once, however many times a repeatable journey ran. |
| Finished | Of them, the share who completed it within 14 days. |
| Median time | Half of those who finished took less than this. |
| Skipped | The share who skipped the whole journey. |
| Abandoned | The share who stopped making progress (idle) or ran out of time on a step. |

Opening a journey shows every step in order: how many players reached it, completed or skipped it,
how long they spent on it, and how the runs that ended there ended. The step that loses the most
players is called out in red, and **Stalled at this step** lists who stopped there, linked to their
profiles. **Open as a funnel** opens the built-in funnel each journey adds under Activity › Journeys.

For a journey that starts on the first join (the onboarding), the page compares the new players who
finished it with those who did not: back the next day, a week and a month later, came back within a
week, and bought something. **Players › First steps** adds the same comparison, a *Finished the
tutorial* step in the new-player journey, and where the new players who never came back stopped in
it. A player's timeline shows each journey run and how it ended.

Journeys are read from the events of the last 90 days. Older ExyliaJourney versions do not announce
their journeys: names and steps then come from what players did.

## Sessions

A **session** is one network connection, from the moment a player connects to the proxy (or a
standalone server) until they leave. Moving between backends does not start a new one.

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| KPIs | Sessions in the range, players and sessions per player, average and median length. |
| All sessions | Newest first: player, start, hostname, country, client, how it ended and its length. |

Because a session is a network connection, this list is always the whole network, whatever server the
filter picks.
