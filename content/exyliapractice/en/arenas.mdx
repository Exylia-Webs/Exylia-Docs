You will end with an arena that is enabled, restores itself between matches and can be cloned. Everything is edited from `/epc` → **Manage arenas**; an arena is a row in `practice_arenas`.

## Before you start

- ExyliaPracticeCore is installed: see [Installation](/docs/exyliapractice/installation). FastAsyncWorldEdit is needed to save schematics, which an arena cannot be enabled without (see the warning below).
- The arena is already built in a world on your server, with walls, floor and decoration.
- You have `exyliapractice.admin` (operators do).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the arena">
    Run `/epc`, click **Manage arenas**, then **Create**. Chat asks for an id, then a display name. The setup screen (titled **ARENA: your-id**) opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the two spawns">
    Click **Spawn 1** and **Spawn 2** with the left button; a wizard asks you to stand where each side starts. The right button teleports you to a spawn that is already set. Both are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the playable region">
    Click **Playable region** and select the area the match happens in. Leaving it through its sides or bottom kills the player; standing above its top does not.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the copy region">
    Click **Copy region** and select a larger box that contains the playable region, with walls, floor and the spectator platform. Clones are pasted from it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what the arena is used for">
    The five **Use** buttons (queue, duel, party, bot, event) narrow which matches may use the arena. Leave them alone and the arena accepts every kind.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable it">
    Click **Status**. While something is missing, the button's lore lists it (see [What blocks an arena from being enabled](#what-blocks-an-arena-from-being-enabled)) and the click is refused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it to a kit">
    Click **Kits** and add the kits that may be played here, or do it from the kit's **Compatible arenas** screen: it is the same list. See [Create kits and categories](/docs/exyliapractice/kits).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Optional on the same screen: **Spectator spawn**, **Party FFA spawn**, **Extra regions** ([guide](/docs/exyliapractice/extra-regions)), the two protection radii, **Start animation**, **Priority**, **Name** and **Icon**.

## Check that it works

- **Status** reads enabled and its lore no longer lists anything missing.
- Click **View clones**: the original instance is listed as available.
- Queue or duel with a kit that includes the arena: both players land on spawn 1 and spawn 2. See [Play your first match](/docs/exyliapractice/first-steps).

## The two regions

This is the part that confuses people, so it is worth being exact.

| Region | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Playable region** | The area a match happens in. Leaving it through its sides or bottom kills you; standing over its top does not. It is what a **regeneration** restores between matches, and it is what carries the kit's rules as region policies. |
| **Copy region** | A larger box around the arena. It is what a **clone** is pasted from, and it is the origin every clone's coordinates are measured against. |

The copy region should contain the playable region: walls, floor, decoration, the spectator platform, anything that has to exist for a copy of this arena to look and play right.

<Callout type="note" title="Why the playable one is smaller">
Regenerating the whole copy region between every match would rebuild walls nobody touched. The playable region is the part players can damage, so it is the only part restored.
</Callout>

## The four spawns

| Spawn | Required | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| **Spawn 1** | Yes | The first side of every match |
| **Spawn 2** | Yes | The second side |
| **Spectator spawn** | No | Where a spectator lands |
| **Party FFA spawn** | No | The centre of a party FFA; players are scattered around it |

In a 1v1 or a party duel, players alternate between spawn 1 and spawn 2. In a party FFA everybody starts around the FFA spawn within `party.ffa-spawn-radius` blocks (7 by default).

## Protection radii

Two buttons set how far from a spawn or an extra region nobody may place or break blocks. Left click adds 1, right click removes 1, Shift adds or removes 5. The minimum is `0`.

| Button | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Spawn protection** | Blocks may not be placed or broken within this many blocks of either spawn |
| **Extra region protection** | Same, measured from the edge of every extra region |

Both default to `0`, which means no protection. They only matter for kits that allow building.

## Schematics

Every arena owns two schematic files, named after its id:

| File | Saved from | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `practice_arena_<id>` | The copy region | Pasting clones |
| `practice_arena_<id>_playable` | The playable region | Regeneration between matches |

Both are saved automatically the moment you set the matching region. The **Schematic** button re-saves both with a left click and restores the arena from them with a right click. Re-save after any rebuild: a regeneration restores the file, not the blocks currently standing.

## What blocks an arena from being enabled

While the arena is disabled, the **Status** button's lore lists what is missing, and a click refuses with a line pointing at it:

- Spawn 1
- Spawn 2
- Playable region
- Copy region
- Copy schematic (save it from the copy region)
- Playable schematic (save it from the playable region)

<Callout type="warning" title="The two schematic entries are also how a missing FAWE shows up">
The check asks the schematic store whether the file exists. On a server without FastAsyncWorldEdit the answer is always no, so the arena can never be enabled and the reason reads as a missing schematic rather than a missing plugin. The console said the real reason once, at startup.
</Callout>

## Going further

### Narrow what an arena is used for

An arena declares which kinds of match it accepts, from the **Use** buttons on its setup screen:

| Button | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Use: queue** | Ranked and unranked matchmaking, and plain 1v1 duels. |
| **Use: duel** | Duels a player sent by hand. |
| **Use: party** | Party FFA and party team fights. |
| **Use: bot** | Fights against bots. |
| **Use: event** | Other plugins' events (tournaments, FFA, the sandbox) that borrow an arena copy through the [API](/docs/exyliapractice/api). |

A map built for party fights is never handed to a queue match, and a bot arena is never duelled in.

<Callout type="note" title="An arena that declares none accepts everything">
Which is exactly what every row written before this existed says, so nothing had to be migrated: an empty list means "any match", and picking usages is how you narrow it.
</Callout>

### Change how 1v1 fighters wait

**Start animation** cycles through the server default, `none`, `gate`, `rise` and `cage`. It only affects 1v1 fighters during the start countdown and needs PacketEvents. `gate` needs marker blocks built within 8 blocks of each spawn; `rise` and `cage` need nothing. The block types and the server-wide default are in [Configuration](/docs/exyliapractice/configuration) under `game.start-animation`.

### Pick which arena is used first

**Priority** works like the kit one: left click adds 1, right click removes 1, Shift adds or removes 10.

<Details title="Instances and how a match picks one">
One arena is one or more **instances**. The original, the built one in your world, is instance `<id>`. Every clone is `<id>_clone_<n>`. Each instance is independently `AVAILABLE`, `IN_USE`, `REGENERATING` or `DISABLED`, and taking one is a compare-and-set, so two matches can never land in the same arena.

When a match asks for an arena it goes through its kit's compatible list in order, taking the first free instance of any of them. Only when every instance of every compatible arena is busy does it shuffle the list and [clone one](/docs/exyliapractice/clones).
</Details>

<Details title="The world border">
During a match every fighter and spectator sees a world border around the arena, sent to them alone: centred on the playable region and as wide as its longer side, so it never cuts into the play area. It does no damage and never tints the screen; leaving the playable region is still what the plugin enforces. A player combat-tagged in a lobby PvP zone sees the same around that zone until the tag ends. `world-border.enabled: false` stops drawing them.
</Details>

<Details title="Deleting an arena">
Right click the arena in the list and confirm. Deleting removes the row, unregisters every clone's region and resets the clone counter. It does not delete the schematic files and it does not touch the built arena in your world.

A kit whose compatible list still names the deleted arena keeps naming it. The entry matches nothing and is skipped; tidy it up from the kit's **Compatible arenas** screen.
</Details>
