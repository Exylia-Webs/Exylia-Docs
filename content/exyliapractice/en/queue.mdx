The queue pairs two players who want the same kit, in the lobby, without anyone sending a challenge. By the end of this page the queue works for your kits and you know which knobs change who meets whom.

## Before you start

- A category and a kit with **Queue** on in **Playability & ranked**, and an arena linked to it: see [Play your first match](/docs/exyliapractice/first-steps).
- The lobby spawn is set: `/epc` → **Set lobby spawn**.
- For ranked kits, how ratings work: [ELO and ranks](/docs/exyliapractice/elo).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the kit queueable">
    `/epc` → **Manage kits** → **All kits** → your kit → **Playability & ranked**. Turn on **Queue**. Turn on **Ranked** only if the kit should move ELO. A kit that is disabled, or has Queue off, never shows in the queue menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Queue as a player">
    Run `/queue` (or `/q`, or use the **Queues** item in the lobby hotbar). Pick a category, then a kit. Clicking a kit joins its queue and clicking it again leaves only that one. `/queue <category>` skips the category list; the id tab-completes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Queue for several kits at once">
    Keep clicking kits. The player stays in the lobby, free to walk around, with an action bar showing the kits, the elapsed time and the rating band being searched. More than three kits collapse to `first, second, third +N`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave or rejoin">
    `/leavequeue` leaves every queue, and the hotbar item **Leave queue** appears while queued. `/requeue` rejoins every kit the player was queued for when their last match started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how long a match is">
    In `config.yml`, `queue.rounds-to-win` is the rounds a player needs to take a queue match, ranked or not: `1` is a single fight, `2` is best of 3, `3` is best of 5 (range 1 to 10). A kit can set its own under **Kit rules** → **Win conditions**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/queue`, `/q` | Open the queue menu |
| `/queue <category>` | Open one category's kits |
| `/quickqueue`, `/qq`, `/quick-queue` | Join a random kit that already has somebody waiting; if nobody is waiting, any enabled kit |
| `/requeue`, `/rq` | Rejoin every kit you were queued for when your last match started |
| `/leavequeue`, `/lq` | Leave every queue |

<Callout type="note" title="Ranked needs a permission, and everybody has it">
Joining a ranked kit's queue needs `exyliapractice.queue.ranked`, which every player holds by default. Remove it from a group to keep them on unranked kits: the ranked kit is refused and `/quickqueue` only picks unranked ones. Leaving a queue never needs it.
</Callout>

## Check that it works

- With two accounts on different IPs, queue both for the kit. Within a second or two both get a **match found** title and are sent to the arena.
- `/queue` lists your category and kit. If the kit is missing, it is disabled, has no category, or Queue is off.
- If two players wait forever, read [How a match is found](#how-a-match-is-found), then [Common problems](/docs/exyliapractice/troubleshooting). On a test server the anti-boost same-IP check is the usual cause.

## How a match is found

The queue ticks once a second. For each kit with two or more players waiting:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sort by rating">
    Everybody waiting for that kit is sorted by their MMR on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk adjacent pairs">
    Only neighbours in that sorted list are considered: the closest possible ratings first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the radius">
    The pair matches if their rating difference is within the **wider** of their two search radii.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check anti-boosting">
    Same IP, or having played each other too recently, refuses the pair. See [ELO](/docs/exyliapractice/elo#anti-boosting).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take them">
    The pair is removed from **every** queue they were in, both are claimed, and the match starts. Their queue list is saved first so `/requeue` can rebuild it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Unranked kits skip the rating checks">
A kit that is not ranked has no ELO at stake: any two players waiting on it are paired, the radius and anti-boosting checks are skipped, and the action bar reads *any rank*.
</Callout>

### The search radius

It starts at `elo.matchmaking-base-radius` and widens by `elo.matchmaking-expand-rate` every `elo.matchmaking-expand-interval-seconds`, up to `elo.matchmaking-max-radius`.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `matchmaking-base-radius` | `150` | MMR either side, at the moment of queueing |
| `matchmaking-expand-rate` | `50` | Added per interval |
| `matchmaking-expand-interval-seconds` | `15` | How often it widens |
| `matchmaking-max-radius` | `600` | The ceiling |
| `matchmaking-ignore-elo` | `false` | When true, any two players match and the action bar reads *any rank* |

With the defaults a player starts searching at ±150 and reaches the ±600 ceiling after 2m15s.

<Callout type="note" title="The band shown is the first kit you queued for">
The action bar renders one rating band, taken from the kit the player joined first. Someone queued for a kit where they are Copper and another where they are Nova sees the Copper band. Matchmaking still uses the correct per-kit rating for each queue.
</Callout>

## Going further

<Details title="Who cannot join">
`/queue` refuses when any of these is true:

- The player is not free: in a match, editing a kit, spectating, in a [party](/docs/exyliapractice/parties) (queue as a party in a party duel instead), or claimed by another plugin.
- Their stats have not finished loading from the database.
- The queue is locked, which happens during a [season rotation](/docs/exyliapractice/seasons).
- No lobby spawn is set.
- The kit is disabled, or has **Queue** turned off.
- No arena is available for the kit right now.
</Details>

<Details title="The teaser bar">
When somebody is waiting, everyone else in the lobby sees a boss bar naming them and the kits they wait on, with each kit's rank icon. It rotates through the queued players one at a time.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `queue.teaser-enabled` | `true` | Turns the whole thing off |
| `queue.teaser-rotation-seconds` | `5` | How long each player is featured |
| `queue.teaser-max-kits-shown` | `3` | Kits listed before it collapses to `+N` with the global rank |

The bar is only shown to players who could act on it: available, queued, editing a kit, or in a PvP zone. Nobody in a match is offered a queue they cannot join.
</Details>

<Details title="The wait between finding and fighting">
Once a pair is found both players get a **match found** title and a loading action bar. If an arena had to be cloned, that is where the paste happens. If a **free arena already existed**, the plugin still waits `queue.no-clone-extra-delay` ticks (40 by default, two seconds) so a match found instantly does not feel like a teleport glitch.
</Details>
