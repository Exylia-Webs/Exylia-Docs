A party is a group of players who move through ExyliaPracticeCore together and play party-only matches: a free-for-all, two teams, or party against party. By the end of this page players can form parties on your server and you control how big they get and what they can play.

## Before you start

- At least one kit with a party mode on: `/epc` → **Manage kits** → **All kits** → your kit → **Playability & ranked**, then turn on **Party FFA**, **Party split** and/or **Party duel**. The game menu only offers the modes the chosen kit allows.
- For FFA, an arena with its **Party FFA spawn** set: `/epc` → **Manage arenas** → your arena → **Party FFA spawn**. See [Arenas](/docs/exyliapractice/arenas).
- Everybody can create and join parties: `exyliapractice.party` is on by default. A party exists only in memory, so a restart dissolves every party.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the party">
    Run `/party` to open the party menu and click **Create**, or run `/party create`. You become the leader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite players">
    Run `/party invite <player>`. The invitation lasts 60 seconds. The target accepts with `/party join <leader>`, from **Invitations** in the party menu, or turns it down with `/party decline <leader>`. Invitations are refused if the target has **Party invitations** off in `/settings`, is already in a party, or the party is full.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the party or announce it (optional)">
    **Open party** (`/party open`) lets anybody with the leader's name join with `/party join <leader>`. **Announce party** (`/party announce`) broadcasts an open party to the whole server. Closed is the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a game">
    The leader runs `/party game` (or **Party games** in the menu) and chooses **FFA**, **Split**, **Duel** or **Bots**, then a category and a kit. Every member must be free and at least two must be online.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Command | Who | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/party`, `/p`, `/party hub` | Anyone | Open the party menu |
| `/party create` | Anyone | Start a party as leader |
| `/party invite <player>` | Members | Invite somebody |
| `/party join <leader>`, `/party decline <leader>` | Anyone | Answer an invitation, or join an open party |
| `/party invitations`, `/party members` | Anyone | Open those lists |
| `/party leave` | Members | Leave |
| `/party chat` | Members | Toggle party-only chat for yourself |
| `/party cancelgame` | Members | Cancel the running party match. Always allowed during it, whatever `command-whitelist` lists. |
| `/party disband`, `/party kick <player>`, `/party transfer <player>` | Leader | Close the party, remove a member, hand leadership over |
| `/party open`, `/party announce`, `/party game` | Leader | Toggle open/closed, broadcast, open the game menu |

## Check that it works

- Two players: `/party create`, then `/party invite <other>`, then the other runs `/party join <leader>`. The party menu title shows `2/10 members` on a default setup.
- The leader runs `/party game` and picks a kit. If a mode is missing, the kit has it off in **Playability & ranked**. If starting fails, a member is not free: queued, in a match, editing a kit or held by another plugin.
- Membership changes the player's lobby hotbar and sidebar straight away: a party member counts as `IN_PARTY`, not `AVAILABLE`, and cannot use `/queue` or `/ke`.

## Going further

### The game modes

<Cards>
  <Card title="FFA">
    Everybody against everybody, spawned in a ring around the arena's Party FFA spawn within `party.ffa-spawn-radius` blocks (7 by default).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Split">
    The party is shuffled and cut in half into team A and team B. An odd party puts the extra player on team B.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Duel">
    The whole party queues against another party on the same kit. The first two parties waiting on a kit are paired immediately, whatever their sizes; until then the leader can leave the queue with **Leave duel queue**.
  </Card>
</Cards>

All members are claimed together or none of them are. A kit with `points-to-win` above 1 is refused for an FFA: points are per player and an FFA has no opposing side. The **Bots** button puts the whole party against a squad of bots, one each, with nothing recorded: see [Bot PvP](/docs/exyliapractice/bot-pvp).

### Make bigger parties a VIP perk

The size starts at the leader's ceiling and the leader can lower or raise it in the menu with **Party size** (left click +1, right click -1), between `party.min-size` and that ceiling.

```
exyliapractice.party.max_slots.<n>
exyliapractice.party.max_slots.*
```

The highest `<n>` the leader holds wins, capped by `party.max-size`; the wildcard gives `party.max-size` outright and operators hold it by default. With no node the leader gets `party.default-max-size`. Give VIP `exyliapractice.party.max_slots.20` in LuckPerms and their parties hold 20.

Announcements wait `party.announce-cooldown-seconds` (60 by default). A rank waits less with `exyliapractice.party.announce.cooldown.<n>` in seconds; the lowest node the leader holds wins. Deny `exyliapractice.party.open` or `exyliapractice.party.announce` to the default group to make those perks too.

<Details title="Settings, leaving and chat">
`/epc` → **Settings** → **Parties & queue**, or `config.yml`:

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| `party.min-size` | `2` |
| `party.default-max-size` | `10` |
| `party.max-size` | `100` |
| `party.ffa-spawn-radius` | `7` |
| `party.announce-cooldown-seconds` | `60` |

The ceiling is the leader's at the moment of the change: transferring leadership to somebody with a smaller ceiling does not shrink an already-large party, it only stops them raising it further.

`/party chat` is per member, not per party: with it on, everything you type goes to the party instead of global chat.

Leaving, being kicked or disconnecting removes the member. If the leader goes, leadership passes to the first remaining member. If the last member goes, the party is disbanded, which also removes it from the party-duel queue. In the member list, left click kicks and right click transfers leadership.
</Details>
