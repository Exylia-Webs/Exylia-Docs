A special condition is a rule of the form *when this happens to a player, do that*. You attach them to a kit, so one kit can be a sumo and another a point race on the same arena. By the end of this page you will have a kit that is won by touching a trigger.

## Before you start

- A working kit and arena: see [Play your first match](/docs/exyliapractice/first-steps).
- For `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION`, the arena needs [extra regions](/docs/exyliapractice/extra-regions).
- You have `exyliapractice.admin`.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the special conditions of the kit">
    `/epc` → **Manage kits** → **All kits** → your kit → **Kit rules** → **Special conditions**. Each trigger is one item in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the trigger and toggle its actions">
    Every trigger item shows the state of its three actions. Click it:

    | Click | Toggles |
    |---|---|
    | Left | `ADD_POINT` (Add a point) |
    | Right | `LOSS_POINT` (Lose a point) |
    | Shift + left | `DEATH` |

    A trigger can carry several actions. They run in the order you switched them on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how the match ends">
    For a point race, open **Kit rules** → **Win conditions** and set `points-to-win`. The player who reaches it wins the round, or the match when rounds are played. See [Kit rules](/docs/exyliapractice/kit-rules).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make sure the kit is playable">
    On the kit, **Playability & ranked** decides where it can be used (queue, duels, parties). A mode that only works with a specific arena belongs under **Compatible arenas**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The eight triggers

| Trigger | Fires when the player |
|---|---|
| `TOUCH_WATER` | stands in water |
| `TOUCH_LAVA` | stands in lava |
| `TOUCH_END_GATEWAY` | stands in an end gateway |
| `TOUCH_END_PORTAL` | stands in an end portal |
| `TOUCH_NETHER_PORTAL` | stands in a nether portal |
| `STEP_ON_GOLD_PRESSURE_PLATE` | steps on a light weighted pressure plate |
| `FALL_INTO_VOID` | falls into the void, or below the bottom of the arena's playable region |
| `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` | enters the [extra region farthest from their spawn](/docs/exyliapractice/extra-regions) |

Block triggers are checked on the block the player's feet are in, and only when they change block. Standing still in lava fires once, not every tick.

## The three actions

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| `ADD_POINT` | The player gains a point. If that reaches `points-to-win`, they win the round or the match. |
| `LOSS_POINT` | The player loses a point. The score never goes below zero. |
| `DEATH` | The player is eliminated. With `iron-plate-checkpoints` on and a checkpoint set, they are healed and sent back to it instead. |

<Callout type="note" title="A fall without DEATH sends the player back">
When `FALL_INTO_VOID` has actions but none of them is `DEATH`, or has none at all on a kit that plays to points, the fall is not an elimination. After the actions run, the player is healed and sent back to their checkpoint (with `iron-plate-checkpoints` on and one set) or to their spawn. Only a kit with no `FALL_INTO_VOID` actions and no `points-to-win` still kills on a fall.
</Callout>

## What you can build

<Cards>
  <Card title="Sumo">
    `TOUCH_WATER` → `DEATH`. Kit with no items, PvP on, arena is a platform over water.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Capture">
    `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` → `ADD_POINT`, two extra regions, `points-to-win: 3`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Parkour race">
    `FALL_INTO_VOID` → `DEATH` with `iron-plate-checkpoints` on, so a fall returns you to the last heavy plate. `STEP_ON_GOLD_PRESSURE_PLATE` → `ADD_POINT` at the finish.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bridge">
    `FALL_INTO_VOID` → `DEATH`, `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` → `ADD_POINT`, `build` on, `regenerate-after-point` on.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Check that it works

- Start a match with the kit (`/duel <player>` is the quickest) and walk into the trigger. The action fires once per entry.
- For a point race, the sidebar score goes up and the round or match ends at `points-to-win`.
- If nothing fires, the trigger has no action toggled on, or the player is in a block the trigger does not cover (for example a water trigger needs the feet block to be water).

## Going further

<Details title="Revives and DEATH">
An eliminated player in a kit with `revive-time` above zero spectates for that many seconds and comes back at their spawn instead of ending the match. That is what makes a `DEATH` condition a penalty rather than a loss, and how a bridge or parkour kit runs to a point total rather than to a single mistake.
</Details>

<Details title="LOSS_POINT is not decisive by itself">
Because the score stops at zero, `LOSS_POINT` alone never ends a round. Pair it with something that does: a `DEATH` on another trigger, or a point target for the opponent.
</Details>
