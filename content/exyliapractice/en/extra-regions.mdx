You will end with an arena that has one or two extra regions and a kit that scores when a player reaches the far one. On their own extra regions do nothing; they become meaningful once a kit assigns them a role.

## Before you start

- An arena that is already set up: see [Set up arenas](/docs/exyliapractice/arenas).
- A kit that is compatible with that arena: see [Create kits and categories](/docs/exyliapractice/kits).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the extra regions">
    Run `/epc`, click **Manage arenas**, pick the arena, then click **Extra regions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one at each end">
    Click **Add extra region** and select the box with the same selection wizard as the playable region. Do it again for the other end of the arena. A region is added at the end of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Score on arrival">
    Open the kit, click **Kit rules**, then **Special conditions**, and left click the **Enter Far Away Extra Region** trigger to toggle **Add point** on (right click toggles lose point, Shift + left click toggles death). Back on **Kit rules**, open **Win conditions** and set **Points to win** to 3. See [Special conditions](/docs/exyliapractice/special-conditions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove one you do not want">
    In the **Extra regions** list, right click the region to remove it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="This is how a capture kit is built">
Two extra regions, one at each end of the arena. Set `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` → `ADD_POINT` and `points-to-win` to 3. Reaching the enemy's end scores; your own end bounces you back to spawn. No extra plugin, no flags, no scripting.
</Callout>

## Check that it works

- The **Extra regions** button on the arena shows the number you added.
- Start a match with that kit and walk into the far region: the point is awarded. Walk into the one nearest your spawn: you are teleported back to your spawn.

## Target and home

At the start of every match, each player gets their own view of the arena's extra regions, worked out from where they spawn:

| Role | How it is chosen | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| **Target** | The extra region **farthest** from that player's spawn | At least one extra region |
| **Home** | The extra region **nearest** that player's spawn | At least two |

With exactly one extra region, everybody shares it as their target and nobody has a home. With two, the players spawning at opposite ends get mirrored roles automatically: one player's target is the other player's home.

**Home** is a wall. Walking into your own home region teleports you straight back to your spawn. It exists so a player cannot camp their own objective.

**Target** fires the `ENTER_FAR_AWAY_EXTRA_REGION` [special condition](/docs/exyliapractice/special-conditions). Whatever that condition is set to on the kit (add a point, lose a point, die) happens when the player steps into the far region.

## Protecting them

The arena's **Extra region protection** button stops blocks being placed or broken within that many blocks of every extra region's edge, the counterpart of spawn protection, for kits that allow building. Left click adds 1, right click removes 1, Shift changes it by 5; it defaults to `0`.

## Going further

Extra regions are cloned with the arena. Roles are recomputed per match from each player's actual spawn in that instance, so a clone behaves identically to the original. See [Run many matches in one arena](/docs/exyliapractice/clones).

<Details title="Region ids">
Extra regions are stored on the arena with generated ids of the form `practice_arena_<id>_extra_<n>` and no name of their own. In a clone they are offset and re-registered as `practice_arena_<id>_clone_<n>_extra_<i>`.

Ids carry the index they were added at. Removing a region from the middle of the list does not renumber the ones after it, and a region added later reuses an index that the removed one had. That is harmless for gameplay, because roles are computed from distance and never from the id, but an id is not a stable name to build anything else on.
</Details>
