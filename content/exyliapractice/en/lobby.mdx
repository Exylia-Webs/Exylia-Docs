You will end with a lobby that has a spawn, a hotbar that sends players to the queue, a party and their settings, and optionally jump pads, portals and a PvP warm-up zone. Everything outside a match is the lobby's business: what a player is holding, who they can see, and what they can walk into.

## Before you start

- ExyliaPracticeCore is installed: see [Installation](/docs/exyliapractice/installation).
- You have `exyliapractice.admin` (operators do).
- If you run [ExyliaLobby](#going-further) as well, read that section first: it can take over several of these features.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the lobby spawn">
    Stand where players should appear and run `/epc`, then click **Set lobby spawn**. Players return there after every match and when they join the server. Without a spawn the plugin does not teleport anybody to the lobby.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the hotbar">
    Players carry a different hotbar in each state: `lang/<language>/items/lobby.yml` in the lobby, `items/party.yml` in a party and `items/queue.yml` in a queue. The right one is applied whenever their state changes. Each entry is a slot, an item and a list of commands:

    ```yaml
    queue:
      slot: 0
      material: MACE
      name: "<gradient:#FF8C00:#FF4500>⚔ Queues</gradient>"
      lore:
        - "<#888888>Browse available queues"
      commands:
        - "any: player: queue"
    ```

    The shipped lobby hotbar links out to `/ffa`, `/events` and `/rtp`, which belong to other plugins. Remove the entries you do not run, then reload from `/epc` → **Reload**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the lobby features">
    Run `/epc`, click **Settings**, then **Lobby**. Each button flips one setting and shows the current value and the default:

    | Button | What it does |
    |---|---|
    | **Defer to Lobby** | Let ExyliaLobby run jump pads, regions and visibility |
    | **Double jump** | Players double jump in the lobby |
    | **Show VIPs** / **Show party** / **Show others** | What new players see before touching their own settings |
    | **Sneak toggle** | Sneak twice quickly to show or hide other players |
    | **Join message** / **Quit message** | The join and quit announcements |
    | **Command whitelist** | Block non-whitelisted commands in matches, queues and PvP zones |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what players can walk into (optional)">
    Jump pads, portals and PvP zones each have their own screen in `/epc`: see [Jump pads](#jump-pads), [Portals](#portals) and [PvP zones](#pvp-zones) below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

1. Join the server with a normal account. You should appear at the lobby spawn holding the lobby hotbar.
2. Press jump twice in the air: you should double jump.
3. Click the queue item: the `/queue` menu opens.
4. Join a queue: the hotbar switches to the queue one, with **Leave Queue** in the last slot.

<Callout type="note" title="A player inside a PvP zone keeps their kit">
Hotbars are not reapplied to somebody standing in a PvP region: they are holding that zone's kit, and overwriting it mid-fight would be worse than the wrong compass.
</Callout>

## Going further

### ExyliaLobby

With ExyliaLobby installed and `defer-to-exylia-lobby: true` (the default), the lobby jump pads, portal and PvP regions, double jump and the show/hide-players toggle are left to it. Queues, parties and matches are untouched. Set it to `false` to keep this plugin's own lobby features.

`places-scope` (default `default`) says which servers share the arenas, jump pads and the portal and PvP regions: `default` follows the network group in ExyliaLib's `config.yml` (no group means every server shares), `server` gives each server its own, `global` shares with all.

### Hotbar commands

The prefix before the first colon names the click kinds the line answers to (`left`, `right`, `shift_left`, and so on, comma-separated). `any` or no prefix means every click. The rest is a command line as the library reads one, so `console:` works as well as `player:`. Items, names and lore follow the rules in [Colours](/docs/exylialib/colours) and [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

### Double jump

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| `lobby.double-jump-enabled` | `true` |
| `lobby.double-jump-vertical-power` | `0.9` |
| `lobby.double-jump-forward-power` | `0.6` |
| `lobby.double-jump-cooldown-ms` | `500` |
| `lobby.double-jump-sound` | `ENTITY_BAT_TAKEOFF\|0.5\|1.4` |

It needs `exyliapractice.lobby.doublejump`, which everybody holds by default. It is off for anybody with `exyliapractice.lobby.fly`, since they are flying instead, and each player can turn it off for themselves in `/settings` (**Double jump**).

`lobby.effects` is a list of potion effects applied in the lobby, written as `NAME|LEVEL`; `SPEED|2` by default.

### Visibility

Every player decides who they see in the lobby, from `/settings`:

| Setting | Default | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Show VIP** | on | Players with `exyliapractice.lobby.vip` |
| **Show party** | on | Your own party members |
| **Show others** | off | Everybody else |

The defaults for players who have not changed anything are `lobby-visibility.show-vip-default`, `show-party-default` and `show-others-default`; players who already chose keep their own choice.

Sneaking twice quickly in the lobby flips **Show others** without opening the menu. It is on by default; `lobby-visibility.double-sneak-toggle: false` turns it off, and `lobby-visibility.double-sneak-window-ms` (400) is how close together the two sneaks must be.

Anybody with `exyliapractice.lobby.bypass` is always visible regardless. A player who is *not* in the lobby (in a match, in a queue-loading state, held by another mode) is handled separately: the lobby hides them from you and hides you from them.

### Jump pads

A jump pad is a set of block locations that launch whoever steps on them.

Run `/epc` → **Jumpads** → **Create**. Chat asks for an id and a display name, then the setup screen opens:

- **Name**: the display name.
- **Launch direction**: aim where the pad should throw people, then click, without sneaking. The direction is where you were looking when you confirmed.
- **Horizontal power** and **Vertical power**: left click adds 0.1, right click removes 0.1, and Shift-click moves 0.5.
- **Enabled**: switch it off without deleting it.
- **Trigger locations**: **Add location**, then click a block; right click a listed location to remove it. Any number of locations share the pad's direction and power.
- **Delete**.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `jumpad.launch-cooldown-ms` | `500` | Per-player, so one pad cannot be spammed |
| `jumpad.only-lobby-area` | `true` | Pads only fire for players in a lobby state |
| `jumpad.launch-sound` | `ENTITY_SLIME_JUMP\|1.0\|1.2` | |

### Portals

A portal is a region that runs a command, teleports, or both, the moment a player walks into it.

Run `/epc` → **Portals** → **Create**. Each portal has:

- **Portal region**: a selection wizard for the area.
- **Command**: typed in chat without the leading slash and run as the player.
- **Teleport**: left click sets the destination where you stand, right click clears it.

If both a command and a teleport are set, the command runs first. Portals only fire for players in a lobby state, so somebody being carried through by a match is not teleported out of it.

### PvP zones

A PvP zone is a warm-up arena inside the lobby: walk in, get handed a kit, fight, walk out.

Run `/epc` → **PvP regions** → **Create**. Each zone carries:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The display name |
| **Kit** | Left click saves the loadout handed out on entry from your own inventory; right click clears it |
| **PvP region** | Where it is (selection wizard) |
| **Enabled** | A disabled zone tells whoever enters and does nothing else |
| **Combat tag** | How long after being hit a player counts as in combat. Left click +1 second, right click −1, Shift-click ±5; never below 1. |
| **Flag: build** / **Flag: break** | Whether blocks may be placed or broken inside |
| **Flag: drop items** | Whether items may be dropped |
| **Flag: natural regen** | Whether health regenerates |
| **Flag: hunger** | Whether food drains |

Entering saves the player's previous state, claims them as `IN_PVP_ZONE`, and hands them the zone's kit. Leaving restores what they were doing and clears the combat tag.

Being hit inside a zone shows a combat-tag boss bar, configured under `effects.combat-tag-bossbar` in the language's `messages.yml` with `%time%` for the seconds left. While the tag lasts the zone's edge is also drawn as a world border for that player (see `world-border.enabled`).

<Callout type="note" title="A player in a PvP zone can still queue">
`IN_PVP_ZONE` is treated as a lobby-adjacent state throughout: they can join a queue, they see the queue sidebar, and they see the teaser bar. Finding a match pulls them out of the zone.
</Callout>

<Details title="Command whitelist and join messages">
`command-whitelist.enabled` (default `true`) blocks commands for players in a match, in a queue or inside a PvP zone, except the ones in `command-whitelist.commands` (messaging, `/leave`, `/leavequeue`, `/epc` and a few more; `namespace:command` forms match too). `/party cancelgame` is always allowed. Players with `exyliapractice.bypass.command-whitelist` (operators by default) run anything. The lobby itself is not restricted.

`join-quit.join-enabled` and `join-quit.quit-enabled` (both `true`) replace the vanilla join and quit lines with the `join-quit.join` and `join-quit.quit` messages in `messages.yml` (`%player_name%`).
</Details>
