title: "Match history"
description: "Every match, both inventories, its replay, and the window each of them lives in."
---

Every finished match writes one row **per player** to `practice_match_history`. The two rows share a
`matchId`, which is what lets a player look at their opponent's side of the same fight.

## What a row holds

| Group | Fields |
|---|---|
| Identity | `matchId`, `season`, `playerUuid`, `opponentName`, `kitId`, `arenaId`, `matchMode` |
| Outcome | `result` (WIN / LOSS / DRAW), `ranked`, `playedAt`, `durationMs` |
| Rating | `eloBefore`, `eloChange`, `eloAfter`, and the same three for the global ladder |
| Your side | `kills`, `deaths`, `damageDealt`, `hitsDealt`, `hitsReceived`, `totalSwings`, `bestCombo` |
| Their side | The same seven, prefixed `opponent` |
| Inventory | `inventoryData` — the player's inventory as it was when the match ended |

A row is written for every non-cancelled match, ranked or not.

## Viewing it

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/history`, `/mh`, `/match-history` | Your own history |
| `/history player <player>` | Somebody else's |
| `/history inv <matchId>` | The inventory you finished that match with |
| `/history invop <matchId>` | Your opponent's |

`inv` and `invop` open only for a player who fought in that match. The three lookups that read the
database answer at most once every two seconds per player.

The menu opens on the category list, then the kits, then the matches — each row showing the result,
the LP change, the opponent, the kit and how long it took. Left-clicking one opens the two inventory
views; right-clicking it plays its [replay](#replays).

Five filters: **all**, **wins**, **losses**, **ranked**, **unranked**.

<Callout type="note" title="The last 300 matches">
A history query reads a player's 300 most recent matches, ordered by the database, and filters that
window in memory. A kit you have not played in a long time can therefore look emptier than it is — the
match is still in the table, it is just outside the window the menu reads.
</Callout>

Results are cached for two minutes per player, kit and filter.

## Retention

Match rows are deleted after **60 days**. The sweep starts five minutes after boot and runs hourly,
deleting the oldest first, in batches, entirely inside the database with a pause between batches so a
live match's inserts are not held up.

<Callout type="note" title="Deleting a match costs nothing statistically">
Every counter derived from a match — wins, damage, accuracy, combos, time played — was already
committed to the season and lifetime rows when the match ended. The history row is the record, not the
source of truth.
</Callout>

The retention window is not a config key.

## Purging

`/epc` → **Data Management** → **Purge match history** deletes match rows outright, in larger batches
than the background sweep, since nothing is competing with it. It is irreversible and asks for
confirmation.

## The inventory snapshot

The inventory is captured when the match ends: 36 storage slots, 4 armour slots and the offhand,
serialised into the row. That is what `/history inv` renders — the actual items, durability and
enchantments included, as an unclickable menu.

Because it is captured at the end, a player who died holding nothing shows nothing. The snapshot is
what they had, not what the kit gave them.

## Replays

Every recorded match can be watched again from the history menu. A recording is the server's own
record of where everybody was on every tick, not a copy of what any client drew, so watching one
settles whether a hit landed. It includes the arena changing: blocks placed and broken, blocks that
expire, doors, regenerations between points, totems, golden apples and splash potions. Recording runs
for three seconds past the end of the match.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `replay.enabled` | `true` | Record matches at all |
| `replay.ranked-only` | `false` | Record ranked matches only |
| `replay.retention-days` | `14` | Days a recording stays watchable. Past this it is deleted; the history row stays. |
| `replay.max-concurrent-viewers` | `5` | Replays that may be watched at once, server-wide |

Matches against bots are never recorded. Recordings live in their own table, `practice_match_replays`,
one row per match.

### Watching one

The viewer is given a free copy of the match's arena, the same way the queue gets one, and becomes a
flying camera in adventure mode that nothing can hurt, starting behind the first fighter. A border
drawn only for them keeps them in the arena, and the bodies and blocks they see are sent to them
alone, so two people can watch two matches in two copies of one arena without seeing each other.

A replay is refused, with a message, when the viewer is in a match, queued or already watching one,
when every copy of the arena is in use, or when `max-concurrent-viewers` is reached.

The hotbar drives it, from `overlays/replay.yml` in the language folder:

| Slot | Button |
|---|---|
| 1 | Back 10 seconds |
| 2 | Play / pause |
| 3 | Forward 10 seconds |
| 5 | Speed: left-click slower, right-click faster, through `x0.125` to `x4` |
| 7 | From the start |
| 9 | Leave |

The same controls exist as commands, which also take an exact speed or moment:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/replay pause` | Pause or resume |
| `/replay speed <multiplier>` | Any speed from `0.0625` to `8` |
| `/replay seek <minutes> [seconds]` | Jump to that moment |
| `/replay sounds` | Sounds on or off |
| `/replay leave` | Back to the lobby |
