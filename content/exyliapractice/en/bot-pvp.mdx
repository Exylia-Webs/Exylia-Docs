With [ExyliaPracticeBotV3](/docs/exyliapracticebot/introduction) installed, a player can fight bots in a real arena with a real kit, alone or with their whole party. It is practice: nothing is recorded.

Without the bot plugin the module is simply absent. `/botpvp` is not registered, the party menu's **Bots** button shows a barrier and "Bot plugin not installed.", and the kit button that links a mode says the plugin is missing.

## Before you start

- ExyliaPracticeBotV3 is installed. ExyliaPracticeCore finds the bot when its service registers, even when the bot enables later. `/botpvp` is registered only if a plugin named `ExyliaPracticeBotV3` is present while ExyliaPracticeCore enables, so restart after installing it.
- You have a kit and a compatible arena: see [Kits](/docs/exyliapractice/kits) and [Arenas](/docs/exyliapractice/arenas).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Link a kit to a combat mode">
    Run `/epc`, open the kit, click **Playability & ranked**, then **BOT PRACTICE**. Each click cycles through unlinked, then every combat mode the bot offers (`SWORD`, `POT_PVP`, `UHC`, `CRYSTAL_PVP`, `MACE_PVP`, `BOXING`), then unlinked again. Stop on the mode this kit should represent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the kit is enabled">
    A mode is offered only when an **enabled** kit with at least one compatible arena is linked to it. If several kits name the same mode, the one with the highest priority wins, then the id.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the arena take bot fights">
    The kit needs at least one compatible arena, and that arena has to accept the `BOT` usage (the arena setup toggle **Use: bot**), or declare no usage at all, which accepts everything. See [Arenas](/docs/exyliapractice/arenas).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a fight">
    Run `/botpvp` (aliases `/bot-pvp`, `/practicebot`). Pick a mode, then a difficulty (`EASY`, `NORMAL`, `HARD`, `INSANE` or `EXTREME`). Clicking the difficulty starts the fight.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Rules a bot ignores">
Linking a mode to a kit with building off, PvP off, damage disabled, `hits-to-win` or `points-to-win` sends a warning naming the rule (`bot-pvp.rules-warning`). The plugin cannot enforce those on an opponent it does not own, and hit and point targets count hits between players, so the fight becomes last one standing. It warns rather than refuses.
</Callout>

## Check that it works

1. In the lobby run `/botpvp`. You should see the **Mode** menu with one button per linked kit, drawn with the kit's icon and name. With no mode playable you are told "No bot practice modes are set up yet." instead.
2. Click a mode, then a difficulty. After the usual loading screen and countdown a bot spawns and the sidebar switches to the bot practice one.
3. Kill the bot: the fight ends as won, and no stats, ELO or history entry appears.

## Going further

### Who can start a fight

| Way in | Who |
|---|---|
| `/botpvp`, `/bot-pvp`, `/practicebot` | Anybody with `exyliapractice.botpvp`, which everybody holds by default. Deny it to make bot practice a rank perk. |
| `/party game` → **Bots** | The party leader, with `exyliapractice.botpvp`. |
| `openMenu(player, BOT_PVP)` in the [API](/docs/exyliapractice/api) | Runs the same permission check as the command. |

A player on their own brings themselves. A party leader brings every online member, one bot each; a member who is not the leader is refused with the party's "not the leader" message. Everybody in the group must be free (in the lobby or in their party), and the server must have room for one more bot per player: over the bot plugin's ceiling the fight is refused with `%limit%` before any arena is touched. See [Limits](/docs/exyliapracticebot/limits) and [Parties](/docs/exyliapractice/parties).

### How a fight runs

- The kit and arena are taken as for any match, cloning one if every copy is busy (see [Clones](/docs/exyliapractice/clones)), with the usual loading screen and countdown.
- When the countdown ends, one bot per player spawns at the arena's second spawn (the first if it has none), in the chosen mode and difficulty, **wearing the match's own kit**. A kit with nothing in it lets the bot dress itself.
- In a party fight the bots are dealt out so nobody is doubled up on while somebody else is free; the players are one team and cannot hurt each other.
- When a player dies, the bots hunting them move to whoever is left with the fewest bots on them. A player who leaves takes their bot with them.
- Each fighter sees the `effects.bot-actionbar` line: the bots' combined health, the mode, the difficulty and their ping.
- The sidebar is `bot-practice` in `scoreboards.yml`, with `%bot_mode%`, `%bot_difficulty%` and `%bots_alive%`. See [Scoreboards](/docs/exyliapractice/scoreboards).

The fight is **won** when the last bot dies, and **lost** when every player is out. If a bot cannot be spawned the match is cancelled with "The bot could not be spawned. Nothing was recorded."

### What it never does

A fight against bots records nothing: no stats, no ELO, no match history, no replay. It cannot be spectated, and the bed-respawn rule never applies. `%exyliapracticecore_total_bot_players%` counts the players fighting bots right now. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliapractice/placeholders).

<Details title="Menus and messages">
The two menus are `lang/<language>/menus/player/botpvp/botpvp_mode.yml` (one button per playable mode, drawn with the linked kit's icon and name) and `lang/<language>/menus/player/botpvp/botpvp_difficulty.yml` (each difficulty with a one-line summary of how it plays). To restyle them, see [Edit menus](/docs/exylialib/edit-menus).

Everything the module says is under `bot-pvp` in `lang/<language>/messages.yml`; see [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

| Key | Default |
|---|---|
| `unavailable` | Bot practice is not available on this server. |
| `no-modes` | No bot practice modes are set up yet. |
| `no-kit` | That mode has no kit linked to it. |
| `server-full` | The server is already running `%limit%` bots. Try again in a moment. |
| `spawn-failed` | The bot could not be spawned. Nothing was recorded. |
| `starting` | Practising `%mode%` » `%difficulty%`. |
| `rules-warning` | Heads up: `%rule%` does not apply to a bot. The fight will be last one standing. |
| `bot-name` | The bot's nameplate, with `%mode%` and `%difficulty%`. Not `%player%`, on purpose: named after its opponent, two of the same player would stand in the arena. |
| `bot-skin` | `Steve`. Whose skin the bots wear, by player name. |
| `difficulties.<name>` | `name`, `summary`, `icon` and `skin` for each difficulty, keyed by its lowercase name. A blank `skin` keeps `bot-skin`; a blank `icon` keeps the menu's own for that place on the scale; a difficulty nobody lists is shown by its own name. |
| `party-status`, `party-status-unavailable` | The line on the party menu's **Bots** button, with and without the bot plugin. |
</Details>
