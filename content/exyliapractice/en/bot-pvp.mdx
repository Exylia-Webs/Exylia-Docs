With [ExyliaPracticeBotV3](/docs/exyliapracticebot/introduction) installed, a player can fight bots in a
real arena with a real kit, alone or with their whole party. It is practice: nothing is recorded.

Without the bot plugin the module is simply absent. `/botpvp` is not registered, the party menu's
**Bots** button shows a barrier and "Bot plugin not installed.", and the kit button that links a mode
says the plugin is missing.

## Setting it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the bot plugin">
    ExyliaPracticeCore finds the bot when its service registers, even when the bot enables later. `/botpvp`
    is registered only if a plugin named `ExyliaPracticeBotV3` is present while ExyliaPracticeCore
    enables.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a kit to a combat mode">
    `/epc` → the kit → **Playability & ranked** → **BOT PRACTICE**. Each click cycles through unlinked,
    then every combat mode the bot offers (`SWORD`, `POT_PVP`, `UHC`, `CRYSTAL_PVP`, `MACE_PVP`,
    `BOXING`), then unlinked again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the kit an arena that takes bot fights">
    The kit needs at least one compatible arena, and the arena has to accept the `BOT` usage — or
    declare no usage at all, which accepts everything. See [Arenas](/docs/exyliapractice/arenas).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A mode is offered only when an **enabled** kit with at least one compatible arena is linked to it. If
several kits name the same mode, the one with the highest priority wins, then the id.

<Callout type="warning" title="Rules a bot ignores">
Linking a mode to a kit with building off, PvP off, damage disabled, `hits-to-win` or `points-to-win`
sends a warning naming the rule (`bot-pvp.rules-warning`). The plugin cannot enforce those on an opponent
it does not own, and hit and point targets count hits between players, so the fight becomes last one
standing. It warns rather than refuses.
</Callout>

## Starting a fight

| Way in | Who |
|---|---|
| `/botpvp`, `/bot-pvp`, `/practicebot` | Needs `exyliapractice.botpvp`. The node is not declared in `plugin.yml`, so Bukkit gives it to operators only until you grant it. |
| `/party game` → **Bots** | The party leader. No node is checked on this path. |
| `openMenu(player, BOT_PVP)` in the [API](/docs/exyliapractice/api) | Runs the same permission check as the command. |

The flow is two menus:

1. **Mode** (`lang/<language>/menus/player/botpvp/botpvp_mode.yml`) — one button per playable mode, drawn with the linked
   kit's icon and name. With no mode playable the player is told "No bot practice modes are set up yet."
   instead.
2. **Difficulty** (`lang/<language>/menus/player/botpvp/botpvp_difficulty.yml`) — `EASY`, `NORMAL`, `HARD`, `INSANE` and
   `EXTREME`, each with a one-line summary of how it plays. Clicking one starts the fight.

A player on their own brings themselves. A party leader brings every online member, one bot each; a
member who is not the leader is refused with the party's "not the leader" message. Everybody in the group
must be free (in the lobby or in their party), and the server must have room for one more bot per
player: over the bot plugin's ceiling the fight is refused with `%limit%` before any arena is touched. See
[Limits](/docs/exyliapracticebot/limits).

## How a fight runs

- The kit and arena are taken as for any match, cloning one if every copy is busy, with the usual loading
  screen and countdown.
- When the countdown ends, one bot per player spawns at the arena's second spawn (the first if it has
  none), in the chosen mode and difficulty, **wearing the match's own kit**. A kit with nothing in it lets
  the bot dress itself.
- In a party fight the bots are dealt out so nobody is doubled up on while somebody else is free; the
  players are one team and cannot hurt each other.
- When a player dies, the bots hunting them move to whoever is left with the fewest bots on them. A
  player who leaves takes their bot with them.
- Each fighter sees the `effects.bot-actionbar` line: the bots' combined health, the mode, the difficulty
  and their ping.
- The sidebar is `bot-practice` in `scoreboards.yml`, with `%bot_mode%`, `%bot_difficulty%` and
  `%bots_alive%`. See [Scoreboards](/docs/exyliapractice/scoreboards).

The fight is **won** when the last bot dies, and **lost** when every player is out. If a bot cannot be
spawned the match is cancelled with "The bot could not be spawned. Nothing was recorded."

## What it never does

A fight against bots records nothing: no stats, no ELO, no match history, no replay. It cannot be
spectated, and the bed-respawn rule never applies. `%exyliapracticecore_total_bot_players%` counts the
players fighting bots right now. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliapractice/placeholders).

## Messages

Everything the module says is under `bot-pvp` in `lang/<language>/messages.yml`:

| Key | Default |
|---|---|
| `unavailable` | Bot practice is not available on this server. |
| `no-modes` | No bot practice modes are set up yet. |
| `no-kit` | That mode has no kit linked to it. |
| `server-full` | The server is already running `%limit%` bots. Try again in a moment. |
| `spawn-failed` | The bot could not be spawned. Nothing was recorded. |
| `starting` | Practising `%mode%` » `%difficulty%`. |
| `rules-warning` | Heads up: `%rule%` does not apply to a bot. The fight will be last one standing. |
| `bot-name` | The bot's nameplate, with `%mode%` and `%difficulty%`. Not `%player%`, on purpose: named after its opponent, two of the same player would stand in the arena. |
| `bot-skin` | `Steve`. Whose skin the bots wear, by player name. |
| `difficulties.<name>` | `name`, `summary`, `icon` and `skin` for each difficulty, keyed by its lowercase name. A blank `skin` keeps `bot-skin`; a blank `icon` keeps the menu's own for that place on the scale; a difficulty nobody lists is shown by its own name. |
| `party-status`, `party-status-unavailable` | The line on the party menu's **Bots** button, with and without the bot plugin. |
