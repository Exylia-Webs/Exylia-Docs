---
title: "Kit rules"
description: "Every toggle, number and attribute on a kit, with its default and what actually enforces it."
---

The rules are stored as JSON on the kit row and edited from `/epc` → **Manage Kits** → a kit → **Kit
rules**. That screen is a hub of seven cards: **Arena flags**, **Combat**, **Win conditions**,
**Cooldowns**, **Potion effects**, **Attributes** and **Special conditions**. Most rules become
policies on the arena's region for the length of the match; the rest are enforced by the plugin's own
match listener.

<Callout type="tip" title="Times are typed, not clicked">
Every duration on this screen is asked for as a length of time — `250ms`, `3s`, `1m30s` — instead of
being clicked up one second at a time. Anything the plugin writes back reads the same way, so `0s`
means off wherever a rule takes a time.
</Callout>

## Building

Building and item rules, plus `pvp` and `fall-damage`, are set from **Arena flags**: ExyliaLib's flag
editor, where **Default** stores the value in the table.

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `build` | off | Players may place blocks |
| `block-break` | off | Players may break blocks |
| `interact` | on | Players may interact with blocks |
| `player-build-only` | off | Only blocks players placed may be broken |
| `disappearing-blocks-delay` | `0` | Seconds before a placed block vanishes. `0` disables it. |
| `re-give-blocks` | off | The block is handed back when it disappears |

<Callout type="note" title="Adventure mode is derived, not configured">
A player is put in adventure mode when `build`, `block-break` and `player-build-only` are all off,
and in survival otherwise. There is no game-mode setting.
</Callout>

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `build-height-limit` | `0` | How many blocks above their own spawn players may place blocks. Higher is refused with `game.build-height-limit`. `0` means no limit beyond the arena's region. Set from **Combat** (left/right ±1, shift ±5). |

Block placing and breaking are also blocked inside the arena's [spawn protection and extra-region
protection radii](/docs/exyliapractice/arenas#protection-radii), regardless of these rules.

## Items

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `item-drop` | on | Players may drop items |
| `item-pickup` | on | Players may pick items up |
| `ender-pearl-cooldown` | `0` | Cooldown between pearls. `0` uses the server default. |
| `golden-apple-cooldown` | `0` | Cooldown on both golden apple types, in seconds. `0` disables it. |
| `arrow-cooldown` | `0` | Seconds a bow or crossbow waits between shots. A player left with no arrows gets one back when the wait ends. `0` disables it. |

<Callout type="note" title="The cooldowns are in seconds">
Unlike `hit-delay` just below, these are seconds, not ticks — `1` is about the vanilla pearl.
Older builds mislabelled the pearl one as `ticks` in the rules menu; the value it stored was always
seconds, so an existing kit needs no change.
</Callout>

## Damage and health

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `pvp` | on | Players may damage each other |
| `fall-damage` | on | Fall damage applies |
| `disabled-damage` | off | All damage is off |
| `health-regeneration` | on | Natural regeneration |
| `food-decreasing` | on | Hunger drains |
| `hit-delay` | `20` | The player's `maximumNoDamageTicks` — the invulnerability window after a hit, in ticks |

`hit-delay` is genuinely in ticks. `20` is vanilla; lower it for combo kits.

## Win conditions

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `hits-to-win` | `0` | A player who has *received* this many hits is eliminated. `0` disables it. |
| `points-to-win` | `-1` | Points needed to win. `-1` disables it. |
| `kills-give-points` | on | An elimination awards a point |
| `revive-time` | `0` | Seconds an eliminated player spectates before respawning. `0` disables revives. |
| `bed-respawn` | off | Killed players respawn, after `revive-time`, while their team's bed stands. Once an enemy breaks it, their next death is final. |

<Callout type="note" title="Hits-to-win counts hits taken, not dealt">
The sidebar's `%hits%` is your own hits *received*, and `%opp_hits%` is your opponent's. A boxing kit
is `hits-to-win: 100` and nothing else.
</Callout>

Points and revives together are what turn a 1v1 into a first-to-N: an elimination scores, the loser is
revived after `revive-time` seconds, and the arena optionally regenerates between points.

### Bed respawn

With `bed-respawn` on, a bed belongs to the team whose spawn it is closest to. An enemy can always break
it, whatever the kit's break rules or the arena's protection say, and nobody can break their own. The
whole match is told whose bed fell and who broke it. While the bed stands a death never ends the
match, and points are not scored from kills. It applies to team and 1v1 fights, not to a party FFA
or a fight against bots, and the arena is regenerated after the match.

## Movement and objectives

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `iron-plate-checkpoints` | off | Heavy weighted pressure plates set a checkpoint. Dying or leaving the arena returns you there instead of eliminating you. |
| `regenerate-after-point` | off | The arena is restored from its schematic between points |

A player sent back to a checkpoint gets no invulnerability on arrival. Checkpoints are per-plate and one-shot: standing on the same block again does not re-arm it, but a new
plate does. They reset at the start of every round and every point.

## Starting state

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `start-health` | `-1` | Health the player starts with. `-1` means full. |
| `start-food` | `-1` | Food level. `-1` means 20. |
| `start-saturation` | `-1` | Saturation. `-1` means 20. |
| `start-countdown` | on | The match opens with the start countdown. Off, the fight starts as soon as the arena is ready, with no skip vote. Set from **Cooldowns**. |

## Attributes

Set from **Kit attributes**. `-1` on any of them means "leave the vanilla value alone".

| Attribute | Vanilla |
|---|---|
| `max-health` | 20 |
| `attack-damage` | 1 |
| `movement-speed` | 0.1 |
| `knockback-resistance` | 0 |

Attributes are applied as base values at the start of a match and again after every point reset.

## Potion effects

Set from **Potion effects**. Each entry is an effect name, an amplifier and a duration in seconds; a
duration of `-1` lasts the whole match.

Every existing effect on the player is stripped first, so what a kit grants is exactly what the player
has.

## Special conditions

The last block on the rules screen. Eight triggers, three actions — see [its own
page](/docs/exyliapractice/special-conditions).
