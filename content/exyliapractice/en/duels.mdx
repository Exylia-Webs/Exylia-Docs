A duel skips matchmaking: one player picks the opponent, the kit, the number of rounds and (optionally) the arena, and the other accepts. By the end of this page duels work for your kits and you know who may choose what.

## Before you start

- A category and a kit with **Duel** on: `/epc` → **Manage kits** → **All kits** → your kit → **Playability & ranked** → **Duel**. A kit with Duel off is not listed. See [Play your first match](/docs/exyliapractice/first-steps) if you have no kit yet.
- An arena linked to that kit, so a match can start.
- Everybody can already send duels: `exyliapractice.duel` is on by default.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Challenge a player">
    Run `/duel <player>` (or `/d <player>`, or the **Duel** button on a player's row in a menu). Names tab-complete to players who are free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the category and the kit">
    The category list comes first, as everywhere else, then the kits that have **Duel** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the rounds">
    **1 round**, **3 rounds**, **5 rounds** or **Custom**. Custom asks in chat for a number from 1 to 20 (type `cancel` to go back) and needs `exyliapractice.duel.rounds`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the arena">
    Click an arena, or **Random arena**. A chosen arena is tried first and, if it is busy, the kit's other compatible arenas are used, so a preferred arena never makes a duel fail. Without `exyliapractice.duel.arena` this screen is skipped and the duel goes to a random arena.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The other player answers">
    Both players get a message naming the kit and the rounds. The target runs `/duel accept [player]` or `/duel deny [player]`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/duel <player>`, `/d <player>` | Start a challenge |
| `/duel accept [player]` | Accept. With no name, the first pending request. |
| `/duel deny [player]`, `/duel decline [player]` | Decline |
| `/duel cancel` | Withdraw the request you sent |

<Callout type="note" title="Pick the sender when several are waiting">
A player can receive several requests but hold only one outgoing request. Sending a second one asks first: "Cancel your duel request to `<player>` and send this one instead?" and, on yes, replaces it. `/duel accept` with no name takes whichever request it finds first, so name the sender when more than one is waiting. A player whose name is `accept`, `deny`, `decline` or `cancel` can only be challenged from a menu.
</Callout>

## Check that it works

- Challenge a second account: they get the request, `/duel accept` puts both of you in the arena with the kit.
- Wait 30 seconds without answering: the request expires and the sender is told. Change this with `duel.request-expiry-seconds` (5 to 300).
- Turn **Duel requests** off in the target's `/settings`: the challenge is refused.

## Going further

### Make choosing the arena or the rounds a perk

`exyliapractice.duel.arena` and `exyliapractice.duel.rounds` are on for everybody. Deny them to your default group and grant them to VIP in LuckPerms: the default group then duels on a random arena with 1, 3 or 5 rounds, and VIPs get the arena screen and **Custom**.

### Best-of rounds

A duel with more than one round is a best-of: the first player to win `rounds` rounds wins the match. Between rounds both get a *round won* / *round lost* title with the running score, then after `duel.round-end-delay-ticks` (80 by default, four seconds) the arena is regenerated in place and both players are revived, reset to zero points, teleported to their spawns, re-kitted and counted down again. A round that ends in a draw restarts without giving anybody a win. A kit with `points-to-win` makes each round a race to that total, and the sidebar switches to the duel-with-points layout. See [Kit rules](/docs/exyliapractice/kit-rules).

<Details title="Who cannot duel, and ELO">
A duel is refused when either player is not free (queued, in a match, editing a kit, spectating, or held by another plugin) or when the target has **Duel requests** off in `/settings`. Both players are claimed at the same moment on accept; if the second claim fails, the first is released and nothing starts.

A duel is ranked when the **kit** is ranked: there is no separate switch, and a ranked kit moves LP through `/duel` exactly as through `/queue`.

The anti-boost checks (same IP, recent-opponent cooldown) are asked before a **queue** pairing, not before a duel is accepted, so two accounts can duel each other repeatedly for LP. A duel does record the pair as recently matched, which blocks them from meeting through the queue for `elo-security.opponent-cooldown-seconds`. If that matters on your server, turn **Ranked** off on the kits you offer for duelling, or **Duel** off on the ranked ones. See [ELO and ranks](/docs/exyliapractice/elo#anti-boosting).

Settings: `duel.request-expiry-seconds` (30), `duel.round-end-delay-ticks` (80). The replace prompt and every other line are in `messages.yml` under `duel:`; see [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).
</Details>
