Supply drops are crates that fall out of the sky on a parachute and land as a real container anybody can run for. You set them up in game: the kinds of crate (tiers), the areas they fall into (envoys) and, if you want, a timetable so they fall by themselves.

Open the panel with `/supplydropsadmin` (alias `/envoysadmin`), or click **Supply drops** in the hub of `/warzoneadmin`. [Your first supply drop](/docs/exyliawarzone/first-steps) walks through the shortest path; this page covers everything else.

## Before you start

- ExyliaWarzone installed with the supply drops module on (it is by default, see [Installation](/docs/exyliawarzone/installation)).
- Your own permission: `exyliawarzone.supplydrops.admin` (or `exyliawarzone.admin`). See [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions).
- An envoy needs an **Area** before anything drops from the timetable, from **Drop one now** or from `/supplydropsadmin start`. Until then only `/supplydropsadmin here` drops a crate.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an envoy">
    `/supplydropsadmin`, click **Envoys**, then the emerald (**New envoy**). Type an id such as `desert-camp`; the envoy's screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select its area">
    Left-click **Area**. You get a golden axe: left-click a block for the first corner, right-click another for the second, then shift + left-click to confirm. See [Selecting regions](/docs/exylialib/regions) for how the selection tool works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how many crates and which tiers">
    **Crates** is how many fall each run (4 by default, from 1 to 64). **Spacing** is the fewest blocks between two crates of the same run (24 by default). **Tiers**: left-click adds or removes one tier; right-click allows every tier again, which is the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a timetable">
    Click **Timetable**. It opens the [schedule editor](/docs/exylialib/schedules): clock times, days, a repeat every so often inside a window, and how many players must be online. Once saved, the envoy runs by itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Click **Run it now**. The envoy drops as if its timetable had fired. Right-click **Area** to teleport to its middle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="The corners set the height too">
Crates land on ground between the heights of the two corners, searched from the upper one down. Click one corner at or below the ground and the other well above it, or just under the roof for an area inside a building or a cave. Two corners on the same flat ground leave no room for a crate and every run answers **No free spot found**.
</Callout>

## Check that it works

Click **Run it now** on the envoy. Within a few seconds you should see a chat announcement, a beam of light over each spot and a boss bar with the countdown. A crate falls under a parachute, lands as a barrel with a hologram, stays locked for 20 seconds and then opens to the first player who reaches it. If chat says **No free spot found**, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliawarzone/troubleshooting).

## What happens to a drop

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| Announced | Chat announcement, a beam of light over the spot and a boss bar. 60 seconds by default. |
| Falling | The crate comes down under its tier's parachute and touches down on the last second. |
| Locked | A real block with a hologram. Opening it is refused with the time left. 20 seconds by default. |
| Open | Anybody can loot it. The first player to open it is announced. |
| Gone | Emptied, it goes when the inventory is closed. Otherwise it vanishes with what is left after 300 seconds from landing. |

## Going further

### Tiers: rare and common crates

A server with no tiers yet gets three to start from: `common` (weight 70), `rare` (25) and `legendary` (5), so the chances are 70%, 25% and 5%. Open **Tiers** to edit them; the emerald creates one, left-click opens a tier and right-click deletes it.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | A disabled tier is never rolled. |
| **Name** | What players read in announcements and over the crate. |
| **Weight** | How likely it is against the other enabled tiers. A weight of 0 never comes up. |
| **Loot table** | The items a crate holds, in the [loot editor](/docs/exylialib/crates-and-loot). |
| **Canopy** | The block the parachute is drawn with. |
| **Glow** | Colour of the beam, the falling crate and the unlock burst. |
| **Crate block** | Left-click picks a block, right-click follows the config (a barrel). It must hold items. |
| **Timings** | Its own announce, unlock and despawn times. |
| **Preview the fall** | Plays the fall where you stand, for you alone. |
| **Delete** | Removes the tier and its loot table, after a confirmation. |

Each loot line is rolled on its own as a percentage, with an amount between its minimum and maximum. When nothing comes up, one line is given anyway, so a crate never opens empty unless its table is. Loot is items only: no command or money lines.

### Timings

Every drop has three times: how long it is announced, how long it stays locked and how long it stays after landing. Open **Timings** on an envoy or a tier. Left-click sets a time (`30s`, `1m` or `2m30s`), right-click clears it so the drop falls back to the next place that sets it. The order is the tier, then the envoy, then the module config (60, 20 and 300 seconds). The exception is the announce time of an envoy run, which comes from the envoy and then the config, so every crate lands together. An envoy run is announced for at least 5 seconds and a crate stays at least 10 seconds after landing. Keep despawn longer than unlock.

### Drop one by hand

<Commands group="supplydrops" />

`announce` is a length such as `10s` or `2m`; to give one, name the tier first. A tier named in a command drops even when it is disabled. On the admin screen, **Drop one now** is `start` and **Take every crate away** is `stop`, with a confirmation. Every `/supplydropsadmin` subcommand needs `exyliawarzone.supplydrops.admin`; `/supplydrop` is open to everybody and tells a player where the next crate is. See [Commands](/docs/exyliawarzone/commands).

### Change the look

**Animation** holds the five phases of every drop: **Marker** (the beam), **Fall**, **Impact**, **Unlock** and **Vanish**. Each opens the [sequence editor](/docs/exylialib/sequences). In the lines, `%glow%`, `%parachute%` and `%crate%` become the tier's colour, canopy and crate block. Colours in names and messages follow [Colours](/docs/exylialib/colours); announcements are in `lang/<code>/modules/supply-drops/messages.yml` ([Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages)).

### Mute the announcements

Each player mutes them in `/settings`, under **Announcements**, as **Supply drops**. The boss bar stays; to turn it off, empty its text in the messages file.

<Details title="Technical details">

**Where crates land.** A random column of the area with solid ground (not bedrock) and two blocks of air above, never water or lava. It must be outside every [ExyliaLib region](/docs/exylialib/regions) (Warzone's regen zones and duel rooms included), outside every ExyliaProtections claim and at least **Spacing** from the other crates of the run. Each crate tries up to `attempts` columns (20); a run that finds fewer spots drops the ones it found. The spot is checked again on landing: if somebody built there, the crate lands on top, up to 8 blocks higher and never above the area. A spot claimed since, or with no room, calls the crate off with an announcement. A block is never written over.

**Timetable.** A line that fires while the envoy still has crates out from its last run does nothing and is not retried. The schedule form also offers the `envoy-idle` gate, which says the same. Times follow the `timezone` of ExyliaLib's `config.yml`.

**The crate.** A landed crate cannot be broken, blown up, drained by a hopper or sold with a sell wand. When the module stops, every crate is taken away with what is inside; one left by a crash is removed on the next start. Opens and takes are in the audit log (`/exylialib logs`, actions `supply-drop-open` and `supply-drop-take`).

**What players see.** Announcements reach every player who has not muted them, and the console. Every player in a drop's world sees one boss bar for the crate closest to them. The beam and animations are display entities and particles drawn within `view-radius` (160 blocks); a crate dropping under a roof (anything within 12 blocks above) skips the parachute.

**Limits.** At most `max-active` (16) crates are out at once on a server, envoys included; a run is cut to the room left. If every tier an envoy allows is disabled or deleted its runs drop nothing: right-click **Tiers** to allow them all. Tiers are shared by every server on the same database; envoys and their crates belong to one server, and a tier edited on one reaches the others on restart. Defaults and keys: [Configuration](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration#supply-drops).

**Other envoy buttons.** **Status** (a disabled envoy never runs), **Name** (what players read when it starts), **Delete** (removes the envoy, its area and timetable and takes its crates away). In the **Envoys** list, shift-click a row to run it and right-click to delete it.

</Details>
