Multiply what power-ups and duel rooms pay, let players mute the announcements. ExyliaWarzone needs only ExyliaLib; everything on this page is optional.

## Before you start

- ExyliaLib is the only requirement. Every plugin below is optional, and nothing is missing without it.
- A booster plugin built on ExyliaLib's modifiers, or a permissions plugin, if you want multipliers.

## Steps

Boost duel and power-up rewards with a permission:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the source">
    Use `power-ups` or `duel-rooms`, and the type `money`, `xp` or `drops`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant the node">
    Give the group `exylia.modifier.<type>.<source>.<factor>`, for example `exylia.modifier.money.duel-rooms.2`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Claim a duel-room reward or take a power-up with an account in that group. The payout is the configured amount times the factor. Run `/exylialib logs <player>` to see the staff actions listed below appear as you create and delete things.

## Going further

### Boosters

Money, experience and items paid by **power-ups** and **duel rooms** run through ExyliaLib's modifier pipeline, so any booster plugin built on it, and a rank's permissions, can multiply them (see [Economy](/docs/exylialib/economy)). Both sources are listed on booster screens with their own icon: `power-ups` (the rewards of a power-up a player walks into) and `duel-rooms` (the rewards of a duel room's winner). Each takes the types `money`, `xp` and `drops`.

Without a booster plugin, permissions do it: `exylia.modifier.<type>.<source>.<factor>`. For example
`exylia.modifier.money.duel-rooms.2` doubles the money a duel pays that player, and
`exylia.modifier.drops.power-ups.1.5` gives one and a half times the items. Factors from every source of boosts are
multiplied together.

Supply drop crates and loot chests hand out their loot as items in a container, so they are not boosted.

### Rewards that do not fit

A reward a player cannot take because their inventory is full is never dropped on the floor. ExyliaLib keeps it, and
it is handed over the next time they join, with a message saying how many were kept
(`delivered` in `plugins/ExyliaWarzone/lang/<language>/messages.yml`). Staff can review what players are still owed with ExyliaLib's `/exylialib pendingrewards`. See [Rewards](/docs/exylialib/rewards).

### Announcements in /settings

Two kinds of broadcast go through ExyliaLib's announcement channels, so each player can mute them from `/settings`:

| Channel | What it carries |
|---|---|
| `supply-drop-announcements` | Supply drops and envoys about to fall, landing and opening. |
| `duel-announcements` | The winner of each duel room fight, when the room announces results. |

Muting is per player and does not affect anybody else.

### Staff log

ExyliaLib's staff audit log, readable with `/exylialib logs <player>`, records every action below.

<Details title="Logged actions">

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| `supply-drop-open` | The first player opens a supply drop crate. |
| `supply-drop-take` | A player takes items out of a crate. |
| `powerup-zone-create`, `powerup-zone-delete` | A power-up zone is created or deleted. |
| `loot-chest-place`, `loot-chest-remove` | A loot chest is registered or removed. |
| `loot-template-delete` | A loot chest template is deleted. |
| `regenzone-create`, `regenzone-delete` | A regen zone is created or deleted. |
| `regenzone-force-regen` | Staff regenerate a zone by hand and it succeeds. Timed regenerations are not logged. |
| `duel-room-create`, `duel-room-delete` | A duel room is created or deleted. |

</Details>

Banned-item rule creation and deletion are logged too, see [Ban items in an area](/docs/exyliawarzone/banned-items).

### PlaceholderAPI

With PlaceholderAPI installed, the supply drops module registers `%exyliawarzone_supplydrop_...%`. See
[Placeholders](/docs/exyliawarzone/placeholders).

### WorldGuard, claims and other area plugins

Regen zones and duel rooms enforce their own [flags](/docs/exyliawarzone/region-flags) and leave every other
plugin's areas alone. Where a WorldGuard region or a claim covers the same blocks, an action goes through only when
both allow it. The one exception is a running duel: fighters can hit each other even where WorldGuard, spawn
protection or a flag would stop PvP.

<Details title="Technical details">

Boosts go through ExyliaLib's modifier pipeline under the sources `power-ups` and `duel-rooms`; factors from every source are multiplied together. Announcements are two ExyliaLib channels, `supply-drop-announcements` and `duel-announcements`. Rewards that do not fit are kept by ExyliaLib, not by this plugin.

</Details>
