ExyliaWarzone works on its own with ExyliaLib. These are the places where it meets other plugins and the library's
shared systems.

## Boosters

Money, experience and items paid by **power-ups** and **duel rooms** run through ExyliaLib's modifier pipeline, so any
booster plugin built on it, and a rank's permissions, can multiply them. The two sources are listed on booster screens
with their own icon:

| Source | Pays | Types |
|---|---|---|
| `power-ups` | The rewards of a power-up a player walks into. | `money`, `xp`, `drops` |
| `duel-rooms` | The rewards of a duel room's winner. | `money`, `xp`, `drops` |

Without a booster plugin, permissions do it: `exylia.modifier.<type>.<source>.<factor>`. For example
`exylia.modifier.money.duel-rooms.2` doubles the money a duel pays that player, and
`exylia.modifier.drops.power-ups.1.5` gives one and a half times the items. Factors from every source of boosts are
multiplied together.

Supply drop crates and loot chests hand out their loot as items in a container, so they are not boosted.

## Rewards that do not fit

A reward a player cannot take because their inventory is full is never dropped on the floor. ExyliaLib keeps it, and
it is handed over the next time they join, with a message saying how many were kept
(`delivered` in `lang/<language>/messages.yml`). Staff can review what players are still owed with ExyliaLib's
`/exylialib pendingrewards`.

## Announcements in /settings

Two kinds of broadcast go through ExyliaLib's announcement channels, so each player can mute them from `/settings`:

| Channel | What it carries |
|---|---|
| `supply-drop-announcements` | Supply drops and envoys about to fall, landing and opening. |
| `duel-announcements` | The winner of each duel room fight, when the room announces results. |

Muting is per player and does not affect anybody else.

## ExyliaSurvivalCore statistics

When ExyliaSurvivalCore is installed, ExyliaWarzone reports these statistics to it, for its missions, leaderboards
and seasons. Without it nothing is counted and nothing is missing.

| Statistic | Counted when |
|---|---|
| `supply_drops.reached` | A player is the first to open a supply drop crate. |
| `powerups.picked` | A player takes a power-up. |
| `lootchests.opened` | A player takes something from a loot chest. |
| `lootchests.<template>.opened` | The same, per template id. |
| `duels.wins` | A player wins a duel room fight. |

## Staff log

ExyliaLib's staff audit log, readable with `/exylialib logs`, records:

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| `supply-drop-open` | The first player opens a supply drop crate. |
| `supply-drop-take` | A player takes items out of a crate. |
| `powerup-zone-create`, `powerup-zone-delete` | A power-up zone is created or deleted. |
| `loot-chest-place`, `loot-chest-remove` | A loot chest is registered or removed. |
| `loot-template-delete` | A loot chest template is deleted. |
| `regenzone-create`, `regenzone-delete` | A regen zone is created or deleted. |
| `regenzone-force-regen` | Staff regenerate a zone by hand and it succeeds. Timed regenerations are not logged. |
| `duel-room-create`, `duel-room-delete` | A duel room is created or deleted. |

## PlaceholderAPI

With PlaceholderAPI installed, the supply drops module registers `%exyliawarzone_supplydrop_...%`. See
[Placeholders](/docs/exyliawarzone/placeholders).

## WorldGuard, claims and other area plugins

Regen zones and duel rooms enforce their own [flags](/docs/exyliawarzone/region-flags) and leave every other
plugin's areas alone. Where a WorldGuard region or a claim covers the same blocks, an action goes through only when
both allow it. The one exception is a running duel: fighters can hit each other even where WorldGuard, spawn
protection or a flag would stop PvP.
