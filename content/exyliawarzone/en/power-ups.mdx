Power-ups are items that float and spin inside zones you set up in game. The first player to walk into one takes
it: its potion effects, its rewards and a short message. Each zone has its own list of power-ups, its own timer and
its own cap, and spawns them either at fixed spawn points or anywhere inside an area.

<Callout type="note" title="On by default">
The module is switched by `modules.powerups` in `config.yml` (restart to apply). Items and holograms are packets,
not entities: nothing can push them, kill them or pick them up the vanilla way.
</Callout>

## The editor

Open it with `/powerupsadmin` (needs `exyliawarzone.powerups.admin`) or from the **Power-ups** card of
`/warzoneadmin`. The zone list shows each zone with its mode, its interval, whether it is on, whether it can
spawn, and how many power-ups float there now out of its maximum. Left-click a zone to open it, right-click to
delete it (you are asked to confirm, and whatever floats there goes too).

## Setting up a zone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    **Create ▸ Power-up zone** and type a unique ID in lower case. The ID is checked against every server on the
    database. The zone opens already switched on: it starts spawning the moment it has what it needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where they appear">
    **Spawning ▸ Spawn mode** switches between **Spawn points** (the default) and **Area**. For spawn points open
    **Spawn points**, press **Add ▸ Spawn point** and left-click a block. For an area press **Area**, left-click one
    corner, right-click the other, then shift + left-click to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add power-ups">
    **Power-ups ▸ Add ▸ Power-up** creates one and opens its screen: name, icon, effects, rewards, glow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the timing">
    **Timing** holds the interval, how many may float at once, how long each lasts and whether they refill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it is ready">
    The card at the top of the zone shows **Spawning: Ready**. Otherwise it names what is missing: **No power-ups
    yet**, **No area set** or **No spawn points yet**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The top card also switches the zone on or off. Switching it off takes every floating power-up away at once.
**Rename** changes the name the zone is listed by; **Delete** removes the zone, its spawn points and its power-ups.

## Spawn modes

| Mode | Where each power-up appears |
|---|---|
| **Spawn points** | One of the zone's points, chosen at random each time. It floats in the middle of the block above the one you clicked, half a block over the floor. |
| **Area** | A random spot inside the box: a column is picked and searched from the top for two blocks of air. |

Spawn points can repeat, so give a zone more points than its **Max at once** if two power-ups should never share
one. Remove a point with a right-click in the **Spawn points** list.

In **Area** mode, **Placement** decides what a spot needs: **On the ground** (the default) wants a solid block under
it, **Anywhere, even mid-air** lets it float over air. Only loaded chunks are searched, up to
`settings.area-max-attempts` columns (20) per spawn. When none fits, that spawn is skipped and the zone tries again
after its next interval, so an area whose chunks are not loaded stays empty.

## Timing

| Setting | New zone | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Spawn interval** | 1m | Time between two power-ups, typed as `30s`, `5m` or `1h30m`. At least 1 second. |
| **Max at once** | 3 | How many may float at the same time. Left-click +1, right-click -1, never below 1. |
| **Lifetime** | 5m | How long one floats before it goes, with a countdown on its hologram. `0s` keeps it until somebody takes it; anything else is at least 10 seconds. |
| **Refill** | Yes | Whether taken or expired power-ups are replaced. |

A zone spawns at most one power-up per interval until **Max at once** float there. The first one appears as soon as
the zone is ready, and again right after a restart. A taken power-up is replaced as soon as an interval has passed
since the last one appeared.

With **Refill** off, the zone spawns **Max at once** power-ups in all and then stops. Switching the zone off and on,
or restarting the server, starts a new round. A power-up kept until taken that nobody takes for a whole day is
replaced by a fresh one.

## Power-ups and their odds

Each zone holds its own list. A new power-up is called `New PowerUp`, shows a nether star with a white glow, weighs
100 and gives nothing until you set it up. **Picked by**, on the list screen, chooses how the next one is drawn:
**Random** (the default) gives each power-up the same chance, **Weighted** draws by weight. Every card shows its
chance as a percentage.

| On the power-up screen | Does |
|---|---|
| **Icon** | The item that floats: pick a material, the item in your hand or a head. |
| **Name** | Shown on the hologram and in the pickup message. |
| **Weight** | Left-click +10, right-click -10, shift-click ±50, never below 1. Shift-click a card in the list for ±10. |
| **Effects** | The potion effects given on pickup. |
| **Rewards** | Items, commands, money, experience and messages given on pickup. |
| **Glow** | Left-click switches the outline on or off, right-click chooses its colour. |
| **Remove** | Takes it out of the zone with its effects and rewards, after a confirmation. |

The glow colour is one of white, silver, gray, black, red, maroon, yellow, olive, lime, green, aqua, teal, blue,
navy, fuchsia, purple and orange, or any `#rrggbb` value typed by hand.

## What a pickup gives

- **Effects**, set in ExyliaLib's [effect editor](/docs/exylialib/effects): the type, the level, the duration (or
  infinite) and whether particles, the icon and the ambient look show. A totem of undying does not take
  them: they come back for the time they had left. Death clears them as usual.
- **Rewards**, set in ExyliaLib's [reward editor](/docs/exylialib/rewards), each with its own chance. Money,
  experience and items are boosted by [modifiers](/docs/exylialib/economy#modifiers) under the source `power-ups`,
  so a booster plugin or a node such as `exylia.modifier.money.power-ups.2` doubles them. Items that do not fit are
  kept and handed over on the player's next join.
- The message **You picked up** with the power-up's name and a short sound.

With ExyliaSurvivalCore installed, each pickup also counts the `powerups.picked` statistic for its missions and
leaderboards.

## What players see

The icon floats and spins at full brightness (a turn every 4 seconds), with its glow outline when it is on and a few
end rod particles around it. A hologram above it shows the name and, for a power-up that expires, the time it has
left. Players within 48 blocks see it, and those within 24 hear a soft chime when it appears.

## Who can take one

Coming within `settings.pickup-radius` (1 block) of a power-up takes it, and one that appears on top of a player
standing still is taken at once. Only a player really playing can: alive, in survival or adventure, not vanished,
and not idle (ExyliaLib's idle time, 5 minutes by default). A player refused while standing on one takes it the
moment they stop being idle or leave creative or spectator. When two players reach it together, only one gets it.

## Edits while power-ups float

- Effects and rewards are read when the power-up is taken, so an edit already counts for the ones floating.
- Name, icon, glow, spawn mode, spawn points, area and timing apply from the next spawn.
- A floating copy of a power-up you removed from the list gives nothing; switch the zone off and on to clear it.

## On a network

Zones belong to the server they were created on: on a database shared by several servers, each server loads and
lists only its own, while IDs stay unique across all of them.

Every key of `modules/powerups/config.yml` (pickup radius, item size, view distance, hologram, particles, rotation,
the spawn sound) is on the [configuration page](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration#power-ups), and can also be changed
in game from `/warzoneadmin settings`. The module's messages are in `lang/<code>/modules/powerups/messages.yml`.
