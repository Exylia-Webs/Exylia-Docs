Power-ups are items that float and spin inside zones you set up in game. The first player to walk into one takes it: its potion effects, its rewards and a short message. Each zone has its own list of power-ups, its own timer and its own cap, and spawns them at fixed spawn points or anywhere inside an area.

## Before you start

- **PacketEvents must be installed.** Power-up items and holograms are packets. Without PacketEvents the module stays off and its card in `/warzoneadmin` says which plugin it needs. See [Installation](/docs/exyliawarzone/installation) and [Connect other plugins](/docs/exyliawarzone/integrations).
- The module is on by default (`modules.powerups` in `config.yml`; restart to apply).
- Your permission: `exyliawarzone.powerups.admin`.

## Steps

Open the editor with `/powerupsadmin`, or the **Power-ups** card of `/warzoneadmin`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a zone">
    Click **Power-up zone** (the emerald) and type a unique id in lower case, such as `arena-north`. The id is checked against every server on the database. The zone opens already switched on: it starts spawning the moment it has what it needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where they appear">
    Click **Spawning**, then **Spawn mode** to switch between **Spawn points** (the default) and **Area**. For spawn points, click **Spawn points**, click **Spawn point** and left-click a block. For an area, click **Area**, then left-click one corner, right-click the other and shift + left-click to confirm. See [Selecting regions](/docs/exylialib/regions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add power-ups">
    Click **Power-ups**, then **Power-up**. It creates one and opens its screen: name, icon, effects, rewards and glow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the timing">
    Click **Timing**: how often one appears, how many float at once, how long each lasts and whether they refill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it is ready">
    The card at the top of the zone shows **Spawning: Ready**. Otherwise it names what is missing: **No power-ups yet**, **No area set** or **No spawn points yet**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Walk to the zone. Within one spawn interval (a minute for a new zone) a floating, spinning item with a hologram appears. Walk into it: you get its effects and rewards and the message **You picked up** with its name. If nothing shows, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliawarzone/troubleshooting).

## Going further

### Spawn points or area

With **Spawn points**, each power-up appears at one of the zone's points, picked at random, floating half a block above the floor in the block above the one you clicked. Points can repeat, so give a zone more points than its **Max at once** if two power-ups should never share one. Remove a point with a right-click in the **Spawn points** list.

With **Area**, each power-up appears at a random spot inside the box. **Placement** decides what a spot needs: **On the ground** (the default) wants a solid block under it, **Anywhere, even mid-air** lets it float over air. Only loaded chunks are searched; when no spot fits, that spawn is skipped until the next interval.

### Timing

| Setting | New zone | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Spawn interval** | 1m | Time between two power-ups, typed as `30s`, `5m` or `1h30m`. At least 1 second. |
| **Max at once** | 3 | How many may float together. Left-click +1, right-click -1, never below 1. |
| **Lifetime** | 5m | How long one floats, with a countdown on its hologram. `0s` keeps it until somebody takes it; anything else is at least 10 seconds. |
| **Refill** | Yes | Whether taken or expired power-ups are replaced. |

A zone spawns at most one power-up per interval until **Max at once** float there. With **Refill** off it spawns **Max at once** in all and then stops; switching the zone off and on, or restarting, starts a new round.

### Odds between power-ups

On the **Power-ups** screen of a zone, **Picked by** switches between **Random** (every power-up has the same chance, the default) and **Weighted** (by weight). A new power-up weighs 100. On its screen, **Weight** is left-click +10, right-click -10, shift-click ±50, never below 1; shift-click a card in the list for ±10. Every card shows its chance as a percentage.

### Effects and rewards

- **Effects** open the [effect editor](/docs/exylialib/effects): type, level, duration (or infinite) and whether particles show. A totem of undying does not remove them; they come back for the time they had left.
- **Rewards** open the [reward editor](/docs/exylialib/rewards): items, commands, money, experience and messages, each with its own chance. Money, experience and items are boosted by [modifiers](/docs/exylialib/economy#modifiers) under the source `power-ups`, so a node such as `exylia.modifier.money.power-ups.2` doubles them. Items that do not fit are kept and handed over on the player's next join.

With ExyliaSurvivalCore installed, each pickup counts the `powerups.picked` statistic.

### Pause or remove a zone

The top card of a zone switches it on or off; switching it off takes every floating power-up away. **Rename** changes the name the zone is listed by; **Delete** removes the zone, its spawn points and its power-ups, after a confirmation. In the zone list, right-click a zone to delete it.

<Details title="Technical details">

**Power-up screen.** The first item shows the power-up's name and changes its icon (a material, the item in your hand or a head). **Name** is shown on the hologram and in the pickup message. **Glow**: left-click switches the outline on or off, right-click picks its colour (white, silver, gray, black, red, maroon, yellow, olive, lime, green, aqua, teal, blue, navy, fuchsia, purple, orange, or any `#rrggbb` typed by hand). **Remove** takes it out of the zone with its effects and rewards, after a confirmation. A new power-up is called `New PowerUp`, shows a nether star with a white glow and gives nothing until set up.

**Who can take one.** Coming within `settings.pickup-radius` (1 block) takes it, and one that appears on top of a player standing still is taken at once. Only a player really playing can: alive, in survival or adventure, not vanished and not idle (ExyliaLib's idle time, 5 minutes by default). When two players reach it together, only one gets it.

**What players see.** The icon floats and spins (a turn every 4 seconds) at full brightness, with end rod particles and, when on, a glow outline. A hologram shows the name and, for one that expires, the time left. Players within 48 blocks see it; those within 24 hear a chime when it appears. Items and holograms are packets: nothing can push, kill or vanilla-pick them.

**Area search.** Up to `settings.area-max-attempts` columns (20) per spawn, each searched from the top for two blocks of air. A power-up kept until taken that nobody takes for a whole day is replaced by a fresh one.

**Edits while power-ups float.** Effects and rewards are read when the power-up is taken, so an edit counts for floating ones. Name, icon, glow, spawn mode, spawn points, area and timing apply from the next spawn. A floating copy of a power-up you removed gives nothing; switch the zone off and on to clear it.

**On a network.** Zones belong to the server they were created on; each server loads only its own while ids stay unique across all of them. Config keys (pickup radius, item size, view distance, hologram, particles, rotation, sound) are in [Configuration](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration#power-ups) and in `/warzoneadmin settings`. Messages: `lang/<code>/modules/powerups/messages.yml`.

</Details>
