Show the next supply drop on a scoreboard, hologram or tab list. Placeholders need [PlaceholderAPI](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/placeholderapi.6245/); they are registered under the `exyliawarzone` expansion while the supply drops module runs. The other modules have none. The values are the same for every player.

<Placeholders />

A scoreboard line could read:

```text
Next drop: %exyliawarzone_supplydrop_next% min
```

`/supplydrop` gives a player the same information in chat. See [Supply drops](/docs/exyliawarzone/supply-drops).
