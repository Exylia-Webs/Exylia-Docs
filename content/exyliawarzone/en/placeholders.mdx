---
title: "Placeholders"
description: "The four PlaceholderAPI placeholders of the supply drops module."
---

With [PlaceholderAPI](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/placeholderapi.6245/) installed, the supply drops module
registers four placeholders under the `exyliawarzone` expansion. They are there while the module runs; the other
modules have none.

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliawarzone_supplydrop_next%` | Whole minutes until the next envoy timetable fires, rounded up. `-1` when no envoy is on a timetable. Single drops started by command do not count. |
| `%exyliawarzone_supplydrop_active%` | `x, z` of the crate that is out, or empty when none is. With several out, the one closest to its next moment (landing, opening or vanishing). |
| `%exyliawarzone_supplydrop_count%` | How many crates are out right now, falling or landed. |
| `%exyliawarzone_supplydrop_envoys%` | How many of this server's envoys can run: enabled and with an area. |

The values are the same for every player. A scoreboard line could read:

```text
Next drop: %exyliawarzone_supplydrop_next% min
```

`/supplydrop` gives a player the same information in chat, with the tier, the world and the coordinates. See
[Supply drops](/docs/exyliawarzone/supply-drops).
