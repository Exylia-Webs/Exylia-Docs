---
title: "Permissions"
description: "Every permission node of ExyliaWarzone, its default, and what it opens."
---

| Permission | Default | Grants |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliawarzone.admin` | operators | `/warzoneadmin`, its hub, `settings` and `reload`. Holds every module's `.admin` node below. |
| `exyliawarzone.supplydrops.use` | everybody | `/supplydrop`: where the crate that is out is, or when the next one falls. |
| `exyliawarzone.supplydrops.admin` | operators | `/supplydropsadmin`, the tier and envoy editors, and `/supplydropsadmin start`, `here`, `envoy` and `stop`. |
| `exyliawarzone.powerups.admin` | operators | `/powerupsadmin` and the power-up zone editor. |
| `exyliawarzone.lootchests.admin` | operators | `/lootchestsadmin`, the loot chest and template editors, and removing a loot chest by breaking it while sneaking. |
| `exyliawarzone.lootchests.bypass` | operators | Opening a loot chest that is still refilling. It does not open a disabled chest. |
| `exyliawarzone.regenzones.admin` | operators | `/regenzonesadmin` and the regen zone editor. |
| `exyliawarzone.banneditems.admin` | operators | `/banneditemsadmin` and the banned-item rule editor. |
| `exyliawarzone.banneditems.bypass` | nobody | Never stopped by a banned-item rule. Add `.<rule id>` to be let off one rule only. Not even operators have it. |
| `exyliawarzone.duelrooms.admin` | operators | `/duelroomsadmin` and the duel room editor. |
| `exyliawarzone.duelrooms.bypass` | operators | Being left alone by duel rooms: walking into one never makes you a fighter and you are never sent out, so staff can watch from inside. It does not let you hit fighters, be hit by them or break the border. |

Everything a player does in the world needs no permission: grabbing a power-up, opening a loot chest that is ready,
looting a supply drop crate and fighting in a duel room.

<Callout type="warning" title="Operators are not drawn into duels">
Because `exyliawarzone.duelrooms.bypass` defaults to op, an operator walking into a duel room is never made a fighter.
Test rooms with accounts that are not op, or negate the node for yourself.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="The bypass nodes are not in the admin node">
`exyliawarzone.admin` holds the six `.admin` nodes but neither the duel room nor the loot chest bypass. Operators have both by default; a staff rank
that is not op needs them granted on their own.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Module staff and the hub">
A rank with only one module's node, such as `exyliawarzone.lootchests.admin`, uses that module's command and editor.
The editors' **Back** button to the hub and the hub itself need `exyliawarzone.admin`.
</Callout>

ExyliaLib's own nodes apply to what it provides: `exylialib.settings` for `/settings`, where players mute
announcements, `exylialib.logs` for the staff log and `exylialib.pendingrewards` for kept rewards.
