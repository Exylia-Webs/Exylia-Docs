Players need no setup: only `exyliawarzone.supplydrops.use` is given to everyone, and everything else a player does in the world (grabbing a power-up, opening a loot chest that is ready, looting a crate, fighting in a duel room) needs no permission. Everything else is for staff.

## All permissions

<Permissions />

With LuckPerms, give a builder one module only:

```text
/lp group builder permission set exyliawarzone.regenzones.admin true
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Operators are not drawn into duels">
`exyliawarzone.duelrooms.bypass` is op by default, so an operator who walks into a duel room is never made a fighter. Test rooms with accounts that are not op, or negate the node for yourself.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="Bypass nodes are not in the admin node">
`exyliawarzone.admin` holds the six `.admin` nodes but neither the duel room nor the loot chest bypass. A staff rank that is not op needs them granted on their own.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Module staff and the hub">
A rank with only one module's node, such as `exyliawarzone.lootchests.admin`, uses that module's command and editor. The editors' **Back** button to the hub, and the hub itself, need `exyliawarzone.admin`.
</Callout>

## Let one player off one banned-item rule

`exyliawarzone.banneditems.bypass` lets a player through every rule. To let them through one rule only, grant `exyliawarzone.banneditems.bypass.<rule id>`; the rule's menu shows its exact node. See [Banned items](/docs/exyliawarzone/banned-items).
