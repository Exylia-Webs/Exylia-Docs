In about ten minutes ExyliaWarzone gives your combat areas things worth fighting over, all built and edited in game: supply drops falling on a parachute, power-ups to grab, loot chests that refill themselves, zones that put themselves back, duel rooms that seal shut behind two fighters, and rules that ban items in chosen places. No resource pack and no WorldEdit commands: every crate, zone, chest and room is made from a menu.

It is a **premium** plugin, installed through a licence loader. See [Installation](/docs/exyliawarzone/installation), then [Your first supply drop](/docs/exyliawarzone/first-steps).

## What is included

Six modules, each switched on or off in `config.yml` (`modules:`, restart to apply), and the flags their areas carry. Turn off what your map
does not need.

## One hub

`/warzoneadmin` opens the admin hub: a card per module, each saying whether it runs (**ENABLED**, **DISABLED**, or
**NEEDS** plus the plugin it is missing, such as **NEEDS PACKETEVENTS**), and a **Settings** card that opens every setting of every module in a menu. Each module
also has its own command to jump straight to its editor, such as `/supplydropsadmin` or `/duelroomsadmin`, so a
builder can be given one module without the rest.

## Built for real servers

- **Folia.** Every crate, power-up, chest, zone and room runs on the thread of the region it is in.
- **Networks.** Everything is stored in the database `database.yml` points at. Supply drop tiers and loot chest
  templates are shared by every server on it; envoys, zones, chests and rooms belong to the server they were made on.
- **Rewards are never lost.** What does not fit in a full inventory is kept and handed over on the player's next
  join. Money, experience and items from power-ups and duel rooms go through ExyliaLib's boosters.
- **Players choose.** Supply drop and duel announcements can be muted by each player in `/settings`.
- **Six languages.** English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish ship with the plugin. See [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.1 or newer, or Folia 1.21.4 or newer. |
| Java | 21. |
| ExyliaLib | Required, 1.567.0 or newer; installed by the loader. |
| PacketEvents | Optional. Power-ups are drawn with packets, so the **Power-ups** module stays off without it (its hub card reads **NEEDS PACKETEVENTS**). Without it a sealed duel room's world-border overlay is not drawn either; every other module works. |
| Optional | FastAsyncWorldEdit (faster schematics; ExyliaLib's own engine works without it), PlaceholderAPI, ExyliaEconomy or Vault, ExyliaSurvivalCore (statistics). |

## Coming from ExyliaSurvivalCore

These six features used to live in ExyliaSurvivalCore. ExyliaWarzone reads the same tables, copies the old schematics and imports its banned-item rules, so what you built comes along. See [Moving from ExyliaSurvivalCore](/docs/exyliawarzone/migrating).
