ExyliaWarzone fills your combat areas with things worth fighting over, all built and edited in game: supply drops
falling from the sky on a parachute, power-ups to grab, loot chests that refill themselves, zones that put themselves
back, and duel rooms that seal shut behind two fighters. No resource pack and no WorldEdit commands: every crate, zone,
chest and room is made from a menu.

It is a **premium** plugin, installed through a licence loader. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliawarzone/installation).

## What is included

Five modules, each switched on or off in `config.yml`, and the flags their areas carry. Turn off what your map
does not need.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Supply drops" href="/docs/exyliawarzone/supply-drops">
    Crates on a parachute, announced with a beam and a boss bar, locked for a moment after landing. Tiers with their
    own odds and loot, envoys that drop several at once on a timetable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Power-ups" href="/docs/exyliawarzone/power-ups">
    Spinning items at fixed points or anywhere in an area. The first player to walk into one takes its potion effects
    and rewards.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Loot chests" href="/docs/exyliawarzone/loot-chests">
    Any chest or barrel refilled from a template's loot table on a cooldown, with a hologram counting down.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Regen zones" href="/docs/exyliawarzone/regen-zones">
    Areas saved once and pasted back on a fixed timer with a warning, or once nobody has touched them for a while.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Duel rooms" href="/docs/exyliawarzone/duel-rooms">
    Enclosed rooms where a fight starts when enough players walk in: the border closes, the last one standing is paid
    and the room rebuilds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Region flags" href="/docs/exyliawarzone/region-flags">
    PvP, building, block lists, temporary blocks, drops, fall damage and keep inventory for every zone and room.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## One hub

`/warzoneadmin` opens the admin hub: a card per module, each saying whether it runs (**Enabled**, **Disabled**, or
the plugin it is missing), and a **Settings** card that opens every setting of every module in a menu. Each module
also has its own command to jump straight to its editor, such as `/supplydropsadmin` or `/duelroomsadmin`, so a
builder can be given one module without the rest.

## Built for real servers

- **Folia.** Every crate, power-up, chest, zone and room runs on the thread of the region it is in.
- **Networks.** Everything is stored in the database `database.yml` points at. Supply drop tiers and loot chest
  templates are shared by every server on it; envoys, zones, chests and rooms belong to the server they were made on.
- **Rewards are never lost.** What does not fit in a full inventory is kept and handed over on the player's next
  join. Money, experience and items from power-ups and duel rooms go through ExyliaLib's boosters.
- **Players choose.** Supply drop and duel announcements can be muted by each player in `/settings`.
- **Six languages.** English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish ship with the plugin.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.1 or newer, or Folia 1.21.4 or newer. |
| Java | 21. |
| ExyliaLib | Required, 1.481.0 or newer; installed by the loader. |
| PacketEvents | Required by ExyliaLib; power-ups and the world-border overlay of a sealed duel room are drawn with it. |
| Optional | FastAsyncWorldEdit (faster schematics; ExyliaLib's own engine works without it), PlaceholderAPI, ExyliaEconomy or Vault, ExyliaSurvivalCore (statistics). |

## Coming from ExyliaSurvivalCore

These five modules used to live in ExyliaSurvivalCore. ExyliaWarzone reads the same tables and copies the old
schematics, so what you built comes along. See [Moving from ExyliaSurvivalCore](/docs/exyliawarzone/migrating).
