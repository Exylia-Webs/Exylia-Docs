Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## "No free spot found" when an envoy runs

Check, in this order:

1. **The area's height.** The two corners set the heights a crate can land at. If both were clicked on the same flat ground there is no room for a crate; select the area again with one corner well above the ground.
2. **The ground.** A spot needs solid ground (not bedrock) with two blocks of air above, outside every regen zone, duel room and ExyliaProtections claim.
3. **The limit.** At most `max-active` crates (16 by default) can be out on a server at once, envoys included.
4. **The envoy and its tiers.** A disabled envoy, an envoy that is already running, or one whose allowed tiers are all disabled drops nothing and gives the same message.

See [Supply drops](/docs/exyliawarzone/supply-drops).

## A power-up zone shows nothing

Open the zone: its top card says **Spawning** and either ready or what is missing (no power-ups, no area, no spawn points). A ready zone places its first power-up at once, then one per interval. Check that the zone is enabled, the **Power-ups** card in `/warzoneadmin` reads **ENABLED**, and, in **Area** mode, that the area's chunks are loaded. Without `packetevents` power-ups are not drawn. See [Power-ups](/docs/exyliawarzone/power-ups).

## An operator walks into a duel room and nothing happens

`exyliawarzone.duelrooms.bypass` is op by default, so operators are never made fighters. Test with accounts that are not op, or negate the node for yourself. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions).

## A builder with one module's permission cannot go back to the hub

The hub and the **Back** buttons of the editors need `exyliawarzone.admin`. Someone with only `exyliawarzone.lootchests.admin` works from `/lootchestsadmin` directly.

## I can build inside a zone that denies building

Players in creative mode are never held by region flags, so staff can work on an area as it is. Switch to survival to test. See [Region flags](/docs/exyliawarzone/region-flags).

## A module's command or card says the feature is disabled

The module is switched off in `config.yml` under `modules:`, or it needs a plugin that is missing; the hub card says which. Switching a module on or off needs a restart, `/warzoneadmin reload` is not enough.

## My edits to the admin menus disappeared

The screens under `lang/<language>/menus/admin/` are written again from the jar on every start. Chat lines and the labels the screens fill in come from the messages files, which are kept. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration).

## The placeholder shows as text

Install [PlaceholderAPI](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/placeholderapi.6245/) and make sure the supply drops module is enabled. The other modules have no placeholders. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliawarzone/placeholders).
