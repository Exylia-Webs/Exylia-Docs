Loot chests are chests and barrels in your world that fill themselves from a loot table. A player loots one, it empties, and after its cooldown it refills on its own, with a hologram above it counting down. You set them up in game with `/lootchestsadmin` (or the **Loot chests** card of `/warzoneadmin`).

Two things make it up:

- **Template**: an id, a display name and a loot table. It decides what a refill can put in a chest.
- **Loot chest**: a block registered from a template, with its own cooldown, fill mode and state.

## Before you start

- Your permission: `exyliawarzone.lootchests.admin`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions).
- A chest, trapped chest or barrel in the world (any chest block except an ender chest). You can also place chests for the template while you register them.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a template">
    Run `/lootchestsadmin`, click **Templates**, then **Template** (the emerald), and type an id such as `common`. Ids are lowercase (spaces become `_`), up to 64 characters. The template screen opens. **Display name** sets what the admin screens call it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill its loot table">
    Click **Loot table**. This is the [loot editor](/docs/exylialib/crates-and-loot): **An item** adds one line, **Everything in a chest** asks you to click a container and turns every item in it into a line. Edit a line to set its amounts and weight, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the chests">
    Click **Place chests** and type the cooldown every chest you place now will have (`5m`, `1h`, `0s` for none). Then click each chest or barrel: it becomes a loot chest of this template and fills at once. Keep clicking to place more; type `cancel` in chat to stop and the template screen comes back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Open one of the chests. You should see items and a hologram **READY TO LOOT** above it. Take something and close it: the chest empties, the hologram changes to **Refills in 4m 12s** style countdown, and when it ends the chest refills with a short chime. Opening it during the countdown answers "This chest refills in …".

## Going further

### The loot table

Every refill rolls each line on its own. The weight is a percent chance: `100` (or more) always comes up, `25` in about one refill out of four. The stack size is picked between the line's least and most amount. If no line comes up, one is picked at random so a chest never fills empty. **Everything in a chest** gives lines at weight `100`, from 1 up to each stack's size. Tables hold items only, no commands or money. A change reaches each chest at its next refill.

### Looting and the cooldown

Anyone can open a ready loot chest; no permission is needed. A claim or region plugin that keeps a chest shut still keeps it shut.

- **Taking anything is the take.** When the player closes the chest, it is emptied, whatever they left behind included, and the cooldown starts. Opening and closing without taking leaves it as is.
- **While it refills** the chest refuses to open. With `settings.allow-open-on-cooldown`, or `exyliawarzone.lootchests.bypass`, it opens anyway, empty. The bypass skips only the refill cooldown: a disabled chest stays shut for everyone.
- A cooldown of `0s` refills the chest as soon as it is closed. The cooldown keeps counting while the server is off.

### Fill mode

On a placed chest, **Fill mode** switches **Dispersed** (the default for new chests, loot spread over random slots) and **Stacked** (packed from the first slot).

### Manage chests

| Screen | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Placed** (`/lootchestsadmin`) | Every loot chest of this server with its block, template, location, cooldown and state. Left-click edits, shift-left-click teleports you on top of it, right-click deletes. |
| **Loot chest** | **Status** (a disabled chest refuses to open), **Fill mode**, **Cooldown** (applies from the next take), **Teleport**, **Delete**. |
| **Templates** | Every template with its loot lines and placed chests. Left-click edits, right-click deletes. |
| **Template** | **Display name**, **Loot table**, **Place chests**, **Delete**. |

### Remove a chest

- **Delete** on the screens: after a confirm it stops being a loot chest and stays a plain chest, **keeping the loot it holds now**.
- **Sneak and break it** (admins): after a confirm its loot is cleared and the block breaks.
- **Delete a template**: its chests on this server are removed first and stay as plain chests.

<Callout type="warning" title="Deleting keeps the loot inside">
A chest deleted from the screens keeps its loot, and anyone can take it as from any chest. To take a loaded chest out of play, sneak-break it instead.
</Callout>

Nobody else can break a loot chest. Explosions, pistons and fire leave it alone, hoppers and droppers cannot pull from it or push into it, and a sell wand never sells its contents.

<Details title="Technical details">

**Double chests** are one loot chest: register either half and both open, refill and are protected together, filling all 54 slots. Make a double chest before registering it; one placed against a loot chest later joins it. The block's old contents are replaced on registration.

**Hologram.** Ready: **READY TO LOOT**. Refilling: **Refills in 4m 12s**. Disabled: **DISABLED**. It floats `settings.hologram-offset-y` (1.5) blocks above the chest, is seen up to 48 blocks away and updates every second. Switch it off with `settings.hologram-enabled: false`; a change reaches standing chests after a restart. The lines are the `hologram` section of the messages file.

**Edge cases.** A player who logs out with the chest open counts as closing it. A chest whose cooldown ended while the server was off refills at startup; one in an unloaded chunk refills when it loads. A refill plays `sounds.refill` to players within 10 blocks. Anything a player puts inside is cleared at the next refill. A ready chest emptied by something else (a command, an edit) is filled again when a player opens it. Inside a [regen zone](/docs/exyliawarzone/regen-zones) or [duel room](/docs/exyliawarzone/duel-rooms), a paste puts each loot chest back in its own state.

**Networks.** Loot chests belong to the server they were placed on. Templates are stored once for every server on the same database; another server picks up a change when it restarts.

**Statistics and logs.** With ExyliaSurvivalCore installed, a take counts `lootchests.opened` and `lootchests.<template>.opened`. Every placement, removal and template deletion is in `/exylialib logs`.

**Files.** Settings: `modules/loot-chests/config.yml` ([Configuration](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration#loot-chests), or the **Settings** button of `/warzoneadmin`). Messages: `lang/<code>/modules/loot-chests/messages.yml` ([Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages)). Chests built with ExyliaSurvivalCore carry over: see [Move from ExyliaSurvivalCore](/docs/exyliawarzone/migrating).

**Permissions.** `exyliawarzone.lootchests.admin` (op): `/lootchestsadmin`, every screen and removing a chest by sneak-breaking it. `exyliawarzone.lootchests.bypass` (op): opening a chest that is still refilling. `exyliawarzone.admin` includes the admin node but not the bypass. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions).

</Details>
