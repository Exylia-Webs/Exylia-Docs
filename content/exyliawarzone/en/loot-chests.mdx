Loot chests are chests and barrels in your world that fill themselves from a loot table. A player loots one, it
empties, and after its cooldown it refills on its own, with a hologram above it counting down. Everything is set up
in game with `/lootchestsadmin` (or the loot chests card of `/warzoneadmin`).

Two things make it up:

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Template** | An ID, a display name and a loot table. It decides what a refill can put in a chest. |
| **Loot chest** | A block registered from a template. Each one has its own cooldown, fill mode and state. |

## Setting up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a template">
    Open `/lootchestsadmin`, click **Templates**, then **Create template**, and type an ID such as `common`. IDs
    are lowercase (spaces become `_`) and up to 64 characters. The template screen opens. **Display name** sets
    what the admin screens call it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill its loot table">
    Click **Loot table**. This is the ExyliaLib loot editor: **An item** adds one line, **Everything in a chest**
    asks you to click a container and turns every item in it into a line. Edit a line to set its amounts and
    weight, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the chests">
    Click **Place chests** and type the cooldown every chest you place now will have (`5m`, `1h`, `0s` for none).
    Then click each chest or barrel: it becomes a loot chest of this template and fills at once. Keep clicking to
    place more; type `cancel` in chat to stop, and the template screen comes back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The loot table

Every refill rolls each line on its own. The weight is a percent chance: `100` (or more) always comes up, `25` in
about one refill out of four. The stack size is picked between the line's least and most amount. If no line comes
up, one is picked at random so a chest with a loot table never fills empty. A chest imported with **Everything in a
chest** gives lines at weight `100`, from 1 up to each stack's size.

Loot chest tables hold items only, no commands or money. Copied lines paste into any other ExyliaLib loot editor.
A change to the table reaches each chest at its next refill.

## Which blocks

A chest, a trapped chest or a barrel (any chest block except an ender chest). Clicking anything else answers
"Only a chest or a barrel can become a loot chest."

- **Double chests are one loot chest.** Register either half: both halves open, refill and are protected
  together, and a refill fills all 54 slots. The other half cannot be registered a second time.
- **What the block held is replaced.** A chest fills from its template the moment it is placed.
- Make a double chest before you register it: a chest placed against a loot chest later joins it.

## Looting and the cooldown

Anyone can open a loot chest that is ready; no permission is needed. A claim or region plugin that keeps a chest
shut still keeps it shut.

- **Taking anything is the take.** When the player closes the chest, it is emptied, whatever they left behind
  included, and its cooldown starts. Opening and closing it without taking anything leaves it as it is.
- A player who logs out with the chest open counts as closing it.
- **While it refills** the chest refuses to open: "This chest refills in 4m 12s ⌚." With
  `settings.allow-open-on-cooldown`, or the bypass permission, it opens anyway, empty.
- A cooldown of `0s` refills the chest as soon as the player closes it.
- The cooldown keeps counting while the server is off. A chest whose cooldown ended meanwhile refills at startup,
  and one whose chunk is unloaded refills the moment its chunk loads.
- A refill plays a short chime (`sounds.refill`) to players within 10 blocks.
- Anything a player puts into a loot chest is cleared at its next refill.
- A ready chest that something other than a loot emptied (a command, an edit) is filled again the moment a player
  opens it, so it never opens empty while its hologram says it is ready.
- Inside a [regen zone](/docs/exyliawarzone/regen-zones) or a [duel room](/docs/exyliawarzone/duel-rooms), a paste
  puts each loot chest back in its own state: empty while it refills, filled again if it is ready.

**Fill mode** decides where the loot lands: **Dispersed** (the default for new chests) spreads it over random slots,
**Stacked** packs it from the first slot.

## The hologram

| State | Hologram |
|---|---|
| Ready | **READY TO LOOT** |
| Refilling | Refills in 4m 12s ⌚ |
| Disabled | **DISABLED** |

It floats `settings.hologram-offset-y` (1.5) blocks above the chest, is seen from up to 48 blocks away and updates
every second. The three lines are the `hologram` section of the module's messages file. Turn holograms off with
`settings.hologram-enabled: false`; a change to either hologram setting reaches chests already standing after a
restart.

## Managing chests

| Screen | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Placed** (`/lootchestsadmin`) | Every loot chest of this server, oldest first, with its block, template, location, cooldown, fill mode and live state. Left-click edits, shift-left-click teleports you on top of it, right-click deletes. |
| **Loot chest** | **Status** turns it on or off, **Fill mode** switches Dispersed and Stacked, **Cooldown** changes it (from the next take on), **Teleport**, **Delete**. |
| **Templates** | Every template with its loot lines and placed chests. Left-click edits, right-click deletes. |
| **Template** | **Display name**, **Loot table**, **Place chests**, **Delete**. |

A disabled chest refuses to open for everyone, bypass included, and its hologram reads **DISABLED**.

## Removing a chest

| How | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Delete** in the screens | After a confirm, it stops being a loot chest and stays as a plain chest, with whatever it holds right now. |
| Sneak and break it (admins) | After a confirm, its loot is cleared and the block breaks, dropping itself. |
| Delete its template | The template's chests on this server are removed first and stay as plain chests. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Deleting keeps the loot inside">
A chest deleted from the screens keeps the loot it holds, and anyone can take it as from any chest. To take a
loaded chest out of play, sneak-break it instead.
</Callout>

Nobody else can break a loot chest. A player is told "Loot chests cannot be broken."; an admin who breaks one
without sneaking is told to sneak. Explosions, pistons and fire leave it alone, hoppers, droppers and hopper
minecarts cannot pull from it or push into it, and a sell wand never sells its contents: the click opens it as a
plain open would.

## Permissions

| Permission | Default | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliawarzone.lootchests.admin` | op | `/lootchestsadmin`, every loot chest screen, and removing a chest by sneak-breaking it. |
| `exyliawarzone.lootchests.bypass` | op | Opening a chest that is still refilling (it opens empty). |

`exyliawarzone.admin` includes the admin node but not the bypass. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions).

## Networks, statistics and logs

Loot chests belong to the server they were placed on: each server loads and lists only its own. Templates are
stored once for every server on the same database; another server picks up a new or edited template when it
restarts.

With ExyliaSurvivalCore installed, a take counts the player's `lootchests.opened` and
`lootchests.<template>.opened` statistics, which its missions and leaderboards can use. Every placement, removal
and template deletion is written to `/exylialib logs`.

Settings live in `modules/loot-chests/config.yml` and messages in `lang/<code>/modules/loot-chests/messages.yml`.
Every key is on the [configuration page](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration#loot-chests), and the **Settings**
button of `/warzoneadmin` edits them in game. Chests built with ExyliaSurvivalCore carry over: see
[Migrating](/docs/exyliawarzone/migrating).
