A duel room is an area of your world that turns into a closed arena by itself. Players walk in; once enough enemies are inside, the room's faces fill with a border block, a countdown runs and they fight until one is left standing. The winner collects the loot and the room's rewards, then the room opens and rebuilds itself for the next duel. There are no spawns to set and no player command: walking in is joining.

## Before you start

- The module is on by default (`modules.duel-rooms` in `config.yml`).
- Your permission: `exyliawarzone.duelrooms.admin` (included in `exyliawarzone.admin`). See [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions).
- A built arena: at least 3×4×3 blocks, with floor, walls and ceiling.
- PacketEvents is optional here: it only draws a decorative world border for the fighters.

## Steps

Open the editor with `/duelroomsadmin`, or the **Duel rooms** card of `/warzoneadmin`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the room">
    Click **Duel room** (the emerald) and type an id: lowercase, unique across every server sharing the database.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the region">
    **Arena**, then **Room region**. You get the selector: left-click one corner, right-click the other, shift + left-click to confirm. The box is the whole arena: its bottom layer is the floor, its top layer the ceiling, its outer columns the walls. At most `max-border-blocks` (20000) blocks on its faces. See [Selecting regions](/docs/exylialib/regions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the exit">
    **Arena**, then **Exit location**: stand outside the room and sneak-click. Extra players, intruders and the winner are sent here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the schematic">
    **Arena**, then **Schematic**. It saves the arena's blocks as they are now; every duel ends by pasting it back. Save it while the room is clean and empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune it and switch it on">
    **General** sets the **Display name** (titles, boss bars, announcements), **Players needed** (2 to 16, default 2) and the **Border block** (any solid block, default `RED_STAINED_GLASS`). Then click **Status** at the top of the room's screen. A room enables only once it has a region and an exit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Testing as an operator">
`exyliawarzone.duelrooms.bypass` defaults to op. A player holding it is never drawn into a duel and never sent out, so an operator walking into a room sees nothing happen. Test with non-op accounts, or negate the permission.
</Callout>

## Check that it works

With two non-op accounts (or two clans' worth of players), walk both into the room. A boss bar fills as players arrive; the border blocks appear on the faces, a countdown of 3 seconds runs, then **FIGHT**. When one is left, the winner sees **VICTORY**, has 5 seconds to loot, and is sent to the exit as the room reopens and rebuilds. The room list and each room's screen show what it is doing: **Idle**, **Waiting**, **Closing**, **Fighting**, **Looting** or **Reopening**.

## Going further

### Who fights

Everyone who walks in waits. Only living players in survival or adventure without the bypass permission count. When enough are waiting, the fighters are drawn at random, **never two from the same clan or from allied clans** (any clan plugin ExyliaLib supports; without one, everyone counts). Everyone else inside is sent to the exit, and so is anyone who walks in or logs in later. Bypass holders can stay and watch but cannot hit or be hit.

### Winner rewards

Click **Winner rewards** to open the [reward editor](/docs/exylialib/rewards): items, money, experience, commands. They are paid a moment after the win through ExyliaLib's [modifiers](/docs/exylialib/economy) under the source `duel-rooms`, so booster plugins can multiply them. What does not fit, or what a winner who logged off is owed, is handed over on their next join.

A win pays nothing, and is not counted, when the same two players had a paid win against each other within `win-reward-cooldown-seconds` (30 minutes, any server of the network), the opponent shares the winner's address (or the database cannot tell), or an opponent left within `forfeit-grace-seconds` (30 seconds) of the fight starting. A paid win counts as `duels.wins` for ExyliaSurvivalCore when installed.

### Timings and rules per room

Click **Timings & rules**. Each of the eight settings shows whether **This room** sets it or the **Config file** does; a setting the room never set follows the file.

| Button | Key | Default | Click |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Countdown** | `countdown-seconds` | `3s` | Left-click to change, shift-click to follow the file again. |
| **Fight limit** | `max-duration-seconds` | `3m` | The same; `0` is no limit. |
| **Inactivity limit** | `inactivity-seconds` | `30s` | The same; `0` is off. |
| **Loot window** | `loot-seconds` | `5s` | The same. |
| **Winner leaves** | `teleport-winner-out` | on | Cycles on, off, config file. |
| **No flight** | `disable-flight` | on | Cycles on, off, config file. |
| **Kill on leave** | `kill-on-leave` | on | Cycles on, off, config file. |
| **Stalemate** | `stalemate` | `DRAW` | Cycles the three modes, then config file. |

**Duel effects** gives each fighter potion effects for the fight (`-1` lasts the whole fight). The allowed commands, announcements, border block limit, drawn world border, reward cooldown and forfeit grace are server-wide: see [Configuration](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration#duel-rooms) or **Settings** in `/warzoneadmin`.

### Stalemates

A fight stalls when the fight limit runs out (3 minutes) or nobody lands a hit for the inactivity limit (30 seconds). Fighters are warned 10 seconds before the inactivity limit. The room's stalemate mode decides:

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| `DRAW` (default) | No winner; everyone is sent out when the room opens. |
| `SUDDEN_DEATH` | Every fighter loses half a heart more each second than the second before, until one falls. After 60 seconds without a result it is a draw. |
| `MOST_DAMAGE` | Whoever dealt strictly the most damage wins and the rest are killed, so their loot drops. A tie, or no damage, is a draw. |

### Flags and announcements

A room's region carries [region flags](/docs/exyliawarzone/region-flags): click **Room flags** once the region is selected. Leave `KEEP_INVENTORY` off and `ITEM_PICKUP` allowed if the winner should loot; a `PVP` flag that denies combat does not stop a running duel. With `broadcast-results` on, every win is announced to this server. Players mute it in `/settings`, under **Announcements**, as **Duel announcements**; it starts on.

<Details title="Technical details">

**The border.** Real blocks on the six faces of the region. Only air and replaceable blocks (grass, flowers, snow) are filled, so an existing floor or wall stays; fighters standing on a face move one block inward first. During a duel nothing on the faces can be broken, blown up, burned, pushed by a piston or taken by a mob. With `draw-world-border` on, fighters also see a harmless world border (needs PacketEvents; world borders are square, so a long room's short walls have no drawn edge). Walls are recorded in `walls/`: after a crash, every wall left standing comes down on the next start and its arena is rebuilt.

**The fight.** Countdown (`countdown-seconds`): hits do nothing yet. Fight: effects are given and flight taken away. Loot window (`loot-seconds`): VICTORY for the last one standing, DEFEAT for the rest. Reopening: the border comes down; on a draw everyone left is sent to the exit; the winner too while `teleport-winner-out` is on. Fighters can hit each other even where a region flag, spawn protection or WorldGuard would stop it. Until the fight is decided commands are blocked except `allowed-commands` (`msg`, `tell`, `w`, `r`, `reply`) and any teleport out is cancelled. A fighter's own effects of the duel's types are set aside and given back afterwards.

**Endings.** A fighter who dies during the countdown, disconnects or leaves the room cancels the duel; during the fight a death eliminates them (drops go to the winner), a disconnect or leaving kills them with `kill-on-leave` on (otherwise they are just out), and being put in creative or spectator takes them out without killing. A called-off duel brings the border down, rebuilds the arena and lets whoever is inside wait again. If the winner falls within a second of the last loser it is a **double knockout**: nobody wins and nobody is paid. Any other death inside a duel room keeps inventory and levels.

**Rebuilding.** After every duel the room pastes `schematics/duelroom_<id>.schem` back over its region, through FastAsyncWorldEdit when installed or ExyliaLib's built-in engine otherwise, waiting at most 60 seconds so it always reopens. A room without a schematic keeps whatever the fight did to it. **Regenerate now** pastes on demand; deleting a room deletes its schematic.

**Editing a room in use.** Changing the region, the exit or the players needed, disabling it or deleting it stops its running duel and empties its queue. Name, border block, effects, rewards, timings and flags can change mid-duel; the running duel keeps the values it started with, and flag changes apply at once. Each server lists only the rooms it created. Messages and titles: `lang/<code>/modules/duel-rooms/messages.yml`, titles and bars under `visuals` ([Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages)).

</Details>
