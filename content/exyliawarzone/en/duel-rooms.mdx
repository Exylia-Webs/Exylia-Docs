title: "Duel rooms"

A duel room is an area of your world that turns into a closed arena by itself. Players walk in; once enough
enemies are inside, the room's faces fill with a border block, a countdown runs, and they fight until one is left
standing. The winner collects the loot and the room's rewards, then the room opens and rebuilds itself for the
next duel. There are no spawns to set and no player command: walking in is joining.

The module is **on by default** (`modules.duel-rooms` in `config.yml`). Rooms are built in game with
`/duelroomsadmin` (`exyliawarzone.duelrooms.admin`), or from the **Duel rooms** card of `/warzoneadmin`.

## Setting up a room

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    `/duelroomsadmin` → **Create**, then type an ID: lowercase, and unique across every server sharing the database.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the region">
    **Arena** → **Room region** hands you the selector: left-click one corner, right-click the other, shift +
    left-click to confirm. The box is the whole arena: its bottom layer is the floor, its top layer the ceiling, its
    outer columns the walls. At least 3×4×3 blocks, with at most `max-border-blocks` (20000) blocks on its faces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the exit">
    **Arena** → **Exit location**: stand outside the room and sneak-click. Extra players, intruders and the winner
    are sent here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the schematic">
    **Arena** → **Schematic** saves the arena's blocks as they are now; every duel ends by pasting it back. Save it
    while the room is clean and empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune it and enable it">
    **General** sets the display name (shown in titles, boss bars and announcements), **Players needed** (2 to 16,
    default 2) and the **Border block** (any solid block, default `RED_STAINED_GLASS`). Then click the **Status**
    card at the top of the room's screen; a room enables only once it has a region and an exit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Testing as an operator">
`exyliawarzone.duelrooms.bypass` defaults to op. A player holding it is never drawn into a duel and never sent
out, so an operator walking into a room sees nothing happen. Test with non-op accounts, or negate the permission.
</Callout>

The room list and each room's screen show, live, what the room is doing: **Idle**, **Waiting**, **Closing** (the
countdown), **Fighting**, **Looting** or **Reopening** (the border coming down and the arena being rebuilt).

## Who fights

Everyone who walks in waits, with a boss bar filling as players arrive. Only living players in survival or
adventure without the bypass permission count. When enough are waiting, the fighters are drawn at random, **never
two from the same clan or from allied clans** (any clan plugin ExyliaLib supports; without one, everyone counts).
Three clanmates and one outsider make a duel of two, and a player whose waiting company is all on their side is told
only enemies fight here.

Once the duel is drawn, everyone else inside is sent to the exit. Anyone walking in later or logging in inside is
sent out too, and once a second the room clears whoever got in some other way. Bypass holders can stay and watch,
but cannot hit the fighters or be hit by them.

## The border

The border is real blocks placed on the six faces of the region. Only air and replaceable blocks (grass, flowers,
snow) are filled, so an existing floor or wall stays. Fighters standing on a face are moved one block inward first.
While a duel runs, nothing on the room's faces can be broken, blown up, burned, pushed by a piston or taken by a
mob: digging out is leaving, and so is digging in.

With `draw-world-border` on, the fighters also see a harmless world border drawn over the walls. It only draws, and
world borders are square, so a long room's short walls have no drawn edge; turn it off to save client FPS. Walls
are recorded in the plugin's `walls/` folder: after a crash, every wall left standing comes down on the next start
and its arena is rebuilt before the room may close again.

## The fight

1. **Countdown** (`countdown-seconds`, 3s): a title and a short sound each second. Hits do nothing yet.
2. **Fight**: a FIGHT title, the room's duel effects are given and flight is taken away (`disable-flight`). A boss
   bar shows the time left and how many are alive.
3. **Loot window** (`loot-seconds`, 5s): VICTORY for the last one standing, DEFEAT for the rest. The winner's boss
   bar counts down to the room opening.
4. **Reopening**: the border comes down. On a draw everyone left is sent to the exit; the winner is too while
   `teleport-winner-out` is on. The arena is rebuilt, and whoever is still inside starts waiting again.

Fighters can hit each other even where a region flag, spawn protection or WorldGuard would stop it. From the
countdown until the fight is decided, commands are blocked except `allowed-commands` (`msg`, `tell`, `w`, `r`,
`reply`), and any teleport out of the room is cancelled: `/spawn`, chorus fruit, portals, other plugins.

**Duel effects** each have their own length; `-1` lasts the whole fight. A fighter's own effects of those types are
set aside and given back afterwards, totems do not clear the duel's, and every effect is capped to the fight limit
plus the countdown and 30 seconds, so a crash never leaves anyone buffed.

## Stalemates

A fight stalls when the fight limit runs out (`max-duration-seconds`, 3 minutes) or nobody lands a hit for the
inactivity limit (`inactivity-seconds`, 30 seconds), which catches standing still, AFK, running away and refusing to
fight. Fighters are warned 10 seconds before the inactivity limit, or halfway through a shorter one. Any hit with
damage counts, even one absorbed by golden-apple hearts, a shield or armor. Either limit at `0` is off. The room's
`stalemate` mode decides:

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| `DRAW` (default) | No winner; everyone is sent out when the room opens. |
| `SUDDEN_DEATH` | Every fighter loses half a heart more each second than the second before, through armor, resistance and totems, until one falls. After 60 seconds without a result it is a draw. |
| `MOST_DAMAGE` | Whoever dealt strictly the most damage wins and the rest are killed, so their loot drops. A tie, or no damage at all, is a draw. |

## Leaving, dying and other endings

| A fighter... | During the countdown | During the fight |
|---|---|---|
| Dies | The duel is called off. | Eliminated; their drops are the winner's loot. |
| Disconnects | The duel is called off. | Killed on the spot with `kill-on-leave` on, so their loot drops; otherwise just out. |
| Gets out of the room | The duel is called off. | Killed with `kill-on-leave` on; otherwise out, told they forfeited. |
| Is put in creative or spectator | The duel is called off. | Out of the fight, never killed. |

A called-off duel brings the border down, rebuilds the arena and lets whoever is inside wait again; so does a
border that is still not up 15 seconds after the countdown. If the winner falls within a second of the last loser,
it is a **double knockout**: nobody wins and nobody is paid.

A death in the fight drops as usual, and graves plugins leave it alone so the drops stay for the winner. **Any other
death inside a duel room keeps the inventory and levels**: while waiting, in the countdown, in the loot window, or
with no duel running.

## Winner rewards

**Winner rewards** opens ExyliaLib's reward editor: items, money, experience, commands and more. They are paid a
moment after the win through ExyliaLib's modifiers under the source `duel-rooms`, so booster plugins can multiply the
money, experience and drops. What does not fit, or what a winner who already logged off is owed, is handed over on
their next join.

A win pays nothing, and is not counted, when:

- the same two players had a paid win against each other within `win-reward-cooldown-seconds` (30 minutes), either
  way round and on any server of the network;
- the opponent shares the winner's address, or the database cannot tell;
- an opponent disconnected or left within `forfeit-grace-seconds` (30 seconds) of the fight starting.

When the room has rewards, the winner is told the win paid nothing. A paid win counts as `duels.wins` for
ExyliaSurvivalCore's missions, leaderboards and seasons when it is installed.

## Announcements

With `broadcast-results` on, every win is announced to this server: "Steve won a duel against Alex in Pit". Players
mute it in `/settings` → **Announcements** → ExyliaWarzone → **Duel announcements**; it starts on.

## Rebuilding the arena

After every duel, fought or called off, the room pastes `schematics/duelroom_<id>.schem` back over its region
before it can close again. FastAsyncWorldEdit does it when installed, ExyliaLib's built-in engine otherwise; both
read the same files. The room waits at most 60 seconds for the paste, so it always reopens. A room without a
schematic keeps whatever the fight did to it. **Regenerate now** pastes it on demand, and deleting a room deletes
its schematic.

## Per-room timings and rules

**Timings & rules** shows eight settings and, for each, whether **This room** sets it or the **Config file** does.
A setting the room never set follows the file, edits included.

| Button | Config key | Default | Click |
|---|---|---|---|
| Countdown | `countdown-seconds` | `3s` | Left-click to change, shift-click to follow the file again. |
| Fight limit | `max-duration-seconds` | `3m` | The same; `0` is no limit. |
| Inactivity limit | `inactivity-seconds` | `30s` | The same; `0` is off. |
| Loot window | `loot-seconds` | `5s` | The same. |
| Winner leaves | `teleport-winner-out` | on | Cycles on, off, config file. |
| No flight | `disable-flight` | on | Cycles on, off, config file. |
| Kill on leave | `kill-on-leave` | on | Cycles on, off, config file. |
| Stalemate | `stalemate` | `DRAW` | Cycles the three modes, then config file. |

The allowed commands, announcements, border block limit, drawn world border, reward cooldown and forfeit grace are
server-wide: see [Configuration](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration#duel-rooms).

## Room flags

A room's region can carry region flags: **Room flags** on its screen, once the region is selected. They are
explained on [Region flags](/docs/exyliawarzone/region-flags). Leave `KEEP_INVENTORY` off and `ITEM_PICKUP` allowed
if the winner should loot; a `PVP` flag that denies combat does not stop a running duel.

## Editing a room in use

Changing the region, the exit or the players needed, disabling the room or deleting it stops its running duel and
empties its queue. The name, border block, effects, rewards, timings and flags can change mid-duel: the running duel
keeps the values it started with, and flag changes apply at once. Each server lists only the rooms it created, even
on a shared database. Messages, titles and boss bars are in `lang/<language>/modules/duel-rooms/messages.yml`, the
titles and bars under `visuals`.
