---
title: "Configuration"
description: "Every key of config.yml and of each module's config.yml with its default, plus the messages and menu files."
---

ExyliaWarzone keeps its settings in files, and every one of them is also editable in game: the **Settings** card of
`/warzoneadmin` (or `/warzoneadmin settings`) opens each file's keys in a menu, with a short explanation of each. What
you build (tiers, envoys, zones, chests, rooms) is not in these files: it is made in the editors and stored in the
[database](/docs/exyliawarzone/installation#the-database).

Apply a hand edit with `/warzoneadmin reload`. Switching a module on or off needs a restart.

## config.yml

`plugins/ExyliaWarzone/config.yml`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `language` | `default` | Language of the plugin's menus and messages. `default` follows the language set in ExyliaLib's `config.yml`; a code such as `en` or `es` sets this plugin alone. Each is a folder under `lang/` you can edit. |
| `modules.supply-drops` | `true` | Load the [supply drops](/docs/exyliawarzone/supply-drops) module. |
| `modules.powerups` | `true` | Load the [power-ups](/docs/exyliawarzone/power-ups) module. |
| `modules.loot-chests` | `true` | Load the [loot chests](/docs/exyliawarzone/loot-chests) module. |
| `modules.regen-zones` | `true` | Load the [regen zones](/docs/exyliawarzone/regen-zones) module. |
| `modules.duel-rooms` | `true` | Load the [duel rooms](/docs/exyliawarzone/duel-rooms) module. |
| `modules.banned-items` | `true` | Load the [banned items](/docs/exyliawarzone/banned-items) module. Its messages are in `lang/<code>/modules/banned-items/messages.yml`; it has no other file, since rules live in the database. |

A module switched off registers no command and no screen. A module switched on whose required plugin is
missing stays off and its hub card says which plugin it needs.

## Supply drops

`plugins/ExyliaWarzone/modules/supply-drops/config.yml`. Tiers and envoys are not in this file: they are edited in
game with `/supplydropsadmin` and stored in the database. Times are seconds; `90s` or `5m` also work, and the file
writes them back as seconds. A tier or an envoy can override the three timings, see
[Supply drops](/docs/exyliawarzone/supply-drops#timings).

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.announce-seconds` | `60` | Seconds between the announcement and the landing. An envoy run is announced for at least 5. |
| `settings.unlock-seconds` | `20` | Seconds a landed crate stays locked. |
| `settings.despawn-seconds` | `300` | Seconds a crate stays after landing, locked time included, before it vanishes with what is left. At least 10. |
| `settings.view-radius` | `160.0` | Blocks around the spot within which players see the beam, the fall and the other animations. |
| `settings.crate-block` | `BARREL` | The block a crate lands as, unless its tier picks another. It must hold items (`BARREL`, `CHEST`...). |
| `settings.attempts` | `20` | Columns tried inside an envoy's area for each crate before it gives up. |
| `settings.max-active` | `16` | Most crates out at once on this server, falling and landed, envoys included. |
| `animation.marker` | beam, ground ring, pulse, beacon sound | Plays at the spot from the announcement until the landing: the beam players run towards. |
| `animation.fall` | four-ring canopy, cords, the crate, smoke | Plays so that it ends as the crate lands. Skipped when anything is within 12 blocks above the spot. |
| `animation.impact` | anvil sound, canopy collapse, dirt burst, cloud | Plays as the crate lands. |
| `animation.unlock` | door and orb sounds, ring pulse, burst in the tier's colour | Plays as the crate unlocks. |
| `animation.vanish` | the crate shrinking away, break sound, cloud | Plays as the crate vanishes. |

The `animation` lists are ExyliaLib sequence lines (`[DISPLAY]`, `[CIRCLE]`, `[SPHERE]`, `[RING_PULSE]`, `[PARTICLE]`,
`[SOUND]`), best edited from **Animation** in `/supplydropsadmin`. `%glow%` is the tier's colour, `%parachute%` its
canopy block, `%crate%` its crate block and `%seconds%` how long the marker lasts. The `settings` values are also
editable from the **Settings** button of `/warzoneadmin`; `/warzoneadmin reload` applies a hand edit.

Messages are in `plugins/ExyliaWarzone/lang/<code>/modules/supply-drops/messages.yml`: the announcements, the staff
replies, the menu state labels, the boss bar (`bossbar`: `text`, `colour`, `overlay`; an empty `text` hides it), the
hologram over a crate (`hologram.locked` and `hologram.open`) and the editor prompts (`prompts`). The screens are
`lang/<code>/menus/admin/supplydrop_*.yml` and `envoy_*.yml`, rewritten from the jar on every start.

## Power-ups

`plugins/ExyliaWarzone/modules/powerups/config.yml` holds how every power-up looks and how close a player must come.
What each zone spawns, where and how often is set per zone in game (see [Power-ups](/docs/exyliawarzone/power-ups)).
The module's messages, including the two hologram lines and the pickup message, are in
`plugins/ExyliaWarzone/lang/<code>/modules/powerups/messages.yml`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.pickup-radius` | `1.0` | How close, in blocks, a player's feet have to come to a power-up to take it. Never less than 1. |
| `settings.item-scale` | `0.8` | Size of the floating item; 1 is a dropped item's size. |
| `settings.view-distance` | `48.0` | Blocks from which the item and its hologram are drawn. |
| `settings.sound-radius` | `24.0` | Blocks from which the spawn sound is heard. |
| `settings.hologram-enabled` | `true` | Shows the name (and the time left, for a power-up that expires) above each one. |
| `settings.hologram-offset-y` | `1.0` | Height of the hologram above the item, in blocks. |
| `settings.particle-enabled` | `true` | Shows particles around each power-up, five times a second. |
| `settings.rotation-enabled` | `true` | Spins the item. |
| `settings.rotation-speed-degrees` | `90.0` | Degrees a second the item turns; 90 is a turn every 4 seconds. |
| `settings.particle-type` | `END_ROD` | The particle, as a Bukkit particle name. An unknown name shows none. |
| `settings.particle-count` | `3` | Particles in each burst. |
| `settings.particle-offset` | `0.4` | How far, in blocks, the particles spread around the item. |
| `settings.particle-speed` | `0.04` | Speed of the particles. |
| `settings.area-max-attempts` | `20` | Random columns tried per spawn in **Area** mode before giving up until the next interval. |
| `sounds.spawn` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_CHIME\|0.6\|1.4` | Played to players within `sound-radius` when a power-up appears, as `NAME\|volume\|pitch`. Empty for none. The pickup sound is the tag at the start of the `picked-up` message. |

The module itself is switched by `modules.powerups` in `config.yml` (restart to apply). Every key here can also be
changed in game from `/warzoneadmin settings`; changes apply to power-ups spawned from then on, and particles change
at once.

## Loot chests

`plugins/ExyliaWarzone/modules/loot-chests/config.yml` holds who may open a refilling chest, the hologram and the
refill sound. Templates, loot tables, cooldowns and fill modes are set per template and per chest in game (see
[Loot chests](/docs/exyliawarzone/loot-chests)). The module's messages, including the three hologram lines, the
state labels and the editor prompts, are in `plugins/ExyliaWarzone/lang/<code>/modules/loot-chests/messages.yml`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.allow-open-on-cooldown` | `false` | Lets everyone open a loot chest while it refills; it is empty then. Holders of `exyliawarzone.lootchests.bypass` always can. A disabled chest stays shut either way. |
| `settings.hologram-enabled` | `true` | Shows a status hologram (ready, refill countdown, disabled) above each loot chest. Chests already standing follow a change after a restart. |
| `settings.hologram-offset-y` | `1.5` | Height of the hologram, in blocks above the bottom of the chest block. Chests already standing follow a change after a restart. |
| `sounds.refill` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_CHIME\|0.6\|1.6` | Sound played to players within 10 blocks when a chest refills, as `SOUND_NAME\|VOLUME\|PITCH`. |

## Regen zones

`plugins/ExyliaWarzone/modules/regenzones/config.yml`. The `settings` are only what a **new** zone starts
with: each zone keeps its own interval, idle delay and warning, edited on its screen in
`/regenzonesadmin`. Times can be written `30s`, `5m` or `1h30m`; a bare number is seconds. Sounds are
`NAME|volume|pitch`, and a blank one plays nothing. See [Regen zones](/docs/exyliawarzone/regen-zones).

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.default-interval-seconds` | `300` | How often a new zone regenerates in Fixed mode. |
| `settings.default-idle-delay-seconds` | `60` | How long a new zone in Idle mode waits after the last block change. |
| `settings.default-warning-seconds` | `10` | How long before a Fixed regeneration a new zone warns the players inside; `0` turns it off. |
| `sounds.regen` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_CHIME\|0.6\|1.4` | Played to the players inside a zone when it regenerates. |
| `sounds.warning` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING\|0.6\|0.8` | Played to the players inside a Fixed zone when its warning starts. |

The module's messages are in `plugins/ExyliaWarzone/lang/<code>/modules/regenzones/messages.yml`. Its
`visuals` section holds the warning shown before a Fixed regeneration: `regen-warning-title` (a title
with `%zone_name%`, `%time%` and `%seconds%`) and `regen-bossbar` (a countdown boss bar with
`%zone_name%` and `%time%`). A boss bar with a blank text, or a title with a blank text and subtitle, is
not shown.

## Duel rooms

`plugins/ExyliaWarzone/modules/duel-rooms/config.yml`. The first eight `settings` are what every room uses until
it sets its own under **Timings & rules** in `/duelroomsadmin`; a room that never set one follows the file, edits
included. The rest are server-wide. Times can be written `30s`, `5m` or `1h30m`; a bare number is seconds. Sounds
are `NAME|volume|pitch`, played to that player only, and a blank one plays nothing. See
[Duel rooms](/docs/exyliawarzone/duel-rooms).

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.countdown-seconds` | `3` | Seconds between the room closing and the fight starting. Hits do nothing until then. Per room. |
| `settings.max-duration-seconds` | `180` | Longest a fight may last before it stalls (see `stalemate`); `0` is no limit. Per room. |
| `settings.inactivity-seconds` | `30` | How long a fight may go without anyone landing a hit before it stalls; `0` turns it off. Per room. |
| `settings.loot-seconds` | `5` | How long the winner has to collect the loot before the room opens. Per room. |
| `settings.teleport-winner-out` | `true` | Send the winner to the exit when the room opens. A draw always sends everyone out. Per room. |
| `settings.disable-flight` | `true` | Take flight away from fighters for the length of the duel. Per room. |
| `settings.kill-on-leave` | `true` | Kill a fighter who disconnects or gets out mid-fight, so their loot drops. Per room. |
| `settings.stalemate` | `DRAW` | What a stalled fight becomes: `DRAW`, `SUDDEN_DEATH` or `MOST_DAMAGE`. Per room. |
| `settings.allowed-commands` | `[msg, tell, w, r, reply]` | Commands a fighter may still use, without the slash. Every other one is blocked until the fight is decided. |
| `settings.broadcast-results` | `true` | Announce every winner to the server. Players mute it in `/settings`. |
| `settings.max-border-blocks` | `20000` | Most blocks a room's faces may have, checked when its region is selected. |
| `settings.draw-world-border` | `true` | Draw a world border over the walls for the fighters. Only drawn; the blocks hold players in. Off saves client FPS. |
| `settings.win-reward-cooldown-seconds` | `1800` | After a paid win, how long before the same two players can earn rewards and a counted win off each other again, either way round, on every server. `0` turns it off. |
| `settings.forfeit-grace-seconds` | `30` | A fighter who disconnects or leaves within this many seconds of the fight starting hands the win over unpaid and uncounted. `0` turns it off. |
| `sounds.countdown` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_HAT\|1.0\|1.4` | Played every second of the countdown. |
| `sounds.start` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING\|0.8\|2.0` | Played when the fight starts. |
| `sounds.victory` | `ENTITY_PLAYER_LEVELUP\|0.6\|1.0` | Played to the winner. |
| `sounds.defeat` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BASS\|0.7\|0.7` | Played to the losers, and to everyone on a draw. |
| `sounds.expel` | `ENTITY_ENDERMAN_TELEPORT\|1.0\|1.0` | Played to a player sent out of a busy room. |

The module is switched by `modules.duel-rooms` (`true`) in the plugin's `config.yml`; a restart applies it. Its
messages are in `plugins/ExyliaWarzone/lang/<code>/modules/duel-rooms/messages.yml`. The `visuals` section holds
the titles (`countdown-title`, `fight-title`, `victory-title`, `defeat-title`, `draw-title`, `inactive-title`,
`sudden-death-title`) and boss bars (`waiting-bossbar`, `fight-bossbar`, `loot-bossbar`, `sudden-death-bossbar`),
each listing the placeholders it supports in its comment (`%room_name%`, `%seconds%`, `%time_left%`, `%alive%`,
`%winner%`, `%losers%` and others). A boss bar with a blank text, or a title with a blank text and subtitle, is
not shown.

## Messages

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `lang/<language>/messages.yml` | The `prefix` and the lines every module shares: a disabled module, no permission, kept rewards delivered, the on/off labels the menus show, the flag editor's labels. |
| `lang/<language>/modules/<module>/messages.yml` | Each module's chat lines, titles, boss bars, holograms and menu labels. |

Colours are palette tokens such as `{primary}`, `{error}` or `{highlight}`, so recolouring the server through
ExyliaLib never means editing these files. `%prefix%` is replaced by the `prefix` value. A line may open with an
effect tag that never reaches the screen: `[sound:NAME|volume|pitch]` plays a sound to the player who gets the line.

## Menus

The admin screens live in `lang/<language>/menus/admin/`. They are written again from the jar on every start, so an
edit there does not survive a restart. The states and labels they fill in come from the messages files, which are kept.
