A regen zone is a box of the world with a saved snapshot of its blocks. On a timer, or once players stop touching it for a while, the zone is put back exactly as the snapshot says: what was built is removed and what was broken comes back. Each zone is also a region, so the same box can carry flags (PvP, build, break, item drops) and work as a protected area that resets itself.

## Before you start

- The module is on by default (`modules.regen-zones` in `config.yml`).
- Your permission: `exyliawarzone.regenzones.admin` (included in `exyliawarzone.admin`). See [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions).
- The area built the way it should always look.
- FastAsyncWorldEdit is optional; see the note below.

## Steps

Open the editor with `/regenzonesadmin`, or the **Regen zones** card of `/warzoneadmin`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the zone">
    Click **Regen zone** (the emerald) and type an id: lowercase letters, digits, `_` and `-`, up to 64 characters (spaces become `_`). It must be free on every server sharing the database. The zone's screen opens; **Display name** sets the name players see in warnings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the region">
    Click **Zone region**. You get the selector (a golden axe by default): left-click one corner, right-click the opposite one, then shift + left-click to confirm. Both corners are inside the box. See [Selecting regions](/docs/exylialib/regions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the snapshot">
    With the area built, click **Schematic**. The blocks in the box are saved as they are now; entities are not part of it. Chat says when the save is done, or why it failed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it regenerates">
    Click **Regeneration**: pick the **Regen mode**, then the **Regen interval** (Fixed) or the **Idle delay** (Idle), and for Fixed zones the **Regen warning**. Times are typed as `30s`, `5m`, `1h30m` or `1d`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new zone starts on, in Fixed mode, every 5 minutes, with a 1 minute idle delay and a 10 second warning, **Lift trapped players** and **Clean entities** on. It does nothing until it has both a region and a snapshot.

## Check that it works

Break a few blocks inside the zone, then click **Regenerate now** (you confirm first). The blocks come back at once. For the timer, watch the **Status** card: it shows the next regeneration and how many players are inside. A Fixed zone warns the players inside with a title and a boss bar before it fires.

## Going further

### Fixed and Idle

| Mode | Regenerates | Warning |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed** (the card says "fixed timer") | Every interval, whether or not anything changed. At least 5 seconds. | Yes |
| **Idle** (the card says "once block changes stop") | Once the zone has gone the idle delay without a block change; every change starts the delay over. | No |

**Fixed** suits arenas that reset on a schedule. **Idle** suits areas used now and then: a zone being fought over keeps postponing its reset until everybody leaves it alone. A zone nobody touched never regenerates; its screen says **After the next change**. After a restart each Idle zone is put back once, an idle delay later, because changes made while the server was off are never seen.

### The warning

A Fixed zone warns the players standing inside **Regen warning** seconds before it regenerates: a title, a short sound and a boss bar counting down. Only players inside when the warning starts get it. Set `0s` to turn it off.

### What happens during a regeneration

1. With **Clean entities** on, every non-player entity in the box is removed: dropped items and arrows, but also mobs, armour stands and item frames.
2. The blocks go back as the snapshot has them, air included. Chests and other containers get back what they held in the snapshot, [loot chests](/docs/exyliawarzone/loot-chests) in their own state.
3. With **Lift trapped players** on, anyone the new blocks would bury moves up to the nearest free space. Players are never removed.
4. Players inside hear the regen sound.

It only runs while every chunk of the zone is loaded; the screen says **Due (when its chunks load)** until somebody comes near. A failed regeneration leaves the zone as it was and writes the reason to the console.

### Protect the zone with flags

Select the region first, then click **Region flags**: the editor opens over the zone's current flags and they apply when you save. Flags apply to everyone inside except players in creative mode. See [Region flags](/docs/exyliawarzone/region-flags). A `TEMPORARY_BLOCKS` block never vanishes later out of the freshly pasted build: the plugin forgets placed blocks on each regeneration.

### Change a zone later

- **New snapshot**: click **Schematic** again; you are asked first because the old snapshot is lost.
- **New region**: re-selecting keeps flags, block list and temporary block time but takes no new snapshot. Save one right after, or the next regeneration pastes the old build at the new spot.
- **Turned off**: no regenerations or warnings; region and flags stay.
- **Regenerate now**: refused while the zone is regenerating; a Fixed zone's timer starts over.
- **Delete**: removes the zone, its flags and its snapshot. The blocks in the world stay.

<Callout type="note" title="FastAsyncWorldEdit is optional">
With FastAsyncWorldEdit installed, snapshots are saved and pasted through it. Without it, ExyliaLib's built-in engine does the work. Both read and write the same `.schem` files, so installing or removing FAWE never means re-saving. The built-in engine keeps blocks, container contents, sign text and spawner types, but not banner patterns, head owners, lectern books or command block commands, and holds up to 2^26 blocks (about 512 × 256 × 512). Use FAWE for anything bigger.
</Callout>

<Details title="Technical details">

**The zone's screen.** **Status** (on or off, next regeneration, players inside), **Display name**, **Zone region**, **Region flags**, **Schematic**, **Regeneration**, **Lift trapped players**, **Clean entities**, **Regenerate now**, **Delete**. **Next regen** reads as a time, **None** (off, no region or no snapshot), **After the next change**, **Due** or **Regenerating...**. In the zone list, left-click edits and right-click deletes.

**Timers.** The Fixed timer starts when the region is selected and at each server start, and starts over when the zone is turned on, switched to Fixed, given a new interval or regenerated by hand. A change for Idle is a block broken, placed, burnt or blown up inside the zone, also by an explosion that started outside it.

**Where things are kept.** Snapshots: `plugins/ExyliaWarzone/schematics/regenzone_<id>.schem` (files in `schematics/regions/` are read too). Zones: the `sc_regen_zones` table; on a shared database each server lists only its own, and a zone whose world is not loaded shows its region as **Not set**. Creating, deleting and **Regenerate now** are in `/exylialib logs`. Defaults (`settings.default-interval-seconds` 300, `default-idle-delay-seconds` 60, `default-warning-seconds` 10) are in [Configuration](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration#regen-zones) and in the **Settings** button of `/warzoneadmin`. The warning title and boss bar are under `visuals` in `lang/<code>/modules/regenzones/messages.yml`. Zones from ExyliaSurvivalCore are picked up on their own: see [Move from ExyliaSurvivalCore](/docs/exyliawarzone/migrating).

</Details>
