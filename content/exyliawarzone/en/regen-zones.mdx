---
title: "Regen zones"
description: "Areas that put themselves back from a snapshot on a fixed timer or once players stop changing them: creating a zone, Fixed and Idle modes, warnings, what happens to players inside, flags, re-saving and deleting."
---

A regen zone is a box of the world with a saved snapshot of its blocks. On a timer, or once players have
stopped touching it for a while, the zone is put back exactly as the snapshot says: whatever was built is
removed and whatever was broken comes back. Each zone is also a region, so the same box can carry flags
(PvP, build, break, item drops...) and work as a protected area that resets itself.

The module is on by default (`modules.regen-zones` in `config.yml`). Everything is set up in game from
`/regenzonesadmin` (permission `exyliawarzone.regenzones.admin`) or the **Regen zones** card of
`/warzoneadmin` (`exyliawarzone.admin`, which includes it).

## Creating a zone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    In `/regenzonesadmin`, click **Create** and type an id: lowercase letters, digits, `_` and `-`, up
    to 64 characters (spaces become `_`). The id must be free on every server sharing the database. The zone's
    screen opens; **Display name** sets the name players see in warnings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the region">
    Click **Zone region**. You are handed the selector (a golden axe by default): left-click one corner,
    right-click the opposite one, then shift + left-click to confirm. Both corners are inside the box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the snapshot">
    Build the area the way it should always look, then click **Schematic**. The blocks in the box are saved
    as they are now; entities are not part of it. Chat says when the save is done, or why it failed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it regenerates">
    Open **Regeneration**: pick the mode, then the interval (Fixed) or the idle delay (Idle), and for Fixed
    zones the warning. Times are typed as `30s`, `5m`, `1h30m` or `1d`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new zone starts **on**, in Fixed mode, with the defaults from the module's
[configuration](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration#regen-zones): every 5 minutes, a 1 minute idle delay
and a 10 second warning, with **Lift trapped players** and **Clean entities** on. It does nothing until
it has both a region and a snapshot.

## Fixed and Idle

| Mode | Regenerates | Warning |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed** (fixed timer) | Every interval, whether or not anything changed. The interval is at least 5 seconds. | Yes |
| **Idle** (after changes stop) | Once the zone has gone the idle delay without a block change. Every change starts the delay over. | No |

**Fixed** suits arenas and builds that should reset on a schedule. The timer starts when the region is
selected and on every server start, and starts over when the zone is turned on, switched to Fixed, given a
new interval or regenerated by hand.

**Idle** suits areas players use now and then: a zone being fought over keeps postponing its reset until
everybody leaves it alone. A change is a block broken, placed, burnt or blown up inside the zone, also by
an explosion that started outside it. A zone nobody touched never regenerates; its screen says
**After the next change**. Changes made while the server was off are never seen, so after a restart each
Idle zone is put back once, an idle delay later. Switching a zone to Idle or turning an Idle zone on does
the same.

## The warning

A Fixed zone warns the players standing inside it **warning** seconds before it regenerates: a title, a
short sound and a boss bar counting down to the regeneration. Only players inside when the warning starts
get it. Set the warning to `0s` to turn it off. The title and boss bar are under `visuals` in the module's
messages file, the sound in its `config.yml`.

## What happens during a regeneration

1. With **Clean entities** on, every entity in the box that is not a player is removed: dropped items,
   arrows, but also mobs, armour stands and item frames.
2. The blocks go back as the snapshot has them, air included. Chests and other containers get back
   exactly what they held in the snapshot.
3. With **Lift trapped players** on, anyone the new blocks would bury is moved up to the nearest free
   space. Players themselves are never removed.
4. Players inside hear the regen sound.

The plugin also forgets which blocks players had placed in the zone, so a temporary block (from the
`TEMPORARY_BLOCKS` flag) never vanishes later out of the freshly pasted build.

A regeneration only runs while every chunk of the zone is loaded. When it is due and nobody is near, the
screen says **Due (when its chunks load)** and it runs as soon as somebody comes close enough to load them.
A failed regeneration leaves the zone as it was and writes the reason to the console.

<Callout type="note" title="FastAsyncWorldEdit is optional">
With FastAsyncWorldEdit installed, snapshots are saved and pasted through it. Without it, ExyliaLib's
built-in engine does the work. Both read and write the same `.schem` files, so installing or removing FAWE
never means re-saving. The built-in engine keeps blocks, container contents, sign text and spawner types,
but not banner patterns, head owners, lectern books or command block commands, and it handles boxes up to
512 × 256 × 512 blocks. Use FAWE for anything bigger.
</Callout>

## The zone's screen

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Turns the zone on or off. Shows the next regeneration and how many players are inside, live. |
| **Display name** | The name shown in warnings and confirmations. |
| **Zone region** | Selects the box. |
| **Region flags** | Opens the zone's flag editor (needs a region first). |
| **Schematic** | Takes the snapshot, or replaces it. |
| **Regeneration** | Mode, interval or idle delay, and warning. |
| **Lift trapped players** | Moves buried players up after a regeneration. |
| **Clean entities** | Removes every non-player entity before a regeneration. |
| **Regenerate now** | Puts the zone back right away, after a confirmation. |
| **Delete** | Removes the zone, after a confirmation. |

**Next regen** reads as a time, **None** (off, no region or no snapshot), **After the next change** (an
untouched Idle zone), **Due** or **Regenerating...**. The zone list shows the same for every zone, along
with its mode, region, snapshot and state. Left-click a zone to edit it, right-click to delete it.

## Region flags

A zone's box is one of Warzone's regions, so its flags apply to everyone inside it except players in
creative mode. Select the region first, then click **Region flags**: the editor opens over the zone's
current flags and they apply as soon as you save. Every flag, and the block list and temporary block time
that go with them, is explained on [Region flags](/docs/exyliawarzone/region-flags).

## Changing a zone later

- **New snapshot**: after changing the build, click **Schematic** again. You are asked first, because the
  old snapshot is lost.
- **New region**: re-selecting keeps the zone's flags, block list and temporary block time. It does not
  take a new snapshot: save one right after, or the next regeneration pastes the old build at the new spot.
- **Turning it off**: no regenerations and no warnings while it is off. Its region and flags stay in place.
- **Regenerate now**: undoes whatever was built or broken since the snapshot. Refused while the zone is
  already regenerating. On a Fixed zone the timer starts over.
- **Deleting**: removes the zone, its flags and its saved snapshot. The blocks in the world stay as they
  are, and a new zone with the same id starts with no snapshot.

## Where things are kept

- Snapshots: `plugins/ExyliaWarzone/schematics/regenzone_<id>.schem`. Files in `schematics/regions/` are
  read too.
- Zones: the `sc_regen_zones` table. On a database shared by several servers, each server loads and lists
  only its own zones. A zone whose world is not loaded shows its region as **Not set** and does nothing.
- Creating, deleting and **Regenerate now** are recorded in the staff log, `/exylialib logs`.
- Zones and snapshots from ExyliaSurvivalCore are picked up on their own; see
  [Migrating](/docs/exyliawarzone/migrating).
