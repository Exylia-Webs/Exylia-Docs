This page walks through a first setup, one module at a time. Every step happens in game; you need
`exyliawarzone.admin` (operators have it).

## Open the hub

Run `/warzoneadmin`. Each module card reads **Enabled** when it runs. A module reading **Disabled** is switched off in
`config.yml` (`modules:`); switch it on and restart. Click a card to open that module's editor.

## A supply drop

The plugin creates three tiers the first time it starts on an empty database (`common`, `rare` and `legendary`, with
their loot), so only an area is missing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an envoy">
    **Supply drops** card → **Envoys** → the emerald, and type an id such as `spawn-ring`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select its area">
    Left-click **Area** and take the golden axe: left-click one corner, right-click the other, shift + left-click to
    confirm. Click one corner at ground level and the other well above it: the corners set the heights a crate can land
    at.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it">
    **Run it now**. The drop is announced, falls a minute later and unlocks 20 seconds after landing. Add a
    **Timetable** to make it run by itself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/supplydrop` tells any player where the crate is. Details: [Supply drops](/docs/exyliawarzone/supply-drops).

## A power-up zone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the zone">
    **Power-ups** card → **Create ▸ Power-up zone**, and type an id.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add spawn points">
    **Spawn points** → **Add ▸ Spawn point**, and left-click the blocks where power-ups should float.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a power-up">
    **Power-ups ▸ Add ▸ Power-up**, then give it an effect such as Speed II for 30 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The zone's top card reads **Spawning: Ready** once it has what it needs, and the first power-up appears at once, then one per
interval. Details: [Power-ups](/docs/exyliawarzone/power-ups).

## A loot chest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a template">
    **Loot chests** card → **Templates** → **Create template**, type `common`, then fill **Loot table**. **Everything
    in a chest** turns a chest you have already filled into loot lines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    **Place chests**, type a cooldown such as `5m`, and click a chest or barrel. It fills at once. Type `cancel` to stop
    placing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Loot it, close it, and the hologram over it counts down to the refill. Details:
[Loot chests](/docs/exyliawarzone/loot-chests).

## A regen zone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it and select the box">
    **Regen zones** card → **Create**, type an id, then **Zone region** and select the two corners with the golden axe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the snapshot">
    Build the area the way it should always look and click **Schematic**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when">
    **Regeneration**: a **Fixed** interval with a warning first, or **Idle**, once nobody has changed a block for a while.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Details: [Regen zones](/docs/exyliawarzone/regen-zones). To stop players building there at all, set its
[region flags](/docs/exyliawarzone/region-flags).

## A duel room

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it and select the arena">
    **Duel rooms** card → **Create** and type an id, then **Arena** → **Room region** and select the whole box with the
    golden axe: floor, walls and ceiling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the exit and save the schematic">
    **Arena** → **Exit location**: stand outside the room and sneak-click. Then **Schematic**, while the room is clean.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable it and walk in">
    Click the **Status** card. With two players inside (not op: operators are left out by
    `exyliawarzone.duelrooms.bypass`), the border closes, a countdown runs and the fight starts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What the winner gets is set under **Winner rewards**. Details: [Duel rooms](/docs/exyliawarzone/duel-rooms).

## Then

- Every setting of every module is in the hub's **Settings** card, or in `modules/<module>/config.yml`. See
  [Configuration](/docs/exyliawarzone/configuration).
- Give builders one module's node, such as `exyliawarzone.regenzones.admin`, instead of the whole plugin. See
  [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions).
