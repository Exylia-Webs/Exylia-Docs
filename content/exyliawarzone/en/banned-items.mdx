A banned-items rule says which items may not do which things, and where. Use it to forbid golden apples or pearls in a warzone, keep elytra and totems out of a duel arena, or allow only a short list of items inside a region. The check runs on every matching action, so a refusal is instant and the player is told why.

## Before you start

- The module is on by default (`modules.banned-items` in `config.yml`; a restart applies a change).
- Your permission: `exyliawarzone.banneditems.admin` (included in `exyliawarzone.admin`). See [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions).
- Regions are WorldGuard regions, so WorldGuard must be installed and the area must exist as one. Without WorldGuard only `*` and `world:*` places work.

## Steps

Open `/banneditemsadmin` (alias `/banitemsadmin`) or the **Banned items** card of `/warzoneadmin`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the rule">
    Click **Rule** (the Create card) and type an id. The rule starts enabled and empty, and an empty rule bans nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the items">
    Click **Items**, then **Item** to add the one you hold, or **By name** to type an entry (see Item entries below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the actions">
    Click **Actions** and switch on what those items may not do. Each action is a toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the places">
    Click **Places**, then **Regions here** (the regions you stand in), **By name**, or **This world**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rule screen also has **Mode** (blacklist or whitelist), **Message** (what the player reads when refused), **Bypass** and a switch to turn the rule off without deleting it. A rule with no actions or no places, or a blacklist with no items, does nothing.

## Check that it works

Stand in the place, with a non-op account that has no bypass, and try the action with a banned item. The action is cancelled and you read the rule's message. Operators are not exempt, so your own account works too unless you hold a bypass node.

## Going further

### Item entries

A rule's item list takes these entries. Click **By name** and type one, or **Item** to add the item you hold.

<Details title="Item entries you can type">

| Entry | Matches |
|---|---|
| `GOLDEN_APPLE` | That item. |
| `DIAMOND_SWORD:1001` | That item with that custom model data. |
| `#swords` | Every item in that item tag. |
| `potion:strength` | Any potion, splash or lingering potion or tipped arrow carrying that effect. |
| `enchant:sharpness:5` | Anything carrying that enchantment at that level or higher, enchanted books included. The level may be left out. |

</Details>

An entry that names nothing is refused when you add it.

### Actions

<Details title="Every action">

| Action | Stops |
|---|---|
| `use` | Right-clicking with the item at all, from buckets to spawn eggs. |
| `consume` | Eating or drinking it. |
| `throw` | Throwing it: pearls, potions, snowballs, wind charges, tridents. |
| `shoot` | Shooting with it, as the bow or as the arrow. |
| `attack` | Hitting anything with it in hand. |
| `mine` | Breaking blocks with it in hand. |
| `place` | Placing it: blocks, buckets, boats, frames, armour stands, end crystals. |
| `break` | Breaking a block of it. |
| `wear` | Wearing it. Someone who walks in wearing it has it taken off. |
| `glide` | Gliding with it. Someone who walks in wearing it lands. |
| `totem` | A totem saving its holder from death. |
| `drop` | Dropping it. |
| `pickup` | Picking it up. |
| `craft` | Crafting it. |

</Details>

### Places

<Details title="Place entries">

| Entry | Covers |
|---|---|
| `*` | Everywhere on the server. |
| `world:*` | The whole world `world`. |
| `world:spawn` | The region `spawn` of `world`. |
| `spawn` | A region with that id in any world. |

</Details>

Entries cannot contain spaces.

### Whitelist rules

**Mode** turns a rule around. As a blacklist, the listed items cannot do the chosen actions inside its places. As a whitelist, **only** the listed items may do them there and every other item is refused. A whitelist with no items refuses every item.

### Messages and bypass

A refused player reads the rule's **Message**, or, when it is empty, the default line for that action from `modules/banned-items/messages.yml` ([Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages)). The line shows at most once every 1.5 seconds, however many clicks.

Three permission nodes matter here (the full list is on [Permissions](/docs/exyliawarzone/permissions)):

- `exyliawarzone.banneditems.admin` (operators): opens `/banneditemsadmin` and the rule editor.
- `exyliawarzone.banneditems.bypass` (nobody): the holder is never stopped by any rule.
- `exyliawarzone.banneditems.bypass.<rule id>` (nobody): the holder is let off that one rule. The **Bypass** button of a rule shows its exact node.

<Callout type="note" title="Operators meet the rules">
Neither bypass is given to operators, so you can test a rule with your own account. Grant a node on purpose to staff who should be exempt.
</Callout>

<Details title="Technical details">

Rules live in the database. Each server only loads and lists the rules it created, so servers sharing a database keep their own. Rule creation and deletion are written to the staff log (`/exylialib logs`). Item matching, including custom model data, tags, potion effects and enchantments, is done by the module's own matcher on every action; there is no cache to refresh after an edit.

</Details>
