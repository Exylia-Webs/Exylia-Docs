ExyliaLink is the Minecraft side of [Exylia Link](https://link.exylia.net). It links your players' Minecraft accounts to their Discord accounts for a whole network, tells the dashboard who is online (and in which LuckPerms groups), and runs the rewards Link grants. It is free and open source, published under the GNU General Public License v3.0 on [GitHub](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaLink/releases).

## Plugin and dashboard

Two things share the name, and each does a different job.

- **Exylia Link** is the web dashboard at [link.exylia.net](https://link.exylia.net). You sign in with Discord, pick your network and set almost everything there: reward rules, linked-account requirements, who may unlink.
- **ExyliaLink** is this plugin. It connects each server or proxy to that dashboard and does the in-game part: `/link`, warnings, reward commands.

The plugin keeps almost nothing in `config.yml`. What Link does is decided on the dashboard.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Platforms | Paper, Spigot 1.17+ and Folia; Velocity 3; BungeeCord and Waterfall |
| Java | 17 or newer |
| Network | An Exylia Link network on [link.exylia.net](https://link.exylia.net) |
| Optional | LuckPerms, PlaceholderAPI, DiscordSRV (see the table on the [installation page](/docs/exylialink/installation)) |

ExyliaLink does not depend on ExyliaLib: it is standalone, so it runs on proxies and on servers older than ExyliaLib supports. A server that has ExyliaLib needs nothing extra.
