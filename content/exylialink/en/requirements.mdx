You choose which LuckPerms groups must have a linked account. Players in those groups are warned when they join, reminded until they link, and, in restrict mode, cannot use the commands you list.

## Before you start

- Install LuckPerms on the proxy or servers that enforce it: ExyliaLink reads groups from it.
- [Connect](/docs/exylialink/first-steps) every server and proxy.
- Decide who enforces it, see [Proxies and game servers](/docs/exylialink/proxies-and-servers).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the rule on the web">
    Open [link.exylia.net](https://link.exylia.net), go to Settings, Requirements, and choose the groups that must link. The rule reaches every connected server and proxy within moments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose warn or restrict">
    In warn mode players are only told. In restrict mode the commands you list are refused until they link. You can also write your own reason; otherwise players read "Your rank requires a linked Discord account."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the reminder time">
    In `config.yml`, `requirements-reminder-minutes` is how often players are reminded. The default is `10`; `0` warns once and never reminds. Run `/linkadmin reload`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let staff skip it">
    Grant `exylialink.requirements.bypass` with LuckPerms to anyone who should never be warned or restricted. Nobody has it by default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What players see

A player in a required group whose link status is unlinked gets:

- A **LINK REQUIRED** block in chat with a clickable `/link`, and a title on screen.
- A reminder line every `requirements-reminder-minutes` until they link.
- In restrict mode, a refusal "Link your Discord account first" when they type a restricted command.

The warning is sent after their status is known on join, and again when their groups change, when they are unlinked, or when the rule changes. Linking ends the warnings straight away.

## Check that it works

Put a test account in a required group without the bypass permission, join, and read the warning. Run `/link` and link: the reminders stop. In restrict mode, try a listed command before linking.

## Going further

- **Restricted commands** are matched by their first word, with or without the slash or a `plugin:` prefix, so `/essentials:msg` counts as `msg`. `/link` is never blocked.
- **If Exylia Link is unreachable**, ExyliaLink keeps the last rule and statuses it knew and blocks nothing.
- **Turn off the title.** Set `requirements.title` in `lang/<code>/messages.yml` to an empty text. See [Translations](/docs/exylialink/translations).
