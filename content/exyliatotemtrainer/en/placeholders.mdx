Placeholders let you show a player's totem stats, their running session or a leaderboard position on a sidebar, a menu or another plugin. Click one to copy it.

## How the name is written

A placeholder is `%exyliatotemtrainer_` followed by its own name. It is written the same way everywhere: in the plugin's own files (`scoreboards.yml`, `menus/`, `messages.yml`) and through PlaceholderAPI in other plugins. Inside the plugin's files you do not need PlaceholderAPI; other plugins do.

```text
%exyliatotemtrainer_wins%
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Renamed after 1.0.0">
Up to 1.0.0 every name carried the group `totem`, such as `%exyliatotemtrainer_totem_wins%`. Those forms no longer resolve. Write `%exyliatotemtrainer_<name>%` and update any `scoreboards.yml` or `menus/main.yml` generated before.
</Callout>

Every placeholder is answered for the player looking at it. Outside a session or a duel, or before the profile has loaded, it answers a neutral value instead of showing its own name: `0` for a number, `0 - 0` for the score, `none` for `state` and `match_state`, and a dash for a mode, opponent or grade. The exceptions are `format` outside a duel, and `pops`, `streak`, `best` and `average` for a player whose profile has not loaded: those are left as written.

## Leaderboards

A board is about one mode, over every speed, sorted by one category. `<mode>` is a mode id from `config.yml` (`normal`, `infinite`, `random`, `chaos`, `acceleration` and `blackout` as shipped) and `<n>` is the position, counting from 1. The plain form answers the player's name and the one ending in `_value` the number that put them there.

<Placeholders group="leaderboard" />

Written out with a mode and a position:

```text
%exyliatotemtrainer_top_chaos_rating_1%
%exyliatotemtrainer_top_chaos_rating_1_value%
%exyliatotemtrainer_top_acceleration_fastest_average_3%
```

A mode id with underscores in it is matched whole: `%exyliatotemtrainer_top_my_mode_best_pops_1%` finds the mode `my_mode`. Until the board has been read once, both forms answer the loading text from `messages.yml` and start the read. A position past the end of the board, an unknown mode or an unknown category leaves the text as written. See [Read profiles and leaderboards](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/stats) for how a board is built.
