At the end of this page you have one arena, a solo session you played in it, and a duel request sent. You only use menus and the chat.

An arena is a place with two spawns. It is ready when it is enabled and both spawns are set in loaded worlds. Many sessions and duels can run in the same arena at once; players in different ones cannot see or touch each other.

## Before you start

Install the plugin ([Install ExyliaTotemTrainer](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/installation)) and have the `exyliatotemtrainer.admin` permission.

## Create the arena

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the admin menu">
    Run `/totemtraineradmin`, then click **Arenas**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create Arena** and type a short id in chat, such as `totem1`. It is lowercased and refused if it already exists. The arena's screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Click **Display name** and type what players will see. Colours work, up to 64 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set both spawns">
    Click **Spawn A**, then stand where the spawn should be and follow the prompt in chat. Do the same for **Spawn B**. In a duel the first player appears at spawn A and the second at spawn B; solo sessions use spawn A.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it is ready">
    Go back to **Arenas**. Your arena is listed as ready. One marked incomplete is missing a spawn or is disabled: open it and click **Status** to enable it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Nothing starts without an arena">
Solo training and duels both need a ready arena. Without one the player reads "No arena is ready. Ask an administrator." See [Common problems](/docs/exyliatotemtrainer/troubleshooting).
</Callout>

## Play a solo session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the solo screen">
    Run `/totem` and click **Solo training**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a speed">
    Click **Speed** to step through 25, 20, 15, 10 and 5 ticks. 20 is the default, and your choice is remembered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Click a mode. Each shows what it does and your best at that speed. The session starts: your inventory is saved, you are teleported to the arena, the totems are handed out and a countdown runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Punch the air to start now">
    A swing during the countdown skips it. Then put the next totem in a hand before every hit, with `F`, the hotbar or the inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/totem train <mode> [ticks]` does the same without the menu, and `/totem leave` ends a session.

## Check that it works

You should land in your arena, see the training sidebar, get a title and sound for each pop, and at the end see a summary screen with your rating and numbers. **Play again** repeats the same mode and speed. Leaving puts you back where you were with your inventory restored.

## Play a duel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an opponent">
    Run `/totem` and click **Duels**. Everyone online is listed, free players first; busy ones cannot be clicked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the rules">
    On the opponent's screen set the speed and the best-of length, then click a mode. That click sends the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    They get a centred message with **[✔ ACCEPT]** and **[✘ DECLINE]** buttons, or run `/totem accept`. A request expires after 30 seconds, and you wait 15 seconds before sending another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play the rounds">
    Both players are teleported, a countdown runs, and each round goes to whoever keeps a totem in hand. The first to win more than half the rounds wins the series.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/totem duel <player> [mode] [ticks] [bestOf]` sends a request straight from chat.
