A **state** is a temporary condition on a player, given by the `flag` step and taken away by `unflag` or when it
runs out:

```yaml
- action: flag
  flag: FROZEN
  seconds: 3
```

Giving a state a player already has replaces it: the new length starts from now. A negative `seconds` lasts
until it is removed, the player dies or leaves. With `states.clear-on-death` (default `true`) every state ends on
death; every state ends when the player leaves.

While a state lasts the player sees a boss bar counting it down, for example `❄ FROZEN » 2.4 ⌚`. Each bar is
under `states.<state>` in `messages.yml` and can be changed to a title, action bar, sound or nothing.

## The states

| State | What it does |
|---|---|
| `INVULNERABLE` | No damage, except the void and `/kill`. |
| `FROZEN` | Cannot move from their block. They can still look around. |
| `NO_BUILD` | Cannot place or break blocks. With `only`, just those blocks. |
| `NO_ITEM_USE` | Cannot use items, eat or drink, and cannot glide with an elytra (gliding stops when it starts). With `only`, just those items. |
| `EFFECT_IMMUNITY` | New or changed potion effects are refused. With `only`, just those effects. |
| `UNSTOPPABLE` | No knockback, and Slowness, Mining Fatigue and Weakness are refused. |
| `SILENCED` | Special items are refused: "Your special items are silenced." |
| `LIFESTEAL` | Melee damage dealt to players heals the holder by `value` — a share, `0.5` = half. |
| `FLIGHT` | Can fly, in survival and adventure. When it ends they stop flying and take no fall damage for `states.flight-landing-grace` (4 s), told "Your flight has ended." |
| `NO_FALL_DAMAGE` | No fall damage. |
| `VANISHED` | Hidden from every player, including those who join later. With `states.reveal-on-attack` (default `true`) attacking someone reveals them: "You struck from the shadows and were revealed." |
| `UNDYING` | One lethal hit — except the void and `/kill` — is refused: the player is healed to `value` of their maximum health (default `0.5`), fire and falling are cleared, `states.undying-effects` play and a **LAST STAND** title shows. The state then ends. |

Names are case-insensitive, and `no-build` works as well as `NO_BUILD`.

## Narrowing with only

`NO_BUILD`, `NO_ITEM_USE` and `EFFECT_IMMUNITY` take an `only` list. Empty means everything.

For `NO_BUILD` and `NO_ITEM_USE` an entry is a material or a group:

| Entry | Matches |
|---|---|
| `COBWEB` | That material. |
| `@FOOD` | Anything edible. |
| `@POTION` | Potions. |
| `@BLOCK` | Any block. |
| `@SWORD`, `@PEARL`... | Materials whose name ends with `_SWORD`, `_PEARL`... |

```yaml
- action: flag
  flag: NO_ITEM_USE
  seconds: 10
  only: ['@FOOD', '@POTION']    # cannot eat or drink, can still fight
```

For `EFFECT_IMMUNITY` the entries are effect names:

```yaml
- action: flag
  flag: EFFECT_IMMUNITY
  seconds: 60
  only: [POISON, WITHER, LEVITATION]
```

## Checking a state

- In a chain: `require` with `flag:` or `missing-flag:`. See [Action language](/docs/exyliaspecials/actions#require).
- Anywhere: `%exyliaspecials_state_<state>%` and `%exyliaspecials_state_<state>_remaining%`. See
  [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaspecials/placeholders).
- From another plugin: `isFrozen` and `isInvulnerable`. See [API](/docs/exyliaspecials/api).

## Settings

Under `states:` in `config.yml`:

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `clear-on-death` | `true` | Every state ends on death. |
| `flight-landing-grace` | `4s` | No fall damage for this long after `FLIGHT` ends. |
| `reveal-on-attack` | `true` | `VANISHED` ends when its holder attacks. |
| `undying-effects` | three lines | Effect lines played when `UNDYING` saves a player. |
