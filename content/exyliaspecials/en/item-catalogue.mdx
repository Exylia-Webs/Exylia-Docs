description: "The 70 items that ship with ExyliaSpecials: what each one does, its material, limit and cooldown, and the settings it exposes to the in-game form."
---

70 items ship with the plugin, in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish. Each is a normal item file in
`lang/<language>/items/`: read it to see exactly how it is built, copy it to start one of your own, or change its
numbers from its **Settings** form in `/specialsadmin`.

Settings are listed as `key = default (min–max)`. Where an item shows `${uses}` or `${cooldown}`, those come from its
settings. Every item without a stated limit has unlimited uses. No bundled item sets a permission or region
rules.

<Callout type="note" title="Allies and enemies">
The plugin has no notion of teams. Items that buff or cleanse "allies" — **Bard's Anthem**, **Cleansing Flare** —
affect every player in range, enemies included.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Siege Forge's sweep removes terrain">
The sneak ability of **Siege Forge** removes, with no drops, the default debris list of the `blocks` step within
5 blocks — natural stone, dirt, sand, gravel, netherrack and obsidian included, not only what players built. Only
WorldGuard and other protection plugins limit it. Give the step a `materials:` list if that is not wanted. See
[Action language](/docs/exyliaspecials/actions#blocks).
</Callout>
