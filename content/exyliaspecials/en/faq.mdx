Longer fixes, with the steps, are in [Common problems](/docs/exyliaspecials/troubleshooting).

## Is ExyliaSpecials free?

No. It is a premium plugin, bought on [BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/exyliaspecials.49406/) and installed as a loader jar that checks the licence on every start. See [Installation](/docs/exyliaspecials/installation).

## The plugin does not start

It needs ExyliaLib 1.448.0 or newer (`packetevents` is optional); the loader installs it, or stages a newer one, and asks for a restart. On a licence error in the console, check that `Exylia-Specials-Loader.jar` is the jar from your purchase.

## I updated from 3.x and my items stopped working

Version 4 is a rewrite: items handed out by 3.x are plain items now and the old configuration is not read. Start from a clean folder and give the items again. See [Installation](/docs/exyliaspecials/installation#coming-from-3x).

## Where are the item files?

In `plugins/ExyliaSpecials/lang/<language>/items/`, for example `lang/en/items/`. Changing `language` in `config.yml` reads another folder. See [Installation](/docs/exyliaspecials/installation#languages).

## I deleted an item's file and it came back

Bundled files that are missing are written again on every start and reload. To take an item out of play, give it a `permission:` nobody has. See [Special items](/docs/exyliaspecials/items#permission).

## I changed an item's uses but players' items did not change

The number of uses is written on each copy when it is given, so a new `uses` applies to copies given from then on. Every other change (numbers, lore, features, cooldowns) reaches existing copies on their next use.

## My ability says nothing and does nothing on a hit

Hit triggers are silent. A cooldown, a region rule, PvP being off where either player stands, or a combo that is not complete yet all fail quietly; try the item on a right-click to see the message. See [How a use is checked](/docs/exyliaspecials/items#how-a-use-is-checked).

## My damage ability does not hurt mobs

`damage`, `target_radius` and `target_chain` take players only unless you write `only-players: false` on each. See [Action language](/docs/exyliaspecials/actions#damage).

## A HELD ability ignores its cooldown

Abilities on `HELD` never start their cooldown or spend uses; they run every second while held. Use a state or a `stack` with a window if it must not fire that often. See [Triggers](/docs/exyliaspecials/items#triggers).

## %targets_found% shows up as text in my message

Step variables are not filled in `message` text or `play` effect lines. Use them in `require` checks, numbers and potion lines. See [Action language](/docs/exyliaspecials/actions#numbers-and-variables).

## Compressor Mint and Compressor Vault do nothing

They ship without recipes, meant for your own currency items. Open the item in `/specialsadmin` and add recipes with its editor. See [Tools](/docs/exyliaspecials/tools#in-game-editors).

## Where did the combat tag and the ender pearl cooldown go?

To ExyliaCombat: tagging, logout NPCs, the safe-zone barrier and vanilla item cooldowns live there now. ExyliaSpecials reads the tag of ExyliaCombat, DeluxeCombat or PvPManager, whichever is installed, and old settings are copied to `exyliacombat-export.yml`. See [Combat](/docs/exyliaspecials/combat).

## The 1v1 item says there is no arena

At least one arena must be enabled with both spawns set. See [Duels](/docs/exyliaspecials/duels#building-an-arena).

## Can only one duel run per arena?

Without a snapshot, yes. Take one with `/specialsadmin arena snapshot` and install FastAsyncWorldEdit: each duel then gets its own copy, up to 16 per arena. See [Duels](/docs/exyliaspecials/duels#copies-and-snapshots).

## /msg is blocked during a duel even though it is allowed

`duels.allowed-commands` takes names without the slash: write `msg`, not `/msg`. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaspecials/configuration#configyml).
