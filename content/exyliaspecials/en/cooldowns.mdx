You will change how long a special item waits between uses, clear a cooldown that is in the way, and decide
whether the action bar shows it. Cooldowns show on the item as the vanilla cooldown sweep, on the action bar, and as
a HUD cooldown on Lunar and Feather clients. Cooldowns of five minutes or more survive a restart; shorter ones do
not.

Cooldowns on **vanilla items** (ender pearls, golden apples, totems) are no longer part of ExyliaSpecials: they
moved to ExyliaCombat with the combat tag. See [Combat](/docs/exyliaspecials/combat#moving-from-an-older-version).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change an item's cooldown">
    Open the item with `/specialsadmin`, right-click it in the list and use the **Editor ▸ settings** entry to edit
    its values, such as `cooldown`. Or edit the file: `cooldown: {seconds: 30}` on the item, or on one feature to give that
    feature its own. Add `combat-seconds` for a different wait while the player is combat-tagged. See
    [cooldown](/docs/exyliaspecials/items#cooldown).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear your own cooldown">
    Run `/specialsadmin`, open **Your cooldowns**, click a cooldown to clear it, or **Cooldowns ▸ Clear all**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear or change another player's">
    Run `/specialsadmin cooldown remove Steve freeze`, or one of the commands below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Use the item, then look at the action bar: `FREEZE 24s ⌚`. After `cooldown remove`, the item works again at once.

## The action bar

While a special item is cooling, the action bar lists it. The line is shared through ExyliaLib, so ExyliaCombat's
combat time and vanilla cooldowns appear on the same bar.

```text
FREEZE 24s ⌚ | FLIGHT 41s ⌚
```

Turn it off with `actionbar.enabled: false` in `config.yml` (default `true`). The layout — `separator` and `format` (`%item%`, `%time%`) — is under `actionbar` in `messages.yml`. Lunar and
Feather players get the HUD cooldowns instead and do not see the action bar.

## Commands


<Commands group="cooldowns" />

`<id>` is a special item id, `vanilla:<material>` such as `vanilla:ender_pearl` (a cooldown ExyliaCombat put on),
or `ALL` (every special item). The commands set it (up to 86400 seconds, 0 clears), add to it, take from it, or remove it; `remove` without an id clears every special item cooldown. All need `exyliaspecials.admin.cooldown`.

The **Your cooldowns** screen of `/specialsadmin` lists your own special item cooldowns; a click clears one, and
**Clear all** clears every special item cooldown you have.

## Placeholders

`%exyliaspecials_cooldown_<id>%` and `%exyliaspecials_oncooldown_<id>%`. See
[Placeholders](/docs/exyliaspecials/placeholders).
