title: "Cooldowns"
description: "Cooldowns of special items: where they are set, the action bar, and the commands that change them. Vanilla item cooldowns live in ExyliaCombat."
---

Special item cooldowns show on the item as the vanilla cooldown sweep, on the action bar, and as a HUD cooldown on
Lunar and Feather clients.

Cooldowns of five minutes or more survive a restart; shorter ones are lost.

Cooldowns on **vanilla items** — ender pearls, golden apples, totems — are no longer part of ExyliaSpecials: they
moved to ExyliaCombat with the combat tag. See [Combat](/docs/exyliaspecials/combat#moving-from-an-older-version).

## Special items

Set with `cooldown:` on the item or on a feature. See [Special items](/docs/exyliaspecials/items#cooldown).

## The action bar

While a special item is cooling, the action bar lists it. The line is shared through ExyliaLib, so ExyliaCombat's
combat time and vanilla cooldowns appear on the same bar.

```text
FREEZE 24s ⌚ | FLIGHT 41s ⌚
```

| `actionbar.` | Default | |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `true` | |

The layout — `separator` and `format` (`%item%`, `%time%`) — is under `actionbar` in `messages.yml`. Lunar and
Feather players get the HUD cooldowns instead and do not see the action bar.

## Commands

| Command | |
|---|---|
| `/specialsadmin cooldown set <player> <id> <seconds>` | Sets it; 0 clears. Up to 86400. |
| `/specialsadmin cooldown add <player> <id> <seconds>` | Adds to what is left. |
| `/specialsadmin cooldown decrease <player> <id> <seconds>` | Takes from what is left. |
| `/specialsadmin cooldown remove <player> [id]` | Clears it; every special item cooldown without an id. |

`<id>` is a special item id, `vanilla:<material>` such as `vanilla:ender_pearl` (a cooldown ExyliaCombat put on),
or `ALL` (every special item). All need `exyliaspecials.admin.cooldown`.

The **Your cooldowns** screen of `/specialsadmin` lists your own special item cooldowns; a click clears one, and
**Clear all** clears every special item cooldown you have.

## Placeholders

`%exyliaspecials_cooldown_<id>%` and `%exyliaspecials_oncooldown_<id>%`. See
[Placeholders](/docs/exyliaspecials/placeholders).
