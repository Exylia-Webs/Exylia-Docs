---
title: "Item cooldowns"
description: "Cooldowns on ordinary Minecraft items: triggers, combat values, per-world and per-region values, region use limits and how they are shown."
---

The `vanilla-cooldowns` module puts cooldowns on ordinary items, keyed by material: ender pearls, golden
apples, totems, fireworks. A cooldown is shown on the item itself in the hotbar, in the
[action bar](/docs/exyliacombat/combat-tag#action-bar), and on the HUD of clients that draw cooldowns. An
item on cooldown simply does nothing when used.

Items made by ExyliaSpecials are left alone: they have cooldowns of their own.

## The file

`modules/vanilla-cooldowns/config.yml`, one entry per material under `cooldowns`:

```yaml
cooldowns:
  ENDER_PEARL:
    trigger: LAUNCH
    cooldown: 4.0
    combat-cooldown: 14.0
    display-name: '{primary}Ender Pearl'
    regions:
      crystal_hole:
        cooldown: 1.0
    worlds: {}
  ENCHANTED_GOLDEN_APPLE:
    trigger: CONSUME
    cooldown: 300.0
    combat-cooldown: -1.0
    display-name: '{secondary}Enchanted Golden Apple'
    regions:
      pot:
        max-uses: 1
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `trigger` | What starts the cooldown, see below. Left out, an edible item is `CONSUME` and anything else `USE`. |
| `cooldown` | Seconds. |
| `combat-cooldown` | Seconds while the player is tagged. `-1` keeps `cooldown`. |
| `display-name` | How the item is named in the action bar and the admin panel. Empty: the material name. |
| `worlds.<world>.cooldown` | Seconds in that world. `-1` keeps the item's value. |
| `regions.<region>.cooldown` | Seconds in that region. `-1` keeps the item's value. |
| `regions.<region>.max-uses` | Uses allowed in that region, counted until the server restarts or the module is reloaded. `-1`: no limit. |

Which value applies, where the player stands: the world's value first, then the highest priority region's,
then `combat-cooldown` while tagged, then `cooldown`. Regions are WorldGuard regions and ExyliaLib regions,
by id, when those [safe-zone sources](/docs/exyliacombat/restrictions#safe-zones) are on.

A region with `max-uses` refuses the item once the player has used it that many times there, and the action
bar shows `actionbar.format-limited` (uses so far over the limit) while they stand in it.

## Triggers

| Trigger | Checked and started when |
|---|---|
| `USE` | The item is right-clicked. |
| `USE_ON_ELYTRA` | The item is right-clicked while gliding. |
| `CONSUME` | The item is eaten or drunk. |
| `LAUNCH` | The item is thrown: ender pearls, splash and lingering potions (configured as `SPLASH_POTION`), snowballs, eggs. |
| `BOW_SHOOT` | A bow or crossbow of that material is shot. |
| `ELYTRA_BOOST` | A firework boosts an elytra flight. |
| `RESURRECT` | A totem saves the player from death. While on cooldown, the totem does not save them. |

## Defaults

| Material | Trigger | Cooldown | In combat | Regions in the default file |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ENCHANTED_GOLDEN_APPLE` | `CONSUME` | 300s | same | `pot`, `mazoswap`, `mazo`: 1 use |
| `TOTEM_OF_UNDYING` | `RESURRECT` | 0s | same | `pot`, `mace`: 4 uses |
| `ENDER_PEARL` | `LAUNCH` | 4s | 14s | `crystal_hole`, `crystal_flat`, `mace`: 1s |
| `GOLDEN_APPLE` | `CONSUME` | 3s | same | `crystal_hole`, `crystal_flat`: 2s |
| `FIREWORK_ROCKET` | `ELYTRA_BOOST` | 12s | same | `__global__`: 0s |

The region names are examples from a practice server: rename them to your own regions or remove them.

## Staff

`/combatadmin cooldown set|add|decrease <player> <id|ALL> <seconds>` and
`/combatadmin cooldown remove <player> [id]` change a player's cooldowns, up to 86400 seconds. An id is the
material in lower case, such as `ender_pearl` (`vanilla:ender_pearl` also works); `ALL` is every configured
item. The admin panel lists every cooldown of a player, with left click to set, right click to add,
shift-left to decrease and shift-right to remove. See [Commands](/docs/exyliacombat/commands).

`%exyliacombat_cooldown_<material>%` and `%exyliacombat_oncooldown_<material>%` show a cooldown in other
plugins, such as `%exyliacombat_cooldown_ender_pearl%`.
