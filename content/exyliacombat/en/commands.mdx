Every ExyliaCombat command, with the permission it needs. A command only exists while its module is on: `/pvp` needs `pvp-toggle`, `/pvpstats` needs `stats`, and so on.

## Player commands

<Commands group="player" />

The three stats and PvP permissions are everyone's by default. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliacombat/permissions).

## Admin commands

All of them live under `/combatadmin`. Every one except the panel also runs from the console. The players they take must be online.

<Commands group="admin" />

## Rename a command

Every name can be changed. The first name in the list is the command, the rest are aliases, and an empty list registers no command. Renaming needs a restart.

- `commands.combat-time` and `commands.admin` in `config.yml`.
- `command-labels` in the settings of `pvp-toggle`, `newbie-protection` and `bounties`.
- `stats-command` and `top-command` in the settings of `stats`.

## The admin panel

`/combatadmin` opens a list of every online player with their combat state. The **Settings** button (under "Plugin") opens ExyliaLib's settings screens, where you can change every key of `config.yml` and of each module in game. `/exylialib config ExyliaCombat` opens the same screens. Messages and menus stay in their files.

The hub also has cards for Kill rewards and Bounties when those modules are on. Click a player to open their card:

| Card | Click | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Combat tag | left, right | Asks how long to tag them for, or untags them. |
| Cooldowns | click | Opens their item cooldowns: left set, right add, shift-left decrease, shift-right remove, and a **Remove all** button after a confirmation. |
| Newbie protection | left, right | Asks how long to protect them for, or removes it. |
| PvP | click | Switches their PvP. |
| Stats | click | Opens ExyliaLib's player reset: their stats, PvP switch, newbie protection and pending combat log are deleted after a confirmation, and they are kicked while it runs. Needs `exyliacombat.admin` and `exylialib.admin.wipe`. |

Cards of modules that are off are not drawn. Each button checks the same permission as its command.

## Reset a player or a season

ExyliaCombat has no reset command of its own. ExyliaLib resets one player with `/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaCombat` and empties a table with `/exylialib wipe ExyliaCombat <table|@kind|all>`. Both tables hold player progress, so a new-season wipe clears stats and bounty history too. See [ExyliaLib's commands](/docs/exylialib/commands#moving-and-wiping-data).

<Details title="Menu actions for your own menus and NPCs">
| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| `exyliacombat:leaderboard <stat>` | Opens the leaderboard of that stat. |
| `exyliacombat:stats` | Opens the clicker's stats, or on a leaderboard row, that player's. |
| `exyliacombat:admin_players` | Opens the admin panel. Needs `exyliacombat.admin`. |
| `exyliacombat:admin_settings` | Opens the settings screens. Needs `exyliacombat.admin`. |
</Details>
