---
title: "Placeholders"
description: "Every placeholder the plugin answers: the combat tag, item cooldowns, the PvP switch, both protections, a player's stats and the leaderboards."
---

The plugin registers its placeholders through ExyliaLib under the group `exyliacombat`, so the name is the
same inside Exylia plugins' own files and through **PlaceholderAPI**: `%exyliacombat_in_combat%`. Nothing has
to be downloaded from the eCloud. Every value is about the viewer and is read from memory; a placeholder
of a module that is off is not registered.

## Combat tag

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliacombat_in_combat%` | `true` or `false`. |
| `%exyliacombat_combat_time%` | Combat time left. |
| `%exyliacombat_opponent%` | Name of the player they are fighting, empty when none. |
| `%exyliacombat_provider%` | Who decides the tag: `BuiltIn`, `Manual`, `DeluxeCombat`, `CelestCombat` or `PvPManager`. |

## Item cooldowns: `vanilla-cooldowns`

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliacombat_cooldown_<material>%` | Cooldown left, such as `%exyliacombat_cooldown_ender_pearl%`. |
| `%exyliacombat_oncooldown_<material>%` | `true` or `false`. |

## Protection

| Placeholder | Module | Value |
|---|---|---|
| `%exyliacombat_pvp%` | `pvp-toggle` | `true` or `false`, forced worlds included. |
| `%exyliacombat_pvp_state%` | `pvp-toggle` | The `state-on` or `state-off` label from the module's messages. |
| `%exyliacombat_protected%` | `newbie-protection` | `true` or `false`. |
| `%exyliacombat_protection_time%` | `newbie-protection` | Newbie protection left. |
| `%exyliacombat_respawn_protected%` | `respawn-protection` | `true` or `false`. |
| `%exyliacombat_respawn_protection_time%` | `respawn-protection` | Respawn protection left. |

## Stats: `stats`

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliacombat_kills%` | Kills. |
| `%exyliacombat_deaths%` | Deaths. |
| `%exyliacombat_kdr%` | Kills over deaths, two decimals. |
| `%exyliacombat_streak%` | Current kill streak. |
| `%exyliacombat_best_streak%` | Best kill streak. |
| `%exyliacombat_points%` | Points. |
| `%exyliacombat_combat_logs%` | Punished combat logs. |

## Leaderboards: `stats`

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliacombat_top_<stat>_<rank>_name%` | The name at that place, empty when nobody holds it. |
| `%exyliacombat_top_<stat>_<rank>_value%` | Their value, `0` when nobody holds it. |

`<stat>` is `kills`, `deaths`, `kdr`, `streak`, `beststreak` or `points`, and `<rank>` starts at 1:
`%exyliacombat_top_kills_1_name%`, `%exyliacombat_top_points_3_value%`. Leaving the last part out gives the
name. The boards are the cached ones behind `/combattop`, refreshed every `leaderboard.refresh-interval`, and
hold `leaderboard.size` players.
