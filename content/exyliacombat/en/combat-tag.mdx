## How a player is tagged

With the built-in provider, a player is tagged whenever another player hurts them, and the attacker is
tagged too. What counts as a hit:

- any damage whose culprit is a player: melee, arrows and other projectiles, and whatever else ExyliaLib
  traces back to a player;
- a splash potion with a harmful effect that reaches the player;
- a lingering cloud with a harmful effect a player left behind.

Both players are tagged for `combat.duration` (default `15s`). A new hit renews the tag, but never shortens
one already running. Hitting yourself tags nobody, and nobody is tagged when either player stands in a world
listed in `combat.blacklisted-worlds`.

Hitting a [logout NPC](/docs/exyliacombat/logging-out#the-logout-npc) also tags the attacker, when the
module's `tag-attackers` is on.

## How the tag ends

| Reason | When |
|---|---|
| It runs out | `combat.duration` after the last hit. |
| The player dies | Always. |
| The player kills their opponent | With `combat.untag-on-kill` (default `true`): the killer is untagged when the player they killed was the one they were fighting, or when no opponent was on record. A killer still fighting someone else stays tagged. |
| A blacklisted world | Moving into a world listed in `combat.blacklisted-worlds` untags the player. |
| Logging out | See [Logging out](/docs/exyliacombat/logging-out). |
| Staff or a plugin | `/combatadmin untag`, the admin panel, or `Combat.untag` from another plugin. |

With `combat.notifications.on-tag` and `on-untag` (both `true`), the player is told in chat when they enter
and leave combat: `combat.tagged` and `combat.untagged` in `messages.yml`.

`exyliacombat.bypass` does not stop a player from being tagged. It frees them from every
[restriction](/docs/exyliacombat/restrictions) and from the barrier, but they still show as in combat
to other plugins and are still punished for logging out unless they also have `exyliacombat.bypass.logout`.

## Providers

`combat.provider` in `config.yml` picks who decides the tag:

| Value | Who tags |
|---|---|
| `AUTO` (default) | The first one installed of DeluxeCombat, CelestCombat and PvPManager, in that order; the built-in tag when none is. |
| `BUILTIN` | ExyliaCombat itself, with every rule on this site. |
| `MANUAL` | Nobody tags on hits: players are only tagged by `/combatadmin settag`, the admin panel or another plugin through the API. |
| `DELUXECOMBAT`, `CELESTCOMBAT`, `PVPMANAGER` | That plugin. When it is missing or cannot be hooked, the built-in tag is used and the console says so. |

The console names the provider on every start, and `%exyliacombat_provider%` shows it. `/combatadmin reload`
switches provider when `combat.provider` changed, or when `AUTO` would now pick another one.

What each provider covers:

| | Built-in | Manual | External plugin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tagged by hits | yes | no | by that plugin |
| Logout punishment, logout NPC | yes | no | by that plugin |
| `combat-restrictions`, `pvp-barrier` | yes | no | no |
| `/combatadmin settag`, `untag` | yes | yes | no: "Another combat plugin decides who is in combat." |
| `%exyliacombat_opponent%` | yes | no | CelestCombat only |
| Action bar, boss bar, titles, sounds, nametags | yes | yes | yes |
| PvP switch, newbie and respawn protection, stats, rewards | yes | yes | yes |

The modules that refuse hits (PvP switch, newbie and respawn protection) and the ones that count kills work
with any provider: they listen to the hits and deaths themselves.

## `/combat`

`/combat`, also `/ct`, tells a player how long their tag still lasts and who they are fighting:
`timer.tagged` in `messages.yml`, with `%time%` and `%opponent%` (`timer.no-opponent` when nobody is named),
or `timer.not-tagged`. The names come from `commands.combat-time` in `config.yml`.

## Showing the tag

Every mode below can be combined with the others. They follow the tag whichever provider decides it.

### Action bar

The `action-bar` module draws the combat timer and the [item cooldowns](/docs/exyliacombat/cooldowns) above
the hotbar, through ExyliaLib's shared action bar (other plugins' segments sit after this one). Its text is
in `messages.yml` under `actionbar`:

| Key | Default |
|---|---|
| `actionbar.combat-format` | `{error}⚔ Combat %time% ⌚` |
| `actionbar.format` | `%item%&r {info}%time% ⌚`, an item on cooldown |
| `actionbar.format-limited` | `%item% {success}%actual%{muted}/{error}%max% {warning}⚠`, an item with a per-region use limit. Also `%remaining%` and `%usage%`. |
| `actionbar.separator` | `&r {muted}\| &r`, between two entries |

The combat timer is left out of the action bar when `action-bar` in `modules/combat-display/config.yml` is
`false`.

<Callout type="note" title="Lunar players see it on the HUD instead">
The tag is also sent to the client as a HUD cooldown with a diamond sword icon, and item cooldowns with
their item. A player whose client draws HUD cooldowns (Lunar through Apollo, or the SurvivalCore mod) gets
no combat action bar from this plugin, since it would show the same thing twice. Feather draws no HUD
cooldowns, so Feather players keep the action bar. See ExyliaLib's
[client integration](/docs/exylialib/client-integration).
</Callout>

### Boss bar, titles and sounds

The `combat-display` module, `modules/combat-display/config.yml`. Text in `lang/<language>/modules/combat-display/messages.yml`, where
`%time%` is the tag left and `%opponent%` the player last fought.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `action-bar` | `true` | Whether the `action-bar` module shows the combat timer. |
| `boss-bar.enabled` | `false` | A boss bar that drains as the tag runs out, and grows back when a hit renews it. |
| `boss-bar.colour` | `RED` | `PINK`, `BLUE`, `RED`, `GREEN`, `YELLOW`, `PURPLE` or `WHITE`. |
| `boss-bar.overlay` | `PROGRESS` | `PROGRESS`, `NOTCHED_6`, `NOTCHED_10`, `NOTCHED_12` or `NOTCHED_20`. |
| `titles.on-tag`, `titles.on-untag` | `false`, `false` | A title when the tag starts and when it ends. |
| `titles.fade-in`, `stay`, `fade-out` | `0s`, `1500ms`, `500ms` | Title timings. |
| `sounds.on-tag` | `ITEM_ARMOR_EQUIP_IRON\|1.0\|0.8` | Played when the tag starts. `NAME\|volume\|pitch`; empty for none. |
| `sounds.on-untag` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING\|1.0\|1.4` | Played when it ends. |
| `sounds.countdown` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_HAT\|0.8\|1.2` | Played once a second during the last seconds of the tag. |
| `sounds.countdown-seconds` | `3` | How many last seconds play it. |

| Message | Default |
|---|---|
| `boss-bar` | `{error}&lIN COMBAT {letters_black}» {info}%time% ⌚` |
| `tag-title`, `tag-subtitle` | `{error}&lIN COMBAT`, `{letters}Do not log out for {info}%time% ⌚` |
| `untag-title`, `untag-subtitle` | `{success}&lSAFE`, `{letters}You are out of combat` |

### Nametags

The `combat-nametag` module is off by default. While a player is tagged, their name above the head changes for others, and goes back when
the tag ends. Needs PacketEvents; without it the module warns once and does nothing.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `colour` | `RED` | Name colour: `BLACK`, `DARK_BLUE`, `DARK_GREEN`, `DARK_AQUA`, `DARK_RED`, `DARK_PURPLE`, `GOLD`, `GRAY`, `DARK_GRAY`, `BLUE`, `GREEN`, `AQUA`, `RED`, `LIGHT_PURPLE`, `YELLOW` or `WHITE`. Empty leaves it alone. |
| `glow` | `false` | Outlines the player through walls, in the name colour (white without one). |
| `viewers` | `EVERYONE` | `EVERYONE`: every online player sees it. `OPPONENT`: only the player they are fighting. |

The `prefix` (`{error}⚔ `) and `suffix` (empty) live in `lang/<language>/modules/combat-nametag/messages.yml`.

<Callout type="warning" title="It takes the name out of TAB's team">
A colour, prefix or suffix puts the player in a team of its own for the tag's length, so a TAB or nametag
plugin's prefix is not shown meanwhile. A glow alone leaves the team untouched. This is why the module is
off by default.
</Callout>
