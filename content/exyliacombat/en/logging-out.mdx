---
title: "Logging out"
description: "What happens to a player who leaves while tagged: the punishment, who is spared, commands, the broadcast, the Discord webhook and the logout NPC."
---

Logging out is punished with the built-in provider only. With an external provider, that plugin handles its
own combat loggers; with `MANUAL`, nobody is punished.

## Who is punished

A tagged player who leaves the server is a combat logger, except:

| Case | What happens |
|---|---|
| The server is stopping or restarting | Nobody is punished. |
| They have `combat.logout.exempt-permission` | Default `exyliacombat.bypass.logout`. Empty: nobody is exempt. |
| They stand in a world of `combat.logout.exempt-worlds` | Not punished there. |
| They were kicked | Punished only with `combat.punish-on-kick` (default `false`): staff kicks and anti-cheat kicks are spared. A timeout is always punished. |
| They switched server under `cross-server` with `on-switch: CARRY` | The tag goes with them. See [Networks](/docs/exyliacombat/cross-server). |

## What happens, in order

<Steps>
  <Step title="CombatLogEvent">
    ExyliaLib's cancellable `CombatLogEvent` is fired while the player is still online. A plugin that
    cancels it spares them everything below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commands">
    `combat.commands-on-logout` run, with `%player%`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Broadcast and webhook">
    `combat.logout-broadcast` in `messages.yml` is sent to everyone else online, and
    `combat.logout.webhook` is posted to Discord.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Punishment">
    `combat.logout-punishment` is applied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="CombatUntagEvent">
    `CombatUntagEvent` fires with `LOGOUT` (`KICKED` for a spared kick), whether or not the log was
    cancelled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The `stats` module counts every punished logout as a combat log (`%exyliacombat_combat_logs%`).

## The punishment

| `combat.logout-punishment` | What happens |
|---|---|
| `KILL` (default) | The player dies on the spot, and `combat.commands-on-player-death` run. With the `logout-npc` module on, an NPC is left behind instead. |
| `DROP_INVENTORY` | Everything they carry falls where they stood, without a death. |
| `NOTHING` | No punishment. The commands, the broadcast and the webhook still run. |

With [loot protection](/docs/exyliacombat/protection#loot-protection) on, the items a `DROP_INVENTORY`
logout drops are reserved for the opponent, like the drops of a PvP death.

## Commands

`combat.commands-on-logout` and `combat.commands-on-player-death` take `%player%` (or `%player_name%`).
A line runs from the console unless it starts with `player:`; `console:` can be written for clarity, and a
leading `/` is dropped. At quit time only console commands are reliable.

## Broadcast

`combat.logout-broadcast` in `messages.yml`, sent to every other online player, with `%player%`,
`%opponent%` and `%time%` (the tag that was left). Empty sends nothing.

```yaml
logout-broadcast: '%prefix% {highlight}%player% {error}logged out in combat{letters}.'
```

## Discord webhook

`combat.logout.webhook` posts a message for every combat log, sent off the main thread. An empty `url`
sends nothing; a URL that is not a Discord webhook is ignored with a warning in the console.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `combat.logout.webhook.url` | empty | The webhook URL, `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/...`. |
| `combat.logout.webhook.message` | a red `COMBAT LOG` card | The message, in ExyliaLib's webhook template format. |

The default card has the title `COMBAT LOG`, the description `**%player%** logged out in combat.`, the
player's head as the author icon and four inline fields: Opponent, Time left, World and Punishment. Values:
`%player%`, `%opponent%` (`-` when none), `%time%`, `%world%`, `%punishment%` (`kill`, `drop inventory` or
`nothing`) and `%player_head%`. Every key of the template (colour, fields, buttons, footer, coalescing) is
described in ExyliaLib's [Discord webhooks](/docs/exylialib/discord-webhooks#the-template).

## The logout NPC

With `logout-punishment: KILL` and the `logout-npc` module on (default), a combat logger does not die on the
spot. They leave an NPC behind: a mannequin with their skin on 1.21.9 or newer, a zombie otherwise. It wears
their armour and held items and has their health, attributes and potion effects. The rest of their
inventory is held by the plugin, saved to the database.

- **The NPC is killed:** its killer gets the logger's whole inventory as drops (unless the world has
  `keepInventory`), with no experience. When the logger comes back, they are moved to where it died and
  killed, and `commands-on-npc-death` and `combat.commands-on-player-death` run for them. With loot
  protection on, the drops are reserved for whoever killed the NPC.
- **The NPC survives** its lifetime: it disappears, and when the logger comes back they get everything back,
  with the health the NPC had, where it stood.
- **The logger comes back while the NPC still stands:** the NPC is removed, they get everything back where it
  stood, and they are tagged again for the time it had left.

The NPC's name counts down: `combat.logout-npc-name` in `messages.yml`, with `%player%`, `%time%` and
`%health%`, and the mannequin carries `combat.logout-npc-tag` (`⚠ Disconnected in combat`) under it. It
does not move, does not burn, is never targeted by mobs and cannot be turned into another mob. With
WorldGuard, it can be hit wherever PvP is allowed.

A restart while NPCs stand removes them and gives the loggers everything back when they return. Pending
loggers are restored by the core even with the module switched off.

`modules/logout-npc/config.yml`:

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `reset-combat-timer` | `true` | `true`: the NPC lives for the full `combat.duration`. `false`: only for the tag the player had left. |
| `only-player-damage` | `true` | Only players can damage the NPC. |
| `tag-attackers` | `true` | Players who hit it are tagged. |
| `mannequin` | `true` | A skinned mannequin on 1.21.9+. `false`, or an older server: a zombie. |
| `announce-radius` | `50` | Players within this many blocks are sent `combat.logout-npc-spawned`. `0`: nobody. |
| `commands-on-npc-death` | `[]` | Commands run when the owner reconnects after their NPC was killed. `%player%`; `console:` (default) or `player:`. |
| `blacklisted-worlds` | `[]` | Worlds where no NPC is left: the logger is killed on the spot instead. |

<Callout type="note" title="The NPC announcement is empty by default">
`combat.logout-npc-spawned` in `messages.yml` ships empty, so nobody is told an NPC spawned until you write
a line there (`%player%` is the logger).
</Callout>
