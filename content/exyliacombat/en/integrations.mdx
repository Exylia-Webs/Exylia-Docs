Every plugin here is optional. Each is only used when it is installed and enabled, and a missing one costs
nothing.

| Plugin | What it adds |
|---|---|
| `packetevents` | The PvP barrier as translucent tiles (`style: DISPLAY`), and faster fake-block walls. Required by `combat-nametag`. Without it the barrier is drawn with fake blocks. |
| `PlaceholderAPI` | The plugin's placeholders in other plugins: scoreboards, tab, holograms. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliacombat/placeholders). |
| `WorldGuard` | Safe zones from `pvp deny` (`safe-zones.worldguard`), region ids for item cooldowns and `kill-rewards.disabled-regions`, and logout NPCs that can be hit wherever PvP is allowed. |
| `Lands`, `GriefPrevention`, `Towny`, `SuperiorSkyblock2` | Safe zones from areas, claims, towns and islands where PvP is off. See [Safe zones](/docs/exyliacombat/restrictions#safe-zones). |
| `DeluxeCombat`, `CelestCombat`, `PvPManager` | Can decide the tag instead of the built-in one, with `combat.provider` on `AUTO` or their name. See [Providers](/docs/exyliacombat/combat-tag#providers). |
| `Apollo` (Lunar Client) | The combat tag and item cooldowns as HUD cooldowns; those players get no combat action bar. |
| `FeatherServerAPI` | Detected by ExyliaLib's client integration. Feather draws no HUD cooldowns, so Feather players keep the action bar. |

## Through ExyliaLib

Some integrations come with ExyliaLib, and need nothing in ExyliaCombat's files:

| What | Used for |
|---|---|
| EssentialsX, CMI | `on-tag.disable-god-mode` and `on-tag.disable-vanish` of [combat restrictions](/docs/exyliacombat/restrictions#on-entering-combat). Without either, god mode means Bukkit invulnerability. |
| A clan plugin ExyliaLib supports | `share-with-clan` and `share-with-allies` of [loot protection](/docs/exyliacombat/protection#loot-protection). |
| The server's economy | Money for [kill rewards](/docs/exyliacombat/kill-rewards#money), through ExyliaLib's `economy.yml`. |
| ExyliaLib regions | Safe zones from regions that declare `pvp: false`: arenas and spawns other Exylia plugins create. |
| Redis | The [cross-server](/docs/exyliacombat/cross-server) tag and the shared player record. |
| Discord | The [combat log webhook](/docs/exyliacombat/logging-out#discord-webhook). |

## Other Exylia plugins

- **Every Exylia plugin** (ExyliaSpecials, ExyliaClans, ExyliaMines and the rest) reads the tag through
  ExyliaLib's `Combat` facade, which ExyliaCombat answers, so they all agree on who is fighting.
- **ExyliaSpecials**: its special items are skipped by [item cooldowns](/docs/exyliacombat/cooldowns), since
  they keep cooldowns of their own. Its old combat settings are imported on first start, see
  [Installation](/docs/exyliacombat/installation#coming-from-exyliaspecials).
