## Is ExyliaCombat free?

No. It is a premium plugin installed as a loader jar that checks the licence on every start. It is not sold
on BuiltByBit: request your key on the [Exylia Discord](https://discord.exylia.net) and put it in
`license_key` in `loader.yml`. See [Installation](/docs/exyliacombat/installation).

## I am never restricted when I test it

Operators have `exyliacombat.bypass` by default, which frees them from every restriction and the barrier,
and `exyliacombat.bypass.logout`, which spares them the logout punishment. Test with a normal player, or
deny both nodes. Creative and Spectator also keep their flight and elytra.

## Nothing is restricted, and logging out is not punished

Another combat plugin is deciding the tag. With `combat.provider: AUTO`, DeluxeCombat, CelestCombat or
PvPManager take over as soon as they are installed, and then restrictions, the barrier and the logout
punishment are theirs. The console names the provider on start. Set `combat.provider: BUILTIN` and run
`/combatadmin reload`.

## The barrier does not show, or walking into spawn is not refused

The plugin needs a source that says where PvP is off. Check that the spawn has `pvp deny` in WorldGuard (or
is a claim, town or island with PvP off), that its source is on under `safe-zones` in `config.yml`, and that
the player stands outside the zone: the wall is not drawn from inside one. Without PacketEvents the barrier is drawn with fake
blocks instead of tiles.

## My TAB prefixes disappear during a fight

That is `combat-nametag`: a colour, prefix or suffix puts a tagged player in a team of its own. Use only
`glow: true` with an empty `colour`, `prefix` and `suffix`, or switch the module off (it is off by default).

## Can a player escape a fight with `/server`?

Not without consequence on a network running `cross-server`. With `on-switch: CARRY` the tag follows them to
the new server; with `PUNISH` switching counts as a combat log. Without the module, a switch is a logout like
any other and is punished. See [Networks](/docs/exyliacombat/cross-server).

## Where did a combat logger's items go?

With the logout NPC they are held in the database until the player returns: they get everything back if the
NPC survived, and their killer got it as drops if it did not. With `DROP_INVENTORY` they fell where the player
stood. See [Logging out](/docs/exyliacombat/logging-out).

## Players farm kills on each other

`kill-abuse` is on by default: a second kill of the same victim within 5 minutes, and kills between accounts
on one address, count for neither stats nor rewards. Raise `repeat-kills.window`, add a `lockout`, or switch
on `low-playtime` to also stop fresh accounts being fed to a main. See
[Kill rewards](/docs/exyliacombat/kill-rewards#kill-abuse).

## Kills give no money

`money.enabled` in `modules/kill-rewards/config.yml` is `false` by default, and needs an economy ExyliaLib
can use. Kills flagged by `kill-abuse` and kills in `disabled-worlds` or `disabled-regions` pay nothing either.

## I changed a module switch and nothing happened

Module switches and command names are read at startup: restart the server. Everything else applies with
`/combatadmin reload`.
