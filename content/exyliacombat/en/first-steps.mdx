At the end of this page ExyliaCombat tags players who fight, blocks their escape commands, draws a wall at the edge of your spawn and punishes anyone who logs out mid-fight. The defaults work out of the box, so you only test them.

## Before you start

Install the loader jar and make sure ExyliaLib is on the server. See [Install ExyliaCombat](/docs/exyliacombat/installation). For the spawn wall you also need `packetevents` and a WorldGuard region with `pvp deny` around your spawn.

You need two accounts that are **not operators**. Operators hold `exyliacombat.bypass`, so they are never restricted. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliacombat/permissions).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the server">
    The console ends with `ExyliaCombat enabled` and names the combat provider it picked. With no other combat plugin installed, that is the built-in one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag yourself">
    On your operator account, run `/combatadmin settag 15 <player>` with the name of a test player. They are tagged for 15 seconds, the same length as a real fight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the timer">
    The test player runs `/combat` and sees how long the tag has left. The time also shows above the hotbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try to escape">
    While tagged, the test player runs `/spawn` or `/home`. The command is refused. Only `/msg`, `/tell`, `/w`, `/r`, `/reply` and `/discord` work during combat by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fight for real">
    Let the two test accounts hit each other. Both are tagged for 15 seconds, and a new hit does not shorten a running tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log out in combat">
    While tagged, one test account leaves the server. They are killed, and everyone else sees the combat log announcement. With the logout NPC on, an NPC wearing their gear stays behind first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

You should see the timer while fighting, refused commands and a punished logout. To see the wall, tag a test player and walk them towards your spawn: a red wall appears at the edge of the safe zone and stops them. If nothing happens, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliacombat/troubleshooting).

## Good first choices

- **Tag length**: 15 seconds is a common value. Change `combat.duration` in `config.yml`.
- **Modules**: all the main ones are already on. Switch off what you do not want under `modules` in `config.yml`, then restart. Cross-server, loot protection, respawn protection and nametags start off.
- **Another combat plugin**: if you already run DeluxeCombat, CelestCombat or PvPManager, ExyliaCombat uses it as the tag. See [Combat tag](/docs/exyliacombat/combat-tag#providers).
