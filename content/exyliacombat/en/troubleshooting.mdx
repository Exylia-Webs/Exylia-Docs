Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

The console names what is missing. ExyliaLib is required. The loader installs it for you and asks for a restart. See [Install ExyliaCombat](/docs/exyliacombat/installation).

## I am never restricted when I test

Operators hold `exyliacombat.bypass`, which frees them from every restriction and the barrier, and `exyliacombat.bypass.logout`, which spares them the logout punishment. Test with a normal player, or deny both permissions. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliacombat/permissions).

## Nothing is restricted and logging out is not punished

Another combat plugin is deciding the tag. With `combat.provider: AUTO`, DeluxeCombat, CelestCombat or PvPManager take over as soon as they are installed, and the restrictions, the barrier and the logout punishment become theirs. The console names the provider on start. To use ExyliaCombat's own rules, set `combat.provider: BUILTIN` in `config.yml` and run `/combatadmin reload`. See [Combat tag](/docs/exyliacombat/combat-tag#providers).

## A logout was not punished

The player is spared when the server is stopping, when they hold `exyliacombat.bypass.logout`, when they stand in a world listed in `combat.logout.exempt-worlds`, or when they were kicked and `combat.punish-on-kick` is `false` (the default). A timeout is always punished. See [Punish combat logging](/docs/exyliacombat/logging-out).

## The barrier does not show, or entering spawn is not refused

The plugin needs a source that says where PvP is off. Check that:

- The spawn has `pvp deny` in WorldGuard, or is a claim, town or island with PvP off.
- That source is on under `safe-zones` in `config.yml`.
- The player stands outside the zone: the wall is not drawn from inside one.

Without `packetevents` the barrier is drawn with fake blocks instead of translucent tiles. See [Restrict a tagged player](/docs/exyliacombat/restrictions).

## A command is still allowed in combat

By default only `/msg`, `/tell`, `/w`, `/r`, `/reply` and `/discord` work while tagged. If you set `commands.mode` to `BLOCK`, only the listed commands are refused. Check `commands` in `modules/combat-restrictions/config.yml`.

## Kills do not count

`kill-abuse` is on by default. A second kill of the same victim within five minutes, and kills between accounts on the same address, count for neither stats nor rewards. Two friends testing from one home count as one address. See [Stop kill farming](/docs/exyliacombat/kill-rewards#kill-abuse).

## Kills give no money

`money.enabled` in `modules/kill-rewards/config.yml` is `false` by default, and it needs an economy that ExyliaLib can use. Kills flagged by `kill-abuse`, and kills in `disabled-worlds` or `disabled-regions`, pay nothing either. Kill streaks and milestones also need the `stats` module on.

## I changed a module switch and nothing happened

Module switches and command names are read at startup. Restart the server. `/combatadmin reload` reads the settings and messages, not the switches.

## My TAB prefixes disappear during a fight

That is `combat-nametag`: a colour, prefix or suffix puts a tagged player in a team of its own. Use only `glow: true` with an empty `colour`, `prefix` and `suffix`, or switch the module off. It is off by default.

## A placeholder shows as text

PlaceholderAPI is missing, the module of that placeholder is off, or the stat name is misspelled. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliacombat/placeholders).
