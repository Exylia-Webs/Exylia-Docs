These placeholders need PlaceholderAPI. ExyliaArmorSkin registers them under the identifier `exyliaarmorskin`; click one to copy it.

<Placeholders />

They count what the crate or the shop unlocked, not what a permission grants. `tier_count` needs no player, so it works in a hologram; the other three answer empty there.

## Example

```yaml
lines:
  - " {letters_black}▎ {letters}Keys {letters_black}» {info}%exyliaarmorskin_keys%"
  - " {letters_black}▎ {letters}Skins {letters_black}» {info}%exyliaarmorskin_unlocked%"
```

<Callout type="note" title="No placeholder for the skin being worn">
There is no placeholder for the skin on a piece. A plugin that needs it asks the [API](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/api)'s `shown`, which answers after the mode, the armour and the permission have had their say.
</Callout>
