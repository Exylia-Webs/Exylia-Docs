---
title: "Placeholders"
description: "Four placeholders about the crate: keys, what it unlocked, and how many skins a rarity holds."
---

Registered with PlaceholderAPI under the identifier `exyliaarmorskin` when PlaceholderAPI is installed.
Every placeholder below is written in full, and clicking one copies it.

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliaarmorskin_keys%` | The crate keys the player holds on their account. |
| `%exyliaarmorskin_unlocked%` | How many skins the crate (or the shop) unlocked for them that `skins.yml` still declares. |
| `%exyliaarmorskin_unlocked_<id>%` | `true` or `false`: whether they unlocked that skin — `%exyliaarmorskin_unlocked_galaxy%`. |
| `%exyliaarmorskin_tier_count_<tier>%` | How many skins a rarity holds — `%exyliaarmorskin_tier_count_legendary%`. |

They count what was unlocked only, not what a permission grants. `tier_count` answers without a
player, so it works in a hologram; the other three answer empty there. See
[The crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate).

## Examples

```yaml
lines:
  - " {letters_black}▎ {letters}Keys {letters_black}» {info}%exyliaarmorskin_keys%"
  - " {letters_black}▎ {letters}Skins {letters_black}» {info}%exyliaarmorskin_unlocked%"
```

<Callout type="note" title="Nothing here describes what a player is wearing">
There is no placeholder for the skin on a piece. A plugin that needs it asks the API's `shown`, which
answers after the mode, the armour in the slot and the permission have had their say — see
[API](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/api).
</Callout>
