ExyliaArmorSkin puts a **skin** on a piece of armour: a colour, an optional trim and an optional animation, drawn on the way to the client. The netherite chestplate stays a netherite chestplate on the server, and everyone sees blue leather with a vex trim. Enchantments, attributes, durability and other plugins' data are never touched.

## What you need

A Paper 1.21.1+ (or Folia 1.21.4+) server on Java 21 with ExyliaLib and packetevents. Details in [Installation](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/installation).

## What you will have in 10 minutes

1. The plugin installed and running ([Installation](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/installation)).
2. Yourself wearing a skin from the wardrobe and another from an item ([Your first skin](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/first-steps)).
3. A skin of your own in `skins.yml`, animated if you like ([Create and edit skins](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/skins)).
4. Optionally, a crate players win skins from ([Set up the crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate)).

Forty skins ship, every one animated and with a set bonus, so players have something to wear before you write anything.

## Two ways a skin gets worn

| Way | Whose it is | Which server it suits |
|---|---|---|
| **A skin item** | The armour's: one value is written onto the piece and travels with it through chests, trades and deaths. | Survival, economy |
| **The wardrobe** | The player's: stored per player and per slot in a table, and it lands on whatever they equip. | Practice, PvP |

`behaviour.mode` decides which is read — `item`, `wardrobe` or `both` — and is covered in
[The wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/wardrobe).

A skin item is used by dragging: drop it onto a piece of armour in your own inventory and it is
applied; drop a **remover** onto skinned armour and the skin comes off and is handed back as the item
again. The wardrobe is `/wardrobe`: one screen, click a slot, click a skin — or, with
`menu.style: SPATIAL`, a [3D wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/3d-wardrobe) in the world where players try
skins on their own body and buy and save in place. The crate can open in the world too.

<Callout type="note" title="The armour is never rewritten">
An applied skin item writes a single value onto the piece; a wardrobe choice writes nothing at all.
Everything else is packets, so what any other plugin reads from that armour is exactly what it had.
</Callout>

## What ships

Forty skins in `skins.yml` and forty-one animations in `animations.yml`, built from seven animation types: `rainbow`, `pulse`, `frames`, `wave`, `sweep`, `bloom` and `flicker`. Each skin carries a **set bonus**: particles around the body, a line when the set closes and another when it breaks, once every piece it fits is worn with it.

Each skin owns a permission derived from its id, `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>`, with a node per slot underneath it (`exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>.helmet`). A player owns a skin through that node or through an **unlock**: won from the [crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate), bought in the wardrobe with currency, or handed over by an admin, and kept for good whatever happens to their rank. A wearer who owns the skin neither way is shown their real armour.

Lobbies can show skins off with a **showcase**: a mannequin that turns on the spot in one skin after another, wearing the skin of a player watching. Every menu and message ships in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Russian and Turkish.

## What the client is shown

<Details title="What the client is shown">

| On the real item | On the visual |
|---|---|
| Netherite chestplate | The leather piece of the same slot, in the skin's colour |
| Its trim, or none | The skin's trim, when the skin sets one |
| Its display name | The same name, or the vanilla name of the real material |
| Its lore | The same lore, then a blank line, then the `armor-lore` lines |
| Its enchantments | Hidden, but the glint is kept |
| Its durability | The real damage **and the real maximum**, so the bar reads true |
| Its attributes | Hidden, and never replaced by leather's |

</Details>

## Who sees a skin

<Details title="Who sees a skin">

| Viewer | Sees |
|---|---|
| Everyone tracking the wearer | The skin, on the body |
| The wearer, in their own inventory and paper doll | The skin |
| The wearer **in creative** | The real armour — a creative client echoes its slots back to the server, so it must be shown the truth |
| Anyone looking at a skinned piece in a chest, hotbar or on the cursor | The real item with the skin's lore lines added |
| Everyone, when the **wearer** owns that skin neither by permission nor by unlock | The real armour — the id stays on the item, or the row stays in the wardrobe, and the look returns with the node |
| Anyone looking at an **elytra** | The elytra. It sits in the chest slot but is not armour, and dressing it as a chestplate would cost the wearer their flight |

</Details>
