---
title: "The 3D wardrobe"
description: "The wardrobe standing in the world: try skins on your own body, buy and save in place, revert, unequip and open the crate without an inventory window."
---

The 3D wardrobe is the same wardrobe as [the inventory one](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/wardrobe), drawn in
the world in front of the player instead of in a chest. The skins run on an arc at eye level, the
pieces being dressed sit above them, and in the middle the player stands on a pedestal wearing what they
are trying on. Owning, wearing and buying go through the same code as the inventory wardrobe, so the two
screens never disagree about what a player has.

## Opening it

| How | What opens |
|---|---|
| `/wardrobe 3d` (alias `/armorskins 3d`) | Always the 3D wardrobe. |
| `/wardrobe classic` | Always the inventory wardrobe. |
| `/wardrobe` | Whichever `menu.style` in `config.yml` names: `CLASSIC` (default) or `SPATIAL`. The API's `openWardrobe`, `/armorskinsadmin wardrobe open` and the `armorskin:wardrobe` action follow the same setting. |
| A placed spot | `/armorskinsadmin spatial place` leaves a small clickable hologram where you stand. Clicking it walks the player onto the spot and opens the wardrobe there, facing the way the spot faces. Spots are kept in `spatial/spots.yml`. |

```yaml title="config.yml"
menu:
  style: SPATIAL   # CLASSIC: the inventory menu
```

Every way in asks for `exyliaarmorskin.wardrobe`, and with `behaviour.mode: item` it answers
`wardrobe-disabled` exactly as the inventory wardrobe does.

<Callout type="note" title="Older clients get the classic wardrobe">
Drawing it needs a 1.21.4 client or newer. A player on an older client who asks for the 3D wardrobe is
shown the inventory one instead, with nothing to configure.
</Callout>

## Using it

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| The title | The skin looked at, else the one being tried, with one quiet line: worn, owned, its price or locked. |
| The pieces | **Full set**, Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots, as plain words. The one being dressed is underlined. |
| The arc | Four skins either side of the body. The mouse wheel or the arrows turn it a page. |
| Clicking a skin | Tries it on the piece being dressed, or on every piece it fits for the full set. The body wears it at once, owned or not; nothing is saved yet. |
| The body | Clicking it turns it to its side, its back and its front again; the arrows beside it and the wheel over it turn it too. |
| The primary button | **SAVE** wears every piece that was changed. A draft holding a skin the player does not own reads **BUY & SAVE** with the price, then **CONFIRM** for the second click that pays. |
| The icons | Clear (the dressed piece, or the full set, back to bare), revert the drafts to what is worn, unequip every skin, the crate, the guided tour again, and leave. Revert and unequip only show while they would do something. |

An **Unsaved** pill sits beside the button while something is tried but not saved. Leaving with changes
unsaved drops them, and the player is told so. The first time a player opens the wardrobe a short guided
tour points out the skins, the pieces, the button and the icons; the `?` icon shows it again.

While it is open the player is held still and sees nobody, and nobody sees them.

<Callout type="note" title="Buying follows the shop">
BUY & SAVE buys the **whole** skin at the [shop's](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate#buying-with-currency)
price, exactly like the inventory wardrobe's buy button, and only for a player holding
`exyliaarmorskin.buy`: without it a locked skin reads locked, never its price. A draft holding a skin
nobody sells stops SAVE with `save-blocked`, naming the skin and the piece. The crate icon needs
`exyliaarmorskin.crate`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/permissions).
</Callout>

## The layout

Where every piece stands is `spatial/wardrobe.yml`; every word it draws is under `spatial` in
`lang/<language>/messages.yml`. Coordinates are blocks on a board standing `anchor.distance` (4.5) in
front of the player's eyes.

| Block | What it sets |
|---|---|
| `anchor` | How far ahead the board stands and how high it is lifted, so its lowest piece stays above the floor. |
| `hide-others` · `freeze` | Hide everybody else, and hold the player still, while it is open. Both `true`. |
| `theme` | Text sizes, the page arrows, the locked and worn markers. |
| `panels` | The title, the piece words, the primary button, the Unsaved pill, the icon row and the hint line. |
| `preview` | The body on its pedestal: its size, `turn-speed`, `facing` and `turn-controls`, the arrows that turn it. |
| `carousel` | The arc: how many skins, their spacing and curve. |
| `intro` | The guided tour's steps. |
| `backdrop` | The soft dark backdrop behind the board, so it reads on sky and ground alike. |

Pieces are moved in game rather than by hand: `/armorskinsadmin spatial edit` opens the wardrobe for an
admin to aim at a piece, click it and move it with the mouse wheel.

## Admin commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `spatial place` | Sets a 3D wardrobe down where you stand, facing the way you face. |
| `spatial remove` | Removes the placed wardrobe nearest you, up to four blocks away. |
| `spatial edit` | Opens the wardrobe to move its pieces. |

All under `/armorskinsadmin`, so all need `exyliaarmorskin.admin`.

<Callout type="tip" title="The crate has a 3D opening too">
`crate.opening: SPATIAL` plays the crate in the world in front of the player, and the prize is tried on
your own body. See [The crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate#the-3d-opening).
</Callout>
