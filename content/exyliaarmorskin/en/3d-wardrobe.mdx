The 3D wardrobe is the wardrobe drawn in the world in front of the player: skins on an arc, the player on a pedestal wearing what they are trying on. You end with `/wardrobe` opening it, or a clickable spot in your lobby that does.

It is the same wardrobe as [the inventory one](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/wardrobe): owning, wearing and buying use the same code, so the two never disagree about what a player has.

## Before you start

- The inventory wardrobe works, see [Set up the wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/wardrobe). With `behaviour.mode: item` there is no wardrobe at all.
- Players need a 1.21.4 client or newer. An older client asking for the 3D wardrobe gets the inventory one instead, with nothing to configure.

## Make it the default

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change the style">
    In `config.yml`:
    ```yaml title="config.yml"
    menu:
      style: SPATIAL   # CLASSIC: the inventory menu
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/armorskinsadmin reload`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Run `/wardrobe`. `/wardrobe 3d` (alias `/armorskins 3d`) always opens the 3D one and `/wardrobe classic` always the inventory one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Place one in your lobby

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stand where it goes">
    Face the way the wardrobe should face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the wizard">
    Run `/armorskinsadmin spatial place` and confirm in the setup wizard. A small clickable hologram appears. Spots are kept in `spatial/spots.yml`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Click the hologram: the player walks onto the spot and the wardrobe opens there, facing the way the spot faces. `/armorskinsadmin spatial remove` removes the nearest spot, up to four blocks away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

The skins stand on an arc at eye level, the pieces above them, and you in the middle. Click a skin: your body wears it at once, owned or not, and nothing is saved yet. The **Unsaved** pill appears beside the main button. Press **SAVE** to wear it. Sneak to leave.

## Using it

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| The title | The skin looked at, else the one being tried, with one quiet line: worn, owned, its price or locked. |
| The pieces | **Full set**, Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots, as plain words. The one being dressed is underlined. |
| The arc | Four skins either side of the body. The mouse wheel or the arrows turn a page. |
| Clicking a skin | Tries it on the piece being dressed, or on every piece it fits for the full set. |
| The body | Clicking it turns it to its side, its back and its front again; the arrows beside it and the wheel over it turn it too. |
| The main button | **SAVE** wears every piece that was changed. A draft holding a skin the player does not own reads **BUY & SAVE** with the price, then **CONFIRM** for the second click that pays. |
| The icons | Clear (the dressed piece, or the full set, back to bare), revert the drafts to what is worn, unequip every skin, the crate, the guided tour again (`?`), and leave. Revert and unequip only show while they would do something. |

Leaving with unsaved changes drops them, and the player is told so. The first time a player opens the wardrobe, a short guided tour points out the skins, the pieces, the button and the icons. While it is open the player is held still and sees nobody, and nobody sees them.

<Callout type="note" title="Buying follows the shop">
BUY & SAVE buys the **whole** skin at the [shop's](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate#buying-with-currency) price, exactly like the inventory wardrobe's buy button, and only for a player holding `exyliaarmorskin.buy`: without it a locked skin reads locked, never its price. A draft holding a skin nobody sells stops SAVE and names the skin and the piece. The crate icon needs `exyliaarmorskin.crate`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/permissions).
</Callout>

## Going further

### Move the pieces

Run `/armorskinsadmin spatial edit`: the wardrobe opens for you to aim at a piece, click it and move it with the mouse wheel. Layout is saved in `spatial/wardrobe.yml`.

### Change the words

Every word it draws is under `spatial` in `lang/<language>/messages.yml`.

<Callout type="tip" title="The crate has a 3D opening too">
`crate.opening: SPATIAL` plays the crate in the world in front of the player, and the prize is tried on your own body. See [Set up the crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate#the-3d-opening).
</Callout>

## Technical reference

Coordinates are blocks on a board standing `anchor.distance` (4.5) in front of the player's eyes.

<Details title="Layout file: spatial/wardrobe.yml">

Where every piece stands is `spatial/wardrobe.yml`; every word it draws is under `spatial` in
`lang/<language>/messages.yml`. Coordinates are blocks on a board standing `anchor.distance` (4.5) in
front of the player's eyes.

| Block | What it sets |
|---|---|
| `anchor` | How far ahead the board stands and how high it is lifted, so its lowest piece stays above the floor. |
| `hide-others` · `freeze` | Hide everybody else, and hold the player still, while it is open. Both `true`. |
| `theme` | Text sizes, the page arrows, the locked and worn markers. |
| `panels` | The title, the piece words, the primary button, the Unsaved pill, the icon row and the hint line. |
| `preview` | The body on its pedestal: its size, `turn-speed`, `facing` and `turn-controls`, the arrows that turn it. |
| `carousel` | The arc: how many skins, their spacing and curve. |
| `intro` | The guided tour's steps. |
| `backdrop` | The soft dark backdrop behind the board, so it reads on sky and ground alike. |

Pieces are moved in game rather than by hand: `/armorskinsadmin spatial edit` opens the wardrobe for an
admin to aim at a piece, click it and move it with the mouse wheel.

</Details>
