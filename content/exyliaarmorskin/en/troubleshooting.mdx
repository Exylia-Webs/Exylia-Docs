Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

ExyliaArmorSkin needs `packetevents` (hard requirement) and `ExyliaLib` in `plugins/`. The server refuses to enable it without packetevents, and nothing works without ExyliaLib. Needs Paper 1.21.1 or newer (Folia 1.21.4 or newer) and Java 21. See [Installation](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/installation).

## A player does not see the skin they applied

By default a player who owns the skin neither by permission nor by unlock is shown their real armour. Grant `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>` (or `exyliaarmorskin.skin.*`), hand an unlock with `/armorskinsadmin unlocks grant <player> <skin>`, or set `behaviour.hide-without-permission: false`. Then run `/armorskinsadmin refresh <player>`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/permissions).

## Nobody sees any skin in one world

`behaviour.disabled-worlds` in `config.yml` lists worlds where skins are hidden. Remove the world and run `/armorskinsadmin reload`.

## I see my real armour in creative

This is on purpose. A creative client sends its inventory back to the server, so it is always shown the real items. Other players still see the skin.

## An elytra shows no skin

An elytra sits in the chest slot but is not armour, and dressing it as a chestplate would cost the wearer their flight. It is never skinned.

## `/wardrobe` says it is disabled or locked

- `wardrobe-disabled`: `behaviour.mode` is `item`. Set it to `wardrobe` or `both` and run `/armorskinsadmin reload`.
- `feature-locked`: the player lacks `exyliaarmorskin.wardrobe`. Everybody has it by default, so a permission plugin is denying it.

## A skin item does nothing when I drag it

Skin items are read only inside your own inventory window, onto a piece of armour. Dropping one onto armour in a chest does nothing. The chat message tells you why: `not-armor`, `unsupported-piece` (the skin does not include that piece), `already-skinned` (use a remover first).

## A skin disappeared after I edited `skins.yml`

The id changed. The armour keeps the id it was given, so renaming an entry orphans every piece wearing it: they show their real look, and a remover says `unknown-skin`. Restore the key and the look comes back.

## The shop shows no prices, or players cannot buy

- `shop.enabled` must be `true`, and the skin needs a price: its own `price`, or one from `shop.tier-prices` for its rarity.
- A currency is needed: ExyliaEconomy or Vault. With neither, nothing is for sale.
- The player needs `exyliaarmorskin.buy`; without it a locked skin never shows its price.

## The crate says I have no keys, or does not open

Keys live on the account: check with `/armorskinsadmin keys check <player>` and add with `/armorskinsadmin keys give <player> 1`. Other messages: `crate.disabled` means `crate.enabled` is off, `crate.busy` means the last opening is still falling, `feature-locked` means the rank lacks `exyliaarmorskin.crate`. See [Set up the crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate).

## A crate winner is not worn

With `crate.reward: UNLOCK` (default) the skin is unlocked on the account, and the player wears it from the wardrobe. On an `item` server there is no wardrobe, so set `crate.reward` to `ITEM` or `BOTH`.

## The 3D wardrobe or crate shows the inventory one

Drawing 3D needs a 1.21.4 client or newer; older clients always get the inventory screens. If you want it for everybody, ask players to update.

## A change in `skins.yml` or a menu does not show

Run `/armorskinsadmin reload`. It re-reads every file and redraws everyone online.
