Effects are what a skin does beyond looking different: a sound or title when it goes on, particles while it is worn, and a bonus when the whole set is on. You end with a skin that plays a sound when equipped and a particle around the body when the set is complete. Every part is optional.

## Before you start

You have a skin, see [Create and edit skins](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/skins). The forty shipped skins already carry effects, so you can copy from them in `skins.yml`.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the skin">
    In `skins.yml`, find your skin and add an `effects` block under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a sound on equip">
    ```yaml title="skins.yml"
    skins:
      inferno:
        effects:
          on-equip:
            sound:
              name: ITEM_ARMOR_EQUIP_NETHERITE
              volume: 0.8
              pitch: 1.2
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a particle while the set is worn">
    ```yaml title="skins.yml"
    skins:
      inferno:
        effects:
          set:
            ambient:
              particle: 'FLAME|4|0.4|0.01'
              interval: 0.25
              radius: 32.0
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/armorskinsadmin reload`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Put the skin on: you hear the sound. Wear every piece the skin fits and flames appear around your body; take one off and they stop. If you hear nothing, check the sound name (a Minecraft sound id such as `ITEM_ARMOR_EQUIP_NETHERITE`) and see [Common problems](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/troubleshooting).

## Going further

### Titles, action bars and more on equip

`on-equip` and `on-unequip` are standard ExyliaLib effects played to the wearer. Each can carry a `title`, `action-bar`, `boss-bar`, `sound`, `particle` and `firework`. The full reference is the [ExyliaLib effects module](/docs/exylialib/effects).

<Callout type="note" title="They fire on a real equip, not on every redraw">
`on-equip` plays when a piece is equipped or when its skin changes on the body. Joining, respawning, changing world, `/armorskinsadmin refresh` and `/armorskinsadmin reload` rebuild the visual **silently**, so a player logging in is not greeted by four titles. Swapping armour back and forth plays them at most once a second.
</Callout>

### Particles on each piece

`ambient` repeats at **each worn piece**, at that piece's own height, for as long as the piece is worn. `particle` is `NAME|count|spread|speed`, `sound` is `NAME|volume|pitch`, `interval` is seconds between repetitions (minimum `0.05`, default `1.0`) and `radius` is how many blocks around can see or hear it (default `32.0`). Details below.

### Set bonus

`effects.set` fires when a player wears **every piece the skin fits** with that same skin: all four when `pieces` is empty, two for a skin with `pieces: [helmet, chestplate]`.

| Key | What it is |
|---|---|
| `on-complete` | An effect played once, the moment the set becomes complete. |
| `on-break` | An effect played once, the moment it stops being complete. |
| `ambient` | The same keys as the per-piece ambient, played **around the player** at a height of 1.0 while the set holds. |

The set is judged from the topmost skinned piece downwards: whatever skin sits in the first occupied slot is the only one that can complete. Three `inferno` pieces and an `ocean` helmet complete nothing.

Every shipped skin carries the full show: a sound as it goes on, a particle around the body while the set holds, an action bar line `✦ Full set » <NAME>` when it closes and `✧ Set broken` when it ends. Changing straight from one complete set to another plays the new `on-complete` but not the old `on-break`.

### The sound of applying and removing

Putting a skin on and taking it off with a skin item or remover play their own sound, set under `effects.on-apply` and `effects.on-remove` in `config.yml` (defaults `ITEM_ARMOR_EQUIP_LEATHER` and `ITEM_BUNDLE_REMOVE_ONE`, volume 0.7). They only fire on a successful drag in the inventory: a refused apply plays nothing, and neither does the [API](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/api).

<Details title="Effect sections and ambient keys">

**`on-equip` and `on-unequip`**

Both are standard ExyliaLib effects, so each one may carry any combination of six sections. They are
played **to the wearer**.

| Section | Keys |
|---|---|
| `title` | `text`, `subtitle`, `fade-in`, `stay`, `fade-out`, `countdown`, `time-style` |
| `action-bar` | `text`, `duration`, `countdown`, `time-style` |
| `boss-bar` | `text`, `colour`, `overlay`, `countdown`, `count-up`, `progress`, `time-style` |
| `sound` | `name`, `volume`, `pitch`, `category` |
| `particle` | `name`, `count`, `spread`, `speed` |
| `firework` | `colours`, `fades`, `shape`, `flicker`, `trail` |

A section that says nothing is left out of the file entirely, which is why the default `skins.yml`
shows no `sound:` block under `on-equip`. Write the section by hand and it is read from then on. The
full reference lives in the [ExyliaLib effects module](/docs/exylialib/effects).

<Callout type="note" title="They fire on a real equip, not on every redraw">
`on-equip` plays when a piece is equipped or when its skin changes on the body. Joining, respawning,
changing world, `/armorskinsadmin refresh` and `/armorskinsadmin reload` all rebuild the visual **silently** — a
player logging in is not greeted by four titles. Swapping armour back and forth plays them at most
once a second: a change inside that second redraws the piece without a sound.
</Callout>

**`ambient`**

Repeated for as long as the piece is worn.

| Key | Default | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `particle` | `''` | `NAME\|count\|spread\|speed`. Empty for none. |
| `sound` | `''` | `NAME\|volume\|pitch`. Empty for none. |
| `interval` | `1.0` | Seconds between repetitions. Values below `0.05` are raised to it. |
| `radius` | `32.0` | Blocks around the player that can see or hear it. Values below `1.0` are raised to it. |

Both lines are pipe-separated, and a namespaced key survives because the colon is never a separator:
`minecraft:flame|10|0.4|0.01` is valid. The particle line also accepts the longer
`NAME|count|dx|dy|dz|speed` form when you want the spread to differ per axis.

### Where it plays

At the wearer's feet plus the height of the piece the skin is on, so four skinned pieces are four
different heights:

| Piece | Height |
|---|---|
| `helmet` | 1.7 |
| `chestplate` | 1.2 |
| `leggings` | 0.7 |
| `boots` | 0.2 |

### What it costs

One repeating task per **worn piece that declares an ambient**, bound to the player's entity so Folia
is happy and so it dies with them. It is cancelled the moment the piece comes off, the skin changes,
or the player quits; nothing survives a plugin disable.

<Callout type="warning" title="An interval of 0.05 is a task every tick, per piece, per player">
The floor exists so a typo cannot ask for less, not as an invitation. A particle once a second on a
chestplate is what the defaults are shaped for.
</Callout>

</Details>
