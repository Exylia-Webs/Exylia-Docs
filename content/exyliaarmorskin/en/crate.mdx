title: "The crate"
description: "Rarities, both kinds of key, the reels that fall, buying a skin with currency, and the blocks that open the crate."

The crate is how skins are won rather than given. A key is spent, a reel falls, and what it lands on
is unlocked on the player's account: the whole skin, every piece it fits, owned for good whatever
happens to their rank afterwards. The crate itself is ExyliaLib's; this plugin hands it the skins in
`skins.yml` and reads its settings from `config.yml` and its lines from `messages.yml`.

It works in every `behaviour.mode`. On an `item` server there is no wardrobe to wear an unlock in, so
set `crate.reward` to `ITEM` or `BOTH` there and the crate hands over the skin item.

## Rarities

Every skin declares a `tier` in `skins.yml`, and `crate.tiers` in `config.yml` declares what the tiers
are:

```yaml title="config.yml"
crate:
  tiers:
    common:
      name: "Common"
      color: "{muted}"
      chance: 60.0
      priority: 1
    rare:
      name: "Rare"
      color: "{info}"
      chance: 25.0
      priority: 2
    epic:
      name: "Epic"
      color: "{accent}"
      chance: 12.0
      priority: 3
    legendary:
      name: "Legendary"
      color: "{highlight}"
      chance: 3.0
      priority: 4
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `name` | What menus and messages call it. |
| `color` | The colour its name and its skins are written in. |
| `chance` | How often a crate lands on it, **relative to the other rarities**. |
| `priority` | Where it sits in menus; lower comes first. |

The forty shipped skins are 14 common, 12 rare, 9 epic and 5 legendary: one colour and a trim stay
common, and the more a skin moves the rarer it is. The full list is on
[Skins](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/skins#the-forty-that-ship).

Nothing here is hard-coded. Delete `legendary`, invent `mythic`, rename all four: the key is the id
`skins.yml` writes as `tier`, and a skin naming a rarity that no longer exists counts as the first
one. A skin with no `tier` is `common`.

<Callout type="note" title="chance is a weight, not a percentage">
The shares are worked out from the weights, so four rarities weighted `60/25/12/3` and four weighted
`6/2.5/1.2/0.3` behave identically. Deleting a rarity re-spreads its odds across the rest instead of
leaving a three-percent hole.
</Callout>

## Keys

Two kinds, and the crate takes both.

### On the account

A number on the player's crate row. Nothing can be dropped, duped, or lost to a full inventory:

```text
/armorskinsadmin keys give <player> <amount>
/armorskinsadmin keys take <player> <amount>
/armorskinsadmin keys set <player> <amount>
/armorskinsadmin keys check <player>
```

Safe to run from console and for players who are offline — a shop, a vote listener or a reward plugin
hands keys over the same way:

```text
[console] armorskinsadmin keys give %player% 1
```

Every player is given `crate.start-keys` keys — none by default — the first time they are seen.

### In the hand

An item, so it can be sold, put in a kit, dropped and given to a friend:

```text
/armorskinsadmin item key <player> [1-64]
```

Right click a bound crate block holding one and it opens on the spot: one key, one reel, no question
screen. Left clicking never spends one — that is how a player asks what a block is. How the key is
drawn is `crate.key-item` in `config.yml` (material and glow) and `key-item` in `messages.yml` (name
and lore); it is recognised by what it carries in its data, not by its name or material. Keys that do
not fit in the inventory wait and are handed over on the player's next join, with `rewards-claimed`.

## Opening

`/wardrobe crate`, the **SKIN CRATE** button in the corner of the wardrobe, or a bound block clicked
without a key in hand. The first screen asks only one thing: how many at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="How many">
    `menus/crate.yml`. Choices are drawn up to `crate.max-at-once`, capped at seven by the width of
    the screen, and spread the way the reels will be — where you click is where that reel falls. A
    choice the player cannot afford is drawn by `empty_template` with how many keys it is missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The reels fall">
    `menus/crate_open.yml`. One reel per crate, each running the full height of the chest, each with
    its own finishing line `crate.stagger-seconds` after the one before it. Every face is the skin as
    it is worn, on its chestplate, and keeps its name and the pieces it fits the whole way down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What went past stays">
    Nothing is cleared when a reel stops. What it came within one slot of stays on screen, because
    half of what a crate is worth is the legendary that went past the line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paid out as each reel lands">
    Keys are spent and prizes decided before the first frame is drawn. Each reel pays out as it
    stops, with `crate.on-win` or `crate.on-duplicate` and its own line in chat. Closing the window
    mid-fall costs nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wear it in one click">
    Clicking a skin that landed puts it on every piece the player may wear it on, straight into the
    wardrobe. On an `item` server the click just closes the window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Refusal | Message |
|---|---|
| `crate.enabled` is off | `crate.disabled` |
| Not enough keys | `crate.no-keys` — `%amount%` is the cost, `%keys%` what they hold. |
| Their last opening is still falling | `crate.busy` |
| No skin is declared | `crate.empty` |

## What comes out

`crate.reward` decides:

| Value | What the player gets |
|---|---|
| `UNLOCK` | The skin is unlocked on the account, every piece it fits. It cannot be traded. The default. |
| `ITEM` | Only its skin item. Nothing is unlocked, so no opening is ever a duplicate. |
| `BOTH` | Unlocked on the account, and handed over as a skin item as well. |

`crate.won` names what came out (`%reward%`) and its rarity (`%tier%`).

## Duplicates

Landing on a skin the player has already unlocked hands keys back, as many as
`crate.duplicate-refund` says, with `crate.duplicate` (`%refund%` is how many). At the default of `1` a
duplicate is a free re-roll rather than a loss.

A skin the player only owns through a permission is **not** a duplicate: it is unlocked for good, so it
outlives the rank that granted it.

## Buying with currency

A skin the player does not own can also be bought from the wardrobe. While `shop.enabled` is on and the
skin has a price, its locked row is drawn by `purchasable_template` instead of `no_permissions_template`,
with the price in its lore; clicking it asks to confirm, charges the price and unlocks the skin exactly
as a crate would — the whole skin, for good.

```yaml title="config.yml"
shop:
  enabled: true
  tier-prices:
    common: 500
    rare: 1500
    epic: 4000
    legendary: 10000
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `true` | Off, locked skins look locked as before and nothing is for sale. |
| `tier-prices` | above | The price of every skin of a rarity that sets none of its own. A rarity left out keeps its skins out of the shop. |

A skin's own `price` in `skins.yml` wins over its rarity's:

```yaml title="skins.yml"
skins:
  galaxy:
    tier: epic
    price: 2500                              # the default currency
  eclipse:
    tier: legendary
    price: {amount: 50, currency: gems}      # a named currency
```

The money goes through ExyliaLib's economy, so it is whichever of ExyliaEconomy or Vault the server
runs; with neither, every price is unavailable and nothing is offered. One purchase per player at a
time, and an unlock that fails after the charge gives the money back. What the player is told — the
confirmation, success, not enough money, already owned — is ExyliaLib's own wording, under `purchase`
in ExyliaLib's `messages.yml`.

A skin is offered wherever the player may not wear it on the piece being dressed, so a player who
owns one piece through a per-piece node can still buy the rest: the purchase is only refused as already
owned once they may wear every piece it fits.

## Settings

```yaml title="config.yml"
crate:
  enabled: true
  start-keys: 0
  duplicate-refund: 1
  reward: UNLOCK
  blocks: []
  max-at-once: 4
  spin-frames: 34
  stagger-seconds: 2.0
  key-item:
    material: TRIPWIRE_HOOK
    glow: true
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `true` | Off, the crate says so instead of opening. |
| `start-keys` | `0` | Keys a player is given the first time they are seen. |
| `duplicate-refund` | `1` | Keys handed back for a skin already unlocked. |
| `reward` | `UNLOCK` | `UNLOCK`, `ITEM` or `BOTH`, as above. |
| `blocks` | `[]` | The blocks that open the crate. Bind them in game, not by hand. |
| `max-at-once` | `4` | How many crates one player may open in one go. The screen fits seven. |
| `spin-frames` | `34` | How many faces the first reel runs through before it stops. Capped at 200, about eleven seconds. |
| `stagger-seconds` | `2.0` | How long after one reel stops the next one does. `0` stops them together. |
| `key-item` | `TRIPWIRE_HOOK`, glowing | How the key that can be held is drawn. Its name and lore are in `messages.yml`. |

`on-spin`, `on-win` and `on-duplicate` are full ExyliaLib effect blocks — title, action bar, boss bar,
sound, particle and firework — played on every falling tick, and then once per reel as it lands. They
ship as a hi-hat, a level-up and a bass note.

## Crate blocks

A physical block, anywhere in the world, that opens the crate when it is clicked:

```text
/armorskinsadmin crate block add      # the block you are looking at, up to six blocks away
/armorskinsadmin crate block remove
/armorskinsadmin crate block list
/armorskinsadmin crate block clear
```

They are written to `config.yml`, not into the world, so a bound block survives a schematic paste, a
world edit and a rollback. While it is bound it cannot be broken, blown up or pushed away, and clicking
it no longer does what its material does — a crate drawn on a barrel must not open a barrel.

## Handing skins over without a crate

```text
/armorskinsadmin unlocks grant <player> <skin>
/armorskinsadmin unlocks revoke <player> <skin>
/armorskinsadmin unlocks clear <player>
```

The same unlock a crate writes, for a reward plugin, a support ticket or a wiped season. It outlives a
rank running out and needs no permissions plugin.

<Callout type="tip" title="Permissions and unlocks are two separate ways in">
A player may wear a skin they unlocked **or** one their rank grants. Selling one as a rank is still one
node — `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>` — and it is independent of anything they unboxed or bought.
</Callout>
