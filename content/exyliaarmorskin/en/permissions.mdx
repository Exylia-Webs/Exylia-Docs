## The nodes

| Permission | Default | Grants |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliaarmorskin.wardrobe` | `true` | `/wardrobe` and `/wardrobe crate`. |
| `exyliaarmorskin.admin` | `op` | Every subcommand of `/armorskinsadmin` — see [Commands](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/commands). |
| `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>` | — | Wearing that one skin, on every piece it fits. |
| `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>.<piece>` | — | That skin on one slot only: `helmet`, `chestplate`, `leggings` or `boots`. |
| `exyliaarmorskin.skin.*` | `op` | Every skin. |

Every player may open the wardrobe out of the box; an operator owns every skin, and everybody else owns
none until you grant a node, they win one from the [crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate) or they buy
one.

## Two ways to own a skin

| Way | What it covers | How it ends |
|---|---|---|
| A node | The skin, or one piece of it. | When the rank or the node goes. |
| An unlock | The whole skin, every piece it fits. Written by the crate, the shop or `/armorskinsadmin unlocks grant`. | Only with `unlocks revoke` or `unlocks clear`: it outlives any rank. |

Either one is enough. Everything that asks whether a player owns a skin — the wardrobe, the
`hide-without-permission` check and the API's `canUse` — asks both.

## The per-skin node

There is no `permission` key in `skins.yml`: the node is **derived from the id**, lowercased, so the
skin `galaxy` is always `exyliaarmorskin.skin.galaxy`. Add a skin and its node exists; rename it and
the node renames with it.

## Selling half a skin

Each slot has a node of its own, `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>.<piece>`. The whole-skin node covers all
four; a piece node covers exactly one.

```text
/lp group vip permission set exyliaarmorskin.skin.celestial.helmet true
```

In the wardrobe a skin the player owns for one piece is offered on that piece, and dressing the full
set dresses only the pieces they are allowed to wear — a rank holding one piece still gets it rather
than being refused outright. With the [shop](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate#buying-with-currency) on, the
rest of the skin is offered for sale.

```text
/lp group vip     permission set exyliaarmorskin.skin.galaxy true
/lp group mvp     permission set exyliaarmorskin.skin.* true
```

## What ownership is checked for

<Steps>
  <Step title="The wardrobe">
    Wearing a skin from the wardrobe needs the node or an unlock. A skin the player does not own is
    drawn locked, or for sale, and clicking it does not dress them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Applying an item">
    Never checked. A skin item is a real item — bought, traded and lost like any other — and holding
    one is the proof of ownership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Being seen wearing it">
    With `behaviour.hide-without-permission` on, a piece whose skin the **wearer** owns neither by node
    nor by unlock is drawn as the real armour — to them and to everyone else — and it plays no effects.
    The id stays on the item, or the row in the wardrobe, untouched.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The wearing check runs whenever the piece is reconciled: on equip, on join, on respawn, on
`/armorskinsadmin refresh` and on `/armorskinsadmin reload`. Revoking a node mid-session therefore
takes effect on the next of those — `/armorskinsadmin refresh <player>` makes it immediate. An unlock
from the crate or the shop redraws the player at once.

## `hide-without-permission`

```yaml title="config.yml"
behaviour:
  hide-without-permission: true
```

| Value | Result |
|---|---|
| `true` (default) | A skin is owned: a wearer without the node or an unlock is shown the real armour. |
| `false` | Whatever is on the armour, or in the wardrobe, is shown — the item is the permission. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Items need hide-without-permission off to be sold on their own">
Applying a skin item never asks for a node, but with this switch on a player who applied one without
owning the skin is still shown their real armour. To sell skins as items alone, turn it off, or hand an
unlock with the item.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="Commands and most of the API are never gated">
`/armorskinsadmin give` hands out any skin item to any player, `unlocks grant` hands over any skin, and
`apply()` in the API writes any skin onto any piece — those are the tools you use to grant a skin. The
API's `select` is the exception: it goes through the wardrobe and is refused for a skin the player does
not own.
</Callout>

## Selling skins

| You want | Do |
|---|---|
| A skin bought once and kept for good | Let the shop sell it, or sell crate keys, or run `unlocks grant` from your store. |
| A rank perk | Grant `exyliaarmorskin.skin.galaxy` to the rank. |
| A skin that stops working when a rank expires | Grant only the node, leave `hide-without-permission: true`. The armour keeps the id — or the wardrobe keeps the row — and the look returns the day the rank does. |
| A skin on one slot only | Grant `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>.<piece>` instead of the whole node. |
| A consumable item | Give the item and turn `hide-without-permission` off, or grant the node or an unlock with it. |

<Callout type="tip" title="consume-skin-item decides whether a skin item is single-use">
With `consume-skin-item: true` — the default — applying eats the item, so a skin item is a consumable.
Turn it off and one item skins an unlimited number of pieces.
</Callout>
