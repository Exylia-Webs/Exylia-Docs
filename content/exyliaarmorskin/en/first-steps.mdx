In about five minutes you will wear a skin from the wardrobe, wear another from an item, and take both off.

## Before you start

ExyliaArmorSkin and its requirements are installed: see [Installation](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/installation). You are an operator, so you own every skin; a normal player owns none until you give one (step 1 below).

## Wear a skin from the wardrobe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give a player a skin">
    Operators own every skin. For anybody else, hand one over for good, or grant its permission:
    ```text
    /armorskinsadmin unlocks grant <player> galaxy
    /lp group default permission set exyliaarmorskin.skin.galaxy true
    ```
    Or let them win one: `/armorskinsadmin keys give <player> 1`, then `/wardrobe crate`. See [Set up the crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put on an armour piece">
    Wear any armour, for example a netherite chestplate. The skin is drawn over it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the wardrobe">
    Run `/wardrobe`. The armour along the top is what you are dressing; the grid below is every skin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dress a piece, or all four">
    Left click a piece to focus it, then click a skin. The **Full set** row dresses every piece you are allowed to wear at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it off">
    Click the skin that is already on, or right click the piece. **Undress all** empties everything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Wear a skin from an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give yourself the item">
    `/armorskinsadmin give <you> galaxy`. The command completes every skin id and takes an optional amount from 1 to 64.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it onto armour">
    Open your inventory, pick the skin item up with the cursor and click a piece of armour. The skin is applied and the item is used up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it off again">
    `/armorskinsadmin remover <you>`, then click that piece with the remover on the cursor. The skin comes back as an item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

- You see the colour (and trim) on your body in third person (F5) and in your inventory. Other players see it too.
- Wear all four pieces with the same skin and its set bonus starts: `inferno` announces the set and surrounds you with flames until you take a piece off.
- The armour itself is unchanged. Enchantments, armour points and durability stay exactly as they were.

<Callout type="warning" title="Players are shown the skin only if they own it">
Applying an item never asks for a permission, but by default a player who owns the skin neither by permission nor by unlock is shown their real armour. Grant `exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>` or an unlock, or set `hide-without-permission: false` in `config.yml`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/permissions).
</Callout>

## Write your own skin

Add an entry to `skins.yml`. The key is the id stored on armour; everything else is how it looks:

```yaml title="skins.yml"
skins:
  ruby_ward:
    name: '{error}&lRUBY WARD'
    pieces: []            # empty means all four
    color: '#c0102a'
    trim-pattern: ward
    trim-material: redstone
    icon: WARD_ARMOR_TRIM_SMITHING_TEMPLATE
    animation: ''         # a preset from animations.yml, or rainbow / pulse / frames
```

Then run `/armorskinsadmin reload`. The skin exists at once, `/armorskinsadmin list` shows it, and anyone already wearing it sees the new colour.

<Callout type="tip" title="Change the name, not the key">
The key is what the armour stores. Renaming it orphans every piece already wearing it, and they show their real look again.
</Callout>

<Details title="What the item commands say when something is wrong">
| Situation | Message key |
|---|---|
| The clicked item is not armour | `not-armor` |
| The skin does not list that piece | `unsupported-piece` |
| The armour already wears a skin | `already-skinned` |
| The remover was used on unskinned armour | `no-skin` |
| The skin id on the armour is no longer in `skins.yml` | `unknown-skin` |
| Removing with no free inventory slot | `inventory-full`: the skin stays and the remover is not used up |

Skin items are only read inside your own inventory window (including the armour slots). Dropping one onto armour in a chest does nothing.
</Details>
