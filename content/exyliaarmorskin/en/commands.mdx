Every command, with the permission it needs. Players have one command, `/wardrobe` (alias `/armorskins`). Everything else is `/armorskinsadmin` (alias `/armorskinadmin`) and needs `exyliaarmorskin.admin`. Skin names and player names complete as you type.

<Commands />

## Good to know

- Typed bare, `/armorskinsadmin` opens the admin hub: the catalogue, players, showcases and crate blocks from one screen. The subcommands are shortcuts to it.
- `wardrobe clear`, `crate block clear` and `showcase clear` only say what they would delete until you run them again with `confirm` at the end.
- `wardrobe clear`, `keys` and `unlocks` also work on players who are offline. The other subcommands need the player online.
- `give` hands any skin to anybody, without checking permissions. If the player's inventory is full, the item waits and is handed over the next time they join.
- `/wardrobe` refuses to open when `behaviour.mode` is `item` (you get the `wardrobe-disabled` message), because a wardrobe choice would never be read. `/wardrobe crate` still opens.
- Skin items need no command: players drag them onto armour in their inventory.

## What reload does

`/armorskinsadmin reload` re-reads `config.yml`, `messages.yml`, `skins.yml`, `animations.yml` and the menus under `lang/<language>/menus/`, rebuilds every skin and redraws everyone online without playing equip effects. A changed `language` is picked up too. Skins that another plugin registered through the [API](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/api) are left alone. It does not reload ExyliaLib: use `/exylialib reload` for that.

Nothing is removed from anybody: the armour keeps its skin and the wardrobe keeps its rows.

## What refresh does

`/armorskinsadmin refresh <player>` re-reads the armour a player is wearing and re-sends what everyone sees. Use it after changing a permission: the permission is only checked while the visual is rebuilt, so this makes it apply at once. From the console without a player it refreshes everyone online.

## Reset a player's data

Taking back every unlock, wardrobe row and key of a player is ExyliaLib's job: `/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaArmorSkin`, or `/exylialib wipe ExyliaArmorSkin <table|@kind|all>` for a whole season. See [ExyliaLib commands](/docs/exylialib/commands#resetting-a-player).
