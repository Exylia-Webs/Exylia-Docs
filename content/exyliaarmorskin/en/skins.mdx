A skin lives in `skins.yml` under the id the armour will store. Ids are matched **trimmed and
lowercased**, so `Galaxy` and `galaxy` are the same skin.

```yaml title="skins.yml"
skins:
  galaxy:
    name: '{primary}&lGALAXY'
    tier: epic
    price: ''
    pieces: []
    color: '#4a1b8c'
    trim-pattern: eye
    trim-material: amethyst
    icon: GLOBE_BANNER_PATTERN
    animation: galaxy
    effects:
      on-equip:
        sound:
          name: BLOCK_PORTAL_TRIGGER
          volume: 0.8
          pitch: 1.2
      set:
        ambient:
          particle: 'END_ROD|2|0.5|0.01'
          interval: 0.4
          radius: 32.0
```

## Keys

| Key | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | text | Shown on the skin item and in every message that names the skin. Takes palette tokens like `{info}` and `&` codes. Blank falls back to the id. |
| `tier` | id | Its crate rarity: a key under `crate.tiers` in `config.yml`. Empty is `common`; one that no longer exists counts as the first rarity. See [The crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate). |
| `price` | number or section | What it costs in the wardrobe shop: `2500` in the default currency, or `{amount: 50, currency: gems}`. Empty uses its tier's price from `shop.tier-prices`. See [Buying with currency](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate#buying-with-currency). |
| `pieces` | list | `helmet`, `chestplate`, `leggings`, `boots`. **Empty means all four.** A skin that does not list a piece is refused on it with `unsupported-piece`. |
| `color` | hex | The leather colour, `#0066ff`. Empty keeps leather brown. Anything unparseable is reported in the console and treated as empty. |
| `trim-pattern` | key | A trim pattern such as `vex` or `minecraft:vex`. |
| `trim-material` | key | A trim material such as `diamond`. |
| `icon` | material | The material the **skin item** is made of. Empty uses `skin-item.material` from `config.yml`. |
| `animation` | name | A preset from `animations.yml`, or a bare type (`rainbow`, `pulse`, `frames`). Empty for a still colour. |
| `effects` | section | Sounds, titles and particles: `on-equip`, `on-unequip`, the per-piece `ambient` and the `set` bonus for wearing every piece the skin fits. See [Effects](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/effects). |

<Callout type="warning" title="A trim needs both halves">
`trim-pattern` and `trim-material` are only used together. Writing one without the other logs
`a trim needs both trim-pattern and trim-material; ignoring the trim` and the skin is drawn with its
colour alone.
</Callout>

## How the skin item looks

The skin item is built from `skin-item`: its name and lore in `messages.yml`, its glow in `config.yml`,
with two substitutions: `%skin%` is the skin's `name` and `%pieces%` is the list of pieces it fits. Its material is the
skin's `icon`, or `skin-item.material` when the skin sets none.

The skin's colour and trim are then painted **onto that item**, which only shows if the material can
carry them: point `icon` at a leather piece and the item previews the skin exactly. A pottery sherd
or a banner pattern simply keeps its own texture.

## What happens to a skin you delete

The id stays on the armour. Nothing resolves it any more, so the piece shows its real look again, and
a remover used on it answers `unknown-skin` rather than turning the skin into nothing. Put the entry
back — same key — and every piece wearing it comes back too.

## Reloading

`/armorskinsadmin reload` re-reads `config.yml`, `animations.yml` and `skins.yml`, drops the cached trims,
rebuilds the lore lines and redraws every online player. File entries are replaced wholesale; skins
another plugin registered through the [API](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/api) survive a reload untouched.
