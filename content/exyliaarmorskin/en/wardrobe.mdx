The wardrobe is a menu where players pick a skin for each armour piece, or for all four at once, and wear it. You end with `/wardrobe` working the way you want: items only, wardrobe only, or both.

A skin item writes onto the **armour**. The wardrobe stores the choice against the **player**, so it survives death, trading and a change of gear, and the armour is never written to.

## Before you start

You have at least one skin, see [Create and edit skins](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/skins). Players need `exyliaarmorskin.wardrobe`, which everyone has by default.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick where skins come from">
    In `config.yml`, set `behaviour.mode` to `item`, `wardrobe` or `both` (the default). See the three modes below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/armorskinsadmin reload`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a player skins">
    Operators own every skin. Others own a skin through its permission (`exyliaarmorskin.skin.<id>`), `/armorskinsadmin unlocks grant <player> <skin>`, the [crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate) or the shop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Run `/wardrobe`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="There is a 3D wardrobe too">
With `menu.style: SPATIAL` in `config.yml`, `/wardrobe` opens the wardrobe in the world instead: the player tries skins on their own body and saves or buys in place. `/wardrobe 3d` and `/wardrobe classic` open either one. See [Set up the 3D wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/3d-wardrobe).
</Callout>

## Check that it works

`/wardrobe` opens a 54-slot window. The armour along the top is what the player currently looks like; the grid below is every skin. Left-click a piece, click a skin, and the skin is drawn on you. Click the worn skin again to take it off. **Undress all** empties everything.

## The three modes

`behaviour.mode` decides where a piece's skin comes from:

| Mode | Where it comes from | What it implies |
|---|---|---|
| `item` | Only what a skin item wrote onto the armour. | The skin is traded, dropped and lost with the piece. No wardrobe, no database. |
| `wardrobe` | Only what the player picked in `/wardrobe`. | It follows the player and lands on whatever they equip. Skin items do nothing. |
| `both` | The armour's own skin, and the wardrobe for armour that carries none. | The default. |

In `both` the **armour wins**: a skinned piece is drawn with its own skin, and the wardrobe only covers bare pieces.

## What the buttons do

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Undress all** (slot 45) | Takes everything off. |
| **Filter** (slot 52) | Shows only the skins the player owns. Remembered like the tab. |
| **Close** (slot 49) | Closes the menu. |
| **Crate** (slot 53) | Opens the [crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate); shows the keys held and skins collected. |
| Page buttons (slots 48 and 50) | Turn the skin grid. |

| Click | On | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Left | An armour row | Focuses it: the grid now dresses that piece. |
| Right | An armour row | Clears that piece. |
| Left | The **Full set** row | Focuses every piece at once. |
| Right | The **Full set** row | Clears the whole wardrobe. |
| Any click | A skin | Wears it on the focus, or takes it off when it is already worn. |
| Any click | A skin for sale | Asks to confirm, charges the price and unlocks it. See [Buying with currency](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate#buying-with-currency). |

<Callout type="note" title="Dressing the whole set dresses what you are allowed to wear">
With the full set focused, a skin is applied to every piece the player may wear it on, so a rank that owns one piece of a skin still gets that piece rather than being refused.
</Callout>

## Going further

Change the look of the menu by editing `lang/<language>/menus/wardrobe.yml` (see [Menus](/docs/exylialib/menus)). Where the server's menu style is `SCREEN`, `/wardrobe` opens as a native screen (`screens/wardrobe.html`, with the crate and its reels) with a real body in a window that tries on the skin under the cursor. A screen that fails to load falls back to the chest menu.

Clearing a wardrobe hands out no item, because none was ever consumed. Skin items and removers belong to `item` and `both`.

## Technical reference

For people who edit the menu file.

<Details title="Slots and lists of the menu">

| Section | Slots | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `targets` | `2,3,4,5,6` | What is being dressed: the full set, then helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. |
| `skins` | `10-16,19-25,28-34,37-43` | Every skin, paged with the buttons at slots `48` and `50`. |

**Undress all** sits at slot `45` and takes everything off; **Filter** at `52` shows only the skins the
player owns (remembered like the tab); **Close** at `49`; **Crate** at `53` opens
the [crate](/docs/exyliaarmorskin/crate) and shows the keys the player holds and how many skins they
have collected.

Each armour row is drawn in the leather piece of its own slot, tinted with the skin worn there, so the
top of the screen is what the player currently looks like. A grid row is drawn in **the piece being
dressed**, so the skin is previewed where it is going to end up; a skin that does not fit the focused
piece is drawn in a piece it does fit.

</Details>

<Details title="Templates of the wardrobe menu">

| Section | Template | Used when |
|---|---|---|
| `targets` | `item_template` | An armour slot that is not focused. |
| | `focused_template` | The armour slot being dressed. |
| | `set_template` | The full-set row, not focused. |
| | `focused_set_template` | The full-set row while it is focused. |
| `skins` | `item_template` | A skin the player may use and that fits. |
| | `selected_template` | The skin worn on the focused piece — or on every piece the skin fits, with the set focused. |
| | `unsupported_template` | A skin whose `pieces` do not include the focused slot. |
| | `no_permissions_template` | A skin the player does not own. |
| | `purchasable_template` | A skin the player does not own that the shop sells, while `shop.enabled` is on. |

`item_template` is the plain state and must stay first in each list. The locked and unsupported rows
carry no action of their own: clicking one does nothing. A player owns a skin through its node or an
unlock, from the crate or the shop.

</Details>

<Details title="Wardrobe placeholders">

These are the wardrobe's own tokens, filled in while the screen is drawn. They are not
PlaceholderAPI placeholders and only work inside this file.

The `targets` list holds two kinds of row — the full-set row first, then one row per piece — and each
kind carries its own tokens.

| Placeholder | Where | What it draws |
|---|---|---|
| `%target%` | `targets` | What the row dresses: a piece key, or `set` on the full-set row. |
| `%target_name%` | `targets` | Its name from `wardrobe`: `full-set`, or the piece's own name. |
| `%dressed%` | `targets`, full-set row | How many of the four pieces carry a skin. |
| `%pieces%` | `targets`, full-set row | How many pieces there are in total — four. |
| `%piece%` | `targets`, piece rows | The piece's key: `helmet`, `chestplate`, `leggings` or `boots`. |
| `%piece_material%` | `targets`, piece rows | The leather material of that slot, to draw the row with. |
| `%skin_name%` | `targets`, piece rows | The skin worn on that piece, or `wardrobe.empty`. |
| `%skin_color%` | `targets`, piece rows | That skin's hex colour, or undyed leather when it has none. |
| `%skin_trim_pattern%` | `targets`, piece rows | That skin's trim pattern, empty when it has no trim. |
| `%skin_trim_material%` | `targets`, piece rows | That skin's trim metal, empty when it has no trim. |
| `%skin_id%` | `skins` | The skin's id. |
| `%skin_name%` | `skins` | Its display name. |
| `%skin_icon%` | `skins` | Its `icon`, or the default skin item's material. |
| `%skin_preview%` | `skins` | The armor piece it is previewed in — the one being dressed when the skin fits it. |
| `%skin_color%` | `skins` | Its hex colour, or undyed leather when it has none. |
| `%skin_trim_pattern%` | `skins` | Its trim pattern, empty when it has no trim. |
| `%skin_trim_material%` | `skins` | Its trim metal, empty when it has no trim. |
| `%skin_pieces%` | `skins` | The pieces it fits. |
| `%skin_worn%` | `skins` | The pieces the player is wearing it on, empty when none. |
| `%skin_worn_name%` | `skins` | The same, but `wardrobe.empty` when none. |
| `%price%` | `skins`, `purchasable_template` | The price, written the way its currency writes it. |
| `%keys%` | anywhere | The crate keys the player holds. |
| `%unlocked_count%` | anywhere | How many skins they own, by unlock or permission. |
| `%catalogue_count%` | anywhere | How many skins the server declares. |

</Details>

<Details title="Menu actions">

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| `armorskin:wardrobe` | Opens the wardrobe `menu.style` names, after checking `exyliaarmorskin.wardrobe`. |
| `armorskin:focus <target>` | Dresses one piece, or `set` for all four. |
| `armorskin:wear <skin>` | Wears a skin on the focus, or takes it off when it is already worn. |
| `armorskin:clear <target>` | Clears one piece, or `set` for the whole wardrobe. |
| `armorskin:buy <skin>` | Buys a locked skin with currency, then reopens the wardrobe on the same focus. Needs `exyliaarmorskin.buy`. |
| `armorskin:crate` | Opens the crate. |
| `armorskin:equip <skin>` | Wears a skin on every piece the player may, from outside the wardrobe: what a crate reel's winner does when clicked. |

</Details>

<Details title="Wording in messages.yml">

The few strings one template draws for every row live under `wardrobe` in `messages.yml`:

```yaml title="messages.yml"
wardrobe:
  full-set: "{primary}&lFULL SET"
  helmet: "{primary}&lHELMET"
  chestplate: "{primary}&lCHESTPLATE"
  leggings: "{primary}&lLEGGINGS"
  boots: "{primary}&lBOOTS"
  piece-names:
    helmet: "Helmet"
    chestplate: "Chestplate"
    leggings: "Leggings"
    boots: "Boots"
  empty: "{muted}Nothing"
```

`piece-names` is how a piece is written inside a sentence or a list, such as `%skin_pieces%`.

</Details>

<Details title="Where the wardrobe is stored">

One row per player in `armorskin_wardrobe`: the uuid, and one column per piece holding a skin id.
Reads never block — a wardrobe still coming back from the database answers "no skin yet", and the
player is redrawn as soon as it lands.

The table is classified for ExyliaLib, so `/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaArmorSkin` and
`/exylialib wipe` clear it along with the crate's keys and unlocks. See
[ExyliaLib commands](/docs/exylialib/commands#resetting-a-player).

<Callout type="tip" title="Nothing has to be given back">
Clearing a wardrobe hands out no item, because none was ever consumed: a wardrobe choice is a row,
not an item. Skin items and removers belong to `item` and `both`.
</Callout>

</Details>
