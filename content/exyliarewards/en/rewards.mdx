A reward is something a player claims: a list of money, items, commands, experience and the rest, a
**trigger** that says when it can be claimed, and requirements that say who may. Every reward is a row in
the database, created and edited in game with `/rewardsadmin`.

## Triggers

| Trigger | When it can be claimed |
|---|---|
| Daily | Once per day, from the reset hour. |
| Weekly | Once per week, from the weekly reset day at the reset hour. |
| Monthly | Once per month, from the first of the month at the reset hour. |
| Cooldown | Again once its wait has passed since the last claim. |
| Once | Once per player, ever. |
| Playtime | Once the player has actively played its duration; again every repeat when it has one. |
| Condition | Once, when a placeholder check passes, such as `%statistic_mob_kills% >= 20`. |
| Calendar | A grid of days, one claimed per day. See [Login calendars](/docs/exyliarewards/calendar). |

### The clock

Daily, weekly and monthly rewards, streaks, calendars and vote days all follow one clock, set in
`config.yml` under `clock`: a timezone, a reset hour and the day weeks start on. A day starts at the reset
hour, not at midnight: with a reset hour of 5, 03:00 still belongs to yesterday. Nothing is scheduled: a
player who logs in after a week away is simply in a later period, and a server that was off over a reset
needs no catch-up.

### Playtime

Playtime is ExyliaLib's tracker: time a player is idle (five minutes without moving, looking, typing or
clicking, by default) is not counted, and with every server on one database the total is network-wide. A
playtime reward with a **repeat** can be claimed again each time the player plays that much more: the
playtime it needs is its duration plus one repeat per claim already made. Without a repeat it is claimed
once.

### Condition

A condition reward waits for its **unlock condition**, a placeholder check in ExyliaLib's condition syntax:
operators `>=`, `<=`, `>`, `<`, `==` and `!=`. The check is asked again every minute and whenever the
player opens a rewards screen. Once it passes, the reward is claimed once, like a one-time reward.

## Requirements

A reward that fails a requirement is **locked** for that player. They are asked in this order, and the
first that fails is the line the player reads:

| Requirement | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Permission | none | A node the player needs. A reward can be **shown** locked (default) or **hidden** from players without it. |
| Condition | none | A placeholder check, as the unlock condition above. Unlike the unlock condition, it keeps the reward locked rather than waiting. |
| Worlds | all | The worlds it can be claimed in, comma separated. |
| Claim first | none | Reward IDs the player must have claimed before this one. An ID that no longer names a reward is ignored. |
| One per address | off | Only one account per address may claim it per period: per day, week or month, per day for a calendar, within the wait for a cooldown, and once for good for the rest. |
| Playtime range | none | The least playtime needed, and the most allowed: a reward for new players stops being offered once they pass it. |
| Claim limit | unlimited | How many times it can be claimed in total. Once and Condition rewards, and Playtime rewards without a repeat, are always claimed once. |

<Callout type="note" title="One per address asks the database">
The accounts sharing an address come from ExyliaLib's join records, on every server of the network. When
the database cannot answer, the claim is refused rather than let through.
</Callout>

## Contents

What a reward pays is an ExyliaLib reward list: money, items, commands, experience, messages and potion
effects, each with its own chance, amount, permission and condition. It is edited with the library's reward
editor; see [Rewards in ExyliaLib](/docs/exylialib/rewards) for every field. In a command entry,
`%player%`, `%reward%` and `%streak%` are filled in for the claim.

How the list is handed out is the reward's **Handed out as** option:

| Mode | What the player gets |
|---|---|
| Everything | The whole list. The default. |
| Pick one | One group of their choice. Entries that share a name are one group; entries with no name come with every group. With more than one group, claiming opens a choice; with one, it is paid straight away. |
| Mystery roll | One entry, rolled by weight among those the player is eligible for, and hidden until claimed. The entry's own chance still applies, so a roll can come up with nothing. |

## Streaks

Daily, weekly, monthly and cooldown rewards count streaks when their **Streak** option is on (the default
for those triggers).

- A calendar-period reward continues its streak when the last claim was in the period just before this
  one; anything else starts it again at 1.
- A cooldown reward continues it when the last claim was within two waits.

**Streak bonuses** are extra reward lists by streak day, paid with the claim on that day. With a **streak
cycle**, the days run 1 to the cycle and start over, so a 7-day cycle pays its day-7 bonus on days 7, 14,
21 and so on. Without one (`0`), the day is the streak itself, so a day-30 bonus is paid once, on the 30th
claim in a row.

### Streak freezes

A freeze keeps a streak alive over one missed period. When a claim would start a live streak over, the
plugin counts the periods (or waits) missed since the last claim; if the player holds that many freezes,
they are spent and the streak continues. Vote streaks spend them the same way. Freezes are held per player
across the network.

| Way | How |
|---|---|
| Given | `/rewardsadmin freeze give <player> <amount>`. A reward's command entry can run it, so any reward can grant freezes: `rewardsadmin freeze give %player% 1`. |
| Bought | `/rewards freeze buy [amount]`, at `streak-freezes.price` in the plugin's `currency`, when the price is above 0. |
| Checked | `/rewards freeze` shows the balance and the price. |

`streak-freezes.max` caps how many a player may hold by buying; `0` is no cap.

## Notices

| Notice | When |
|---|---|
| Ready on join | On join, how many rewards are ready (`notifications.ready-on-join`). |
| Ready notice | A reward with the **Ready notice** option tells the player once when it becomes claimable, checked every minute. |
| Toasts | With `notifications.toasts` on (the default), both are said with one advancement toast, "N reward(s) ready", at most once per `toast-cooldown-minutes`; joining always shows one. Off, they are chat lines with a clickable `[✔ CLAIM]`. |

Players switch these notices off for themselves in ExyliaLib's `/settings`, under **Rewards ready**.

**Auto-claim** claims a reward for the player the minute it becomes ready, quietly. A pick-one reward with
a choice to make is never auto-claimed: the player is told it is ready instead.

## What players see

`/rewards` opens the hub: the player's ready count, playtime and streak freezes, and a card for each part.

| Card | Opens |
|---|---|
| Periodic rewards | Daily, weekly, monthly, cooldown, one-time and condition rewards. |
| Playtime rewards | Playtime rewards. `/playtime` opens this list directly. |
| Login calendar | Calendar rewards. Shown while one exists. |
| AFK zones | The AFK zones list. Shown while the module runs. |
| Votes | The `/vote` screen. Shown while the module runs. |
| Referrals | The `/referral` summary. Shown while the module runs. |

In a list, each reward is drawn as **ready**, **waiting** (with its countdown), **used** or **locked** (with
the reason). Left-click claims it, right-click previews its contents. Disabled rewards are not listed.
Every count and countdown updates on its own while the screen is open.

A claim is recorded in the database first and paid only once that write wins, so a double click or two
servers at once pay it once. The payout is delivered by id: an item that does not fit, or a player who left
meanwhile, gets it on their next join.

## The reward editor

`/rewardsadmin` lists every reward. **Create reward** asks the trigger, an ID (lowercase, no spaces) and a
display name, then an icon, then opens the contents editor. Left-click a reward to edit it, right-click to
delete it (asked to confirm; every claim of it is forgotten).

A reward's screen has a card per part:

| Card | Holds |
|---|---|
| Status | Enabled or disabled. A disabled reward is hidden and cannot be claimed. |
| General | Display name, description, icon and list order (lower comes first). |
| When | The trigger, and what it needs: the wait of a cooldown, the playtime and repeat of a playtime reward, the unlock condition, or a calendar's length, day counting and catch-up price. |
| Requirements | Permission (and shown or hidden when locked), condition, playtime range, claim limit, claim first, one per address and worlds. |
| Rewards | The contents, streak bonuses by day, a calendar's days and its completion bonus. |
| Options | Streak, streak cycle, auto-claim, ready notice and how the list is handed out. |
| Stats | Claims in total, this week, players who claimed it, active streaks and the best streak, network-wide. |
| Player view | The preview a player sees. |

A new reward starts enabled, with a one-hour wait, shown when locked, unlimited claims, the ready notice on
and its streak on when the trigger counts one. Two admins editing different fields of one reward keep both
changes: each edit applies to the reward as it is now.

`/rewardsadmin stats` prints the same numbers for every reward. They are worked out half a minute after
start and then every five minutes.
