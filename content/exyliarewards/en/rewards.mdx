Build daily, weekly, monthly, cooldown, one-time, playtime and condition rewards in game, then add a streak bonus, a VIP-only requirement or streak freezes. No file to edit.

## Before you start

- ExyliaRewards is running. See [Installation](/docs/exyliarewards/installation).
- You are an operator or hold `exyliarewards.admin`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliarewards/permissions).
- For your very first reward, follow [Your first reward](/docs/exyliarewards/first-steps) instead. This page is the full guide.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reward editor">
    Run `/rewardsadmin`, then click **Rewards ▸ Create reward**. Left-click an existing reward to edit it; right-click deletes it after a confirmation, and every claim of it is forgotten.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick when it can be claimed">
    The first question is "When can it be claimed?". Pick one of the triggers below.

    | Trigger | When it can be claimed |
    |---|---|
    | Daily | Once per day, from the reset hour. |
    | Weekly | Once per week, from the weekly reset day at the reset hour. |
    | Monthly | Once per month, from the first of the month at the reset hour. |
    | Cooldown | Again once its wait has passed since the last claim. |
    | Once | Once per player, ever. |
    | Playtime | Once the player has actively played its duration; again every repeat when it has one. |
    | Condition | Once, when a placeholder check passes, such as `%statistic_mob_kills% >= 20`. |
    | Calendar | A grid of days, one claimed per day. See [Login calendars](/docs/exyliarewards/calendar). |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it an ID, a name and an icon">
    The ID is lowercase with no spaces, and players never see it. The display name is what they read. Then pick the icon item that stands for the reward in menus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what it gives">
    The contents editor opens. Add money, items, commands, experience, messages or potion effects. This is ExyliaLib's reward editor: see [Rewards in ExyliaLib](/docs/exylialib/rewards) for every field. In a command entry, `%player%`, `%reward%` and `%streak%` are filled in for the claim. Money needs an [economy plugin](/docs/exylialib/economy).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the extra question of the trigger">
    Cooldown asks for the wait. Playtime asks for the playtime needed and an optional repeat. Condition asks for the unlock condition. You can change all of these later under **When**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the reward screen">
    The screen has a card per part: **Status**, **General**, **When**, **Requirements**, **Rewards**, **Options**, **Stats** and **Player view**. A new reward starts enabled, with a one-hour wait, shown when locked, unlimited claims, the ready notice on, and its streak on when the trigger counts one. Two admins editing different fields of one reward keep both changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

1. Run `/rewards`, click **Rewards ▸ Periodic rewards** (or **Playtime rewards**) and find the reward. Ready, waiting (with a countdown), used and locked (with the reason) are drawn differently.
2. Left-click to claim it. Right-click previews what it gives without claiming.
3. To test again as an admin, run `/rewardsadmin reset <player> <reward>` to forget the claim, or `/rewardsadmin give <player> <reward>` to pay it at once, wait or not.

A disabled reward is not listed. If it does not appear, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliarewards/troubleshooting).

## Going further

### Limit it to VIPs or a place

Open the reward, then **Requirements**. Set **Permission** (shown locked, or hidden, for players without it), **Worlds**, **Claim first** (rewards to claim before this one), **Playtime range** (a reward for new players stops being offered after the maximum) or **Claim limit**. The first requirement that fails is the line the player reads. Give the permission to VIP with your permissions plugin.

### Add a streak bonus

Daily, weekly, monthly and cooldown rewards count streaks. Open the reward, then **Options**, and set a **Streak cycle** (for example 7). Then under **Rewards ▸ Streak bonuses** add a bonus for day 7: it is paid with the claim on days 7, 14, 21 and so on. Without a cycle (0), a day-30 bonus is paid once, on the 30th claim in a row.

### Streak freezes

A freeze keeps a streak alive over one missed period. When a claim would start a live streak over, the plugin counts the periods (or waits) missed; if the player holds that many freezes, they are spent and the streak continues. Vote streaks spend them the same way. Freezes are held per player across the network.

- **Give**: `/rewardsadmin freeze give <player> <amount>`. A reward's command entry can run it, so any reward can grant freezes: `rewardsadmin freeze give %player% 1`.
- **Sell**: set a price with `streak-freezes.price` in `config.yml` (0, the default, means not for sale). Players run `/rewards freeze buy [amount]`.
- **Check**: `/rewards freeze` shows the balance and the price.

`streak-freezes.max` (default 5) caps how many a player may hold by buying; `0` is no cap.

### Let players choose or roll

Under **Options ▸ Handed out as**: **Everything** (the default) pays the whole list; **Pick one** lets the player choose one group (entries that share a name are one group; entries with no name come with every group); **Mystery roll** gives one entry rolled by weight, hidden until claimed. With Pick one and a single group, it is paid straight away.

### Tell players it is ready

A reward's **Ready notice** option tells the player once when it becomes claimable, checked every minute. On join, `notifications.ready-on-join` says how many are ready. With `notifications.toasts` on (the default) both are one advancement toast, at most once per `toast-cooldown-minutes` (30 by default); joining always shows one. Off, they are chat lines with a clickable claim button. Players switch these off for themselves in ExyliaLib's `/settings`, under **Rewards ready**.

**Auto-claim** claims the reward for the player the minute it becomes ready. A pick-one reward with a choice to make is never auto-claimed.

### Change the day change

Daily, weekly and monthly rewards, streaks, calendars and vote days follow one clock under `clock` in `config.yml`: a timezone (empty means the server's), a reset hour (0 by default) and the day weeks start on (Monday). A day starts at the reset hour: with a reset hour of 5, 03:00 still belongs to yesterday. Nothing is scheduled, so a server that was off over a reset needs no catch-up.

<Details title="Technical details">

**Playtime.** ExyliaLib's tracker: idle time (five minutes without moving, looking, typing or clicking, by default) is not counted, and with every server on one database the total is network-wide. A playtime reward with a repeat needs its duration plus one repeat per claim already made. Without a repeat it is claimed once.

**Condition.** The check uses ExyliaLib's condition syntax, with the operators `>=`, `<=`, `>`, `<`, `==` and `!=`. As an unlock condition it is asked again every minute and whenever the player opens a rewards screen, and the reward is then claimed once. As a requirement it keeps the reward locked instead of waiting.

**Requirements, in the order they are checked.**

| Requirement | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Permission | none | A node the player needs. Shown locked (default) or hidden. |
| Condition | none | A placeholder check that keeps the reward locked while it fails. |
| Worlds | all | The worlds it can be claimed in, comma separated. |
| Claim first | none | Reward IDs to claim before this one. An ID that names no reward is ignored. |
| One per address | off | One account per address may claim per period: per day, week or month, per day for a calendar, within the wait for a cooldown, and once for good for the rest. |
| Playtime range | none | The least playtime needed and the most allowed. |
| Claim limit | unlimited | Total claims. Once and Condition rewards, and Playtime rewards without a repeat, are always claimed once. |

One per address reads ExyliaLib's join records, on every server of the network. When the database cannot answer, the claim is refused rather than let through.

**Streak rules.** A calendar-period reward continues its streak when the last claim was in the period just before; anything else starts it again at 1. A cooldown reward continues it when the last claim was within two waits.

**Claims.** A claim is recorded in the database first and paid only once that write wins, so a double click or two servers at once pay it once. An item that does not fit, or a player who left meanwhile, gets it on the next join.

**Cards of the reward screen.**

| Card | Holds |
|---|---|
| Status | Enabled or disabled. |
| General | Display name, description, icon and list order (lower first). |
| When | The trigger and what it needs: wait, playtime and repeat, unlock condition, or a calendar's length, day counting and catch-up price. |
| Requirements | The table above. |
| Rewards | Contents, streak bonuses by day, a calendar's days and completion bonus. |
| Options | Streak, streak cycle, auto-claim, ready notice, handed out as. |
| Stats | Claims in total and this week, players, active streaks, best streak, network-wide. |
| Player view | The preview a player sees. |

`/rewardsadmin stats` prints the same numbers for every reward, worked out half a minute after start and then every five minutes.

</Details>
