---
title: "Login calendars"
description: "Calendar rewards: a grid of days claimed one per day, login or date counting, each day's own rewards, the completion bonus and catching up a missed day."
---

A calendar reward is a grid of days. The player claims one day per day, at the plugin's reset hour like
every daily reward, and finishing the grid pays a completion bonus. It is a reward like any other, created
in `/rewardsadmin` with the **Calendar** trigger, so it has the same requirements and options; see
[Rewards](/docs/exyliarewards/rewards).

## Length

**Length** is how many days the grid has: up to 63, or `0` for a calendar as long as the current month. A
new calendar has 28.

## Day counting

| Mode | Day `k` is |
|---|---|
| One day per login (default) | The `k`-th day the player comes to claim. One day per clock day, in order; nothing is ever missed. A month calendar starts over with the month; a fixed-length one starts over the day after it was completed. |
| Calendar dates | A date: the `k`-th day of the month, or of the current block of days for a fixed length. Every player shares the same blocks. A day whose date passed unclaimed is **missed**. |

## What a day pays

A claimed day pays three things together:

1. The reward's **contents**, on every day, handed out as its mode says (everything, pick one or mystery).
2. That day's own list, set in **Calendar days** in the editor. A day with no list adds nothing.
3. The **completion bonus**, when the day claimed completes the grid.

In the editor, **Rewards → Calendar days** lists every day of the grid; click one to edit its list, and the
**Completion bonus** button edits the bonus.

## Catching up

In date mode a missed day stays on the grid, marked `✘`, and can be caught up:

| Who | How |
|---|---|
| Players with `exyliarewards.calendar.catchup` | Click it, for free. |
| Everybody else | Click it and pay the reward's **catch-up price**, in the plugin's `currency`. With a price of `0` a missed day cannot be caught up. |

The price is taken before the claim is written and given back if the claim does not land, so a catch-up is
never free by accident and never charged for nothing. A missed day can be caught up even after today's day
is claimed.

## What players see

The **Login calendar** card of `/rewards` lists the calendar rewards; clicking one opens its grid. Each day
is drawn as claimed (`✔`), ready to claim, locked (still to come) or missed (`✘`), with the completion bonus
beside the grid. `/rewards claim <id>` claims the day due today.

Completing a calendar sends the player a line, and can be announced on Discord
(`calendar-complete` in `discord.yml`).
