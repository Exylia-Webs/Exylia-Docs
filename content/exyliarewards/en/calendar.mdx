Build a grid of days the player claims one per day, with a bonus for finishing it and, if you want, paid catch-up for missed days. A calendar is a normal reward with the **Calendar** trigger, so it has the same requirements and options as any other.

## Before you start

- You can open `/rewardsadmin`. See [Create rewards](/docs/exyliarewards/rewards).
- For paid catch-up, an [economy plugin](/docs/exylialib/economy).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the reward">
    Run `/rewardsadmin`, click **Rewards ▸ Create reward**, pick **Calendar** for "When can it be claimed?", then give it an ID, a name and an icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what every day pays">
    The contents editor opens. Whatever you add here is paid on every day of the grid, handed out as the reward's mode says (everything, pick one or mystery roll).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the length">
    Open the reward, click the **When** card, then **Reward ▸ Length**. A new calendar has 28 days. Type up to 63, or `0` for a calendar as long as the current month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how days are counted">
    In **When**, click **Reward ▸ Day counting** to switch between **One day per login** (the default) and **Calendar dates**. See below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give single days their own prize">
    Open **Rewards ▸ Calendar days**. It lists every day of the grid: click **Calendar ▸ Day 7**, for example, to give day 7 an extra list. A day with no list adds nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the completion bonus">
    In the same screen click **Calendar ▸ Completion bonus** and add what finishing the whole grid pays.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/rewards`, click **Rewards ▸ Login calendar** and click the calendar. Each day is drawn as claimed (`✔`), ready to claim, locked (still to come) or missed (`✘`), with the completion bonus beside the grid. Click the ready day: you receive the reward contents plus that day's own list. `/rewards claim <id>` claims the day due today. The **Login calendar** card shows only while a calendar reward exists.

Completing a calendar sends the player a line, and can be announced on Discord with `calendar-complete` in `discord.yml`.

## Going further

### One day per login or calendar dates

| Mode | Day `k` is |
|---|---|
| One day per login (default) | The `k`-th day the player comes to claim. One day per clock day, in order, so nothing is ever missed. A month calendar starts over with the month; a fixed-length one starts over the day after it was completed. |
| Calendar dates | A date: the `k`-th day of the month, or of the current block of days for a fixed length. Every player shares the same blocks. A day whose date passed unclaimed is missed. |

### Let players catch up missed days

Only in calendar-dates mode, where a missed day stays on the grid marked `✘`.

- Players with `exyliarewards.calendar.catchup` click it and claim it for free. Give it to a rank to make it a perk.
- Everybody else pays the reward's **Catch-up price** (in **When**), in the plugin's `currency`. With a price of `0`, a missed day cannot be caught up.

The price is taken before the claim is written and given back if the claim does not land. A missed day can be caught up even after today's day is claimed.

### Make the calendar a rank perk

Everyone holds `exyliarewards.calendar` by default, which opens the calendar. Deny it to the default group and grant it to VIP. A day already earned is never held back by it: claiming needs only `exyliarewards.use`.

<Details title="Technical details">

- The calendar turns at the reset hour like every daily reward (`clock` in `config.yml`).
- Claimed days are stored as a bit mask per player, with the cycle they belong to, so a new month or block starts clean with nothing written.
- A claimed day pays three things together: the reward contents, that day's own list, and the completion bonus when the claimed day completes the grid.
- One per address and the other requirements of [Create rewards](/docs/exyliarewards/rewards) apply per day.

</Details>
