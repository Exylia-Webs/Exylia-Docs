Show live player data from ExyliaRewards in scoreboards, tab lists, holograms and chat. Click a placeholder to copy it.

The placeholders work inside Exylia plugins' own files, and in any other plugin through **PlaceholderAPI**: `%exyliarewards_ready%`. Nothing has to be downloaded from the eCloud. Each group answers while its module runs, and a player's own numbers read as zero or none until their data is loaded on join.

## Rewards

`<id>` is a reward's ID and may hold underscores: `%exyliarewards_vip_daily_cooldown%` works. An ID that names no reward answers the **None** label.

<Callout type="warning" title="IDs that start like another placeholder">
The longest placeholder name that matches wins. A reward whose ID starts with another name of this group followed by `_` (`playtime`, `ready`, `freezes`, or `afk_`, `votes_`, `referrals_` and `battlepass_` names) is answered by that placeholder instead. The preset playtime rewards (`playtime_1h` and the rest) are in that case: give a reward an ID such as `pt_1h` if you need its per-reward placeholders.
</Callout>

## AFK zones

`<n>` is a place from `1` to `10`; past the end of the top, the name shows the **None** label and the value shows `—`.

## Battle pass

They read the season shown to the player. `<season>` is a season's ID.

<Placeholders group="battlepass" />

The labels (**Ready**, **None**, **Unlimited**, **Yes**, and the rest) are in the messages file, so they follow your language and colours.

## Examples

```text
%exyliarewards_daily_streak%
%exyliarewards_daily_cooldown%
%playtime_next_reward_remaining%
%exyliarewards_afk_top_1_name%
%exyliarewards_votes_party_left%
%exyliarewards_battlepass_tier%
```
