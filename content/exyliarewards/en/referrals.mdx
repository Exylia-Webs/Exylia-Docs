title: "Referrals"
description: "New players naming who invited them: the join window, the checks, settling in, referrals.yml, milestones and resetting a referral."
---

A new player types `/referral <player>` to name who invited them. Nothing is paid yet: both players are
rewarded once the new player has played `referrals.qualify-minutes` (default 60) since naming somebody,
idle time not counted, as ExyliaLib's playtime tracker counts it on every server of the network.

## Naming who invited you

`/referral <player>` (also `/ref` and `/referrals`) needs `exyliarewards.referrals.use`. It is refused when:

| Refusal | Why |
|---|---|
| Too late | More than `referrals.join-window-hours` (default 72) have passed since the player's first join anywhere on the network. |
| Yourself | A player cannot name themselves. |
| Same connection | With `referrals.block-same-address` on (the default), the two accounts used the same address within the last 30 days, on any server, or are online from it now. When the records cannot answer, the referral is refused. |
| Already named | A player names one referrer, once. |
| Circular | The player being named already named this player: two players cannot invite each other. |

When it is accepted, the referrer is told wherever they are on the network, or on their next join.

## Settling in

Once the new player has played long enough, both are paid at the same moment, and the referrer's count of
players brought goes up. If the count reaches a **milestone**, the referrer is paid that too. Only one server
can settle a referral, so a player moving servers at that minute pays nobody twice. Either player may be
offline or on another server: what they earn is delivered by id, and the line saying why reaches them where
they are, or on their next join.

## referrals.yml

```yaml
invited:
  rewards:
    - type: ECONOMY
      value: '50'
  message: '%prefix% {success}You settled in! {highlight}%friend% {letters}invited you ...'

referrer:
  rewards:
    - type: ECONOMY
      value: '150'
  message: '%prefix% {highlight}%friend% {letters}is settling in, thanks to you ...'

milestones:
  '5':
    rewards:
      - type: ECONOMY
        value: '750'
    message: '%prefix% {highlight}5 {letters}friends brought to the server ...'
```

| Section | Paid |
|---|---|
| `invited` | To the new player, once they have played long enough. |
| `referrer` | To whoever invited them, at the same moment. |
| `milestones` | Once to a referrer when exactly that many of the players they invited have settled in. The bundled file has 5 and 15. |

Every block takes `rewards`, an ExyliaLib reward list (see
[Rewards in ExyliaLib](/docs/exylialib/rewards#in-a-config-file)), and `message` (empty for none). In
messages: `%player%` (who is paid), `%friend%` (the other player), `%count%` (players the referrer brought),
`%rewards%` and `%money%`. Money and items are boosted for the `referrals` source. Like `votes.yml`, the file
is yours and reloads with `/rewardsadmin reload`.

## Seeing your referrals

`/referral` with no name shows who invited you, how many players you brought, how many are still settling
in, the next milestone, and how new players name you. The **Referrals** card of `/rewards` shows the same.

## Resetting a referral

`/referraladmin reset <player>` (also `/refadmin reset`, `exyliarewards.referrals.admin`) forgets who a
player named, so they can name somebody again. It asks for `confirm` first:
`/referraladmin reset <player> confirm`. Only a referral nobody was paid for yet can be reset; one that
already settled in stays.
