Let new players name who invited them with `/referral <player>`, and pay both of them once the newcomer has played long enough, with extra rewards for referrers who bring a number of friends. Rewards live in `referrals.yml`.

## Before you start

- Players need `exyliarewards.referrals.use`, which everyone has by default. Staff who reset referrals need `exyliarewards.referrals.admin`.
- A money reward needs an [economy plugin](/docs/exylialib/economy).
- Referrals use ExyliaLib's playtime tracker and join records, so with several servers on one database they work network-wide.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the file">
    Open `plugins/ExyliaRewards/referrals.yml`. The file is yours: what you change is never overwritten.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set what the newcomer gets">
    Under `invited`, edit `rewards` (an ExyliaLib reward list; see [Rewards in ExyliaLib](/docs/exylialib/rewards#in-a-config-file)) and the `message` sent when they are paid. Paid to the new player once they have played long enough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set what the referrer gets">
    Under `referrer`, edit `rewards` and `message`. Paid to whoever invited them, at the same moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add milestones">
    Under `milestones`, each key is a number of friends. It is paid once to a referrer when exactly that many of the players they invited have settled in. The file ships with 5 and 15.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/rewardsadmin reload`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In messages, `%player%` is who is paid, `%friend%` the other player, `%count%` the players the referrer brought, and `%rewards%` and `%money%` what was paid.

## Check that it works

With a second account that is new to the server (and on another connection if `block-same-address` is on), run `/referral <referrer>`. The newcomer is told how long they must play, and the referrer is told wherever they are on the network, or on their next join. Run `/referral` with no name on either account: it shows who invited you, how many players you brought, how many are still settling in, and the next milestone. The **Referrals** card of `/rewards` shows the same.

After `referrals.qualify-minutes` of playtime (default 60, idle time not counted), both are paid at the same moment, and if the referrer reaches a milestone they are paid that too.

## Going further

### Change the rules

In `config.yml` under `referrals`, or in `/rewardsadmin`, **Plugin ▸ Settings**, **Referrals**:

- `qualify-minutes` (default 60): playtime the newcomer needs.
- `join-window-hours` (default 72): hours after their first join anywhere on the network during which a player can still name somebody.
- `block-same-address` (default on): refuses accounts that used the same address.

### Why a referral is refused

| Refusal | Why |
|---|---|
| Too late | More than `join-window-hours` since the player's first join anywhere on the network. |
| Yourself | A player cannot name themselves. |
| Same connection | With `block-same-address` on, the two accounts used the same address within the last 30 days, on any server, or are online from it now. When the records cannot answer, the referral is refused. |
| Already named | A player names one referrer, once. |
| Circular | The player being named already named this player. |

### Reset a mistaken referral

`/referraladmin reset <player>` (also `/refadmin reset`) forgets who a player named, so they can name somebody again. It asks for `confirm` first: `/referraladmin reset <player> confirm`. Only a referral nobody was paid for yet can be reset; one that already settled in stays.

<Details title="Technical details">

| Section of `referrals.yml` | Paid |
|---|---|
| `invited` | To the new player once they have played long enough. |
| `referrer` | To whoever invited them, at the same moment. |
| `milestones` | Once to a referrer when exactly that many invited players have settled in. |

Every block takes `rewards` and `message` (empty for none). Money and items are boosted for the `referrals` source. `/referral` is also `/ref` and `/referrals`.

Only one server can settle a referral, so a player moving servers at that minute pays nobody twice. Either player may be offline or on another server: what they earn is delivered by id, and the line saying why reaches them where they are, or on their next join.

</Details>
