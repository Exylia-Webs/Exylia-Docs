Pay players every time they vote, reward daily vote streaks, and fill a network-wide vote party that pays everyone or sends a piñata. Votes arrive through NuVotifier, what they pay is in `votes.yml`, and the party is edited in game with `/voteadmin`.

## Before you start

- Install **NuVotifier** (or the original Votifier) and register your server on the voting sites. Without it the module still runs, the console says so, and votes only arrive through `/voteadmin fake`.
- You hold `exyliarewards.votes.admin`. Players need `exyliarewards.votes.use`, which everyone has by default.
- A money reward needs an [economy plugin](/docs/exylialib/economy).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="List your vote sites">
    Open `plugins/ExyliaRewards/votes.yml`. Under `sites`, replace the sample pages with your server's own. Each site has a `name`, a `url` and an `icon`. These are what `/vote` shows players.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set what a vote pays">
    Under `vote`, edit `rewards`: an ExyliaLib reward list (see [Rewards in ExyliaLib](/docs/exylialib/rewards#in-a-config-file)). Add a `message` sent once the vote is paid. In messages, `%player%`, `%rewards%`, `%money%`, `%service%`, `%streak%` and `%total%` are filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/rewardsadmin reload`. The file is yours: what you change is never overwritten.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test vote">
    Run `/voteadmin fake <player> [service]`. It sends a test vote down the whole path, rewards and party included.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

The player receives the `vote` rewards and its message. `/vote` (or `/votes`) lists the sites, and clicking one sends its link in chat. Beside the sites it shows the player's votes today, total, streak, best streak and the party's progress. The **Votes** card of `/rewards` opens the same screen.

## Going further

### Reward vote streaks

A vote on the day after the last one extends the streak; a day without a vote starts it over. Under `streaks`, each key is a number of days and is paid once when the streak reaches exactly that many days. The file ships with 3, 7 and 30. A second vote on the same day pays `vote` again but leaves the streak where it is. Days follow the plugin's [clock](/docs/exyliarewards/configuration), so the streak turns at the daily reset hour. [Streak freezes](/docs/exyliarewards/rewards#streak-freezes) keep a streak that would start over, one per day missed, when the player holds them.

### Fill a vote party

Every vote on the network fills the party. When it reaches the target (50 by default, `0` turns the party off), every server plays a countdown (a melody, a boss bar and a tick each second, 10 seconds by default) and then pays the `party` block of `votes.yml` to its online players, sends a piñata, or both. `party.commands` run once for the whole network.

Run `/voteadmin` to open the party. Its cards are **Vote party ▸ Party** (target, party mode and milestones), **Vote party ▸ Countdown & bar**, **Vote party ▸ Party rewards** (left-click edits the rewards, right-click the commands), **Vote party ▸ Piñatas**, **Vote party ▸ Start it now** and **Vote party ▸ Remove the piñatas**. The party mode is `REWARDS` (default), `PINATA` or `BOTH`.

Every server also announces how close the party is when the votes left reach each milestone (10, 5 and 1 by default). `/voteadmin party` starts the party now, whatever its count.

### Add a piñata

A piñata is a mob the plugin spawns itself: players hit it until it breaks. Nothing else has to be installed.

1. In `/voteadmin`, click **Vote party ▸ Piñatas**, then **Piñatas ▸ Create piñata**.
2. Open **Piñatas ▸ Spawn points** and, standing where you want piñatas to land, click **Spawn points ▸ Add one here**. A wizard asks you to confirm the spot.
3. Open the piñata and set **Piñata ▸ Rewards**: left-click is paid on every hit, right-click to whoever breaks it, shift-click to everyone who hit it once it breaks.
4. Set the party mode to `PINATA` or `BOTH`, or test it at once with **Piñata ▸ Send one here**, or `/voteadmin pinata [id]`.

`/voteadmin pinata stop` takes every piñata on this server away, paying nothing. A `PINATA` or `BOTH` party pays the rewards instead when there is no enabled piñata or no enabled spawn point, and the console says why.

### Remind players to vote

Players who have not voted today are reminded every `votes.reminder-minutes` (default 30, `0` never), and as they join when `votes.remind-on-join` is on. These and the vote memory below are also in `/rewardsadmin`, **Plugin ▸ Settings**, **Votes**.

<Details title="Technical details">

**Sections of `votes.yml`.**

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| `sites` | The pages `/vote` lists: `name`, `url`, `icon`. |
| `vote` | Paid for every vote. |
| `streaks` | Paid once when the streak reaches that many days. |
| `party` | Paid to everybody online on every server when the party starts; `commands` run once for the network from one server's console. |

Every block takes `rewards` and `message` (empty for none). A file from an older version with `money: 30` in a block still pays it, read as a money entry at the head of the list.

**A vote, start to finish.** NuVotifier hands the vote to one server, which stores it. Later copies of the same vote, however late, are turned away for `votes.remember-votes-days` (default 14). Every server announces it on the **Votes** channel, which players can mute in `/settings`. The server its player is on pays it. An offline player is paid on their next join on any server; a vote for a name that never joins is dropped after `votes.pending-vote-days` (default 30, `0` keeps it for ever). It counts towards the party network-wide, offline players included. Votes are matched to players by name, case aside, boosted for the `votes` source and delivered by id.

**Party keys in `config.yml`** under `votes.party`: `target` (50), `mode`, `milestones` (10, 5, 1), `bar-mode` (`AFTER_VOTE` or `ALWAYS`), `bossbar-seconds` (8), and `countdown` with `seconds` (10), `tick-sound`, `end-sound` and `melody`. One server takes the party, decides what it does and tells the network.

**Piñata cards.**

| Card | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Status | Enabled or disabled; a disabled piñata is never sent. |
| Weight (`10`) | How likely a party sends it against other enabled piñatas. |
| Mob | The creature (a llama by default) and the name over its head; `%health%` and `%max_health%` show the hits left. |
| Hits | Left-click: its hits (`40`), lifetime (`5m`), roaming range (`12` blocks). Right-click: hits added per player online (`5`) and the cap (`300`, `0` for none). |
| Look | Colours, outline, aura and reaction to hits. |
| Tricks | What it does while hit. |
| Rewards | As above. Boosted like votes; what does not fit is delivered later. |

A piñata always runs around, never fights back and drops nothing but its rewards. The server that takes the party picks the piñata and the spawn point for everybody and only the server the point belongs to spawns it; the network hears when it is on its way, appears, and who broke it. While one stands its chunk is held loaded and players of that server see a boss bar with the hits left.

</Details>
