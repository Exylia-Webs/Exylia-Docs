## Is ExyliaRewards free?

No. It is a premium plugin installed as a loader jar that checks the licence on every start. It is not sold
on BuiltByBit: request your key on the [Exylia Discord](https://discord.exylia.net) and put it in
`license_key` in `loader.yml`. See [Installation](/docs/exyliarewards/installation).

## The plugin does not start

ExyliaLib 1.344.0 or newer is needed; the loader installs it, or stages a newer one, and asks for a restart
— restart once more. If the console reports a licence error, check the key in `loader.yml`.

## Where are the rewards? There is no rewards file

Rewards, AFK zones, codes and piñatas are rows in the database, edited in game: `/rewardsadmin`,
`/afkzonesadmin`, `/rewardsadmin codes` and `/voteadmin`. Only what votes and referrals pay is in files,
`votes.yml` and `referrals.yml`.

## I deleted the preset rewards and want them back

`/rewardsadmin presets` adds every preset whose ID is free. They are never written again on their own.

## Daily rewards reset at the wrong time

Days start at `clock.reset-hour` in `clock.timezone`; an empty timezone is the server's. Set both in
`config.yml` and reload.

## "Your rewards are still loading"

A player's claims are read from the database as they join, and a playtime reward also waits for their
playtime to be read. Until then nothing can be claimed. It lasts a moment; if it does not, check the
database connection.

## Votes arrive but nobody is paid

Votes are paid to the player with that name when they are online, or on their next join. Check the name the
vote site sends. Without NuVotifier, votes only arrive through `/voteadmin fake`; the console says so on
start.

## The vote party paid rewards instead of sending a piñata

A party set to `PINATA` or `BOTH` pays when there is no enabled piñata whose mob can spawn (ExyliaMobs
installed and the mob present) or no enabled spawn point. The console says which.

## Several servers: do players get paid twice?

No, as long as every server's `database.yml` points at the same database. Claims, code uses, votes, the
party and referrals are each taken by exactly one server. Turn on Redis too, so announcements and the vote
party reach every server at once. See [Network and data](/docs/exyliarewards/network).

## A player's inventory was full

Nothing is lost. What does not fit is kept and handed over on their next join, and staff can see it with
`/exylialib pendingrewards`.

## Two accounts from one house farm an AFK zone

Only one account per address earns in a zone. For rewards, switch on **One per address** in the reward's
requirements; for referrals, keep `referrals.block-same-address` on.

## Can boosters multiply what the plugin pays?

Yes. Money, items and experience go through ExyliaLib's modifiers under the sources `rewards`,
`playtime-rewards`, `afk-zones`, `votes` and `referrals`.

## I ran AFK zones, votes or referrals in ExyliaSurvivalCore

Their tables keep the same names here (`sc_afk_zones`, `sc_votes`, `sc_voters`, `sc_referrals` and the
rest), so on the same database the zones, vote totals and referrals are there as they were.
