The usual causes, with the fix for each.

## The plugin does not start

ExyliaRewards needs ExyliaLib 1.570.1 or newer. The loader installs it, or stages a newer one, and asks for a restart: restart once more. If the console reports a licence error, send that line to support. See [Installation](/docs/exyliarewards/installation).

## A module answers that it is off

Each module (rewards, AFK zones, votes, referrals, battle pass) is switched on in `config.yml` under `modules`. A change needs a restart. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliarewards/configuration).

## "Your rewards are still loading"

A player's claims are read from the database as they join, and a playtime reward also waits for their playtime. Until then nothing can be claimed. It lasts a moment; if it does not, check the database connection in ExyliaLib's `database.yml`. See [Database](/docs/exylialib/database).

## A player cannot claim a reward

- **It is disabled.** Open `/rewardsadmin`, the reward, **Status**.
- **It is locked.** The reward asks for a permission, a playtime range, a condition or another reward claimed first, and the list shows the reason. Change it under **Requirements**.
- **It is used.** The daily, weekly and monthly rewards wait for the next reset; a reward with a claim limit stops at it. Staff holding `exyliarewards.bypass` skip both.
- **They lack `exyliarewards.use`.** Claiming needs it. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliarewards/permissions).
- **Two accounts from one address.** With **One per address** on, only one account of a house claims it.

To test as a player, use `/rewardsadmin give <player> <id>` or `/rewardsadmin reset <player> <id>`.

## Daily rewards reset at the wrong time

Days start at `clock.reset-hour` in `clock.timezone`; an empty timezone is the server's. Set both in `config.yml` and run `/rewardsadmin reload`.

## Votes arrive but nobody is paid

Votes are paid to the player with that name when they are online, or on their next join. Check the name the vote site sends. Without NuVotifier (or Votifier), votes only arrive through `/voteadmin fake <player>`, and the console says so on start. See [Votes](/docs/exyliarewards/votes).

## The vote party paid rewards instead of sending a piñata

A party set to `PINATA` or `BOTH` pays rewards when there is no enabled piñata or no enabled spawn point. The console says which.

## A reward is missing, or I deleted the presets

Rewards are rows in the database, not a file. `/rewardsadmin presets` adds every preset whose ID is free. See [Installation](/docs/exyliarewards/installation#the-preset-rewards).

## A reward's placeholder shows the wrong value

An ID that starts like another placeholder of the plugin (`playtime`, `ready`, `freezes`, `afk_`, `votes_`, `referrals_`, `battlepass_`) is answered by that placeholder. The preset `playtime_1h` rewards are in that case: give your reward an ID such as `pt_1h`. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliarewards/placeholders). Placeholders in other plugins also need PlaceholderAPI.

## A player's inventory was full

Nothing is lost. What does not fit is kept and handed over on their next join, and staff see it with `/exylialib pendingrewards`.

## Players are paid twice on a network

Not if every server's `database.yml` points at the same database. Turn on Redis too, so announcements and the vote party reach every server at once. See [Network and data](/docs/exyliarewards/network).
