ExyliaRewards gives players reasons to come back. Daily, weekly and monthly rewards with streaks, timed
and one-time gifts, playtime milestones, login calendars and redeem codes; zones that pay players for
staying in them; votes with a network vote party and piñatas; and referrals that pay both players once
the new one has settled in.

Everything players earn is built and edited in game. Rewards, AFK zones, codes and piñatas are not kept in
files: they are rows in the database, edited from menus, so a change made on one server reaches every
server that shares the database.

It is a **premium** plugin, installed through a licence loader. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliarewards/installation).

## The modules

Each module is switched on or off in `config.yml` under `modules`. A change needs a restart.

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| `rewards` | Claimable rewards: daily, weekly, monthly, on a cooldown, once, after an amount of playtime, when a placeholder check passes, or as a login calendar. Streaks with bonus days, streak freezes, pick-one and mystery contents, requirements, auto-claim and ready notices. It also runs `/redeem` codes. |
| `afk-zones` | Areas that pay players for staying inside them, on a ladder of tiers that pays more the longer they stay, with a boss bar countdown and an AFK top. |
| `votes` | Votes arriving through NuVotifier: what every vote pays, daily vote streaks, reminders, and a network vote party with a countdown, milestones and an optional ExyliaMobs piñata. |
| `referrals` | New players name who invited them, and both are paid once the new player has played long enough, idle time not counted. Milestones for referrers. |

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.4 or newer, or a fork of it. Folia supported. |
| Java | 21 |
| Required dependency | `ExyliaLib` 1.344.0 or newer — installed for you if it is missing |
| Optional dependencies | `PlaceholderAPI` (placeholders in other plugins), `NuVotifier` (votes), `ExyliaMobs` (vote party piñatas) |

<Callout type="note" title="About ExyliaLib">
Configuration, menus and prompts, the reward lists and their editor, the economy, boosters, the database,
the playtime tracker, regions and the Discord webhooks all come from ExyliaLib. ExyliaRewards is written on
top of it and does not run without it, which is why the loader installs it on a server that lacks it.
</Callout>

## Works with

| Plugin | What happens |
|---|---|
| Booster plugins | Money, items and experience paid by every module go through ExyliaLib's modifiers, so boosters multiply them. See [Network and data](/docs/exyliarewards/network#boosters). |
| ExyliaMobs | A vote party can send one of its mobs as a piñata. See [ExyliaMobs](/docs/exyliamobs/introduction). |
| ExyliaSurvivalCore | When installed, its missions and seasons can count what players do here: rewards claimed, playtime rewards, codes, AFK payouts, votes, piñata hits and referrals. Its old AFK zones, votes and referrals tables are read as they are. |

## Languages

The plugin ships English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish (`en`, `es`, `fr`, `pt`, `ru`,
`tr`): every message and every menu. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliarewards/configuration#language).
