Mark a box in a world that pays the players standing in it, on a timer, with higher tiers for players who stay longer. You build it in game with `/afkzonesadmin`; players list zones with `/afkzone`.

## Before you start

- You hold `exyliarewards.afkzones.admin`. Players need `exyliarewards.afkzones.use`, which everyone has by default. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliarewards/permissions).
- You know the corners of the area. The editor uses ExyliaLib's shared block selector (a wand and two corners).
- Zones belong to the server they are created on. Create them on the server where the area is.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the zone">
    Run `/afkzonesadmin`, click **Zones ▸ Create zone** and type an ID (lowercase, no spaces). The zone screen opens. A new zone is enabled and pays every 60 seconds, but pays nothing until its area and rewards are set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the area">
    Click **Zone ▸ General**, then **Zone ▸ Zone area**, and select the box with the wand and two corners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the interval">
    In **General**, click **Zone ▸ Base interval** and type the time between payouts, such as `1m`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the rewards">
    Back on the zone screen, click **Zone ▸ Rewards**, then **Zone ▸ Base rewards**. Add what each payout gives in ExyliaLib's [reward editor](/docs/exylialib/rewards).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Click **Zone ▸ Display** to set the display name and the icon players see in lists. A new zone uses its ID as name and a bed as icon.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Walk into the area in survival. You see a title, hear the enter sound and a boss bar counts down to the next payout. When it ends you are paid the zone's rewards and the next countdown starts. Walk out: a summary of everything you received is sent. `/afkzone` lists the zones of this server with yours highlighted.

If nothing happens, check the area was saved, the zone is enabled, and you are alive in survival or adventure and not vanished. See [Common problems](/docs/exyliarewards/troubleshooting).

## Going further

### Reward players who stay longer

Open **Zone ▸ Tiers** and click **Tiers ▸ Add tier**. Give it a name and the minutes a player must stay to reach it. It starts on the zone's interval and pays nothing until you click **Tier ▸ Rewards** and add some. Each tier also has **Reached after** and **Interval**, and can be deleted with **Tier ▸ Delete**.

The zone's own interval and rewards are its base tier, at minute zero. A zone with no extra tiers simply pays its base rewards every interval. Reaching a higher tier is said in chat and moves the running countdown onto it at once, never making the payout later. Leaving and coming back starts again at the bottom.

### Cap the money per day

In **Zone ▸ General**, set **Zone ▸ Daily cap**: the first cap's worth of paid AFK time every day pays in full. Time past it pays only **Zone ▸ After the cap** percent of each payout's money, experience and items (`0` pays nothing). Commands and messages cannot be reduced, so a reduced payout skips them. A cap of `0` is no cap, the default. The day follows the plugin [clock](/docs/exyliarewards/configuration) and starts over at the reset hour. A payout that falls partly past the cap is paid in proportion. The boss bar shows the time used today against the cap, and the player is told once per day and stay when it is reached and when it pays in full again.

### Show an AFK top

Every payout adds its interval to the player's paid AFK time, network-wide. The ten players with the most are cached and refreshed every minute for the `afk_top` placeholders; see [Placeholders](/docs/exyliarewards/placeholders#afk-zones).

### What players see

The **AFK zones** card of `/rewards` and `/afkzone` list the enabled zones of this server, with the zone the player is in, their tier and their paid AFK time. Clicking a zone, or `/afkzone preview <zone>`, shows its tiers: when each is reached, how often it pays and what. The sounds are `afk-zones.enter-sound` and `afk-zones.reward-sound` in `config.yml`; the bar, titles, summary and every line are in `messages.yml` under `afk-zones`.

<Details title="Technical details">

**Who earns.**

- Only a player who is playing: alive, in survival or adventure, and not vanished. Staff in creative or spectator get no stay; a player who switches mode inside keeps the countdown but is not paid.
- One account per address per zone. A player whose address matches somebody already in the zone is told so and earns nothing there. When the database cannot answer, entry is refused.
- A player who logs in inside a zone starts a stay there. Dying takes the bar down; respawning inside starts the countdown again, respawning outside ends the stay.

**Full inventories.** Money, commands and other non-item rewards are always paid. When the inventory is full, the first payout's items still go through and are kept for the next join, with a warning; later payouts skip the items until there is room again.

**Live edits.** Every countdown and payout reads the zone as it is now: an edit reaches players already inside on their next payout, and a zone switched off or deleted pays nothing more. Changing the area or the status puts everybody online in or out at once.

**Zones belong to one server.** Each zone is stored with the server it was created on, and each server only loads its own. The paid AFK time and the top are shared by the network.

**Zone screen cards.** Status; General (area, base interval, daily cap, after the cap); Tiers; Rewards (base rewards); Display; Player view (the tier list a player sees).

</Details>
