An AFK zone is a box in a world that pays the players standing in it, on a timer. The longer a player stays
without leaving, the higher the **tier** they reach, and a higher tier can pay more, more often. Zones are
created and edited in game with `/afkzonesadmin` (`exyliarewards.afkzones.admin`), and players list them
with `/afkzone`.

## How a stay pays

1. Walking in starts a **stay**: a title, the `afk-zones.enter-sound`, and a boss bar counting down to the
   next payout.
2. When the countdown ends, the player is paid the rewards of the tier they are on, with a title and the
   `afk-zones.reward-sound`, and the next countdown starts.
3. Each countdown starts on the tier the player's uninterrupted time in the zone has reached. Reaching a
   higher tier says so in chat.
4. Leaving ends the stay and sends a summary of everything received. Coming back starts again at the bottom.

A zone's own interval and rewards are its **base** tier, at minute zero. Extra tiers are reached after a
number of minutes in the zone and each has its own interval and rewards, so a zone with no extra tiers
simply pays its base rewards every interval.

Every countdown and payout reads the zone as it is now: an edit reaches players already inside on their
next payout, and a zone switched off or deleted pays nothing more.

## Who earns

- Only a player who is **playing**: alive, in survival or adventure, and not vanished. Staff in creative or
  spectator get no stay; a player who switches mode inside keeps the countdown but is not paid.
- **One account per address per zone.** A player whose address matches somebody already in the zone is told
  so and earns nothing there. When the database cannot answer, entry is refused.
- A player who logs in inside a zone starts a stay there. Dying takes the bar down; respawning inside the zone
  starts the countdown again, respawning outside ends the stay.

## Full inventories

Money, commands and other non-item rewards are always paid. When the player's inventory is full, the first
payout's items still go through and are kept for their next join, with a warning; the payouts after that
skip the items until there is room again, so a player full overnight does not pile up a batch every
interval.

## The AFK top

Every payout adds its interval to the player's **paid AFK time**, network-wide. The ten players with the
most are cached and refreshed every minute, for the `afk_top` placeholders; see
[Placeholders](/docs/exyliarewards/placeholders#afk-zones).

## The zone editor

`/afkzonesadmin` lists this server's zones. **Create zone** asks an ID (lowercase, no spaces) and opens it.
Left-click a zone to edit it, right-click to delete it (asked to confirm).

| Card | Holds |
|---|---|
| Status | Enabled or disabled. |
| General | **Zone area**: select the box with ExyliaLib's shared block selector (a wand and two corners); **Base interval**: the time between base payouts, such as `1m`. |
| Tiers | The extra tiers. **Add tier** asks a name and the minutes a player must stay to reach it; it starts on the zone's interval and pays nothing until you give it rewards. Each tier has its name, **Reached after**, **Interval** and **Rewards**, and can be deleted. |
| Rewards | The base rewards, in ExyliaLib's reward editor. |
| Display | The display name and the icon. |
| Player view | The tier list a player sees. |

A new zone is enabled, pays every 60 seconds, has the zone's ID as its name and `RED_BED` as its icon, and
pays nothing until its area and rewards are set. Changing the area or the status puts everybody online in or
out of the zone at once.

<Callout type="note" title="Zones belong to one server">
Each zone is stored with the server it was created on, and each server only loads its own. Several servers
on one database keep their zones apart; the paid AFK time and the top are shared.
</Callout>

## What players see

`/afkzone` lists the enabled zones of this server, with the zone the player is in, their tier and their paid
AFK time. Clicking a zone, or `/afkzone preview <zone>`, shows its tiers: when each is reached, how often it
pays and what. The **AFK zones** card of `/rewards` opens the same list.

The bar, both titles, the summary and every line are in `messages.yml` under `afk-zones`.
