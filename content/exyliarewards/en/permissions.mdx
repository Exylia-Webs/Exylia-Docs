Every permission of ExyliaRewards. Players can already use rewards, the calendar, playtime, codes, AFK zones, votes, referrals and the battle pass; staff features need an admin node. Give them with a permissions plugin such as LuckPerms.

<Permissions />

## Give a permission

With LuckPerms, to give VIP the calendar and take it from everyone else:

```text
/lp group default permission set exyliarewards.calendar false
/lp group vip permission set exyliarewards.calendar true
```

`exyliarewards.calendar` and `exyliarewards.playtime` default to everyone on purpose: they exist so a rank can lose them. A reward already earned is never held back, since claiming needs only `exyliarewards.use`.

A reward and a redeem code can also require a permission of their own, set in their editor. A reward can be shown locked to players without it, or hidden from them. `exyliarewards.battlepass.*` does not include `exyliarewards.battlepass.premium`: grant that one on its own.

<Callout type="warning" title="Keep two nodes for staff">
`exyliarewards.admin` opens every reward editor and can give any reward. `exyliarewards.bypass` skips a reward's wait and claim limit, so a player holding it can claim again and again.
</Callout>

The menu actions that open player screens check the node of the thing they open: `open_hub`, `open_category`, `claim`, `preview`, `open_calendar` and `calendar_claim` need `exyliarewards.use` (the playtime and calendar lists also need their own node), and the AFK zones, votes and referrals cards of the hub need their `use` node. A reward's own permission and requirements still apply to every claim.
