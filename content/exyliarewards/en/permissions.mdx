---
title: "Permissions"
description: "Every node the plugin checks, its default, and what it gates."
---

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliarewards.use` | everyone | `/rewards` and its subcommands, `/playtime`. |
| `exyliarewards.redeem` | everyone | `/redeem`. |
| `exyliarewards.bypass` | nobody | Claiming without waiting, and past a reward's claim limit. |
| `exyliarewards.calendar` | everyone | Opening the login calendar. |
| `exyliarewards.playtime` | everyone | `/playtime` and the playtime rewards list. |
| `exyliarewards.calendar.catchup` | nobody | Catching up a missed login calendar day for free. |
| `exyliarewards.admin` | operators | `/rewardsadmin` and every subcommand, the reward and code editors, and `/playtime <player>`. |
| `exyliarewards.afkzones.use` | everyone | `/afkzone`, `/afkzone preview` and the `afk_preview` menu action. |
| `exyliarewards.afkzones.admin` | operators | `/afkzonesadmin` and the zone editor. |
| `exyliarewards.afkzones.*` | operators | Both AFK zone nodes. |
| `exyliarewards.votes.use` | everyone | `/vote` and the `votes_link` menu action. |
| `exyliarewards.votes.admin` | operators | `/voteadmin` and every subcommand, and the vote party editor. |
| `exyliarewards.votes.*` | operators | Both vote nodes. |
| `exyliarewards.referrals.use` | everyone | `/referral`. |
| `exyliarewards.referrals.admin` | operators | `/referraladmin reset`. |
| `exyliarewards.referrals.*` | operators | Both referral nodes. |

A reward and a redeem code can also require a permission of their own, set in their editor. A reward can be
shown locked to players without it, or hidden from them.

`calendar` and `playtime` exist so a rank can lose them: deny one to the default group and grant it to VIP.
A reward already earned is never held back, since claiming needs only `exyliarewards.use`.

The menu actions that open player screens check the node of the thing they open: `open_hub`, `open_category`,
`claim`, `preview`, `open_calendar` and `calendar_claim` need `exyliarewards.use` (the playtime and calendar lists also
their own node above), and the AFK zones, votes and referrals cards of the hub need their `use` node. A reward's own
permission and requirements still apply to every claim.
