---
title: "Permissions"
description: "ExyliaProxyUtils defines no permissions; which permissions decide what player-proxy: commands may do."
---

ExyliaProxyUtils defines **no permission nodes**. It registers no commands, and nothing it does is
offered to players directly.

Two things decide what runs:

| Line | Checked against |
|---|---|
| `player-proxy:<command>` | The player's permissions **on the proxy**, as if they had typed the command. A player without Velocity's or BungeeCord's permission for `/server` cannot be moved by `player-proxy:server lobby`. Grant proxy permissions with your proxy's permission plugin (LuckPerms on Velocity, for example). |
| `console-proxy:<command>` | Nothing: it runs as the proxy console. |

Commands on the backends that use the proxy — ExyliaStaff's `/staffadmin import` (`exyliastaff.admin`),
teleports, network kicks — are gated by those plugins' own permissions.
