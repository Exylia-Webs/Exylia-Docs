The placeholders work inside Exylia plugins' own files and, with PlaceholderAPI installed, in any other plugin. Nothing needs downloading from the eCloud. Every value is read from memory.

`<game>` is a game id: `baccarat`, `blackjack`, `coinflip`, `crash`, `doubleornothing`, `hilo`, `horserace`, `keno`, `limbo`, `liveroulette`, `mines`, `plinko`, `roulette`, `scratch`, `sicbo`, `slots`, `tower`, `videopoker` or `wheel`. The id never changes when you rename a game.

<Placeholders />

A round counts as a win when it paid more than the stake and as a loss when it paid less. A round that gave the stake back counts as neither.

```text
%exyliacasino_blackjack_wins%
%exyliacasino_mines_win_rate%
%exyliacasino_liveroulette_playing%
```
