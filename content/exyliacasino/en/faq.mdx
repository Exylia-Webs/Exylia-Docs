## Is ExyliaCasino free?

No. It is a premium plugin installed as a loader jar that checks the licence on every start. It is not sold
on BuiltByBit: request your key on the [Exylia Discord](https://discord.exylia.net) and put it in
`license_key` in `loader.yml`. See [Installation](/docs/exyliacasino/installation).

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is a hard dependency: without it the server never loads the plugin. ExyliaLib 1.356.0 or
newer is needed too; the loader installs it, or stages a newer one, and asks for a restart — restart once
more. If the console reports a licence error, check the key in `loader.yml`.

## "There is no currency you can bet with"

No economy is installed, or `wager.currencies` lists none the player may use. Install ExyliaEconomy, Vault
with an economy plugin, or PlayerPoints, and check the ids in `wager.currencies` (empty offers every one).

## Can players lose a stake to a crash or a restart?

No. Every stake is held by ExyliaLib's escrow before the round starts. On a normal stop every round is
settled as it stands: a decided result is paid, a cash-out game is cashed out. On a crash, whatever was
still held is refunded on the next start, and what cannot be handed over at once is delivered on the
player's next join.

## Are the odds real?

Yes. Every result is drawn with a secure random before any animation; the animation only shows it. Games
with an `rtp` key are built to return exactly that on average, and the plugin's tests check the maths of
every game. Blackjack and video poker show their `rtp` but their odds come from their rules and pay table,
and depend on how the player plays. See [Games](/docs/exyliacasino/games).

## Can I change a game's RTP?

In every game with an `rtp` key: Mines, Tower, Crash, Hi-Lo, Double or Nothing, Coinflip, Wheel of Fortune,
Slots, Plinko, Scratch Card and Horse Race. Keno's comes from its pay table, Roulette's and Baccarat's from
the rules of the game.

## A player walked away and lost their round

A round left untouched for the game's `idle` time is treated as leaving, and a round under way is forfeited.
Raise `idle` in that game's module file if your players need longer.

## Players cannot use commands while they play

Only the game's own command and `arena.allowed-commands` work during a round. Add the ones they need there,
without the slash.

## The tables are too big, or cut at the edges

Lower `screen.zoom` to draw every board smaller. On a field of view under 90, lower `screen.max-wide` and
`screen.max-tall` (95 and 65 fit every board at 70). See
[Configuration](/docs/exyliacasino/configuration).

## Does it work with ExyliaTabletop?

Yes. Both list their games on the same ExyliaLib games screen, and a player busy in one is busy in the
other: someone in a casino round cannot be seated at a Tabletop match, and the other way round.

## How do I open one game from my own menu or an NPC?

Use the menu action `casino:play <game>`, such as `casino:play blackjack`. It does what `/blackjack` typed
alone does.
