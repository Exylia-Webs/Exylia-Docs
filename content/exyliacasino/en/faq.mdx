## Is ExyliaCasino free?

No. It is a premium plugin installed as a loader jar that checks the licence on every start. It is not sold on BuiltByBit: request your key on the [Exylia Discord](https://discord.exylia.net) and put it in `license_key` in `loader.yml`. See [Installation](/docs/exyliacasino/installation).

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is a hard dependency, and ExyliaLib 1.592.0 or newer is needed. The loader installs or stages ExyliaLib and asks for a restart; restart once more. A licence error in the console means the key in `loader.yml` is wrong. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/exyliacasino/troubleshooting).

## "There is no currency you can bet with"

No economy is installed, or `wager.currencies` lists none the player may use. Install ExyliaEconomy, Vault with an economy plugin, or PlayerPoints, and check the ids in `wager.currencies` (empty offers every one). See [Configuration](/docs/exyliacasino/configuration#stakes-wager).

## Can players lose a stake to a crash or a restart?

No. Every stake is held by ExyliaLib's escrow before the round starts. On a normal stop every round is settled as it stands, and after a crash whatever was still held is refunded on the next start. See [Playing a round](/docs/exyliacasino/playing).

## Are the odds real, and can I change a game's RTP?

Yes. Every result is drawn with a secure random before any animation, which only shows it. `rtp` sets the return in Mines, Tower, Crash, Hi-Lo, Double or Nothing, Coinflip, Wheel of Fortune, Slots, Plinko, Scratch Card, Horse Race and Limbo. In Blackjack and Video Poker `rtp` is only shown to players; the rules and pay table decide the odds. See [Games](/docs/exyliacasino/games).

## A player walked away and lost their round

A round left untouched for the game's `idle` time shows a 15 second warning, then cashes out, plays itself out or is forfeited depending on the game. Raise `idle` in that game's module file if your players need longer. See [Playing a round](/docs/exyliacasino/playing#when-the-idle-clock-runs-out).

## Why does `/roulette 100 red` not place a bet?

Roulette, Live Roulette, Sic Bo, Baccarat, Blackjack, Wheel of Fortune, Keno, Horse Race and Crash are shared tables. The command opens the table screen, and the stake is the chips the player puts on the felt; a stake or option typed in the command is ignored. See [Tables](/docs/exyliacasino/tables).

## Can the casino lose more than it takes?

Not with the house bank on: wins are paid only from its balance. With it off (the default), nothing is limited. See [House bank](/docs/exyliacasino/bank).

## Players cannot use commands while they play

Only the game's own command and `arena.allowed-commands` work during a round. Add the ones they need there, without the slash. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliacasino/configuration#while-they-play-arena).

## The boards are too big, or cut at the edges

Lower `screen.zoom` to draw every board smaller. On a field of view under 90, lower `screen.max-wide` and `screen.max-tall` (95 and 65 fit every board at 70). See [Configuration](/docs/exyliacasino/configuration#size-screen).

## Which languages does it ship?

Six: English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliacasino/configuration#language).

## Why is there no `/cadmin`?

The admin command is `/casinoadmin`. `cadmin` is not set by default because ExyliaClans uses it; servers that already had it keep it. See [Commands](/docs/exyliacasino/commands).

## How do I open one game from my own menu or an NPC?

Use the menu action `casino:play <game>`, such as `casino:play blackjack`. It does what `/blackjack` typed alone does. See [Edit menus](/docs/exylialib/edit-menus).

## Does it work with ExyliaTabletop?

Yes. Both list their games on the same ExyliaLib games screen, and a player busy in one is busy in the other: someone in a casino round cannot be seated at a Tabletop match, and the other way round.
