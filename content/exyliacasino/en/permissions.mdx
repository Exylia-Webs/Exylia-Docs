---
title: "Permissions"
description: "The two nodes the plugin declares and what each one gates."
---

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliacasino.play` | everyone | `/casino`, every game's command, `/<game> leave`, and the `casino:play` menu action. |
| `exyliacasino.admin` | operators | `/casinoadmin`, `/casinoadmin reload` and the buttons of the admin hub. |

Every menu action checks its node itself, so copying a button into a screen players can open hands them
nothing they did not already have.

There is no node per game. To keep a game from everybody, switch it off with `enabled: false` in its module
file; to keep it from some players, deny its command with your permissions plugin.
