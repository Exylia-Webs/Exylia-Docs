At the end of this page one game works in game, from `/casino` and from a table you placed in the world. You only use the chat and the admin screen.

## Before you start

- Install the plugin: [Install ExyliaCasino](/docs/exyliacasino/installation). The console must end with `ExyliaCasino enabled`.
- Have an economy plugin: ExyliaEconomy, Vault with an economy plugin, or PlayerPoints. Without one there is no currency to bet in.
- Give yourself `exyliacasino.admin` (operators have it). Everybody already has `exyliacasino.play`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliacasino/permissions).
- Have at least 10 in your balance: the smallest stake is 10 by default.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Play one round from /casino">
    Run `/casino` and click **Coinflip**. Pick a currency if you are asked, type a stake such as `10`, choose a side. The coin is drawn in front of you in a dark room. When it lands, the result shows on a plaque and in chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press AGAIN or LEAVE">
    After a second the buttons **AGAIN**, **CHANGE BET** and **LEAVE** come up under the board. Click **LEAVE** to get your view back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the admin screen">
    Run `/casinoadmin` and click **World tables**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a Blackjack table">
    Click to place a table and choose **Blackjack**. Then click the block (or the NPC) that should open the game. The chat says the table is placed, and a label floats over it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sit at it">
    Click the block. The table screen opens. Click a chip, put it on the felt, press **READY**. The cards are dealt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

- The coin or the cards are drawn in front of you, and the result reaches chat.
- Your balance goes down by the stake and up again if you win.
- The label over the table says it is free, and shows your name while you play.
- `/casinoadmin bank` shows the casino's figures. See [House bank](/docs/exyliacasino/bank).

## Good first choices

- **Switch off what you do not want.** Set `enabled: false` in a game's `modules/<game>/config.yml`, then run `/casinoadmin reload`. The game leaves `/casino`.
- **Cap stakes.** Set `wager.maximum` in `config.yml`, or give a rank `exyliacasino.maxbet.<n>`. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliacasino/configuration#stakes-wager).
- **Only at tables.** Turn on **Tables only** in `/casinoadmin`, **Settings**, **Tables** once every game you offer has a table. See [Tables](/docs/exyliacasino/tables).
