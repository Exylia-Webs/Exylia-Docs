## Starting a round

There are three ways in, and they all lead to the same flow:

| Way | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/casino` | Opens the casino category of the games screen: every game the server has on, with its RTP and how many are playing. Clicking one starts it. |
| `/<game> [stake] [option]` | Starts that game: `/mines 500 5`, `/coinflip 50 heads`. For the nine shared-table games it opens the table screen, see below. |
| `casino:play <game>` | A menu action, the same as typing `/<game>` alone. For a server's own menus and NPCs. |

A solo game typed with no stake (`/mines`, its card on `/casino`) opens its own screen, where the player picks a chip and the option and presses **PLAY** (see [On the screen](#on-the-screen)). When a stake is typed, whatever is left out is asked in dialogs. At a shared table the stake is the chips on the felt (see [Shared tables](#shared-tables)):

1. **The currency**, only when the player may bet in more than one and typed no amount. A choice dialog
   lists them.
2. **The stake**, in an amount dialog that states the minimum. A stake typed in the command is used as it
   is.
3. **The game's option**, when the game has one: the mines on the board, the side of the coin. See
   [Games](/docs/exyliacasino/games).

A stake typed in the command uses the server's default currency when the player may bet in it, and the
first currency offered otherwise. Amounts accept the short forms of ExyliaLib's economy, such as `1.5k`.

### Shared tables

Roulette, Live Roulette, Sic Bo, Baccarat, Blackjack, Wheel of Fortune, Keno, Horse Race and Crash are shared tables. `/<game>`, its card on `/casino` and a table placed in the world all open the table screen. A stake or option typed in the command is ignored: the player picks a chip and clicks the felt, and each chip is held separately. They press **READY**, and the throw goes when everyone with chips is ready or the `betting` time ends. **REPEAT** puts down the chips of the last round again. `/<game> leave` always works. See [Tables](/docs/exyliacasino/tables).

### When a stake is refused

| Refused because | The player reads |
|---|---|
| No currency they may use is offered | There is no currency you can bet with. |
| Not a positive amount | That is not an amount you can bet. |
| Under `wager.minimum` or over `wager.maximum` | The smallest bet is … / The largest bet is … |
| They cannot afford it | You need … to place that bet. |
| They are combat tagged | You can't bet while in combat. |
| They are already in a round | You already have a bet going. |
| Another plugin holds them, such as an ExyliaTabletop match | You are busy in … |
| Another plugin advises against taking them | You can't right now: … |
| Two rounds started inside `wager.cooldown` | Slow down. Try again in … |
| The game is switched off | … is not available on this server. |

Every check runs **before** anything is charged.

## On the screen

Every game is played on a screen. The stake is held by ExyliaLib's escrow and the player is claimed, so every
other plugin sees them busy. While a round is up the player cannot be hurt, does not get hungry, cannot drop,
pick up, break or place anything, and cannot move items in their inventory.

The ten solo games (Mines, Tower, Hi-Lo, Double or Nothing, Coinflip, Limbo, Plinko, Slots, Scratch Card and
Video Poker) share one layout:

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chip rack** | The chips of `wager.chips`, as stakes. The last chip, **CUSTOM**, takes an amount typed in a dialog: left-click uses the amount, right-click changes it. Chips outside `wager.minimum` and `wager.maximum` are refused when played. |
| **MONEY / ITEM** | Double or Nothing can be staked with an item instead of money, see `item-stakes`. |
| **Option stepper** | `‹` and `»` change the game's option before the round: the mines, the side of the coin, the risk. Locked while a round runs. |
| **PLAY** | Starts the round with the chip and option shown. |
| **AUTO** | In Coinflip, Limbo and Slots, plays round after round at the same stake until a loss or win limit is reached or the player stops it. |
| **LIVE WINS** | The latest big wins on the server. |
| **BALANCE, LAST RESULTS** | The player's balance and their last results at that game. |
| **LEAVE** | Leaves the game, see [How a round ends](#how-a-round-ends). |

The nine shared-table games use the table screen described in [Tables](/docs/exyliacasino/tables) instead.

Commands are blocked during a round, except the game's own command (so `/mines leave` works) and those
listed in `arena.allowed-commands` (`msg`, `r`, `tell` by default). A blocked command answers "Leave the
game with /mines leave first."

<Callout type="info" title="The 3D form is gone">
The room, the guide panels and the board drawn in front of the player belonged to the 3D form. Every game is
now on a screen, so `booth`, `guide`, `screen` and `arena.freeze` no longer change how a game looks or holds
the player. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliacasino/configuration).
</Callout>

## The result

The verdict appears on the screen in place: how much was won and its multiplier, that the stake came back, or
that it was lost. The same result goes to chat.

A round's multiplier is of the stake: ×2 pays back twice the stake, ×1 gives it back, ×0 loses it. A double
or a split in blackjack puts in a second stake, held by the escrow like the first, and the result pays both
hands.

## Playing again

When a money round ends, after `again.delay` the screen offers the next round: **PLAY** plays again at the
chip shown, and the chip rack and the option stepper work again, so a player can change the bet or the option
first. The screen waits `again.timeout` for an answer. With `again.enabled: false` the player is sent away when
a round ends. **AUTO** waits `again.delay` between rounds, half of it in turbo.

## How a round ends

| What happens | What it costs |
|---|---|
| The round plays out | Paid as the result says. |
| **LEAVE**, closing the screen, `/<game> leave`, dying, a teleport not made by a plugin, changing world | Closing the screen or pressing LEAVE in a round whose stake would be lost asks first. Leaving forfeits the stake, except where the game keeps what is already decided (Plinko's dropped balls, Double or Nothing after a win). A round not yet started is refunded. |
| Quitting the server | The safest move is played for the player and settled, never a refund, so quitting never dodges a loss. Mines, Tower and Hi-Lo cash out what was earned (forfeit if nothing yet); a game with nothing to choose plays out. Paid now or on their next join. |
| Left untouched for the game's `idle` time | A warning shows 15 seconds before. Then it depends on the game, see below. Any click restarts the clock. |
| The server stops | Settled as it stands, never forfeited: a result already decided is paid, a cash-out game is cashed out, blackjack and video poker are played out, and a bet not placed yet is refunded. |

Dying in a round keeps the inventory and the levels. `/casinoadmin reload` does not touch rounds under way:
they are left to finish, and a game switched off only refuses new rounds.

### When the idle clock runs out

| Game | What happens |
|---|---|
| Mines, Tower, Hi-Lo, Double or Nothing | Cashed out, if anything was earned; otherwise the stake is forfeited. |
| Slots, Scratch Card, Limbo (not rolled yet), Plinko (nothing dropped yet) | The round plays itself out: it spins, rolls, scratches or drops for the player. |
| Plinko with balls already dropped | Walking away keeps what was decided. |
| Every other solo game | Treated as leaving: the round under way is forfeited. |

The shared-table games have no round of their own, so this clock does not apply: they follow the table's
countdowns.

Slots and the Wheel also spin on their own after `auto-spin`, well before that.
