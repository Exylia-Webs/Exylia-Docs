ExyliaCasino is a casino played against the house. A player picks a game, puts up a stake in one of the
server's currencies, and the game is drawn in front of them as a 3D table: cards are dealt and flipped,
wheels brake to a stop, balls bounce down pegs, tiles turn over. While they play, a dark room is drawn
round them, only for them, so they see the table and nothing of the world behind it.

Every game states its **return to player** (RTP): the share of every stake it pays back on average. The
`/casino` menu shows it on each game's card, and every table shows it under its heading. The result of a
round is drawn with a secure random before any animation starts; the spin, the deal or the drop only shows
it.

It is a **premium** plugin, installed through a licence loader. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliacasino/installation).

## The games

Seventeen games, each a module of its own with its own file, command and messages. Every one can be
switched off, renamed or retuned without touching the others. See [Games](/docs/exyliacasino/games) for
the rules, options and RTP of each.

| Kind | Games |
|---|---|
| Cash out | Mines, Tower, Crash, Hi-Lo, Double or Nothing |
| Cards | Blackjack, Baccarat, Video Poker |
| Wheels | Roulette, Live Roulette, Wheel of Fortune, Coinflip |
| Draws | Slots, Plinko, Keno, Scratch Card, Horse Race |

Double or Nothing is the one game staked with an item instead of money: the item in the player's hand.
Live Roulette is the one game shared by everyone: one wheel for the whole server, on a fixed cycle.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.4 or newer, or a fork of it. Folia supported. |
| Java | 21 |
| Required dependencies | `packetevents`, and `ExyliaLib` 1.356.0 or newer — installed for you if it is missing |
| An economy | `ExyliaEconomy`, or `Vault` with an economy plugin, or `PlayerPoints`: stakes are taken in their currencies |
| Optional dependencies | `PlaceholderAPI`, for the placeholders in other plugins |

<Callout type="note" title="About ExyliaLib">
Configuration, menus and prompts, the escrow that holds every stake, the economy, the database, the stage
the tables are drawn on, the room, effects and the shared games catalogue all come from ExyliaLib.
ExyliaCasino is written on top of it and does not run without it, which is why the loader installs it on a
server that lacks it.
</Callout>

## ExyliaTabletop

ExyliaTabletop is the sister plugin with games played player against player. Both meet in ExyliaLib:

- **One games catalogue.** `/casino` opens the casino category of the library's games screen, the same
  screen ExyliaTabletop lists its games in.
- **One busy state.** While a round of the casino is up, the player is busy for every other plugin, and a
  player busy in ExyliaTabletop is refused here before any stake is charged.

Neither needs the other.

## Languages

The plugin ships English, Spanish, Russian and Turkish (`en`, `es`, `ru`, `tr`): every message, every
game's texts and every menu. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliacasino/configuration#language).
