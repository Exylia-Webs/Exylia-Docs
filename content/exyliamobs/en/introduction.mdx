ExyliaMobs lets you build custom mobs and bosses from a menu in game, give them skills, fight phases, a look and reward lists, and keep them alive in areas called generators. You never edit a mob in a file.

In about ten minutes you can have a custom mob that you spawned yourself and that pays its killer. See [Your first mob](/docs/exyliamobs/first-steps).

## What you can do

- **Build a mob** from `/mobsadmin`: name, type, equipment, attributes, potion effects, skills, phases and a look. See [Create a mob](/docs/exyliamobs/building-mobs).
- **Decide what it pays**: ten reward lists, from the top damager's to loot thrown out where it dies. See [Reward players for kills](/docs/exyliamobs/rewards).
- **Keep mobs alive** in an area with a generator: while a player is near it spawns waves of the mobs you choose. See [Keep mobs alive with a generator](/docs/exyliamobs/generators).
- **Give a mob a 3D model** made in Blockbench, with no resource pack. See [Give a mob a 3D model](/docs/exyliamobs/models).
- **Change the shared rules** (how much damage a mob needs to be paid, how many may be alive) from a settings page. See [Change the shared settings](/docs/exyliamobs/settings).

Three showcase mobs come ready to spawn: `stone_golem`, `crawler` and `wisp`.

## What you need

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.1 or newer, or a fork of it. Folia 1.21.4 or newer works too. |
| Java | 21 |
| Required plugins | `packetevents`, and ExyliaLib 1.573.0 or newer (the loader installs it if it is missing) |
| Optional plugins | ExyliaEconomy or Vault for money rewards, PlaceholderAPI |

ExyliaMobs is a premium plugin installed through a licence loader. See [Install ExyliaMobs](/docs/exyliamobs/installation).

<Callout type="note" title="Where the mob engine lives">
Skills, phases, reactions and models are ExyliaLib's. ExyliaMobs is the part you use: it stores your mobs, gives them their editors, places the generators and pays for the kills. The details of skills and models are on [Mobs and models](/docs/exylialib/mobs-and-models).
</Callout>

## Good to know

- **Players have no commands and need no permissions.** They meet the mobs in the world. Staff need `exyliamobs.admin`, see [Permissions](/docs/exyliamobs/permissions).
- **Mobs are shared by a network.** Every server pointed at the same database spawns from the same mobs. Generators belong to the server where you created them.
- **Languages**: English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish. The plugin follows the `language` in ExyliaLib's `config.yml`. See [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).
