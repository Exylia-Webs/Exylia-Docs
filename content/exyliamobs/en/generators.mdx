A generator is an area of the map that keeps a number of mobs alive while somebody is near it. Nobody
near, nothing spawns; the mobs already out stay.

## The generator list

`/mobsadmin` → **GENERATORS** lists this server's generators: their status, world, mobs, and how many are
alive out of the maximum.

| Click | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left-click a generator | Opens its screen. |
| Shift-click a generator | Teleports you to the middle of its area. |
| Right-click a generator | Deletes it, after a confirmation. Its live mobs are removed. |
| **CREATE GENERATOR** | Asks for an id (up to 64 characters, unique across the network) and opens it. |

A status reads **No area** until the area is selected, **No mobs** until it has one, then **Enabled** or
**Disabled**.

## Setting one up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    **CREATE GENERATOR**, and type its id.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the area">
    **AREA** → left-click. ExyliaLib's shared selector hands you the wand: mark two corners. The box between
    them, floor to ceiling, is the area. See [Walking somebody through a setup](/docs/exylialib/input-and-editors#walking-somebody-through-a-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it mobs">
    **MOBS** opens a list: add mobs from the ones that exist, and click one to set its weight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune the numbers">
    Max alive, per wave, interval, activation radius and per player. A new generator is already enabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The generator screen

Every edit applies on the next pass of the generator timer.

| Button | Default | What it sets |
|---|---|---|
| **STATUS** | Enabled | Toggles it. A disabled generator spawns nothing; its live mobs stay. |
| **AREA** | — | Left-click selects it again; right-click teleports you there. |
| **ACTIVATION** | 24 blocks | How close a player has to be to the area for it to spawn, `0` to `256`. `0` means inside the area. Distance is measured to the nearest block of the area; spectators do not count. |
| **PER PLAYER** | None | Each player near beyond the first adds this many to the max alive and to each wave, `0` to `100`. |
| **MOBS** | None | Which mobs it spawns, each with a weight. |
| **MAX ALIVE** | 6 | It stops spawning once this many of its mobs are alive, `1` to `500`. |
| **INTERVAL** | 20 s | How often a wave spawns, typed as `10s`, `30s` or `1m`. At least one second. |
| **PER WAVE** | 2 | How many appear in each wave, up to the max alive, `1` to `100`. |
| **REMOVE ITS MOBS** | — | Removes the mobs it keeps alive. It refills on its next wave. |
| **BACK** | — | The generator list. |
| **DELETE GENERATOR** | — | Deletes it, after a confirmation, and removes its live mobs. |

### Weights

Each mob in the list has a weight; a mob's chance per spawn is its weight over the total, shown in its
lore. A weight of `0` never spawns it, and a list where every weight is `0` spawns nothing. A mob that was
deleted is skipped.

## How a wave spawns

Every `tick-seconds` (2 s by default) one timer walks this server's generators. A generator spawns when it
is enabled, has an area, its interval has passed, and at least one player is within its activation radius.
Then, with N players near:

- **room** is its max alive plus `N − 1` times its per-player number, less its mobs already alive;
- the **wave** is its per-wave number plus the same per-player extra, cut to that room and to what is left
  of the server-wide cap, `max-alive` in `config.yml` (200 by default, every generator together).

For each mob in the wave it picks a mob by weight and a random column of the area. The spot is the highest
solid floor in that column, inside the area's height, with two open, dry blocks above it. A column in an
unloaded chunk, or with no such floor, is tried again elsewhere, up to four times, before that mob is
skipped until the next wave. Generators **never load a chunk**, and each spawn runs on the thread that owns
its spot, so the same code runs on Folia.

## WorldGuard

A spawn the server cancels is nearly always WorldGuard: with `mobs.block-plugin-spawning` on (its default),
the `mob-spawning` and `deny-spawn` flags cancel plugin spawns too. ExyliaMobs leaves that decision to the
region owner and says so in the console, once per generator:

```text
Mob generator crypt could not spawn at 120, 64, -40: … If a WorldGuard region there has mob-spawning
or deny-spawn set, allow it for this area or set mobs.block-plugin-spawning: false in WorldGuard's world
config. Logged once per generator.
```

## On a network

Point several servers at the same database and:

| What | How |
|---|---|
| **Mobs** | Shared. Every server spawns from the same templates, and an id is unique across all of them. |
| **Generators** | Each belongs to the server it was created on — named by the proxy when ExyliaLib's bridge is up, or by `server-id` in the Redis block of `database.yml` otherwise — and only that server loads and runs it. Its id is still unique across the network. |
| **Changes** | With Redis on, a mob or generator saved on one server is read again on the others at once. Without Redis, other servers pick a change up on their next restart: `/mobsadmin reload` does not read mobs or generators again. See [Redis](/docs/exylialib/database#redis). |
