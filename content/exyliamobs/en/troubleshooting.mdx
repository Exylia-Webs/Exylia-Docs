Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is required: without it the server never loads ExyliaMobs. ExyliaLib 1.573.0 or newer is needed too; the loader installs it or stages a newer one and asks for a restart, so restart once more. If the console reports a licence error, check `license_key` in `plugins/ExyliaMobs/loader.yml`. See [Install ExyliaMobs](/docs/exyliamobs/installation).

## A generator spawns nothing

Check, in order:

1. Its status reads **Enabled** (not No area, No mobs or Disabled).
2. A player who is not a spectator is within its **Activation** radius.
3. It has not reached its **Max alive**.
4. The server has not reached the **Alive limit** (200 by default). See [Change the shared settings](/docs/exyliamobs/settings).
5. The area has a solid floor with two open blocks above it inside its height, in loaded chunks.
6. At least one of its mobs has a weight above `0`.

If the console says a spawn was cancelled, it is a WorldGuard flag. See [WorldGuard](/docs/exyliamobs/generators#worldguard).

## `/mobsadmin spawn` says the mob could not spawn

The chat says "… could not spawn here. The spot may be blocked or protected." Move to an open spot outside a region that forbids mob spawning.

## A mob died and paid nothing

- Killed by health: it pays only when players dealt at least the **Damage share** of its damage (half by default). A mob that a farm, lava or other mobs mostly killed pays nothing.
- In hits mode: it pays only when its last hit breaks it, never through `/kill` or the void.
- Its lifetime ran out: only the **Escaped** list pays.
- The reward list you filled is not the one that applies. See [Reward players for kills](/docs/exyliamobs/rewards#when-a-mob-pays).

## Items did not drop on the ground

In Ground mode, items come out at the mob only when their player is online on that server and within 32 blocks. Otherwise they go to the player, and what does not fit is kept for their next join.

## A model does not show or shows problems

Run `/mobsadmin models`. The model must be a `.bbmodel` in `plugins/ExyliaMobs/models/`, read again with `/mobsadmin models reload`. Meshes, locators and Molang are left out and counted as problems; the console lists them. See [Give a mob a 3D model](/docs/exyliamobs/models).

## My menu edits were lost

The ten screens are rewritten from the jar on every start. Change their text in `messages.yml`, which is kept. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliamobs/configuration#messages-and-menus).

## A change on one server does not reach another

Without Redis, servers read mobs and generators from the database only on start, and `/mobsadmin reload` does not read them again. Turn on Redis in `database.yml`. See [On a network](/docs/exyliamobs/generators#on-a-network).

## A mob says "Unknown type"

It was stored by a newer server version whose creature this server does not know. Pick a new type with **Type** on its screen.
