You end up with the server-wide rules of ExyliaMobs changed from a menu, without opening a file. They apply to every mob and generator.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Run `/mobsadmin` and click **Settings**. Two cards appear: **Mobs** and **Visuals**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a page">
    Click **Mobs** or **Visuals**. Each setting shows its current value and its default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a value">
    Click the setting and follow the prompt. The change is saved in `config.yml`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mobs

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Damage share** | 0.5 | The share of a mob's damage, 0 to 1, that players must deal for it to pay rewards and money. |
| **Alive limit** | 200 | The most generator mobs alive at once on this server, all generators together. |
| **Reward items** | Drop on the ground | Where a reward list's items go when the mob does not choose: on the ground, or into the inventory. |
| **Nearby radius** | 24 blocks | Within how many blocks of where a mob ends players earn its nearby list. |

## Visuals

Visuals never change health, damage, drops or rewards.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Reactions** | On | Whether mobs react at all. Off keeps every mob vanilla-looking: no entrances, hit sparks, ragdolls, piñata candies or floating numbers. |
| **Number range** | 24 blocks | Within how many blocks damage, healing and hits-left numbers show. |
| **Screen shake** | 1 | How much of each shake plays: 1 as designed, 0 none, up to 3. |
| **Effects at once** | 24 | The most large effects played at once on this server. Past it the next is not drawn, and the skill still lands. |
| **Effect radius** | 64 blocks | Within how many blocks of a mob its skill effects and aura are seen. |

Everything else (the generator timer, auras and skill colours) stays in `config.yml`. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliamobs/configuration).
