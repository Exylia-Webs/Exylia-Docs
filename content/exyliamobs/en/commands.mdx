ExyliaMobs has one command, `/mobsadmin`, also written `/custommobs`. Every form of it needs `exyliamobs.admin`, which operators have by default. Players have no command: they meet the mobs in the world. Mob and generator names complete as you type.

<Commands />

<Callout type="note" title="Remove all reaches every mob this plugin runs">
Without a generator, `killall` and the **Remove all** button remove every mob of ExyliaMobs on this server, including mobs other plugins spawned through its [shared store](/docs/exyliamobs/api).
</Callout>

## What reload does

`/mobsadmin reload` reads `config.yml` and the messages again, gives the mobs their auras, visuals and colours again, restarts the generator timer and reloads the menus. Mobs and generators live in the database and are not read again. The menus also reload with `/exylialib reload`.
