## Is ExyliaMobs free?

No. It is a premium plugin installed as a loader jar that checks the licence on every start. It is not sold
on BuiltByBit: request your key on the [Exylia Discord](https://discord.exylia.net) and put it in
`license_key` in `loader.yml`. See [Installation](/docs/exyliamobs/installation).

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is a hard dependency: without it the server never loads the plugin. ExyliaLib 1.331.0 or
newer is needed too; the loader installs it, or stages a newer one, and asks for a restart — restart once
more. If the console reports a licence error, check the key in `loader.yml`.

## Do players need a resource pack for the models?

No. Every cube of a model is drawn with a block, item or player-head display. See
[3D models](/docs/exyliamobs/models).

## A generator spawns nothing

Check, in order: its status reads **Enabled** (not No area, No mobs or Disabled); a player who is not a
spectator is within its activation radius; it has not reached its max alive; the server has not reached
`max-alive` in `config.yml`; the area has a solid floor with two open blocks above it inside its height, in
loaded chunks; and at least one of its mobs has a weight above `0`. If the console says a spawn was
cancelled, it is a WorldGuard flag. See [Generators](/docs/exyliamobs/generators#worldguard).

## A mob died and paid nothing

A mob killed by health pays only when players dealt at least `min-player-share` of its damage: one killed
mostly by a farm, lava or other mobs pays nothing. A mob in hits mode pays only when its last hit breaks
it, never through `/kill` or the void. A mob whose lifetime ran out pays only its escaped list. See
[When a mob pays](/docs/exyliamobs/rewards#when-a-mob-pays).

## Items did not drop on the ground

In `DROP` mode items come out at the mob only when their player is online on that server and within 32
blocks; otherwise they go to the player instead, and what does not fit is kept for their next join.

## My changes to the menu files were lost

The seven screens are rewritten from the jar on every start. Change their text through `messages.yml`,
which is kept. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliamobs/configuration#messages-and-menus).

## I edited a mob on one server and another server still has the old one

Without Redis, servers read mobs and generators from the database only on start; `/mobsadmin reload` does
not read them again. Turn on Redis in `database.yml` for changes to reach every server at once. See
[On a network](/docs/exyliamobs/generators#on-a-network).

## I deleted a showcase mob and it did not come back

That is on purpose: each showcase model and mob is handed over once. To get a model back, delete its line
in `models/.bundled` and restart. To get a mob back, delete its line in `showcase-mobs.txt` and restart.

## Can another plugin spawn these mobs?

Yes, through ExyliaLib's shared mob store, without depending on ExyliaMobs. ExyliaRewards' vote piñatas
work that way. See [API](/docs/exyliamobs/api).
