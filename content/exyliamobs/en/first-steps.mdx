At the end of this page you have a mob that you built, spawned and killed for a reward, and a generator that keeps it alive in an area. You only use `/mobsadmin` and chat.

## Before you start

Install the plugin and make sure you have `exyliamobs.admin`. See [Install ExyliaMobs](/docs/exyliamobs/installation).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the mob">
    Run `/mobsadmin`, click **Mobs**, then **Create mob**. Type an id in chat, for example `frost-knight`: you use it in commands and it must be unique across your network. Then pick the creature it is. The mob is saved at once and its screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and make it tougher">
    Click **Name** and type what players should read over its head. `%health%` and `%max_health%` stay current. Then click **Attributes** and set its health and damage. A blank value keeps the vanilla one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spawn it">
    Click **Spawn one here**. The mob appears where you stand. Hit it a few times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it pay">
    Click **Rewards**, pick **Top damager** and add a reward, such as an item or money. See [Reward players for kills](/docs/exyliamobs/rewards).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kill it">
    Spawn another one and kill it. The player who dealt the most damage gets the reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it alive in an area">
    Go back to `/mobsadmin`, click **Generators**, then **Create generator** and type an id such as `crypt`. Click **Area** and left-click to mark two corners with the wand. Click **Mobs**, add your mob and leave its weight as it is. Stand near the area and wait: waves appear. See [Keep mobs alive with a generator](/docs/exyliamobs/generators).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

The mob appears where you stand and the chat says it spawned. After you kill it, the top damager receives the reward. Near the generator's area a few mobs appear every 20 seconds, up to 6 alive.

If nothing spawns, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliamobs/troubleshooting).

<Callout type="tip" title="Want something to look at first?">
Run `/mobsadmin spawn stone_golem` to meet a showcase mob. It wears a 3D model with no resource pack.
</Callout>
