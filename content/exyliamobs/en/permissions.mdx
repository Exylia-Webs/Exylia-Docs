ExyliaMobs has one permission. Players need none: they fight custom mobs and are paid by them without any setup.

<Permissions />

Every button of the screens checks the permission itself, so copying a menu button into a menu players can open gives them nothing.

## Give it to staff

With LuckPerms:

```text
/lp group admin permission set exyliamobs.admin true
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Keep it for trusted staff">
`exyliamobs.admin` can delete mobs and generators, remove every custom mob and spawn up to 50 of any mob at once.
</Callout>
