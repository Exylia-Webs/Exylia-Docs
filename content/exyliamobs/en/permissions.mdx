---
title: "Permissions"
description: "The one node the plugin declares and what it gates."
---

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliamobs.admin` | operators | `/mobsadmin` and every subcommand, and every button of its screens. |

Every button checks the node itself, so copying one into a screen players can open hands them nothing.

Players need no permission to fight custom mobs or to be paid by them.
