You end up with a mob that pays the right players the right things. A mob pays through up to ten reward lists, each paid to different players.

## Before you start

Create the mob first. See [Create a mob](/docs/exyliamobs/building-mobs).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open its rewards">
    In the mob's screen click **Rewards**. It asks which list and shows how many rewards each holds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a list">
    Start with **Top damager**. The table below says who each list pays.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add rewards">
    ExyliaLib's reward editor opens: items, commands, money in any currency, experience and effects, each with its chance and conditions. See [Rewards](/docs/exylialib/rewards#editing-on-screen).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where items go">
    Click **Item delivery** and step each list between its default, **Ground** and **Inventory**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The ten lists

| List | Paid to |
|---|---|
| **Top damager** | The player who dealt the most damage. |
| **Second damager**, **Third damager** | The next two down the damage ranking. |
| **Shared by damage** | Every player who hurt it, with money and experience cut to their share of the players' damage. Items and the rest are given whole. |
| **Every hit** | The player behind each hit, as the hit lands. |
| **Last hit** | Whoever dealt the killing or breaking blow. |
| **Everyone** | Every player who hurt it, once each. |
| **Nearby** | Every player within the nearby radius of where it ended, hit or not. Spectators are left out. |
| **Death loot** | Thrown out where it ended, for anyone: items on the ground, experience as orbs. What needs a player (money, commands, rewards with a permission or condition) goes to the top damager. |
| **Escaped** | Every player who hurt it, when it leaves unbeaten because its lifetime ran out. |

The nearby radius is a setting (24 blocks by default), see [Change the shared settings](/docs/exyliamobs/settings).

## When a mob pays

| How it ended | What pays |
|---|---|
| Its lifetime ran out | Only the escaped list. |
| Broken, in hits mode | Every list but escaped. It never pays through `/kill`, the void or anything but the last hit. |
| Killed, by health | Every list but escaped, but only if players dealt at least half of its damage. A mob that a farm, lava or other mobs mostly killed pays nothing. |

The half is the **Damage share** setting (`min-player-share`, 0.5 by default).

## Where items go

Each list's items either burst out where the mob died (**Ground**) or go to the player (**Inventory**). Money, commands and experience always reach the player.

A list on its default follows the mob's mode, and a mob with none follows **Reward items** in the settings (Ground out of the box). The death loot always drops.

In Ground mode, items come out at the mob only when the player is online on that server and within 32 blocks of it. Otherwise they go the Inventory way.

### Rewards kept for later

What a full inventory or an absent player cannot take is kept, not dropped, and handed over on their next join with "Delivered … rewards that were waiting for you." See [Pending rewards](/docs/exylialib/rewards#pending-rewards).

## Boosters

Every list passes through ExyliaLib's modifiers, so boosters multiply the mob's money, experience and items. The source they see is the mob's **Booster source**: `mobs` unless you set one. Give a boss its own source, such as `bosses`, to boost it apart from ordinary mobs. Leave it blank or type `mobs` to go back. See [Modifiers](/docs/exylialib/economy#modifiers).

## Check that it works

Spawn the mob with **Spawn one here**, kill it, and look at what you receive. If you got nothing, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliamobs/troubleshooting#a-mob-died-and-paid-nothing).

<Details title="Technical details">
Ties in damage are broken by player id, so every server ranks alike. The list keys are `top`, `second`, `third`, `shared`, `hit`, `last-hit`, `everyone`, `nearby`, `death` and `escaped`; the API uses `SECOND`, `THIRD`, `SHARED`, `HIT`, `LAST_HIT`, `EVERYONE`, `NEARBY`, `DEATH` and `ESCAPED`, see [API](/docs/exyliamobs/api).
</Details>
