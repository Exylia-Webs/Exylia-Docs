title: "Rewards"
description: "The ten reward lists a mob pays, when it pays at all, where items go, boosters, rewards kept for absent players and kill counters."
---

A mob pays through up to ten reward lists, each paid to different players. **REWARDS** on its screen asks
which list, showing how many rewards each holds, and opens ExyliaLib's reward editor on it: items,
commands, money in any currency, experience and effects, each with its chance and conditions. See
[Rewards](/docs/exylialib/rewards#editing-on-screen) for what a reward can be.

## The lists

| List | Paid to |
|---|---|
| **Top damager** | The player who dealt the most damage. |
| **Second damager**, **Third damager** | The next two down the damage ranking. Equal damage is broken by player id, so every server ranks alike. |
| **Shared by damage** | Every player who hurt it, with money and experience cut to their share of the players' damage. Items and the rest are given whole. |
| **Every hit** | The player behind each hit: every counted hit in hits mode, every damaging hit otherwise. Paid as the hit lands, whatever happens to the mob after. |
| **Last hit** | Whoever dealt the killing or breaking blow. |
| **Everyone** | Every player who hurt it, once each. |
| **Nearby** | Every player within `nearby-radius` blocks of where it ended, hit or not. Spectators are left out. |
| **Death loot** | Thrown out where it ended, for anyone: items land on the ground, experience comes out as orbs. What needs a player (money, commands, rewards with a permission or condition) goes to the top damager. |
| **Escaped** | Every player who hurt it, when it leaves unbeaten because its lifetime ran out. |

## When a mob pays

| How it ended | What pays |
|---|---|
| Its lifetime ran out | Only the escaped list. |
| Broken, in hits mode | Every list but escaped. Hits mode never pays through `/kill`, the void or anything but the last hit. |
| Killed, by health | Every list but escaped, **only if** players dealt at least `min-player-share` of its damage (half, by default). A mob a farm, lava or other mobs mostly killed pays nothing. |

The every-hit list is the exception: it is paid hit by hit, before anyone knows how the mob will end.

## Where items go

Each list's items either **burst out where the mob died** (`DROP`, shown as Ground) or **go to the player**
(`INVENTORY`). Money, commands and experience always reach the player either way.

**ITEM DELIVERY** on the mob's screen lists every list but the death loot (which always drops) with its
current mode. Clicking a list steps it: default → Ground → Inventory → default. A list on the default
follows the mob's mode, and a mob with none follows `reward-mode` in `config.yml` (`DROP` out of the box).

In `DROP`, items come out at the mob only when the player is online, on that server and within 32 blocks of
it; otherwise nothing is left on the ground for nobody, and the items go the `INVENTORY` way.

### Rewards kept for later

The `INVENTORY` way hands rewards over through ExyliaLib's delivery. What a full inventory or an absent
player cannot take is **kept**, not dropped, in `exylia_pending_rewards`, and handed over on their next
join with "Delivered … rewards that were waiting for you." See
[Pending rewards](/docs/exylialib/rewards#pending-rewards).

## Boosters

Every list is passed through ExyliaLib's modifiers before it is given, so boosters multiply the mob's
money, experience and items. The source they see is the mob's **BOOSTER SOURCE**: `mobs` unless set. Give a
boss its own source, such as `bosses`, to boost it apart from ordinary mobs; leave it blank or type `mobs`
to go back to the default. See [Modifiers](/docs/exylialib/economy#modifiers).

## Kill counters

With ExyliaSurvivalCore installed, the top damager of a mob that paid gets two statistics counters raised
by one: `mobs_killed`, and `mobs_killed_<mob id>`. Missions, leaderboards and seasons can read them.

<Callout type="note" title="Show them on the stats screen">
ExyliaSurvivalCore's shipped `mobs_killed` stat reads the counter `kills.mobs`, not the `mobs_killed`
counter ExyliaMobs raises. To rank ExyliaMobs kills, add a stat with `type: COUNTER` and
`counter: 'mobs_killed'` (or `mobs_killed_<mob id>` for one mob). See
[Statistics](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/combat#statistics).
</Callout>
