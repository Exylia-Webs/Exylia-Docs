At the end of this page ExyliaMobs runs and `/mobsadmin` opens the panel.

## Before you start

ExyliaMobs is a premium plugin. It ships through a licence loader, so the file you install is `Exylia-Mobs-Loader.jar`. It is not sold on BuiltByBit: ask for your licence key on the [Exylia Discord](https://discord.exylia.net) and manage your keys and servers at [licenses.exylia.net](https://licenses.exylia.net/).

You need `packetevents` on the server. Without it ExyliaMobs does not load at all. ExyliaLib 1.573.0 or newer is needed too, but the loader installs it for you.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop in the jars">
    Put `Exylia-Mobs-Loader.jar` and `packetevents` in `plugins/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start once, then add your key">
    The loader writes `plugins/ExyliaMobs/loader.yml`. Stop the server, put your key in `license_key` and start again. If ExyliaLib was missing or too old, the loader installs or stages it and asks for a restart: restart once more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it generate its files">
    The plugin writes `config.yml`, its messages and menus, and the three showcase models. The console ends with `ExyliaMobs enabled`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Run `/mobsadmin` as an operator. Three showcase mobs are already there: `stone_golem`, `crawler` and `wisp`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Optional plugins

| Plugin | What it adds |
|---|---|
| ExyliaEconomy or Vault | Money rewards. ExyliaMobs loads after them. |
| PlaceholderAPI | Loaded before ExyliaMobs when present. ExyliaMobs registers no placeholders of its own. |
| ExyliaSurvivalCore | Counts the top damager's kills as statistics counters. See [Reward players for kills](/docs/exyliamobs/rewards#kill-counters). |
| WorldGuard | Not needed, but its spawn flags can block generators. See [Keep mobs alive with a generator](/docs/exyliamobs/generators#worldguard). |

<Callout type="note" title="ExyliaLib is installed for you">
The loader lists ExyliaLib as a soft dependency on purpose: it is the loader that installs it, so it has to start without it.
</Callout>

## Check that it works

The console shows `ExyliaMobs enabled` and `/mobsadmin` opens **Custom mobs**. If it does not, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliamobs/troubleshooting).

## Files it creates

Everything lives in `plugins/ExyliaMobs/`.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `loader.yml` | Your `license_key`, and `version` (`auto` keeps the plugin on the newest release). |
| `config.yml` | What every mob and generator shares. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliamobs/configuration). |
| `database.yml` | Where mobs and generators are stored. Written by ExyliaLib. |
| `lang/<language>/messages.yml` | Every chat line, prompt and editor title. Kept across updates. |
| `lang/<language>/menus/admin/` | The ten screens. Rewritten from the jar on every start. |
| `models/` | The Blockbench models mobs can wear. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Do not edit the menu files">
The files in `lang/<language>/menus/admin/` are replaced from the jar every time the server starts, so your edits are lost. Their header says edits are kept; for this plugin that is not true. Change what the screens say in `messages.yml`.
</Callout>

## Updating

Replace the loader jar and restart, or leave `version: auto` in `loader.yml`. `config.yml` and the messages are kept and new keys are added with their defaults.

<Details title="Technical details">
Mobs and generators are stored in the database `database.yml` points at (H2 in a file by default, or MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL or MongoDB through ExyliaLib): the tables `sc_mob_templates` and `sc_mob_generators`. Rewards a player could not take are kept in `exylia_pending_rewards`. Live mobs are not stored: they are removed on stop and generators spawn fresh ones after the start. The files `models/.bundled` and `showcase-mobs.txt` remember which showcase pieces were already handed over, so one you delete does not come back.
</Details>
