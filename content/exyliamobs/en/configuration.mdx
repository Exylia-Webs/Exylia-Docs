Mobs and generators are edited in game with `/mobsadmin`, not in a file. `config.yml` holds only what every
mob and generator on the server shares. Apply a change with `/mobsadmin reload`.

## Settings: `settings`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `min-player-share` | `0.5` | The share of a mob's damage, `0` to `1`, players have to deal for it to pay. A mob a farm, lava or other mobs mostly killed pays nothing. Not used in hits mode, which pays when broken. |
| `max-alive` | `200` | The most generator mobs alive at once on this server, all generators together. Mobs spawned by hand do not count. |
| `tick-seconds` | `2` | How often the generators are checked, such as `2s`. A bare number is seconds. |
| `reward-mode` | `DROP` | Where a reward list's items go when neither the list nor the mob chooses: `DROP` bursts them out where it died, for anyone to pick up; `INVENTORY` hands them to the list's player. Money, commands and experience reach the player either way. |
| `effect-radius` | `64.0` | Blocks from a mob within which its skill effects and aura are seen. |
| `nearby-radius` | `24.0` | Blocks from where a mob ends within which players earn its nearby list, hit or not. |

## Auras

Auras a mob's look can wear, picked by name on its LOOK button. Each is the
[sequence](/docs/exylialib/sequences) lines of one frame, drawn every other tick at its feet. `%angle%`,
`%angle2%` and `%angle3%` turn a little each frame, a third of a turn apart, so a shape with
`rotate:%angle%` spins around the mob. A mob naming an aura that is not here wears the first one.

```yaml
auras:
  confetti:
    lines:
      - '[CIRCLE] DUST;color:{accent};size:1.2;radius:1.1;points:1;y:1.3;rotate:%angle%'
      - '[CIRCLE] DUST;color:{highlight};size:1.2;radius:1.1;points:1;y:1.3;rotate:%angle2%'
      - '[CIRCLE] DUST;color:{info};size:1.2;radius:1.1;points:1;y:1.3;rotate:%angle3%'
      - '[PARTICLE] DUST;color:{success};size:0.8;count:1;offset:0.6,0.5,0.6;y:1.5'
  sparks:
    lines:
      - '[CIRCLE] FIREWORK;radius:1.0;points:3;y:1.4;rotate:%angle%'
      - '[PARTICLE] END_ROD;count:1;offset:0.5,0.6,0.5;speed:0.01;y:1.4'
  galaxy:
    lines:
      - '[CIRCLE] DUST;color:{primary};size:1.1;radius:1.2;points:2;y:0.3;rotate:%angle%'
      - '[CIRCLE] DUST;color:{secondary};size:1.1;radius:0.9;points:2;y:2.1;rotate:%angle2%'
      - '[CIRCLE] END_ROD;radius:1.2;points:1;y:1.2;rotate:%angle3%'
  firestorm:
    lines:
      - '[CIRCLE] FLAME;radius:0.9;points:2;y:0.2;rotate:%angle%'
      - '[CIRCLE] FLAME;radius:0.7;points:2;y:1.2;rotate:%angle2%'
      - '[PARTICLE] LAVA;count:1;offset:0.4,0.3,0.4;y:1.2'
  waterworks:
    lines:
      - '[CIRCLE] SPLASH;radius:1.0;points:4;y:1.6;rotate:%angle%'
      - '[CIRCLE] BUBBLE_POP;radius:0.8;points:2;y:0.6;rotate:%angle2%'
      - '[PARTICLE] DRIPPING_WATER;count:1;offset:0.5,0.2,0.5;y:2.0'
  ender:
    lines:
      - '[CIRCLE] REVERSE_PORTAL;radius:1.1;points:3;y:0.4;rotate:%angle%'
      - '[CIRCLE] WITCH;radius:0.8;points:1;y:1.8;rotate:%angle2%'
      - '[PARTICLE] PORTAL;count:3;offset:0.4,0.6,0.4;speed:0.3;y:1.0'
```

Add a section to add an aura; it shows up on every mob's LOOK button after a reload.

## Visuals: `visuals`

How mobs react to what happens to them — how they enter, take hits, heal, die and look when nearly done,
and the numbers that float out of them — and the colours their skills are drawn in. Each mob's LOOK button
picks its own reactions and each skill its own style; these are the limits and colours every mob shares.
**Visuals only**: none of this changes health, damage, drops or rewards.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `reactions` | `true` | Whether mobs react at all. `false` keeps every mob vanilla-looking: no entrances, hit sparks, ragdolls, piñata candies or floating numbers. |
| `indicator-range` | `24.0` | Blocks from a mob within which its damage, healing and hits-left numbers show. |
| `shake` | `1.0` | How much of each screen shake plays: `1` as designed, `0` none, up to `3`. |
| `max-casts` | `24` | The most large effects (entrances, deaths and skill styles) mobs play at once on this server. Past it the next one is simply not drawn, and the skill still lands. |

### Skill colours: `visuals.theme`

The colours skill styles are drawn in, by role. Each is a palette token such as `{warning}`, which follows
`colors.yml`, or a `#rrggbb` colour. A skill's own TINT replaces its style's main colour. A blank value draws
the default.

| Key | Default | Drawn for |
|---|---|---|
| `telegraph` | `{warning}` | The warning on the ground before an attack lands. |
| `danger` | `{error}` | What hurts: a meteor's circle, a charge's lane, a barrage. |
| `heal` | `{success}` | Healing. |
| `shield` | `{info}` | Shields and wards. |
| `arcane` | `{primary}` | Summons, blinks and shockwaves that only push. |
| `fire` | `#ff7a1a` | Fire. |
| `frost` | `#9be7ff` | Ice and slowness. |
| `poison` | `#7bc043` | Poison. |
| `shock` | `#8fd3ff` | Lightning. |
| `crit` | `{warning}` | A hard hit landing: a charge's daze. |

## Messages and menus

Every line is in `lang/<language>/messages.yml`: the chat answers, the prompts, the editor titles, the
lore of a mob in a generator, and the words the screens fill in (`menu`, `model`), what each reward list is
called (`payouts`), each model state (`model.states`), each way of dying (`model.deaths`) and each body
(`model.bodies`). Colours are palette tokens, so recolouring the server never means editing it. A message
may open with an effect tag that never reaches the screen: `[sound:NAME|volume|pitch]` plays a sound.
`%prefix%` is replaced by the `prefix` value, `{primary}&lMOBS {letters_black}•` by default.

`new-template-name` is the name a new mob starts with (`%id%` in upper case); `prompt-name` keeps
`%health%` and `%max_health%` as written, for the mob to fill in.

The plugin has no `language` key of its own: it follows `language` in ExyliaLib's `config.yml`, and ships
`en`, `es`, `fr`, `pt`, `ru` and `tr`. See [Languages](/docs/exylialib/library-config#languages).

The seven screens are under `lang/<language>/menus/admin/` and are **replaced from the jar on every start**,
so edits to them do not last. Change their words through `messages.yml`.
