Short answers to what server owners ask most. For step-by-step fixes see [Common problems](/docs/exyliamarket/troubleshooting).

## Nothing can be listed or bought

There is no economy. Install ExyliaEconomy, or Vault with an economy plugin; the console says which module is waiting for one on every start. See [Installation](/docs/exyliamarket/installation) and ExyliaLib's [economy](/docs/exylialib/economy) page.

## A player says an item disappeared

Look in `/collect`: anything the market could not hand over (an expired listing, an auction won while away, a full inventory, a cancelled trade) waits there and is never dropped. Items delivered into a buy order wait in `/orders mine` and appear in `/collect` too. Staff can trace any deal with `/marketadmin log <player>`. See [Collect and notices](/docs/exyliamarket/collect).

## Why does an item sell for less than worth.yml says?

The shop wins when it also buys that item, at its own price. Nothing sells for more than the shop charges for it or than crafting it from shop items costs, and the shop's sell limit may be used up. Run `/selladmin` to see where the shop and the worth list disagree. See [Selling and worth](/docs/exyliamarket/selling).

## Players got autosell without running /autosell

Autosell starts **on**. Giving a rank `exyliamarket.sellwands.autosell` turns it on for them at once; each player can turn it off with `/autosell` or in `/settings`. See [Give sell wands and autosell](/docs/exyliamarket/sell-wands).

## Shop edits on one server do not show on another

Turn Redis on in `database.yml` on every server. Without it, the other servers only read the catalogue again when they restart; stock and limits are safe either way. See [Shop](/docs/exyliamarket/shop).

## One server of the network should have its own market

Set `network-group: survival` in ExyliaLib's `config.yml` on both survival servers and `network-group: fullpvp` on the other (or `network.group` in this plugin's `config.yml`), then restart. Each module can also be `global` or in a group of its own, and what was written before stays with the old group. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliamarket/configuration#network).

## A shop should only be sold on some servers

Open the shop in `/shopadmin`, then **Placement** and **Servers**, and type their `server-id`s, or right-click for this server only. To deliver a rank bought on the hub on survival, set the product's **Delivery** server instead. See [Shop](/docs/exyliamarket/shop).

## A rare shop item never runs out

A product whose stock is full when its restock is due is skipped without moving its next restock, so every purchase is refilled within a minute. Until that is fixed, use per-player limits for rare items. See [Shop](/docs/exyliamarket/shop#known-issues).

## Can I change the menus?

The player screens, yes: they are under `lang/<code>/` and a release keeps your edits. The staff screens under `menus/admin/` are rewritten on every start. `/marketadmin reload` applies an edit. See [Edit menus](/docs/exylialib/edit-menus).

## How do I wipe the market or reset a player?

With ExyliaLib: `/exylialib wipe ExyliaMarket <table|*>` and `/exylialib resetplayer <player>`. The market has no wipe command of its own. See [Administration](/docs/exyliamarket/administration#wiping-and-resetting).

## Do I need PlaceholderAPI?

Only to show the placeholders in other plugins; Exylia plugins read them without it. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliamarket/placeholders).
