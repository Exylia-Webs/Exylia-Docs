## Nothing can be listed or bought

There is no economy. Install ExyliaEconomy, or Vault with an economy plugin; the console says which module
is waiting for one on every start.

## A player says an item disappeared

Look in `/collect`. Anything the market could not hand over — an expired listing, a won auction while away,
an inventory that was full, a trade that was called off while they were offline — waits there and is never
dropped. Items delivered into a buy order wait on the order, in `/orders mine`, and appear in `/collect` too.
Staff can trace any deal with `/marketadmin log <player>`.

## Why does an item sell for less than worth.yml says?

Three things hold a sale price down. The shop wins when it also buys that item, at its own price. Nothing
sells for as much as the shop charges for it, or as much as crafting it from shop items costs. And the shop's
sell limit may be used up. Run `/selladmin` to see where the shop and the worth list disagree.

## Players got autosell without running /autosell

Autosell's switch starts **on**. Giving a rank `exyliamarket.sellwands.autosell` turns it on for them at once;
each player can turn it off with `/autosell` or in `/settings`.

## Shop edits on one server do not show on another

Turn Redis on in `database.yml` on every server. Without it, the other servers only read the catalogue again
when they restart. Stock and limits are safe either way.

## A rare shop item never runs out

A product whose stock is full when its restock is due is skipped without moving its next restock, so after
the first quiet period every purchase is refilled within a minute. Until that is fixed, rely on per-player
limits for rare items. See [Shop](/docs/exyliamarket/shop#known-issues).

## Can I change the menus?

The player screens, yes: they are under `lang/<code>/`, and a release keeps your edits. The staff screens
under `menus/admin/` are rewritten on every start. `/marketadmin reload` applies a menu edit.

## Do I need PlaceholderAPI?

Only to show the placeholders in other plugins. Exylia plugins read them without it.

## I used ExyliaSurvivalCore's market. What carries over?

There is no importer. The auction house, auctions, orders and shop tables keep the `sc_` names SurvivalCore
used, and sell wands made by SurvivalCore keep working. Check what your database holds before going live.

## Where do I get help?

On the [Exylia Discord](https://discord.exylia.net), where the licence is requested too.
