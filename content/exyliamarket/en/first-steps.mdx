At the end of this page you have sold one item on the ExyliaMarket auction house to a second account and seen the money arrive. Every module is on by default, so there is nothing to switch on first.

## Before you start

- ExyliaMarket is installed, see [Installation](/docs/exyliamarket/installation).
- The server has an economy (ExyliaEconomy, or Vault with an economy plugin). Without one nothing can be listed or bought. See [ExyliaLib economy](/docs/exylialib/economy).
- You have two accounts, and the second one is not an operator, so you test what players really see.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold something to sell">
    On the first account, hold a stack in your main hand, for example 16 cobblestone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the listing">
    Run `/ah sell 100`. The whole stack is listed at 100. The review screen opens, and nothing is charged or taken yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the review">
    The screen shows the listing fee, the sales tax and what you will receive. By default the fee is 2% (charged now) and the tax is 5% (taken from the sale). Click **Price** to change the price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it up">
    Click **Put it up**. The stack leaves your inventory and the listing appears on the board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy it from the other account">
    On the second account, run `/ah`, left-click the listing and click **Buy it**. The item goes to the buyer's inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the seller was paid">
    On the first account the money has arrived, minus the tax. The seller is told on whichever server they are, or on their next join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

- `/ah` shows your listing while it is up, and `/ah mine` lists your own.
- After the sale the first account's balance went up by the price minus the 5% tax. The 2% fee was paid when the listing went up and is never refunded.
- Anything that could not be handed over, such as an item bought with a full inventory, waits in `/collect`.

If nothing opens or you get "no permission" or a disabled message, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliamarket/troubleshooting).

## What to decide next

- **Fees and taxes**: each module has them in `/marketadmin`, and ranks can have their own with [numbered permissions](/docs/exyliamarket/permissions#numbered-nodes).
- **Who gets autosell**: `exyliamarket.sellwands.autosell` is for operators only by default.
- **The default shop**: `/shop` is already filled on a fresh database. Run `/selladmin` once your prices are final, it lists prices that would let players earn money by buying and selling back.
- **More than one server**: one database and a name per server, see [Installation](/docs/exyliamarket/installation#a-network).
