title: "First steps"
description: "From a fresh install to a first listing, a first buy order, a first sale to the shop and a first trade, and the settings worth looking at on day one."
---

This assumes the plugin is installed with an economy, as on [Installation](/docs/exyliamarket/installation),
and that you are testing with two accounts, one of them without operator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the shop">
    Run `/shop`. On an empty database the default shop is already there: 17 categories, ready to buy and
    sell. Hold any ore and run `/worth` to see what it sells for, and notice the line under the item in your
    inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell something">
    Run `/sell`, drop a few items into the window, watch the total and press **Confirm**. `/sell all` does
    the same for your whole inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List on the auction house">
    Hold a stack and run `/ah sell 100`. The review screen shows the fee, the tax and what you will
    receive; press **Confirm**. From the other account, `/ah` and buy it. The seller is told and paid at
    once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a buy order">
    Run `/orders`, **New order**, pick an item, an amount and a price each, then **Confirm**. From the other
    account, carry some of that item, open the order and confirm the fill. Back on the first account,
    `/orders mine` collects what was delivered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try the bazaar">
    `/bazaar diamond`: place a sell offer from one account and buy it instantly from the other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade">
    Sneak and right-click the other account, or run `/trade <player>`. Put items in, both accept, and the
    swap happens after the countdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    `/marketadmin` shows every module and its state. Click a module to change its settings in game.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to decide on day one

| Question | Where |
|---|---|
| Which modules does this server want? | `modules` in `config.yml`, then restart. |
| Who gets autosell? | `exyliamarket.sellwands.autosell` is operators-only by default. |
| How big are fees and taxes? | Each module's **Fees & tax** page in `/marketadmin`. |
| How many listings, auctions, orders and offers per rank? | The numbered nodes on [Permissions](/docs/exyliamarket/permissions#numbered-nodes). |
| Does the default shop suit the economy? | `/shopadmin`, or `example-catalogue: false` before the first start for an empty shop. |
| Is there more than one server? | One database, Redis on, a name per server: [Installation](/docs/exyliamarket/installation#a-network). |
| Should big deals reach Discord? | `discord.yml`: [Administration](/docs/exyliamarket/administration#discord). |

Run `/selladmin` once the shop and the worth list are as you want them: it lists every price that would let
players print money by buying and selling back, or by crafting.
