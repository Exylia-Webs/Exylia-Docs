The bazaar is a two-sided order book for each product. Players place **buy orders** (their money is held) and **sell offers** (their items are held) at a price per unit, or buy and sell **instantly** against the best of them. The command is `/bazaar` (also `/bz`) and the module is on by default.

## Before you start

The server needs an economy, see [ExyliaLib economy](/docs/exylialib/economy). The bazaar trades in one currency (the server default unless you set `currency`), and products are plain materials chosen by staff.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a product">
    Run `/bazaar` for the front with its categories, or `/bazaar diamond` to go straight to a product. The product screen shows the best buy and sell prices, the top prices on each side with how many units wait at each, the last price, average and trend, and how many you carry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade at once">
    Click **Buy now** to buy from the cheapest sell offers (it starts at 64 units), or **Sell now** to sell into the highest buy orders (it starts at what you carry). The review shows how many units are available, the total, the average and the worst price it reaches, and for a sale the tax and what you receive. Use **Change amount** to adjust it, then click the confirm button (for example "Buy 64").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or leave an order or offer">
    Click **Buy order** or **Sell offer**, choose a price (**Beat the best** by one step, **Match the best**, or **Your price**), set the amount, then click **Place buy order** or **Place sell offer**. A buy order holds the money for all of it, a sell offer holds the items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

- `/bazaar mine` (the **My orders** tab) lists your open orders and offers with how much has filled.
- On a second account, place the opposite side at a matching price. Both fill at once, and the seller is paid minus the tax.
- When an order of yours fills while you are away, a sell offer pays you at once and a buy order's items wait in [`/collect`](/docs/exyliamarket/collect). You hear about it on any server.

## Going further

### How orders match

- The best price trades first, and among equal prices the oldest.
- **Every fill happens at the price of the order that was waiting.** A buy order placed above the cheapest offer buys at the offer's price and is refunded the difference.
- A new order first fills against everything its price crosses, and only what is left waits in the book.
- Your own orders and offers never fill each other.
- If the book moved between the review and your click, fewer units trade, never at a worse price than the review showed. Unspent money is refunded and unsold items handed back.

Instant trades need `exyliamarket.bazaar.instant`, orders and offers `exyliamarket.bazaar.create`. Everyone has both unless you deny them.

### Tax, fee and limits

| What | Who pays | When |
|---|---|---|
| Tax, 1.25% | The seller, out of what they receive. Buyers pay no tax. | Every fill. `exyliamarket.bazaar.tax.<n>` sets it per rank, the lowest winning. |
| Flat fee, 0 | Whoever places an order or offer. | When it is placed, even if it fills at once. Not refunded. Instant trades pay none. |

- **Open orders and offers**: 14 together by default, or `exyliamarket.bazaar.limit.<n>`.
- **Units**: one order, offer or instant trade moves at most 2304.
- **Price**: at least the currency's smallest amount per unit, at most `max-unit-price` (no ceiling by default).
- **Daily volume**: money traded per day (UTC), unlimited by default, or `exyliamarket.bazaar.volume.<n>`.

<Callout type="warning" title="How the daily volume is counted">
The limit is checked before a trade: the trade that crosses it still goes through, and an order already waiting in the book keeps filling. `-1` turns the limit off, and `0` blocks every bazaar trade.
</Callout>

### Expiry and cancelling

Orders and offers last 168 hours (a week, `offer-hours`; `0` keeps them forever), checked every minute.

| What ends | Buy order | Sell offer |
|---|---|---|
| It expires | Money left is refunded to the balance. | Items left go to `/collect`. |
| You cancel it (right-click in **My orders**, then confirm) | Refunded to the balance. | Into your inventory, the rest to `/collect`. |
| Staff cancel it | Refunded to the balance. | To `/collect`. |

<Callout type="note" title="The expiry message says /collect for both">
The shipped line for an expired order reads "waiting in /collect" whichever side it was. For a buy order the money is on the balance, not in `/collect`.
</Callout>

### Products and categories

On the first start with no products the shipped catalogue is added: 227 products in seven categories (mining 55, farming 42, mobs 33, woods 26, blocks 36, fishing 9, oddities 26). Materials your server version does not know are skipped. Categories and their icons are `categories` in `config.yml` and their names are in the bazaar's `messages.yml`. A product in a category that is no longer configured is not on any category screen, but `/bazaar <item>` still opens it.

```yaml title="modules/bazaar/config.yml"
categories:
  mining:
    icon: DIAMOND_PICKAXE
  farming:
    icon: GOLDEN_HOE
```

### The products editor

`/bazaaradmin` (also `/bzadmin`, needs `exyliamarket.bazaar.admin`) opens the staff hub with **Products** and **Orders and offers**. In the products editor:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Put items down** | Adds a product for every plain material among the items you place in the window, then gives them back. |
| **Import my inventory** | The same, from your inventory. |
| **Load default catalogue** | Adds whatever of the shipped 227 is missing, never twice. |
| A row | Sets that product's category and position. |

Only plain, stackable materials can be products. Nothing changes until you save, and a product removed from the list is deleted. Orders on a removed product stay until they are cancelled or expire. The offers list cancels any order or offer with a right-click and a confirmation, and `/bazaaradmin cancel <id>` does it by id.

## Settings

<Details title="Settings: modules/bazaar/config.yml">

Under `settings:`, also editable in game from `/marketadmin`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `currency` | `''` | The currency the bazaar trades in. Empty is the server's default currency. |
| `tax-percent` | `1.25` | Taken from what a seller is paid. |
| `flat-fee` | `0.0` | Charged per order or offer placed. |
| `max-offers-per-player` | `14` | Open orders and offers per player. |
| `daily-volume` | `-1` | Money one player may trade per day. `-1` for no limit. |
| `max-amount` | `2304` | Most units one order, offer or instant trade may move. |
| `max-unit-price` | `-1.0` | Highest price per unit. `-1` for no ceiling. |
| `price-step` | `0.1` | How far **Beat the best** moves past the best price. Never less than the currency's smallest amount. |
| `offer-hours` | `168` | How long an order or offer stays. `0` for forever. |
| `levels-shown` | `5` | Prices of each side the product screen lists. |
| `toast-frame` | `TASK` | The toast when an order of yours fills: `TASK`, `GOAL`, `CHALLENGE` or `NONE`. |

With nothing on a side, the other side's best price is used as the starting price, then the 7-day average. Bazaar prices are also [placeholders](/docs/exyliamarket/placeholders#bazaar-prices).

</Details>
