The bazaar is a two-sided order book for each product. Players place **buy orders** (their money is held)
and **sell offers** (their items are held) at a price per unit, or buy and sell **instantly** against the
best of them. Products are plain materials, chosen by staff and filed in categories.

Module id `bazaar`, file `modules/bazaar/config.yml`, command `/bazaar` (also `/bz`).

## Using it

`/bazaar` opens the front with its categories; `/bazaar <item>` goes straight to one product, such as
`/bazaar diamond`. `/bazaar mine` lists your open orders and offers.

A product's screen shows the best buy and sell prices and the spread, the top prices on each side with how
many units wait at each (`levels-shown`, 5 by default), the last price, average and trend from the
[price index](/docs/exyliamarket/prices), how many you carry, and a button for the 7-day price chart. It
updates while it is open.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Instant buy** | Buys now from the cheapest sell offers. Starts at 64 units. |
| **Instant sell** | Sells now into the highest buy orders. Starts at what you carry. |
| **Buy order** | Places an order at your price; money for all of it is held. |
| **Sell offer** | Places an offer at your price; the items are held. |

Each opens a review first. An instant trade shows how many units are available, the total, the average and
the worst price it reaches, and for a sale the tax and the payout. If the book moved between the review and
**Confirm**, fewer units trade, never at a worse price than the review showed: unspent money is refunded
and unsold items handed back.

An order or offer starts at **best + step**: one `price-step` past the best price on its side. **Match**
uses the best price exactly. With nothing on that side the other side's best price is used, then the 7-day
average.

## How orders match

- The best price trades first, and among equal prices the oldest.
- **Every fill happens at the price of the order that was waiting.** A buy order placed above the cheapest
  offer buys at the offer's price and is refunded the difference.
- A new order first fills against everything its price crosses; only what is left waits in the book.
- Your own orders and offers never fill each other.

When an order of yours fills while you are away, a sell offer pays you at once, minus tax, and a buy order's
items go to [`/collect`](/docs/exyliamarket/collect). You hear about it on any server, with a toast framed by
`toast-frame`.

## Money

| What | Who pays | When |
|---|---|---|
| `tax-percent` (1.25% by default) | The seller, out of what they receive | Every fill. Buyers pay no tax. |
| `flat-fee` (0 by default) | Whoever places an order or offer | When it is placed, even if it fills at once. Not refunded. Instant trades pay none. |

### Limits

- **Open orders and offers**: `max-offers-per-player` together, or the player's
  `exyliamarket.bazaar.limit.<n>`. Checked before placing, even an order that would fill at once.
- **Units**: one order, offer or instant trade moves at most `max-amount`.
- **Price**: at least the currency's smallest amount per unit, at most `max-unit-price`.
- **Daily volume**: money traded per day, bought and sold together, `daily-volume` or the player's
  `exyliamarket.bazaar.volume.<n>`. Days are UTC, so it resets at 00:00 UTC.

<Callout type="warning" title="How the daily volume is counted">
The limit is checked **before** a trade: the trade that crosses it still goes through, and an order already
waiting in the book keeps filling. Each fill adds its value in whole currency units to both sides.
`-1` turns the limit off; **`0` blocks every bazaar trade**, since any volume is at the limit.
</Callout>

## Expiry and cancelling

Orders and offers last `offer-hours` (168, a week, by default; `0` keeps them forever), checked every minute.

| What ends | Buy order | Sell offer |
|---|---|---|
| It expires | Money left is refunded to the balance | Items left go to `/collect` |
| You cancel it (right-click in `/bazaar mine`, then confirm) | Refunded to the balance | Into your inventory, the rest to `/collect` |
| Staff cancel it | Refunded to the balance | To `/collect` |

<Callout type="note" title="The expiry message says /collect for both">
The shipped line for an expired order reads "… is waiting in /collect" whichever side it was. For a buy
order the money is on the balance, not in `/collect`.
</Callout>

## Products and categories

The shipped catalogue is filled in the first time the products table is empty: **227 products** in seven
categories — mining 55, farming 42, mobs 33, woods 26, blocks 36, fishing 9, oddities 26. Materials the
server's version does not know are skipped.

Categories and their icons are in `config.yml`; their names are in the bazaar's `messages.yml` under
`categories`. A product in a category that is no longer configured is not shown on any category screen,
but `/bazaar <item>` still opens it.

```yaml title="modules/bazaar/config.yml"
categories:
  mining:
    icon: DIAMOND_PICKAXE
  farming:
    icon: GOLDEN_HOE
```

### The products editor

`/bazaaradmin` (also `/bzadmin`) opens the staff hub: the products editor and every open order and offer.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add** | Pick one material from a search. Only plain, stackable materials can be products. |
| **Put items down** | Adds a product for every plain material among the items you place in the window, then gives them back. |
| **Import my inventory** | The same, from your inventory. |
| **Load default catalogue** | Adds whatever of the shipped 227 is missing, never twice. |
| A row | Sets that product's category and position. |
| **Save** | Writes the list. Nothing changes until then; a product removed from the list is deleted. |

New products land in the shipped catalogue's category when they are part of it, otherwise in the first
configured category. Orders on a removed product stay until they are cancelled or expire.

The offers list cancels any order or offer with a right-click and a confirmation, and
`/bazaaradmin cancel <id>` does it by id. Everything here needs `exyliamarket.bazaar.admin`.

## Settings

Under `settings:` in `modules/bazaar/config.yml`, and editable in game from `/marketadmin`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `currency` | `''` | The currency the bazaar trades in. Empty is the server's default currency. |
| `tax-percent` | `1.25` | Taken from what a seller is paid. |
| `flat-fee` | `0.0` | Charged per order or offer placed. |
| `max-offers-per-player` | `14` | Open orders and offers per player. |
| `daily-volume` | `-1` | Money one player may trade per day. `-1` for no limit. |
| `max-amount` | `2304` | Most units one order, offer or instant trade may move. |
| `max-unit-price` | `-1.0` | Highest price per unit. `-1` for no ceiling. |
| `price-step` | `0.1` | How far **best + step** moves past the best price. Never less than the currency's smallest amount. |
| `offer-hours` | `168` | How long an order or offer stays. `0` for forever. |
| `levels-shown` | `5` | Prices of each side the product screen lists. |
| `toast-frame` | `TASK` | The toast when an order of yours fills: `TASK`, `GOAL`, `CHALLENGE` or `NONE`. |

Bazaar prices are also [placeholders](/docs/exyliamarket/placeholders#bazaar-prices).
