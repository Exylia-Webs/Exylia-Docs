Every placeholder below is written in full, and clicking one copies it.

## How the name is written

The plugin registers its placeholders through ExyliaLib. PlaceholderAPI reaches them with the plugin's name
in front, so a scoreboard or a hologram asks for:

```text
%exyliamarket_auctionhouse_listings%
```

That is the form this page documents. Inside an Exylia plugin's own files — menus, messages, another Exylia
plugin's scoreboard — the registered name also stands on its own: `%auctionhouse_listings%`. The price and
bazaar placeholders are registered with `exyliamarket` already in their name, so they are written the same
both ways.

No expansion needs downloading. Without PlaceholderAPI installed only the Exylia form works. A module that
is switched off registers none of its placeholders.

## Auction house

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliamarket_auctionhouse_listings%` | Listings up right now, network-wide. |
| `%exyliamarket_auctionhouse_value%` | What every listing up adds up to, as plain money. |
| `%exyliamarket_auctionhouse_mine%` | Listings the reader has up. Needs a player. |

## Auctions

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliamarket_auctions_running%` | Auctions running right now. |
| `%exyliamarket_auctions_mine%` | Auctions the reader is running. Needs a player. |
| `%exyliamarket_auctions_leading%` | Auctions the reader holds the top bid on. Needs a player. |

## Buy orders

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliamarket_orders_mine%` | The reader's open orders. Needs a player. |
| `%exyliamarket_orders_ready%` | Items delivered into the reader's orders and waiting to be collected. Needs a player. |
| `%exyliamarket_orders_open_total%` | Open orders on the whole market. |

## Bazaar prices

The material follows the name, in any case, underscores included: `diamond`, `iron_ingot`. A material that
does not exist, or one with nothing on that side of the book, answers the `none` word of the bazaar's
`messages.yml`.

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliamarket_bazaar_buy_<item>%` | What one costs to buy right now: the lowest sell offer. |
| `%exyliamarket_bazaar_sell_<item>%` | What one sells for right now: the highest buy order. |
| `%exyliamarket_bazaar_spread_<item>%` | The gap between the two. |

## Market prices

What players actually paid each other for a plain item over the auction house, auctions, buy orders and
the bazaar. The shop is not part of it. See [Prices](/docs/exyliamarket/prices).

The material follows the name. Add `:<currency>` for a currency other than the default one:
`%exyliamarket_price_diamond:gems%`. With no sale of it in the last seven days the answer is `—`.

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliamarket_price_<item>%` | What one went for in the latest sale. |
| `%exyliamarket_avg7d_<item>%` | The 7-day average per item, weighted by items. |
| `%exyliamarket_trend_<item>%` | `▲`, `▼` or `▬`: the last seven days against the seven before, moving more than 5%. |

<Callout type="note" title="Figures follow a refresh">
The price index is worked out again every `prices.refresh-seconds` (60 by default) from every server's
rows, so a sale shows up in these placeholders one refresh later, on every server alike. The bazaar and
auction counts are live.
</Callout>

## Inside menus

The review and price-chart screens fill values of their own — `%market_average%`, `%market_suggested%`,
`%price_low%` and the like — that only those screens resolve. They are listed with each screen on
[Prices](/docs/exyliamarket/prices#on-the-screens).
