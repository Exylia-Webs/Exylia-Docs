The server's own shop: categories of products with a buy price, a sell price or both. The whole catalogue
lives in the database and is edited from the game — there is no shop file to write. Every server on the
same database sells the same catalogue.

Module id `shop`, file `modules/shop/config.yml`, commands `/shop` and `/shopadmin` (also `/shopa`).

## For players

`/shop` opens the front: one button per category, and a **Sell** button that opens the
[`/sell` window](/docs/exyliamarket/selling) on a left-click and sells everything on a right-click.
`/shop <category>` opens a category straight away by its id.

A category without the player's permission is hidden, or drawn locked when `hide-locked-categories` is off.
Inside one, each product shows its buy and sell price, whether the price is moving, its discount, stock, how
many more you may buy or sell this period, and how many you carry.

| Click | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left | Buy: opens the amount screen. |
| Right | Sell: opens the amount screen. |
| Shift + left / right | Buys or sells `shift-click-amount` units at once. |

The amount screen has ±1, ±16, ±64, **1** and **Max** buttons. The amount stops at `max-amount`, the stock,
what your limit leaves, what you can pay and what fits in your inventory; for selling, at what you carry and
your sell allowance. **Confirm** only appears when the trade can happen.

- **Buying** checks that everything fits **before** any money is taken.
- **Selling** takes matching items from your inventory (not armour or off hand), then pays. Selling a
  product that has stock adds it back, up to its maximum.

<Callout type="note" title="A unit is the product's whole stack">
A product's price is for the item as it was set up, amount included. A product made from 16 arrows sells
16 at a time, and players' items are matched exactly — name, lore and enchantments — with only the count
ignored.
</Callout>

## Products

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Type | **Item**, or **Command**: console commands run on purchase, with `%player%`. Command products are bought one at a time and never sold back. |
| Item, name, description | What is sold and how it is shown. A blank name uses the item's own. |
| Buy price, sell price | Either may be off. Off on both sides shows "Not on sale right now". |
| Currency | Any currency of the server. |
| Pricing | **Fixed**, **By demand** or **Random**. See below. |
| Stock and restock | `-1` is unlimited. Stock is shared by the whole network and refilled **to the maximum** every restock period. |
| Buy limit, sell limit, period | Units each player may buy and sell per period. The period starts with the player's first trade of it. |
| Permission | A node to buy or sell it. Shop admins always pass. |
| Discount | A percentage off the buy price, until a date. |
| Enabled, page, position | Whether players see it, and where. |

### How prices move

| Pricing | How |
|---|---|
| **Fixed** | Always the set price. |
| **By demand** | Every unit bought adds `demand.step-percent` of the base price, every unit sold takes it away, between `floor-percent` and `ceiling-percent` of base. With nobody trading it drifts back by `decay-percent-per-hour` points an hour. A batch is priced unit by unit. |
| **Random** | A new draw every `random.reset-hours`, up to `spread-percent` away from base. The same on every server. |

Draws and rotations follow UTC: a 6-hour draw changes at 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC.

### What the sell price is held to

- **Discounts** apply to the buy price only. A product's and its category's discount do not add up: the
  larger one wins.
- The **sell bonus** (`exyliamarket.shop.sell.<multiplier>`) and `shop-sell` boosters raise what selling
  pays. See [Selling](/docs/exyliamarket/selling#sell-bonus).
- Selling never pays as much as buying the same units back costs right now, nor as much as crafting the
  item from things the shop sells. The price is held one step below both, so the shop cannot be farmed.

## Categories

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Id | Lower-case, fixed once created. What `/shop <category>` takes. |
| Name, description, icon | How it is drawn on the front. |
| Position | Its slot on the front: 21 places, three rows of seven. |
| Pages | 1 to 10, each with 45 product slots. |
| Permission | A node to open it. Blank is everybody. |
| Currency | The currency of products **created later** in it. |
| Discount | Off the buy price of every product in it, until a date. |
| Rotation | Shows only a few of its products at a time — `rotation size` of them, drawn again every period. |
| Enabled | Whether players see it. |

<Callout type="warning" title="21 categories at most">
The front has 21 places. Categories beyond that are not drawn, in `/shop` or in the editor, and nothing
warns about it.
</Callout>

## The editor

`/shopadmin` opens the categories exactly where `/shop` draws them.

- **Click** a category to edit it; **shift-click** picks it up, and the next click drops it on an empty
  place, swaps it with another, or puts it back.
- **Create** asks for an id. **Load default shop**: left-click adds what is missing, right-click replaces
  everything (see below).

A category's screen has **Enabled**, **General** (name, description, icon, pages), **Sales** (currency,
discount and its duration, permission), **Rotation**, **Products**, **Player view** and **Delete**, which
deletes every product in it after a confirmation.

In the products grid, **holding an item on the cursor and clicking an empty slot creates a product** from
it, both prices off. The command block creates a command product. Click a product to edit it, shift-click
to move it, the page arrows carry it to another page. A product's screen has **Enabled**, **General** (item,
name, description, position), **Prices** (buy, sell, currency, pricing, discount), **Stock** (setting it
fills it), **Limits** (buy, sell, period, permission), **Commands** and **Delete**.

## The default catalogue

On a shop with no categories and no products, and with `example-catalogue` on, the plugin writes its
default shop: **17 categories and 402 products**, plus a **Premium** category of 6 products priced in the
first currency that is not the default, when the server has one. Materials the server's version does not
know are skipped.

| Category | Notes |
|---|---|
| blocks, wood, colours, ores, farming, food, mobs, armoury, redstone, brewing, nether, end, miscellaneous | Raw materials sell for their value and buy for three times it; crafted gear is buy only. Farming crops price **by demand**. |
| perks | Four command products behind `exyliamarket.shop.perks`. |
| daily-deals | Rotates 7 of 16 bulk lots a day, 20% off for 365 days from the install. |
| enchanter | Rotates 6 of 17 books every 12 hours, priced at random. |
| black-market | Small stock, restock timers and per-player limits. |

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/shopadmin defaults add` | Adds whatever of the default shop and worth list is missing. Never changes an existing row. |
| `/shopadmin defaults replace` | Backs up the shop and `worth.yml` to `backups/shop-<date>/`, then replaces both with the defaults. A player is asked to confirm; the console is not. If the backup fails, nothing changes. |
| `/shopadmin log <player> [rows]` | What a player bought and sold, newest first, 10 rows by default and 50 at most. |

`exyliamarket.shop.perks` is not declared in `plugin.yml`, so it is operators-only until a rank is given it.

## A network

Every server keeps the catalogue in memory. With **Redis** on, an edit made on one server reaches the others
at once; **without Redis, other servers only see an edit after their next restart**. Stock is safe either
way: a purchase only completes once the database has taken the change. Each player's limits are read when
they join and merged with what the server they came from wrote.

## Settings

`modules/shop/config.yml`. The catalogue is not here.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.hide-locked-categories` | `true` | Hide a category the player has no permission for, or draw it locked. |
| `settings.shift-click-amount` | `1` | Units a shift-click buys or sells. |
| `settings.max-amount` | `2304` | Most units the amount screen or a shift-click buys at once. |
| `settings.example-catalogue` | `true` | Fill an empty shop with the default catalogue. |
| `demand.step-percent` | `1.0` | Percent of base one unit moves the price. |
| `demand.floor-percent` | `50.0` | Lowest the price may drift to, as a percent of base. |
| `demand.ceiling-percent` | `200.0` | Highest it may drift to. |
| `demand.decay-percent-per-hour` | `5.0` | Points it recovers per hour with nobody trading. |
| `random.spread-percent` | `25.0` | How far from base a draw may land. |
| `random.reset-hours` | `6` | How long one draw lasts. |
| `sounds.bought`, `sounds.sold`, `sounds.failed` | | `NAME\|volume\|pitch`. |

## Known issues

<Callout type="warning" title="Limited stock refills too often">
A product whose stock is full when its restock comes due is skipped without its next restock being moved.
From then on any purchase is refilled within a minute, so rare items with small stock — the default
black market's — are held back only by each player's limit.
</Callout>

- The front shows a rotating category's whole pool as its product count, not the rotation size.
- Chat counts items (units × the product's stack), `/shopadmin log` counts units.
- Two admins editing the same category at once: the last save wins the whole row.
- `/shopadmin defaults replace` keeps players' limit counters: the default products get the same ids again.
