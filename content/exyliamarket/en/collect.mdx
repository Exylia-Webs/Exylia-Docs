Most deals finish while somebody is elsewhere: a listing sells overnight, an auction ends while the winner
is on another server, a box does not fit. Nothing the market owes a player is ever dropped on the floor.

## Where things go

| Who | Items | Money |
|---|---|---|
| Online on this server, with room | Into the inventory. | To the balance. |
| Online, inventory full | What fits, the rest to `/collect`. | To the balance. |
| Dead, leaving, offline or on another server | All to `/collect`. | To the balance, by id. |

Money always goes straight to the balance. Two things keep their items elsewhere:

- **Buy orders** keep delivered items on the order; `/collect` lists them too.
- **Bazaar buy orders** that fill put their items in `/collect` directly.

## /collect

`/collect` opens one screen of everything waiting: each delivery — an expired listing, a won auction, what
did not fit, a sell wand from staff — and every buy order with items ready. It needs no permission.

- Click a delivery to claim it, or **Claim all**, oldest first.
- A claim takes only what fits. What does not stays as one delivery, never split or dropped.
- Each delivery shows how long it has waited and, for an order, where its items came from.

Nothing is handed over on join any more. A player is told on join how many deliveries wait, and claims them
when they have room.

## Notices

A player is told about their own deals wherever they are: at once on this server, on the server they are on
in a network, or on their next join, exactly once.

Missed notices are not replayed line by line. On join they are summed up in one line that opens
`/collect` — "sold 3 listings for $45k · 1 order filled" — followed by how many deliveries wait. The parts
and their words are in `messages.yml` under `notices`.

These are not the announcement channels: muting the auction house or auctions in `/settings` silences other
people's deals, never a player's own.

## Feedback

Each is switched under `feedback` in `config.yml` and worded under `feedback` in `messages.yml`.

| Key | Default | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `toasts.auction-house-sold` | `GOAL` | A toast when a listing of yours sells. |
| `toasts.auctions-won` | `CHALLENGE` | When you win an auction. |
| `toasts.auctions-sold` | `GOAL` | When an auction of yours is won. |
| `toasts.orders-filled` | `TASK` | When a seller fills an order of yours. |
| `toasts.orders-completed` | `GOAL` | When an order of yours is complete. |
| `sell-indicators` | `true` | A floating `+money` label over a sale: over the player for `/sell` and autosell, over the container for a wand. |
| `wand-particles` | `HAPPY_VILLAGER\|12\|0.4\|0.4\|0.4\|0` | Particles over a container a wand sold from: `NAME\|count\|dx\|dy\|dz\|speed`. Empty for none. |
| `autosell-report` | `ACTION_BAR` | How the periodic autosell report arrives: `TOAST`, `ACTION_BAR`, `CHAT` or `NONE`. |
| `auction-countdown-seconds` | `60` | A boss bar over an auction's last seconds for its leader and those who bid on it here. `0` for none. |
| `outbid-action-bar` | `true` | An action bar as well as the chat line when you are outbid. |
| `trade-countdown` | `BOSS_BAR` | The trade countdown on screen: `BOSS_BAR`, `TITLE` or `NONE`. |

Toast frames are `TASK`, `GOAL`, `CHALLENGE` or `NONE`. The bazaar's toast is its own `toast-frame`.
