`MarketService` answers two things other plugins need from the market: which of its modules are running,
and selling a drop through the player's autosell. Four events report what happens on the market. Prices are
not here: ask ExyliaLib's `Prices`, which the shop and the worth list answer.

```java
// A plugin that hands out drops: offer each one to the player's autosell first.
boolean sold = ExyliaAPI.get(MarketService.class)
        .map(market -> market.autoSell(player, drop))
        .orElse(false);
if (!sold) player.getInventory().addItem(drop);
```

<Callout type="note" title="Adding it to your project">
The artifact, the repository and the `plugin.yml` line are the same for every Exylia plugin and live on the
[public API page](/docs/exylialib/api). The market contract arrived in the public API **1.11.0**, shipped
with ExyliaLib **1.276.0**, in the package `net.exylia.lib.api.market`.
</Callout>

An empty `Optional` from `ExyliaAPI.get` means the server has no ExyliaMarket. The service is registered once
the modules have started and goes away when the plugin disables.

## Methods

| Method | What it does |
|---|---|
| `boolean isModuleEnabled(String moduleId)` | Whether a module is switched on and running: `auction-house`, `auctions`, `orders`, `bazaar`, `shop`, `sell`, `worth`, `sell-wands` or `trade`. |
| `boolean autoSell(Player player, ItemStack drop)` | Offers a drop to the player's autosell. `true` when it was taken and must **not** be given as well; `false` when the player has autosell off or lacks the permission, nobody buys the item, or the sell wands module is off. Paid in batches every `autosell.flush-seconds`, not at once. Call it on the player's own thread. The stack is never changed. |

<Callout type="warning" title="The module ids in the javadoc">
The interface's javadoc lists `market` among the module ids and leaves out `bazaar`. The auction house's id is
`auction-house`: `isModuleEnabled("market")` always answers `false`.
</Callout>

## Events

All in `net.exylia.lib.api.market.event`.

| Event | Cancellable | When |
|---|---|---|
| `MarketDealEvent` | No | A deal between two players finished, money and items settled. |
| `SellWandUseEvent` | Yes | A player is about to sell a container's contents with a sell wand. |
| `TradeRequestEvent` | No | A trade request was delivered. |
| `MarketLogEvent` | No | Deprecated, and no longer fired. |

### MarketDealEvent

For a reputation plugin to open a rating, or to hand the seller back part of the tax. With nobody listening
the deal stands exactly as it was paid.

| Getter | What it is |
|---|---|
| `getSource()` | `market` (auction house), `auctions`, `orders`, `trade`, or `bazaar`. |
| `getBuyer()`, `getBuyerName()` | Who paid; the name may be `null`. |
| `getSeller()`, `getSellerName()` | Who was paid. |
| `getCurrency()` | The currency id, blank for the default. |
| `getPrice()` | What the buyer paid, before tax. |
| `getTax()` | The sales tax the seller paid, zero for none. |

A trade has no buyer or seller: it fires once per side, with that side as the buyer, the other as the seller,
a price of what the trade was worth in the default currency (money in it plus what the items sell for) and
no tax. A bazaar fill uses the source `bazaar`, which is not one of the class's constants.

<Callout type="warning" title="Usually off the main thread">
`MarketDealEvent` is fired from a database callback in most cases. Schedule before touching the world or a
player's inventory.
</Callout>

### SellWandUseEvent

Fired on the player's own thread before anything is sold or the click is taken. Cancel it for a container
that decides itself when it opens — a loot chest, a supply drop crate: the wand does nothing and the click
goes on as a plain open.

```java
@EventHandler
public void onWand(SellWandUseEvent event) {
    if (crates.isCrate(event.getBlock())) event.setCancelled(true);
}
```

| Getter | What it is |
|---|---|
| `getPlayer()` | Who holds the wand. |
| `getBlock()` | The container clicked. |

### TradeRequestEvent

On the main thread, both players on this server, after the request was delivered. For saying something to
either side before they deal, such as a warning about a low reputation.

| Getter | What it is |
|---|---|
| `getFrom()` | Who asked. |
| `getTo()` | Who was asked. |

### MarketLogEvent

Deprecated in the API and not fired by ExyliaMarket any more: the market records into ExyliaLib's shared
staff log instead, browsed with `/exylialib logs`.

## What is not there

No method lists, creates or cancels listings, auctions, orders or offers, edits the shop or reads a
player's history. Placeholders cover the read-outs most integrations want: see
[Placeholders](/docs/exyliamarket/placeholders).
