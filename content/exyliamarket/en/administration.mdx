## The panel

`/marketadmin` (also `/exyliamarket`) opens one panel for everything staff manage. It opens for anybody
holding **any** of the plugin's staff nodes; each card then asks for its own. From the console it prints
the usage.

| Card | Opens | Node |
|---|---|---|
| **Server shop** | The shop editor. | `exyliamarket.shop.admin` |
| **Bazaar** | Its settings, and the products editor and offers. | `exyliamarket.bazaar.admin` |
| **Auction house** | Its settings, and every listing. | `exyliamarket.auctionhouse.admin` |
| **Auctions** | Its settings, and every running auction. | `exyliamarket.auctions.admin` |
| **Buy orders** | Its settings, and every open order. | `exyliamarket.orders.admin` |
| **Trade** | Its settings. | `exyliamarket.trade.admin` |
| **Sell** | Its settings. | `exyliamarket.sell.admin` |
| **Sell wands** | Its settings. | `exyliamarket.sellwands.admin` |
| **Worth list** | The worth editor. | `exyliamarket.worth.admin` |
| **Price checks** | The price index and retention settings. | `exyliamarket.prices.admin` |
| **Market log** | The log. | `exyliamarket.admin.logs` |
| **Stats** | The stats screen. | `exyliamarket.admin.logs` |
| **Reload** | `/marketadmin reload`. | `exyliamarket.admin.reload` |

Each card shows whether its module is on, and live counts: listings, auctions, orders, offers,
categories, priced items and today's deals.

## Settings in game

Each module's card opens its settings in themed pages — **Fees & tax**, **Limits**, **Rules**,
**Broadcasts**, **Bidding**, **Durations** and so on. Click a value to change it: numbers, money, percentages,
hours and seconds are typed with their range shown, switches flip, the blocked materials open a list.
Values that bound each other are checked against each other (a minimum never above its maximum).

A change is written to the module's own file, under the same key as by hand, and that module reloads at
once. Nothing is stored anywhere else. The shop's `config.yml`, `discord.yml` and the module switches are
only edited in the files.

## Moderation lists

| Command | List | What staff do |
|---|---|---|
| `/ahadmin` | Every listing, newest first. | Left-click takes one down (the item goes back to the seller); right-click opens its log. |
| `/auctionadmin` | Every running auction, ending soonest first. | Left-click removes it, refunding the top bidder; right-click opens its log. |
| `/ordersadmin` | Every open order, newest first. | Left-click cancels and refunds it; right-click opens the buyer's log. |
| `/bazaaradmin` | The products editor and every open order and offer. | Right-click cancels an order or offer. |

Every removal asks first, tells the owner, and is written to the market log as `market-remove`,
`auction-remove`, `order-remove` or `bazaar-remove`. See each module's page for the commands that do the same
by id.

## The market log

`/marketadmin log [player]` opens the log of the whole network, or of one player as the one who acted or the
one it happened to. It needs `exyliamarket.admin.logs`.

Each line is who did what, with which item, for how much, to whom, on which server and when. Click a name to
open that player's log. Filter by module and by action, left-click forward and right-click back.

| Module | Actions |
|---|---|
| `auction-house` | `market-listed`, `market-buy`, `market-remove` |
| `auctions` | `auction-opened`, `auction-bid`, `auction-auto-bid`, `auction-sale`, `auction-remove` |
| `orders` | `order-fill`, `order-complete`, `order-collect`, `order-cancel`, `order-remove` |
| `bazaar` | `bazaar-fill`, `bazaar-cancel`, `bazaar-remove` |
| `shop` | `shop-buy`, `shop-sell` |
| `trade` | the swap, with both sides' items and money |

Lines are kept `logs.keep-days` (30 by default, `0` forever). The same records also go to the ExyliaLib staff
log every Exylia plugin shares, browsed with `/exylialib logs`.

## The stats screen

`/marketadmin stats` shows today and the last seven days: volume and deals per module and currency, the
taxes taken, the items that moved most, and the players who sold and bought most (ranked in the default
currency). The figures are worked out from the log every `logs.stats-refresh-seconds` (60 by default) and
the screen says how long ago.

## Reloading

`/marketadmin reload` (or the panel's button) re-reads `config.yml`, `discord.yml`, every module's files, the
worth list and every menu. Turning a module on or off still needs a restart; the reload names the modules
waiting for one. `/exylialib reload` also re-reads this plugin's menus.

## Discord

`discord.yml` posts the market's big moments to a channel: an auction house sale, an auction won, a buy order
completed, a bazaar trade, a large shop purchase and a removal by staff. It is off until `enabled` is true and
a webhook URL is set.

```yaml title="discord.yml"
enabled: true
webhook: https://discord.com/api/webhooks/<id>/<token>
auction-house-sale:
  enabled: true
  above: 10000
  webhook: ''
  template:
    title: AUCTION HOUSE SALE
    description: '**%player%** bought **%amount%x %item%** from **%counterparty%**.'
```

| Event | Default `above` |
|---|---|
| `auction-house-sale` | `10000` |
| `auction-won` | `10000` |
| `order-completed` | `10000` (the whole order's price) |
| `bazaar-sale` | `10000` |
| `shop-purchase` | `50000` |
| `admin-removal` | — (`above` is not read) |

`above` is in the default currency; `0` posts every deal, and a deal in another currency only posts when it is
`0`. Each event may name its own `webhook`. Templates are ExyliaLib's webhook cards — title, description,
fields, colour, buttons, footer — with `%player%`, `%player_head%`, `%counterparty%`, `%item%`, `%amount%`,
`%price%`, `%tax%`, `%currency%`, `%module%`, `%action%` and `%server%`. What players typed is escaped, so it
never pings.
