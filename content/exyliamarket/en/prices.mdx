The market keeps an index of what players **actually paid each other** for each item: the last sale, the
24-hour and 7-day averages, the median, the range, the volume and the trend, per material and currency.
Players see it as suggestions on the review screens, a question before a price far from the market, a
7-day chart and [placeholders](/docs/exyliamarket/placeholders#market-prices).

## What counts

- Every finished sale of the **auction house**, **auctions**, **buy orders** and the **bazaar**.
- Not the shop, `/sell` or trades: shop prices are the server's, not the market's.
- Only **plain** items: nothing renamed, enchanted, damaged or carrying lore or data. A material's price is
  the price of the material, not of somebody's sword.
- Every figure is **per item**: a sale of 16 for 320 is 16 at 20. Averages are weighted by items; the
  median, low and high are per sale.
- Each currency is kept apart.

## How it is kept

Each server adds its sales to day rows of its own in memory and writes the changed ones every
`prices.refresh-seconds` (60 by default), then reads the last fourteen days of every server's rows back and
works the figures out again. Screens and placeholders read the figures from memory and never wait on the
database. A sale shows up one refresh later, on every server alike.

Days are UTC. The trend compares the last seven days with the seven before: **up** or **down** past 5%,
**flat** otherwise.

## Trusted figures

An item needs `prices.min-sales` sales in the last seven days (3 by default) before it is suggested or a
deal is questioned. With fewer, the screens say there are not enough trades yet.

## The stray-price question

Nothing ever refuses a deal on price: a price far below the average may be a gift, one far above a
collector's. The player is asked once, with the figures, and their answer stands.

| Setting | Default | When it asks |
|---|---|---|
| `prices.warn-below-percent` | `50` | Listing on the auction house, or setting an auction's buy-it-now, below this % of the 7-day average. |
| `prices.warn-above-percent` | `200` | Buying on the auction house, buying an auction out, or placing a buy order above this % of it. |

`0` turns either off.

## On the screens

The auction house and buy order reviews have a **market price** button with the 7-day average, last sale,
median, trend, sales and a suggested price; clicking it sets the price. Menu files may use these on those
screens:

| Value | What it is |
|---|---|
| `%market_known%` | `true` with enough sales. |
| `%market_average%`, `%market_last%`, `%market_median%` | The 7-day average, last sale and median, per item. |
| `%market_trend%`, `%market_sales%` | The trend and the number of sales in 7 days. |
| `%market_suggested%` | The 7-day average for the amount being listed. |

### The price chart

`/worth history [material]`, a shift-click on the auction house board, the bazaar's product screen and the
worth menu all open a material's last seven days as a chart: one column a day, oldest on the left, its
height that day's average against the week's range. A day without sales is a grey column.

| Value | What it is |
|---|---|
| `%price_item%`, `%price_currency%` | What it is about. |
| `%price_last%`, `%price_last_when%` | The last sale and how long ago. |
| `%price_average24%`, `%price_average7%`, `%price_median%` | The averages and the median. |
| `%price_low%`, `%price_high%` | The week's range. |
| `%price_volume%`, `%price_sales%` | Items traded and number of sales. |
| `%price_previous%`, `%price_trend%` | The week before and the trend. |

The chart's file is `lang/<code>/menus/price_history.yml`; the date under each column follows
`prices.day-format` in `messages.yml`.

## History and retention

Every sale is also two lines of `market_history`, one for the buyer and one for the seller, read by each
module's history screen. The index keeps its own daily rows, so pruning history never costs a chart a day.

| Setting | Default | What it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| `prices.keep-history-days` | `90` | Each player's market history. `0` forever. |
| `prices.keep-daily-days` | `365` | The daily rows behind the charts. `0` forever. |

All of `prices` is in `config.yml`; everything but `refresh-seconds` and `keep-daily-days` can also be
changed in game from `/marketadmin` → **Price checks**.
