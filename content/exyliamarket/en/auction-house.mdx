The auction house is a board where a player puts an item up at a fixed price and anybody else buys it. The module is on by default and its command is `/ah` (also `/auctionhouse`).

## Before you start

The server needs an economy, see [ExyliaLib economy](/docs/exylialib/economy). The first run-through with two accounts is in [Your first listing](/docs/exyliamarket/first-steps).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold the item and run /ah sell">
    Hold a stack in your main hand and run `/ah sell <price>`, for example `/ah sell 100`. The whole stack is listed at that price. If the server has more than one currency, add it (`/ah sell 100 <currency>`) or pick one from the screen that opens. You can also open `/ah` and click **Sell an item**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the review screen">
    Nothing is charged or taken yet. The screen shows the price, the price per item, how long the listing stays up, the listing fee charged now, the sales tax taken on a sale and what you receive. Click **Price** to change the price. When the item has enough player sales, **Market price** shows the 7-day average and sets a suggested price for the stack when clicked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it up">
    Click **Put it up**. The item leaves your inventory and the listing appears on the board. A price far below the 7-day average asks once more first, see [Prices](/docs/exyliamarket/prices).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy a listing">
    On another account run `/ah`, left-click a listing and click **Buy it**. The money is taken first, then the listing is claimed. If somebody else got it first, the money goes straight back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

- `/ah` shows the listing on the board, and `/ah mine` (also `/ah listings`) shows yours, how many you have up against your limit and what they add up to.
- After the sale the seller has the price minus the sales tax. The buyer has the item, and whatever did not fit in their inventory waits in [`/collect`](/docs/exyliamarket/collect).
- The seller is told on whichever server they are, or on their next join.

## Going further

### Fees and tax

| | Default | Charged |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | 2% | To the seller when the listing goes up. Never refunded, not even when it expires: that is what stops the auction house being free storage. |
| Sales tax | 5% | Out of what the seller is paid when it sells. |

A listing of 1,000 pays a fee of 20 when it goes up and pays the seller 950 when it sells. Both can be set per rank with the numbered permissions `exyliamarket.auctionhouse.fee.<n>` and `exyliamarket.auctionhouse.tax.<n>` (whole percents, the lowest one a player holds wins), see [Permissions](/docs/exyliamarket/permissions#fees-and-taxes-the-lowest-wins). The tax is read from the seller when the listing sells. A seller who is offline then pays the configured rate.

### How many listings, how long, how much

By default a player has 5 listings up at once and a listing stays up 48 hours. Give a rank more with `exyliamarket.auctionhouse.listings.<n>`. When a listing runs out, a sweep every minute sends the item back to its seller, who is told it is on its way. The fee is not returned.

The price limits are amounts of the default currency. `min-price` is 1 and `max-price` has no ceiling by default. A listing in another currency only has to be worth that currency's smallest amount.

### Taking a listing back

Open `/ah mine` and click the listing. The item comes back, the fee does not. From the same screen:

- **History**: your latest purchases and sales.
- **Watchlist**: the listings you right-clicked that are still up. It is a bookmark list only, nothing notifies you about a watched listing.

### Finding things on the board

| Click on a listing | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left | Buy. |
| Right | Add to or remove from your **Watchlist**. |
| Shift | The item's 7-day price chart, or the inside of a shulker box or bundle. |

- **Sort**: Newest first, Cheapest first, Most expensive first, Ending soonest first, Cheapest each first.
- **Category**: left-click for the next category, right-click for the previous one.
- **Search**: up to 32 characters, matched against the material, the seller's name and the item's name. Right-click clears it.

Sort, category and search are remembered until the player leaves. A listing also shows what one sells for to the server when the shop or the worth list buys it back.

### Announcements

A sale from 50 and a new listing from 50 (default currency) are announced to the server, and one seller's next listing is not announced again for 60 seconds. Players mute these in `/settings`; muting never hides their own sales. Set the thresholds to `-1` to never announce.

### Staff

`/ahadmin` (also `/auctionhouseadmin`, needs `exyliamarket.auctionhouse.admin`) lists every listing, newest first. Left-click takes one down after a confirmation and the item goes back to the seller, who is told. Right-click opens its line in the market log, see [Administration](/docs/exyliamarket/administration#the-market-log). From chat: `/ahadmin remove <id>` takes one down and `/ahadmin clear <player>` takes down everything a player has up.

## What is checked when listing

In this order: an economy exists, the currency is one the server has, the item is not in the blocked list, is not damaged (unless allowed), is not too heavy, the price is within the limits, the player is under their listing limit, and they still carry the whole amount. A player cannot buy their own listing unless `allow-self-purchase` is on. Taking it back is the way.

## Settings

<Details title="Settings: modules/auction-house/config.yml">

Under `settings:`, also editable in game from `/marketadmin`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-listings-per-player` | `5` | Listings up at once. `exyliamarket.auctionhouse.listings.<n>` replaces it for a rank. |
| `min-price` | `1.0` | Least a listing may be priced at, in the default currency. |
| `max-price` | `-1.0` | Most a listing may be priced at. `-1` for no ceiling. |
| `listing-hours` | `48` | How long a listing stays up. The in-game editor starts at 1; `0` in the file means never. |
| `listing-fee-percent` | `2.0` | Charged when listing. Not refunded. |
| `sales-tax-percent` | `5.0` | Taken from what the seller is paid. |
| `allow-self-purchase` | `false` | Whether a player may buy their own listing. |
| `broadcast-above` | `50.0` | Announce a sale from this price. `-1` never. |
| `broadcast-listed-above` | `50.0` | Announce a new listing from this price. `-1` never. |
| `broadcast-listed-cooldown-seconds` | `60` | Seconds before one seller's next listing is announced again. |
| `blocked-materials` | `BEDROCK`, `COMMAND_BLOCK`, `BARRIER`, `STRUCTURE_BLOCK` | Materials that may never be listed. |
| `allow-damaged-items` | `true` | Whether a damaged item may be listed. |
| `max-item-kilobytes` | `64` | Heaviest item that may be listed, because every viewer of the board is sent it. `0` for no cap. |
| `min-counted-value` | `10.0` | Smallest sale, in the default currency, that counts toward statistics and missions. |
| `history-size` | `45` | Purchases and sales the history screen shows. |

Under `sounds:` there are `listed`, `bought`, `sold` and `cancelled`, each written `NAME|volume|pitch`.

<Callout type="note" title="Some sounds play twice">
Several shipped messages start with their own `[sound:…]` tag, the same sound the `sounds:` section plays. Changing a sound in `config.yml` does not remove the one in the message: edit or remove the tag in the module's `messages.yml` as well.
</Callout>

</Details>
