---
title: "Auction house"
description: "Fixed-price listings between players: listing through the review screen, fees and tax, buying, looking inside boxes, the board's sorts and filters, the watchlist, expiry and the settings."
---

Players put an item up at a fixed price and anybody else buys it. The item leaves the seller's inventory
when the listing goes up, lives in the database row while it is up, and leaves that row exactly once —
sold, taken back or expired — so two buyers clicking the same listing on the same tick can never both get
it. The loser of that race is refunded.

Module id `auction-house`, file `modules/auction-house/config.yml`, command `/ah` (also `/auctionhouse`).

## Listing

| From | How |
|---|---|
| The **Sell** button of `/ah` | Put the item into the one-slot window, pick a currency if the server has more than one, type the price. |
| `/ah sell <price> [currency]` | Lists the whole stack in your main hand at that price. With no currency typed and more than one to choose from, a picker opens. |

Both end on the **review screen**, and nothing is charged or taken until **Confirm**:

- the item, the currency and how long the listing stays up;
- the whole price and the price per item, with a click to change it;
- the fee charged now, the tax taken on a sale, and **You receive**;
- your balance now and after the fee;
- a **market price** button, when the item has enough player sales: the 7-day average, last sale, median,
  trend and the suggested price for the whole stack. Clicking it sets the price.

A price far below the 7-day average asks once more before listing (`prices.warn-below-percent`). See
[Prices](/docs/exyliamarket/prices).

### What is checked

In this order: an economy exists, the currency is one the server has, the item is not in
`blocked-materials`, is not damaged unless `allow-damaged-items`, is not heavier than `max-item-kilobytes`,
the price is within `min-price` and `max-price`, the player is under their listing limit, and they still
carry the whole amount.

`min-price` and `max-price` are amounts of the **default currency**: a listing in another currency only
has to be worth that currency's smallest amount, with no ceiling.

### Fee and tax

| | Default | Charged |
|---|---|---|
| `listing-fee-percent` | 2% | To the seller when the listing goes up. **Never refunded**, not even when it expires: that is what stops the auction house being free storage. |
| `sales-tax-percent` | 5% | Out of what the seller is paid when it sells, rounded up to the currency's smallest amount. |

A listing of 1,000 pays a fee of 20 when it goes up, and 950 to the seller when it sells.

## Buying

Left-click a listing to open its confirm screen: the price, your balance before and after, whether you can
afford it and the time left. A price per item far above the 7-day average asks once more
(`prices.warn-above-percent`). The money is taken first, then the listing is claimed; if somebody else got
it first, the money goes straight back.

The item goes into your inventory, and whatever does not fit to [`/collect`](/docs/exyliamarket/collect).
The seller is paid at once and told on whichever server they are, or on their next join. You cannot buy your
own listing unless `allow-self-purchase` is on; take it back instead.

### Looking inside

A shulker box or bundle with something in it can be opened from the board, your listings or the confirm
screen with a shift-click: a read-only view of what is inside, boxes within boxes included.

## The board

Each listing shows its price, the price per item, the seller, the time left and, when the shop or the worth
list buys it back, what one sells for to the server.

| Click | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left | Buy. |
| Right | Add to or remove from your watchlist. |
| Shift | The item's 7-day price chart, or the inside of a box. |

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sort** | Newest → Cheapest → Most expensive → Ending soonest → Cheapest per item. |
| **Category** | Left-click for the next category, right-click for the previous. The categories are `categories` in `config.yml`. |
| **Search** | Up to 32 characters, matched against the material, the seller's current name and the item's name. Right-click clears it. |

Sort, category and search are remembered until the player leaves.

## Your listings, history and watchlist

`/ah mine` (also `/ah listings`) lists what you have up, how many against your limit and what they add up
to per currency. Click one to take it back: the item comes back, the fee does not. From there:

- **History** — your latest purchases and sales, `history-size` of them.
- **Watchlist** — the listings you right-clicked that are still up. It is a bookmark list only: nothing
  notifies you about a watched listing.

## Expiry

A listing stays up `listing-hours` (48 by default; `0` for never). Every minute a sweep sends what ran out
back to its seller, through the same claim as a sale, so a listing being bought at that moment is not also
returned. The seller is told it is on its way back.

## Announcements

A sale at or above `broadcast-above`, and a new listing at or above `broadcast-listed-above`, are announced
to the server. Both thresholds are in the default currency, so a listing in another currency is never
announced. A seller's next listing is not announced again for `broadcast-listed-cooldown-seconds`. Players
mute these in `/settings` under **Market** → **Auction House**; muting never hides their own sales.

## Staff

`/ahadmin` (also `/auctionhouseadmin`) lists every listing, newest first. Left-click takes one down after a
confirmation: the item goes back to the seller, who is told. Right-click opens its line in the market log.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/ahadmin remove <id>` | Takes one listing down. |
| `/ahadmin clear <player>` | Takes down everything a player has up. |

Both need `exyliamarket.auctionhouse.admin` and must be run by a player in game.

## Settings

Under `settings:` in `modules/auction-house/config.yml`, editable in game from `/marketadmin`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-listings-per-player` | `5` | Listings up at once. `exyliamarket.auctionhouse.listings.<n>` replaces it; `0` is unlimited. |
| `min-price` | `1.0` | Least a listing may be priced at, in the default currency. |
| `max-price` | `-1.0` | Most a listing may be priced at. `-1` for no ceiling. |
| `listing-hours` | `48` | How long a listing stays up. |
| `listing-fee-percent` | `2.0` | Charged when listing. Not refunded. |
| `sales-tax-percent` | `5.0` | Taken from what the seller is paid. |
| `allow-self-purchase` | `false` | Whether a player may buy their own listing. |
| `broadcast-above` | `50.0` | Announce a sale from this price. `-1` never. |
| `broadcast-listed-above` | `50.0` | Announce a new listing from this price. `-1` never. |
| `broadcast-listed-cooldown-seconds` | `60` | Seconds before one seller's next listing is announced again. |
| `blocked-materials` | `BEDROCK`, `COMMAND_BLOCK`, `BARRIER`, `STRUCTURE_BLOCK` | Materials that may never be listed. |
| `allow-damaged-items` | `true` | Whether a damaged item may be listed. |
| `max-item-kilobytes` | `64` | Heaviest item that may be listed: every viewer of the board is sent it. `0` for no cap. |
| `min-counted-value` | `10.0` | Smallest sale, in the default currency, that counts toward statistics and missions. |
| `history-size` | `45` | Purchases and sales the history screen shows. |

And under `sounds:` — `listed`, `bought`, `sold`, `cancelled` — each written `NAME|volume|pitch`.

<Callout type="note" title="Some sounds play twice">
Several shipped messages start with their own `[sound:…]` tag, the same sound the `sounds:` section plays.
Changing a sound in `config.yml` does not remove the one in the message: edit or remove the tag in the
module's `messages.yml` as well.
</Callout>
