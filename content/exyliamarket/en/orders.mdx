A buy order asks for an amount of an item at a price per unit. The buyer pays for all of it when the order goes up, anybody can sell into it (the buyer does not have to be online) and is paid at once. The command is `/orders` (also `/order`) and the module is on by default.

## Before you start

The server needs an economy, see [ExyliaLib economy](/docs/exylialib/economy). To test the whole loop, use two accounts as in [Your first listing](/docs/exyliamarket/first-steps).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place an order">
    Hold 1 of the item you want, then run `/orders create <amount> <price>`, for example `/orders create 500 3` to order 500 of it at 3 **each**. With several currencies, add one or pick it from the screen that opens. Or run `/orders`, click **Order an item**, and pick the item from the list: **In your hand** for an exact order, or any item for a material order. Then you set the amount and the price each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the review">
    The screen shows the item and how it is matched, the amount, the price each and the whole price, the creation fee, the total charged, the seller tax and your balance before and after. Change **Amount** or **Price each** there, or click **Market price** to use the 7-day average. A price far above the market asks once more, see [Prices](/docs/exyliamarket/prices).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place order">
    Click **Place order**. The whole price plus the 1% creation fee is taken now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it from another account">
    Carry the item, run `/orders` and click the order. The screen shows how many you carry that match and what you are paid after tax. Click **Sell** (it shows the amount) to sell every matching item in your inventory and hotbar, up to what the order still needs. You are paid at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

- `/orders mine` shows your order with how much has been filled.
- The delivered items wait on the order, not in the general inbox. Left-click the order in `/orders mine` to take what fits, and `/collect` also lists orders with items waiting. A finished and empty order disappears.
- The seller's balance went up by their share minus the 5% tax. The buyer is told on whichever server they are, or on their next join.

## Going further

### Two kinds of order

| Made from | Kind | What it accepts |
|---|---|---|
| Any item picked in the list | **Material order** ("Any plain item") | Any plain item of that material: not renamed, no lore, no enchantments, no custom model, not damaged. |
| **In your hand**, or `/orders create` | **Exact order** ("Exactly this item") | Only items identical to the one held, enchantments and name included. |

`material-orders-accept` lets a material order take more than plain items. List any of `NAME`, `LORE`, `ENCHANTMENTS`, `CUSTOM_MODEL_DATA`:

```yaml title="modules/orders/config.yml"
settings:
  material-orders-accept:
  - NAME
```

<Callout type="warning" title="The buyer always receives plain items">
A material order delivers plain copies of its material, whatever the seller handed in. With `ENCHANTMENTS` accepted, an enchanted item sold into the order loses its enchantments on the way.
</Callout>

### Cancelling and expiry

Right-click the order in `/orders mine` and confirm to close it: the money not yet spent is refunded, the creation fee is not, and what was already delivered stays to collect. Staff cancelling and an order running out after 72 hours (`order-hours`) work the same way. An expired order stops taking fills at once, and the sweep that refunds it runs every minute.

### Limits

A player has 5 open orders by default, and a rank can have more with `exyliamarket.orders.limit.<n>`. An order is between 1 and 2304 units, the item cannot be blocked (bedrock, command blocks, barriers, structure blocks, spawners), damaged or heavier than 64 KB. In the default currency the whole price must be between `min-price` and `max-price`. Nobody can fill their own order unless `allow-self-fill` is on, and filling needs `exyliamarket.orders.fill`.

Fees and tax can be set per rank with `exyliamarket.orders.fee.<n>` and `exyliamarket.orders.tax.<n>`, the lowest winning, see [Permissions](/docs/exyliamarket/permissions#fees-and-taxes-the-lowest-wins).

### The board

Sort cycles Best paid first, Newest first and Most wanted first. **Search** matches the material, the buyer's name or the item's name, and right-click clears it. Screens update while they are open.

### Staff

`/ordersadmin` (also `/ordersa`, needs `exyliamarket.orders.admin`) lists every open order, newest first. Left-click cancels one after a confirmation, right-click opens the buyer's market log. `/ordersadmin cancel <id>` does the same by id.

## Settings

<Details title="Settings: modules/orders/config.yml">

Under `settings:`, also editable in game from `/marketadmin`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-orders-per-player` | `5` | Open orders per player. `exyliamarket.orders.limit.<n>` replaces it. |
| `max-amount` | `2304` | Most units one order may ask for. |
| `min-price` | `1.0` | Least the whole price may be, in the default currency. |
| `max-price` | `-1.0` | Most the whole price may be, in the default currency. `-1` for no ceiling. |
| `order-hours` | `72` | How long an order takes fills. |
| `creation-fee-percent` | `1.0` | Charged on top of the price when the order is placed. Not refunded. `exyliamarket.orders.fee.<n>` sets it per rank. |
| `sales-tax-percent` | `5.0` | Taken from what a seller is paid. `exyliamarket.orders.tax.<n>` sets it per rank. |
| `allow-self-fill` | `false` | Whether a player may sell into their own order. |
| `blocked-materials` | `BEDROCK`, `COMMAND_BLOCK`, `BARRIER`, `STRUCTURE_BLOCK`, `SPAWNER` | Materials nobody may order. |
| `allow-damaged-items` | `false` | Whether an order may ask for a damaged item. |
| `max-item-kilobytes` | `64` | Heaviest item that may be ordered. `0` for no cap. |
| `min-counted-value` | `10.0` | Smallest fill, in the default currency, that counts toward statistics and missions. |
| `material-orders-accept` | `[]` | Parts a material order lets through anyway. |

</Details>
