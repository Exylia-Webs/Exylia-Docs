A buy order asks for an amount of an item at a price per unit, and the buyer pays for all of it when the
order goes up. Anybody may sell into it — online or not, the buyer does not have to be there — and is paid
at once. What sellers deliver waits on the order until the buyer collects it.

Module id `orders`, file `modules/orders/config.yml`, command `/orders` (also `/order`).

## Placing an order

There are two ways in, and they make two kinds of order.

| From | Order | What it accepts |
|---|---|---|
| **New order** on the board | A **material order**, for any item picked from a searchable list of every item | Any *plain* item of that material: not renamed, no lore, no enchantments, no custom model, not damaged, holding nothing. |
| **In your hand** in that same list, or `/orders create <amount> <price> [currency]` | An **exact order** | Only items identical to the one held, enchantments and name included. |

The board's flow asks for the item, then the amount, then the currency (skipped when the server has only
one), then the price **per unit**. `/orders create` takes the same values typed, with `<price>` per unit:
`/orders create 500 3` orders 500 of what you hold at 3 each. Without a currency typed and more than one to
choose from, a picker opens.

Both end on the **review screen**: the item and how it is matched, amount, price each and whole price, the
creation fee, the total charged, the seller tax, your balance before and after. Amount and price can be
changed there, and a **market price** button sets the price to the 7-day average of that item. Nothing is
charged until **Confirm**; a price far above the market asks once more first. See
[Prices](/docs/exyliamarket/prices).

### What is checked

- The item is not in `blocked-materials`, is not damaged unless `allow-damaged-items`, and is not heavier
  than `max-item-kilobytes`.
- The amount is between 1 and `max-amount` (2304 by default, 36 stacks).
- The price per unit is at least the currency's smallest amount. In the **default currency** the whole
  price must also be between `min-price` and `max-price`; other currencies have no bounds.
- The player has fewer open orders than their limit: `max-orders-per-player`, or their
  `exyliamarket.orders.limit.<n>`.

On confirm the whole price **plus** `creation-fee-percent` of it is taken. The fee is never refunded: it is
what stops orders being used as free storage for money.

### Accepting more than plain items

`material-orders-accept` lets a material order take items that would otherwise not count as plain. List
any of `NAME`, `LORE`, `ENCHANTMENTS`, `CUSTOM_MODEL_DATA`:

```yaml title="modules/orders/config.yml"
settings:
  material-orders-accept:
  - NAME
```

<Callout type="warning" title="The buyer always receives plain items">
A material order delivers plain copies of its material, whatever the seller handed in. With
`ENCHANTMENTS` accepted, an enchanted item sold into the order loses its enchantments on the way.
</Callout>

## Filling an order

Click an order on the board to open its **fill** screen: what it wants, how many you carry that match,
how many will sell (the smaller of what you carry and what is still wanted) and what you will be paid
after tax. **Confirm** sells every matching item in your storage and hotbar slots, up to what the order
still needs; there is no picking a smaller amount.

The seller is paid at once: a proportional share of what the buyer put up, minus `sales-tax-percent`. You
cannot fill your own order unless `allow-self-fill` is on.

The buyer is told on whichever server they are, or on their next join, and sees a toast when the order is
filled and again when it is complete. See [Collect and notices](/docs/exyliamarket/collect).

## Collecting, cancelling and expiry

Delivered items wait **on the order**, not in the general inbox. Left-click it in `/orders mine` to take
what fits in your inventory; `/collect` lists every order with items waiting too. An order that is
finished and empty disappears.

| What happens | The money left | The items delivered |
|---|---|---|
| The buyer cancels it (right-click in `/orders mine`, then confirm) | Refunded to the balance | Stay to collect |
| Staff cancel it | Refunded to the balance | Stay to collect |
| It runs out after `order-hours` (72 by default) | Refunded to the balance | Stay to collect |
| It is complete | Nothing left | Stay to collect |

An expired order stops taking fills at once; the sweep that refunds it runs every minute.

## The board

Sorted by **Best paid** (unit price, highest first), **Newest** or **Most wanted** (units still needed),
cycled with the sort button. The search matches the material, the buyer's name or the item's name;
right-click clears it. Every screen updates while it is open.

## Staff

`/ordersadmin` (also `/ordersa`) lists every open order, newest first. Left-click cancels one after a
confirmation; right-click opens the buyer's market log. `/ordersadmin cancel <id>` does the same by id.
Both need `exyliamarket.orders.admin`.

## Settings

All under `settings:` in `modules/orders/config.yml`, and editable in game from `/marketadmin`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-orders-per-player` | `5` | Open orders per player. `exyliamarket.orders.limit.<n>` replaces it. |
| `max-amount` | `2304` | Most units one order may ask for. |
| `min-price` | `1.0` | Least the whole price may be, in the default currency. |
| `max-price` | `-1.0` | Most the whole price may be, in the default currency. `-1` for no ceiling. |
| `order-hours` | `72` | How long an order takes fills. |
| `creation-fee-percent` | `1.0` | Charged to the buyer on top of the price when the order is placed. Not refunded. |
| `sales-tax-percent` | `5.0` | Taken from what a seller is paid. |
| `allow-self-fill` | `false` | Whether a player may sell into their own order. |
| `blocked-materials` | `BEDROCK`, `COMMAND_BLOCK`, `BARRIER`, `STRUCTURE_BLOCK`, `SPAWNER` | Materials nobody may order. |
| `allow-damaged-items` | `false` | Whether an order may ask for a damaged item. |
| `max-item-kilobytes` | `64` | Heaviest item that may be ordered. `0` for no cap. |
| `min-counted-value` | `10.0` | Smallest fill, in the default currency, that counts toward statistics and missions. |
| `material-orders-accept` | `[]` | Parts a material order lets through anyway: `NAME`, `LORE`, `ENCHANTMENTS`, `CUSTOM_MODEL_DATA`. |
