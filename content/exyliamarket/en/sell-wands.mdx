Two ways of selling without opening `/sell`, both through it: the same prices, the shop's limits and
permissions, the sell bonus and the `shop-sell` and `worth-sell` boosters. See
[Selling and worth](/docs/exyliamarket/selling).

Module id `sell-wands`, file `modules/sell-wands/config.yml`. It needs the `sell` module: with `sell` off it
stays off too, with a line in the console.

## Sell wands

A wand is handed out by staff:

```text
/sellwandadmin give <player> <multiplier> <uses>
```

- `<multiplier>` from `0.1` to `10`: what the wand multiplies a sale by.
- `<uses>`: `-1` for unlimited, otherwise 1 or more.
- `<player>` may be anybody, on any server: a full inventory, an absent or offline player gets it from
  [`/collect`](/docs/exyliamarket/collect).

Each wand is unique and never stacks with another. Wands made by ExyliaSurvivalCore's old sell wands keep
working: the plugin reads the same item data.

### Using one

Right-click a container with the wand in your main hand: a chest (the whole double chest), barrel,
shulker box, hopper, furnace, dispenser, dropper, brewing stand — any block that holds items. Everything
sellable inside is sold at once, multiplied by the wand, and the rest stays. Particles burst over the
container (`feedback.wand-particles`) with a floating label of what it paid (`feedback.sell-indicators`).

- It needs `exyliamarket.sellwands.use`.
- Protection plugins answer first: a click they cancel does nothing.
- A container with nothing to sell costs neither a use nor the cooldown.
- The cooldown, `wand.cooldown-seconds`, is network-wide and survives relogs and server switches.
- The last use breaks the wand.

A plugin can stop a wand on its own containers — a loot chest, a supply drop — by cancelling
`SellWandUseEvent`; the click then opens the container normally. See [API](/docs/exyliamarket/api).

## Autosell

With autosell on, what a player breaks is sold instead of dropping: every block drop the shop or worth list
buys is collected and sold every `autosell.flush-seconds`. Mob drops and fishing are not included. What
cannot be sold — a limit reached, a missing permission — goes to the inventory or `/collect`, never the
ground.

Every `autosell.report-seconds` the player is told what was sold, the way `feedback.autosell-report` says:
`ACTION_BAR` (the default), `TOAST`, `CHAT` or `NONE`.

| To use it | |
|---|---|
| Permission | `exyliamarket.sellwands.autosell` — **operators only by default**. |
| Switch | `/autosell`, or **Market** → **Autosell** in `/settings`. The same setting. |

<Callout type="note" title="Autosell starts on">
The switch defaults to **on**: a player who is given the permission starts autoselling straight away,
without running `/autosell`.
</Callout>

Other plugins feed drops of their own into a player's autosell through `MarketService.autoSell`, which is
how a mines plugin sells what its blocks drop.

## Settings

`modules/sell-wands/config.yml`, editable in game from `/marketadmin`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `wand.material` | `BLAZE_ROD` | The item a wand is. |
| `wand.cooldown-seconds` | `1.0` | Seconds between two uses of a wand. |
| `autosell.flush-seconds` | `5` | Seconds between sales of what a player broke. |
| `autosell.report-seconds` | `60` | Seconds between the reports. `0` for none. |

Statistics counted: `sellwands.uses`, `sellwands.items` and `autosell.items`.
