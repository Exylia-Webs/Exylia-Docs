## Economy

Every price goes through ExyliaLib's economy facade, so the market pays in whatever the server runs:

- **ExyliaEconomy**: every currency it keeps can be used. A listing, auction or buy order picks one (a
  picker opens when there is more than one), the shop prices each product in its own, the bazaar trades in
  `settings.currency`, and a trade offers any of them.
- **Vault** alone: one currency, the Vault economy.
- **None**: every module that moves money says so in the console and refuses to price anything.

Fees, taxes and prices are rounded to each currency's decimals. Thresholds written as numbers — minimum
prices, announcements, `min-counted-value`, webhook `above` — are amounts of the **default** currency and do
not apply to deals in another one.

Payment reasons, for ExyliaEconomy's ledger: `market:buy`, `market:sale`, `market:fee`, `market:refund`,
`bazaar:buy`, `trade:pay`, `trade:receive`, `shop:sell`, `sell:command`, `sell:wand`, `sell:autosell`, and the
like.

## ExyliaLib

| Feature | What the market uses it for |
|---|---|
| `/settings` | A **Market** category with **Auction House** and **Auctions** announcements, **Autosell**, **Trade requests** and **Worth under items**. Each is listed only while its module runs. |
| Boosters | Two money sources, `shop-sell` and `worth-sell`, which booster screens list and sales multiply by. |
| Pending rewards | Everything waiting in `/collect`, in `exylia_pending_rewards`. |
| Notices | Telling a player about their deals on any server, or on their next join. |
| Staff log | Every market record, browsed with `/exylialib logs`. |
| Prices | The shop and the worth list answer ExyliaLib's `Prices`, so any plugin asking what an item sells for gets the market's answer. |

## ExyliaSurvivalCore

ExyliaMarket keeps no statistics of its own. With ExyliaSurvivalCore installed, it counts into its
statistics, which missions, seasons and leaderboards read:

| Key | Counted |
|---|---|
| `market.listed`, `market.bought`, `market.sold` | Auction house listings and sales. |
| `market.spent`, `market.earned` | Money moved on the auction house. |
| `auctions.listed`, `auctions.bids`, `auctions.won`, `auctions.sold` | Auctions. |
| `auctions.spent`, `auctions.earned` | Money moved in auctions. |
| `orders.placed`, `orders.filled`, `orders.received` | Buy orders: `filled` and `received` count items. |
| `bazaar.orders-placed`, `bazaar.offers-placed`, `bazaar.bought`, `bazaar.sold` | The bazaar, in items. |
| `bazaar.spent`, `bazaar.earned` | Money moved on the bazaar. |
| `sellwands.uses`, `sellwands.items`, `autosell.items` | Wands and autosell. |
| `trade.completed` | Finished trades. |

Money keys are written as `<module>.<what>` for the default currency and also as
`<module>.<currency>.<what>` for each currency, so gems are never added to coins. A deal in the default
currency below the module's `min-counted-value` counts nothing, so two accounts trading dirt for 1 farm
nothing; deals in other currencies always count.

Its reputation listens to `MarketDealEvent`, fired for every auction house sale, auction won, order filled,
bazaar fill and trade.

## Exylia Analytics

With the ExyliaAnalytics agent installed, every settled deal is reported once, by the server that settled
it — auction house sales, auction wins and buyouts, order fills, bazaar fills, shop purchases and sales,
`/sell` by channel, and each kind of item a trade moved — and every five minutes a snapshot of what each
market holds. Without it nothing is sent.
