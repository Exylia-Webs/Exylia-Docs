Two modules sell items back to the server. **Sell** owns `/sell` and its window, and draws what each item
is worth under it. **Worth** owns `worth.yml`, a price per material for items the shop does not buy. Sell
wands and autosell sell through the same path; see [Sell wands](/docs/exyliamarket/sell-wands).

Files `modules/sell/config.yml` and `modules/worth/worth.yml`.

## Where a price comes from

1. **The shop.** If a shop product sells that item back, its price is the one paid, and its limits and
   permission apply. When several products buy it, the oldest enabled one decides.
2. **The worth list.** Otherwise, the item's material in `worth.yml`.

The shop always wins, even when the player cannot use that product: without its permission the item does
not sell at all, and once its sell limit is used up the rest comes back unsold. The worth list does not
take over.

## /sell

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/sell` | Opens the sell window. |
| `/sell hand` | Sells the stack in your main hand. |
| `/sell all` | Sells everything sellable in your inventory: the 27 storage slots, plus the hotbar when `sell-all-scope` is `ALL` (the default). |
| `/sell sales` | Opens your sales of today (see [balancing](#balancing-sales-to-the-server)). |

All of them need `exyliamarket.shop.sell`. `/sell all` asks first when it would pay at least
`confirm-sell-all-above` of the default currency (5,000 by default, `-1` never asks).

### The sell window

Drop items into the top five rows like a chest. The bottom row holds:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add sellable** | Moves every sellable stack from your inventory into the window, following `sell-all-scope`. |
| **Cancel** | Hands everything back. |
| **Total** | What it all comes to, per currency, bonuses included, and how many items will come back unsold. Updated on every click and every second. |
| **Confirm** | Sells. |

Items that do not sell, and leftovers below a product's stack, come back to your inventory, the rest to
[`/collect`](/docs/exyliamarket/collect). Closing the window with ESC — or quitting, dying, being kicked, a
shutdown — **sells** what is in it while `sell-on-close` is on, as it is by default; turned off, all of
those hand everything back and only **Confirm** sells.

### Shulker boxes and bundles

A filled shulker box or bundle sells **what it carries**, boxes inside boxes included, and comes back with
the rest still inside; the box itself is never sold. This works for `/sell hand`, `/sell all`, the window
and sell wands. Autosell leaves boxes alone: a box a player breaks is their storage, not a drop. What stays
inside is packed to the front of the box.

## Sell bonus

`exyliamarket.shop.sell.<multiplier>` multiplies everything a player sells to the server: shop products,
worth items, wands and autosell. The number is a plain multiplier, not a percentage:

| Node | Pays |
|---|---|
| `exyliamarket.shop.sell.1.25` | ×1.25 |
| `exyliamarket.shop.sell.2` | ×2 |

The highest one held wins; anything below 1 does nothing. `plugin.yml` lists the pattern in a comment; each
number is its own node, so none is declared.

ExyliaLib boosters stack on top: `shop-sell` for shop products and `worth-sell` for worth items, each per
currency. A worth item's price after bonus, booster and wand is still held below what the shop charges for
the same item and below what crafting it from shop items costs.

## The worth list

```yaml title="modules/worth/worth.yml"
DIAMOND: 50
IRON_INGOT: 6
EMERALD: "40:gems"
```

One line per material: a price per single item, or `"price:currency"` for a currency other than the
default. The material is matched in any case. Only plain items sell this way — no custom name, lore,
custom model or plugin data, no filled container. Enchantments, damage and potion type are **not** checked:
an enchanted tool sells at its material's price.

The file is written once, when it does not exist, from the plugin's value table minus every material the
default shop already buys back. After that it is yours and never overwritten.

Edit it in game:

- `/marketadmin setworth <price> [material]` (the old `/setworth` still works) prices the item in hand or a named material. `0` removes it.
- `/marketadmin` → **Worth** opens the worth editor: add from a material picker, edit, remove, find any
  item. Nothing changes until it is saved.

Both keep a line's currency; the currency itself is only changed in the file. Both need
`exyliamarket.worth.admin`.

## /worth

| Command | What it says |
|---|---|
| `/worth` | What the stack in your hand sells for, each and in total. |
| `/worth <material>` | One plain item of it, and a full stack. |
| `/worth inventory` | Everything you carry that sells, added up per currency. |
| `/worth menu` | Every price, browsable. |
| `/worth history [material]` | The 7-day price chart of what players paid each other. |

Each answer is followed by what players paid for it on the market when there are enough sales.
`/worth` shows base prices, without the sell bonus or boosters, and does not check limits or permissions,
so `/worth inventory` may differ from what `/sell all` pays.

The worth menu lists every material that sells, with its price each and per stack, who buys it — the shop
or the worth list — its category and its market price. The category button steps through `categories.shown`
in `config.yml` (left forward, right back), the search matches the name or the material, and a row opens its
price chart.

## The line under items

With `lore.enabled`, every item that sells shows what it sells for, in inventories and containers. It is
drawn on packets only: the item itself never carries the line. It shows base prices, and is not shown in
creative.

- `lore.menus` draws it on items in plugin menus too; off by default, except in the sell window.
- Under a filled shulker box or bundle it shows what the contents sell for, at any depth.
- Each player can hide it for themselves in `/settings` under **Worth under items**.

## The price audit

`/selladmin` (or `/selladmin audit`) checks every price that could print money:

| Finding | Level |
|---|---|
| An item sells for at least what the shop charges for it. | Error |
| A recipe's result sells for more than its ingredients cost in the shop. | Error |
| A recipe's result sells for more than its ingredients sell for. | Warning |
| The shop and `worth.yml` price the same material differently (the shop's price is paid). | Warning |
| A `worth.yml` line that is not an item, has no positive price, or names an unknown currency. | Error |

It reads crafting, smelting and stonecutter recipes, the cheapest choice of each ingredient. It runs ten
seconds after start and every half hour; the console hears only a change. A player with
`exyliamarket.sell.admin` is told on join while problems remain.

In game, `/selladmin` and the **Price audit** card of `/marketadmin` open a screen of every finding, errors
first. Click one for its details, with a button that opens the shop product or the worth list where it is
fixed; **Check again** runs the audit at once. From the console, the findings are printed as lines.

## Balancing sales to the server

Off by default. With `balance.enabled: true`, sales **to the server** cannot print money faster than the
economy can take it. It covers `/sell` and its window, sell wands, autosell and the shop's own sell buttons,
and only sales in the balanced currency (`balance.currency`, blank for the default currency).

- **Daily tiers.** Per player and per item, the base value sold today (before bonuses and boosters) pays full
  price up to `daily.tiers.full-up-to`, `reduced-percent` of it up to `reduced-up-to`, and `beyond-percent`
  after that. Defaults: 250,000, 750,000, 50% and 20%. The player is told when a sale drops into a lower tier.
  `daily.groups` count several materials together; a group with `own-tiers: true` uses its own tiers (the
  default `nether-fungus` group does). The amounts start over at `reset-hour` (server time, default `0`).
- **Rested bonus.** Every offline hour fills a pool by `rested.per-offline-hour-percent` of `full-up-to` (5%),
  up to `rested.cap-days` days (1); sales it covers pay `rested.bonus-percent` more (20%).
- **Market index.** One multiplier on every balanced sale, `(target-per-player / actual) ^ elasticity`, kept
  between `index.min` and `index.max` and moving at most `max-daily-change-percent` a day. The actual money per
  active player (seen within `active-days`) comes from ExyliaEconomy or any plugin that reports the money
  supply; without data the index keeps its last value. Off by default: tune `target-per-player` first.
- **Boosters.** With `boosters.first-tier-only` (default), the sell bonus and boosters multiply only the
  first tier. Materials in `boosters.excluded` are never multiplied.

A sale tried before the player's sales of today have been read (a moment after joining) is refused, never
paid at full price. Staff with `exyliamarket.sell.admin` get the **Economy** card in `/marketadmin`: the payout,
the market index and its trend, the top items and the top sellers. The tables are `market_sell_daily`,
`market_sell_rest` and `market_sell_index`; daily rows older than `keep-days` (30) are deleted.

## Settings

`modules/sell/config.yml`, editable in game from `/marketadmin` (the balance has its own **Sales balance** page).

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.sell-all-scope` | `ALL` | `STORAGE` for the 27 storage slots, `ALL` to include the hotbar. Also what **Add sellable** takes from. |
| `settings.sell-on-close` | `true` | Closing the window sells what is in it. |
| `settings.confirm-sell-all-above` | `5000` | `/sell all` asks first from this much of the default currency. `-1` never asks. |
| `balance.enabled` | `false` | Balance sales to the server, as above. The other `balance.*` keys are described there. |
| `lore.enabled` | `true` | Draw the worth line under items. |
| `lore.menus` | `false` | Also on items of plugin menus. |
| `sounds.sold`, `failed`, `added`, `returned` | | `NAME\|volume\|pitch`. |

Payments carry the reason `sell:command`, `sell:wand` or `sell:autosell`, and `shop:sell` from the shop's
own screens, which is what ExyliaEconomy's ledger and Exylia Analytics show.
