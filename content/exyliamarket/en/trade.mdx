"You drop yours first" is the most reported scam on a survival server. A trade fixes it with a window in
which nothing changes hands until both players have accepted exactly what is on screen and a short
countdown has run out.

Module id `trade`, file `modules/trade/config.yml`, command `/trade`.

## Asking

| How | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/trade <player>` | Sends a request. |
| Sneak + right-click a player | The same, when `request-on-shift-click` is on. It runs `/trade <player>` as the player typed it, so plugins that block commands in combat or jail stop it too. |
| `/trade accept [player]` | Accepts that player's request, or the newest one. |
| `/trade deny` | Denies the newest request. |

Two players asking each other are both accepting. A request is forgotten after `request-expire-seconds`.

Before a request goes out: not yourself, not already trading, not in creative unless `allow-creative`, the
same world when `same-world` is on, within `max-distance` blocks, and the other player accepts trade
requests — **Market** → **Trade requests** in `/settings`, on by default.

## The window

Your grid is on the left, the other player's on the right. Put items in your grid; the other side mirrors
theirs live. Shift-clicking from your inventory fills your grid; anything that could move items the wrong
way is blocked.

- **Money**: with `allow-money` on, the money button opens a page of currencies. Left-click one to offer an
  amount, up to your balance; right-click clears it. Opening the page withdraws both accepts.
- **Accept**: your status button. Any change to either grid or either offer withdraws both accepts.
- **Cancel**: calls the trade off.

When both have accepted, a countdown of `confirm-delay-seconds` (3 by default) runs, in the window and as
the `feedback.trade-countdown` says — `BOSS_BAR`, `TITLE` or `NONE`. Anything changed during it starts over.
At zero both grids are compared again, money moves both ways, then the items, each through
[delivery](/docs/exyliamarket/collect): what does not fit waits in `/collect`.

## What calls a trade off

Closing the window, the cancel button, dying, leaving the server, switching to creative (unless allowed),
another world, walking beyond `max-distance` (checked every second), money no longer there at the swap, a
restart. Each grid goes back to its owner, through `/collect` if they are not there. Taking damage does not
cancel a trade.

<Callout type="warning" title="A crash loses what is in the grids">
Items in an open trade are held in memory. A server crash, as opposed to a restart, loses them.
</Callout>

## Currencies on the money page

`currencies` lists the currency ids that may be offered. Empty, it is every currency the server has, the
default first. The page holds 28. Item and experience currencies are offered as well when the economy
lists them, despite what the key's comment says.

## The window file

`lang/<code>/modules/trade/window.yml` holds the title (`%partner%`), the money page's title and empty text,
and every button: `separator`, `frame`, `own-waiting`, `own-ready`, `partner-waiting`, `partner-ready`,
`info`, `countdown`, `money-own`, `money-partner`, `money-entry`, `money-back`, `cancel`. A key missing from
it is drawn as a barrier. The slots are fixed.

## Settings

Under `settings:` in `modules/trade/config.yml`, editable in game from `/marketadmin`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `request-expire-seconds` | `30` | Seconds before a request is forgotten. `0` or less: never. |
| `request-on-shift-click` | `true` | Sneak + right-click a player asks them to trade. |
| `max-distance` | `10.0` | Blocks apart two players may be. `-1` (or any value up to 0) is anywhere. |
| `same-world` | `true` | Both players in the same world. |
| `confirm-delay-seconds` | `3` | Seconds between both accepts and the swap. |
| `allow-money` | `true` | Money can be offered alongside items. |
| `currencies` | `[]` | Currencies that may be offered. Empty is every one. |
| `allow-creative` | `false` | Whether a player in creative may trade. |
| `blocked-materials` | `[]` | Materials that may not be put in a trade, inside boxes and bundles too. |
| `max-item-kilobytes` | `64` | Heaviest item that may be offered. `0` for no cap. |

And under `sounds:` — `request`, `opened`, `accepted`, `countdown`, `completed`, `cancelled`.

A finished trade is written to the market log with both sides' items and money, counts `trade.completed`,
and fires `MarketDealEvent` once per side. See [Integrations](/docs/exyliamarket/integrations).
