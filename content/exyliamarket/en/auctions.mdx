title: "Auctions"
description: "Timed auctions in any currency: opening one, the bid steps, anti-snipe, auto-bids held up to a ceiling, buy-it-now, how an auction ends, the countdown and the settings."
---

An auction sells to whoever bids highest before the time runs out, in whichever of the server's currencies
the seller chose. Every bid is **paid when it is placed** and held by the auction until it is beaten or the
auction ends, so a bid is a promise the bidder cannot walk away from.

Module id `auctions`, file `modules/auctions/config.yml`, command `/auction` (also `/auctions`).

## Opening an auction

| From | How |
|---|---|
| The **Sell** button of `/auction` | Hold the item, pick a currency if there is a choice, then fill the form: starting price, buy-it-now (optional) and hours. |
| `/auction sell <start> [hours] [buyout] [currency]` | Opens it at once, with the stack in your main hand. `hours` defaults to `default-hours`; a buyout of `0` is none. |

There is no review screen: the auction opens as soon as the checks pass. From the form, a buy-it-now price
far below the 7-day average asks once more.

The checks: a currency the server has, an item in hand, not in `blocked-materials`, not damaged unless
`allow-damaged-items`, not heavier than `max-item-kilobytes`, a start of at least `min-start-price` (the
currency's smallest amount for any currency but the default), a buyout above the start, hours between
`min-hours` and `max-hours`, and fewer running auctions than the player's limit.

`listing-fee-percent` **of the starting price** is charged when the auction opens and is not refunded, not
even when nobody bids. A starting price at or above `broadcast-opened-above` is announced.

## Bidding

`/auction` lists every running auction, ending soonest first; the sort also cycles Newest, Cheapest and
Most expensive, and the search matches the material, the item's name and the seller. Click one to open its
bid screen: current bid, next bid, how many bids, the leader, the time left and your own ceiling, updated
while it is open.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Bid** | Bids exactly the next bid the button shows. If the auction moved in the meantime the click is refused and nothing is charged. |
| **Custom bid** | Type any amount from the next bid up. |
| **Auto-bid** | Sets a ceiling: see below. |
| **Buy it now** | Only when the auction has a buyout. Pays it and ends the auction at once. |

### The next bid

With no bids, the next bid is the starting price. After that it is the top bid plus the larger of
`min-bid-increment-percent` of it and `min-bid-increment`. The absolute step is in the default currency;
in any other currency the step is at least that currency's smallest amount. The seller cannot bid.

A bid or ceiling at or above the buyout price **is** a buyout, at the buyout price.

### Auto-bids

An auto-bid is the most you are willing to pay. **The whole ceiling is taken from your balance at once**
and held, and the auction answers for you:

- On an auction with no bids, it stands at the starting price with you leading.
- Beating the leader's ceiling, it stands one step above the beaten ceiling, never above yours.
- Not beating it, the leader's ceiling answers at once, one step above you, and your money comes straight
  back. Equal ceilings go to whoever set theirs first.

The leader may raise or lower their ceiling without moving the price, as long as it stays at or above the
next bid. When the auction ends, everything held above the winning price is refunded.

### Being outbid

Your held money comes back at once, and you are told on whichever server you are, or on your next join,
with a **[BID AGAIN]** link that runs `/auction view <id>`. With `feedback.outbid-action-bar` on, an action
bar says it too. The seller hears about each new bid.

### Anti-snipe

A bid that moves the price with less than `anti-snipe-seconds` left (30 by default) pushes the end back to
that many seconds from now, so the last word is not whoever clicked last. A leader only raising their own
ceiling does not extend it.

### The countdown

For the last `feedback.auction-countdown-seconds` (60 by default, `0` for none) a boss bar counts down for
the leader and for everybody who bid on it on this server since the server started. The seller has none.

## How it ends

The sweep runs every second, so an auction ends to the second.

| Ending | The item | The money |
|---|---|---|
| With bids | To the winner: inventory, the rest to [`/collect`](/docs/exyliamarket/collect). | The seller is paid the winning bid minus `sales-tax-percent`. What the winner held above it comes back. |
| Bought now | To the buyer. | As above; the previous leader is refunded and told. |
| No bids | Back to the seller. | The fee is not refunded. |

The other bidders get a *lost* line in their history. A win at or above `broadcast-above` is announced.

## Your auctions and bids

| Command | Screen |
|---|---|
| `/auction mine` (also `/auction selling`) | What you are running, against your limit. Click one to cancel it — only while it has **no bids**: an auction with bids runs to the end. |
| My bids, from `/auction` | Running auctions you bid on, leading or outbid, with your ceiling. |
| History, from `/auction` | Auctions you won, lost and sold. |

## Staff

`/auctionadmin` (also `/auctionsadmin`) lists every running auction, ending soonest first. Left-click
removes one after a confirmation, even with bids: the item goes back to the seller and the top bidder's
held money is refunded, and both are told. Right-click opens its line in the market log.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/auctionadmin remove <id>` | Removes one auction. |
| `/auctionadmin clear <player>` | Removes every auction a player is running. |

Both need `exyliamarket.auctions.admin` and must be run by a player in game.

## Settings

Under `settings:` in `modules/auctions/config.yml`, editable in game from `/marketadmin`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-auctions-per-player` | `3` | Auctions running at once. `exyliamarket.auctions.limit.<n>` replaces it. |
| `min-start-price` | `1.0` | Least an auction may start at, in the default currency. |
| `min-bid-increment-percent` | `5.0` | A new bid beats the top one by at least this share of it… |
| `min-bid-increment` | `1.0` | …and by at least this much, whichever is more. |
| `min-hours` | `1` | Shortest an auction may run. |
| `max-hours` | `72` | Longest an auction may run. |
| `default-hours` | `24` | How long it runs when the seller does not say. |
| `anti-snipe-seconds` | `30` | The window a late bid pushes the end back to. `0` turns it off. |
| `listing-fee-percent` | `2.0` | Of the starting price, charged when it opens. Not refunded. |
| `sales-tax-percent` | `5.0` | Taken from the winning bid before the seller is paid. |
| `broadcast-above` | `50.0` | Announce a win from this price. `-1` never. |
| `broadcast-opened-above` | `50.0` | Announce an auction opening from this starting price. `-1` never. |
| `broadcast-opened-cooldown-seconds` | `60` | Seconds before one seller's next auction is announced again. |
| `blocked-materials` | `BEDROCK`, `COMMAND_BLOCK`, `BARRIER`, `STRUCTURE_BLOCK` | Materials that may never be auctioned. |
| `allow-damaged-items` | `true` | Whether a damaged item may be auctioned. |
| `max-item-kilobytes` | `64` | Heaviest item that may be auctioned. `0` for no cap. |
| `min-counted-value` | `10.0` | Smallest bid or win, in the default currency, that counts toward statistics and missions. |
| `history-size` | `45` | Auctions the history screen shows. |

And under `sounds:` — `listed`, `bid`, `outbid`, `won`, `sold`, `cancelled` — each `NAME|volume|pitch`.
Announcements are muted per player in `/settings` under **Market** → **Auctions**.
