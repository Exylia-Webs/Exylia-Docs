You end up with the emote catalogue drawn in the world in front of the player instead of in an inventory. The emote they pick is danced live on a pedestal by a body in their own skin, so they see it before they perform it. It is the same catalogue as [the classic menu](/docs/exyliaemotes/menu).

## Before you start

You need the plugin installed, see [Install ExyliaEmotes](/docs/exyliaemotes/installation). Players need `exyliaemotes.command.emote`, the same node as the classic menu. The selector needs a Minecraft 1.21.4 client or newer; older clients get the classic window.

## Steps

Make `/emote` open the selector:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the style">
    In `plugins/ExyliaEmotes/config.yml`, set `menu.style: SPATIAL`. The default is `CLASSIC`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/emotesadmin reload`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Or place a selector in the lobby that players click:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stand where it should be">
    Walk to the spot and run `/emotesadmin spatial place`. Confirm where you stand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click it">
    The selector is a hologram in the world. Clicking it walks the player onto the spot and opens the selector there, facing the way you placed it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/emote`: with `SPATIAL` the selector opens in front of you. `/emote 3d` (alias `showroom`) opens it with any style, and `/emote classic` always opens the chest window. You should see tabs along the top, an arc of emotes, a pedestal dancing the one you look at, and **PLAY** under it. Sneak to leave.

## Going further

### What players see

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tabs | The categories along the top, plus **All emotes** and the favourites star. The mouse wheel steps through them. |
| Name and description | The emote under the crosshair, with its rarity and whether it is yours, and up to three lines of description. |
| The arc | Four emotes either side of the preview. The wheel or the arrows turn a page. Owned emotes come first. |
| Preview | The chosen emote danced by a body in the viewer's skin. A duet is danced by two. |
| **PLAY** | On an owned emote, closes the selector and performs it. On one for sale it reads **BUY & PLAY**, then **CONFIRM** with the price. With no price it reads **LOCKED**. |
| Icons | Favourite, replay, open the crate, help (the tour again) and leave. |

While it is open the player is held still and sees nobody. A duet cannot be started from here: PLAY on one tells the player to use `/emote play <id> <player>`. Locked emotes follow the same rules as the classic menu (`behaviour.hide-without-permission`, `behaviour.require-permission`), and buying uses the same shop and price.

### The tour

The first time a player opens it, a guided tour points at the arc, the preview, PLAY and the icons. The **?** icon shows it again. `/emotesadmin spatial tour <player>` makes it show on that player's next open.

### Remove or move a selector

`/emotesadmin spatial remove` removes the nearest placed one, up to four blocks away. `/emotesadmin spatial edit` moves the pieces of the layout: look at a piece and click it, the mouse wheel moves it, **F** changes the axis, and the next click saves it. Sneak to leave.

### Older clients

<Callout type="note" title="Older clients get the classic window">
A player joining with a client older than 1.21.4 (through ViaBackwards) is opened the classic window instead, wherever they asked from. With no ViaVersion on the server, every client counts as the server's version.
</Callout>

### The crate from here

The crate icon opens the crate. With `crate.opening: SPATIAL`, the opening is drawn in the world too, see [The crate](/docs/exyliaemotes/crate#opening-in-3d).

<Details title="Technical details: files and words">
The positions of every piece are in `spatial/emotes.yml`. Coordinates are blocks on a board a little over four blocks in front of the eyes. Placed spots are kept in `spatial/spots.yml`.

Every word drawn on the selector is in `messages.yml` under `spatial`, so it follows the server language like the rest; `spatial.catalog` holds the buttons, the hint lines and the tour's captions. The ExyliaLib [colours](/docs/exylialib/colours) work in them. When a release changes the shipped layout it raises `menu-version` at the top of the file, and `/exylialib updates` offers the change to a server that edited its copy.

`/emotesadmin reload` re-reads the layout and the words, and redraws every selector that is open.

| How it opens | What opens |
|---|---|
| `/emote 3d` (alias `showroom`) | The selector, always. |
| `/emote` | The selector with `menu.style: SPATIAL`; the classic window with `CLASSIC`. |
| `/emote classic` | The classic window, always. |
| A placed selector | The selector, at the spot. |
</Details>
