Every emote is an entry in `emotes.yml`. The key is its **id**: it is what commands, permissions,
placeholders and the crate call it, lowercased. The tables below are the shipped catalogue in the
order each tab draws it.

| Column | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Rarity** | Its `tier`: the colour it is named in, how often the crate lands on it, and which `exyliaemotes.tier.<tier>` grants it. |
| **Kind** | `solo`, or `duet` for one that takes two players. See [Duets](/docs/exyliaemotes/duets). |
| **Plays** | `endless` loops until the player stops it; `once` ends on its own. |
| **Cooldown** | Its own cooldown. `—` means only the server's `behaviour.cooldown` applies. |
| **Prop** | What is drawn besides the body. |

## Writing an entry

```yaml
categories:
  social:
    name: 'Social'
    material: PLAYER_HEAD
    priority: 1

emotes:
  wave:
    category: social
    tier: common
    name: '{primary}&lWAVE'
    material: PLAYER_HEAD
    priority: 1
    cooldown: 8s
    description:
      - '{letters}A hand up and two turns of the wrist.'
      - '{letters}The one everybody tries first.'
    effects:
      - '[RAGDOLL] {victim};intact:0;follow:1;loop:true;tempo:0.90-1.20;loop_from:0.42;keys:...'
      - '[CAMERA] loop:true;keys:0.00 distance=2.9 yaw=25 pitch=12 height=1.5 | 5.00 yaw=~360 ease=linear'
      - '[SOUND] BLOCK_AMETHYST_BLOCK_CHIME;0.4;1.9;repeat:240;every:0.44'
```

### A category

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | The key | What the tab is called. |
| `material` | `PAPER` | What the tab is drawn as. |
| `priority` | `999` | Where the tab sits; lower comes first. |

A category with no emotes in it gets no tab. The key is also its permission:
`exyliaemotes.category.<key>`.

### An emote

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `category` | `social` | Which tab it appears under. An emote naming an undeclared category is logged and kept: no tab shows it, but commands can still play and grant it. |
| `tier` | `common` | Its rarity, one of `config.yml`'s `tiers` keys. One that is not declared is logged, and the emote falls back to the first rarity. |
| `kind` | `solo` | `duet` for two players. `duo`, `pair`, `two` and `together` are read as `duet`; anything else is `solo`. |
| `name` | The key | What menus, messages and placeholders call it. |
| `material` | `BARRIER` | What it is drawn as in the menu and the crate. |
| `description` | Empty | One line or a list of lines. `<nl>` also breaks a single line. `lore` is read as the same key. |
| `priority` | `999` | Where it sits in its tab; lower comes first. |
| `cooldown` | None | Its own cooldown, written out: `5s`, `1m30s`. A bare number is seconds. One that cannot be read is logged and ignored. |
| `gap` | `1.51` | For a duet, how far apart the two bodies stand, in blocks. Never less than `0.5`. |
| `effects` | — | The steps that play. An emote with none is logged and refuses to play. |

A mistyped entry is reported on the console with the entry it came from and never costs the server its
other emotes.

## The steps

An emote is one `[RAGDOLL]` line and one `[CAMERA]` line, plus any sounds and props around them. Every
token of ExyliaLib's sequence module can be used; the comment at the top of the shipped `emotes.yml`
explains the body and camera parameters frame by frame.

| Line | What it is |
|---|---|
| `[RAGDOLL] {victim};...;keys:<frames>` | The body. `{victim}` is the player performing it: the body wears their skin and faces where they were looking. |
| `[CAMERA] keys:<frames>` | The shot the player watches from. It always looks at the body. |
| `[SOUND]`, `[CIRCLE]`, `[SCATTER]`, `[SPHERE]`, `[FIREWORK]`, `[DELAY]` | Everything else: what is heard, and props drawn around the body. |

<Callout type="note" title="Nothing is heard past sixteen blocks">
A `[SOUND]` volume above `1.0` is lowered to `1.0` as the file is read. Above one the client does not
play it louder, it plays it further — sixteen blocks times the number — and an emote is played over
and over in lobbies by anybody who feels like it.
</Callout>

### Endless emotes

An emote is endless when its `[RAGDOLL]` line says `loop:true`. It is written as an entry and a
cycle:

| Parameter | What it does |
|---|---|
| `loop:true` | The body plays its cycle again and again. |
| `loop_from:<seconds>` | Where the entry ends and the cycle starts. The last frame has to leave the body in exactly the pose this point left it in, or it snaps back once a cycle. |
| `tempo:<min>-<max>` | A speed rolled once per play and shared by every piece of that body, so the same dance is a shade quicker or slower every time. |
| `accel:` / `max_speed:` | Wind the cycle up a little every time round, up to a ceiling. Only `six_seven` does it. |

The camera of an endless emote loops too (`loop:true`, usually a full turn with `yaw=~360`), and a
sound that has to keep the beat is written `repeat:<times>;every:<seconds>`: its `every:` follows the
rolled tempo.

An endless emote has no length of its own. It lasts until the player stops it, or until
`behaviour.endless-limit` — five minutes by default — ends it for a player who walked away from their
keyboard mid-dance.

### Without a camera

With `camera.enabled: false` every `[CAMERA]` line is taken out as the catalogue is compiled. The emote
still plays for everybody around the player; the player simply does not see it. A showcase never plays
the camera line either — see [Showcases](/docs/exyliaemotes/showcases).

<Callout type="warning" title="Renaming a key is not free">
The key is the id, the permission and what the crate unlocked. An emote whose key you rename is a new
emote: whoever owned the old one by permission or by crate unlock does not own the new one.
</Callout>
