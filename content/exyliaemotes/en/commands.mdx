Every command, with the permission it needs. Emote ids and player names complete as you type, and a player only sees the emotes they own.

## Player commands

<Commands group="player" />

`/emote` on its own only opens the menu. To perform an emote you always write `/emote play <emote>`, so an emote id can never collide with a subcommand such as `accept`. The name after `accept` and `deny` in a chat button is ignored: a player only ever has one invitation waiting. See [Duets](/docs/exyliaemotes/duets).

## Admin commands

Everything under `/emotesadmin` needs `exyliaemotes.admin`. On its own it opens the admin hub: the catalogue, players, showcases and crate blocks in one menu, plus a **SETTINGS** button that edits `config.yml` and `emotes.yml` in game. The subcommands are shortcuts to the same things.

<Callout type="note" title="A player does not have to be online">
`keys`, `unlocks` and `player favourites clear` take a name and write straight to the player's account when they are offline, so a reward or a support ticket is settled at once. Everything else that names a player needs them online.
</Callout>

## Good to know

- `/emotesadmin play` skips only the ownership check. A player already performing, in combat, on cooldown or in a world where emotes are off is not made to perform anyway.
- `crate block add`, `showcase add`, `preview location` and `spatial place` ask you to walk to a spot and confirm. `crate block remove` uses the block you are looking at.
- `crate block clear` and `showcase clear` do nothing until you add `confirm`.
- To wipe everything a player has (keys, unlocks, favourites), use ExyliaLib: `/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaEmotes @account`. See [ExyliaLib's commands](/docs/exylialib/commands#moving-and-wiping-data).

### What `reload` does

`/emotesadmin reload` re-reads `config.yml`, `messages.yml`, `emotes.yml`, the menus of the language in use and `spatial/emotes.yml`, rebuilds the crate, restarts every showcase and redraws every open 3D selector. It does not reload ExyliaLib; `/exylialib reload` does that.

<Callout type="warning" title="Renaming an emote key makes a new emote">
An emote whose key you renamed is a new emote: whoever owned the old one does not own the new one.
</Callout>
