Quick answers about updates, requirements and writing your own emotes.

## Will an update overwrite my emotes.yml?

No. Your catalogue is `lang/<code>/emotes.yml`: new emotes from an update are added to it; anything you changed or deleted is left alone.

## Can I write my own emotes?

Yes — that is what `emotes.yml` is for. An emote is a `[RAGDOLL]` line and a `[CAMERA]` line; the
comment at the top of the shipped file documents both, and [Emotes](/docs/exyliaemotes/emotes) covers
every key of an entry. A line that cannot be read is reported on the console; the rest of the catalogue
still loads.

## Does it need PacketEvents?

Yes, it will not load without it (see [Installation](/docs/exyliaemotes/installation)). The camera, the hidden player and the way out of an emote are all
packets.

## Is there an API?

No. ExyliaEmotes publishes no service in `exylia-api`.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, on Folia 1.21.4 or newer. On Paper it runs from 1.21.1.

## What do players on older clients see?

The [3D selector](/docs/exyliaemotes/3d-selector) and the 3D crate opening need a 1.21.4 client. A player joining with an older one
(through ViaBackwards) is opened the classic menu and the inventory reels instead, without being told
anything is missing.

## Where do I get support?

On the [Exylia Discord](https://discord.exylia.net).
