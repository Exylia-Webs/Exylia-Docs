You end up with a lobby centrepiece: a spot that performs emotes by itself, over and over, for whoever walks past. Nobody is performing. The body wears the skin of a player standing nearby, so the crowd watching is the cast.

## Before you start

You need the plugin installed and permission `exyliaemotes.admin`, see [Permissions](/docs/exyliaemotes/permissions). Decide which emotes to show, or leave the list empty to use every solo emote.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the placement wizard">
    Stand in the lobby and run `/emotesadmin showcase add`. You can also use `/emotesadmin` and click **Showcases**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk to the spot and confirm">
    The place is written to `showcase.locations` in `config.yml` and starts at once. Place as many as you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what it plays (optional)">
    In `config.yml`, list emote ids under `showcase.emotes`, for example `[wave, dance, floss]`, then run `/emotesadmin reload`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Stand within 24 blocks of the spot. A body wearing your skin performs an emote, rests three seconds, then performs another. Walk further away and nothing plays. `/emotesadmin showcase list` prints every showcase.

## Going further

### Remove a showcase

`/emotesadmin showcase remove` removes the one nearest you, up to five blocks away (alias `showcase delete`). `/emotesadmin showcase clear confirm` removes all of them.

### How it chooses

Every place is checked once a second. Only players within `showcase.radius` (24 blocks by default) count, so an empty lobby costs one distance check a second. It picks an emote at random from `showcase.emotes` (or from the whole catalogue when empty), never the one that just played unless it is the only one. One watcher at random lends the body their skin, and the body turns to face another one. A one-shot emote plays to its end; an endless one is shown for eight seconds. Then it rests `showcase.pause-seconds` (three by default).

### What a showcase never plays

- **Duets.** Half a handshake is a body reaching out at nothing, so they are never picked, whatever `showcase.emotes` lists.
- **`[CAMERA]` lines.** A decoration must never take the eyes of somebody who only walked past it.
- **`[COMMAND]`, `[POTION]`, `[TITLE]`, `[ACTION_BAR]`, `[MESSAGE]`.** They act on a real player rather than drawing something.

What is left is scenery: the body, its sounds and the props drawn around it.

<Details title="Settings">
```yaml
showcase:
  locations: []
  pause-seconds: 3.0
  radius: 24.0
  emotes: []
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `locations` | `[]` | Where emotes play on a loop, as `server,world,x,y,z,yaw,pitch`. Set them with `showcase add`. |
| `pause-seconds` | `3.0` | The rest between one emote ending and the next starting. |
| `radius` | `24.0` | How close a player has to be for a showcase to play and to see it, in blocks. |
| `emotes` | `[]` | The emote ids a showcase picks from. Empty picks from every solo emote. |

A location in a world that is not loaded yet is logged and starts the moment that world loads, so a hub a world manager brings up after the plugin works as expected. `/emotesadmin reload` restarts every showcase.
</Details>
