---
title: "Showcases"
description: "Places that perform emotes on their own, one after another, cast with the players standing nearby."
---

A **showcase** is a spot that performs emotes by itself, over and over, for whoever walks past: a lobby
centrepiece rather than something a player asked for. Nobody is performing. The body wears the skin of
a player standing nearby, so the crowd watching is the cast.

## Setting one up

```text
/emotesadmin showcase add
```

Writes where you stand to `showcase.locations` in `config.yml` and starts it at once. Place as many as
you like.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `showcase add` | Plays emotes on a loop where you stand. |
| `showcase remove` | Removes the showcase nearest you, up to five blocks away. Alias: `showcase delete`. |
| `showcase list` | Prints every showcase. |
| `showcase clear` | Removes all of them. |

## What it plays

Every place is checked once a second, on its own region:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Is anybody watching?">
    Only players within `showcase.radius` — 24 blocks by default — count. With nobody that close
    nothing plays, so an empty lobby costs one distance check a second.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an emote">
    At random from `showcase.emotes`, or from the whole catalogue when that list is empty — never the
    one that just played, unless it is the only one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a cast">
    One watcher at random lends the body their skin, and the body turns to face another one, so a
    showcase between two players has one of them waving at the other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rest">
    A one-shot emote plays to its end; an endless one is shown for eight seconds and then ended. Then
    `showcase.pause-seconds` — three by default — before the next.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only the players within `radius` see it.

## What it never plays

| Left out | Why |
|---|---|
| Duets | A showcase plays one body at one spot. Half a handshake is a body reaching out at nothing, so duets are never picked, whatever `showcase.emotes` lists. |
| `[CAMERA]` lines | A decoration must never take the eyes of somebody who only walked past it. |
| `[COMMAND]`, `[POTION]`, `[TITLE]`, `[ACTION_BAR]`, `[MESSAGE]` | They act on a real player rather than drawing something. |

What is left is scenery: the body, its sounds, and whatever props are drawn around it.

## Settings

```yaml
showcase:
  locations: []
  pause-seconds: 3.0
  radius: 24.0
  emotes: []
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `locations` | `[]` | Where emotes play on a loop, as `server,world,x,y,z,yaw,pitch`. Set them with `showcase add`. |
| `pause-seconds` | `3.0` | The rest between one emote ending and the next starting. |
| `radius` | `24.0` | How close a player has to be for a showcase to play and to see it, in blocks. |
| `emotes` | `[]` | The emote ids a showcase picks from. Empty picks from every solo emote. |

A location in a world that is not loaded yet is logged and starts the moment that world loads — a hub a
world manager brings up after the plugin works as expected. `/emotesadmin reload` restarts every
showcase.
