---
title: "Introduction"
description: "What ExyliaEmotes is: 68 emotes performed by an animated body and filmed from outside, so the player watches themselves do it."
---

ExyliaEmotes lets a player perform an **emote**: a wave, a dance, a handshake, a crown lowering onto
their head. The emote is not an animation of the player's own model. The real player is hidden, an
animated **body** is drawn where they stand, and a **camera** is opened outside it so the player can
watch themselves perform it. When it ends the body disappears, the camera closes and the real player
is back exactly where they were.

Everything visual belongs to ExyliaLib: the body is a `[RAGDOLL]` line and the camera a `[CAMERA]`
line, written in `emotes.yml` and compiled once by the sequence module. What this plugin owns is the
catalogue, who may play what, and when an emote has to stop. An emote somebody can write in a file is
an emote a server can add without waiting for a release.

## The catalogue

**68 emotes in four tabs** ship in `emotes.yml`:

| Tab | Icon | Emotes | What is in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `social` | `PLAYER_HEAD` | 11 | Greetings, and the two that take a friend. |
| `fun` | `SLIME_BALL` | 21 | Showing off, dancing, and the ones aimed at somebody in particular. |
| `meme` | `NOTE_BLOCK` | 19 | The ones the internet already knows. |
| `party` | `FIREWORK_ROCKET` | 17 | Celebrations, and props that do something that cannot happen. |

**52 of them never stop**: they loop until the player presses a key, is hit or asks. The other 16 play
once. Two — `highfive` and `handshake` — take two players. The full list is in
[Emotes](/docs/exyliaemotes/emotes).

## How it plays out

<Steps>
  <Step title="A player opens the menu">
    `/emote` is one screen: the tabs down the side, that tab's emotes beside them, their favourites
    first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They click one">
    The window closes and the emote starts where they are standing, facing the way they were
    looking. Everybody around them sees the body; they see it from the camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They get out">
    Any click, crouching, jumping, a walking key, a hit, or a punch at the body ends it. A one-shot
    emote also ends on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Owning an emote

A player may perform an emote when **either** is true:

| Way | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| A permission | `exyliaemotes.emote.<id>`, the whole catalogue, its tab or its rarity. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaemotes/permissions). |
| A crate unlock | Won out of the crate or granted by an admin, and kept on the player's account for good. See [The crate](/docs/exyliaemotes/crate). |

Previewing an emote on the server's preview stage never needs either.

## What the emote does not do

It does not protect anybody. The real player is still standing where they started, with a hitbox,
unable to see who is next to them. Being hit ends the emote and the damage lands as it always would;
nothing is cancelled.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21 or newer. Folia supported. |
| Required | `packetevents` |
| Recommended | `ExyliaLib` (a soft depend: the loader installs it) |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI` |
| Database | Always. Favourites live in one table, keys and unlocks in ExyliaLib's crate table. |
