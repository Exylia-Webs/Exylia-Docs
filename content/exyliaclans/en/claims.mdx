Give a clan its own protected piece of land: one rectangle that the leader marks with a golden hoe and pays for from the clan bank.

## Before you start

- The clan needs money in its bank, see `/clan deposit`. The price comes out of the **clan bank**, never out of the player's own balance. Money setup is in [Economy](/docs/exylialib/economy).
- The member claiming needs the `CLAIM_LAND` rank permission, see [Ranks](/docs/exyliaclans/roles).
- Decide how land is protected before anyone claims, see [Protection mode](#protection-mode). The shipped default needs WorldGuard.
- The `claims` module must be on, see [Turn clan systems on and off](/docs/exyliaclans/modules).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a selection">
    Run `/clan claim`. You get a **golden hoe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the two corners">
    Left-click a block for corner 1, right-click a block for corner 2. Glass and diamond-block towers show each corner, only to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay and confirm">
    **Sneak and click** (any click, left or right, on a block or in the air). The price is taken from the clan bank and the land is yours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To cancel, run `/clan claim` again. Sneaking turns every click into a confirmation, so hold shift only when you want to buy the land.

## Check that it works

Run `/clan map`. Your claim shows as green glass pillars with an emerald block. Walk over its border: a message names the clan that owns the land, on the way in and on the way out. Then ask somebody outside the clan to break a block inside it: it should be refused.

## Price

```
price = base-price + (X × Z) × price-per-block
```

With the defaults, a base of 500 and 1.0 per block, a 20 × 20 rectangle costs 500 + 400 = **900**. A clan owns at most one claim, between 25 and 10 000 blocks by default.

A claim is refused when:

- the clan already has one, or the two corners are in different worlds;
- the area is under `min-area` (25) or over `max-area` (10 000);
- it overlaps a WorldGuard region or another clan's claim;
- it is within `min-separation` blocks (5) of another clan's claim; `0` turns this off;
- the clan bank cannot pay.

## Protection mode

```yaml
claims:
  protection-mode: WORLDGUARD
```

| Mode | Who enforces it |
|---|---|
| `BUILTIN` | This plugin. It checks block break and place, doors, containers, buckets and item frames on every action. |
| `WORLDGUARD` | A WorldGuard region named after the clan, created and resized with the claim. Every member is a region member and the leader is the owner. |

<Callout type="danger" title="WORLDGUARD without WorldGuard means no protection">
`WORLDGUARD` is the shipped default. If WorldGuard is not installed, no region is created and the built-in checks stay off too: claims are drawn and paid for but protect nothing. Set `BUILTIN` before anybody claims.
</Callout>

Under `BUILTIN`, three rank permissions decide who may act inside the claim: `BUILD`, `OPEN_DOORS` and `OPEN_CONTAINERS`. A player from another clan is always refused. `exyliaclans.claim.bypass` skips all of it. Under `WORLDGUARD` those three permissions are not read; the region decides.

## Going further

### Raidable claims

When the owning clan's DTR drops below zero its claim stops protecting anything. It protects again once the DTR regenerates above zero. See [DTR](/docs/exyliaclans/dtr).

### Shrink a claim

Run `/clan reclaim` (needs `CLAIM_LAND`). It uses the same tool, with the current bounds shown in orange glass and gold blocks. The new rectangle must fit **inside** the old one, so a reclaim only shrinks a claim. It costs `reclaim-fee` (default `0.0`), is off if `reclaim-enabled` is `false`, and is refused while the clan is raidable.

### Give the land up

Run `/clan unclaim` (needs `UNCLAIM_LAND`). It is refused unless the clan's DTR state is `NORMAL`, so a clan cannot drop its land in the middle of a raid.

<Callout type="warning" title="Unclaiming clears the clan home">
The home has to be inside the claim, so removing the claim removes the home too. Setting it again means claiming again first.
</Callout>

### See every claim nearby

`/clan map` draws every claim within `map-radius` (200 blocks) as coloured pillars, only for you: green for your own, blue for an ally's, red for anybody else's. It refreshes every five seconds and switches itself off after two minutes, or when you run it again, change world or log out.

<Details title="Technical details">
```yaml
claims:
  base-price: 500.0
  price-per-block: 1.0
  min-area: 25
  max-area: 10000
  pillar-duration-seconds: 10
  min-separation: 5
  map-radius: 200
  reclaim-enabled: true
  reclaim-fee: 0.0
  protection-mode: WORLDGUARD
```

All defaults above are the ones the plugin writes into `config.yml`. A claim is stored as `minX`, `minZ`, `maxX`, `maxZ` and has no vertical bound: it owns its columns from bedrock to sky. `exyliaclans.claimarea.<n>` on the clan leader replaces `max-area` (highest wins, read while the leader is online).

The golden hoe cannot be dropped or stored (opening any inventory is blocked during a session), does not drop on death and is removed from the inventory on join. Towers are packet-only, so no block is placed, and only empty space is drawn, so a tower breaks where terrain is in the way.

Under `BUILTIN`, blocks of a claim whose owner is not raidable are removed from explosion block lists. Under `WORLDGUARD` the region's own flags decide, and a raid sets the region's `BUILD` flag to `ALLOW`.

Blocked actions print their own line, at most once every 1.5 seconds per player. With no claim inside the radius `/clan map` draws nothing and tells the radius, but still counts as open, so the next `/clan map` reports it as hidden.

`pillar-duration-seconds` is generated into the file, but nothing in the claim flow shows pillars for it: the only pillars a player sees are the selection preview and `/clan map`.
</Details>
