## A player cannot use a command

Every `/clan` node defaults to `true`, so check whether a group negates it. More often the answer is the
clan: most subcommands also need a rank permission, and the shipped **Member** rank holds only `FOCUS`.
See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaclans/permissions) and [Roles](/docs/exyliaclans/roles).

## Claims are not protecting anything

`claims.protection-mode` ships as `WORLDGUARD`. If WorldGuard is not installed, no region is created and
the built-in listener stands down, so the claim is drawn and paid for and protects nothing. Set it to
`BUILTIN`, or install WorldGuard.

Check next that the owning clan is not raidable: a raidable clan's claim is open on purpose.

## A member cannot break blocks in our own claim

Under `BUILTIN` that is the `BUILD` rank permission, which the shipped **Member** rank does not have.
Tick it in `/clan` → **Roles**, or add it to `default-roles` for clans created from now on.

## `/clan sethome` says I must be inside the claim

You must — while the claims module is on. Claim first, then set the home inside it. With
`modules.claims: false` the home can be set anywhere.

## We unclaimed and lost our home

That is deliberate: the home lives inside the claim, so removing the claim removes it. Both are written
to the log.

## `/clan deposit` says the economy is unavailable

There is no economy plugin ExyliaLib can read. Install one — the bank is what pays for land, so without
it no claim can be bought either.

## A clan cannot disband or unclaim

Both are refused unless the DTR state is `NORMAL`. A clan whose member has just **died** is `FROZEN` for
ten minutes — leaving and being kicked cost nothing and freeze nothing, and a death in a
`no-loss-worlds` world costs and freezes nothing either. `/clanadmin disband` bypasses the check.

## We are stuck raidable

DTR regenerates `regen-amount` (0.1) every `regen-interval-seconds` (45 s) — but only while the clan is
**not frozen**, and every death restarts the ten-minute freeze. A clan being farmed never leaves the
freeze. Lower `frozen-duration-seconds`, raise `regen-amount`, or add the world to `no-loss-worlds` if
deaths there should not count.

## Deaths in the lobby cost us DTR

Add the world to `dtr.no-loss-worlds`. The lobby, the event world and any practice world belong there.

## Typing `.` in chat sends to my clan

`settings.chat.quick-clan-prefixes` is `["."]` and `quick-ally-prefixes` is `["@"]`. Change them to
something nobody starts a sentence with — `c:` and `a:` — or empty the lists to disable the shortcuts.

## The menus and messages are in the wrong language

`language` in `config.yml` decides it. `default` follows ExyliaLib's own `language`; `en`, `es`, `pt` or
`fr` set this plugin alone. A server that ran 1.1.0 is on `custom`, its own files from before — set
another value to switch. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaclans/configuration#language).

## Our invitation or alliance request vanished

Both expire after `settings.invite-expire-seconds` (60 s) and the player or the requesting clan is told.
Raise it, or set `0` to keep them until they are answered. They are stored, so a restart no longer
drops them.

## "You invited that player recently"

A clan can invite the same player once every `settings.invite-cooldown-seconds` (60 s). Creating a clan
and sending mail have waits of their own — see [Commands](/docs/exyliaclans/commands#anti-spam-waits).

## We are allied but still hitting each other

Both clans need friendly fire **on** for allies to be protected — one is not enough. Check the world is
not in `settings.forced-ff-worlds`, which overrides every clan's setting.

## Members can no longer focus, camp or rally

Those three are rank permissions since 1.2.0: `FOCUS`, `CAMP` and `RALLY`. Ranks that existed before the
update were given all three once; a new clan's **Member** rank holds only `FOCUS`. Tick the others in
`/clan` → **Roles**.

## The webhook button is missing from the settings menu

`webhooks.enabled` is `false`. With it on, every member sees the button, and one without
`MANAGE_WEBHOOK` is refused on click. See [Discord webhooks](/docs/exyliaclans/webhooks).

## Our clan lost a level

Deaths cost 5 EXP and going raidable costs 50, and the level is recomputed from the total. Members are
never kicked for it; the clan just cannot recruit past the smaller cap until it climbs back.

## The kills leaderboard is higher than the members' kills add up to

`rivals.kill-stat-multiplier` inflates the clan's recorded kill for a rival kill; the per-player row
always records one. Set it to `1.0` to keep the two in step.

## A scoreboard slot is blank

An online-roster slot with nobody in it resolves to nothing. Use the fallback syntax —
`%exyliaclans_clan_online_member_5|%` — or check the console: a placeholder nothing answers is reported
once by name.

## `/clan map` shows nothing

Nothing is claimed within `claims.map-radius` (200 blocks). The command says so and names the radius. The
map also switches itself off after two minutes.

## Menu edits are not showing up

Check you are editing the folder of the language in use, `lang/<language>/menus/`. `/clanadmin reload`
recompiles every menu. If the file is still not read, the console names the part it
could not make sense of — the rest of the screen opens without it.

## I changed default-roles and existing clans did not change

They will not. `default-roles` describes a clan on the day it is created; after that its ranks are rows in
`clan_roles` the clan itself edits.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes — `folia-supported: true`, and scheduling goes through ExyliaLib's Folia-aware scheduler.

## Can I run it without packetevents?

No. It is a hard dependency, and the server will not enable the plugin without it.
