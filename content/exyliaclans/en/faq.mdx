Something is not working? Start with [Common problems](/docs/exyliaclans/troubleshooting). This page answers what people ask before they buy or while they plan their setup.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, on Folia 1.21.4 or newer (Paper from 1.21.1). Scheduling goes through ExyliaLib's Folia-aware scheduler.

## Can I run it without packetevents?

Yes. It is an optional dependency: without it the plugin enables normally and only the packet-drawn extras stay off (the friend/enemy colouring, the focus glow, the claim selection tool and the `/clan map` pillars).

## Do I need WorldGuard?

No. Set `claims.protection-mode` to `BUILTIN` and the plugin enforces claims itself. The shipped default is `WORLDGUARD`, which protects nothing when WorldGuard is missing. See [Claim land for a clan](/docs/exyliaclans/claims#protection-mode).

## Do I need an economy plugin?

Land is paid from the clan bank, which uses the currency `bank-currency` names. Any economy ExyliaLib can read works, and the bank can also use items or experience. See [Install ExyliaClans](/docs/exyliaclans/installation).

## Which languages are included?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Russian and Turkish. `language: default` follows ExyliaLib's. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaclans/configuration#language).

## Can I turn parts of it off?

Yes. Each of the thirteen modules has its own switch in `config.yml` and can be disabled without touching the rest. See [Turn clan systems on and off](/docs/exyliaclans/modules).

## Can several servers share clans?

Yes. Servers that share one database and Redis behave as one clan system. See [Database](/docs/exyliaclans/database#several-servers).

## How many claims can a clan own?

One rectangle, between 25 and 10 000 blocks by default. See [Claim land for a clan](/docs/exyliaclans/claims).

## Are clan wars scheduled?

No. A clan declares a war, the other clan accepts, and the fight starts then. See [Run clan wars](/docs/exyliaclans/clan-wars).

## I changed default-roles and existing clans did not change

They will not. `default-roles` describes a clan on the day it is created; after that its ranks are rows in `clan_roles` that the clan itself edits. See [Ranks](/docs/exyliaclans/roles).
