ExyliaClans has four commands: `/clan` for players, `/clanadmin` for staff, and `/lfc` and `/lfp` to find a clan or members. Most things you can type are also buttons in the `/clan` menu.

Two checks decide whether a command works. The server permission (a [permission node](/docs/exyliaclans/permissions) from LuckPerms) says the player may run it at all. The clan's own [rank permission](/docs/exyliaclans/roles) says whether their rank may do it inside the clan. Both must pass. Every player node is `true` by default, so out of the box the rank decides.

Commands that leave, decline, cancel or remove never need a node, so nobody gets stuck in something they cannot undo. They are marked below.

## /clanadmin

Staff need `exyliaclans.admin` and the node of the subcommand they run. `/clanadmin` also works from the console, except where it needs an online player (`forcejoin`).

<Commands group="admin" />

### War arenas and kits

Set these up once with `/clanadmin war ...`. They need `exyliaclans.admin.war`. See [Clan wars](/docs/exyliaclans/clan-wars).

## /clan style

`/clan style` lets the leader draw the clan name in colour. The name players type does not change: the styled text must read exactly as the name, ignoring case. A clan called `Nova` can be styled `<gradient:#8a51c4:#59a4ff>Nova</gradient>` or `&b&lNOVA`, never `Nova!`.

What the leader may use depends on the [style nodes](/docs/exyliaclans/permissions#style-nodes), which are `op` by default. Obfuscated text, clicks, hovers and placeholders are always refused, and the style is capped at 512 characters. `/clan style reset` goes back to the plain name.

## Anti-spam waits

| Action | Wait | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| `/clan create` | Between two clans one player creates | `settings.create-cooldown-seconds`, 120 |
| `/clan invite` | Before inviting the same player again | `settings.invite-cooldown-seconds`, 60 |
| `/clan mail send` and `sendclan` | Between two mails | `mail.send-cooldown-seconds`, 30 |

Set a wait to `0` to turn it off. Ranks can get a shorter wait with the [number permissions](/docs/exyliaclans/permissions#per-rank-numbers).

## Reload

`/clanadmin reload` re-reads `config.yml`, `wars.yml` and the language files, starts or stops modules, and recompiles every menu. No restart is needed. `/clanadmin disband <clan> confirm` is the console form of disband.

Wiping the plugin's data or resetting one player is done with ExyliaLib's `/exylialib wipe` and `/exylialib resetplayer`, see [ExyliaLib commands](/docs/exylialib/commands).
