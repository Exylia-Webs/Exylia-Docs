Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

The console names what is missing. ExyliaLib is required. See [Install ExyliaClans](/docs/exyliaclans/installation).

## A player cannot use a command

Every `/clan` node is allowed by default, so first check whether a permission group negates it. More often the cause is the clan: most subcommands also need a rank permission, and the shipped **Member** rank holds only `FOCUS`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaclans/permissions) and [Ranks and permissions](/docs/exyliaclans/roles).

## Claims do not protect anything

`claims.protection-mode` ships as `WORLDGUARD`. Without WorldGuard no region is created and the built-in protection stands down, so the claim is drawn and paid for and protects nothing. Set it to `BUILTIN` or install WorldGuard.

Then check that the owning clan is not raidable: a raidable clan's claim is open on purpose.

## A member cannot build in our own claim

With `BUILTIN` protection this is the `BUILD` rank permission, which the shipped **Member** rank does not have. Tick it in `/clan`, **Settings**, **Roles**, or add it to `default-roles` for clans created from now on.

## `/clan sethome` says I must be inside the claim

That is required while the claims module is on. Claim first, then set the home inside it. With `modules.claims: false` the home can be set anywhere.

## We unclaimed and lost our home

This is on purpose: the home lives inside the claim, so removing the claim removes it. Both are written to the clan log.

## `/clan deposit` says the economy is unavailable

The currency named by `bank-currency` cannot be read. Install an economy plugin that ExyliaLib can read, or point `bank-currency` at another currency from ExyliaLib's `economy.yml`, items and experience included. Without the bank no claim can be bought.

## A clan cannot disband or unclaim

Both need the clan to be in the `NORMAL` DTR state. A clan whose member just died is frozen for ten minutes. Leaving and being kicked cost nothing and freeze nothing, and neither does a death in a world listed in `no-loss-worlds`. `/clanadmin disband` skips the check. See [DTR](/docs/exyliaclans/dtr).

## The clan is stuck raidable

DTR regenerates `regen-amount` (0.1) every `regen-interval-seconds` (45) only while the clan is not frozen, and every death restarts the freeze. A clan being farmed never leaves it. Lower `frozen-duration-seconds`, raise `regen-amount`, or add the world to `no-loss-worlds`.

## Deaths in the lobby cost DTR

Add the world to `dtr.no-loss-worlds`. The lobby, event worlds and practice worlds belong there.

## Typing a dot in chat sends to my clan

`settings.chat.quick-clan-prefixes` is `["."]` and `quick-ally-prefixes` is `["@"]`. Change them to something nobody starts a sentence with, such as `c:` and `a:`, or empty the lists to turn the shortcuts off. See [Chat and mail](/docs/exyliaclans/chat-and-mail).

## The menus and messages are in the wrong language

The `language` key in `config.yml` decides. `default` follows ExyliaLib's own language, and `en`, `es`, `fr`, `pt`, `ru` or `tr` set this plugin alone. A server that ran 1.1.0 is on `custom`, its own files from before. Set another value to switch. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaclans/configuration#language).

## An invitation or alliance request vanished

Both expire after `settings.invite-expire-seconds` (60), and the player or the requesting clan is told. Raise it, or set `0` to keep them until answered. They are stored, so a restart does not drop them.

## "You invited that player recently"

A clan can invite the same player once every `settings.invite-cooldown-seconds` (60). Creating a clan and sending mail have waits of their own, see [Commands](/docs/exyliaclans/commands#anti-spam-waits).

## Allies are still hitting each other

Both clans need friendly fire on for allies to be protected. Also check that the world is not in `settings.forced-ff-worlds`, which overrides every clan's setting.

## Members can no longer focus, camp or rally

Those are the rank permissions `FOCUS`, `CAMP` and `RALLY`. Ranks that existed before 1.2.0 were given all three once, and a new clan's **Member** rank holds only `FOCUS`. Tick the others in `/clan`, **Settings**, **Roles**.

## The webhook button is missing from the settings menu

`webhooks.enabled` is `false`. With it on, every member sees the button, and one without `MANAGE_WEBHOOK` is refused on click. See [Discord webhooks](/docs/exyliaclans/webhooks).

## Our clan lost a level

Deaths cost 5 EXP and going raidable costs 50, and the level is recomputed from the total. Members are never kicked for it, but the clan cannot recruit past the smaller cap until it climbs back. See [Clan levels](/docs/exyliaclans/levels).

## The kills leaderboard is higher than the members' kills

`rivals.kill-stat-multiplier` inflates the clan's recorded kill for a rival kill, while the player's own row records one. Set it to `1.0` to keep the two in step.

## A scoreboard slot is blank

An online-roster slot with nobody in it shows nothing. Use the fallback form, for example `%exyliaclans_clan_online_member_5|%`, and see [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaclans/placeholders).

## `/clan map` shows nothing

Nothing is claimed within `claims.map-radius` (200 blocks). The command says so. The map also switches itself off after two minutes.

## Menu edits do not show up

Check that you edit the folder of the language in use, `lang/<language>/menus/`, then run `/clanadmin reload`. If a file is still not read, the console names the part it could not understand, and the rest of the screen opens without it.

## I changed default-roles and existing clans did not change

They will not. `default-roles` describes a clan on the day it is created. After that its ranks belong to the clan, which edits them itself.
