## Is ExyliaCrates free?

No. It is a premium plugin, bought on [BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/exyliacrates.132054/) and installed
as a loader jar that checks the licence on every start. Bought on BuiltByBit, the licence is embedded in the
jar. See [Installation](/docs/exyliacrates/installation).

## I used the crates of ExyliaSurvivalCore. What do I do?

Update ExyliaSurvivalCore to a release without the crates module and install ExyliaCrates next to it. Nothing
is imported: build the crates again from `/cratesadmin`, and create keys with the ids the old crates had so
key items already in players' hands keep opening them. Grant `exyliacrates.use` and `exyliacrates.admin` in
place of the old nodes. See [Installation](/docs/exyliacrates/installation#coming-from-exyliasurvivalcore).

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is a hard dependency: without it the server never loads the plugin. The server must be Paper
1.21.1 or newer (Folia 1.21.4 or newer), and ExyliaLib 1.571.1 or newer is needed too; the loader installs it, or stages a newer one, and asks for a restart — restart once more. If
the console reports a licence error, check that `Exylia-Crates-Loader.jar` is the jar from your purchase, and
contact support if it is.

## A reward changed rarity when I edited another one

A rarity follows the reward's share of the crate, read against every other reward. Adding a reward, removing
one or changing a weight moves every share, so a reward can cross into the next band. The editor shows each
row's share and rarity as you click; set the weights until the prizes sit where you want them. See
[Rewards and rarities](/docs/exyliacrates/rewards#rarities).

## A player spent a key and got nothing

A draw retries a reward whose own chance misses, but only 16 times. A crate whose rewards all have a low chance
can miss every time; the key is spent and the player is told the crate has no rewards. Keep chance at 100 and
use the weight to make a reward rare. See [Rewards and rarities](/docs/exyliacrates/rewards#how-a-prize-is-drawn).

## "Somebody is opening this crate. Wait a moment."

The crate is shared: everybody nearby watches one opening at a time on each block. Switch it to **Opener only,
many at once** in **Opening** → **Audience**, or place it on more blocks. See
[Openings](/docs/exyliacrates/openings#shared-or-private).

## "Your keys are still loading."

A player's balance is read from the database when they join. Until it is back they cannot open a crate. It
takes a moment on a healthy database; if it lasts, check the console for a database error.

## Players open crates from anywhere with /crates open

That needs `exyliacrates.open.remote`, which everybody holds by default. Deny it to keep every crate to its
block; players keep `/crates`, previews and their history. To lock a single crate, deny
`exyliacrates.crate.<id>`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliacrates/permissions#features-as-rank-perks).

## How do I let VIPs open more at once?

Grant the rank `exyliacrates.multiopen.<n>`, such as `exyliacrates.multiopen.25`: the highest number held
replaces `settings.max-multi-open` for that player. See
[Permissions](/docs/exyliacrates/permissions#multi-open-limit-per-rank).

## Where are the settings in game?

**Settings** in the corner of `/cratesadmin` edits the main opening and display values of `config.yml`; `tiers.yml` and the animation files are edited in the files or the browser editor. See
[Crates](/docs/exyliacrates/crates#settings).

## How do I reset a player's keys or wipe the history?

With ExyliaLib: `/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaCrates @account @history` for one player, or
`/exylialib wipe ExyliaCrates crates_openings` for the whole history. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliacrates/installation#wiping-and-resetting-data).

## A crate became free after I deleted a key

Deleting a key removes it from every crate's costs, and a crate left with no cost opens for free. Give it
another cost in **Opening cost**, or disable it. See [Keys and costs](/docs/exyliacrates/keys#deleting-a-key).

## I edited hologram-lines and nothing changed

Holograms are drawn when a crate block is registered. After `/cratesadmin reload`, change anything in a crate's General,
Opening or Presence section (or restart) to redraw its holograms. See [Crates](/docs/exyliacrates/crates#the-hologram).

## I edited a crate on one server of my network and the others still show the old one

Each server reads the crates and keys at startup. Restart the others. Edit crates on one server only, so an
older copy is never saved back over the newer one. See [Installation](/docs/exyliacrates/installation#networks).

## How do I give keys from my web store?

`cratesadmin key virtual {username} <key> <amount>` from the console. It works on offline players and on any
server of the network, and the player is told on their next join. See
[Keys and costs](/docs/exyliacrates/keys#from-a-web-store).

## Can I make a crate that costs money?

Yes: **Opening cost** → **Add money**, with or without keys beside it. It needs ExyliaEconomy or a Vault
economy. See [Keys and costs](/docs/exyliacrates/keys#money).

## Can players mute the win announcements?

Yes, from the Announcements screen of `/settings` (**Crate wins**). `settings.broadcast-enabled: false` turns
them off for everybody. See [Openings](/docs/exyliacrates/openings#announcements).

## The animation is too long

Click during it: it plays four times quicker (`settings.click-speed-up`). Or shorten the phases in its file
under `animations/`, or pick another animation, or **Instant**. See [Animations](/docs/exyliacrates/animations).
