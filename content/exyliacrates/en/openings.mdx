## From the click to the prize

<Steps>
  <Step title="The checks">
    The crate must be enabled and hold a reward the player can win. Their keys must have loaded since they
    joined ("Your keys are still loading."), their own previous opening must be over, and on a shared crate
    nobody else's opening may be playing on that block ("Somebody is opening this crate. Wait a moment.").
  </Step>
  <Step title="The charge">
    Every cost is charged, all or nothing. See [Keys and costs](/docs/exyliacrates/keys#how-a-charge-works).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The draw">
    The prizes are drawn and the guarantee counter is written in the same step, so what the animation lands
    on is exactly what is handed over. The opening is written to the history and the
    [`CrateOpenEvent`](/docs/exyliacrates/api#crateopenevent) fires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The animation">
    The crate's animation plays over the block. It shows the rarest prize won; the others come out with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The reveal">
    The prize comes out: it is handed over now, the rarity's celebration plays, a label floats over it —
    "You won …" for the opener, "*player* won …" for everybody else — and the opener reads what they won in
    chat. A rarity that broadcasts is announced to the server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With the **Instant** animation the prize simply appears over the crate for about two seconds.

If an animation never reaches its reveal — it broke, or a player kept it waiting — the plugin reveals for it
once its time is up, so a prize is never lost to an animation.

## Shared or private

**Opening** → **Audience** on a crate's overview.

| | Everyone, one at a time | Opener only, many at once |
|---|---|---|
| Who sees the animation | The opener and everybody within `settings.watch-radius` (24 blocks), as they walk in and out of it | The opener alone |
| Openings on one block | One at a time; others are told to wait | Any number at once |
| Floating icon and hologram during the opening | Hidden for everybody watching | Hidden for the opener only |

A player busy with an opening of their own does not see other openings, so two animations never mix on one
screen. The labels, effects and celebration follow the same audience. Announcements and the chat summary do
not depend on it.

## Opening away from the block

`/crates open <crate>` — and clicking the crate item on the contents screen — opens a crate from anywhere.
The animation plays on the ground about three blocks in front of the player, seen by whoever the crate's
audience allows. It takes no block, so it never makes anybody wait. Add `true` to open as many as they can pay for: `/crates open vote true`.

Every player with `exyliacrates.use` can do this for every enabled crate. There is no per-crate permission
and no switch to require standing at the block.

## Opening several at once

Sneak while right-clicking the block, shift-click the crate item on the contents screen, or add `true` to
`/crates open`: the crate opens as many times as every cost allows, up to `settings.max-multi-open` (10).

All the prizes come out together. The animation lands on the rarest; when it comes out, up to seven prizes
line up under it, rarest last, each with a count when it was won more than once. The chat summary lists them
the same way:

```text
CRATES • You opened Vote and won 10 rewards:
 ▎ Diamond x3
 ▎ 500 Coins x6
 ▎ Netherite Sword
```

The guarantee counter advances once per key.

## Speeding up

A click while your own opening plays — any click, anywhere — plays the rest of it `settings.click-speed-up`
times quicker (4 by default), up to the reveal. Once per opening; the reveal plays at its own pace.
`click-speed-up: 1` turns it off.

When an animated opening starts, the opener is reminded over the hotbar: `speed-up-hint` in `messages.yml`.
Pick a Card ignores clicks while the player is choosing a card, since that click is the choice.

## Leaving mid-opening

A prize is handed over whatever happens to the opening:

| The opener | What happens |
|---|---|
| Dies | Paid at once and told in chat what they won. |
| Changes world | Paid at once and told in chat what they won. |
| Disconnects | Paid at once, through the delivery queue: the prize waits for their next join. No chat summary and no announcement. |
| The server stops | Paid at once, before the plugin shuts down. |

Prizes that do not fit in the inventory, or that reach a player who is not online, are kept by ExyliaLib's
delivery queue and handed over on their next join: "Delivered 3 rewards that were waiting for you."

## History

Every reward won is one row of history: who, which crate, what, its rarity and when.

| Screen | Opened with | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Your openings | `/crates history`, or **Your openings** on a contents screen | The player's own latest 108 rewards, newest first |
| Server openings | `/cratesadmin history`, or **Server history** on the crate list | Everybody's latest 108 |

Rows older than `settings.history-retention-days` (30) are deleted a minute after start and every six hours
after. `0` keeps them forever.

## Announcements

When somebody wins a reward whose rarity has `broadcast: true` in `tiers.yml` — legendary and epic by
default — the server reads:

```text
CRATES • Steve pulled Netherite Sword from Vote » LEGENDARY
```

That is `broadcast` in `messages.yml`. Announcements go through ExyliaLib's **Crate wins** channel
(`crate-announcements`): every player can mute it from the Announcements screen of `/settings`.
`settings.broadcast-enabled: false` turns announcements off for everybody.

A bulk opening announces each broadcasting prize once, however many times it was won.

## Other plugins

| Plugin | What it does with openings |
|---|---|
| ExyliaSurvivalCore | Counts `crates.opened` and `crates.<id>.opened` for every player, one per opening, which missions and seasons read. Its boosters multiply crate prizes under the source `crates`. |
| Any plugin | Listens for [`CrateOpenEvent`](/docs/exyliacrates/api#crateopenevent). |

Prizes are paid through ExyliaLib's reward modifiers with the source `crates` and the scope `crate:<id>`, so a
modifier provider can boost every crate or one crate alone.
