| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliacrates.use` | everyone | `/crates` and its subcommands: the key list, previews, opening from anywhere and the player's own history. |
| `exyliacrates.admin` | operators | `/cratesadmin` and every subcommand, every staff screen and button, the crate wand, and the **Server history** button. |

## What use does and does not gate

`exyliacrates.use` gates the **commands** only. Opening a crate by right-clicking its block and seeing its
contents by left-clicking it need no permission at all.

It does let a player open **any enabled crate from anywhere** with `/crates open`, and from the contents
screen. There is no per-crate permission and no setting that ties opening to standing at the block. To keep
a crate to its block, take `exyliacrates.use` away — players then lose `/crates`, `/crates preview` and
`/crates history` as well — or give the crate a cost only reachable there.

## Limiting who can win what

Per-reward permissions and conditions, set in the reward editor, take a reward out of the draw for players
who lack them. The contents screen shows those rewards as **Locked** and works the odds out over what is
left. See [Rewards and rarities](/docs/exyliacrates/rewards#how-a-prize-is-drawn).

## ExyliaSurvivalCore nodes

`exyliasurvivalcore.crates.use` and `exyliasurvivalcore.crates.admin`, which gated the crates of
ExyliaSurvivalCore, are not checked by this plugin. Grant the two nodes above instead.
