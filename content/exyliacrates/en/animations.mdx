A crate opens with one of eleven animations, or with **Instant**, which shows the prize over the crate at
once. Pick one from **Opening** → **Animation** on a crate's overview: left-click chooses, right-click plays
it in front of you for free. Every crate can use a different one.

| Id | Name | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `wheel_of_fortune` | Wheel of Fortune | A wheel of prizes spins and slows onto one. |
| `carousel` | Carousel | A strip of prizes slides past a marker. |
| `cosmic_orb` | Cosmic Orb | The lid opens and an orb cycles until it bursts. |
| `pick_a_card` | Pick a Card | The player picks one card out of a fan. |
| `tier_slots` | Tier Slots | Three gem lanes decide the rarity, then the prize. |
| `rift` | Rift | A portal tears open and pulls one prize out. |
| `tesseract` | Tesseract | A glass cube rolls like a die onto the prize. |
| `event_horizon` | Event Horizon | A black hole devours all but one prize. |
| `constellation` | Constellation | A comet hops the stars and lights the prize. |
| `orrery` | Orrery | Planets align and the sun picks the prize. |
| `starfall` | Starfall | Prizes rain down as meteors onto the crate. |
| `NONE` | Instant | No animation: the prize appears at once. |

The names and one-line descriptions shown in the picker are `screens.animations` in `messages.yml`. A crate
stored with an id no animation answers to opens with the Wheel of Fortune.

## How they all work

Every animation is drawn with display entities sent as packets, so nothing is spawned in the world and
nothing can be picked up. They face the player who opened the crate, and everybody watching sees the same
thing from where they stand.

The prize is decided before an animation starts. An animation can never pay the wrong prize or pay twice: it
draws the crate's own rewards as fillers, at their real odds, and lands on the prize that was drawn. The
fillers are spread apart so the same icon rarely sits next to itself.

Every opening runs through the same five phases:

| Phase | What happens | Lines from |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | The stage appears. | `phases.intro` in the animation's file |
| Spin | The prizes move, the winner no different from the rest. | `phases.spin` |
| Stop | The movement lands on the winner. | `phases.stop` |
| Reveal | The winner leaves the others, and is handed over. | `phases.reveal` |
| Celebrate | The rarity's celebration. | `celebrate` of the rarity in `tiers.yml` |

So a legendary sounds the same whatever animation the crate uses. The phase lines are ExyliaLib sequences;
`%reward%`, `%tier%`, `%tier_color%`, `%player%`, `%crate%` and `%amount%` are filled in on every line and
`{item:reward}` draws the prize. `[LID] open` opens a chest's lid when the crate block is one.

Each animation has a deadline — its own timings plus two or three seconds. If it has not revealed by then,
the plugin reveals for it.

### Common keys

Every file has its own keys, below, and most share these:

| Key | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `intro-seconds`, `spin-seconds`, `stop-seconds` | How long each phase takes. |
| `reveal-seconds` | How long the prize stays out, celebration included. |
| `height`, `forward` | Where the stage stands: blocks above the crate's floor, and toward the player. |
| `icon-scale` | Size of each prize, 1 being a dropped item's size. |
| `deceleration` | How it brakes: 2 is steady, higher keeps its speed longer and creeps at the end. At least 1.2. |
| `near-miss` | How often, or how close, it nearly stops on another prize first. |
| `tick-sound`, `pitch-rise` | `NAME\|volume\|pitch` of the tick, and how far its pitch climbs as it slows, as a share of the written pitch. |
| `facing-offset` | Turns every model about the vertical, in degrees, if a client draws them the wrong way round. |
| `phases` | `intro`, `spin`, `stop` and `reveal`: effect lines per phase. |

Block materials, palette tokens (`{primary}`) and icon sources (a material, a head, an item from another
plugin) are accepted where the tables say so. A value outside a key's range is clamped to it.

Edit a file and run `/cratesadmin reload`: the next opening plays the new values.

## Wheel of Fortune

`animations/wheel_of_fortune.yml`. A wheel of the crate's rewards stands over the block, facing the opener. A
flapper at the top clicks on every peg; the wheel brakes, creeps, and stops with the prize under it. The
prize comes out toward the player.

|---|---|---|

| `radius` | 1.05 | Radius of the circle the prizes sit on. |
| `segments` | 10 | Prizes on the wheel, 6 to 16. |
| `icon-scale` | 0.5 | |
| `radial-icons` | true | Prizes turn with the wheel like printed segments; false keeps them upright. |
| `turns` · `turns-variance` | 3.0 · 0.6 | Whole turns before it lands, and how many either side a spin may make. |

| `tick-sound` · `pitch-rise` | `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_HAT\|0.6\|1.0` · 0.7 | |

prize. It cracks in the colour of the prize's rarity — never shown before the crack — and the prize floats out.

## Pick a Card

`animations/pick_a_card.yml`. Face-down cards are dealt in a fan in front of the opener and shuffled. The
opener **picks one by looking at it and clicking**: the card looked at lifts, and a title says how long they
have. It turns over to show the prize, then the others turn over to show what else the crate holds.

The prize was decided when the key was spent, so every card is the winning card. A player who waits too long
gets the card they are looking at, or any. Clicks while choosing never speed the opening up.

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `cards` | 5 | Cards in the fan, 3 to 7. |
| `card-back` | `PURPLE_STAINED_GLASS_PANE` | The back of every card: an icon source. |
| `edge-glow` | `{secondary}` | Outline of a face-down card; empty for none. |
| `card-scale` · `height` | 0.8 · 1.35 | |
| `fan-radius` · `fan-degrees` · `fan-distance` | 2.4 · 70 · 0.9 | The fan's curve, the angle it covers, and how far in front of the crate its middle card is. |
| `deal-gap` | 0.18 | Seconds between two cards dealt. |
| `shuffles` · `shuffle-seconds` | 6 · 0.32 | Swaps the shuffle makes (0 to 20) and seconds per swap. |
| `timeout-seconds` | 15 | Time to pick, at least 3. |
| `hover-lift` · `aim-tolerance` | 0.16 · 0.42 | How far the card looked at lifts, and how far from its middle a look still counts. |
| `reveal-seconds` | 3.4 | |
| `deal-sound` · `shuffle-sound` · `hover-sound` · `flip-sound` | page turn, page turn, hat, book put | A card dealt, a swap, the card looked at (opener only), a card turned over. |

Its title and subtitle are `pick-title` and `pick-subtitle` in `messages.yml`; `%seconds%` is the time left.

## Tier Slots

`animations/tier_slots.yml`. Three lanes of gems, one gem per rarity in `tiers.yml`, spin in front of the
opener and stop left to right. The third lane always lands on the prize's rarity. The first two land on a
pair: the same rarity (three of a kind) or, as a near miss, the next rarer rarity the crate really holds,
which the third lane then breaks. The prize rises out of the middle lane — or the last, after a near miss.

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `intro-seconds` · `stop-seconds` · `reveal-seconds` | 0.8 · 0.9 · 3.4 | |
| `first-stop-seconds` · `lane-gap-seconds` | 2.4 · 0.8 | Until the first lane stops, then between the first and the second. |
| `last-lane-seconds` | 1.6 | How long the last lane keeps crawling after the second stops. |
| `settle-seconds` | 0.18 | How long a lane takes to click into place. |
| `height` · `forward` | 2.3 · 0.15 | |
| `lane-spacing` · `row-spacing` · `gem-scale` | 0.62 · 0.5 · 0.42 | |
| `rows` | 3 | Gems a lane shows at once, 3 or 5. |
| `speed` | 16.0 | Gems per second a lane starts at, 4 to 24. |
| `deceleration` · `last-lane-deceleration` | 2.6 · 4.2 | |
| `near-miss` | 0.25 | Share of spins whose first two lanes pair on the next rarer rarity, when the crate has one. |
| `gems` | `legendary: NETHER_STAR`, `epic: AMETHYST_SHARD`, `rare: DIAMOND`, `common: QUARTZ` | The gem of each rarity, by its id in `tiers.yml`. |
| `fallback-gems` | `NETHER_STAR`, `AMETHYST_SHARD`, `DIAMOND`, `EMERALD`, `QUARTZ` | Gems for rarities not listed, rarest first. |
| `gem-glow` | true | Outline each gem in its rarity's colour. |
| `payline-block` · `payline-color` | `WHITE_CONCRETE` · `{highlight}` | |
| `tick-sound` · `lane-stop-sound` · `pitch-rise` | hat · bass drum · 0.4 | |

Add a rarity to `tiers.yml` and give it a gem here, or it takes the next fallback gem.

## The instant reveal

Choosing **Instant** (stored as `NONE`) skips the animation: the prize grows over the crate for about two
seconds while its rarity celebrates. Nothing to configure; the celebration still comes from `tiers.yml`.
