title: "First steps"
description: "A first crate in a few minutes: create it, give it rewards, place it with the wand, get a key and open it."
---

This page builds one crate, `vote`, from nothing. Every step happens in game, as an operator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the crate">
    Run `/cratesadmin`. The **Crates** screen lists every crate; click **Create crate** and type `vote` as
    its id. Ids are lowercase letters, numbers, `-` and `_`, at most 64 characters, and they never change.

    The crate is created with the display name `VOTE`, an ender chest icon, the Wheel of Fortune animation,
    one reward per key, and a cost of **one `vote` key** — a key with the same id is created with it when
    there is none. Its overview screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it rewards">
    Open **Rewards**, then click **Rewards** again to open the editor. Add a few: an item from your hand, a
    command, money, experience. Each one has a **weight**: shift + left-click raises it, shift + right-click
    lowers it, and every row shows its real share as you go. Save.

    The weight also decides the rarity. With the default `tiers.yml`, a reward weighted 1 or less is
    **legendary**, up to 5 **epic**, up to 25 **rare**, and anything heavier **common**. A new reward starts
    at weight 1, so give the common ones a heavier weight — 50, 100 — and leave the prize at 1. See
    [Rewards and rarities](/docs/exyliacrates/rewards).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Open **Presence** and click **Crate wand**. Right-click any block with the wand: that block is now the
    `vote` crate, with a hologram and the crate's icon turning over it. Left-click with the wand removes it.
    One crate can stand on as many blocks as you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a key">
    For yourself: `/cratesadmin key give <your name> vote 3` hands you three key items. To test without the
    command, **Keys** on the crate list → `vote` → **Get keys**.

    For a store or a vote listener that has to reach offline players, use the balance instead:
    `/cratesadmin key virtual <player> vote 1`. See [Keys and costs](/docs/exyliacrates/keys).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Left-click the block to see the contents and the odds. Right-click it to open one key; sneak and
    right-click to open as many as you hold, up to ten at once. Click during the animation to play it four
    times quicker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Watch the animation before players do">
On the crate's overview, **Opening** → **Preview** plays the crate's animation in front of you without a
key, without paying anything and without writing history. **Animation** lists all eleven; right-click one
there to preview it before choosing it.
</Callout>

## What to do next

- Charge money too, or a second key, from **Opening cost**. See [Keys and costs](/docs/exyliacrates/keys).
- Promise a legendary after 50 openings from **Rewards** → **Guarantee**. See
  [Rewards and rarities](/docs/exyliacrates/rewards#the-guarantee).
- Make the opening private so several players can open the same block at once. See
  [Openings](/docs/exyliacrates/openings#shared-or-private).
- Show players how many keys they hold on a scoreboard with `%exyliacrates_crate_keys_vote%`. See
  [Placeholders](/docs/exyliacrates/placeholders).
