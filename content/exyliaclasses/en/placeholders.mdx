Use these to show a player's class, energy and marks in other plugins. Outside Exylia plugins they need PlaceholderAPI. Click a placeholder to copy it.

<Placeholders />

All of them describe the player the text is shown for. The mark ones describe marks **on** that player (what others stacked on them), not marks they put on someone else.

## In a scoreboard

```yaml
lines:
  - " {muted}❙ {letters}Class: {highlight}%exyliaclasses_classes_class%"
  - " {muted}❙ {letters}Energy: {highlight}%exyliaclasses_classes_energy%{muted}/%exyliaclasses_classes_max_energy%"
```

## Show the mark icon on a name tag

`%exyliaclasses_classes_mark_display_space%` is empty when the player is not marked, so it leaves no stray space:

```text
%exyliaclasses_classes_mark_display_space%%player_name%
```

<Callout type="warning" title="The no-class answer is a Spanish word">
`%exyliaclasses_classes_class%` answers `Ninguna` when the player is in no class. That word is fixed in the plugin and cannot be changed in a file, and `%name|fallback%` does not replace it. On an English server, hide the line behind `%exyliaclasses_classes_in_class%` instead.
</Callout>

<Details title="Technical details">
ExyliaClasses registers these names through ExyliaLib under `classes_`. PlaceholderAPI puts the plugin in front, which gives `%exyliaclasses_classes_class%`. Inside the text of Exylia plugins the registered name stands on its own, without the plugin in front.
</Details>
