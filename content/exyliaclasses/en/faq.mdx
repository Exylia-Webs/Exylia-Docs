Longer fixes, with the steps, are on [Common problems](/docs/exyliaclasses/troubleshooting).

## Why does nobody enter a class?

The armour must match the class file exactly in every slot it declares, the player needs the class's `permission` unless it is `NONE`, and the file must not log an `Invalid material` warning. See [Classes](/docs/exyliaclasses/classes).

## Can two classes use the same armour?

Avoid it. Classes are matched in no defined order, so two classes that ask for the same set give one or the other unpredictably, and differently after a reload.

## Why does the warmup take a second longer than I wrote?

The first countdown message goes out one second in and the class activates one second after the message that reads `1`, so `time: 5` lands at six seconds.

## Why did a player lose their class?

Death and disconnect end a class, and so does any armour change that breaks the set, including a piece breaking from durability or another plugin swapping the gear. Ability cooldowns are kept.

## Why do my ability items disappear?

One item is consumed per successful use. A use that fails (on cooldown, not enough energy) consumes nothing. See [Abilities](/docs/exyliaclasses/abilities).

## Why does an ability do nothing?

If its `type` is `TO_ALLY`, that type resolves to no targets while still spending energy, starting the cooldown and consuming the item. Use `TO_CLAN`.

## Why do clan abilities only reach the caster?

No clan plugin is installed or the caster has no clan. ExyliaLib detects FactionsUUID, HuskTowns, ZelTeams, RunithClans, UltimateClans, Kingdoms, SimpleClans and ExyliaClans; without one, everyone is clanless and `TO_ENEMY` hits everyone in range.

## Why do marks not add damage?

`extra-damage` on a mark level is a number shown in the mark messages, the mark bar and `%exyliaclasses_classes_mark_extra_damage%`; the plugin does not apply it. The backstab `extra-damage` is real damage. See [Weapons](/docs/exyliaclasses/weapons).

## Why do marks never expire?

The mark bar is what times a mark out. With `mark-bar.enabled: false` nothing counts down, so a mark lasts until the player dies, quits or leaves the class. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaclasses/configuration).

## Why does the backstab kill people in full netherite?

Its `extra-damage` is taken straight off health, so armour, Protection, Resistance and absorption do nothing against it. With `can-dead: false` it never delivers the killing blow, but the ordinary sword swing still can.

## Why is my energy stuck at zero?

Either the class has no `energy` block, so an ability with an `energy-cost` can never be used, or the player just entered the class: energy starts at zero. See [Energy](/docs/exyliaclasses/energy).

## Why does the class placeholder say "Ninguna"?

`%exyliaclasses_classes_class%` answers that fixed word when the player has no class and it cannot be changed. Hide the line behind `%exyliaclasses_classes_in_class%`. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaclasses/placeholders).

## Can I add a class without restarting?

Yes. Put the file in `lang/<language>/classes/` and run `/classesadmin reload`. Everyone online is re-matched and loses their energy and marks, so do it between fights.

## Does ExyliaClasses save anything to disk?

No. There is no database and no player data file: a class, its energy and its marks live in memory while the player is online.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes. The plugin is declared `folia-supported` and runs everything that touches a player on that player's own thread.
