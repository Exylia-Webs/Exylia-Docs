ExyliaClasses has two staff commands and nothing for players. A player enters a class by wearing its armour and uses abilities by right-clicking items. See [Your first class](/docs/exyliaclasses/first-steps).

<Commands />

The older spellings `/classes <command>`, `/eclasses <command>` and `/exyliaclasses <command>` still work as aliases.

## What reload does

`/classesadmin reload` runs six steps in order and reports each one on its own, so a broken class file names itself instead of failing the whole reload.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configuration files">
    `config.yml` and `lang/<language>/messages.yml` are read again from disk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The message prefix">
    Applied again from `messages.yml`, so `%prefix%` in other Exylia plugins matches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The debug flag">
    Picked up from `config.yml` without a restart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The settings menus">
    The `/classesadmin settings` menus are loaded again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Class definitions">
    Every `.yml` in `lang/<language>/classes/` is loaded again. New files appear and deleted files disappear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Online players">
    Every online player loses their class and is matched against the new definitions. A player whose armour still fits a class starts that class's warmup again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Reload between fights">
Step six clears energy and marks for every player online and starts a new warmup for anyone whose armour still fits. Ability cooldowns keep running.
</Callout>

<Details title="Technical details">
`/classesadmin reload` runs from the console too. `/classesadmin settings` needs a player. The settings hub has one card per boss bar (`energy-bar` and `mark-bar` in `config.yml`); each page switches the bar on or off and picks its colour and segments, showing the current value and the default. Language, debug and the class files stay in their files. The settings do not reload ExyliaLib: `/exylialib reload` does that, see [ExyliaLib commands](/docs/exylialib/commands).
</Details>
