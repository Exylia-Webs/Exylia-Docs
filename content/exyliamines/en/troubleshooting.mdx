Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

The console names what is missing. ExyliaLib is required. See [Install ExyliaMines](/docs/exyliamines/installation).

## The mine stays empty

A classic mine needs an area and at least one block in its composition. Open `/minesadmin`, your mine, and check **Area** and **Composition**, then click **Resets**, **Force reset**. From the console or chat, `/minesadmin reset <id>` refuses a mine without an area or a composition and says which one is missing. A realistic mine is never filled: it only regrows the blocks already there. See [Classic mines](/docs/exyliamines/classic-mines).

## A mine does not refill on time

A refill that falls due while none of the mine's chunks are loaded waits until they all are. It happens as soon as someone comes close. The interval, the warning and the threshold are in **Resets**.

## `/mine <id>` does not work

`/mine` checks, in order, that the mine exists and is enabled, that the player may enter it, that it has an entry point and that the player is not in combat. Enable it with `/minesadmin enable <id>`, set **Teleports**, **Entry teleport**, and check the mine's access permission. Staff can always use `/minesadmin tp <id>`. See [Players' menu](/docs/exyliamines/players-menu#going-to-a-mine).

## A player cannot break blocks in a mine

A mine can name an access permission in **General**. A player without it cannot break anything inside the area and is told they have no access. Give them the permission or clear it. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliamines/permissions).

## The realistic mine does not grow blocks back

Only the materials added in **Regen levels** are the mine's. A block with no level inside the area is left alone. See [Realistic mines](/docs/exyliamines/realistic-mines).

## There is no hologram

Holograms are drawn with packetevents. Without it the plugin runs and only the packet-drawn extras stay off. See [Holograms](/docs/exyliamines/holograms).

## A placeholder shows as text

- PlaceholderAPI is not installed.
- The mine id is misspelled, or you wrote the in-plugin form `%mine_...%` in another plugin: there it needs `%exyliamines_mine_...%`.

See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliamines/placeholders).

## Fortune does nothing

A rank node `exyliamines.fortune.<n>` only raises a cap. Where the mine's maximum Fortune is `0`, Fortune stays off. See [Fortune](/docs/exyliamines/fortune#rank-caps).
