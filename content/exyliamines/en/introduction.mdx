ExyliaMines runs the mines of a server: areas players mine for their resources that come back on their
own. Everything about a mine — where it is, what it is made of, when it refills, what it pays — is built
and edited in game from a menu. There is no mine file to write by hand.

It is **free and open source**, released under the GPL-3.0 licence and downloaded from the
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaMines/releases). The mines used to live inside
ExyliaSurvivalCore; they are now a plugin of their own, and a server coming from there has its mines,
settings, messages and mining history brought over on its own. See [Migration](/docs/exyliamines/migration).

## Two kinds of mine

| Kind | How it comes back |
|---|---|
| **Classic** | The whole area is refilled at once from a weighted **composition** of blocks: on a timer, once a share of it has been mined, or when an admin forces it. Players inside are warned first and moved clear of the new blocks. |
| **Realistic** | Nothing is refilled. Each block the player breaks grows back on its own after its **level**'s regeneration time, optionally passing through a **degrade chain** — stone, then cobblestone, then the ore again — that the player mines through. |

Both kinds share everything else: a permission, auto-pickup, custom loot per block, break effects,
potion effects kept on whoever is inside, lucky rewards, Fortune rules, boosts, a hologram and the
players' `/mines` menu.

## What is included

<Cards>
  <Card title="Classic mines" href="/docs/exyliamines/classic-mines">
    A weighted composition, an interval, a warning, an emptiness threshold, a safe teleport and buried
    players lifted to free air. Where each mine stands in its cycle survives a restart.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Realistic mines" href="/docs/exyliamines/realistic-mines">
    Levels with their own regeneration time, degrade chains mined stage by stage, and broken blocks that
    heal after a restart.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Area and shapes" href="/docs/exyliamines/area">
    Cuboid, sphere or cylinder, cut out of the box you select. Explosions, pistons, fluids and placed
    blocks cannot farm a mine.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Loot and effects" href="/docs/exyliamines/loot-and-effects">
    Item, money and command loot per block in place of the vanilla drop, sounds and particles per mine
    or per block, and potion effects inside.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lucky rewards and boosts" href="/docs/exyliamines/boosts">
    Rare prizes any block may give, and timed multipliers of drops, experience and money started by
    command or on a weekly schedule.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Statistics" href="/docs/exyliamines/stats">
    Blocks mined per player and per mine, a top ten per mine, all readable from placeholders.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21 or newer, or a fork of it. Folia supported. Spigot is not: the plugin uses Paper's API. |
| Java | 21 |
| Required dependencies | `packetevents`, and `ExyliaLib` 1.230.0 or newer — installed for you if it is missing |
| Optional dependencies | `ExyliaSurvivalCore`, `PlaceholderAPI`, `Vault`, `Worlds` or `Multiverse-Core` |

<Callout type="note" title="About ExyliaLib">
Typed configuration, the menus, the database, regions and the block selector, holograms, loot tables,
effect sequences, schedules, teleports and the update checks all come from ExyliaLib. ExyliaMines is
written on top of it and does not run without it, which is why it downloads the library itself on a
server that lacks it. See [Installation](/docs/exyliamines/installation).
</Callout>

## Conventions

- Values marked as **default** are what the plugin writes the first time it starts. If the file
  already exists, your value is kept.
- Identifiers between `<` and `>` are variables. `/mine <id>` means `/mine coal`, not `/mine <coal>`.
- A length of time accepts a bare number of seconds or the written form: `300`, `5m` and `1h30m` all
  work, in the files and in the prompts.
- Colours use ExyliaLib palette tokens (`{primary}`, `{highlight}`, `{muted}`); MiniMessage tags such
  as `<click>` and `<hover>` work too, as the default reset announcement shows.
