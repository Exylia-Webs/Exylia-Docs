## Is ExyliaMines free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence. Download it from the
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaMines/releases); the source is in the same
repository.

## I used the mines of ExyliaSurvivalCore. What do I do?

Update ExyliaSurvivalCore, or turn its `mines` module off, install ExyliaMines next to it and start the
server once. The mines, the blocks they were regrowing, their settings and messages and every player's
mining history come over on their own, once. `exyliasurvivalcore.mines.admin` keeps working, and
`/minesadmin` keeps its name. Through PlaceholderAPI, `%exyliasurvivalcore_mine_…%` becomes
`%exyliamines_mine_…%`. See [Migration](/docs/exyliamines/migration).

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is a hard dependency: without it the server never loads the plugin. ExyliaLib is installed
for you when it is missing — the plugin downloads it, asks for a restart and disables itself until then. If
an `ExyliaLib.jar` is already in `plugins/` and still did not load, the console says so: fix the errors
ExyliaLib printed above it. See [Installation](/docs/exyliamines/installation).

## A classic mine never refills

It needs three things: to be **enabled**, a **region**, and at least one block in its **composition**. Then
two reasons it can still wait: nobody is near it — a refill falling due while the mine's chunks are unloaded
is held until they load — or a refill is already running. **Resets** → **Force reset** says whether the
region or the composition is missing.

## A realistic mine does nothing when I break its blocks

Its blocks are exactly the materials added as **levels**; nothing else in the area is the mine's. A block
of another material is left to the server — or refused, with **restrict breaking** on. Add the material as a
level. See [Realistic mines](/docs/exyliamines/realistic-mines).

## "This mine has no entry point"

The mine has no **entry teleport**. For a classic mine, set it in **Teleports** → **Entry teleport**. A
realistic mine has no teleports section in the editor, so it cannot be given one there; send players with a
warp of your own.

## Players can't break anything in a mine

The mine names a permission they do not have — **General** → **Access permission**. Without it nothing in
the area can be broken and they are told "You don't have access to this mine.". Grant the node or clear the
permission.

## WorldGuard denies building there. Do I need a flag for the mine?

No. The mine performs its own breaks and never forces a cancelled break through, so the region can deny
building as it likes: mining still works, and nothing but the mine's own blocks can be broken. See
[Area and shapes](/docs/exyliamines/area#how-a-break-inside-a-mine-works).

## Mined blocks give nothing

The material has a **loot table**, and a loot table replaces the vanilla drop entirely, even on a roll where
none of its lines came up. Each line is its own chance in percent; add a line at `100` for whatever should
always drop. See [Loot and effects](/docs/exyliamines/loot-and-effects#loot-tables).

## Fortune does nothing on my custom loot

`fortune.mode` in `config.yml` decides it, and so may the mine's own **Fortune** setting: `OFF` turns it off
for custom loot, and a highest level of `0` turns Fortune off altogether in that mine. `target: ITEMS`, the
default, leaves money alone. See [Fortune](/docs/exyliamines/fortune).

## My placeholders show up unresolved

Through PlaceholderAPI the plugin's name goes in front: `%exyliamines_mine_reset_coal%`. Inside Exylia
plugins' own files it does not: `%mine_reset_coal%`. A mine id is part of the name, so check the spelling
of the id too. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliamines/placeholders).

## The leaderboard is behind

It is read back from the database once a minute, and the counts are written every five seconds. A player's
own numbers move with every block; the top ten moves in steps.

## A boost from the schedule never started

Check the console after `/minesadmin reload`: a time that is not `HH:mm` or a day that is not `MONDAY` to
`SUNDAY` is named there. Times follow ExyliaLib's timezone, not the machine's, and `min-players` skips a
start while fewer players are online. The `boost` of the entry has to be an id under `boosts`.

## Can several servers share the mines?

They can share one database, and each server then runs its own mines: a mine belongs to the server it was
created on, and ids are unique across all of them. See [Integrations](/docs/exyliamines/integrations#networks).

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, it declares Folia support. Every block change runs on the thread that owns the block, and the API
events are called on the region thread of the block they are about.
