Three roots. `/mines` and `/mine` are the players'; `/minesadmin` is staff's and opens the editor every mine
is built in.

## Player commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/mines` | `exyliamines.menu` | Opens the list of enabled mines. Players only. |
| `/mine <id>` | `exyliamines.teleport` | Sends the player to the mine's entry point. Tab completion suggests every enabled mine. Players only. |

`/mine` checks that the mine exists and is enabled, that the player may enter it, that it has an entry
point and that the player is not in combat, in that order. See
[Players' menu](/docs/exyliamines/players-menu#going-to-a-mine).

## Staff commands

Everything below needs `exyliamines.admin`.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/minesadmin` | Opens the list of mines: create, edit, delete. Players only. |
| `/minesadmin edit <id>` | Opens that mine's setup screen straight away. Players only. |
| `/minesadmin tp <id>` | Goes to the mine even when it is disabled or has no entry point yet: its entry point when it has one, otherwise the first spot with room to stand above the middle of its area. No permission, enabled or combat check. Players only. |
| `/minesadmin reset <id\|all>` | Refills a classic mine now, with no warning first; players inside are moved clear of the new blocks. `all` refills every enabled classic mine with an area and a composition and says how many. A realistic mine, or one without an area or a composition, is refused. |
| `/minesadmin enable <id>` | Turns the mine on, as the toggle on its setup screen does. Players already standing in it enter it. |
| `/minesadmin disable <id>` | Turns the mine off. Players inside leave it as if they had walked out, and its hologram is redrawn. |
| `/minesadmin reload` | Reloads the files. |
| `/minesadmin import <catamines\|realmines>` | Imports that plugin's mines. See [Migration](/docs/exyliamines/migration#from-catamines-or-realmines). |
| `/minesadmin boost start <id>` | Starts a boost from `boosts.yml`; one already running starts over. |
| `/minesadmin boost custom <multiplier> <duration> [mines]` | Starts a boost typed out: `1.2 30m gold,iron`. No mines means every mine. |
| `/minesadmin boost give <player> <id>` | Gives one online player a boost from `boosts.yml`: it multiplies only what they break. |
| `/minesadmin boost give-custom <player> <multiplier> <duration> [mines]` | `boost custom` for one online player. |
| `/minesadmin boost stop <id\|all> [player]` | Ends one running boost, or all of them. Name the player to end the boost given to them. |
| `/minesadmin boost list` | Opens the running boosts menu; a click stops one after a confirmation. From the console, lists them in chat. |

Everything but the bare `/minesadmin`, `edit` and `tp` also runs from the console. Tab completion offers
every mine on the server, enabled or not, to `edit`, `tp`, `enable` and `disable`; the classic mines and
`all` to `reset`; the boosts of `boosts.yml` to `start` and `give`; the running ones and `all` to `stop`;
and the enabled mines to `custom` and `give-custom`. See [Boosts](/docs/exyliamines/boosts).

### What reload does

`/minesadmin reload` runs six named steps and reports how many ran and in how many milliseconds:

| Step | What it does |
|---|---|
| `configs` | Reads `config.yml`, `templates.yml`, `boosts.yml` and `messages.yml` again, then the prefix and the debug switch. A changed `language` applies here. |
| `timers` | Restarts the mine clocks, so a changed `classic-tick-interval-seconds` or `realistic-tick-interval-seconds` applies. |
| `areas` | Registers every mine's area again and re-sorts who is inside, so a changed presence margin applies. |
| `boosts` | Applies the schedule of `boosts.yml` again. |
| `holograms` | Redraws every hologram with the lines as they are now. |
| `menus` | Compiles every menu again. |

The `menus` step also runs when ExyliaLib itself is reloaded, so a recoloured palette reaches the menus.

Reload does **not** touch the mines. They live in the database and are edited from `/minesadmin`, where a
change applies the moment it is made.

### Web editor

`/exylialib editor ExyliaMines` opens the plugin in the browser: `config.yml`, `templates.yml`,
`boosts.yml` and the mines themselves, which can be created, edited and deleted there as in `/minesadmin`.
Each needs `exyliamines.admin`. Applying settings applies them as reload's `timers` and `areas` steps do;
applying boosts reschedules them. Changes to loot, lucky rewards or effects ask for `confirm`, since they
can run console commands. See the [library's web editor page](/docs/exylialib/web-editor).

<Callout type="note" title="Building a mine is in the editor">
There is no command to create or delete a mine, or to change its area, composition, levels or reset
times. All of that is a button in `/minesadmin`; the subcommands above are shortcuts for what staff do
every day. See [Mines](/docs/exyliamines/mines).
</Callout>
