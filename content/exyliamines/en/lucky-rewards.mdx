---
title: "Lucky rewards"
description: "Rare prizes any block of a mine may give on top of its own loot, the title the finder sees and who hears about it."
---

Lucky rewards are rare prizes a mine hands out on top of whatever its blocks pay: a key, a stack of
diamonds, a sum of money, a command. They belong to the mine, not to a material, so any block broken there
may give one.

## Setting them up

**Rewards** → **Lucky rewards** on the setup screen opens ExyliaLib's loot editor, with the same three
kinds of line as a loot table: an item, money, or a command. See
[Loot and effects](/docs/exyliamines/loot-and-effects#loot-tables) for what each one is and how a command
line chooses who runs it.

Each line is **its own chance**, a percentage that may go well below one: `0.05` is one block in two
thousand. Every line is rolled on its own every time a block of the mine is broken through the mine — a
composition block, a level, a degrade stage — so most breaks give nothing and, rarely, two prizes come
up together.

## What is applied to a prize

| What | Applies? |
|---|---|
| Fortune | Only to the **chance**, through `fortune.lucky-per-level` in `config.yml`. Off by default; `0.1` makes every chance ×1.3 at Fortune III. The prize itself is never multiplied by Fortune. |
| Boosts | Yes: `drops` multiplies items and `money` multiplies money. |
| ExyliaSurvivalCore | Item prizes go through the miner's drop boosters and auto-sell. |
| Auto-pickup | Yes, as the mine says. |

## Who hears about it

For every prize, the finder sees the `lucky.found` title, and the `lucky.announcement` line goes to whom
`announcements.lucky` in `config.yml` says:

| Value | Who |
|---|---|
| `OFF` | Nobody. |
| `NEARBY` | Players within `announcements.radius` blocks of the block, 64 by default. The default. |
| `SERVER` | Everyone online. |

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaMines/lang/en/messages.yml"
lucky:
  announcement: '[sound:ENTITY_PLAYER_LEVELUP|1.0|1.4]%prefix% {highlight}%player% {letters}found {accent}%reward% {letters}in {highlight}%mine_name%{letters}!'
  found:
    text: '{accent}&l✦ LUCKY BLOCK'
    subtitle: '{letters}%reward%'
    fade-in: 0.25
    stay: 1.5
    fade-out: 0.5
    time-style: auto
  secret-reward: a secret reward
  editor-title: '{primary}&lLUCKY REWARDS {letters_black}» {letters}%mine_name%'
  count: '{success}%count% reward(s)'
```

`%reward%` names the prize the way a player would:

| Prize | `%reward%` |
|---|---|
| An item | Its custom name, or the line's name, preceded by the amount when more than one: `3x Diamond`. |
| Money | The line's name. |
| A command | `secret-reward`, "a secret reward": a command is not something to read out in chat. |

The announcement also takes `%player%`, `%mine_name%` and `%mine_id%`; the title takes `%reward%` and
`%mine_name%`. Empty a line to turn it off.
