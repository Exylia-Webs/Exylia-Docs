## Plugin nodes

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliamines.menu` | everyone | `/mines`. |
| `exyliamines.teleport` | everyone | `/mine <id>`, and going to a mine from the `/mines` menu. |
| `exyliamines.admin` | operators | `/minesadmin` and every subcommand and screen behind it. Also passes every mine's own permission, may break anything inside a mine with **restrict breaking** on, and may place a mine's own blocks inside it. |
| `exyliasurvivalcore.mines.admin` | operators | The name `exyliamines.admin` had in ExyliaSurvivalCore. Grants `exyliamines.admin`. |

<Callout type="note" title="The ExyliaSurvivalCore name still works">
`exyliasurvivalcore.mines.admin` is what staff were granted while mines lived in ExyliaSurvivalCore.
ExyliaMines declares it too, with `exyliamines.admin` as its child, so a permissions setup from before the
move keeps working untouched.
</Callout>

Every button of the staff screens checks `exyliamines.admin` itself, so copying one into a menu players can
open hands them nothing. The one exception is the players' "go to this mine" action, which runs the
checks of `/mine` instead.

## A mine's own permission

Each mine may name a permission of its own, set from **General** → **Access permission** in its setup
screen. It can be any node: `mines.vip`, `rank.miner`. A player who lacks it is kept out of three things:

| What | What happens |
|---|---|
| Walking in | The player is told "You don't have access to this mine." and gets nothing of it: no title, no bars, no potion effects. |
| Breaking | Nothing inside the area can be broken, the mine's blocks or anything else. |
| Going there | `/mine` and the `/mines` menu refuse; the menu shows the mine as locked. |

A mine without one is open to everyone. `exyliamines.admin` passes all three.

## Examples

Locking a mine to a rank, after setting its permission to `mines.vip`:

```text
/lp group vip permission set mines.vip true
```

Taking teleports away while keeping the list:

```text
/lp group default permission set exyliamines.teleport false
```

Handing the editor to staff who are not operators:

```text
/lp group staff permission set exyliamines.admin true
```
