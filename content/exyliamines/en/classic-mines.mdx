A classic mine is refilled as a whole from its **composition**: every block of its area is replaced by a block drawn at random from a weighted list, on a timer you choose.

## Before you start

You need a classic mine with its area selected. See [Create and edit mines](/docs/exyliamines/mines) and [Select a mine's area](/docs/exyliamines/area). For a full walkthrough from nothing, see [Your first mine](/docs/exyliamines/first-steps).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the mine">
    Run `/minesadmin` and left-click your classic mine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose its blocks">
    Click **Mine ▸ Composition**, then **Composition ▸ Add block** and pick a block. It arrives with a weight of `10`. Add every block the mine should contain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set when it refills">
    Back on the setup screen click **Mine ▸ Resets**. Click **Resets ▸ Interval** and type a time such as `90`, `5m` or `1h30m`. The minimum is 5 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the warning">
    Click **Resets ▸ Warning** and type how long before a refill players inside are told, for example `10s`. `0s` switches the warning off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refill it now">
    Click **Resets ▸ Force reset**. It needs a region and at least one block in the composition.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new classic mine starts with an interval of **5 minutes** and a warning of **10 seconds**. These come from `settings.default-reset-interval-seconds` (`300`) and `settings.default-reset-warning-seconds` (`10`) in `config.yml`; change them there, or under **Plugin ▸ Settings ▸ New mines**, to change what *new* mines start with. Existing mines keep their own values.

## Check that it works

Run `/mine <id>` as a player and break a few blocks, then run `/minesadmin reset <id>`: the area refills at once. To see the timer, wait for the interval, or set a short interval such as `30s` while you test. Walking in plays a sound, shows the "MINE" title and a "next reset" boss bar that empties as the refill comes closer. If something does not work, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliamines/troubleshooting).

## The composition

**Composition** lists every entry with its weight and the chance it works out to: the entry's weight over the sum of all weights. Weights are relative, not percentages. Stone at `70` and coal ore at `30` is the same mine as stone at `7` and coal ore at `3`.

| Click on an entry | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left-click | Weight +1. |
| Right-click | Weight -1, never below `0.1`. |
| Shift-left-click | Asks for an exact weight, at least `0.1`. |
| Middle-click | Opens the entry's loot table. See [Loot and effects](/docs/exyliamines/loot-and-effects). |
| Drop key (`Q`) | Opens the entry's break effects. |
| Shift-right-click | Removes the entry, after a confirmation. |

A material that cannot be placed as a block is refused. Use each material once: loot and effects are looked up by material, so a second entry would only weigh into the draw.

Only the materials of the composition are the mine's own. Breaking one is a mine break, with its loot and its count towards the threshold; anything else inside the area is left alone, or refused with **Mining ▸ Restrict breaking** on. See [Create and edit mines](/docs/exyliamines/mines#mining).

## When it refills

Three things refill a classic mine:

| Trigger | Set in | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Interval** | **Resets ▸ Interval** | Refills it every interval. 5 minutes by default, 5 seconds at the least. |
| **Threshold** | **Resets ▸ Threshold** | Refills it early once this share of it has been mined since the last refill. `0`, the default, turns it off. Left-click adds 5%, right-click removes 5%, middle-click asks for an exact value from 0 to 100. |
| **Force reset** | **Resets ▸ Force reset** | Refills it now. |

Every refill, whatever caused it, starts the interval over. Shortening the interval applies at once; lengthening it applies from the next refill. The threshold counts the blocks broken **through the mine** since its last refill, against the number of blocks in its shape.

<Callout type="note" title="An unloaded mine waits">
A refill that falls due while none of the mine's chunks are loaded is held until they all are. Nobody can mine a mine nobody is near, and filling it would load every chunk at once. It happens as soon as somebody comes close enough.
</Callout>

## The warning

**Resets ▸ Warning** sets how long before an interval refill the players inside are told: 10 seconds by default. When it starts, everyone inside sees the `visuals.reset-warning-title` title and the `visuals.reset-bossbar` countdown, which takes the place of the "next reset" bar until the refill. The warning belongs to the interval: a threshold or a forced refill happens straight away.

## What happens to players inside

In order, when a refill begins:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The safe teleport">
    If **Teleports ▸ Safe teleport** is set, everyone inside is sent there first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Loose entities are cleared">
    With `settings.clean-entities-on-reset` on (the default), dropped items, experience orbs, projectiles, minecarts, end crystals and fireworks inside the shape are removed. Mobs, armour stands, item frames and paintings are left. Players never are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The area is refilled">
    Over a few ticks. All the refills running at once share about 4 milliseconds a tick, so several mines resetting together cost what one does, spread over more ticks. A block that already is the material drawn for it is not touched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buried players are lifted">
    With **Teleports ▸ Lift buried players** on (the default), anyone the new blocks buried is moved up to the first gap two blocks tall above them, at most two blocks above the mine's top. Somebody under a roof with no gap goes to the safe teleport, or else the entry teleport.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It is announced">
    The `sounds.reset` sound plays to the players inside, other plugins receive `MineResetEvent`, and the `reset-announcement` line goes to whoever `announcements.reset` says.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="A refill overwrites everything in the shape">
Every block inside the shape is redrawn from the composition, including anything a player placed there since the last refill. Keep decoration outside the selected box, or outside the sphere or cylinder cut out of it.
</Callout>

## Teleports

| Button | Click | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Teleports ▸ Entry teleport** | left / shift | Where `/mine <id>` and the `/mines` menu send players. Left-click sets it where you stand; shift-click clears it. Without one, `/mine` answers "This mine has no entry point." |
| **Teleports ▸ Safe teleport** | left / shift | Where players inside are sent at the start of every refill. Optional. |
| **Teleports ▸ Lift buried players** | click | Toggles lifting players the refill buried. On by default. |

## Going further

### Who reads the reset announcement

`announcements.reset` in `config.yml` (also under **Plugin ▸ Settings ▸ Announcements**):

| Value | Who |
|---|---|
| `OFF` | Nobody. |
| `NEARBY` | Players within `announcements.radius` blocks of the mine's centre, 64 by default. The default. |
| `SERVER` | Everyone online. |

The default line carries a clickable **[Go]** that runs `/mine <id>`.

### Silence the titles and bars

Empty a title's or a bar's `text` in `messages.yml` to turn it off, or set a sound to `""` in `config.yml`.

<Details title="Technical details">
Where each classic mine stands in its cycle (blocks mined since the last refill, and when the next one is due) is saved every 30 seconds and when the server stops. After a restart the countdown takes up where it was and the threshold keeps counting from the same share. A refill that fell due while the server was off happens as soon as the mine's chunks are loaded. `settings.classic-tick-interval-seconds` (1 by default) is how often classic mines are checked for a due refill.
</Details>
