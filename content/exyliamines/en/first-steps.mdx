Everything below happens in menus. The only things typed are the mine's id and the occasional number.

## A first classic mine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    `/minesadmin` with `exyliamines.admin`. It opens the list of this server's mines, empty for now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create classic mine** and type an id in chat, such as `coal`. Ids are lowercase letters,
    digits, `_` and `-`, up to 64 characters, and an id any server on the same database already uses is
    refused. The mine is created enabled, named after its id, and its setup screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the area">
    **Area** → **Mine region**. You get ExyliaLib's selection wand: mark the two opposite corners of
    the box and confirm. The blocks between them, both corners included, are the mine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what it is made of">
    Back on the setup screen, **Block composition** → **Add block** and pick a block. Each entry starts
    with a weight of `10`; left- and right-click raise and lower it by one. Add stone, coal ore and iron
    ore, and give stone the largest weight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it">
    **Resets** → **Force reset**. The area fills from the composition over a few ticks. From now on it
    refills on its own every five minutes, the default interval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it an entry point">
    **Teleports** → **Entry teleport**, then stand where players should arrive and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Any player with the default permissions can now run `/mine coal`, or open `/mines` and click it.

<Callout type="tip" title="Or start from a template">
**Create from template** on the list offers ready mixes from `templates.yml`: coal, iron, gold, diamond,
nether and a realistic "natural ores". The new mine arrives with its name, icon and composition already
set, so only the area and the teleports are left. See [Mines](/docs/exyliamines/mines#templates).
</Callout>

## A first realistic mine

A realistic mine never fills its area. It watches the blocks that are already there and grows each one
back after it is broken, so it suits a natural cave or a hand-built ore wall.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    **Create realistic mine** on the list, and an id such as `cave`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the area">
    **Area** → **Mine region**, around blocks that already stand in the world.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a level per block that should grow back">
    **Regen levels** → **Add level**, and pick a block, for instance `IRON_ORE`. It starts with a
    regeneration time of 60 seconds. Only the materials added as levels are the mine's; anything else
    inside the area is left alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally, a degrade chain">
    Open the level, **Degrade chain** → **Add stage**: a delay, then a block. With `STONE` then
    `COBBLESTONE`, a broken iron ore turns to stone, the player mines the stone, it turns to cobblestone,
    they mine that too, and the ore comes back when its 60 seconds are up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Break an iron ore inside the area: it disappears, and comes back on its own. See
[Realistic mines](/docs/exyliamines/realistic-mines).
