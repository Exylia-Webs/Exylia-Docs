---
title: "Statistics"
description: "Blocks mined per player and per mine, each mine's top ten, how the counts are written, and where they show."
---

The plugin counts every block a player breaks through a mine — composition blocks, levels and degrade
stages alike — per player and per mine. That is the whole of its statistics, and it is what the
leaderboards are made of.

## Where they show

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `%mine_player_blocks%` | Blocks the reader mined across every mine. |
| `%mine_player_blocks_<id>%` | Blocks the reader mined in one mine. |
| `%mine_blocks_<id>%` | Blocks everybody mined in one mine. |
| `%mine_top_name_<id>_<place>%` | The name of the player at that place of the mine's top ten, from 1. |
| `%mine_top_blocks_<id>_<place>%` | Their blocks. |
| The `/mines` menu | Each mine's "Mined by you", and the total. |
| The hologram | `%total_blocks%`, if you add it to the lines. |

There is no leaderboard screen of its own: place the `top` placeholders in a hologram plugin, a
scoreboard or a menu. A place nobody holds yet reads `—` for the name and `0` for the blocks. See
[Placeholders](/docs/exyliamines/placeholders).

## How they are counted and written

- A break only adds to memory. The counts go to the database **every five seconds**, in one batch, and
  when the server stops.
- They are written as **additions**, never as a saved total. Two servers of a network counting the same
  player at the same moment both land; neither overwrites the other.
- A player's own counts are read when they join and kept current as they mine, so their placeholders move
  with every block.
- Each mine's total and top ten are read back from the database **once a minute**, which is why a
  leaderboard moves in steps.

Counts live in the `mines_stats` table, one row per player and mine.

<Callout type="warning" title="Deleting a mine deletes its counts">
Deleting a mine also deletes every player's count for it, from memory and from the database. A mine you
only want to close for a while is better **disabled** than deleted.
</Callout>

## Bringing the history over

A server coming from ExyliaSurvivalCore keeps its leaderboards: the blocks each player mined per mine, as
the survival core's stats module counted them, are imported once. See
[Migration](/docs/exyliamines/migration#mining-history).
