---
title: "Fortune"
description: "What a Fortune pickaxe does in a mine: vanilla drops, custom loot, money and lucky chances, the server-wide rules and each mine's own Fortune screen."
---

Fortune works in a mine on two layers. **Vanilla drops** are worked out by the server's own loot tables
with the tool in hand, so under `VANILLA` and `CHANCE` Fortune and Silk Touch behave there exactly as
anywhere else. **Custom loot** — the loot tables, the money they pay, the lucky rewards — is never seen by
those tables, so the plugin adds Fortune to it itself, as `config.yml` says. `OFF` and `LINEAR` take over
the vanilla drops too.

## The server-wide rules

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaMines/config.yml"
fortune:
  mode: VANILLA
  target: ITEMS
  per-level: 0.25
  lucky-per-level: 0.0
  max-level: -1
  silk-touch: true
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `mode` | `VANILLA` | How Fortune changes the mine's drops. See the table below. |
| `target` | `ITEMS` | What the mode multiplies: `ITEMS` (vanilla drops included), `MONEY` or `BOTH`. Commands are never multiplied. |
| `per-level` | `0.25` | What each level adds with `LINEAR` and `CHANCE`: `0.25` makes Fortune III ×1.75. Each mine may set its own. |
| `lucky-per-level` | `0.0` | Each level raises the chance of every lucky reward by this share: `0.1` makes Fortune III ×1.3. `0` leaves them alone. |
| `max-level` | `-1` | The highest Fortune level counted in mines, for vanilla drops and custom loot alike. `-1` counts any level, `0` turns Fortune off. |
| `silk-touch` | `true` | Whether Silk Touch works in mines. |

### The modes

| Mode | Vanilla drops | Custom loot at Fortune level `L` |
|---|---|---|
| `OFF` | Fortune is taken off the tool: a Fortune pickaxe drops as one without it. | Nothing. Custom loot ignores Fortune. |
| `VANILLA` | As in Minecraft: ores only. | The ore formula: the amount is multiplied by 1, or by 2 up to `L + 1`, the bonus coming up more often the higher the level — ×2.2 on average at Fortune III, as a diamond ore drops. |
| `LINEAR` | Every drop, plain blocks included, is multiplied by `1 + L × per-level` instead of Minecraft's formula, so a diamond block grows like a diamond ore. | The amount is multiplied by `1 + L × per-level`, the fraction rolled: ×1.75 at Fortune III with the default, so 1 item becomes 2 three times in four. |
| `CHANCE` | As in Minecraft: ores only. | Amounts stay as they are; the chance of every line rises instead, to `chance × (1 + L × per-level)`, at most 100. |

`LINEAR` only takes over the vanilla drops when `target` is `ITEMS` or `BOTH`; with `target: MONEY` they
stay as in Minecraft. A tool that keeps Silk Touch drops the block itself, which `LINEAR` never multiplies.

With `target: MONEY` or `BOTH`, `VANILLA` and `LINEAR` multiply the money a line pays the same way.
`CHANCE` ignores `target`: it raises the chance of every line of the table, money and command lines
included.

`lucky-per-level` works whatever the mode, `OFF` included, and counts the level after the mine's limits.

## Each mine's own settings

**Rewards** → **Fortune** on a mine's setup screen opens the mine's **Fortune** screen, which overrides four
of those settings for that mine alone. Mode, highest level and Silk Touch step through their values on a
click — left-click forward, right-click back — and back to following `config.yml`:

| Button | Steps through |
|---|---|
| **Mode** | Follow `config.yml` → `OFF` → `VANILLA` → `LINEAR` → `CHANCE` → follow `config.yml` |
| **Highest level** | Follow `config.yml` → `0` (no Fortune) → `1` → … → `5` → any → follow `config.yml` |
| **Per level** | Left-click asks for the share each level adds, from `0` to `10`, prefilled with the current one; right-click goes back to `config.yml`'s `per-level`. |
| **Silk Touch** | Follow `config.yml` → allowed → denied → follow `config.yml` |

A setting the mine leaves to the file reads "(default)" next to its value in the menu, so you always see
what actually applies; the **Fortune** button on the rewards page shows all four. `target` and
`lucky-per-level` are server-wide only.

## How the limits apply

The highest level and the Silk Touch switch are applied to a **copy** of the tool the drops are worked out
with: a Fortune V pickaxe in a mine capped at 2 drops as a Fortune II one, and a Silk Touch pickaxe in a
mine that denies it drops as if it had no Silk Touch. The real pickaxe is untouched and still takes the
durability.

With ExyliaSurvivalCore installed, its enchantment limits for the place are applied first, then the
mine's.

<Callout type="note" title="Silk Touch and experience">
A tool that still has Silk Touch after the limits gets no experience from ores, as in vanilla: the ore
itself drops instead.
</Callout>
