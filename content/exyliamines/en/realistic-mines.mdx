A realistic mine never refills its area. It looks after the blocks already standing in it: each one a player breaks disappears and grows back on its own, after a time you set per material. It suits a natural cave, an ore wall built by hand, or a quarry that should look untouched.

Nothing scans the area to find out what it holds. The mine's blocks are exactly the materials you add as **levels**.

## Before you start

You need a realistic mine with its area selected, around blocks that already stand there. See [Create and edit mines](/docs/exyliamines/mines) and [Select a mine's area](/docs/exyliamines/area).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the mine">
    Run `/minesadmin` and left-click your realistic mine. To skip typing, use **Create ▸ From template** and the `ores` template, which makes one level per ore.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a level for each block">
    Click **Mine ▸ Regen levels**, then **Levels ▸ Add level** and pick a block, for example `DIAMOND_ORE`. A material that cannot be placed as a block is refused, and so is one that already has a level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how long it takes to grow back">
    Click the level, then **Level ▸ Regen time** and type a time such as `30`, `30s` or `5m`. At least one second. A new level starts at 60 seconds, from `settings.default-realistic-regen-seconds` in `config.yml`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for every block that should grow back">
    Click **Back ▸ Levels**, then add the next level. Blocks that are not levels are not the mine's and never grow back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Go to the area and break one of the blocks you added a level for. It disappears, drops its loot, and comes back once its regen time has passed. A realistic mine has no **Teleports** section: send players there with a warp of your own.

<Callout type="note" title="Realistic mines have no entry point">
The setup screen of a realistic mine has no **Teleports** section, so it cannot be given an entry point from the editor. `/mine <id>` and the `/mines` menu then answer "This mine has no entry point." for it. Entering one shows no title and no "next reset" bar, since there is no reset to count down to; potion effects and the boost bar work as in a classic mine.
</Callout>

## Levels

**Mine ▸ Regen levels** lists them in order.

| Click on a level | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left- or right-click | Opens the level. |
| Shift-right-click | Removes it, after a confirmation. |

Inside a level:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Level ▸ Regen time** | How long after the break the block is back. |
| **Level ▸ Loot table** | What breaking this material pays instead of its vanilla drop. See [Loot and effects](/docs/exyliamines/loot-and-effects). |
| **Level ▸ Break effects** | Effects played when this material breaks, in place of the mine's own. |
| **Level ▸ Degrade chain** | The stages the block passes through while it grows back. |
| **Level ▸ Move up** / **Move down** | Reorders the list. Levels are matched by material, so the order is only how the list reads. |
| **Level ▸ Remove** | Removes the level. |

## What a break does

Breaking a level's material inside the mine:

1. The block is broken by the mine, with the level's loot table if it has one and the tool's vanilla drop otherwise.
2. It turns to **air**, or to the first stage of its degrade chain.
3. Once the level's regen time has passed since that break, the block is back as it was.

`sounds.block-mined` in `config.yml` plays to the miner on every break of a realistic mine. It is empty, so silent, by default.

## Degrade chains

A degrade chain is what a broken block shows while it grows back, instead of a plain hole. Open a level, click **Level ▸ Degrade chain**, then **Chain ▸ Add stage**: it asks for the delay first, then the block.

| Click on a stage | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left-click | Moves it up. |
| Right-click | Moves it down. |
| Middle-click | Opens the stage's own loot table. |
| Shift-right-click | Removes it, after a confirmation. |

Stages run strictly in list order, and **the player drives them**: a stage stays until somebody mines it.

- The first stage appears its delay after the original break; until then the spot is air. A delay of `0` places it at once.
- Mining a stage brings in the next one, its delay counted from that break.
- Mining the last stage leaves the spot as air.
- Whatever stage the block has reached, it heals back to the level's material once the level's regen time has passed since the **original** break. That deadline is a safety net for a player who never comes back to finish the chain, and it also means a chain mined quickly still waits, as air, for the rest of the regen time.

For example, a `DIAMOND_ORE` level with a regen time of 2 minutes and a chain of `STONE` (0s) then `COBBLESTONE` (0s): the ore breaks into stone, the stone into cobblestone, the cobblestone into air, and the diamond ore is back two minutes after the first swing.

A stage pays its own loot table when it has one, and the vanilla drop of its block otherwise. Its break effects are the mine's global ones, unless the stage's block is also a level with effects of its own.

<Callout type="warning" title="Chain blocks belong to the mine everywhere in the area">
Every material used in a chain counts as one of the mine's blocks, so a player can mine a stage. That holds anywhere in the area: a `STONE` already standing in the cave when `STONE` is a stage is broken through the mine, pays its vanilla drop and, not being mid-regeneration, **never grows back**. Pick stage blocks that do not occur naturally in the area, or add that material as a level of its own.
</Callout>

## Only what the mine still owns grows back

When a block is due to come back (a stage appearing, or the final heal) the mine checks what is there first. It writes its block only over air, the level's own material or one of its stages. Anything else was put there by a player after the break, and overwriting it would delete it, together with whatever a chest held. That spot is left as the player made it and the mine stops regrowing it.

Placing the mine's own materials inside the area is refused anyway, so what a player can put in a hole is always something of their own. See [Select a mine's area](/docs/exyliamines/area#what-cannot-farm-a-mine).

<Details title="Technical details: restarts">
Every block mid-regeneration is written to the database (`mines_broken_blocks`). A block broken a moment ago goes out on the next tick; stage changes and heals are batched every five seconds.

When the plugin starts, **every block still broken is healed at once**, whatever time it had left: a restart or reload regrows them all rather than resuming their timers. Then:

- A block whose chunk is not loaded stays tracked and is retried every 10 seconds until it is; its row is only deleted once the block is back.
- A block in a world that is not loaded yet waits for that world to load.
- A row for a level or a mine that has been removed is discarded.
- A row for a mine this server does not know is left alone: on a shared database, that mine belongs to another server.

`settings.realistic-tick-interval-seconds` (1 by default) is how often realistic mines are checked for blocks to grow back.
</Details>
