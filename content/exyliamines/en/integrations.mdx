Nothing below is required. Each plugin is detected on its own, and without it the mine works the same,
only without what it adds.

## ExyliaSurvivalCore

With ExyliaSurvivalCore installed, four of its systems reach into every mine:

| System | What it does in a mine |
|---|---|
| **Boosters** | The miner's drop and experience boosters multiply vanilla drops, experience and item loot; their money boosters multiply the money loot pays through its economy. Mines count as the `mines` source. |
| **Auto-sell** | Every drop and every loot item is offered to the miner's auto-sell first. What it sells is not given as well. |
| **Enchantment limits** | The limits it sets for the place are applied to the tool before the mine's own Fortune and Silk Touch limits. |
| **Blocked items** | A player holding an item its rules forbid cannot break the mine's blocks with it. |

These apply after the mine's own [boosts](/docs/exyliamines/boosts), so the two stack.

The survival core is asked on every break rather than once at startup, so it may be enabled after
ExyliaMines, or reloaded, without anything going stale.

<Callout type="warning" title="An older ExyliaSurvivalCore with its own mines module">
Releases of ExyliaSurvivalCore from before the move still have a `mines` module. If it is on, both plugins
would claim every block broken in a mine, so ExyliaMines warns in the console at startup:
"ExyliaSurvivalCore still runs its own mines module." Update the survival core, or turn its `mines`
module off. Its mines are not imported while that module runs — see [Migration](/docs/exyliamines/migration).
</Callout>

## PlaceholderAPI

Every placeholder is published to PlaceholderAPI under the plugin's name, with nothing to download from
the eCloud: `%exyliamines_mine_reset_coal%`. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliamines/placeholders).

## Money

Money lines in loot tables and lucky rewards are paid through ExyliaLib's economy, which needs an economy
plugin behind it — **Vault** with one installed, or PlayerPoints. Without one, money lines pay nothing and
everything else still works. With ExyliaSurvivalCore, money is offered to its economy first, which is what
lets its boosters and its own currencies apply.

## World managers

**Worlds** and **Multiverse-Core** load their worlds after the plugins enable. A mine in such a world is
registered the moment the world loads, and the realistic blocks it was regrowing there are picked up
again. Nothing to configure.

## Combat plugins

`/mine` and the `/mines` menu refuse to send a player who is in combat. Combat is read from ExyliaLib's
combat module, which follows **DeluxeCombat** or **PvPManager** when one is installed.

## Protection plugins and anticheats

No flags, no exceptions to add. A mine performs its own breaks after the anticheats have had their say and
never forces a cancelled break through, so WorldGuard regions and nuker checks keep working inside mines.
See [Area and shapes](/docs/exyliamines/area#how-a-break-inside-a-mine-works).

## Other plugins' tools

A plugin breaking blocks for a player — a 3x3 pickaxe, a vein miner, an enchantment — should hand each
block to the mine through `MinesService.breakBlock`, so every one gets the mine's permission, loot and
regeneration. See the [API](/docs/exyliamines/api).

## Networks

Several servers can share one database. Each mine belongs to the server it was created on, and each server
loads and runs only its own:

- **Ids are unique across the network.** Creating, cloning or importing a mine checks the whole table, so
  a mine on one server can never overwrite another server's.
- **Boosts** belong to the server they were started on.
- **Statistics** are kept per mine id and written as additions, so counts from different servers never
  overwrite each other.
- **Broken blocks** of a mine another server owns are left alone.

The server's name is the one ExyliaLib knows it by on the network. A mine row written before servers were
recorded — an old import — belongs to whichever server reads it.

## Folia

Supported. Every block change runs on the thread that owns the block, every player change on the thread
that owns the player, and the events the plugin fires are called on the region thread of the block they
are about.
