By the end of this page you have a series of events played one after another with the same players, a score for each stage, and a champion who gets a prize. A series is built from the menus out of events that already exist, so no file editing is needed.

## Before you start

- At least two event configurations that are enabled and ready; see [Create an arena](/docs/exyliaevents/arenas).
- Two or more free players online: players not already in an event.
- Only one gauntlet runs at a time. Starting a second is refused with a message.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the series">
    Run `/eventsadmin`, click **Gauntlets**, then **Create gauntlet**. Type an id, then a name, in the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the stages">
    In the series, click **Add stage** and pick an event. Repeat in the order you want them played. A series needs at least two stages, and every event type works as a stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange them">
    On a stage, left-click moves it earlier, shift-left-click moves it later and right-click removes it. **Name** and **Icon** change how the series looks in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Click **Start gauntlet**, or in the list shift-left-click the series. Or run `/eventsadmin gauntlet start <series>`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

With two players online and free, start the series. Both are placed in the first stage, and after `lobby-seconds` (20 by default) the stage begins. When it ends the top three standings are broadcast, and after `between-stages-seconds` (8) the next stage starts. After the last stage the champion is announced. `/eventsadmin gauntlet stop` ends the series and the stage it is playing.

## Going further

### Reward the champion

Each event's normal [rewards](/docs/exyliaevents/rewards) still pay out per stage. `gauntlet.champion-commands` in `config.yml` is the prize for the whole series; use `%player%` and `%series%`. Several players on a tie each get it.

### Let players build their own line

A player with `exyliaevents.start` runs `/events`, clicks **Start event**, then **Gauntlet**. Every click adds one of the startable events, up to 12 stages, and **Start gauntlet** runs it. Left-click on **Your line** undoes the last stage and right-click clears it. That line is not saved: it belongs to that run. The usual `/events start` rules apply, including the start cooldown (unless they have `exyliaevents.cooldown.bypass`) and the main lobby.

### Late arrivals

Whoever joins a stage's lobby is in the gauntlet from then on, so arriving late to the series does not stop a player scoring in what is left of it.

## Configuration

Edit these in `config.yml` or in the settings screen of `/eventsadmin`.

```yaml title="config.yml"
gauntlet:
  points-winner: 10
  points-participant: 2
  lobby-seconds: 20
  between-stages-seconds: 8
  champion-commands: []
```

<Details title="How a run goes and what each key does">

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `points-winner` | `10` | Points for winning a stage. |
| `points-participant` | `2` | Points for playing a stage without winning. |
| `lobby-seconds` | `20` | Lobby seconds before a stage is force-started, even if its own countdown had not finished. |
| `between-stages-seconds` | `8` | The pause between one stage and the next. |
| `champion-commands` | empty | Console commands for each champion. `%player%` and `%series%`. |

1. The stage's event starts and the roster is pushed into it.
2. A stage with fewer than two players is dropped and announced, and the series moves on.
3. After `lobby-seconds` the event is force-started.
4. When it ends, winners earn `points-winner` and every other player who took part earns `points-participant`.
5. After the last stage, whoever has the most points (above zero) is crowned.

`start` takes every online player who is not in an event and needs at least two. A series with fewer than two stages cannot start. Series are stored in the database table `event_gauntlets` (id, display name, icon material, stages), the icon defaulting to `NETHER_STAR`.

</Details>
