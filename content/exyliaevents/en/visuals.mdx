## Sidebars

`scoreboards.yml` holds one sidebar per event type, plus a `lobby` board shown while players wait.
Each is an ExyliaLib sidebar: a title, a list of lines and a refresh interval in ticks.

```yaml title="scoreboards.yml"
scoreboards:
  lobby: ...
  tnttag: ...
  spleef: ...
  csgo: ...
  topkiller_teams: ...
```

Each of the 51 registered types has its own key, team variants included, so a Quake board and a Quake Teams
board are edited separately.

The values available inside the lines are the ones that type provides — its round, its timer, its
score, the player's own kills — because each event builds its own scoreboard context.

<Callout type="note" title="TAB is not required for sidebars">
The sidebar is drawn by ExyliaLib either way. What TAB changes is the **handover**: when TAB is
installed, leaving an event restores the player to their normal TAB scoreboard and nametag rather
than to nothing.
</Callout>

## Boss bars

`config.yml` carries a boss bar per type that has one, in that type's own block:

```yaml title="config.yml"
tnttag:
  boss-bar:
    text: "{primary}Eliminating in {error}%time% ⌚"
    color: PURPLE
    style: NOTCHED_20
  tagged-action-bar:
    text: "..."
```

Types with their own visual blocks include TNT Tag, Tag Game, Block Party, Build Battle, Quake,
CS:GO, Parkour, Chairs, Slap, Gem Crawler, Pillars of Fortune, LMS, Simon Says, Survival Games and
Mace Roulette and Tournament, alongside `winner-fireworks` for the celebration at the end.

Mace Roulette's block carries the bar the hunter races against, the title the chosen player is shown,
and two sounds written as plain names rather than effects:

```yaml title="config.yml"
maceroulette:
  spin-tick-sound: BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_HAT
  spin-stop-sound: "ITEM_MACE_SMASH_GROUND|1.0|1.0"
```

`spin-tick-sound` is the tick as the mace moves from player to player: it is played at a pitch the
spin works out as it slows, and an effect carries only one fixed pitch. `spin-stop-sound` is written
`NAME|volume|pitch`.

Tournament's block is three separate effects rather than a bar: a freeze title before a match, the
same countdown on the action bar, and an "you're next" title for whoever fights after.

## Nametags

Seven types take their players' names off for as long as the game runs, because they are played on
not knowing where somebody is and a name is drawn through the wall they hide behind: Hide and Seek,
Block Morph Hunt, Murder Mystery, Quake, Quake Teams, CS:GO and CS:GO Teams.

With TAB installed, TAB hides the names and gives them back. Without it, the players are put in a
scoreboard team that shows no names, and each one goes back to the team they were in before when they
leave or the event ends, so a rank plugin's colour is returned rather than lost.

## Visibility

`player-isolation` in `config.yml`, on by default, keeps everybody in an event — players and
spectators — out of sight of the rest of the server, in the world and in the tab list, and shows them
only each other. That is also what stops two events in nearby arenas from showing each other's
players. Turned off, everybody sees everybody.

Inside an event, spectators are hidden from the players in the game.

## Music

Events can play music discs to their participants:

```yaml title="config.yml"
settings:
  event-music-enabled: true
  event-music-volume: 0.5
  event-music-interval: 150
  event-music-discs: [MUSIC_DISC_PIGSTEP, MUSIC_DISC_CAT, ...]
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `event-music-enabled` | `true` | Whether events play music at all. |
| `event-music-volume` | `0.5` | How loud. |
| `event-music-interval` | `150` | Seconds between discs. |
| `event-music-discs` | 19 discs | The pool it picks from. |

A player who does not want it uses `/events music`, which is stored per player and remembered.

Each configuration has its own switch too: **Music** on the setup screen, stored as `music-enabled` and
on by default, for an arena that should stay quiet while the others play. Chairs is the exception to
all three switches — its music is the game, so it always plays.

<Callout type="tip" title="Turning it off server-wide">
`event-music-enabled: false` silences it for everyone and makes the per-player toggle moot — the
usual choice on a server that already has its own ambience.
</Callout>
