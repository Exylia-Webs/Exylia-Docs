ExyliaRandomKitsCore is drop-in PvP where **every life starts with randomly rolled items**. A player connects, their items are rolled by rarity, they fight, they die, and the next life rolls again. Nobody really dies: lethal damage is cancelled, the kill is credited, and the player is healed and sent to a spawn with new items. RandomKits runs on a dedicated server: whoever connects is already in the arena, and the server has exactly one arena.

Items are **procedural**: the server keeps one item pool in `items.yml`, and every life rolls each piece, its amount and its enchantments on their own rarity, so no two lives are quite the same. Across lives players keep a level, prestige, upgrades, perks, missions and a record, and several servers of a network can run as one pool.

It is a **premium** plugin, installed through a licence loader. See [Install ExyliaRandomKitsCore](/docs/exyliarandomkits/installation).

## Before you start

You need a Paper 1.21.4 (or newer) server on Java 21 and a built arena: a PvP area, a safe box and somewhere for players to spawn. Folia is supported. The rest is optional (see [Requirements](#requirements)).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the loader">
    Drop the loader jar into `plugins/` and start the server. See [Install ExyliaRandomKitsCore](/docs/exyliarandomkits/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the arena">
    Run `/rkadmin setup` and follow the wizard. See [Set up the arena](/docs/exyliarandomkits/arenas).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune what players roll">
    `items.yml` ships ready, so lives roll items at once. Change the pool when you want to. See [Items and rarities](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let people play">
    Nothing to configure: whoever connects is moved into the arena. See [Play a round](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A life in an arena

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Join** | Connecting is enough: once their profile loads, the player is moved into the arena. |
| **Roll** | Each piece of the life and each enchantment level draws its own rarity by weight. The life's rarity is the commonest one whose budget covers what the life holds, so a strong piece is paid for by weaker ones. In a **Draft** arena the player picks one of a few rolled loadouts instead. |
| **Spawn** | The items land where the player's saved layout puts them. Spawn protection runs until it ends or they attack. |
| **Fight** | Kills, assists, streaks and evolution at streak milestones; rewards come from `triggers.yml`. |
| **Death** | The killer is credited and the player starts a new life at a spawn, with new items. |
| **Leave** | There is no leave command; going elsewhere is the proxy's job. A player who disconnects outside combat comes back with the same life (`rejoin.keep-life` in `play.yml`). |

## Check that it works

Connect with a test account. You should land in the arena with a title and a set of items, and `/rk` should open the hub. If not, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliarandomkits/troubleshooting).

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.4 or newer, or a fork of it. Folia supported. |
| Java | 21 |
| Required | The newest `ExyliaLib`, installed for you if it is missing |
| Optional | `packetevents` (only the extras drawn with packets stay off without it), `PlaceholderAPI` for the placeholders in other plugins, `FastAsyncWorldEdit` for arena schematics, an economy for upgrade and perk costs and money rewards, `ExyliaDuels` for the Random kit, `ExyliaProxyUtils` and Redis for a network |

<Callout type="note" title="About ExyliaLib">
Configuration, menus, prompts and the setup wizard, player stays, teleports, regions and schematics, rewards, sequences, the economy, the database, the web editor and the network all come from ExyliaLib. ExyliaRandomKitsCore is written on top of it and does not run without it, which is why the loader installs it on a server that lacks it.
</Callout>

## Languages

The plugin ships English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish (`en`, `es`, `fr`, `pt`, `ru`, `tr`): every message and every menu. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliarandomkits/configuration#language) and [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).
