ExyliaRandomKitsCore is drop-in PvP arenas where **every life starts with randomly rolled items**. A player types
`/rk play`, their items are rolled by rarity, they fight, they die, and the next life rolls again. Nobody really
dies: lethal damage is cancelled, the kill is credited, and the player is healed and sent to a spawn with
new items. When they leave, they get back exactly what they came with.

Items are **procedural**: the server keeps one item pool in `items.yml`, and every life rolls each piece, its
amount and its enchantments on their own rarity, so no two lives are quite the same. Across lives players keep a
level, prestige, upgrades, perks, missions and a record, and several servers of a network can run as one
pool.

It is a **premium** plugin, installed through a licence loader. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliarandomkits/installation).

## A life in an arena

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Join** | `/rk play [arena]` saves the player's things, moves them in and empties their inventory. |
| **Roll** | Each piece of the life and each enchantment level draws its own rarity by weight, and the life's rarity is its second-rarest piece. In a **Draft** arena the player picks one of a few rolled loadouts instead. |
| **Spawn** | The items land where the player's saved layout puts them. Spawn protection runs until it ends or they attack. |
| **Fight** | Kills, assists, streaks and evolution at streak milestones; rewards come from `triggers.yml`. |
| **Death** | The killer is credited, consumables drop, and the player starts a new life at the spawn farthest from the others. |
| **Leave** | `/rk leave` gives everything back exactly as it was. |

See [Playing](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing).

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.4 or newer, or a fork of it. Folia supported. |
| Java | 21 |
| Required dependencies | `packetevents`, and the newest `ExyliaLib`, installed for you if it is missing |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI` for the placeholders in other plugins, `FastAsyncWorldEdit` for arena schematics, an economy for upgrade and perk costs and money rewards, `ExyliaDuels` for the Random kit, `ExyliaProxyUtils` and Redis for a network |

<Callout type="note" title="About ExyliaLib">
Configuration, menus, prompts and the setup wizard, player stays, teleports, regions and schematics,
rewards, sequences, the economy, the database, the web editor and the network all come from ExyliaLib.
ExyliaRandomKitsCore is written on top of it and does not run without it, which is why the loader installs
it on a server that lacks it.
</Callout>
