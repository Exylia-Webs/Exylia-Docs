---
title: "Upgrading from kits"
description: "What changed when kits gave way to the item pool, what was removed, what stays in the database and what a server owner does after updating."
---

Earlier versions gave every life a **kit**. Now every life rolls its own items from `items.yml`: see
[Items and rarities](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits). Updating needs no migration by hand.

## What is gone

| Removed | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| Kits, their slots and variants, and `/rkadmin kit ...` | The item pool, `items.yml`. |
| Per-kit permissions | Nothing: every player rolls from the same pool. |
| Kit levels, kit upgrades, **Ascended** kits and kit mastery (`kit-upgrades` and `mastery` in `progression.yml`, `on-mastery-<n>`) | Account upgrades, prestige and perks. See [Progression](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression). |
| Kit balance and `balance.yml`, `/rkadmin` → **Balance** | Nothing: items are weighted in `items.yml` and `rarities.yml`. |
| The kit catalogue (`/rk kits`) and discovered kits | Nothing. |
| **Kit of the Hour** events and the arena kit pool (`/rkadmin arena pool`) | Rarity rush and the other events. See [Events and seasons](/docs/exyliarandomkits/events-and-seasons). |
| The ten starter kits and the kit importers (`/rkadmin import`) | The default `items.yml`. |
| `roll.anti-repeat`, the `layout` map of `config.yml`, `evolution.kits`, the Duels `kits` and `count-stats` keys | Layout roles come from the categories of `items.yml`. |
| The `KIT_KILLS` mission type and the `kit` field of missions | `RARITY_KILLS` and the other types. |
| The placeholders `kit`, `kit_id`, `discovered`, `kit_level_<kit>`, `mastery_<kit>`, `mastery_title_<kit>` and `ascended_<kit>` | `rarity` and `rarity_id`. |

## What stays

Nothing is deleted from the database. The old kit, kit stats and balance tables, and the per-kit records in
player profiles, stay untouched; they are simply no longer read. Levels, prestige, upgrades, perks, missions,
statistics, seasons and arenas are kept as they were.

## On the first start

- `items.yml` is written with the default pool. Tune it before opening the arenas to players.
- The keys that no longer exist leave `config.yml`, `progression.yml` and `events.yml`, and `balance.yml` is
  no longer read. The old `hour_kit` event leaves the default rotation.
- Saved hotbar layouts keyed by the old roles (`WEAPON`, `BOW`...) fall back to each category's default `slot`
  until the player saves a layout again.
- The player menus under `lang/<language>/menus/user/` keep the server's copy: the removed buttons (the kit
  catalogue, kit upgrades, kits found) stop doing anything, and their removal waits in `/exylialib updates`.
  Admin menus are regenerated.
