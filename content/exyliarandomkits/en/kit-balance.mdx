Earlier versions gave every life a **kit**. Now every life rolls its own items from `items.yml`. Updating needs no migration by hand: follow the steps below once, and check that lives roll as you expect.

## Before you start

Update the plugin jar and start the server once. Nothing is deleted from the database: the old kit, kit stats and balance tables, and the per-kit records in player profiles, stay untouched; they are simply no longer read. Levels, prestige, upgrades, perks, missions, statistics, seasons and arenas are kept as they were.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Let the first start write the new files">
    `items.yml` is written with the default pool, and the keys that no longer exist leave `config.yml`, `progression.yml` and `events.yml`. `balance.yml` is no longer read. The old `hour_kit` event leaves the default rotation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune the item pool">
    Do it before opening the arena to players. See [Items and rarities](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the player menus">
    The player menus under `lang/<language>/menus/user/` keep the server's copy: the removed buttons (the kit catalogue, kit upgrades, kits found) stop doing anything, and their removal waits in `/exylialib updates`. Admin menus are regenerated. See [Edit menus](/docs/exylialib/edit-menus).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell players about their layout">
    Saved hotbar layouts keyed by the old roles (`WEAPON`, `BOW`...) fall back to each category's default `slot` until the player saves a layout again with `/rk layout`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Join the arena and die a few times: every life should bring a different set of items and a title with its rarity. `/rkadmin` shows the **Items** and **Rarities** buttons. No error about missing kits should appear in the console.

<Details title="What was removed and what replaces it">
| Removed | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| Kits, their slots and variants, and `/rkadmin kit ...` | The item pool, `items.yml`. |
| Per-kit permissions | Nothing: every player rolls from the same pool. |
| Kit levels, kit upgrades, **Ascended** kits and kit mastery (`kit-upgrades` and `mastery` in `progression.yml`, `on-mastery-<n>`) | Account upgrades, prestige and perks. See [Progression](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression). |
| Kit balance and `balance.yml`, `/rkadmin` → **Balance** | Nothing: items are weighted in `items.yml` and `rarities.yml`. |
| The kit catalogue and discovered kits | Nothing. |
| **Kit of the Hour** events and the arena kit pool | Rarity rush and the other events. See [Events and seasons](/docs/exyliarandomkits/events-and-seasons). |
| The ten starter kits and the kit importers (`/rkadmin import`) | The default `items.yml`. |
| `roll.anti-repeat`, the `layout` map of `config.yml`, `evolution.kits`, the Duels `kits` and `count-stats` keys | Layout roles come from the categories of `items.yml`. |
| The `KIT_KILLS` mission type and the `kit` field of missions | `RARITY_KILLS` and the other types. |
| The placeholders `kit`, `kit_id`, `discovered`, `kit_level_<kit>`, `mastery_<kit>`, `mastery_title_<kit>` and `ascended_<kit>` | `rarity` and `rarity_id`. |
</Details>
