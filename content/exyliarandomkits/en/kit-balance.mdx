title: "Kit balance"
description: "How every kit is judged by its K/D against the others, the Balance screen, suggested weights, the automatic cycle and every key of balance.yml."
---

Kit balance looks at how every kit actually does. Each kit's **K/D** over a rolling window is compared with
the pooled K/D of every kit: a kit far above the middle is **overpowered**, far below **underpowered**. For
each one it suggests the weight one step towards the middle, to apply by hand or on a timer.

Kills, deaths, rolls and how long each life lasted are counted per kit and per day. Every server of a network
group adds to the same numbers.

## The Balance screen

`/rkadmin` → **Balance** lists every kit, best performing first, the ones without enough fights last.

| Line | What it shows |
|---|---|
| K/D | The kit's K/D, and how far it is from the middle (`1` is the middle). |
| Average life | How long a life with this kit lasts. |
| Fights, Rolls | Kills plus deaths, and how often it was rolled, in the window. |
| Verdict | Overpowered, underpowered, balanced, or not judged yet. |
| Weight | Its weight now, and its base. |
| Suggested | The weight one step towards the middle. |

- **Left-click** applies the suggestion.
- **Right-click** resets the kit's numbers, after a confirmation: its days are deleted on every server of
  the group.

**How it judges**, at the bottom, shows the window, the step, the bounds, whether the automatic cycle is on
and how often it runs.

### Weights and their base

A kit's **base** is the weight an admin gave it. Suggested weights never leave `min-factor`..`max-factor`
times the base, so a kit can never be balanced out of the game or into every roll. Setting a weight by hand
(the kit screen, `/rkadmin kit weight`, the web editor or an import that replaces the kit) makes that the new
base and restarts the kit's numbers. A kit weighted `0` is off on purpose and stays so.

## The automatic cycle

With `auto: true`, every `interval` one server of the group moves every judged kit's weight one step towards
the middle. Every change is written to the staff audit log (`/exylialib logs`).

## `balance.yml`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `auto` | `false` | Moves the weights on its own every `interval`. |
| `interval` | `6h` | Time between two automatic cycles; at least one minute. |
| `window` | 7 days | How far back the numbers go, in whole days. |
| `min-sample` | `200` | Kills plus deaths a kit needs in the window before it is judged. |
| `tolerance` | `0.15` | How far from the middle still counts as balanced: 15%. |
| `step` | `0.1` | The share a weight moves per cycle: 10%. At most 0.9. |
| `min-factor` | `0.5` | The lowest weight, as a share of the base. |
| `max-factor` | `2.0` | The highest weight, as a share of the base. |
| `refresh` | `5m` | How often the numbers are read again for the screen; at least 30 seconds. |

Only arena fights are counted. Random kit duels in ExyliaDuels never move these numbers, even with
`duels.count-stats` on, which only feeds kit stats and mastery. See
[Network and ExyliaDuels](/docs/exyliarandomkits/network#exyliaduels).
