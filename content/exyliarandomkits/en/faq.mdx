## Is ExyliaRandomKitsCore free?

No. It is a premium plugin, bought on [BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/exylia-randomkitscore.134730/) and installed as a loader jar that checks the
licence on every start. Bought on BuiltByBit, the licence is embedded in that jar: there is no key to enter.
See [Installation](/docs/exyliarandomkits/installation).

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is a hard dependency: without it the server never loads the plugin. ExyliaLib is needed too;
the loader installs it, or stages a newer one, and asks for a restart — restart once more. If the console
reports a licence error, check the key in `loader.yml`.

## "There is no arena ready yet"

An arena is ready once it has a PvP region and at least one spawn, and its world is loaded. Open
`/rkadmin` → **Arenas**: each arena shows its state. See [Arenas](/docs/exyliarandomkits/arenas).

## A player joins and gets no items

Nothing rolled for them: `items.yml` has no categories, or every category failed its `chance`. Open the item pool
in the [web editor](/docs/exyliarandomkits/web-editor-and-import) and check that at least one category always appears.
See [Items and rarities](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits).

## Does a player lose their own items?

No. Players start empty-handed in a dedicated arena server, so there is nothing of theirs to lose. Arena items never leave the arena: they cannot be thrown, put in containers or picked up by
anyone outside it.

## A kill paid nothing

One of the anti-abuse rules refused it: same address or account, the same victim too many times, a victim
who had just spawned, or one who was AFK. The killer is told, and the kill is in the staff audit log
(`/exylialib logs`). See [Playing](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing#anti-abuse).

## How do I make an arena where everyone is equal?

Turn **Upgrades** off in the arena's **Gameplay** screen: account upgrades and the rewards
bonus count for nothing there. Turn **Perks** off too if equipped perks should not fire. See
[Arenas](/docs/exyliarandomkits/arenas#pure-arenas).

## Where do I set what a kill pays?

`triggers.yml`. By default a kill pays 10, an assist 3 and ending a streak 25, in the server's default
currency. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliarandomkits/configuration#rewards-triggersyml).

## I used the kits version. What happens to my kits?

Nothing is deleted, but kits are no longer used: lives roll from `items.yml`. See
[Upgrading from kits](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kit-balance).

## Which languages are included?

English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish. Every message and menu can be edited under
`lang/<language>/`.
