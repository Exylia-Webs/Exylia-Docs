This page follows one life from the moment a player connects to the moment they die and start the next. It is also what you tell your players: they never join anything, they connect and fight.

## Before you start

The arena must be ready: see [Set up the arena](/docs/exyliarandomkits/arenas). Players need no permission to play: every player node, such as `exyliarandomkits.play` and `exyliarandomkits.reroll`, is on by default (see [Permissions](/docs/exyliarandomkits/permissions)). Everything starts from `/rk` (also `/randomkits`), the hub: **Profile**, **Hotbar layout**, **Leaderboards**, **Progression**, **Reroll kit**, the running or next **Event** and the **Season**.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    When the profile has loaded, the player is moved to a spawn of the arena, emptied there and put in survival. Every 10 seconds the plugin also sends in anyone who is not in the arena yet, for example right after the arena became ready. A player who left outside combat comes back with the same life: items, armour, effects, health and streak (`rejoin.keep-life` in `play.yml`; off, every join rolls new items).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the roll">
    A title shows the rarity of the life and the items appear in the hotbar and armour slots. See [The roll](#the-roll).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reroll or pick">
    In a Random arena, click **Reroll kit** in `/rk` or run `/rk reroll` to roll again, if you have a reroll left. In a Draft arena, pick one of the loadouts offered. See [Random and Draft](#random-and-draft).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fight">
    Spawn protection ends when you attack. Kills raise your streak and pay out. See [Streaks and evolution](#streaks-and-evolution).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Die and start again">
    A death never kicks you out: you are healed and sent to a spawn with new items. Your streak ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The roll

Every life rolls its own items, piece by piece, from `items.yml`: each category (weapon, armour pieces, food, blocks and so on) rolls whether it appears, which item, how many and which enchantments, and every roll lands on a **rarity**. The life's rarity is the commonest one whose budget covers what the life holds, so one lucky piece is paid for by weaker ones elsewhere. See [Items and rarities](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits).

The title shows the life's rarity, and the rarity's announcement and sound play (see [Rarities](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits#rarities)). The `luck` upgrade and perks tilt the odds. After 10 lives in a row below epic the next life has an epic piece at least, and after 40 below legendary a legendary one (bad luck protection).

### Random and Draft

Each arena has a roll mode, chosen in [the setup wizard](/docs/exyliarandomkits/arenas#the-setup-wizard):

- **Random** gives the rolled items at once.
- **Draft** offers `roll.draft-size` different loadouts (3 by default, from 2 to 9) to pick from, in a menu titled **Pick your kit** or as clickable chat buttons (`roll.draft.display`: `MENU` or `CHAT`). Nobody can hurt a player while they pick. After `roll.draft-timeout` (10 seconds) the first loadout is taken. `/rk draft` reopens the menu after closing it.

### Rerolls

`/rk reroll` (or the hub's **Reroll kit**, which shows how many are left) rolls all the items again for one of the life's rerolls. It works only while the life is fresh: within 10 seconds of spawning (`rerolls.window` in `progression.yml`) and before dealing any damage. A player starts without rerolls. They come from the `rerolls` account upgrade and the **Second Opinion** perk, which gives one free reroll each life. See [Progression](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression). In a Random arena the player is told how many are left after every spawn. A reroll is refused while the Mirror event runs, and none come from upgrades or perks in a [pure arena](/docs/exyliarandomkits/arenas#pure-arenas).

## The items on the body

**Layouts.** Armour and the offhand always go to their own slot. Every other item goes where the player's layout puts its **role**: the id of its `items.yml` category in upper case (`WEAPON`, `BOW`, `PEARLS`, `POTIONS`, `FOOD`, `BLOCKS`, `ARROWS`, `UTILITIES` or any category you add). A player arranges it once and every life lands that way, whatever it rolled:

- `/rk layout` (or **Hotbar layout** in the hub) opens the editor: click a role to pick it up, click a slot to put it there, then **Save**. **Reset** forgets it.
- `/rk layout save` takes where the current items are now, in an arena, as the layout.
- `/rk layout reset` forgets it.

Without a saved layout, each category's `slot` in `items.yml` is used (0-8 hotbar, 9-35 inventory; `-1` is the first free slot).

**Spawn protection.** A new life cannot be hurt for the arena's spawn protection (3 seconds for a new arena), plus the `spawn-protection` upgrade where upgrades count. Attacking ends it.

## Going back to the safe box

`/spawn` (also `/rk spawn`) takes a player back to the arena's safe box with everything they carry. It needs the player to stand still for 5 seconds first (`spawn.warmup` in `play.yml`); moving or being hit cancels it, and from inside the safe box it is instant. It is refused while in combat. To turn it off, set `spawn.enabled: false` in `play.yml`.

A hit puts both players in combat for 15 seconds (`combat.window`); the action bar counts it down. Leaving the server in combat counts as dying to the last player who hit you (`combat.logout-is-death`).

## Deaths

A death in an arena never really happens: lethal damage is cancelled, the kill is credited, and the player is healed and sent to a spawn with new items. A real death that gets past it (`/kill`, another plugin) is counted the same, with nothing dropped.

- **The killer** is whoever struck the last blow, or the last player who hurt the victim within `assists.window` (15 seconds). Falling below the arena's lowest box by more than `death.void-margin` (2 blocks) is a death too, credited to that last attacker.
- **Drops** follow `death.drops`: `NONE`, `CONSUMABLES` (the roles in `death.consumable-roles`: by default food, potions, arrows and utilities) or `ALL`. They are removed after `death.drop-lifetime` (10 seconds).
- **Assists** go to every other player who dealt at least `assists.minimum-percent` (25%) of the damage the victim took within the window.

## Streaks and evolution

Each kill adds one to the life's streak; a death ends it.

- Streaks in `streaks.milestones` (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 50) are announced to the arena.
- Ending a streak of at least `streaks.end-minimum` (5) is announced and runs `on-streak-end` for the killer.
- **Evolution**: at the streaks of `evolution.steps` the gear the player wears is upgraded in place. Each step is applied once, on the kill that reaches it, and lasts until the next death.

| Streak | Default step |
|---|---|
| 3 | A golden apple. |
| 5 | One extra enchantment roll on a gear piece with room for it. |
| 10 | One gear piece raised a rarity, and Speed until the next death. |

## Anti-abuse

A kill that breaks one of these rules earns **no rewards and no stats** for either player. The killer is told, and the kill is written to the staff audit log (`/exylialib logs`).

| Key | Default | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `anti-abuse.same-address` | `true` | Killer and victim share an address or account. A database that cannot answer counts as the same. |
| `anti-abuse.repeat-limit` | `3` | One killer killed the same victim more than this many times within `repeat-window` (10 minutes). `0` turns it off. |
| `anti-abuse.fresh-spawn` | `3s` | The victim spawned less than this long ago. |
| `anti-abuse.afk` | `60s` | The victim had not moved for this long. `0s` turns it off. |

## The nemesis

A player who kills you at least `nemesis.min-kills` (2) times becomes your **nemesis**: the most kills wins, the most recent a tie. Each player keeps track of `nemesis.tracked` (5) rivals. Killing your nemesis is **revenge**: it runs `on-revenge` and settles the score. Getting a new nemesis runs `on-nemesis-new` for the victim.

## Arena blocks

Inside the PvP box players may place blocks, which disappear after `arenas.placed-blocks` (30 seconds; `0s` keeps them until the next reset), and only blocks players placed can be broken. The arena's reset puts every block back from its schematic. See [Resets](/docs/exyliarandomkits/arenas#resets).

## Check that it works

Connect with two accounts. Both should land in the arena with a title and a full set of items. Kill the other account: you should see the kill counted in `/stats`, and the victim should be healed and sent to a spawn with new items. Run `/rk reroll` right after spawning without any reroll: you get the message that you have none left.

<Details title="Staff and chat rules inside the arena">
Only the commands in `allowed-commands` of `config.yml` run in an arena (by default `rk`, `randomkits` and the private message commands); the safe box adds `arenas.safe-commands`. Walking or teleporting out of the arena's boxes is refused. Containers cannot be opened in the PvP box (a crafting table can), arena items cannot be thrown away by hand, and nobody outside an arena picks up one left on the floor. Staff step out to build with `/rkadmin exit` and back in with the same command. On a network, a player who joins a full server is sent to another server of the group: see [Network and ExyliaDuels](/docs/exyliarandomkits/network).
</Details>

<Details title="Evolution step fields">
A step has `streak`, `upgrades` (pieces raised one rarity), `enchant-rolls`, `items` (`item`, `amount`, `role`), `effects` (`NAME|LEVEL|SECONDS`) and `feedback` (ExyliaLib sequence lines).
</Details>

Messages, titles and menus follow the player's language: see [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).
