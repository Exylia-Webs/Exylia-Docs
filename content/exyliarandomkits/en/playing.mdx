Everything a player does starts from `/rk` (also `/randomkits`), the hub: **Play**, **Leave arena**,
**Profile**, **Hotbar layout**, **Leaderboards**, **Progression**, **Reroll kit**, the running or
next **Event** and the **Season**.

## Joining and leaving

`/rk play` joins any ready arena, `/rk play <arena>` that one. The **Play** button opens the arena list.

- The player's things are saved by ExyliaLib's stays, they are moved to a spawn, emptied there and put in
  survival. A player already in an arena, or combat tagged, is refused.
- `/rk leave` takes them out and gives everything back **exactly** as they came in.
- Quitting is never a combat log: their things come back on their next join. A crash does the same.
- While in an arena only the commands in `allowed-commands` run (by default `rk`, `randomkits` and the
  private message commands), so `/rk leave` always works.
- Walking out of the arena's boxes is not allowed, and neither is a teleport out of them. Containers cannot
  be opened (a crafting table can), arena items cannot be thrown away by hand, and nobody outside an
  arena picks up one left on the floor.

On a network, `/rk play` may send the player to another server of the group first. See
[Network and ExyliaDuels](/docs/exyliarandomkits/network).

## The roll

Every life rolls its own items, piece by piece, from `items.yml`: each category (weapon, armour pieces, food,
blocks and so on) rolls whether it appears, which item, how many and which enchantments, and every roll lands
on a **rarity**. The life's rarity is its second-rarest piece. See
[Items and rarities](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits).

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Bad luck protection | `roll.pity: {epic: 10, legendary: 40}` | After 10 lives in a row below epic the next life has an epic piece at least; after 40 below legendary, a legendary one. |

The title shows the life's rarity, and the rarity's `announce` and `feedback` play (see
[Rarities](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits#rarities)). The `luck` upgrade and perks tilt the rarity weights.

### Random and Draft

Each arena has a roll mode:

- **Random** gives the rolled items at once.
- **Draft** offers `roll.draft-size` (3, from 2 to 9) different loadouts to pick from, in a menu or as clickable chat buttons
  (`roll.draft.display`: `MENU` or `CHAT`). Nobody can hurt a player while they pick. After
  `roll.draft-timeout` (10s) the first loadout is taken. `/rk draft` reopens the menu after closing it.

### Rerolls

`/rk reroll` (or the hub's **Reroll kit**) rolls the items again, for one of the life's rerolls, while the
life is fresh: within `rerolls.window` in `progression.yml` (10s) after spawning, and before dealing any
damage. Rerolls come from the `rerolls` account upgrade and the **Second Opinion** perk; a player without any
has none. In a Random arena the player is told how many they have left after each spawn.

## The items on the body

**Layouts.** Armour and the offhand always go to their own slot. Every other item goes where the player's
layout puts its **role**: the id of its `items.yml` category in upper case (`WEAPON`, `BOW`, `PEARLS`,
`POTIONS`, `FOOD`, `BLOCKS`, `ARROWS`, `UTILITIES` or any category you add). A player arranges it once and
every life lands that way, whatever it rolled:

- `/rk layout` opens the editor: click a role to pick it up, click a slot to put it there, then **Save**.
- `/rk layout save` takes where the current items are now, in an arena, as the layout.
- `/rk layout reset` forgets it.

Without a saved layout, each category's `slot` in `items.yml` is used (0-8 hotbar, 9-35 inventory; `-1` is the
first free slot).

**Spawn protection.** A new life cannot be hurt for the arena's spawn protection (3s for a new arena), plus
the `spawn-protection` upgrade where upgrades count. Attacking ends it. Lives start at a random spawn among
the half farthest from the other players.

## Deaths

A death in an arena never really happens: lethal damage is cancelled, the kill is credited, and the player
is healed and sent to a spawn with new items. A real death that gets past it (`/kill`, another plugin) is
counted the same, with nothing dropped.

- **The killer** is whoever struck the last blow, or the last player who hurt the victim within
  `assists.window` (15s). Falling below the arena's lowest box by more than `death.void-margin` (2 blocks)
  is a death too, credited to that last attacker.
- **Drops** follow `death.drops`: `NONE`, `CONSUMABLES` (the roles in `death.consumable-roles`: by default
  food, potions, arrows and utilities) or `ALL`. They are removed after `death.drop-lifetime` (10s).
- **Assists** go to every other player who dealt at least `assists.minimum-percent` (25%) of the damage the
  victim took within the window.

## Streaks and evolution

Each kill adds one to the life's streak; a death ends it.

- Streaks in `streaks.milestones` (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 50) are announced to the arena.
- Ending a streak of at least `streaks.end-minimum` (5) is announced and runs `on-streak-end` for the
  killer.
- **Evolution**: at the streaks of `evolution.steps` the gear the player wears is upgraded in place. Each step
  is applied once, on the kill that reaches it, and lasts until the next death.

| Streak | Default step |
|---|---|
| 3 | A golden apple. |
| 5 | One extra enchantment roll on a gear piece with room for it. |
| 10 | One gear piece raised a rarity, and Speed until the next death. |

A step has `streak`, `upgrades` (pieces raised one rarity), `enchant-rolls`, `items` (`item`, `amount`, `role`),
`effects` (`NAME|LEVEL|SECONDS`) and `feedback` (ExyliaLib sequence lines).

## Anti-abuse

A kill that breaks one of these rules earns **no rewards and no stats** for either player. The killer is
told, and the kill is written to the staff audit log (`/exylialib logs`).

| Key | Default | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `anti-abuse.same-address` | `true` | Killer and victim share an address or account. A database that cannot answer counts as the same. |
| `anti-abuse.repeat-limit` | `3` | One killer killed the same victim more than this many times within `repeat-window` (10m). `0` turns it off. |
| `anti-abuse.fresh-spawn` | `3s` | The victim spawned less than this long ago. |
| `anti-abuse.afk` | `60s` | The victim had not moved for this long. `0s` turns it off. |

## The nemesis

A player who kills you at least `nemesis.min-kills` (2) times becomes your **nemesis**: the most kills wins,
the most recent a tie. Each player keeps track of `nemesis.tracked` (5) rivals. Killing your nemesis is
**revenge**: it runs `on-revenge` and settles the score. Getting a new nemesis runs `on-nemesis-new` for the
victim.

## Arena blocks

Inside the PvP box players may place blocks, which disappear after `arenas.placed-blocks` (30s; `0s` keeps
them until the next reset), and only blocks players placed can be broken. The arena's reset puts every
block back from its schematic. See [Arenas](/docs/exyliarandomkits/arenas#resets).
