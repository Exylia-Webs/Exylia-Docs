A kit is a list of **slots**. On every life each slot rolls whether it appears, then one of its
**variants** by weight, with an amount and enchantment levels picked from ranges. Every life is a different
kit of the same family.

Kits live in the database and are edited in game, in the [web editor](/docs/exyliarandomkits/web-editor-and-import)
or with `/rkadmin kit ...`. All of it needs `exyliarandomkits.admin`.

## What a kit holds

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Id | Lowercase letters, digits, `-` and `_`, up to 32. |
| Name | What players read in titles, menus and messages. |
| Rarity | One of the ids of `rarities.yml`. |
| Weight | Its chance against the other kits of its rarity. `0` never rolls it. |
| Permission | When set, only players with it can roll the kit. |
| Icon | The item it is shown with in menus. |
| Effects | Potion effects worn while the kit lives, as `NAME\|LEVEL\|SECONDS`. |
| Enabled | Disabled kits are never rolled. |
| Slots | What it may hold. |

### Slots

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Role | Where it goes. `HELMET`, `CHESTPLATE`, `LEGGINGS`, `BOOTS` and `OFFHAND` always take their own slot; every other role (`WEAPON`, `BOW`, `PEARLS`, `POTIONS`, `FOOD`, `BLOCKS`, `ARROWS`, `UTILITY`, or any name you write) is placed by the player's [layout](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing#the-kit-on-the-body). |
| Saved position | The inventory slot it was saved from: where it goes when the player's layout says nothing about its role. |
| Chance | Percent chance the slot appears at all; `100` always. |
| Variants | What it may hold, rolled by weight. |

### Variants

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Item | An ExyliaLib item source: a material, or the whole item as it was saved (name, lore, enchantments, potion). |
| Weight | Its chance against the slot's other variants. |
| Amount | A fixed amount or a range, picked on every roll. |
| Enchantments | Extra enchantments rolled on top, as `sharpness:1-3` (a level between the two) or `unbreaking:2`. A level of `0` leaves it off, so `protection:0-1` is a coin flip. |
| Tier | How much better than the slot's plain variants it is. [Kit upgrades](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression#kit-upgrades) give higher tiers more weight. `0`, the default, is a plain variant. |
| Min kit level | The kit level a player needs before it can roll at all; `0` for everyone. When a player qualifies for no variant of a slot, every variant can roll. |

## Building kits in game

`/rkadmin` → **Kits** lists every kit with its rarity. From there:

- **Create from inventory** asks for an id and saves what you carry, one slot per item.
- **Import starter kits** adds the bundled kits whose ids are free.
- **Import from a plugin** opens the import screen. See
  [Importing kits](/docs/exyliarandomkits/web-editor-and-import#importing-kits).

Saving from an inventory makes every item one slot with one variant, its role inferred: by the slot it was
in for armour and the offhand, then by what it is (a sword, axe, trident or mace is `WEAPON`; a bow or
crossbow `BOW`; arrows `ARROWS`; pearls `PEARLS`; potions `POTIONS`; food `FOOD`; blocks `BLOCKS`; anything
else `UTILITY`). A second item of the same role becomes `WEAPON_2`, and so on. `/rkadmin kit save <id>
[rarity]` does the same from the command line; saving over an existing id keeps its name, weight,
permission, effects and enabled state and replaces its slots.

### The kit screen

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Enables or disables the kit. |
| **Display name**, **Icon** | What players read and see. |
| **Rarity** | Left-click the next rarity, right-click the previous one. |
| **Weight** | Its weight, and its chance within its rarity. Setting it restarts the kit's [balance](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kit-balance) numbers. |
| **Permission** | Left-click sets it, right-click lets everyone roll it. |
| **Effects** | The potion effects worn while the kit lives. |
| **Slots** | Every slot. Hold an item and **Add** it as a new slot; open a slot to edit its **Role**, **Saved position**, **Chance** and **Variants**. In a variant: **Item**, **Weight**, **Amount**, **Enchantments**, and **Tier** (left-click) and minimum kit level (right-click). |
| **Replace from inventory** | Every slot becomes what you carry now, after a confirmation. |
| **Test kit** | Gives you this kit now; you must be in an arena. |
| **Delete kit** | Removes it for good, after a confirmation. |

From the command line: `/rkadmin kit name <id> <name>`, `kit rarity <id> <rarity>`, `kit weight <id>
<weight>`, `kit permission <id> <permission|none>` and `kit delete <id>`. Slots and variants are edited in the
screen or the web editor.

## Rarities

`rarities.yml` lists the rarities from the most common to the rarest. A rarity's position is its rank:
"rarer" in missions, perks and bad luck protection means further down the list.

| Rarity | Display | Weight | Announce |
|---|---|---|---|
| `common` | `{letters_black}&lCOMMON` | 60 | `SELF` |
| `rare` | `{info}&lRARE` | 25 | `SELF` |
| `epic` | `{accent}&lEPIC` | 10 | `ARENA` |
| `legendary` | `{highlight}&lLEGENDARY` | 4 | `SERVER` |
| `mythic` | `{primary}&lMYTHIC` | 1 | `SERVER` |

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `id` | What kits, pity rules and events refer to it by. |
| `display` | How it is written in messages and titles. |
| `weight` | Its chance in the first half of a roll. A rarity with no kit a player may get is skipped, its weight shared among the others. |
| `announce` | `NONE`, `SELF` (the title only), `ARENA` (also told to the arena) or `SERVER` (to everyone). |
| `feedback` | ExyliaLib sequence lines played for the player who rolled it (sounds, rings, beams, fireworks); `%kit%` and `%rarity%` are filled in. |

`/rkadmin` → **Rarities** shows each rarity's weight, its share of a roll, how many kits it has and how it is
announced. Rarities are edited in `rarities.yml` and applied with `/rkadmin reload` (or the web editor).

Every roll also runs the `on-roll-<rarity>` trigger, such as `on-roll-legendary`.

### Bad luck protection

`roll.pity` in `config.yml` maps a rarity id to a number of rolls: after that many rolls in a row below the
rarity, the next roll is at least that rarity. By default 10 for `epic` and 40 for `legendary`; `0` turns a
rule off. The `luck` upgrade makes those thresholds shrink. See
[Progression](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression#account-upgrades).

## The starter kits

Ten balanced kits ship inside the jar. `/rkadmin setup` or **Import starter kits** adds them; they are not read
otherwise, and once imported they are edited like any other kit.

| Kit | Rarity |
|---|---|
| Warrior | common |
| Archer | common |
| Tank | common |
| Assassin | rare |
| Alchemist | rare |
| Pyro | rare |
| Ranger | epic |
| Knight | epic |
| Berserker | legendary |
| Void Walker | mythic |

The Warrior, for example: an iron or chainmail helmet, chestplate (iron with `protection:0-1`) and leggings,
iron boots, an iron sword with `sharpness:0-1` or an iron axe, 8 to 16 cooked beef, a 50% chance of one or two
golden apples, and 16 to 32 cobblestone.
