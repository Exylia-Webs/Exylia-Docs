There are no kits to build. The server keeps one **item pool**, `items.yml`, and on every life the plugin
rolls each of its **categories** piece by piece: armour, weapons, food, potions, utilities. Every item and
every enchantment level draws its own rarity, so a leather helmet with Protection IV and a netherite one
with Blast Protection II are both possible.

`items.yml` ships with a balanced pool. Edit it on disk and apply it with `/rkadmin reload`, or edit it in the
[web editor](/docs/exyliarandomkits/web-editor-and-import) (`/rkadmin` → **Items** gives the link). It is read
on every roll. All of it needs `exyliarandomkits.admin`.

## How a life is rolled

A life walks the categories of `items.yml` in order:

1. A category appears on its `chance` (`100` always), and only after the category it `requires`, if any:
   arrows need a bow.
2. It gives `picks` items (`1`, or a range such as `1-3`), never the same line twice while others are left.
3. Each item draws a **rarity** from the weights in `rarities.yml`, shifted by the player's
   [luck](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression#account-upgrades), then one of the category's lines of that
   rarity by `weight`. A category with no line of that rarity takes the nearest commoner one, else the nearest
   rarer one.
4. Each item rolls `enchant-rolls` enchantments (`0`, `1` or a range such as `0-2`): one the item accepts and
   that does not conflict with what it already has (the server's own rules), at a level drawn by rarity:
   the highest level whose rarity is the one drawn or a commoner one. A draw below every level gives nothing.

### A life's rarity

The rarity that is announced, shown in `%exyliarandomkits_rarity%` and counted by rarity missions, perks and
bad luck protection is that of the life's **second-rarest piece** (a life with one piece is its own): one
lucky item is not a lucky life, two are.

## `items.yml`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `categories.<id>` | thirteen categories | One list of items. Its id, in upper case, is also its [layout](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing#the-items-on-the-body) role. |
| `chance` | `100` | Percent chance the category appears in a life. |
| `picks` | `1` | How many items it gives: `1` or a range like `1-3`. |
| `slot` | `-1` | Where it lands unless the player's layout says otherwise: `0-8` hotbar, `9-35` inventory, `36` boots, `37` leggings, `38` chestplate, `39` helmet, `40` off hand, `-1` the first free slot. |
| `enchant-rolls` | `0` | Enchantments each item tries to get. |
| `requires` | `""` | Another category id; this one only rolls in lives that got that one. |
| `items` | | One line per item. |
| `enchantments.<name>` | | The rarity of each level of an enchantment, from level 1 up. |

The default categories are `helmets`, `chestplates`, `leggings`, `boots`, `swords`, `axes`, `ranged`, `arrows`
(requires `ranged`), `offhand`, `tools`, `food`, `potions` and `utilities`.

An item line is `<item> <rarity> [amount] [potion=TYPE] [weight=N]`:

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| `<item>` | A material, or any ExyliaLib item source (`bytes:...`, `nexo:...`). |
| `<rarity>` | An id of `rarities.yml`. |
| `amount` | `1`, or a range such as `8-16`, picked on every roll. |
| `potion=TYPE` | The potion type, for potion items. |
| `weight=N` | Its share against the other items of its rarity in the category (`1` by default). |

```yaml
categories:
  helmets:
    chance: 100.0
    picks: '1'
    slot: 39
    enchant-rolls: 0-2
    requires: ''
    items:
    - LEATHER_HELMET common
    - DIAMOND_HELMET epic
    - NETHERITE_HELMET legendary
  potions:
    chance: 70.0
    picks: 1-2
    slot: 4
    enchant-rolls: '0'
    items:
    - SPLASH_POTION rare 2-4 potion=STRONG_HEALING
    - POTION epic 1 potion=STRENGTH
enchantments:
  protection: [common, rare, epic, legendary]
  sharpness: [common, rare, rare, epic, legendary]
```

Here Protection I is common, II rare, III epic and IV legendary. A roll that draws `epic` gives Protection III;
one that draws `rare` gives II.

`/rkadmin` → **Items** shows how many categories and items the pool holds.

## Rarities

`rarities.yml` lists the rarities from the most common to the rarest. A rarity's position is its rank:
"rarer" in missions, perks and bad luck protection means further down the list.

| Rarity | Display | Weight | Announce |
|---|---|---|---|
| `common` | `{letters_black}&lCOMMON` | 60 | `SELF` |
| `rare` | `{info}&lRARE` | 25 | `SELF` |
| `epic` | `{accent}&lEPIC` | 10 | `ARENA` |
| `legendary` | `{highlight}&lLEGENDARY` | 4 | `SERVER` |
| `mythic` | `{primary}&lMYTHIC` | 1 | `SERVER` |

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `id` | What `items.yml`, pity rules and events refer to it by. |
| `display` | How it is written in messages and titles. |
| `weight` | Its chance every time a piece or an enchantment level draws a rarity. |
| `announce` | `NONE`, `SELF` (the title only), `ARENA` (also told to the arena) or `SERVER` (to everyone). |
| `feedback` | ExyliaLib sequence lines played for the player who rolled it (sounds, rings, beams); `%kit%` (the rarest item) and `%rarity%` are filled in. |

`/rkadmin` → **Rarities** shows each rarity's weight, its share of a draw, how many items it has and how it
is announced. Rarities are edited in `rarities.yml` and applied with `/rkadmin reload` (or the web editor).
Every life also runs the `on-roll-<rarity>` trigger, such as `on-roll-legendary`.

### Bad luck protection

`roll.pity` in `config.yml` maps a rarity id to a number of lives: after that many lives in a row below the
rarity, the next life is at least that rarity. The plugin gets there by rolling pieces again, gear first,
until the life reaches the rarity that is due. By default 10 for `epic` and 40 for `legendary`; `0` turns a
rule off. The `luck` upgrade makes those thresholds shrink. See
[Progression](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression#account-upgrades).
