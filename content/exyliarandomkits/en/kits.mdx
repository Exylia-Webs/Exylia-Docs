There are no kits to build. The server keeps one **item pool**, `items.yml`, and on every life the plugin rolls each of its **categories** piece by piece: sword, armour, blocks, pearls, food, potions. At the end of this page you can add an item, make a category rarer and change how often each rarity appears.

## Before you start

You need `exyliarandomkits.admin`. `items.yml` and `rarities.yml` are in the plugin folder and ship with a balanced pool, so leave them as they are until you want to change something. You can edit them on disk, or in the [web editor](/docs/exyliarandomkits/web-editor-and-import): `/rkadmin` → **Items** links to it. Apply a disk edit with `/rkadmin reload` (or the **Reload** button of `/rkadmin`). Players see the change on their next roll.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pool">
    Open `plugins/ExyliaRandomKitsCore/items.yml`. `/rkadmin` → **Items** shows how many categories and items it holds and links to the web editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an item to a category">
    Under `categories`, find the category, such as `swords`, and add a line to its `items` list: `<item> <rarity>`. For example `DIAMOND_SWORD epic`. The rarity is one of `rarities.yml`: `common`, `rare`, `epic`, `legendary` or `mythic`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it more or less likely">
    Add `weight=N` to the line: its share against the other items of the same rarity in the category (`1` by default). `IRON_SWORD common weight=3` is picked three times as often as a common line without a weight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/rkadmin reload`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/rkadmin give <player>` for a player who is in the arena, or die a few times yourself. The new item should show up in some lives, in the rarity you gave it. Use `/rk reroll` right after spawning to roll again quickly if you have a reroll.

## How a life is rolled

1. The life first draws its **rarity** from the weights in `rarities.yml`, shifted by the player's [luck](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression#account-upgrades).
2. That rarity has a **budget** in `items.yml` (`budget`: common 3, rare 6, epic 10, legendary 15, mythic 22). Each piece costs `2^rank - 1` (common 0, rare 1, epic 3, legendary 7, mythic 15). Enchantments are free.
3. Categories are rolled in a random order, each followed by the categories that `requires` it (arrows need a ranged weapon). A category appears on its `chance` and gives `picks` items. Each item draws a rarity, then one of the category's lines of that rarity by `weight`; if the category has none, it takes the nearest commoner rarity, else the nearest rarer one. An item the life can no longer pay for is not taken.
4. What the draws leave unspent goes on upgrades, one random piece one rarity up at a time. So a strong piece is paid for by weaker ones elsewhere, and a life is worth what its rarity says.
5. Each item rolls `enchant-rolls` enchantments (`0`, `1` or a range such as `0-2`), one the item accepts and that does not conflict with what it has, at the highest level whose rarity is the one drawn or a commoner one.

### A life's rarity

The rarity that is announced, shown in `%exyliarandomkits_rarity%` and counted by rarity missions, perks and bad luck protection is the **commonest rarity whose budget covers what the life holds**. Without a `budget` in `items.yml` it is instead how the life ranks against every life the pool can roll.

### Bad luck protection

`roll.pity` in `config.yml` maps a rarity id to a number of lives: after that many lives in a row below the rarity, the next life is at least that rarity. The plugin gets there by rolling pieces again until the life reaches the rarity that is due. By default 10 for `epic` and 40 for `legendary`; `0` turns a rule off. The `luck` upgrade makes those thresholds shrink. See [Progression](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression#account-upgrades).

## Rarities

`rarities.yml` lists the rarities from the most common to the rarest. A rarity's position is its rank: "rarer" in missions, perks and bad luck protection means further down the list.

| Rarity | Weight | Announce |
|---|---|---|
| `common` | 60 | `SELF` |
| `rare` | 25 | `SELF` |
| `epic` | 10 | `ARENA` |
| `legendary` | 4 | `SERVER` |
| `mythic` | 1 | `SERVER` |

To make legendary items rarer, lower the weight of `legendary` in `rarities.yml`, then `/rkadmin reload`. The share of a rarity is its weight divided by the sum of all weights. `announce` decides who hears about a roll: `NONE`, `SELF` (the title only), `ARENA` (also told to the arena) or `SERVER` (to everyone).

`/rkadmin` → **Rarities** shows each rarity's weight, its share of a draw, how many items it has and how it is announced. It only shows them; edit them on disk or in the web editor. The names players read ("Common", "Épico"...) come from `rarities.<id>` in `lang/<language>/content.yml`, see [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages), and the colours from [Colours](/docs/exylialib/colours). Every life also runs the `on-roll-<rarity>` trigger, such as `on-roll-legendary`.

## Going further

- **Add a new category**: copy a category block under `categories` with a new id. Its id in upper case becomes a role players can place on their [hotbar layout](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing#the-items-on-the-body).
- **Keep every life playable**: keep a common weapon, food, blocks and water at `chance: 100`.
- **Pairs**: the default pool rolls a costly category with a free one that requires it, such as `end_crystals` with `obsidian`: both or neither.

<Details title="Category and item line fields">
| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `categories.<id>` | the default pool | One list of items. Its id in upper case is also its layout role. |
| `chance` | `100` | Percent chance the category appears in a life. |
| `picks` | `1` | How many items it gives: `1` or a range like `1-3`. |
| `slot` | `-1` | Where it lands unless the player's layout says otherwise: `0-8` hotbar, `9-35` inventory, `36` boots, `37` leggings, `38` chestplate, `39` helmet, `40` off hand, `-1` the first free slot. |
| `enchant-rolls` | `0` | Enchantments each item tries to get. |
| `requires` | `""` | Another category id; this one only rolls in lives that got that one. |
| `items` | | One line per item. |
| `enchantments.<name>` | | The rarity of each level of an enchantment, from level 1 up. |

An item line is `<item> <rarity> [amount] [potion=TYPE] [weight=N]`: `<item>` is a material or any ExyliaLib item source (`bytes:...`, `nexo:...`); `amount` is `1` or a range such as `8-16`; `potion=TYPE` is for potion and tipped arrow items.

```yaml
categories:
  swords:
    chance: 100.0
    picks: '1'
    slot: 0
    enchant-rolls: 0-2
    requires: ''
    items:
    - STONE_SWORD common
    - DIAMOND_SWORD rare
    - NETHERITE_SWORD legendary
enchantments:
  protection: [common, rare, epic, legendary]
  sharpness: [common, rare, rare, epic, legendary]
```

Here Protection I is common, II rare, III epic and IV legendary. A roll that draws `epic` gives Protection III.
</Details>

<Details title="Rarity fields">
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `id` | What `items.yml`, pity rules and events refer to it by. |
| `display` | How it is written in messages and titles; `%name%` is its name in the server's language. |
| `weight` | Its chance every time a piece or an enchantment level draws a rarity. |
| `announce` | `NONE`, `SELF`, `ARENA` or `SERVER`. |
| `feedback` | ExyliaLib sequence lines played for the player who rolled it (sounds, rings, beams); `%kit%` and `%rarity%` are filled in. |
</Details>
