At the end of this page the arena has a fighting box, several spawns, an optional safe box above the fight, and blocks that go back to how you built them. Everything is done in game and saved at once; nothing needs a reload.

ExyliaRandomKitsCore runs **one arena per server**. There is no command to create, list or delete arenas: the setup wizard makes the arena, and running it again redoes it. Every `/rkadmin arena ...` command acts on that arena, so none of them takes an id.

## Before you start

You need `exyliarandomkits.admin` and a built map in a loaded world. To save and restore blocks, FastAsyncWorldEdit is used when installed; otherwise ExyliaLib's own engine does it. For a first arena in five minutes see [Your first round](/docs/exyliarandomkits/first-steps).

An arena is **ready**, and players are sent in, once it has a PvP region, at least one spawn and its world is loaded.

## The setup wizard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Stand next to the map and run `/rkadmin setup`, or open `/rkadmin`, click **Arena** and, if no arena exists yet, the wizard starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the PvP region">
    The prompt reads "Select the PvP region of the arena". Left-click one corner, right-click the opposite corner, Shift + left-click to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark a spawn">
    Stand where a life should start, face the way players should look and sneak-click. A spawn in another world than the region is left out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the roll mode">
    **Random** gives the items at once; **Draft** lets the player pick one of a few loadouts. See [Random and Draft](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing#random-and-draft).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the summary">
    Nothing is saved until you confirm. On finish the arena is saved and its blocks are saved too, so resets work from the start. Running the wizard again replaces the PvP region, spawn and mode and removes the old spawns; the safe box stays if it is in the same world.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add spawns

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the arena screen">
    Run `/rkadmin`, click **Arena**, then **Spawns**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add where you stand">
    Click **Add spawn here**, walk to the spot, face where players should look and sneak-click. Repeat for every spawn. Or run `/rkadmin arena addspawn` and sneak-click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check or remove one">
    Each spawn is listed as **Spawn #n**. Left-click teleports you there, right-click removes it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/rkadmin arena setspawn` does the same as adding, but replaces every spawn with the new one. Spawns must be in the same world as the arena's regions.

A life starts at a random spawn among the half farthest from the other players, so a new life is not dropped next to a fight. Add as many as the map has room for.

## Add a safe box

The safe box is an optional area, such as a spawn platform above the fight, where nobody is hurt.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Regions">
    In `/rkadmin` → **Arena**, click **Regions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select it">
    Click **Safe region** (or run `/rkadmin arena setregion safe`) and select two corners as before. The PVP region has its own button, **PVP region**, to redo the fighting box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove it later if you want">
    **Remove safe region** makes lives start straight in the fight.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The PvP box and the safe box together are the arena: walking or teleporting out of them is not allowed, and falling below the lowest of them by more than `death.void-margin` blocks (2 by default) is a death. Only blocks players placed can be broken in the PvP box.

Players can use `/spawn` to go back to the safe box. See [Play a round](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing#going-back-to-the-safe-box).

## Choose the roll mode and spawn protection

Open **Arena** → **Gameplay**.

- **Roll mode**: click to switch between Random and Draft. By command, `/rkadmin arena mode RANDOM` or `DRAFT`.
- **Spawn protection**: click and type a time such as `3s` or `1m`; `0s` turns it off. By command, `/rkadmin arena protection <seconds>`. A new arena starts with `arenas.spawn-protection` from `config.yml` (3 seconds). Attacking ends the protection.

### Pure arenas

In the same screen, **Upgrades** and **Perks** switch each on or off for the arena. With **Upgrades** off the arena is **pure**: account upgrades (luck, rerolls, rewards bonus, extra spawn protection) do not count there, so everyone is equal. With **Perks** off, equipped perks do not fire there. XP and levels are earned either way.

## Resets

An arena's blocks are put back from its **schematic**, `schematics/randomkits_<arena>.schem` in the plugin folder.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish building the map">
    Build, then keep the arena as it should look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the blocks">
    In **Arena** → **Resets**, click **Save blocks** (or run `/rkadmin arena schematic`). This is what every reset puts back; the wizard saved a first version when it finished.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it resets">
    **Reset interval** resets every so often (type `10m` or `1h`; `0s`, the default, never). **Reset now** moves players to spawns and puts the blocks back (`/rkadmin arena reset`).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A reset needs a saved schematic; without one it fails and says so. Blocks a player places also disappear by themselves after `arenas.placed-blocks` (30 seconds by default; `0s` keeps them until the next reset).

## Check that it works

1. Run `/rkadmin`, click **Arena**: the screen shows whether the arena is ready, its mode and how many are fighting.
2. Break and place a few blocks, then click **Reset now**: the map goes back to the saved version.
3. Join with a second account: you should start at a spawn far from the first player, protected for a few seconds.

## Going further

<Details title="Region flags of the two boxes">
`/rkadmin flags pvp` and `/rkadmin flags safe` open an editor for the region flags of the PvP box and the safe box. Changes are saved in `config.yml` under `arenas.pvp-flags` and `arenas.safe-flags` and apply to the arena. The PvP box defaults to `pvp: true` and `player_build_only: true`; the safe box turns off PvP, building, breaking, item drops and pickups and fall damage. `arenas.safe-containers` lets players open chests and ender chests in the safe box, and `arenas.safe-commands` lists the commands allowed there on top of `allowed-commands`.
</Details>

<Details title="Arena commands">
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/rkadmin setup` | Starts the setup wizard. |
| `/rkadmin arena setregion <pvp\|safe>` | Selects a box. A selection in another world is refused once the arena has a box or spawn. |
| `/rkadmin arena addspawn` / `setspawn` | Adds a spawn / replaces every spawn. |
| `/rkadmin arena mode <RANDOM\|DRAFT>` | Sets the roll mode. |
| `/rkadmin arena protection <seconds>` | Sets spawn protection. |
| `/rkadmin arena schematic` | Saves the PvP box for resets. |
| `/rkadmin arena reset` | Resets the blocks now. |
| `/rkadmin arena interval <seconds>` | Resets every so often; `0` never. |
| `/rkadmin exit` | Steps out of the fight to build, and back in. |
</Details>
