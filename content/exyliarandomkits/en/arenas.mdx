Arenas are built in game, from `/rkadmin` → **Arenas** or with `/rkadmin arena ...`. Both need
`exyliarandomkits.admin`. Every change is saved at once; nothing needs a reload.

An arena is a **PvP box** in a world, with at least one **spawn**. It is **ready**, and players can be sent
to it, once it has both and its world is loaded. The list and the arena screen show its state, its world,
its mode and how many players are fighting in it.

## The setup wizard

`/rkadmin setup` (or **Arenas** → the setup button) builds a first arena in one pass:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Id">
    Lowercase letters, digits, `-` and `_`, up to 32. An id that exists is refused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="PvP region">
    Select the box players fight in with the selection wand: both corners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spawn">
    Stand where a life should start and confirm. The direction you face is kept. A spawn in another world
    than the region is left out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Starter kits">
    Yes (the default) imports the ten bundled kits; ids that already exist are skipped. See
    [Kits](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits#the-starter-kits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Roll mode">
    **Random** or **Draft**. See [Playing](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing#random-and-draft).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Summary">
    Everything is shown before anything is made. On finish the arena is saved and its blocks saved as the
    schematic resets put back. Add more spawns and a safe box afterwards from its screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## By hand

```text
/rkadmin arena create main
/rkadmin arena setregion main pvp
/rkadmin arena setregion main safe
/rkadmin arena addspawn main
/rkadmin arena addspawn main
/rkadmin arena schematic main
```

`create` makes the arena in the world you stand in. `setregion` starts a wand selection; once the arena has a
box or a spawn, a selection in another world is refused. `setspawn` replaces every spawn with the spot where
you stand, `addspawn` adds one. Spawns must be in the arena's world.

## The arena screen

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Whether it is ready, its world, its mode and how many are fighting. |
| **Regions** | The **PvP region**, the box players fight in, where only blocks players place can be broken; the optional **Safe region**, a box where nobody is hurt, for spawns above the fight; and **Remove safe region**, so lives start straight in the fight. |
| **Spawns** | Every spawn with its coordinates: left-click teleports there, right-click removes it. **Add spawn here** adds where you stand, facing where you look. |
| **Kit pool** | Which kits this arena rolls: click a kit to roll it here or stop rolling it. **All kits** rolls every kit, and every kit made later. |
| **Gameplay** | **Roll mode** (Random or Draft), **Spawn protection**, **Upgrades** and **Perks**. |
| **Resets** | **Save blocks**, **Reset interval** and **Reset now**. |
| **Teleport** | Takes you to the first spawn. |
| **Delete arena** | Removes it for good, after a confirmation. Players in it are sent out first. |

### Boxes

The PvP box and the safe box together are the arena: walking or teleporting out of them is not allowed, and
falling below the lowest of them by more than `death.void-margin` blocks is a death. PvP is off inside the
safe box.

### Spawns

A life starts at a random spawn among the half farthest from the other players, so a new kit is not dropped
next to a fight. Add as many as the arena has room for.

### Pool and mode

`/rkadmin arena pool <id> <kits...|all>` sets the pool from a list of kit ids separated by spaces or commas;
`all` (an empty pool) rolls every kit. `/rkadmin arena mode <id> RANDOM|DRAFT` sets the roll mode.

### Spawn protection

`/rkadmin arena protection <id> <seconds>`. A new arena starts with `arenas.spawn-protection` from
`config.yml` (3s). The `spawn-protection` upgrade adds to it where upgrades count. Attacking ends it.

### Pure arenas

With **Upgrades** off, an arena is **pure**: account and kit upgrades, mastery and the rewards bonus do not
count there, so everyone is equal. With **Perks** off, equipped perks do not fire there. XP and levels are
earned in every arena.

## Resets

An arena's blocks are put back from its **schematic**, `schematics/randomkits_<arena>.schem` in the plugin folder, saved with
FastAsyncWorldEdit when it is installed and ExyliaLib's own engine otherwise.

| Command | Button | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/rkadmin arena schematic <id>` | **Save blocks** | Saves the PvP box as it is now: what every reset puts back. Do it after building. |
| `/rkadmin arena reset <id>` | **Reset now** | Moves the players to spawns, then puts the blocks back. |
| `/rkadmin arena interval <id> <seconds>` | **Reset interval** | Resets every so often; `0`, the default, never. |

A reset needs a saved schematic; without one it fails and says so.

## Deleting

`/rkadmin arena delete <id>` or **Delete arena**. Players in it leave first and get their things back.
