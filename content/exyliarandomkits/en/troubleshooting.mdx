Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

Open the console: it names what is missing. ExyliaRandomKitsCore needs ExyliaLib, the newest release. The loader installs it for you; if it had to, restart once more. If the console reports a licence error, check the loader jar and the `loader.yml` it wrote. See [Install ExyliaRandomKitsCore](/docs/exyliarandomkits/installation).

## "This server has no arena ready yet"

The server has no arena, or its arena is missing a piece. An arena is ready once it has a PvP region and at least one spawn, and its world is loaded. Run `/rkadmin setup` to build it, or open `/rkadmin` and click **Arena** to see which piece is **Not set**. See [Your first round](/docs/exyliarandomkits/first-steps).

## "All regions and spawns must be in the same world"

The arena lives in one world. A region or spawn you place in another world is refused, once the arena already has a region or a spawn. Stand in the arena's world and place it again, or run `/rkadmin setup` in the new world to redo the arena there. See [Arenas](/docs/exyliarandomkits/arenas).

## The arena reset fails

A reset puts back the blocks saved as the arena's schematic. Without a saved one it fails and says so. Run `/rkadmin arena schematic` once the map is built, then `/rkadmin arena reset`. It also needs the PvP region first.

## A player joins and gets no items

Nothing rolled for them: `items.yml` has no categories, or every category failed its `chance`. Check that at least one category always appears. See [Items and rarities](/docs/exyliarandomkits/kits).

## "Your rank does not include that feature"

The player has the root permission but not the feature's own node, such as `exyliarandomkits.reroll`. Grant it to their rank. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliarandomkits/permissions).

## Rerolls do nothing

A player gets rerolls from the `rerolls` account upgrade, the **Second Opinion** perk, or a `exyliarandomkits.rerolls.<n>` permission, and only in the first seconds of a life, before dealing damage. Without any of those they have none. See [Progression](/docs/exyliarandomkits/progression).

## A kill paid nothing

An anti-abuse rule refused it: same address or account, the same victim too many times, a victim who had just spawned, or one who was AFK. The killer is told. See [Playing](/docs/exyliarandomkits/playing#anti-abuse).

## `/spawn` says it is disabled

`/spawn` is turned off in this server's settings. When it is on, it takes the player back to the safe box after they stand still for the warm-up, and it is refused in combat.

## A placeholder shows as text

- PlaceholderAPI is not installed. Inside Exylia plugins' own files placeholders work without it.
- The name is misspelled. Copy it from [Placeholders](/docs/exyliarandomkits/placeholders).
- A leaderboard position is higher than the board's size (`stats.leaderboard-size`, 10 by default): it shows `-`.
