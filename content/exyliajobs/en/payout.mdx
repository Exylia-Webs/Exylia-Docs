At the end of this page you can tell why a player received a particular amount for a block, a mob or an item, and which setting to change if it is not what you wanted.

## Before you start

You need a job with at least one rule ([Change what a job pays](/docs/exyliajobs/jobs-and-actions)) and an economy plugin (ExyliaEconomy or Vault). Without one, the console warns once and only experience is paid.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the price the player sees">
    Run `/jobs`, click the job, then **Actions & rates**. Each target shows what it pays right now. An arrow means the market, fatigue, budget or caps moved the price away from the base you wrote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the arrow">
    Market rate: other players flood this target, or nobody works it. Fatigue: this player repeated it in the same spot. Budget: the whole server is near its hourly limit. Caps: this player is near their own limit. Each is explained in [Protect your economy](/docs/exyliajobs/economy-guard).
  </Step>
  <Step title="If there is no arrow and nothing is paid">
    A guard refused the action: wrong game mode, a disabled world, a placed block, an idle player and so on. See [Stop farms and exploits](/docs/exyliajobs/anti-exploit) and [Common problems](/docs/exyliajobs/troubleshooting).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Do the action in survival. Money and experience are added up and shown once every five seconds as one summary (a corner popup by default), not a chat line per block. The amount should match the price on **Actions & rates** for that moment.

## Going further

### Change how the summary is shown

In `config.yml`, `feedback.mode` is `TOAST` (default), `BOSS_BAR`, `ACTION_BAR` or `NONE`, and `feedback.window` is how often it is paid (5 seconds).

### Change the base pay of one thing

That is the rule in the job, not a formula: edit it in the job's **Actions** screen.

### Change a payout from another plugin

`JobPayoutEvent` fires after every step and may change or cancel the money and experience. See [API](/docs/exyliajobs/api).

<Details title="The path every paid action follows">

Every action runs the same path, once for each job the player holds that pays for it.

1. **Guards.** The action is refused unless the player is in survival or adventure, not vanished, holds `exyliajobs.use`, is outside `anti-exploit.disabled-worlds` and `disabled-regions`, is not paused by an admin, and every integration check allows it ([Stop farms and exploits](/docs/exyliajobs/anti-exploit), [Work with other plugins](/docs/exyliajobs/integrations)). Idle players are skipped unless the job allows them.
2. **The steps**, in order:

| # | Step | What it does | Touches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `base` | The rule's money times the amount, or `price-share` times the item's sell price; the rule's xp; both times the origin share (placed, spawner, bone meal, TNT). | money, xp |
| 2 | `level` | `1 + income-bonus x (level - 1)` for money, `1 + xp-bonus x (level - 1)` for xp. | money, xp |
| 3 | `modifiers` | ExyliaLib modifiers: boosters and ranks, type `money` or `xp`, source `jobs`, scope `job:<id>`. Prestige stars also act here. | money, xp |
| 4 | `market` | The target's market rate. | money |
| 5 | `fatigue` | The player's fatigue in this area. | money, xp |
| 6 | `budget` | The global pacing factor. | money |
| 7 | `caps` | The player's soft and hard caps. | money |

3. **`JobPayoutEvent`** fires after every step. A plugin may change the money and experience, or cancel it.
4. Every step records what it needs, perks fire (`JOB_<ACTION>` triggers), the money joins the wallet and the experience goes to the job.

Mines can zero a job's pay at the end of the chain when the job is not allowed there; ExyliaStaff and protections are decided in the guards.

**The wallet.** Money is added up per player and job and deposited once per `feedback.window`, with one summary. The deposit is made with the default currency under the reason `jobs:job:<id>` and recorded in the ExyliaLib ledger as created money for that job. Amounts below the currency's decimals carry to the next deposit; a player who leaves keeps nothing below one decimal.

**Menu quotes.** **Actions & rates** shows each target's pay by running the same steps without side effects, with an arrow when the market, fatigue, budget or caps differ from the base.
</Details>
