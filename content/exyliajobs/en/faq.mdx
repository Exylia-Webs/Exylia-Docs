## Is ExyliaJobs free?

No. It is a premium plugin bought on [BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/exylia-jobs.136377/) and installed as a loader jar that checks the licence on every start. See [Installation](/docs/exyliajobs/installation).

## Players earn no money

- No economy plugin: the console warns once and only experience is paid.
- The player lacks `exyliajobs.use`, is in creative or spectator, vanished, in a disabled world or region, paused by an admin, or in staff mode, a minigame or (with defaults) idle.
- The block was placed by a player, the mob came from a spawner, or the crop was not ripe. See [Anti-exploit](/docs/exyliajobs/anti-exploit).
- The pay factor, fatigue or market rate is low: look at **Actions and rates** and the dashboard.

## Pay dropped after a while

That is the guard: fatigue after 64 actions in one spot, a flooded target's market rate, or the budget pacing. Move, change target, or raise the budget.

## Can I turn the guard off?

`budget.mode: off`, `market.enabled: false`, `fatigue.enabled: false` or `features: {economy: false}`. Not recommended.

## How do I add a job?

Create `lang/<language>/jobs/<id>.yml` and run `/jobsadmin reload`, or create it in the in-game editor. See [Jobs and actions](/docs/exyliajobs/jobs-and-actions).

## I changed a shipped job file but it did not update

Updates never overwrite your edits: new keys are added and a changed default waits in `/exylialib updates`. A shipped job you removed is not reinstalled while `<id>.yml.deleted` exists.

## Do perks work after I leave a job?

Only in jobs the player holds; choices are kept.

## Can I move from Jobs Reborn?

Yes. See [Migration](/docs/exyliajobs/migration). AdvancedJobs is read by guesswork, and EcoJobs on MongoDB, PostgreSQL or Redis is not read.

## Do rewards drop if the inventory is full?

No. Milestone rewards, order items and refunds are queued and claimed on join.
