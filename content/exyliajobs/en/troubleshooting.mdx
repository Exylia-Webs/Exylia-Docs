Find the symptom, then check the causes in order. Most "nothing is paid" cases are one of the first three.

## Players earn nothing

1. **No economy plugin.** Install ExyliaEconomy or Vault. Without one the console warns once and only experience is paid.
2. **Missing permission.** The player needs `exyliajobs.use`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliajobs/permissions).
3. **Not holding the job.** Run `/jobs`; a job only pays once the player has joined it, unless `auto-join` is on in `config.yml`.
4. **A guard refuses the action.** Creative and spectator, vanished players, `disabled-worlds` and `disabled-regions`, a player paused from the economy screen, staff mode, minigames and idle players are not paid. Blocks a player placed, mobs from spawners and unripe crops pay nothing either. Each rule is in [Stop farms and exploits](/docs/exyliajobs/anti-exploit).
5. **The target is not in the job.** A job pays only for the blocks, mobs or items written in its file. Look at **Actions & rates** in the job hub.

## Pay dropped after a while

That is the economy guard working: fatigue after 64 actions in one spot, a flooded target's market rate, or the budget pacing factor. Move, change target, or raise the budget. See [Protect your economy](/docs/exyliajobs/economy-guard). The **Actions & rates** screen shows an arrow where something changed the price.

## A player cannot join a job

Joining is refused when the job is disabled (`enabled: false`) or when a leave cooldown or fee blocks leaving another job. With every slot taken the plugin asks which job to leave instead of refusing. Raise slots with `exyliajobs.slots.<n>`. See [Set levels, slots and leaving](/docs/exyliajobs/levels-and-slots).

## My job file change did not apply

Run `/jobsadmin reload`. The message tells you how many problems the jobs had; each one is written in the console with the file and the entry. A bad entry costs only that entry. Make sure you edited the file for the server's language, `plugins/ExyliaJobs/lang/<language>/jobs/<id>.yml`. A shipped job you deleted is not reinstalled while `<id>.yml.deleted` exists.

## Perks do not fire

Perks work only in jobs the player holds, in tiers they reached, and only after the job was really paid for that action. A placed block, a spawner mob or an idle player never sets a perk off. See [Add perks and prestige](/docs/exyliajobs/perks-and-prestige).

## Placeholders show nothing

Install PlaceholderAPI. Job ids are lower case (`%exyliajobs_level_miner%`). The progress placeholders read online players from memory. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliajobs/placeholders).

## Work order items did not arrive

Items that do not fit in the inventory are queued and claimed on join; the buyer can also use **Claim deliveries** in **My orders**. Milestone rewards and refunds are queued the same way.

## The plugin does not start

ExyliaJobs needs ExyliaLib and packetevents. The loader installs ExyliaLib and a fresh install needs one more restart. See [Install ExyliaJobs](/docs/exyliajobs/installation).
