At the end of this page you have chosen how many jobs players hold at once, what leaving a job costs, and when level rewards pay.

## Before you start

You need `exyliajobs.admin` and an idea of the rule you want: a few jobs per player (the default is 2), every job for everyone, or a fee to switch. If you use a fee, an economy plugin must be installed.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Run `/jobsadmin`, click **Settings**, then **Jobs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the slots">
    Click **Job slots** and set the number of jobs a player holds at once (1 to 64, default 2). To give everyone every job with no joining or leaving, turn on **Auto-join** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price leaving a job">
    Set the leave rules: **Experience kept** (the share kept when leaving, default 100%), **Leave cooldown** (a wait before leaving another job, default none) and **Leave fee** (default 0, charged in the default currency).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a rank more slots">
    Give the rank `exyliajobs.slots.<n>` in your permissions plugin, for example `exyliajobs.slots.4`. The highest one wins, and it never lowers the configured value.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

As a player run `/jobs` and join jobs until the slots are full. Click one more: the plugin does not refuse, it asks which job to leave and confirms. After leaving, join the same job again: your experience is back to the share you kept.

## Going further

### Levels and milestone rewards

The experience needed for each level comes from the job's curve ([Change what a job pays](/docs/exyliajobs/jobs-and-actions)). Each level reached plays the level-up effect and gives that level's **milestone rewards**; what does not fit in the inventory is queued and claimed on join. At max level experience stops. A milestone pays **once per player per job**: leaving, kept experience and prestige never repeat it. Turn on `milestones.repeat-after-prestige` to pay them again on the climb after a prestige.

### Joining and leaving

Joining is instant. With every slot taken, a player with one job sees "leave X first, keep N% experience?" and a player with several chooses which one goes, then confirms: both happen in one step. Leaving always asks first, via **Leave** in the job hub or `/jobs leave <job>`. A leave blocked by the cooldown or an unaffordable fee is refused with the time or the amount.

### Turn a job off without losing progress

Switch off **Enabled** for the job. Nobody can join it and it pays nothing, but levels are kept.

### Top lists

`/jobs top <job>` (or **Top players** in the job hub) ranks players by prestige, then level, then experience.

<Details title="Technical details">

| `config.yml` key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `slots` | 2 | Jobs held at once (1 to 64). |
| `auto-join` | false | Every player holds every job: no joining, leaving or slots. |
| `leave.kept-xp` | 1.0 | Share of the job's total experience kept when leaving; joining again starts there. |
| `leave.cooldown` | 0s | Wait after leaving before leaving another. |
| `leave.fee` | 0 | Money charged to leave, in the default currency; recorded as a sink. |
| `milestones.repeat-after-prestige` | false | Milestones pay again after a prestige. |
| `storage.flush-interval` | 30s | Progress is written this often and when a player leaves. |
| `storage.top-refresh` | 1m | The top list is read again this often. |

Experience for a level is at least 1. `income-bonus` and `xp-bonus` apply per level above 1. `JobLevelUpEvent`, `JobJoinEvent` and `JobLeaveEvent` exist; the join and leave events can be cancelled. See [API](/docs/exyliajobs/api).
</Details>
