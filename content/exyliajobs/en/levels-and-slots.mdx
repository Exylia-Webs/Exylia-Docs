## Levels

Experience needed for a level comes from the job's `curve` ([Jobs and actions](/docs/exyliajobs/jobs-and-actions)), at least 1. Each level reached fires `JobLevelUpEvent`, plays the level-up effect and gives that level's **milestone rewards** (`rewards:` in the job file, ExyliaLib rewards; what does not fit in the inventory is queued and claimed on join). At max level the experience stops. `income-bonus` and `xp-bonus` apply per level above 1.

A milestone pays **once per player per job**: leaving, kept experience and prestige never repeat it. With `milestones.repeat-after-prestige: true` they pay again on the climb after a prestige.

## Joining and leaving

A player holds jobs up to their slots. Joining is instant. With every slot taken, the plugin does not refuse: with one job held it asks "Leaves X first, keeps N% experience?", and with several it asks which one goes, then confirms and does both in one step. Leaving always asks first.

| `config.yml` key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `slots` | 2 | Jobs held at once (1 to 64). |
| `auto-join` | false | Every player holds every job: no joining, leaving or slots. |
| `leave.kept-xp` | 1.0 | Share of the job's total experience kept when leaving; joining again starts there. |
| `leave.cooldown` | 0s | Wait after leaving before leaving another. |
| `leave.fee` | 0 | Money charged to leave, in the default currency; recorded as a sink. |

`exyliajobs.slots.<n>` raises the slots (the highest one wins and never lowers the configured value). A leave blocked by the cooldown or an unaffordable fee is a real rule and is refused with the time or amount. `JobJoinEvent` and `JobLeaveEvent` can be cancelled.

A disabled job (`enabled: false`) cannot be joined and pays nothing, but levels are kept.

## Top lists

`/jobs top <job>` ranks players by prestige, then level, then experience. The list is read again every `storage.top-refresh` (1 minute). Progress is written every `storage.flush-interval` (30 seconds) and when a player leaves.
