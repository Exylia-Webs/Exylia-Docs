Everything you can write in a job file or a settings file can also be edited from menus, with a check before every save. At the end of this page you have edited a job in game, changed a setting, and know where the web editor and the players admin are.

## Before you start

You need `exyliajobs.admin` (operators have it). Admin screens are written again from the jar on every start, so do not hand-edit the admin menu files.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the admin hub">
    Run `/jobsadmin`. The hub has six cards: **Editor**, **Economy**, **Work orders**, **Players**, **Migration** and **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a job">
    Click **Editor**, then the job. Its hub has **General**, **Actions**, **Milestone rewards**, **Anti-exploit**, **Perks** and **Delete**. In **General** you change the name, icon, description, max level, level curve, income bonus, XP bonus, price share and whether the job is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a rule">
    In **Actions**, click a rule to edit its money and experience, or click **Add ▸ Action**, pick the action, then the target. You can pick the target from the block you look at, the item in your hand, a mob, a biome or a typed id. Removing a rule asks to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a setting">
    Back in the hub, click **Settings**. There is one card per theme: **Jobs**, **Anti-exploit**, **Market & fatigue**, **Budget & caps**, **Work orders**, **Perks & prestige** and **Integrations**. Each value explains itself, shows the current value and its default; click to edit (numbers are typed) and shift-right-click to reset.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Change one rule's money in **Actions**, then run `/jobs`, click the job and **Actions & rates**: the new price is there at once, with no reload. If the editor refuses a save, it says which part the loader would drop.

## Going further

### Use the web editor

Run `/exylialib editor`. Every job file is registered there as `job-<id>`, with the same check as the in-game editor and a reload when you apply. The settings files (`config.yml`, `economy.yml`, `perks.yml`, `orders.yml`, `integrations.yml`) are editable there too.

### Manage players

Click **Players** in the hub. It lists online players and has **Search player** for anybody who ever played. A player's screen lets you set a level or experience, take them out of a job, reset them (**Reset**, ExyliaLib's player reset) and, with the economy guard on, **Pause or resume** their pay. You can also use `/jobsadmin give level <player> <job> <level>` and `/jobsadmin give xp <player> <job> <amount>`.

### Delete a job

**Delete** in the job's hub removes its file. Players keep their levels in case the job returns.

<Details title="Technical details">

Every save goes through the real job loader first. It is refused only for what the loader would drop (a problem the file already had does not block you), written atomically and followed by a reload, so the catalogue, screens and features see it at once.

The language, report, storage and network keys of the settings stay in the YAML files.

Online players are edited in memory on their own thread; other players are written to the table. All admin screens need `exyliajobs.admin`.
</Details>
