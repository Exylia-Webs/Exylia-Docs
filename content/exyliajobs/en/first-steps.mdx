At the end of this page you have joined Miner, been paid for breaking stone, seen the pay table and posted a work order. You only use menus and chat; the twelve jobs are ready as they come.

## Before you start

Install the plugin and make sure you have an economy plugin (ExyliaEconomy or Vault). Without one, experience is paid but money is not. See [Install ExyliaJobs](/docs/exyliajobs/installation). Join the server as an operator so you can also open the admin screens.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the jobs">
    Run `/jobs`. Every job is a card with your level and a prompt to join. Click **Miner**: you join it and its hub opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work">
    Break stone and ores. Money and experience are added up and paid once every five seconds, as one summary in a corner popup, not a chat line per block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the pay table">
    Open the Miner hub (`/jobs`, then click Miner) and click **Actions & rates**. It lists what each block pays you right now, with an arrow where the market or fatigue changed the base price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Level up">
    Level 10 pays a milestone reward and opens the first perk tier. In the hub click **Perks** and pick one of the two perks. You can change it later for a fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post a work order">
    Run `/jobs orders`, then click **Post an order**. It asks for the item, the job whose workers may deliver, the units, the price per unit and how long it stays up, and holds the price until players deliver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the guard">
    Run `/jobsadmin economy`. The dashboard shows the budget, the pay factor, the money created per hour and the top earners.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

After a few blocks you should see an earnings popup, and `%exyliajobs_level_miner%` (or the **Miner** card) should show your level and experience. If nothing is paid, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliajobs/troubleshooting).

## What to do next

- Change what a job pays: [Change what a job pays](/docs/exyliajobs/jobs-and-actions).
- Set the budget before your players find the best farm: [Protect your economy](/docs/exyliajobs/economy-guard). The first admin to join is told about it once.
- Give a rank more slots or orders: [Permissions](/docs/exyliajobs/permissions).
