---
title: "First steps"
description: "From the first job to your first payout, level, perk and work order, in a few minutes."
---

This walk-through uses the files as they are generated. Join as an operator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the jobs">
    `/jobs` opens the list. Each card shows the job, your level and a prompt to join. Click **Miner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work">
    Break stone and ores. Money and experience are added up and paid once every five seconds, with one summary shown as a corner toast.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the pay table">
    In the job hub open **Actions and rates**: what each target pays you now, with an arrow where the market or fatigue changed it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Level up">
    Level 10 pays a milestone reward and opens the first perk tier: **Perks** offers two perks, you pick one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post a work order">
    `/jobs orders` opens the board. **Post an order** asks for an item, a job, the units, the unit price and a duration, and holds the price until players deliver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the guard">
    `/jobsadmin economy` opens the dashboard: the budget, the pay factor, money created per hour and the top earners.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Next

- Change what jobs pay in [Jobs and actions](/docs/exyliajobs/jobs-and-actions), or in the [editors](/docs/exyliajobs/editors).
- Set the budget before your players find the best farm: [Economy guard](/docs/exyliajobs/economy-guard). The first admin to join is told about it once.
- Give players `exyliajobs.slots.<n>` or `exyliajobs.orders.limit.<n>`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliajobs/permissions).
