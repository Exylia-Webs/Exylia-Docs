## Notes

- A disabled job does not exist for `/jobs join`, `/jobs leave` and `/jobs top`: the player is told the job is unknown.
- `/jobsadmin reload` reads `config.yml`, `economy.yml`, `perks.yml`, `orders.yml`, `integrations.yml` and every job file again. A bad job entry costs that entry only, and the count of problems is shown. Changing the `features` list needs a restart.
- `/jobsadmin give level` only sets the number. To pay the milestone rewards and play the effects, use `/jobsadmin give xp` instead.
- The import command is explained in [Import from another jobs plugin](/docs/exyliajobs/migration).

<Details title="Menu actions for your own menus">
Screens open each other through `exyliajobs:` actions, and your own menus can use them: `open_jobs`, `open_job`, `open_perks`, `open_orders`, `open_admin_hub`, `open_admin_jobs`, `open_admin_economy`, `open_admin_orders`, `open_admin_players`, `open_admin_migration` and `settings`. The admin ones need `exyliajobs.admin`.
</Details>
