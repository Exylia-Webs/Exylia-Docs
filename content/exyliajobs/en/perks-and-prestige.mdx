At the end of this page players choose between two perks at each tier of a job, you can change the perks and the swap fee, and you have decided whether players may prestige for permanent bonuses.

## Before you start

You need a job with perk tiers; all twelve shipped jobs have five (the first opens at level 10). Players need that job and the level of the tier. Prestige needs a player at the job's max level (50 by default).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a perk as a player">
    Run `/jobs perks <job>`, or click the job in `/jobs` and then **Perks**. Each tier shows two cards, `a` and `b`. Click one to choose it; in an empty tier it is immediate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap a perk">
    Click the other card in a tier you already chose. It asks to confirm and charges the swap fee (250 by default; 0 makes it free). The fee counts as money the jobs took back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a perk as an admin">
    Run `/jobsadmin`, click **Editor**, the job, then **Perks**. Pick a perk and edit it in ExyliaLib's ability editor. Saving writes it back to the job file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the fee and prestige rules">
    Run `/jobsadmin`, click **Settings**, then **Perks & prestige**. There you set **Swap fee**, **Prestige** (on or off), **Most stars**, **Money per star**, **Experience per star** and **Keep perks**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Give yourself levels with `/jobsadmin give level <player> <job> <level>`, open `/jobs perks <job>` and choose a perk at level 10. Do the matching action: the perk fires only after the job really paid for it. To test prestige, give yourself the max level and run `/jobs prestige <job>`: you confirm, the level returns to 1 and you gain one star.

## Going further

### Prestige

At max level, `/jobs prestige <job>` (or the **Prestige** card in the Perks screen) asks to confirm, sets the level to 1, adds one star and plays an effect. Each star adds 2% money and 2% experience by default, up to 10 stars. After the first prestige every tier stays open, so chosen perks stay active while the level restarts at 1. Turn **Prestige** off to remove it altogether.

Milestone rewards pay again after a prestige only if `milestones.repeat-after-prestige` is on ([Set levels, slots and leaving](/docs/exyliajobs/levels-and-slots)). Show the stars with `%exyliajobs_stars_<job>%` ([Placeholders](/docs/exyliajobs/placeholders)).

### Write your own perk

A perk is an entry in the job file under `perks`. Each key is the **level that opens a tier**; each tier holds two choices, `a` and `b` (a third is ignored with a warning).

```yaml
perks:
  10:
    a:
      name: 'Prospector'
      icon: RAW_GOLD
      description: ['{highlight}6% {letters}chance an ore drops', 'twice its usual loot.']
      trigger: JOB_BREAK
      chance: 6
      conditions:
        - block: ['#minecraft:coal_ores', ancient_debris]
      effects:
        - drops: {multiplier: 2}
```

`name`, `icon` and `description` draw the card; everything else is an ExyliaLib ability: `trigger`, `chance`, `cooldown`, `conditions`, `effects`. The shipped perks use the effects `potion`, `xp`, `drops`, `reveal`, `smelt`, `break-area`, `replant`, `grow`, `fish-hook`, `item` and `heal`. A perk without a known trigger is skipped. Perks only work in jobs the player holds and in tiers they have reached.

<Details title="Triggers and prestige keys">

Each action type has a trigger `JOB_<ACTION>`: `JOB_BREAK`, `JOB_PLACE`, `JOB_HARVEST`, `JOB_PLANT`, `JOB_STRIP_LOG`, `JOB_KILL`, `JOB_BREED`, `JOB_TAME`, `JOB_SHEAR`, `JOB_MILK`, `JOB_FISH`, `JOB_SMELT`, `JOB_BREW`, `JOB_ENCHANT`, `JOB_REPAIR`, `JOB_SMITHING`, `JOB_CRAFT`, `JOB_EAT`, `JOB_COLLECT`, `JOB_VILLAGER_TRADE`, `JOB_EXPLORE`.

A `JOB_` trigger fires only after that job **was actually paid** for the action, once per job, so placed blocks, spawner mobs, idle players and every refused action never set a perk off. Breaks and harvests fire once the break is final (drops, `smelt`, `break-area` and `xp` work); kills carry the death and its drops. The built-in triggers `BLOCK_BREAK`, `KILL`, `FISH` and `CAST` also work, but meet the same guards. `FISH` carries the catch, so a double catch is a `FISH` perk.

`perks.yml`:

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `swap-fee` | 250 | Money charged to swap a chosen perk; 0 for free. |
| `prestige.enabled` | true | Whether players may prestige. |
| `prestige.max-stars` | 10 | Most stars per job; 0 for no limit. |
| `prestige.money-per-star` | 0.02 | +2% money per star; goes through the economy guard. |
| `prestige.xp-per-star` | 0.02 | +2% experience per star. |
| `prestige.keep-perks` | true | After the first prestige every tier stays open. |
| `prestige.star` | `★` | The symbol `%exyliajobs_stars_<job>%` repeats once per star. |

`JobPerkChosenEvent` and `JobPrestigeEvent` can be cancelled.
</Details>
