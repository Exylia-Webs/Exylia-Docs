## Perks are data

Perks live in each job file under `perks`, built on ExyliaLib abilities (see ExyliaLib's `ability.md`). Each key is the **level that opens a tier**; each tier holds two choices, `a` and `b` (a third is ignored with a warning). A player picks one per tier.

```yaml
perks:
  10:
    a:
      name: 'Prospector'
      icon: RAW_GOLD
      description: ['{highlight}6% {letters}chance an ore drops', 'twice its usual loot.']
      trigger: JOB_BREAK
      chance: 6
      conditions:
        - block: ['#minecraft:coal_ores', ancient_debris]
      effects:
        - drops: {multiplier: 2}
```

`name`, `icon` and `description` draw the card; everything else is an ability (`trigger`, `chance`, `cooldown`, `conditions`, `effects`). The shipped perks use the effects `potion`, `xp`, `drops`, `reveal`, `smelt`, `break-area`, `replant`, `grow`, `fish-hook`, `item` and `heal`. A perk without a known trigger is skipped. Perks only work in jobs the player holds and in tiers they have reached.

## Triggers

Each action type has a trigger `JOB_<ACTION>`: `JOB_BREAK`, `JOB_PLACE`, `JOB_HARVEST`, `JOB_PLANT`, `JOB_STRIP_LOG`, `JOB_KILL`, `JOB_BREED`, `JOB_TAME`, `JOB_SHEAR`, `JOB_MILK`, `JOB_FISH`, `JOB_SMELT`, `JOB_BREW`, `JOB_ENCHANT`, `JOB_REPAIR`, `JOB_SMITHING`, `JOB_CRAFT`, `JOB_EAT`, `JOB_COLLECT`, `JOB_VILLAGER_TRADE`, `JOB_EXPLORE`.

A `JOB_` trigger fires only after that job **was actually paid** for the action, once per job, so placed blocks, spawner mobs, idle players and every refused action never set a perk off. Breaks and harvests fire once the break is final (drops, `smelt`, `break-area` and `xp` work); kills carry the death and its drops. The built-in triggers `BLOCK_BREAK`, `KILL`, `FISH` and `CAST` also work, but meet the same guards (and placed blocks and farmed mobs are refused). `FISH` carries the catch, so a double catch is a `FISH` perk.

## Choosing and swapping

`/jobs perks <job>` or the hub's **Perks** card. Choosing in an empty tier is immediate. Swapping asks to confirm and costs `perks.swap-fee` (250, 0 for free), recorded as a sink. `JobPerkChosenEvent` can be cancelled. Admins edit a perk in game with ExyliaLib's ability editor; saving writes it back to the job file.

## Prestige

At max level, `/jobs prestige <job>` (or the Perks screen) asks to confirm, then sets the level to 1, adds one star and plays an effect. `perks.yml`:

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `prestige.enabled` | true | Whether players may prestige. |
| `prestige.max-stars` | 10 | Most stars per job; 0 for no limit. |
| `prestige.money-per-star` | 0.02 | +2% money per star; goes through the economy guard. |
| `prestige.xp-per-star` | 0.02 | +2% experience per star. |
| `prestige.keep-perks` | true | After the first prestige every tier stays open, so chosen perks stay active although the level restarts at 1. |
| `prestige.star` | `★` | The symbol `%exyliajobs_stars_<job>%` repeats once per star. |

Milestone rewards pay again after a prestige only with `milestones.repeat-after-prestige`. `JobPrestigeEvent` can be cancelled.
