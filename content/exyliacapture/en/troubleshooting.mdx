Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

ExyliaLib is missing. The console names it when ExyliaCapture is disabled. Put `ExyliaLib.jar` in `plugins/` before the first boot. See [Install ExyliaCapture](/docs/exyliacapture/installation).

## The event will not start

`/captureadmin start <id>` tells you why, one line per reason. The usual ones:

- It is disabled. Open `/captureadmin`, **Configurations**, your event, and click the button until it reads **ENABLED**.
- Where it runs is not set. Click **Capture zone** (or the type's own place button) and mark it.
- It is already running. Stop it with `/captureadmin stop <id>`.

## Nobody gains points in the zone

The game mode and state matter. A player is ignored while dead, in creative, in spectator, or in staff mode or vanished with ExyliaStaff. Test in survival mode, and not as a staff member on duty.

## Conquest never gives points

Conquest gives a zone to a clan, so it needs a detected clan plugin, and players without a clan are ignored. See [Clans](/docs/exyliacapture/clans).

## A player won while offline

Nothing is lost. The reward is saved and handed over the next time they join, and so are items that did not fit in a full inventory.

## A placeholder shows as text

- PlaceholderAPI is not installed.
- The event is new: run `/captureadmin reload`.
- The id is misspelled. The placeholder is left visible on purpose, so a typo shows. Check the spelling on [Placeholders](/docs/exyliacapture/placeholders).

## The leaderboard is empty after a win

It is a snapshot refreshed every 60 seconds. Wait a minute and open `/capture top` again.

## Changing database.yml does nothing

`/captureadmin reload` does not reopen the database. Restart the server.

## The admin command /cadmin does not work

It was removed. Use `/captureadmin`.
