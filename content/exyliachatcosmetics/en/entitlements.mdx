A player owns a cosmetic through a permission or through a grant from you. A grant can be permanent or expire, and you can take it back at any time.

## Before you start

A cosmetic entry with `permission: false` is owned by grants only, whichever permission a player holds. For everything else, both ways work and can be mixed.

## Give a cosmetic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the command">
    `/cca give <player> <type:id> [duration]`, for example `/cca give Notch tag:heart` (permanent) or `/cca give Notch chat_color:aurora 30d` (30 days). Durations are `30m`, `2h`, `14d`, `1w`; decimals are allowed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note where it came from (optional)">
    Add `--source <name>`, `--ref <id>` and `--note <text>` so you can tell a purchase from a reward later, for example `--source PURCHASE --ref order-1042`. An unknown source is stored as `EXTERNAL`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check">
    `/cca check <player> <type:id>` says whether they own it, and `/cca list <player>` lists their grants with a number each.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Or give it by permission: `exyliachatcosmetics.<type>.<id>` unlocks one, and the wildcards below unlock more. Use your permissions plugin, for example LuckPerms, and see [Permissions](/docs/exyliachatcosmetics/permissions).

| Node | Grants |
|---|---|
| `exyliachatcosmetics.tag.heart` | one cosmetic |
| `exyliachatcosmetics.tag.category.premium` | every cosmetic in one tab of a type |
| `exyliachatcosmetics.tag.*` | every cosmetic of a type |
| `exyliachatcosmetics.*` | everything |

## Check that it works

The player runs `/cc tags` (or the menu of that type). The cosmetic is no longer locked and they can click it to wear it.

## Going further

### Take it back

- `/cca remove <player> <type:id> [source] confirm` revokes every active grant of that cosmetic, or only those of one source.
- `/cca revoke <id>` revokes exactly one, by the number `/cca list` prints.
- `/cca unequip <player> <type>` and `/cca equip <player> <type:id>` change what they wear. `/cca clear <player> confirm` takes everything off an online player.

A player may hold several grants for one cosmetic (a permanent one from a rank, a 30-day purchase). Removing one never touches the others.

### When a grant expires

The cosmetic stops being drawn, the player gets an `expired` message, and an online sweep runs every 30 seconds. With `expiry.notify-on-join` on (default), a player is told on join about temporary grants running low and about worn ones that ran out while they were away. Expired and revoked rows stay for `expiry.retention-days` (default `30`) so `/cca list` still shows what was given, then are deleted.

### Grants on a network

Each grant remembers the server scope it was given under, set by `ownership.scope` in `config.yml` (see [Configuration](/docs/exyliachatcosmetics/configuration)). With a scope other than `global`, a grant counts only on servers of that scope. A cosmetic bought with one of the `ownership.global-currencies`, and every grant made before 1.4.0, counts on every server. <Since v="1.4.0" />

### Why a cosmetic stays "on" after losing the rank

What a player wears is stored on their row, and ownership is asked again whenever a line is drawn. A cosmetic they no longer own is simply not drawn, and it comes back the day it is theirs again, still where they left it. Nothing is removed because a permission said no: a permission plugin still loading on join must never take someone's cosmetics away for good. The only removal that is written down is a custom cosmetic that was deleted.

<Details title="Technical details">

Three separate questions: available (exists, not `hidden`), owned (a valid permission or grant) and equipped (worn). Ownership sources:

| Source | Stored | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| `PERMISSION` | no, asked live | `exyliachatcosmetics.<type>.<id>` and the wildcards; ignored for `permission: false` |
| `ADMIN` | yes | `/cca give` without `--source` |
| `PURCHASE`, `REWARD`, `EVENT`, `ACHIEVEMENT`, `EXTERNAL` | yes | `/cca give --source <name>`, or the [API](/docs/exyliachatcosmetics/api) |
| the maker of a custom cosmetic | implicit | their row, permanent |
| a group named by a rank colour | implicit | the `groups` list in `rank-colors.yml` |

A grant row carries `expires_at` (`0` is permanent) and `revoked_at`; an inactive row is simply not a grant, so an offline player's grant stops counting without anything running.

Tables (H2 by default, others as in [Database](/docs/exylialib/database)): `chatcosmetics_profiles`, `chatcosmetics_entitlements`, `chatcosmetics_custom`, `chatcosmetics_loadouts`, `chatcosmetics_tokens`. A profile is read once on join and held in memory, so chat adds no queries. Writes are batched and flushed every 5 seconds, on quit and on shutdown.

</Details>
