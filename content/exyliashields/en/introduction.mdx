ExyliaShields is a shield design editor. A player stacks patterns and colours into a design, saves it in a numbered slot, and whatever is in the active slot is drawn on the shield they carry. No loom, no banner, no crafting.

In about ten minutes you will have a design on your own shield, and know how to give players more slots, more layers and more colours.

## What you need

- Paper 1.21.1 or newer, or Folia 1.21.4 or newer.
- packetevents is required and ExyliaLib is recommended. See [Install ExyliaShields](/docs/exyliashields/installation).
- Optional: PlaceholderAPI for [placeholders](/docs/exyliashields/placeholders), and ExyliaEconomy or Vault so players can buy locked patterns and colours.

## What players get

- **43 patterns and 16 colours**, stacked up to six layers deep by default.
- **Slots**: saved designs, one worn at a time. Everybody gets one; a rank can get more.
- **A community browser** to import designs other players made.
- **A crate** that unlocks patterns and colours with keys, and a **shop** to buy them with money.
- **3D menus** drawn in the world in front of the player, for clients 1.21.4 or newer.

A player may use a pattern or colour when a permission grants it, when a crate landed on it, or when they bought it. By default only operators hold the permissions, so on a new server give your players some palette first: see [Your first shield](/docs/exyliashields/first-steps).

## The screens

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **MY SHIELDS** | The saved designs. `/shields` opens here. |
| **SHIELD EDITOR** | Building one design: add a layer, dye it, remove it, preview it. |
| **SELECT COLOR** | Choosing the base or the dye. |
| **COMMUNITY DESIGNS** | The most-used designs on the server, importable by id. |
| **SHIELD CRATE** | Keys spent on reels that land on a pattern or colour. |
| **3D designer and picker** | The editor and the slot list drawn in the world. See [3D menus](/docs/exyliashields/3d-menus). |
