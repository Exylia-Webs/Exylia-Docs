Every ExyliaShields command, with the permission it needs. Players use `/shields`; staff use `/shieldsadmin`. Player names complete as you type, and `keys` and `unlocks` also work on players who are offline.

<Commands />

## Players

Everything else a player does (the editor, the colour picker, the community browser) is reached from the menus. Deleting or resetting a design asks for confirmation first.

On a client older than 1.21.4 the 3D menus cannot be drawn, so `/shields 3d` and `/shields 3d slots` open the inventory menus instead.

## Staff

`/shieldsadmin` opens the admin hub. The subcommands are shortcuts to the same tools. `showcase add`, `crate block add` and `spatial place` ask you to confirm the spot with a gesture (sneak and click keeps the way you face), or cancel.

Unlock ids are `pattern:<id>` and `color:<id>`, for example `pattern:creeper` and `color:red`. Tab completion lists them. Keys and unlocks are written to the player's account, so they survive a rank running out.

<Callout type="warning" title="The old /shields spellings">
Up to 1.4 the staff commands were subcommands of `/shields` (`/shields reload`, `/shields keys give`, `/shields crate block add`). Since 1.5.0 they live under `/shieldsadmin`. The old spellings still run, then tell the staff member the new one. Update scripts, NPCs and menus that call them.
</Callout>

## What reload does

`/shieldsadmin reload` re-reads `config.yml`, `shields.yml`, `messages.yml` and the menus of the language in use. Open 3D menus redraw with the new catalogue, prices and words. It does not touch what players saved, which is in the database.

## Reset a player

To delete a player's slots, designs, keys and unlocks, use ExyliaLib: `/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaShields`. To empty tables in bulk, use `/exylialib wipe`. See [ExyliaLib commands](/docs/exylialib/commands#moving-and-wiping-data).
