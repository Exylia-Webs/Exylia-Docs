---
title: "Placeholders"
description: "The slots a player uses, the design they wear, and what they have unlocked."
---

Every placeholder below is written in full, and clicking one copies it. They are registered through
ExyliaLib, so they work in any Exylia text and, with PlaceholderAPI installed, everywhere else.

## The design they wear

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliashields_slots_used%` | How many slots hold a design. |
| `%exyliashields_slots_max%` | How many slots their permissions allow. |
| `%exyliashields_active_slot%` | The slot they are wearing, counted from one as the menu shows it. |
| `%exyliashields_layers%` | How many layers the worn design has. `0` when the slot is empty. |
| `%exyliashields_base_color%` | The worn design's base colour, by its display name. Empty when the slot is empty. |

## The crate

| Placeholder | Description |
|---|---|
| `%exyliashields_keys%` | How many crate keys they hold. |
| `%exyliashields_unlocked%` | How many patterns and colours they have unlocked from the crate or the shop. |
| `%exyliashields_unlocked_<id>%` | `true` or `false` for one entry, its id with `pattern_` or `color_` in front — `%exyliashields_unlocked_pattern_stripe_bottom%`, `%exyliashields_unlocked_color_red%`. |
| `%exyliashields_tier_count_<tier>%` | How many entries a rarity holds — `%exyliashields_tier_count_legendary%`. |

`%exyliashields_unlocked%` counts only what was won or bought, not what a permission grants.

The design placeholders answer empty for a player whose slots have not loaded yet rather than `0`, so a
scoreboard does not flash a wrong number on join.
