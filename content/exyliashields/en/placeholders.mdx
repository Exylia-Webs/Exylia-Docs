Show a player's shield data in scoreboards, holograms, tab lists and chat. Click a placeholder to copy it. They work in any Exylia text, and everywhere else when PlaceholderAPI is installed.

<Placeholders />

## Notes

`%exyliashields_unlocked%` counts only what was won in the crate or bought, not what a permission grants.

The design placeholders answer empty, not `0`, for a player whose slots have not loaded yet, so a scoreboard does not flash a wrong number on join. `%exyliashields_layers%` is `0` and `%exyliashields_base_color%` empty when the worn slot has no design.

<Details title="Technical details">
`%exyliashields_unlocked_<id>%` takes the entry's id with `pattern_` or `color_` in front, such as `%exyliashields_unlocked_color_red%`. `%exyliashields_tier_count_<tier>%` takes a rarity id from `crate.tiers` in `config.yml` and counts the patterns and colours that name it in `shields.yml`.
</Details>
