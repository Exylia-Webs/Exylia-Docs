At the end of this page you have a design on the shield in your hand, and your players can make their own.

## Before you start

Install the plugin: see [Install ExyliaShields](/docs/exyliashields/installation). Players own no pattern or colour at first. Operators hold every one, but a normal player sees them all locked until a permission, a crate or a purchase unlocks them. Give your default group a free starter palette. With LuckPerms:

```text
/lp group default permission set exyliashields.color.* true
/lp group default permission set exyliashields.pattern.stripe_top true
/lp group default permission set exyliashields.pattern.circle true
```

The ids are the keys in `shields.yml`. To give everything, use `exyliashields.pattern.*`.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your shields">
    Run `/shields`. The **MY SHIELDS** menu opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a design">
    Click **Shields ▸ New shield**. Everybody has one slot without any permission. The **SHIELD EDITOR** opens on a new slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the base colour">
    Click **Design ▸ Base color**, then a colour. You return to the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a dye">
    Click **Design ▸ Dye color** and choose the colour the next layer will have. It starts on purple.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add layers">
    Click a **Pattern** in the list. It is added on top of the stack in the current dye. Change the dye and click another pattern to stack more. Click a layer in the layer list to remove it. A design holds six layers by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wear it">
    Click **Design ▸ Set as active**. The menu closes and chat says the design is active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Hold a shield in either hand. It shows your design. Swap slots in `/shields` with a right-click on a slot to wear it; a left-click edits it.

## Going further

- **Preview**: **Design ▸ Pattern preview** turns the live preview on and off.
- **Start over**: **Design ▸ Reset design** removes every layer; **Design ▸ Delete design** removes the slot. Both ask you to confirm.
- **Import**: click **Shields ▸ Import by ID** and type a design id, or open **Shields ▸ Community browser** and click one.

<Callout type="tip" title="Locked patterns">
A locked pattern says so in its lore. If the shop is on and an economy is installed, it shows a price and a click buys it. See [The crate and the shop](/docs/exyliashields/crate).
</Callout>
