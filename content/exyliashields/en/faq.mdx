## Can a player wear somebody else's shield design?

Not by picking the item up: the design belongs to the player, not the shield. They can import the design by id from the community library, which copies it into one of their own slots. See [The community library](/docs/exyliashields/community).

## How many layers can a design have?

Six by default (`max_layers` in `shields.yml`), or the number in `exyliashields.layers.<n>` for a player who holds it. Every layer is a banner pattern the client draws on every shield in view, so raising it has a real cost.

## How many designs can a player save?

One, or the highest `exyliashields.max_slots.<n>` they hold. See [Sell slots, layers and colours](/docs/exyliashields/ranks).

## Can players keep a design when the rank ends?

Designs are stored on the player. Patterns and colours won in the crate or bought stay too. Patterns granted by a rank permission go with the rank.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes. Paper 1.21.1 or newer, Folia 1.21.4 or newer.

## Do I need the 3D menus?

No. They are an option for 1.21.4 or newer clients; everybody else gets the inventory menus. See [3D menus](/docs/exyliashields/3d-menus).

## Are there placeholders or an API?

Yes. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliashields/placeholders) and [the API](/docs/exyliashields/api).

## Where are designs stored?

In the database in `database.yml` (H2 by default), under the player. See [Install ExyliaShields](/docs/exyliashields/installation#tables).

## Where do I get support?

On the [Exylia Discord](https://discord.exylia.net).
