Besides the inventory menus, ExyliaShields draws three menus **in the world**, on a stage a few blocks in
front of the player: the **3D designer**, the **3D shield picker** and the **3D community browser**. They are packets only — no
resource pack, nothing placed in the world, and nobody else sees them.

| Menu | Opened by | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **3D designer** | `/shields 3d`, or **EDIT** / **NEW SHIELD** in the picker | The editor and the colour picker in one: a paint grid of dyes on one side, the layer stack on the other (top layer first, a row for where a new layer goes, the base at the bottom), every pattern along the bottom as a small banner, and the shield front-on in the middle wearing the draft. |
| **3D shield picker** | `/shields 3d slots`, or `/shields` with `menu.style: SPATIAL` | Every saved shield on a plinth, the one looked at turning in the middle. **EQUIP** wears it, **EDIT** opens it in the designer, **NEW SHIELD** starts one on the first free slot, **COMMUNITY** opens the browser. |
| **3D community browser** | **COMMUNITY** in the picker or the designer | The designs players shared, in two tabs (most taken, newest) around the one looked at, with its author, layers and uses. **IMPORT** copies the chosen design into a free slot and returns to the menu it came from, showing it; the back arrow returns without importing. |

## The designer

Every click changes a **draft**, never the saved design:

- A pattern is laid on top as a new layer, in the paint colour; with a layer chosen it replaces that
  layer's pattern instead.
- A dye recolours the chosen layer, or the base.
- **UNDO** takes one click back, **REVERT** drops them all.
- **SAVE** writes the draft to the slot under the same rules as the inventory editor: a slot the player
  has, no more layers than their limit, and every new pattern and colour theirs.

A draft that uses a pattern or colour not unlocked yet reads **BUY & SAVE** with the total. The first
click asks, the second pays at the shop's prices and saves. A part nobody sells blocks the save. A
saved design is published to the [community library](/docs/exyliashields/community) as the inventory
editor does.

Each menu shows a short guided tour the first time a player opens it. Sneaking leaves it.

## Making /shields open them

```yaml title="config.yml"
menu:
  style: CLASSIC
```

| Value | What `/shields` opens |
|---|---|
| `CLASSIC` | The inventory slot list. The default. |
| `SPATIAL` | The 3D shield picker, whose **EDIT** and **NEW SHIELD** open the 3D designer. |

`/shields 3d` and `/shields 3d slots` open the 3D menus whatever `menu.style` says. The
[API's](/docs/exyliashields/api) `openSlots` follows `menu.style` like the command.

<Callout type="note" title="Clients older than 1.21.4">
The 3D menus need a 1.21.4 or newer client. An older one gets the inventory menus instead: the slot
list, or the editor on the slot it was opening. Nothing has to be configured for that.
</Callout>

Both menus need `exyliashields.command.shields`, like `/shields`.

## Placing one in the world

A 3D menu can also stand fixed in a lobby, where players walk up to it:

```text
/shieldsadmin spatial place designer    # a placement wizard: walk there, sneak-click to keep your facing
/shieldsadmin spatial place wardrobe    # the picker
/shieldsadmin spatial place community   # the community browser
/shieldsadmin spatial remove            # the one within 4 blocks of you
```

`/shieldsadmin spatial edit <designer|wardrobe|community>` opens one for you to move its pieces with the mouse
wheel, and `/shieldsadmin spatial tour <player>` shows that player every tour again. See
[Commands and permissions](/docs/exyliashields/commands#shieldsadmin).

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `spatial/designer.yml` | The designer's layout: where every piece sits, its size, the stage distance and the theme. |
| `spatial/wardrobe.yml` | The picker's layout. |
| `spatial/community.yml` | The browser's layout. |
| `spatial/crate.yml` | The [3D crate opening](/docs/exyliashields/crate#the-3d-opening), written the first time it is used. |

Every word the menus draw is in `lang/<code>/messages.yml`, under `designer`, `wardrobe` and `community`, so a
translation restyles them without touching the layout. Coordinates are blocks on the board, a plane
`anchor.distance` ahead of the player's eyes; the comment at the top of each file draws the layout.
