## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is required. Without it the server disables ExyliaShields and says why in the console. Install it, then restart. `ExyliaLib` is not a hard requirement, but without it the crate, the shop and the `/exylialib` commands are missing.

## Players see every pattern locked

A normal player owns nothing at first. Give your default group a palette, or let them win one: see [Sell slots, layers and colours](/docs/exyliashields/ranks). Operators always see everything unlocked, so test with a non-op account.

## A player is told "Your rank does not include this"

They lack one of `exyliashields.command.shields`, `exyliashields.browser` or `exyliashields.crate`. Everybody holds those by default, so a group or another plugin is denying one. Check with your permission plugin. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliashields/permissions).

## A player has only one slot

One slot is the default. Grant `exyliashields.max_slots.<n>` to the ranks that should have more.

## Designs disappear from shields

This is `force_system`, on by default: a player without `exyliashields.use` has the design removed whenever they equip a shield. Grant the node, or set `force_system: false` in `config.yml` and run `/shieldsadmin reload`. It also happens in a world listed under `disabled-worlds`, where shields show plain until the player leaves it.

## A design vanished when the shield broke

It should not. The design is stored on the player, not on the item. If a new shield stays plain, check `exyliashields.use` and that the slot is still the active one (`/shields`, right-click the slot).

## An imported design looks different

An import is drawn with the importer's own palette. A layer using a pattern they do not own is not a way around locking.

## The shop shows nothing for sale

It needs ExyliaEconomy or Vault, `shop.enabled: true`, and a price for the entry's rarity under `shop.tier-prices` (or on the entry). Without all three a locked row stays locked. See [The crate and the shop](/docs/exyliashields/crate).

## The crate does nothing

Check `crate.enabled`, that the player has keys (`/shieldsadmin keys <player>`), and that the crate is not already open. Staff can give keys with `/shieldsadmin keys give <player> <amount>`.

## A player gets the inventory menu instead of the 3D one

The 3D menus need a 1.21.4 or newer client, and PacketEvents on the server. Older clients get the inventory menus. See [3D menus](/docs/exyliashields/3d-menus).

## Reset one player

`/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaShields` deletes their slots, library designs, keys and unlocks. See [ExyliaLib's commands](/docs/exylialib/commands#moving-and-wiping-data).
