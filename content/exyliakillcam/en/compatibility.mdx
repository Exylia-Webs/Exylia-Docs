At the end of this page you know how ExyliaKillCam avoids fighting with your other plugins. It needs no setup: the defaults are the safe ones.

## Deaths another plugin is in charge of

While `deaths.skip-held-players` is on (default, keep it), a death does not play at once when:

- another plugin holds the player through ExyliaLib's sessions (a duel, an event, a match, a queue, an FFA arena);
- another plugin took over what they drop;
- they are watching any replay.

The death is kept. Once the plugin lets the player go (a second after the release, if nobody else holds them) the killcam plays or is offered according to their [mode](/docs/exyliakillcam/modes).

With the option off, the killcam is not delayed, but it never starts for a player who is held at that moment: in `AUTO` they stay put, and `/killcam` says "You cannot watch a killcam right now." until the other plugin lets them go.

## While a player watches

ExyliaKillCam holds the viewer under the claim `killcam`, so a duel request or a queue never takes them halfway. When another plugin must take them anyway, the killcam ends, the player is put back where they were and gets the summary.

## Staff in vanish

With `recording.hide-vanished` on (default), players in vanish are never in a killcam. A vanished player who dies gets no killcam of their own either.

## ExyliaKillEffect

With [ExyliaKillEffect](/docs/exyliakilleffect/introduction) 1.9.0 or newer and `kill-effects.replay` on, the killer's kill effect plays again at the body, for the viewer alone. Only the effect that really played on that death is shown. It follows the viewer's own particle choice in ExyliaKillEffect. Older versions are skipped silently.

## PacketEvents and Folia

Every body in a killcam is a packet, so PacketEvents is required to show one. ExyliaKillCam runs on Folia.
