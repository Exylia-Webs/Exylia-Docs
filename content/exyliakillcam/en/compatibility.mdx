title: "Compatibility"
description: "Deaths other plugins are in charge of, the viewer held while watching, vanished players, disabled worlds, ExyliaKillEffect, PacketEvents and Folia."
---

A killcam takes a player somewhere else for a few seconds. ExyliaKillCam is built so that never fights
with another plugin: it leaves alone the deaths others are in charge of, and while somebody watches, it
holds them so nothing else picks them up halfway.

## Deaths another plugin is in charge of

While `deaths.skip-held-players` is on (the default), a death that happens while:

- another plugin **holds** the player through ExyliaLib's sessions: a duel, an event, a match, a queue,
  an arena — anything that has claimed them;
- another plugin has **claimed what they drop** through ExyliaLib, as a duel that keeps its own loot
  does;
- they are watching a replay of any kind.

is not shown at once: the plugin is in charge of the player and usually has its own ending. The death is
kept, and once that plugin lets the player go (a second after the release, if nobody else holds them
by then) the killcam is played or offered according to the player's mode. A death another plugin
cancels but holds the player for still counts. Keep the option on. An ExyliaFFA kill is credited to its
killer even when nobody landed the last blow.

With it off, the killcam is not delayed: it starts after the respawn, but never for a player who is held at
that moment. A player in `AUTO` then simply stays put, and `/killcam` answers *"You cannot watch a
killcam right now."* The death is kept, so `/killcam` can show it once the other plugin lets them go.

## The viewer is held

While a player watches, ExyliaKillCam holds them under the claim `killcam` in ExyliaLib's sessions. To
every other plugin that asks, they are busy: a duel request or a queue never takes them halfway.

When another plugin must have them anyway, it asks ExyliaKillCam to let go: the killcam ends, the player is
put back where they were and gets the summary, and the other plugin carries on. The claim is released
whenever the killcam ends, however it ends.

## Vanished players

With `recording.hide-vanished` on (the default), players in vanish are never in a recording: nobody sees
staff in a killcam, not even the staff member's victim. ExyliaLib reads vanish from the `vanished`
metadata most vanish plugins set (SuperVanish and PremiumVanish among them), and from EssentialsX and CMI.

A vanished player who is killed gets no killcam themselves: there are no frames of them to show.

## Disabled worlds

`deaths.disabled-worlds` lists worlds, by name and in any case, where nobody gets a killcam. A death there
is not kept, so `/killcam` cannot show it either.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaKillCam/config.yml"
deaths:
  disabled-worlds:
    - lobby
    - minigames
```

## ExyliaKillEffect

With [ExyliaKillEffect](/docs/exyliakilleffect/introduction) 1.9.0 or newer installed and
`kill-effects.replay` on, the killcam ends on the killer's kill effect, drawn again at the body:

- only the effect that really played on this victim, within 15 seconds of the death, is shown — a kill
  without an effect shows none;
- it is drawn for the viewer alone; nobody else on the server sees it;
- the viewer's own particle choice in ExyliaKillEffect is respected: on `NONE` or `SELF_ONLY` (only their
  own kills) they see nothing;
- older versions of ExyliaKillEffect are skipped without a word, and the killcam ends on the body.

## PacketEvents

Every body, arrow and swing in a killcam is a packet, and the skip button is drawn by packets too. Without
PacketEvents the plugin still starts and still keeps deaths, but never shows a killcam: `/killcam` says
*"Killcams cannot be shown on this server. PacketEvents is missing."*

## Folia

ExyliaKillCam runs on Folia (`folia-supported: true`). Every step runs on the thread that owns the player
or the place: the respawn, the shot, the cut and the kill effect at the body. ExyliaLib's black box and
replays work per region there.
