At the end of this page the killcam is as long, as slow and as close as you want.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Run `/killcamadmin settings` and click **Playback**. Or open `plugins/ExyliaKillCam/config.yml`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the values">
    Click the setting you want: **Seconds before**, **Slow motion**, **Slow speed**, **First person**, **Hold on body**, **Narrate hits**. Each one explains itself in its lore. The `killcam` keys below do the same from the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Edits made in the file need `/killcamadmin reload`. Then die once to see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each setting does

| Menu button | `config.yml` key | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seconds before | `killcam.seconds-before` | `6` | Seconds of the fight shown before the death. At least 1 (menu: 1 to 60). |
| Slow motion | `killcam.slow-motion-seconds` | `1.0` | How long the last moment plays slowly. `0` means never (menu: 0 to 10). |
| Slow speed | `killcam.slow-motion-speed` | `0.35` | Playback speed in slow motion: `0.5` is half speed (menu: 0.1 to 1). |
| First person | `killcam.first-person` | `true` | Through the killer's eyes. `false` watches from behind the victim. |
| Hold on body | `killcam.hold-seconds` | `3` | Seconds the camera stays on the body after the death, for the fall and the kill effect. At least 1. |
| Narrate hits | `killcam.narrate-hits` | `true` | Writes each hit the victim took in their chat as the clip passes it: who, how much, which hit. |

Times can be written as `10s` or `5m` in the file. The killcam always uses the killer's view when the killer is in the recording; otherwise it uses the view from behind the victim.

## Check that it works

Die to a player after a change. You should see the new length and the camera you picked.

## Going further

### Bigger or smaller fights

**Recording radius** (`recording.radius`, default `24`, 8 to 96 blocks) is how far around the victim the killcam shows. Raise it for chases and long-range fights, lower it on busy servers to save memory.

### The kill effect

With ExyliaKillEffect 1.9.0 or newer installed, **Replay kill effect** (`kill-effects.replay`, default on) plays the killer's effect again at the body, for the viewer alone. See [Compatibility](/docs/exyliakillcam/compatibility#exyliakilleffect).

<Details title="Technical details">
The camera cuts on the death to 3.5 blocks from the body, on the killer's side and slightly above. Slow speed is kept between 0.0625 and 1. The recording keeps `seconds-before` plus three seconds around every player and is served by ExyliaLib, see [Replays](/docs/exylialib/replays). Full list of keys: [Configuration](/docs/exyliakillcam/configuration).
</Details>
