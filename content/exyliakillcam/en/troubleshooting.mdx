Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin stays off

ExyliaLib is missing. The console names the version it needs and where to download it. Install it and restart. See [Install ExyliaKillCam](/docs/exyliakillcam/installation).

## A player died and got no killcam

Check in order:

1. PacketEvents is installed.
2. The player has `exyliakillcam.use`.
3. A player killed them, or a mob with `deaths.mobs` on. Dying alone gives nothing.
4. The world is not in `deaths.disabled-worlds`.
5. No duel, event or queue held them (see [Work alongside duels and events](/docs/exyliakillcam/compatibility)).
6. Their mode is not `OFF`. In `ASK` the killcam is only offered in chat.
7. They respawned within a minute of the death.
8. They had just joined or the server had just started: there was not enough recorded.

## `/killcam` says "You have no recent death to watch."

There is no kept death, or it is older than `killcam.rewatch-minutes` (default 5, or the player's `exyliakillcam.rewatch.<n>`). Only the last death is kept, and it is lost when the player leaves.

## `/killcam` says "You cannot watch a killcam right now."

The player is dead, already watching, or another plugin holds them (a duel, an event, a queue). Try again when they are free.

## `/killcam` says "Killcams cannot be shown on this server."

PacketEvents is missing. Install it next to ExyliaLib.

## The killcam does not show exactly what the player saw

It is recorded on the server, which never knew what the player's screen showed. A player on a bad connection may have seen a hit land that the server decided missed. The killcam shows where everybody really was and which hits really counted.

## The kill effect does not play at the end

It needs ExyliaKillEffect 1.9.0 or newer and `kill-effects.replay` on. Only the effect that really played on that death is shown, and a viewer whose ExyliaKillEffect particles are off sees none.

## My change to config.yml did nothing

Run `/killcamadmin reload`.
