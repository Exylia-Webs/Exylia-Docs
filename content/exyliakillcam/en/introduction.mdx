ExyliaKillCam shows a player how they died. After they respawn they watch the last seconds of the fight again through their killer's eyes, then land back exactly where they were, with a line in chat saying who killed them, with what and from how far. It is free and open source (GNU GPL v3.0), published on [GitHub](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaKillCam/releases).

## What your players see

- A **KILLCAM** title, *Killed by* the killer, and a line above the hotbar with a progress bar and the time.
- The fight replayed from the killer's point of view. The last second plays in slow motion.
- On the killing blow the camera cuts to the body while it falls. With [ExyliaKillEffect](/docs/exyliakilleffect/introduction) the killer's kill effect plays again, for them alone.
- A **SKIP** button on the hotbar to leave at any time.
- A summary in chat: killer, hearts they had left, weapon, distance and a **[▶ WATCH AGAIN]** button.

There is no resource pack and no client mod. The server keeps the last seconds around every player in memory and plays the right ones back to the victim alone. Nothing is built or changed in the world, and the fight goes on for everyone else.

## What you need

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper or Folia 1.21 or newer, Java 21 |
| `ExyliaLib` | Required |
| `PacketEvents` | Required to show a killcam. Without it the plugin starts but never shows one |
| `ExyliaKillEffect` 1.9.0+ | Optional. Plays the killer's kill effect again at the end |

  </Step>
