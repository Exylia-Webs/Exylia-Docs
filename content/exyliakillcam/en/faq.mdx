## Is it free?

Yes. ExyliaKillCam is free and open source, under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Download it from
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaKillCam/releases); the source is in the same
repository. There is no licence key and nothing to buy.

## Why does the killcam not show exactly what I saw?

Because it is recorded on the server, and the server never knew what your screen showed. A client
predicts its own movement and is corrected afterwards, so a player on a bad connection may have watched a
hit land that the server decided had missed — or the other way round. The killcam shows the server's
answer: where everybody really was and which hits really counted. Field of view, view bobbing and the
camera tilt on taking damage were never on the server either, so they are not in a killcam.

That is the right answer for a killcam, even when it is not the popular one. See
[What is not in it](/docs/exylialib/replays#what-is-not-in-it) on ExyliaLib's replays page.

## A player died and got no killcam

Check, in order: that PacketEvents is installed; that they have `exyliakillcam.use`; that a player killed
them (or a mob, with `deaths.mobs` on); that the world is not in `deaths.disabled-worlds`; that no duel,
event or queue was holding them; that their mode is not `OFF` (`ASK` only offers it in chat); and that
they respawned within a minute of the death. A player who had just joined, or a server that just started,
may not have enough recorded yet.

## Does it cost much memory?

The recording is ExyliaLib's black box, one for the whole server: a few seconds of frames around every
player, about `seconds-before` plus three, never written anywhere until a death asks for them. Each death
then keeps one recording, with its ground, per player — only the last one — until
`killcam.rewatch-minutes` has passed (checked every minute) or the player leaves. Lower
`rewatch-minutes` or `recording.radius` on a very busy server.

## What happens when a player quits?

A player who leaves while watching is put back where they were before leaving, by ExyliaLib, so they log in
where they respawned. Their kept death is dropped: `/killcam` after rejoining has nothing to show.

## What happens on a reload?

`/killcam reload` reads the files again and reopens the recording with the new settings. Killcams being
watched carry on, and deaths already kept can still be watched. The recording starts filling again, so a
death in the first seconds after a reload may have a shorter clip, or none.

When the plugin stops — the server shuts down, or the plugin is disabled — everybody watching is put back
where they were first.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes. Every step runs on the thread that owns the player or the place, and ExyliaLib's recording and
stages work per region.

## Does it change the real world?

No. The fight is laid down on ExyliaLib's temporary world, on a plot of its own, and the bodies are
packets only the viewer sees. The place where the fight happened is never touched, and the fight goes on
for everybody else while the victim watches.

## The kill effect did not play at the end

It needs ExyliaKillEffect 1.9.0 or newer and `kill-effects.replay` on. Only the effect that really played
on that death is shown, so a kill without one shows none — and a viewer whose ExyliaKillEffect particles
are on `NONE` or `SELF_ONLY` sees none either.

## Is there an API?

No public API, events or placeholders.
