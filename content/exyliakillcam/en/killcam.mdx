This page explains what happens from the moment somebody dies until they are back in the game. You do not need it to run the plugin; it answers "why did it do that?".

## What is recorded

ExyliaKillCam is always recording. ExyliaLib's **black box** keeps the last seconds around every player in memory (`killcam.seconds-before` plus three, and nothing is written to disk). On a death, the seconds before it and two after are cut out. A capture holds:

- everything within `recording.radius` blocks (24 by default) of where the victim was: whoever and whatever was near them;
- no player in vanish, while `recording.hide-vanished` is on;
- no terrain of its own: the killcam is watched where it happened, on the real ground.

A player the box has no frames of (just joined, or the server just started) gets no killcam, and nothing is said.

At the death the killer's figures are read: their name (a mob's custom name or type), their health, the item in their main hand and the distance between the two.

## After the respawn

What happens next is the player's [mode](/docs/exyliakillcam/modes): `AUTO` starts the killcam, `ASK` offers it in chat, `OFF` does nothing. It happens once, on the respawn that follows the death, if that comes within a minute.

## The shot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Where it happened">
    Once the recording is ready the player is taken to the real place of the death as a ghost nobody sees. The recorded fight is drawn for them alone and whoever is really there now is hidden from them. An open inventory is closed first. The viewer cannot be hurt and flies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The title">
    **KILLCAM**, *Killed by* the killer. The line above the hotbar shows the killer, a progress bar and the time out of the clip's length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Through the killer's eyes">
    With `killcam.first-person` on, the camera is the killer's own view. When it is off, or the killer is not in the recording, the clip is watched from behind the victim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Slow motion">
    The last `killcam.slow-motion-seconds` play at `killcam.slow-motion-speed`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The hits you took">
    With `killcam.narrate-hits` on, each hit the victim took is written in their chat as the clip passes it: who, how many hearts, which hit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The cut">
    On the death the camera cuts to 3.5 blocks from the body, on the killer's side, and holds there while the body falls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The kill effect">
    With ExyliaKillEffect 1.9.0 or newer and `kill-effects.replay` on, the killer's effect plays again at the body, for the viewer alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back">
    After `killcam.hold-seconds` on the body, the viewer is put back exactly as they were, and the summary arrives.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The summary

Sent when a killcam ends, however it ended (at the end, skipped, or taken over by another plugin): who killed them, the hearts the killer had left, the weapon (or *Fists*), the distance in blocks and a **[▶ WATCH AGAIN]** button that runs `/killcam`. The texts are `summary` and `fists` in `messages.yml`; see [Edit the messages and the skip button](/docs/exyliakillcam/messages).

## Watching again

`/killcam` plays the last death again while it is younger than `killcam.rewatch-minutes` (5 by default, or `exyliakillcam.rewatch.<n>` minutes for a rank). Only the last death is kept: a new one replaces it and leaving the server drops it.

## Skipping

Three ways out, all to the same place with the summary: the **Skip** button on the hotbar, `/killcam skip`, or another plugin taking the player over. Skipping while it is still loading cancels it before the player moves.

## Memory

One recording serves the whole server. Each death keeps one small recording per player, the last one, until the rewatch window passes (checked every minute) or the player leaves. On a very busy server lower `killcam.rewatch-minutes` or `recording.radius`.
