Two roots: `/killcam` (alias `/exyliakillcam`) for players and `/killcamadmin` for staff.

## Player commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/killcam` | `exyliakillcam.rewatch` | Plays your last death again, while it is younger than `killcam.rewatch-minutes` (or the player's `exyliakillcam.rewatch.<n>` rank value). See [Watching again](/docs/exyliakillcam/killcam#watching-again). |
| `/killcam skip` | none | Leaves the killcam you are watching, like the **SKIP** button on the hotbar. Does nothing when you are not watching one. |
| `/killcam mode <auto\|ask\|off>` | `exyliakillcam.mode` | What you get after dying. The same choice as ExyliaLib's `/settings`. See [Modes](/docs/exyliakillcam/modes). |

All three are for players only.

## Admin commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/killcamadmin reload` | `exyliakillcam.admin` | Reloads `config.yml`, `messages.yml` and `hotbar.yml`, and opens the recording again with the new settings. Works from the console. |
| `/killcamadmin settings` | `exyliakillcam.admin` | Opens the in-game settings: a hub with a **Deaths** page and a **Playback** page for the values an admin tunes (rewatch window, recording radius, slow motion, hold time, auto respawn, mob deaths, disabled worlds, hit narration and more). Players only. |

The old spellings `/killcam reload`, `/killcam settings` and the same under `/exyliakillcam` keep working as aliases.

`hotbar.yml` is also read again on `/exylialib reload`. What a reload does to killcams in progress and
kept deaths is in the [FAQ](/docs/exyliakillcam/faq#what-happens-on-a-reload).
