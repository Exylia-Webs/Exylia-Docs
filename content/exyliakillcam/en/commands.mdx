Two roots: `/killcam` (alias `/exyliakillcam`) for players and `/killcamadmin` for staff.

<Commands />

The old spellings `/killcam reload`, `/killcam settings` and the same under `/exyliakillcam` still work.

## What reload does

`/killcamadmin reload` reads `config.yml`, `messages.yml` and `hotbar.yml` again and reopens the recording with the new settings. Killcams being watched carry on and deaths already kept can still be watched, but a death in the first seconds after a reload may have a shorter clip. `hotbar.yml` is also read on `/exylialib reload`.

## The settings menu

`/killcamadmin settings` opens a hub with two pages. **Playback** holds Default mode, Seconds before, Slow motion, Slow speed, First person, Hold on body and Narrate hits. **Deaths** holds Mob deaths, Disabled worlds, Auto respawn, Rewatch window, Recording radius, Hide vanished and Replay kill effect. See [Change how the killcam plays](/docs/exyliakillcam/playback).
