One command, `/killcam`, with the alias `/exyliakillcam`.

## Player commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/killcam` | `exyliakillcam.use` | Plays your last death again, while it is younger than `killcam.rewatch-minutes`. See [Watching again](/docs/exyliakillcam/killcam#watching-again). |
| `/killcam skip` | `exyliakillcam.use` | Leaves the killcam you are watching, like the **SKIP** button on the hotbar. Does nothing when you are not watching one. |
| `/killcam mode <auto\|ask\|off>` | `exyliakillcam.use` | What you get after dying. The same choice as ExyliaLib's `/settings`. See [Modes](/docs/exyliakillcam/modes). |

All three are for players only.

## Admin commands

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/killcam reload` | `exyliakillcam.admin` | Reloads `config.yml`, `messages.yml` and `hotbar.yml`, and opens the recording again with the new settings. Works from the console. |

`hotbar.yml` is also read again on `/exylialib reload`. What a reload does to killcams in progress and
kept deaths is in the [FAQ](/docs/exyliakillcam/faq#what-happens-on-a-reload).
