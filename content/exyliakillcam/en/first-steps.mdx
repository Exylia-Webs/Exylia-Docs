At the end of this page you have watched a killcam in game. Nothing needs setting up first.

## Before you start

Install the plugin ([Install ExyliaKillCam](/docs/exyliakillcam/installation)) and get a second player. By default a death to a mob or to yourself gives no killcam.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get killed by a player">
    Let a friend kill you. You respawn on your own, without the death screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch">
    A moment later the title **KILLCAM** appears, *Killed by* your friend. You see the fight through their eyes a few seconds before the kill. The line above the hotbar shows a progress bar and the time, and the hotbar holds one **Skip** button. Your own items are still there underneath.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The death">
    The last second slows down. On the killing blow the camera cuts to your body and stays there while it falls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back">
    You are put back exactly where you respawned. Chat shows who killed you, their health left, their weapon and the distance, with a **[▶ WATCH AGAIN]** button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/killcam` within five minutes of the death: the killcam plays again. Run `/killcam skip`, or click **Skip**, to leave one early.

If nothing happened, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliakillcam/troubleshooting).

## What a fresh server does

| What | Default |
|---|---|
| Who gets a killcam | Everyone, in every world |
| Which deaths | Deaths to a player, arrows included. Not mobs, not yourself |
| What a player gets | `AUTO`: the killcam starts by itself and the death screen is skipped |
| Length | The 6 seconds before the death, the last second at 0.35x speed |
| Shot | Through the killer's eyes, then 3 seconds on the body |
| Watch again | `/killcam`, for 5 minutes |
| Duels, events, queues | Left alone: a player another plugin holds gets no killcam until it lets them go |

## Going further

The three settings most servers change first, in `plugins/ExyliaKillCam/config.yml` (`/killcamadmin reload` applies them):

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaKillCam/config.yml"
killcam:
  default-mode: ASK     # offer it in chat instead of starting it
  seconds-before: 8     # a longer clip
deaths:
  mobs: true            # mob kills get one too
```

Players can also choose for themselves with `/killcam mode auto`, `ask` or `off`.
