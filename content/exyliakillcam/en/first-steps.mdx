## What a fresh server does

Out of the box, with nothing edited:

| What | Default |
|---|---|
| Who gets a killcam | Everybody (`exyliakillcam.use`), in every world |
| Which deaths | Only deaths to a player, arrows included; not to mobs, not to yourself |
| What a player gets | `AUTO`: the killcam starts by itself, and the death screen is skipped |
| How much is shown | The 6 seconds before the death, the last 1 second at 0.35× speed |
| How it is shot | Through the killer's eyes, then 3 seconds on the body |
| Watching again | `/killcam`, for 5 minutes after the death |
| Duels, events, queues | Left alone: a player another plugin holds gets no killcam |

## A first killcam

You need two players: a death to a mob or to yourself gives nothing on a fresh server.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get killed">
    Let a friend kill you. There is no death screen: you respawn on your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch">
    A moment later the screen titles **KILLCAM** — *Killed by* your friend — and you are looking through
    their eyes a few seconds before the kill. The line above the hotbar shows the time and a progress bar,
    and the hotbar holds a single **SKIP** button; your own items are still there, underneath.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The death">
    The last second slows down. On the killing blow the camera cuts to your body, from your killer's side,
    and stays there while it falls. With ExyliaKillEffect, their kill effect plays again, for you alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back">
    You are put back exactly where you respawned, as you were. Chat says who killed you, with what, from
    how far, how many hearts they had left, and offers **[▶ WATCH AGAIN]**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose">
    `/killcam mode ask` gets you a clickable line in chat after each death instead; `/killcam mode off`
    gets you nothing. `/killcam` still shows your last death either way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What most servers change first

All of it is in `plugins/ExyliaKillCam/config.yml`; `/killcam reload` applies an edit.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaKillCam/config.yml"
killcam:
  default-mode: ASK        # offer it in chat instead of starting it
  seconds-before: 8        # a longer clip
  auto-respawn: false      # keep the death screen
deaths:
  mobs: true               # mob kills get one too
  disabled-worlds:
    - lobby
```
