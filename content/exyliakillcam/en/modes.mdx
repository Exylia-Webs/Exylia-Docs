title: "Modes"
description: "AUTO, ASK and OFF: what each player gets after dying, how they choose with /killcam mode or /settings, the server default and the automatic respawn."
---

Every player decides what they get after somebody kills them. The choice is theirs, kept per player by
ExyliaLib's player settings, so it survives restarts and follows them across servers that share
ExyliaLib's database.

## The three modes

| Mode | Name in chat | After the respawn |
|---|---|---|
| `AUTO` | *shown by itself* | The killcam starts on its own. |
| `ASK` | *offered in chat* | A chat line with a clickable **[▶ WATCH KILLCAM]**. |
| `OFF` | *off* | Nothing. |

In every mode, `/killcam` still plays the last death while it is kept (`killcam.rewatch-minutes`).

The names in the middle column are `mode-auto`, `mode-ask` and `mode-off` in `messages.yml`; the same
words label the choice on `/settings`.

## The server default

`killcam.default-mode` in `config.yml` is what a player gets until they choose: `AUTO` by default. A
player who never chose follows it, so changing it and running `/killcam reload` changes it for all of
them at once. A player who chose something else keeps their choice; one who picked the same mode as the
default is stored as following it, and moves with it.

## Choosing

```text
/killcam mode auto
/killcam mode ask
/killcam mode off
```

The answer confirms it: *Killcam after dying » offered in chat*.

The same choice is on ExyliaLib's `/settings` screen, as **Killcam** with a spyglass icon, for players
with `exyliakillcam.use`. That screen exists only when the server turns the command on, in
`plugins/ExyliaLib/config.yml`:

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaLib/config.yml"
settings:
  command:
    enabled: true
```

See the [library's player settings page](/docs/exylialib/player-settings#the-command). Both ways write
the same value: what a player picks on one is what the other shows.

## Automatic respawn

With `killcam.auto-respawn` on (the default), a player whose mode is `AUTO` and whose death got a killcam
skips the death screen: they respawn on their own a tenth of a second after dying, and the killcam follows
without a click. Players in `ASK` or `OFF`, and deaths that get no killcam, keep the death screen as
usual.

Turn it off to keep the death screen for everybody; the killcam then starts when the player clicks
**Respawn**, as long as that is within a minute of the death.
