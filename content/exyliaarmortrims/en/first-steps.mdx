---
title: "First steps"
description: "From a clean server to a player wearing a trim, both ways round."
---

Out of the box the mode is `both`: trim items **and** the menu work.

## Through the menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the command node">
    `exyliaarmortrims.command.armortrim` is held by everybody by default. Without it nothing opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant the halves">
    A pattern and a material — `exyliaarmortrims.pattern.sentry` and
    `exyliaarmortrims.material.netherite` — or the lot with `exyliaarmortrims.pattern.*` and
    `exyliaarmortrims.material.*`. A crate key or the shop works too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    `/armortrim`. The armor is along the top and the patterns are below, each drawn as its smithing
    template. `/armortrim wardrobe` opens [the 3D wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/wardrobe) instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose">
    Left click a piece along the top to engrave that one, or FULL SET for all four. Pick a pattern,
    then a material: the trim goes on. Click the material that is already on to take it off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Through an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give yourself a trim item">
    `/armortrimsadmin give <player> sentry/netherite 1`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop it onto a piece">
    Pick the item up on the cursor and click a piece of armor **in your own inventory**. The armor
    keeps everything it had; it only gains one value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="To take it off">
    `/armortrimsadmin remover <player>` and drop the remover onto the piece. The trim comes off and is
    handed back to you as an item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking it works

| What to look at | What should happen |
|---|---|
| The player with the armor on | The engraving shows, to them and to everyone else |
| The same piece in a chest | The trim and the `armor-lore` lines show |
| The player in creative | They see their armor **without** the trim, while everyone else still sees it |
| Dropping the piece | It lands bare, because the real item never carried the trim |

<Callout type="tip" title="Start by selling a few halves">
A pattern or a material is one node and one crate prize. Hand out a couple of common ones to everybody,
keep the rare ones for the crate and the shop, and save `exyliaarmortrims.pattern.*` with
`exyliaarmortrims.material.*` — all 198 combinations — for the top rank.
</Callout>
