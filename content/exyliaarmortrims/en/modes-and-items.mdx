Decide whether trims belong to the armor (traded, lost on death, sold as items) or to the player (kept through the menu), and learn to hand out trim items and removers.

## Before you start

You only need the plugin installed. The default, `both`, already works for most servers, so change it only if you want one of the two ways on its own.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the mode">
    Open `config.yml` and set `behaviour.mode`:
    - `item`: trims travel on the armor. Trade them, lose them on death, sell them as items. No menu and no per-player choices table.
    - `menu`: trims belong to the player and land on whatever they equip. Trim items do nothing.
    - `both`: the default. An engraved piece is drawn with its own trim, and the player's menu choice fills the pieces that carry none.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/armortrimsadmin reload`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a trim item (item and both modes)">
    Run `/armortrimsadmin give <player> sentry/netherite 1`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply it">
    The player picks the item up on the cursor and clicks a piece of armor in their own inventory. The piece keeps everything it had and gains the trim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take a trim off">
    Run `/armortrimsadmin remover <player>`, and the player drops the remover onto the trimmed piece the same way. The trim comes off and is handed back to them as a trim item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

After applying, the piece shows the trim to the wearer and to everyone around, and the trim item is used up. After the remover, the piece is bare again and a trim item is in the player's inventory. If nothing happens, check the mode: in `menu` mode items do nothing, and in `item` mode `/armortrim` answers that the menu is off.

## Going further

### Keep items or use them up

```yaml title="config.yml"
behaviour:
  mode: both
  consume-remover: true
  consume-trim-item: true
  hide-without-permission: true
```

Set `consume-trim-item: false` and a trim item is reusable. Set `consume-remover: false` and one remover removes any number of trims.

### Expired ranks

With `hide-without-permission` on, the default, a player who no longer owns a trim (by node or crate unlock) stops seeing it, but loses nothing: the value is still on the armor and in their row, and the look returns with the node. Trim items never ask for a permission: holding one is the proof of ownership.

### The sound when a trim goes on or off

```yaml title="config.yml"
effects:
  on-apply:
    sound:
      name: BLOCK_SMITHING_TABLE_USE
      pitch: 1.2
  on-remove:
    sound:
      name: BLOCK_GRINDSTONE_USE
```

They are full ExyliaLib effects: title, action bar, boss bar, sound, particles and fireworks. Both play for the menu and for the items.

<Callout type="note" title="Trim items do nothing else">
A trim item or remover is inert, whatever it is drawn as: an ender pearl cannot be thrown and food cannot be eaten. It can only be dropped onto armor. Both are recognised by their data, never by name or material, so renaming them breaks nothing.
</Callout>

<Details title="Technical details">

**What each mode reads.**

| Mode | Where the drawn trim comes from |
|---|---|
| `item` | Only what a trim item wrote onto the armor. |
| `menu` | Only what the player picked in `/armortrim` or the 3D wardrobe. |
| `both` | The armor's own trim, and the menu's choice for armor that carries none. The armor wins. |

**The trim item.** It is applied by dropping it onto a piece of armor in the player's own inventory.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Armor only | Anything else is ignored, and the click carries on as normal. |
| One at a time | A piece that already carries a trim is refused: take it off with a remover first. |
| Permission | None is asked. |
| Consumption | `consume-trim-item` decides whether it is used up. |

What is written onto the armor is one value, `exyliaarmortrims:trim`, the `pattern/material` pair. Armor and trim items written before 1.5.0 may carry a preset id instead; it is read as the look that preset stood for. The vanilla trim and everything else on the item are left alone.

**The remover.**

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| A free slot | Without a free inventory slot nothing happens and it says so: the armor is not touched until the item fits. |
| Consumption | `consume-remover` decides whether it is used up. |
| Unknown trim | A trim the registry no longer knows is refused rather than turned into nothing. |

Both items are drawn from `trim-item` and `remover-item`: material and glow in `config.yml`, name and lore in `messages.yml`. Items given by command that do not fit in the inventory wait and are handed over on the player's next join.

**The menu always asks.** Whether a player owns a trim, by its node or by a crate unlock, is checked by the menu and the wardrobe; a trim item never checks.
</Details>
