Players use `/armortrim`. Everything else is `/armortrimsadmin` (alias `/armortrimadmin`), which needs `exyliaarmortrims.admin`. Typed on its own it opens the admin hub: catalogue, players, showcases and crate blocks from one screen. The subcommands are shortcuts to the same things.

## Player commands

<Commands group="player" />

With `behaviour.mode: item` the menu is off and `/armortrim` answers with the `menu-disabled` message. The crate works in every mode. A feature denied by its permission answers with `feature-locked`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/permissions).

## Admin commands

A trim is always written `pattern/material`, for example `sentry/netherite`. Tab completion suggests every combination in `trims.yml`. `[piece]` is `helmet`, `chestplate`, `leggings` or `boots`; with no piece the command covers all four.

### Items

<Commands group="items" />

Items that do not fit in the player's inventory wait and are handed over on their next join.

### What somebody wears

<Commands group="players" />

`set` and `clear` also work on a player who is offline. `set` writes the choice **even when the player does not own the trim**, so running it twice leaves the same result. With `hide-without-permission` on, the player will not see the trim until they own it by permission or unlock. If nothing changed, you are told with `nothing-changed`.

### Keys and unlocks

<Commands group="keys" />

All of them work on offline players. `<prize>` is `pattern:<id>` or `material:<id>`; tab completion suggests them. See [The crate](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate).

<Callout type="note" title="Taking back everything a player unlocked">
That is ExyliaLib's player reset: `/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaArmorTrims @account` clears their unlocks and choices. See [Resetting a player](/docs/exylialib/commands#resetting-a-player).
</Callout>

### The crate and showcases

<Commands group="showcase" />

`crate block add` and `showcase add` open a placement wizard: walk to the spot, confirm with the gesture it asks for and cancel to go back. Showcases are covered in [Showcases](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/showcases).

### Reload

`/armortrimsadmin reload` re-reads `config.yml`, `messages.yml` and `trims.yml`, recompiles the menus of the current language, rebuilds every trim's visual, the crate and the showcases, and redraws everybody online, open wardrobes included. A mode changed from `item` to `menu` or `both` brings up the menu right then, and a changed `language` is picked up without a restart. It does not reload ExyliaLib (that is `/exylialib reload`) and does not reopen the database connection.
