A trim is a **pattern** plus a **material**, combined by the player in the menu or the 3D wardrobe.

Both lists live in `trims.yml`, which has two sections: `patterns` and `materials`.

## Patterns and materials

These are the two lists a player combines a trim from. The key is the game's registry key — `sentry`,
`netherite` — and `minecraft:sentry` works just as well: the namespace is trimmed when it is read.

```yaml title="trims.yml"
patterns:
  sentry:
    name: "{letters}Sentry"
    icon: "SENTRY_ARMOR_TRIM_SMITHING_TEMPLATE"
    tier: common
    price: ""

materials:
  netherite:
    name: "{letters_black}Netherite"
    icon: "NETHERITE_INGOT"
    tier: epic
    price: ""
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `name` | What is shown. Takes palette tokens, MiniMessage and gradients. |
| `icon` | The item the entry is drawn with in the menu. `BARRIER` when left empty. |
| `tier` | The crate rarity it falls in on its own: a key of `crate.tiers` in `config.yml`. Empty means `common`. |
| `price` | What it costs in the shop. Empty uses its rarity's price from `shop.tier-prices`. A number is the default currency; `{amount: 50, currency: gems}` names one. |

Each pattern and each material is a crate prize of its own, won or bought separately and combined in
the menu — see [The crate](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate).

<Callout type="note" title="The list is yours, not vanilla's">
Patterns and materials are **listed** on purpose rather than read from the server's registries. A
listed half is one you chose to sell: you can rename it, draw it with another icon, and take it away.
One the file does not list is not offered, and a new Minecraft version cannot add to your shop by
accident.
</Callout>

Eighteen patterns ship — Sentry, Vex, Wild, Coast, Dune, Wayfinder, Raiser, Shaper, Host, Ward,
Silence, Tide, Snout, Rib, Eye, Spire, Flow, Bolt — and eleven materials — Quartz, Iron, Copper, Gold,
Lapis, Diamond, Emerald, Redstone, Amethyst, Netherite, Resin.

Their shipped rarities follow how hard each one is to find in vanilla:

| Rarity | Patterns | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Sentry, Wild, Coast, Dune, Wayfinder, Raiser, Shaper, Host | Quartz, Iron, Copper, Lapis, Redstone |
| Rare | Vex, Ward, Tide, Snout | Gold, Diamond, Emerald, Resin |
| Epic | Rib, Eye, Spire, Flow, Bolt | Amethyst, Netherite |
| Legendary | Silence | — |

## How a trim is stored

A trim has no id of its own: it is stored as `pattern/material` — `sentry/netherite` — on armor, in the
player's row and in every command, placeholder and API call. It is named with the `menu.custom-name`
template from `messages.yml`, which defaults to `%pattern% » %material%`.

It is worn once the player owns **both halves**, each by a node or a crate unlock. Owning a pattern
alone does nothing; owning every pattern and one material gives eighteen trims.

## Coming from presets

Up to 1.4, `trims.yml` also had a `presets:` section: named combinations with an id, a node and a crate
slot of their own. 1.5.0 removed them, and the first start converts what they left behind:

| What | What happens |
|---|---|
| The `presets:` section | Moved out of `trims.yml` into `legacy-presets.yml` in the plugin folder, as `id: pattern/material`, and logged once. |
| A preset id on armor (trim items, item mode) | Read as the look it stood for, so the piece keeps its engraving. |
| A preset id in a player's row | Rewritten to its `pattern/material` the first time the row loads. |
| A crate unlock of a preset | Turned into unlocks of its pattern and its material on the player's next join; the preset unlock is then dropped. |
| `exyliaarmortrims.trim.<preset>` and `exyliaarmortrims.custom` | No longer read. Grant `exyliaarmortrims.pattern.<id>` and `exyliaarmortrims.material.<id>` instead. |
| A preset's `price`, `pieces` and `icon` | Gone: halves are priced by their own `price` or their rarity, and every trim fits every piece. |

A preset id that is in neither `legacy-presets.yml` nor a `pattern/material` form is dropped from the
row rather than left behind.

<Callout type="warning" title="Ranks that sold presets">
A player whose preset came from a **permission**, not a crate, keeps the look on their row but now needs
both halves to wear it. With `hide-without-permission` on, their armor shows bare until the rank grants
the pattern and the material nodes.
</Callout>

## Broken entries

An entry with no id is skipped; one with no `name` uses its id, and one with no `icon` is drawn as a
`BARRIER`. The rest of the file loads regardless.

## Trims from code

Another plugin reads the catalogue and puts trims on armor through the API, always as
`pattern/material`. See [API](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/api).
