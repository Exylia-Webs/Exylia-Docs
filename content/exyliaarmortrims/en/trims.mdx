Choose which patterns and materials your players can combine, rename them, give them icons and put prices on them. A trim is one **pattern** plus one **material**, and both lists live in `trims.yml`.

## Before you start

ExyliaArmorTrims is installed and has started once, so `trims.yml` exists. See [Install ExyliaArmorTrims](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/installation).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open trims.yml">
    It sits in the plugin's folder and has two sections: `patterns` and `materials`. Eighteen patterns and eleven materials ship, which is 198 combinations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove what you do not want to offer">
    Delete the entry. A pattern or material the file does not list is not offered, even if the game has it. A new Minecraft version never adds to your shop by accident.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename it and pick an icon">
    `name` is what players read in the menu. `icon` is the item it is drawn with. Both are covered in the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set its rarity and price">
    `tier` is the crate rarity it falls in. `price` is what it costs in the shop; leave it empty to use its rarity's price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/armortrimsadmin reload`. It re-reads `trims.yml` and redraws everybody online.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An entry looks like this. The key is the game's registry key, `sentry` or `netherite`; `minecraft:sentry` works too.

```yaml title="trims.yml"
patterns:
  sentry:
    name: "{letters}Sentry"
    icon: "SENTRY_ARMOR_TRIM_SMITHING_TEMPLATE"
    tier: common
    price: ""

materials:
  netherite:
    name: "{letters_black}Netherite"
    icon: "NETHERITE_INGOT"
    tier: epic
    price: ""
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `name` | What is shown. Takes palette tokens, MiniMessage and gradients. |
| `icon` | The item the entry is drawn with in the menu. `BARRIER` when left empty. |
| `tier` | The crate rarity it falls in: a key of `crate.tiers` in `config.yml`. Empty means `common`. |
| `price` | What it costs in the shop. Empty uses its rarity's price from `shop.tier-prices`. A number is the default currency; `{amount: 50, currency: gems}` names one. |

## Check that it works

Run `/armortrim` as a player who holds a pattern and a material. The pattern you renamed shows its new name and icon, and the ones you deleted are gone. A locked entry with a price shows it, and clicking it asks to buy. See [Set up the crate](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate#buying-with-currency).

## Going further

### Sell halves, not combinations

Each pattern and each material is a crate prize and a permission of its own: `exyliaarmortrims.pattern.<id>` and `exyliaarmortrims.material.<id>`. A player wears a trim once they own **both** halves. Owning every pattern and one material gives eighteen trims. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/permissions) and [Set up the crate](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate).

### Trims from code

Another plugin reads the catalogue and puts trims on armor through the API, always as `pattern/material`. See [API](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/api).

## Coming from presets

Up to 1.4, `trims.yml` also had a `presets:` section: named combinations with an id, a node and a crate slot of their own. 1.5.0 removed them, and the first start converts what they left behind:

| What | What happens |
|---|---|
| The `presets:` section | Moved out of `trims.yml` into `legacy-presets.yml` in the plugin folder, as `id: pattern/material`, and logged once. |
| A preset id on armor (trim items, item mode) | Read as the look it stood for, so the piece keeps its engraving. |
| A preset id in a player's row | Rewritten to its `pattern/material` the first time the row loads. |
| A crate unlock of a preset | Turned into unlocks of its pattern and its material on the player's next join; the preset unlock is then dropped. |
| `exyliaarmortrims.trim.<preset>` and `exyliaarmortrims.custom` | No longer read. Grant `exyliaarmortrims.pattern.<id>` and `exyliaarmortrims.material.<id>` instead. |
| A preset id in `showcase.only` (`config.yml`) | Rewritten to its `pattern/material` (since 1.5.1), so the showcase does not stand empty. One with no look on record is removed with a console warning naming it. |
| A preset's `price`, `pieces` and `icon` | Gone: halves are priced by their own `price` or their rarity, and every trim fits every piece. |

A preset id that is in neither `legacy-presets.yml` nor a `pattern/material` form is dropped from the row rather than left behind.

<Callout type="warning" title="Ranks that sold presets">
A player whose preset came from a **permission**, not a crate, keeps the look on their row but now needs both halves to wear it. With `hide-without-permission` on, their armor shows bare until the rank grants the pattern and the material nodes.
</Callout>

<Details title="Technical details">

The shipped rarities follow how hard each one is to find in vanilla:

| Rarity | Patterns | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Sentry, Wild, Coast, Dune, Wayfinder, Raiser, Shaper, Host | Quartz, Iron, Copper, Lapis, Redstone |
| Rare | Vex, Ward, Tide, Snout | Gold, Diamond, Emerald, Resin |
| Epic | Rib, Eye, Spire, Flow, Bolt | Amethyst, Netherite |
| Legendary | Silence | none |

**How a trim is stored.** A trim has no id of its own: it is stored as `pattern/material`, such as `sentry/netherite`, on armor, in the player's row and in every command, placeholder and API call. It is named with the `menu.custom-name` template from `messages.yml`, which defaults to `%pattern% » %material%`.

**Broken entries.** An entry with no id is skipped; one with no `name` uses its id, and one with no `icon` is drawn as a `BARRIER`. The rest of the file loads regardless.
</Details>
