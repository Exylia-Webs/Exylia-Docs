Let players win trims instead of being given them. A key is spent, a reel falls, and the pattern or material it lands on is unlocked on the player's account for good, whatever happens to their rank afterwards. You can also sell the same halves for currency.

It works in every mode: keys, the crate screens and crate blocks are there even with `mode: item`. Opening it needs `exyliaarmortrims.crate`, which everybody holds by default.

## Before you start

Your patterns and materials are listed in `trims.yml` with a rarity each. See [Set up patterns and materials](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/trims). A prize is always one half: `pattern:<id>` such as `pattern:sentry`, or `material:<id>` such as `material:netherite`. A combination is never a prize.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give yourself a key">
    Run `/armortrimsadmin keys give <your name> 3`. The keys live on your account: nothing can be dropped, duped or lost to a full inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the crate">
    Run `/armortrim crate`, or use the crate button of the trim menu, or the crate icon of the [3D wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/wardrobe). The first screen asks how many to open at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the reels">
    One reel falls per crate. Each pays out as it stops: the prize is unlocked and a line says what came out and its rarity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the win">
    Click the prize on the reels to open the trim menu, where it is combined with the halves you own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bind a block (optional)">
    Look at a block up to six blocks away and run `/armortrimsadmin crate block add`. Clicking it now opens the crate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

`/armortrimsadmin keys check <your name>` shows fewer keys than before, and the prize you won appears unlocked in the trim menu. If it says you have no keys, the crate answers `crate.no-keys`. If `crate.enabled` is off it says the crate is disabled.

## Going further

### Give keys to players

Account keys are a number, safe to hand over from console or to offline players:

```text
/armortrimsadmin keys give <player> <amount>
/armortrimsadmin keys take <player> <amount>
/armortrimsadmin keys set <player> <amount>
/armortrimsadmin keys check <player>
```

A shop, a vote listener or a reward plugin gives them the same way: `armortrimsadmin keys give %player% 1`. Every player gets `crate.start-keys` keys, none by default, the first time they are seen.

A key can also be an **item**, so it can be sold, put in a kit, dropped or given to a friend:

```text
/armortrimsadmin item key <player> [1-64]
```

Right click a bound crate block holding one and it opens on the spot: one key, one reel, no question screen. Left clicking never spends one. Keys that do not fit in the inventory wait and are handed over on the player's next join.

### Change the rarities

Every pattern and material declares a `tier` in `trims.yml`, and `crate.tiers` in `config.yml` declares what the tiers are. Delete `legendary`, invent `mythic`, rename all four: the key is the id `trims.yml` writes as `tier`. An entry naming a rarity that no longer exists falls back to the first one, and an entry with no `tier` is `common`.

`chance` is a weight, not a percentage. Four rarities weighted `60/25/12/3` behave like `6/2.5/1.2/0.3`, and deleting a rarity re-spreads its odds across the rest.

### Let players open several at once

`crate.max-at-once` (4 by default, seven at most) is how many crates one player opens in one go. Give a rank `exyliaarmortrims.crate.multi.<n>` to raise it; the highest one a player holds wins.

### Decide what a win gives

`crate.reward`: `UNLOCK` unlocks the prize on the account and is the default. `ITEM` hands over a token item instead, and `BOTH` does both. A pattern or a material has no item of its own, so here `ITEM` and `BOTH` behave as `UNLOCK`: a key never buys nothing.

### Duplicates

Landing on something already owned hands keys back, as many as `crate.duplicate-refund` says (1 by default), so a duplicate is a free re-roll rather than a loss.

## Buying with currency

Anything still locked can be bought instead of won. A locked pattern or material with a price shows it in the menu; clicking it asks the player to confirm, charges them and unlocks it exactly as a crate would. The 3D wardrobe sells the same halves through **BUY & SAVE**. Buying needs `exyliaarmortrims.buy`, which everybody holds by default.

```yaml title="config.yml"
shop:
  enabled: true
  tier-prices:
    common: 500
    rare: 1500
    epic: 4000
    legendary: 10000
```

An entry in `trims.yml` can set its own `price`, which wins over its rarity's:

```yaml title="trims.yml"
patterns:
  silence:
    tier: legendary
    price: 25000                          # the default currency
materials:
  netherite:
    tier: epic
    price: {amount: 50, currency: gems}   # a named currency
```

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Currencies | A number is ExyliaLib's default currency; `currency` names another one. The economy is ExyliaLib's, over ExyliaEconomy, Vault or PlayerPoints. |
| Not for sale | Something already owned is simply wearable. An entry with no price, priced in a currency no economy serves, or a player without `exyliaarmortrims.buy`, shows as locked. |
| A typo | A price that cannot be read (zero, negative, text) is no price: the entry is not for sale rather than free. |
| One at a time | One purchase per player at once. A failed unlock gives the money back. |

The `shop.enabled` switch turns it off. The confirmation and every outcome (bought, already owned, not enough money) are ExyliaLib's own lines, under `purchase` in its `messages.yml`. After the purchase the menu reopens.

## The 3D opening

`crate.opening: SPATIAL` plays an opening in front of the player instead of the reels: a crate in the world, a reel slowing onto the prize, the reveal and a preview of it. A pattern is tried on the player as a full set in the first material of the catalogue, a material shows its icon. Sneaking skips to the end and hands the prize over at once. Keys, odds, duplicates and payouts do not change. Its look is `spatial/crate.yml`, which is ExyliaLib's crate scene. A client older than 1.21.4 gets the reels.

## Crate blocks

```text
/armortrimsadmin crate block add      # the block you are looking at, up to six blocks away
/armortrimsadmin crate block remove
/armortrimsadmin crate block list
/armortrimsadmin crate block clear
```

They are written to `config.yml`, not into the world, so a bound block survives a schematic paste, a world edit and a rollback. While it is bound it cannot be broken, blown up or pushed away, and clicking it no longer does what its material does: a crate drawn on a barrel must not open a barrel.

## Hand trims over without a crate

```text
/armortrimsadmin unlocks grant <player> <prize>
/armortrimsadmin unlocks revoke <player> <prize>
```

`<prize>` is `pattern:<id>` or `material:<id>`. It is the same unlock a crate writes, for a reward plugin or a support ticket. It outlives a rank running out and needs no permissions plugin. To take back everything a player unlocked, use ExyliaLib's [player reset](/docs/exylialib/commands#resetting-a-player) with `@account`.

<Callout type="tip" title="Permissions and unlocks are two separate ways in">
A player may use a half they unlocked **or** one their rank grants. Selling every combination as a rank is still two lines, `exyliaarmortrims.pattern.*` and `exyliaarmortrims.material.*`, and it is independent of anything they unboxed.
</Callout>

<Details title="Settings and defaults">

```yaml title="config.yml"
crate:
  enabled: true
  start-keys: 0
  duplicate-refund: 1
  reward: UNLOCK
  blocks: []
  max-at-once: 4
  spin-frames: 34
  stagger-seconds: 2.0
  opening: INVENTORY
  key-item:
    material: TRIPWIRE_HOOK
    glow: true
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `true` | Off, the crate says so instead of opening. |
| `start-keys` | `0` | Keys a player is given the first time they are seen. |
| `duplicate-refund` | `1` | Keys handed back for something already owned. |
| `reward` | `UNLOCK` | `UNLOCK`, `ITEM` or `BOTH`. |
| `blocks` | `[]` | The blocks that open the crate. Bind them in game, not by hand. |
| `max-at-once` | `4` | How many crates one player may open in one go. The screen fits seven. |
| `spin-frames` | `34` | How many faces the first reel runs through before it stops. Capped at 200, about eleven seconds. |
| `stagger-seconds` | `2.0` | How long after one reel stops the next one does. `0` stops them together. |
| `opening` | `INVENTORY` | `INVENTORY`: the reels fall down `menus/crate_open.yml`. `SPATIAL`: the opening plays in the world. |
| `key-item` | above | How the held key is drawn. Its name and lore are in `messages.yml` under `key-item`. The key is recognised by its data, not by name or material. |

`on-spin`, `on-win` and `on-duplicate` are full ExyliaLib effect blocks played as the reels turn and then once per reel as it lands. They ship as one sound each: `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_HAT`, `ENTITY_PLAYER_LEVELUP` and `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BASS`.

**Rarities as shipped** (`crate.tiers`):

| Id | Name | `chance` | `priority` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `common` | Common | 60.0 | 1 |
| `rare` | Rare | 25.0 | 2 |
| `epic` | Epic | 12.0 | 3 |
| `legendary` | Legendary | 3.0 | 4 |

`priority` is where the rarity sits in menus; lower comes first. The shipped catalogue has 29 prizes: 8 common patterns and 5 common materials, 4 and 4 rare, 5 and 2 epic, and 1 legendary pattern (Silence).

**The opening, step by step.**

| Screen | What happens |
|---|---|
| `menus/crate.yml` | Asks how many at once, up to `crate.max-at-once` or the player's `exyliaarmortrims.crate.multi.<n>`, spread the way the reels will be: where you click is where that reel falls. |
| `menus/crate_open.yml` | One reel per crate, each finishing `crate.stagger-seconds` after the one before. A pattern is drawn on a diamond chestplate wearing it, a material as its own icon. What a reel came within one slot of stays on screen. |

Keys are spent and prizes decided before the first frame is drawn. Each reel pays out as it stops, with `crate.on-win` or `crate.on-duplicate` and its own line in chat. Closing the window mid-fall costs nothing.

**Refusals.**

| Refusal | Message |
|---|---|
| No `exyliaarmortrims.crate` | `feature-locked` |
| `crate.enabled` is off | `crate.disabled` |
| Not enough keys | `crate.no-keys`: `%amount%` is the cost, `%keys%` what they hold. |
| Their last opening is still falling | `crate.busy` |
| The catalogue is empty | `crate.empty` |

`crate.won` names what came out (`%reward%`) and its rarity (`%tier%`); `crate.duplicate` shows `%refund%`.
</Details>
