title: "The crate"
description: "Rarities, both kinds of key, the reels that fall, the shop, and the blocks that open the crate."
---

The crate is how trims are won rather than given. A key is spent, a reel falls, and what it lands on
is unlocked on the player's account — owned for good, whatever happens to their rank afterwards. The
crate itself is ExyliaLib's; this plugin hands it the catalogue and reads its settings and lines from
`config.yml` and `messages.yml`.

It works in every mode: keys, the crate screens and crate blocks are there even with `mode: item`.

## What it can land on

Three kinds of prize, every one of them in `trims.yml`:

| Prize | Stored as | What winning it gives |
|---|---|---|
| A preset | Its id — `shogun` | The preset, on every piece it fits. |
| A pattern | `pattern:<id>` — `pattern:sentry` | That pattern on the CUSTOM tab. |
| A material | `material:<id>` — `material:netherite` | That material on the CUSTOM tab. |

A combination built by hand is never a prize; its two halves are. Winning either half opens the CUSTOM
tab even without `exyliaarmortrims.custom`, since a half would be useless otherwise, and a combination
is worn once the player owns both halves, by node or by unlock.

## Rarities

Every preset, pattern and material declares a `tier` in `trims.yml`, and `config.yml` declares what
the tiers are, under `crate.tiers`:

```yaml title="config.yml"
crate:
  tiers:
    common:
      name: "Common"
      color: "{muted}"
      chance: 60.0
      priority: 1
    rare:
      name: "Rare"
      color: "{info}"
      chance: 25.0
      priority: 2
    epic:
      name: "Epic"
      color: "{accent}"
      chance: 12.0
      priority: 3
    legendary:
      name: "Legendary"
      color: "{highlight}"
      chance: 3.0
      priority: 4
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `name` | What menus and messages call it. |
| `color` | The colour its name and its prizes are written in. |
| `chance` | How often a crate lands on it, **relative to the other rarities**. |
| `priority` | Where it sits in menus; lower comes first. |

The shipped catalogue has 47 prizes:

| Rarity | Presets | Patterns | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 7 | 8 | 5 |
| Rare | 6 | 4 | 4 |
| Epic | 3 | 5 | 2 |
| Legendary | 2 — SHOGUN, SILENCE | 1 — Silence | 0 |

Nothing here is hard-coded. Delete `legendary`, invent `mythic`, rename all four: the key is the id
`trims.yml` writes as `tier`, and an entry naming a rarity that no longer exists falls back to the
first one. An entry with no `tier` is `common`.

<Callout type="note" title="chance is a weight, not a percentage">
The shares are worked out from the weights, so four rarities weighted `60/25/12/3` and four weighted
`6/2.5/1.2/0.3` behave identically. Deleting a rarity re-spreads its odds across the rest instead of
leaving a three-percent hole.
</Callout>

## Keys

Two kinds, and the crate takes both.

### On the account

A number on the player's crate row. Nothing can be dropped, duped, or lost to a full inventory:

```text
/armortrimsadmin keys give <player> <amount>
/armortrimsadmin keys take <player> <amount>
/armortrimsadmin keys set <player> <amount>
/armortrimsadmin keys check <player>
```

Safe to run from console and for players who are offline — a shop, a vote listener or a reward plugin
hands keys over the same way:

```text
[console] armortrimsadmin keys give %player% 1
```

Every player is given `crate.start-keys` keys — none by default — the first time they are seen.

### In the hand

An item, so it can be sold, put in a kit, dropped and given to a friend:

```text
/armortrimsadmin item key <player> [1-64]
```

Right click a bound crate block holding one and it opens on the spot: one key, one reel, no question
screen. Left clicking never spends one — that is how a player asks what a block is. How the key is
drawn is `crate.key-item` in `config.yml` (material and glow) and `key-item` in `messages.yml` (name and
lore); it is recognised by what it carries in its data, not by its name or material. Keys that do not
fit in the inventory wait and are handed over on the player's next join, with `rewards-claimed`.

## Opening

`/armortrim crate`, the **TRIM CRATE** button in the corner of the trim menu, or a bound block clicked
without a key in hand. The first screen asks only one thing: how many at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="How many">
    `menus/crate.yml`. Choices are drawn up to `crate.max-at-once`, capped at seven by the width of
    the screen, and spread the way the reels will be — where you click is where that reel falls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The reels fall">
    `menus/crate_open.yml`. One reel per crate, each running the full height of the chest, each with
    its own finishing line `crate.stagger-seconds` after the one before it. A preset or a pattern is
    drawn on a diamond chestplate wearing it, a material as its own icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What went past stays">
    Nothing is cleared when a reel stops. What it came within one slot of stays on screen, because
    half of what a crate is worth is the legendary that went past the line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paid out as each reel lands">
    Keys are spent and prizes decided before the first frame is drawn. Each reel pays out as it
    stops, with `crate.on-win` or `crate.on-duplicate` and its own line in chat. Closing the window
    mid-fall costs nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the win">
    Clicking a prize on the reels uses it: a preset is put on every piece the player may wear it on
    and the trim menu opens; a pattern or a material opens the CUSTOM tab, where it is combined.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Refusal | Message |
|---|---|
| `crate.enabled` is off | `crate.disabled` |
| Not enough keys | `crate.no-keys` — `%amount%` is the cost, `%keys%` what they hold. |
| Their last opening is still falling | `crate.busy` |
| The catalogue is empty | `crate.empty` |

## What comes out

`crate.reward` decides:

| Value | What a crate hands over |
|---|---|
| `UNLOCK` | The prize is unlocked on the account. It cannot be traded. The default. |
| `ITEM` | Only the preset's trim item. Nothing is unlocked, so no opening is ever a duplicate. |
| `BOTH` | Unlocked, and handed over as a trim item as well. |

A pattern or a material has no item of its own: under `ITEM` it is unlocked instead, so a key never
buys nothing. `crate.won` names what came out (`%reward%`) and its rarity (`%tier%`).

## Duplicates

Landing on something the player already owns hands keys back, as many as `crate.duplicate-refund`
says, with `crate.duplicate` (`%refund%` is how many). At the default of `1` a duplicate is a free
re-roll rather than a loss.

## Buying with currency

Anything still locked can be bought instead of won. A locked preset, pattern or material with a price
is drawn by the menu's `*_purchasable_template`, with its price; clicking it asks the player to
confirm, charges them and unlocks it exactly as a crate would.

```yaml title="config.yml"
shop:
  enabled: true
  tier-prices:
    common: 500
    rare: 1500
    epic: 4000
    legendary: 10000
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `true` | Whether locked entries can be bought from the trim menu. |
| `tier-prices` | above | The price of each rarity, for an entry that sets none of its own. A rarity left out is not for sale. |

An entry in `trims.yml` can set its own `price`, which wins over its rarity's:

```yaml title="trims.yml"
presets:
  shogun:
    tier: legendary
    price: 25000                          # the default currency
  silence:
    tier: legendary
    price: {amount: 50, currency: gems}   # a named currency
```

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Currencies | A number is ExyliaLib's default currency; `currency` names another one. The economy is ExyliaLib's, over ExyliaEconomy, Vault or PlayerPoints. |
| Not for sale | Something already owned, by node or by unlock, is simply wearable. An entry with no price, or priced in a currency no economy serves, is drawn by `*_locked_template` as before. |
| A typo | A price that cannot be read — zero, negative, text — is no price: the entry is not for sale rather than free. |
| One at a time | One purchase per player at once. A failed unlock gives the money back. |

The confirmation and every outcome — bought, already owned, not enough money — are ExyliaLib's own
lines, under `purchase` in its `messages.yml`. After the purchase the menu reopens on the tab it was
bought from.

## Settings

```yaml title="config.yml"
crate:
  enabled: true
  start-keys: 0
  duplicate-refund: 1
  reward: UNLOCK
  blocks: []
  max-at-once: 4
  spin-frames: 34
  stagger-seconds: 2.0
  key-item:
    material: TRIPWIRE_HOOK
    glow: true
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `enabled` | `true` | Off, the crate says so instead of opening. |
| `start-keys` | `0` | Keys a player is given the first time they are seen. |
| `duplicate-refund` | `1` | Keys handed back for something already owned. |
| `reward` | `UNLOCK` | `UNLOCK`, `ITEM` or `BOTH` — see above. |
| `blocks` | `[]` | The blocks that open the crate. Bind them in game, not by hand. |
| `max-at-once` | `4` | How many crates one player may open in one go. The screen fits seven. |
| `spin-frames` | `34` | How many faces the first reel runs through before it stops. Capped at 200, about eleven seconds. |
| `stagger-seconds` | `2.0` | How long after one reel stops the next one does. `0` stops them together. |
| `key-item` | above | How the key that can be held is drawn. Its name and lore are in `messages.yml`. |

`on-spin`, `on-win` and `on-duplicate` are full ExyliaLib effect blocks — title, action bar, boss bar,
sound, particle and firework — played as the reels turn, and then once per reel as it lands. They ship
as one sound each: `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_HAT`, `ENTITY_PLAYER_LEVELUP` and `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BASS`.

## Crate blocks

A physical block, anywhere in the world, that opens the crate when it is clicked:

```text
/armortrimsadmin crate block add      # the block you are looking at, up to six blocks away
/armortrimsadmin crate block remove
/armortrimsadmin crate block list
/armortrimsadmin crate block clear
```

They are written to `config.yml`, not into the world, so a bound block survives a schematic paste, a
world edit and a rollback. While it is bound it cannot be broken, blown up or pushed away, and clicking
it no longer does what its material does — a crate drawn on a barrel must not open a barrel.

## Handing trims over without a crate

```text
/armortrimsadmin unlocks grant <player> <prize>
/armortrimsadmin unlocks revoke <player> <prize>
/armortrimsadmin unlocks clear <player>
```

`<prize>` is a preset id, `pattern:<id>` or `material:<id>`. The same unlock a crate writes, for a
reward plugin, a support ticket or a wiped season. It outlives a rank running out and needs no
permissions plugin.

<Callout type="tip" title="Permissions and unlocks are two separate ways in">
A player may use a trim they unlocked **or** one their rank grants. Selling every preset as a rank is
still one line — `exyliaarmortrims.trim.*` — and it is independent of anything they unboxed.
</Callout>
