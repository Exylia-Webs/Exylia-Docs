## Installing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop the jars in">
    `ExyliaArmorTrims.jar` and `packetevents` in `plugins/`. `ExyliaLib` is a soft depend: if it is
    missing, the loader installs it on the first start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the server">
    It writes `config.yml`, `trims.yml` and the language files under `lang/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the mode">
    `behaviour.mode` in `config.yml`: `item`, `menu` or `both`. See
    [Modes and items](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/modes-and-items).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant the permissions">
    Players can open the menu, the wardrobe, the crate and the shop by default; patterns and materials
    are locked until a node, an unlock or a purchase hands them over. See
    [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/permissions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `config.yml` | The language, the mode, how the items are drawn, effects, showcases, the crate and the shop. |
| `trims.yml` | The patterns and materials on offer, and each one's rarity and price. |
| `lang/<language>/messages.yml` | Every line the plugin sends, the items' names and lore, and the menu wording. |
| `lang/<language>/menus/trims.yml` | The classic trim screen. |
| `lang/<language>/menus/crate.yml` · `crate_open.yml` | The crate's two screens. |
| `spatial/wardrobe.yml` | Where each piece of [the 3D wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/wardrobe) stands. Its words are in `messages.yml`. |
| `spatial/spots.yml` | The wardrobes placed in the world with `/armortrimsadmin wardrobe place`. |
| `legacy-presets.yml` | Written once when a server coming from 1.4 or older starts: the presets `trims.yml` had, as `pattern/material`. Data, not configuration — do not edit it. |
| `database.yml` | Where the rows below are stored. H2 by default. |

`<language>` is `language` in `config.yml`, or ExyliaLib's own when that is `default`. English,
Spanish, Portuguese, French, Russian and Turkish ship (`en`, `es`, `pt`, `fr`, `ru`, `tr`); any other code starts from
the English files.

<Callout type="note" title="Coming from 1.1 or older">
The first start sets `language: custom` and moves `menus/trims.yml` to `lang/custom/`, and the strings
that lived in `config.yml` — messages, item names and lore, armor lore, menu wording and the
placeholder fallback — move to `lang/custom/messages.yml`, so nothing you edited is lost. Set
`language` to `default` to use the shipped translations instead.
</Callout>

## The database

Opened in every mode: crate keys and unlocks live in ExyliaLib's `exylia_crate_players` table, and
items that did not fit in an inventory wait in `exylia_pending_rewards` until the player's next join.

With `menu` or `both`, choices also live in the `armortrim_selection` table, one row per player and one
column per piece:

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `uuid` | The player. |
| `helmet`, `chestplate`, `leggings`, `boots` | A `pattern/material` pair — `sentry/netherite`. |

Four columns rather than a JSON blob on purpose: they can be read, queried and exported. A row written
while presets existed still holds a preset's bare id; it is rewritten to the look that preset stood
for the first time the row loads.

<Callout type="note" title="A slot may hold something that no longer exists">
A value the catalogue does not know — a pattern or a material you removed from `trims.yml` — is not
drawn and is **not cleaned up**. It comes back the day the entry does, which is what a typo in a
reload deserves.
</Callout>

## Wipes and player resets

Every table declares what it holds, so ExyliaLib's `/exylialib wipe` and `/exylialib resetplayer`
cover them without a command of this plugin's own:

| Table | Kind |
|---|---|
| `armortrim_selection` | `@account` — a player's choices survive a season. |
| `exylia_crate_players` | `@account` — crate keys and unlocks. |
| `exylia_pending_rewards` | `@progress` — items waiting for a join. |

A plain `resetplayer` clears progress, world and cache only, so a player's trims and unlocks stay; name
`@account` to take them too — `/exylialib resetplayer Steve ExyliaArmorTrims @account`. See
[Commands in ExyliaLib](/docs/exylialib/commands#resetting-a-player).

## Updating

Replace the jar and restart. `/armortrimsadmin reload` re-reads `config.yml`, `messages.yml`,
`trims.yml` and the menus, and redraws everybody online.

<Callout type="warning" title="Coming from 1.4 or older">
The first start of 1.5.0 moves the `presets:` section out of `trims.yml` into `legacy-presets.yml`.
Armor and rows carrying a preset id keep its look, and crate unlocks of a preset become unlocks of its
two halves. Preset **permission nodes** are no longer read: grant the pattern and material nodes
instead. See [Trims](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/trims#coming-from-presets).
</Callout>
