Placeholders need PlaceholderAPI. Each trim placeholder names a **piece** first and then what you want to know: `%exyliaarmortrims_<piece>_<value>%`, where the piece is `helmet`, `chestplate`, `leggings` or `boots`.

## All placeholders

<Placeholders />

The crate placeholders count what the crate and the shop unlocked only, not what a permission grants. See [The crate](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate).

## Example

```text
%exyliaarmortrims_helmet_name%
%exyliaarmortrims_boots_pattern_name%
%exyliaarmortrims_chestplate_has_trim%
```

## What they answer

They answer with what the player **chose in the menu**, not with what they are wearing at that moment. A scoreboard saying "Helmet: Sentry Netherite" keeps saying it while its owner swims without a helmet. The trim engraved on one particular piece of armor is not here; for that, see the [API](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/api).

| Situation | Answer |
|---|---|
| A piece with no choice | `placeholders.none` from `messages.yml`, `None` by default. `has_trim` returns `false`. |
| A mistyped piece or an unknown value | The same fallback. |
| Mode `item` | Always the fallback: in that mode there is no menu choice to ask about. |
