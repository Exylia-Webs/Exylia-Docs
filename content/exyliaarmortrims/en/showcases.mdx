At the end of this page your lobby has a figure in a full diamond set that turns once on the spot, wearing one trim, and then moves on to the next, with the trim's name floating above it. Players see it from up to 24 blocks away by default.

## Before you start

You need the `exyliaarmortrims.admin` permission and at least one pattern and one material in `trims.yml`. See [Set up patterns and materials](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/trims).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the spot">
    Stand where the figure should be, facing the way it should end up facing (toward the crowd).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the showcase">
    Run `/armortrimsadmin showcase add`. A placement wizard opens: confirm with the gesture it asks for, or cancel to go back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more or tidy up">
    `/armortrimsadmin showcase list` shows them all. `/armortrimsadmin showcase remove` removes the one nearest you, up to five blocks away. `/armortrimsadmin showcase clear` says what it would delete; run it again with `confirm` to do it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Walk within 24 blocks of the spot. The figure turns for six seconds wearing one trim, rests, and starts the next. With nobody near, nothing plays. A player who hides cosmetics is neither shown the figure nor used as its body.

## Choose what it shows

The `showcase` block of `config.yml`:

```yaml
showcase:
  locations: []
  pause-seconds: 3.0
  radius: 24.0
  only: []
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `locations` | `[]` | Where showcases stand. Written by `/armortrimsadmin showcase add`. |
| `pause-seconds` | `3.0` | The rest between one turn ending and the next starting. |
| `radius` | `24.0` | How close a player has to be to see it, in blocks. |
| `only` | `[]` | The trims it picks from, as `pattern/material`. Empty picks from every combination the catalogue offers. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Preset ids in only">
A list written before 1.5.0 with preset ids, such as `only: [shogun]`, matches nothing any more and the showcase stays empty. Rewrite it as `pattern/material` values, or empty it.
</Callout>
