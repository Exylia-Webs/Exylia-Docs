ExyliaArmorTrims lets players put any armor pattern with any material on their armor, without a smithing table, a template or a material spent. They choose in a 3D wardrobe or an inventory menu, or drop a trim item onto a piece. Out of the box it works the day you install it.

The armor is never changed. The server keeps the exact same chestplate, with the same name, enchantments and data from other plugins. The trim is added only on the way to the client, so everybody sees it engraved.

## What you will have in ten minutes

A player runs `/armortrim`, picks a pattern and a material they own, and the trim appears on their armor for everyone to see. You decide how they get patterns and materials: a permission per rank, a crate, or the shop. Start with [Your first trim](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/first-steps).

## What you need

- Paper 1.21.1 or newer, or Folia 1.21.4 or newer.
- `packetevents` in `plugins/`. It is required.
- `ExyliaLib`. The loader installs it on the first start if it is missing.
- Optional: `PlaceholderAPI`, and an economy (ExyliaEconomy, or Vault with an economy plugin) if you want the shop.

Players on a client older than 1.21.4 can use everything except the 3D wardrobe and the 3D crate opening, which fall back to inventory screens. Steps are in [Install ExyliaArmorTrims](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/installation).

## How players wear a trim

A trim is a **pattern** plus a **material**, and the player owns each half separately. `trims.yml` ships 18 patterns and 11 materials, 198 combinations. The list is yours: a pattern the file does not list is not offered, and every one you keep can be renamed, given another icon and priced.

| Way in | How it works |
|---|---|
| A permission | One node per pattern and per material. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/permissions). |
| The crate | A key is spent and the pattern or material it lands on is unlocked for good. See [Set up the crate](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate). |
| The shop | A locked half is bought with currency, priced by its rarity or on its own. |

Two places to choose: the **3D wardrobe**, the menu standing in the world around the player ([Place the 3D wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/wardrobe)), and the **classic menu**, one inventory window ([Customise the trim menu](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/menu)). `behaviour.menu-style` decides which one `/armortrim` opens.

Where the trim is kept is the **mode**. In `item` mode the trim is written onto the armor and travels with it through chests, trades and deaths. In `menu` mode it belongs to the player and lands on whatever they equip. `both`, the default, does both. See [Choose how trims are worn](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/modes-and-items).

<Details title="What each viewer sees">

| Viewer | Sees |
|---|---|
| Anyone tracking the wearer | The armor with its trim. |
| The wearer, in their inventory and on their doll | The same. |
| The wearer **in creative** | The real armor, without the trim. A creative client sends its slots back to the server, so it has to be shown the truth. |
| Anyone looking at a trimmed piece in a chest, the hotbar or on the cursor | The real item, with the trim and the `armor-lore` lines added. |
| Anyone looking at an **elytra** | The elytra. It sits in the chest slot but is not armor, and dressing it as a chestplate would cost the wearer their flight. |
| Everyone, once the wearer no longer owns the trim | Bare armor, while `hide-without-permission` is on. The value stays stored and the look returns with the node or the unlock. |

The visual is a copy of the real item with one component added, so material, name, enchantments, attributes, durability and custom model data are untouched. A client older than 1.21.4, or a player without `exyliaarmortrims.wardrobe`, always gets the classic menu.

The database is H2 by default, through ExyliaLib: crate keys and unlocks, items waiting for a join, and the menu's choices.
</Details>

<Callout type="note" title="Presets are gone since 1.5.0">
Earlier versions had named presets (SHOGUN, POLTERGEIST and so on). Custom is now the only system. What players wore keeps its look, and crate unlocks of a preset become unlocks of its two halves. See [Coming from presets](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/trims#coming-from-presets).
</Callout>

Showcases, spots in a lobby that show trims off on their own, are covered in [Add trim showcases](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/showcases).
