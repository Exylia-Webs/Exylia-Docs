---
title: "Introduction"
description: "What ExyliaArmorTrims is: any pattern with any material, chosen in a 3D wardrobe or an inventory menu, or carried as an item, and drawn with packets."
---

ExyliaArmorTrims puts a **trim** on a piece of armor. A trim is a pattern and a material, and it is
drawn on the way to the client: the chestplate is still exactly the same chestplate on the server, and
everybody sees it engraved.

The armor is never converted or rebuilt. The visual is a copy of the real item with **one component
added** — the trim — so material, name, enchantments, attributes, durability, custom model data and
any other plugin's data stay exactly where they were. The item the server holds never carries a trim.

## Two ways to wear a trim

| Way | Whose it is | Which server it suits |
|---|---|---|
| **Item** | The armor's: one value is written onto the item and travels with it through chests, trades and deaths. | Survival, economy |
| **Menu** | The player's: stored per player and per piece, and it lands on whatever they equip. | Practice, PvP |

`behaviour.mode` decides which is used — `item`, `menu` or `both` — and is covered in
[Modes and items](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/modes-and-items).

## A pattern and a material

Every trim is the player's own combination: a pattern, then a material. `trims.yml` ships 18 patterns
and 11 materials — 198 combinations — but the list is **yours**: a pattern the file does not list is
not offered, and every one you keep can be renamed, drawn with any icon and priced on its own.

Each half is owned separately — by a permission, a crate unlock or a purchase — and a trim is worn
once the player owns both. See [Trims](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/trims).

<Callout type="note" title="Presets are gone in 1.5.0">
Earlier versions also had named presets (SHOGUN, POLTERGEIST…). They were removed: custom is the only
system. What players wore keeps its look, and crate unlocks of a preset become unlocks of its two
halves — see [Trims](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/trims#coming-from-presets).
</Callout>

## Two screens to choose on

| Screen | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The 3D wardrobe** | The trim menu standing in the world: the player in the middle wearing what they try on, the options around them, buy and save in place. See [The 3D wardrobe](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/wardrobe). |
| **The classic menu** | One inventory window: the armor along the top, the patterns and then the materials below. See [The menu](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/menu). |

`behaviour.menu-style` decides which one `/armortrim` opens. A client older than 1.21.4, or a player
without the wardrobe's permission, always gets the classic menu.

## What the client sees

| Viewer | Sees |
|---|---|
| Anyone tracking the wearer | The armor with its trim |
| The wearer, in their inventory and on their doll | The same |
| The wearer **in creative** | The real armor — a creative client sends its slots back to the server, so it has to be shown the truth |
| Anyone looking at a trimmed piece in a chest, the hotbar or on the cursor | The real item, with the trim and the `armor-lore` lines added |
| Anyone looking at an **elytra** | The elytra. It sits in the chest slot but is not armor, and dressing it as a chestplate would cost the wearer their flight |
| Everyone, once the wearer no longer owns the trim | Bare armor, while `hide-without-permission` is on. The value stays stored and the look returns with the node or the unlock |

## What it costs the player

Nothing in game: no smithing table, no template, no material spent. What limits it is what the player
owns, and there are three ways in:

| Way | How |
|---|---|
| A permission | A node per pattern and per material — see [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/permissions). |
| The crate | A key spent, a reel falls, and the pattern or material it lands on is unlocked for good — see [The crate](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate). |
| The shop | Any half still locked bought from the menu or the wardrobe with currency, priced by its rarity or on its own. |

And, if you sell trim items, whatever getting one costs.

## Showcases

Spots in a lobby that show trims off on their own: a body wearing a watcher's skin stands in a diamond
set wearing one combination, turns once on the spot and moves on to the next. See
[Configuration](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/configuration#showcase).

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.1 or newer, or Folia 1.21.4 or newer. |
| Client | Any version the server accepts. The 3D wardrobe and the 3D crate opening need 1.21.4 or newer; older clients get the inventory screens. |
| Required | `packetevents` |
| Recommended | `ExyliaLib` (a soft depend: the loader installs it) |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI`; an economy for the shop — ExyliaEconomy, or Vault with an economy plugin |
| Database | H2 by default, through ExyliaLib: crate keys and unlocks, items waiting for a join, and the menu's choices |
