The 3D wardrobe is the trim menu drawn in the world, in front of the player. They stand in the middle wearing what they are trying on, the options sit in an arc around them, and trying, buying and saving all happen without an inventory window. This page makes `/armortrim` open it, and places wardrobes at fixed spots such as a lobby.

It writes the same choices the [classic menu](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/menu) writes and follows the same ownership, shop and price rules.

## Before you start

- `behaviour.mode` must be `menu` or `both`. With `item` nothing opens.
- Players need a client on 1.21.4 or newer, and `exyliaarmortrims.wardrobe`, which everybody holds by default. Anyone else gets the classic menu.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make /armortrim open it">
    In `config.yml` set `behaviour.menu-style: spatial`, then run `/armortrimsadmin reload`. `/armortrim wardrobe` opens the wardrobe whatever this says.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place one in the lobby (optional)">
    Stand where players should stand, facing the way they should face, and run `/armortrimsadmin wardrobe place`. A small clickable hologram appears in front of you. Clicking it walks the player onto the spot and opens the wardrobe there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it on">
    Open it. Look at an option and click to try it on: the body wears it at once. Pick a **Pattern**, then a **Material**. The piece selector chooses Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots or **All**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Nothing is kept until **SAVE**. When the draft holds halves the player does not own yet, the button reads **BUY & SAVE** with their total: the first click asks, **CONFIRM** pays, unlocks them and saves. A half that is not for sale makes it **LOCKED** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/armortrim wardrobe`. You should see yourself on a pedestal with the options in an arc around you. Click a pattern and a material and your body wears the trim. Click SAVE, leave with the **✕ Leave** icon or by sneaking, and your armor shows the trim in the world. Leaving with changes unsaved discards them and says so in chat.

Buying needs `exyliaarmortrims.buy` and the [shop](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate#buying-with-currency). Without either, a half the player does not own can be tried on but not saved.

## Going further

### Make the wardrobe a VIP perk

Players hold `exyliaarmortrims.wardrobe` by default. Deny it to the default group and grant it to the rank in your permissions plugin: those without it get the classic menu from `/armortrim`, and `feature-locked` from `/armortrim wardrobe` and from a placed wardrobe.

### Show the guided tour again

The first time a player opens the wardrobe, a short tour points at the arc, the preview, SAVE and the icons. The **? Help** icon shows it again, and an admin can make it show on a player's next open:

```text
/armortrimsadmin wardrobe tour <player>
```

### Remove a placed wardrobe

```text
/armortrimsadmin wardrobe remove    # the one nearest you, up to four blocks away
```

Spots are written to `spatial/spots.yml`.

## Layout and words

Where every piece stands is `spatial/wardrobe.yml`: positions on a board in front of the player, in blocks, plus the arc, the preview and the tour. Rather than editing numbers, move the pieces in game:

```text
/armortrimsadmin wardrobe edit
```

It opens the wardrobe with nothing clickable but its pieces; click one, then the mouse wheel moves it and the change is saved to the file.

Every word the wardrobe draws is in `lang/<language>/messages.yml` under `wardrobe`, and the centre stage's own words under `wardrobe.stage`: steps, card lines, hints, the SAVE labels, the icons, the armor names and the tour.

<Details title="Technical details">

**Who gets what.**

| Who | What opens |
|---|---|
| A client older than 1.21.4 | The classic menu: it cannot draw the wardrobe. |
| A player without `exyliaarmortrims.wardrobe` | The classic menu from `/armortrim`; `feature-locked` from `/armortrim wardrobe` and from a placed wardrobe. |
| Anybody with `mode: item` | Nothing: `menu-disabled`. |

**What is on it.**

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| The steps | **Pattern**, then **Material**. The arc shows the open step's options; the mouse wheel steps through them. |
| The card | The option looked at, or the one the piece is trying: its name, and one line: worn, owned, its price, or locked. |
| The piece selector | Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots or **All**. |
| The armor selector | Your own armor, or Leather, Copper (1.21.9 servers), Chainmail, Iron, Golden, Diamond or Netherite. Only the preview changes; nothing is saved. |
| The arc | Four options either side of the body, with arrows and a page counter. Locked ones carry a lock, the worn one a tick. |
| The preview | The player, wearing what is being tried on, on a pedestal. Clicking the body, or the arrows beside it, turns it to the side and the back. |
| SAVE | The one primary button, with a pill beside it while something waits to be saved. |

**The icons.** Each shows only while it can do something, and its words show under the one looked at.

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Clear this piece | Drafts the piece being edited bare. |
| ↺ REVERT | Drops every change and moves the pattern and material markers back to what the piece wears. |
| ✘ UNEQUIP | Takes every trim off at once. |
| CRATE | Opens [the crate](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate), with the keys the player holds. Needs `exyliaarmortrims.crate`. |
| ? Help | Shows the guided tour again. |
| ✕ Leave | Closes the wardrobe. Sneaking does the same. |

**Opening ways.**

| Way | Detail |
|---|---|
| `/armortrim wardrobe` | Always the wardrobe, whatever `menu-style` says. |
| `/armortrim` | The wardrobe when `behaviour.menu-style` is `spatial`; the classic menu when it is `classic`, the default. |
| A placed wardrobe | Clicking its hologram walks the player onto the spot and opens it there. |

</Details>
