title: "The 3D wardrobe"

The 3D wardrobe is the trim menu drawn in the world, in front of the player. They stand in the middle
wearing what they are trying on, the options sit in an arc around them, and everything — trying,
buying, saving — happens without an inventory window.

It writes the same choices the [classic menu](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/menu) writes, follows the same
ownership, shop and price rules, and needs a mode that uses the menu (`menu` or `both`).

## Opening it

| Way | Detail |
|---|---|
| `/armortrim wardrobe` | Always the wardrobe, whatever `menu-style` says. |
| `/armortrim` | The wardrobe when `behaviour.menu-style` is `spatial`; the classic menu when it is `classic`, the default. |
| A placed wardrobe | Clicking its hologram walks the player onto the spot and opens it there. See [Placed wardrobes](#placed-wardrobes). |

```yaml title="config.yml"
behaviour:
  menu-style: spatial   # classic or spatial
```

| Who | What opens |
|---|---|
| A client older than 1.21.4 | The classic menu: it cannot draw the wardrobe. |
| A player without `exyliaarmortrims.wardrobe` | The classic menu from `/armortrim`; `feature-locked` from `/armortrim wardrobe`. |
| Anybody with `mode: item` | Nothing: `menu-disabled`. |

## What is on it

From top to bottom:

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| The steps | **Pattern**, then **Material**. The arc shows the open step's options; the mouse wheel steps through them. |
| The card | The option looked at, or the one the piece is trying: its name, and one line — worn, owned, its price, or locked. |
| The piece selector | Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots or **All**, which trims every piece at once. |
| The armor selector | Your own armor, or Leather, Copper (1.21.9 servers), Chainmail, Iron, Golden, Diamond or Netherite. Only the preview changes; nothing is saved. |
| The arc | Four options either side of the body, with arrows and a page counter. Locked ones carry a 🔒, the worn one a ✔. |
| The preview | The player, wearing what is being tried on, on a pedestal. Clicking the body, or the arrows beside it, turns it to the side and the back. |
| The hint | What a click would do right now. |
| SAVE | The one primary button, with a pill beside it while something waits to be saved. |
| The icons | Clear this piece, revert, take every trim off, open the crate, help and leave. Each shows only while it can do something, and its words show under the one looked at. |

## Trying on and saving

Choosing a pattern or a material only **tries it on**: the body wears it at once, owned or not. Nothing
is kept until SAVE.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the piece">
    One piece, or All.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a pattern, then a material">
    Locked options can be tried on too: the card says how they are had.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    SAVE writes every drafted piece. When the draft holds halves the player does not own yet, the button
    reads **BUY & SAVE** with their total: the first click asks, **CONFIRM** pays, unlocks them and
    saves. A half that is not for sale makes it **LOCKED** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Clear this piece | Drafts the piece being edited bare. |
| Revert | Drops every change and moves the pattern and material markers back to what the piece wears. |
| Unequip | Takes every trim off at once. |
| Crate | Opens [the crate](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate), with the keys the player holds. Needs `exyliaarmortrims.crate`. |
| Help | Shows the guided tour again. |
| Leave | Closes the wardrobe. Sneaking does the same. |

Leaving with changes unsaved discards them, and says so in chat. Buying needs `exyliaarmortrims.buy`
and the [shop](/docs/exyliaarmortrims/crate#buying-with-currency); without either, a half the player
does not own can be tried on but not saved.

## The guided tour

The first time a player opens the wardrobe, a short tour points at the arc, the preview, SAVE and the
icons. The help icon shows it again, and an admin can make it show on a player's next open:

```text
/armortrimsadmin wardrobe tour <player>
```

## Placed wardrobes

A wardrobe can stand at a fixed spot — a lobby, a shop — with a small clickable hologram in front of
it. Clicking the hologram walks the player onto the spot and opens the wardrobe there, facing the way
it was placed.

```text
/armortrimsadmin wardrobe place     # where you stand, facing the way you face
/armortrimsadmin wardrobe remove    # the one nearest you, up to four blocks away
```

Spots are written to `spatial/spots.yml`.

## Layout and words

Where every piece stands is `spatial/wardrobe.yml`: positions on a board in front of the player, in
blocks, plus the arc, the preview and the tour. Rather than editing numbers, move the pieces in game:

```text
/armortrimsadmin wardrobe edit
```

It opens the wardrobe with nothing clickable but its pieces; click one, then the mouse wheel moves it
and the change is saved to the file.

Every word the wardrobe draws is in `lang/<language>/messages.yml` under `wardrobe`, and the centre
stage's own words under `wardrobe.stage`: steps, card lines, hints, the SAVE labels, the icons, the
armor names and the tour.
