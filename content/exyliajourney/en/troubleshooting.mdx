Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

The console names what is missing. ExyliaJourney needs ExyliaLib 1.593.0 or newer; the loader installs it and asks for a restart. See [Installation](/docs/exyliajourney/installation).

## A journey does not start for players

Check these, in order:

1. The journey has a trigger. A journey made in the editor has none: click **Start & access**, then **Add trigger**.
2. It is switched on (`enabled: true`). `journeys/example.yml` ships switched off.
3. The player is not blocked by its `requirements`, a cooldown, an earlier completion on a journey that is not repeatable, or another active journey in the same group.
4. Set `debug: true` in `config.yml` and reload. The console says why each start was refused.

To start one by hand: `/journeyadmin start <player> <journey>`. See [Journeys](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys#triggers).

## FIRST_JOIN did not start for me

`FIRST_JOIN` starts only for a player who has never played on the server. Players who joined before ExyliaJourney was installed never get it; give them a `JOIN` or `PERMISSION` trigger instead. To test, use **Test run**, `/journeyadmin test <journey>`, or a fresh account.

## I see no floating text, beacon, glow or NPC

These need `packetevents`. Without it players still get every journey, with the step in chat, an action bar line, particles and footprints. Install packetevents and restart. See [Installation](/docs/exyliajourney/installation#packetevents) and the [FAQ](/docs/exyliajourney/faq).

## A step never completes

- The objective filter is too narrow (a block, item or mob that does not match). Open the step, **Objective**, and check **Filter**.
- Blocks a player placed do not count for `BREAK_BLOCK` filters, items another player dropped do not count for pickups, and mobs from spawners or spawn eggs do not count for kills.
- A `PLACEHOLDER` step needs a placeholder that resolves to a number. Check it with PlaceholderAPI's parse command.
- To move a stuck player on, `/journeyadmin trigger <player> <key>` fires a custom key, or `/journey skip` lets them skip a skippable step.

## A player got no rewards

A run where any step was skipped gives no journey completion rewards, so skipping cannot be used to collect them. `/journeyadmin complete <player> <journey>` pays them in full.

## A player lost a journey

A step's `timeout` fails the step and stops the whole journey. Its triggers can start it again from the first step. Remove the time limit, or make sure the journey has a trigger.

## A player skipped onboarding by mistake

A skipped journey never starts again from a trigger. `/journeyadmin reset <player> <journey>` clears it, or `/journeyadmin start <player> <journey>` starts it anyway.

## A dialogue did not open

Another dialogue or a cinematic may be open, the trigger may be cooling down or already used, or the player may not meet the file's `require`. `/journeyadmin dialogue list` shows the loaded ones. See [Dialogues](/docs/exyliajourney/dialogues#triggers).
