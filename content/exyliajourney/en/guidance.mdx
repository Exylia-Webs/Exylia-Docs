By the end of this page a step shows the player where to go: a beam of light over the place, footprints along
a path, text with the distance, and a glow on the NPC to talk to. Every piece is drawn for that player alone (a
hundred new players crossing the same square each see only their own beacon) and each one can be switched off
per step and per player.

## Before you start

You need a journey with a step: see [Add steps and objectives](/docs/exyliajourney/steps). Without a
target (a place or an NPC) there is nothing to point at; a step with no target still shows its text and
trail.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step's guidance">
    Run `/journeyadmin`, click your journey, **Steps**, the step, then **Guidance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point at a place">
    Click **Target** and stand where the beacon should be. A step that asks to reach a place or talk to an NPC
    already has a target, so you can skip this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what shows">
    Click **Beacon**, **Waypoint** and **Glow** to switch each on or off. **Text panel**, **Panel lines** and
    **Panel place** change the text shown for the step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a trail">
    Click **Trail** and choose a recorded route. No route yet? Record one first, see
    [Recording a route](#recording-a-route).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play a cinematic when the step starts">
    Click **Cinematic** and pick one. The step does not wait for it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Click **Test from here** on the step (or **Preview as a player**). You should see the beam over the target
with its distance, the step text, and footprints leading to it. Walk away and the way-back markers point to
the route; stand idle for 45 seconds and the beacon pulses with a hint.

## Going further

### The target

The beacon, the waypoint, the glow and the distance on the panel all point at the step's **target**:

1. `guidance.target`, when set;
2. otherwise the objective's `location` (a `REACH`, or any objective limited to a place);
3. otherwise, for `INTERACT_NPC`, where that NPC stands, and it follows the NPC if it moves.

A step with none of these has no beacon, waypoint or distance; its text and trail still show.

### Text

The step tracker is a boss bar at the top of the screen with the journey, the step and how far along it is.
Where the step's full text (journey, step number, title, description, the extra `lines`, progress and
distance to the target, or *Head to `<world>`* in another world) shows is the server's choice,
`guide.step-text` in `config.yml`. Change it in game with **Settings**, **Guidance**, **Step text** (the
journeys list's settings hub, see [The editor](/docs/exyliajourney/editor)):

| `guide.step-text` | What it is |
|---|---|
| `PANEL` (default) | A small subtitle riding the player's view near the bottom of the screen, above the hotbar, like a cinematic subtitle. It goes down while a cinematic plays and comes back after it. |
| `TRACKER` | A quest tracker on the left edge of the screen. Players can hide it in `/settings`. |
| `CHAT` | Told in chat once when the step starts. Bedrock players and servers without PacketEvents always get this. |

The text updates in place as progress changes. There is **no panel behind it by default**
(`guide.panel-background-opacity: 0`); raise it to draw one. The text is hidden while a conversation plays.
The same settings page has **Tracker colour** and **Footprints**. Colours follow
[Colours](/docs/exylialib/colours).

A step's text mode (**Text panel** in the editor, `guidance.text.mode` in the file) adds to that:

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| `COMPANION` (default) | Only the tracker and text above. |
| `ANCHORED` | Also a sign standing at a place, always facing the player and growing with distance so it stays readable from across a square. It stands at `anchor` (**Panel place**), or over the target when there is no anchor. |

Sizes, distances, backgrounds and line widths are in
[Configuration](/docs/exyliajourney/configuration#guide); the text's lines are in `messages.yml`
(`guide.panel-*`), see [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

### Beacon

A beam of light over the target, outlined in the step's `color` (a palette token such as `{accent}` or
`#rrggbb`), with a label showing `label`, or the step title when blank, and the distance in metres. It is
drawn at full brightness so it reads at night and through terrain.

Beyond the player's view distance the beam is replaced by a short stand-in a few blocks ahead in the
target's direction (`beacon-marker-distance`, 30), so it reads as a mark on the horizon. Nothing is drawn
while the target is in another world. When a hint is sent, the beam pulses.

### Trail and routes

A trail is a line of small glowing markers on the ground along a recorded **route**, drawn only from the
point nearest the player to `ahead` blocks along it (14 by default, at least 4). Walking costs one marker
appearing ahead and one disappearing behind. A player who strays more than `trail-off-route` blocks (6)
from the route sees a short line of way-back markers pointing back to it.

### Recording a route

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Run `/journeyadmin route record <id>`, or in `/journeyadmin` click **Routes**, then **Record route**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk it">
    A point is recorded every 1.5 blocks and the path is drawn behind you as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop">
    Sneak twice quickly, or run `/journeyadmin route stop`. The path is thinned and saved to
    `routes/<id>.yml`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Generate route** on the same list makes one for you, and a route's screen has **Preview** (draws it for ten
seconds, like `/journeyadmin route preview <id>`), **Record again**, **Extend** and **Reverse**. A route needs
at least two points. `route-simplify` in `config.yml` (0.4 blocks) thins it further before it is drawn.

<Details title="Route and trail files">

```yaml
# routes/spawn-path.yml: a world and a list of points; can be written by hand
world: world
points:
  - 0.5,64.0,0.5
  - 6.2,64.0,3.1
  - 12.0,65.0,4.8
```

```yaml
# on the step
trail:
  route: spawn-path
  ahead: 14
```

</Details>

### Waypoint

The waypoint puts a mark at the target:

- on the vanilla **locator bar**, when the server and the player's client are both 1.21.6 or newer and the
  installed PacketEvents knows the packet;
- as a **minimap waypoint** for players on Lunar or Feather.

It is shown only while the player has the beacon switched on, and is removed while they are in another
world than the target.

### Glow

The glow outlines the thing to act on, for that player alone, in `glow-color`:

- the NPC of an `INTERACT_NPC` step: this plugin's, ExyliaDisplays' or any other plugin's;
- the target block of an `INTERACT_BLOCK` or `BREAK_BLOCK` step that has a place, through terrain.

### Cinematic

`cinematic: <id>` plays that cinematic when the step starts. Unlike a `WATCH_CINEMATIC` objective, the step
does not wait for it. See [Create a cinematic](/docs/exyliajourney/cinematics).

### Hints

A player idle on a step for its `hint-after` (45 s by default) gets the step's `hint` in chat, once per step,
with the beacon pulsing and the hint sound. *Idle* means no progress on that step. A step with no `hint`
of its own sends a line pointing at `/journey`.

### Each player's switches

Players choose what they see from `/journey`, or ExyliaLib's `/settings` under **Journey guidance**:

| Switch | Turns off |
|---|---|
| Tracker | The step text (panel or tracker). |
| Beacon | The beam and the waypoint. |
| Trail | The trail. |
| Journey sounds | The step sound and the hint sound. |

`/journey hide` hides all guidance at once and shows it again. Progress counts either way.

### Without PacketEvents, and on Bedrock

Without PacketEvents, and always for Bedrock players one by one, guidance is drawn with what every client
understands: the step in chat when it starts, one action bar segment with the title, progress and distance, a
particle column over the target (or a particle mark towards it when far) and particle footprints along the
trail. There is no waypoint and no glow. See [Installation](/docs/exyliajourney/installation#packetevents).

<Details title="Guidance in the step file">

```yaml
guidance:
  text:
    enabled: true
    mode: COMPANION          # or ANCHORED (adds a sign at a place)
    lines: ['{muted}It sits in the middle of the square.']
    anchor: world,0,66,0     # where an ANCHORED panel stands
  beacon:
    enabled: true
    color: '{accent}'
    label: Fountain
  trail:
    route: spawn-path
    ahead: 14
  waypoint: true
  glow: true
  cinematic: intro           # played when the step starts
  target: world,0,64,0       # where the beacon, waypoint and distance point
```

A step with no `guidance` section gets the defaults: tracker and text, beacon, waypoint and glow on, no trail,
no cinematic. `text: false` and `beacon: false` switch one piece off; `trail: spawn-path` is the short form
of a trail.

</Details>
