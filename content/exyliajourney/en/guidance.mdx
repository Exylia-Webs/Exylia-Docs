Guidance is everything that shows a player where to go while a step runs. Every piece is drawn for that
player alone — a hundred new players crossing the same square each see only their own beacon — and each one
can be switched off per step and per player.

```yaml
guidance:
  text:
    enabled: true
    mode: COMPANION          # or ANCHORED
    lines: ['{muted}It sits in the middle of the square.']
    anchor: world,0,66,0     # where an ANCHORED panel stands
  beacon:
    enabled: true
    color: '{accent}'
    label: Fountain
  trail:
    route: spawn-path
    ahead: 14
  waypoint: true
  glow: true
  cinematic: intro           # played when the step starts
  target: world,0,64,0       # where the beacon, waypoint and distance point
```

A step with no `guidance` section gets the defaults: companion text, beacon, waypoint and glow on, no trail,
no cinematic. `text: false` and `beacon: false` switch one piece off; `trail: spawn-path` is the short form
of a trail.

## The target

The beacon, the waypoint, the glow and the distance on the panel all point at the step's **target**:

1. `guidance.target`, when set;
2. otherwise the objective's `location` (a `REACH`, or any objective limited to a place);
3. otherwise, for `INTERACT_NPC`, where that NPC stands — and it follows the NPC if it moves.

A step with none of these has no beacon, waypoint or distance; its text and trail still show.

## Text

The panel shows the journey's name and the step number, the step title, its description, the extra
`lines`, the progress (`3/10`) and the distance to the target — or, when the target is in another world,
*Head to `<world>`*. It updates in place as progress changes; nothing is sent to chat.

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| `COMPANION` (default) | A panel floating a little ahead of the eyes and below the line of sight, gliding after the view rather than stuck to it. It steps aside while the player sprint-jumps, rides, flies, glides or is in combat, and comes back after a second of calm. |
| `ANCHORED` | A panel standing at a place, always facing the player, growing with distance so it stays readable from across a square. It stands at `anchor`, or over the target when there is no anchor. |

Size, distance, background, line width and the step-aside speed are in
[Configuration](/docs/exyliajourney/configuration#guide); the panel's lines are in `messages.yml`
(`guide.panel-*`).

## Beacon

A beam of light over the target, outlined in the step's `color` (a palette token such as `{accent}` or
`#rrggbb`), with a label showing `label` — or the step title when blank — and the distance in metres. It is
drawn at full brightness so it reads at night and through terrain.

Beyond the player's view distance the beam is replaced by a short stand-in a few blocks ahead in the
target's direction (`beacon-marker-distance`, 30), so it reads as a mark on the horizon. Nothing is drawn
while the target is in another world. When a hint is sent, the beam pulses.

## Trail and routes

A trail is a line of small glowing markers on the ground along a recorded **route**, drawn only from the
point nearest the player to `ahead` blocks along it (14 by default, at least 4). Walking costs one marker
appearing ahead and one disappearing behind. A player who strays more than `trail-off-route` blocks (6)
from the route sees a short line of way-back markers pointing back to it.

```yaml
trail:
  route: spawn-path
  ahead: 14
```

### Recording a route

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    `/journeyadmin route record <id>`, or **RECORD ROUTE** in the editor's route list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk it">
    A point is recorded every 1.5 blocks and the path is drawn behind you as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop">
    Sneak twice quickly, or `/journeyadmin route stop`. The path is thinned and saved to
    `routes/<id>.yml`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/journeyadmin route preview <id>` draws a saved route for ten seconds. A route file is a world and a list
of points; it can be written by hand too:

```yaml
world: world
points:
  - 0.5,64.0,0.5
  - 6.2,64.0,3.1
  - 12.0,65.0,4.8
```

A route needs at least two points. `route-simplify` in `config.yml` (0.4 blocks) thins it further before it
is drawn.

## Waypoint

`waypoint: true` puts a mark at the target:

- on the vanilla **locator bar**, when the server and the player's client are both 1.21.6 or newer and the
  installed PacketEvents knows the packet;
- as a **minimap waypoint** for players on Lunar or Feather.

It is shown only while the player has the beacon switched on, and is removed while they are in another
world than the target.

## Glow

`glow: true` outlines the thing to act on, for that player alone, in `glow-color`:

- the NPC of an `INTERACT_NPC` step — this plugin's, ExyliaDisplays' or any other plugin's;
- the target block of an `INTERACT_BLOCK` or `BREAK_BLOCK` step that has a place, through terrain.

## Cinematic

`cinematic: <id>` plays that cinematic when the step starts. Unlike a `WATCH_CINEMATIC` objective, the step
does not wait for it. See [Cinematics](/docs/exyliajourney/cinematics).

## Hints

A player idle on a step for its `hint-after` (45 s by default) gets the step's `hint` in chat, once per step,
with the beacon pulsing and the hint sound. *Idle* means no progress on that step. A step with no `hint`
of its own sends a line pointing at `/journey`.

## Each player's switches

Players choose what they see from `/journey`, or ExyliaLib's `/settings` under **Journey guidance**:

| Switch | Turns off |
|---|---|
| Companion text | The text panel. |
| Beacon | The beam and the waypoint. |
| Trail | The trail. |
| Journey sounds | The step sound and the hint sound. |

`/journey hide` hides all guidance at once and shows it again. Progress counts either way.

## Without PacketEvents, and on Bedrock

Without PacketEvents — and always for Bedrock players, one by one — guidance is drawn with what every
client understands: the step in chat when it starts, one action bar segment with the title, progress and
distance, a particle column over the target (or a particle mark towards it when far) and particle
footprints along the trail. There is no waypoint and no glow. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliajourney/installation#packetevents).
