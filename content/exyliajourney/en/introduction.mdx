ExyliaJourney turns the first minutes on a server into a guided journey. Instead of a wall of chat text and
a `/help` nobody reads, a new player is shown where to go and what to do, one step at a time: a panel floats
ahead of their eyes with the current step and its progress, a beam of light stands over the place they need
to reach, footprints on the ground lead the way, and the NPC they have to talk to glows for them alone.
Every step reacts to what the player actually does — breaking a block, crafting a pickaxe, typing a
command, clicking an NPC — and moves on by itself.

It is a **premium** plugin, installed through a licence loader. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliajourney/installation).

## The problem it solves

Most players who leave a server for good do it in their first session, and most of them leave because they
did not understand what to do next. A journey answers that question at every moment, in the world rather
than in chat, and measures the answer: the [funnel](/docs/exyliajourney/analytics) shows how many players
reach each step, how many get through it, and which step loses the most of them. That is the step to fix.

## What a journey can be

- **Onboarding** that starts on the first join: find the spawn fountain, talk to the guide, craft a
  pickaxe, claim a kit.
- **Tutorials** for one feature, started by walking into a region, clicking an NPC or running a command.
- **Quest chains**, where completing one journey starts the next.
- **Feature discovery** hooked into other Exylia plugins: open a crate, join a clan, mine in a mine,
  queue a Practice match. See [Integrations](/docs/exyliajourney/integrations).

## Cinematics

A journey can open a step with a cinematic: the camera flies through keyframes recorded in game, with
real subtitles and a speaker's name, letterbox bars, fades and sounds, for the viewer alone. Players far
from the scene are moved to it and put back exactly where they stood. See
[Cinematics](/docs/exyliajourney/cinematics).

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper or Folia 1.21.4 or newer. |
| Java | 21. |
| ExyliaLib | Required, installed by the loader. |
| PacketEvents | Strongly recommended. Without it guidance and cinematics fall back to chat, the action bar and particles. See [Installation](/docs/exyliajourney/installation#packetevents). |
| Optional | PlaceholderAPI, ExyliaDisplays, ExyliaAnalytics. |

## How to read this documentation

Start with [Installation](/docs/exyliajourney/installation) and
[First steps](/docs/exyliajourney/first-steps): one command builds a working journey around you. Then
[Journeys](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys), [Steps and objectives](/docs/exyliajourney/steps) and
[Guidance](/docs/exyliajourney/guidance) explain the parts. The Reference section lists every command,
permission, placeholder and configuration key.
