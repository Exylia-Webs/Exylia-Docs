Players need no setup: everybody can open `/journey`, and only operators get `/journeyadmin`. Give a node with your permissions plugin, for example `/lp group staff permission set exyliajourney.admin true` in LuckPerms.

## Journeys for a rank

A journey's own `requirements.permission` and the `PERMISSION` trigger accept any permission you choose, such as `rank.vip`. It does not exist until you use it. See [Journeys](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys#triggers).
