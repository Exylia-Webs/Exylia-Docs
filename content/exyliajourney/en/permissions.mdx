---
title: "Permissions"
description: "The two permission nodes of ExyliaJourney and their defaults."
---

| Permission | Default | Grants |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliajourney.use` | everybody | `/journey`, `/journey skip`, `/journey skip journey`, `/journey hide` and `/journey track`. |
| `exyliajourney.admin` | operators | Every `/journeyadmin` command: the editor and its settings pages, starting, stopping, completing and resetting journeys, triggers, dialogues, routes, cinematics, stats, the demo and reloads. |
| `exyliajourney.focus.bypass` | nobody | [Focus mode](/docs/exyliajourney/focus-mode) never hides, blocks or holds back a player who has it. Operators do not get it, so admins can test focus mode. |

Taking `exyliajourney.use` away does not stop journeys: a player without it still gets their journeys and
their guidance, and progress still counts; they only lose the commands and the `/journey` screen.

A journey's own `requirements.permission` and the `PERMISSION` trigger use any permission you choose; see
[Journeys](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys#triggers).
