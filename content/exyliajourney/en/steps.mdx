A step is one thing for the player to do. By the end of this page you can add steps to a journey, choose what each one asks for, and give it a hint, a time limit and rewards. Every step has an objective the plugin watches for, the guidance that shows the way, and optional text, effects and rewards.

## Before you start

You need a journey: see [Set up a journey](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys) or [Your first journey](/docs/exyliajourney/first-steps). Open it with `/journeyadmin` and click the journey.

## Add a step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step list">
    On the journey, click **Steps**. The steps are listed in order. Shift-left-click a step to move it earlier, shift-right-click to move it later. A step the plugin cannot read is shown in red.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add step">
    Pick a template: **Go here**, **Talk to an NPC**, **Break blocks**, **Place blocks**, **Craft items**, **Defeat mobs**, **Run a command**, **Watch a cinematic**, **Wait**, **Custom key**, or **Any objective** for the full list of twenty-three types. **Talk to an NPC** needs an NPC placed on the server first; without one the plugin says so and goes back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    A wizard asks only what the template needs: the block or item, the mob, how many, the command, the cinematic, how long. For a place, stand where you want it and sneak and click. Nothing is saved until you confirm the review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep going or test">
    After each step a window asks to add another step, try it (plays the journey from its first step as a player) or be done. Closing the window is done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a quick "walk to this spot" step with a beacon and a footprint trail, click **Go here step** in the step list, stand where players should arrive and sneak and click.

## Change what a step asks for

Click a step, then **Objective**. The first card shows the type; click it to pick another. Below it you only see the fields that type uses:

- **Amount**: how many times, or the target value for a level, playtime in minutes or a wait in seconds.
- **Filter**: the blocks, items or mobs that count. Empty means anything counts.
- **Worlds**: limit it to certain worlds.
- **Place** and **Radius**: left-click Place to use where you stand; for actions, they must happen within the radius. "Break ten stone **in the tutorial mine**" is a Break blocks step with a place and a radius.
- **Region**: an ExyliaLib region, for reaching a place.
- **NPC**, **Command**, **Chat text**, **Conversation**, **Cinematic**, **Condition**, **Custom key** and **Wait**, one per type.

The types, in plain words:

| Ask the player to | Type in the menu |
|---|---|
| Walk somewhere | **Reach a place** |
| Click an NPC, right-click a block | **Talk to an NPC**, **Use a block** |
| Break, place, craft, smelt, enchant, fish, pick up, eat or drink, wear | **Break blocks**, **Place blocks**, **Craft items**, **Smelt items**, **Enchant items**, **Fish**, **Pick up items**, **Eat or drink**, **Wear or hold** |
| Kill mobs or players | **Kill mobs**, **Kill players** |
| Type a command or a chat line | **Run a command**, **Say something** |
| Reach a level or play a while | **Reach a level**, **Play a while** |
| Wait, watch, listen | **Wait**, **Watch a cinematic**, **Conversation** |
| Meet a condition or something another plugin reports | **Meet a condition**, **Custom key** |

The exact rules of each type, with examples, are in the technical details below.

## Add a hint and a time limit

Click the step, then **Text & timing**:

- **Title** and **Description** are what the player reads on the panel and in `/journey`.
- **Hint** is a line sent once when the player is stuck: the beacon pulses and a sound plays with it. **Hint after** is the idle time before it (45 seconds by default; `0s` turns it off for this step).
- **Time limit** fails the step when it runs out: the **whole journey stops**, the player is told they ran out of time and the step counts as abandoned. The journey's triggers can start it again from the first step.
- **Skippable** says whether players may skip this step. Skipping asks for confirmation; a step that cannot be skipped answers with a refusal.

For the sound, toast and rewards of a step, click **Effects & rewards**: **On start**, **On complete** and **Rewards**. They work like the journey's own, see [Effect lines](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys#effect-lines). A skipped step pays nothing, and that run loses the journey's completion rewards (see [Skipping](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys#skipping)). For the beacon, trail and panel, click **Guidance**: see [Guidance](/docs/exyliajourney/guidance). For a conversation in a step, see [Dialogues](/docs/exyliajourney/dialogues#in-a-step).

## Copy, test and delete a step

On the step's page:

- **Duplicate step** copies it.
- **Test from here** plays the journey for you starting at this step; nothing is saved, counted or rewarded.
- **Preview as a player** shows you this step's guidance for 30 seconds, as a player on it sees it.
- **Delete** removes it.

## Check that it works

1. Click **Test from here** on the step. The step appears in chat and as floating text.
2. Do what it asks. The step completes, its feedback plays (sound, toast, particles) and the next one starts.
3. Run `/journeyadmin test stop` to end the test.

A step with an amount above one shows a count (`3/10`) on the panel and in `/journey`; so do level, playtime and wait steps.

## Step keys

Every key of a step in `journeys/<id>.yml`, for those who edit the file by hand.

<Details title="Step keys and an example">
```yaml
steps:
  - id: tools
    title: '{highlight}Craft a wooden pickaxe'
    description: ['Open a crafting table and make one.']
    objective:
      type: CRAFT
      filter: [WOODEN_PICKAXE]
    guidance:
      beacon: false
    on-start: ['[SOUND] BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_CHIME;1;1.4']
    on-complete: ['console: give %player_name% torch 8']
    rewards: [ { type: EXPERIENCE, value: '20' } ]
    hint-after: 40s
    hint: 'Right-click a crafting table.'
    skippable: true
    timeout: 10m
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | `step-<n>` | Unique within the journey, lower case. Progress, stats and placeholders follow it, so keep it when you reword a step. |
| `title` | the id | The step's name on the panel, the toast and in `/journey`. |
| `description` | none | Lines under the title on the panel and in `/journey`. |
| `objective` | none | Required. A step without a readable objective is reported and skipped. |
| `guidance` | everything on | How the way is shown. See [Guidance](/docs/exyliajourney/guidance). |
| `dialogue` | none | A conversation in the dialogue box. See [Dialogues](/docs/exyliajourney/dialogues#in-a-step). |
| `focus` | the journey's | `true`, `false` or a section that changes the journey's [focus mode](/docs/exyliajourney/focus-mode) for this step. |
| `on-start`, `on-complete` | none | [Effect lines](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys#effect-lines). |
| `rewards` | none | Paid when the step is completed. |
| `hint-after` | `hint-after` in `config.yml` (45 s) | Idle time on the step before the hint is sent. `0s` turns it off for this step. |
| `hint` | a line pointing at `/journey` | The hint. Sent once per step. |
| `skippable` | `true` | Whether players may skip this step. |
| `timeout` | none | A time limit; running out stops the whole journey. |
</Details>

<Details title="Objective keys and every objective type">
Every objective has a `type`; the other keys depend on it.

| Key | Default | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `amount` | `1` | How many times, or the target value for `LEVEL` (level), `PLAYTIME` (minutes) and `WAIT` (seconds). |
| `filter` | any | Material or entity names, upper case. `materials`, `blocks`, `items` and `entities` are read as aliases. |
| `worlds` | any | World names the action must happen in. |
| `location` | none | `world,x,y,z`. For `REACH`, the place to reach; for the others, the action must happen within `radius` of it. |
| `radius` | `3` for `REACH`, `4` otherwise | Blocks around `location`. |
| `region` | none | An ExyliaLib region id, for `REACH`. |
| `npc`, `command`, `text`, `cinematic`, `condition`, `key`, `duration` | none | One per type, as the table below says. |

| Type | Parameters | Done when | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DIALOGUE` | a `dialogue` section on the step | The step's conversation has been heard out; the conversation itself says when. | `{ type: DIALOGUE }` |
| `REACH` | `location` + `radius`, or `region` | The player stands within the radius, or inside the region. Checked on every move and once a second, so teleports count. | `{ type: REACH, location: 'world,0,64,0', radius: 4 }` |
| `INTERACT_NPC` | `npc` | The player clicks that NPC: the bare id for this plugin's and ExyliaDisplays' NPCs, `<plugin>:<id>` for another plugin's. The NPC glows for them. | `{ type: INTERACT_NPC, npc: guide }` |
| `INTERACT_BLOCK` | `filter`, optional `location` + `radius` | The player right-clicks a matching block with the main hand. | `{ type: INTERACT_BLOCK, filter: [LECTERN] }` |
| `BREAK_BLOCK` | `amount`, `filter`, `worlds`, optional `location` + `radius` | The player breaks matching blocks. Blocks a player placed do not count, for the materials the filter names; a step with no filter does not check. | `{ type: BREAK_BLOCK, amount: 10, filter: [STONE, DEEPSLATE] }` |
| `PLACE_BLOCK` | same | The player places matching blocks. Each spot counts once per session, so placing and breaking the same block again does not count twice. | `{ type: PLACE_BLOCK, amount: 4, filter: [TORCH] }` |
| `CRAFT` | same | The player crafts a matching item; counts the recipe's result amount per craft. A shift-click that crafts a whole stack counts as one craft. | `{ type: CRAFT, filter: [WOODEN_PICKAXE] }` |
| `SMELT` | same | The player takes smelted items out of a furnace; counts the items taken. | `{ type: SMELT, amount: 8, filter: [IRON_INGOT] }` |
| `ENCHANT` | same | The player enchants a matching item at an enchanting table. | `{ type: ENCHANT, filter: [DIAMOND_SWORD] }` |
| `FISH` | same | The player catches matching items; counts the stack caught. | `{ type: FISH, amount: 3, filter: [COD, SALMON] }` |
| `KILL_MOB` | same, `filter` holds entity types | The player kills matching mobs. Mobs from spawners, spawn eggs and dispensed eggs, and built iron golems and snow golems, do not count. | `{ type: KILL_MOB, amount: 5, filter: [ZOMBIE] }` |
| `KILL_PLAYER` | `amount`, `worlds`, optional `location` + `radius` | The player kills other players. | `{ type: KILL_PLAYER, amount: 1, worlds: [pvp] }` |
| `PICKUP_ITEM` | same as `BREAK_BLOCK` | The player picks up matching items; counts the stack picked up. Items any player dropped do not count. | `{ type: PICKUP_ITEM, amount: 16, filter: [OAK_LOG] }` |
| `CONSUME` | same | The player eats or drinks a matching item. | `{ type: CONSUME, filter: [BREAD] }` |
| `EQUIP` | `filter` | The player wears, or holds in either hand, a matching item. Checked once a second. | `{ type: EQUIP, filter: [IRON_CHESTPLATE] }` |
| `RUN_COMMAND` | `command` | The player runs the command. Compared by prefix, any case, slash optional: `/spawn` counts `/spawn now`. Counted after the command ran. | `{ type: RUN_COMMAND, command: /kit starter }` |
| `CHAT` | `text` | The player sends a chat line containing the text, any case. | `{ type: CHAT, text: hello }` |
| `LEVEL` | `amount` | The player's experience level reaches `amount`. Checked once a second. | `{ type: LEVEL, amount: 10 }` |
| `PLAYTIME` | `amount` | The player's playtime reaches `amount` minutes, from ExyliaLib's playtime tracking (network-wide, idle time excluded). Checked once a second. | `{ type: PLAYTIME, amount: 60 }` |
| `WAIT` | `duration` | That much time has passed since the step started. A bare number is seconds. | `{ type: WAIT, duration: 30s }` |
| `WATCH_CINEMATIC` | `cinematic` | The cinematic, played when the step starts, ends: watched, skipped or refused alike, so a cinematic that cannot play never holds a journey. | `{ type: WATCH_CINEMATIC, cinematic: intro }` |
| `PLACEHOLDER` | `condition` | The condition is true. Checked once a second; fails closed. | `{ type: PLACEHOLDER, condition: '%vault_eco_balance% >= 100' }` |
| `CUSTOM` | `key`, `amount` | The key is fired for the player that many times. A step asking for `exylia:mines:break` counts `exylia:mines:break:gold` too, never the other way round. | `{ type: CUSTOM, key: exylia:crates:open, amount: 3 }` |

Every type that counts an action respects `worlds` and an optional `location` + `radius`. An empty `filter` accepts anything. What farming is not covered is in the [FAQ](/docs/exyliajourney/faq#common-questions). The CUSTOM keys other Exylia plugins fire are listed on [Integrations](/docs/exyliajourney/integrations).
</Details>

<Details title="A step the plugin cannot read, and what happens on completion">
A step whose objective is missing what it needs (a `REACH` with no place, an `INTERACT_NPC` with no NPC, a `PLACEHOLDER` whose condition does not parse) is reported naming the file and the path, and skipped until it is fixed. The rest of the journey still runs. The editor keeps such a step in the file and marks it, so a step can be half-built in game without breaking the journey around it.

Progress counts only for the step a player is on. When a step's objective is met:

1. its rewards are paid and its `on-complete` lines run;
2. the step feedback plays (sound, toast, particles);
3. `JourneyStepCompleteEvent` is fired;
4. the next step starts, or the journey is completed.
</Details>
