---
title: "Focus mode"
description: "Keep a player on the journey: hide other players, block commands, interactions, drops and damage, and hold them inside a radius while a step runs."
---

Focus mode is for the moments a journey must not be interrupted: a first-join tutorial, a scripted scene, a
step in a dangerous place. While it applies, the player is kept on the step they are tracking. It is off
unless a journey or a step asks for it, and every part is optional.

```yaml
# journeys/tutorial.yml
focus:
  hide-players: true
  block-commands: true
  allowed-commands: [msg, spawn]
  block-interact: true
  no-drop: true
  no-damage: true
  leash:
    center: target
    radius: 40
steps:
  - id: boss
    focus: false            # this step lets the player go
    objective: { type: KILL_MOB, amount: 3, filter: [ZOMBIE] }
```

## Keys

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `hide-players` | `false` | Other players are hidden from this player and this player from them. Chat still works. It also covers players who join later. |
| `block-commands` | `false` | Commands are refused, except those in `allowed-commands`, `/journey` and `/journeys`, and the command a `RUN_COMMAND` step is waiting for. |
| `allowed-commands` | none | Command labels still allowed, with or without the slash. |
| `block-interact` | `false` | Doors, chests, buttons, pressure plates and other blocks that can be used, and entities, are refused. What the step needs still works (see below). |
| `no-drop` | `false` | Items cannot be dropped. |
| `no-damage` | `false` | The player takes no damage, except from the void. |
| `leash` | none | How far the player may go. `leash.radius` (blocks, above 0) is required; `leash.center` is `target` (the step's target: its location or NPC) or `world,x,y,z`. |

`focus: true` switches on everything but the leash, which needs a radius. `focus: false` turns focus off.

## Per journey and per step

`focus` at the top of a journey applies to every step. A step may carry its own `focus`: `true`, `false`, or a
section whose keys change only what they name, keeping the journey's value for the rest. A bad value is
reported and the journey's own focus is kept.

Focus follows the journey the player **tracks** and its current step, so a player with two journeys is held
only by the one they follow.

## What always stays possible

- A step that needs the player's hands (`PLACE_BLOCK`, `CONSUME`, `EQUIP`, `FISH`, `CRAFT`, `SMELT`,
  `ENCHANT`) is never blocked from using blocks or entities.
- An `INTERACT_BLOCK` step still allows its block, at its place. An `INTERACT_NPC` step leaves every entity
  clickable, because an NPC may belong to any plugin.
- The leash puts a player who goes beyond the radius back where they last stood inside it, or at the centre if
  they never did. It is checked once a second.
- A refusal is said at most once every few seconds, however often the player tries.

## Letting staff through

`exyliajourney.focus.bypass` exempts a player: journeys never hide, block or hold them. It is **not** given to
operators, so admins test focus mode as a player would. Give it to the staff who need to move freely.

Focus mode is separate from cinematics: a cinematic hides other players by default through its own setting.
See [Cinematics](/docs/exyliajourney/cinematics).
