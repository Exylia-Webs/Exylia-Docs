Every command, with the permission it needs. Journey, route, cinematic and dialogue ids complete as you type. `/journey` also answers to `/journeys` and `/quest`; `/journeyadmin` to `/jadmin`.

<Commands />

## Which ones need a player

`demo`, `stats`, `test`, `preview`, `step add`, `route record`, `route stop`, `route preview`, `route extend`, `route reverse` and `cinematic record` need a player in game. The others also work from the console. From the console, `dialogue open` needs the player's name.

## What reload does

`/journeyadmin reload` reloads `config.yml`, messages, journeys, cinematics, routes, menus and NPCs, and says how many problems it found. Players keep the step they are on. Edits made in [the editor](/docs/exyliajourney/editor) never need a reload.
