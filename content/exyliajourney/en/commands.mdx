## Players

`/journey` has the aliases `/journeys` and `/quest`. Every subcommand needs `exyliajourney.use`, which
everybody has by default.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/journey` | Opens your journey: its steps with their state and progress, the rewards, and the guidance switches. |
| `/journey skip` | Skips the cinematic you are watching at once, if it is skippable. Otherwise asks you to confirm, then skips the current step of your tracked journey; a step that cannot be skipped answers with a refusal. |
| `/journey skip journey` | Asks you to confirm, then skips your whole tracked journey. It pays nothing and never starts again on its own. A journey that cannot be skipped answers with a refusal. |
| `/journey hide` | Hides all guidance, or shows it again. Progress still counts. |
| `/journey track <journey>` | Follows another of your active journeys: its step becomes the one the guidance shows. |

## Admins

`/journeyadmin` has the alias `/jadmin`. Every subcommand needs `exyliajourney.admin`, which operators have
by default.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/journeyadmin` | Opens the [editor](/docs/exyliajourney/editor). |
| `/journeyadmin start <player> <journey>` | Starts a journey for a player, ignoring enabled, requirements, cooldown, group and earlier completions or skips. Refused only when it is already active. |
| `/journeyadmin stop <player> <journey>` | Stops an active journey without completing it. No rewards; its triggers may start it again. |
| `/journeyadmin complete <player> <journey>` | Completes a journey now, starting it first if needed, with its completion rewards in full, even if the player skipped steps. The steps left are not paid. |
| `/journeyadmin reset <player> <journey>` | Forgets everything the player did on that journey: progress, completion, skip and cooldown. Works for journeys that no longer exist, to clear leftovers. |
| `/journeyadmin trigger <player> <key> [amount]` | Fires a CUSTOM key for the player, `amount` times (1). `npc:<id>` counts as clicking that NPC. Says how many steps counted it. |
| `/journeyadmin stats <journey>` | Opens the journey's [funnel](/docs/exyliajourney/analytics). |
| `/journeyadmin demo` | Builds a working journey, route, cinematic and NPC around you and starts it. See [First steps](/docs/exyliajourney/first-steps). |
| `/journeyadmin reload` | Reloads `config.yml`, messages, journeys, cinematics, routes, menus and NPCs, and says how many problems it found. |

### Routes

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/journeyadmin route record <id>` | Starts recording a route: a point every 1.5 blocks as you walk. |
| `/journeyadmin route stop` | Stops and saves it. Sneaking twice quickly does the same. |
| `/journeyadmin route preview <id>` | Draws a saved route to you for ten seconds. |
| `/journeyadmin route delete <id>` | Deletes a route file. |

### Cinematics

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/journeyadmin cinematic record <id>` | Starts the recorder hotbar: mark, undo, preview, save, cancel. |
| `/journeyadmin cinematic play <id> [player]` | Plays a cinematic to you, or to another player. A `WATCH_CINEMATIC` step waiting for it counts it. |
| `/journeyadmin cinematic delete <id>` | Deletes a cinematic file. |

`demo`, `stats`, `route record`, `route stop`, `route preview` and `cinematic record` need a player; the
rest also work from the console.
