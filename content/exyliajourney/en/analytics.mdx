A journey exists to keep new players, so ExyliaJourney measures how well each one does it. By the end of this
page you can open a journey's funnel, find the step that loses the most players and, if you run ExyliaAnalytics,
chart the same data there.

## Before you start

You need a journey that players have started. Counters begin when the journey first runs; admin test runs
send and count nothing. See [Set up a journey](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the funnel">
    Run `/journeyadmin stats <journey>`. Or run `/journeyadmin`, then either right-click the journey, or open it
    and click **Funnel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the header">
    The header shows the whole journey: how many times it was started, completed, skipped and abandoned, its
    completion rate and the average time to finish it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the weak step">
    Below, one card per step, in order. The step that loses the most players is shown in red. That is the
    step to make clearer, shorter or better guided: see [Show players the way](/docs/exyliajourney/guidance) and
    [Add steps and objectives](/docs/exyliajourney/steps).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Start a journey with a real account (not **Test run**) and finish its first step: the first card's Reached and
Completed go up by one. If the funnel stays empty, nobody has started the journey yet.

## Going further

### Reading a step's card

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Reached | Players who started this step. |
| Share | Reached, over the journey's starts. |
| Completed | Players who completed it. |
| Pass rate | Completed, over reached. |
| Skipped | Players who skipped it. |
| Abandoned | Players who stopped making progress on it (see below). |
| Average time | How long it took to complete, on average. |

Counters are only ever added to, never rewritten, so several servers sharing one database build one funnel
together. Renaming a step's `id` starts its counters from zero; rewording its title does not.

### Abandoned steps

A step counts **once** as abandoned when it sees no progress for `abandon-after` (`config.yml`, 7 days by
default; in game, **Settings**, **General**, **Abandoned step**):

- the player left and never came back while on it (offline players are checked once an hour);
- or the player keeps playing but does nothing for it.

A step that runs out of its `timeout` fails: the journey stops (its triggers can start it again, from the
first step) and the step counts as abandoned too, with the reason `timeout`. `abandon-after: 0s` turns idle
abandonment off.

### ExyliaAnalytics

With ExyliaAnalytics installed, every journey reports its events to the Exylia Analytics platform, where
they can be charted and turned into funnels next to the rest of the server's data. Without it nothing is
sent and nothing needs configuring.

<Details title="Events sent to ExyliaAnalytics">

| Event | When | Properties |
|---|---|---|
| `journey.start` | A journey starts. | `journey`, `total_steps`, `cause` (`first_join`, `join`, `command`, `region_enter`, `npc_click`, `journey_complete`, `permission`, `custom`, `dialogue` or `api`) |
| `journey.step_complete` | A step is completed. | `journey`, `step`, `step_number`, `seconds` |
| `journey.step_skip` | A step is skipped. | `journey`, `step`, `step_number` |
| `journey.skip` | A whole journey is skipped. | `journey`, `step`, `step_number` (where it was) |
| `journey.complete` | A journey is completed. | `journey`, `seconds` |
| `journey.abandon` | A step counts as abandoned. | `journey`, `step`, `step_number`, `reason` (`idle` or `timeout`) |
| `journey.stop` | A journey is stopped by an admin (command or editor) or another plugin. A timeout is an abandon, not a stop. | `journey`, `step`, `step_number`, `reason` (`admin` or `api`) |
| `journey.catalog` | Server-wide, one per loaded journey: on start, after a reload or an editor change, and once a day. | `journey`, `name` (plain text), `steps` (step ids in order, comma separated), `steps_truncated` (only when the ids did not fit in 200 characters), `total_steps`, `triggers` (comma separated), `repeatable`, `skippable` |

`step_number` counts from 1. Admin test runs send nothing.

</Details>
