A journey exists to keep new players, so ExyliaJourney measures how well each one does it.

## The funnel

`/journeyadmin stats <journey>`, **FUNNEL** on a journey's screen, or a right-click on it in the journey list,
opens its funnel.

The header shows the whole journey: how many times it was started, completed, skipped and abandoned, its
completion rate and the average time to finish it. Below, one card per step, in order:

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Reached | Players who started this step. |
| Share | Reached, over the journey's starts. |
| Completed | Players who completed it. |
| Pass rate | Completed, over reached. |
| Skipped | Players who skipped it. |
| Abandoned | Players who stopped making progress on it (see below). |
| Average time | How long it took to complete, on average. |

The step that loses the most players is shown in red. That is the step to make clearer, shorter or better
guided.

Counters are only ever added to, never rewritten, so several servers sharing one database build one funnel
together. Renaming a step's `id` starts its counters from zero; rewording its title does not.

## Abandoned steps

A step counts **once** as abandoned when it sees no progress for `abandon-after` (`config.yml`, 7 days by
default):

- the player left and never came back while on it — offline players are checked once an hour;
- or the player keeps playing but does nothing for it.

A step that runs out of its `timeout` counts as abandoned too, with the reason `timeout`. `abandon-after:
0s` turns idle abandonment off.

## ExyliaAnalytics

With ExyliaAnalytics installed, every journey reports its events to the Exylia Analytics platform, where
they can be charted and turned into funnels next to the rest of the server's data. Without it nothing is
sent and nothing needs configuring.

| Event | When | Properties |
|---|---|---|
| `journey.start` | A journey starts. | `journey`, `cause` (`first_join`, `join`, `command`, `region_enter`, `npc_click`, `journey_complete`, `permission`, `custom` or `api`) |
| `journey.step_complete` | A step is completed. | `journey`, `step`, `step_number`, `seconds` |
| `journey.step_skip` | A step is skipped. | `journey`, `step`, `step_number` |
| `journey.skip` | A whole journey is skipped. | `journey`, `step`, `step_number` (where it was) |
| `journey.complete` | A journey is completed. | `journey`, `seconds` |
| `journey.abandon` | A step counts as abandoned. | `journey`, `step`, `step_number`, `reason` (`idle` or `timeout`) |

`step_number` counts from 1.
