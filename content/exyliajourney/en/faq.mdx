## Bedrock

**Do Bedrock players get journeys?** Yes, every journey, every objective and every reward. What changes is
how the way is shown: a Bedrock client draws display entities poorly or not at all and has no locator bar,
so Bedrock players get the step in chat when it starts, an action bar line with title, progress and
distance, a particle column over the target and particle footprints along trails. Cinematics are sent as
their subtitles in chat and count as watched. This is decided player by player, so Java players on the same
server keep the full guidance.

## Performance

**What does it cost on a busy server?** Very little.

- Every listener first asks whether anybody, then whether this player, waits for that kind of objective. On
  a server where nobody is on a `BREAK_BLOCK` step, breaking a block costs one set lookup.
- Polled objectives (`LEVEL`, `PLAYTIME`, `WAIT`, `EQUIP`, `PLACEHOLDER`, and `REACH` as a safety net for
  teleports) are checked once a second, only for players whose current step is one of them.
- Guidance is packets to one player. A still player costs nothing on the wire; a walking one costs a panel
  glide every few steps and one trail marker in and out per marker spacing.
- Progress writes are batched (`save-interval`), and placeholders are read from memory.
- On Folia, everything about a player runs on that player's region thread.

## Without PacketEvents

**What do players see without PacketEvents?** The same journeys with simpler guidance: the step in chat when
it starts, an action bar line, particle beams and footprints. There is no floating panel, no waypoint, no
glow, no toasts and no NPCs of the plugin's own (ExyliaDisplays NPCs still work). Cinematics keep their
subtitles, in chat at their times, and their sounds, but the camera does not move. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliajourney/installation#packetevents).

## Editing a live journey

**What happens to players on a journey I edit?** They stay on it. An edit in the editor replaces the live
journey at once and moves each player to the same step by its id, keeping their count. If their step was
deleted, they continue on the step now in its place, started afresh. If an edit leaves the journey
unreadable, the last working version stays live.

A hand edit followed by `/journeyadmin reload` keeps each player on the same step **number** instead. Add,
remove or reorder steps of a journey players are on from the editor.

**I renamed a step's id. Why did its stats reset?** The funnel counts each step by its id. Change the
`title` freely; keep the `id`.

## Common questions

**A `FIRST_JOIN` journey did not start for me.** It starts only for a player who has never played on the
server before — players who joined before ExyliaJourney was installed never get it; give them a `JOIN` or
`PERMISSION` journey instead. To test it, use the editor's **TEST ON YOURSELF**, `/journeyadmin start`, or a
fresh account.

**A journey does not start at all.** Turn on `debug: true`: the console says why each start was refused
(disabled, skipped, already done, cooldown, group busy, requirements, cancelled). Also check the journey
has a trigger: a journey created in the editor has none.

**A player skipped the onboarding by mistake.** A skipped journey never starts again from a trigger.
`/journeyadmin reset <player> <journey>` clears it, or `/journeyadmin start` starts it anyway.

**Players run out of time and lose the journey.** A step's `timeout` stops the whole journey. Remove the
time limit, or make the journey's triggers able to start it again.

**My `PLACEHOLDER` step never completes.** Conditions fail closed: a placeholder that does not resolve to a
number never passes a numeric comparison. Check the placeholder with PlaceholderAPI's parse command.

**Can two journeys run at the same time?** Yes, if they are in different groups. Progress counts on all of
them; the guidance shows the tracked one.

**Does a cinematic hold a journey forever if it cannot play?** No. A `WATCH_CINEMATIC` step completes when the
cinematic ends in any way, refused or skipped included.
