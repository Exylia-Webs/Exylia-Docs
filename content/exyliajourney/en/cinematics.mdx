A cinematic is a camera flight through the world: keyframes recorded in game, with subtitles and a speaker's
name, letterbox bars, a fade in and out, and sounds — all for the viewer alone. Each cinematic is one file,
`cinematics/<id>.yml`.

A journey plays one in two ways:

- `guidance.cinematic: <id>` on a step plays it when the step starts; the step does not wait for it.
- A `WATCH_CINEMATIC` objective plays it when the step starts, and the step is done when it ends.

`/journeyadmin cinematic play <id> [player]` plays one outside any journey, and other plugins can play one
through the [API](/docs/exyliajourney/api).

## Making one

There are two ways in game, and they write the same file.

### Shot by shot

**CREATE CINEMATIC** in the editor's cinematic list asks for an id and walks you through it with windows
and on-screen prompts, no tools to learn. **ADD SHOTS** on a cinematic's screen does the same at the end of
one that exists. For each shot:

<Steps>
  <Step title="How it follows the last one">
    **Cut** jumps straight to the new view; **Fly over** has the camera travel there from the last shot. The
    first shot has no question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where it starts">
    Stand where the camera starts, look at what it shows, then sneak and click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What the camera does">
    **Stays still**, **Travels to another view** (you stand where it ends and sneak-click again), **Circles
    around** what you looked at, **Rises** (a crane, keeping it in view), **Slides sideways** past it, or
    **Moves in** toward it. Anything but travelling then asks how long: 3, 5, 8 or 12 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What is said">
    Type `Speaker: text`, or just the text, or leave it empty. A shot with a line then asks whether the
    cinematic should **wait for a click** (a stop) or **go on by itself**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What next">
    **Add a shot**, **Watch it** (plays what you have so far), **Undo the last shot**, or **Save and finish**.
    Leaving with unsaved shots asks first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Sounds, fades and the rest wait in the cinematic's screen after saving. Making a journey step that needs a
cinematic, when there is none yet, offers to make one this way first and then puts it on the step.

### With the recorder

`/journeyadmin cinematic record <id>`, or **RECORD AGAIN** on a cinematic's screen, gives you a hotbar of tool
cards while you fly. It replaces your hotbar and is only drawn, so nothing is lost if the server stops; your
flying speed is put back when you stop. Starting a recording while another one runs (a route or a cinematic)
offers to save that one first.

The first page holds the everyday tools:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mark keyframe** | Saves where your eyes are and where you look as the next keyframe. |
| **Next timing** | Seconds the camera takes to reach the next keyframe (default 3). Left and right click change it by 0.5 s, with sneak by 0.1 s. |
| **Easing** | How the camera moves into the next keyframe. |
| **Hold** | Seconds the camera stays still on the next keyframe. |
| **Cut** | The next keyframe is jumped to instead of flown to. Teleport or change world first. |
| **Preview** | Plays everything, the last 5 seconds, from the selected keyframe, or at 1x, 0.5x or 2x. Sneak while watching to pause there. |
| **Undo · redo** | Walks back and forth through keyframes and subtitles. |
| **Save** | Writes the file; its sounds and settings are kept. Sneak and right-click cancels without saving. |
| **Tools** | Opens the second page. |

The second page, for fine work:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Select keyframe** | Steps through the keyframes; you are moved to the one you pick so you can tweak it. |
| **Edit keyframe** | On the selected one: look from here instead, insert one after it from here, duplicate it (stay there longer) or delete it. |
| **Subtitles** | Write or edit the line at the playhead, time lines live while watching, or load a script and time it live. Type `Speaker: line`. A line follows the keyframe before it, so retiming keeps it with its shot. |
| **Playhead** | Where new subtitles go and where a preview resumes. |
| **Shot helpers** | Keyframes made for you after the last one: **orbit** the block you look at, **dolly** in toward it, **pan** (press here, then at the end view), **crane** up aimed at it, and with the drop key **slide** sideways past it. |
| **Flying speed** | Faster or slower flight while recording. |
| **Stops** | Makes the cinematic wait for a click at the selected keyframe or the playhead, or edits the stop's text. Right-click removes the nearest. |
| **Polish** | **Even out** the camera speed over the whole take, **aim** the selected keyframe at the block you look at, or stretch the whole cinematic slower (x1.25) or faster (x0.8). Lines and stops keep their moment. |

Recording again over an existing cinematic keeps its subtitles, sounds and settings.

A cinematic's screen in `/journeyadmin` also has the **Timeline** (keyframes, subtitles, title cards, actions,
sounds and stops on a time grid), **Pacing**, **Screen** and **Title cards** cards, and **Sky & chain**. See
[The editor](/docs/exyliajourney/editor).

## The file

```yaml
skippable: true
letterbox: true
fade-in: 0.5
fade-out: 0.5
keyframes:
  - { at: 'world,10.00,70.00,0.00', yaw: 90.0, pitch: 25.0, time: 0.0, easing: SMOOTH }
  - { at: 'world,0.00,70.00,10.00', yaw: 180.0, pitch: 25.0, time: 3.0, easing: LINEAR, hold: 1.5 }
subtitles:
  - { start: 0.5, duration: 4.0, speaker: Guide, text: '{letters}Welcome to the server.' }
sounds:
  - { time: 0.0, sound: BLOCK_AMETHYST_BLOCK_CHIME, volume: 1.0, pitch: 0.8 }
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `skippable` | `true` | Whether the viewer may skip it. |
| `letterbox` | `true` | Two black bars, top and bottom. Write it as a section (`enabled`, `size`) to change the bar height: a fraction of the screen, `0.093` by default, `0.01` to `0.4`. |
| `fade-in`, `fade-out` | `0.5` | Seconds of fade from and to black. `0` for none. |
| `keyframes` | none | At least two, or `scenes`. See below. |
| `subtitles` | none | Lines shown over the view. |
| `titles` | none | Big centred title cards. |
| `sounds` | none | Sounds played at their times, for the viewer alone. |
| `actions` | none | Lines run for the viewer at a time. |
| `subtitle-style`, `title-style` | `config.yml`'s | How the text looks. |
| `hide-hud` | `config.yml`'s | What of the viewer's screen is hidden. |
| `time`, `weather`, `next` | none | The sky and what plays after. |

Times are seconds from the start. A cinematic lasts until the last thing it shows, plays or runs: a keyframe
and its hold, a subtitle, a title card, an action, a stop. A shot lasts up to **three minutes**; keyframes
past that are dropped.

Stops, `advance`, `typewriter`, `shakes`, `actors`, `choices` and `scenes` are on
[Directing a cinematic](/docs/exyliajourney/directing).

### Keyframes

| Key | Default | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `at` | none | `world,x,y,z`: where the camera is. A keyframe without it is reported and skipped. |
| `yaw`, `pitch` | `0` | Where it looks. A pan across north turns the short way. |
| `time` | three seconds per keyframe | When the camera is there. Keyframes are put in time order. |
| `easing` | `SMOOTH` | How the camera arrives at this keyframe. |
| `hold` | `0` | Seconds the camera stays still here before moving on. |
| `look-at` | none | From here to the next keyframe the camera aims at `world,x,y,z` or `npc:<id>` instead of using `yaw` and `pitch`. |
| `cut` | `false` | Jump here instead of flying. A keyframe in another world than the one before is always a cut. |
| `fade` | `0` | Seconds of black around a cut. A cut that moves the viewer far always gets a little. |
| `wait` | none | `true`, or a section with `timeout`: the cinematic stops here until the viewer clicks. See [Directing](/docs/exyliajourney/directing). |
| `orbit` | none | In place of `at`: a ring of keyframes around a point, each aimed at it. |

```yaml
  - time: 12.0
    orbit: { center: 'world,0.00,64.00,0.00', radius: 10, height: 5, degrees: 360, duration: 8, from: 0 }
```

| Easing | Movement |
|---|---|
| `SMOOTH` | One curve through the neighbouring keyframes instead of stopping at each: the right choice for a recorded path. Anything unknown is read as `SMOOTH`. |
| `LINEAR` | Constant speed. |
| `EASE_IN` | Starts slow, speeds up. |
| `EASE_OUT` | Starts fast, slows down. |
| `EASE_IN_OUT` | Slow at both ends. |

The camera holds the first keyframe until its time and the last one until the cinematic ends. Where a
cinematic is cut into shots (a change of world, or a far cut), each shot is played on its own and the viewer is
moved between them.

### Subtitles

| Key | Default | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `start` | `0` | When it appears. |
| `at` | none | `{ keyframe: 3, offset: 0.5 }`: appear half a second after keyframe 3 (counted from 1). The line then follows that keyframe when the cinematic is retimed. |
| `duration` | its reading time | How long it stays, at least half a second. Without it, a line stays as long as it takes to read at `cinematics.reading-speed`, at least `min-subtitle`. |
| `speaker` | none | A name shown on its own line above the text. |
| `text` | none | The line, in Exylia text notation. A subtitle with no text is skipped. |
| `position` | the style's | `BOTTOM`, `CENTER` or `TOP`. |
| `align` | `CENTER` | `LEFT`, `CENTER` or `RIGHT`. |
| `offset` | `0` | Moves it up by a fraction of the screen's height, from -1 (down) to 1. |
| `line` | start order | Its slot when lines overlap, 0 nearest the base. |
| `color` | none | The speaker's colour, a palette placeholder. |
| `style` | none | A style for this line alone. |

When two lines overlap they stack; with no `line` the one that started last is nearest the base. The look of
every line is the `subtitle-style` of the cinematic, else of `config.yml`. A style holds any of:

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `background` | Panel colour, a palette token or `#hex`. |
| `opacity` | `0` (no panel, the default) to `1`. |
| `shadow` | Text shadow. |
| `width` | Pixels before a line wraps, 40 to 1000. |
| `scale` | Text size, 0.3 to 4. |
| `see-through` | Whether the panel can be seen through. |
| `gap` | Space between stacked lines, a fraction of the screen. |
| `speaker`, `speaker-format` | Whether the speaker is shown, and how (`%speaker%`; empty uses `messages.yml`'s `cinematic.subtitle-speaker`, `cinematic.subtitle-line`). |

### Title cards

```yaml
titles:
  - { start: 1.0, duration: 3.0, text: '{highlight}&lThe Capital' }
```

Big centred text over the picture, styled by `title-style`. `duration` defaults to 3 seconds, at least half a
second.

### Sounds

| Key | Default |
|---|---|
| `time` | `0` |
| `sound` | none (a sound name, `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_CHIME` or `minecraft:block.note_block.chime`) |
| `volume` | `1` |
| `pitch` | `1` |

### Actions

```yaml
actions:
  - { time: 4.0, run: ['[SOUND] entity.experience_orb.pickup', '[PARTICLE] ...'] }
  - { at: { keyframe: 2, offset: 0.5 }, run: '[ACTION] ...' }
```

`run` is one ExyliaLib interaction line or a list, run for the viewer at that time: `[SOUND]`, `[PARTICLE]`,
`[TITLE]`, `console:` and the rest. Like a subtitle, an action can be anchored to a keyframe with `at`.

### The sky and what comes next

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `time` | The viewer's time of day while watching: ticks `0`-`24000`, or `day`, `noon`, `sunset`, `night`, `midnight`, `sunrise`. |
| `weather` | `CLEAR` or `RAIN`, for the viewer alone. |
| `next` | Another cinematic id, played right after this one. A cinematic cannot name itself. |

The viewer keeps their own time and weather when the keys are left out.

### Defaults in `config.yml`

The `cinematics` section holds the defaults every file inherits:

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `move-viewer-beyond` | `96` | See [Far viewers](#far-viewers). |
| `subtitle-style`, `title-style` | no panel, shadow, 360 / 160 px | The style above. |
| `hide-hud` | all six | What is hidden while watching. |
| `reading-speed` | `15` | Characters a second a line with no `duration` is read at. |
| `min-subtitle` | `1.5` | The least a line stays. |

The typewriter and stop settings (`typewriter`, `scene-typewriter`, `typewriter-sound`, `continue-sound`,
`wait-timeout`, `hold-drift`) are on [Directing a cinematic](/docs/exyliajourney/directing#defaults).

## Playback

While a cinematic plays, the viewer is frozen, drawn as a spectator and cannot be hurt. The subtitles, bars
and fade ride the camera, so they move with the view on the same frame however fast it pans.

### What is hidden

`hide-hud` (a list, a comma-separated string, `true`, or `ALL`) names what of the viewer's screen is hidden
while they watch, and comes back when it ends:

| Value | Hides |
|---|---|
| `BOSSBAR`, `SCOREBOARD`, `ACTIONBAR`, `TITLES` | That part of the screen. |
| `CHAT` | Chat: messages are held back, then delivered. |
| `PLAYERS` | Every other player, so only the world, NPCs and the cinematic's own actors are in the shot. |

All six are hidden by default. A cinematic that sets `hide-hud` replaces the list; `hide-hud: []` hides
nothing. Remove `PLAYERS` from `config.yml` to show other players again everywhere.

A cinematic is **refused** — the player is told they can't watch one right now — when:

- the player is in combat (through ExyliaLib's combat check);
- the player is already watching one;
- and when the cinematic's first keyframe is in a world that is not loaded (the server logs it).

A refused cinematic still ends a `WATCH_CINEMATIC` step, so a journey is never stuck behind one.

### Far viewers

A client only draws the chunks around the player's own body, so a camera far away would film the void. A
viewer farther than `cinematics.move-viewer-beyond` blocks (96) from the first keyframe is moved next to it
first and put back exactly where they stood when it ends. Leaving the server mid-cinematic puts them back
before they go, so they log in where they were. `0` never moves anybody.

### Skipping

A skippable cinematic sends a clickable line when it starts; clicking it, or `/journey skip`, ends it. A
skip counts as an ending for a `WATCH_CINEMATIC` step. Dying or changing world ends a cinematic too.

## Bedrock and servers without PacketEvents

- **Bedrock players** cannot draw the camera. They get the cinematic's subtitles in chat under a short
  header, and it counts as watched. Stops are not waited for and the answers at the end are not asked.
- **Without PacketEvents**, journey cinematics keep their subtitles and sounds: the camera does not move,
  the subtitles arrive in chat at their times and the sounds play.
- `JourneyService.playCinematic` refuses Java players on a server without PacketEvents. See
  [API](/docs/exyliajourney/api).
