`/journeyadmin` opens the editor. Every change is written straight back to the YAML file — top-level
comments and key order are kept — and goes live at once, without a reload. Anything the editor can do can
also be written by hand in the files; the two never disagree.

## The screens

```
Journeys (list)
├── CREATE JOURNEY · CINEMATICS · ROUTES
└── Journey (hub)
    ├── STATUS                 enabled / disabled
    ├── STEPS (list)           add, reorder with shift-clicks
    │   └── Step (hub)
    │       ├── OBJECTIVE      type and its fields
    │       ├── GUIDANCE       text, lines, place, beacon, waypoint, glow, trail, target, cinematic
    │       ├── TEXT & TIMING  title, description, hint, hint after, time limit, skippable
    │       ├── EFFECTS & REWARDS  on start, on complete, rewards
    │       ├── DUPLICATE STEP
    │       └── DELETE STEP
    ├── TRIGGERS (list)        add, edit, remove
    ├── REQUIREMENTS & GROUP   permission, condition, group
    ├── REWARDS & EFFECTS      rewards, on start, on complete
    ├── SETTINGS               name, description, icon, priority, repeatable, cooldown, skippable
    ├── FUNNEL                 the journey's stats
    ├── CHECK                  what the plugin could not read
    ├── TEST ON YOURSELF
    └── DELETE JOURNEY
```

### Journeys

The root lists every journey file with its id, steps, group, triggers and state. Left-click edits it,
right-click opens its [funnel](/docs/exyliajourney/analytics). A journey the plugin cannot load is shown
as a barrier with the reason; clicking it opens the editor to fix it. The bottom row holds **CREATE
JOURNEY**, **CINEMATICS** and **ROUTES**.

**CREATE JOURNEY** asks for an id and a name and writes a journey with one step, a `REACH` where you stand. It is enabled but has no trigger yet, so it reaches nobody until you add one.

Adding a step does not end the flow: after each step a window asks to **add a step**, **try it** (plays the
journey from its first step as a player) or be **done**, so a whole journey can be made without looking for a
button. Closing the window is done. Picking a cinematic step template, or a step's cinematic, when there is
no cinematic yet offers to make one shot by shot first and then uses it.

### Journey

The hub of one journey: **STATUS** switches it on and off; **STEPS**, **TRIGGERS**, **REQUIREMENTS &
GROUP**, **REWARDS & EFFECTS** and **SETTINGS** open their pages; **FUNNEL** opens its stats; **CHECK**
lists the problems found in the file. **TEST ON YOURSELF** starts the live version for you, ignoring
requirements, cooldown and group. **DELETE JOURNEY** asks for confirmation.

### Steps and step

The step list shows each step in order; shift-left and shift-right move a step up and down. **ADD STEP**
asks for the objective type. A step the plugin cannot read is shown in red.

A step's hub opens four pages:

| Page | Fields |
|---|---|
| **OBJECTIVE** | The type (click to pick another), then only the fields that type uses: amount, filter, worlds, place (left-click sets where you stand), radius, region, NPC (picked from every NPC placed on the server), command, chat text, cinematic, condition, custom key, wait. |
| **GUIDANCE** | Text panel (left: on/off, right: companion/anchored), panel lines, panel place, beacon (left: on/off, right: label, shift-left: colour), waypoint, glow, trail (left: route, right: how far ahead, shift-right: remove), target, cinematic. |
| **TEXT & TIMING** | Title, description, hint, hint after, time limit, skippable. |
| **EFFECTS & REWARDS** | On start and on complete lines, and the step's rewards through ExyliaLib's reward editor. |

### Triggers

One card per trigger, with a click per field: the region or sphere of a `REGION_ENTER` (shift-left sets the
centre where you stand, shift-right the radius), the NPC, journey, permission or key of the others.
Right-click removes a trigger. **ADD TRIGGER** asks for its type.

### Cinematics

The list of cinematics: left-click edits, right-click plays it to you. **CREATE CINEMATIC** asks for an id,
then walks you through the cinematic shot by shot with no tools to learn (see
[Cinematics](/docs/exyliajourney/cinematics#shot-by-shot)).

A cinematic's hub opens:

- **KEYFRAMES**, **SUBTITLES** and **SOUNDS**, each a list you can edit entry by entry;
- **TIMELINE**: keyframes, subtitles, title cards, actions, sounds and stops on one time grid, with a cell size
  and a preview of the page you are on;
- **FADES** (left: in, right: out), **LETTERBOX** and **SKIPPABLE**;
- **PACING**: left-click switches between playing by itself and waiting for a click after every line,
  right-click sets the typing speed (off, then faster each click);
- **SCREEN**, **TITLE CARDS** and **SKY & CHAIN**: the text style, the title cards, and the time, weather and
  next cinematic;
- **ADD SHOTS**: left-click adds shots at the end, shot by shot; right-click opens the advanced hotbar
  recorder (**RECORD AGAIN** over the existing keyframes);
- **WATCH IT** and **DELETE CINEMATIC**.

Saving a cinematic from the recorder or the shot-by-shot flow opens its screen. See
[Cinematics](/docs/exyliajourney/cinematics) and [Directing a cinematic](/docs/exyliajourney/directing).

### Routes

The list of routes: left-click opens one, right-click previews it. **RECORD ROUTE** asks for an id and starts
recording. A route's screen has **RECORD AGAIN**, **PREVIEW** and **DELETE ROUTE**. See
[Guidance](/docs/exyliajourney/guidance#recording-a-route).

## Edits while players are on a journey

An edited journey replaces the live one immediately, and every player on it stays where they were:

- on the **same step**, found by its id, wherever it moved — with their count kept;
- if their step was deleted, on the step **now in its place**, started afresh;
- if the journey got shorter, on its last step.

When an edit leaves the journey unreadable (a step half-built), the last working version stays live, the
broken step is kept in the file and marked in the editor, and **CHECK** says what is wrong.

Cinematics and routes are edited the same way and go live at once. Starting a recording while another
route or cinematic recording runs offers to save that one first.

Saving a route, a cinematic or a "go here" step opens what it just saved.

## Editing the files by hand

Edit the YAML and run `/journeyadmin reload`. It reloads `config.yml`, the messages, every journey,
cinematic and route, the menus and the plugin's NPCs, and says how many problems it found.

Players on a journey you changed stay on their step by its id, exactly as with an editor edit. If their
step is gone, they continue on the step now in its place, started afresh; if the journey got shorter than
that, on its last step.

The editor's own words are in `lang/<language>/editor.yml`.
