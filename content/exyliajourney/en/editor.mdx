By the end of this page you can make, change and test a journey, a cinematic or a route from `/journeyadmin` without opening a file. Every change is written straight back to the YAML file (top-level comments and key order are kept) and goes live at once, with no reload. Anything the editor does can also be written by hand; the two never disagree.

## Before you start

You need the `exyliajourney.admin` permission and to be in game: several buttons use where you stand. Run `/journeyadmin`, which opens the **Journeys** list.

## Make your first journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create journey">
    It is in the bottom row of the **Journeys** list. A short wizard asks for an id, a name, an icon, what starts it and its first step. Nothing is written until you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the rest of the steps">
    After each step a window offers to add another, to try it, or to finish. Closing the window finishes. See [Add steps](/docs/exyliajourney/steps).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Status to turn it on">
    The wizard leaves the new journey disabled until you enable it. A disabled journey never starts by itself; players already on it carry on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Test run">
    You play the live version from its first step. Nothing is saved, counted or rewarded, and `/journeyadmin test stop` ends it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The wizard's questions and trigger choices are on [Create a journey](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys). In the Journeys list, a journey the plugin cannot load shows as a barrier with the reason; clicking it opens the editor to fix it.

## Check that it works

The **Status** button on the journey's page shows its state, whether it is live, how many players are on it now, and a **Check** line with the problems found in the file. A step the plugin cannot read is marked **Incomplete** in the step list, with its problems in the hover text. Click it to fix it.

## The screens

Left-click opens, right-click does the second action noted below, and shift-clicks move things.

```
Journeys
├── Create journey · Cinematics · Routes · Settings
└── Journey
    ├── Status                      on / off
    ├── Steps
    │   └── Step
    │       ├── Objective           type and its fields
    │       ├── Guidance            panel, beacon, waypoint, glow, trail, target, cinematic
    │       ├── Text & timing       title, description, hint, hint after, time limit, skippable
    │       ├── Effects & rewards   on start, on complete, rewards
    │       ├── Duplicate step
    │       ├── Test from here
    │       ├── Preview as a player
    │       └── Delete
    ├── Start & access              triggers, permission, condition, group
    ├── Rewards & effects           rewards, on start, on complete
    ├── Settings                    name, description, icon, priority, repeatable, cooldown, skippable
    ├── Funnel                      the journey's stats
    ├── Test run
    ├── Copy journey
    └── Delete
```

<Details title="Journeys, journey hub and triggers">
In the **Journeys** list, left-click a journey to edit it and right-click to see its [funnel](/docs/exyliajourney/analytics). The bottom row holds **Create journey**, **Cinematics**, **Routes** and **Settings** (the plugin settings, below).

On a journey's page:

- **Status** switches it on and off.
- **Steps**, **Start & access**, **Rewards & effects** and **Settings** open their pages.
- **Funnel** opens its stats.
- **Test run** starts the live version for you from the first step.
- **Copy journey** makes a new journey with every step and setting of this one, disabled until you enable it, and opens the copy.
- **Delete** removes the file after a confirmation; players on it stop.

**Start & access** opens **Triggers**, **Permission**, **Condition** and **Group**. **Triggers** has one card per trigger with a click per field: left-click sets a field, and on a `REGION_ENTER` shift-left sets the centre where you stand and shift-right the radius. Right-click removes a trigger, and **Add trigger** asks for its type. The trigger types are on [Create a journey](/docs/exyliajourney/journeys#triggers).
</Details>

<Details title="Steps and the step page">
The step list shows each step in order; shift-left-click moves a step earlier and shift-right-click moves it later. **Add step** opens a template picker; the "Any objective" template opens the full list of types. **Go here step** adds a step that reaches where you stand.

A step's page opens four pages:

| Page | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | The type (click to pick another), then only the fields that type uses: **Amount**, **Filter**, **Worlds**, **Place** (left-click sets where you stand), **Radius**, **Region**, **NPC** (picked from every NPC placed on the server), **Command**, **Chat text**, **Conversation**, **Cinematic**, **Condition**, **Custom key** and **Wait**. |
| **Guidance** | **Text panel** (left: on/off, right: companion/anchored), **Panel lines**, **Panel place**, **Beacon** (left: on/off, right: label, shift-left: colour), **Waypoint**, **Glow**, **Trail** (left: route, right: how far ahead, shift-right: remove), **Target**, **Cinematic**. |
| **Text & timing** | **Title**, **Description**, **Hint**, **Hint after**, **Time limit**, **Skippable**. |
| **Effects & rewards** | **On start** and **On complete** lines, and **Rewards** through ExyliaLib's reward editor. |

**Test from here** plays the journey for you starting at that step, with nothing saved, counted or rewarded. **Preview as a player** draws the step's guidance for you for 30 seconds, as a player on it sees it. Both close the menu.
</Details>

## Plugin settings

Click **Settings** at the bottom right of the **Journeys** list. The hub has three pages, each showing the current value and the default of every setting; click a setting to change it. For how the value cards behave, see [In-game settings](/docs/exylialib/config-menus).

| Page | Settings |
|---|---|
| **General** | **Language**, **Join delay** (how long after a new player joins their first journeys start), **Hint delay** (idle time before a step's hint shows, `0` turns default hints off), **Abandoned step** (time without progress that counts as abandoned in stats, `0` turns it off), **Step toast** (the frame shown when a step is done). |
| **Guidance** | **Tracker colour** (the step bar at the top of the screen), **Step text** (`PANEL`, `TRACKER` or `CHAT`), **Footprints** (`PATH` walks, `STRAIGHT` is a line, for a target with no recorded route). See [Guidance](/docs/exyliajourney/guidance). |
| **Cinematics** | **Viewer move** (viewers farther than this many blocks from the first shot are moved there, `0` never moves), **Typewriter** (characters a second for subtitles, `0` shows them whole), **Stop timeout** (a stop waiting for a click goes on after this long, `0` waits for ever). |

The rest of `config.yml` stays in the file.

## Cinematics

**Cinematics** lists every cinematic: left-click edits, right-click plays it to you. **Create cinematic** asks for an id, then walks you through it shot by shot (see [Cinematics](/docs/exyliajourney/cinematics#shot-by-shot)). A cinematic's page opens:

- **Keyframes**, **Subtitles** and **Sounds**, each a list you edit entry by entry.
- **Timeline**: keyframes, subtitles, title cards, actions, sounds and stops on one time grid, with a cell size and a preview of the page you are on.
- **Fades** (left: in, right: out), **Letterbox** and **Skippable**.
- **Pacing**: left-click switches between playing by itself and waiting for a click after every line; right-click sets the typing speed (off, then faster each click).
- **Screen**, **Title cards** and **Sky & chain**: the text style, the title cards, and the time, weather and next cinematic.
- **Add shots**: left-click adds shots at the end, shot by shot; right-click opens the advanced hotbar recorder, and **Record again** replaces the existing keyframes.
- **Watch it** and **Delete**.

Saving a cinematic from the recorder or the shot-by-shot flow opens its page. Picking a cinematic step template, or a step's cinematic, when none exists yet offers to make one first and then uses it. See [Directing a cinematic](/docs/exyliajourney/directing).

## Routes

**Routes** lists every route: left-click opens one, right-click previews it. **Record route** asks for an id, then gives you hotbar tools to walk the path, pause, take back and save. **Generate route** makes one from where you stand to a place you walk to, the way a player can walk, through a short wizard. A route's page has **Record again** (replaces it), **Extend** (moves you to its end and goes on recording), **Preview**, **Reverse** (trails following it lead the other way; saved and live at once) and **Delete**. Starting a recording while another route or cinematic recording runs offers to save that one first. See [Guidance](/docs/exyliajourney/guidance#recording-a-route).

## Edits while players are on a journey

An edited journey replaces the live one immediately, and every player on it stays where they were:

- on the **same step**, found by its id, wherever it moved, with their count kept;
- if their step was deleted, on the step **now in its place**, started afresh;
- if the journey got shorter, on its last step.

When an edit leaves the journey unreadable (a step half-built), the last working version stays live, the broken step is kept in the file and marked in the editor, and the **Check** line says what is wrong. Cinematics and routes are edited the same way and go live at once.

## Editing the files by hand

Edit the YAML and run `/journeyadmin reload`. It reloads `config.yml`, the messages, every journey, cinematic and route, the menus and the plugin's NPCs, and says how many problems it found. Players on a changed journey stay on their step by its id, exactly as with an editor edit. The editor's own words are in `lang/<language>/editor.yml`; to change menu text, see [Edit menus](/docs/exylialib/edit-menus).
