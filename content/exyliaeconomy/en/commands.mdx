Three spellings reach the same actions. `/balance`, `/pay`, `/baltop` and `/wallet` are the words every player already knows and always use the default currency. `/economy` does everything for any currency. Each stored currency also gets a command of its own, such as `/shards`. What changes somebody else's money lives in `/economyadmin`.

A player argument accepts anybody the network knows, online, offline or on another server. A currency argument suggests only the currencies the sender may use. Amounts are typed as `100`, `2.5k` or `1m`.

## One command per currency

Each stored currency has its own command, named in the currency editor under **Commands** (see [Create a currency](/docs/exyliaeconomy/currencies)). You can switch the command off or rename it there.

## Staff commands

<Commands group="admin" />

<Callout type="warning" title="Reset, giveall and rollback ask first">
`reset`, `giveall` and `rollback` do nothing until you confirm. Players get a confirmation, and the console adds `confirm` at the end of the same command. `take` is refused when a player on this server holds less than the amount.
</Callout>
