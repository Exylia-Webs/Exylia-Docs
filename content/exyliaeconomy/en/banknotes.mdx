A banknote is money printed as paper. `/withdraw <amount> [currency]` takes the amount from the player
and hands them a note; right-clicking the note, or `/deposit` while holding it, pays it back to whoever
redeems it. Notes can be given, dropped, traded between players and stored in chests like any item.

## Which currencies print notes

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaEconomy/config.yml"
banknotes:
  - default
  - shards
```

Currency ids, with `default` meaning the default currency. Only stored currencies print notes; an item,
experience or display-only currency on this list is refused. An empty list turns banknotes off. A fresh
server writes `[default]`.

## Withdrawing

`/withdraw <amount> [currency]` needs `exyliaeconomy.withdraw`, given to everyone by default. Without a
currency it uses the default one. It is refused, before anything is taken, when:

- the currency is not on the `banknotes` list — *"… cannot be withdrawn as a banknote."*;
- the amount is not a positive amount, or rounds to nothing in the currency's decimals;
- the player's balance is still loading;
- they cannot afford it;
- they have no free inventory slot — *"Free a slot in your inventory first."*

The note is recorded in the database first, then the money is taken and the note handed over in the
same tick. If the money cannot be taken after all, the record is discarded and nothing is handed out.
Two withdrawals closer than half a second are one.

## What a note is

The item is drawn from the `banknote` section of `messages.yml`:

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaEconomy/lang/en/messages.yml"
banknote:
  material: PAPER
  name: '{primary}&lBANKNOTE &8[{success}%amount%&8]'
  lore:
    - ''
    - '{secondary}Information:'
    - ' {letters_black}▎ {letters}Currency {letters_black}» {highlight}%currency%'
    - ' {letters_black}▎ {letters}Signed by {letters_black}» {highlight}%issuer%'
    - ' {letters_black}▎ {letters}Issued {letters_black}» {info}%date%'
    - ''
    - '{warning}➥ Right-click to redeem'
    - ''
```

`%amount%` is the formatted amount, `%currency%` the currency's plural name, `%issuer%` who printed it,
`%date%` the day it was printed. The note always glows and never stacks.

What a note is worth is not on the item. The item carries only the note's id; the amount, currency and
issuer are a row in `exylia_banknotes`, written before the money is taken. Renaming or editing the item
changes nothing.

## Redeeming

Right-click with a note in either hand, at the air or at a block, or run `/deposit` while holding one
(main hand first, then off hand). Right-clicking needs no permission; `/deposit` needs
`exyliaeconomy.use`. Either needs the currency's own permission, when it names one.

The note leaves the hand **before** anything else happens, then its row is claimed in the database, then
the money is paid:

| What the database says | What the player sees |
|---|---|
| Not redeemed yet | *"Redeemed a banknote of …"* — the money is theirs. |
| Already redeemed, or no such note | *"This banknote was already redeemed or is not valid."* — the item is gone. |
| The currency refused the payment (its ceiling) | The note comes back, unredeemed, with the reason. |
| The database did not answer | The note comes back: *"The banknote could not be processed right now."* |

<Callout type="tip" title="A copied note pays once">
A duplicated stack, a creative clone, the same note redeemed on two servers at once: only one claim on
the row can win, so the money is paid exactly once on the whole network and every other copy simply
disappears.
</Callout>

## Per-server currencies

A note of a currency that is **not** shared across servers is a balance on the server that printed it.
Redeeming it on any other server is refused and the note comes back: *"This banknote can only be
redeemed on the server that printed it."* A note of a shared currency redeems anywhere.

## Kept out of crafting and trading

A note is paper, and vanilla would happily turn paper into books, maps and rockets, or trade it to a
librarian. Any inventory holding a note refuses to produce a result:

- crafting tables, the inventory grid and crafters;
- anvils, grindstones, smithing tables, looms, cartography tables and stonecutters;
- villager trades.

## In the ledger

A note's two halves are two ledger lines: `note:withdraw` on the issuer, `note:redeem` on whoever
redeemed it. A rollback never reverts either, because the other half is a note somebody may still hold.
See [Ledger](/docs/exyliaeconomy/ledger).

<Callout type="note" title="Fixing a stuck note">
If a payment is refused and the database then fails to mark the note unredeemed again, the note comes
back to the player but stays marked redeemed, and the console names its id. Set `redeemed_at` to `0` on
that row of `exylia_banknotes` to make it redeemable again. A player who leaves before a refused note
can be handed back is logged the same way, with the row to pay by hand.
</Callout>
