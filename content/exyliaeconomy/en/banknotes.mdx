A banknote is money printed as paper. Players run `/withdraw <amount> [currency]` and get a note they can give, drop, trade and keep in chests like any item. Whoever right-clicks it, or runs `/deposit` while holding it, gets the money.

## Before you start

You need a stored currency, see [Create a currency](/docs/exyliaeconomy/currencies). Only stored currencies print notes; item, experience and display-only currencies have no switch. A new currency has banknotes off, so a fresh server prints none until you turn one on.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the currency">
    Run `/economyadmin` and left-click the currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn banknotes on">
    Click **Payments**, then **Banknotes**. It now reads on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let players use it">
    `/withdraw` needs `exyliaeconomy.withdraw`, given to everyone by default. Redeeming needs no permission, see [Permissions](/docs/exyliaeconomy/permissions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Print one">
    With some balance, run `/withdraw 100 <currency>`. You get a paper item named **BANKNOTE** with the amount; without a currency word the default currency is used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cash it">
    Right-click with the note in either hand. You read *"Redeemed a banknote of …"* and the balance is back. `/deposit` does the same while you hold it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Going further

### When a withdrawal is refused

It is refused, before anything is taken, when the currency's **Banknotes** switch is off (*"… cannot be withdrawn as a banknote."*), when the amount is not positive or rounds to nothing in the currency's decimals, when the balance is still loading, or when the player cannot afford it.

With a full inventory the withdrawal still works: the note is queued and handed over when a slot frees up or on the next join. Two withdrawals closer than half a second are one.

### What a note is

The item is drawn from the `banknote` section of `plugins/ExyliaEconomy/lang/en/messages.yml` (`lang/es/messages.yml` for Spanish), so you can change its material, name and lore. See [colours](/docs/exylialib/colours) and [messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaEconomy/lang/en/messages.yml"
banknote:
  material: PAPER
  name: '{primary}&lBANKNOTE &8[{success}%amount%&8]'
  lore:
    - ''
    - '{secondary}Information:'
    - ' {letters_black}▎ {letters}Currency {letters_black}» {highlight}%currency%'
    - ' {letters_black}▎ {letters}Signed by {letters_black}» {highlight}%issuer%'
    - ' {letters_black}▎ {letters}Issued {letters_black}» {info}%date%'
    - ''
    - '{warning}➥ Right-click to redeem'
    - ''
```

`%amount%` is the formatted amount, `%currency%` the currency's plural name, `%issuer%` who printed it and `%date%` the day it was printed. The note always glows and never stacks.

What a note is worth is not on the item. The item carries only the note's id; the amount, currency and issuer are a row in `exylia_banknotes`, written before the money is taken. Renaming or editing the item changes nothing.

### Redeeming

Right-click with a note in either hand, at the air or at a block, or run `/deposit` while holding one (main hand first, then off hand). Right-clicking needs no permission; `/deposit` needs `exyliaeconomy.use`. Either needs the currency's own permission when it names one.

The note leaves the hand **before** anything else happens, then its row is claimed in the database, then the money is paid:

| What the database says | What the player sees |
|---|---|
| Not redeemed yet | *"Redeemed a banknote of …"*: the money is theirs. |
| Already redeemed, or no such note | *"This banknote was already redeemed or is not valid."* The item is gone. |
| The currency refused the payment (its ceiling) | The note comes back, unredeemed, with the reason. |
| The database did not answer | The note comes back: *"The banknote could not be processed right now."* |

<Callout type="tip" title="A copied note pays once">
A duplicated stack, a creative clone, the same note redeemed on two servers at once: only one claim on the row can win, so the money is paid exactly once on the whole network and every other copy disappears.
</Callout>

### Per-server currencies

A note remembers which balance it was printed from. A currency kept per server or per network group (see [Network](/docs/exyliaeconomy/network)) only redeems its notes where that balance is the same: anywhere else the redeem is refused and the note comes back with *"This banknote can only be redeemed on the server that printed it."* A note of a global currency redeems anywhere.

### Kept out of crafting and trading

A note is paper, and vanilla would turn paper into books, maps and rockets, or trade it to a librarian. Any inventory holding a note refuses to produce a result: crafting tables, the inventory grid, crafters, anvils, grindstones, smithing tables, looms, cartography tables, stonecutters and villager trades.

### In the ledger

A note's two halves are two ledger lines: `note:withdraw` on the issuer and `note:redeem` on whoever redeemed it. A rollback never reverts either, because the other half is a note somebody may still hold. See [Ledger](/docs/exyliaeconomy/ledger).

<Details title="Fixing a stuck note">
The note is recorded in the database first, then the money is taken and the note handed over in the same tick. If the money cannot be taken after all, the record is discarded and nothing is handed out.

If a payment is refused and the database then fails to mark the note unredeemed again, the note comes back to the player but stays marked redeemed, and the console names its id. Set `redeemed_at` to `0` on that row of `exylia_banknotes` to make it redeemable again. A player who leaves before a refused note can be handed back is logged the same way, with the row to pay by hand.
</Details>
