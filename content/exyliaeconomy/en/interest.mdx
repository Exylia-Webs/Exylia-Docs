title: "Interest"
description: "A percentage of a balance paid at every interval: the settings on a currency, when a payout happens, who earns it, and why nobody is paid twice."
---

Interest pays a percentage of a stored currency's balance at a fixed interval. Nothing is paid until a
currency has a rate: in `/economyadmin`, open the currency, then **Interest**. A new currency has none.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Rate | `0` | Percent of the balance per payout, `0` to `100`. `0` pays nothing. |
| Every | `1h` | How often: `30m`, `1h`, `1d`. At least one minute; anything shorter is raised to it. |
| Most per payout | `0` | The most one payout gives, typed like an amount (`500`, `2k`). `0` for no cap. |
| Interest while offline | off | Off pays the players online. On also pays every balance of players who are not. |

Each currency has its own, so `dollars` can pay hourly and `shards` never. Item, experience and
display-only currencies earn nothing. A server that came from the old `interest` block of `config.yml` finds
it already copied onto its currencies; see [Migration](/docs/exyliaeconomy/migration#an-old-configyml).

## When a payout happens

Time is cut into slots of the interval, counted from the Unix epoch, so a `1h` currency pays at the top
of every hour and a `1d` currency at midnight UTC — whatever time the server started. The plugin looks
every 30 seconds, so a payout lands within half a minute of the boundary.

A slot is never paid on the start that finds it: the first payout is at the first boundary after the
server comes up. A boundary that passed while the server was down is not paid later.

## What a payout is

The balance at that moment times `rate` percent, cut to the currency's decimals and capped at `max`. A
balance of zero earns nothing, and an amount that rounds to nothing is not paid.

The player is told *"Interest +12.50 · next in 59m ⌚"*. Empty `interest-paid` in `messages.yml` to pay
silently. The ledger records it as `interest`.

A deposit the ceiling refuses is not paid, and that slot is not tried again.

## Who earns it

With **Interest while offline** off, every player online on each server who:

- has `exyliaeconomy.interest`, granted to everyone by default;
- may use the currency, when it names a permission;
- has their balance loaded.

With it on, every server additionally sweeps the currency's whole balance table and pays
every player who is not online there, queued for whoever holds them. A permission cannot be checked for
somebody offline, so **every** stored balance earns, `exyliaeconomy.interest` or not.

<Callout type="warning" title="Interest while offline pays everybody">
Taking `exyliaeconomy.interest` away from a group only stops their payouts while they are online. With
**Interest while offline** on their offline balances keep earning. Leave it off unless that is what you want.
</Callout>

## Nobody is paid twice

Each payout is claimed in the database first, per player and slot, in `exylia_economy_claims`. Only one
claim can win:

- a player connected to two servers of a shared currency at once is paid once;
- a restart in the middle of a slot never pays that slot again;
- with offline interest on, every server sweeping the same table pays each balance once, and a server
  that crashes halfway leaves the rest to the others.

A currency kept per server or per group is one balance per server or group, so each of those pays its own.
Claims are deleted two days after their slot.
