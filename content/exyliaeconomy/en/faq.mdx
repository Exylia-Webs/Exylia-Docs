## Is it free?

Yes. ExyliaEconomy is free and open source, under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Download it from
[Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/plugin/exyliaeconomy); the source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaEconomy). There is no licence key and nothing to buy.

## I used the economy of ExyliaSurvivalCore. What changes?

The automatic import was removed in 1.2.1, so a server still on the module moves over with an earlier
release first, then updates. The commands keep their names, and only the placeholders change prefix, from
`%exyliasurvivalcore_economy_…%` to `%exyliaeconomy_economy_…%`; the old `exyliasurvivalcore.economy…`
permissions no longer grant anything, so grant the `exyliaeconomy.…` nodes. See
[Migration](/docs/exyliaeconomy/migration).

## Will my shop / jobs / auction plugin see the money?

Yes, if it uses Vault and Vault is installed. One stored currency — `dollars` on a fresh server — is
published as the Vault economy. If EssentialsX or CMI is also installed, it keeps serving Vault and your
currency sits beneath it; make sure **Vault on top** (on by default) is on in `/economyadmin` → **Economy settings**, or remove the
other economy, and import its balances first with `/economyadmin import vault dollars`. See
[Vault](/docs/exyliaeconomy/vault).

## Can I undo what a player did?

`/economyadmin rollback <player> 1h` reverts their movements of the last hour, line by line, after a
confirmation. Payments, exchanges and banknotes are left out because their other side is somebody else's
balance or a note still in circulation. See [Ledger](/docs/exyliaeconomy/ledger#rollback).

## Where do I get help?

On the Exylia Discord: [discord.exylia.net](https://discord.exylia.net). Bug reports and feature requests
are also welcome as issues on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaEconomy).
