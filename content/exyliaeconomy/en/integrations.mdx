## Other Exylia plugins

ExyliaLib talks to economies and keeps none of its own. ExyliaEconomy is one of them: every stored, item
and experience currency registers with the library's economy facade, so a shop, a market, a kit, a reward
or a mission in any Exylia plugin prices and pays in them by id, with nothing written for this plugin in
particular.

What that gives every plugin on the server:

- **One list of currencies**, in the sort order set in `/economyadmin`, for every currency picker.
- **One permission rule**: a currency that names a permission is hidden and refused for anyone without it,
  in every plugin that asks.
- **One look**: name, symbol, decimals and format, including the display-only overlays for `vault` and
  `points`.
- **One ledger**: whatever reason a plugin pays with — `shop:sell`, `market:fee` — is a ledger line, shown
  with the label in `messages.yml`.
- **One event**: every change to a stored balance fires ExyliaLib's `BalanceChangeEvent`, once, on the
  server that wrote it — so a change queued on one server and applied on another is announced once, where
  it lands.

ExyliaSurvivalCore loads after ExyliaEconomy for this reason: its shop, market, auctions, orders, trades,
boosters and rewards all pay through the facade.

## Vault plugins

Everything that only speaks Vault — ShopGUIPlus, EconomyShopGUI, ChestShop, QuickShop, Jobs,
zAuctionHouse, AuctionHouse and the rest — runs on the stored currency published to Vault. See
[Vault](/docs/exyliaeconomy/vault).

`plugin.yml` lists these under `loadbefore`, so ExyliaEconomy enables first and they find the Vault economy
during their own enable: ExyliaSurvivalCore, ExyliaMines, ShopGUIPlus, EconomyShopGUI, ChestShop,
QuickShop, Jobs, zAuctionHouseV3 and AuctionHouse.

## PlaceholderAPI

With PlaceholderAPI installed, every placeholder is exposed under the plugin's identifier,
`%exyliaeconomy_…%`, with no expansion to download. Without it, they still work inside every Exylia
plugin's own files. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaeconomy/placeholders).

## ExyliaAnalytics

When ExyliaAnalytics is installed, each server hands it the money supply of every stored currency: the sum
of every balance and how many players hold any, read from the database a minute after the start and every
ten minutes after that. A per-server currency is reported under its per-server key. The individual
movements reach ExyliaAnalytics through the balance-change events.

See [ExyliaAnalytics' economy page](/docs/exyliaanalytics/economy) for what the dashboard does with them.

## Folia

`folia-supported: true`. Every message, menu and inventory change runs on the thread that owns the player,
the periodic sweeps run off the main thread, and nothing waits on the database on a game thread.
