Rewards pay a mix of coins, gems, a boost potion and roulette spins. Coins are written in **minutes of income**: a prize worth 15 minutes pays what the player earns in 15 minutes. `min-income-per-minute` in `config.yml` (20 by default) is the least it pays per minute, so a new player's first rewards are not empty. Prizes are defined in `rewards.yml`.

What each player has is kept in their profile, so it survives rebirths. Days start at the `resets` hour of `config.yml`.

## Gifts

Gifts open as the player plays. Idle time does not count. Each gift has an `after` in active minutes played today. The default list runs from 1 minute (2 minutes of income) to 180 minutes (30 minutes of income, 8 gems and a Growth boost of 30 minutes). `/clicker gifts` opens them.

## Daily streak

Players claim once a day. The default has seven days, and the seventh pays the most, then it starts again. With `grace` on, one missed day a week does not break the streak. A player who joins with a streak reward waiting is told. Open it with `/clicker streak`.

## Roulette

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| `free-per-day` | 1 free spin a day |
| `minutes-per-spin` | An active minute count that earns a spin, 30 |
| `max-earned` | Spins earned this way per day, 4 |
| `gem-price` | A spin bought with gems, 10. 0 means not for sale. |

It has up to seven slices, each with a `weight` shown to players as odds: Coins, Gems, Big Coins, Luck Potion, Spin Again, Gem Pile and Jackpot by default. `/clicker roulette` opens it.

## Codes

Codes are created by staff and redeemed by players with `/clicker code <code>`.

```text
/clickeradmin code create welcome 30 5 100 7d
```

This creates `welcome`, worth 30 minutes of income and 5 gems, with 100 uses, expiring in 7 days. Uses and expiry are optional: no uses means unlimited. Names are lower case letters, digits, `_` and `-`, 2 to 32 long. A use is taken safely even when two servers redeem at once. List codes with `/clickeradmin code list` and remove one with `/clickeradmin code delete <code>`. The admin panel also has a Codes screen.

## AFK area

Players who stand in the AFK area earn `afk.gems` every `afk.every` (1 gem every 5 minutes by default). Set the area with `/clickeradmin afk area`.
