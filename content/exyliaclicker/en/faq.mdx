## Can players steal from each other's farms?

No. Every farm is drawn to its owner alone, so players can share a zone's space without seeing each other's crops.

## Which servers does it run on?

Paper 1.21.1 and newer, and Folia 1.21.4 and newer. It needs PacketEvents to draw the farms.

## Is it a premium plugin?

Yes. It is loaded through a licence key, and you set it up in [Installation](/docs/exyliaclicker/installation).

## Can I make my own game?

Yes. Copy `themes/harvest`, change the files and set `theme`. See [Theme files](/docs/exyliaclicker/theme-files).

## Can several servers share one progress?

Yes. Set `profiles` in `config.yml` to `global`, or to a network group. Use the same `resets` on each.

## What do 15K, 2.5M and 500T mean?

Thousand, million and trillion. Amounts in the plugin, and the ones you type in commands and screens, use that notation.

## How do I reset a season?

Use ExyliaLib's wipes, or reset one farmer from `/clickeradmin`. See [Admin panel](/docs/exyliaclicker/admin-panel).

## How do I know my prices are right?

Run `/clickeradmin simulate`. See [Balancing](/docs/exyliaclicker/balancing).
