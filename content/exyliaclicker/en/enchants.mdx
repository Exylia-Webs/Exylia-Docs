Enchants raise the tool's stats. They stay through tool tiers and through rebirths. `enchants.yml` defines them.

## Farmer XP and levels

The XP bar fills as players harvest, and shows on the vanilla XP bar for that player only. The level is spent at the table. `level-xp` is the XP for level 1 (50 by default) and each next level needs `level-growth` times more (1.12).

## The table

`/clicker enchant` opens the table. It offers `offers` enchants (3), and each costs its level in Farmer levels times `cost` (1). Every `bookshelf-every` rebirths (1) count as a bookshelf, up to 15, and more bookshelves offer more levels at once.

## Enchants

Each enchant names the stat it raises and the amount per level (flat, or a percent with `%`):

| Enchant | Stat |
|---|---|
| Efficiency | Harvest speed |
| Fortune | Yield |
| Luck of the Field | Luck |
| Sweeping Edge | Area |
| Keen Edge | Crit chance |
| Wisdom | Farmer XP gain |
| Treasure Hunter | Treasure |

Three specials come only from books: Midas, Chain and Shockwave. Chain has a one in four chance to take a neighbour of the same kind with the harvest. Shockwave fires every 20 hits.

Other fields: `max-level`, `rebirths-per-level` and `rebirths-from` (from that many rebirths on, each level needs more rebirths), and `weight`, how often the table offers it (0 means books only).

## Books

One in `book-chance` harvests drops a book (1/1500 by default, before luck). The book is a random table enchant by weight, with the specials at a small fixed weight. `/clicker books` opens the player's books.

## The anvil

The table stops at `max-level`. The anvil goes past it with books: each level past the maximum costs gems (`anvil-gems`, 25 by default), and each next level costs twice as much. Open it with `/clicker anvil`.
