Every zone has one helper, bought with coins once the zone is open. A helper harvests the zone for its owner: the player sees it work in the zone they stand in, and its harvests are counted in every other zone.

## Offline work

While the owner is away the helper keeps working at `offline-rate` of its pace (0.3 by default) for `offline-hours` (8 by default). The Night Shift perk adds hours. When the player returns, they see a summary of what the helpers brought in.

## Tracks and levels

A helper has four tracks. A helper's level is the sum of its tracks.

| Track | What it raises |
|---|---|
| Speed | Hits a second. |
| Power | Percent of the owner's power per hit. |
| Reach | Nodes it tends, nearest the middle of the zone. |
| Cargo | Items it holds when the backpack is full. |

Each track's price is its cost times the helper's `cost-scale`. Past every track's top, **stars** add a percent to speed, power and cargo. Milestones give bonuses at set levels (`speed`, `power`, `cargo`, `all` or the helper's own skill).

## Skills

Each default helper has a skill:

| Helper | Zone | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| Allay | Meadow | Rolls rare drops too. |
| Sniffer | Hayfield | Every 25th harvest finds everything. |
| Iron Golem | Pumpkin Patch | Hits crit at the owner's odds, three times as hard. |
| Bee | Mushroom Grove | 10% of what it harvests comes back Golden. |
| Piglin | Nether Fields | Barters: twice the barters. |
| Enderman | End Gardens | Tends the whole zone. |
| Breeze | Moon Garden | Hits five nodes at once. |
| Stellar Allay | Nebula | 0.5% of what it harvests comes back Galactic. |

In `helpers.yml` each helper sets its mob (any spawnable entity type), a job line players read, its price and luck, how many nodes each hit takes, whether it barters and whether it flies.

## On rebirth

A rebirth keeps the helper but starts its upgrade levels over. The Loyal Helpers perk keeps a percent of the levels.

## Commands

`/clicker helper` opens the helpers. `show`, `buy`, `upgrade` and `star` take a zone, and `upgrade` also takes a track and an amount. See [Commands](/docs/exyliaclicker/commands).
