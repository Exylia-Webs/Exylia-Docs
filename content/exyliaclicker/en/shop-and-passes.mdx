`shop.yml` of the theme lists what your store sells. Players see it in `/clicker shop` (passes) and `/clicker boosts` (potions). Set `store-url` in `config.yml` to link your store from the pass shop.

## Passes

A pass is permanent. Each sets the stat it raises and the amount, flat (`3`), a percent (`30%`) or a multiplier (`x2`). The defaults:

| Pass | Effect |
|---|---|
| 2x Coins | Every sale pays twice the coins. |
| Lucky | Half again the odds of golden crops and finds. |
| Auto Clicker | Harvests 3 ripe crops every second by itself. |
| Big Pack | The backpack holds twice the items. |
| Swift | The tool lands 30% more hits a second. |
| Auto Sell | A full backpack sells itself. |
| Remote Sell | `/clicker sell` has no cooldown. |
| Multi-Rebirth | Rebirth several times at once. |
| VIP Zone | Opens the zones that name this pass, such as the Golden Orchard. |

Passes with special work leave `stat` empty: `auto_sell`, `remote_sell`, `multi_rebirth` and `vip_zone`. `auto_hits` is a stat: how many ripe crops the Auto Clicker harvests a second. A disabled pass does not appear.

### Give a pass

```text
/clickeradmin pass give Steve vip_zone
/clickeradmin pass take Steve vip_zone
```

Gives work for offline players and from any server, because what a player got is kept in one table for the network. A give from another server shows when the player joins or opens the shop. A pass is also owned with the permission `exyliaclicker.pass.<id>`. No one has that permission by default, not even ops, so an op does not own every pass.

## Boost potions

A potion adds its stat while it is drunk, for its minutes. The defaults are the Coin Potion (2x coins), the Luck Potion (2x luck) and the Growth Potion (100% faster growth). Players hold potions of several sizes in minutes and drink the shortest or the longest. A running boost shows as a potion icon.

```text
/clickeradmin boost give Steve coins 30 2
/clickeradmin boost global coins 15
```

The first gives two 30 minute Coin Potions. The second starts a boost for everyone on this server, with a boss bar that shows the one with the most time left.
