## Rebirth

A rebirth gives up the run: coins, upgrades, tool and zones start over. The player keeps the coin multiplier, tokens, perks, enchants, the index, helpers (their upgrade levels start over unless Loyal Helpers keeps some), and your rewards progress.

`rebirth.yml` sets the formula.

- **Price**: the first rebirth costs `cost` (10M by default). Each next one costs `cost-growth` times more (1 by default, so none), and the nth also costs n to the `cost-power` times more (4.5 by default: the 10th costs about 32K times the first).
- **Coin multiplier**: each rebirth adds `multiplier` (0.25 by default, so R4 earns x2), and also multiplies the whole multiplier by `multiplier-growth` (1.06). Set the growth to 1 to turn that off.
- **Tokens**: each rebirth pays `tokens` (1) plus one more every `tokens-every` rebirths (5).

`/clicker rebirth` opens the screen. `/clicker rebirth <count>` rebirths several times at once, up to what the Multi-Rebirth perk or pass allows.

### Milestones

Rebirths a player needs before each automation turns on. They are earned, never sold.

| Milestone | Default |
|---|---|
| Upgrade buyer | 3 |
| Remembered zones | 10 |
| Auto rebirth | 25 |

## Perks

Tokens buy lasting perks in the token shop (`/clicker perks`). A perk with a `stat` raises it by `per-level` for each level. These perks do what their id says:

| Id | Effect |
|---|---|
| `head_start` | Each run starts with `per-level` coins, times 10 for every level past the first. |
| `savings` | Keeps `per-level` percent of the coins through a rebirth. |
| `starter_tool` | Each run starts with that many tool tiers bought. |
| `token_bonus` | `per-level` percent more tokens per rebirth. |
| `multi_rebirth` | One more rebirth at once per level. |
| `loyal_helpers` | Keeps `per-level` percent of helper levels through a rebirth. |
| `auto_sell` | The backpack sells itself when it fills. |
| `night_shift` | Helpers work `per-level` more hours while their owner is away. |

The default shop also has Riches, Green Thumb+, Fortune and Deep Pockets. A `max-level` of 0 means no maximum.

## Cards

After each rebirth the player picks one of three cards for that run: for example Rapid Growth, Golden Touch, Iron Start, Merchant, Big Pack or Strong Arms. Every `rare-every` rebirths (5) one of the three is a rare card, kept for good, such as Eternal Luck.

## Eras

An era changes how the map looks for a range of rebirths. Each player sees their own. `eras.yml` sets for each era:

- `rebirths`: rebirths needed to reach it. The first era starts at 0.
- `time`: the time of day the player sees, in ticks (6000 noon, 12500 sunset, 18000 night). -1 follows the world.
- `weather`: clear, rain, or empty to follow the world.
- `particle`: a particle that drifts around the player now and then.
- `swaps`: blocks drawn as others for this player, for example oak planks as spruce planks. Only blocks of the same shape and feel are swapped; the rest are left out and logged. Players' items are not swapped.
- `scene`: a camera shot played when the player reaches the era.

The default eras are Homestead (0), Village (5), Kingdom (10), Copper Age (20), Crystal (35) and Celestial (50).

<Callout type="tip" title="Build once, preview every era">
Build the map in the first era's blocks. Then preview each era on yourself with `/clickeradmin era <era>`, and run `/clickeradmin era` to go back to your own.
</Callout>
