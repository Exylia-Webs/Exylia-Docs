ExyliaClicker is an endless clicker and farming game inside Minecraft. Players hit crops, fill a backpack, sell it for coins, buy upgrades, open new zones and then **rebirth**: they give up the run for a lasting multiplier and start again, faster. Numbers keep growing past what a normal game shows, written as 15K, 2.5M or 500T.

It supports Paper 1.21.1 and newer, and Folia 1.21.4 and newer.

<Callout type="note" title="Not released yet">
ExyliaClicker 1.0.0 is documented from its source. It has not been released, so details can still change.
</Callout>

## Every farm is drawn for one player

Each player sees their own crops. The nodes they hit are sent to them alone, so a hundred players can stand in the same zone and none of them takes a crop from another. The same goes for the golden chicken and the treasure villager that cross the farm now and then, and for the **era**, which changes how the whole map looks for each player depending on their rebirths.

## How a run plays

1. Hit crops in the first zone. Each ripe crop harvested goes into the backpack.
2. Sell the backpack at a sell point.
3. Spend coins on upgrades, a better tool and the next zone.
4. When a rebirth is worth it, rebirth: coins, upgrades, tool and zones start over, and you keep a coin multiplier, tokens, your index, your enchants and your helpers.
5. Spend tokens on perks, pick a card for the next run, and go faster.

## What you need

- A Paper 1.21.1+ or Folia 1.21.4+ server.
- [ExyliaLib](/docs/exylialib/introduction), which ExyliaClicker cannot start without.
- PacketEvents, which ExyliaLib needs to draw the per-player crops.
- PlaceholderAPI is optional. Install it to use the [placeholders](/docs/exyliaclicker/placeholders) in other plugins.
- A place to build the zones. See [First steps](/docs/exyliaclicker/first-steps).
