Coins buy three things during a run: upgrades, tools and zones. A rebirth resets all three. See [Rebirth, perks and eras](/docs/exyliaclicker/rebirth-and-eras).

## Stats

Every player has stats. `upgrades.yml` gives each one a starting value and lists the upgrades that raise them.

| Stat | What it does | Starts at |
|---|---|---|
| `power` | Damage per hit. | 0 |
| `harvest_speed` | Clicks per second that land. | 0 |
| `crit_chance` | Percent chance of a critical hit. | 5 |
| `crit_power` | How many times a critical hit hits. | 2 |
| `yield` | Items per harvest. | 1 |
| `double_drop` | Percent chance of a double drop. | 0 |
| `luck` | Times the odds of variants and rare drops. | 1 |
| `growth_speed` | Percent faster regrowth. 100 regrows twice as fast. | 0 |
| `farm_size` | Nodes per zone. | 9 |
| `backpack` | Items the backpack holds. | 100 |
| `sell_bonus` | Times what a sale pays. | 1 |
| `area` | The square a hit reaches. | 0 |
| `xp_gain` | Farmer XP per harvest. | 1 |
| `gem_chance` | Percent of harvests that drop a gem. | 0.2 |
| `treasure` | Percent more rare finds. | 0 |
| `midas` | Percent more Golden and Rainbow nodes. | 0 |
| `coin_multiplier` | Times what a sale pays. Rebirths raise it. | 1 |

## Upgrades

An upgrade names the stat it raises, the amount **per level** and its **cost**. `per-level` is flat (`25`), a percent of the stat (`4%`) or a multiplier. The cost is the first level's price and the number each next price is multiplied by (`60` and `1.16`).

- **Soft cap**: levels up to it count in full. Past it each level counts less, by `falloff`. With a falloff of 0.5, 100 levels past the cap count as 10. A soft cap of 0 counts every level in full.
- **Zone**: the zone a player must have opened first. Empty means from the start.
- **Icon**: the item the upgrade is drawn as.

Players buy with `/clicker upgrade <upgrade> [amount]`. The amount can be a number or `max`, which buys every level the coins pay for.

## Tools

`tools.yml` is the tool ladder. Every player starts with the first tier and buys the next with coins. The default ladder has eight tiers: Bare Hands, Wooden Hoe, Stone Hoe, Iron Hoe, Golden Hoe, Diamond Hoe, Netherite Hoe and Star Scythe.

Each tier sets **power** (damage per hit), **harvest speed**, **area** (the square hit around the clicked node: 1 is the node alone, 3 a 3x3, 5 a 5x5), a **price**, an optional **zone** to open first and the **rebirths** needed.

Past the last tier, players buy **stars**. Each star adds a percent of power to the tool.

## Zones

`zones.yml` lists the zones in the order players reach them. The first one is open from the start.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `price` | Coins to open it. |
| `rebirths` | Rebirths needed first. |
| `multiplier` | What everything harvested there is worth, times its base value. |
| `luck` | Multiplies the odds of variants and rare drops there. |
| `nodes` | Which nodes it grows, with weights where several share a floor. |
| `pass` | A pass from `shop.yml` the player must own. Empty for none. |

The default theme has nine zones: Meadow, Hayfield, Golden Orchard (needs the VIP Zone pass), Pumpkin Patch, Mushroom Grove, Nether Fields, End Gardens, Moon Garden and Nebula. A zone a player has not opened is hidden behind a wall they cannot pass. Opening one asks for confirmation.

Set where a zone is with `/clickeradmin zone area <zone>`, as in [First steps](/docs/exyliaclicker/first-steps).
