ExyliaClicker has three permissions. Players can play without any setup.

<Permissions />

## Give a pass with a permission

A pass from `shop.yml` is owned when the player has `exyliaclicker.pass.<pass>`, for example for a rank on your store or a Discord booster. The pass id is the one in `shop.yml`. With LuckPerms:

```text
/lp group vip permission set exyliaclicker.pass.vip_zone true
```

A pass given by `/clickeradmin pass give` or bought in the shop is kept on the account; the permission works alongside it. These permissions are false for everyone, operators included, so a group never gets a pass by accident.

## Let staff use the admin panel

Give a staff group `exyliaclicker.admin`. It opens `/clickeradmin`, the settings screens and the pace tools. The web editor needs ExyliaLib's own `exylialib.editor`, and wipes need `exylialib.admin.wipe`.

## Stop a group from playing

Set `exyliaclicker.play` to false for that group. They can no longer use `/clicker`.

<Callout type="info" title="Operators">
Operators have `exyliaclicker.admin` and `exyliaclicker.play`. They do not own passes unless you give them one.
</Callout>
