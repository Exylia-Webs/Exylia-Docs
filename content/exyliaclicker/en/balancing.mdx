Two tools tell you whether the game is paced well: a simulator that plays your theme through before a single player has, and a funnel that counts what real farmers did. Use the simulator every time you edit prices, multipliers or rebirth costs.

## Pace simulator

`/clickeradmin simulate` (or **Growth**, then **Pace**) plays the theme through as a model farmer and reports in chat how long each step takes. It runs in the background, so the server does not lag, and only one run goes at a time. The console can run it too.

The model farmer clicks 6 times a second, walks 10 seconds to sell when the backpack is full, plays 2 hours a day, buys whatever brings the next rebirth closest and rebirths the moment it can. It stops after 400 hours of play. It reads your current theme files and the slots of the zones you have set, so set your zone areas first.

The report compares four medians with the pace the game is designed for. A value inside its range is green, outside is orange, and the first line says **on the plan's pace** or **off the plan's pace**.

| Step | Wanted |
|---|---|
| A purchase (an upgrade) | Every 10 to 30 seconds. Counted until the Upgrade Buyer milestone, after which upgrades buy themselves. |
| A tool or a zone | Every 3 to 8 minutes |
| A rebirth | Every 20 to 60 minutes |
| An era | Every 1 to 3 days of play |

The last line tells you on which day the last era is reached, or that it was not reached in 400 hours.

### Read the result

- **Purchases too slow**: lower the first costs or the `cost` growth of the upgrades in `upgrades.yml`, or raise `per-level`.
- **Tools and zones too slow or too quick**: change `price` in `tools.yml` and `zones.yml`.
- **Rebirths too slow**: change `cost`, `cost-growth` and `cost-power` in `rebirth.yml`. The in-game Rebirth settings page edits them too.
- **Eras too slow**: change the `rebirths` each era asks for in `eras.yml`.

Run it again after each change. Real players are somewhat faster than the model: it leaves out enchants, combo and Frenzy, mastery, index pages, rare drops, offline helper work, quests and rewards, so a theme that is a little slow in the simulator is usually right in play.

## Growth funnel

`/clickeradmin growth` shows how far farmers got, counted again every ten minutes and over everyone who ever joined, offline players included:

| Card | Counts |
|---|---|
| **First sale** | Farmers who sold their backpack at least once. |
| **First rebirth** | Farmers who rebirthed at least once. |
| **Coming back** | Farmers who played again a day and a week after joining, out of those who joined that long ago. |
| **Where they leave** | The furthest zone of farmers gone for a week, most first (the top three). |

A low first sale means new players do not find the sell point or cannot reach it. A low first rebirth means the first run is too long. Many farmers gone at the same zone means that zone's price is a wall. Fix it, run the simulator, and watch the funnel move.
