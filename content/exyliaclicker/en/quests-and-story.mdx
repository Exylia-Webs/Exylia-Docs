## Quests

Every day and every week each player gets a few quests drawn from the pool in `quests.yml`: 3 daily and 3 weekly by default. The screen lays three of each out as two rows. Rewards are paid the moment a quest is done.

A new day or week is noticed when the player next opens their quests, so nothing is scheduled. Days and weeks start at the `resets` clock of `config.yml`. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaclicker/configuration).

### What a quest counts

| Action | Counts |
|---|---|
| `harvest` | Harvests. The thing is a zone id. |
| `rare` | Golden, Rainbow or Galactic finds. |
| `sell` | Crops sold. |
| `upgrade` | Upgrade levels. The thing is an upgrade id. |
| `tool` | A tool tier or star. |
| `zone` | Opening a zone. |
| `rebirth` | Rebirths. |
| `enchant` | Enchants. The thing is an enchant id. |
| `helper` | Hiring, upgrading or starring a helper. |
| `frenzy` | Reaching a Frenzy. |

A `thing` that is empty or `*` counts anything, and globs such as `gold*` work. Each quest sets the amount, a name where `%amount%` is filled in, an icon, and its reward: coins worth some minutes of the player's own income, plus gems. `min-rebirths` draws a quest only for players with at least that many rebirths.

### Swapping

A player can swap an unfinished quest for a random other one, for `reroll-gems` gems (5 by default). They are asked to confirm.

## The story

A story walks a new player through the farm. A speaker, by default Farmer Hazel, talks, then the step's task follows. The default steps: harvest 15 crops, sell at a sell point, buy 3 upgrades, get a better tool, open the Hayfield, hire a helper, find a rare crop, reach a Frenzy, enchant the tool and rebirth. On a selling step a beacon points at the nearest sell point. A step that is already done, for example a zone opened before its turn, is skipped. A player who rebirthed before the story existed does not see it.

Edit the speaker and the steps in `quests.yml`.

## Other plugins

Every action is also fired as a custom objective of type `exylia:clicker:<action>`, so another plugin, such as a battle pass, can count the same actions.

## Admin

`/clickeradmin quests reset <player>` starts a player's quests and story over.
