Two small reasons to keep playing: tops to climb, and visitors to catch. Both need no setup.

## Tops

`/clicker top` opens the tops. A row for the viewer's own place is always shown, and a top lists up to 100 farmers. The tops are counted again every minute.

| Top | Ranks | Starts over |
|---|---|---|
| **All time** | Rebirths since the farm opened. | Never. |
| **This week** | Rebirths since the week started, so anyone can lead it. | Every week. |
| **Luckiest** | Golden and rarer crops harvested this week. | Every week. |
| **Your era** | Rebirths, among farmers in the viewer's own era only, so a newcomer can lead too. | Never. |
| **Index** | Drops and variants found in every zone's index. See [Index and variants](/docs/exyliaclicker/index-and-variants). | Never. |

Days and weeks start at the `resets` clock of `config.yml`: its `timezone`, its `hour` (0 to 23) and its `week-start` day (Monday by default). Give every server of a network the same clock. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaclicker/configuration).

The same numbers are available to other plugins as plain-number placeholders, `%exyliaclicker_rebirths_raw%`, `%exyliaclicker_luck_raw%` and `%exyliaclicker_index_raw%`. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaclicker/placeholders).

## Visitors

Now and then a visitor walks across a farmer's view. It is drawn for that player alone, so nobody else sees it or can steal it. Hitting it before it leaves pays its prize and shows what was caught.

| Visitor | How often | Crosses in | Pays by default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Golden chicken** | Weight 92 of 100 | 18s | 1 minute of income |
| **Treasure villager** | Weight 8 of 100 | 24s | 10 minutes of income, 5 gems and a roulette spin |

A visitor comes about every 4 minutes, give or take 1 minute, and only while the player stands on a farm of a zone they have opened. A player's visitors keep counting while they play; each catch is added to their profile per kind. A visitor shows a short message above the hotbar when it comes and when it leaves.

### Change them

Everything is in `rewards.yml`, under `visitors`:

| Key | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `enabled` | Turns visitors off for everyone. |
| `every` / `jitter` | How often one comes, and the random spread around it. |
| `kinds` | One entry per kind: `name`, `mob`, `glow`, `weight`, `crosses-in`, `sound` and the prize. |

`mob` is any spawnable living entity type; an unknown one falls back to a chicken. `glow` is a palette colour such as `highlight` or `accent`, empty for none. The prize is written like every reward: `minutes` of income, `gems`, a `boost` as `<boost>:<minutes>` and `spins`. Add a kind by adding an entry. In game, **Settings**, then **Rewards**, has the switch and the wait. See [Admin panel and settings](/docs/exyliaclicker/admin-panel).

### Send one now

`/clickeradmin visitor <player> <kind>` sends a visitor across an online player's farm now, for example `golden_chicken` or `treasure_villager`. An unknown kind lists the ones that exist.
