ExyliaClicker registers its placeholders with PlaceholderAPI under `exyliaclicker`. PlaceholderAPI is optional: without it, the plugin's own sidebar, HUD and tab list still work.

<Placeholders />

## Examples

A line for a scoreboard or a tab list:

```text
%exyliaclicker_tag% %player_name%
Coins: %exyliaclicker_coins% (%exyliaclicker_income%)
Zone: %exyliaclicker_zone% / Era: %exyliaclicker_era%
Next: %exyliaclicker_goal_upgrade% in %exyliaclicker_goal_upgrade_time%
```

The `_bar` placeholders return a small progress bar, so one line shows how close the next goal is. The `_raw` placeholders return a plain number with no notation, to feed leaderboards and conditions in other plugins.

<Callout type="info" title="When a value is empty">
For a player who is offline or whose profile is not loaded yet, a placeholder returns nothing. Away from a farm, `%exyliaclicker_zone%` shows the no-zone text of `interface.yml`, `Town` by default.
</Callout>
