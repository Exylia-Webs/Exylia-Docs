## Nodes

Players hit **nodes**. A zone grows several kinds, defined in `nodes.yml`:

- **Crop**: grows through its stages and can be harvested only when ripe.
- **Block**: breaks after a number of hits (`health`) and comes back after its regrow time.
- **Search**: like a block, with rare finds among its drops.

Every node has a drop list with how many it gives and, for rare drops, a chance written as `1/60`. A drop with no chance comes with every harvest. Each drop has a sale value, and a rarity from common to secret. A drop can announce itself to the whole server when somebody finds it.

Nodes are drawn for each player alone. A player's **farm** has as many nodes per zone as their `farm_size` stat allows.

## Power and tools

Each hit deals the player's **power**. Crops need 1. Blocks and search nodes name the power they need. Power past what a node needs brings more of its drops: four times the power gives twice the drops. The tool adds its own power, speed and area on top of the upgrades. See [Upgrades, tools and zones](/docs/exyliaclicker/upgrades-tools-zones).

## The click cap

`max-clicks-per-second` in `config.yml` (15 by default) is the most clicks per second a player lands, whatever their harvest speed. Faster clicks are ignored. When the harvest speed stat is above the cap, the extra turns into power, so speed upgrades never go to waste.

## Combo and Frenzy

Clicks on crops with no pause longer than the window fill a combo bar, and the hit sound climbs with it. A full bar starts a **Frenzy**: for a few seconds the player's own harvests are worth more.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| `combo.window` | 2s |
| `combo.frenzy-hits` | 60 hits in a row |
| `combo.frenzy-time` | 10s |
| `combo.frenzy-multiplier` | 2 |

The bar only shows from a few hits in a row, so a stray click draws nothing. Frenzy still obeys the click cap. Turn the whole feature off with `combo.enabled: false`.

## Mastery

Each node has a mastery level that rises with the harvests of that node: level 1 at 1K, level 2 at 10K, and one more for every ten times. Each level adds a lasting 5% to the node's value.

## The backpack and selling

Harvests go into the backpack, which holds the `backpack` stat in items (100 at the start). When it is full the player must sell. A sell spot sells for free at any time. `/clicker sell` sells from anywhere with a cooldown. The Auto Sell perk or pass sells a full backpack by itself.

A sale pays the items' value times the sell bonus, the coin multiplier and any running boosts. The catch-up bonus, **tailwind**, raises coin earnings for players behind the server's median rebirths, up to `tailwind.max-bonus` (100%) at no rebirths, fading to nothing at the median.
