## Variants

A node can grow as a **variant**. It is decided when the node grows and shown by its glow before anyone hits it. `variants` in `config.yml` sets each one:

| Variant | Chance | Worth | Pity | First |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Golden | 1/50 | x10 | 150 | 40 |
| Rainbow | 1/1000 | x50 | 3000 | 0 |
| Galactic | 1/25000 | x250 | 75000 | 0 |

The rarest is rolled first. Luck and the Midas stat raise the odds. A node type must allow variants for them to appear.

## Pity

Bad luck ends. After `pity` of a player's own harvests without that variant, the crop they just picked grows back as one. A player's very first one comes after `first` harvests, so a new farmer meets a Golden crop early. `first` 0 means the normal pity applies, and `pity` 0 means never. The index counts it down.

## The index

`/clicker index` opens the index. Per zone, a page lists every drop of its crops and every variant they grow as, each found or not yet, with the real odds. What a player found and their pity counters are kept through rebirths.

Each page a player completes adds `index.per-zone` to `index.stat` for good (a 5% coin multiplier per page by default, written like an upgrade: `5`, `5%` or `x1.05`). The index also has a top. See [Tops and visitors](/docs/exyliaclicker/tops-and-visitors).
