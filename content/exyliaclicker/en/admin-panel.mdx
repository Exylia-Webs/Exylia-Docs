`/clickeradmin` opens the admin panel, a screen of cards that cover everything you do while running the server. It needs `exyliaclicker.admin`, which operators have by default.

## The panel

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Zones** | Lists every zone with its area and slots, and sets each area with the area wizard. Also sets the AFK area. The first line of the panel tells you whether any zone still has no slots. |
| **Spots** | Adds, moves and removes the sell points and the story speaker farmers click. |
| **Codes** | Lists codes with their uses and expiry. Create one (it asks for its name, income and gems) or right-click one to delete it, after a confirm. |
| **Farmers** | Players who are online now. Pick one to give coins, give a pass, give a boost, reset their quests, open their profile or reset the farmer. |
| **Content** | Sends you a link to the web editor. See below. |
| **Growth** | The farmer funnel and the pace simulator. See [Balancing](/docs/exyliaclicker/balancing). |
| **Settings** | The settings worth changing in game. |
| **Reload** | Reads every file again and rebuilds the farms. Farmers keep playing. |

Each card has a command: `/clickeradmin settings`, `growth`, `spots`, `zone area <zone>`, `afk area`, `code`, `give`, `pass`, `boost` and `reload`. See [Commands](/docs/exyliaclicker/commands). Places are always set with a wizard: you walk there, select the corners and confirm, and you can cancel back to the screen you came from.

## Settings in game

`/clickeradmin settings` opens a hub with one card per theme, each showing its two values that matter. Each card opens a page where every setting has a short explanation, its current value and its default.

- **Click** edits a value. Numbers and amounts are typed in chat, choices and switches are picked.
- **Shift-right-click** resets a changed value to its default.
- Changes apply at once and are saved to the file.

| Page | Settings |
|---|---|
| **Farming** | Click cap, remote selling wait, income floor, tailwind on and its bonus, menu style, store link. |
| **Combo** | Combo on or off, window, hits for Frenzy, Frenzy length and pay. |
| **Luck** | Golden, Rainbow and Galactic odds and pity, and the first Golden. |
| **Rebirth** | Cost, cost growth and power, multiplier and growth, tokens and token bonus, rare card, upgrade buyer, kept zones, auto rebirth. |
| **Helpers** | Offline rate and hours, track levels, star bonus. |
| **Rewards** | Visitors and their wait, AFK gems and pay, free spins, spin price, streak grace. |

Everything else stays in the files, or in the web editor.

## Web editor

**Content** runs ExyliaLib's web editor for this plugin. It uploads what is editable, answers with a link, and you edit in the browser: settings, messages, menus and the theme files (zones, nodes, upgrades, tools, rebirth, enchants, helpers, eras, shop, quests, rewards, releases). Nothing changes on the server until you click **Apply** in chat. It needs `exylialib.editor`. Every step and the safety rules are in [Web editor](/docs/exylialib/web-editor).

## Resets and wipes

- **Reset one farmer**: **Farmers**, pick the player, **Reset farmer**. It deletes their coins, zones, helpers, quests and every record, and asks you to confirm.
- **Reset quests only**: **Reset quests** on the same screen, or `/clickeradmin quests reset <player>`.
- **Wipe everything or a season**: ExyliaLib's `/exylialib wipe` and `/exylialib resetplayer` cover every ExyliaClicker table: profiles count as progress, the store of passes as account data, and codes as setup. See [Database](/docs/exylialib/database).

## Gifts for players

On a farmer's screen, or by command:

```text
/clickeradmin give <player> 2.5M
/clickeradmin pass give <player> <pass>
/clickeradmin boost give <player> <boost> <minutes> [amount]
/clickeradmin boost global <boost> <minutes>
```

Coins are given to online players, and the amount can be written 15K or 2.5M. Passes can be given to offline players too. A global boost runs for everyone on this server with a boss bar. See [Shop, boosts and passes](/docs/exyliaclicker/shop-and-passes).

## Preview an era

`/clickeradmin era <era>` shows you an era as a player sees it. `/clickeradmin era` alone goes back to your own. See [Rebirth, perks and eras](/docs/exyliaclicker/rebirth-and-eras).
