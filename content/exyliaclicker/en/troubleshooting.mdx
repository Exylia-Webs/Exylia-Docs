Find your symptom, then try the causes in order.

## A zone shows no crops

1. The zone has no area. Open `/clickeradmin`, then **Zones**. A zone without slots is counted on the first card. Set its area with the wizard.
2. The area does not match the node. A node needs its floor blocks with air above them inside the area. Check `floor` in `nodes.yml`.
3. The player has not opened the zone yet. Farms are drawn per player, only for zones they own, and only when they stand on one.
4. PacketEvents is missing. The farm is drawn to each player with packets, so check the console at start. See [Installation](/docs/exyliaclicker/installation).

## Clicks are ignored

Clicks above `max-clicks-per-second` in `config.yml` (15 by default) are dropped. Raise it, or lower the cap only if an auto clicker is the problem.

## `/clicker sell` does not work

Away from a sell point, it waits `remote-sell-cooldown` (5 minutes by default) between sales. The Remote Sell pass removes that wait. Add a sell point in **Spots** if farmers should sell on the farm.

## A pass does not apply

- The permission must be `exyliaclicker.pass.<pass>` with the exact pass id from `shop.yml`.
- Check the player's permissions with your permissions plugin, then ask them to reopen the menu.
- Passes given with `/clickeradmin pass give` are kept on the account, so a missing pass there means the command named a different id.

## Placeholders are empty

- The player is offline or their profile has not loaded yet.
- PlaceholderAPI is not installed. The plugin's own sidebar and HUD work without it, other plugins need it.
- Away from a farm, `%exyliaclicker_zone%` shows the no-zone text of `interface.yml`, `Town` by default.

## Visitors do not come

They come only to a player who stands on a farm of a zone they own, about every 4 minutes. Check `enabled` in the `visitors` block of `rewards.yml`, or send one with `/clickeradmin visitor <player> <kind>` to test.

## An era does not change the blocks

Only blocks of the same shape are swapped. Others are left out and logged. Preview with `/clickeradmin era <era>`.

## A theme change does nothing

A new `theme` in `config.yml` needs a restart. Edits to the theme's files need `/clickeradmin reload`.

## Menus look different on some players

`menu-style` is `SCREEN` by default. Bedrock players and old clients always get chest windows.

## Weekly rewards or tops reset at the wrong time

Days and weeks follow `resets` in `config.yml`. Give every server of a network the same `timezone`, `hour` and `week-start`. Tops are counted again every minute, so a score can lag a little.

## Progress differs between servers

`profiles` decides what is shared. Set `global` or the same network group on every server to share it.
