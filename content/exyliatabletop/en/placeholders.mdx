Use these in scoreboards, tab lists, holograms and menus. They work in Exylia plugins' own files, and through PlaceholderAPI in any plugin that supports it. Nothing has to be downloaded from the eCloud. Every value is read from memory.

Replace `<game>` with a game's id: `tictactoe`, `rps`, `fourinarow`, `cards`, `battleship`, `memory`, `gomoku`, `dotsandboxes`, `reversi`, `checkers`, `liarsdice`, `highcard`, `ludo`, `diceduel`, `reflexes`, `poker`, `twentyone`, `hotpotato`, `truco`, `generala`, `chess` or `escoba`. Pick a Card is `cards`. The id never changes when a game's name or command does.

<Placeholders />

A forfeit counts as a win for the winner and a loss for the player who left. A Truco win by a pair counts a win for each partner. A match called off counts for nobody. `offers` counts open tables anybody may take from the lobby; `playing` also counts matches played at tables in the world.

## Examples

```text
%exyliatabletop_checkers_wins%
%exyliatabletop_poker_win_rate%
%exyliatabletop_tictactoe_offers%
```
