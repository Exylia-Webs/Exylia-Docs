title: "Configuration"
description: "Every key of config.yml: language, stakes, currencies and the server fee, waiting times, what players may do while they play, the board look, the room, the side panels, tables in the world and the command names."
---

`config.yml` holds what every game shares. Each game's own settings are in `modules/<game>/config.yml`,
described on [Games](/docs/exyliatabletop/games). Times are written `30s`, `5m` or `1h30m`; colours use
palette tokens such as `{primary}`. Apply a change with `/tabletopadmin reload`, except where a key says it
needs a restart.

## Language

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `language` | `default` | `default` follows the language set in ExyliaLib's `config.yml`; `en`, `es`, `ru`, `tr`, `fr` or `pt` sets this plugin alone. Each is a folder under `lang/` you can edit. |
| `debug` | `false` | Explains in the console what the plugin is doing. |

## Stakes: `wager`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `wager.currencies` | `[]` | Currencies a stake may use, by their ExyliaLib id. Empty offers every currency the player may use. |
| `wager.minimum` | `10` | Smallest stake a player may put in. |
| `wager.maximum` | `0` | Largest stake. `0` for no limit. |
| `wager.fee-percent` | `5.0` | Share of the pot the server keeps from a win, from 0 to 100. A draw is refunded in full. Players with `exyliatabletop.bypass.fee` keep everything. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Only currencies earned by playing">
List only currencies players earn in game. Never offer a currency bought with real money.
</Callout>

The fee is rounded down to the currency's decimals and is not paid to anyone: it leaves the economy.

## Waiting times: `matches`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `matches.open-expiry` | `10m` | How long an open table waits in the lobby before its stakes are refunded. A table in the world waits `tables.fill-time` instead. |
| `matches.invite-expiry` | `60s` | How long an invitation waits for an answer. |
| `matches.join-cooldown` | `1s` | Time from one attempt to create or join to the next, so a stake is never charged twice by a double click. |
| `matches.start-delay` | `3s` | Countdown before the first move, once every player is seated. |

## While they play: `arena`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `arena.allowed-commands` | `[msg, r, tell]` | Commands a player may still type during a match, without the slash. The game's own commands always work, so `/<game> leave` does. |
| `arena.freeze` | `true` | Keeps players inside their room: they may walk around in it, never out. At a table in the world, keeps a standing player within `tables.reach` of their seat. |

## The board look: `theme`

Shared by every board: ExyliaLib's stage theme.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `theme.table-block` | `BLACK_CONCRETE` | The block behind every board. |
| `theme.frame-block`, `theme.frame-glow` | `GRAY_CONCRETE`, `{secondary_light}` | The frame round it, and its outline colour. |
| `theme.button-block`, `theme.button-glow` | `GRAY_CONCRETE`, `{primary}` | What buttons are made of, and their outline under the crosshair. |
| `theme.hover-sound`, `theme.press-sound` | empty, empty | Left empty on purpose: every game plays its own hover and press sounds from its `sounds` section. Filling them adds a second sound on top. |
| `theme.verdict-block` | `BLACK_CONCRETE` | The plaque a finished game's verdict lands on. |
| `theme.verdict-win`, `-lose`, `-draw` | `{success}`, `{error}`, `{warning}` | Its outline on a win, a loss and a draw. |
| `theme.rain-item`, `theme.rain-count` | `AMETHYST_SHARD`, `10` | A win rains this item over the board, this many. Empty or `0` for none. |
| `theme.fireworks`, `theme.win-colours` | `true`, `[{primary}, {secondary_light}, {info}]` | A win sets off fireworks only the winner sees, in these colours. |
| `theme.zoom` | `2.4` | How many times nearer, and so larger, every board is drawn than its game's own distance says. `1` draws them as each game's file places them. |
| `theme.max-wide`, `theme.max-tall` | `110`, `80` | A board never spans more than this many degrees across, or up and down: it stops growing there. Lower them to fit every board on a narrow field of view. |
| `theme.nearest` | `2.5` | The nearest a board may come to the eyes, in blocks. |

## The room: `room`

A closed dark room drawn round a player while they play, only for them, so they see the board and nothing
of the world behind it. Other players are hidden from them meanwhile.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `room.enabled` | `true` | Draws the room. Off, the board stands in the open world; the blocks round the player are still cleared and other players still hidden. |
| `room.block`, `room.floor` | `BLACK_CONCRETE`, `true` | The block of its walls, ceiling and floor, and whether it has a floor (off shows the ground). |
| `room.reach`, `room.ceiling` | `4.5`, `3.5` | How far from the eyes the walls stand, and how far above the eyes the ceiling is, in blocks. |
| `room.stars`, `room.star-colours`, `room.star-scale` | `40`, `[{secondary}, {secondary_light}, {letters}]`, `0.32` | Faint twinkling stars on the walls and ceiling: how many (`0` for none), their colours and their size. |

## Side panels: `guide`

While a match is played, two panels stand at the player's sides, a turn of the head away and never over the
board: how the game is played on their left, how it is won on their right.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `guide.enabled` | `true` | Shows the panels. Off shows none. |
| `guide.distance`, `guide.lift` | `1.9`, `0.05` | How far from the eyes they stand, and how far above eye level their middle is (negative is below), in blocks. |
| `guide.scale` | `0.42` | How big their text is drawn; the titles are drawn larger. |

## Tables in the world: `tables`

Tables an admin sets down with `/tabletopadmin table` (see
[Commands](/docs/exyliatabletop/commands#setting-a-table-down)). Players sit down at them and play in sight of
whoever stands round; a hand, dice under a cup or a fleet stay hidden from those watching. No room is
drawn there.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `tables.only` | `false` | Matches are played at the tables alone: `create`, `duel`, the lobby's **OPEN A TABLE** and the API open no table anywhere else. |
| `tables.sit` | `true` | Players sit down on their seat (set seats on stairs or slabs). Off, they stand at it. |
| `tables.reach` | `0.6` | How far a standing player may step from their seat while they play, in blocks. |
| `tables.watch-radius` | `12` | How near a player must stand to watch the boards, in blocks. |
| `tables.fill-time` | `90s` | How long a table waits for its seats to fill before it is closed, every stake refunded and everyone at it stood up, so nobody keeps a table from others by sitting at it. |
| `tables.busy-lift` | `1.8` | How far the hologram rises while the table is played, so it stands clear over the boards. |
| `tables.hologram` | ExyliaLib's table look | The hologram over the centre of each table: an ExyliaLib hologram section. Its lines come from `seating` in `messages.yml`. |

## Command names

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `games-command` | `[games]` | Names of the command that opens the games catalogue. The first is the command. Needs a restart. |
| `tabletop-command` | `[tabletop]` | Names of the command that opens the catalogue on this plugin's games alone. The first is the command. Needs a restart. |
| `admin-command` | `[tabletopadmin, tta]` | Names of the admin command; the first is the command, the rest aliases. Needs a restart. |

Each game's command is set by `commands` in its own module file, and also needs a restart.

## Messages and menus

Every line a player reads is in `lang/<language>/messages.yml` and in each game's
`lang/<language>/modules/<game>/messages.yml`. Colours are palette tokens, so recolouring the server never
means editing them. A message may open with an effect tag that never reaches the screen:
`[sound:NAME|volume|pitch]` plays a sound, `[center]` centres the line. `%prefix%` is replaced by the
`prefix` value; an empty message is not sent.

| Section of `messages.yml` | Holds |
|---|---|
| `general` | Tables closed while the escrow settles, a game switched off, the cooldown and a blocked command. |
| `wager` | The stake checks, the currency, amount and seats prompts, and a duel sent to more players than the game seats. |
| `tables` | Opening, joining, answering, leaving and expiry, the invitation block and its ACCEPT, DECLINE and START buttons. Every line can use `%game%`, `%amount%` (the stake), `%pot%`, `%seated%`, `%seats%`, `%min%`, `%missing%` and `%command%`. |
| `offer` | The confirmation before taking a seat from the lobby, and `announce`, the line told to the whole server when a table opens for anybody (`%player%`, `%game%`, `%amount%`, `%pot%`, `%command%`). |
| `match` | The countdown, the board heading and turn lines, the boss bar, the results and their titles, the leave confirmation, the leave button in the hotbar, a match called off and the titles of the side panels. |
| `modes` | The Tabletop category card in the games catalogue and the description on each game's card (`%min%`, `%max%`, `%command%`). |
| `admin` | Reload, calling a match off and its confirmation. |
| `labels` | The words the admin screens show for a match's kind and state. |
| `seating` | Tables in the world: the hologram over a table (free, waiting, being played), sitting down, the refusals, and the admin lines of `table create`, `remove` and `list`. |

The lobby is `lang/<language>/menus/user/lobby.yml`: yours to restyle, and a release only adds what is
missing. The admin screens under `menus/admin/` are rewritten from the jar on every start.
