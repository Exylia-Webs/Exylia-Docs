Every way into a match ends at the same lobby of open tables. This page covers finding a match, choosing the stake and how a match is played, paid and ended.


## Finding a match

Every way in leads to the same tables:

| Way | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/games` | Opens ExyliaLib's games catalogue. Under **Tabletop**, every game the server has on has a card with its seats and the players at it right now; clicking one opens its lobby. |
| `/tabletop` | The same screen, on this plugin's games alone. |
| `/<game>` | Opens that game's lobby: `/ttt`, `/poker`, `/battleship`. |
| `/<game> create [amount] [currency]` | Opens a table anybody may take. |
| `/<game> duel <player> [player...] [amount] [currency]` | Invites one player or several. |
| A table in the world | Click it to open the game's lobby. See [Tables in the world](#tables-in-the-world). |

### The lobby

The lobby lists the game's open tables, oldest first, and follows them live: a table somebody opens or
fills appears or vanishes while the screen is open, and its age counts up. Tables in the world are not
listed: they are joined where they stand.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| A table | The host's head, the stake, the pot once every seat is taken, the currency and how long it has been listed. A click asks "Stake … against …? The winner takes …" and seats you on a yes. |
| **Your table** | Your own table, while it waits. A click closes it and refunds your stake. |
| **Open a table** | Asks for the stake and opens a table. Replaced by **Close your table** while you host one. |
| **Your record** | Your wins, losses, draws and win rate at this game, the open tables, the matches being played and the server fee. |
| **Challenge a player** | How to invite players: `/<game> duel <player> [player...] <amount>`. |

## Choosing the stake

Whatever is left out of the command is asked:

1. **The seats**, only when a host opens a table of a game for more than two: a choice dialog lists every
   count from 2 up to the game's `players` (2 or 4 for Truco). A `duel` is never asked: it seats the
   players invited and the host.
2. **The currency**, only when the player may play for more than one and typed no amount. A choice dialog
   lists them.
3. **The stake**, in an amount dialog that states the minimum (and the maximum, when there is one).

An amount typed in the command uses the server's default currency when the player may use it, and the first
currency offered otherwise. Amounts accept the short forms of ExyliaLib's economy, such as `2.5k`.

Every seat pays the same stake, the host's included: the host's stake is held as soon as the table opens,
each guest's as they sit down.

### When a seat is refused

| Refused because | The player reads |
|---|---|
| The currency typed is not offered | … can't be staked here. |
| Not a positive amount | That is not an amount you can stake. |
| Under `wager.minimum` or over `wager.maximum` | The smallest stake is … / The largest stake is … |
| They cannot afford it | You need … to take that seat. |
| They are already at a table | You are already at a table. |
| A duel invites more players than the game seats | … seats at most … players. |
| Another plugin holds them | You are busy in … |
| Another plugin advises against seating them | Its own reason. |
| Two attempts inside `matches.join-cooldown` | Slow down. Try again in … |
| The game is switched off | … is not available on this server. |
| The escrow is still settling the last run | Tables are closed for a moment. Try again shortly. |

Every check runs **before** anything is charged. A charge that fails answers "Your stake could not be taken.
Nothing was charged."

## Tables and invitations

- **One table at a time.** A player has one table, open, invited or played, across the whole server.
- **An open table** seats as many players as its host picked. It waits in the lobby for
  `matches.open-expiry` (10 minutes). If nobody fills it, it is closed and every stake refunded. When it
  opens, the whole server reads "… opened a … table for …" with a clickable **[JOIN]** that opens the
  game's lobby. The announcement goes out on ExyliaLib's `tabletop-tables` channel, which each player can
  mute in `/settings`. Invitations and tables in the world are not announced.
- **An invitation** goes to one player or several, and seats exactly them and the host: its seats are kept
  for them, nobody else may take one. It reaches each of them as a centred block in chat, with the game,
  the stake, the pot and clickable **ACCEPT** and **DECLINE** buttons, and waits `matches.invite-expiry`
  (60 seconds). `/<game> accept [player]` and `/<game> deny [player]` answer it too; without a name they
  answer the newest. If every invitee declines, the host is refunded; if some decline and the rest sat
  down, the host may start with them.
- **Tables of more than two** start when every seat is taken. Each new player is announced to the table
  with the seats left and the command others join with. Once enough are seated, the host gets a clickable
  **START** button and may also type `/<game> start` to begin without waiting. Enough is two, except where
  the game cannot be played by fewer: a Truco table of four starts only when full.
- **Before the start**, a guest who leaves gets their seat and stake back; a host who leaves or types
  `/<game> cancel` closes the table and every stake is refunded.

## Tables in the world

An admin sets a table down for one game with `/tabletopadmin table create <game>`: the wizard asks them to click the block or NPC where it goes (see [Commands](/docs/exyliatabletop/commands#setting-a-table-down)). A hologram over it names the game. Tables are saved in `tables.yml`.

A table in the world is a click spot and nothing more: a click opens the game's lobby, the same lobby `/<game>` opens. The match is not played at the table. Players open a table, take one or invite somebody from the lobby, exactly as described on this page, and the match is played on each player's own screen wherever they stand. The click checks no permission; the game's own rules (its `exyliatabletop.game.<id>` node, the stake, the cooldown) apply from the lobby on.

## The match

When the table fills, each player stays **where they stand** and keeps their inventory. If an admin set a play spot (`/tabletopadmin spot set`), players are sent there instead and brought back to where they were when they leave, quit, die or the server stops (a player who quit returns on their next join). A countdown title (`matches.start-delay`, 3 seconds) names the opponents, then the match opens.

Every one of the 22 games is played on a **match screen** that opens for every seat, whatever the player's menu style:

- **The board** fills the left, under the game's name.
- **The right column** shows the turn clock (**TURN ENDS IN**), the **POT**, the **PLAYERS** with whose turn it is and the stake each put in (free tables show no pot or stake), then the game's own buttons.
- **The headline** says **YOUR TURN** or the rival's turn, and the result when it ends. The **RULES** button opens how the game is played and how it is won.
- **LEAVE** at the bottom asks to confirm and forfeits. Closing the screen mid-match asks the same question; answer no and the screen comes back.

Clicking a square, a card or a button plays it. In turn-based games whoever moves first is drawn at random, so hosting is never an advantage. A turn that runs out is played for the player with an automatic move; it is never a forfeit. See [Games](/docs/exyliatabletop/games) for what each game plays.

A match needs a client that can draw screens: Java 1.20.2 or newer, with PacketEvents on the server. A player who cannot have one is not seated: the table is called off and every stake refunded ("A player could not be seated."). Bedrock players cannot play.

<Details title="Settings that no longer change a match">
Every game shipped with ExyliaTabletop 1.0.0 is played on the match screen alone, so the settings of the old 3D board do nothing: `matches.form` (`AUTO`, `SCREEN`, `WORLD`), the `room`, `theme` and `guide` sections and `arena.freeze`. They stay in `config.yml` so older files keep loading. `matches.form: WORLD` does not give a 3D board.
</Details>

While a match is up the player cannot be hurt, does not get hungry, cannot drop or pick up items, and cannot move items in their inventory. Commands are blocked, except the game's own commands (so `/ttt leave` works) and those listed in `arena.allowed-commands` (`msg`, `r`, `tell` by default). A blocked command answers "Leave the match with /ttt leave first."

## The result

| Outcome | What happens |
|---|---|
| A win | The winner takes the pot less the server fee: "You won …! (server fee …)" and a **VICTORY** title. Every other player reads "You lost …" and a **DEFEAT** title. |
| A win by a pair (Truco for four) | The pot less the server fee is split evenly between the two partners; each reads their own share. |
| A draw | Every stake is refunded, with a **DRAW** title. |

The verdict also shows in the screen's headline before it closes.

The **server fee** is `wager.fee-percent` (5% by default) of the pot, rounded down to the currency's
decimals so rounding favours the winner. The fee is not paid to anyone: it leaves the economy. A winner with
`exyliatabletop.bypass.fee` keeps the whole pot.

Every finished match counts for every seat: a win for the winner (for each partner of a winning pair), a
loss for the others, a draw for all.
Matches called off are not counted.

## How a match ends

| What happens | What it costs |
|---|---|
| The game plays out | Paid or refunded as the result says. |
| `/<game> leave` (asked to confirm), quitting, dying, a teleport not made by a plugin, changing world | The player forfeits. In a game of two, the opponent wins the pot. In a game of more, the player is taken out, their stake stays in the pot and the rest play on; the last one left wins. |
| A player leaves during the start countdown | The match is called off and every stake refunded. |
| The match outlasts its game's time limit | Called off and every stake refunded. The limit is worked out from the game's turn time and length (Ludo has its own `max-duration`; Truco and Escoba allow an hour). |
| An admin calls it off | Every stake refunded: "The match was called off. Every stake was refunded." |
| The server stops or the plugin is disabled | Every table, waiting or played, is called off and refunded. |

Dying in a match keeps the inventory and the levels. `/tabletopadmin reload` does not touch matches under
way: they are left to finish, and a game switched off only refuses new tables.

<Callout type="note" title="Teleports by other plugins">
A teleport whose cause is a plugin (or unknown) does not forfeit a match, so another plugin moving a
player does not cost them their stake. A teleport by command, an ender pearl or a portal does; so does
any change of world.
</Callout>
