## Finding a match

Every way in leads to the same tables:

| Way | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/games` | Opens ExyliaLib's games catalogue. Under **Tabletop**, every game the server has on has a card with its seats and the players at it right now; clicking one opens its lobby. |
| `/<game>` | Opens that game's lobby: `/ttt`, `/poker`, `/battleship`. |
| `/<game> create [amount] [currency]` | Opens a table anybody may take. |
| `/<game> duel <player> [amount] [currency]` | Invites one player. |

### The lobby

The lobby lists the game's open tables, oldest first, and follows them live: a table somebody opens or
fills appears or vanishes while the screen is open, and its age counts up. Invitations that opened up to
everyone are listed too.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| A table | The host's head, the stake, the pot once every seat is taken, the currency and how long it has been listed. A click asks "Stake … against …? The winner takes …" and seats you on a yes. |
| **YOUR TABLE** | Your own table, while it waits. A click closes it and refunds your stake. |
| **OPEN A TABLE** | Asks for the stake and opens a table. Replaced by **CLOSE YOUR TABLE** while you host one. |
| **YOUR RECORD** | Your wins, losses, draws and win rate at this game, the open tables, the matches being played and the server fee. |
| **CHALLENGE A PLAYER** | How to invite one player: `/<game> duel <player> <amount>`. |

## Choosing the stake

Whatever is left out of the command is asked:

1. **The currency**, only when the player may play for more than one and typed no amount. A choice dialog
   lists them.
2. **The stake**, in an amount dialog that states the minimum (and the maximum, when there is one).

An amount typed in the command uses the server's default currency when the player may use it, and the first
currency offered otherwise. Amounts accept the short forms of ExyliaLib's economy, such as `2.5k`.

Every seat pays the same stake, the host's included: the host's stake is held as soon as the table opens,
each guest's as they sit down.

### When a seat is refused

| Refused because | The player reads |
|---|---|
| No currency they may use is offered | There is no currency you can play for. |
| The currency typed is not offered | … can't be staked here. |
| Not a positive amount | That is not an amount you can stake. |
| Under `wager.minimum` or over `wager.maximum` | The smallest stake is … / The largest stake is … |
| They cannot afford it | You need … to take that seat. |
| They are already at a table | You are already at a table. |
| Another plugin holds them | You are busy in … |
| Another plugin advises against seating them | Its own reason. |
| Two attempts inside `matches.join-cooldown` | Slow down. Try again in … |
| The game is switched off | … is not available on this server. |
| The escrow is still settling the last run | Tables are closed for a moment. Try again shortly. |

Every check runs **before** anything is charged. A charge that fails answers "Your stake could not be taken.
Nothing was charged."

## Tables and invitations

- **One table at a time.** A player has one table, open, invited or played, across the whole server.
- **An open table** waits in the lobby for `matches.open-expiry` (10 minutes). If nobody fills it, it is
  closed and every stake refunded.
- **An invitation** reaches the player as a centred block in chat, with the game, the stake, the pot and
  clickable **ACCEPT** and **DECLINE** buttons. It waits `matches.invite-expiry` (60 seconds).
  `/<game> accept [player]` and `/<game> deny [player]` answer it too; without a name they answer the
  newest. A decline or an expiry refunds the host.
- **Tables of more than two** start when every seat is taken. Each new player is announced to the table
  with the seats left and the command others join with. Once the game's minimum is seated, the host gets a
  clickable **START** button and may also type `/<game> start` to begin without waiting. An invitation to
  such a table opens to everyone once the invitee sits down, and from then on waits as long as an open
  table does.
- **Before the start**, a guest who leaves gets their seat and stake back; a host who leaves or types
  `/<game> cancel` closes the table and every stake is refunded.

## The match

When the table fills, each player is seated **where they stand**: nobody is teleported, and their
inventories stay theirs. A countdown title (`matches.start-delay`, 3 seconds) names the opponents, then:

- **The room.** A closed dark room is drawn round each player, only for them, and other players are hidden
  from them meanwhile. It is styled by `room` in `config.yml`.
- **The board.** The game is drawn in front of their eyes, on a dark board with a softly lit frame, under a
  heading with the game and the pot.
- **The clock.** A boss bar says whose turn it is and how long is left.
- **Held still.** With `arena.freeze` on, they cannot walk away from their board.

The crosshair plays the game: what can be clicked glows under it with a soft sound, and a left or right click
presses it. In turn-based games whoever moves first is drawn at random, so hosting is never an advantage. A
turn that runs out is played for the player with an automatic move; it is never a forfeit. See
[Games](/docs/exyliatabletop/games) for what each game plays.

While a match is up the player cannot be hurt, does not get hungry, cannot drop, pick up, break or place
anything, and cannot move items in their inventory. Commands are blocked, except the game's own commands
(so `/ttt leave` works) and those listed in `arena.allowed-commands` (`msg`, `r`, `tell` by default). A
blocked command answers "Leave the match with /ttt leave first."

## The result

| Outcome | What happens |
|---|---|
| A win | The winner takes the pot less the server fee: "You won …! (server fee …)" and a **VICTORY** title. Every other player reads "You lost …" and a **DEFEAT** title. |
| A draw | Every stake is refunded, with a **DRAW** title. |

The verdict also lands on each board, and stays there for three seconds before the room is taken down.

The **server fee** is `wager.fee-percent` (5% by default) of the pot, rounded down to the currency's
decimals so rounding favours the winner. The fee is not paid to anyone: it leaves the economy. A winner with
`exyliatabletop.bypass.fee` keeps the whole pot.

Every finished match counts for every seat: a win for the winner, a loss for the others, a draw for all.
Matches called off are not counted.

## How a match ends

| What happens | What it costs |
|---|---|
| The game plays out | Paid or refunded as the result says. |
| `/<game> leave` (asked to confirm), quitting, dying, a teleport not made by a plugin, changing world | The player forfeits. In a game of two, the opponent wins the pot. In a game of more, the player is taken out, their stake stays in the pot and the rest play on; the last one left wins. |
| A player leaves during the start countdown | The match is called off and every stake refunded. |
| The match outlasts its game's time limit | Called off and every stake refunded. The limit is worked out from the game's turn time and length (Ludo has its own `max-duration`). |
| An admin calls it off | Every stake refunded: "The match was called off. Every stake was refunded." |
| The server stops or the plugin is disabled | Every table, waiting or played, is called off and refunded. |

Dying in a match keeps the inventory and the levels. `/tabletopadmin reload` does not touch matches under
way: they are left to finish, and a game switched off only refuses new tables.

<Callout type="note" title="Teleports by other plugins">
A teleport whose cause is a plugin (or unknown) does not forfeit a match, so another plugin moving a
player does not cost them their stake. A teleport by command, an ender pearl or a portal does; so does
any change of world.
</Callout>
