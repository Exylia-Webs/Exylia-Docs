---
title: "Permissions"
description: "The five nodes the plugin declares and what each one gates."
---

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliatabletop.play` | everyone | `/<game>` (the lobby), taking a seat, `accept`, `deny`, `start`, `cancel`, `leave`, `/games`, and the `tabletop:lobby`, `tabletop:join`, `tabletop:cancel` and `tabletop:games` menu actions. A game's card in the catalogue is shown only to players with it. |
| `exyliatabletop.create` | everyone | `/<game> create`, the lobby's **OPEN A TABLE** button and the `tabletop:create` action. |
| `exyliatabletop.duel` | everyone | `/<game> duel`. |
| `exyliatabletop.bypass.fee` | nobody | A win pays the whole pot, without the server fee. |
| `exyliatabletop.admin` | operators | `/tabletopadmin` and its subcommands, and the admin screens and their buttons. |

Every menu action checks its node itself, so copying a button into a screen players can open hands them
nothing they did not already have.

There is no node per game. To keep a game from everybody, switch it off with `enabled: false` in its module
file; to keep it from some players, deny its command with your permissions plugin.
