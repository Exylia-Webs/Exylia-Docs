---
title: "Permissions"
description: "The nodes the plugin declares: access, one per game, stake caps and fees per rank, and admin."
---

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliatabletop.play` | everyone | `/<game>` (the lobby), taking a seat, `accept`, `deny`, `start`, `cancel`, `leave`, `/games`, `/tabletop`, and the `tabletop:lobby`, `tabletop:join`, `tabletop:cancel`, `tabletop:leave` and `tabletop:games` menu actions. A game's card in the catalogue is shown only to players with it. Leaving never needs it, see below. |
| `exyliatabletop.create` | everyone | `/<game> create`, the lobby's **OPEN A TABLE** button and the `tabletop:create` action. |
| `exyliatabletop.duel` | everyone | `/<game> duel`, to one player or several. |
| `exyliatabletop.game.<id>` | everyone | Playing one game (`chess`, `tictactoe`, `poker`...: the game's id, as in `tabletop:lobby <game>`). See below. |
| `exyliatabletop.wager.max.<n>` | nobody | The largest stake the player may put in, `n` in whole currency units. The highest one they hold wins. See below. |
| `exyliatabletop.fee.<n>` | nobody | The server fee on their wins in whole percent, `n` from 0 to 100. The lowest one they hold wins. |
| `exyliatabletop.bypass.fee` | nobody | A win pays the whole pot, without the server fee. |
| `exyliatabletop.admin` | operators | `/tabletopadmin` and its subcommands, `table create`, `edit`, `remove` and `list` included, and the admin screens and their buttons. |

Every menu action checks its node itself, so copying a button into a screen players can open hands them
nothing they did not already have.

Sitting down at a table in the world checks no node: clicking one seats any player, and the first sets
its stake. To keep players from a table, do not set it down where they can reach it.

## Games and ranks

Every game has its own node, `exyliatabletop.game.<id>`, which everybody holds by default (`exyliatabletop.game.*` holds them all). Deny one to the default group and grant it to a rank to make that game a perk: the game's command, its lobby, its card in the catalogue, taking a seat and accepting an invitation all answer "Your rank does not include …". Getting out is never behind a node: declining an invitation, closing your own table and leaving a match work for everyone. To keep a game from everybody, switch it off with `enabled: false` in its module file.

Stakes and the fee can differ per rank. `exyliatabletop.wager.max.<n>` replaces `wager.maximum` for the player (the highest wins, and `exyliatabletop.wager.max.0` lifts the cap); it changes nothing while `wager.maximum` is `0`, since nobody is capped then. `exyliatabletop.fee.<n>` replaces `wager.fee-percent` (the lowest wins), and `exyliatabletop.bypass.fee` still beats it.
