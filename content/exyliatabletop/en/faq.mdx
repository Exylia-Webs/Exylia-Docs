Short answers. For something that is not working, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliatabletop/troubleshooting).

## Is ExyliaTabletop free?

No. It is a premium plugin sold on [BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/exylia-tabletop-18-board-card-duels.134403/). The jar you download is a loader that checks the licence on every start. The licence is already inside it, so there is no key to enter. See [Install ExyliaTabletop](/docs/exyliatabletop/installation).

## Why are players not asked for a stake?

Tables are free in three cases: no economy plugin is installed, `wager.enabled` is `false`, or `wager.currencies` lists no currency the player can use. In all of them the table simply opens free. There is no refusal message, so nothing tells the player why. Install ExyliaEconomy, Vault with an economy plugin or PlayerPoints, and set up the currency in [Economy](/docs/exylialib/economy). To keep tables free on purpose, set `wager.enabled` to `false`.

## Does anybody play against the server?

No. Every game is player against player. The winner takes what the other players put in, minus the server fee.

## Can players lose a stake to a crash or a restart?

No. ExyliaLib holds every stake from the moment a player sits down. On a normal stop, every table is called off and refunded, waiting or being played. After a crash, whatever was still held is refunded on the next start. What cannot be handed over at once is delivered when the player next joins.

## Where does the fee go?

Nowhere. `wager.fee-percent` of the pot is kept back from the winner and leaves the economy. Set it to `0` for no fee, or give `exyliatabletop.bypass.fee` to the players who should keep the whole pot.

## A player's turn ran out. Did they lose?

No. A turn that runs out is played for them with an automatic move. What loses the match is leaving it: `/<game> leave`, quitting, dying, a teleport not made by a plugin, or changing world.

## Can more than two players sit at a table?

In some games. Pick a Card seats up to 8. Poker, Generala and Hot Potato seat up to 6. Liar's Dice and Ludo seat up to 4. Truco is played by two, or by four in pairs.

The host picks how many seats the table has, up to `players` in the game's module file. `/<game> duel` with several names invites all of them. A table starts when it is full, or earlier when the host clicks **START** or types `/<game> start` with at least two players seated. A Truco table of four starts only when full. See [Games](/docs/exyliatabletop/games).

## Can matches be played at a table in the world?

Not exactly. Set a table down with `/tabletopadmin table create <game>` and click the block or NPC where it goes. Clicking it opens the game's lobby. The match itself is played on each player's screen, not at the table, and nobody watches it from the world. See [Tables in the world](/docs/exyliatabletop/playing#tables-in-the-world).

## Players complain about the table announcements

Each player can mute the `tabletop-tables` channel in ExyliaLib's `/settings`. Only tables opened for anybody are announced. Invitations and tables in the world never are.

## Players cannot use commands while they play

During a match only the game's own commands and `arena.allowed-commands` work. Add the ones they need there, without the slash.

## A player cannot open the match screen

Match screens need Minecraft 1.20.2 or newer and PacketEvents on the server. Bedrock players cannot play. See [Playing](/docs/exyliatabletop/playing#the-match).

## Do several servers share tables?

No. Tables, invitations and matches live on the server where they were opened. Records do follow players: when servers point at one database, wins, losses and draws are added up safely and never lost. See [Database](/docs/exylialib/database).

## How do I change the messages or colours?

Messages live in the plugin's `lang` files and use ExyliaLib's palette. See [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages) and [Colours](/docs/exylialib/colours).

## How do I open one game from my own menu or an NPC?

Use the menu action `tabletop:lobby <game>`, for example `tabletop:lobby checkers`. It opens the game's lobby the way `/checkers` does. From code, use `TableService.openLobby`. See [API](/docs/exyliatabletop/api).
