## Is ExyliaTabletop free?

No. It is a premium plugin sold on
[BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/exylia-tabletop-18-board-card-duels.134403/), installed as a
loader jar that checks the licence on every start. Bought there, the licence is embedded in the jar: there
is no key to enter. See [Installation](/docs/exyliatabletop/installation).

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is a hard dependency: without it the server never loads the plugin. ExyliaLib 1.356.0 or
newer is needed too; the loader installs it, or stages a newer one, and asks for a restart — restart once
more. If the console reports a licence error, check that the jar is the one you downloaded from BuiltByBit.

## "There is no currency you can play for"

No economy is installed, or `wager.currencies` lists none the player may use. Install ExyliaEconomy, Vault
with an economy plugin, or PlayerPoints, and check the ids in `wager.currencies` (empty offers every one).

## Does anybody play against the server?

No. Every game is played player against player, and the winner takes what the other players put in, less the
server fee.

## Can players lose a stake to a crash or a restart?

No. Every stake is held by ExyliaLib's escrow from the moment a player sits down. On a normal stop every
table, waiting or played, is called off and refunded. On a crash, whatever was still held is refunded on the
next start, and what cannot be handed over at once is delivered on the player's next join.

## Where is the fee paid?

Nowhere: `wager.fee-percent` of the pot is kept back from the winner and leaves the economy. Set it to `0`
for no fee, or give `exyliatabletop.bypass.fee` to the players who should keep the whole pot.

## A player's turn ran out. Did they lose?

No. A turn that runs out is played for them with an automatic move. What loses the match is leaving it:
`/<game> leave`, quitting, dying, a teleport not made by a plugin or changing world.

## Can more than two players sit at a table?

In Pick a Card (up to 8), Liar's Dice and Ludo (up to 4), Poker and Hot Potato (up to 6). Raise `players` in
the game's module file. A table starts when it is full, or earlier when its host clicks **START** or types
`/<game> start` with at least two seated.

## Players cannot use commands while they play

Only the game's own commands and `arena.allowed-commands` work during a match. Add the ones they need there,
without the slash.

## The boards are too big, or cut at the edges

Lower `theme.zoom` to draw every board smaller. On a narrow field of view, lower `theme.max-wide` and
`theme.max-tall`. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliatabletop/configuration).

## Do several servers share tables?

No. Tables, invitations and matches live on the server they were opened on. Records do follow players: with
servers pointing at one database, wins, losses and draws are added up atomically and never lost.

## How do I open one game from my own menu or an NPC?

Use the menu action `tabletop:lobby <game>`, such as `tabletop:lobby checkers`, which opens its lobby as
`/checkers` does. From code, `TableService.openLobby`. See [API](/docs/exyliatabletop/api).
