At the end of this page you have a Tic-Tac-Toe table standing in your world, and you have played a match on it. You need two players: yourself and a friend, or a second account.

Nothing needs setting up for players to play: `/ttt` already works for everyone. A table in the world is the nicest first thing to build, because players find it by walking up to it.

## Before you start

- Install the plugin and its dependencies. See [Install ExyliaTabletop](/docs/exyliatabletop/installation).
- Have an economy plugin (ExyliaEconomy, Vault with an economy, or PlayerPoints) if you want matches played for a stake. Each player needs at least 10 of its currency, the default smallest stake. With no economy plugin, every table is free: there is no stake step and nobody pays or wins money.
- You need the `exyliatabletop.admin` permission, which operators have.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place the table">
    Stand near where the table should go and run `/tabletopadmin table create tictactoe`. The wizard says **PLACE TABLE**: click the block or the NPC where the table goes. A hologram appears over it with the game's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the table as the first player">
    Click the table. The Tic-Tac-Toe lobby opens. Click **Open a table**, then type the stake when asked, for example `10`. With no economy there is no question. Your stake is held and your table appears in the lobby.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sit down as the second player">
    The second player clicks the same table, then your table in the lobby, and confirms "Stake 10 against ...?". Both stakes are held and a countdown of three seconds starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play">
    A dark room closes around each player and the board stands in front of them. Aim at a square and click it. The player who moves first is drawn at random.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

The match ends with a **VICTORY** title for the winner, who takes both stakes less the server fee (5% by default), and **DEFEAT** for the other. A free table has no money to take, only the titles. A draw refunds both stakes. Open the lobby again and **Your record** shows the result.

If something refuses, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliatabletop/troubleshooting).

## Good first values

- **Stakes**: players can open tables for any amount from `wager.minimum` (10) up. Set `wager.maximum` in `config.yml` if you want a cap.
- **Games**: all twenty-two are on. Switch off the ones you do not want with `enabled: false` in their `modules/<id>/config.yml`.
- **Server fee**: `wager.fee-percent` is 5. Set it to 0 to keep every stake in the economy.
