By default a grave keeps every item and what vanilla would have dropped as experience orbs, in every world. Here you change that.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Run `/gravesadmin`, click **Settings**, then **Contents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the experience">
    Click **Experience** to choose **None** (the orbs drop as usual), **Vanilla** (the default, at most 100 points) or **All** (every point, levels included).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the share">
    Click **Experience kept** to choose which percentage of that the grave keeps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose items">
    Click **Ignored items** for materials that never go into a grave and drop on the floor, and **Kept items** for materials the player keeps in their inventory through death.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch off a world">
    Click **Settings**, then **Graves**, then **Disabled worlds** and add the world. Nobody gets a grave there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Die in the world with items from your lists: kept items stay in your inventory, ignored items drop on the floor and everything else is in the grave.

## Going further

### Material names

Both item lists take Bukkit material names such as `TOTEM_OF_UNDYING` or `COMPASS`. A name that is not a material is skipped.

### Worlds with their own rules

In `config.yml`, `graves.disabled-worlds` is a list of world names, in any case. A world that keeps inventories after death never makes graves either.

## The rules in detail

### Experience

| `contents.experience` | What the grave keeps |
|---|---|
| `NONE` | Nothing. The orbs drop as they would without graves. |
| `VANILLA` (default) | What vanilla would have dropped as orbs, at most 100 points. |
| `ALL` | Every point the player had, levels included. They respawn at level 0. |

`contents.experience-percent` (default `100`) is the share of that the grave keeps, 0 to 100; the rest
is lost. A rank can keep more with `exyliagraves.experience.<percent>`: the largest number a player holds
wins (still at most 100). At `0` nothing is kept and the orbs drop as usual. A world or plugin that keeps the level puts
nothing in the grave.

### Item rules

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `contents.ignored-materials` | Materials that never go into a grave: they drop on the floor as usual. |
| `contents.kept-materials` | Materials the player keeps through death, in their inventory. |

Both take Bukkit material names, such as `TOTEM_OF_UNDYING` or `COMPASS`. A name that is not a material is
skipped. They only apply to deaths that would make a grave: in a disabled world, or without
`exyliagraves.use`, a kept material drops like anything else.
