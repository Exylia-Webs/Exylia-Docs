Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin stays off

ExyliaLib is required. Without it ExyliaGraves says in the console where to get it and stays disabled until you install it and restart. If an ExyliaLib jar is in `plugins/` but failed to load, fix the library's own errors first. See [Install ExyliaGraves](/docs/exyliagraves/installation).

## A player died and got no grave

Check in this order. In every case the drops fall as usual.

1. The player has `exyliagraves.use`.
2. The world is not in `graves.disabled-worlds` and does not keep inventories.
3. The death was not inside an arena or duel that another plugin owns.
4. The player dropped something: at least one item or some experience.
5. There was a free block for the head at most six blocks above where they died.

See [How graves work](/docs/exyliagraves/graves#when-a-death-makes-a-grave) and [Choose what goes in a grave](/docs/exyliagraves/contents).

## The teleport button does nothing or says to walk

Teleporting is off by default. Switch it on, see [Let players teleport to their graves](/docs/exyliagraves/teleport). With a cost, the player needs the money in `teleport.currency` and an economy plugin must be installed. A teleport to a grave on another server needs Redis.

## Graves from one server show on another

Two servers share the database with the same `server-id`, so each thinks every grave is its own. Give every server its own `server-id` under `redis` in `database.yml`. See [Run it on a network](/docs/exyliagraves/network#which-server-draws-a-grave).

## The head is still there but the grave is gone

The grave was taken on another server (staff handing it back, for example) and this server has no Redis to hear about it. The next click finds the row gone and takes the head down. Configure Redis in `database.yml` so it comes down at once.

## Players cannot open a grave

It is protected. By default only the owner and the killer can open it for the first 15 minutes. See [Choose who can open a grave](/docs/exyliagraves/protection). Staff with `exyliagraves.bypass.protection` open any grave.

## A grave disappeared before it should

Either it expired, see [Set how long graves last](/docs/exyliagraves/expiry), or the player has more graves than the limit and the oldest was tipped out where it stood. Its contents dropped and the owner got a notice.

## My edits to the admin menu disappeared

The staff screen under `lang/<code>/menus/admin/` is rewritten from the jar on every start and every menu reload. The players' screen under `menus/user/` keeps your edits. See [Install ExyliaGraves](/docs/exyliagraves/installation#generated-files).

## A placeholder shows as text

PlaceholderAPI is not installed, or the plugin reading it is not asking PlaceholderAPI. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliagraves/placeholders).
