ExyliaGraves has two commands: `/graves` for players and `/gravesadmin` for staff. Player names complete as you type and accept anybody ExyliaLib knows, online, offline or on another server.

<Commands />

Collecting, opening and teleporting are clicks, not commands: click the grave's head, or a grave in `/graves`. See [How graves work](/docs/exyliagraves/graves).

## What the staff commands do

- `/gravesadmin` with no argument opens a screen of the online players. Click one to see their graves. The same screen has a **Settings** card, see [Hand back or remove graves](/docs/exyliagraves/staff#settings).
- `/gravesadmin list <player>` and `/gravesadmin restore <player>` can only be run by a player in game, because they open menus.
- `/gravesadmin reload` reads `config.yml`, `messages.yml` and the menus again and works from the console. Graves standing are not touched.
