Two commands. `/graves` is the player's list; everything about somebody else's graves lives in
`/gravesadmin`. A player argument accepts anybody ExyliaLib knows, online, offline or on another server,
and suggests the names it has seen.

## Player commands

| Command | Aliases | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/graves` | `/grave` | `exyliagraves.menu` | Your graves on every server, with the time each has left. Players only. See [The /graves menu](/docs/exyliagraves/menu). |

Collecting, looking inside and teleporting are clicks, not commands: on the head itself, or on a grave in
`/graves`.

## /gravesadmin

`/gravesadmin` (alias `/graveadmin`) on its own, with `exyliagraves.admin.view`, prints the usage.

| Command | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/gravesadmin list <player>` | `exyliagraves.admin.view` | Somebody's graves on every server: look inside, teleport, hand back or remove. Players only. |
| `/gravesadmin restore <player>` | `exyliagraves.admin.restore` | Hands every grave the player has, on every server, back to them, after a confirmation. Players only. |
| `/gravesadmin reload` | `exyliagraves.admin.reload` | Reloads `config.yml`, `messages.yml` and the menus. Works from the console. |

See [Staff tools](/docs/exyliagraves/staff) for what each one does to a grave.

<Callout type="note" title="Coming from ExyliaSurvivalCore">
`/graves` and `/grave` keep their names. The node is now `exyliagraves.menu`, granted to everybody by
default; `exyliasurvivalcore.graves` no longer opens it.
</Callout>
