---
title: "Staff tools"
description: "Anybody's graves from /gravesadmin list, looking inside, teleporting, handing back or removing one, restoring all of a player's graves, and the audit log."
---

Every staff tool takes a grave the way a player does, by deleting its row. Staff handing a grave back
while a looter clicks it, or while it expires, hands it over once.

## /gravesadmin list

`/gravesadmin list <player>` (permission `exyliagraves.admin.view`, players only) opens a screen with
every grave that player has, on every server of the network. The player can be online, offline or on
another server, as long as ExyliaLib knows the name.

| Click | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Left | Teleport to the grave, free, ignoring combat and whether teleporting is on. | `exyliagraves.admin.view` |
| Right | Look inside, protected or not. | `exyliagraves.admin.view` |
| Shift-left | Hand it back to its owner, after a confirmation. | `exyliagraves.admin.restore` |
| Shift-right | Remove it and everything in it, after a confirmation. | `exyliagraves.admin.remove` |

Each row's click hints list only what the viewer may do. After a confirmation, or after cancelling one,
staff are taken back to that player's graves.

The screen is `lang/<code>/menus/admin/graves.yml`, rewritten from the jar on every start and every menu
reload.

## Handing graves back

**Hand back** deletes the grave and gives its items and experience to the owner by id, through
ExyliaLib's reward store: straight into their inventory when they can take it, otherwise on their next
join, on any server. Staff read *"Grave handed back to Steve."*

`/gravesadmin restore <player>` (permission `exyliagraves.admin.restore`, players only) does it for every
grave the player has, on every server, after one confirmation: *"Hand 3 grave(s) back to Steve?"* The
answer counts the graves this call actually took: *"3 grave(s) handed back to Steve."* A grave somebody
else took in between is simply not counted. With none standing: *"Steve has no graves standing."*

## Removing a grave

**Remove** deletes the grave and everything in it. Nothing is dropped or delivered. The audit log keeps a
summary of what it held.

## Where the head comes down

A head only ever comes down on the server it stands on. A grave taken from another server is taken down
there at once when Redis is configured; without Redis, it stays until somebody clicks it there (*"That
grave is gone."*) or it reaches its expiry. See [Network](/docs/exyliagraves/network).

## Audit log

Every grave goes to ExyliaLib's audit log, read in game with `/exylialib logs <player>` (or `*` for
everybody), optionally filtered by plugin and action (permission `exylialib.logs`).

| Action | When | Recorded with |
|---|---|---|
| `grave-create` | A death made a grave. | The owner, where, and every item. |
| `grave-collect` | The owner collected it. | Every item. |
| `grave-loot` | Somebody else collected it. | Who, the owner, and every item. |
| `grave-expire` | It expired and dropped, or was tipped out by the limit. | A summary of its contents. |
| `grave-delete` | It expired with `on-expire: DELETE`. | A summary of its contents. |
| `grave-restore` | Staff handed it back. | Who, the owner, and a summary. |
| `grave-remove` | Staff removed it. | Who, the owner, and a summary. |

The summary is the list of stacks and the experience, as text. Only `grave-create`, `grave-collect` and
`grave-loot` keep the items themselves.

## Reloading

`/gravesadmin reload` (permission `exyliagraves.admin.reload`) reads `config.yml`, `messages.yml` and both
menus again. The menus are also reloaded when ExyliaLib itself is reloaded, so a palette change reaches
them. Graves standing are not touched.
