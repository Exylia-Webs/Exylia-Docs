Use these to show a player's graves in any plugin that reads PlaceholderAPI placeholders, or in Exylia files. Click a placeholder to copy it.

<Placeholders />

## Good to know

- They are registered through ExyliaLib as the group `exyliagraves`, so nothing needs downloading. Without PlaceholderAPI installed only Exylia files read them.
- They read memory, never the database, and only count graves standing on **this** server.
- They need a player: they answer for the player who is looking. With no grave, the position and time ones are empty. A scoreboard line that should only show with a grave can use the fallback after `|`, see [ExyliaLib text](/docs/exylialib/text#placeholders).
- `%exyliagraves_latest_time%` reads `forever` (or your language's word) for a grave that never expires.

```text
Your grave: %exyliagraves_latest_x% %exyliagraves_latest_y% %exyliagraves_latest_z% (%exyliagraves_latest_world%)
```

The lines of `messages.yml` and the menus have values of their own, such as `%x%`, `%time_left%` or `%grave_world%`. They are listed on [Configuration](/docs/exyliagraves/configuration).
