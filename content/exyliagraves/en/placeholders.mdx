Every placeholder below is written in full, and clicking one copies it.

## How the name is written

The placeholders are registered through ExyliaLib as one group, `exyliagraves`, which is also the
plugin's name. PlaceholderAPI and Exylia files both read them the same way:

```text
%exyliagraves_count%
```

No expansion needs downloading. Without PlaceholderAPI installed only Exylia files read them.

## The viewer's graves

They read memory, never the database, and only count graves standing on **this** server.

| Placeholder | Returns |
|---|---|
| `%exyliagraves_count%` | How many graves the viewer has standing here. |
| `%exyliagraves_latest_world%` | The world of their newest grave, or nothing without one. |
| `%exyliagraves_latest_x%` | Its block X, or nothing. |
| `%exyliagraves_latest_y%` | Its block Y, or nothing. |
| `%exyliagraves_latest_z%` | Its block Z, or nothing. |
| `%exyliagraves_latest_time%` | How long it has left, such as `42m 10s`, or *forever* for one that never expires. |

All of them need a player viewer. A scoreboard line that only shows when there is a grave can use the
library's fallback after `|`, as described on the [library's text page](/docs/exylialib/text#placeholders).

## Inside messages and the hologram

The lines of `messages.yml` have values of their own, such as `%x%`, `%time_left%` or `%protection%`, and
the menus have `%grave_…%` values for each row. They are listed on
[Configuration](/docs/exyliagraves/configuration).
