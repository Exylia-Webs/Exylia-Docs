By default only the owner (and the player who made the kill) can open a grave for the first 15 minutes; after that anybody can. Here you change that.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Run `/gravesadmin`, click **Settings**, then **Graves**. You need `exyliagraves.admin`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the protection time">
    Click **Protection** and pick how long only the owner may open a grave: `0` is anybody from the start, and the unlimited value (`-1`) is only the owner, ever.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide about the killer">
    Click **Killer loot** to switch on or off whether the player who made the kill may open a protected grave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or edit the file">
    The same values are `graves.protection-time` and `graves.killer-can-loot` in `plugins/ExyliaGraves/config.yml`. Run `/gravesadmin reload` after editing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Die with an item, then ask another player to click your grave. During protection they read who the grave belongs to and how long until it opens to everybody. The hologram's third line shows the same state.

## Going further

### Make a grave owner-only forever

Set `graves.protection-time: -1` and `graves.killer-can-loot: false`. Only the owner and staff with `exyliagraves.bypass.protection` can open it.

### Give a rank a longer protection

Grant `exyliagraves.protection.<minutes>`. See [Give ranks longer or more graves](/docs/exyliagraves/ranks).

## The rules in detail

| `graves.protection-time` | Who may open it |
|---|---|
| `15` (default) | The owner, and for those 15 minutes nobody else. After that, anybody. |
| `0` | Anybody from the start. |
| `-1` | Only ever the owner. |

During a timed protection the player who made the kill is let in too while `graves.killer-can-loot` is
`true` (the default). A grave protected for good lets nobody but the owner in, killer or not. Staff with
`exyliagraves.bypass.protection` open any grave.

A rank can be given a longer protection with `exyliagraves.protection.<minutes>`: the largest number a
player holds wins, and it never shortens `graves.protection-time` (nor changes a `-1`).

A player who may not open it is told whose grave it is and how long until it opens to everybody, or that
only the owner ever can. The hologram's third line and the menus show the same state: *"Only the owner can
open it"*, *"Anybody can open it"*, or *"Protected » 12m 3s"*.
