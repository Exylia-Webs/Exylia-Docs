## When a death makes a grave

A grave is made when a player dies and every one of these holds:

- they have `exyliagraves.use` (everybody by default);
- the world does not keep inventories, and no plugin kept it for this death;
- the world is not in `graves.disabled-worlds` (names, any case);
- no other plugin owns the death: a player held in an arena through ExyliaLib sessions, or a duel that
  claimed its drops (ExyliaSurvivalCore's duel rooms do), gets no grave — those drops are that plugin's;
- there is something to keep: at least one item, or some experience.

Otherwise the drops fall exactly as they would without the plugin.

The drops are taken out of the death at `HIGH`, one step before `HIGHEST`, where death plugins usually
scatter what is left: by then there is nothing of the grave's left to scatter. The grave itself is only
stored at `MONITOR`, once the event has settled, from what really left the player. A plugin that cancels
the death later, keeps the inventory, keeps the level, or keeps an item through the death's keep list is
respected: none of that goes into the grave too.

## Where the head goes

| Situation | Where |
|---|---|
| The player died in a block a head can replace | That block. |
| They died inside something else | The first such block above, up to six blocks up. |
| They fell into the void | The highest block of that column, one above. |
| No room at all | No grave: the drops fall as they would have. |

A head can replace air, water, lava, short and tall grass, ferns, snow, seagrass and dead bushes. That
means a death in a lake or a lava pool leaves the grave **in** the water or lava, not above it. A block
that already holds a grave, or that another Exylia plugin has claimed through ExyliaLib, is never used.

When the grave goes, what it replaced is put back: a water or lava source stays a source, seagrass becomes
water, anything else becomes air. Flowing liquid never becomes a new source.

The head is skinned with the owner's face. Above it floats the hologram, three lines by default — whose
grave, how many stacks and the countdown, and who may open it — refreshed every second. Both are only a
picture of the database row: they are drawn when the chunk loads and the text is dropped when it unloads.

<Callout type="note" title="Somebody built over a grave">
If a block other than a head now stands where a grave's head was (WorldEdit, a reset, a crash), the
block is left alone and the grave shows as text only. Clicking that block still collects the grave.
</Callout>

## Nothing breaks a grave

The head cannot be mined: breaking it is cancelled with *"Click the grave to collect it; it cannot be
broken."* Explosions skip it, withers, endermen and falling blocks cannot change it, fire does not burn
it, flowing water or lava does not wash it away, and a piston that would move it does not fire.

## Collecting

A click on the head — left or right, main hand — collects it. There is no window onto a grave's real
contents: the click deletes the row, and if that delete is the one that removed it, everything is handed
over:

- items go into the collector's inventory;
- when the owner collects it themselves and `collect.auto-equip` is on, armour and the offhand item go
  straight back on, into empty slots only;
- the experience is given with the items;
- what does not fit is kept in ExyliaLib's reward store and handed over on the player's next join, on any
  server of the network, with *"N item(s) from your graves were handed to you."*

Everything goes by player id, so a player who dies, quits or switches servers between the click and the
delivery still gets it all, once.

The owner reads *"Grave collected: N item(s) and N experience."*; anybody else reads that they looted the
grave, and the owner is sent a notice (see [Notices](#notices)).

If the database cannot be reached at that moment, the grave stays standing and the player reads *"The
grave could not be opened right now. It is still there; try again in a moment."*. If somebody else got it
first, *"That grave is gone."*

## Protection

| `graves.protection-time` | Who may open it |
|---|---|
| `15` (default) | The owner, and for those 15 minutes nobody else. After that, anybody. |
| `0` | Anybody from the start. |
| `-1` | Only ever the owner. |

During a timed protection the player who made the kill is let in too while `graves.killer-can-loot` is
`true` (the default). A grave protected for good lets nobody but the owner in, killer or not. Staff with
`exyliagraves.bypass.protection` open any grave.

A player who may not open it is told whose grave it is and how long until it opens to everybody, or that
only the owner ever can. The hologram's third line and the menus show the same state: *"Only the owner can
open it"*, *"Anybody can open it"*, or *"Protected » 12m 3s"*.

## Looking inside

With `exyliagraves.preview` (everybody by default) a player can look at what a grave holds without taking
anything:

- **sneak-click** the head, while `collect.sneak-to-preview` is on (the default);
- **right-click** a grave in [`/graves`](/docs/exyliagraves/menu).

Only graves they may open: someone else's protected grave answers *"You cannot look inside that grave."*
Staff with `exyliagraves.admin.view` look inside any. Sneak-clicking without the preview permission
simply collects.

The preview is a copy. Every click and drag is cancelled while it is open, the player's own inventory
included, so not even a shift-click or a double-click moves an item; the grave's row is still the only
place its items are.

## Experience

| `contents.experience` | What the grave keeps |
|---|---|
| `NONE` | Nothing. The orbs drop as they would without graves. |
| `VANILLA` (default) | What vanilla would have dropped as orbs, at most 100 points. |
| `ALL` | Every point the player had, levels included. They respawn at level 0. |

`contents.experience-percent` (default `100`) is the share of that the grave keeps, 0 to 100; the rest
is lost. At `0` nothing is kept and the orbs drop as usual. A world or plugin that keeps the level puts
nothing in the grave.

## Item rules

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `contents.ignored-materials` | Materials that never go into a grave: they drop on the floor as usual. |
| `contents.kept-materials` | Materials the player keeps through death, in their inventory. |

Both take Bukkit material names, such as `TOTEM_OF_UNDYING` or `COMPASS`. A name that is not a material is
skipped. They only apply to deaths that would make a grave: in a disabled world, or without
`exyliagraves.use`, a kept material drops like anything else.

## Expiry

`graves.expire-time` (default `60` minutes) is how long a grave stands. `-1` keeps it until it is
collected. A rank can be given longer with `exyliagraves.expire.<minutes>`: the largest number a player
holds wins, and a number below the configured time is ignored. With `-1` configured, graves never expire
whatever the permissions say; `0` also never expires, but there an `exyliagraves.expire.<minutes>` node
still gives its holder a timed grave.

Expired graves are swept once a second. What happens to the contents is `graves.on-expire`:

| Value | What happens |
|---|---|
| `DROP` (default) | Items and experience drop where the grave stood. An unloaded chunk is loaded in the background first. |
| `DELETE` | They are gone. The audit log keeps a summary of what the grave held. |

Anything other than `DELETE` drops. A grave whose world is no longer loaded on this server has no floor to
drop on, so its contents are handed to the owner instead.

An expiry goes through the same delete as a click, so a grave being collected at that exact moment is not
also dropped. A delete that fails leaves the grave standing and is tried again a minute later.

## Limits

`graves.max-per-player` (default `5`) is how many graves one player may have standing **on this server**.
Dying with that many tips the oldest one out where it stands — its contents drop, whatever `on-expire`
says — and the owner is told it expired. `-1` (or `0`) is no limit.

`exyliagraves.limit.<n>` raises it for a rank: the largest number wins, and a number below the configured
limit is ignored. With `-1` configured there is no limit for anybody; with `0`, a `limit` node still
gives its holder one.

Graves still being written, or being taken at that moment, are not counted.

## Notices

The owner is told when something happens to a grave while they are not looking:

| Event | Message |
|---|---|
| Somebody looted it | *"Steve looted your grave in world: 12 item(s)."* |
| It expired and dropped | *"One of your graves expired and its 12 item(s) are on the ground where it stood."* |
| It expired and was deleted | *"One of your graves expired and its 12 item(s) are gone."* |

These are ExyliaLib notices: shown at once when the owner is on this server, on the server they are on
when Redis is configured, and otherwise on their next join.

## Back on their feet

After respawning, when the player has a grave standing on this server:

- `respawn.point-compass` (default `true`) points their compass at their newest grave, when it is in the
  world they respawned in;
- `respawn.remind` (default `true`) says where it is: *"Your grave is at x, y, z (world). Your compass
  points to it."*

## Audit log

Every grave created, collected, looted, expired, deleted on expiry, handed back or removed is recorded in
ExyliaLib's audit log, under the actions `grave-create`, `grave-collect`, `grave-loot`, `grave-expire`,
`grave-delete`, `grave-restore` and `grave-remove`. See [Staff tools](/docs/exyliagraves/staff#audit-log).
