## What a fresh server does

Out of the box, with nothing edited:

| What | Default |
|---|---|
| Who gets a grave | Everybody (`exyliagraves.use`), in every world |
| How long it stands | 60 minutes, then its contents drop where it stood |
| Who may open it | Only the owner for the first 15 minutes, and the killer; then anybody |
| Graves per player | 5 standing on this server; a sixth death tips the oldest out |
| Experience | What vanilla would have dropped, all of it |
| Teleport from `/graves` | Off |
| After respawning | The compass points to the newest grave, and a chat line says where it is |

## A first grave

<Steps>
  <Step title="Die">
    Jump off something with a few items. Where you fell there is now a player head with your face and three
    floating lines: whose grave, how many stacks and the countdown, and who may open it. Chat says
    where the grave is and how long it will stand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respawn">
    Your compass points at the grave, and chat reminds you where it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look inside">
    Walk back and **sneak-click** the head. A window shows what it holds; nothing can be taken out of it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect it">
    Click the head, left or right. Everything goes back into your inventory, your armour and offhand item
    straight back on. Whatever does not fit is kept for you and handed over on your next join.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    `/graves` lists every grave you have, on every server of the network. With none, the screen says so.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What most servers change first

All of it is in `plugins/ExyliaGraves/config.yml`; `/gravesadmin reload` applies an edit.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaGraves/config.yml"
graves:
  expire-time: 2h          # longer graves
  protection-time: -1      # only the owner, ever
  killer-can-loot: false   # the killer does not get in either
  disabled-worlds:
    - arena
teleport:
  enabled: true
  cost: 500                # in ExyliaLib's default currency
```

Ranks can be given more with permissions, without touching the file:

```text
/lp group vip permission set exyliagraves.expire.180 true
/lp group vip permission set exyliagraves.limit.10 true
```
