For fixes start with [Common problems](/docs/exyliagraves/troubleshooting). These are the quick answers.

## Is it free?

Yes. ExyliaGraves is free and open source under the GNU General Public License v3.0, with no licence key.
Download it from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/plugin/exyliagraves); the source is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaGraves).

## A player died and got no grave

Check that they have `exyliagraves.use`, that the world is not in `graves.disabled-worlds` and does not
keep inventories, that the death was not in an arena or duel another plugin owns, that they dropped
something, and that a free block exists at most six above where they died. In each case the drops fall
as usual. The full checklist is in [Common problems](/docs/exyliagraves/troubleshooting#a-player-died-and-got-no-grave).

## Can somebody steal from a grave?

Only after its protection ends (15 minutes by default), or the killer during it while
`graves.killer-can-loot` is on. `graves.protection-time: -1` keeps every grave its owner's for good, and
the owner is told who looted it. See [Protection](/docs/exyliagraves/protection).

## What if my inventory is full when I collect?

Nothing is lost: what did not fit is kept and handed over on your next join, on any server, with a line
saying how many items you got. See [Collecting](/docs/exyliagraves/graves#collecting).

## What happens to a grave that expires while I am offline?

With `graves.on-expire: DROP` its contents are on the ground where it stood; with `DELETE` they are gone.
Either way you are told on your next join. See [Expiry](/docs/exyliagraves/expiry).

## Can ranks get longer protection, more graves or more experience?

Yes, with permission numbers such as `exyliagraves.expire.<minutes>`, `exyliagraves.protection.<minutes>`,
`exyliagraves.limit.<n>` and `exyliagraves.experience.<percent>`; a number can only raise what
`config.yml` sets. See [Ranks](/docs/exyliagraves/ranks).

## The teleport button does nothing or says I have to walk

Teleporting is off by default: set `teleport.enabled: true`. A cost needs the money in `teleport.currency`
(ExyliaLib's default currency when empty), and a teleport to a grave on another server needs Redis. See
[The /graves menu](/docs/exyliagraves/teleport).

## The head is still there but says the grave is gone

The grave was taken on another server and this one has no Redis to hear about it; the click finds the row
gone and takes the head down. Configure Redis in `database.yml` so it comes down at once. See
[Network](/docs/exyliagraves/network#with-and-without-redis).

## Graves from one server show up on another

Both servers share the database under the same `server-id`, so each thinks every grave is its own. Give
every server its own `server-id` under `redis` in `database.yml`. See
[Network](/docs/exyliagraves/network#which-server-draws-a-grave).

## My edits to a staff menu disappeared

The files under `lang/<code>/menus/admin/` are replaced from the jar on every start and every reload.
Edit `menus/user/graves.yml` instead, which is yours, or copy a staff file under another name. See
[Configuration](/docs/exyliagraves/configuration#menus).

## Does ExyliaGraves have an API?

No public API or events. Grave activity reaches the ExyliaLib audit log (`/exylialib logs`), and the
[placeholders](/docs/exyliagraves/placeholders) expose a player's graves on this server.

## Where do I get help?

On the Exylia Discord: [discord.exylia.net](https://discord.exylia.net). Bug reports and feature requests
are also welcome as issues on the GitHub repository.
