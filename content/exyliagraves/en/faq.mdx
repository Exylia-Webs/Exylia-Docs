## Is it free?

Yes. ExyliaGraves is free and open source, under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Download it from
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaGraves/releases); the source is in the same
repository. There is no licence key and nothing to buy.

## I used the graves of ExyliaSurvivalCore. What changes?

The graves module is gone from ExyliaSurvivalCore and lives here now. The table is still `sc_graves`, so
graves standing when you switch are kept as long as `plugins/ExyliaGraves/database.yml` opens the
database that holds them — nothing is copied from ExyliaSurvivalCore's folder. The settings, messages
and menu are new files with new keys, and the node is `exyliagraves.menu`. See
[Network](/docs/exyliagraves/network#coming-from-exyliasurvivalcore).

## A player died and got no grave

Check, in order: that they have `exyliagraves.use`; that the world is not in `graves.disabled-worlds` and
does not keep inventories; that the death was not in an arena or duel another plugin owns; that they
dropped something; and that there was a free block at most six above where they died. In every one of
those cases the drops fall as usual.

## Can somebody steal from a grave?

Only once its protection is over (15 minutes by default), or the killer during it while
`graves.killer-can-loot` is on. Set `graves.protection-time: -1` to keep every grave its owner's for good.
The owner is told who looted it, wherever they are.

## My inventory was full when I collected

Nothing is lost. What did not fit is kept and handed over on your next join, on any server, with a line
saying how many items were handed to you.

## A grave expired while I was offline

With `graves.on-expire: DROP` its contents are on the ground where it stood; with `DELETE` they are gone.
Either way you are told on your next join.

## The head is still there but says the grave is gone

The grave was taken on another server (staff handing it back, for example) and this server has no Redis
to hear about it. The click finds the row gone and takes the head down. Configure Redis in
`database.yml` so it comes down at once.

## Graves from one server show up on another

Both servers share the database with the same `server-id`, so each thinks every grave is its own. Give
every server its own `server-id` under `redis` in `database.yml`. See
[Network](/docs/exyliagraves/network#which-server-draws-a-grave).

## The teleport button does nothing / says I have to walk

Teleporting is off by default: set `teleport.enabled: true`. With a cost, the player needs the money in
ExyliaLib's default currency, and an economy has to be installed. A teleport to a grave on another
server needs Redis.

## My edits to the admin menu disappeared

`lang/<code>/menus/admin/graves.yml` is replaced from the jar on every start and every reload. Edit the
players' screen under `menus/user/` instead — that one is yours.

## Is there an API?

No public API or events. Grave activity reaches the ExyliaLib audit log (`/exylialib logs`), and the
placeholders expose a player's graves on this server.

## Where do I get help?

On the Exylia Discord: [discord.exylia.net](https://discord.exylia.net). Bug reports and feature requests
are also welcome as issues on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaGraves).
