title: "Configuration"
description: "Every key of config.yml: language, requests, match times, modes, bets, spectators, after the duel, history, replays, network, the duel scoreboards and the command allowlist, plus messages and menus."
---

`plugins/ExyliaDuels/config.yml` holds everything that is not a message, a menu, an arena or a kit. Arenas
and kits, with their flags and rules, are made in game with `/duelsadmin`. Every key of this file can be
changed in game too: the panel's **Settings** card holds the modes, bets and limits, and **All settings**
opens every key. Both write this file. Times are written `30s`, `5m`, `1h30m` or `30d`.
Apply a hand edit with `/duelsadmin reload`.

## Language

<Field name="language" type="string" defaultValue="default">
`default` follows the language set in ExyliaLib's `config.yml`; `en`, `es`, `fr`, `pt`, `ru` or `tr` sets
this plugin alone. Each is a folder under `lang/` you can edit.
</Field>

<Field name="debug" type="boolean" defaultValue="false">
Explains in the console what the plugin is doing.
</Field>

## Challenges: `requests`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `requests.expiry` | `60s` | How long a challenge waits for an answer. |
| `requests.cooldown` | `10s` | Time a player waits between two challenges sent. `exyliaduels.cooldown.<n>` gives a rank `<n>` seconds instead, the lowest winning. Skipped with `exyliaduels.bypass.cooldown`. |

## The fight: `match`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `match.countdown` | `5s` | Countdown before the fight, with both players frozen in place. |
| `match.max-duration` | `10m` | Longest a fight may last, every round of it together. When it runs out the duel is a draw, whatever the round score, and every stake is refunded. |
| `match.end-delay` | `3s` | Time the result stays on screen before both players are sent back, and between two rounds. |

Each round after the first starts with the same `match.countdown`.

## Modes: `modes`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `modes.own-inventory` | `true` | Players may fight with their own inventory. |
| `modes.risk` | `true` | With their own inventory, players may agree that the loser's inventory goes to the winner. |

A kit is always offered while at least one kit is enabled, or another plugin offers one. Which modes a
player may pick also depends on their rank: see [Permissions](/docs/exyliaduels/permissions#challenge-options).

## Bets: `bets`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `bets.money.enabled` | `true` | Money bets. |
| `bets.money.currency` | `""` | The currency a new money bet starts with, by its ExyliaLib id. Empty uses the server's default. It is also the currency `money_won` records count. |
| `bets.money.currencies` | `[]` | The currencies a bet may use, by their ExyliaLib id. Empty offers every currency the player may use. |
| `bets.money.minimum` | `100` | Smallest stake per player. |
| `bets.money.maximum` | `0` | Largest stake per player. `0` means no limit. `exyliaduels.bet.max.<n>` gives a rank a largest stake of `<n>` (`0`: none) instead, the highest winning. |
| `bets.money.tax-percent` | `5.0` | Share of the pot destroyed when a duel is won, from `0` to `100`. Draws and cancelled duels are refunded in full. `exyliaduels.tax.<n>` gives a winner's rank `<n>` percent instead, the lowest winning. Players with `exyliaduels.bypass.tax` keep everything. |
| `bets.items.enabled` | `true` | Item bets: players on the same server put their stakes in a trade window and confirm. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Only currencies earned by playing">
List only currencies players earn in game in `bets.money.currencies`. Never offer a currency bought with
real money. See [ExyliaLib's economy](/docs/exylialib/economy) for the currency ids.
</Callout>

## Spectators: `spectators`

<Field name="spectators.enabled" type="boolean" defaultValue="true">
Players may watch a duel with `/duel spectate <player>`, in spectator mode inside the arena.
</Field>

## After the duel: `after-duel`

<Field name="after-duel.window" type="duration" defaultValue="5m">
How long the **[INVENTORY]**, **[YOURS]** and **[⟲ REMATCH]** buttons of the end-of-duel block work. `0s`
turns them off. See [Duelling](/docs/exyliaduels/dueling#after-the-duel).
</Field>

## History: `history`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `history.enabled` | `true` | Stores every decided duel for `/duel history`, draws included. Cancelled duels never. |
| `history.keep` | `45` | How many duels each player keeps, newest first. `0` keeps every one. |
| `history.max-age` | `30d` | Duels older than this are deleted. `0s` keeps them however old. |

## Replays: `replays`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `replays.enabled` | `true` | Records every duel that reaches the fight. Needs `history.enabled`: a replay is reached from its duel there, and goes when both players' rows of it are gone. |
| `replays.kit-only` | `false` | Records only duels fought with a kit. |
| `replays.max-age` | `14d` | Replays older than this are deleted, even while the history still lists the duel. `0s` keeps them as long as the history does. |

## Network: `network`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `network.enabled` | `false` | Players on other servers can be challenged, and server-kit duels are fought on the arena server. |
| `network.arena-server` | `arena` | The `server-id` of the server that hosts network duels, as the proxy names it. |
| `network.arenas-scope` | `default` | Which servers share the arenas. `default` follows the network group set in ExyliaLib's `config.yml` (identical servers of a pool share arenas; no group means every server shares), `server` gives each server its own, `global` shares with all, or name a group. |
| `network.shared-economy` | `false` | Whether every server shares one economy. Only then may players on different servers bet money. |

See [Network](/docs/exyliaduels/network) before turning it on.

## The duel scoreboards: `scoreboard`, `hits-scoreboard`, `rounds-scoreboard`

The sidebar both fighters see from the countdown to the end of the duel. `scoreboard` is the usual one;
`hits-scoreboard` replaces it in a duel with a hits-to-win kit, and `rounds-scoreboard` in a duel with a kit
of several rounds (it wins when a kit has both). The three have the same keys.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `scoreboard.enabled` | `true` | Shows the board. |
| `scoreboard.title` | `['{error}&lDUEL']` | The title. Several entries animate it, one per refresh. |
| `scoreboard.lines` | see below | The lines, top first, at most 15. |
| `scoreboard.update.interval` | `10` | Ticks between refreshes (20 ticks are one second). |
| `scoreboard.update.smart` | `true` | Sends only the lines that changed. |
| `scoreboard.update.cache` | `true` | Kept for older files; changes nothing. |

```yaml
lines:
  - '{letters_black}Duel · %exyliaduels_mode%'
  - ''
  - '{error}⚔ Opponent'
  - '  {letters_black}· {highlight}{icon:sword} {letters}Name{letters_black}: {highlight}%exyliaduels_opponent%'
  - '  {letters_black}· {error}{icon:heart} {letters}Health{letters_black}: {error}%exyliaduels_opponent_health%'
  - ''
  - '{error}✦ Match'
  - '  {letters_black}· {info}{icon:clock} {letters}Time{letters_black}: {info}%exyliaduels_time%'
  - '  {letters_black}· {success}{icon:gold} {letters}Pot{letters_black}: {success}%exyliaduels_pot%'
  - ''
  - '{muted}{server-ip}'
```

The boards follow the card layout of the other Exylia sidebars. On a server that already has the file, the new defaults wait in `/exylialib updates`.

`hits-scoreboard` opens with both hit counts, and `rounds-scoreboard` with both round counts and the round:

```yaml
lines:
  - '{letters_black}Duel · %exyliaduels_mode%'
  - ''
  - '{error}⚔ Rounds'
  - '  {letters_black}· {success}{icon:star} {letters}You{letters_black}: {success}%exyliaduels_rounds_won%{letters_black}/{info}%exyliaduels_rounds_to_win%'
  - '  {letters_black}· {error}{icon:star} {letters}Them{letters_black}: {error}%exyliaduels_opponent_rounds_won%{letters_black}/{info}%exyliaduels_rounds_to_win%'
  - '  {letters_black}· {info}{icon:tag} {letters}Round{letters_black}: {info}%exyliaduels_round%'
  - ''
  - '{error}✦ Match'
  - '  {letters_black}· {highlight}{icon:sword} {letters}Opponent{letters_black}: {highlight}%exyliaduels_opponent%'
  - '  {letters_black}· {info}{icon:clock} {letters}Time{letters_black}: {info}%exyliaduels_time%'
  - '  {letters_black}· {success}{icon:gold} {letters}Pot{letters_black}: {success}%exyliaduels_pot%'
  - ''
  - '{muted}{server-ip}'
```

## Commands during a duel: `allowed-commands`

<Field name="allowed-commands" type="list" defaultValue="[duel, msg, tell, w, whisper, m, r, reply]">
The only commands a player can use from an accepted challenge until they are home again, and while
spectating, written without the slash. Everything else is refused. Aliases with a plugin prefix
(`essentials:msg`) are matched too.
</Field>

## Messages

`lang/<language>/messages.yml` holds every chat line, title, boss bar and label, grouped as `challenge`,
`request`, `bet`, `fight`, `spectate`, `network`, `stats`, `after-duel`, `replay`, `admin`, `labels` and
`rules`. Colours use palette tokens
such as `{primary}`, so recolouring the server never means editing this file.

- `%prefix%` is replaced by the `prefix` value. An empty message is not sent.
- A message may open with an effect tag, which never reaches the screen: `[sound:NAME|volume|pitch]` plays a
  sound, `[center]` centres the line.
- `request.received` is the block the challenged player reads. `%accept%` and `%decline%` are the buttons,
  styled by `labels.accept-button` and `labels.decline-button`.
- `fight.countdown-title`, `fight.fight-title`, `fight.victory-title` and the others are the titles;
  `fight.time-bar` is the boss bar, with `%time_precise%` the time left.
- `after-duel.summary` is the block both players read once a duel is decided, with `%view%`, `%own%` and
  `%rematch%` the buttons, styled by `after-duel.view-button`, `own-button` and `rematch-button`.
- `replay.hud` is the line above the replay hotbar while watching.
- `rules` holds what kit rules say during a duel (rounds won and lost, build height, item cooldowns) and the
  prompts of the screens that set them.
- `labels` are the words injected into menus and messages (enabled, disabled, ready, random, the mode
  names), so one palette change restyles every screen.

## Menus

The player screens under `lang/<language>/menus/user/` (the challenge screen, the end-of-duel inventory
view, the history and a duel's screen) and the replay hotbar, `lang/<language>/overlays/replay.yml`, are
yours to restyle: an update only adds what is missing. The admin screens under `menus/admin/` are
regenerated from the jar on every start. See
[ExyliaLib's menus](/docs/exylialib/menus) for the format.

Where the server's menu style is `SCREEN` (ExyliaLib's `screens.yml`), the challenge and the history open as full screens instead of chest windows, with the kit and arena pickers inside the screen (`screens/duel_challenge.html`, `screens/duel_history.html`).
