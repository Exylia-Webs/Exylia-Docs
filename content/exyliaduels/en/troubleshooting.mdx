Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is required: without it the server never loads ExyliaDuels. ExyliaLib is required too. The loader installs it, or stages a newer one, and asks for a restart: restart once more. If the console reports a licence error, check `loader.yml`. See [Install ExyliaDuels](/docs/exyliaduels/installation).

## "There is no arena ready for duels yet"

No arena is ready. An arena is ready only while it is enabled, both spawns are set, its world is loaded and its schematic is saved. Open `/duelsadmin`, click **Arenas**, and read which ones say **Not ready**. Set the **Area** and both spawns, as in [Your first duel](/docs/exyliaduels/first-steps).

## Own-inventory challenges are refused

Own-inventory duels only use arenas that are not bound to a kit. If every ready arena has a **Kit** set, the challenge screen opens on a server kit instead. Set an arena's **Kit** to **Any kit** to allow own-inventory duels there. Own-inventory duels can also be turned off in `/duelsadmin`, **Settings**, **Modes and bets** (`modes.own-inventory`).

## "Your rank does not include that option"

The challenger lacks the permission for something in the challenge: `exyliaduels.mode.own`, `exyliaduels.mode.risk`, `exyliaduels.bet.money`, `exyliaduels.bet.items`, `exyliaduels.arena.choose` or the kit's node. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaduels/permissions).

## "Wait ... before sending another challenge"

The cooldown between two challenges is 10 seconds by default (`requests.cooldown`). Give a rank a shorter one with `exyliaduels.cooldown.<n>`, or let staff skip it with `exyliaduels.bypass.cooldown`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaduels/permissions#numbers-per-rank).

## "No arena is free right now"

The arena was busy and all its copies were in use. Raise **Copies** in the arena's settings (0 to 64), or build another arena. The duel is called off and nothing is lost.

## Money bets are refused

- The stake is under the minimum or over the maximum: the message names the limit. See [Set up bets and tax](/docs/exyliaduels/bets).
- One of the players cannot afford it ("You need ... for that bet").
- The players are on different servers and `network.shared-economy` is off. Money bets, item bets and own-inventory duels need both players on the same server unless the economy is shared. See [Network duels](/docs/exyliaduels/network).

## Players cannot use a command during a duel

That is intended. From an accepted challenge until they are home, and while spectating, only the commands in `allowed-commands` work. Add the ones you want to `config.yml`, without the slash, then run `/duelsadmin reload`. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaduels/configuration).

## A duel has no replay

Replays need `history.enabled` and `replays.enabled` to be on. With `replays.kit-only` on, only server-kit duels are recorded. A replay is deleted after `replays.max-age` (14 days by default). Watching one also needs the same arena on this server and a free copy of it: "That duel's arena is not on this server" or "No copy of that arena is free right now" tell which. See [History and replays](/docs/exyliaduels/history).

## A placeholder shows as text or nothing

- Outside Exylia plugins' own files you need PlaceholderAPI installed.
- The duel placeholders (`%exyliaduels_opponent%`, `%exyliaduels_time%`, ...) are empty outside a duel on purpose.
- Leaderboards are empty for the first seconds after a start and refresh once a minute.

See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaduels/placeholders).
