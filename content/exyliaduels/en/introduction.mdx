ExyliaDuels is survival 1v1 duels. A player types `/duel <player>`, picks the terms on one screen and sends
them; the challenged player reads them in chat and clicks **ACCEPT** or **DECLINE**. Both are taken into an
arena the server built, frozen through a countdown, and fight until one of them dies, falls out, leaves or
the clock runs out. Then both go home exactly as they came, and every stake is paid or refunded.

Every stake is paid or refunded **exactly once**, even through crashes, quits and restarts, on one server or
across a network.

It is a **premium** plugin, installed through a licence loader. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliaduels/installation).

## Ways to fight

| Mode | What happens to the inventory |
|---|---|
| **Own inventory, keep** | Players fight with what they carry. Nothing is lost: both go home with exactly what they had. |
| **Own inventory, risk** | Players fight with what they carry, and the loser's whole inventory goes to the winner. |
| **Server kit** | Both wear a kit the server defines. Kit items never leave the arena and nobody keeps them. |

On top of any mode a challenge can carry a **money bet** (both stake the same amount, the winner takes the pot
less an optional tax) and an **item bet** (both put items in a trade window and confirm, the winner takes
both). See [Duelling](/docs/exyliaduels/dueling).

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 1.21.4 or newer, or a fork of it. Folia supported. |
| Java | 21 |
| Required dependencies | `packetevents`, and the newest `ExyliaLib`, installed for you if it is missing |
| An economy | Only for money bets: `ExyliaEconomy`, `Vault` with an economy plugin, `PlayerPoints`, or any currency ExyliaLib serves |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI` for the placeholders in other plugins, `FastAsyncWorldEdit` for arena schematics, `ExyliaProxyUtils` on the proxy for network duels |

<Callout type="note" title="About ExyliaLib">
Configuration, menus and prompts, the escrow that holds every stake, the trade window, player snapshots,
the arena pool and its schematics, teleports, the economy, the database and the network all come from
ExyliaLib. ExyliaDuels is written on top of it and does not run without it, which is why the loader installs
it on a server that lacks it.
</Callout>
