---
title: "Permissions"
description: "Every node the plugin declares, what each one gates, and the numbered nodes that give a rank its own cooldown, bet limit and tax."
---

Every player feature has a node everybody holds by default, so a server makes one a rank perk by denying it
to the default group. Declining a challenge and leaving a spectated duel or a replay never need one.

## Player features

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliaduels.duel` | everyone | `/duel <player>`, `accept`, `toggle`, every button of the challenge screen, and the end-of-duel buttons. |
| `exyliaduels.stats` | everyone | `/duel stats`. |
| `exyliaduels.history` | everyone | `/duel history`: your own finished duels. |
| `exyliaduels.history.others` | operators | `/duel history <player>`: anybody's. |
| `exyliaduels.replay` | everyone | Watching the replays of your own duels. |
| `exyliaduels.replay.others` | operators | Watching the replay of anybody's duel. |
| `exyliaduels.spectate` | everyone | `/duel spectate`. |
| `exyliaduels.rematch` | everyone | The **[⟲ REMATCH]** button of the end-of-duel block. |

## Challenge options

Checked on the challenger only, when the challenge is sent: the player challenged answers whatever they were
sent. An option the challenger's rank does not include is refused with "Your rank does not include that
option."

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliaduels.mode.own` | everyone | Challenging with your own inventory. Without it the challenge screen opens on a kit. |
| `exyliaduels.mode.risk` | everyone | Turning on a risk duel, where the loser's inventory goes to the winner. |
| `exyliaduels.bet.money` | everyone | Putting money on a challenge. |
| `exyliaduels.bet.items` | everyone | Putting items on a challenge. |
| `exyliaduels.arena.choose` | everyone | Picking the arena instead of a random one. |
| `exyliaduels.kit.*` | everyone | Challenging with every kit whose own node is not set. |
| `exyliaduels.kit.<id>` | not declared | Challenging with one kit. Another plugin's kits are `exyliaduels.kit.<source>:<kit>`, such as `exyliaduels.kit.randomkits:random`. |

A kit whose own `exyliaduels.kit.<id>` node is not set follows `exyliaduels.kit.*`. To sell a kit, deny
`exyliaduels.kit.*` to the default group and grant `exyliaduels.kit.<id>` to the rank. A kit a player may
not use is not shown to them in the kit picker.

## Per-rank numbers

Grant a number in the node itself. When a player holds several, the one in the "Wins" column applies;
without any, the value in `config.yml` does.

| Permission | What the number is | Wins | Default from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `exyliaduels.cooldown.<n>` | Seconds between two challenges sent. | lowest | `requests.cooldown` |
| `exyliaduels.bet.max.<n>` | Largest money stake; `0` for no limit. | highest | `bets.money.maximum` |
| `exyliaduels.tax.<n>` | Whole percent of a won pot that is destroyed, read from the winner. | lowest | `bets.money.tax-percent` |

`exyliaduels.bypass.cooldown` and `exyliaduels.bypass.tax` still remove the wait and the tax whatever
number a rank has.

## Bypasses and staff

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliaduels.bypass.cooldown` | operators | Sending challenges without waiting between them. |
| `exyliaduels.bypass.tax` | nobody | Keeping the whole money pot of a won duel, untaxed. |
| `exyliaduels.admin` | operators | `/duelsadmin`, `/duelsadmin reload`, every admin screen and button, and walking or teleporting into an arena in use. |

Every menu action checks its node itself, so copying a button into a screen players can open hands them
nothing they did not already have.

<Callout type="tip" title="Bypass tax is nobody's by default">
`exyliaduels.bypass.tax` is not even given to operators. Grant it explicitly to whoever should keep the
whole pot.
</Callout>
