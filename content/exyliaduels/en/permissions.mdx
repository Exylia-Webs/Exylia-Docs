---
title: "Permissions"
description: "The six nodes the plugin declares and what each one gates."
---

| Permission | Default | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliaduels.duel` | everyone | `/duel <player>`, `accept`, `decline`, `toggle`, and every button of the challenge screen. |
| `exyliaduels.stats` | everyone | `/duel stats`. |
| `exyliaduels.spectate` | everyone | `/duel spectate` and `/duel leave`. |
| `exyliaduels.bypass.cooldown` | operators | Sending challenges without waiting `requests.cooldown` between them. |
| `exyliaduels.bypass.tax` | nobody | Keeping the whole money pot of a won duel, untaxed. |
| `exyliaduels.admin` | operators | `/duelsadmin`, `/duelsadmin reload`, every admin screen and button, and walking or teleporting into an arena in use. |

Every menu action checks its node itself, so copying a button into a screen players can open hands them
nothing they did not already have.

<Callout type="tip" title="Bypass tax is nobody's by default">
`exyliaduels.bypass.tax` is not even given to operators. Grant it explicitly to whoever should keep the
whole pot.
</Callout>
