At the end of this page you have one arena where two players can fight with `/duel <player>`. You only use the admin panel and chat.

An arena needs three things before duels can be sent to it: an **area** (the box that is saved and copied), **two spawns** (one per fighter) and to be **enabled**. A new arena is already enabled and may make 4 copies of itself while it is busy.

## Before you start

Install the plugin and make sure you have the `exyliaduels.admin` permission. See [Install ExyliaDuels](/docs/exyliaduels/installation). Build the arena itself first, in any world, with walls or a drop so nobody can run away.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the arena">
    Run `/duelsadmin`, click **Arenas**, then **Create arena**. Type a short id in chat, for example `classic`. The arena is created empty and its settings menu opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the area">
    Click **Area**. Left-click one corner of the arena, right-click the opposite corner, then Shift + left-click to confirm. The arena is saved as a schematic for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the two spawns">
    Click **Spawn a**, stand where the challenger starts and sneak-click. Then click **Spawn b** and do the same where the challenged player starts. The direction you face is kept. A spawn outside the area is refused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it is ready">
    Go back to **Arenas**. Your arena must say **Ready**. If it says "Not ready (world or schematic missing)", set the **Area** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Challenge a friend">
    Ask a second player to join. Run `/duel <their name>`. In the challenge screen leave **Mode** on your own inventory and click **Send challenge**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept">
    The other player reads the terms in chat and clicks **[✔ ACCEPT]**, or runs `/duel accept`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Both players are taken to the arena and frozen through a 5 second countdown, then fight. When one dies, falls out of the arena or leaves, both are sent back exactly as they came, and an end-of-duel message offers **[INVENTORY]**, **[YOURS]** and **[⟲ REMATCH]**. The duel is saved in `/duel history`.

You cannot duel yourself, so testing needs a second account or a friend. If the challenge says "There is no arena ready for duels yet", see [Common problems](/docs/exyliaduels/troubleshooting).

## Make a kit

Own-inventory duels need no kit. To give both players the same gear, add a server kit:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dress as the kit">
    Put on and carry exactly what both players should receive, armour and off hand included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    In `/duelsadmin` click **Kits**, then **Create kit**, and type an id such as `nodebuff`. The kit starts as what you are wearing and is **disabled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable it">
    Click **Status** to enable it. Open **Items** if you want to rearrange the items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From now on the challenge screen offers the kit. See [Create arenas and kits](/docs/exyliaduels/arenas-and-kits) for names, icons and kit arenas.

<Callout type="tip" title="Good first choices">
Leave **Copies** at 4 so several duels can run in the same arena at once. Leave bets for later: they are on by default and need an economy. See [Set up bets and tax](/docs/exyliaduels/bets).
</Callout>
