title: "Network"
description: "Challenging players on other servers, server-kit duels on an arena server, what a network needs, and what never crosses servers."
---

With `network.enabled: true` players can challenge anyone on the network, and server-kit duels are fought on
one **arena server**. Both players are sent there, fight, and are sent back to their own server afterwards.

## What a network needs

<Steps>
  <Step title="One database">
    The same database on every server in `plugins/ExyliaDuels/database.yml` (MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL or
    MongoDB), so kits, records and the escrow are shared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redis">
    Redis enabled in that same `database.yml` on every server, with the same Redis and `key-prefix`, and a
    **different `server-id` on each server**. See [ExyliaLib's database](/docs/exylialib/database).
  </Step>
  <Step title="ExyliaProxyUtils">
    [ExyliaProxyUtils](/docs/exyliaproxyutils/introduction) on the proxy, with the proxy's server names
    **equal to the backends' `server-id`s**: that name is where messages and players are sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="One arena server">
    The arenas built on it, its `server-id` written in `network.arena-server` on every server, and
    `network.enabled: true` there too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shared economy, if any">
    `network.shared-economy: true` only when every server reads the same balances (a shared ExyliaEconomy,
    or a Vault economy on the shared database). Otherwise players on different servers cannot bet money.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How a network duel goes

- `/duel <player>` finds the player wherever they are and tab-completes every name on the network. The
  request, the answer and its expiry travel between the two servers; the challenged player gets the same
  clickable block as on one server.
- **Server-kit duels** may span the network. On accept, both players are held where they are, the arena
  server is told who is coming, and both are sent there. Once both have arrived it reads the kit fresh from
  the database, holds the money bets, runs the countdown, the kit and the fight, and sends each player back
  to their own server.
- If somebody does not arrive within **30 seconds**, the duel is called off and whoever did arrive is sent
  back; nothing was taken. A player who still turns up within two minutes after that is sent straight home.
- A held player who is never sent is let go after 30 seconds.
- A server-kit duel between two players on the **same** server that has no arena for it (no ready arena
  for that kit, or the chosen arena is not ready here) is also sent to the arena server.

## What never crosses servers

| Term | Across servers |
|---|---|
| Own inventory, keep or risk | No: a player's inventory lives on their server. "Fighting with your own inventory needs both players on the same server." |
| Item bets | No: "Item bets need both players on the same server." |
| Money bets | Only with `network.shared-economy: true`, and never in a currency of items or experience, which stay on the server the player carries them on. |
| Server kit | Yes. |

When the players are on different servers the challenge screen opens on a server kit and refuses to switch
to own inventory or turn on an item bet. The arena picker then lists every enabled arena, since the duel is
fought on the arena server.

## Spectating

Arenas, spectators and their schematics are local to the server that built them: spectate a network duel
from the arena server.
