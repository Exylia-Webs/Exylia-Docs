## Challenging

`/duel <player>` opens the challenge screen. It tab-completes every player on this server, and every player
on the network when [network duels](/docs/exyliaduels/network) are on.

Before the screen opens, the plugin checks that:

- the player is not challenging themselves;
- the target takes challenges (`/duel toggle` turns them off);
- neither player is already in a duel, watching one or on their way to one, or held by another plugin
  (an ExyliaLib session such as a queue or a minigame);
- neither player is combat tagged;
- at least one arena is ready (or, on a network, a kit duel could be fought on the arena server);
- the challenger is not on the challenge cooldown (`requests.cooldown`, or the rank's
  `exyliaduels.cooldown.<n>`, skipped with `exyliaduels.bypass.cooldown`).

### The challenge screen

One screen, one card per term. Every click redraws it in place; ESC closes it and leaves nothing behind.

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Challenge** (top) | The opponent's head. |
| **Mode** | Switches between **own inventory** and **kit**. Only the modes the server allows are offered. |
| **Kit** or **Risk** | With a kit, opens a picker of the enabled server kits, then the kits other plugins offer (such as the Random kit), only those the player's rank includes. With their own inventory, toggles **risk**: whether the loser's inventory goes to the winner. |
| **Arena** | Opens a picker: **Random**, or any ready arena. An arena bound to a kit is listed as `Name (Kit)`, and picking it also picks its kit. |
| **Money bet** | Asks for the currency, when more than one is offered, then the amount each player stakes, between the minimum and the maximum (the rank's `exyliaduels.bet.max.<n>` when it has one); `0` removes the bet. |
| **Item bet** | Toggles an item bet: both players fill a trade window before the fight. |
| **Send challenge** | Checks everything again and sends it. |

The screen opens on own inventory when the server allows it and an arena takes it; otherwise on the first
enabled kit. A change that the chosen arena no longer takes (a different kit, or own inventory in a
kit-bound arena) puts the arena back to **Random**. A money bet the player cannot afford, or under the
minimum, is refused when typed, so the screen never shows a bet that cannot go out.

Each option sits behind a node everybody holds by default (`exyliaduels.mode.own`, `mode.risk`,
`bet.money`, `bet.items`, `arena.choose`, `kit.<id>`). A button the player's rank does not include answers
"Your rank does not include that option." and changes nothing. See
[Permissions](/docs/exyliaduels/permissions#challenge-options).

### The request

On send, the cooldown starts and the challenged player receives a centred block in chat: who, the mode, the
arena, the bet and how long it waits, with clickable **[✔ ACCEPT]** and **[✘ DECLINE]** buttons. A kit
with hits to win or rounds says so after its name: "(first to 5 hits)", "(best of 3)". A
challenge waits `requests.expiry` (60 seconds by default); when it runs out both are told.

Each player has at most one challenge out: a new one replaces the previous. Leaving the server drops every
challenge a player sent or received.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/duel accept [player]` | Accepts that player's challenge; without a name, the newest one. |
| `/duel decline [player]` | Declines it; without a name, the newest one. The challenger is told. Never needs a permission. |

## From accept to fight

Accepting checks the terms, both players and both balances **again**: the server, the arenas or either
player may have changed since the challenge was sent. Then:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Both players are claimed">
    The moment the duel is accepted, both players are held for it, so no other plugin takes them while the
    stakes are collected. From now until they are home, only the commands in `allowed-commands` work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Item bet">
    With an item bet, a trade window opens for both. Each puts their stake in, both see both offers live
    (with the money bet and the mode under the title), and both confirm. Neither may confirm an empty offer.
    Closing it, or two minutes passing, calls the duel off; nothing was lost. Confirmed items go to the
    escrow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Money bet">
    Both stakes are taken by ExyliaLib's escrow. If either player cannot pay, the duel is called off and
    whatever was taken goes back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="An arena">
    The chosen arena, or a random one. When it is busy, a copy is made in the temporary world, up to the
    number of copies the arena allows. When none is free, the duel is called off and every stake goes back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Into the arena">
    Each player's state is saved, then they are taken to their spawn: the challenger to spawn A, the
    challenged player to spawn B. In a kit duel both are put in survival and dressed in the kit; a kit from
    another plugin is put on by that plugin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Countdown">
    Both start even: survival, full health and food, no potion effects, no fire, unless the kit's
    [fight start](/docs/exyliaduels/arenas-and-kits#rules) says otherwise. They are frozen in place through
    `match.countdown` (5 seconds by default) with a countdown title, and the duel scoreboard comes up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fight">
    **FIGHT!** Both are released, the kit's potion effects are given, and a boss bar shows the time left of
    `match.max-duration`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The fight

- **Only the two opponents can hurt each other.** This runs last, so a no-PvP region of another plugin does
  not stop a duel, and nobody outside can join one. Fighters and spectators cannot hurt players outside the
  duel.
- **Nobody leaves the arena.** A fighter cannot walk out of the arena's columns, and a teleport away is
  refused with "You can't leave the arena during a duel." Nobody else walks or teleports into an arena in use, except
  players with `exyliaduels.admin`.
- **Falling out loses.** Dropping more than a block below the arena's floor counts as a death, and so does
  dropping below the kit's **fall depth**.
- **The kit's rules apply**: its arena flags, no damage, hit delay, regeneration, hunger, the ender pearl
  and golden apple cooldowns and the build height. See [Arenas and kits](/docs/exyliaduels/arenas-and-kits#rules).
- **Skill plugins are held back.** AuraSkills and mcMMO give fighters no experience, abilities or combat
  stats until they are home.
- **Commands** outside `allowed-commands` are refused.
- **Dropping items** is refused in kit duels and own-inventory duels without risk: what the snapshot gives
  back would come back twice. In a risk duel the items are the players' to throw.
- **The ender chest** cannot be opened from the arena on: it is restored with the player afterwards.
- **Dying** never really happens: the death is cancelled and nothing drops. During the fight it loses the
  duel. Graves, death chests and kill rewards never see a duel death.

## Rounds

A kit with **Rounds** set is played as a best of 3, 5 or more. Losing a round (a death, a fall, or the
opponent landing the hits to win) scores it for the other player: both see the score in chat and as a
ROUND WON or ROUND LOST title for `match.end-delay`. Then the arena's blocks are put back, what was left
lying around is cleared, and both start again from their spawns with the kit and its fight start (a kit from
another plugin is put on again by it, with the same roll), frozen through `match.countdown` with the round
number under the title.

- The first to win the kit's rounds wins the duel. Only the duel decides records, bets and stakes.
- `match.max-duration` covers every round together: running out is a draw, whatever the score, and every
  stake is refunded.
- Quitting during any round forfeits the whole duel.
- The score is on `rounds-scoreboard`, and the challenge reads "(best of N)".
- Own inventory duels, kept or at risk, are always a single fight.

## How a duel ends

| Ending | Result |
|---|---|
| A player dies or falls out | The other wins. With rounds, the other wins the round. |
| A player lands the kit's hits to win | They win (the round, with rounds). The other is told "… landed N hits first." |
| A player quits during the fight | The other wins: "… left the duel. You win!". Combat-log punishments are cancelled; quitting already forfeits. |
| A player quits before the arena | The duel is called off; every stake goes back. |
| `match.max-duration` runs out | A **draw**: every stake goes back. |
| Another plugin takes a player back | Before the fight it is called off; during it, a draw. |

Both see a VICTORY, DEFEAT or DRAW title, and the loser watches the end as a spectator. The result stays on
screen for `match.end-delay` (3 seconds by default). Then spectators are sent back, both players are restored, the stakes are settled, and the arena is
freed and regenerated.

### Who gets what

| Stake | Winner | Draw or called off |
|---|---|---|
| Money | Both stakes, less `bets.money.tax-percent` (5% by default) or the winner's `exyliaduels.tax.<n>`, which is destroyed: a money sink. Players with `exyliaduels.bypass.tax` keep it all. | Refunded in full. |
| Items | Both stakes, sent to the winner's inventory. | Refunded. |
| Risk duel | The loser's whole inventory, armour and off hand (the cursor and crafting grid included). The loser keeps everything else they had. | Nobody loses anything. |
| Kit | Nobody keeps the kit. Both get their own things back. | Same. |

A player who quit gets their things back on their next join. Wins, losses and streaks are only recorded when
there is a winner; a draw changes no record.

<Callout type="note" title="Crashes and restarts">
Money and items are written to the escrow before they move, and each player is saved before the teleport
in. A crash refunds what was held on the next start and gives each player their saved state on their next
join; a risk duel's inventory is the escrow's, so it is never given back twice. On a plugin disable,
running duels are dropped the same way, and players still online are sent to the main world's spawn.
</Callout>

## After the duel

Once a duel that reached the fight is decided, both players read a centred block in chat: **DUEL OVER**
with the opponent, the duration ⌚ and, for a kit of rounds or hits, the score. Three buttons work for
`after-duel.window` (5 minutes by default; `0s` turns them off):

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[INVENTORY]** | A read-only screen with what the opponent ended with, captured the moment the duel was decided, before anything was given back or taken as a stake: armour, off hand, every slot, and a card with the result, health, food, active effects, potions and soups left, hits and rounds. A button switches to the other player. |
| **[YOURS]** | The same screen for what you ended with. |
| **[⟲ REMATCH]** | The same challenge again, sent as an ordinary request: every check, the cooldown and the toggle apply, and the other player gets the usual accept and decline block. Same mode, kit and risk; the arena while it still exists, else a random one; the money bet only while both can still pay it; never an item bet, since both would have to fill the trade window again. Needs `exyliaduels.rematch`. |

A button clicked after its window, or by somebody who was not in the duel, answers "That duel can no longer
be looked back on." On a network the summary is kept in the shared database until its window closes, so the
buttons work from whichever server the players were sent back to.

## History

`/duel history` lists your finished duels, newest first: result, opponent, mode, score, duration ⌚ and
date, with your wins, losses and draws among them. `/duel history <player>` shows anybody's and needs
`exyliaduels.history.others`. Each duel opens a screen with the mode and arena, the score, the money bet,
the time, the server that decided it, and **Watch replay**.

Every duel that reached the fight is stored, draws included; a duel called off before the fight never is.
Each player keeps `history.keep` duels (45 by default) for up to `history.max-age` (30 days).

## Replays

Every duel that reaches the fight is recorded (or only kit duels, with `replays.kit-only`): both fighters,
every arrow, pearl and potion, every block placed, broken or blown up inside the arena, and each round.
**Watch replay** gives the viewer a free copy of the same arena, where they fly around the fight as a camera
nobody can touch, driven from a hotbar:

| Slot | Button |
|---|---|
| 1 | **« 10 seconds** back. |
| 2 | **Play / pause**. |
| 3 | **10 seconds »** ahead. |
| 5 | **Speed**: left-click slower, right-click faster. |
| 6 | **A fighter's eyes**: each fighter's view in turn, then your own again. |
| 7 | **From the start**. |
| 9 | **Leave**. |

The time, length and speed show above it. `/duel leave`, quitting or a stop also leave, and the viewer is
put back exactly as they were. The hotbar is drawn for the viewer only: what they really hold stays
untouched.

- Your own duels need `exyliaduels.replay`; anybody's, `exyliaduels.replay.others`.
- The replay is watched in its own arena: a server without that arena refuses it with "That duel's arena is
  not on this server.", and when every copy is in use, "No copy of that arena is free right now."
- One replay is stored for both players and deleted with the last history row that lists it, or after
  `replays.max-age` (14 days).

## Spectators

`/duel spectate <player>` watches a duel being fought on this server, from inside the arena, in spectator
mode. It tab-completes the players fighting here. The spectator is saved and taken between the two spawns,
a little above them; both fighters are told who is watching.

A spectator cannot leave the arena's box and can only use the commands in `allowed-commands`.
`/duel leave` sends them back exactly as they came, and so does the end of the duel. Switched off with
`spectators.enabled: false`.

## Records

Every player has a record: wins, losses, current win streak, best streak and money won. `/duel stats`
shows your own, `/duel stats <player>` anybody's the server knows.

Money won only counts payouts in the configured currency (`bets.money.currency`, or the server's default):
amounts in different currencies do not add up.

Win rate, games played, leaderboards of wins and streaks, and the last duel's opponent and result are
placeholders: see [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaduels/placeholders).

`/duel toggle` stops or resumes receiving challenges; it is stored with the record, so it follows the
player across servers sharing the database.
