Use these to show duel records and live duel data in ExyliaLib texts and, through PlaceholderAPI, in holograms, tab lists and chat. Click a placeholder to copy it.

<Placeholders />

## Leaderboards

Replace `<stat>` with `wins`, `streak` or `best_streak`, and `<n>` with a position from 1 to 10:

```text
%exyliaduels_top_name_wins_1%        name of the first by wins
%exyliaduels_top_value_wins_1%       their wins
%exyliaduels_top_name_best_streak_3% third by best streak
```

Each board keeps the top ten players with a value above zero and is read from the database once a minute. For the first seconds after a start the boards are empty, and an empty position shows `---` (name) or `0` (value).

## Live duel values

The `opponent`, `opponent_health`, `time`, `mode`, `pot`, `hits`, `rounds` and `round` placeholders answer only while the player is in a duel and are empty otherwise. The hits placeholders belong to kits won by landing hits, and the rounds placeholders to kits played in several rounds. These are the ones the match scoreboards use, see [Configuration](/docs/exyliaduels/configuration).

<Callout type="note" title="PlaceholderAPI">
Inside Exylia plugins' own files the placeholders work as they are. In other plugins you need PlaceholderAPI installed. Nothing has to be downloaded from its eCloud.
</Callout>
