---
title: "Placeholders"
description: "Every placeholder the plugin answers: the viewer's duel record, and the duel they are fighting."
---

The plugin registers its placeholders through ExyliaLib under the group `exyliaduels`, so the name is the
same inside Exylia plugins' own files and through **PlaceholderAPI**: `%exyliaduels_wins%`. Nothing has to
be downloaded from the eCloud. Every value is read from memory.

## The record

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliaduels_wins%` | Duels won. |
| `%exyliaduels_losses%` | Duels lost. |
| `%exyliaduels_streak%` | Current win streak. A loss resets it. |
| `%exyliaduels_best_streak%` | Best win streak ever. |
| `%exyliaduels_money_won%` | Money won in the configured currency, formatted. |
| `%exyliaduels_accepting%` | Whether the viewer takes challenges: `true` or `false`. |
| `%exyliaduels_in_duel%` | Whether the viewer is in a duel, watching one or on their way to one: `true` or `false`. |

## The duel

Empty outside a duel. These are the ones the duel scoreboard uses.

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliaduels_opponent%` | The opponent's name. |
| `%exyliaduels_opponent_health%` | The opponent's health, with one decimal, such as `17.5`. |
| `%exyliaduels_time%` | How long the fight has lasted, as `1:35`. `0:00` during the countdown. |
| `%exyliaduels_mode%` | The kit's name, or `Own inventory` or `Risk`. |
| `%exyliaduels_pot%` | What the winner takes before tax: both money stakes, formatted, or `None`. |

The words `Own inventory`, `Risk` and `None` are the `labels.board-*` keys in `messages.yml`.

## Examples

```text
%exyliaduels_wins%
%exyliaduels_best_streak%
%exyliaduels_opponent_health%
```
