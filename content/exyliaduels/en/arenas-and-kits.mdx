Everything is set up in game with `/duelsadmin` (also `/dueladmin`), which needs `exyliaduels.admin`. The
panel has three cards: **Arenas** (with how many exist and how many are ready), **Kits** and **Settings**.
Every change is saved at once; nothing needs a reload.

## Arenas

An arena is a box in a world with two spawns. It is saved as a schematic, regenerated from it after every
duel, and copied into ExyliaLib's temporary world while it is busy.

### Creating one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    **Arenas** → **Create arena**, and type an id. The arena is created empty and its hub opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Area">
    Click **Area** and select the whole arena with the selection wand: both corners. Selecting it saves
    the schematic for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spawns">
    Click **Spawn A**, stand where the challenger starts and sneak-click; then **Spawn B** for the challenged
    player. The direction you face is kept. A spawn outside the box is refused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copies and kit">
    Set how many **copies** it may have, and optionally bind it to a **kit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An arena is **ready**, and duels can be sent to it, only while it is enabled, both spawns are set, its world
is loaded and its schematic is saved. Anything else keeps it out, so a duel is never sent to half an arena.
The list and the hub show **Ready** or **Not ready (world or schematic missing)**.

### The arena hub

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Enables or disables the arena, and shows whether it is ready and how busy it is right now: in use, copies made and the maximum. |
| **Display name** | What players read in the arena picker and the request. |
| **Icon** | The item it is shown with in the arena picker. |
| **Kit** | **Any kit**, or one kit it is bound to. |
| **Area** | The box: world and size. Selecting it again saves a new schematic. |
| **Spawn A**, **Spawn B** | Where the challenger and the challenged player start. |
| **Copies** | How many copies may be made in the temporary world while it is busy, `0` to `64`; `0` never copies. New arenas start with `4`. |
| **Schematic** | Saves the box again as the schematic. Do it after building. |
| **Teleport** | Takes you to spawn A. |
| **Delete arena** | Deletes it, after a confirmation. |

In the arena list, left-click opens an arena and right-click deletes it, after a confirmation. Deleting keeps
its schematic file; a duel being fought in it keeps it until the duel ends.

### Copies and regeneration

When a duel needs an arena that is busy, a copy of its schematic is pasted in the temporary world, up to its
**Copies**. After every duel the arena, or the copy, is regenerated from the schematic, so blocks placed in a
fight are gone. On start, idle arenas are regenerated too: a crash mid-duel leaves no trace.

Renaming an arena or changing its kit keeps the copies already made; changing its box, spawns or copies
drops them.

### Kit arenas

An arena bound to a kit (a Mace arena, an Elytra Spear arena) only hosts duels with **that kit**:

- In the challenge screen, picking the arena picks its kit too.
- A challenge on any other terms is refused, on send and again on accept: "The arena … only allows the …
  kit."
- A **random** arena for a kit duel prefers the arenas bound to that kit, then the unbound ones.
- Own-inventory duels only ever use unbound arenas.

Deleting a kit unbinds every arena bound to it.

## Kits

A kit is what both players wear in a server-kit duel. Kit items never leave the arena: they cannot be
dropped, and they are taken off when the duel ends.

**Kits** → **Create kit**, and type an id. The new kit starts as **what you are wearing**, and **disabled**.
In its screen:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Enables or disables it. Only enabled kits can be picked. |
| **Display name** | What players read. |
| **Icon** | Its item in the kit picker. |
| **Items** | Opens the loadout editor: inventory, armour and off hand. |
| **Delete kit** | Deletes it, after a confirmation. |

The server-kit mode is offered while at least one kit is enabled.

## Settings

Saved straight to `config.yml`.

| Button | Key |
|---|---|
| **Own inventory** | `modes.own-inventory` |
| **Risk duels** | `modes.risk` |
| **Money bets** | `bets.money.enabled` |
| **Item bets** | `bets.items.enabled` |
| **Minimum bet**, **Maximum bet** | `bets.money.minimum`, `bets.money.maximum` (`0` for no limit) |
| **Tax** | `bets.money.tax-percent`, `0` to `100` |

See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaduels/configuration) for what each one does.
