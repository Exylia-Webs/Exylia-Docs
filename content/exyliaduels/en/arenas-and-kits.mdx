Everything is set up in game with `/duelsadmin` (also `/dueladmin`), which needs `exyliaduels.admin`. The
panel has four cards: **Arenas** (with how many exist and how many are ready), **Kits**, **Settings** and
**All settings**. Every change is saved at once; nothing needs a reload.

An arena only provides the place. How a duel is played, its arena flags and its rules, belongs to what the
players fight with: each server kit, each kit another plugin offers, and own inventory carry their own. A kit
with nothing set plays as a plain duel.

## Arenas

An arena is a box in a world with two spawns. It is saved as a schematic, regenerated from it after every
duel, and copied into ExyliaLib's temporary world while it is busy.

### Creating one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    **Arenas** → **Create arena**, and type an id. The arena is created empty and its hub opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Area">
    Click **Area** and select the whole arena with the selection wand: both corners. Selecting it saves
    the schematic for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spawns">
    Click **Spawn A**, stand where the challenger starts and sneak-click; then **Spawn B** for the challenged
    player. The direction you face is kept. A spawn outside the box is refused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copies and kit">
    Set how many **copies** it may have, and optionally bind it to a **kit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An arena is **ready**, and duels can be sent to it, only while it is enabled, both spawns are set, its world
is loaded and its schematic is saved. Anything else keeps it out, so a duel is never sent to half an arena.
The list and the hub show **Ready** or **Not ready (world or schematic missing)**.

### The arena hub

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Enables or disables the arena, and shows whether it is ready and how busy it is right now: in use, copies made and the maximum. |
| **Display name** | What players read in the arena picker and the request. |
| **Icon** | The item it is shown with in the arena picker. |
| **Kit** | **Any kit**, or one kit it is bound to. |
| **Area** | The box: world and size. Selecting it again saves a new schematic. |
| **Spawn A**, **Spawn B** | Where the challenger and the challenged player start. |
| **Copies** | How many copies may be made in the temporary world while it is busy, `0` to `64`; `0` never copies. New arenas start with `4`. |
| **Schematic** | Saves the box again as the schematic. Do it after building. |
| **Teleport** | Takes you to spawn A. |
| **Delete arena** | Deletes it, after a confirmation. |

In the arena list, left-click opens an arena and right-click deletes it, after a confirmation. Deleting keeps
its schematic file; a duel being fought in it keeps it until the duel ends.

### Copies and regeneration

When a duel needs an arena that is busy, a copy of its schematic is pasted in the temporary world, up to its
**Copies**. After every duel the arena, or the copy, is regenerated from the schematic, so blocks placed in a
fight are gone. On start, idle arenas are regenerated too: a crash mid-duel leaves no trace.

Renaming an arena or changing its kit keeps the copies already made; changing its box, spawns or copies
drops them.

### Kit arenas

An arena bound to a kit (a Mace arena, an Elytra Spear arena) only hosts duels with **that kit**:

- In the challenge screen, picking the arena picks its kit too.
- A challenge on any other terms is refused, on send and again on accept: "The arena … only allows the …
  kit."
- A **random** arena for a kit duel prefers the arenas bound to that kit, then the unbound ones.
- Own-inventory duels only ever use unbound arenas.

Deleting a kit unbinds every arena bound to it.

## Kits

A kit is what both players wear in a server-kit duel. Kit items never leave the arena: they cannot be
dropped, and they are taken off when the duel ends.

**Kits** → **Create kit**, and type an id. The new kit starts as **what you are wearing**, and **disabled**.
In its screen:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Enables or disables it. Only enabled kits can be picked. |
| **Display name** | What players read. |
| **Icon** | Its item in the kit picker. |
| **Items** | Opens the loadout editor: armour, off hand, inventory and hotbar. |
| **Arena flags** | The flags every duel with this kit plays under. See [Arena flags](#arena-flags). |
| **Fight start** | Health, food, potion effects and attributes both players start with. |
| **Combat** | Damage, hit delay, healing, hunger, item cooldowns and build height. |
| **Win condition** | Hits to win, fall depth and rounds. |
| **Delete kit** | Deletes it, after a confirmation. |

The kit mode is offered while at least one kit is enabled, or another plugin offers one. Who may pick a kit
is up to `exyliaduels.kit.<id>`: see [Permissions](/docs/exyliaduels/permissions#challenge-options).

### Arena flags

ExyliaLib's flag editor, over the arena the duel is given: **PvP**, **building**, **breaking**,
**player-placed blocks only**, **interacting**, **timed blocks** that vanish after a number of seconds,
**giving placed blocks back**, **item drop**, **item pickup** and **fall damage**. A flag left on
**Default** keeps the arena's own answer. The flags hold only the two fighters of the duel, and each duel
sets them afresh, so a copy never keeps the flags of the kit fought there before.

### Rules

Each kit sets how its duels are played on three pages. A rule left on its default plays as a plain duel, and
everything a rule changes on a player is given back with the rest of them when the duel ends.

**Fight start**

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Starting health** | Health both start the fight on, never above their maximum. Unset is full. |
| **Starting food**, **Starting saturation** | From `0` to `20`. Unset is full food and the usual saturation. |
| **Potion effects** | Given the moment the fight starts, timed or for the whole duel. |
| **Attributes** | Base values of **maximum health** (vanilla `20`), **movement speed** (`0.1`), **attack damage** (`1`) and **knockback resistance** (`0` to `1`). Unset keeps the player's. |

**Combat**

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| **No damage** | Hits push but hurt nobody, for Sumo or Boxing. |
| **Hit delay** | How long a player is safe after a hit, typed as `250ms` or `1s` (vanilla `1s`). Lower for combo kits. |
| **Natural regeneration** | Whether a full food bar heals. On by default. |
| **Hunger** | Whether the food bar goes down. On by default. |
| **Ender pearl cooldown**, **Golden apple cooldown** | Time between two of them, with the item's cooldown sweep. `0s` for none. |
| **Build height** | How many blocks above their own spawn players may build. `0` for no limit. |

**Win condition**

A death always loses. On top of it:

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Hits to win** | The first to land this many hits wins, for Boxing. Both counts are on the scoreboard, and the challenge reads "first to N hits". `0` turns it off. |
| **Fall depth** | Dropping this many blocks below their own spawn loses, for Sumo. `0` turns it off. |
| **Rounds** | Rounds a player must win to take the duel: `2` is a best of 3. `1` is a single fight. See [Rounds](/docs/exyliaduels/dueling#rounds). |

### Kits from other plugins

Another plugin can offer kits to the challenge screen, such as the **Random kit** of
[ExyliaRandomKitsCore](/docs/exyliarandomkits/network#exyliaduels). They are listed in **Kits** after the
server's, marked **From &lt;source&gt;**. Their items, name and icon belong to the plugin that offers them,
so their screen only sets **Arena flags**, **Fight start**, **Combat** and **Win condition**, applied after
the source has dressed the players. They cannot be deleted here, and on a server without that plugin they
are simply not offered. Their permission node is `exyliaduels.kit.<source>:<kit>`, such as
`exyliaduels.kit.randomkits:random`.

## Settings

**Settings** is saved straight to `config.yml`.

| Button | Key |
|---|---|
| **Own inventory** | `modes.own-inventory` |
| **Risk duels** | `modes.risk` |
| **Money bets** | `bets.money.enabled` |
| **Item bets** | `bets.items.enabled` |
| **Minimum bet**, **Maximum bet** | `bets.money.minimum`, `bets.money.maximum` (`0` for no limit) |
| **Tax** | `bets.money.tax-percent`, `0` to `100` |
| **Own inventory rules** | The arena flags and rules of own inventory duels. See below. |

See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaduels/configuration) for what each one does. **All settings**, on the panel,
opens every key of `config.yml` in ExyliaLib's settings screens, and BACK returns to the panel.

### Own inventory rules

Every duel fought with the players' own items, kept or at risk, plays under one set of **Arena flags**,
**Combat** rules and a **Win condition** of **hits to win** and **fall depth**. The start state, effects,
attributes and rounds are left out: the players' own items decide how they start, and they cannot be put
back between rounds, so an own inventory duel is always a single fight.
