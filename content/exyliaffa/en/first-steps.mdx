At the end of this page you have one arena that players can join from `/ffa`. You only use the admin panel and chat.

An arena needs four things before it can be switched on: an **area**, at least one **spawn**, at least one **kit** and a **saved map**. The map is saved for you when you set the area.

## Before you start

Install the plugin and make sure you have the `exyliaffa.admin` permission. See [Install ExyliaFFA](/docs/exyliaffa/installation).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the lobby">
    Stand where players should return after leaving an arena. Run `/ffaadmin`, click **Lobby location**, then sneak and click where you stand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the arena">
    In the admin panel click **Arenas**, then **Create arena**. Type a short id in chat, using letters, numbers, `-` or `_` and no spaces, for example `nodebuff`. You will use it in commands. Then type the name players will see. The arena's settings menu opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the area">
    Click **Set bounds**. Left-click one corner of the fighting area, right-click the opposite corner, then Shift + left-click to confirm. The map is saved automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a spawn">
    Click **Manage spawns**, then **Add spawn**. Type a name, then stand inside the area where players should appear, and sneak and click. The spawn must be inside the area you marked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a kit">
    Put in your inventory exactly what players should receive, armour and offhand included. Go back, click **Manage kits**, then **Create kit** and type a name. Then left-click the new kit to save your inventory into it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the arena on">
    Back in the settings menu click **Toggle arena**. If something is missing, the chat tells you what.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Run `/ffa` and click your arena, or run `/ffa join nodebuff`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

You should appear at your spawn with the kit, with the arena sidebar on screen. Run `/ffa leave` to go back to the lobby. If the arena does not appear in `/ffa`, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliaffa/troubleshooting).

## Good first values

- **Max players**: 30 to 50 feels busy without chaos.
- **Protected radius**: 3 to 5 blocks around each spawn stops spawn-killing.
- **Regeneration time**: leave it at 0 until the map gets damaged.
- **Save inventory on leave**: turn it on so players get back what they had before.

<Details title="Technical details">
What happens when a player joins: if the arena has more than one spawn and its spawn mode is selectable, the spawn menu opens first. Then, if the kit mode is selectable and there is more than one kit, the kit menu opens. With one of each, the player goes straight in. The same three steps exist in the API: `FFAAPI.joinFFA`, `joinFFAWithSpawn` and `joinFFAWithKit`, see [API](/docs/exyliaffa/api).
</Details>
