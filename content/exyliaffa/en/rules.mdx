Rules decide what players can do in one arena. Each arena has its own, and a change applies at once: no restart, and players do not need to rejoin.

## Open the rules

Run `/ffaadmin`, click **Arenas**, left-click your arena, then click **Rules**. Four cards open their own screen:

| Card | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Arena flags** | Building, PvP, interacting, item drops and pickups, fall damage. A flag left on Default uses the arena's normal value. |
| **Combat** | Anti free-hit, and the ender pearl and golden apple cooldowns. See [Combat](/docs/exyliaffa/combat). |
| **Kills & deaths** | Healing on a kill, clearing effects, dropping items on death. |
| **Survival** | Natural healing and hunger. |

## What you can set

| Rule | Normal | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Player vs player | On | Off turns the arena into a lobby. |
| Fall damage | On | Damage when landing. |
| Natural healing | On | Turn it off for nodebuff style. |
| Hunger | On | Off, food stops mattering. |
| Heal on kill | On | Fully heals the killer. |
| Clear effects on kill | Off | Removes the killer's potion effects. |
| Break blocks | On | Allows breaking. |
| Place blocks | On | Allows placing. |
| Only break player blocks | Off | Protects the original map. |
| Temporary blocks | Off | Placed blocks disappear after the seconds you set. |
| Give blocks back | Off | The block returns to the inventory when it disappears. |
| Interact | On | Chests, buttons, doors. |
| Drop and pick up items | On | Allows both. |
| Drop items on death | Off | The dead player drops their inventory. |
| Ender pearl and golden apple cooldown | 0 | Seconds between uses. 0 is off. |

<Callout type="tip" title="A clean building arena">
Only break player blocks, temporary blocks at 8 seconds and give blocks back. Players can use blocks to fight, the map does not degrade and nobody runs out of blocks.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Drops on death with refilling kits">
Dropping items on death with kits that refill fills the floor with duplicates. Use it only where loot is part of the game.
</Callout>

## Rules for common arenas

<Cards>
  <Card title="NoDebuff">Natural healing off, no breaking or placing, heal on kill on, plus Reset kit after kill in the arena.</Card>
  <Card title="Sumo">PvP on, fall damage off, no building, no item drops.</Card>
  <Card title="Build UHC">Only break player blocks, temporary blocks at 10 seconds, give blocks back, golden apple cooldown of 8.</Card>
  <Card title="Chaos">Everything on, drops on death, no cooldowns. Pairs well with a short regeneration.</Card>
</Cards>

<Details title="Technical details">
Eleven flags are enforced by ExyliaLib's region system: `blockBreak`, `blockPlace`, `pvp`, `interact`, `playerBuildOnly`, `temporaryBlocks`, `temporaryBlocksSeconds`, `reGiveBlocks`, `itemDrop`, `itemPickup`, `fallDamage`. The rest are applied by ExyliaFFA: `healthRegeneration`, `foodDecrease`, `healOnKill`, `clearEffectsOnKill`, `dropItemsOnDeath`, `enderPearlCooldown`, `goldenAppleCooldown`, `antiFreeHit`, `antiFreeHitHitsToLink` (3), `antiFreeHitIsolateLinkedPlayers`. Each arena stores its own block. Defaults are those of the Normal column.
</Details>
