ExyliaFFA gives your server free-for-all PvP arenas. Players join from `/ffa`, get a kit, fight, and climb a leaderboard. You build and manage every arena from menus in game, without editing files.

In about ten minutes you can have one arena running. [Your first arena](/docs/exyliaffa/first-steps) walks you through it.

## What you get

<Cards>
  <Card title="As many arenas as you want" href="/docs/exyliaffa/arenas">
    Each arena has its own area, player limit, rules, kits, spawns and rank requirement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Kits and spawns" href="/docs/exyliaffa/kits">
    Hand out one kit or let players choose, lock kits and spawns behind a rank, and let players rearrange their own kit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rules per arena" href="/docs/exyliaffa/rules">
    Turn building, fall damage, regeneration and cooldowns on or off for each arena.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stats and leaderboards" href="/docs/exyliaffa/stats">
    Kills, deaths, streaks and more, per arena and for the whole server.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fair fights" href="/docs/exyliaffa/combat">
    Combat tag, kill-farming protection and a 1v1 link that stops third parties from stealing kills.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Arena reset" href="/docs/exyliaffa/regeneration">
    Restore the arena to its saved state on a timer, so broken blocks come back.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## What you need

- A Paper server (1.21.1 or newer) or Folia (1.21.4 or newer), running Java 21.
- The plugins ExyliaLib, FastAsyncWorldEdit and packetevents.

Everything else is optional. [Install it](/docs/exyliaffa/installation) has the details.

## How these docs are organised

- **Getting started**: install the plugin and build your first arena.
- **Guides**: one page per task, such as adding spawns or creating a kit.
- **Reference**: every command, permission, placeholder and setting, for when you need to look something up.
- **Help**: common problems and quick answers.

Colours, messages and languages, menus, economy and the database are shared by all Exylia plugins. They are explained once in the [Customise section](/docs/exylialib/colours), not on every plugin.
