A kit is the inventory, armour and offhand players fight with. Each kit belongs to one arena. You save it from what you carry.

## Create a kit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the items">
    Put the items, armour and offhand you want in your own inventory, in the order players should get them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the kit list">
    Run `/ffaadmin`, click **Arenas**, left-click your arena, then click **Manage kits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Create kit**, type a name in chat, and your inventory is saved as the kit. The name accepts colours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish it">
    Set the icon, a permission if the kit is restricted, and a priority. A higher priority shows higher in the menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use PracticeCore kits

If you run ExyliaPracticeCore, an arena can hand out its kits so you keep one copy.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    In the arena's kit list, click **PracticeCore kits**. Every enabled kit shows as linked or not linked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a kit">
    Click one to link it, click again to unlink it. Linking creates a kit with the same name and icon.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Items are read from PracticeCore each time, so a change there reaches the arena at once. To change the items, edit the kit in PracticeCore. Unlinking deletes the kit in the arena. Without PracticeCore a linked kit hands out nothing until it is installed again.

## Let players choose, or choose for them

This is set per arena with **Kit mode**:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Selectable">With more than one kit, a menu opens on entry. The kit they pick is the one they respawn with.</Card>
  <Card title="Random">The game gives one kit at random among the kits the player may use. Good for chaos arenas.</Card>
</Cards>

With a single kit, nobody sees a menu and everyone receives it.

## Make a kit for a rank

Click the kit's permission and type a node such as `exyliaffa.kit.premium`. Only players with it can use the kit. Locked kits show as locked in the menu. In random mode, kits the player cannot use are left out of the draw, so a premium rank gets a wider mix in the same arena. Give the permission as described in [Permissions](/docs/exyliaffa/permissions#restrict-an-arena-kit-or-spawn).

## Players build their own kit

Click **Create player-built kit** in the kit list to make a kit with no items. Each player builds theirs from the **kit room**, a catalogue of items that you edit with the **Kit room** button. Tick the categories players may take from, or none for all. Saving is refused when the inventory is empty or holds something the kit room does not offer.

## Let players rearrange a kit

Any player can put the same items in other slots without changing the kit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Run `/ffa kiteditor` and pick a kit. You are invulnerable and cannot place, break, drop or fire anything meanwhile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move the items">
    Put the items in the slots you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or discard">
    `/ffa savekit` saves it, `/ffa cancelkit` leaves without saving, and `/ffa resetkit <kit>` returns to the original order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="After you change a kit">
If you change a kit's items, old layouts may no longer make sense. Run `/ffaadmin resetlayout <player> <kit>` to clear one player's layout.
</Callout>

## Refill the kit after a kill

Turn on **Reset kit after kill** in the arena settings. A kill refills the killer's kit with the same potions, food and durability. In nodebuff arenas, pair it with the rule that heals on a kill, see [Rules](/docs/exyliaffa/rules). Without it, the first player to chain kills wins by attrition.

<Details title="Technical details">
Fields: name, icon (default chest), permission (empty means everyone), priority (default 0). Kit ids are generated and are what `/ffa resetkit` takes. Kits are not shared between arenas. A linked kit is read from PracticeCore whenever it is handed out or opened in the layout editor, and setting its contents from the FFA menu is refused. The permission is checked in the selector, on a join or respawn that names the kit, and in the API. `/exylialib resetplayer <player> ExyliaFFA` deletes all layouts with the player's stats.
</Details>
