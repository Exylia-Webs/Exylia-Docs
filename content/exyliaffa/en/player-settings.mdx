Every player decides what they see. They run `/ffa settings` and click a switch. Choices are saved in the database and survive reconnects and restarts.

## The switches

| Button | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Scoreboard** | The in-arena sidebar. |
| **Spawn waypoints** | Markers over each spawn of the arena. |
| **Combat action bar** | The combat tag countdown above the hotbar. |
| **Linked opponent bar** | The bar with your 1v1 opponent: name, hearts, ping and time. |
| **Combat messages** | Notices when you enter and leave combat. |
| **Kill streak notifications** | Notices about your own streak. |
| **Kill streak broadcasts** | Announcements of other players' streaks. |
| **Kill / death messages** | Other players' kill and death messages. |
| **Glow on linked player** | The outline on the opponent you are linked with. |
| **Spectator hint** | The hint on the action bar in spectator mode. |

All start switched on, except the scoreboard, which follows `scoreboards.yml`.

<Callout type="note" title="When the server turns something off">
If you disable the sidebar in `scoreboards.yml`, the player's **Scoreboard** switch has no effect and the menu shows it that way. A player cannot enable what the server does not offer, so nobody thinks the plugin is broken.
</Callout>

## Spawn waypoints

With the switch on, every spawn of the arena is drawn as a red marker on the player's screen. The markers belong to ExyliaFFA: they do not replace or delete the ones your lobby plugin draws, and they disappear when the player leaves the arena. A spawn without a location draws nothing. Spawns are explained in [Add spawns](/docs/exyliaffa/spawns).

## Glow on the linked player

When the [anti free-hit](/docs/exyliaffa/combat) link is active and the switch is on, the opponent is outlined. Only the linked player sees it, it uses no scoreboard teams, so it does not clash with your rank prefixes, and it disappears when the link ends.

## Edit the menu

The menu is `menus/user/settings.yml` in each language folder under `lang/`. You can change icons, slots, texts and sounds. If you move a button, keep the action it already has, since that ties it to its setting. See [Menus](/docs/exylialib/menus).

<Details title="Technical details">
Internal keys, in the order of the table: `scoreboard`, `waypoints`, `combat_bar`, `linked_bar`, `combat_msgs`, `ks_notifs`, `ks_broadcasts`, `kd_broadcasts`, `glow`, `spec_hint`. They are what the API's `FfaSetting` exposes, see [API](/docs/exyliaffa/api). The glow is sent by packets through packetevents.
</Details>
