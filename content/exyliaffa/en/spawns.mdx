---
title: "Spawns"
description: "Several spawn points per arena, with name, icon, permission and priority."
---

An arena can have as many spawns as you like. With one, players always appear there. With several,
a selector opens on entry and on respawn, or, in `RANDOM` spawn mode, the player is dropped at the
emptiest one.

## Fields

| Field | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | generated | Internal identifier. |
| `arenaId` | — | The arena it belongs to. |
| `displayName` | — | Name in the selector. Accepts colours. |
| `permission` | empty | Permission required to choose it. |
| `iconMaterial` | `COMPASS` | Icon in the menu. |
| `location` | — | The exact location, taken from where you stood when creating it. |
| `priority` | `0` | Order in the menu. |

## How a spawn is chosen

The arena's **spawn mode**, toggled with **Spawn mode** in its settings menu, decides whether the
player picks:

| Spawn mode | What happens on entry and on respawn |
|---|---|
| `SELECTABLE` (default) | With more than one spawn, the spawn selector opens. With one, the player goes there. |
| `RANDOM` | No selector. The player is dropped at the emptiest spawn. |

Whenever the player does not choose, the plugin looks at the spawns they are allowed to use and picks
one with the fewest fighters within 30 blocks, breaking ties at random. That spreads a busy arena out
instead of stacking everyone on one point.

The spawn selector comes **before** the kit one. The full sequence on entry is:

```text
/ffa join <arena>
   ├── spawnMode SELECTABLE and more than one spawn?  → spawn selector
   ├── kitMode SELECTABLE and more than one kit?  → kit selector
   └── join
```

## Common uses

<Cards>
  <Card title="Map zones">
    One spawn per region of the map lets players pick where to drop. Works very well in large arenas
    where the centre gets crowded.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Premium spawns">
    A permission-gated spawn, in an advantageous position or simply a more comfortable one, as a rank
    reward.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tactical respawn">
    With several spawns spread out, dying does not mean always returning to the same place and being
    camped there.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout type="warning" title="Spawns must sit inside the region">
A spawn outside the arena bounds makes the player appear "outside" and be returned immediately.
Always place spawns inside the marked region.
</Callout>

## Protection

Spawn protection is not configured per spawn but on the arena: a radius, a shape and a mode applied
to **all** of that arena's spawns. See
[Arenas · Spawn protection](/docs/exyliaffa/arenas#spawn-protection).

## Waypoints

If the player has the waypoints setting on, each spawn of the arena is drawn as a marker on their
client. It is switched off per player from `/ffa settings`, with no effect on anyone else.
