A spawn is a point where players appear when they join or respawn. With one spawn, everyone appears there. With several, players pick one or the game picks the emptiest.

## Before you start

You need an arena with its area marked, see [Create and manage arenas](/docs/exyliaffa/arenas). Every spawn must be inside that area, or players are sent back out as soon as they appear.

## Add a spawn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the spawn list">
    Run `/ffaadmin`, click **Arenas**, left-click your arena, then click **Manage spawns**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add spawn**, type a name in chat, then stand where players should appear and sneak and click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Right-click the spawn in the list to teleport to it and check it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change a spawn

Each spawn in the list has actions on different clicks:

| Click | Action |
|---|---|
| Left-click | Set its location to where you stand. |
| Right-click | Teleport to it. |
| Shift + left-click | Rename it. |
| Shift + right-click | Change its icon. |
| Middle-click | Set a permission. |
| Swap key (F) | Set its priority, the order in the menu. |
| Drop key (Q) | Delete it. |

## Let players choose, or choose for them

Open the arena's settings and click **Spawn mode**:

- **SELECTABLE**: when the arena has more than one spawn, a menu opens on joining and on respawning.
- **RANDOM**: no menu. The player appears at the emptiest spawn they are allowed to use.

If the player does not choose, the game picks the spawn with the fewest fighters within 30 blocks. That spreads a busy arena out.

## Make a spawn for VIPs

Middle-click the spawn and type a permission such as `exyliaffa.spawn.tower`. Only players with it can choose that spawn. Give the permission to the rank in your permissions plugin, see [Permissions](/docs/exyliaffa/permissions).

## Protect the spawn

Spawn protection is a setting of the arena, shared by all its spawns. See [Protect players when they appear](/docs/exyliaffa/arenas#protect-players-when-they-appear).

<Details title="Technical details">
On joining, the spawn menu comes before the kit menu. Players with the spawn markers setting on see each spawn of their arena drawn as a marker, see [Player settings](/docs/exyliaffa/player-settings). A spawn with no location draws nothing. A new spawn is rejected if its location is outside the area.
</Details>
