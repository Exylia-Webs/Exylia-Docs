Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

The console names what is missing. ExyliaLib, FastAsyncWorldEdit and packetevents are all required. See [Install ExyliaFFA](/docs/exyliaffa/installation).

## The arena does not show in `/ffa`

The arena is switched off, or it is missing something. Open `/ffaadmin`, **Arenas**, your arena, and click **Toggle arena**. If it cannot be enabled, the chat lists what is missing: a kit, a spawn, the area or the saved map. Fix each in [Your first arena](/docs/exyliaffa/first-steps).

## Players appear and are sent back out

The spawn is outside the arena's area. Add the spawn again while standing inside the area, or mark a bigger area with **Set bounds**.

## The arena does not regenerate

Check that **Regeneration time** is not `0s`, and that the arena has a saved map. Click **Schematic** with a left-click to save it again. See [Reset an arena automatically](/docs/exyliaffa/regeneration).

## Kills are not counting

Kill-farming protection refuses a kill when the same player is killed more than three times in two minutes, or when both players share an IP address. Players testing from the same home count as the same IP. Tune it in [Combat and anti-abuse](/docs/exyliaffa/combat).

## A placeholder shows as text

- PlaceholderAPI is not installed.
- The arena name is misspelled.
- The leaderboard position is above the leaderboard size (50 by default).

See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaffa/placeholders).

## The scoreboard does not show

The player may have turned it off in `/ffa settings`. If nobody sees it, it is switched off for the whole server in `scoreboards.yml`. See [Edit the scoreboard](/docs/exyliaffa/scoreboard).

## The kit does not refill after a kill

That is an arena setting, not a kit setting: turn on **Reset kit after kill** in the arena's settings.

## I changed the database and nothing happened

The database is opened when the server starts. Restart the server: `/ffaadmin reload` does not change it.

## Commands are refused in the arena

While a player is in combat, escape commands are blocked, and inside the arena only a short list of commands is allowed. See [Combat and anti-abuse](/docs/exyliaffa/combat).
