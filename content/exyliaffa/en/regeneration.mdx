Regeneration restores the arena's blocks to the state you saved, so broken or placed blocks do not pile up. It needs FastAsyncWorldEdit.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark the area">
    In the arena's settings, click **Set bounds**. This also saves the map as the "clean" version. Build the arena the way you want it to look first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the interval">
    Click **Regeneration time** and type how often it should restore, such as `5m` or `90s`. `0s` turns it off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it run">
    The clock starts when the arena starts. Each cycle warns the players inside, restores the blocks and counts again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To restore now, click **Force regenerate**, which also restarts the countdown. To keep the current look as the new clean version, click **Schematic**: left-click saves again, right-click restores.

<Callout type="warning" title="No saved map, no arena">
An arena cannot be turned on without a saved map. If **Toggle arena** refuses, click **Set bounds** first. Saving again overwrites the previous map, so save with the arena as you want it clean.
</Callout>

## Choose an interval

| Arena | Interval |
|---|---|
| Nodebuff with no building | Not needed |
| Temporary blocks | 5 to 10 minutes |
| Free building | 2 to 5 minutes |
| Delicate decorative map | 1 to 2 minutes |

<Callout type="tip">
A very short interval interrupts fights. If the arena degrades fast, turn on [Only break player blocks and temporary blocks](/docs/exyliaffa/rules) instead of lowering the timer.
</Callout>

## Show the countdown

Use `%regen%` on the scoreboard, it is in the default lines. When regeneration is off, the line shows the fallback text from `messages.yml`. Players also get messages when it starts and ends, editable in `messages.yml`.

<Details title="Technical details">
Schematics are saved as `ffa_arena_<arenaId>` in the server's schematic folder. The clock is anchored to the arena's location, so on Folia regeneration runs on its own region and does not block the rest of the world. The internal value is `-1` when disabled.
</Details>
