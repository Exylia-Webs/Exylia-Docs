At the end of this page a bot wearing your skin is fighting you in your own world. You edit one line in a file and use the `/bot` menu; there is no arena to build and nothing to save.

## Before you start

- ExyliaPracticeBot and ExyliaLib are installed on a Paper or Folia 1.21.9+ server. See [Installation](/docs/exyliapracticebot/installation).
- You can run `/botadmin reload` (permission `exyliapracticebot.admin`). Players only need `exyliapracticebot.use`, which everyone has by default.
- You know the exact folder name of the world you will fight in.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Allow your world">
    A bot can only exist in a world listed in `worlds`. A fresh install lists Exylia's own `exylia_sandbox_*` worlds, which your server does not have, so nothing is allowed yet. Open `plugins/ExyliaPracticeBotV3/config.yml` and replace the list with your world names, written exactly as the folders are named.

    ```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaPracticeBotV3/config.yml"
    worlds:
      - pvp_sandbox
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/botadmin reload`. No restart needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stand in that world and open the menu">
    Go anywhere in the world and run `/bot`. The main menu is one button per decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Click **Mode** (top middle). Left-click goes to the next mode, right-click to the previous one, and the line under the name says what that mode fights with. There are seven; `POT_PVP` is the default. The mode changes how the bot fights, not its gear. See [Modes](/docs/exyliapracticebot/modes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a difficulty">
    Click **Difficulty**, right next to Mode. Left-click goes up, right-click goes down. There are five, from Easy to Extreme; `NORMAL` is the default and the best place to start. Insane and Extreme are walls, not fair fights. See [Difficulty](/docs/exyliapracticebot/difficulty).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spawn it and fight">
    Click the green **Spawn bot** button on the bottom bar, or run `/bot spawn`. The bot appears where you stand, with your skin and name. Hit it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="If you skip the first step">
Every spawn is answered with "You can't spawn a bot in this world. Please use /rtp first", from the command and from the menu button alike. It is the same check in both places. The fix is always the `worlds` list.
</Callout>

## Check that it works

The bot follows you, hits back, and heals, blocks or trades the way its mode and difficulty say. Nothing you do to it drops loot or gives experience. If you get the world message instead, your world name does not match a `worlds` entry exactly, or you skipped `/botadmin reload`. More in [Common problems](/docs/exyliapracticebot/troubleshooting).

## Going further

- **Reopen the menu without typing.** Shift right-click your own bot. Only its owner can, and only while `interaction.shift-click-opens-menu` is on.
- **Close the distance.** `/bot tpto` takes you to the bot, `/bot tpme` brings it to you, and the **Teleport** button does the same.
- **Make it a punching bag.** Turn **Fight back** off and it becomes a moving punching bag. Turn **Follow you** off and it holds its ground, swinging only at what comes to it. **Shield up** makes it never swing and keep its shield raised at you, so you can practise axe-then-mace stun slams.
- **Change its gear.** Left-click a piece of armour in the left column for the next tier, right-click for its enchantment. Main hand, off hand and potions are on the right. See [Equipment](/docs/exyliapracticebot/equipment).
- **Fine-tune.** **Fine tuning** opens the second screen: damage, reach, attack and movement speed, health, totems and the sandbox toggles.
- **Send it away.** `/bot remove` removes it, and `/bot reset` puts every one of your settings back to the server default. The menu has **Remove bot** and **Reset settings** buttons.

Your settings are stored against your UUID. They are waiting for you the next time you log in, on this server or on any server that shares the database.

<Details title="When a bot disappears or comes back">
- The bot is removed when you die, when you change world, and when you get more than 200 blocks away from it. The chat message says which one it was.
- After the bot dies it respawns two seconds later by default (`respawn.enabled`, `respawn.delay-seconds`).
- Every slider in the fine-tuning screen is clamped to the bounds of the `limits` block of `config.yml`. See [Limits](/docs/exyliapracticebot/limits) and [Configuration](/docs/exyliapracticebot/configuration).
- Each player has one bot, and it fights only its owner.
</Details>
