Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not load

ExyliaPracticeBot needs **Paper or Folia 1.21.9 or newer**; an older server refuses the jar with an `api-version` error. It also needs ExyliaLib. Without it you never see `ExyliaPracticeBotV3 enabled!` in the console. PacketEvents is optional. See [Install ExyliaPracticeBot](/docs/exyliapracticebot/installation).

## "You can't spawn a bot in this world"

The world you stand in is not in the `worlds` list of `plugins/ExyliaPracticeBotV3/config.yml`. The list ships with Exylia's own sandbox worlds, so on your server nothing is allowed until you put your own world names in. An empty list allows nothing. Run `/botadmin reload` afterwards. See [Your first bot](/docs/exyliapracticebot/first-steps).

## "You already have a bot. Use /bot remove first."

Each player gets one bot. Run `/bot remove`, or click the remove button in the `/bot` menu, then spawn again.

## "The server is already running N bots"

The server hit `max-bots` (16 by default), minus the slots kept in `reserved-for-matches`. Raise `max-bots` only after watching your tick time with the current number running. Staff with `exyliapracticebot.bypass.limit` can use the reserved slots. See [Limits](/docs/exyliapracticebot/limits).

## The bot vanished on its own

The bot is removed when you die, change world, log out, or get more than 200 blocks away. The first three tell you which one it was. After a server restart no bots remain by design. See [Behaviour](/docs/exyliapracticebot/behaviour).

## The bot comes back after I kill it

`respawn.enabled` is on, with a two second delay by default. Turn it off in `config.yml` to keep a killed bot gone until the player spawns another.

## Nothing I do kills the bot

`advanced.health.invincible` is on, or the totems are. `advanced.health.totems` is `-1` by default, which means the bot re-arms with a totem forever. Both are on the **Fine tuning** screen.

## A mode, difficulty or button does nothing

The player lacks the permission: `exyliapracticebot.mode.<mode>`, `exyliapracticebot.difficulty.<level>`, `exyliapracticebot.spawn`, `exyliapracticebot.teleport` or `exyliapracticebot.advanced`. They get "You don't have permission to do that.", and the mode and difficulty buttons skip what they cannot pick. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliapracticebot/permissions).

## A denied mode still shows on a player's bot

Permissions gate picking, not what is already saved. The player keeps the old mode until they change it or use `/bot reset`. Keep the server defaults `bot.mode` and `bot.difficulty` on values everybody may use.

## `/bot tpto` says the bot must be in sight

You can only teleport to a bot you can see, in your own world. Use `/bot tpme` to bring it to you.

## Settings are not saved between servers

Settings live in the `practicebot_players` table. Servers share them only when they point `database.yml` at the same MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL or MongoDB database. See [Database](/docs/exylialib/database).
