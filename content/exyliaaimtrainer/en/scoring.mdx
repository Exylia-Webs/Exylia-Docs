A session produces three different numbers, and each one answers a different question.

| Number | What it says | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Score** | How well the drill was played, in the drill's own currency. | Deciding a duel round. The `score` board. |
| **Difficulty** | How hard the player's settings made the drill. | Multiplying the score. |
| **Rating** | The score times the difficulty. | Personal bests, the `rating` board and the overall board. |

A hit also gets a **grade**, `PERFECT` to `SLOW`, which is feedback only: it never changes a score.

## The score

Everything tunable lives under `scoring`:

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaAimTrainer/config.yml"
scoring:
  hit-points: 10
  miss-penalty: 4
  expire-penalty: 6
  false-start-penalty-millis: 150
  reaction-reference-millis: 250
  reaction-scale: 1000
  tracking-scale: 10
  combo-points: 5
  size-exponent: 1.0
  distance-exponent: 0.75
  min-difficulty: 0.25
  max-difficulty: 6.0
```

### `FLICK`: points per minute

```text
points = hits × hit-points − misses × miss-penalty − expired × expire-penalty
score  = points × 60 / seconds played
```

Per minute, so a 30-second drill and a 60-second one compare. Points at or below zero score `0`. Menus
write the score with `pts/min`.

Sixty hits and six misses in 30 seconds: 600 − 24 = 576 points, times 60 / 30, **1152**.

### `COMBO`: points per minute, plus combos

```text
points = hits × hit-points − misses × miss-penalty + combo hits × combo-points
score  = points × 60 / seconds played
```

A **combo hit** is a hit that came within the drill's `combo-window` of the previous hit. Rushed hits are
misses. Forty hits, four misses and thirty combo hits in 30 seconds: 400 − 16 + 150 = 534, times 2,
**1068**.

### `REACTION`: the average against a reference

```text
average = (sum of reactions + false starts × false-start-penalty-millis) / reactions answered
score   = reaction-scale × reaction-reference-millis / average
```

An average of exactly `reaction-reference-millis` scores `reaction-scale`: 250 ms is 1000. Faster is more,
slower is less. Nothing answered scores `0`. Menus write the score with `pts`.

Ten reactions averaging 250 ms score **1000**. The same ten with one false start: (2500 + 150) / 10 =
265 ms, **943**.

### `TRACK`: time on target

```text
score = percent of ticks on target × tracking-scale
```

On target 62.5% of the session: **625**. Menus write the score with `pts`.

<Callout type="note" title="Seconds played, not seconds configured">
The per-minute score divides by the time the run actually lasted. A completed drill lasted its
`duration`. A run cut short — the player left, replayed or was pulled into a practice match — is still
stored, scored over the time it ran.
</Callout>

## Difficulty

```text
difficulty = weight × (1 / size multiplier) ^ size-exponent × (distance multiplier) ^ distance-exponent
```

Clamped to `min-difficulty..max-difficulty` and rounded to two decimals. `weight` is the drill's; the two
multipliers are the player's [settings](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/settings). A multiplier below `0.05` counts
as `0.05`.

With the default exponents, a smaller target is worth exactly its inverse and a farther one a little less
than its distance:

| Size | Distance | Difficulty at weight 1.0 |
|---|---|---|
| ×1.00 | ×1.00 | 1.00 |
| ×0.50 | ×1.00 | 2.00 |
| ×1.00 | ×2.00 | 1.68 |
| ×0.50 | ×2.00 | 3.36 |
| ×2.50 | ×1.00 | 0.40 |
| ×1.00 | ×0.25 | 0.35 |
| ×0.10 | ×1.00 | 6.00 (clamped from 10) |

The drill's weight multiplies the whole thing before the clamp: `precision` at weight 1.6 with half-size
targets is 3.20.

In a duel both players play the same rules, so their difficulty is the same. With `match.use-preferences`
off, which is the default, it is the drill's weight alone. See [Duels](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/duels).

## Rating

```text
rating = score × difficulty, rounded, never below 0
```

The rating is the one number that compares two players who set their targets up differently: a player who
shoots half-size targets at twice the distance outranks one who shoots big ones up close with the same
score, and nobody climbs by making their targets easy.

When a session's rating beats the player's best for that drill, it is a **personal best**: the record keeps
it, together with the size and distance it was set at, and what it rose by is added to the player's
**overall rating**, the sum of their best rating in every drill. Every other best on the record — score,
accuracy, streak, hits, combo, reaction — is kept on its own, whatever the rating did. The boards and their
categories are on [Statistics](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/stats).

## Grades

A hit is graded against thresholds under `performance`. A threshold is the slowest time, in seconds, that
still earns the grade, inclusive; anything slower than `ok` is `SLOW`.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaAimTrainer/config.yml"
performance:
  flick:
    perfect: 0.3
    excellent: 0.45
    good: 0.65
    ok: 0.9
  reaction:
    perfect: 0.2
    excellent: 0.26
    good: 0.33
    ok: 0.42
```

| Grade | `flick` | `reaction` |
|---|---|---|
| `PERFECT` | 0.30 s | 0.20 s |
| `EXCELLENT` | 0.45 s | 0.26 s |
| `GOOD` | 0.65 s | 0.33 s |
| `OK` | 0.90 s | 0.42 s |
| `SLOW` | slower | slower |

What is timed depends on the kind:

| Kind | Timed | Thresholds |
|---|---|---|
| `FLICK` | From the previous hit to this one. The first hit of a run has nothing to time and is not graded. | `flick` |
| `COMBO` | From the previous hit that landed to this one. The first is not graded. | `flick` |
| `REACTION` | From the light to the hit, minus the player's ping. | `reaction` |
| `TRACK` | Nothing: tracking has no hits to grade. | — |

A grade counts towards the grade totals on the result screen and drives the feedback below. It does not
touch the score, the rating or the boards.

## Ping, misses and the rest

### Ping correction

A reaction is corrected by the player's ping: the light took half a round trip to reach them and the click
half a round trip to come back, so both halves are taken off and two players on different connections are
graded on their hands. The ping is read when the run starts and again every second. A corrected reaction
is never below 1 ms.

Moving targets are judged the same way. The plugin's own checks on a strafing target or a fighter — the ray
it casts behind a swing, the precision of a hit, and every tracking check — test the target where the
player's client was drawing it when the aim left it: the plugin keeps every position it sent and rewinds by
the ping plus the two ticks the client eases a move over. Tracking forgives half a tick either side for
jitter.

### Misses and accuracy

```text
accuracy = hits / (hits + misses)
```

A **miss** is any of:

- A swing that hit none of the player's targets. Before calling it a miss, the plugin casts its own ray
  through the crosshair, so a client whose reach never grew still clicks where it looks.
- In an `ordered` drill, a hit on a target that is not lit.
- In a 1.9 fight, a rushed hit.

A miss costs `miss-penalty`, breaks the streak and ends the combo. A swing in a `TRACK` drill is neither.

An **expired** target costs `expire-penalty` and breaks the streak, but is not a shot and does not change
the accuracy. A **false start** adds `false-start-penalty-millis` to the reaction average and breaks the
streak, and is not a shot either.

### Rushed hits

With `attack-cooldown: true`, a hit is judged by how charged the swing was, the way the game judges a crit,
a sweep or a sprint knockback:

```text
cooldown = 20 / attack speed of the item in hand   (ticks)
charge   = min(1, (ticks since the last swing + 0.5) / cooldown)
rushed   = charge ≤ 0.9
```

| Weapon | Cooldown | Ticks a swing needs |
|---|---|---|
| Any sword | 12.5 ticks | 11 |
| Diamond axe | 20 ticks | 18 |

Every swing, hit or not, and every change of held slot starts the count over. The first swing of a run is
never rushed. A rushed hit is a miss: it counts against accuracy, costs `miss-penalty`, knocks nothing back
and ends the combo.

### Precision

Every hit is also measured for how central it was: the plugin's own ray from the eyes through the target,
scored 1 dead centre and 0 at an edge, taking the worse of the two directions across the face it hit. The
run's **precision** is the average, as a percentage. A hit the plugin's ray missed — the crosshair moved in
the few milliseconds before the click arrived — is left out rather than scored as an edge.

Precision is shown on the result screen. It is not part of the score or the rating.

## Feedback

What a hit looks and sounds like is an ExyliaLib effect under `feedback`. Each grade has an `effect` and a
`chat` line, empty for none. By default a grade shows no title, writes the action bar below for 1.5 seconds,
and plays `ENTITY_EXPERIENCE_ORB_PICKUP` at volume 0.7 with a pitch that drops with the grade:

| Grade | Action bar | Pitch |
|---|---|---|
| `perfect` | `{success}⚡ PERFECT {muted}· {letters}%time%s {muted}· {info}x%streak%` | 1.8 |
| `excellent` | `{success_light}✦ EXCELLENT {muted}· {letters}%time%s {muted}· {info}x%streak%` | 1.5 |
| `good` | `{info}✔ GOOD {muted}· {letters}%time%s {muted}· {info}x%streak%` | 1.2 |
| `ok` | `{warning}• OK {muted}· {letters}%time%s {muted}· {info}x%streak%` | 1.0 |
| `slow` | `{error}▼ SLOW {muted}· {letters}%time%s {muted}· {info}x%streak%` | 0.7 |

An ungraded first hit plays only the `good` sound, and only for a player with hit sounds on.

| Section | When | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `miss` | A click that hit nothing | Action bar `{error}✘ MISS {muted}· {letters}%accuracy%% accuracy`, sound `ENTITY_PLAYER_ATTACK_NODAMAGE` 1.0 · 1.0 |
| `expired` | A `FLICK` target ran out of time | Action bar `{warning}⌛ EXPIRED {muted}· {letters}%accuracy%% accuracy`, sound `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_SNARE` 0.6 · 0.7 |
| `false-start` | A `REACTION` click before the light | Action bar `{error}✘ TOO EARLY {muted}· {letters}wait for the light`, sound `ENTITY_VILLAGER_NO` 0.8 · 0.9 |
| `armed` | A `REACTION` target lit up | Sound `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING` 1.0 · 1.8 |
| `rushed` | A `COMBO` hit inside the cooldown | Action bar `{error}✘ RUSHED {muted}· {letters}wait for the cooldown`, sound `ENTITY_PLAYER_ATTACK_WEAK` 1.0 · 1.0 |

Sound values are `volume · pitch`. These texts, and the grade chat lines, know six placeholders:

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%grade%` | The grade name, empty when there is none. |
| `%time%` | The flick or reaction time in seconds, two decimals, no unit. |
| `%hits%` | Hits so far. |
| `%streak%` | Hits in a row. |
| `%combo%` | The current combo. |
| `%accuracy%` | Accuracy so far, as a whole number. |

A player who turned **Hit feedback** or **Hit sounds** off in their settings gets these effects without the
action bar or without the sound; see [Settings](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/settings#the-toggles). The countdown,
the session and duel moments are documented on [Configuration](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/configuration).
