Every player tunes their own targets. Size and distance change how hard every drill is and move their
rating with it; colour and style are looks only; five toggles take parts of the screen away. The choices
are stored on the player's profile row, so they survive a restart and a statistics reset.

## Where players change them

The **Settings** screen opens from:

- `/aim settings`;
- the main menu;
- the **Your settings** button of the drill list;
- the comparator in slot 6 of the hotbar during a drill, with a right click.

The screen's top item shows the **difficulty** the current size and distance are worth on a drill of weight
1.0, and each of the two buttons says what it is worth on its own. Every change is saved and redrawn in place.

## Size and distance

Both are **multipliers** of the drill's own numbers, so one choice serves every drill.

| Setting | Scales | Smaller | Larger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Size | The width and height of every target. | Harder, worth more rating. | Easier, worth less. |
| Distance | Both ends of the drill's distance range. | Easier, worth less. | Harder, worth more rating. |

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| Left click | One step up: bigger, or farther. |
| Right click | One step down. |
| Shift click | Asks for an exact value, between the range's ends. |

A value is always brought inside the range the server offers and rounded to two decimals. How much each
one moves the rating is on [Scoring](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/scoring#difficulty).

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaAimTrainer/config.yml"
settings:
  size:
    min: 0.1
    max: 2.5
    step: 0.05
    default: 1.0
  distance:
    min: 0.25
    max: 3.0
    step: 0.05
    default: 1.0
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `min` | The smallest value anybody may choose. Never below `0.01`. |
| `max` | The largest. Never below `min`. |
| `step` | How far one click moves the value. Between `0.01` and the width of the range. |
| `default` | What a player who never changed it uses. Brought inside the range. |

<Callout type="note" title="Narrowing a range">
A stored value outside a narrowed range is brought inside it the next time it is read, so nobody is left
stranded at a value the server no longer allows. Their old records keep the size and distance they were set
at.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Distance in a fight">
A `COMBO` target walks in only as far as the drill's nearest distance times the player's distance
multiplier, while the fight keeps the game's three blocks of reach. The shipped fights start at 3 blocks, so
a distance above ×1.00 moves the fighter to the edge of reach, and a little past ×1.10 at size ×1.00 keeps it
out of reach altogether. Lower `settings.distance.max`, or the fights' `distance.min`, if that is not
wanted.
</Callout>

## Colour and style

A target is a block display with an outline. The **style** picks the block, the **colour** dyes it and draws
the outline. Left click moves to the next one, right click to the previous.

| Colour | `dye` | Outline |
|---|---|---|
| `lime` | `LIME` | `#8fffc1` |
| `red` | `RED` | `#ff5555` |
| `orange` | `ORANGE` | `#ff9500` |
| `yellow` | `YELLOW` | `#ffd700` |
| `cyan` | `CYAN` | `#59d4ff` |
| `blue` | `BLUE` | `#59a4ff` |
| `purple` | `PURPLE` | `#aa76de` |
| `magenta` | `MAGENTA` | `#ff6b9d` |
| `white` | `WHITE` | `#ffffff` |
| `black` | `BLACK` | `#333333` |

| Style | Name | `suffix` | `material` | Block with `lime` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `concrete` | Concrete | `CONCRETE` | — | `LIME_CONCRETE` |
| `wool` | Wool | `WOOL` | — | `LIME_WOOL` |
| `glass` | Glass | `STAINED_GLASS` | — | `LIME_STAINED_GLASS` |
| `terracotta` | Glazed | `GLAZED_TERRACOTTA` | — | `LIME_GLAZED_TERRACOTTA` |
| `lantern` | Sea lantern | — | `SEA_LANTERN` | `SEA_LANTERN` |
| `target` | Target block | — | `TARGET` | `TARGET` |

The block is the colour's `dye` and the style's `suffix` joined by an underscore. A style with a `material`
of its own ignores the dye and always draws that block, but still takes the colour's outline. A
combination that is not a block — a typo, a dye with no such variant — draws `LIME_CONCRETE` rather than
nothing.

Some targets are drawn **grey** while they do not count yet: a reaction target before its light, a tracking
target while the crosshair is off it, the unlit targets of an ordered drill. That look is the same style in
grey (`GRAY_WOOL`, `GRAY_STAINED_GLASS`, …), or `GRAY_CONCRETE` for a style with its own material, and has
no outline.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaAimTrainer/config.yml"
settings:
  colours:
    lime:
      name: Lime
      dye: LIME
      glow: "#8fffc1"
    # … one block per colour
  styles:
    concrete:
      name: Concrete
      suffix: CONCRETE
      material: ""
    lantern:
      name: Sea lantern
      suffix: ""
      material: SEA_LANTERN
    # … one block per style
  default-colour: lime
  default-style: concrete
  spawn-animation-ticks: 2
```

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `colours.<id>.name` | What the settings screen and its messages call the colour. |
| `colours.<id>.dye` | The Minecraft colour prefix a style is combined with. |
| `colours.<id>.glow` | The outline colour, as `#rrggbb`. Anything unreadable is white. |
| `styles.<id>.name` | What the screen calls the style. |
| `styles.<id>.suffix` | The block name a dye is put in front of. |
| `styles.<id>.material` | A fixed block that ignores the colour. Empty uses the suffix. |
| `default-colour`, `default-style` | What a new player gets. An id that is not in its map falls back to the first entry. |
| `spawn-animation-ticks` | Ticks a new target takes to grow in from 5% of its size. `0` pops it in at once. Clamped to `0..20`. |

The key of each block is what a player's row stores. Removing a colour or a style sends every player who had
it back to the default; an empty `colours` or `styles` map brings the shipped ones back.

## The toggles

Five switches, on by default, in the row below size, distance, colour and style. Each one is the player's
alone.

| Toggle | Shown as | Turns off |
|---|---|---|
| `sidebar` | Sidebar | The sidebar during a drill or a duel. |
| `boss_bar` | Boss bar | The boss bar with the drill, the time, hits, accuracy and score. |
| `action_bar` | Hit feedback | The action bar of the hit, miss, expired, false start, armed and rushed effects. |
| `hit_sounds` | Hit sounds | The sound of those same effects. |
| `particles` | Hit particles | The particles drawn where a target was hit. |

What they do not turn off: the titles and chat lines of those effects, the countdown, the session-complete,
personal-best and duel effects, the experience bar and the hotbar. Those are the server's, under `feedback`
and `hud` — see [HUD](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/hud). The boss bar also needs `hud.boss-bar.enabled`, and the
particles need a `hud.hit-particle` line; a player cannot turn on what the owner turned off.

The names on the screen come from `messages.yml`, under `menu.toggles`.

<Callout type="note" title="Standing still is not a setting">
Every player is frozen in place from the countdown until they leave the arena: the client's own movement is
dropped, so a drill measures the hands and not the legs. There is no toggle for it.
</Callout>

## When a change applies

Settings are read when a drill starts, and again every time it is replayed. A change made during a drill or
a duel — from the hotbar, for instance — is saved and answered with *"Saved. It applies from your next
drill."*; the run already going keeps what it started with. The sidebar toggle is the exception and applies
at once.

A player whose profile has not finished loading after they join plays with the defaults, and a change they
make in that moment is not saved.

## In a duel

A duel builds its rules once, from the player who sends the challenge, and both players play by them.

| | `match.use-preferences: false` (default) | `match.use-preferences: true` |
|---|---|---|
| Size and distance | ×1.00 for both: the drill as written. | The challenger's, for both. |
| Colour and style | The challenger's, for both. | The challenger's, for both. |
| Toggles | Each player's own. | Each player's own. |

Either way both players shoot identical targets, which is what keeps two scores comparable. A rematch reuses
the same rules. See [Duels](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/duels).
