An arena is a place with **one spawn**. It is never locked: any number of drills and duels run in the
same arena at once, every player in it is isolated from every other, and each sees only their own
targets. The direction the spawn faces is where the targets appear. The arena knows nothing about who is
inside it beyond a count.

Arenas live in the database, in `aim_arenas`, not in a YAML file. Every server pointing at the same
database shares them, and a reload does not touch them.

<Callout type="warning" title="No arena, nothing to play">
A drill and a duel both need a ready arena. With none, a drill reads "No arena is ready. Ask an
administrator." before the player is touched, and so do both sides of an accepted duel. There is no
training where the player stands.
</Callout>

## Creating an arena

Everything happens in `/aimtraineradmin` → **Arenas**. There is nothing to type on a command line beyond
`reload`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **New arena** and type an **id** when asked, such as `aim1`. It is refused if an arena already
    uses it, and stored lowercased. The arena appears enabled, with no spawn, named after the id as you
    typed it, and the chat says "Arena `<id>` created. Set its spawn next."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    On the arena screen, **Display name** opens a text input: up to 64 characters, colours allowed. This
    is what players read; the id stays as it was.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the spawn">
    Click **Spawn**, then stand where it goes, **facing the open space where the targets should appear**.
    The setup wizard stores your position and your facing, and the chat says "Spawn of `<id>` set. Face
    where the targets should appear."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it is ready">
    The summary item shows the arena as ready once it is enabled and its spawn is in a loaded world. The
    arena list marks each one **READY** or **INCOMPLETE**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where the targets appear

A player is frozen on the spawn, so every drill is laid out around the spawn's position and facing.

- Flick targets are placed around the eyes, within the drill's horizontal spread either side of that
  facing and its range of elevations, never in the floor.
- A tracking target strafes in an arc in front of the player; a fighter circles round them and closes in.
- A target is placed at the drill's distance **times the player's distance setting**. The shipped
  `precision` drill reaches 12 blocks, and a player may push the distance to ×3.00, so leave a clear
  space of well over thirty blocks in front of the spawn if you keep those defaults.

Drills and settings are on [Drills](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/drills) and
[Settings](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/settings).

## Enabled, disabled, deleted

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Disable** | No new drill or duel is sent there. Every drill running in it is stopped and its player sent home; every duel running in it is cancelled. The chat says "Arena `<id>` disabled. Sessions there were stopped." |
| **Enable** | The arena is offered again, once its spawn is set. |
| **Delete** | Behind a confirmation screen. Stops every drill and duel running in it the same way, then removes the row. |

A duel stopped this way is cancelled: both players are sent back and nothing is stored. A drill stopped
this way is stored like any run cut short. See [Training](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/training).

## How an arena is picked

When a drill or a duel starts, the plugin takes the **ready** arena hosting the **fewest players**: one per
drill, two per duel. Ten players training at once therefore spread across the arenas instead of stacking
on the first one.

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaAimTrainer/config.yml"
arena:
  max-players-per-arena: 0
  return-location: ""
```

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-players-per-arena` | `0` | Players one arena may host at once, drills and duels counted together. `0` is unlimited. An arena at its cap is skipped. |
| `return-location` | `""` | Where a player is sent after leaving, as `world,x,y,z` or `world,x,y,z,yaw,pitch`. Empty sends them back to where they came from. |

The cap is compared with the count before the newcomers arrive, so an arena one player below its cap still
takes a duel and ends one over it.

With `return-location` empty, a player goes back to the exact spot they started from. If that spot is
unknown, they go to their world's spawn.

<Callout type="note" title="Right after a start">
Arenas are read from the database asynchronously once the plugin enables; the console prints
"Loaded `<n>` arenas and `<m>` drills" when they are in. Until then there is no ready arena to pick: a drill
is refused with the no-arena line, and an accepted duel does nothing.
</Callout>

## The rest of the admin menu

The main screen shows how many arenas exist and how many are ready, the duels and drills running, and the
players online.

| Screen | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Arenas** | The list above, sorted by id, each with its id, enabled state, spawn and current load of duels and drills. |
| **Running duels** | Every duel in progress with its players, series score, round, format, drill, arena and state. Clicking one cancels it behind a confirmation: both players are sent back and nothing is stored. |
| **Players** | Everyone online, sorted by name, with their rating, accuracy, sessions, wins, losses and state. A free player: click to reset their statistics. A busy player: **left click** stops their drill, **right click** resets their statistics. Both resets go through a confirmation. |
| **Reload** | Runs the same reload as `/aimtraineradmin reload` and reopens the menu. |

<Callout type="note" title="Stopping a player stops a drill">
The left click on a busy player stops a **drill** only. A player in a duel is left where they are; cancel
the duel from **Running duels** instead.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="What a reset clears, and what it keeps">
Resetting a player sets their profile back to zero, deletes every drill record they hold — the drill boards
are read from those records — and deletes their session history. Their **duel history rows are kept**,
although the confirmation screen says "Every record, session and duel". The duel record on the profile —
wins, losses — is zeroed with the rest. The player's settings and their last drill and format are kept.
</Callout>

## The admin menu files

The admin screens in `menus/admin/` are **rewritten from the jar on every start and every reload**, so a
button added in a release reaches servers that already have the folder. Nothing an owner is meant to edit
lives there. How the player-facing menus are updated is on [Menus](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/menus).
