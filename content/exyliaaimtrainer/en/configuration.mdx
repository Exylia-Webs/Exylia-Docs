Everything is generated in `plugins/ExyliaAimTrainer/` on first boot, with comments. Edit the files there,
never the copies in the jar. Times are seconds with decimals unless a key says ticks; twenty ticks are one
second. Distances and sizes are blocks. Colours use palette tokens such as `{primary}` or `{success}`.

## config.yml

Sixteen drills ship; two are shown here. The whole list, and what each number does to a drill, is on
[Drills](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/drills).

### `training`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `countdown` | `3.0` | Seconds before the first target is live, in a drill and in every duel round. A punch skips it. |
| `rest-seconds` | `5.0` | Seconds a player may stay idle in the arena after a drill ends before being sent home. Replaying from the summary or the hotbar starts the drill again; a screen the player opened themselves — settings, the drill list — keeps them there and starts the wait over. |
| `default-drill` | `gridshot` | The drill `/aim train` starts without a name. An id that is not in `drills` falls back to the first drill listed. |
| `allowed-commands` | `[aim, aimtrainer, at, msg, r, tell, w]` | Command labels a player may still run while in a drill or a duel. A `plugin:command` prefix is stripped before the check. Everything else is refused with `command-blocked`. |

### `drills`

One block per drill; a new key is a new drill, no code involved. The key is the drill id used by
commands, records, boards and placeholders. Menus list drills in file order, and a duel sent with
`/aim duel <player>` and no drill plays the **first** one. See [Drills](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/drills)
for how the four kinds use these numbers.

<Callout type="note" title="An empty drills section brings the shipped ones back">
If `drills` is missing or empty when the file is read, the sixteen shipped drills are used. To offer
fewer, delete the blocks you do not want and keep at least one.
</Callout>

| Key | Rule | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `display-name` | | What players see. Palette tokens allowed. |
| `icon` | Blank is `TARGET`. | Material or head used in menus. |
| `description` | | One line shown under the drill in menus. |
| `kind` | Missing is `FLICK`. | `FLICK`, `REACTION`, `TRACK` or `COMBO`. |
| `weight` | Zero or less is `1.0`. | How hard the drill is by itself; multiplies the rating of every session. |
| `duration` | Zero or less is `30.0`. | Seconds a session lasts. A `REACTION` drill ends on its attempts; this is its cap. |
| `targets` | At least `1`. | Targets on screen at once. `FLICK` only; the other kinds use one. |
| `size` | Zero or less is `0.8`. | Edge of a target in blocks, before the player's size setting. |
| `height` | At least `0`. | Height of a target, or `0` for a cube. The player-shaped targets ship with `1.8`. |
| `distance.min`, `distance.max` | `min` at least `1.0`; `max` at least `min`. | How far from the eyes a target is placed, before the player's distance setting. |
| `spread.yaw` | `0` to `340`. | Total horizontal spread in degrees around where the player faces: `60` is 30 to either side. |
| `spread.pitch-min`, `spread.pitch-max` | `-80` to `80`; `max` at least `min`. | Lowest and highest elevation; negative is below eye level. |
| `grid` | Left out unless used. | `columns`, `rows`, `spacing`: targets on fixed cells straight ahead instead of anywhere in the spread. `columns: 0` turns it off; with columns, `rows` is at least `1` and a `spacing` of zero or less is `10.0` degrees. |
| `lifetime` | At least `0`. | Seconds a `FLICK` target stays up before it counts as expired. `0` never expires. |
| `ordered` | | `FLICK` only: targets come in order and only the lit one counts; the rest are misses. |
| `return-to-centre` | | `FLICK` only: every other target sits dead ahead. |
| `min-separation` | At least `0`. | Degrees kept between two targets on screen. |
| `weapon` | | A material put in the player's hand for the drill. Empty keeps the hand empty. |
| `motion` | Left out unless used. | A strafing target: `speed-min`, `speed-max` (blocks per second; `0` turns it off), `acceleration`, `arc-degrees`, `switch-min-ticks`, `switch-max-ticks`, `hop-chance` (`0` to `1`), `hop-height`, `hop-ticks`. With a `speed-max` above zero, an `acceleration` of zero or less is `8.0`, an `arc-degrees` of zero or less is `60.0`, and the tick keys are at least `1`. |
| `reaction` | Left out unless used. | `min-delay` (at least `0.2`), `max-delay` (at least `min-delay`), `attempts`. `attempts: 0` turns it off. |
| `combo` | Left out unless used. | `knockback` (`0` turns it off), `knockback-up` (zero or less is `0.4`, at most `1.0`), `approach-speed` (zero or less is `4.0`), `combo-window` (zero or less is `0.7`), `attack-cooldown`. |

A section that is off is left out of the file when it is written.

### `settings`

The ranges and choices a player picks from in `/aim settings`. See
[Settings](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/settings).

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `size` | `0.1` – `2.5`, step `0.05`, default `1.0` | Target size as a multiplier of the drill's own. `min` is at least `0.01`, `max` at least `min`; `step` is what one click moves, between `0.01` and the width of the range; `default` is what a player who never changed it uses, brought inside the ends. Anything between the ends can also be typed. |
| `distance` | `0.25` – `3.0`, step `0.05`, default `1.0` | Target distance as a multiplier, with the same rules. |
| `colours` | ten colours | One block per colour; the key is what the player's row stores. `name` is shown, `dye` is the Minecraft colour prefix a style is combined with, `glow` is the outline as `#rrggbb`. An empty map brings the shipped ten back. |
| `styles` | six styles | One block per style. `suffix` is combined with the colour's `dye` (`LIME` + `CONCRETE`); a style with a `material` of its own ignores the colour and always draws that block. An empty map brings the shipped six back. |
| `default-colour`, `default-style` | `lime`, `concrete` | What a new player gets. An id that does not exist is the first key of its map. |
| `spawn-animation-ticks` | `2` | Ticks a target takes to grow in. `0` to `20`; `0` pops it in at once. |

A player whose stored size, distance, colour or style falls outside what the file now offers is brought
back inside it, so narrowing a range never strands anybody.

### `scoring`

How a session becomes a score, and a score a rating. The formulas are on
[Scoring](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/scoring).

| Key | Default | Floor | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hit-points` | `10` | `1` | `FLICK` and `COMBO`: points per hit. |
| `miss-penalty` | `4` | `0` | Points lost per miss. |
| `expire-penalty` | `6` | `0` | Points lost per target that expired. |
| `false-start-penalty-millis` | `150` | `0` | `REACTION`: milliseconds added to the average per click before the light. |
| `reaction-reference-millis` | `250` | `1` | `REACTION`: an average this fast scores exactly `reaction-scale`. |
| `reaction-scale` | `1000` | `1` | |
| `tracking-scale` | `10` | `1` | `TRACK`: points per percent of the session spent on target. |
| `combo-points` | `5` | `0` | `COMBO`: extra points for a hit that extends a combo. |
| `size-exponent` | `1.0` | | Difficulty is `(1 / size multiplier)` to this power. |
| `distance-exponent` | `0.75` | | …times `distance multiplier` to this power. |
| `min-difficulty`, `max-difficulty` | `0.25`, `6.0` | `0.01`; `max` at least `min` | The difficulty a session is clamped to, whatever the settings say. |

### `performance`

How a single hit is graded, for feedback only; grades do not change the score. A threshold is the slowest
time, in seconds, that still earns that grade. Slower than `ok` is `slow`.

| Key | Default | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| `flick` | `0.30 / 0.45 / 0.65 / 0.90` | The time from the previous hit to this one. |
| `reaction` | `0.20 / 0.26 / 0.33 / 0.42` | The time from the target lighting up to the hit. |

### `feedback`

Every entry is an ExyliaLib effect: `title`, `action-bar`, `boss-bar`, `sound`, `particle` and
`firework`, each optional. A sub-section that does nothing is left out of the file, and removing a section
keeps that channel quiet.

| Key | Fires when | Placeholders |
|---|---|---|
| `perfect`, `excellent`, `good`, `ok`, `slow` | A hit earned that grade. Each has an `effect` and a `chat` line, empty for none. | `%grade%`, `%time%` (seconds, two decimals), `%hits%`, `%streak%`, `%combo%`, `%accuracy%` |
| `miss` | A click that hit nothing. | the same, `%grade%` empty |
| `expired` | A `FLICK` target ran out of time. | the same |
| `false-start` | A `REACTION` click before the light. | the same |
| `rushed` | A `COMBO` hit inside the attack cooldown. | the same |
| `armed` | A `REACTION` target lit up. | — |
| `countdown` | Counting down before the first target; refreshed with the time left. | `%time%` |
| `go` | The first target is live. | — |
| `session-complete` | A drill ended. | — |
| `personal-best` | The run beat the player's best rating on that drill. | `%rating%` |
| `match-found` | Both duellists have arrived in the arena. | — |
| `round-won`, `round-lost` | A round was decided. | — |
| `round-draw` | A round ended without a winner and is replayed. | — |
| `match-won`, `match-lost` | The duel was decided. | — |

Players can switch channels off for themselves: with **Hit feedback** off, the action bar of the grade,
`miss`, `expired`, `false-start`, `rushed` and `armed` effects is dropped; with **Hit sounds** off, their
sound. The title, the boss bar, the particle, the firework and the grade's `chat` line are always played.
The very first hit of a session has no time to grade and plays only the sound of `good`.

The effect keys are ExyliaLib's; see the [library's effects page](/docs/exylialib/effects).

### `hud`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `boss-bar.enabled` | `true` | Whether a boss bar shows during a drill or a round. Each player can also turn it off. |
| `boss-bar.text` | drill · time · hits · accuracy · score | Knows `%drill%`, `%time%`, `%hits%`, `%misses%`, `%accuracy%`, `%score%`, `%streak%` and `%opponent_score%`. Its progress is the time left. |
| `boss-bar.colour` | `PURPLE` | `PINK`, `BLUE`, `RED`, `GREEN`, `YELLOW`, `PURPLE` or `WHITE`. |
| `boss-bar.overlay` | `PROGRESS` | `PROGRESS`, `NOTCHED_6`, `NOTCHED_10`, `NOTCHED_12` or `NOTCHED_20`. |
| `boss-bar.refresh-ticks` | `5` | Ticks between two redraws. At least `1`. |
| `experience-bar` | `true` | The seconds left on the experience bar. One packet a second; the real experience is sent back on exit. |
| `hotbar` | `true` | The Change drill, Settings, Play again and Leave buttons drawn over the hotbar, from `menus/hotbar.yml`. |
| `hit-particle` | `ELECTRIC_SPARK\|8\|0.15\|0.15\|0.15\|0.05` | Particles where a target was hit, in ExyliaLib's particle notation. Empty for none. Each player can turn them off. |

The sidebar lives in `scoreboards.yml`. See [HUD](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/hud).

### `match`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `formats` | `[1, 3, 5]` | Best-of lengths offered in the duel screen and accepted by `/aim duel`. An empty list is `[3]`. |
| `default-format` | `3` | The length used when a command leaves it out and when a player has not picked one before. Keep it in `formats`: it is not corrected if it is not. |
| `allow-even-formats` | `false` | Whether even lengths in `formats` are offered at all. |
| `use-preferences` | `false` | Whether a duel keeps the challenger's size and distance. `false` plays the drill as written for both. |
| `round-delay` | `3.0` | Seconds between a round ending and the next countdown. |
| `result-delay` | `5.0` | Seconds the result stays on screen before both players are sent back. |
| `duel-request-expiry` | `30.0` | Seconds a request stays open. Never less than one. |

See [Duels](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/duels).

### `practice`

Only read when ExyliaPracticeCore is installed. See [Compatibility](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/compatibility).

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `train-while-queued` | `true` | Lets a player waiting in a practice queue start a drill; they are handed back the moment a match is found. `false` refuses them with `cannot-borrow`. |
| `queue-prompt` | `true` | Sends the `practice.queue-prompt` lines to a player who joins a queue. Needs `train-while-queued` on, and is skipped for a player already in a drill or a duel. |
| `ranked-only` | `false` | Only prompt in ranked queues. |

### `leaderboard`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `entries` | `10` | Rows shown per board. |
| `cache-seconds` | `300` | Seconds a board is kept before the database is asked again. Never less than five. A new personal best drops the cached boards at once. |
| `accuracy-min-shots` | `20` | Shots a session needs before its accuracy can become the player's best accuracy on that drill. |

### `history`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `entries` | `20` | Rows shown in `/aim sessions` and in `/aim history`, each. |
| `retention-days` | `30` | Session and duel rows older than this are deleted. `0` keeps everything. |
| `prune-interval-minutes` | `60` | How often the prune runs. At least `1`; the first sweep is a minute after start. |

### `arena`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-players-per-arena` | `0` | Players one arena hosts at once: one per drill, two per duel. `0` is unlimited. A new drill goes to the ready arena with the fewest players. |
| `return-location` | empty | Where a player is sent after leaving, as `world,x,y,z` or `world,x,y,z,yaw,pitch`. Empty sends them back to where they came from. |

### `chat`

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `isolation` | `true` | Keeps a drill's or a duel's chat inside it: whoever is in one reads only that one, and the rest of the server reads none of it. Read per message, so a reload applies it at once. `false` leaves chat as the server's chat plugin delivers it. |

### `debug`

`false` by default. `true` prints debug lines to the console.

## When the file is from an older version

`config.yml` carries a version, and a file written by an older release is migrated on load. Each step
only moves what is still exactly as shipped; a value you changed and a drill you added are never touched.

| From | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Shipped drills still at their old duration (60 or 45 seconds) run 30 seconds. The quiet note-block `miss` and `rushed` sounds, if still at their old values, become `ENTITY_PLAYER_ATTACK_NODAMAGE` and `ENTITY_PLAYER_ATTACK_WEAK`. |
| 2 | Shipped drill descriptions still reading as shipped are replaced by the shorter lines. |
| 3 | A `gridshot` without a `grid` block is copied to a new `scatter` drill and `gridshot` becomes the 3×3 grid, unless `scatter` already exists. Every `combo.cooldown-ticks` becomes `combo.attack-cooldown` (`true` for any value above zero). `combo` and `combo_19` get `weapon: DIAMOND_SWORD` if they have none, and their names and descriptions are updated if unchanged. `sixshot`, `microshot`, `spidershot` and `axe` are added if missing. |
| 4 | `training.rest-seconds` still at the old `20` becomes `5`. |

## messages.yml

Every line the plugin says. `%prefix%` expands to the `prefix` value at the top of the file,
`{primary}&lAIM &8•&r` by default. Colours are palette tokens; MiniMessage tags work too, and the duel
request uses `<click>` and `<hover>` for its Accept and Decline buttons.

A line may open with an effect tag, which never reaches the screen. `[sound:NAME|volume|pitch]` plays a
sound to whoever receives the line; the defaults use `ENTITY_VILLAGER_NO` for a refusal,
`ENTITY_EXPERIENCE_ORB_PICKUP` for something that worked and `BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING` for something that
wants attention. `[center]` centres the line in chat, as the duel request and the queue prompt do; two tags
share one bracket, `[center; sound:…]`.

| Section | Holds |
|---|---|
| `no-permission`, `player-only`, `player-not-found` | The three command refusals. The last knows `%player%`. |
| `reload` | `started`, `done` (`%steps%`, `%ms%`), `failed`. |
| `common` | `busy`, `busy-elsewhere` (`%plugin%`), `busy-advice` (`%reason%`), `not-in-anything`, `left`, `command-blocked`, `no-packets`, `no-arena`, and the words `yes` and `no`. |
| `training` | `started` (`%drill%`, `%difficulty%`), `stopped`, `unknown-drill` (`%drill%`), `rest-hint` (`%seconds%`), and the single line sent beside the summary screen: `personal-best` when the rating beat the player's best, `below-best` when it did not. Both know `%drill%`, `%kind%`, `%score%`, `%rating%`, `%difficulty%`, `%hits%`, `%misses%`, `%expired%`, `%shots%`, `%accuracy%`, `%streak%`, `%precision%`, `%avg_flick%`, `%best_flick%`, `%avg_reaction%`, `%best_reaction%`, `%false_starts%`, `%on_target%`, `%longest_lock%`, `%combo%`, `%ping%`, `%duration%`, `%best_rating%` and `%delta%`. |
| `settings` | What the settings screen says when a value changes: `size` and `distance` (`%value%`, `%difficulty%`), `colour` and `style` (`%value%`), `toggled` (`%setting%`, `%state%`), the words `on` and `off`, `applies-next-drill` for a change made while in a drill, and `input-title` (`%min%`, `%max%`) for the window that asks for an exact value. |
| `match` | `starting` (`%opponent%`), `round-start` (`%round%`, `%rounds%`, `%drill%`), `round-won`, `round-lost`, `round-draw` (`%round%`), `ready` and `opponent-ready` (`%opponent%`), `no-arena`, `unknown-format` (`%format%`), `cancelled`, `opponent-left` (`%opponent%`), `forfeited`; the `round-summary` list (`%round%`, `%winner%`, `%score%`, `%opponent_score%`, `%hits%`, `%accuracy%`, `%score_for%`, `%score_against%`) and the `match-summary` list (`%winner%`, `%loser%`, `%score_winner%`, `%score_loser%`, `%format%`, `%drill%`, `%hits%`, `%accuracy%`, `%duration%`). |
| `duel` | `request-sent` (`%target%`), the `request-received` list (`%player%`, `%drill%`, `%format%`, `%seconds%`), `accepted`, `expired` (`%player%`, to whoever was challenged), `expired-sent` (`%target%`, to whoever challenged), `no-pending`, `target-busy` (`%target%`), `target-not-ready` (`%target%`, `%reason%`), `self-duel`, `denied` (`%player%`), `denied-by` (`%target%`), and `awaiting-answer`: the reason other plugins read while a request waits for an answer. |
| `practice` | The `queue-prompt` list, whose button runs `/aim`; `match-found`; `cannot-borrow`. |
| `admin` | What the admin screens confirm: `arena-created`, `arena-removed`, `arena-exists`, `arena-not-found`, `arena-renamed` (`%arena%`, `%name%`), `spawn-set`, `arena-enabled`, `arena-disabled` (all `%arena%`), `match-cancelled` (`%id%`), `session-stopped` and `stats-reset` (`%player%`), `cancelled`. |
| `menu` | Words menus need that are not a whole line: `victory-title`, `defeat-title`, `none` (`—`), `loading`, the empty texts `empty-history`, `empty-records`, `empty-leaderboard`, `empty-opponents`, `empty-arenas`, `empty-matches`, `empty-sessions`; `state-none`, `state-training`, `state-match` for `%exyliaaimtrainer_state_label%`; `reason-busy`; `set` and `not-set`; `overall-board`; and the maps below. |

`menu` also holds four maps:

menu:

      icon: IRON_SWORD
  kinds:
    flick: Flicking
    reaction: Reaction
    track: Tracking
    combo: Combo

`categories` names and iconises the seven leaderboard categories, `kinds` names each drill kind on a row,
`units` is what follows a score of that kind, and `toggles` names the five switches in the settings screen.
A category missing from the map shows its key on a `PAPER` icon.

Two keys are written to the file and read by nothing: `menu.reason-self` and `menu.difficulty-label`.

## scoreboards.yml

Two sidebars, `training` and `match`, each an ExyliaLib sidebar with `enabled`, `title`, `lines` and
`update` (`interval` in ticks, `10` by default, `smart`, `cache`). The lines are driven by the plugin's
`%exyliaaimtrainer_*%` placeholders. A player sees the board of what they are doing only while they are in a drill or
a duel, and only with their **Sidebar** switch on. See [HUD](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/hud) and
[Placeholders](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/placeholders).

## menus/

Every screen is a YAML file under `menus/`, plus `hotbar.yml` for the buttons shown during a drill.
`menus/admin/` and `menus/hotbar.yml` are rewritten from the jar on every start and every reload; the
other files are kept up to date without undoing your edits. The file list, what each screen does and every
`aimtrainer:` action are on [Menus](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/menus).

## database.yml

Written by ExyliaLib the first time the plugin starts: H2 by default, with MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL and
MongoDB as alternatives. Arenas, profiles, records, session history and duel history all live there. See
the [library's database page](/docs/exylialib/database).
