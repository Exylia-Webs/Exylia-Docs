Quick answers to what owners and players ask most. For a step-by-step fix, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/troubleshooting).

## The plugin does not start

`packetevents` is a hard dependency: without it the server never loads the plugin. `ExyliaLib` is listed as soft only so the loader can install it, but the plugin needs it to run. Check both are in `plugins/`. See [Installation](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/installation).

## "Targets need PacketEvents, which this server does not have."

A target is a pair of packet entities the server never has, so without a loaded PacketEvents there is nothing to draw and every drill and duel request is refused with that line. The console says so at startup: *"PacketEvents is missing: targets cannot be drawn and no drill can start"*.

## "No arena is ready. Ask an administrator."

Drills and duels both need an arena. An arena is ready when it is **enabled**, has its **spawn** set, and that spawn is in a loaded world; in `/aimtraineradmin`, **Arenas**, a row marked **[NO SPAWN]** is missing one of those. The same line appears when every ready arena is full (`arena.max-players-per-arena`, `0` is unlimited) or in the first moments after boot, before the arenas are read from the database. See [Arenas](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/arenas).

## "Finish what you are doing first."

The player is already in a drill or a duel (a drill does not stop them challenging, a duel does). "You are busy in `<plugin>`" means another Exylia plugin holds them, such as an FFA arena, an event or a practice match, and "Not right now: `<reason>`" means a plugin advises against it in its own words.

## A player cannot duel somebody

The refusal line says why: the target is busy, is `<reason>` (this plugin's own reason is "answering a duel request"), is waiting in an ExyliaPracticeCore queue, or you must "Wait `<time>` before challenging again" (`match.duel-request-cooldown`, fifteen seconds by default). A player has one request waiting at most, a newer one replaces the older, and requests expire after `match.duel-request-expiry` (thirty seconds). See [Duels](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/duels).

## Hits feel off, or depend on ping

The client does the aiming: every target has a hitbox the client tests with its own raycast, so a target is hit where the player sees it. Reaction times have the player's ping subtracted (capped at `scoring.max-ping-correction-millis`, 100 ms, and never more than half the time), and moving targets are judged where the client drew them by rewinding the ping plus two ticks. A reaction under `min-reaction-millis` (80 ms) is a false start. See [Scoring](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/scoring).

## Why does spam clicking not help?

Every swing that hits nothing is a miss: `scoring.miss-penalty` (4) points against the `hit-points` (10) of a hit, and lower accuracy. In a fight, a hit on a target still red from the last one does nothing, and in a 1.9 drill (`attack-cooldown: true`) a swing charged no more than 90% is **rushed** and counts as a miss. See [Training](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/training).

## What is the difference between score and rating?

The **score** is the drill's own number (points per minute, a reaction score, or time on target) and decides duel rounds. The **rating** is the score times the **difficulty** of the settings it was played at, clamped between `scoring.min-difficulty` (0.25) and `max-difficulty` (6.0); the boards rank by it, so nobody climbs by making targets easy. See [Scoring](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/scoring).

## Can players walk during a drill?

No, every player is frozen during a drill because it measures their hands. They can only run the commands in `training.allowed-commands` (`aim`, `aimtrainer`, `at`, `msg`, `r`, `tell` and `w` by default), and an ender pearl, a teleport by another plugin or leaving the arena's world takes them out of the session.

## A player stays in the arena after the drill ends

That is the rest: `training.rest-seconds`, five by default. **Play again** replays the drill, **Change drill** picks another, and the time running out sends an idle player home; a player with a settings or drill screen open keeps waiting. Set it to `0` to send players home as soon as a drill ends.

## How do I add a drill?

Write a new block under `drills` in `config.yml` and run `/aimtraineradmin reload`. A drill is a `kind` (`FLICK`, `REACTION`, `TRACK` or `COMBO`) and a set of numbers, so a new one gets the same menus, records and boards. Renaming a key starts that drill over with empty boards. See [Tune and add drills](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/drills).

## Duels feel unfair when players use different settings

By default they do not use them: with `match.use-preferences: false` both sides play the drill as written and share one seed, so the same targets appear in the same places in the same order. Turning it on applies the challenger's size and distance to both. See [Duels](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/duels).

## What is a draw, and why was a duel cancelled?

A round goes to the higher score, then the better accuracy, then more hits; level on all three is a draw and the round is replayed. Three draws in a row mean neither side is shooting, and the duel is cancelled with nothing stored.

## Can I offer a best-of-2 or best-of-4?

List the length in `match.formats` and set `match.allow-even-formats: true`. An even series can end level once every round has been played; the winner of the last round then takes the duel.

## A player is not on the leaderboard

A board never places a zero and has one row per player. An accuracy only counts from sessions of at least `leaderboard.accuracy-min-shots` shots (twenty), and only drill sessions that ran their course reach the boards; duels set no records. Java and Bedrock have separate boards, and a board is cached for `leaderboard.cache-seconds` (five minutes) unless the player beat their best rating. See [Statistics](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/stats).

## How do I reset a player's statistics?

Open `/aimtraineradmin`, **Players**, pick the player and use the reset button; the list shows online players only. It goes through ExyliaLib's player reset: it asks first, kicks the player while it runs (the staff member needs `exylialib.admin.wipe`) and deletes their profile, settings and every drill record. Session and duel history stays unless the staff member adds history to the reset. See [Statistics](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/stats#resetting-a-player).

## Can queued ExyliaPracticeCore players train?

Yes, with `practice.train-while-queued` on (the default). They keep their place in the queue and go to the match the moment it is found. See [Compatibility](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/compatibility).

## Players in a drill cannot see the server chat

That is `chat.isolation`, on by default: whoever is in a drill or a duel reads only that one. Set it to `false` to leave chat as your chat plugin delivers it.

## My edits to a menu or a message do not show

The plugin reads the `lang/<code>/` folder of the language `config.yml` names (`default` follows ExyliaLib), so edit the files of the language in use. A server updated from before languages has its old files in `lang/custom/`. See [Configuration · language](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/configuration#language), [Menus](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/menus) and [Edit menus](/docs/exylialib/edit-menus).

## Can Bedrock players play?

Yes, through Geyser and Floodgate. Their targets are drawn as slimes and they are ranked on Bedrock boards of their own, since touch and controller aim is not the same contest as a mouse. See [Compatibility](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/compatibility#bedrock-players).

## My PlaceholderAPI placeholders show up unresolved

Every placeholder is `%exyliaaimtrainer_<name>%`, for example `%exyliaaimtrainer_wins%`. The older `%exyliaaimtrainer_aim_wins%` and `%aim_wins%` no longer resolve. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/placeholders).

## Where are the arenas and the statistics?

In the database, not in YAML: `aim_arenas`, `aim_profiles`, `aim_records`, `aim_sessions` and `aim_matches`. H2 by default; point `database.yml` at MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL or MongoDB to share them between servers (see [Database](/docs/exylialib/database)). History rows older than `history.retention-days` (30) are deleted, and a reload does not touch any of it.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, it declares Folia support, and every event it fires runs on the thread that owns the player or the arena it is about.
