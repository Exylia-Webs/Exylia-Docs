Everything below happens in menus. The only thing typed is the arena's id.

## Creating an arena

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the admin menu">
    `/aimtraineradmin` (or `/aimadmin`) with `exyliaaimtrainer.admin`. The overview shows how many
    arenas exist, how many are ready, and how many duels and drills are running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    **Arenas** → **New arena**. Type a short id such as `aim1`; it is stored lowercased and refused if
    it already exists. The arena is created enabled, named after what you typed, with no spawn yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    On the arena screen, **Display name** asks for what players see. Colours are allowed, 64
    characters at most.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the spawn">
    Click **Spawn**, then stand where it goes and **face the open space**: the targets appear in the
    direction the spawn looks. The wizard stores your position and rotation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it reads ready">
    An arena is ready when it is enabled and its spawn is set in a loaded world. The arena list shows
    every arena as `READY` or `INCOMPLETE`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="No arena, no drill">
Solo drills need a ready arena as much as duels do. Without one, `/aim train` and every drill button
answer "No arena is ready. Ask an administrator."
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="One arena is enough to start">
An arena is a place, not a slot. Any number of drills and duels share it: every player in it is
invisible to everyone else and sees only their own targets. When there are several, each new drill or
duel goes to the ready arena hosting the fewest players. `arena.max-players-per-arena` caps that count
(a drill is one player, a duel two); the default, `0`, is no cap.
</Callout>

## A first drill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    `/aim` (or `/aimtrainer`, `/at`) opens the main menu. Click **Drills**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a drill">
    Each row shows what the drill trains, how long it runs and your best rating in it. Clicking starts
    it: you are sent to the arena, your inventory is saved and emptied, you are put in adventure mode,
    and a countdown of `training.countdown` seconds runs, 3 by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Punch the air to start now">
    A swing during the countdown skips it. Then shoot with left click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    When the drill ends you stay in the arena with the summary on screen for `training.rest-seconds`,
    5 by default. Punch the air to play it again with your current settings; the hotbar has
    **Change drill**, **Settings**, **Play again** and **Leave** on right click. When the rest runs out
    and you are not in a screen you opened, you are sent back where you came from, with your inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/aim train [drill]` does the same without the menu — with no name it starts `training.default-drill`,
`gridshot` by default — and `/aim leave` ends a drill at any point.

<Callout type="tip" title="Settings are worth a look">
`/aim settings` sets target size and distance as multipliers of the drill, colour and style, and which
parts of the HUD you want. Smaller and farther targets raise the difficulty, and the difficulty
multiplies the rating the boards rank. Changes apply from the next drill. See
[Settings](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/settings).
</Callout>

## A first duel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an opponent">
    `/aim` → **Duel** lists everyone online you can see, free players first; busy ones cannot be
    clicked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the settings">
    On the opponent's screen choose a best-of format, then click a drill. That click sends the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The other side accepts">
    They receive a centred prompt with clickable **Accept** and **Decline** buttons, or run
    `/aim accept` or `/aim deny`. The request expires after `match.duel-request-expiry` seconds, 30 by
    default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play the rounds">
    Both players are sent to an arena and a countdown runs; each punches the air to be ready, and the
    round starts as soon as both have. Both shoot the same targets in the same places, and the higher
    score takes the round. See [Duels](/docs/exyliaaimtrainer/duels) for ties and formats.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/aim duel <player> [drill] [bestOf]` sends a request straight from the command line. Without a drill
it uses the first one in `config.yml`, without a format `match.default-format`.
