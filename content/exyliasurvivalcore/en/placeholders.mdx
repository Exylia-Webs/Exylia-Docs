Every placeholder below is written in full, and clicking one copies it. They need PlaceholderAPI, then work in any plugin that reads it: scoreboards, holograms, tab lists and chat.

The prefix is `exyliasurvivalcore`, so a home count is `%exyliasurvivalcore_homes_count%`. The shorter `survivalcore` prefix is gone: `%survivalcore_homes_count%` no longer resolves.

<Callout type="note" title="A disabled module registers no placeholders">
A module turned off in `config.yml` registers nothing, so its placeholders stay on screen exactly as you wrote them. Turn the module on and reload to bring them back.
</Callout>

Durations are written in full, such as `1h 5m 3s`, unless a row says they are short. Words such as `Ready`, `Now` and `—` are the module's own wording and follow the server language. Placeholders of the plugins that took modules over (economy, market, rewards, combat, protections, crates, mines) are on those plugins' pages.

## Statistics

<Placeholders group="statistics" />

`<stat>` is a key from `modules/stats/stats.yml`. The shipped keys are `kills`, `deaths`, `kdr`, `best_kill_streak`, `current_streak`, `duels_won`, `balance`, `bounties_claimed`, `bounties_earned`, `votes_total`, `votes_best_streak`, `market_sold`, `market_earned`, `blocks_mined`, `farming_harvested`, `crates_opened`, `missions_completed`, `mobs_killed` and `playtime`. Several are filled by counters that other plugins keep; a stat nobody counts shows `0`.

`<key>` in `stats_counter_` is a counter key such as `crates.opened`. It does not have to be declared in `stats.yml`: a counter exists as soon as something counts it.

<Callout type="warning" title="Stat ids are not the streak placeholder names">
`%exyliasurvivalcore_stats_streak%` and `%exyliasurvivalcore_stats_best_streak%` read the counters directly and work. They are not stat ids: inside `stats_value_`, `stats_rank_` and `stats_top_*` the ids are `current_streak` and `best_kill_streak`. So `%exyliasurvivalcore_stats_value_streak%` answers the unknown word and `%exyliasurvivalcore_stats_value_current_streak%` works. `current_streak` ships with `leaderboard: false`, so its top rows answer with a dash by design.
</Callout>

The position comes first in the leaderboard placeholders, because a stat id may contain underscores and a position may not:

```text
#1 %exyliasurvivalcore_stats_top_name_1_kills% — %exyliasurvivalcore_stats_top_value_1_kills%
```

## Player settings

<Placeholders group="player-settings" />

One name per preference, each answering `true` or `false`. Every setting is on until the player turns it off. The names are the setting ids with dashes turned into underscores. The three `tpa_` ones read straight through to the TPA module, so a player never has two answers to one question.

## Boosters

<Placeholders group="boosters" />

The `global_` ones ask for no viewer. Every running booster of that type counts, and they combine by the module's stacking rule, capped at its maximum multiplier.

## Missions

<Placeholders group="missions" />
