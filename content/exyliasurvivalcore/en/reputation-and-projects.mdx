Two small modules built around other players: **reputation**, which turns finished deals into a score,
and **projects**, a goal the whole network builds together. Each is switched in `config.yml` under
`modules:` as `reputation` and `projects`.

## Reputation

Players rate each other after a real deal. A rating is `+1` or `-1`, and a player's score is positive
minus negative. A finished trade, a market purchase or a won auction opens one rating for the buyer (or
for the player who received the items) to give to the other side. A filled buy order is never rated: the
player who fills it did not choose the other party.

### What counts as a rating

- A deal can be rated **once**, by the one player it was opened for, for `rating-days` days.
- A deal worth less than `min-deal-value` (in the default currency) opens nothing. For a trade, the value
  is the default-currency money in it plus what the server shop pays for its items. Market and auction
  deals in another currency can always be rated.
- No rating is opened between two accounts that share an address, on any server. When that cannot be
  answered, nothing is opened.
- A rating from one player to the same player only counts again after `pair-cooldown-hours`.

The prompt to rate, the note that somebody rated you and the refund line travel over a network channel, so
the player sees them on whichever server they are on.

### Tiers and the tax refund

A score falls in a **tier**, and each tier gives back a share of the sales tax the player pays as a
seller on the market, on auctions and on buy orders. The refund is deposited right after the sale in the
currency the tax was taken in, and money boosters do not apply to it.

| Tier | From score | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| Untrusted | -5 | 0% |
| Newcomer | -4 | 0% |
| Trusted | 10 | 10% |
| Respected | 30 | 25% |
| Renowned | 75 | 50% |

A player is in the highest tier whose `min-score` their score reaches, or the lowest one when it reaches
none. This needs [ExyliaMarket](/docs/exyliamarket/introduction) for the sales and ExyliaEconomy for the
payout; without them ratings still work.

When a trade request involves a player whose score is `warn-at-or-below` or lower, the other side gets a
warning with it.

### Configuration

`modules/reputation/config.yml`:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.rating-days` | `3` | Days a deal can still be rated |
| `settings.pair-cooldown-hours` | `24` | Hours before a rating of the same player counts again |
| `settings.min-deal-value` | `10.0` | The smallest deal that can be rated |
| `settings.warn-at-or-below` | `-5` | Score at or under which trade requests carry a warning |
| `settings.remind-on-join` | `true` | Remind players with deals left to rate as they join |
| `tiers.<id>` | five tiers above | `min-score`, `name` and `tax-rebate-percent` (0 to 100) |

Messages are in `modules/reputation/messages.yml`. The values staff tune are also editable in game from
the admin settings screen for reputation.

### Commands and permission

| Command | Aliases | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/reputation [player]` | `/rep` | Shows your score, tier and refund, or another player's |
| `/reputation pending` | | Lists the deals you can still rate (the latest five) |
| `/reputation rate <deal> <up\|down>` | | Rates a deal. It is the target of the buttons in the prompt |

All of them need `exyliasurvivalcore.reputation`.

### Placeholders and statistics

| Placeholder | Returns |
|---|---|
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_reputation_score%` | The reader's score |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_reputation_positive%` | Positive ratings received |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_reputation_negative%` | Negative ratings received |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_reputation_tier%` | The tier name |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_reputation_rebate%` | The tier's refund percentage |

They answer `0` (and the lowest score's tier) until the player's row is read on join. Ratings also count
the [stat counters](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/missions-and-seasons#which-counters-can-be-named)
`reputation.positive`, `reputation.negative`, `reputation.score` and `reputation.given`, so a mission or
season can use them.

## Projects

A community project is a goal the **whole network** builds at a time: the first project in the file that
is not completed. Players give materials, and when every requirement is in, the project completes on one
server, runs its commands once, pays its contributors and the next project starts.

Only plain items count: a renamed or enchanted stack stays in the inventory. Whatever a project has no
room for any more, because another server filled it first, comes back to the player through the reward
queue.

### projects.yml

`modules/projects/projects.yml`, yours to edit. The shipped file has the *Iron Forge* (x1.5 money for
three days) and the *Great Library* (x1.5 experience for three days). Reload the plugin to apply an edit.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `name`, `icon` | How the menu shows it |
| `description` | What it is, one line each |
| `perk` | What completing it does, as the menu tells it |
| `requirements` | `MATERIAL: amount` |
| `commands` | Run once, from the console, as it completes (the shipped ones use `boostersadmin global ...`) |
| `min-items` | Items a player must have given to be paid `contributors` |
| `contributors` | Paid to each of them: `money`, `rewards`, `message` |
| `top` | Paid to the largest contributors by place (`'1'`, `'2'`, ...) with the same fields |

Money is in the default currency and multiplied by money boosters for `projects`; another currency is
written `money: {amount: 500, currency: gems}`. Message placeholders: `%player%`, `%project%`, `%money%`,
`%amount%` and `%place%`. Contributors are paid by id, so a player who is offline or on another server is
still paid, and every step is claimed so it happens once however many servers take part.

### Menu and commands

`/project` opens the menu: the project with its progress bar, one item per material (click to give yours),
**Give everything**, **Your part** and **Top builders**. The menu file is `modules/projects/menus/project.yml`.

| Command | Aliases | Permission | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/project` | `/projects` | `exyliasurvivalcore.projects` | Opens the menu |
| `/project give` | | `exyliasurvivalcore.projects` | Gives everything the project still needs |
| `/projectadmin reset <project> [confirm]` | `/projecta` | `exyliasurvivalcore.projects.admin` | Opens a project again from nothing; progress, contributions and completion go |

### Placeholders and statistics

`%exyliasurvivalcore_projects_name%` is the current project's name and `%exyliasurvivalcore_projects_percent%`
its progress as a whole percentage. Giving counts `projects.donated`, and starting and completing count
`projects.started` and `projects.completed`.
