Two small modules built around other players: **reputation**, which turns finished deals into a score, and
**projects**, a goal the whole network builds together. Each is switched on in `config.yml` under
`modules:` as `reputation` and `projects` ([Switch modules on and off](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/modules)).

## Reputation

Players rate each other after a real deal. A rating is `+1` or `-1`, and a player's score is positive minus
negative. A finished trade, a market purchase or a won auction opens one rating for the buyer (or for the
player who received the items) to give to the other side. A filled buy order is never rated: the player who
fills it did not choose the other party.

### What counts as a rating

- A deal can be rated **once**, by the one player it was opened for, for `rating-days` days.
- A deal worth less than `min-deal-value` (in the default currency) opens nothing. For a trade, the value
  is the default-currency money in it plus what the server shop pays for its items. Market and auction
  deals in another currency can always be rated.
- No rating is opened between two accounts that share an address, on any server. When that cannot be
  answered, nothing is opened.
- A rating from one player to the same player only counts again after `pair-cooldown-hours`.

The prompt to rate, the note that somebody rated you and the refund line travel over a network channel, so
the player sees them on whichever server they are on.

### Change the tiers and the tax refund

A score falls in a **tier**, and each tier gives back a share of the sales tax the player pays as a seller
on the market, on auctions and on buy orders. The refund is deposited right after the sale in the currency
the tax was taken in, and money boosters do not apply to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tiers">
    Edit `modules/reputation/config.yml`, or open Plugin ▸ Settings ▸ Reputation from
    `/survivalcoreadmin`. Each block under `tiers:` is one tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set its score and refund">
    Give it a `min-score`, a `name` and a `tax-rebate-percent` from 0 to 100.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/survivalcoreadmin reload`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The shipped tiers:

| Tier | From score | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| Untrusted | -5 | 0% |
| Newcomer | -4 | 0% |
| Trusted | 10 | 10% |
| Respected | 30 | 25% |
| Renowned | 75 | 50% |

A player is in the highest tier whose `min-score` their score reaches, or the lowest one when it reaches
none. The refund needs [ExyliaMarket](/docs/exyliamarket/introduction) for the sales and ExyliaEconomy for
the payout; without them ratings still work.

When a trade request involves a player whose score is `warn-at-or-below` or lower, the other side gets a
warning with it.

### Check that it works

Finish a trade worth at least `min-deal-value` between two accounts on different addresses. Each side
should get a prompt to rate, and `/reputation pending` lists it.

<Details title="Settings (modules/reputation/config.yml)">

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.rating-days` | `3` | Days a deal can still be rated |
| `settings.pair-cooldown-hours` | `24` | Hours before a rating of the same player counts again |
| `settings.min-deal-value` | `10.0` | The smallest deal that can be rated |
| `settings.warn-at-or-below` | `-5` | Score at or under which trade requests carry a warning |
| `settings.remind-on-join` | `true` | Remind players with deals left to rate as they join |
| `tiers.<id>` | five tiers above | `min-score`, `name` and `tax-rebate-percent` (0 to 100) |

Messages are in `modules/reputation/messages.yml`. The values staff tune are also editable in game from the
admin settings screen for reputation.
</Details>

`/reputation [player]` (alias `/rep`) shows a score, tier and refund. `/reputation pending` lists the latest
five deals you can still rate, and `/reputation rate <deal> <up|down>` is the target of the buttons in the
prompt. All of them need `exyliasurvivalcore.reputation`.

<Placeholders group="reputation" />

They answer `0` (and the lowest score's tier) until the player's row is read on join. Ratings also count the
[stat counters](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/missions-and-seasons#which-counters-can-be-named)
`reputation.positive`, `reputation.negative`, `reputation.score` and `reputation.given`, so a mission or
season can use them.

## Projects

A community project is a goal the **whole network** builds at a time: the first project in the file that is
not completed. Players give materials, and when every requirement is in, the project completes on one
server, runs its commands once, pays its contributors and the next project starts.

Only plain items count: a renamed or enchanted stack stays in the inventory. Whatever a project has no room
for any more, because another server filled it first, comes back to the player through the reward queue.

### Add a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the file">
    Edit `modules/projects/projects.yml`. Projects are built in file order, so put the next one below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and list what it needs">
    Give it a `name`, `icon`, `description` and `requirements` as `MATERIAL: amount`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what completing it does">
    Put console commands in `commands`, and the line the menu shows in `perk`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the builders">
    Fill `contributors` (everyone who gave at least `min-items`) and `top` (by place).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/survivalcoreadmin reload`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The shipped file has the *Iron Forge* (x1.5 money for three days) and the *Great Library* (x1.5 experience
for three days); copy one as your starting point.

<Details title="Project keys">

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `name`, `icon` | How the menu shows it |
| `description` | What it is, one line each |
| `perk` | What completing it does, as the menu tells it |
| `requirements` | `MATERIAL: amount` |
| `commands` | Run once, from the console, as it completes (the shipped ones use `boostersadmin global ...`) |
| `min-items` | Items a player must have given to be paid `contributors`. Default `1` |
| `contributors` | Paid to each of them: `money`, `rewards`, `message` |
| `top` | Paid to the largest contributors by place (`'1'`, `'2'`, ...) with the same fields |

Money is in the default currency and multiplied by money boosters for `projects` (see
[Economy and boosters](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/economy)); another currency is written
`money: {amount: 500, currency: gems}`. Rewards use the [Exylia reward list](/docs/exylialib/rewards).
Message placeholders: `%player%`, `%project%`, `%money%`, `%amount%` and `%place%`. Contributors are paid by
id, so a player who is offline or on another server is still paid, and every step is claimed so it happens
once however many servers take part.
</Details>

### Give materials

`/project` (alias `/projects`, `exyliasurvivalcore.projects`) opens the menu: the project with its progress
bar, one item per material (click to give yours), **Give everything**, **Your part** and **Top builders**.
`/project give` hands over everything the project still needs from your inventory. The menu file is
`modules/projects/menus/project.yml`.

`/projectadmin reset <project> [confirm]` (alias `/projecta`, `exyliasurvivalcore.projects.admin`) opens a
project again from nothing; progress, contributions and completion go.

<Placeholders group="projects" />

Giving counts `projects.donated`, and starting and completing count `projects.started` and
`projects.completed`.
