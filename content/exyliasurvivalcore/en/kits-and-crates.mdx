You will have a kit players claim with `/kit`, with a cooldown and a preview, and optionally an item spawner
that drops a glowing item every few minutes. Everything is built from menus; there is no kit file.

<Callout type="note" title="Crates and loot chests moved">
Crates are now [ExyliaCrates](/docs/exyliacrates/introduction) and loot chests are part of
[ExyliaWarzone](/docs/exyliawarzone/loot-chests). This page covers kits and item spawners.
</Callout>

## Before you start

The module `kits` must be on in `config.yml` ([Switch modules on and off](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/modules)).
Kits are stored in the database, so every server on the network sees the same kits.

## Create a kit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the kit editor">
    Run `/kitadmin` (permission `exyliasurvivalcore.kits.admin`) and click **Create ▸ Kit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and give it items">
    Click **Kit ▸ Display name**, then **Kit ▸ Items** to fill it with ExyliaLib's loadout editor.
    **Kit ▸ Bonuses** adds console commands that run with the items; named ones show in the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can claim it and how often">
    **Kit ▸ Permission** limits who sees and claims it. **Kit ▸ Cooldown** is typed as a duration such as `1d`
    or `12h` (default 24 hours). **Kit ▸ Max uses** is `-1` for unlimited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click **Kit ▸ Status** so the kit is enabled. A disabled kit does not exist for players.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The other buttons of the editor: **Icon**, **Description**, **Sort order** (where it sits in the list),
**Equip armour** (armour goes on instead of into the bag), **Drop when full**, **First join kit**,
**Preview**, **Clone** and **Delete**.

## Check that it works

Run `/kit`. You should see your kit in one of four states: ready, on cooldown, used up or locked. Click it
to preview, then claim it. A second `/kit <id>` straight away is refused until the cooldown ends.

## Going further

### Give a kit to new players

Turn on **Kit ▸ First join kit**. It is handed over the first time a player joins, as long as the kit is
enabled.

### Choose what happens when the bag is full

With **Kit ▸ Drop when full** on, a claim goes through and what does not fit is kept in ExyliaLib's pending
reward queue and handed over the next time the player joins; nothing is dropped on the floor. With it off, a
claim that would not fit is refused before anything is given.

### Let staff skip the rules

`exyliasurvivalcore.kits.bypass` skips cooldowns and use limits. Staff can also run
`/kitadmin give <player> <kit>` (gives it whether or not the player has waited), `/kitadmin reset <player>
<kit>` (forgets that player's cooldown and use count) and `/kitadmin preview <kit>` (previews it without the
kit's permission). Another plugin can do the same through the [API](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/api).

### Previews open containers

A shulker box or bundle inside a kit can be opened in the preview, so a player sees what is inside before
claiming. `preview.container-hint` in `modules/kits/messages.yml` is the lore added to those containers.

### Kit counters

Claims count `kits.claimed` and `kits.<kit>.claimed` in the
[stat counters](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/missions-and-seasons#which-counters-can-be-named), so a mission can
ask for them.

<Details title="Kit settings (modules/kits/config.yml, under settings:)">

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `default-cooldown-ms` | `86400000` (24 hours) | Cooldown given to a new kit |
| `default-max-uses` | `-1` | Use limit given to a new kit |
| `show-locked-kits` | `true` | Show kits the player has no permission for, as locked |

Each state of the list has its own template in `modules/kits/menus/list.yml`. Menu text is in
`modules/kits/menus/` and `modules/kits/messages.yml`.
</Details>

## Item spawners

A block that drops a glowing item at a random interval, under a countdown hologram. A spawner has a loot
table, a minimum and a maximum interval, and a list of locations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Run `/spawnersadmin` and click **Create ▸ Spawner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the loot and the timing">
    Use **Spawner ▸ Loot table**, **Spawner ▸ Min interval** and **Spawner ▸ Max interval**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add where it drops">
    Click **Spawner ▸ Spawn locations**, then **Add ▸ Location** while standing at the spot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click **Spawner ▸ Status**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Item spawners belong to the server they were placed on, and each server loads only its own.

<Callout type="warning" title="Off when ExyliaSpawners is installed">
When [ExyliaSpawners](/docs/exyliaspawners/introduction) is on the server, this module stays off and
ExyliaSpawners provides item spawners.
</Callout>

<Details title="Item spawner settings (modules/item-spawners/config.yml, under settings:)">

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `detection-radius` | `2.0` | How far to look for an item already lying there |
| `sound-radius` | `16.0` | How far the spawn sound carries |
| `hologram-enabled`, `hologram-offset-y` | `true`, `2` | The countdown above the spawner |
| `glow-enabled`, `glow-color` | `true`, `gold` | The dropped item's outline; one of the sixteen named text colours |
| `particle-enabled`, `particle-type` | `true`, `END_ROD` | Particles at the spawn |
| `particle-count`, `particle-offset`, `particle-speed` | `8`, `0.3`, `0.05` | Particle burst |
| `sounds.spawn` | `ENTITY_ITEM_PICKUP\|1.2\|0.8` | Sound as `NAME\|VOLUME\|PITCH` |

The hologram lines (`hologram.ready`, `hologram.countdown`, `hologram.disabled`, `hologram.no-location`)
are in `modules/item-spawners/messages.yml`.
</Details>
