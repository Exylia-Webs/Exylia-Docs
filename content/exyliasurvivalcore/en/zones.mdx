You will have a portal that sends players somewhere or runs commands on them, and a jump pad that throws
them into the air. Both are built from admin menus and stored in the database. Each records the server that
created it, so on a network whose servers share one database each server loads only its own. A row written
before servers were recorded belongs to every server that reads it.

## Before you start

The modules `portals` and `jumpads` must be on in `config.yml`
([Switch modules on and off](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/modules)).

<Callout type="warning" title="ExyliaLobby takes over on lobbies">
With `defer-to-exylia-lobby: true` (the default) in `config.yml`, the `portals`, `jumpads` and `join-leave`
modules stay off while [ExyliaLobby](/docs/exylialobby/introduction) is enabled, because its areas, hotbar
and movement features replace them. Set it to `false` to keep these modules running next to ExyliaLobby.
Spawn is not affected. If your portals or jump pads do nothing, check this first.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="Other zones moved out">
Mines are [ExyliaMines](/docs/exyliamines/introduction). Regenerating zones, power-ups and loot chests are
[ExyliaWarzone](/docs/exyliawarzone/introduction). AFK zones are
[ExyliaRewards](/docs/exyliarewards/afk-zones).
</Callout>

## Create a portal

A portal is a region plus a destination, a list of commands, or both.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal editor">
    Run `/portalsadmin` (permission `survivalcore.portals.admin`) and click **Create ▸ Portal**, then the new
    portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the region">
    Click **Portal ▸ Region** and select the area. Walking into it triggers the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set where it sends players">
    Stand at the destination and left-click **Portal ▸ Teleport** to set it there. Right-click clears it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or run commands">
    Click **Portal ▸ Commands** to manage commands run for the player who walks in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and limit it">
    **Portal ▸ Name** is what the entry title shows (a portal with no name shows its id). **Portal ▸ State**
    turns it on or off. **Portal ▸ Permission**: left-click to type a node, right-click to clear it. Anybody
    without it is told `portals.no-permission` and is not taken.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Check that it works

Walk into the region. You should be teleported (or the commands run) and see the entry title. A destination
that cannot be reached tells the player why, with the teleport lines described in
[Movement](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/movement).

<Details title="Portal settings (modules/portals/config.yml)">

```yaml
settings:
  cooldown-seconds: 2
effects:
  enter-sound: "BLOCK_PORTAL_TRAVEL|0.4|1.6"
```

`cooldown-seconds` is how long the **same** portal waits before it takes the same player again, which stops
two portals pointed at each other bouncing somebody back and forth. `0` turns it off. The wait is kept in
memory and cleared when the player leaves. The entry title (`%portal%` is the portal's name) is
`enter-title` in `modules/portals/messages.yml`.
</Details>

## Create a jump pad

A block that launches whoever steps on it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the jump pad editor">
    Run `/jumpadmin` (permission `exyliasurvivalcore.jumpads.admin`) and click **Create ▸ Jumpad**, then the
    new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the blocks that trigger it">
    Click **Jumpad ▸ Trigger blocks**, then **Add ▸ Trigger**. One jump pad can cover many blocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune the launch">
    **Jumpad ▸ Horizontal power** and **Jumpad ▸ Vertical power**: left-click adds 0.1, right-click removes
    0.1, shift-click moves by 0.5. **Jumpad ▸ Direction mode** toggles between a fixed direction and the
    player's own facing; **Jumpad ▸ Launch direction** sets the fixed one by looking toward it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose glide and turn it on">
    **Jumpad ▸ Glide** opens the elytra after the launch. **Jumpad ▸ Status** turns the pad on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Check that it works

Step on a trigger block. You should be launched, and with glide on the elytra opens once you leave the
ground.

<Details title="Jump pad settings">
`settings.launch-cooldown-ms` in `modules/jumpads/config.yml` is `500` by default: the wait between
launches per player. A pad is only looked up when a player changes block, not on every movement packet.
</Details>
