title: "Protection economy"
description: "Protection banks, upkeep and abandonment, selling and giving protections, rent, and level upgrades."

Five features put money into protections. Each one is off by default and turned on with its own switch
under `submodules` in `modules/protections/config.yml`: `bank`, `upkeep`, `market`, `rent` and
`upgrades`. All money moves through the server's economy and its currencies (see
[Economy](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/economy)); a currency setting left blank means the default currency.

For plots, which rent builds on, see [Protections](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/protections).

## Banks

Every protection has a shared bank. Members put money in, and it pays the upkeep, and rent and upgrades
when you set it to.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `bank.currency` | blank | The currency banks hold and upkeep is paid in |
| `bank.max-balance` | `0` | The most one bank may hold. `0` is no limit |

| Command | Who |
|---|---|
| `/protections bank` | Shows the balance. Needs `bank-deposit` |
| `/protections bank deposit <amount>` | Role permission `bank-deposit` |
| `/protections bank withdraw <amount>` | Role permission `bank-withdraw` |

All three act on the protection the player stands in. In the protection's menu, left-clicking the
**Bank** button deposits and right-clicking withdraws; both ask for an amount. The default `member` and
`trusted` roles have `bank-deposit`. No default role has `bank-withdraw`, so only the owner withdraws
until a role is given it.

A deposit leaves the player first. If the bank would go over `max-balance`, or is busy, the money goes
back. A withdrawal the player cannot receive goes back into the bank. A bank is **busy** when another
server is changing it at the same moment and it could not wait its turn; nothing moves and the player
is told to try again.

When a protection is deleted, for any reason, its bank is paid to the owner, even if they are offline.
Merging two protections moves the second bank into the first.

## Upkeep

With upkeep on, protections pay every period. One that stops paying, or whose players stop playing, is
**abandoned**: open to everyone for a few days, then deleted.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `upkeep.period-hours` | `24` | Hours between two payments |
| `upkeep.tier-costs` | `small: 15`, `medium: 40`, `large: 100` | Cost of one period per tier |
| `upkeep.default-cost` | `15` | Cost of a tier left out of `tier-costs` |
| `upkeep.cost-per-block` | `0` | Added per block of protected area |
| `upkeep.charge-owner` | `true` | Whether an online owner pays what the bank cannot |
| `upkeep.grace-periods` | `3` | Unpaid periods that abandon a protection |
| `upkeep.inactive-days` | `30` | Days without the owner or a member playing that abandon it. `0` turns it off |
| `upkeep.abandoned-days` | `3` | Days an abandoned protection stays open before it is deleted |
| `upkeep.warn-hours` | `12` | Hours before a payment the bank cannot cover when members are warned |
| `upkeep.check-minutes` | `10` | Minutes between two checks. Read when the server starts |
| `upkeep.announce` | `false` | Whether an abandonment is announced to everyone with raid announcements on |

### The arithmetic

One period costs the tier's cost plus `cost-per-block` × the protected area (width × depth, in blocks).
A protection by selection pays as the tier `selection.tier` names. Upkeep is paid in `bank.currency`.

The first period is free. A protection made with upkeep on owes its first payment one period after it
was made. A protection that already existed owes it one period after upkeep first checks it.

Every `check-minutes`, the server a protection is on works out how many periods are due:

1. **The bank pays** every due period it can cover in full. With 50 in the bank, a cost of 20 and two
   periods due, it pays both and keeps 10. With only 30, it pays one and one stays owed.
2. **The owner pays the rest** when `charge-owner` is on and they are online on that server. What they
   cannot afford stays owed. They are told what their balance paid.
3. **Abandonment.** The protection is abandoned once it owes `grace-periods` periods, or once
   `inactive-days` have passed since the owner or any member last joined or left a server. It stops being
   abandoned the moment neither is true.

A period counts as owed from the moment it is due. With 24-hour periods and 3 grace periods, a
protection whose bank is empty is abandoned 48 hours after its payment was due, the third period falling
due at that moment.

Only the owner and players added as members count towards activity, not clan members. A player upkeep
has never seen does not make a protection inactive; when upkeep starts, every owner and member without a
record counts as seen at that moment.

### Warnings and paying

- Within `warn-hours` of a payment the bank cannot cover, online defenders are told once for that
  payment. Checks happen every `check-minutes`, so the warning comes at the first check inside that
  window.
- A member joining is told, two seconds in, which of their protections owe upkeep or are abandoned.
- Any member pays what is owed with `/protections upkeep pay`, or by clicking the **Upkeep** button of
  the protection's menu. The full debt leaves their own balance, in `bank.currency`.
- `/protections upkeep` shows the state, the cost per period, the time to the next payment and what is
  owed.

### Abandoned

While a protection is abandoned:

- Every player who is not the owner or a member may do what the raids' `raidable-permissions` allow
  there, whether raids are on or not. See [Protection raids](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/protection-raids#the-raid).
- Anybody may enter, whatever the lock or the bans say. With the WorldGuard backend, its mirrored region
  lets everybody build, as during a raid.
- It cannot be picked up, moved, deleted, sold, given away, offered for rent or upgraded, and a listed
  sale is cancelled.
- Online defenders are told, and when `announce` is on so is every player with raid announcements on.
- The upkeep button and `%exyliasurvivalcore_protections_upkeep_due%` show how long is left before it is
  deleted.

It is restored when its debt is paid, or when inactivity was the reason and the owner or a member comes
back: joining sends the reminder and the protection is checked again at once, as long as that player is
on the protection's server. An abandonment with nothing owed is cleared by `/protections upkeep pay`
without charging anything.

Once `abandoned-days` have passed, the next check deletes it. Its defenders online are told, and its
bank goes to the owner.

<Callout type="warning" title="abandoned-days: 0 deletes at the next check">
The deletion is due as soon as the protection is abandoned. The next check, within `check-minutes`,
removes it.
</Callout>

Turning `submodules.upkeep` off stops charging and ends every abandonment's effect. What was owed and
when a protection was abandoned stay stored, and apply again if upkeep is turned back on.

| Upkeep state | When |
|---|---|
| Paid | Nothing owed |
| Due | At least one period owed |
| Abandoned | Abandoned |

Admins forgive an abandonment from the admin menu, and the in-game tier editor writes a tier's upkeep
cost. See [Administering protections](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/protection-admin).

## Selling and giving protections

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `market.currency` | blank | Currency protections are sold in |
| `market.tax-percent` | `5` | Percent of the price kept as tax; the seller gets the rest |
| `market.max-price` | `500000` | The highest price. `0` is no limit |
| `market.keep-members` | `false` | Whether members, clans and overrides stay with the land when it changes owner |

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/protections market` | Opens every protection for sale, on any server, newest first |
| `/protections sell <price>` | Lists the protection you stand in. Owner only |
| `/protections unsell` | Takes it off sale |
| `/protections transfer <player>` | Gives it away. Owner only |

The protection's menu has a **Sell or give away** button: left-click lists or cancels the sale, and
right-click gives it away. The protections list has a **Protections for sale** button.

**Listing.** The price must be above 0 and at most `max-price`. It is refused while the protection is
raidable, damaged, abandoned or has tenants. The listing keeps the currency `market.currency` names at
that moment. It is cancelled on its own, and the seller told, when the protection becomes raidable or
abandoned, or changes owner.

**Buying.** In the market menu, left-click buys and right-click teleports to the protection to look
first. The buyer cannot be the owner and must pass the same checks as placing that tier: its
permission, their protection limit, the tier's limit and the world's limit. After a confirmation the
price leaves the buyer. If somebody else bought it first, or it stopped being for sale, the money goes
back.

The seller receives the price minus `tax-percent`: a protection sold for 10,000 at 5% pays 9,500. The
tax is kept between 0 and 100. The seller is paid even if offline.

**Giving.** The player must be online on the same server, since limits are read from their permissions.
The tier's permission and the limits are checked against them. After a confirmation marked as
dangerous, the protection is theirs, for free.

**What changes hands.** A sale or a gift keeps the land, its bank, its upkeep, its lives, its roles,
flags, bans, greeting, plots and level. With `keep-members: false`, the players and clans added to it
and their overrides are removed. Plots keep their own members. The new owner is never left banned from,
or a member of, their own land.

## Rent

An owner rents out a plot, or with `rent.whole-protection` the whole protection, for a price per period.
The tenant becomes a member of what they rent until it ends.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `rent.whole-protection` | `false` | Whether whole protections may be rented, not only plots |
| `rent.currency` | blank | Currency rent is paid in |
| `rent.max-price` | `50000` | The highest rent per period. `0` is no limit |
| `rent.min-period-minutes` | `60` | The shortest period an offer may have |
| `rent.max-periods` | `4` | The most periods one rent may be paid for, the first included |
| `rent.tenant-role` | `tenant` | The role tenants hold. A protection without it gets it |
| `rent.tenant-permissions` | `break`, `place`, `containers`, `doors`, `redstone`, `buckets`, `animals`, `ride`, `decorations`, `trade`, `leash` | What that role starts with when it is created |
| `rent.pay-to-bank` | `true` | Whether rent goes into the protection's bank |
| `rent.owner-can-evict` | `false` | Whether a tenant can be evicted before the rent ends |
| `rent.relist` | `true` | Whether a target is offered again, on the same terms, when its rent ends |

### Offering

`/protections rent offer <price> <period>` offers the plot you stand in, or the whole protection when
you stand outside every plot or plots are off. The period is a duration such as `12h`, `1d` or `7d`.
`/protections rent cancel` withdraws it. Both need the role permission `manage-rent`.

In menus, the **Rent out** button of a plot, and of the protection when `whole-protection` is on, asks
for the price and then the period; clicking it again cancels the offer.

An offer is refused when:

- it is for the whole protection and `whole-protection` is off,
- somebody rents it right now,
- the price is not above 0 or is over `max-price`,
- the period is shorter than `min-period-minutes`,
- the protection is raidable, damaged or abandoned.

The offer takes `rent.currency` and `rent.max-periods` as they stand when it is made.

### Renting

`/protections rent`, or the **Land for rent** button of the protections list, opens the player's own
rentals first and every offer on any server after them. Left-click rents; right-click teleports there to
look.

Renting is refused to the owner, to anybody already a member of that plot or protection, to players
banned from it, and while the protection is raidable or abandoned. After a confirmation the tenant pays
one period. If somebody else took it first, the money goes back.

The tenant then holds `tenant-role` in the plot, or in the whole protection. The role is created with
`tenant-permissions` in a protection that lacks it. A tenant may enter a locked protection and teleport
to its home.

**Where the money goes.** Into the protection's bank when `pay-to-bank` is on, banks are on and
`rent.currency` is the same as `bank.currency`. If the bank is full or busy, and in every other case, it
goes to the owner.

### Renewing and ending

The tenant renews by left-clicking their rental in the rent menu. Each renewal pays one more period and
adds one period to the end. A rent may be paid for `max-periods` periods in total, the first one
included: with the default 4, a tenant rents once and renews three times. Past that, it ends when its
time runs out. A rent that has ended cannot be renewed.

When the time runs out, the tenant loses the plot's permissions at once. At the next check, every
`upkeep.check-minutes` whether upkeep is on or not, the tenant is removed and told, and with `relist` on
the target is offered again on the same terms.

While a protection has a tenant, it cannot be picked up, moved, deleted, sold or given away, and a rented
plot cannot be deleted.

### Eviction

Removing a tenant from the members of what they rent is refused while `owner-can-evict` is `false`.
With it on, the remover is asked to confirm, pays the tenant back for the time left, and the rent ends.

The refund is the price × the time left ÷ one period, rounded down to cents. It counts every period
paid: a rent of 100 a day, renewed once and evicted 12 hours in, pays back 150.00. It is paid in the rent's
currency, from the remover's balance, and the eviction does not happen if they cannot afford it.

## Level upgrades

Each tier can be upgraded through levels that grow the land and raise its limits.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `upgrades.pay-from-bank` | `false` | Whether the protection's bank pays upgrades |
| `upgrades.currency` | blank | Currency upgrades cost |
| `upgrades.base-max-members` | `8` | Members, players and clans together, before any upgrade. `0` is no limit |
| `upgrades.base-max-subregions` | `2` | Plots before any upgrade. `0` is no limit |
| `upgrades.tiers` | see below | The levels of each tier |

```yaml
upgrades:
  tiers:
    small:
      levels:
        '1': { cost: 3000, radius-bonus: 5, max-members: 12, max-subregions: 3, bonus-lives: 1, permission: "" }
        '2': { cost: 6000, radius-bonus: 10, max-members: 16, max-subregions: 4, bonus-lives: 2, permission: "" }
```

| Tier | Level | Cost | Radius bonus | Members | Plots | Bonus lives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `small` | 1 | 3,000 | 5 | 12 | 3 | 1 |
| `small` | 2 | 6,000 | 10 | 16 | 4 | 2 |
| `medium` | 1 | 8,000 | 5 | 12 | 4 | 1 |
| `medium` | 2 | 16,000 | 10 | 16 | 6 | 2 |
| `large` | 1 | 20,000 | 5 | 16 | 6 | 1 |
| `large` | 2 | 40,000 | 10 | 24 | 8 | 2 |

Levels are numbered from 1. `radius-bonus` and `bonus-lives` are totals at that level, not additions to
the level before. `max-members` and `max-subregions` are the limits at that level, `0` for none.
`permission` is needed to reach the level; blank needs none.

### Upgrading

`/protections upgrade`, or the **Upgrade** button of the protection's menu, takes the protection to its
next level. Only the owner may, standing on the protection's server and world. The button shows what
the next level brings and what it costs.

The upgrade is refused while the protection is raidable, damaged or abandoned, when there is no next
level, or without the level's `permission`. The grown land must stay inside the world border and pass
every check new land passes: no overlap, the minimum distance to other owners, allowed worlds and
WorldGuard regions.

The land grows by the difference in `radius-bonus` on all four sides, around where it already is. A
`small` protection of radius 10 covers 21 × 21 blocks; level 1 makes it 31 × 31 and level 2 makes it
41 × 41. Its height does not change.

After a confirmation, the price is paid by the bank when `pay-from-bank` is on, banks are on and
`upgrades.currency` is the same as `bank.currency`. Otherwise the owner pays. If the land changed while
the confirmation was open, the payment goes back.

### What a level gives

- **Land:** the radius bonus, always, whether upgrades are on or not. Turning upgrades off never shrinks
  land, and a picked-up core keeps its level.
- **Members:** while upgrades are on, adding a player or a clan past `max-members` is refused.
- **Plots:** while upgrades are on, the level's `max-subregions`. With upgrades off,
  `subregions.max-per-protection` applies instead.
- **Lives:** while upgrades are on, `bonus-lives` is added to the tier's lives. See
  [Protection raids](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/protection-raids#lives).

A protection whose level no longer exists in the config counts as the highest level its tier still has.

## Map markers and admin

On BlueMap and Dynmap, protections for sale, for rent and abandoned are drawn in their own colours (see
[Protections](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/protections)). The admin menu filters protections by these states,
forgives abandonments, sets levels and transfers ownership. See
[Administering protections](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/protection-admin).

## Placeholders

These read the protection the player stands in, and are blank outside one.

| Placeholder | What it returns |
|---|---|
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_protections_bank%` | Its bank balance. Blank while banks are off |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_protections_upkeep_cost%` | What one period costs. Blank while upkeep is off |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_protections_upkeep_due%` | Time to the next payment, time left before deletion when abandoned, or `Overdue`. Blank while upkeep is off |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_protections_sale_price%` | Its price when for sale |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_protections_level%` | Its level |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_protections_rent_tenant%` | Who rents the plot the player stands in, or the whole protection outside every plot |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_protections_rent_expiry%` | How long that rent has left |
