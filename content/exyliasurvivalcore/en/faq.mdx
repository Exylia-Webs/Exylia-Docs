## My admin menu edits keep disappearing

Everything under `menus/admin/` is regenerated from the jar on **every** start and every
`/survivalcoreadmin reload`. Edit the owner menus under `lang/<code>/` instead: those keep your edits.

## A menu I edited did not get the new button

Your edits win. When a new version changes a default you had left alone, the change waits in
`/exylialib updates` for you to apply or keep; only keys that did not exist before are added on their
own. A menu whose shipped `menu-version` went up is replaced whole, and your previous file is kept
beside it. See [Menus](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/menus).

## I turned a module off and nothing changed

Module switches need a restart. `/survivalcoreadmin reload` re-reads the configuration files, the module
settings and the menus, but never enables or disables a module; it names the ones still waiting.

## Where did crates, the shop, protections and the rest go?

They are their own plugins now, with their own data and commands. The list, with a link for each, is on
the [Modules](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/modules#moved-to-other-plugins) page. A leftover
`modules/blocked-items/rules.yml` is read by ExyliaWarzone, not by this plugin.

## Why are jumppads, join messages and portals off?

When ExyliaLobby is enabled, `jumpads`, `join-leave` and `portals` stay off and defer to it. This is the
`defer-to-exylia-lobby` setting in the core configuration. See
[Configuration](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/configuration).

## Stacked items and mobs look wrong next to ExyliaSpawners

Spawner stacking belongs to ExyliaSpawners; the `optimization` module yields to it. See
[Optimization](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/optimization).

## Missions never finish, or seasons rank nobody

`stats` is off. Missions measure progress against the stat counters and seasons rank them, and only the
`stats` module keeps those counters.

## A permission does nothing

Check the prefix. A few older administration nodes start with `survivalcore.` rather than
`exyliasurvivalcore.`; grant them exactly as spelled on the
[Permissions](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/permissions) page. Players' nodes default to `true` and
administration nodes to `op`, so you only grant what you want to change.

## `%exyliasurvivalcore_stats_value_streak%` shows nothing

`streak` is not a stat id. Use `%exyliasurvivalcore_stats_value_current_streak%` or
`%exyliasurvivalcore_stats_rank_best_kill_streak%`, and see [Placeholders](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/placeholders)
for the ids that exist.

## Two players in the same repair group pay different prices

They are not in the same group. Repair groups resolve in **file order**, not by price: the first group
in `groups.yml` whose node a player holds is the one that applies. Write the best group first.

## What does the plugin need to run?

Paper 1.21.1 or newer (Folia 1.21.4 or newer) and ExyliaLib. PacketEvents is optional. See
[Installation](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/installation).

## Can two servers share this?

Yes. Point them at the same database and homes, warps, kits, ranks, statistics and the other stored data
are shared; homes and warps carry the server they were set on, so `/home` and `/warp` cross the network,
and TPA requests reach players on another server. See [Database](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/database).
