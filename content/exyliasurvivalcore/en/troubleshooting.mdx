Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

The console names what is missing. ExyliaLib is required. See [Install ExyliaSurvivalCore](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/installation).

## A module's commands or placeholders do not exist

When a module is switched off, its commands and placeholders are not registered at all. Switch it on in the configuration and restart: module switches are not applied by a reload, and the reload message names the ones waiting. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/configuration).

## A placeholder shows as text

- PlaceholderAPI is not installed.
- The module behind the placeholder is switched off.
- The name is misspelled. Copy it from [Placeholders](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/placeholders).

## A warp or kit does not show for players

It is disabled, or the player lacks its permission. Open `/warpadmin` or `/kitadmin`, pick it and check **Status** and **Permission**.

## `/rtp` does not offer my world

Only worlds listed and enabled in `modules/rtp/worlds.yml` can be reached. Add the world there and reload. See [Your first spawn, warp and kit](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/first-steps).

## `/stats` does nothing

The command is not registered in this version. Read statistics through [placeholders](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/placeholders#statistics) instead.

## My edit to a menu disappeared

Everything under `menus/admin/` is regenerated from the jar on every start and every reload. Edit the owner menus under `lang/<code>/` instead. See [Menus](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/menus).

## An admin permission does nothing

The older administration nodes use the `survivalcore.` prefix, not `exyliasurvivalcore.`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/permissions).
