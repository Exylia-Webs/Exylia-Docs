Every command of ExyliaSurvivalCore, with its aliases and the permission it checks. Give a command to a rank with the permission in its row; see [Permissions](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/permissions).

Commands are registered when the plugin starts, by the module that owns them. A module switched off in `config.yml` has no commands at all, and changing a switch needs a restart to add or remove its commands.

`<x>` is required and `[x]` optional. A permission on a command applies to all its subcommands unless a subcommand names its own. A row with no permission can be used by everyone.

<Callout type="note" title="Admin roots">
The plugin's own staff command is `/survivalcoreadmin` (alias `/survivalcore`). Each module with staff tools keeps its own admin command, such as `/kitadmin`, `/warpadmin` and `/rtpadmin`.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="/stats does not open anything">
The source defines `/stats [player]`, but the command is never registered, so typing it does nothing. Statistics reach players through [placeholders](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/placeholders#statistics) and the settings and stats screens of the modules.
</Callout>

While `combat-blocks-teleports` is on, `/spawn`, `/home`, `/warp`, `/rtp`, `/back`, `/tpa` and portals are refused during combat. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/configuration#configyml).

Boosters take a multiplier above 1 and up to 100, and a duration such as `30m` or `2h`. There is no missions admin command; missions are edited in `missions.yml`.

Commands of modules that moved to their own plugins are on those plugins' pages: [ExyliaEconomy](/docs/exyliaeconomy/introduction), [ExyliaMarket](/docs/exyliamarket/introduction), [ExyliaProtections](/docs/exyliaprotections/introduction), [ExyliaRewards](/docs/exyliarewards/introduction), [ExyliaCombat](/docs/exyliacombat/introduction), [ExyliaWarzone](/docs/exyliawarzone/introduction), [ExyliaCrates](/docs/exyliacrates/introduction), [ExyliaMines](/docs/exyliamines/introduction), [ExyliaGraves](/docs/exyliagraves/introduction), [ExyliaPlayerVaults](/docs/exyliaplayervaults/introduction) and [ExyliaMobs](/docs/exyliamobs/introduction).
