---
title: "Market, auctions and orders"
description: "Player listings at a fixed price, an auction house with held bids and anti-snipe, and buy orders paid up front that anybody can fill."
---

Three modules let players trade with each other without meeting. The **market** sells at a price the
seller sets, the **auction house** sells to whoever bids highest, and **orders** turn it round: a
buyer pays first and sellers fill the order.

Each one has its own switch under `modules` in `config.yml` (`market`, `auctions`, `orders`), and all
three are on by default. Each needs an economy. Without one the module still loads, logs a warning, and
refuses every sale, bid and order.

## What the three share

**Any currency.** A price can be in any currency registered on the server, with the default one listed
first. When a player leaves the currency out of a command and there is more than one, a picker opens
(`menus/currency_select.yml`). With a single currency nobody is asked. Currencies themselves are
covered on [Economy](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/economy).

**The item leaves the hand.** Listing or auctioning takes the whole stack in the main hand at once. If
the database write fails, the stack and the fee come back.

**Delivery never drops anything.** Items handed out by these modules (a purchase, an auction won, an
expired listing, a delivered order) go through the plugin's reward store. Anything that does not fit in
the inventory, and anything owed to a player who is offline, is kept and handed over on their next
join.

**Limits come from permissions.** The configured per-player limit applies unless the player holds a
numbered permission, in which case the highest number they hold wins. A configured limit of `0` or
less means no limit, and the menus then show `∞`.

| Limit | Permission |
|---|---|
| Market listings | `exyliasurvivalcore.market.listings.<number>` |
| Running auctions | `exyliasurvivalcore.auctions.limit.<number>` |
| Open orders | `exyliasurvivalcore.orders.limit.<number>` |

**Fees and taxes are percentages.** They are rounded to two decimals and then to the currency's own
precision. Prices in the settings (`min-price`, `max-price`, `broadcast-above`) are plain numbers,
compared against the price in whichever currency it was set in.

**Announcements are in the default currency only.** A market sale or an auction won at or above
`broadcast-above` is announced to the server only when it was priced in the default currency, since
100 gems and 100 coins are not the same amount. Each player can turn market and auction announcements
off in `/settings`. See [Quality of life](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/quality-of-life).

## Market

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/market`, `/ah` | Opens the board |
| `/market sell <price> [currency]` | Lists the stack in your hand |
| `/market mine`, `/market listings` | Your own listings |
| `/marketadmin remove <id>`, `/ahadmin` | Takes one listing down and returns it to its seller |
| `/marketadmin clear <player>` | Takes down everything a player has up |

`exyliasurvivalcore.market` opens the market, `exyliasurvivalcore.market.sell` is needed to list, and
`exyliasurvivalcore.market.admin` covers `/marketadmin`. The admin commands run from a player only,
not the console.

### Listing

Hold the item and use `/market sell`, or press **Sell what you hold** on the board or on your listings.
The button asks for the currency, then for the price in chat.

A listing is refused when the material is in `blocked-materials`, when the item is damaged and
`allow-damaged-items` is off, when the price is outside `min-price` and `max-price`, or when the
player is at their limit. The **listing fee** is then charged: a percentage of the asking price. It
is not refunded when the listing expires or is cancelled, which is what stops the market being used
as free storage.

### Buying

Clicking a listing opens a confirmation showing the total, the price per item, the currency, and the
buyer's balance before and after. Confirming takes the money first and then claims the listing. If
somebody else got there first, the money goes straight back.

The seller is paid the price minus `sales-tax-percent`, online or not, and the stack is delivered to
the buyer. `allow-self-purchase` is off by default: cancelling is how you take your own listing back.

### Expiry and cancelling

A listing lasts `listing-hours`. `0` or less means it never expires. Expired listings disappear from
the board and can no longer be bought. A check every minute sends them back to the seller, who is told
if they are online. Listings that ran out while the server was down go back the same way once it
starts.

Clicking a listing in **My listings** takes it back.

### Looking inside

A shulker box or bundle with something in it is drawn with its own template. **Shift-click** it on the
board or in your listings, or use **Look inside** on the confirmation, to open it. A box inside the box
opens the same way, and **Back** climbs out one level at a time. The screen shows a copy, so nothing
in it can be taken, and looking buys nothing. `preview.container-hint` is the lore added to a container
drawn inside the preview, with `%items%`.

Every listing's lore also describes the stack: its size, how many items are inside it, its
enchantments (including those stored in an enchanted book) and, when the shop module buys that item,
what the shop pays for one.

### The board

**Search** filters by material, seller name or item name, up to 32 characters. Right-click clears it.
**Order** steps through newest first, cheapest first and most expensive first. Search and order are
forgotten when the player leaves.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-listings-per-player` | 5 | Listings one player may have up |
| `min-price` | 1.0 | Lowest asking price |
| `max-price` | -1.0 | Highest asking price. `-1` is no ceiling |
| `listing-hours` | 48 | How long a listing stays up |
| `listing-fee-percent` | 2.0 | Charged when listing, never refunded |
| `sales-tax-percent` | 5.0 | Taken from the seller's payout |
| `allow-self-purchase` | `false` | Whether a player may buy their own listing |
| `broadcast-above` | 100000.0 | Announce a sale at or above this. `-1` never |
| `blocked-materials` | `BEDROCK`, `COMMAND_BLOCK`, `BARRIER`, `STRUCTURE_BLOCK` | Never listable |
| `allow-damaged-items` | `true` | Whether damaged tools and armour may be listed |

These live under `settings` in `modules/market/config.yml`. `sounds` sets `listed`, `bought`, `sold`
and `cancelled`.

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_market_listings%` | Listings up on the server |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_market_value%` | The asking prices of every listing, added up |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_market_mine%` | Listings the reader has up |

<Callout type="note" title="Totals mix currencies">
`%exyliasurvivalcore_market_value%` and the **Worth** line in My listings add every price together,
whatever currency each one is in, and format the sum as money in the default currency. On a server
with one currency the figure is exact.
</Callout>

## Auctions

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/auction`, `/auctions` | Opens the auction house |
| `/auction sell <start> [hours] [buyout] [currency]` | Auctions the stack in your hand |
| `/auction mine`, `/auction selling` | Your own auctions |
| `/auctionadmin remove <id>`, `/auctionsadmin` | Takes one auction down |
| `/auctionadmin clear <player>` | Takes down everything a player is running |

`exyliasurvivalcore.auctions` opens the auction house, `exyliasurvivalcore.auctions.sell` is needed to
sell, and `exyliasurvivalcore.auctions.admin` covers `/auctionadmin`, which runs from a player only.

### Opening an auction

In the command, hours come before the buyout. Leaving the hours out uses `default-hours`, and a buyout
of `0` means no buyout. **Auction what you hold** asks for the currency, then opens a form with the
starting price, an optional buy-it-now price and the hours.

An auction is refused when the material is blocked, the item is damaged and `allow-damaged-items` is
off, the start is below `min-start-price`, the buyout is not above the start, the hours fall outside
`min-hours` and `max-hours`, or the player is at their limit. The fee is a percentage of the
**starting** price and is not refunded, even if nobody bids.

### Bidding

The first bid must be at least the starting price. After that, each bid must beat the top bid by
`min-bid-increment-percent` of it or by `min-bid-increment`, whichever is more.

Clicking an auction opens the bid screen:

- **Bid** places exactly the next valid bid.
- **Bid another amount** takes a typed amount, from the next valid bid up to the buyout.
- **Buy it now** appears only when the auction has a buyout.

A bid is **held**. The money leaves the bidder when the bid is placed and stays with the auction. When
somebody outbids them, it goes straight back, whether they are online or not. Raising your own top bid
works the same way: the old amount comes back and the new one is held. Nobody can bid on their own
auction.

A bid that reaches the buyout price counts as a buyout, and the buyer pays the buyout price.

### Anti-snipe

A bid placed with less than `anti-snipe-seconds` left pushes the end back to that many seconds from
the bid, so the last word does not go to whoever clicked last. `0` turns it off.

### How an auction ends

Auctions are checked every second.

| Ending | Item | Money |
|---|---|---|
| Time runs out with bids | To the top bidder | Top bid to the seller, minus `sales-tax-percent` |
| Time runs out with no bids | Back to the seller | Nothing, and the fee stays spent |
| Buyout | To the buyer | Buyout price to the seller, minus tax; the top bid goes back to its bidder |
| Seller cancels | Back to the seller | Only allowed while nobody has bid |
| Staff remove it | Back to the seller | The top bid goes back to its bidder |

Items go to the winner through the reward store, so a winner who is offline receives them on their
next join.

### The board

An auction is drawn in one of three templates in `modules/auctions/menus/auctions.yml`: `item` for one
you can bid on, `leading` for one you are winning, and `mine` for your own, which opens My auctions
when clicked. **Sort** steps through ending soonest (the default), newest, cheapest and most expensive,
by current price. **Search** works as on the market. The lore shows what is inside a shulker box or
bundle, but auctions have no look-inside screen.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-auctions-per-player` | 3 | Auctions one player may run at a time |
| `min-start-price` | 1.0 | Lowest starting price |
| `min-bid-increment-percent` | 5.0 | A new bid must beat the top one by this share of it… |
| `min-bid-increment` | 1.0 | …or by this much, whichever is more |
| `min-hours` | 1 | Shortest auction |
| `max-hours` | 72 | Longest auction |
| `default-hours` | 24 | Used when the seller does not say |
| `anti-snipe-seconds` | 30 | See above. `0` is off |
| `listing-fee-percent` | 2.0 | Of the starting price, never refunded |
| `sales-tax-percent` | 5.0 | Of the winning bid |
| `broadcast-above` | 100000.0 | Announce a win at or above this. `-1` never |
| `blocked-materials` | `BEDROCK`, `COMMAND_BLOCK`, `BARRIER`, `STRUCTURE_BLOCK` | Never auctionable |
| `allow-damaged-items` | `true` | Whether damaged items may be auctioned |

These live under `settings` in `modules/auctions/config.yml`. `sounds` sets `listed`, `bid`, `outbid`,
`won`, `sold` and `cancelled`.

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_auctions_running%` | Auctions running on the server |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_auctions_mine%` | Auctions the reader is running |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_auctions_leading%` | Auctions the reader is currently winning |

## Orders

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/orders`, `/order` | Opens the board of open orders |
| `/orders create <amount> <price> [currency]` | Orders more of what you are holding |
| `/orders mine` | Your orders, and what has arrived for them |
| `/ordersadmin cancel <id>`, `/ordersa` | Closes somebody's order and refunds them |

`exyliasurvivalcore.orders` opens orders, `exyliasurvivalcore.orders.create` is needed to place one,
and `exyliasurvivalcore.orders.admin` covers `/ordersadmin`.

### Placing an order

Hold **one** of the item you want. It becomes the order's template: sellers must deliver items that
match it exactly, with the same name, enchantments and other data. The amount is how many units you
want, from 1 to `max-amount`, and **the price is for all of them**. **Order what you hold** asks for
the amount, the currency and then the price.

The full price plus `creation-fee-percent` is taken **up front**. The fee is never refunded. Blocked
materials cannot be ordered, and neither can damaged items; orders have no setting to allow them.
Only orders still taking items count towards the limit, so a finished order waiting to be collected
does not.

### Filling an order

Anybody can sell into an open order, and the buyer does not need to be online. The board lists open
orders with the best price per unit first. Clicking one shows how many matching items you carry, how
many will be sold and what you will earn. Confirming takes them from your inventory and hotbar (not
the off-hand, armour slots or containers), up to what the order still needs, and pays you at once
minus `sales-tax-percent`.

A seller is paid for the units they deliver, as that share of the total rounded down. The last unit
settles the remainder, so the partial payments always add up to exactly the order's price. If the
order filled up or closed while you were confirming, the items it no longer needs come back to you.

`allow-self-fill` is off by default.

### Collecting, closing and expiry

Delivered items wait in the order. In **My orders**, left-clicking an order collects as many as fit in
your inventory, and the rest stay ready for later. Right-clicking closes the order: no more items are
taken, the part of the price not yet paid to sellers is refunded, and anything already delivered can
still be collected.

An order lasts `order-hours`. `0` or less means it never expires. Every minute, expired orders are
closed and refunded the same way, and the buyer is told if they are online. An order is deleted once
it is closed or complete and nothing is left to collect.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Taking items | Open |
| Complete | Everything asked for has arrived |
| Ran out | Expired, not yet closed by the minute check |
| Closed | Cancelled or expired, and refunded |

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `max-orders-per-player` | 5 | Open orders one player may have |
| `max-amount` | 2304 | Most units one order may ask for |
| `min-price` | 1.0 | Lowest total price |
| `max-price` | -1.0 | Highest total price. `-1` is no ceiling |
| `order-hours` | 72 | How long an order takes items |
| `creation-fee-percent` | 1.0 | Charged on top of the price, never refunded |
| `sales-tax-percent` | 5.0 | Taken from what a seller is paid |
| `allow-self-fill` | `false` | Whether a player may sell into their own order |
| `blocked-materials` | `BEDROCK`, `COMMAND_BLOCK`, `BARRIER`, `STRUCTURE_BLOCK`, `SPAWNER` | Never orderable |

These live under `settings` in `modules/orders/config.yml`.

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_orders_mine%` | The reader's orders still taking items |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_orders_ready%` | Items delivered to the reader and not collected yet |
| `%exyliasurvivalcore_orders_open_total%` | Orders taking items on the server |

## Across servers

Orders are built for several servers sharing one database. Every change to an order holds a lock row
that exactly one server can take at a time, and reads the order fresh from the database. Other servers
refresh their copy when an order changes. A player who hits an order another server is changing sees
*"Somebody else is using that order right now"* after about two seconds of retries.

<Callout type="warning" title="Run the market and the auction house on one server">
The market and the auction house read their rows once, when the module starts, and never hear about
changes made on another server. A listing or auction can still end only once, because ending one
deletes its row and only the server whose delete succeeds settles it. What goes wrong is everything
else: a listing put up on one server is not visible on another until that server restarts, and two
bids on the same auction from two servers each rewrite the whole row, so one bid overwrites the other
and the overwritten bid is never refunded.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="Admin commands need an id nobody is shown">
`/marketadmin remove`, `/auctionadmin remove` and `/ordersadmin cancel` take the row's id, a UUID. No
menu, message or tab completion shows it, so it has to come from the database: `sc_market_listings`,
`sc_auctions` or `sc_orders`. `clear <player>` needs no id.
</Callout>

## In the ledger and the stat counters

Every movement of money carries a reason in the economy ledger:

| Module | Reasons |
|---|---|
| Market | `market:fee`, `market:fee-refund`, `market:buy`, `market:refund`, `market:sale` |
| Auctions | `auctions:fee`, `auctions:fee-refund`, `auctions:bid`, `auctions:bid-refund`, `auctions:outbid`, `auctions:buyout`, `auctions:buyout-refund`, `auctions:sale`, `auctions:cancel-refund` |
| Orders | `orders:place`, `orders:place-refund`, `orders:fill`, `orders:refund` |

They also feed the stat counters that [missions and seasons](/docs/exyliasurvivalcore/missions-and-seasons)
measure:

| Module | Counters |
|---|---|
| Market | `market.listed`, `market.bought`, `market.spent`, `market.<currency>.spent`, `market.sold`, `market.earned`, `market.<currency>.earned` |
| Auctions | `auctions.listed`, `auctions.bids`, `auctions.won`, `auctions.spent`, `auctions.sold`, `auctions.earned` |
| Orders | `orders.placed`, `orders.filled`, `orders.earned`, `orders.received` |
