You tell ExyliaLobby which worlds and boxes are "the lobby". The lobby rules, hotbar, tab list and everything else apply only to players inside one of them.

## Before you start

Know where your lobby is: a whole world (a dedicated hub) or a part of a world (the spawn of a survival server). On a dedicated hub you only need to add its world.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the areas screen">
    Run `/lobbyadmin` and click **Areas**. It lists every lobby area; the first time it shows the world `world`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a whole world">
    Stand in the world and click **Add this world**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or add a box">
    Click **Add box** and follow the chat: mark the two corners. Use this for a hub inside another world.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove one">
    Right-click an area in the list to remove it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Walk out of the area, or `/tp` to a world that is not listed. Your hotbar and tab list disappear and you get back whatever the lobby had changed (game mode, effects, items). Walk back in and they return.

## Going further

- **WorldGuard region.** Needs WorldGuard. Add the region id under `worldguard-regions` in `config.yml` and run `/lobbyadmin reload`; the in-game screen edits worlds and boxes only.
- **Several areas.** Add as many as you need; a player is in the lobby while inside any of them.
- **Leaving.** Leaving an area gives every change back, so lobby effects never stick to a player.

<Details title="Technical details">

`config.yml` keeps the areas under `areas`:

```yaml
areas:
  worlds:
    - world
  boxes:
    spawn-box:
      world: survival
      min: '-50 0 -50'
      max: '50 255 50'
  worldguard-regions:
    - lobby
```

| Key | What it is |
|---|---|
| `worlds` | Whole worlds that are lobby. Default `world`. |
| `boxes` | Cuboids inside other worlds. Each has a `world` and two corners, `min` and `max`, as `'x y z'`. |
| `worldguard-regions` | WorldGuard region ids that count as lobby. |

Worlds and boxes are ExyliaLib regions, so crossing an edge, a teleport, a world change and Folia's unreported moves are all caught. WorldGuard regions have no events and are checked twice a second, only while some are configured.

Entering fires a [`LobbyEnterEvent`](/docs/exylialobby/api) and every module's `enter` runs; leaving fires a `LobbyLeaveEvent` and every module gives back what it changed. Editing the areas, a reload, or switching a module on or off moves everyone affected through the same path.

</Details>
