By the end of this page you have a lobby where players land on your spawn, cannot break anything and can pick a server from the hotbar. It takes about five minutes.

## What a fresh server already does

With nothing edited, fourteen of the sixteen modules are on, and the lobby is the world named `world`. Inside it players:

- Are sent to spawn when they join, and back to it when they fall under Y -70 or die.
- Are put in Adventure mode with full health and hunger, at noon with clear weather.
- Cannot build, break, open containers, drop or pick up items, or take damage from the causes the protection module lists.
- Get the lobby hotbar: Servers (slot 0), Games (1), Parkour (2), Visibility (6), Gadgets (7) and Settings (8).
- See a tab header and footer and a chat announcement every five minutes.

Chat and onboarding are off by default.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the hub">
    Run `/lobbyadmin`. You see four cards: **Modules**, **Areas**, **Settings** and **Reload**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the lobby">
    Open **Areas** and add the world you stand in, a box, or a WorldGuard region. The lobby rules only apply inside these areas. See [Define the lobby areas](/docs/exylialobby/areas). On a dedicated hub, the world is all you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the spawn">
    Stand where players should appear. In **Modules**, open the **Spawn** card and use **Set spawn**. One point is kept per world.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your servers">
    Open the selector module's settings and list your servers (`servers`) and, if you have several lobbies, `lobbies`. The key of each entry is only a name. `server` is the id your proxy knows. See [Add servers and show live data](/docs/exylialobby/selector-and-hud).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it as a player">
    Join with a player account without operator rights. You should land on the spawn, be unable to break blocks, and see the hotbar. Click **Servers** to confirm your entries and counts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="You cannot build either">
Protection also applies to admins. Run `/lobbyadmin build` (permission `exylialobby.build`) to switch yourself in and out of build mode, and switch it off when you finish.
</Callout>

## Switch modules and reload

The **Modules** card shows one card per module. Click one to switch it live; the choice is written to `config.yml`. From the console use `/lobbyadmin module <id> [on|off]`. `/lobbyadmin reload` reads the configuration again, and the menus you edit in game save themselves.
