Players pick a harmless toy from a menu and use it with a click: a grappling hook, an ender ride, a paint blaster and four more. You choose which are available and who can use them.

## Before you start

The Gadgets module is on by default. The hotbar button **Gadgets** and the command `/gadgets` open the menu.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Run `/gadgets` or click **Gadgets** in the hotbar. The menu lists the gadgets the player may use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Equip one">
    Click a gadget. It goes into one hotbar slot (slot 5 by default). Right-click it to use it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it away">
    Open the menu again and click the **Your gadget** card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Equip the Grappling Hook and right-click a wall: you are pulled to it. Nothing breaks and nobody is hurt.

## Going further

- **Give a gadget to a rank.** Grant `exylialobby.gadget.<id>` in your permissions plugin. Ender Ride (`ender`) and Trampoline (`trampoline`) are off for everyone until you grant them; the other five are open to everyone.
- **Change a cooldown.** Run `/lobbyadmin`, click **Settings**, then **Gadgets**: one cooldown per gadget, and the **View radius** within which players see paint and trampolines.
- **Turn one off.** In `modules/gadgets/config.yml` set its `enabled` to `false`, then run `/lobbyadmin reload`. It disappears from the menu.

| Gadget | Use |
|---|---|
| Grappling Hook | Right-click a wall or the floor: it pulls you there. |
| Ender Ride | Right-click: ride your own pearl through the air and land safely. |
| Paint Blaster | Right-click: shots colour the ground for a few seconds. |
| Knockback Stick | Hit a player: pushes players who allow it, never hurts. |
| Leap Feather | Right-click: boost where you look, landing softly. |
| Snowball Cannon | Right-click: a burst of snowballs that nudge players who allow it. |
| Trampoline | Right-click: a bouncy pad under your feet for a few seconds. |

<Details title="Settings">

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `command` | `[gadgets]` | Labels of the command that opens the menu. Empty removes it. |
| `slot` | `5` | The hotbar slot (0-8) an equipped gadget is placed in. The hotbar module keeps it free of buttons. |
| `view-radius` | `32` | Players within this many blocks see paint and trampolines. |
| `paint-colors` | seven concretes | The blocks the paint blaster colours with, one per shot. |
| `gadgets` | all seven | One entry per gadget id. |

Each gadget has `enabled` (off hides it and refuses it), `permission` (empty is everyone), `cooldown`
(seconds between uses, decimals allowed: `0.5` is half a second), `material` (the item it is carried as), `power` (pull, launch, push or bounce
strength) and `seconds` (how long the paint or trampoline lasts; unused by the others).

</Details>

<Details title="Full-screen menu">

In ExyliaLib's `SCREEN` menu style, the gadgets list opens as a full screen (`screens/gadgets.html`) <Since v="1.7.0" />, with
its words in `lang/<code>/screens/gadgets.yml`. It reads the same data and actions as the chest menu, which Bedrock and
older clients still get. Put your own rules in `screens/custom.css`; `/exylialib screens check` lists anything wrong.

</Details>
