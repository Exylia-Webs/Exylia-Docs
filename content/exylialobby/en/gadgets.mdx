Gadgets (`gadgets`) are toys. `/gadgets` (or the hotbar button) opens a menu; the chosen gadget is placed in
one hotbar slot and used with a click.

| Gadget | Use | Permission (default) |
|---|---|---|
| Grappling Hook (`grapple`) | Right-click: cast at a wall or the floor, it pulls you there. | `exylialobby.gadget.grapple` (everyone) |
| Ender Ride (`ender`) | Right-click: ride your own pearl through the air and land safely. | `exylialobby.gadget.ender` (nobody) |
| Paint Blaster (`paint`) | Right-click: shots colour the ground for a few seconds. Nothing breaks. | `exylialobby.gadget.paint` (everyone) |
| Knockback Stick (`knockback`) | Hit a player: pushes players who allow it. It never hurts. | `exylialobby.gadget.knockback` (everyone) |
| Leap Feather (`leap`) | Right-click: boost where you look, landing softly. | `exylialobby.gadget.leap` (everyone) |
| Snowball Cannon (`snowball`) | Right-click: a burst of snowballs that nudge players who allow it. | `exylialobby.gadget.snowball` (everyone) |
| Trampoline (`trampoline`) | Right-click: a bouncy pad under your feet for a few seconds. | `exylialobby.gadget.trampoline` (nobody) |

## Settings

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `command` | `[gadgets]` | Labels of the command that opens the menu. Empty removes it. |
| `slot` | `5` | The hotbar slot (0-8) an equipped gadget is placed in. The hotbar module keeps it free of buttons. |
| `view-radius` | `32` | Players within this many blocks see paint and trampolines. |
| `paint-colors` | seven concretes | The blocks the paint blaster colours with, one per shot. |
| `gadgets` | all seven | One entry per gadget id. |

Each gadget has `enabled` (off hides it and refuses it), `permission` (empty is everyone), `cooldown`
(seconds between uses, decimals allowed: `0.5` is half a second), `material` (the item it is carried as), `power` (pull, launch, push or bounce
strength) and `seconds` (how long the paint or trampoline lasts; unused by the others).

## Screen

In ExyliaLib's `SCREEN` menu style, the gadget list opens as a full screen (`screens/gadgets.html`), with its words in
`lang/<code>/screens/gadgets.yml`. It reads the same data and actions as the chest menu, which Bedrock and older
clients still get. Put your own rules in `screens/custom.css`; `/exylialib screens check` lists anything wrong.
