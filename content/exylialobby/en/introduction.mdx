ExyliaLobby is a complete lobby for Paper 1.21.1+ and Folia 1.21.4+. It is free and open source,
published under the GNU General Public License v3.0 on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaLobby/releases).

Every part is a **module**. Sixteen of them ship, each one switches on or off live from `/lobbyadmin`
without a restart, and a module that is off registers nothing and costs nothing. Everything an admin
tunes (settings, hotbar buttons, launch pads, courses, servers, triggers) is edited in game.

The lobby rules apply only inside the **lobby areas** you define: whole worlds, boxes inside other worlds,
or WorldGuard regions. A player who leaves them gets back exactly what the lobby changed (game mode,
hotbar, effects, experience bar).
