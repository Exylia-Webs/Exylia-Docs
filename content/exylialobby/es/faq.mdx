## Why is the chat module not doing anything?

`chat` and `onboarding` are off by default. Switch them on in `/lobbyadmin` → Modules.

## How do I add a lobby area?

`/lobbyadmin` → Areas. The comment in `config.yml` mentions `/lobbyadmin area`, but areas are managed from
that screen. See [Lobby areas](/docs/exylialobby/areas).

## I cannot build in the lobby

Protection applies to everyone, admins included. Run `/lobbyadmin build` (`exylialobby.build`) to toggle
build mode.

## The hotbar buttons do not show

- ExyliaPracticeCore installed: its lobby hotbar stays in charge. Set `force: true` in the hotbar module to
  draw this one.
- PacketEvents missing: buttons are drawn as an overlay and need it.
- The player is not inside a lobby area.
- A button with `requires` is drawn only while that module is on.

## Everyone can see everyone

Visibility needs PacketEvents. Without it every call does nothing.

## The selector shows no player counts

Counts and status come from the proxy through ExyliaLib. Check the network link in ExyliaLib, and that
`server` matches the id the proxy uses.

## A player lost their items or effects after leaving the lobby

They should not: whatever a module changes is given back when the player leaves, quits or the module is
switched off, and the state module keeps the original on the player so a crash does not lose it. If it
happens, switch on `debug` in `config.yml` and report the log.

## Endless parkour does nothing

It needs PacketEvents, an endless area (`/lobbyadmin parkour endlessarea <name>`) and the mode enabled under
`modes`.

## Where is player data kept, and how do I reset it?

Parkour records and stats and PvP stats are in ExyliaLib's database. `/exylialib resetplayer` and
`/exylialib wipe` cover them; the plugin has no wipe command of its own.

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes, Folia 1.21.4+ and Paper 1.21.1+.
