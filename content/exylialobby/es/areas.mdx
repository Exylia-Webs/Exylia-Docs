---
title: "Lobby areas"
description: "Where the lobby is: whole worlds, boxes inside other worlds and WorldGuard regions."
---

The lobby rules, the hotbar, the sidebar and everything else apply only to players **inside a lobby
area**. The areas are in `config.yml` under `areas`, and the **Areas** card of `/lobbyadmin` edits them in
game.

```yaml
areas:
  worlds:
    - world
  boxes:
    spawn-box:
      world: survival
      min: '-50 0 -50'
      max: '50 255 50'
  worldguard-regions:
    - lobby
```

| Key | What it is |
|---|---|
| `worlds` | Whole worlds that are lobby. On a dedicated hub, list its world here. Default `world`. |
| `boxes` | Cuboids inside other worlds, such as the spawn of a survival server. Each has a `world` and two corners, `min` and `max`, as `'x y z'`. |
| `worldguard-regions` | WorldGuard region ids that count as lobby. Needs WorldGuard. |

A player is in the lobby while inside any of them.

## How crossings are noticed

Worlds and boxes are ExyliaLib regions, so walking across an edge, a teleport, a world change and Folia's
unreported moves are all caught. WorldGuard regions have no events and are checked twice a second, and only
while some are configured.

Entering fires a [`LobbyEnterEvent`](/docs/exylialobby/api) and every module's `enter` runs; leaving fires
a `LobbyLeaveEvent` and every module gives back what it changed. Editing the areas, a reload, or switching a
module on or off moves everyone affected through the same path, so nobody is left holding lobby state.
