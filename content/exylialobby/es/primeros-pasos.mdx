## What a fresh server does

With nothing edited, fourteen of the sixteen modules are on. The lobby is the world named `world`.
Players inside it:

- Are sent to spawn when they join, and back to it when they fall under Y -70 or die.
- Are put in Adventure mode with full health and hunger, and the time fixed at noon with clear weather.
- Cannot build, break, open containers, drop or pick up items, or take damage from the causes listed in
  the protection module.
- Get the lobby hotbar: Servers (slot 0), Games (1), Parkour (2), Visibility (6), Gadgets (7) and Settings (8).
- See a sidebar, a tab header and footer, a rotating boss bar and a chat announcement every five minutes.

**Chat** and **onboarding** are off by default.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the lobby">
    In `/lobbyadmin`, open **Areas** and add the world, a box or a WorldGuard region. See
    [Lobby areas](/docs/exylialobby/areas). On a dedicated hub, put its world in `areas.worlds`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the spawn">
    Stand where players should appear and use the spawn card in `/lobbyadmin`. A point is kept per world.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your servers">
    List them in the selector module (`servers`, and `lobbies` for the lobby selector). The key of each
    entry is only a name; `server` is the id the proxy knows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching modules

`/lobbyadmin` → **Modules** shows one card per module. Click to switch it live; the choice is written to
`config.yml`. The console form is `/lobbyadmin module <id> [on|off]`. See [Modules](/docs/exylialobby/modulos).

## Building in the lobby

Protection also applies to admins. Run `/lobbyadmin build` (permission `exylialobby.build`) to switch
yourself in and out of build mode.

## Reloading

`/lobbyadmin reload` reads the configuration again. The menus you edit in game save themselves.
