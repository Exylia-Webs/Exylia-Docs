On its first start the plugin writes six screens into `plugins/ExyliaScreens/screens/`, ready to open. They are
meant to be used, not only read: change the words, items and commands and they are your server's menus. Each
screen has its own `<id>.css` next to it, and its words in `lang/<code>/screens/<id>.yml` for every language.

<Callout type="note" title="Written once">
Each example is copied once, and `.examples-written` lists what was written. A file already there is never replaced, and an example added by an update still reaches a server that has the others. To get a deleted one back, delete its line from `.examples-written` and restart. The examples are written in the server's language when the plugin has it (see [Translations](/docs/exyliascreens/translations)).
</Callout>

## Server selector: `/play`

`servers.html` · also `/servers`, `/modes`

Three mode cards, each with its icon, a short description, a status block and a button. A card reads its server
live from the proxy: `online('survival')` says whether it is up and `players('survival')` how many are on it,
refreshed every few seconds. The top right shows the whole network, `players()`. A click sends the player there
with `[SERVER] survival`. A server that is down refuses with a shake and a toast instead of sending anyone into a
dead server. The third card is marked `disabled`: drawn muted, it says "Coming soon".

**Make it yours.** Each card picks its colour with one line, `style="--tone: {success}"`, and the card, its name
and its button follow it (the rules are in `servers.css`). To add a mode, copy a `<card>`, change the server name
in `require`, `action` and the two `players`/`online` calls, and pick a tone. Discord and Store open a toast
whose link the player clicks; put your own addresses in.

**Motion.** The title types itself out, the cards arrive one after another (`stagger`), each icon floats, the
"Online" dot beats and the arrow on each button nudges.

<Callout type="note" title="Player counts need the proxy bridge">
`players('name')` and `online('name')` come from ExyliaLib's proxy module, which needs ExyliaProxyUtils 2.2.0 or
newer on the proxy. Without it they read 0 and offline, and `players()` counts this server only.
</Callout>

## Daily rewards: `/rewards`

`rewards.html` · also `/daily`

Seven day cards. Today's card glows and pulses; claiming it runs its console command, adds one to the streak and
starts a 24 hour wait. Claimed days show a tick, later days a dot, and day 7 starts the week again. Under the
cards, a line counts down to the next reward and a bar fills with the streak.

It is built from three pieces you can reuse in any screen:

- `data('streak')`, kept per player across restarts with `[DATA] set streak ...` (see
  [Player data](/docs/exyliascreens/player-data)).
- `cooldown="daily 24h"` on the card, which refuses a second claim until the time is up, and `cooldown('daily')`,
  the seconds left, shown with `fmt.time(...)`. A wait of 5 minutes or more survives restarts.
- A `<template name="day">`, so each day is one line at the bottom of the file:

```html
<day n="5" item="golden_apple" reward="2 Golden apples" command="give %player_name% golden_apple 2"/>
```

**Make it yours.** Change each day's `item`, `reward` (its words) and `command`. The streak only grows: a missed
day does not reset it.

**Motion.** The cards drop in one by one with a `bounce`, today's card pulses, day 7's item floats, a claim pops
the card and an early click shakes it.

## Welcome tour: `/welcome`

`welcome.html` · also `/tour`

Four short pages for new players, each a picture and a paragraph. A and D, the buttons or the dots at the top
turn the pages, and the last one closes the tour and remembers it with `[DATA] set welcomed true`, so an open
item or a join command can skip players who saw it (`data('welcomed')`).

**Make it yours.** Every page is one `<case>` inside the `<switch>`: change its item, title and text. To add a
page, copy a case, add a `<tab>` to the dots and raise the `4`s.

**Motion.** Pages slide in and out; the title types itself out and the paragraph rises in after it
(`stagger` with `text-reveal`); the picture floats; the last button pulses.

## Hub, profile and shop

`hub.html` (`/hub`, `/warps`), `profile.html` (`/profile`, `/me`) and `shop.html` (`/shop`) link to each other.

- **Hub**: the player's face, warps and servers as tiles, and a VIP tile that only opens for a permission and
  shakes for everyone else.
- **Profile**: the player's turning body and their balance, level, world and rank, all read live.
- **Shop**: four items paid through the server's economy. A price turns red when the player cannot afford it, a
  click they cannot pay shakes the card, a purchase pops it, and the sword asks first.

The hub and shop have chest fallbacks (`menus/hub_chest.yml`, `menus/shop_chest.yml`) for players who chose chest
menus; see [Opening and fallbacks](/docs/exyliascreens/opening-and-fallbacks).
