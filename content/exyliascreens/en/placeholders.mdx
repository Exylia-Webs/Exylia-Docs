---
title: "Placeholders"
description: "The %exyliascreens_...% placeholder, through PlaceholderAPI and ExyliaLib."
---

Identifier `exyliascreens`. It resolves for the player viewing.

| Placeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| `%exyliascreens_data_<key>%` | The value of `<key>` the menus remembered for the player, or empty when there is none. See [Player data](/docs/exyliascreens/player-data). |

Menus also read any other placeholder PlaceholderAPI or ExyliaLib knows (`%player_name%`, `%exyliaffa_total_players%`...) in any text, and `data('key')` in expressions.
