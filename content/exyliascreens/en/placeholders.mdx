ExyliaScreens adds one placeholder, under the identifier `exyliascreens`. It resolves for the player looking at it, and works in any text a menu shows. Outside menus (a scoreboard plugin, a chat format) it needs [PlaceholderAPI](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/placeholderapi.6245/).

<Placeholders />

Values are saved with `[DATA] set <key> <value>` and read with `data('key')` in expressions. See [Player data](/docs/exyliascreens/player-data).

## Placeholders from other plugins

Menus, the HUD, scoreboards and boss bars read any placeholder PlaceholderAPI or ExyliaLib knows (`%player_name%`, `%server_online%`). The `%player_...%` ones need PlaceholderAPI with its Player expansion.

`%player_hud%` comes from ExyliaLib, not from this plugin: it is true when a HUD is drawn for the player, so other plugins can show something else to players without one.
