This walk-through uses the example files as they are generated. Join as an operator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the hub">
    `/hub` opens the example screen: a cursor, your face and buttons to Profile and Shop. Move the mouse, click, and press Shift to close it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See what is loaded">
    `/screens list` shows every menu, its command and its form. `hub` shows as `screen » hub_chest`: the form, then its fallback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the fallback">
    `/screens open hub_chest` opens the chest version of the same hub, the one a client that cannot draw screens gets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your own">
    Create `plugins/ExyliaScreens/screens/rules.html` with the file below. Save it: it is read within two seconds, no reload needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    `/screens check` lists anything it does not understand, with `file:line:col`. Run it after every edit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```html
<screen command="rules" aliases="r">
  <panel>
    <h1>{primary}Rules</h1>
    <p>Be kind. No cheating. Have fun.</p>
    <button action="close">Got it</button>
  </panel>
</screen>
```

`/rules` and `/r` now open it. Shift closes any screen, and the `close` and `back` actions do too.
