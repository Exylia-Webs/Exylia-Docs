---
title: "Configuration"
description: "Every key of config.yml."
---

`plugins/ExyliaScreens/config.yml` has four settings. `/screens reload` applies them.

| Key | Default | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `language` | `default` | The language of the plugin's messages. `default` follows ExyliaLib's `config.yml`; a code such as `en` or `es` sets this plugin alone, and is a folder under `lang/` you can edit. |
| `follow-player-style` | `true` | Whether a player who chose chest windows in `/settings` gets a screen or 3D menu's fallback when it has one. Off: they get the form the owner wrote, as long as their client can draw it. |
| `open-on-join` | empty | The id of a menu opened for every player who joins, or empty for none. |
| `open-on-join-delay` | `40` | Ticks after joining before it opens (20 = one second), so the world has loaded. |

Everything else is files: `screens/`, `menus/`, `spatial/`, `open-items.yml`, `lang/` and `messages.yml`. The look of every screen can also be changed for all plugins in
`plugins/ExyliaLib/screens/theme.css`, and the player-facing menu style options belong to ExyliaLib's `screens.yml`.
