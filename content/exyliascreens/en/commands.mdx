ExyliaScreens has one staff command: `/screensadmin`, also typed as `/screens` or `/exyliascreens`. Every subcommand needs [`exyliascreens.admin`](/docs/exyliascreens/permissions). Players open menus with the commands the menus name.

## Commands your menus install

Every menu's `command` and `aliases` become real commands when the files load and on reload (the example `hub` screen gives `/hub` and `/warps`). They are for players only, check the menu's `permission` and `require`, and take the words listed in its `args`. `/screensadmin list` shows them all.
