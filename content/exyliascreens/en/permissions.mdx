Players need no permission to open a menu unless you give that menu one. Only `/screensadmin` is locked, to operators.

## All permissions

<Permissions />

To give it to a rank with LuckPerms: `/lp group admin permission set exyliascreens.admin true`.

## Lock one menu behind a rank

A menu's own permission is whatever its `permission` attribute says, with any node you choose (for example `server.vip`). The plugin does not declare these nodes, so create them in your permission plugin. Without a `permission`, everybody can open the menu.

A player without it gets a no-permission message. For finer checks inside a menu, `require` and `deny` accept `perm('node')`. See [Actions and requirements](/docs/exyliascreens/actions-and-requirements).

The same goes for `scoreboards.yml`, `hud.yml` and `bossbars.yml` entries: their `permission` key shows an entry only to players who hold it.
