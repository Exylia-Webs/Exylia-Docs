| Node | Default | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| `exyliascreens.admin` | op | Every `/screensadmin` subcommand: list, open, check, reload and the importers. Also the web editor for `open-items.yml`. |

## A menu's own permission

A menu's permission is whatever its `permission` says, in any node you choose. Without one, everybody can open it. The plugin does not declare these nodes. A player without it gets a no-permission
message, and `require` and `deny` can add a finer check with `perm('node')`.
