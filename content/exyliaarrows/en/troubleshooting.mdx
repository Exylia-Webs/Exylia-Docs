Find your symptom, then follow the fix.

## The plugin does not start

The console names what is missing. `packetevents` is required, and ExyliaLib is installed by the loader on the first start if it is absent. See [Install ExyliaArrows](/docs/exyliaarrows/installation).

## Nothing plays when a player shoots

Check in this order:

1. **Is the projectile allowed?** By default only arrows, spectral arrows and tridents carry an effect (`projectiles.all-types` is `false`). Add types to `projectiles.default-allowed-types` or set `all-types` to `true`.
2. **Is there an effect to play?** With `behaviour.mode: bow` the menu choice is never read, and with `player` a bound bow is never read. See [Bind effects to bows](/docs/exyliaarrows/modes-and-tokens).
3. **Does the player still own it?** A choice they no longer have the permission for is taken off at the shot. Give the permission back and they pick it again.
4. **Are they in a disabled world?** `disabled-worlds` in `config.yml` stops every effect there.
5. **Is the player set to see nothing?** A player on the visibility mode "None" sees no effects, not even their own.
6. **Is a WorldGuard region denying it?** ExyliaLib registers the flag `arrows-effects`. Denied in a region, launch, trail and impact do not play there.

## The trail is missing but the impact plays

The server hit `trail.max-active` (200 trails at once, 10 per player). Shots over the limit still play their launch and impact and just leave no trail. Raise the numbers, or set them to `0` for no limit. A trail also stops after `trail.max-duration` seconds (10 by default).

## The menu says it is disabled

`behaviour.mode` is `bow`. On that server the menu would change nothing, so it refuses to open. Set the mode to `player` or `both`.

## Previews say they are not set up

No preview stage exists yet. Stand where previews should play and run `/arrowsadmin preview location`. There is no default on purpose, because a guessed position could put the player inside terrain.

## An effect I bound to a bow stopped playing

The bow stores the effect's key. If you renamed the key in `effects.yml`, the bow now names nothing. Renaming a key also changes its permission, so rename effects only when you must. Restore the old key, or bind the bow again with `/arrowsadmin bow bind <effect>`.

## A player says their effect disappeared

Their permission went away, usually because a rank expired. An effect won in the crate or bought in the shop is saved on the player and stays. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarrows/permissions).

## Locked effects show no price

The shop needs `shop.enabled`, a price (the effect's own `price` or its rarity in `shop.tier-prices`), an economy plugin (ExyliaEconomy or Vault) and the player holding `exyliarrows.buy`. With any of them missing the effect shows as locked without a price.

## The menus are in the wrong language

`language` in `config.yml` picks the folder under `lang/`. `default` follows ExyliaLib's own language. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaarrows/configuration#language).

## Effects lag during big fights

Every particle and display line is a packet per viewer. Check how many lines an effect has, lower `count:` on its particle lines, and keep `trail.max-active` low enough for your server.
