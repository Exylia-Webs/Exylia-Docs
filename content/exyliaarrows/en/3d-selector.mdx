title: "3D selector"
description: "The effect selector on the centre stage: an arc of effects in the world, each one flown in front of the player before they wear it."
---

The 3D selector is the effect menu drawn in the world instead of in an inventory. An arc of effects
stands in front of the player, the one they look at is named above it, and clicking one fires it on a
small stage in the middle: the launch, the trail along its flight and the impact where it lands. They
see the effect before they wear it, and nothing is kept until they press **EQUIP**.

It is built from display entities and packets only: no resource pack, nothing other players see.

## Opening it

| Way | What it takes |
|---|---|
| `/arrows` | `menu.style: SPATIAL` in `config.yml`. With `CLASSIC` (the default) it opens [the classic menu](/docs/exyliaarrows/menu). |
| `/arrows 3d` | Nothing: it always opens the 3D one. Alias: `spatial`. |
| A placed selector | `/arrowsadmin spatial place` sets one down where you stand. Its hologram opens it; clicking walks the player onto the spot. |
| `/arrowsadmin menu open <player>` | Opens whichever `menu.style` picks, for someone else. |

The same checks run every way in: the menu is off on a `bow` server (`menu-disabled`), and an effect
the player wears without the rank it needs is taken off first, as the classic menu does.

<Callout type="note" title="Older clients get the classic menu">
A player whose client is older than 1.21.4 (joining through ViaBackwards) cannot draw it. Every way of
opening it opens the classic menu for them instead, so `menu.style: SPATIAL` is safe on a server that
lets old clients in.
</Callout>

## Using it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look and click">
    Looking at an effect names it, with its rarity and whether it is worn, owned, for sale or locked.
    Resting on one shows a short description. Clicking one tries it: it plays on the stage at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Equip or buy">
    The one filled button is the next step: **EQUIP** for an effect they own, **BUY** with its price
    for one the [shop](/docs/exyliaarrows/configuration#shop) sells, then **CONFIRM** to pay. A
    locked effect that is not for sale says how it is had instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave">
    Sneaking, or **LEAVE**, closes it. Closing on an effect tried but not equipped says
    `spatial.discarded` and keeps what they had.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The tabs across the top filter by category, with **All** and the favourites (★) beside them; the
mouse wheel pages the arc. The icon row under the button stars the effect, takes the worn one off,
reverts what was tried, turns random on or off, replays the preview, opens the crate (with the keys
held), shows the tour again, and leaves.

While it is open the player is held still and nobody else is drawn, so the stage is theirs alone.

The rules are the classic menu's: effects they own come first, `behaviour.hide-without-permission`
hides locked ones (except on the favourites tab), and the [feature
nodes](/docs/exyliaarrows/permissions#features-as-rank-perks) apply. A player without
`exyliarrows.buy` sees no price; random, favourites and the crate answer `feature-locked` without
theirs.

## The guided tour

The first time a player opens it, a short tour points at the shelf of effects, the preview, the
button and the icons. **? Help** shows it again, and `/arrowsadmin spatial tour <player>` shows it on
their next open.

## Setting it up

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/arrowsadmin spatial place` | Sets a fixed selector down where you stand, facing the way you face. |
| `/arrowsadmin spatial remove` | Removes the one nearest you, up to four blocks away. |
| `/arrowsadmin spatial edit` | Opens it to move its pieces: aim at one, click, then the mouse wheel moves it. |
| `/arrowsadmin spatial tour <player>` | Shows the tour to a player again. |

Where every piece stands is `spatial/effects.yml`: the board's distance from the eyes, its height, the
tabs, the title, the description, the button, the icons and the stage. The file only places things.
Every word on it is under `spatial` in `lang/<code>/messages.yml`, so it follows the server's language
like the rest — see [Configuration](/docs/exyliaarrows/configuration#messagesyml). When a release
changes the shipped layout it raises `menu-version`, and a copy you edited is offered the new one in
`/exylialib updates` rather than overwritten.

`/arrowsadmin reload` re-reads the layout, and an open selector shows the new catalogue, prices and
words at once.

<Callout type="tip" title="The crate in 3D too">
`crate.opening: SPATIAL` gives the crate the same treatment: a crate in front of the player, a reel
slowing onto the prize, and the effect won played on a mannequin. See [The opening in
3D](/docs/exyliaarrows/crate#the-opening-in-3d).
</Callout>
