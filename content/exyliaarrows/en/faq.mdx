Something not working? Start with [Common problems](/docs/exyliaarrows/troubleshooting). The answers below are about how ExyliaArrows behaves.

## Does losing a permission break a bow?

No. A bound bow plays its effect for whoever holds it, and binding a token asks for no permission: holding the token is the proof. That is what makes a bound bow worth trading. See [Bind effects to bows](/docs/exyliaarrows/modes-and-tokens).

## Can players see each other's effects?

That is each viewer's own choice: `ALL`, `NONE`, `SELF_ONLY` or `OTHERS_ONLY`, set with `/arrows visibility` or from the menu. `particles.default-visibility` in `config.yml` is what new players start with (`ALL`).

## Are the bodies real entities?

No. A `[NPC]` line draws a display body: nothing can hit it, loot it or collide with it, and it disappears when its `life` runs out. See [Sequences](/docs/exyliaarrows/sequences#npc-the-body).

## Does the bow lore get written to the item?

Never. The lines are drawn on packets only, so any plugin reading that bow finds the item it always had, and renaming an effect updates every bow on the server at once. You can turn them off with `bow-lore.enabled`.

## Can I write my own effects?

Yes, that is what `effects.yml` is for. Every effect is a list of lines, and the shapes and options are in [Sequences](/docs/exyliaarrows/sequences). A line that cannot be read is reported on the console and skipped, and the rest of the effect still plays. You can also ask an AI to write one: see [Create effects with AI](/docs/exyliaarrows/create-with-ai).

## Can a region turn effects off?

Yes. ExyliaLib registers a WorldGuard flag called `arrows-effects`. Deny it in a region and launch, trail and impact effects stop there. Without WorldGuard the flag does nothing. To switch effects off in whole worlds, use `disabled-worlds` in [Configuration](/docs/exyliaarrows/configuration#disabled-worlds).

## Does an effect from the crate disappear when a rank runs out?

No. An effect won from the crate or bought from the menu is saved on the player's own account, not granted as a permission, so a rank ending does not remove it. Only `/arrowsadmin unlocks revoke <player> <effect>` takes one back, or ExyliaLib's [`/exylialib resetplayer` and `/exylialib wipe`](/docs/exylialib/commands#resetting-a-player), which clear it with the rest of the player's data. An effect that came from a permission node does go with the node. See [Set up the crate](/docs/exyliaarrows/crate#what-a-win-is-worth) and [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarrows/permissions).

## Can I sell effects?

Yes, in two ways. Sell the permission `exyliarrows.effect.<id>` (or a whole category or rarity) through your rank plugin, or turn on the in-game shop so players pay with currency from the menu. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliaarrows/permissions#sell-effects) and [Buying with currency](/docs/exyliaarrows/crate#buying-with-currency).

## Can I change the language of the menus?

Yes. `language` in `config.yml` picks the folder under `lang/`: `default` follows ExyliaLib, or set `en`, `es`, `pt`, `fr`, `ru` or `tr`. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaarrows/configuration#language) and [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

## Does it work on Folia?

Yes. `plugin.yml` declares `folia-supported: true`. ExyliaArrows needs Paper or Folia with the 1.21 API and the PacketEvents plugin; see [Install ExyliaArrows](/docs/exyliaarrows/installation) for the requirements.
