`/arrows` opens a window of 54 slots titled **ARROW EFFECTS**: category tabs down the left, the effects of the open tab in a grid, and buttons along the top and bottom. By the end of this page you know what each button does and how to change the layout, hide locked effects or sell them.

This is the classic menu, which `/arrows` opens while `menu.style` is `CLASSIC` (the default). With `SPATIAL` it opens the [3D selector](/docs/exyliaarrows/3d-selector) instead, and a player whose client is older than 1.21.4 still gets this screen.

<Callout type="note" title="It refuses to open in bow mode">
With `behaviour.mode: bow` nothing on this screen would ever be read, so the menu says it is disabled instead of opening a window that changes nothing. See [Bind effects to bows](/docs/exyliaarrows/modes-and-tokens).
</Callout>

## What players see

- **Tabs.** The first tab is always the player's **Favourites**, then one tab per category.
- **Grid.** The effects of the open tab. Effects the player may use come first, then the locked ones.
- **Top row.** **Your effect** (click to take the effect off), **Filter** (switches between every effect and only the ones the player owns) and **Crate** (opens [the crate](/docs/exyliaarrows/crate)).
- **Bottom row.** **Particle visibility** (steps through who they see), **Close** and **Random effect** (every shot plays a different effect from the ones they own).

| Click on an effect | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left | Wears the effect, or takes it off if it is already worn. On a locked effect that is for sale, buys it. |
| Right | Previews it: the player is moved to the preview stage, the effect plays for them alone and they are put back where they were. Locked effects can be previewed too. |
| Shift | Stars it as a favourite, or removes the star. |

## Before you start

Set the preview stage with `/arrowsadmin preview location`, or right-click will say previews are not set up. See [Your first effect](/docs/exyliaarrows/first-steps#set-the-preview-stage).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu file">
    Open `plugins/ExyliaArrows/lang/<code>/menus/effects.yml`, where `<code>` is your `language` in `config.yml`. The file is yours to restyle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change what you want">
    Edit titles, icons, the names of the buttons or the slots in the lists below. Each button is an entry with a `slot`, a material, a name and actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    Run `/arrowsadmin reload`, then `/arrows`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Open `/arrows`. Your change is on screen. If an entry is wrong, the console says which. A release only adds what is missing in your file; when it has to change the layout it raises `menu-version` at the top of the file, replaces it and keeps your previous one beside it.

## Going further

### Hide locked effects

Set `behaviour.hide-without-permission: true` in `config.yml`. Locked effects disappear from the grid, except on the favourites tab.

### Sell locked effects

With `shop.enabled` on and an economy plugin installed, a locked effect that has a price is drawn with its price. A left click asks the player to confirm, charges them and unlocks it for good, and the menu comes back with the row now theirs. A player without `exyliarrows.buy` sees the row locked, with no price. Set the prices in [Configuration](/docs/exyliaarrows/configuration#shop) and see [Set up the crate and the shop](/docs/exyliaarrows/crate#buying-with-currency).

### Favourites

The favourites tab is the player's shortlist, drawn as `menu.favourites-icon` in `config.yml` and named `menu.favourites-name` in `messages.yml`.

- Starring is free: owning an effect is not required to star it, so a star does not vanish the day a rank lapses.
- Effects stay in the order they were starred.
- A locked effect is drawn on this tab even on a server that hides locked effects elsewhere.
- A player empties the list with Shift-click on the open tab (it asks to confirm first) or `/arrows favourites clear`.

### Change the wording

The few strings one template draws for every row live in `messages.yml`, under `menu`:

```yaml title="lang/<code>/messages.yml"
menu:
  empty: "{muted}Nothing"
  tab-open: "{success}✔ You are here"
  tab-closed: "{info}{icon:click} Click to open"
  bows-any: "Any bow"
  favourites-name: "Favourites"
  favourite-on: "{highlight}★ In your favourites"
  favourite-off: "{muted}☆ Not in your favourites"
  random-name: "{highlight}Random"
  random-on: "{success}✔ Every shot is a surprise"
  random-off: "{accent}{icon:click} Click to surprise yourself"
  default-marker: "{muted}Default for your rank"
  filter-all: "{info}Every effect"
  filter-owned: "{success}Only the ones you own"
```

Colours and messages work as everywhere else in Exylia, see [Colours](/docs/exylialib/colours) and [Messages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

<Callout type="tip" title="The window follows the choice">
Every way a player's choice can change (their own click, a command, an admin, or a rank that ran out) redraws the windows they have open. Nothing has to be closed and reopened.
</Callout>

<Details title="Slots, templates, placeholders and actions">
| Section | Slots | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `categories` | `9,18,27,36` | The tabs down the left, paged with the buttons at slots `0` and `45`. |
| `grid` | `11-16,20-25,29-34,38-43` | The open category's effects, paged at slots `47` and `52`. |

Fixed buttons: **Your effect** at slot `3`, **Filter** at `4`, **Crate** at `5`, **Particle visibility** at `48`, **Close** at `49`, **Random effect** at `50`. `none_owned_template` is what a tab shows when the filter leaves it empty, and `empty_template` is drawn on the favourites tab when nothing is starred. The **Crate** button opens `menus/crate.yml`.

### Templates

Each list picks a template per row. `item_template` is the plain state and must stay first.

| Section | Template | Used when |
|---|---|---|
| `categories` | `item_template` | Another tab is open. |
| | `open_template` | This is the tab being looked at. |
| | `favourites_template` | The shortlist tab, while another tab is open. |
| | `favourites_open_template` | The shortlist tab while it is open — the only one carrying the "empty the list" action. |
| `grid` | `item_template` | An effect the player owns. |
| | `selected_template` | The effect currently worn. |
| | `locked_template` | An effect they do not own. |
| | `purchasable_template` | An effect they do not own that the shop sells, while an economy can take its price. |
| | `empty_template` | Drawn on the favourites tab when nothing is starred. |

### Placeholders

These are the menu's own tokens, filled in while the screen is drawn. They are not PlaceholderAPI
placeholders and only work inside this file.

| Placeholder | Where | What it draws |
|---|---|---|
| `%category_id%` | `categories` | The tab's id — `favourites` on the shortlist tab. |
| `%category_name%` | `categories` | The tab's display name. |
| `%category_material%` | `categories` | The material its icon is drawn with. |
| `%category_total%` | `categories` | How many effects the tab holds. |
| `%category_owned%` | `categories` | How many of those the player owns. |
| `%category_status%` | `categories` | `menu.tab-open` or `menu.tab-closed`, depending on whether this is the tab being looked at. |
| `%effect_id%` | `grid` | The effect's id. |
| `%effect_name%` | `grid` | Its display name, with its colours. |
| `%effect_material%` | `grid` | The material it is drawn with. |
| `%effect_description%` | `grid` | Its description from `effects.yml`. |
| `%effect_bows%` | `grid` | What it may be bound to, in words. |
| `%effect_tier_id%` | `grid` | Its rarity's id. |
| `%effect_tier%` | `grid` | The rarity's name, in the rarity's colour. |
| `%effect_tier_color%` | `grid` | That colour on its own. |
| `%price%` | `purchasable_template` | What it sells for, with its currency. |
| `%effect_favourite%` | `grid` | `menu.favourite-on` or `menu.favourite-off`, depending on whether it is starred. |
| `%current_effect%` | Anywhere | The effect the player is wearing, `menu.random-name` on random, or `menu.empty`. |
| `%random_status%` | Anywhere | `menu.random-on` or `menu.random-off`. |
| `%favourites_count%` | Anywhere | How many effects they have starred. |
| `%keys%` | Anywhere | Crate keys they hold. |
| `%unlocked_count%` | Anywhere | How many effects they have unlocked, from the crate or the shop. |
| `%catalogue_count%` | Anywhere | How many effects the server declares. |
| `%particle_visibility%` | Anywhere | The player's current visibility mode, named as `particles.name-*` in `messages.yml` says. |
| `%visibility_all%` | Anywhere | The name of the "everyone" mode, for a button that offers it. |
| `%visibility_none%` | Anywhere | The name of the "nobody" mode. |
| `%visibility_self_only%` | Anywhere | The name of the "only me" mode. |
| `%visibility_others_only%` | Anywhere | The name of the "only others" mode. |

### Actions

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| `arrows:open` | Opens the screen. |
| `arrows:category <id>` | Switches tab. `favourites` is a valid id. |
| `arrows:choose <id>` | Wears an effect, or takes it off when it is already worn. |
| `arrows:clear` | Takes the effect off. |
| `arrows:random` | Turns the random choice on, or off when it is already on. |
| `arrows:favourite <id>` | Stars or unstars. |
| `arrows:favourites_clear` | Empties the shortlist. |
| `arrows:preview <id>` | Previews an effect and reopens the menu afterwards. |
| `arrows:visibility` | Steps the viewer's visibility on by one. |
| `arrows:buy <id>` | Asks the player to confirm, then buys a locked effect the shop sells. |

An effect a player plays only because their rank gives it (`behaviour.defaults`) is drawn as the one
worn, marked with `menu.default-marker`, and never as locked or for sale. Clicking it takes it off.

Where the server's menu style is `SCREEN` (ExyliaLib's `screens.yml`), `/arrows` opens the same
selector as a native screen (`screens/effects.html`, one with the crate and its reels) with a live
window flying the trail under the cursor. A screen that fails to load falls back to this chest menu.
</Details>
