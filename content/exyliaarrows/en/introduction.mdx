ExyliaArrows makes every shot look like something: a flash when the arrow leaves the bow, a trail along its flight and an impact where it lands. You get 120 ready-made effects in six categories, and you decide how players get them: from a menu, from a token on their bow, from a crate or from a shop.

In about ten minutes a player of yours will wear an effect and you will have seen all three moments in game. Start with [Your first effect](/docs/exyliaarrows/first-steps).

## What you get

- **120 effects, twenty per category.** Ember (`ember`, fire and forge work), Frost (`frost`, ice and snow), Storm (`storm`, wind and lightning), Void (`void`, sculk and souls), Prism (`prism`, crystal and starlight) and Goofy (`goofy`, rubber chickens and a sad trumpet). The list is in [Add and edit effects](/docs/exyliaarrows/effects).
- **Real objects, not only particles.** Effects are built from blocks and items that turn, fall and grow, drawn by each player's own client, with particles around them for light.
- **Three moments per effect.** Launch (the shot leaving the bow), trail (the flight) and impact (the landing, which can differ for a player and a block).
- **Two menus.** The classic inventory menu, and the [3D selector](/docs/exyliaarrows/3d-selector) that stands in the world and plays each effect in front of the player before they wear it.
- **A crate and a shop.** Keys open a crate with odds by rarity, and locked effects can be bought with money. See [Set up the crate and the shop](/docs/exyliaarrows/crate).

## Three ways players own an effect

| Way | Whose it is | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| **The menu** | The player's. They pick one in `/arrows` and it plays on whatever they shoot. | Practice, PvP |
| **A bow** | The bow's. A token is dropped onto a bow and travels with it through chests, trades and deaths. | Survival, economy |
| **The crate or the shop** | Theirs for good. A key opens the crate, or a locked effect is bought from the menu. It is saved on the player, not rented with a rank. | Any server with keys or money |

`behaviour.mode` in `config.yml` picks whether the menu choice, the bow, or both are read. The default is `both`. See [Bind effects to bows](/docs/exyliaarrows/modes-and-tokens).

## Before you start

| You need | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper. The plugin declares `api-version: '1.21'` and is built against Paper 1.21.4. Folia is supported (`folia-supported: true`). |
| `packetevents` | Required. The plugin does not load without it. |
| ExyliaLib | The loader installs it on the first start if it is missing. See [Install ExyliaArrows](/docs/exyliaarrows/installation). |
| Optional | PlaceholderAPI for [placeholders](/docs/exyliaarrows/placeholders), ExyliaEconomy or Vault for the shop, WorldGuard for the `arrows-effects` flag. |
| Players' clients | The 3D selector and the 3D crate opening need a 1.21.4 client. Older clients (for example through ViaBackwards) get the inventory menus. |

<Callout type="note" title="Minimum server version">
The build files do not state a lowest tested Paper or Folia version. The plugin loads on servers that accept `api-version: '1.21'`, and it is compiled against 1.21.4. If you run something older than 1.21.4, test it before you sell effects.
</Callout>

## Going further

- **Which projectiles carry an effect.** By default arrows, spectral arrows and tridents. `projectiles.all-types` in `config.yml` extends it to every launched projectile, and one effect can name projectiles of its own.
- **Who sees what.** Each player chooses what they see with the **Particle visibility** button: everyone's effects, nobody's, only their own or only other people's.
- **Write your own.** Add effects to `effects.yml` by hand or [with an AI assistant](/docs/exyliaarrows/create-with-ai).

<Details title="Technical details">
Every effect declares up to five triggers in `effects.yml`:

| Trigger | When it plays | What it can afford |
|---|---|---|
| `launch` | The shot leaving the bow. | One short, quiet flash. Optional. |
| `trail` | Along the flight, every `trail-interval` ticks. | One or two small displays plus a particle line. No sound. This is the one with a budget. |
| `hit` | Wherever it lands. | The payoff: under a second, a dozen displays at most. |
| `hit-entity` | Only when it lands on something alive. | Falls back to `hit` when not declared. |
| `hit-block` | Only when it lands on a block. | Falls back to `hit` when not declared. |

An arrow lives about forty ticks, so a trail is drawn on every one of those ticks for every shot. Launch, trail and impact are compiled apart for that reason.

Visibility modes are stored as `ALL`, `NONE`, `SELF_ONLY` and `OTHERS_ONLY`. The player's choice, visibility and favourites live in one table in the plugin's database; keys and unlocks live in ExyliaLib's crate table.
</Details>
