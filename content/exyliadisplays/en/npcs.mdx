---
title: "NPCs"
description: "Creating NPCs, their skin, pose, size, outline and equipment, where they look, who sees them, their label, and a hologram that follows them."
---

An NPC is a player figure standing at a place, facing a way. Like holograms it is packets only: it
cannot be hurt, pushed or killed, and it costs nothing when nobody is near.

## Creating one

| From | How |
|---|---|
| The list | `/npcadmin`, then **CREATE NPC**: a window with a name, a preset and a skin (your own name by default). |
| A command | `/npcadmin create <name> [skin]`: the skin is an account name, `@mirror` or `@none`; without one, **your** skin. |
| The file | A new entry under `npcs:` in `npcs.yml`. Only `location` is needed. |

It stands where you stand. With `face-creator` on (the default) it faces you; off, it looks where you
were looking, level. It gets the `default-view-distance` and `default-look` of `config.yml` and a label
with its name, or the preset's label (`%npc%` becomes its name) and actions. A preset's skin wins over the
one typed, unless the preset has none.

The shipped presets:

| Preset | What it does |
|---|---|
| `simple` | Only its name as a label. |
| `guide` | Right-click: a villager sound and a welcome bubble only you see. Left-click: it flinches and complains. |
| `portal` | Any click: swings, plays a portal sound and sends you to the `survival` server. |
| `shopkeeper` | Right-click: swings and runs `shop` as the player. |
| `mirror` | Wears each viewer's own skin and says "Looking good." |

## The editor

`/npc <name>` (or a left-click in the list):

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| Header | Left: shows or hides it. Right: renames it. |
| **APPEARANCE** | Skin type, account, texture and signature, pose, scale and outline. |
| **EQUIPMENT** | Six slots. Left-click a slot to put the item in your hand there, right-click to empty it. |
| **LABEL** | Left: the label lines and their height. Right: link a hologram. |
| **INTERACTIONS** | The click lines, permission, cooldown, the two refusal messages and face-the-clicker. |
| **VISIBILITY** | Look mode and range, view distance, permission. |
| **LOCATION** | Left: exact position and facing. Right: move here. Shift-left: centre on its block. Shift-right: turn to face me. |
| **TELEPORT**, **DUPLICATE**, **FINE ADJUST**, **DELETE** | As for holograms. A duplicate stands in the same place and carries no linked hologram. |

## Skin

| `skin.type` | Command | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| `player` | `/npc <name> skin Notch` | The skin an account wears. |
| `texture` | `/npc <name> texture <value> [signature]` | An uploaded skin: the base64 value and Mojang's signature, as mineskin.org gives them. |
| `mirror` | `/npc <name> skin @mirror` | Every viewer sees their own skin. |
| `none` | `/npc <name> skin @none` | The default skin. |

<Callout type="warning" title="A texture needs its signature">
Without the signature, the client draws the default skin. Copy both values.
</Callout>

## Pose, size and outline

| Setting | Command | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pose | `/npc <name> pose <pose>` | `standing`, `sneaking`, `lying`, `crawling`, `spinning` | `standing` |
| Scale | `/npc <name> scale <n>` | `0.0625` to `16`; 1 is a player | `1` |
| Glow | `/npc <name> glow <#RRGGBB\|none>` | An outline, drawn in the nearest team colour | none |

## Equipment

Slots: `head`, `chest`, `legs`, `feet`, `hand`, `off_hand`.

```text
/npc guide equip hand                    # the item in your hand
/npc guide equip hand DIAMOND_SWORD
/npc guide equip head playerhead-Notch
/npc guide equip head basehead-<texture>
/npc guide equip hand air                # empties the slot
/npc guide equip clear                   # empties every slot
```

The file keeps each slot as an icon string: a material, `playerhead-<name>`, `basehead-<texture>`, or a
`bytes:` item for an item from your hand that carries more than a material.

## Where it looks

| Mode | Command | |
|---|---|---|
| `fixed` | `/npc <name> look fixed` | Always the way it faces. |
| `nearest` | `/npc <name> look nearest 8` | At the nearest player in range; everybody sees the same head. |
| `each-viewer` | `/npc <name> look each-viewer 8` | Every player in range sees it looking at **them**. Nothing turns on the server. |

The number is the look range in blocks (default 8). `each-viewer` is the default for new NPCs
(`default-look`).

With `face-clicker` on (default), a click that runs its actions also turns it toward the clicker for
`turn-back` (default 4 seconds), then back. That turn is never saved.

## Who sees it

- `/npc <name> viewdistance <blocks>`: how far away it is seen (default 48).
- `/npc <name> permission [node]`: only players with the node see it, its label included. No node clears
  it.

An NPC in a world that is not loaded waits and appears when the world loads.

## The label

The label is the lines above its head, the name players read. It is a hologram, so every
[line tag](/docs/exyliadisplays/line-tags) works in it, and clicking it does what clicking the NPC does.

```text
/npc guide label set <size:1.2><shine>{primary}&lGUIDE    # replaces every line with this one
/npc guide label add <opacity:200>{letters_black}Right-click me
/npc guide label remove 2
/npc guide label clear
/npc guide label height 0.4
```

`height-offset` is the gap between the top of its head (which follows its pose and scale) and the label's
bottom line. Default `0.25`. A label with no lines is no label.

## A linked hologram

For something more than a name — pages, a click box of its own, per-viewer placeholders — link any
hologram of `holograms.yml`:

```text
/npc guide link guide_board
/npc guide unlink
```

The NPC moves that hologram above its head (over the label, if it has one) by writing its `location` in
`holograms.yml`, and does it again every time the NPC moves. The hologram is still edited as a hologram.
Linking needs the hologram module on.

<Callout type="warning" title="What breaks a link">
- Renaming or deleting the hologram does not update the NPC: `linked-hologram` keeps the old name and
  nothing follows until you link again.
- Moving the hologram on its own is undone the next time the NPC changes.
- Two NPCs linked to the same hologram move it back and forth.
</Callout>

## Commands at a glance

See [Commands](/docs/exyliadisplays/commands#npc) for the full list. `/npc` competes with Citizens'
`/npc` when both are installed; use `/npcs <name>` or `/exylianpc <name>` then. `/npcadmin` never clashes.
