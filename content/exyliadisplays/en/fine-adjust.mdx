You end up with a hologram or NPC placed exactly where you want it, by scrolling the mouse wheel while you look at it. It moves live on every notch; nothing is written to the file until you finish.

## Before you start

You need a hologram or an NPC, see [Create and edit a hologram](/docs/exyliadisplays/holograms) or [Create and dress an NPC](/docs/exyliadisplays/npcs).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Run `/holo <name> adjust` or `/npc <name> adjust`, or click **Fine adjust** in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move it">
    Scroll to change the value by one step. Sneak and scroll for a bigger or smaller step. Press **F** to go to the next axis (X, Y, Z, forward, turn, tilt, scale...); sneak and press **F** to go back. The two boss bars show the axis, the value and the step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or cancel">
    Click, or run the start command again, to save and finish. Run `/holo <name> adjust cancel` (or `/npc <name> adjust cancel`) to put everything back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

The hologram or NPC moves as you scroll. After you save, run `/holo <name> info` or look at the file: the new coordinates are there.

## Going further

Sneak and click snaps to the block centre, half a block for height and the nearest 15 degrees. `/holo <name> adjust save` and `/npc <name> adjust save` also save.

### Keys, axes and when it ends on its own

<Details title="Keys, axes and when it ends">

#### Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Scroll | Changes the value by one step. |
| Sneak + scroll | A bigger or smaller step. |
| F | Next axis; sneak + F goes back. |
| Sneak + click | Snap: block centre, half a block for height, the nearest 15°. |
| Click, or the start command again | Saves and finishes. |
| `/holo <name> adjust cancel` · `/npc <name> adjust cancel` (or **Undo** in chat) | Puts everything back. |

`/holo <name> adjust save` and `/npc <name> adjust save` also save. Two boss bars show the axis, the
value and the step, and the keys.

#### Axes

| Hologram | NPC |
|---|---|
| X, Y, Z | X, Y, Z |
| Forward (where you look) | Forward (where you look) |
| Turn | Turn |
| Tilt | Tilt |
| Scale | Scale (`0.0625` to `16`) |
| Line spacing | Label height (`-2` to `5`) |

For an NPC, its label and its linked hologram follow it live, and both `npcs.yml` and `holograms.yml` are
written once, when you save.

#### When it ends on its own

Leaving, dying, changing world or teleporting far saves and finishes. Deleting or renaming what you are
adjusting stops the session with nothing to save, as does a reload that removes it from its file. Stopping
the server saves every running session.

One player adjusts a given hologram or NPC at a time. While somebody adjusts one, a hand edit of the file
or another admin's change to it still shows underneath.

</Details>
