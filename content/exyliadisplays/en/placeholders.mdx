ExyliaDisplays answers placeholders for its [leaderboards](/docs/exyliadisplays/leaderboards). They work in holograms, in an NPC's skin and in any plugin that reads PlaceholderAPI placeholders. Click one to copy it.

Replace `<stat>` with an id from `leaderboards.yml` (by default `kills`, `deaths`, `mobs`, `playtime`, `money`, `diamonds`, `jumps`), `<period>` with `all-time`, `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly` or `yearly`, and `<place>` with a number from 1 up.

<Placeholders />

For example, `%exyliadisplays_lb_kills:weekly:1:name%` is this week's best killer. An NPC whose skin is that placeholder wears their skin; see [Statues](/docs/exyliadisplays/npcs#statues).

Holograms and NPCs also read every placeholder other plugins give through PlaceholderAPI, such as `%player_name%`. A hologram that uses one is drawn on its own for each viewer.
