Every command, with its aliases and the permission it needs. Hologram and NPC names complete as you type.

Four command families:

- `/displaysadmin` is the plugin as a whole (modules, reload, demo).
- `/holoadmin` is every hologram at once; `/holo <name> ...` is one hologram.
- `/npcadmin` is every NPC at once; `/npc <name> ...` is one NPC.

The commands of a module that is switched off do not exist. Commands that need a place or a screen (`create`, `movehere`, `tp`, `face`, `adjust`, `near`, `page <n>`, `lines`, `equipment`) are for players; the rest also run from the console. A name with no setting opens the editor for a player and prints the details for the console.

<Callout type="note" title="Next to Citizens">
Citizens also registers `/npc`, and the two compete for it. Use `/npcs <name>` or `/exylianpc <name>` instead; `/npcadmin` does not clash. Once you have [imported your Citizens NPCs](/docs/exyliadisplays/migration) and removed Citizens, `/npc` works again.
</Callout>
