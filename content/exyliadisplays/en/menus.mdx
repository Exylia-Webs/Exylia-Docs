ExyliaDisplays has six screens, all for admins. They are ExyliaLib menus under
`lang/<code>/menus/admin/`, **rewritten from the jar on every start**: edits to them are lost, so a
release always reaches them.

| Screen | Opened by | File |
|---|---|---|
| Holograms | `/holoadmin` | `hologram_list.yml` |
| A hologram | `/holo <name>` | `hologram_hub.yml` |
| Its lines | `/holo <name> lines`, **LINES** | `hologram_lines.yml` |
| NPCs | `/npcadmin` | `npc_list.yml` |
| An NPC | `/npc <name>` | `npc_hub.yml` |
| Its equipment | `/npc <name> equipment`, **EQUIPMENT** | `npc_equipment.yml` |

Every button needs the module's permission: `exyliadisplays.holograms` or `exyliadisplays.npcs`.

## The lists

One item per hologram or NPC, with what it reads (or its skin as a head), its world, position, line count
or actions, and its state.

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| Left | Opens its editor. |
| Shift-left | Teleports you to it. |
| Right | Deletes it, after asking. |

**CREATE HOLOGRAM** / **CREATE NPC** opens the creation window with the presets of `config.yml`.

## A hologram

See [Holograms](/docs/exyliadisplays/holograms#the-editor) for every button and
[Lines](/docs/exyliadisplays/holograms#lines) for the lines screen.

## An NPC

See [NPCs](/docs/exyliadisplays/npcs#the-editor). The equipment screen shows the six slots
(**HEAD**, **CHEST**, **LEGS**, **FEET**, **MAIN HAND**, **OFF HAND**): left-click puts the item in your
hand there, right-click empties it.

## Dialog windows

Every editing window is an ExyliaLib dialog: one window per section with every field in it, validated as
you type and saved together; closing it goes back to the screen. Clients older than 1.21.6 and Bedrock
players get the same fields through chat or a form. Single values (a new name, a copy's name) use
ExyliaLib's text input.

## Actions

The buttons call actions in the `exyliadisplays` namespace, with `holo_` and `npc_` prefixes:

| Screen | Actions |
|---|---|
| Hologram list | `holo_create`, `holo_edit`, `holo_row_teleport`, `holo_row_delete` |
| Hologram editor | `holo_toggle`, `holo_rename`, `holo_open_lines`, `holo_appearance`, `holo_visibility`, `holo_visibility_mode`, `holo_click`, `holo_location`, `holo_move_here`, `holo_centre`, `holo_face`, `holo_attach`, `holo_detach`, `holo_teleport`, `holo_clone`, `holo_adjust`, `holo_open_list`, `holo_delete` |
| Hologram lines | `holo_line_text`, `holo_line_style`, `holo_line_up`, `holo_line_down`, `holo_line_duplicate`, `holo_line_delete`, `holo_add_line`, `holo_add_item`, `holo_add_block`, `holo_add_head`, `holo_all_lines`, `holo_page_next`, `holo_page_previous`, `holo_page_add`, `holo_page_click`, `holo_page_delete`, `holo_open_hub` |
| NPC list | `npc_create`, `npc_edit`, `npc_row_teleport`, `npc_row_delete` |
| NPC editor | `npc_toggle`, `npc_rename`, `npc_appearance`, `npc_open_equipment`, `npc_label`, `npc_link`, `npc_interactions`, `npc_visibility`, `npc_location`, `npc_move_here`, `npc_centre`, `npc_face_me`, `npc_teleport`, `npc_clone`, `npc_adjust`, `npc_open_list`, `npc_delete` |
| NPC equipment | `npc_equip <slot>`, `npc_unequip <slot>`, `npc_open_hub` |

They act on the hologram or NPC the screen is about, so they are only useful inside these screens.
