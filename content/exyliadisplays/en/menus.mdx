You end up knowing how to run everything from the screens: the two lists, each hologram's or NPC's editor, the lines and equipment screens, and the settings pages.

## Before you start

You need `exyliadisplays.holograms` (for holograms) or `exyliadisplays.npcs` (for NPCs); every button checks it. Operators have both, see [Permissions](/docs/exyliadisplays/permissions).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a list">
    Run `/holoadmin` for holograms or `/npcadmin` for NPCs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one">
    Left-click an entry to open its editor, shift-left-click to teleport to it, right-click to delete it (it asks first). Click **Create hologram** or **Create NPC** to make a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the editor">
    Every card has a left-click and often a right-click or shift-click action: the lore on each card says which. The hologram editor is in [Create and edit a hologram](/docs/exyliadisplays/holograms#the-editor), the NPC one in [Create and dress an NPC](/docs/exyliadisplays/npcs#the-editor).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

The editor opens and the card you clicked opens its window or screen. Closing a window returns you to the screen. If a window does not open, see [Common problems](/docs/exyliadisplays/troubleshooting).

## Going further

The screens are ExyliaLib menus, see [Menus](/docs/exylialib/menus) for how they work. They are not meant to be edited: they live in `lang/<code>/menus/admin/` and are **rewritten from the jar on every start**, so edits are lost and a release always reaches them. Their language follows your language setting, see [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

<Details title="Screens and files">

| Screen | Opened by | File |
|---|---|---|
| Holograms | `/holoadmin` | `hologram_list.yml` |
| A hologram | `/holo <name>` | `hologram_hub.yml` |
| Its lines | `/holo <name> lines`, **LINES** | `hologram_lines.yml` |
| NPCs | `/npcadmin` | `npc_list.yml` |
| An NPC | `/npc <name>` | `npc_hub.yml` |
| Its equipment | `/npc <name> equipment`, **EQUIPMENT** | `npc_equipment.yml` |

</Details>

<Details title="Actions the buttons call">

The buttons call actions in the `exyliadisplays` namespace, with `holo_` and `npc_` prefixes:

| Screen | Actions |
|---|---|
| Hologram list | `holo_create`, `holo_edit`, `holo_row_teleport`, `holo_row_delete` |
| Hologram editor | `holo_toggle`, `holo_rename`, `holo_open_lines`, `holo_appearance`, `holo_visibility`, `holo_visibility_mode`, `holo_click`, `holo_location`, `holo_move_here`, `holo_centre`, `holo_face`, `holo_attach`, `holo_detach`, `holo_teleport`, `holo_clone`, `holo_adjust`, `holo_open_list`, `holo_delete` |
| Hologram lines | `holo_line_text`, `holo_line_style`, `holo_line_up`, `holo_line_down`, `holo_line_duplicate`, `holo_line_delete`, `holo_add_line`, `holo_add_item`, `holo_add_block`, `holo_add_head`, `holo_all_lines`, `holo_page_next`, `holo_page_previous`, `holo_page_add`, `holo_page_click`, `holo_page_delete`, `holo_open_hub` |
| NPC list | `npc_create`, `npc_edit`, `npc_row_teleport`, `npc_row_delete` |
| NPC editor | `npc_toggle`, `npc_rename`, `npc_appearance`, `npc_open_equipment`, `npc_label`, `npc_link`, `npc_interactions`, `npc_visibility`, `npc_location`, `npc_move_here`, `npc_centre`, `npc_face_me`, `npc_teleport`, `npc_clone`, `npc_adjust`, `npc_open_list`, `npc_delete` |
| NPC equipment | `npc_equip <slot>`, `npc_unequip <slot>`, `npc_open_hub` |

They act on the hologram or NPC the screen is about, so they are only useful inside these screens.

</Details>

The **Settings** card of either list opens a hub with one page per theme (modules, holograms, NPCs, leaderboards); values are typed in a window and saved to `config.yml`. A hologram's hub also carries **SIZE BY DISTANCE**, **LEADERBOARD**, **TEMPLATES** and **UNDO** cards. See [Holograms](/docs/exyliadisplays/holograms#the-editor) and [Leaderboards](/docs/exyliadisplays/leaderboards).
