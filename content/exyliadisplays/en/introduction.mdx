ExyliaDisplays puts **holograms and NPCs** in the world of a Paper or Folia server, and gives both the
same click system. It is free and open source, published under the GNU General Public License v3.0 on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Exylia-Plugins/ExyliaDisplays/releases).

Everything it shows is packets, drawn by ExyliaLib: nothing is a real entity, nothing is saved in the
chunks, and nothing ticks on the server. A hologram or an NPC exists for whoever is close enough to see
it and costs nothing when nobody is.

Each one can be edited three ways that always agree: screens and dialog windows in game, `/holo` and
`/npc` commands, or `holograms.yml` and `npcs.yml` by hand, which are applied the moment you save them.

It used to be called **ExyliaHolograms**. A server coming from it keeps its holograms and its permission;
see [Migration](/docs/exyliadisplays/migration).

## What is included

<Cards>
  <Card title="Holograms" href="/docs/exyliadisplays/holograms">
    Text lines, floating items, blocks and player heads in one stack, each line with its own size,
    motion and colour effects.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Line tags" href="/docs/exyliadisplays/line-tags">
    `<size:2>`, `<spin>`, `<shine>`, `<typewriter>`, and lines only some players see: `<range:8>`,
    `<perm:vip>`, `<if:"%player_level% >= 10">`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pages" href="/docs/exyliadisplays/pages">
    A hologram with several pages, where every player reads their own and turns it with a click or on
    a timer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="NPCs" href="/docs/exyliadisplays/npcs">
    Player NPCs with a skin (or each viewer's own), a pose, a size, an outline, equipment, a label and a
    head that turns toward each viewer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Interactions" href="/docs/exyliadisplays/interactions">
    One set of click lines for both: commands, `[SERVER]`, `[TELEPORT]`, `[MESSAGE]`, and NPC speech
    bubbles with `[SAY]`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Imports" href="/docs/exyliadisplays/migration">
    DecentHolograms, HolographicDisplays, FancyHolograms, Citizens and FancyNpcs, read without touching
    their files.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper or Folia 1.21.4 or newer |
| Java | 21 |
| Required | `packetevents`, and `ExyliaLib` 1.299.0 or newer (the 2.4.0 jar) — installed automatically when missing |
| Optional | `PlaceholderAPI`, whose placeholders work in every line and every action |
| Clients | Dialog windows need a 1.21.6+ client; older clients and Bedrock players get the same fields through chat or forms |

<Callout type="note" title="About ExyliaLib">
The holograms, the NPCs, the line tags, the click lines, the screens and the dialog windows all come
from ExyliaLib. ExyliaDisplays is what keeps them in files, edits them and gives them commands; every
Exylia plugin that shows a hologram draws it the same way.
</Callout>

## Conventions

- Values marked as **default** are what the plugin writes when the key is missing.
- Identifiers between `<` and `>` are variables: `/holo <name> addline <text>` means
  `/holo spawn addline Welcome`.
- A hologram or an NPC is named by its **name**: lowercase letters, digits, `_` and `-`, up to 32
  characters. Commands lowercase what you type.
- Colours use ExyliaLib palette tokens (`{primary}`, `{highlight}`, `{letters}`), `&` codes, hex and
  MiniMessage.
- Line numbers in commands start at 1.
