Several things decide whether a player sees a hologram, and all of them must agree:

1. it is `enabled` and its world is loaded;
2. the player is within `view-distance` (default 48 blocks);
3. the player has its `permission`, when it has one;
4. the player passes its `visibility` list;
5. if it is attached to an entity, that entity is in the world.

On top of those, each [line](/docs/exyliadisplays/line-tags#who-sees-a-line) can have its own
`<range>`, `<perm>` or `<if>`.

Changing a permission or a list is read by the next visibility check: nothing is redrawn and nobody sees
it flicker.

## Distance, permission and per-player

| Setting | Command | |
|---|---|---|
| View distance | `/holo <name> viewdistance <blocks>` | Above 0. |
| Permission | `/holo <name> permission [node]` | Only players with it see the hologram. No node clears it. |
| Per-player | `/holo <name> perplayer <true\|false>` | Every viewer gets their own copy. Costs one set of packets per viewer. |
| Refresh | `/holo <name> refresh <ticks>` | Ticks between placeholder refreshes, 1+ (default 20). |
| Auto-update | `/holo <name> autoupdate <true\|false>` | Whether the lines refresh on their own (default `true`). |

You rarely need `per-player`: a hologram with a placeholder in any line is already drawn per viewer.
Turn it on only for lines without a placeholder that must still differ.

The **VISIBILITY** window in the editor holds all of these plus the page interval and the player lists.

## Chosen players

`visibility.mode` picks how the player list is read:

| Mode | Who sees it | List used |
|---|---|---|
| `all` (default) | Everyone except the players listed. | `hidden-from` |
| `manual` | Only the players listed. | `shown-to` |

```text
/holo nametag visibility manual
/holo nametag show Notch
/holo nametag show @a
/holo nametag hide Notch
/holo nametag viewers
/holo nametag viewers clear
```

`show` and `hide` work in both modes: in `all` they take a player off or put them on `hidden-from`; in
`manual` they put them on or take them off `shown-to`. `@a` means every player online. A player is named
by name or UUID and must have joined the server before. `viewers` lists the list the mode reads;
`viewers clear` empties it, so in `all` everyone sees it again and in `manual` nobody does.

Only the exceptions are written, as UUIDs, so a renamed player keeps their place and a hologram
everybody sees costs two empty lists:

```yaml
visibility:
  mode: manual
  shown-to: ['069a79f4-44e9-4726-a5be-fca90e38aaf5']
  hidden-from: []
```

Right-clicking **VISIBILITY** in the editor switches between the two modes.

## Attaching to a player or a mob

A hologram can ride an entity instead of standing at its location.

```text
/holo nametag attach Notch           # a player, by name or UUID
/holo nametag attach <uuid>          # any entity by UUID
/holo nametag attach nearest         # the entity nearest to you, within 16 blocks
/holo nametag attachoffset 0.8       # blocks above the entity, -16 to 16 (default 0.5)
/holo nametag detach
```

| What it rides | Saved | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| A player | Yes, `attach.entity` | Rides them whenever they are online, joining, respawning and changing worlds included. Hidden while they are away. |
| A mob saved with its chunk | Yes | Rides it whenever its chunk is loaded. Hidden while it is not. |
| A mob that is not saved (not persistent) | No | Ridden until it is gone or the server restarts, without being written down. The command says so. |

A player who is offline when you attach can still be named, as long as they joined before. Range is
measured from the ridden entity, and a viewer who comes into range later sees it mounted.

```yaml
attach:
  entity: '069a79f4-44e9-4726-a5be-fca90e38aaf5'
  offset: 0.5
```

An unreadable `entity` (a typo in the file) is treated as no attachment. `location` is kept while
attached and used again after `detach`.
