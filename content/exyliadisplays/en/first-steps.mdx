Everything below needs `exyliadisplays.admin` (or `exyliadisplays.holograms` and `exyliadisplays.npcs`),
which operators have by default.

## A hologram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Stand where you want it and run `/holoadmin create spawn`, or open `/holoadmin` and click
    **CREATE HOLOGRAM**. The window asks for a name and a preset; the hologram appears 2 blocks above
    your feet, facing you, and its editor opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write its lines">
    Click **LINES**. Left-click a line to change its text, right-click it for size, motion and who sees
    it, shift-click to move it up or down, press **F** to duplicate it and **Q** to remove it.
    **EDIT ALL AT ONCE** edits every line in one box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    **LOCATION**: left-click for exact coordinates and rotation, right-click to move it to you,
    shift-left to centre it on its block, shift-right to turn it to face you. **FINE ADJUST** moves it
    with the mouse wheel; see [Fine adjust](/docs/exyliadisplays/fine-adjust).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it clickable (optional)">
    **CLICK ACTIONS**, one line each, such as `right: [SERVER] survival`. See
    [Interactions](/docs/exyliadisplays/interactions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The same hologram from commands:

```text
/holoadmin create spawn <size:2><shine>{primary}&lWELCOME
/holo spawn addline {letters}to the server
/holo spawn addline <range:8><typewriter>{letters_black}Right-click for the rules
/holo spawn click add right: [MESSAGE] {letters}No griefing, no cheating.
```

## An NPC

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    `/npcadmin create guide Notch`, or open `/npcadmin` and click **CREATE NPC**. The window asks for a
    name, a preset (a guide that greets you, a server portal, a shopkeeper, a mirror) and a skin. It
    stands where you stand and faces you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dress it">
    **APPEARANCE** for skin, pose, size and outline; **EQUIPMENT** to put the item in your hand in a
    slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    **LABEL** for the lines above its head (every hologram line tag works there), or right-click it to
    link a whole hologram that follows the NPC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it something to do">
    **INTERACTIONS**: the click lines, a permission and a cooldown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The same NPC from commands:

```text
/npcadmin create guide Notch
/npc guide label set <size:1.2>{primary}&lGUIDE
/npc guide action add right: [SAY] {letters}Welcome, {highlight}%player_name%{letters}!;6s
/npc guide action add left: [ANIMATE] hurt
/npc guide action cooldown 2s
```

Without a skin, `/npcadmin create` dresses the NPC in **your own** skin; write `@none` for the default
skin or `@mirror` for each viewer's own.

## Or write the file

Open `plugins/ExyliaDisplays/holograms.yml`, add an entry and save. It appears within a second, and every
player with `exyliadisplays.holograms` is told it was applied:

```yaml title="plugins/ExyliaDisplays/holograms.yml"
holograms:
  rules:
    location: {world: world, x: 0.5, y: 66.0, z: 0.5}
    lines:
      - '<size:1.6>{primary}&lRULES'
      - '{letters}No griefing, no cheating.'
```

A hand-written entry only needs the keys it changes. See
[Editing the files](/docs/exyliadisplays/editing-files).
