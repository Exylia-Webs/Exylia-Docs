Three commands move a player around once the sandbox worlds exist: `/tpa` to visit somebody, `/back` to return to where they were, and `/rekit` to put their kit back on. None of them works while the player is combat-tagged. Only `/rekit` has a cooldown.

## Before you start

Players need a kit and at least one world must be ready. See [Your first sandbox](/docs/exyliasandbox/first-steps). The commands and permissions are listed in [Commands](/docs/exyliasandbox/commands) and [Permissions](/docs/exyliasandbox/permissions).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask to teleport with /tpa">
    A player runs `/tpa <player>`. The **target** must already be inside a sandbox world; asking somebody in the lobby is refused. If the requester is already in a sandbox, the request carries the kit they wear. Otherwise the usual kit rule applies: none refuses, one goes straight through, two or more opens the kit list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The target answers">
    The target gets a centred message with clickable **ACCEPT** and **DECLINE** buttons that run `/tpaccept` and `/tpdeny`. With no name, those commands take the newest request still alive. A request expires after 60 seconds, and both sides are told.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return with /back">
    `/back` takes a player to the last place they were inside a sandbox world. Run it again and it returns them to where they stood, so it works as a return trip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the kit back with /rekit">
    Inside a sandbox world `/rekit` re-applies the kit the player is **wearing** (not their favourite) and refills health, food and saturation. Between two uses they wait 30 seconds by default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

With two players inside a sandbox, one runs `/tpa <other>`, the other clicks **ACCEPT**, and the first lands next to them. Then `/back` sends them to where they were before. After a death, `/back` brings the player to the spot they died, wearing a kit they pick if they have several.

## Going further

### Give a rank a shorter /rekit wait

Give the rank `exyliasandbox.rekit.cooldown.<seconds>`, for example `exyliasandbox.rekit.cooldown.10`. If a player holds several, the lowest wins. For everyone else change `rekit.cooldown-seconds` in `config.yml`. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliasandbox/configuration).

### Change how long a request lives

`tpa.request-timeout-seconds` in `config.yml` (60 by default; the settings menu allows 5 to 3600).

### When /back has nowhere to go

The player is told there is no saved location, or that it is not in one of the plugin's worlds. Nothing is saved after a restart.

<Details title="Technical details">
- `/tpa`: a second request to the same player is refused (`tpa.already-sent`); a disconnect drops every request to and from that player. `/tpaccept` requires the accepter to be inside a sandbox world (`tpa.accept-outside-world`); `/tpdeny` has no such check. A player building a kit, waiting in a queue, held by another activity or combat-tagged can neither send nor accept; accepting is refused with `tpa.player-unavailable` if the requester became one of those since. Accepting claims the requester, saves and clears their inventory if they were outside, applies the chosen kit and moves them. `/tpa` needs `exyliasandbox.command.tpa`; `/tpdeny` never needs a permission.
- `/back`: one point per player, and only a location inside a sandbox world. It is written when a player already inside re-rolls with `/rtp`, when a `/tpa` they sent goes through, when a teleport pad picks them up from inside, and **when they die** (the death location becomes the point). `/back` from the lobby runs the kit rule (none refuses with `queue.no-kit`, one goes through, several open the kit list). From inside a sandbox the kit is kept. The point is used once and cleared on disconnect.
- `/rekit`: refused when not in a sandbox world (`rekit.not-in-world`) or with no active kit (`rekit.no-kit`); the cooldown message `rekit.cooldown` shows the time left (`%time%`).
- The `/back` point, pending requests and the worn kit live in memory. A restart clears all three.
</Details>
