---
title: "Teleports"
description: "`/tpa` between players inside a sandbox, `/back` to where you last were, and `/rekit`."
---

Three commands move a player around once the sandbox worlds exist. Only `/rekit` has a cooldown, and
none of them works while the player is combat-tagged: they are told `general.in-combat`.

## `/tpa`

`/tpa <player>` asks to be teleported to somebody. The **target** must already be inside a sandbox
world; asking somebody in the lobby is refused with `tpa.target-outside-world`.

If the requester is already inside a sandbox, the request carries the kit they are wearing. Otherwise
the usual kit branch applies: none refuses, one goes straight through, two or more opens the kit list
first.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/tpa <player>` | Sends a request. A second request to the same player is refused with `tpa.already-sent` |
| `/tpaccept [player]` | Accepts. With no name it takes the **newest** request still alive |
| `/tpdeny [player]` | Denies. With no name, same selection |

Requests expire after `tpa.request-timeout-seconds`, sixty by default, and both sides are told. A
disconnect drops every request to and from that player.

The target receives the request as a centred block with clickable **ACCEPT** and **DECLINE** buttons
that run `/tpaccept` and `/tpdeny` for them.

`/tpaccept` requires the accepter to be inside a sandbox world; `/tpdeny` has no such check. A player
who is building a kit, waiting in a queue, held by another activity or combat-tagged can neither send a
request nor accept one, and accepting is refused with `tpa.player-unavailable` when the requester has
since become one of those.

Accepting claims the requester, snapshots and clears their inventory if they were outside, dresses them
in the chosen kit, and moves them.

## `/back`

`/back` returns a player to the last place they were inside a sandbox world. Only one point is kept per
player, and only ever a location inside a sandbox world.

It is written when:

- a player already inside a sandbox re-rolls with `/rtp`;
- a `/tpa` is accepted and the requester was already inside;
- a teleport pad picks up a player who was already inside;
- **a player dies** — the death location becomes the point.

`/back` from the lobby runs the kit branch. `/back` from inside a sandbox keeps the kit, and the spot
they were standing on becomes the new point, so `/back` twice is a return trip.

It refuses with `back.no-location` when there is no point, or when the world it names is no longer a
sandbox world.

## `/rekit`

`/rekit` puts the active kit back on and sets health, food and saturation to full. It only works while
standing in a sandbox world, and it re-applies the kit the player is **wearing** — not their favourite.
A player who arrived without a kit is told `rekit.no-kit`. Between two uses the player waits
`rekit.cooldown-seconds` from `config.yml`, thirty by default, and is told `rekit.cooldown` with the
time left.

## What is not persisted

The `/back` point, the pending TPA requests and the kit a player is wearing all live in memory. A
restart clears all three.
