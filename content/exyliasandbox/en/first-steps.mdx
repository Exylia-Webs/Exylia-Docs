At the end of this page players can run `/rtp`, land in a sandbox world with a kit, and get back to the lobby with `/leavesandbox`. You use the admin panel and chat.

## Before you start

ExyliaSandBox must be installed and the server must have started once, so the five default worlds exist. See [Install ExyliaSandBox](/docs/exyliasandbox/installation).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the lobby">
    Run `/sandboxadmin`, click **Set lobby**, walk to where players should arrive and sneak-click. Players return here when they die, leave or disconnect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the kit creator room">
    In `/sandboxadmin`, click **Set kit creator** and do the same in the room where players will build kits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the worlds">
    In `/sandboxadmin`, click **Worlds**. Five worlds come ready: `desert`, `plains`, `snowy`, `mushroom` and `badlands`, each with a 3000-block border. Left-click a world to configure it, right-click to teleport into it. To add your own, click **Create World**; see [Worlds](/docs/exyliasandbox/worlds).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a kit">
    Run `/kitcreator` and click **Create Kit**. The kit room opens; click items to take copies. Run `/kitsave` to store your whole inventory, including armour and offhand. Your first saved kit becomes your favourite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give players what they need">
    Players can already use everything. To give a rank more kit slots, give it `exyliasandbox.max_kits.3`. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliasandbox/permissions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/rtp` and pick a world. With one usable kit you go straight in; with several you choose one. Two ticks after arriving you are reset (survival, full health and food), the sandbox sidebar appears and the kit goes on.

Run `/leavesandbox` to get out. Your inventory comes back and the sidebar disappears. Dying does the same: nothing drops and there is no respawn screen. `/back` takes you to where you died.

If a world is not ready, `/rtp <world>` refuses it. If a player has no kit, they are told to run `/kitcreator`. See [Common problems](/docs/exyliasandbox/troubleshooting).

## Going further

### Copy a template instead of building a kit

Admins build templates in `/sandboxadmin` → **Predefined kits**. Players copy one with `/predefinedkits`: click the template, then click one of their own slots. It overwrites whatever was in the slot.

### Use a teleport pad

A teleport pad sends the player in with their **Favorite** kit and shows no kit menu. A player with no favourite is told so and stays where they are. See [TP regions](/docs/exyliasandbox/tp-regions).

### Put the numbers on a lobby board

`%exyliasandbox_total_players%` and `%exyliasandbox_queue_total%` show players inside and waiting. See [Placeholders](/docs/exyliasandbox/placeholders).

<Details title="Technical details">
Kit slots per player: the highest `exyliasandbox.max_kits.<n>` node counts; with none, one slot. Players inside a sandbox world or queue can only run the commands in `config.yml` under `command-whitelist.commands`, unless they have `exyliasandbox.bypass.blocked-commands`. Importing a world with **Import World** asks for a folder name, checks it contains `level.dat`, and the plugin never changes or deletes its terrain afterwards.
</Details>
