`/sandboxadmin` opens every admin screen of ExyliaSandBox in one place: worlds, kits, teleport pads, the lobby, the in-game settings and reload. This page is the map; each area has its own guide.

## Before you start

You need `exyliasandbox.admin`. Every button behind the panel re-checks it, so a player who somehow reaches a screen still cannot press anything.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Run `/sandboxadmin`. The hub shows eight buttons: **Worlds**, **Kit room**, **Predefined kits**, **TP regions**, **Set lobby**, **Set kit creator**, **Settings** and **Reload**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the area">
    - **Worlds**: create, import, tune and delete worlds. See [Worlds](/docs/exyliasandbox/worlds).
    - **Kit room**: ExyliaLib's editor for the shelf players take items from. See [Kit room](/docs/exyliasandbox/kit-room#editing-it).
    - **Predefined kits**: the admin kit templates. See [Kits](/docs/exyliasandbox/kits#predefined-kits). `/sandboxadmin predefinedkits` opens the list directly.
    - **TP regions**: the teleport pads. See [Teleport pads](/docs/exyliasandbox/tp-regions).
    - **Set lobby** and **Set kit creator**: see below.
    - **Settings** and **Reload**: see below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/sandboxadmin` as an admin and click **Worlds**: your worlds appear, the ready ones glowing. Without the permission nothing opens.

## Going further

### Set the lobby and the kit creator room

**Set lobby** and **Set kit creator** do not save your position on the click. Each starts an ExyliaLib wizard: walk to the spot and sneak-click to confirm it with the way you face, or cancel to return to the panel. Players return to the lobby when they die, leave or disconnect.

### Settings

**Settings** opens a hub with two pages. Each card shows the value and its default; clicking edits it (numbers and times are typed, shift-right-click resets). Everything else in `config.yml` stays in the file.

| Page | Cards |
|---|---|
| Teleports | Pair distance, Search attempts, Border margin, Request timeout |
| Rules | Re-kit cooldown, Command filter, Allowed commands |

The cards edit `rtp.queue-pair-distance`, `rtp.search-attempts`, `rtp.border-margin`, `tpa.request-timeout-seconds`, `rekit.cooldown-seconds`, `command-whitelist.enabled` and `command-whitelist.commands`. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliasandbox/configuration).

### Reload

**Reload**, or `/sandboxadmin reload` (also from the console), re-reads `config.yml`, `messages.yml` and `scoreboards.yml`, refreshes the scoreboard and recompiles the menus. It acts at once, with no confirmation. Worlds, kits, pads and statistics are not touched: they live in the database. It reloads only ExyliaSandBox; ExyliaLib has its own reload.

<Callout type="warning" title="Admin screens are rewritten on every start">
Everything under `lang/<language>/menus/admin/` is regenerated from the jar each time the plugin enables, and each file says so at the top. Edit `menus/user/` freely; edits under `menus/admin/` are undone at the next restart. See [Edit menus](/docs/exylialib/edit-menus).
</Callout>

<Details title="Technical details">
- Worlds list: each row shows the id, whether it is managed or imported, its status, the players inside, the biome and the border radius. Left-click opens the world screen; right-click teleports you in without claiming a session. **Create World** and **Import World** start chat wizards and end on the world screen with nothing written until **Save**. **Reset to defaults** restores the five shipped worlds through a confirmation.
- World screen buttons: Icon, Name, Biome and Generation (not available on imported worlds), Border, Gamerules, Teleport, Players, Save, and a delete slot behind a confirmation. Players shows one head per player; clicking one teleports you to them. Details are in [Worlds](/docs/exyliasandbox/worlds).
- `/sandboxadmin` needs `exyliasandbox.admin` for every subcommand, including `reload`.
</Details>
