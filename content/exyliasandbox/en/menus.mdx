Twenty menu files ship per language, under `lang/<language>/menus/`, in two folders that behave
differently.

| Folder | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `menus/user/` | Yours to edit. A missing file is written back and a new key is added; a changed default waits in `/exylialib updates`, and anything you edited is left alone |
| `menus/admin/` | **Rewritten from the jar on every start.** Each file says so at the top |

## The player screens

### `user/world_selector.yml`

Opened by `/rtp` and `/rtpqueue`. A paginated list of every configured world.

Each row's **stack size is the number of players in that world**, and the glow is on only when it is
`READY`. Clicking a world runs an action the row carries: the RTP branch or the queue branch. A world
that is not ready carries an empty action, so the click does nothing at all.

### `user/kit_selector.yml`

One file, five uses: picking a kit for `/rtp`, for a queue, for the fast queue, for `/back` and for a
`/tpa`. Only kits with contents are listed, sorted by slot. What the click does is decided by whichever
flow opened it.

### `user/kit_creator.yml`

`/kitcreator`. The player's slots, with the title showing `<used>/<max>`. **+ NEW KIT** takes the first
free slot; **PREDEFINED KITS** opens the templates; a saved kit opens its options.

### `user/kit_options.yml`

One kit: Edit, Favorite, Rename, Change Icon, Delete, and a preview of the kit itself.

### `user/predefined_kits.yml`

The admin templates that have contents. Clicking one opens the copy screen.

### `user/predefined_kit_copy.yml`

One row per slot the player is allowed, drawn from two templates: an empty slot in green, and a taken
slot in amber, glowing, showing the kit already there and saying it will be overwritten. Clicking
copies.

## The admin screens

### `admin/admin.yml`

The hub. See [The admin panel](/docs/exyliasandbox/admin-panel).

### `admin/world_list.yml`

Every world, paginated, with its type, status, player count, biome and border radius. Left-click
configures, right-click teleports. **CREATE WORLD**, **IMPORT WORLD** and **Reset to Defaults** sit at
the bottom.

### `admin/world_config.yml`

One world's draft: icon, name, biome, generation preset, border, gamerules, teleport, players, save and
delete. Nothing is written until **SAVE WORLD**.

### `admin/world_settings.yml`

The difficulty cycle and thirteen gamerule toggles, each with its own icon and an `ENABLED` /
`DISABLED` state.

### `admin/world_players.yml`

One head per player in the world, showing game mode, health, level and coordinates. Clicking one
teleports you to them.

### `admin/tp_region_list.yml`

Every teleport pad, with its world, interval, target and spread. Left-click edits, right-click
deletes through a confirmation screen. **CREATE TP REGION** at the bottom.

### `admin/tp_region_editor.yml`

One pad: name, bounds, target world, interval, spread radius, save, and a delete in the last slot — also
through a confirmation screen.

### `admin/predefined_kit_list.yml`

The templates. Clicking one opens its editor; **+ CREATE KIT** starts a new one.

### `admin/predefined_kit_editor.yml`

One template: change icon, rename, description, edit contents, and delete through a confirmation.

### `admin/predefined_practice_kits.yml`

Every enabled ExyliaPracticeCore kit, one click from being linked to the template being edited, or
unlinked again. See [Kits](/docs/exyliasandbox/kits#linking-a-practicecore-kit).

### `admin/settings.yml`, `admin/settings_teleports.yml`, `admin/settings_rules.yml`

The settings hub and its two pages. See [The admin panel](/docs/exyliasandbox/admin-panel#settings).

### `admin/confirm.yml`

The shared confirmation screen. Five things go through it: resetting the worlds to defaults, deleting a
world, deleting a predefined kit, deleting a teleport pad and deleting a saved kit.

## The SCREEN style

Under ExyliaLib's SCREEN menu style, the world selector and the kit screens open as one on-screen page
instead of chest windows: `screens/sandbox.html`, a side bar with **Worlds**, **Your kits** and **Kit
library**, and a body wearing the kit under the cursor. It stands for `user/world_selector.yml`,
`kit_selector.yml`, `kit_creator.yml`, `kit_options.yml`, `predefined_kits.yml` and
`predefined_kit_copy.yml`, which supply its rows and values. Its words live in
`lang/<language>/screens/sandbox.yml`, its look in `screens/theme.css`; put your own rules in a
`custom.css` beside it and the shipped files keep updating. The kit room and the kit editor stay chest
windows. A file that cannot be read keeps the chest menus and logs why.

## Actions you can use elsewhere

Four actions are registered and used by none of the shipped screens, so they are free for your own
menus, hotbar items or NPCs:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| `sandbox:open_world_selector` | Opens the world list in RTP mode |
| `sandbox:open_kitroom` | Opens the kit room |
| `sandbox:join_no_kit <world>` | Joins that world's queue with no kit chosen. The player still needs one saved |
| `sandbox:leave_queue` | Leaves the queue |
