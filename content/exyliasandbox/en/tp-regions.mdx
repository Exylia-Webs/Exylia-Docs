A teleport pad is a box you draw in the lobby. Players stand in it, a countdown runs, and at zero everybody inside is sent into a sandbox world together, spread on a circle and facing the middle. It is the no-command way in: a player with a favourite kit never types anything.

## Before you start

- The lobby is set and your worlds exist. See [Your first sandbox](/docs/exyliasandbox/first-steps).
- Players need a **Favorite** kit. The pad never opens a kit menu; it uses that kit only. See [Kits](/docs/exyliasandbox/kits).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    Run `/sandboxadmin` and click **TP regions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the pad">
    Click **Create TP region**. Type a name in chat, then you get a selection wand: click the two opposite corners of the box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the target">
    Click **Target world** to cycle through `Random` and each of your worlds. `Random` picks a ready world every time the countdown fires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the countdown">
    Click **Interval** and type a time in chat: `30s`, `5m` or `1h30m`. The minimum is 5 seconds. A new pad starts at 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the spread">
    Click **Spread radius**: left-click adds one block, right-click removes one (never below one). It is the radius of the circle players land on. A new pad starts at 3.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Leaving with **Back** discards everything you changed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To move the box later, open the pad from the list (left-click) and click **Bounds**; it gives you the wand again. If another Exylia plugin is already holding your block selection, finish or cancel that one first.

## Check that it works

Stand in the box. A hologram appears over its centre, plus a boss bar and a title counting down. At zero you land in a sandbox world wearing your favourite kit, and everybody else on the pad lands around you.

## Going further

### Who the pad skips

| Skipped | What they see |
|---|---|
| Players in the kit creator | Nothing; they are mid-build |
| Players waiting in a queue | Nothing; they are already on their way in |
| Players busy in another Exylia activity | A "not available" message, and the countdown shows again |
| Players with no favourite kit | A message asking them to set one in kit options; they stay put |

If the pad has a target world and it is not ready, nobody is sent and everyone is told there is no world available. It does not fall back to a random world. With `Random`, any ready world is picked.

### Protection

Inside the box building and breaking are refused and PvP is allowed. A player who logs in already standing on the pad is counted straight away.

### Delete a pad

Right-click it in the list, or use the last button of the editor. Both ask you to confirm first.

## The visuals

The text is in `messages.yml` under `tp-region`; the hologram's look stays in `config.yml` under `tp-region`. The bar and the title are ordinary effect blocks, so you can add a sound or particle to either. Colours and effects are explained in [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

| Key | What it is |
|---|---|
| `messages.yml` → `tp-region.bossbar` | The bar each occupant sees (colour, overlay, text) |
| `messages.yml` → `tp-region.countdown-title` | The title and subtitle, re-sent every second |
| `messages.yml` → `tp-region.hologram-lines` | The floating text, top line first |
| `config.yml` → `tp-region.hologram` | Scale, billboard, view distance, offsets and refresh |

Inside these, `%region%` is the pad's name, `%world%` the target world, `%time%` the seconds left and `%total_players%` the players **on that pad** (not the players in the sandbox, which is what the placeholder of the same name means elsewhere). `tp-region.hologram.config.update-interval` is in ticks; the shipped `20` is once a second.

<Details title="Technical details">
- Storage: one row per pad in `sandbox_tp_regions` (twelve-character id, display name, interval in seconds, target world id (empty means random), spread radius, world and six coordinates). `worlds-scope` in `config.yml` decides which servers share these rows; see [Configuration](/docs/exyliasandbox/configuration).
- Entry detection uses ExyliaLib's region registry. Players already inside when the plugin loads are counted as having entered.
- The pad's name keeps your own colour formatting.
- Messages you can edit: `no-favorite-kit`, `teleported`, `no-world`, `no-location`, `wand-given`, `selection-busy`, `region-created`, `region-saved`, `region-deleted`.
- Only players with `exyliasandbox.admin` can use the editor buttons.
</Details>
