A kit is a whole inventory a player saved: storage, armour and offhand. It sits in a numbered slot per player and dresses them the moment they enter a sandbox world. This page covers building one, how many slots a player gets, and the templates an admin publishes for them to copy.

## Before you start

- The lobby and the kit creator room are set. See [Your first sandbox](/docs/exyliasandbox/first-steps).
- The player is standing in the lobby world: the creator refuses to open from anywhere else, or while the player is already in a sandbox.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the kit creator">
    Run `/kitcreator`. The title shows `<used>/<max>`, the slots used and the slots the player may have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a kit">
    Click **Create Kit** and type a name in chat. The plugin takes the first free slot, teleports the player to the kit creator room and empties their inventory. The real inventory is held aside and given back on the way out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take gear from the kit room">
    The [kit room](/docs/exyliasandbox/kit-room) opens by itself five ticks after arriving; `/kitroom` opens it again. Click an item to take a copy, and arrange your inventory, armour and offhand as you want them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Run `/kitsave`. Everything the player wears and carries is written to the slot, and they go back to the lobby with their own inventory. Nothing is saved until they run it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Run `/kitcreator` again: the kit is listed with its name. Run `/rtp`; with one usable kit you go straight in wearing it. The first kit a player ever saves becomes their favourite automatically.

<Callout type="warning" title="The creator is a mode, not a menu">
While in the kit creator a player cannot `/rtp`, join a queue, `/back`, `/rekit`, use `/tpa`, be picked up by a teleport pad or `/leavesandbox`. Disconnecting releases the claim and gives their inventory back.
</Callout>

## Going further

### Give a rank more kit slots

The number of slots is a permission tier. The plugin walks down from `exyliasandbox.max_kits.100` to `exyliasandbox.max_kits.1` and takes the first node the player holds; with none, they get **one**. The highest node wins, so grant exactly one per group. The limit applies to the title counter, to **Create Kit** (it refuses with a "maximum number of kits" message when every slot is taken) and to the copy screen, which draws exactly that many rows. See [Permissions](/docs/exyliasandbox/permissions).

### Edit, rename, favourite or delete a kit

Click a saved kit in `/kitcreator` to open its options.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Re-enters the creator wearing that kit; `/kitsave` writes it back |
| **Favorite** | Toggles the favourite star |
| **Rename** | Chat prompt |
| **Icon** | Chat prompt for a material name |
| **Delete** | Removes the kit after a confirmation. Refused for the kit being edited |

The preview in the middle shows the kit as it will be applied.

<Callout type="note" title="The favourite is a toggle, and only one kit can hold it">
Setting a favourite clears the star from every other kit of that player. Clicking the star on the current favourite turns it off and leaves them with **none**, which is what a teleport pad refuses on.
</Callout>

### Which kit is used, when

| Entry | Kit |
|---|---|
| `/rtp`, `/rtpqueue`, `/rtpqueuefast`, `/back`, `/tpa` with two or more usable kits | The one they pick from the kit list |
| Any of those with exactly one usable kit | That one, with no menu |
| Any of those with none | Refused with a line telling them to run `/kitcreator` |
| Any of those from **inside** a sandbox world | The kit they are already wearing |
| A teleport pad | The **favourite**, and nothing else |
| `/rekit` | The kit they are already wearing, re-applied |

Only kits with contents are listed. An empty slot is never offered.

## Predefined kits

A predefined kit is an admin-built template stored server-wide with an id, a name, a description and an icon. Players never wear one directly: they **copy** it into one of their own slots, overwriting what was there.

### Publish one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    Run `/sandboxadmin` and click **Predefined kits** (or run `/sandboxadmin predefinedkits`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the kit">
    Click **Create Kit** and type a name. The id is a slug of that name and never changes on rename. You enter the same kit creator players use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and save">
    Take gear from the kit room and run `/kitsave`. You return to the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Polish it">
    Click the kit to open its editor: **Edit contents** re-enters the creator, **Rename**, **Icon** and **Description** are chat prompts, and **Delete** asks for a confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How players copy one

`/predefinedkits` (or **Predefined kits** in `/kitcreator`) opens the list. Clicking a template shows the player's own slots: green and empty, or amber and glowing with a kit already there ("Click to overwrite it"). Clicking a slot copies the template in. `/predefinedkits <kit> <slot>` does the same in one line, with no screens.

### Linking a PracticeCore kit

With ExyliaPracticeCore installed, the template editor has a **PracticeCore kit** button. It lists every enabled PracticeCore kit; clicking one links it, clicking the linked one unlinks it. A linked template shows and copies PracticeCore's items as they are right now, nothing is copied into this plugin, and **Edit contents** refuses with a line saying to edit it in PracticeCore. Unlinking brings back the template's own items, which were never touched. Without PracticeCore the button says it is not installed, and a linked template resolves to an empty kit that no player can copy.

<Details title="What is stored">
One row per kit in `sandbox_player_kits`, keyed `<uuid>:<slot>`, holding the storage contents, the armour, the offhand, the name, the icon and the favourite flag. Predefined kits have the same shape in `sandbox_predefined_kits`, keyed by their slug; the PracticeCore link is its `practice_kit` column.

Kits are cached per player and written back when they disconnect. The active slot a player is wearing is **not** stored: a restart leaves nobody wearing anything.
</Details>
