The kit room is a shelf. A player opens it while building a kit, clicks an item, and gets a **copy** —
the shelf itself never empties and never changes.

`/kitroom` opens it, `/kitroom <category>` opens it on one category, and it opens by itself five ticks
after a player enters the kit creator.

<Callout type="note" title="It is ExyliaLib's kit room">
Since 1.2.x the shelf, its editor and its storage belong to ExyliaLib's kit room module; ExyliaSandBox
only seeds it and decides where it opens. ExyliaSandBox's own `user/kitroom.yml` and the two
`admin/kitroom_category_*.yml` screens no longer exist, and the room is always a chest window, even
under the SCREEN menu style: the player builds in their own inventory.
</Callout>

## The screen

The categories are tabs along the bottom, and the items of the current tab fill the rows above. A
category's tab is drawn with its first item.

## What a click takes

| Click | What you get |
|---|---|
| Left or right click | One stack, as stored |
| Shift + left or right click | A full stack, up to the material's maximum |
| F | The item into your offhand; whatever was there comes back to your inventory |

A full inventory refuses with a short line in chat. Clicks are rate-limited to one every 150 ms, so a held
click cannot fill the inventory in a blink. Players can take as many copies as they like: the room limits
which items exist, not how many.

## The shipped categories

The first time the plugin runs with an empty room, it is seeded with five categories holding roughly 130
pre-enchanted items between them.

| Id | Name | Icon | What is in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `armaduras` | Armor | Netherite chestplate | Netherite and diamond gear with Protection IV, Unbreaking III, Mending; Sharpness V swords, tridents with Impaling V and Loyalty III, maces, elytra |
| `consumibles` | Consumables | Golden apple | Golden and enchanted golden apples, pearls, cobwebs, XP bottles, water buckets, totems, obsidian, end crystals, respawn anchors, glowstone |
| `explosivos` | Explosives | TNT | TNT, TNT minecarts, fireworks, wind charges, breeze rods |
| `pociones` | Potions | Splash potion | Twenty-three splash potions |
| `flechas` | Arrows | Tipped arrow | Twenty-three tipped-arrow types, plus spectral and normal arrows |

<Callout type="note" title="The shipped ids are in Spanish">
`armaduras`, `consumibles`, `explosivos`, `pociones` and `flechas` are the ids the plugin seeds; the
display names are English, and a server seeded before 1.1.0 keeps its Spanish ones. Rename the display
names from the editor at any time; the ids are what `/kitroom <category>` takes and they do not change
with a rename.
</Callout>

## Editing it

`/sandboxadmin` → **KIT ROOM** opens ExyliaLib's staff editor: a list of the categories, where you create,
rename and delete them. Editing a category asks for its name and then opens its items in the library's
item list editor; the back button returns to the admin panel. The editor and its screens are the
library's, so their wording and layout follow ExyliaLib, not this plugin's `lang/` files. **Reset to
Defaults** no longer exists; an admin who empties the room on purpose gets the seed again on the next start.

## What is stored

The room lives in ExyliaLib's table `exylia_kitroom`, in this plugin's database, and is held in memory.

A server that ran the older shelf keeps its categories: the first time the new table is empty, the rows of
`sandbox_kitroom_categories` and `sandbox_kitroom_items` are copied in, categories in their priority order
and items in slot order. The old rows are left where they are and never written again. Two things do not
carry over: a category's own icon (the tab shows its first item) and the gaps between items.
