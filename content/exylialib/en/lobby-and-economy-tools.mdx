description: "Hotbars, double jump and jump pads, dialogues, the kit room and the economy ledger."

Small services that several plugins share. Hotbars, movement and dialogues are for lobbies and story content; the kit room
and the ledger serve PvP and economy plugins.

## Hotbars

A lobby hotbar (1.559.0): named sets of buttons drawn in a player's inventory, each with its slot, look, clicks, permission
and cooldown, frozen in place. `net.exylia.lib.hotbar.Hotbars`.

```java
hotbars = Hotbars.of(this, actions)                  // actions: the namespace the lines compile in
        .register(HotbarSet.of("lobby", List.of(
                HotbarItem.of("selector", 0, selectorItem)
                        .withActions(List.of("right: lobby:selector"))
                        .withCooldown(Duration.ofMillis(500)),
                HotbarItem.of("cosmetics", 4, cosmeticsItem)
                        .withPermission("lobby.cosmetics", lockedCosmeticsItem)   // null: hidden instead
                        .withCommands(List.of("right: player: cosmetics")))))
        .onCooldown((player, left) -> messages.send(player, "hotbar-wait", left));

hotbars.show(player, "lobby");    // entering the lobby
hotbars.hide(player);             // leaving it
hotbars.refresh(player);          // a permission or a button's state changed
```

- A button's `actions` and `commands` are click-prefixed lines as a menu or overlay item writes them (`right:`, `left:`,
  `shift_right:`, `drop:`, `swap:`, no prefix for every click). Every line compiles at `register`; an unregistered action
  fails there.
- `HotbarSet.of(name, items)` is the lobby default: every slot frozen, the rest blank, no pickups, never redrawn.
  `withInventory(lock, hideRest, pickup)` and `withRefresh(UiRefresh)` change it.
- Built on [overlays](/docs/exylialib/overlays): nothing is written into the real inventory, so a crash can never leave a
  working button behind as a real item. It needs PacketEvents (`Hotbars.isAvailable()`).
- **A button is drawn, not held.** Hitting with one hits like a bare hand. Combat gear must be real items the plugin gives.
- A cooldown (through the shared cooldowns) plays the refusal sound and calls `onCooldown`. A button with a permission is
  drawn only for holders; the others see its `locked` look or an empty slot. Permissions are re-read every two seconds.

## Movement

A lobby's two ways of getting around faster (1.559.0): `DoubleJump` and `JumpPads` in `net.exylia.lib.movement`.

```java
doubleJump = DoubleJump.of(this)
        .forward(0.9).vertical(0.75)              // the defaults
        .cooldown(Duration.ofSeconds(1))
        .permission("lobby.doublejump")           // optional
        .when(player -> lobby.contains(player))
        .start();

pads = JumpPads.of(this).cooldown(Duration.ofSeconds(1)).when(lobby::contains).pads(settings.pads()).start();
pads.put(JumpPad.of("east", PadTrigger.at(target.getLocation()), 1.6, 0.9).towards(-90f));
```

- **Double jump** uses the flight toggle: an eligible player on the ground may fly, and pressing jump in the air is cancelled
  and turned into the launch. It only touches its own flight (creative, spectator and `/fly` are left alone), takes it off
  when the player stops being eligible, and cancels the fall damage of the landing. The predicate runs on every block crossed
  on the player's thread: read memory.
- **Jump pads** fire from a `PadTrigger`: `at(location)`, `materials(pad[, below])` or `plates(below)`. `JumpPad(id, trigger,
  power, vertical, yaw, effect)` throws the player the way they look, or at a fixed yaw with `towards(yaw)`.
- The pads are the plugin's data: nothing is saved here. Hand the list in at start and after every edit (`put`, `remove`,
  `set`); `padAt(location)` answers "which pad is this?".
- Creative and spectator players, players failing the permission or predicate and anyone in the cooldown are never thrown.

## Dialogues

RPG conversations in a box at the bottom of the screen (1.590.0), with no resource pack: the speaker's name and face, each line
typed out, answers under the last line. `net.exylia.lib.dialogue`.

```java
Component guide = Text.of("{highlight}Guide").build();
Dialogue talk = Dialogue.of(List.of(
                Dialogue.Line.of(Text.of("{letters}Welcome, traveller.").build()).speaker(guide).face("Notch"),
                Dialogue.Line.of(Text.of("{letters}Shall I show you around?").build()).speaker(guide).face("Notch")))
        .answers(List.of(Text.of("{letters}Lead the way").build(), Text.of("{letters}Not now").build()))
        .onLine(index -> playVoice(player, index));

Dialogues.of(this).open(player, talk).thenAccept(result -> {
    if (result.finished() && result.answer() == 0) tour(player);
});
```

| `mode` | Where | How the player answers |
|---|---|---|
| `HUD` (default) | a [HUD](/docs/exylialib/hud) widget over the hotbar | They keep walking. A sneak or click goes on (the first press finishes the line); under the last line the wheel moves the choice, a number key picks, a sneak or click confirms. |
| `SCREEN` | a [screen](/docs/exylialib/screens) with a cursor | They stand still. A click on the box goes on; under the last line they click an answer. Closing cancels. |

- One conversation per player across plugins; opening another, `Dialogues.close(player)`, quitting or dying ends the first
  with `Result.CANCELLED`.
- `onLine(index)` runs on the player's thread as each line starts: sounds, actions, a camera cue.
- Bedrock, a server without PacketEvents, and (in `HUD` mode) a player who hid their HUD read every line in chat and answer in
  a choice input.
- `width` (pixels the text wraps at, 220) and `speed` (letters a second, 40; 0 shows lines whole); `hints(...)` replaces the
  small line under the box.

## Kit room

Categories of items players take copies of to build their own kits (1.511.0). Staff fill the room in game; every click hands
a copy into the player's inventory; the plugin checks what they built before saving, so a kit only holds what the room offered.

```java
KitRoom room = KitRooms.of(plugin);
room.edit(admin, () -> AdminHub.open(admin));        // staff: categories and their items
room.open(player, List.of("gear", "potions"));      // players: take copies; empty list = every category
if (!room.allows(items, List.of("gear", "potions"))) refuse();   // before saving a build
```

- Per plugin: each keeps its own categories in its own database (`exylia_kitroom`, `DataKind.SETUP`). An empty room starts
  with defaults (gear in three tiers, consumables, splash potions and arrows).
- A click takes one stack, shift a full stack, F puts it in the off hand. Amounts are not checked: the room limits *which*
  items, not how many.
- `KitRooms.of(plugin, seed)` fills the room from `seed` the first time its table is empty, to move a room a plugin kept itself.
- Always a chest window, in the SCREEN menu style too. Saving a build, and where it is built, is the plugin's job.

## Ledger

How much money the server's plugins created and took out of the economy, per hour, plugin and source (1.478.0).
`net.exylia.lib.ledger.Ledger`.

```java
Ledger ledger = Ledger.of(this);
Ledger.created(this, "daily-reward", player.getUniqueId(), amount);
Ledger.sunk(this, "order-tax", player.getUniqueId(), tax);

long now = Ledger.currentHour();
ledger.top(Ledger.Flow.CREATED, null, now - 24, now + 1, 10).thenAccept(top -> ...);   // top earners, last day
```

- **Created** is money that enters from nothing (a job payout, a sell to the server); **sunk** is money that leaves (a tax, a
  fee). A transfer between players is neither. Every `Modifiers.pay` that deposits is recorded as created, under the calling
  plugin; sinks are recorded with `Ledger.sunk`.
- Reads: `day(day)`, `hour(hour)` (every `Entry`, all servers), `top(flow, currency, fromHour, toHour, limit)` (at most 31 days),
  `player(uuid, flow, currency, from, to)` and the per-plugin overload that a plugin caps its own payouts on.
- Nothing is recorded until a plugin opens the ledger. Counts are kept in memory and written every minute with atomic
  increments, so servers sharing a database add up. Amounts keep two decimals. Rows older than 90 days are purged.
- Tables `exylia_ledger` and `exylia_ledger_players` (`HISTORY`, owned by `player`) are covered by `/exylialib wipe`.
