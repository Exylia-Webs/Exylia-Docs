---

Newer modules for screens drawn in the world and for small services plugins share. All are packet-only or
server-side: no resource pack.

| Module | Entry point | Since | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spatial | `SpatialMenus.of(plugin)` | 1.406.0 | In-world 3D menus: glass panels, lists, grids, a carousel of plinths and a live preview, laid out in YAML and moved in game. Looked at to aim, clicked to choose, scrolled to page, sneaked out of. Includes `SpatialCatalog`, a cosmetic shop and wardrobe. |
| Stage | `Stage` | 1.356.0 | Per-player screens built from packet displays: a board in front of a seat, buttons picked by looking, a dark room round the player, a countdown with boss bars, cards and dice. |
| Crate scene | `CrateScene` | 1.418.0 | The in-world crate opening, turned on per crate with `crate.opening: SPATIAL` (default `INVENTORY`): a reel slows onto the prize, then the prize is tried on where the crate stood, with "open another" and "done". |
| Guide | `Guides.of(plugin)` | 1.391.0 | Showing one player where to go: a companion text ahead of their eyes, beacons, ground trails, locator-bar and modded waypoints, per-viewer glow. |
| Abilities | `Abilities.of(plugin)` | 1.385.0 | Triggered abilities from YAML (trigger, conditions, chance, cooldown, effects scaled by level) with an in-game editor, plus `AreaBreaks` (3x3, 3x3x3, veins, trees). |
| Action bars | `ActionBars.register(plugin, id, priority, supplier)` | 1.323.0 | One action bar line per player shared by every plugin: each plugin hands over a segment, drawn in order and separated by `|`, sent only when it changes. No more flicker between plugins. |
| Build zones | `BuildZones.canBuild(player, block)` | 1.432.0 | One answer to "may this player build, break, use or open this block" across WorldGuard, ExyliaLib regions, ExyliaProtections, Lands, GriefPrevention, Towny and SuperiorSkyblock2. |
| Runtime commands | `RuntimeCommands.of(plugin)` | 1.333.0 | Commands named by a plugin's data (a shop opened with `/blocks`, a currency with `/gems`), installed into the command map and replaced as a set. |

## Notes

- **Build zones** is for actions outside a block event (a menu opening a block's storage, a remote sell). Inside
  a block event at `ignoreCancelled = true` the claim plugins have already spoken. A server owner can limit
  which sources count; `deniedBy` reports which one refused.
- **Runtime commands**: `install(commands)` replaces everything the plugin installed before. A name another
  plugin holds still answers under the plugin's prefix.
- **Action bars**: call `ActionBars.refresh(player)` when a segment changed and should not wait for the next
  refresh.
- **Guides, stages and spatial menus** need PacketEvents, like the rest of the library's packet features.
