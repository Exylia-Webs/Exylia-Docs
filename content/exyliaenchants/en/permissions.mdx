ExyliaEnchants has one permission node. Players need none: the catalogue, the apply screen, the enchanter and the settings (`/enchants`) are open to everyone.

<Permissions />

Give it to a group with your permissions plugin. With LuckPerms: `/lp group admin permission set exyliaenchants.admin true`. Staff with it can also open every admin screen and settings page.

## Limit an enchant to a rank

Permissions for gameplay are not nodes the plugin ships: you name them yourself, in the files.

- In an enchant file, `permission: vip.enchants` makes the enchant work only for holders of that node. Players without it still wear the item and the enchant shows, but its abilities do not run.
- In `rules.yml`, a rule with `permission: vip.enchants` applies only to holders. See [Presets and rules](/docs/exyliaenchants/presets-and-rules).

Both are checked when an ability would run, so a change needs no restart.

<Callout type="note" title="The console and crates">
`/enchantsadmin give ... -book` from the console needs no permission, because the console has all of them. Give books to players from crates and rewards this way: see [Commands](/docs/exyliaenchants/commands).
</Callout>
