Every enchant has one rarity. The rarity does not change how strong the enchant is; it sets defaults for where the enchant is found, what it costs on the anvil, how much of the [power budget](/docs/exyliaenchants/power-budget) it uses and the odds of its book. An enchant can override any of these in its own file.

ExyliaEnchants 1.1.0 ships 188 enchants: 39 common, 65 rare, 53 epic, 23 legendary and 8 curses.

## Defaults by rarity

| Rarity | Weight | Anvil cost | Table cost (min / max) | Enchanting table | Librarians | Treasure only | Power per level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 10 | 1 | 1 + 10 per level / 21 + 10 | Yes | Yes | No | 1 |
| Rare | 5 | 2 | 15 + 9 / 45 + 9 | Yes | Yes | No | 2 |
| Epic | 2 | 4 | 25 + 8 / 65 + 8 | No | No | No | 3 |
| Legendary | 1 | 8 | 30 + 10 / 80 + 10 | No | No | Yes | 5 |
| Curse | 1 | 1 | 25 / 50 | No | No | Yes | 0 |

Epic and legendary enchants are not in the table or at librarians: players get them from loot, the [enchanter](/docs/exyliaenchants/the-enchanter) and staff. Whether loot holds them is each enchant's `loot` key.

## Book odds by rarity

A book applied through the economy rolls its success and destroy chances inside these ranges. The range is where the odds sit before a player improves them with magic dust. See [Books and applying](/docs/exyliaenchants/books-and-applying).

| Rarity | Success | Destroy |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 80-100% | 0-10% |
| Rare | 60-90% | 5-20% |
| Epic | 40-80% | 10-30% |
| Legendary | 25-60% | 15-40% |
| Curse | 100% | 0% |

You change these in `economy.yml` under `odds`.

## Colours and labels

The names players read are in `messages.yml` under `labels`, with the palette colours the server uses: common `{letters}`, rare `{info}`, epic `{secondary}`, legendary `{highlight}` and curse `{error}`. Change the words there and every screen follows. See [Colours](/docs/exylialib/colours).

## Curses

A curse has power 0 and costs nothing on the budget, and the grindstone does not remove it by default. The eight curses are Curse of Frailty, Hunger, Recoil, Clumsiness, Glass, Noise, Scarcity and Instability. They are treasure, so players meet them as loot and, with `curse-on-fail`, as the price of a failed book. See [Power budget and curses](/docs/exyliaenchants/power-budget).
