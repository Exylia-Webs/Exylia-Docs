Every ExyliaEnchants command, with the permission it needs. `/enchants` is for players and needs no permission. `/enchantsadmin` is for staff and needs `exyliaenchants.admin`. Enchant names and player names complete as you type.

<Commands />

## Notes

- `/enchantsadmin give` puts the enchant on the item the player holds. With `-book` it gives a book instead, and `-safe` gives a guaranteed book (100% success, 0% destroy) and implies `-book`. `--book` works too. An unknown option is refused and the message lists the ones the command takes. The level must be from 1 to the enchant's `max-level`.
- The `-book` form works from the console, which is how a crate or a reward gives a book. An enchant with `books: false` is refused from the console. Run by a player, it warns you and gives the book anyway.
- An enchant you just added to a file is not in the game until the next restart: `give` tells you so. See [Add your own enchant](/docs/exyliaenchants/custom-enchants).
- `/enchantsadmin purge <enchant>` strips the enchant from online players now (inventory and ender chest) and from every other item as it loads. The key stays registered; it is how you remove an enchant for good. Running it twice is refused.
- `/enchantsadmin reload` reads `config.yml`, `economy.yml`, `rules.yml`, `integrations.yml`, `balance.yml`, `menus.yml`, `sets.yml`, `sequences.yml`, the messages and every enchant file again, and shows how many problems it found. The console says which. Some enchant keys need a restart: see [Configuration](/docs/exyliaenchants/configuration#enchant-file).
- The tuner, the dummy and the balance screen are explained in [Tune and balance](/docs/exyliaenchants/balance-tools). The importer is in [Import from another plugin](/docs/exyliaenchants/import).
