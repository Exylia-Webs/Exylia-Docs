Find your symptom, try the fix, and check the console. After any change, `/enchantsadmin reload` reads the files again and reports the problems it found.

## The plugin does not start

ExyliaEnchants needs ExyliaLib. Put `ExyliaLib.jar` in `plugins/` and restart. Run Paper 1.21.4 or newer, or Folia, on Java 21. See [Install ExyliaEnchants](/docs/exyliaenchants/installation).

## A new enchant or a changed value does nothing

- `/enchantsadmin give` says the enchant is new: it is not registered until the next restart.
- Rarity, max level, `applies-to`, costs and the obtaining keys are part of the game's registry and need a restart. Descriptions, `power-cost`, `abilities` and `visuals` reload with `/enchantsadmin reload`.
- Read the console. A mistake costs only that enchant and is logged with the file name. See [Create or change an enchant](/docs/exyliaenchants/custom-enchants).

## An enchant is on the item but does nothing

- It is in `disabled-enchants`, or switched off by the [preset](/docs/exyliaenchants/presets-and-rules) or a rule for this world, region or mode. Items keep a disabled enchant but it stops working.
- It sits in the wrong slot: an enchant only works in the slots it declares (a helmet enchant on a boot does nothing).
- The enchant has its own `worlds`, `regions` or `permission` limits.
- The ability has a chance or a cooldown. Test it on the [training dummy](/docs/exyliaenchants/balance-tools).

## The description and stat lines are missing

The lines under each enchant name need `tooltip.enabled: true` and PacketEvents. A player may also have switched them off in `/enchants settings`: see [Player settings](/docs/exyliaenchants/player-settings). Bedrock players through Geyser need `display-mode: lore`.

## A book is refused

The message names the reason. The book is never spent. See the table in [Books and applying](/docs/exyliaenchants/books-and-applying): wrong item, conflict with another enchant, already at the highest level, a lower level than the item has, or not enough power. For power, raise the limit or lower the costs: [Power budget](/docs/exyliaenchants/power-budget).

## Players cannot buy books

- `economy` is `false`, or the preset is `vanilla-plus`, which turns the economy off.
- `economy.enchanter.enabled` is `false`: players see "The enchanter is closed on this server".
- A card says "Sold out": stock refills every `restock` (10 minutes by default) and resets on restart. See [The enchanter](/docs/exyliaenchants/the-enchanter).
- The player cannot pay: prices are in experience levels unless you set a currency.

## The vanilla /enchant command refuses a custom enchant

`obtaining.vanilla-enchant-command` is `false`. Staff use `/enchantsadmin give`, or set the key to `true`. See [Obtain enchants](/docs/exyliaenchants/obtaining).

## Enchants do not show in the table, trades or loot

The switches `obtaining.enchanting-table`, `villager-trades` and `loot` and the enchant's own `table`, `trade` and `loot` keys must both allow it, and they need a restart. Epic and legendary enchants are not in the table by default.

## Imported items did not convert

The mapping in `import/mapping.yml` must list the enchant, and the target must be registered: restart after an import that translated enchants. See [Import](/docs/exyliaenchants/import).
