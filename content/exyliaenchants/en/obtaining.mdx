Because the enchants are real enchantments registered before the worlds load, the game's own sources already offer them. You decide which sources are on and how often custom enchants show up next to the vanilla ones.

## The sources

| Source | Which enchants | Switch |
|---|---|---|
| Enchanting table | Those with `table: true` (common and rare by default) | `obtaining.enchanting-table` |
| Librarians | Those with `trade: true` (common and rare by default) | `obtaining.villager-trades` |
| Loot chests, fishing treasure, random enchanting | Those with `loot: true` | `obtaining.loot` |
| Anvil books | Merging a book onto an item | `obtaining.anvil-books` |
| Grindstone | Removes enchants with `grindstone: true` | `obtaining.grindstone-removal` |
| Vanilla `/enchant` | Any custom enchant | `obtaining.vanilla-enchant-command` |
| The enchanter | Books and support items for levels or money | `economy.enchanter.enabled` |
| Staff | `/enchantsadmin give` | none |

All the switches are in `config.yml` and default to `true`. In game, open `/enchantsadmin`, then **Settings** and **Obtaining**. A switch that is off overrides every enchant's own key. When it is on, each enchant's key decides. Per enchant, the same keys live in its file: [Configuration](/docs/exyliaenchants/configuration#enchant-file).

<Callout type="warning" title="Restart for the tag switches">
`enchanting-table`, `villager-trades`, `loot`, `table-share` and `disabled-enchants` (for the tags) are read when the server registers the enchants, so they need a restart. The anvil, grindstone and `/enchant` switches reload with `/enchantsadmin reload`.
</Callout>

## Table share

`obtaining.table-share` is the share of what the enchanting table offers that comes from custom enchants. The default `0.35` means about 35% of the offers for a sword, a pickaxe or a helmet are custom enchants and the rest are vanilla.

At every start the plugin scales the weight of the custom enchants for each kind of item so the share holds, whatever you add. The console prints the factors: `table share 35%: custom weights scaled by 0.19 (swords), ...`. Weights end up between 1 and 1024, and loot and librarian odds of those enchants move with them. Lower it for a mostly vanilla table, raise it for a table full of custom enchants.

## The anvil, the grindstone and `/enchant`

- The anvil refuses a merge that would take an item over its [power budget](/docs/exyliaenchants/power-budget).
- Books from the [enchanter](/docs/exyliaenchants/the-enchanter) never go through the anvil while the economy is on: players apply them by dragging. See [Books and applying](/docs/exyliaenchants/books-and-applying).
- The grindstone removes an enchant when both `obtaining.grindstone-removal` and the enchant's `grindstone` key allow it. Curses are not removed by default.
- With `vanilla-enchant-command: false`, `/enchant` with a custom enchant is refused and the player is told to ask staff, who use `/enchantsadmin give`. The check reads typed commands, the console and command blocks. `/execute ... run enchant` is not parsed.

## Switch one enchant off

Add its id to `disabled-enchants` in `config.yml`, or use the enchant's screen in `/enchantsadmin`. Items keep a disabled enchant but it stops working. To remove it from every item, use `/enchantsadmin purge <enchant>`. See [Commands](/docs/exyliaenchants/commands).
