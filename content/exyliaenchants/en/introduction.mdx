ExyliaEnchants adds 188 custom enchantments to your server. They are real enchantments, registered before the worlds load, so the enchanting table, librarians, loot chests, the anvil and the grindstone treat them like vanilla ones. Players need no resource pack.

In about ten minutes you can have it running and hold your first custom enchant. [Your first enchants](/docs/exyliaenchants/first-steps) walks you through it.

## What you get

<Cards>
  <Card title="Every way to obtain" href="/docs/exyliaenchants/obtaining">Table, librarians, loot, anvil and the enchanter, each with its own switch.</Card>
  <Card title="Books and the enchanter" href="/docs/exyliaenchants/the-enchanter">A shop for books and support items, with odds, protection scrolls and slot gems.</Card>
  <Card title="Power budget" href="/docs/exyliaenchants/power-budget">Each item can only carry so much power, so nobody stacks every enchant.</Card>
  <Card title="Presets and rules" href="/docs/exyliaenchants/presets-and-rules">Switch enchants down or off by world, region or game mode.</Card>
  <Card title="Your own enchants" href="/docs/exyliaenchants/custom-enchants">Write a new enchant, or change a shipped one, in a short YAML section.</Card>
  <Card title="Balance tools" href="/docs/exyliaenchants/balance-tools">A live tuner, a training dummy and a balance screen.</Card>
</Cards>

Every enchant has a rarity ([Rarities](/docs/exyliaenchants/rarities)), abilities and an element. Four armour pieces of one element give a [set bonus](/docs/exyliaenchants/set-bonuses). Players browse everything in `/enchants` and can preview an enchant on a mannequin.

If you come from another plugin, [Import](/docs/exyliaenchants/import) converts AdvancedEnchantments, EcoEnchants and ExcellentEnchants enchants and the items that carry them.

## What you need

- A Paper server (1.21.4 or newer) or Folia, running Java 21.
- The plugin ExyliaLib.

PacketEvents is optional but needed for the extra description and stat lines under each enchant name. [Install it](/docs/exyliaenchants/installation) has the details.

## How these docs are organised

- **Getting started**: install and hold your first custom enchant.
- **Guides**: one page per task.
- **How it works**: rarities and what each sets by default.
- **Reference**: every command, permission, placeholder and setting.
- **Help**: common problems and quick answers.

Colours, messages and languages, menus, economy and the database are shared by every Exylia plugin. They are explained once in the [Customise section](/docs/exylialib/colours).
