The enchanter is a menu where players buy books and support items. Players open it with `/enchants enchanter`, or from the catalogue (`/enchants`). It is part of the book economy: it is available while `economy: true` and `economy.enchanter.enabled: true`.

## What it sells

- **Books**, one card per rarity: Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary. Buying one gives a book with a random enchant of that rarity and a random level from 1 to the enchant's `max-level`. Only enchants with `enchanter: true` that are live are rolled.
- **Support items**, on a second screen: Protection scroll, Magic dust, Transmog scroll, Disenchant scroll and Slot gem. How they work is in [Books and applying](/docs/exyliaenchants/books-and-applying).

Each card shows its price, the stock left, and "Sold out" when none is left. A player who cannot pay or finds it sold out is told so.

## Prices and stock

In `economy.yml`, under `enchanter`:

| Item | Price | Stock |
|---|---|---|
| Common book | 8 levels | 32 |
| Rare book | 16 levels | 16 |
| Epic book | 28 levels | 8 |
| Legendary book | 45 levels | 2 |
| Protection scroll | 20 levels | unlimited (`-1`) |
| Magic dust | 6 levels | unlimited |
| Transmog scroll | 4 levels | unlimited |
| Disenchant scroll | 15 levels | unlimited |
| Slot gem | 30 levels | 4 |

Stock is shared by the whole server, lives in memory and refills every `restock` (default `10m`). Use `-1` for unlimited. A restart resets the stock to its full amount.

The price currency is set per item in the `levels` field:

- `levels`: experience levels, the default.
- an empty value: the server's default currency.
- an ExyliaLib currency id.

Money needs an economy plugin. See [Economy](/docs/exylialib/economy) for how currencies are chosen.

<Callout type="note" title="Prices follow your economy">
Epic and legendary books are the prize of the shop, so they are the scarce ones: two legendary books per restock by default. Raise `stock` for a casual server, lower it for a competitive one.
</Callout>

## Make it yours

The menus are `lang/<code>/menus/user/enchanter.yml` and `enchanter_support.yml`. Their layout, colours and icons follow ExyliaLib: see [Menus](/docs/exylialib/menus). In game, **Settings** then **Economy** in `/enchantsadmin` edits the prices and stock.

To remove the shop and keep books from other sources, set `economy.enchanter.enabled: false`. Players then see "The enchanter is closed on this server".
