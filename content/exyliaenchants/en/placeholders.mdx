ExyliaEnchants registers no `%exyliaenchants_...%` placeholders with PlaceholderAPI. Its values describe one enchant, one book or one hit, so they exist only inside its own messages and screens. Use this page to restyle them. PlaceholderAPI is optional and only lets other plugins' placeholders resolve where the plugin reads text.

Every message is in `lang/<code>/messages.yml`, the screens are in `lang/<code>/menus/`, and the colours are the [ExyliaLib palette](/docs/exylialib/colours). The messages are written once for every language folder: see [Messages and languages](/docs/exylialib/messages-and-languages).

<Details title="Where each group is used">
- **messages**: chat and HUD lines in `messages.yml`. Each message lists its own placeholders in the comment above it.
- **tooltip**: the `tooltip:` section of `messages.yml`. `header` is the first line of each enchant, `description` and `stat` follow, and `loreLine` is the single line written into the lore when `display-mode: lore`.
- **menus**: the catalogue, apply, enchanter and settings screens in `menus/user/`.
- **balance**: the tuner boss bar, the dummy's hit line and the simulation output.
- **import**: the lines `/enchantsadmin import` prints.
</Details>
