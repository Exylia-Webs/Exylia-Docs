The power budget stops an item from carrying every enchant at once. Each enchant costs **power** per level. Each item category has a limit. A book or an anvil merge that would go over the limit is refused.

## How power is counted

An enchant costs its `power-cost` times its level. When it has no `power-cost`, the rarity decides:

| Rarity | Power per level |
|---|---|
| Common | 1 |
| Rare | 2 |
| Epic | 3 |
| Legendary | 5 |
| Curse | 0 |

The default limit per category, in `config.yml` under `budget`:

| Category | Limit | Items |
|---|---|---|
| `weapons` | 12 | Swords, axes, mace, trident, bows, crossbows |
| `armour` | 12 | Helmets, chestplates, leggings, boots, elytra |
| `tools` | 10 | Pickaxes, shovels, hoes, fishing rod, and anything else with durability |

So a diamond sword with Bleed III (epic, 3 levels, 9 power) has 3 power left for a common enchant at level 3. Curses cost nothing by default, and an enchant with a negative `power-cost` gives power back.

## Raise the room

- **Slot gems** add `gem-power` (default `2`) to one item's limit, up to `max-gems` (default `3`) per item. Players buy them at [the enchanter](/docs/exyliaenchants/the-enchanter).
- Change the numbers: `budget.weapons`, `budget.armour` and `budget.tools`, or in game **Settings** then **Power** in `/enchantsadmin`.
- Change one enchant's weight on the budget with `power-cost` in its file (-10 to 50). It reloads live.

Turn the whole thing off with `budget.enabled: false`.

## Where it is enforced

- Applying a book: the message says how much power it needs and how much the item has.
- The anvil: a merge that goes over the limit is refused.

## Curses

Curses are enchants with the `curse` rarity. They cost no power by default, and a curse can set a negative `power-cost` (-1 or -2) to give budget back to the item that carries it. The curses are Curse of Frailty, Hunger, Recoil, Clumsiness, Glass, Noise, Scarcity and Instability. They are treasure and the grindstone does not remove them by default. A player can still get one on purpose from loot or, when `curse-on-fail` is above `0` on the survival preset, as the price of a failed book. See [Books and applying](/docs/exyliaenchants/books-and-applying).

The presets decide whether curses exist on your server: `pvp` removes them. See [Presets and rules](/docs/exyliaenchants/presets-and-rules).
