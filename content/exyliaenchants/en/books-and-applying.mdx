Books are how players put enchants on gear when the economy is on (`economy: true` in `config.yml`, the default). A book carries one enchant and one level.

## Apply a book

Two ways, with the same rules:

- **Drag and drop.** In your own inventory, pick up the book and click an item with it, left or right click.
- **The apply screen.** Run `/enchants apply`, put the book in the left slot and the item in the right slot, then click **Book > Apply**. The screen shows the enchant and its success and destroy odds, and a bar while it rolls.

The target must be a single item that has durability and is not itself a book. A refused book is never spent, and the message says why.

| Refusal | Why |
|---|---|
| Does not go on this item | The enchant's `applies-to` does not include it |
| Conflict | The item already has an enchant that cannot share with it |
| Already at its highest level | The item has the enchant at `max-level` |
| Lower level | The item has a higher level of that enchant |
| Not enough power | The item would go over its [power budget](/docs/exyliaenchants/power-budget) |
| Switched off | The enchant is disabled on this server |

A book of the same level as the item's raises it by one level. A book of a higher level replaces it.

## The roll

The book rolls its **success** chance. On a failure it rolls **destroy**:

- Success: the enchant is applied.
- Failure, not destroyed: the book crumbles and the item is unharmed.
- Failure, destroyed: the item is lost. If the item carries a protection scroll, the scroll burns instead and the item survives.

With `curse-on-fail` (`15.0` percent by default, survival preset only), a failed book can also leave a random curse on the item. Set it to `0` to turn it off.

A book that has no odds, because staff gave it, it came from the table or it was loot, always succeeds. With `book-mode: vanilla` in `economy.yml` every book succeeds. `/enchantsadmin give ... -safe` gives a guaranteed book. Odds by rarity are in [Rarities](/docs/exyliaenchants/rarities#book-odds-by-rarity); change them under `odds` in `economy.yml`.

## Support items

Drag these the same way: magic dust onto a book, the others onto an item. Players buy them at [the enchanter](/docs/exyliaenchants/the-enchanter).

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| Protection scroll | The item survives one failed book that would destroy it. The scroll burns |
| Magic dust | Raises a book's success by `dust-bonus` (10) up to `dust-cap` (100) |
| Slot gem | Adds `gem-power` (2) to the item's power limit, up to `max-gems` (3) per item |
| Transmog scroll | Reorders the item's enchants by rarity, curses last, so the tooltip reads well |
| Disenchant scroll | Asks which enchant to lift off and gives it back as a book. Cancelling refunds the scroll |

Their names and lore are in `lang/<code>/support-items.yml`.

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep failures cheap">
Magic dust on the book and a protection scroll on the item turn a risky epic book into a safe bet. Raise the dust prices if you want players to keep gambling.
</Callout>
