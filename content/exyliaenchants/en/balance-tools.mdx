Four admin tools help you decide whether an enchant is too strong. All need `exyliaenchants.admin`, and their settings are in `balance.yml`.

## The training dummy

`/enchantsadmin dummy` places a mob with no AI at your feet. It wears armour, is healed before every hit and cannot be killed, so a kill ability never pays out on it. Hit it and chat shows the base damage, the extra damage each enchant added, and your damage per second over the last ten seconds. `/enchantsadmin dummy remove` removes it. You have one dummy: placing another replaces it. It lives in memory only.

`balance.yml`, `dummy`: `health` (20), `armour` (a list of armour materials, default diamond), `protection` (4, per piece) and `breakdown` (`chat` or `actionbar`).

## The simulation

`/enchantsadmin dummy simulate <enchant> <level> [hits]` rolls the enchant's ATTACK abilities many times with no server involved. It prints each ability's proc rate and damage, then the enchant's damage share: its part of base plus enchant damage. An enchant with no ATTACK ability is refused.

`balance.yml`, `simulate`: `hits` (10000), `attacks-per-second` (1.6, a sword at full charge) and `base-damage` (7).

## The live tuner

`/enchantsadmin tune [enchant]` turns the chance, the cooldown and the main number of each effect into values you change with the mouse wheel while you test the enchant on the dummy.

- Scroll: change the number. Sneak and scroll: change the step.
- Press the swap-hand key (F): go to the next number.
- Click: finish and keep the numbers.

Changes apply in memory a few ticks after you stop scrolling, so the next hit uses them. The file is written only when you click to keep them, and its comments are kept. Cancelling restores the numbers in the file. After saving, `/enchantsadmin tune <enchant> undo` writes back the numbers the session started with. One admin tunes an enchant at a time.

Each value scales the whole formula, so every level of the enchant moves together.

## The balance screen

`/enchantsadmin balance` lists every live enchant with three numbers, sortable by any of them:

- **Damage share**: the part of all enchant damage this enchant deals.
- **Proc rate**: how often its abilities fire when they could.
- **Winrate**: how often fights it was in were won. It needs `min-fights` kills first, and shows "Too few kills" until then.

An enchant above any threshold is flagged. Click one to open the tuner. Thresholds, in `balance.yml` under `outliers`: `damage-share` (0.25), `proc-rate` (0.6), `winrate` (0.65) and `min-fights` (20). Shares and rates go from 0 to 1.

## Metrics

Ability outcomes are counted in memory and written to the database once a minute: see [Database](/docs/exylialib/database) and `database.yml`. `metrics.enabled` (default `true`) turns it off. `metrics.per-player` (default `false`) also keeps each player's procs and damage per enchant, deleted after 30 days.

<Callout type="tip" title="A routine">
After you add or import enchants, run the simulation for the new ones, tune any that stand out, then watch the balance screen for a week of real play.
</Callout>
