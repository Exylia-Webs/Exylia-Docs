Every player can choose how much of other players' enchant effects they see, and whether enchants show extra description and stat lines. Both choices are per player and cost nothing to give: no permission is needed.

## Change your settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Run `/enchants settings`, or open the server-wide `/settings` screen and find the Enchants category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Others' effects">
    Click **Others' effects** to step through **On**, **Reduced** and **Off**. Right-click steps back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enchant details">
    Click **Enchant details** to turn the extra tooltip lines on or off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each choice does

| Setting | Values | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Others' effects | On | You see every effect from other players in full. |
| | Reduced | Sounds and about a third of the particles. |
| | Off | Sounds only. |
| Enchant details | On / Off | The description and stat lines under each enchant name. |

Your own effects are always shown in full. These settings change only what you see, never what the enchant does.

## Defaults

Others' effects starts at **On**. Enchant details starts at the value of `tooltip.player-default` in `config.yml` (`true` by default). The lines also need `tooltip.enabled` and PacketEvents. See [Configuration](/docs/exyliaenchants/configuration).

Settings are stored by ExyliaLib: see [Player settings](/docs/exylialib/player-settings).
