You will browse the catalogue, give yourself an enchanted item, apply a book, and see the result in game.

## Before you start

[Install ExyliaEnchants](/docs/exyliaenchants/installation) and make yourself an operator, or give yourself the permission `exyliaenchants.admin`.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the catalogue">
    Run `/enchants`. Every enchant is a card. Use the **Rarity** and **Item** filters to narrow the list, and click an enchant to preview it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a sword and an enchant">
    Hold a diamond sword. The enchant `bleed` (Bleed) is a good first choice: it applies to swords and opens a wound that deals damage over time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enchant the item directly">
    Run `/enchantsadmin give <your name> bleed 2`. The sword you hold now carries Bleed II.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give yourself a book">
    Run `/enchantsadmin give <your name> bleed 2 -book`. You get a book that rolls its success and destroy odds from its rarity. Add `-safe` for a guaranteed book.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the book">
    Run `/enchants apply`, put the book in the left slot and another sword in the right slot, then click **Book > Apply**. You can also drag the book onto the item in your inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See it work">
    Hit a mob or the training dummy from `/enchantsadmin dummy`. Chat lists what each enchant added to the hit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Hover over the sword. Bleed shows with its level, and, with PacketEvents installed, its description and stats.

## Going further

- Open `/enchants enchanter` to see how players buy books and support items: [The enchanter](/docs/exyliaenchants/the-enchanter).
- Players find enchants in the enchanting table, from librarians and in loot: [Obtain enchants](/docs/exyliaenchants/obtaining).
- To limit how much one item can hold, see the [power budget](/docs/exyliaenchants/power-budget).
- Players can switch off other players' effects and the extra tooltip lines: [Player settings](/docs/exyliaenchants/player-settings).
